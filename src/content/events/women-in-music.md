## Overview

Billboard Women in Music is Billboard's annual event honouring women in the music industry — artists, executives, producers and songwriters whose work shaped the year — held in the [United States](/places/united-states/). Its highest honour is Woman of the Year, first given to Reba McEntire in 2007 and most recently to EJAE, Audrey Nuna and Rei Ami, the singing voices of the fictional group HUNTR/X, in 2026 ([Billboard, via Radio 88.8](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2022/02/07/here-is-every-woman-of-the-year-at-billboards-women-in-music-awards/); [Billboard Philippines](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/news/huntr-xs-ejae-audrey-nuna-and-rei-ami-announced-as-billboards-women-of-the-year-for-2026/)).

The night honours women at every career stage, from rising stars to lifetime icons, and from chart-topping artists to the executives and agents working behind the scenes. The 2025 edition — billed as the 18th overall — honoured 14 artists, including Doechii as Woman of the Year, and five booking agents as Executives of the Year ([Billboard Philippines](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/news/doechii-jennie-aespa-gracie-abrams-and-more-awarded-at-billboard-women-in-music-2025/)). Billboard describes the event as a celebration of "the incredible women who continue to drive innovation, inspire change, and reshape the future of music" ([Billboard Philippines](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/news/doechii-jennie-aespa-gracie-abrams-and-more-awarded-at-billboard-women-in-music-2025/)).

This is Billboard's event, not the separate Women in Music nonprofit — a 501(c)3 organisation founded in New York City in 1985 that runs chapters for women music professionals ([KNKX Public Radio](https://www.knkx.org/jazz/2025-03-03/seattle-organizers-launch-new-women-in-music-chapter)). It is also distinct from the [Billboard Music Awards](/events/billboard-music-awards/), Billboard's separate awards ceremony ([SPIN](https://www.spinmagazine.com/2019/11/billboard-2019-women-in-music/)).

## Origins and format

Billboard's Women in Music began with the Woman of the Year award in 2007, when Reba McEntire became its first recipient for her success as a recording artist, her contributions to the business and her leadership in embracing the changing music business ([Billboard, via Radio 88.8](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2022/02/07/here-is-every-woman-of-the-year-at-billboards-women-in-music-awards/)). The event itself may be a year older: Playbill described the 2014 ceremony as the event's ninth year, which points to a first edition in 2006, while Billboard's own releases called the 2019 event the 14th annual — and later coverage calls the 2025 edition the 18th overall, which implies yet another count ([Playbill](http://www.playbill.com/article/ariana-grande-idina-menzel-and-aretha-franklin-are-honored-at-todays-billboard-women-in-music-awards-com-337233); [Billboard Philippines](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/news/doechii-jennie-aespa-gracie-abrams-and-more-awarded-at-billboard-women-in-music-2025/)). The counts disagree, so this profile does not assert a single edition number.

Early ceremonies were held in [New York City](/places/new-york-city/): the 2014 event took place there on the afternoon of 12 December ([Playbill](http://www.playbill.com/article/ariana-grande-idina-menzel-and-aretha-franklin-are-honored-at-todays-billboard-women-in-music-awards-com-337233)). The event later moved west — the 2019 ceremony was at the Hollywood Palladium in Los Angeles, and the 2024, 2025 and 2026 editions were held at Los Angeles venues, the YouTube Theater and the Hollywood Palladium ([BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwmusic/article/Taylor-Swift-To-Be-Named-Woman-Of-The-Decade-At-Billboards-Women-In-Music-Event-20191118); [Remezcla](https://remezcla.com/music/karol-g-is-named-billboards-2024-woman-of-the-year/?amp)). The 2020 ceremony was streamed, with [Cardi B](/people/cardi-b/) accepting her award on 10 December of that year ([Entertainment Tonight](https://www.etonline.com/breonna-taylors-mom-praises-cardi-bs-efforts-to-spread-the-truth-at-billboard-women-in-music-event)).

## The Woman of the Year award

The Woman of the Year award is the evening's crowning honour, given to a woman whose music, performances and influence defined the year. Taylor Swift is the only person to have received it twice, in 2011 and 2014, and she later received the first-ever Woman of the Decade award at the 2019 event ([Billboard, via Radio 88.8](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2022/02/07/here-is-every-woman-of-the-year-at-billboards-women-in-music-awards/); [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwmusic/article/Taylor-Swift-To-Be-Named-Woman-Of-The-Decade-At-Billboards-Women-In-Music-Event-20191118)). The award's history also tracks the industry's widening scope: Cardi B was the first rapper to receive it in 2020, Karol G the first Latina in 2024, and in 2026 the honour went for the first time to a group — the three singing voices of HUNTR/X ([Entertainment Tonight](https://www.etonline.com/breonna-taylors-mom-praises-cardi-bs-efforts-to-spread-the-truth-at-billboard-women-in-music-event); [Associated Press](https://aseanews.net/2024/03/08/entertainment-karol-g-first-latina-named-woman-of-the-year-by-billboard/); [Billboard Philippines](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/news/huntr-xs-ejae-audrey-nuna-and-rei-ami-announced-as-billboards-women-of-the-year-for-2026/)). No Woman of the Year was named in 2021; the next ceremony came in 2022, when Olivia Rodrigo took the honour ([Billboard Philippines](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/features/here-are-some-of-billboards-most-iconic-woman-of-the-year-awardees-over-the-years-beyonce-taylor-swift-2024/)).

## Other honours

Beyond the headline award, the event recognises careers at every stage and the people behind the artists. The Icon Award goes to artists of extraordinary accomplishment: [Aretha Franklin](/people/aretha-franklin/) received the first one in 2014, and Alanis Morissette followed in 2019 ([Playbill](http://www.playbill.com/article/ariana-grande-idina-menzel-and-aretha-franklin-are-honored-at-todays-billboard-women-in-music-awards-com-337233); [SPIN](https://www.spinmagazine.com/2019/11/billboard-2019-women-in-music/)). [Nicki Minaj](/people/nicki-minaj/) received the Game Changer award in 2019 after becoming the first woman to notch 100 appearances on the Billboard Hot 100 chart, and Brandi Carlile took the Trailblazer award the same year ([SPIN](https://www.spinmagazine.com/2019/11/billboard-2019-women-in-music/)). Executives are honoured too: Roc Nation chief operating officer Desiree Perez was Executive of the Year in 2019, and five booking agents shared the title in 2025 ([SPIN](https://www.spinmagazine.com/2019/11/billboard-2019-women-in-music/); [Billboard Philippines](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/news/doechii-jennie-aespa-gracie-abrams-and-more-awarded-at-billboard-women-in-music-2025/)).

## Going global

In 2023 the franchise expanded beyond the United States with the first Billboard Latin Women in Music, held as Mujeres Latinas en la Música at the Watsco Center in Miami on 6 May and aired on Telemundo and Peacock the next day ([American Songwriter](https://americansongwriter.com/shakira-to-be-named-2023-woman-of-the-year-at-billboards-first-latin-women-in-music-event/)). Shakira was named the first Latin Woman of the Year, with Ana Gabriel, Emilia, Evaluna, Goyo and Thalía also honoured, and the show was hosted by Ivy Queen and Jacqueline Bracamontes ([American Songwriter](https://americansongwriter.com/shakira-to-be-named-2023-woman-of-the-year-at-billboards-first-latin-women-in-music-event/)). Shakira's selection followed a year in which her collaboration "Shakira: BZRP Music Sessions #53" peaked at No. 9 on the Hot 100 — the first Spanish track by a woman to reach the Top 10 — and she had sold more than 95 million records ([American Songwriter](https://americansongwriter.com/shakira-to-be-named-2023-woman-of-the-year-at-billboards-first-latin-women-in-music-event/)).

The international editions continued from 2024, when new Global Force awards recognised women chosen by Billboard's editions around the world, including Annalisa for Italy, Sarah Geronimo for the Philippines and Luísa Sonza for Brazil ([Associated Press](https://aseanews.net/2024/03/08/entertainment-karol-g-first-latina-named-woman-of-the-year-by-billboard/)). By 2025 the main ceremony itself carried local editions' honours, with ANNA named Billboard Italy Woman of the Year and Charlotte Cardin named Billboard Canada Woman of the Year ([Billboard Philippines](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/news/doechii-jennie-aespa-gracie-abrams-and-more-awarded-at-billboard-women-in-music-2025/)).

## Why the event matters

The event exists because women remain a minority at the top of the music business: only 14% of the songwriters and 3.4% of the producers represented on the Billboard Top 100 in 2022 were women, according to a University of Southern California Annenberg Inclusion Initiative report — and that gap is exactly what a night built around women's work is designed to close ([KNKX Public Radio](https://www.knkx.org/jazz/2025-03-03/seattle-organizers-launch-new-women-in-music-chapter)). The same report found only 13.9% of executives across 70 major and independent music companies were women, which is why the ceremony also honours executives and agents alongside artists ([KNKX Public Radio](https://www.knkx.org/jazz/2025-03-03/seattle-organizers-launch-new-women-in-music-chapter)). The speeches have carried that message onto the stage: Cardi B's 2020 honour was presented by Tamika Palmer, the mother of Breonna Taylor, in recognition of the rapper's outspoken political activism, and Karol G delivered her 2024 acceptance speech in Spanish after declaring it the first time a Latina had been named Woman of the Year ([Entertainment Tonight](https://www.etonline.com/breonna-taylors-mom-praises-cardi-bs-efforts-to-spread-the-truth-at-billboard-women-in-music-event); [Associated Press](https://aseanews.net/2024/03/08/entertainment-karol-g-first-latina-named-woman-of-the-year-by-billboard/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Billboard Women in Music?** It is Billboard's annual event honouring women in the music industry — artists, executives, producers and songwriters — with Woman of the Year as its highest honour. The first award went to Reba McEntire in 2007, and the event has since expanded to Latin and international editions ([Billboard, via Radio 88.8](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2022/02/07/here-is-every-woman-of-the-year-at-billboards-women-in-music-awards/); [American Songwriter](https://americansongwriter.com/shakira-to-be-named-2023-woman-of-the-year-at-billboards-first-latin-women-in-music-event/)).

**Who was the first Woman of the Year?** Reba McEntire, in 2007. Billboard chose her for her success as a recording artist, her contributions to the business and her leadership in embracing the changing music business. The award has been presented every year since, except in 2021, when no Woman of the Year was named ([Billboard, via Radio 88.8](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2022/02/07/here-is-every-woman-of-the-year-at-billboards-women-in-music-awards/)).

**Who is the most recent Woman of the Year?** EJAE, Audrey Nuna and Rei Ami, the singing voices of the fictional group HUNTR/X in Netflix's KPop Demon Hunters. They were honoured at the 2026 ceremony on 29 April 2026 at the Hollywood Palladium, the first time a group shared the award ([Billboard Philippines](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/news/huntr-xs-ejae-audrey-nuna-and-rei-ami-announced-as-billboards-women-of-the-year-for-2026/); [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwmusic/article/Photos-KPOP-DEMON-HUNTERS-Stars-Laufey-More-at-Billboard-Women-In-Music-2026-20260430)).

**Is it the same as the Billboard Music Awards?** No. Women in Music is a separate Billboard event honouring women's contributions to the industry, from artists to executives and creators. The Billboard Music Awards are a different Billboard ceremony with their own honorees — Nicki Minaj, for example, has won four Billboard Music Awards ([SPIN](https://www.spinmagazine.com/2019/11/billboard-2019-women-in-music/)).

**What is Billboard Latin Women in Music?** A Spanish-language spin-off of the franchise, first held in Miami in May 2023, honouring Latin women in music. Shakira was its first Latin Woman of the Year, and the show aired on Telemundo and Peacock. It was hosted by Ivy Queen and Jacqueline Bracamontes ([American Songwriter](https://americansongwriter.com/shakira-to-be-named-2023-woman-of-the-year-at-billboards-first-latin-women-in-music-event/)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not claim a single settled edition number: Billboard's own releases counted the 2014 event as its ninth year and the 2019 event as the 14th annual, while later coverage calls the 2025 event the 18th overall, and the two counts cannot both be right on the same baseline ([Playbill](http://www.playbill.com/article/ariana-grande-idina-menzel-and-aretha-franklin-are-honored-at-todays-billboard-women-in-music-awards-com-337233); [Billboard Philippines](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/news/doechii-jennie-aespa-gracie-abrams-and-more-awarded-at-billboard-women-in-music-2025/)). It does not explain the missing 2021 Woman of the Year, because no fully-read source gives a reason, and it does not list recipients for 2008, 2010, 2012, 2013 or 2017, which rest on Wikipedia alone ([Billboard Philippines](https://billboardphilippines.com/music/features/here-are-some-of-billboards-most-iconic-woman-of-the-year-awardees-over-the-years-beyonce-taylor-swift-2024/)). It does not name a founder, since the claim that Tamara Conniff launched the event was not verified in a fully-read source. It does not state which award Kylie Minogue received in 2024, because the Associated Press report only says she was recognised at the event ([Associated Press](https://aseanews.net/2024/03/08/entertainment-karol-g-first-latina-named-woman-of-the-year-by-billboard/)). And it does not rest on Billboard.com directly: that domain is blocked in this environment, so Billboard's own announcements are cited through syndication and Billboard Philippines.
