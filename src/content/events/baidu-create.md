## Overview

Baidu Create is [Baidu](/organizations/baidu/)'s annual AI developer conference, held since 2017 as the company's answer to Apple WWDC and Google I/O ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2018/07/14/3-key-revelations-from-baidus-developer-conference.aspx)). Each edition is where Baidu announces the next phase of its AI strategy: the Kunlun chip and mass-produced self-driving buses in 2018, the Apollo 5.0 platform in 2019, a metaverse-hosted edition in 2021, the ERNIE developer toolkits in 2024, cheaper and faster ERNIE models in 2025, and — in 2026 — a pivot from competing on models to competing on AI agents ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2018/07/14/3-key-revelations-from-baidus-developer-conference.aspx); [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/05/14/baidu-create-2026-ceo-says-ai-is-moving-from-model-competition-to-ai-agent-era-foresees-rise-of-super-individuals/)). The through-line across nine years is consistent: Create is the stage where Baidu's co-founder and CEO [Robin Li](/people/robin-li/) tells developers — and the market — what Baidu thinks the AI era's next act will be.

## Origins: a developer conference for the AI pivot, 2017–2019

Baidu launched Create in 2017, its first-ever AI-focused developer event, explicitly seeking to join the ranks of companies hosting world-class developer conferences ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2018/07/14/3-key-revelations-from-baidus-developer-conference.aspx)). The second edition, in July 2018, set the template: three announcements that together showed Baidu staking claims across chips, cars and partnerships ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2018/07/14/3-key-revelations-from-baidus-developer-conference.aspx)). First, Kunlun, "China's first cloud-to-edge AI chip", built for both inference and training and deployable in data centres, public clouds and autonomous vehicles ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2018/07/14/3-key-revelations-from-baidus-developer-conference.aspx)). Second, autonomous driving: Baidu said it had realised its goal of having an autonomous vehicle in mass production by July 2018, as the 100th Apolong self-driving mini-bus — a 14-seat Level 4 vehicle built with King Long on Apollo 3.0, described as "China's first commercially deployed fully autonomous bus" — rolled off the assembly line live at the conference ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2018/07/14/3-key-revelations-from-baidus-developer-conference.aspx)). Third, a partnership with SB Drive, SoftBank's self-driving subsidiary, to take Apolong to Japan, starting with ten buses ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2018/07/14/3-key-revelations-from-baidus-developer-conference.aspx)). Baidu described Apollo, its autonomous-driving platform, as aiming to be "the Android of the auto industry", with more than 100 global partners ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2018/07/14/3-key-revelations-from-baidus-developer-conference.aspx)).

Create 2019, which kicked off in Beijing on 3 July 2019, widened the aperture from cars to the whole AI stack ([Synced](http://syncedreview.com/2019/07/03/baidu-paddlepaddle-dl-framework-huawei-kirin-soc-a-formidable-partnership/)). Baidu announced that its PaddlePaddle deep-learning framework would power Huawei's Kirin smartphone chips, fully integrated with Huawei's HiAI architecture — a partnership aimed at expanding AI capability on phones that then held 19% of the global smartphone market ([Synced](http://syncedreview.com/2019/07/03/baidu-paddlepaddle-dl-framework-huawei-kirin-soc-a-formidable-partnership/)). Baidu Brain was upgraded to version 5.0, including ERNIE, a knowledge-enhanced language model Baidu said outperformed Google's BERT on Chinese-language tasks ([Synced](http://syncedreview.com/2019/07/03/baidu-paddlepaddle-dl-framework-huawei-kirin-soc-a-formidable-partnership/)). DuerOS, the voice system, reached version 5.0 with full-duplex conversation — Xiaodu could respond without a wake word — on an install base past 400 million and more than 3.6 billion monthly voice queries ([Synced](http://syncedreview.com/2019/07/03/baidu-paddlepaddle-dl-framework-huawei-kirin-soc-a-formidable-partnership/)). Apollo was upgraded to 5.0, with the fleet past two million kilometres across 13 cities, and Baidu announced partnerships with Intel on a Nervana training chip and with Geely, whose Boyue Pro would carry a DuerOS-powered connected-vehicle system ([Synced](http://syncedreview.com/2019/07/03/baidu-paddlepaddle-dl-framework-huawei-kirin-soc-a-formidable-partnership/)).

The 2019 edition is also remembered for an interruption: during Li's keynote, an audience member charged the stage and splashed him with a bottle of water ([Synced](http://syncedreview.com/2019/07/03/baidu-paddlepaddle-dl-framework-huawei-kirin-soc-a-formidable-partnership/)). Li recomposed and told the audience: "There are many difficulties on the road to achieve AI, but they will not impact Baidu's determination" — a line met with applause and chants ([Synced](http://syncedreview.com/2019/07/03/baidu-paddlepaddle-dl-framework-huawei-kirin-soc-a-formidable-partnership/)).

## The metaverse edition and the ERNIE years, 2021–2025

Create 2021, which opened on 27 December 2021, was the conference's most theatrical edition: a three-day event held on XiRang, Baidu's metaverse app — dubbed the "Land of Hope" — which the company described as China's first-ever metaverse technology symposium, with up to 100,000 online attendees interacting simultaneously ([Baidu](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/BIDU/baidu-create-2021-successfully-launched-in-metaverse-ushering-in-a-l13ou8sj7btm.html)). Under the theme "Creators' Spirit", Li said the era of man-computer symbiosis had arrived and creators would usher in a "golden decade of AI" ([Baidu](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/BIDU/baidu-create-2021-successfully-launched-in-metaverse-ushering-in-a-l13ou8sj7btm.html)). He predicted intelligent transportation could cut road accidents by 90% and resolve urban congestion, and said purchase and plate restrictions in first-tier cities would be lifted within five years ([Baidu](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/BIDU/baidu-create-2021-successfully-launched-in-metaverse-ushering-in-a-l13ou8sj7btm.html)). [Apollo Go](/organizations/apollo-go/), the robotaxi service, had provided 115,000 rides by the third quarter of 2021, which Baidu called the world's largest autonomous mobility service, with goals of 65 cities by 2025 and 100 by 2030 ([Baidu](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/BIDU/baidu-create-2021-successfully-launched-in-metaverse-ushering-in-a-l13ou8sj7btm.html)). The edition also launched the XiLing digital-avatar platform and put a number on PaddlePaddle's reach: 4.06 million developers, more than 157,000 enterprises and almost 500,000 models ([Baidu](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/BIDU/baidu-create-2021-successfully-launched-in-metaverse-ushering-in-a-l13ou8sj7btm.html)).

By Create 2024, held on 16 April 2024 in Shenzhen under the theme "Create the Future", the story was ERNIE's scale ([Baidu](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/baidu-presents-a-suite-of-toolkits-and-models-to-supercharge-ai-creativity-at-create)). ERNIE Bot had amassed 200 million users since its debut on 16 March 2023, and the ERNIE API handled 200 million queries a day ([Baidu](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/baidu-presents-a-suite-of-toolkits-and-models-to-supercharge-ai-creativity-at-create)). Li's keynote line was that "in the future, natural language will emerge as the new programming language" and that "simply being able to speak is enough to make you a developer" ([Baidu](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/baidu-presents-a-suite-of-toolkits-and-models-to-supercharge-ai-creativity-at-create)). Baidu launched three plug-and-play toolkits — AgentBuilder for building agents with zero or low code, AppBuilder for AI-native applications, and ModelBuilder curating 77 models — alongside Apollo VTA, a vision foundation model for autonomous driving trained on more than 100 million kilometres of test drives ([Baidu](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/baidu-presents-a-suite-of-toolkits-and-models-to-supercharge-ai-creativity-at-create)).

Create 2025, held on 25 April 2025 in Wuhan, turned the screw on cost. In a 60-minute speech, Li released ERNIE 4.5 Turbo and ERNIE X1 Turbo: the former 80% cheaper than ERNIE 4.5 and priced at 40% of DeepSeek-V3, the latter 50% cheaper than X1 and a quarter of DeepSeek-R1's price, with multimodal capability Baidu said was on par with GPT-4.1 ([Baidu](https://longbridge.com/en/topics/29149096)). The edition launched the world's first e-commerce transaction MCP and search MCP servers, the Xinxiang universal super-agent, the Cangzhou OS content operating system, and a fully self-developed 30,000-card compute cluster; Li also pledged to train 10 million more AI professionals within five years ([Baidu](https://longbridge.com/en/topics/29149096)).

## 2026: the agent era

Create 2026, which opened on 13 May 2026 in Beijing under the theme "Agents at Scale", was where Li declared the model race over as the industry's centre of gravity ([TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/05/14/baidu-create-2026-ceo-says-ai-is-moving-from-model-competition-to-ai-agent-era-foresees-rise-of-super-individuals/)). "For the first time, what really made AI go viral was not the model, but the application," he said, arguing that competition had shifted from model scale and capability to task execution — what users will pay for is no longer whether AI can think, but whether it can get things done ([TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/05/14/baidu-create-2026-ceo-says-ai-is-moving-from-model-competition-to-ai-agent-era-foresees-rise-of-super-individuals/)). His keynote centred on self-evolution as the defining theme of the AI era, unfolding at three levels: agents that learn from their environment and iteratively optimise themselves, individuals entering a "super individual" era in which the smallest productive unit becomes one person plus a fleet of agents, and enterprises flattening as management shifts from supervision to goal alignment ([TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/05/14/baidu-create-2026-ceo-says-ai-is-moving-from-model-competition-to-ai-agent-era-foresees-rise-of-super-individuals/)).

Li also proposed a new metric for the era: Daily Active Agents, the agent equivalent of the mobile internet's daily active users, arguing that tokens measure cost rather than value and predicting that global daily active agents could exceed 10 billion ([TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/05/14/baidu-create-2026-ceo-says-ai-is-moving-from-model-competition-to-ai-agent-era-foresees-rise-of-super-individuals/)). The launches matched the rhetoric: DuMate, a general-purpose agent with a mobile app for customer service, data analysis and poster generation; the Miaoda coding agent and its enterprise version; and Baidu YiJing, a multi-agent digital-human platform for livestreaming and video generation ([TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/05/14/baidu-create-2026-ceo-says-ai-is-moving-from-model-competition-to-ai-agent-era-foresees-rise-of-super-individuals/)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not describe the 2020, 2022 or 2023 editions: no fully-read source was found for them. It does not state a 2019 attendance figure, which appeared only in a source not read in full. It does not present Baidu's benchmark claims — ERNIE beating BERT on Chinese tasks, ERNIE 4.5 Turbo matching GPT-4.1 — as independent findings; they are the company's own claims. It does not claim Create 2021 was objectively China's first metaverse symposium; that is Baidu's description. Attendance and market-leadership figures from Baidu's own releases are reported as the company's figures. What the profile does establish, from independent reporting at both ends of the arc and Baidu's own dated releases in between, is that Create has run annually since 2017 as the public record of Baidu's AI strategy — from chips and robotaxis, through foundation models, to agents.
