## Overview

**PollyGrind Film Festival** is a Las Vegas festival devoted to horror, grindhouse and underground independent film, founded in 2010 by filmmaker Chad Clinton Freeman ([Horror Society](https://www.horrorsociety.com/2013/09/15/pollygrind-film-festival-of-las-vegas-to-world-premiere-18-films-screen-more-than-100-in-october/)). Its second annual edition ran October 7–16, 2011, and gave Adam Rehmeier's [The Bunny Game](/creative-works/the-bunny-game/) its Las Vegas premiere before awarding it the festival's top jury prize, "The Biggest Baddest Mother of the PollyGrind" ([JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/exclusive-details-and-line-up-for-2nd-annual-pollygrind-film-festival/); [Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/28065/exclusive-second-annual-pollygrind-film-festival-announces-winners/)).

## A Las Vegas festival for cinema's underdogs

PollyGrind began in 2010 as a home for films that mainstream festivals would not touch — horror, exploitation, grindhouse and arthouse independents, from features to trailers and music videos ([Horror Society](https://www.horrorsociety.com/2013/09/15/pollygrind-film-festival-of-las-vegas-to-world-premiere-18-films-screen-more-than-100-in-october/)). The filmmaker-friendly reputation stuck: by the fourth annual edition in 2013, the festival drew a record 777 entries and screened more than 100 films from 13 countries, with 18 world premieres, at the Theatre7 venue in the Las Vegas arts district ([Horror Society](https://www.horrorsociety.com/2013/09/15/pollygrind-film-festival-of-las-vegas-to-world-premiere-18-films-screen-more-than-100-in-october/)). Selections went on to land distribution deals from outlets including LionsGate, Showtime, IFC Midnight, Alternative Cinema, Troma and Autonomy Pictures, giving the small festival outsized influence as a launchpad for outsider cinema ([Horror Society](https://www.horrorsociety.com/2013/09/15/pollygrind-film-festival-of-las-vegas-to-world-premiere-18-films-screen-more-than-100-in-october/)). The recognition was external too: MovieMaker Magazine named PollyGrind to its 2012 list of "25 Festivals Worth the Entry Fee," and its readers voted it into the magazine's 2013 Top 5 Coolest Experimental/Underground Film Festivals ([Horror Society](https://www.horrorsociety.com/2013/09/15/pollygrind-film-festival-of-las-vegas-to-world-premiere-18-films-screen-more-than-100-in-october/)).

## The 2011 edition: The Bunny Game's sweep

The second annual edition — billed by Freeman as "PollyGrind2: Return of the Grind" — ran October 7–16, 2011, in Las Vegas, with world premieres including *Finger Bang*, *The Gruesome Death of Tommy Pistol* and *Dear God No!*, plus a slate of Las Vegas premieres headed by *The Bunny Game* ([JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/exclusive-details-and-line-up-for-2nd-annual-pollygrind-film-festival/)). At the awards, Rehmeier's film dominated: it took the top jury prize, "The Biggest Baddest Mother of the PollyGrind," along with Best Sound Design, Best Cinematography and Best Editing, while star Rodleen Getsic won Best Overall Individual Performance as Bunny ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/28065/exclusive-second-annual-pollygrind-film-festival-announces-winners/)). Dread Central described PollyGrind as quickly becoming "one of the up-and-coming premiere genre fests," and Freeman called *The Bunny Game* "the epitome of what PollyGrind is about" — a one-man-crew production he found stylistic, brutal and thought-provoking ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/28065/exclusive-second-annual-pollygrind-film-festival-announces-winners/)). Other 2011 winners included *The Super* (Best Horror Film, Best Director, Best Overall Cast), *Dear God No!* (Best Exploitation Film, audience favorite feature) and *Ratline* (Best Crime Film) ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/28065/exclusive-second-annual-pollygrind-film-festival-announces-winners/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**When did the second PollyGrind Film Festival take place?**
The second annual edition, dubbed "PollyGrind2: Return of the Grind," ran October 7–16, 2011, in Las Vegas, with kickoff programming during the arts district's First Friday event. The lineup mixed world premieres with Las Vegas premieres of films like The Bunny Game, The Super and Ratline.

**Who founded the PollyGrind Film Festival?**
Filmmaker Chad Clinton Freeman founded the festival in 2010 and serves as its director. Horror-industry press consistently identifies him as the festival's founder and programmer, and he announced each year's lineups and winners through the genre press.

**What did The Bunny Game win at PollyGrind?**
At the 2011 edition, Adam Rehmeier's The Bunny Game won the top jury prize, "The Biggest Baddest Mother of the PollyGrind," plus Best Sound Design, Best Cinematography and Best Editing, and its star Rodleen Getsic won Best Overall Individual Performance for her role as Bunny.

**What kinds of films does PollyGrind show?**
Horror, exploitation, grindhouse and underground/arthouse independent film — features, shorts, trailers and music videos. The festival also stages side events like burlesque shows, art exhibits and zombie red-carpet walks, and gives out more than $30,000 in prizes and swag per year.

**Is PollyGrind still running?**
This profile could not verify the festival's activity after 2014 from a full-read source. Its documented editions run 2010–2014, with MovieMaker recognizing it through that period; later status is listed as an open item rather than asserted.
