## Overview

The Billboard Music Awards (BBMAs) are an annual music-awards event built around performance on the Billboard charts. The official BBMAs site says finalists and honorees are selected using album and digital-song sales, radio airplay, streaming, touring and social engagement, with data tracked by Billboard and its partners over a 12-month reporting period ([Billboard Music Awards](https://www.billboardmusicawards.com/about/)). Because the format and distribution can change by edition, this profile does not assign the event a permanent venue or current-year program without a dated source.

## A chart-based awards history

Billboard's historical account says the awards began in 1990 and initially aired yearly through 2006. The show then went on hiatus from 2007 through 2010 and returned on ABC in 2011. From the return onward, the categories honored chart performance over an approximately 12-month span rather than only year-end charts ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/awards/top-billboard-music-award-winners-all-time-1990-2016-7386047/)).

## Recent format and verified 2024 result

The official site describes the 2023 edition as a reimagined format with performances at unique locations across the globe, distributed through BBMAs and Billboard social channels and BBMAs.watch ([Billboard Music Awards](https://www.billboardmusicawards.com/about/)). ABC News reported that the 2024 ceremony took place on December 12, 2024, and that Taylor Swift won 10 awards, bringing her career total to 49 and making her the most decorated artist in the show's history at that time ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Culture/billboard-music-awards-2024-full-winners-list-taylor-swift-makes-history/story?id=116761655)).
