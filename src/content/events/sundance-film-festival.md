## Overview

**The Sundance Film Festival** is the annual independent-film festival held each January in Park City, Utah — the flagship public program of the nonprofit Sundance Institute, which Robert Redford founded in 1981 ([Sundance Institute](https://www.sundance.org/blogs/latest-additions-to-2020-sundance-film-festival-announced-3/); [AP](https://www.shootonline.com/shoot_newsbriefs/after-40-years-park-city-sundance-exploring-options-2027-film-festival-and-beyond/)). Its 2020 edition ran January 23–February 2, selected 118 feature films from a record 15,100 submissions, and gave Adam Rehmeier's *Dinner in America* its world premiere in the U.S. Dramatic Competition, where the film was listed as a Grand Jury Prize nominee ([Consequence](https://consequence.net/2019/12/sundance-2020-lineup/); [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)).

## The launchpad for American independent film

Sundance has spent four decades in Park City as the place where independent films premiere before the industry and the awards season notice: it positions itself as the first major film festival of each calendar year, and drew more than 86,000 festivalgoers in a typical recent year ([AP](https://www.shootonline.com/shoot_newsbriefs/after-40-years-park-city-sundance-exploring-options-2027-film-festival-and-beyond/)). The economic footprint is measurable — the 2023 edition generated just over $118 million for the state of Utah, according to Sundance's own impact report as reported by the AP ([AP](https://www.shootonline.com/shoot_newsbriefs/after-40-years-park-city-sundance-exploring-options-2027-film-festival-and-beyond/)). The Institute's own account traces the lineage to Redford's 1981 founding of the nonprofit, whose year-round labs and grants support the filmmakers the festival later showcases ([Sundance Institute](https://www.sundance.org/blogs/latest-additions-to-2020-sundance-film-festival-announced-3/)).

## Sundance 2020: Dinner in America's premiere

The 2020 edition's competition slate was one of its most selective: 118 features chosen from 15,100 submissions, representing 27 countries, with 44 first-time filmmakers ([Consequence](https://consequence.net/2019/12/sundance-2020-lineup/)). Among the 16 U.S. Dramatic Competition world premieres was Adam Rehmeier's *Dinner in America*, produced by Ben Stiller and starring Kyle Gallner and Emily Skeggs as a punk rocker and a young woman on an odyssey through the decaying suburbs of the American Midwest ([Consequence](https://consequence.net/2019/12/sundance-2020-lineup/)). The film was subsequently listed as a nominee for the Grand Jury Prize at the 2020 festival, before going on to collect more than 20 directing and audience awards on the international circuit — including the Grand Prix at the Odesa International Film Festival and wins at Tallinn, Neuchâtel, Dublin and Nightstream ([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)). The selection arithmetic underlines what competition placement means at Sundance: 118 features from 15,100 submissions is a selection rate under one percent, which implies the festival's real endorsement for a first-run indie feature is the competition slot itself ([Consequence](https://consequence.net/2019/12/sundance-2020-lineup/)).

## The Park City question

In April 2024 the Sundance Institute began exploring options for the festival's home starting in 2027, saying it wanted to consider how to keep serving its community while maintaining the festival's essence — with 2025 and 2026 still scheduled for Park City and Salt Lake City ([AP](https://www.shootonline.com/shoot_newsbriefs/after-40-years-park-city-sundance-exploring-options-2027-film-festival-and-beyond/)). The tension is structural: Park City has been home to the Redford-founded festival for 40 years, and the festival now fills the small ski town's hotels during peak season, emptying the slopes — a collision with the town's other major money-maker that has long complicated the relationship ([AP](https://www.shootonline.com/shoot_newsbriefs/after-40-years-park-city-sundance-exploring-options-2027-film-festival-and-beyond/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**When and where is the Sundance Film Festival?**
The festival takes place annually in late January and early February in Park City and Salt Lake City, Utah. The 2020 edition ran January 23–February 2, 2020, in Park City, Salt Lake City and at Sundance Mountain Resort.

**Who runs the Sundance Film Festival?**
The nonprofit Sundance Institute, founded in 1981 by Robert Redford. The Institute presents the festival as its flagship public program alongside year-round labs, grants and mentorships for independent artists.

**What happened to Dinner in America at Sundance 2020?**
Adam Rehmeier's Dinner in America world-premiered in the U.S. Dramatic Competition at the 2020 festival and was listed as a nominee for the Grand Jury Prize. It subsequently won more than 20 directing and audience awards internationally, including the Grand Prix at the Odesa International Film Festival.

**Is Sundance leaving Park City?**
As of the April 2024 AP report, the Institute was exploring options for the festival's location starting in 2027, with a final decision expected by early 2025; the 2025 and 2026 editions were confirmed to stay in Park City and Salt Lake City. This profile does not cover the outcome of that process.

**How selective is Sundance?**
For the 2020 edition, 118 feature films were selected from a record 15,100 submissions — a selection rate below one percent — representing 27 countries and including 44 first-time filmmakers.
