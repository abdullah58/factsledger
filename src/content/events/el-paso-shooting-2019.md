## Overview

On 3 August 2019, 21-year-old Patrick Wood Crusius opened fire at the Cielo Vista Walmart in [El Paso](/places/texas/), Texas, killing 23 people and injuring 22 others in a racist attack on Hispanic shoppers ([US Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/texas-man-sentenced-90-consecutive-life-sentences-2019-mass-shooting-walmart-el-paso-texas)). The FBI investigated the case alongside the El Paso Police Department, the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives and the Texas Department of Public Safety, and Crusius was prosecuted on federal hate-crime charges; the attack has been described as one of the worst attacks on Hispanics in the U.S. in modern history ([US Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/texas-man-sentenced-90-consecutive-life-sentences-2019-mass-shooting-walmart-el-paso-texas); [NPR](https://www.wamc.org/2025-04-21/el-paso-walmart-shooter-receives-rebuke-and-forgiveness-as-he-gets-23-life-sentences?_amp=true)). Crusius pleaded guilty in federal court in 2023 and in Texas state court in 2025, and will spend the rest of his life in prison.

Crusius drove for more than ten hours from his home in suburban Dallas to El Paso, a city on the [United States](/places/united-states/)-Mexico border whose Walmart is popular with shoppers from both countries ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/man-who-fatally-shot-23-at-el-paso-walmart-set-to-plead-guilty-to-murder-nearly-6-years-on/)). Shortly before the attack he posted a manifesto online saying the shooting was "in response to the Hispanic invasion of Texas", and after his arrest he told officers he had targeted Mexicans ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/man-who-fatally-shot-23-at-el-paso-walmart-set-to-plead-guilty-to-murder-nearly-6-years-on/)). In court documents he admitted he killed and wounded people because of the actual and perceived national origin of the people he expected to be at the Walmart, and that he intended to kill everyone he shot ([US Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/texas-man-sentenced-90-consecutive-life-sentences-2019-mass-shooting-walmart-el-paso-texas)).

## The victims and the rising death toll

The people killed ranged in age from a 15-year-old high school athlete to grandparents, and included immigrants and Mexican nationals who had crossed the border on routine shopping trips ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/man-who-fatally-shot-23-at-el-paso-walmart-set-to-plead-guilty-to-murder-nearly-6-years-on/)). Jordan Anchondo and Andre Anchondo were killed while shopping with their two-month-old child, who survived; Jordan Anchondo shielded the baby from gunfire while her husband shielded them both ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/man-who-fatally-shot-23-at-el-paso-walmart-set-to-plead-guilty-to-murder-nearly-6-years-on/)). Guillermo "Memo" Garcia and his wife Jessica Coca Garcia were fundraising for their daughter's soccer team in the parking lot when both were shot; she recovered from leg wounds, but he died from his injuries nearly nine months after the shooting, raising the death toll from 22 to 23 ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/man-who-fatally-shot-23-at-el-paso-walmart-set-to-plead-guilty-to-murder-nearly-6-years-on/)). That delayed death is why early reports give a toll of 20 or 22 while later accounts give 23 — and why the often-repeated combined figure of "31 killed" for the El Paso and [Dayton shootings](/events/dayton-shooting-2019/) is wrong: 23 plus 9 is 32.

## Prosecution and sentencing

The case ran on two tracks. In federal court, Crusius pleaded guilty on 8 February 2023 to a 90-count indictment — 45 counts under the Matthew Shepard and James Byrd Jr. Hate Crimes Prevention Act and 45 counts of using a firearm during crimes of violence — and on 7 July 2023 was sentenced to 90 consecutive life sentences, one for each count ([US Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/texas-man-sentenced-90-consecutive-life-sentences-2019-mass-shooting-walmart-el-paso-texas)). Federal prosecutors had taken the death penalty off the table. The state case took almost six years: El Paso County District Attorney James Montoya offered a plea deal under which Crusius would not face the death penalty, saying a majority of victims' relatives were eager for the case to be resolved, though not all families agreed ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/man-who-fatally-shot-23-at-el-paso-walmart-set-to-plead-guilty-to-murder-nearly-6-years-on/)). On 21 April 2025 Crusius pleaded guilty to capital murder and aggravated assault with a deadly weapon, and Judge Sam Medrano sentenced him to 23 life sentences without the possibility of parole ([NPR](https://www.wamc.org/2025-04-21/el-paso-walmart-shooter-receives-rebuke-and-forgiveness-as-he-gets-23-life-sentences?_amp=true)). Addressing Crusius, Medrano said: "The community you tried to break has become a symbol of resilience, of love overcoming hate, of humanity enduring in the face of evil" ([NPR](https://www.wamc.org/2025-04-21/el-paso-walmart-shooter-receives-rebuke-and-forgiveness-as-he-gets-23-life-sentences?_amp=true)).

## Aftermath: 8chan, Cloudflare, and the memorial

The attack had an immediate consequence for the infrastructure of the internet. Crusius had posted his manifesto, titled "An Inconvenient Truth", to the imageboard [8chan](/organizations/8chan/) minutes before the attack, characterising himself as a white nationalist ([US Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/texas-man-sentenced-90-consecutive-life-sentences-2019-mass-shooting-walmart-el-paso-texas)); [Cloudflare](/organizations/cloudflare/) said he "specifically referenced the [Christchurch](/events/christchurch-mosque-shootings/) incident" in it ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/terminating-service-for-8chan/)). Cloudflare, which provided security services to 8chan, said the gunman "appears to have been inspired by the forum website known as 8chan" and terminated the site as a customer effective midnight Pacific Time on 5 August 2019, writing that "8chan has repeatedly proven itself to be a cesspool of hate" ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/terminating-service-for-8chan/)). Cloudflare noted that the gunman in the Poway, California synagogue shooting had also posted on 8chan, and that it had removed the [Daily Stormer](/organizations/daily-stormer/) about two years earlier ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/terminating-service-for-8chan/)).

In El Paso itself, the Cielo Vista Walmart reopened on 14 November 2019 after renovation, with a permanent memorial in the parking lot: the Grand Candela, a 30-foot structure of 22 perforated aluminium arcs grouped into a single candle symbolising unity ([Walmart](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2019/10/29/walmart-confirms-cielo-vista-store-will-re-open-nov-14-unveils-design-for-permanent-memorial)). The memorial was designed when the death toll stood at 22, before Garcia's death the following April.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people were killed in the El Paso shooting?

Twenty-three people were killed and 22 injured ([US Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/texas-man-sentenced-90-consecutive-life-sentences-2019-mass-shooting-walmart-el-paso-texas)). Twenty-two people died on the day of the attack, and Guillermo "Memo" Garcia died from his injuries nearly nine months later, raising the toll to 23 ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/man-who-fatally-shot-23-at-el-paso-walmart-set-to-plead-guilty-to-murder-nearly-6-years-on/)).

### Why do some reports say 20 or 22 were killed?

The toll rose over time as victims died of their injuries. Cloudflare's post of 5 August 2019 says the attack killed 20 people, the figure known that day ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/terminating-service-for-8chan/)). Later reporting put the toll at 22, and Garcia's death in April 2020 brought it to the final figure of 23.

### What happened to Patrick Crusius?

He pleaded guilty to 90 federal hate-crime and firearms charges in 2023 and was sentenced to 90 consecutive life sentences ([US Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/texas-man-sentenced-90-consecutive-life-sentences-2019-mass-shooting-walmart-el-paso-texas)). In 2025 he pleaded guilty to capital murder in Texas state court and was sentenced to life in prison without parole ([NPR](https://www.wamc.org/2025-04-21/el-paso-walmart-shooter-receives-rebuke-and-forgiveness-as-he-gets-23-life-sentences?_amp=true)).

### What was the shooter's motive?

Crusius admitted he targeted people because of their actual and perceived national origin and intended to kill everyone he shot ([US Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/texas-man-sentenced-90-consecutive-life-sentences-2019-mass-shooting-walmart-el-paso-texas)). His manifesto described the shooting as a response to the "Hispanic invasion of Texas" and characterised himself as a white nationalist ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/man-who-fatally-shot-23-at-el-paso-walmart-set-to-plead-guilty-to-murder-nearly-6-years-on/)).

### What happened to 8chan after the shooting?

Cloudflare terminated 8chan as a customer effective midnight Pacific Time on 5 August 2019, saying the site had "proven themselves to be lawless and that lawlessness has caused multiple tragic deaths" ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/terminating-service-for-8chan/)). Cloudflare acknowledged the action would almost certainly not remove 8chan from the internet.

## What this profile does not claim

- It does not reproduce the text of the manifesto or describe the violence in graphic detail; it reports only what the perpetrator admitted and what the courts found.
- It does not state the exact number of Mexican nationals among the dead, which was not verified from a fully-read source.
- It does not treat the early tolls of 20 or 22 as errors — they were the figures known at the time — but it uses the final toll of 23 killed and 22 injured throughout.
- It does not assert that removing 8chan from Cloudflare's network removed the site from the internet; Cloudflare itself said it would almost certainly not do so.
