## Overview

The Film Diplomacy Conference 2025 was a three-day event held in Istanbul from December 19 to 21, 2025, under the theme "Building Diplomats Through Lens." Organized by the Film Diplomacy initiative of founder Raheel Ahmad, it brought filmmakers, diplomats, officials and media figures together around the idea that cinema is a working tool of international relations — and presented Turkey's 2017 film *Ayla* as a case study in strengthening ties between Türkiye and South Korea ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/film-diplomacy-conference-2025-opens-in-istanbul-to-merge-cinema-international-relations/3775757); [Yeni Şafak](https://en.yenisafak.com/turkiye/turkish-artists-champion-film-diplomacy-at-istanbul-conference-cite-ayla-3712120)).

The conference opened on Friday, December 19, 2025, in Istanbul and drew participants from around the world for a three-day international program ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/film-diplomacy-conference-2025-opens-in-istanbul-to-merge-cinema-international-relations/3775757)). Ahmad told reporters that film and diplomacy are usually treated as separate worlds — film as entertainment, diplomacy as politics — and that the conference's aim was to connect them under one vision: building diplomats with a lens ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/film-diplomacy-conference-2025-opens-in-istanbul-to-merge-cinema-international-relations/3775757)). He said the organization's primary goal was to raise awareness of conflicts happening around the world, including those in Palestine and Sudan, and claimed the organization had collaborated with the [United Nations](/organizations/united-nations/) Youth Office and 67 organizations worldwide ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/film-diplomacy-conference-2025-opens-in-istanbul-to-merge-cinema-international-relations/3775757)).

## A three-day program: training, filmmaking, awards

The conference was structured as a three-day international event that deliberately mixed learning, production and recognition ([Yeni Şafak](https://en.yenisafak.com/turkiye/turkish-artists-champion-film-diplomacy-at-istanbul-conference-cite-ayla-3712120)). Its first component was a training day led by diplomats, officials, filmmakers, media agencies, sustainability trainers and intercultural experts — a roster that put practitioners of diplomacy and practitioners of cinema in the same rooms ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/film-diplomacy-conference-2025-opens-in-istanbul-to-merge-cinema-international-relations/3775757)). The second was a 24-hour film challenge in which teams produced short films on global peace and cooperation, turning the conference's thesis — storytelling as diplomacy — into a hands-on exercise ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/film-diplomacy-conference-2025-opens-in-istanbul-to-merge-cinema-international-relations/3775757); [Yeni Şafak](https://en.yenisafak.com/turkiye/turkish-artists-champion-film-diplomacy-at-istanbul-conference-cite-ayla-3712120)). The third was an award and screening day, with the short films judged by diplomats, directors and media figures ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/film-diplomacy-conference-2025-opens-in-istanbul-to-merge-cinema-international-relations/3775757)). Across all three days, the programming kept returning to one idea: that cross-cultural understanding and cooperative international dialogue can be strategically pursued through narrative film ([Yeni Şafak](https://en.yenisafak.com/turkiye/turkish-artists-champion-film-diplomacy-at-istanbul-conference-cite-ayla-3712120)).

## The keynote argument: film has been diplomacy since the camera

The conference's headline intellectual argument came from Turkish composer Burak Saygili, who told attendees that films have been used as a diplomatic tool since the invention of the camera ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/films-used-as-diplomacy-tool-since-cameras-invention-turkish-artist-says/3776042)). Saygili argued that Hollywood cinema, like other national cinemas, uses cinema and the camera as a diplomatic instrument, citing *The Pianist*, *Schindler's List* and *Inglourious Basterds* as films used to emphasize the Holocaust and Hitler's atrocities ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/films-used-as-diplomacy-tool-since-cameras-invention-turkish-artist-says/3776042)). His media history ran from the camera's invention — which made written text outdated — to a present in which digital channels have advanced so far that the big screen itself is becoming obsolete; anyone speaking in the field of diplomacy, he said, must work through digital channels ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/films-used-as-diplomacy-tool-since-cameras-invention-turkish-artist-says/3776042)). His caveat was the conference's sharpest line: cultural diplomacy must not turn into cultural imperialism. "Cultures should develop with each other, but we should not impose one culture on another," he said, pointing at globally homogenizing content from major studios ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/films-used-as-diplomacy-tool-since-cameras-invention-turkish-artist-says/3776042); [Yeni Şafak](https://en.yenisafak.com/turkiye/turkish-artists-champion-film-diplomacy-at-istanbul-conference-cite-ayla-3712120)).

## *Ayla* as the conference's case study

The conference's featured proof-of-concept was the 2017 Turkish–South Korean co-production [*Ayla: The Daughter of War*](/creative-works/ayla-the-daughter-of-war/), presented as a successful application of film diplomacy that measurably improved cultural and diplomatic relations between Türkiye and South Korea ([Yeni Şafak](https://en.yenisafak.com/turkiye/turkish-artists-champion-film-diplomacy-at-istanbul-conference-cite-ayla-3712120)). Murat Emir Kaya of the AK Party's Youth Branch in Istanbul singled out a scene about ants — the film tells the story of a Turkish sergeant who helps a little girl during the Korean War — as a successful use of diplomacy, saying it conveyed that Turks had gone to Korea for peace; he added that the film significantly increased cultural and diplomatic relations with South Korea ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/films-used-as-diplomacy-tool-since-cameras-invention-turkish-artist-says/3776042)). Director Can Ülkay emphasized awareness as cinema's most important function, saying some viewers researched the Korean War after watching — and noting, "Koreans really love us a lot … but we were not aware" ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/films-used-as-diplomacy-tool-since-cameras-invention-turkish-artist-says/3776042)).

## Frequently asked questions

**When and where was the Film Diplomacy Conference 2025 held?**
The conference opened in Istanbul on Friday, December 19, 2025, and ran as a three-day international event — December 19 to 21 — with participants from around the world. Anadolu Agency reported the opening from on site that day, and Yeni Şafak confirmed the three-day format ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/film-diplomacy-conference-2025-opens-in-istanbul-to-merge-cinema-international-relations/3775757); [Yeni Şafak](https://en.yenisafak.com/turkiye/turkish-artists-champion-film-diplomacy-at-istanbul-conference-cite-ayla-3712120)).

**Who organized the conference?**
It was organized by the Film Diplomacy initiative, founded by Raheel Ahmad. He told reporters the goal was to connect film and diplomacy under a single vision of "building diplomats with lens," and said the primary purpose was raising awareness of global conflicts ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/film-diplomacy-conference-2025-opens-in-istanbul-to-merge-cinema-international-relations/3775757)).

**What was the 24-hour film challenge?**
It was the conference's hands-on centerpiece: teams had 24 hours to produce short films on global peace and cooperation, and the resulting films were screened and judged by diplomats, directors and media figures on the awards day ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/film-diplomacy-conference-2025-opens-in-istanbul-to-merge-cinema-international-relations/3775757); [Yeni Şafak](https://en.yenisafak.com/turkiye/turkish-artists-champion-film-diplomacy-at-istanbul-conference-cite-ayla-3712120)).

**Why was *Ayla* discussed at the conference?**
The 2017 Turkish–South Korean film was presented as a successful application of film diplomacy: its director Can Ülkay said it raised awareness of the Korean War, and AK Party youth representative Murat Emir Kaya credited it with tangibly improving Türkiye–South Korea cultural and diplomatic relations ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/films-used-as-diplomacy-tool-since-cameras-invention-turkish-artist-says/3776042); [Yeni Şafak](https://en.yenisafak.com/turkiye/turkish-artists-champion-film-diplomacy-at-istanbul-conference-cite-ayla-3712120)).

**Was the conference covered by independent media?**
Yes. Anadolu Agency filed two bylined reports from the event on December 19, 2025, and Yeni Şafak published an independent report; both outlets agree on the event's name, theme, three-day program, speakers and the *Ayla* case study ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/culture/films-used-as-diplomacy-tool-since-cameras-invention-turkish-artist-says/3776042); [Yeni Şafak](https://en.yenisafak.com/turkiye/turkish-artists-champion-film-diplomacy-at-istanbul-conference-cite-ayla-3712120)).
