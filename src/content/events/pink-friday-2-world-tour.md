## Overview

The Pink Friday 2 World Tour was [Nicki Minaj](/people/nicki-minaj/)'s 2024 concert tour in support of her fifth studio album, *Pink Friday 2*. Running from 1 March to 11 October 2024 across North America, Europe and North Africa, it became the highest-grossing tour by a female rapper, grossing $108.8 million from 786,840 tickets across 70 reported shows ([Billboard](https://z89online.com/2024/05/nicki-minajs-pink-friday-2-world-tour-is-already-the-highest-grossing-rap-tour-for-a-woman-and-it-isnt-over-yet/); [Hot 97](https://www.hot97.com/news/50-cent-disputes-nicki-minajs-tour-ranking/)).

Pollstar reported the 37-date routing on 11 December 2023, billing the run as Minaj's first tour since 2019 and her largest to date ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2023/12/11/nicki-minaj-announces-pink-friday-2-world-tour/)). The album it supported, *Pink Friday 2*, had arrived in December 2023 and debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200, giving Minaj the most No. 1 albums of any female rapper ([The Source](https://thesource.com/2024/05/30/nicki-minaj-announces-second-leg-of-record-breaking-pink-friday-2-world-tour/)). Fans nicknamed the run the Gag City World Tour, after a pink metropolis world created by Minaj's Barbz fanbase that the staging brought to life ([The Source](https://thesource.com/2024/05/30/nicki-minaj-announces-second-leg-of-record-breaking-pink-friday-2-world-tour/); [The Daily Aztec](https://thedailyaztec.com/119002/artsandculture/nicki-minaj-delivers-a-spectacle-of-entertainment-at-viejas-arena/)).

## From announcement to opening night

Minaj announced the tour's full routing on 11 December 2023: 37 dates across the [United States](/places/united-states/), Canada, the United Kingdom and Europe, with tickets going on general sale on 15 December ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2023/12/11/nicki-minaj-announces-pink-friday-2-world-tour/)). The routing mixed arena headline dates with festival headline slots at Rolling Loud California in Inglewood and the Dreamville Festival in Raleigh ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2023/12/11/nicki-minaj-announces-pink-friday-2-world-tour/)). It was her first tour since 2019 ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2023/12/11/nicki-minaj-announces-pink-friday-2-world-tour/)). Monica joined as the opening act for the North American leg, confirming the booking on *The Jennifer Hudson Show* on 8 February 2024 ([HotNewHipHop](https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/761953-nicki-minaj-monica-opener-pink-friday-2-tour-hip-hop-news)). The tour opened on 1 March 2024 at the Oakland Arena, where Minaj took in $2.3 million from about 14,000 tickets on the first night ([Billboard](https://z89online.com/2024/05/nicki-minajs-pink-friday-2-world-tour-is-already-the-highest-grossing-rap-tour-for-a-woman-and-it-isnt-over-yet/)). The opening set mixed new album songs with catalogue hits, a pattern the show kept all year: "FTCU" and "Everybody" beside "Anaconda", "Super Bass", "Starships" and "Chun-Li" ([The Source](https://thesource.com/2024/05/30/nicki-minaj-announces-second-leg-of-record-breaking-pink-friday-2-world-tour/)).

## Two legs, two line-ups

The first North American leg ran 34 arena shows from Oakland to Oklahoma City, closing on 13 May 2024 ([Billboard](https://z89online.com/2024/05/nicki-minajs-pink-friday-2-world-tour-is-already-the-highest-grossing-rap-tour-for-a-woman-and-it-isnt-over-yet/)). Minaj then crossed the Atlantic, opening the European leg at Amsterdam's Ziggo Dome on 23 May, with 15 headline dates planned alongside festival appearances that stretched from Mawazine in Rabat, Morocco, to Rolling Loud Europe in Vienna and Wireless in London ([Billboard](https://z89online.com/2024/05/nicki-minajs-pink-friday-2-world-tour-is-already-the-highest-grossing-rap-tour-for-a-woman-and-it-isnt-over-yet/); [Consequence](https://consequence.net/2024/05/how-to-nicki-minaj-pink-friday-2-world-tour/)). Demand then added a second North American leg: 22 more dates announced in late May 2024, running from Philadelphia on 4 September to a hometown finale at UBS Arena on 11 October ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/05/29/nicki-minaj-announces-second-leg-of-pink-friday-2/); [The Source](https://thesource.com/2024/05/30/nicki-minaj-announces-second-leg-of-record-breaking-pink-friday-2-world-tour/)). For that leg Minaj rebuilt the show as "Gag City Reloaded", with new staging, wardrobe and setlist, and a new opening line-up: Tyga, BIA and Skillibeng, announced on 16 August 2024 ([Hot 97](https://www.hot97.com/news/nicki-minaj-announces-surprise-new-tour-openers/)). A first-hand report from the San Diego date on 26 September confirms the new shape: the three openers played in turn before Minaj's pink-metropolis staging took over ([The Daily Aztec](https://thedailyaztec.com/119002/artsandculture/nicki-minaj-delivers-a-spectacle-of-entertainment-at-viejas-arena/)).

## Box office and records

By mid-May the tour had already made history. Figures reported to Billboard Boxscore put the 34-show first North American leg at $67 million from 439,000 tickets, the highest-grossing and best-selling rap tour by a woman in Boxscore history ([Billboard](https://z89online.com/2024/05/nicki-minajs-pink-friday-2-world-tour-is-already-the-highest-grossing-rap-tour-for-a-woman-and-it-isnt-over-yet/)). The leg averaged just under $2 million and about 13,000 tickets a night — nearly triple the per-night pace of her 2012 and 2015–16 tours ([Billboard](https://z89online.com/2024/05/nicki-minajs-pink-friday-2-world-tour-is-already-the-highest-grossing-rap-tour-for-a-woman-and-it-isnt-over-yet/)). The biggest nights clustered around [New York City](/places/new-york-city/): two Brooklyn Barclays Center shows took $4.3 million from 28,100 tickets, and four area shows took $9.3 million from more than 54,000 tickets, with the 30 March Madison Square Garden show the highest-grossing show of the tour so far at $2.857 million ([Billboard](https://z89online.com/2024/05/nicki-minajs-pink-friday-2-world-tour-is-already-the-highest-grossing-rap-tour-for-a-woman-and-it-isnt-over-yet/)). Touring Data's final tally of $108.8 million from 786,840 tickets across 70 reported shows made Minaj the first female rapper to pass $100 million from a single tour — and put a woman's tour among the five highest-grossing hip-hop tours ever, a list previously all male ([Hot 97](https://www.hot97.com/news/50-cent-disputes-nicki-minajs-tour-ranking/); [XXL](https://www.xxlmag.com/nicki-minaj-doja-cat-missy-elliott-and-megan-thee-stallion-220-million-touring/)).

## Disruptions in Europe

The European summer brought the tour's two public setbacks. On 25 May 2024, as Minaj prepared to fly from Amsterdam to Manchester, Dutch police detained her at Schiphol Airport after finding marijuana in her luggage; she was fined and released the same day, but her Co-op Live show in Manchester that night was postponed ([Associated Press](https://gmg-wdiv-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/entertainment/2024/05/25/rapper-nicki-minaj-says-dutch-police-told-her-they-found-pot-in-bags/)). The show was later rescheduled, with Manchester back on the routing for 3 June ([The Source](https://thesource.com/2024/05/30/nicki-minaj-announces-second-leg-of-record-breaking-pink-friday-2-world-tour/)). Six weeks later, on 7 July, Minaj cancelled a headline festival slot at SAGA Festival in Bucharest hours before stage time, saying her security team had advised against travelling because of protests in the area ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/nicki-minaj-cancels-festival-slot-in-romania-following-safety-concerns-over-protest-3772746)). The two episodes were the tour's only cancelled or postponed dates documented in the sources read for this profile, and neither stopped the run: the published European routing still ran through mid-July, and the second North American leg began on schedule in September ([Consequence](https://consequence.net/2024/05/how-to-nicki-minaj-pink-friday-2-world-tour/); [Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/05/29/nicki-minaj-announces-second-leg-of-pink-friday-2/)).

## Reception and legacy

The show won praise for its scale. A first-hand review of the San Diego date described a pink-metropolis staging, multiple costume changes and a set that closed with "Super Bass", "Starships" and "Everybody" to a room on its feet ([The Daily Aztec](https://thedailyaztec.com/119002/artsandculture/nicki-minaj-delivers-a-spectacle-of-entertainment-at-viejas-arena/)). The industry recognition followed the money: the tour was nominated for Hip-Hop Tour of the Year at the 36th Pollstar Awards, announced in November 2024 ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/11/08/nominations-announced-for-36th-annual-pollstar-awards-voting-open-now/)). It did not win — Missy Elliott's Out of This World tour took the award in April 2025 ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/04/17/pollstar-awards-2025-chappell-roan-stevie-nicks-missy-elliott-jelly-roll-taylor-swift-bad-bunny-billie-eilish-among-honorees/)). The tour's standing grew after it ended. In April 2025 Billboard's staff list of the 25 greatest female rappers ranked Minaj No. 1, citing her 149 Hot 100 entries and the Pink Friday 2 World Tour as the highest-grossing tour by a female rapper ([The Source](https://thesource.com/2025/04/09/nicki-minaj-crowned-greatest-female-rapper-of-all-time-by-billboard/)). In that sense the tour did double work: it set a commercial record and became the evidence Billboard used to crown its headliner.

### What was the Pink Friday 2 World Tour?

It was Nicki Minaj's 2024 concert tour supporting her fifth studio album, *Pink Friday 2*. It ran from 1 March to 11 October 2024 across North America, Europe and North Africa, with two North American legs and a European festival run in between ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2023/12/11/nicki-minaj-announces-pink-friday-2-world-tour/); [Consequence](https://consequence.net/2024/05/how-to-nicki-minaj-pink-friday-2-world-tour/)).

### How much did the tour gross?

$108.8 million from 786,840 tickets across 70 reported shows, according to Touring Data figures. That made it the highest-grossing tour by a female rapper and the first by a female rapper to pass $100 million from a single tour. The first North American leg alone took $67 million from 439,000 tickets ([Hot 97](https://www.hot97.com/news/50-cent-disputes-nicki-minajs-tour-ranking/); [Billboard](https://z89online.com/2024/05/nicki-minajs-pink-friday-2-world-tour-is-already-the-highest-grossing-rap-tour-for-a-woman-and-it-isnt-over-yet/)).

### Who opened for Nicki Minaj on the tour?

Monica opened the first North American leg, joining after confirming the booking on *The Jennifer Hudson Show* in February 2024. The second North American leg, subtitled Gag City Reloaded, switched to three openers: Tyga, BIA and Skillibeng, announced in August 2024 ([HotNewHipHop](https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/761953-nicki-minaj-monica-opener-pink-friday-2-tour-hip-hop-news); [Hot 97](https://www.hot97.com/news/nicki-minaj-announces-surprise-new-tour-openers/)).

### Why was the Manchester show postponed?

Minaj was detained by Dutch police at Amsterdam's Schiphol Airport on 25 May 2024 after marijuana was found in her luggage as she prepared to fly to Manchester. She was fined and released the same day, but the Co-op Live show that night was postponed and later rescheduled ([Associated Press](https://gmg-wdiv-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/entertainment/2024/05/25/rapper-nicki-minaj-says-dutch-police-told-her-they-found-pot-in-bags/)).

### Did the tour win any awards?

It was nominated for Hip-Hop Tour of the Year at the 36th Pollstar Awards in November 2024 but did not win; Missy Elliott's Out of This World tour won in April 2025. Its lasting recognition came from Billboard, which cited the tour when ranking Minaj the greatest female rapper of all time ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/11/08/nominations-announced-for-36th-annual-pollstar-awards-voting-open-now/); [The Source](https://thesource.com/2025/04/09/nicki-minaj-crowned-greatest-female-rapper-of-all-time-by-billboard/)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not state a single settled show count: sources give "over 80" shows in total, while the $108.8 million gross rests on 70 reported shows, so the gross and the show total come from different bases ([Hot 97](https://www.hot97.com/news/50-cent-disputes-nicki-minajs-tour-ranking/); [The Source](https://thesource.com/2024/05/30/nicki-minaj-announces-second-leg-of-record-breaking-pink-friday-2-world-tour/)). It does not present the $81 million final-gross figure found in one live-performances listing, which contradicts the Touring Data and XXL figures ([XXL](https://www.xxlmag.com/nicki-minaj-doja-cat-missy-elliott-and-megan-thee-stallion-220-million-touring/)). It does not claim the 14 July 2024 Liège date took place, only that it was the scheduled wrap of the European run ([Consequence](https://consequence.net/2024/05/how-to-nicki-minaj-pink-friday-2-world-tour/)). It does not assert that the second Amsterdam date was cancelled, because no tier1 or tier2 source read for this profile confirms it. And it does not treat gross box-office revenue as Minaj's personal earnings.
