The 90th Academy Awards — the Oscar ceremony honoring 2017's films — took
place on March 4, 2018, at the Dolby Theatre in Hollywood, Los Angeles, with
comedian Jimmy Kimmel hosting for the second consecutive year
([SHOOTonline](https://www.shootonline.com/spw/90th-oscars-nominations-announced/)).
Guillermo del Toro's *The Shape of Water* won Best Picture and led all films
with four Oscars from a field-high 13 nominations
([RogerEbert.com](https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/the-shape-of-water-three-billboards-win-big-at-90th-oscars)).
ABC's telecast drew a record-low 26.5 million U.S. viewers, down about 20%
from the previous year
([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/news-ians/oscars-viewership-ratings-hit-record-low-118030600411_1.html)).

This profile covers the 90th ceremony as a single event — not the Academy
Awards as an institution, which is the subject of a separate profile. The
ceremony was presented by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences in
24 categories ([SHOOTonline](https://www.shootonline.com/spw/90th-oscars-nominations-announced/)),
with 341 feature films eligible as the 2017 releases
([Post Magazine](https://postmagazine.com/press-center/animation/2017/oscars-341-films-qualify-for-best-picture-consideration/)).
The nominees were announced on January 23, 2018, by Tiffany Haddish and Andy
Serkis — joined by Academy president John Bailey — live from the Academy's
Samuel Goldwyn Theater via a global live stream, and final voting ran
February 20–27
([SHOOTonline](https://www.shootonline.com/spw/90th-oscars-nominations-announced/)).
Producers Michael De Luca and Jennifer Todd returned alongside Kimmel, their
reappointment announced May 16, 2017 — roughly six months earlier than usual
([InsideHook](https://www.insidehook.com/film/jimmy-kimmel-invited-back-host-90th-academy-awards)).
The ceremony moved from its usual late-February slot to the first Sunday in
March to avoid clashing with the PyeongChang Winter Olympics closing ceremony,
a shift the Academy makes every four years
([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/academy-timetable-march-2018-oscars/)).

## A night of firsts onstage

The night's lasting story was not the winners list alone but how many firsts
it carried. *The Shape of Water* collected four Oscars — Best Picture, Best
Director for del Toro, Original Score, and Production Design — making del Toro
the fourth Mexican filmmaker in five years to take Best Director, after
Alfonso Cuarón and Alejandro G. Iñárritu
([RogerEbert.com](https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/the-shape-of-water-three-billboards-win-big-at-90th-oscars)).
Gary Oldman won Best Actor for *Darkest Hour* and Frances McDormand her second
Best Actress Oscar for *Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri*; her
speech, ending with every female nominee in the room on their feet, was the
ceremony's signature moment
([RogerEbert.com](https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/the-shape-of-water-three-billboards-win-big-at-90th-oscars)).
Sam Rockwell and Allison Janney took the supporting acting honors
([The Montclarion](https://themontclarion.org/entertainment/the-90th-academy-awards-are-the-most-inclusive-yet/)).
The win was no shoo-in: no film since 1995's *Braveheart* had taken Best
Picture without a Screen Actors Guild ensemble nomination
([RogerEbert.com](https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/the-shape-of-water-three-billboards-win-big-at-90th-oscars)).

| Record set | Who | Category / film |

## The envelope's long shadow

The 90th ceremony was an exercise in atonement. Kimmel, De Luca, and Todd
were rehired despite the 89th Oscars' finale, in which Beatty and Dunaway got
the wrong envelope and announced *La La
Land* instead of *Moonlight*
([InsideHook](https://www.insidehook.com/film/jimmy-kimmel-invited-back-host-90th-academy-awards);
[PopCulture.com](https://popculture.com/news/oscars-2018-academy-awards-envelopes-major-change/)).
The Academy replaced those partners, printed category names in giant letters
on the winner envelopes, and barred the accountants from phones and social media
([PopCulture.com](https://popculture.com/news/oscars-2018-academy-awards-envelopes-major-change/)).
Kimmel's opener was the wound itself — "This year when you hear your name
called, don't get up right away" — and Beatty and Dunaway returned to present
Best Picture correctly, with del Toro checking the envelope himself
([PopCulture.com](https://popculture.com/news/oscars-2018-academy-awards-envelopes-major-change/);
[The Montclarion](https://themontclarion.org/entertainment/the-90th-academy-awards-are-the-most-inclusive-yet/)).

Just 26.5 million people watched — the lowest-rated Oscars telecast since
1974
([AdWeek](https://www.adweek.com/convergent-tv/this-years-oscars-audience-plummeted-20-to-the-shows-lowest-ratings-ever/)).
The drop came despite ABC marketing the show on the promise of another
envelope disaster, implying spectacle-driven promotion could not pull
viewers back to films most had never seen:
only two of the nine Best Picture nominees had grossed more than $100 million
([AdWeek](https://www.adweek.com/convergent-tv/this-years-oscars-audience-plummeted-20-to-the-shows-lowest-ratings-ever/);
[TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/oscars-ratings-why-viewership-sunk-20-percent-all-time-low/)).

## Beyond the telecast: 92 countries and the off-air Oscars

The 90th awards cycle reached beyond what aired on ABC. A record 92
countries submitted films for Best Foreign Language Film, including six
first-time entrants, and a committee process with three "saves" narrowed the
field to nine shortlisted films and then five nominees
([The Playlist](https://theplaylist.net/record-92-countries-submit-foreign-language-film-entries-90th-oscars-20171005/)).
Turkey submitted [*Ayla: The Daughter of War*](/creative-works/ayla-the-daughter-of-war/),
Can Ülkay's film about a Turkish sergeant and a Korean War orphan
([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/amp/ayla-a-story-of-love-turkeys-oscar-entry-121072));
it was not among the five nominees, and Sebastián Lelio's *A Fantastic
Woman* went on to win
([SHOOTonline](https://www.shootonline.com/spw/90th-oscars-nominations-announced/);
[RogerEbert.com](https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/the-shape-of-water-three-billboards-win-big-at-90th-oscars)).

The cycle's other honors were handed out earlier. At the 9th Governors
Awards on November 11, 2017, honorary Oscars went to Charles Burnett, Agnès
Varda, Owen Roizman, and Donald Sutherland — plus a special Oscar for
Alejandro G. Iñárritu's VR installation *Carne y Arena*, "in recognition of
a visionary and powerful experience in storytelling," the first special award
in 22 years
([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/entertainment/hollywood/alejandro-g-inarritus-vr-installation-carne-y-arena-to-receive-special-oscar-award-4910668/)).
None appear in the standard 90th winners lists.

## Frequently asked questions

**When and where were the 90th Academy Awards held?**
The ceremony took place on Sunday, March 4, 2018, at the Dolby Theatre in
Hollywood, Los Angeles, and aired live on ABC at 8:00 p.m. ET
([SHOOTonline](https://www.shootonline.com/spw/90th-oscars-nominations-announced/)).
The date was pushed a week later than the usual late-February slot to avoid
clashing with the PyeongChang Winter Olympics closing ceremony
([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/academy-timetable-march-2018-oscars/)).

**Which film won Best Picture at the 90th Oscars?**
*The Shape of Water*, Guillermo del Toro's fantasy romance, won Best Picture
after leading all films with 13 nominations. It collected four Oscars total —
Picture, Director, Original Score, and Production Design — beating eight other
nominees including *Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri* and *Get Out*
([RogerEbert.com](https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/the-shape-of-water-three-billboards-win-big-at-90th-oscars)).

**What was the envelope mix-up the 90th ceremony was atoning for?**
At the 89th Oscars, presenters Warren Beatty and Faye Dunaway were handed the
wrong envelope by a PricewaterhouseCoopers partner and wrongly announced *La
La Land* as Best Picture instead of the actual winner, *Moonlight*. For the
90th, the Academy replaced the accountants, enlarged the category lettering
on envelopes, and brought Beatty and Dunaway back to present correctly
([PopCulture.com](https://popculture.com/news/oscars-2018-academy-awards-envelopes-major-change/)).

**Why were the 90th Oscars' ratings a record low?**
Only 26.5 million Americans watched — the smallest audience since Nielsen
began tracking in 1974. Analysts blamed a slate of Best Picture nominees few
viewers had seen (only *Get Out* and *Dunkirk* topped $100M), the general
decline of live TV, a 3-hour-50-minute runtime, and predictable results
([AdWeek](https://www.adweek.com/convergent-tv/this-years-oscars-audience-plummeted-20-to-the-shows-lowest-ratings-ever/);
[TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/oscars-ratings-why-viewership-sunk-20-percent-all-time-low/)).

**Was *Ayla: The Daughter of War* nominated for an Oscar?**
No. Turkey selected Can Ülkay's *Ayla* as its official entry for Best Foreign
Language Film at the 90th Academy Awards
([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/amp/ayla-a-story-of-love-turkeys-oscar-entry-121072)),
but it was not among the five nominees announced by the Academy; Chile's *A
Fantastic Woman* won the category
([SHOOTonline](https://www.shootonline.com/spw/90th-oscars-nominations-announced/)).

## What this profile does not claim

- It does not describe the Academy Awards as an institution — that is a separate profile; this one covers the 90th ceremony as an event.
- It does not give a more precise Nielsen viewer figure than 26.5 million; a 26.54-million figure circulates in secondary references but was not re-verified from a full read.
- It does not name the venue of the 9th Governors Awards; the venue was reported only at snippet level and is held for a future update.
- It does not assert that Mary J. Blige was the first person ever nominated for acting and songwriting in the same year; the dual nominations are verified, the "first ever" superlative is not.
- It does not report voting totals, ballot counts, or margins — the Academy does not publish them.
- It does not claim Turkey's Ayla submission was shortlisted; the sources establish it was submitted and not nominated, with the nine-film shortlist stage unconfirmed for this entry.
