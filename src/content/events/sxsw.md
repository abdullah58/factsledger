## Overview

**South by Southwest (SXSW)** is an annual set of film, interactive media and music festivals and conferences held each March in Austin, Texas ([Film Threat](https://filmthreat.com/uncategorized/sxsw-film-announces-more-films-and-the-film-conference-lineup-for-the-2012-sxsw-film-festival/)). It began in 1987 as a 700-registrant music conference, added film in 1994, and grew into an industry marketplace where distribution deals close during the festival itself: on Thursday, March 8, 2012, Autonomy Pictures finalized its U.S.-rights acquisition of Adam Rehmeier's [The Bunny Game](/creative-works/the-bunny-game/) at SXSW ([International Documentary Association](https://www.documentary.org/online-feature/sxsw-music-docs-britpop-reunions-post-alt-road-trips-blues-survivors); [Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/distribution/autonomy-pictures-kicks-off-with-us-rights-deal-on-the-bunny-game/5039067.article)).

## From Austin music conference to industry marketplace

SXSW started in 1987 as the local Austin music scene's bid to promote itself nationally — a laid-back alternative to New York's New Music Seminar, which had become unwieldy and violent; that first edition hosted 172 bands for 700 registrants ([International Documentary Association](https://www.documentary.org/online-feature/sxsw-music-docs-britpop-reunions-post-alt-road-trips-blues-survivors)). The film festival and conference joined in 1994, and by 1997 the event showed more than 100 feature films with attendance near 10,000 ([International Documentary Association](https://www.documentary.org/online-feature/sxsw-music-docs-britpop-reunions-post-alt-road-trips-blues-survivors)). Growth compounded from there: by 2013 SXSW staged over 2,200 official musical acts and more than 130 feature films, with well over 100,000 people coming to at least one official event — a scale that made the Austin convention a deal floor as well as a showcase ([International Documentary Association](https://www.documentary.org/online-feature/sxsw-music-docs-britpop-reunions-post-alt-road-trips-blues-survivors)).

## The 2012 film market: The Bunny Game deal

The 2012 SXSW Film Festival ran March 9–17, opening with the world premiere of Joss Whedon and Drew Goddard's *The Cabin in the Woods* and screening 132 features — 74 of them world premieres — plus 138 shorts, chosen from a record 5,300-plus submissions ([Film Threat](https://filmthreat.com/uncategorized/sxsw-film-announces-more-films-and-the-film-conference-lineup-for-the-2012-sxsw-film-festival/)). It was the festival's business that mattered for *The Bunny Game*: on Thursday, March 8, the new distributor Autonomy Pictures — launched by Derek Curl, David Gregory of Severin Films and Lewis Tice of Danger After Dark to release "uncompromising cinema on a worldwide scale" — finalized its acquisition of the U.S. rights to Rehmeier's film at SXSW ([Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/distribution/autonomy-pictures-kicks-off-with-us-rights-deal-on-the-bunny-game/5039067.article); [Bloody Disgusting](http://bloody-disgusting.com/news/28671/autonomy-pictures-comes-out-playing-the-bunny-game/)). Curl led the negotiations for Autonomy, with Julian Richards of Jinga Films representing the filmmakers; Autonomy planned four films for 2012, starting with *The Bunny Game*, slated for an uncut U.S. release in July ([Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/distribution/autonomy-pictures-kicks-off-with-us-rights-deal-on-the-bunny-game/5039067.article)). The choice was deliberate: Autonomy made the controversial, UK-banned film its launch title, with Curl calling it "the last word on the torture-porn sub-genre" and Richards describing the acquisition as "a bold statement of intent" ([Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/distribution/autonomy-pictures-kicks-off-with-us-rights-deal-on-the-bunny-game/5039067.article)).

## Frequently asked questions

**When and where is SXSW held?**
South by Southwest takes place every March in Austin, Texas. The 2012 film edition ran March 9–17, 2012; the event as a whole spans film, interactive media and music, with conferences alongside the festivals.

**When did SXSW add a film festival?**
The film conference and festival joined SXSW in 1994, seven years after the music event began in 1987. By 1997 the film side was already showing more than 100 features with total festival attendance near 10,000.

**How big was SXSW Film in 2012?**
The 2012 edition screened 132 features (74 world premieres, 17 North American premieres, 11 U.S. premieres) and 138 shorts — nearly 275 films selected from a record 5,300-plus submissions, a 7% increase over 2011. Fifty-eight of the features came from first-time directors.

**What happened with The Bunny Game at SXSW 2012?**
On Thursday, March 8, 2012, the deal for Autonomy Pictures' acquisition of the U.S. rights to The Bunny Game was finalized at SXSW. Autonomy planned the film as the launch title of a four-film 2012 slate, with an uncut U.S. release scheduled for July 2012.

**Who negotiated the Autonomy–Bunny Game deal?**
Derek Curl took point for Autonomy Pictures, and Julian Richards of Jinga Films negotiated on behalf of the filmmakers, according to Screen Daily's report of the finalized deal.
