**India** — officially the **Republic of India**, *Bharat* in Hindi — is a multiparty federal republic of 28 states and 8 union territories, with its capital at New Delhi ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/India)). It is governed by the world's longest written constitution, adopted on 26 November 1949 and in force since 26 January 1950 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/india-news/indian-constitution-turns-75-how-it-became-world-s-longest-written-charter-124112600561_1.html)). India became the world's most populous country in 2023, according to United Nations estimates, and runs the world's largest democratic elections ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/India); [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/elections/lok-sabha-election/voter-base-for-2024-lok-sabha-polls-demographic-breakdown-article-12385821.html)).

## The constitutional order

India's Constitution was adopted by the 299-member Constituent Assembly on 26 November 1949 and came into force on 26 January 1950 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/india-news/indian-constitution-turns-75-how-it-became-world-s-longest-written-charter-124112600561_1.html)). It declares India a sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic, and at roughly 145,000 words it is the longest written constitution in the world ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/india-news/indian-constitution-turns-75-how-it-became-world-s-longest-written-charter-124112600561_1.html)). Drafted under B. R. Ambedkar, it began with 395 articles and has since grown to 448 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/india-news/indian-constitution-turns-75-how-it-became-world-s-longest-written-charter-124112600561_1.html)). The government is a multiparty federal republic with two legislative houses — the Council of States (245 seats) and the House of the People (543) — with Droupadi Murmu as President and Narendra Modi as Prime Minister ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/India); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/india-news/indian-constitution-turns-75-how-it-became-world-s-longest-written-charter-124112600561_1.html)). Article 343 makes Hindi in Devanagari the Union's official language, with English continuing for official purposes; states choose their own official languages, and the Eighth Schedule lists 22 scheduled languages ([Central Institute of Indian Languages](http://www.ciil-ebooks.net/html/languse/policy.html); [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/India)).

## A federal union of 28 states and 8 union territories

India's federal map was redrawn in recent years. On 31 October 2019 the state of Jammu and Kashmir ceased to exist and became two union territories — Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh — under the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019, after Parliament ended the state's special status under Article 370 ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india-news/jammu-kashmir-and-ladakh-become-separate-union-territories-from-thursday-10-points/story-fsYC3R1HFXFZxzlwomycrJ.html)). The reorganization left 28 states and nine union territories ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india-news/jammu-kashmir-and-ladakh-become-separate-union-territories-from-thursday-10-points/story-fsYC3R1HFXFZxzlwomycrJ.html)). On 26 January 2020, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu merged into a single union territory by an Act of Parliament, bringing the count to eight ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/daman-and-diu-dadra-and-nagar-haveli-to-be-one-union-territory-from-january-26/article30350549.ece)). Jammu & Kashmir kept an elected legislature (like Puducherry); Ladakh did not (like Chandigarh) ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india-news/jammu-kashmir-and-ladakh-become-separate-union-territories-from-thursday-10-points/story-fsYC3R1HFXFZxzlwomycrJ.html)). A UN brief notes that under India's federal structure, states set their own policy priorities — Kerala and Tamil Nadu cut fertility below replacement level two decades before the country as a whole ([UN DESA](https://desapublications.un.org/policy-briefs/un-desa-policy-brief-no-153-india-overtakes-china-worlds-most-populous-country)).

## The world's largest electorate

India's general elections are the largest democratic exercise in the world. For the 2024 Lok Sabha general election the Election Commission's rolls listed 96 crore eligible voters, up from roughly 91 crore in 2019 — alone exceeding the combined electorates of the next four largest democracies: the United States, Indonesia, Brazil and Pakistan ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/elections/lok-sabha-election/voter-base-for-2024-lok-sabha-polls-demographic-breakdown-article-12385821.html)). Women were over 47 crore of the electorate, nearly 49 percent, and the gender ratio on the rolls rose from 928 in 2019 to 948 in 2024 ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/elections/lok-sabha-election/voter-base-for-2024-lok-sabha-polls-demographic-breakdown-article-12385821.html)). Running it needed some 12 lakh polling booths and 1.5 crore polling personnel ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/elections/lok-sabha-election/voter-base-for-2024-lok-sabha-polls-demographic-breakdown-article-12385821.html)). Turnout reached 67 percent in 2019, up from 45 percent in the first national general election of 1951–52 — participation rising even as the electorate grew more than fivefold, from 17.32 crore registered voters in 1951 to 96 crore in 2024 ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/elections/lok-sabha-election/voter-base-for-2024-lok-sabha-polls-demographic-breakdown-article-12385821.html)).

## Population and economic scale

India became the world's most populous country in April 2023 at 1,425,775,850, surpassing mainland China — the first change since UN tracking began in 1950 ([UN DESA](https://desapublications.un.org/policy-briefs/un-desa-policy-brief-no-153-india-overtakes-china-worlds-most-populous-country)). The World Bank puts India’s 2024 population at 1,450,935,791 ([World Bank](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/IN/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?format=json&date=2020:2024)). The last completed census was in 2011; the 2021 census was delayed by the pandemic ([UN DESA](https://desapublications.un.org/policy-briefs/un-desa-policy-brief-no-153-india-overtakes-china-worlds-most-populous-country)). The UN projects a population peak around 2064, with fertility at 2.0 births per woman in 2022 ([UN DESA](https://desapublications.un.org/policy-briefs/un-desa-policy-brief-no-153-india-overtakes-china-worlds-most-populous-country)). The World Bank values 2024 nominal GDP at $3.76 trillion, fifth-largest that year — but IMF estimates slipped India to sixth in 2025 at $3.92 trillion without the economy shrinking, implying dollar-denominated rankings now move more on the rupee's exchange rate than on domestic growth ([World Bank](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/IN/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CD?format=json&date=2020:2024); [News18](https://www.news18.com/business/economy/why-india-slipped-from-fifth-to-sixth-largest-economy-despite-being-fastest-growing-major-nation-10038231.html)). The IMF projects 6.5 percent growth for 2026 and 2027, keeping India the fastest-growing major economy ([News18](https://www.news18.com/business/economy/why-india-slipped-from-fifth-to-sixth-largest-economy-despite-being-fastest-growing-major-nation-10038231.html)).

## The stories the state tells its children

India's institutions also commission children's stories. The series [Krish, Trish and Baltiboy](/creative-works/krish-trish-and-baltiboy/), from Mumbai studio Graphiti Multimedia, dramatizes lesser-known freedom fighters including Wazir Ram Singh Pathania (Himachal Pradesh) and Birsa Munda (Jharkhand) ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/event/online-events/awards/krish-trish-and-balti-boy-2-nominated-at-21st-cairo-international-film-festival-for-children/); [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/entertainment/second-season-of-animated-series-krish-trish-and-baltiboy-bharat-hain-hum-launched-at-iffi/)). "Bharat Hain Hum" — Season 2 — was launched by the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting at the International Film Festival of India in Goa, attended by actor Nagarjuna, and streams on the public Waves platform, Doordarshan and OTT ([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/entertainment/second-season-of-animated-series-krish-trish-and-baltiboy-bharat-hain-hum-launched-at-iffi/)). In December 2024 Prime Minister Narendra Modi named it on [Mann Ki Baat](/creative-works/mann-ki-baat/), a "superhit" among children about freedom-struggle figures "not discussed much," in 12 Indian and 7 foreign languages ([Prime Minister's Office](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/pms-address-in-the-117th-episode-of-mann-ki-baat/); [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/entertainment/second-season-of-animated-series-krish-trish-and-baltiboy-bharat-hain-hum-launched-at-iffi/)). Its second film earned a children's-animation nomination at the 21st Cairo International Film Festival for Children; directors [Munjal Shroff](/people/munjal-shroff/) and Tilak Shetty called it an attempt to "resurrect and nurture the dying folk art forms" of India ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/event/online-events/awards/krish-trish-and-balti-boy-2-nominated-at-21st-cairo-international-film-festival-for-children/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Why is the country called both India and Bharat?**
The official name is "Bharat" in Hindi and "Republic of India" in English. Because Hindi in Devanagari is the Union's official language while English continues for official purposes, the country's two official names reflect bilingual administration — not two different entities.

**How many states and union territories does India have?**
India has 28 states and 8 union territories. The count changed twice in recent years: in October 2019 the state of Jammu and Kashmir was split into two union territories, and in January 2020 the union territories of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu were merged into a single territory.

**What makes India's Constitution distinctive?**
At ~145,000 words it is the world's longest written constitution. Adopted on 26 November 1949 and in force from 26 January 1950, it declares India a sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic and has grown from 395 to 448 articles.
