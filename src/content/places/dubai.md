## Overview

Dubai is the most populous emirate of the [United Arab Emirates](/places/united-arab-emirates/) and its commercial hub, a Gulf-coast territory of 4,114 square kilometres whose population reached 4.58 million by the end of 2025 — up about 332,000 people, or 7.5 percent, in a single year ([Qazinform, reporting WAM](https://qazinform.com/news/dubais-population-surpasses-458-million-up-75-fd7522)). The name covers two things at once: the emirate, the UAE's second-largest by area, and the city of the same name that is its capital and the country's most populous city ([Government of Dubai](https://www.dubai.ae/dubai-fact-sheet)).

Dubai sits on the eastern coast of the Arabian Peninsula, to the south-west of the Arabian Gulf, with about 72 kilometres of coast, bordering [Abu Dhabi](/places/abu-dhabi/) to the south, Sharjah to the northeast, and Oman to the southeast ([Government of Dubai](https://www.dubai.ae/dubai-fact-sheet)). It has a tropical desert climate of warm, sunny winters and hot, humid summers, and its currency is the UAE dirham ([Government of Dubai](https://www.dubai.ae/dubai-fact-sheet)). The emirate is ruled by Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, who is also Vice President and Prime Minister of the UAE; the Crown Prince is Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum ([Government of Dubai](https://www.dubai.ae/dubai-fact-sheet)).

## From pearls to a trade and tourism economy

Dubai's modern story starts with pearls. The pearling industry thrived in the 19th and early 20th centuries, providing income and employment across the Gulf, until the economic depression of the late 1920s and early 1930s, coupled with Japan's invention of the cultured pearl, irreparably damaged it ([UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/the-uae/history)). Sheikh Rashid bin Saeed Al Maktoum, de facto Ruler of Dubai since 1939, replaced pearling revenues by building up the shipping industry, and in 1969 the emirate began exporting oil ([UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/the-uae/history)). The UAE was formally established on December 2, 1971, with Dubai as one of the six founding emirates ([UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/the-uae/history)).

Oil never came to dominate Dubai the way it did its neighbour: the economy diversified into trade, tourism, aviation, financial services, and real estate. In 2024 GDP reached AED 540.677 billion at current prices, up 5.8 percent, with growth driven by trade, real estate, financial services, transport, and industry, which together contributed nearly 78 percent of the year's growth ([Dubai Media Office](https://prod.mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2025/august/14-08/dubais-gdp-reaches)). Wholesale and retail trade alone contributed 23 percent of GDP in the first quarter of 2025, with finance and insurance at 13.4 percent and transport and storage at 13 percent ([Dubai Media Office](https://prod.mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2025/august/14-08/dubais-gdp-reaches)). Non-oil foreign trade reached AED 2 trillion in 2023 ([Government of Dubai](https://www.dubai.ae/dubai-fact-sheet)).

The population tells the same growth story in starker numbers: from around 12,000 people in 1881 to more than 4.58 million by the end of 2025, an increase of nearly 380-fold over 144 years, with the current 7.5 percent growth rate among the highest in the emirate's history ([Qazinform, reporting WAM](https://qazinform.com/news/dubais-population-surpasses-458-million-up-75-fd7522)). The daytime population rises to 6.392 million when temporary arrivals for work and tourism are included ([Qazinform, reporting WAM](https://qazinform.com/news/dubais-population-surpasses-458-million-up-75-fd7522)). In 2025 the emirate attracted 19.6 million international tourists and about 96 million passengers through its airports — Dubai International and Al Maktoum — while its seaports include Jebel Ali and Mina Rashid ([Qazinform, reporting WAM](https://qazinform.com/news/dubais-population-surpasses-458-million-up-75-fd7522); [Government of Dubai](https://www.dubai.ae/dubai-fact-sheet)).

## The city where you can hail a driverless Baidu

The fact most Dubai profiles miss is that the emirate is now a live robotaxi market. On March 30, 2026, Dubai's Roads and Transport Authority launched commercial driverless taxi operations in Umm Suqeim and [Jumeirah](/places/jumeirah/), in partnership with [Baidu](/organizations/baidu/)'s [Apollo Go](/organizations/apollo-go/) and WeRide, starting with 100 autonomous taxis ([What's On](https://whatson.ae/2026/03/driverless-taxis-dubai-how-to-book/)). Apollo Go rides are booked through the Apollo Go app, with [Dubai Taxi Company](/organizations/dubai-taxi-company/) supporting local operations, while WeRide cars are booked through [Uber](/organizations/uber/) ([What's On](https://whatson.ae/2026/03/driverless-taxis-dubai-how-to-book/)).

The service then crossed onto the world's biggest ride-hailing platform: since August 19, 2026, Baidu's driverless Apollo Go cars have also been available to riders on Uber in Dubai, according to the RTA's August update ([Devo Dubai](https://devodubai.com/article/2026-10-26/dubai-robotaxis-where-you-can-ride-one-now-which-apps-and-what-it-costs)). The RTA set a fare of Dh5 per ride on Apollo Go from July 15, 2026, after an initial free period ([Devo Dubai](https://devodubai.com/article/2026-10-26/dubai-robotaxis-where-you-can-ride-one-now-which-apps-and-what-it-costs)). By August the robotaxi fleet stood at 144 vehicles, 48 of them in active operation, completing about 50 trips a day; since launch the cars had travelled more than 4 million kilometres and carried 7,613 passenger trips, with 97 percent customer satisfaction, according to the RTA ([Devo Dubai](https://devodubai.com/article/2026-10-26/dubai-robotaxis-where-you-can-ride-one-now-which-apps-and-what-it-costs)). Dubai Taxi Company plans to grow its Apollo Go fleet from 50 vehicles in the first year to more than 1,000 over the coming years, and Dubai's target is for 25 percent of all journeys in the emirate to be autonomous by 2030 ([Devo Dubai](https://devodubai.com/article/2026-10-26/dubai-robotaxis-where-you-can-ride-one-now-which-apps-and-what-it-costs)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not claim a city-only population figure — 4.58 million is the emirate total at end-2025. It does not claim Dubai's economy is oil-based: oil's share is not stated in the sources read, and the profile describes trade, tourism, finance, and real estate as the drivers instead. It does not claim the robotaxi service is citywide: as of October 2026 it operates only in Umm Suqeim and Jumeirah, and Pony.ai, the RTA's third listed partner, has no announced app, area, or fare. It does not present the RTA's fleet and satisfaction figures as independently audited. And it does not claim a 2025 full-year GDP figure — only the 5.4 percent real growth rate reported with the population announcement.
