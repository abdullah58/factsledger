## Overview

Japan is an East Asian island nation of 14,125 islands and the world's fourth-largest economy, governed as a parliamentary constitutional monarchy: Emperor Naruhito is chief of state, and Sanae Takaichi — elected October 21, 2025 — is Japan's first woman prime minister ([CIA World Factbook](https://theodora.com/wfbcurrent/japan/japan_government.html); [Reuters](https://news.tv5.com.ph/breaking/read/sanae-takaichi-elected-to-be-japans-first-female-prime-minister)). Its capital is Tokyo, and the October 2025 census recorded 122.9 million residents, with 29.4% aged 65 or older ([The Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2026/09/29/japan/society/japan-population-fall/)). Japan is also a major film territory: its 2025 box office hit a record ¥274.45 billion (about $1.8 billion), and the [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) biopic [Michael](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/) had a dedicated Japanese distributor, Kino Films, whose June 12, 2026 release added $24.4 million toward the film's $991.4 million worldwide total ([The Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/culture/2026/01/29/film/box-office-revenues-high/); [ComingSoon](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2158649-michael-jackson-biopic-box-office-1-billion)).

Japan's modern state rests on a constitution effective May 3, 1947 — never amended since — and a bicameral National Diet (465-seat lower house, 248-seat upper house) ([CIA World Factbook](https://theodora.com/wfbcurrent/japan/japan_government.html)). It holds 25 [UNESCO](/organizations/unesco/) World Heritage Sites and transacts in the yen ([CIA World Factbook](https://theodora.com/wfbcurrent/japan/japan_economy.html)).

## The box office that gets its own distributor

Japan's film market is large enough to change how a global blockbuster is sold. In 2025, box-office revenues soared 32.6% year on year to a record ¥274.45 billion (about $1.8 billion), per the Motion Picture Producers Association of Japan ([The Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/culture/2026/01/29/film/box-office-revenues-high/)). The surge was homegrown: "Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba — The Movie: Infinity Castle" earned ¥39.14 billion and "Kokuho" ¥19.55 billion, while the Toho group, distributor of the year's top four films, collected ¥160.5 billion — over half the national total. Admissions hit 188.76 million, the second-highest figure on record, and association head Yoshishige Shimatani said men and women of all ages had returned to cinemas.

That scale is why Michael had three distributors: Lionsgate (US), Universal (most international territories), and Kino Films (Japan) ([TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/splash/michael-jackson-biopic-delayed-again-1195911)). Japan's June 12, 2026 release through Kino Films added $24.4 million toward the film's $991.4 million worldwide total as of July 6, 2026 ([ComingSoon](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2158649-michael-jackson-biopic-box-office-1-billion)). In a market this large, territory-specific distribution is not a courtesy — it is how the business is done.

## A constitutional monarchy, led for the first time by a woman

Japan is a parliamentary constitutional monarchy with an unusually unaltered operating document: the constitution took effect May 3, 1947 and has never been amended since ([CIA World Factbook](https://theodora.com/wfbcurrent/japan/japan_government.html)). The emperor is chief of state — Naruhito since May 1, 2019, when his father abdicated — and legislative power sits with the bicameral National Diet: a 465-seat House of Representatives and a 248-seat House of Councillors.

The head of government is now a historic first. On October 21, 2025, Sanae Takaichi won 237 lower-house votes to become Japan's 104th prime minister and its first woman in the office, succeeding Shigeru Ishiba at the head of an LDP coalition with the Japan Innovation Party ([Reuters](https://news.tv5.com.ph/breaking/read/sanae-takaichi-elected-to-be-japans-first-female-prime-minister)). She consolidated power in a February 8, 2026 snap election: the LDP took more than 310 of 465 lower-house seats — a two-thirds majority, the first by any postwar party, on 56.23% turnout ([ANI](https://www.lokmattimes.com/international/japan-pm-takaichis-ldp-secures-landslide-win-in-general-polls-with-two-thirds-majority/)). The coalition remains a minority in the upper house; Takaichi has pledged a "responsible yet aggressive" fiscal policy, including a two-year suspension of the 8% consumption tax on food.

## A shrinking, aging archipelago on a $4.4 trillion economy

A 2023 digital-map recount by the Geospatial Information Authority found 14,125 islands, up from 6,852 in 1987 — better surveying, not new land ([The Japan News](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/society/general-news/20230301-94249/)). Its people are fewer each year: the October 1, 2025 census counted 122,972,528 residents, down 2.5% from 2020 — the survey's third consecutive decline ([The Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2026/09/29/japan/society/japan-population-fall/)). Nationals fell 3.5% to 119.1 million as foreign residents rose 39.8% to a record 3.84 million; the 65-plus share climbed to 29.4%, with Tokyo the only prefecture still growing.

The economy remains one of the world's largest: the CIA's Factbook calls it the "fourth-largest, trade-oriented, and diversified economy," and the IMF's April 2026 Outlook confirmed fourth place in nominal GDP — placing India sixth at $3.92 trillion, behind Japan and the UK ([CIA World Factbook](https://theodora.com/wfbcurrent/japan/japan_economy.html); [Fortune India](https://www.fortuneindia.com/economy/india-sixth-largest-economy-in-world-with-usd-3-92-tn-gdp/153275)). Japan's cultural footprint matches: 25 UNESCO World Heritage Sites, including 3,776-metre Mount Fuji, inscribed in June 2013 as "Fujisan, sacred place and source of artistic inspiration" ([CIA World Factbook](https://theodora.com/wfbcurrent/japan/japan_government.html); [Government of Japan](https://www.gov-online.go.jp/eng/publicity/book/hlj/html/201309/201309_01_en.html)). Osaka-Kansai's 2025 Expo drew 25,578,986 general visitors over 184 days ([The Japan News](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/society/general-news/20251016-286660/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is Japan's prime minister?**
Sanae Takaichi became Japan's first woman prime minister on October 21, 2025, winning 237 votes in the lower house as the country's 104th premier and succeeding Shigeru Ishiba. Her Liberal Democratic Party then won a two-thirds lower-house majority — the first postwar party to do so — in a February 8, 2026 snap election.

**What is Japan's population?**
The October 1, 2025 census recorded 122,972,528 residents, down 2.5% from 2020 — the third consecutive decline in the survey that dates to 1920. Japanese nationals fell to 119.1 million, foreign residents hit a record 3.84 million, and 29.4% of residents were aged 65 or older.

**What role does Japan's emperor play?**
Under the 1947 constitution, the emperor is chief of state: a ceremonial head of the parliamentary constitutional monarchy, not a governing ruler. Naruhito has held the position since May 1, 2019, succeeding his father, who abdicated on April 30, 2019.

**Why did the Michael movie get a separate distributor in Japan?**
Japan's 2025 box office hit a record ¥274.45 billion (~$1.8 billion), making it a territory large enough for dedicated handling: Lionsgate distributed Michael in the US, Universal handled most international markets, and Kino Films managed Japan, where its June 12, 2026 release added $24.4 million.
