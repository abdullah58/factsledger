## Overview

China, officially the People's Republic of China, is the largest country in Asia and the world's second-most populous nation, with Beijing as its capital and an estimated 1,407,698,000 people in 2026 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)). It covers about one-fourteenth of the land area of Earth — almost as large as the whole of Europe — and its economy reached $19,626 billion in 2025, growing 5.0 percent in real terms ([Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/EN/Themes/Countries-Regions/International-Statistics/Country-Profiles/china.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=18)).

China occupies nearly the entire East Asian landmass, stretching about 5,250 kilometres from east to west and 5,500 kilometres from north to south, with a land frontier of roughly 20,000 kilometres and a coastline of some 14,000 kilometres ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)). Fourteen countries border it directly: Mongolia to the north; Russia and North Korea to the northeast; Vietnam, Laos, Myanmar, [India](/places/india/), Bhutan, and Nepal to the south; Pakistan to the southwest; and Afghanistan, Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan, and Kazakhstan to the west ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)). Across the Yellow Sea it faces South Korea and [Japan](/places/japan/), and beyond the South China Sea lies the Philippines ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)).

The country is organised into 33 administrative units directly under the central government: 22 provinces, 5 autonomous regions, 4 municipalities — Chongqing, Beijing, Shanghai, and Tianjin — and 2 special administrative regions, [Hong Kong](/places/hong-kong/) and Macau ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)). Beijing is the capital and the country's cultural, economic, and communications centre; Shanghai is the main industrial city, and Hong Kong is the leading commercial centre and port ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)). The province, or *sheng*, is the enduring unit of Chinese administration, traceable in its current form to the Tang dynasty of 618–907 CE ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)).

## More than 4,000 years of recorded history

With more than 4,000 years of recorded history, China is one of the few existing countries that also flourished economically and culturally in the earliest stages of world civilisation, and Britannica calls it "unique among nations in its longevity and resilience as a discrete politico-cultural unit" ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)). Much of that cultural development happened with relatively little outside influence, Buddhism from India being a major exception, and even foreign conquerors such as the Manchu were largely absorbed into Han Chinese culture ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)). The great majority of the population is Han Chinese, but few countries have as many diverse Indigenous peoples; the Zhuang are the largest minority group, and in the southwest many different ethnic groups are geographically intermixed ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)).

The modern state dates to October 1, 1949, when Mao Zedong proclaimed the People's Republic of China from Tiananmen Square in Beijing, declaring "The Chinese people have stood up!" ([EBSCO Research Starters](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/politics-and-government/mao-zedong-proclaims-communist-peoples-republic-china)). The proclamation followed fourteen years of Japanese occupation from 1931 to 1945 and four years of civil war between Chiang Kai-shek's Nationalists and the Communists; Beijing had fallen to the Communists in January 1949, and Chiang and his followers fled to Taiwan, which has been under separate administration ever since ([EBSCO Research Starters](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/politics-and-government/mao-zedong-proclaims-communist-peoples-republic-china); [Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)). The Soviet Union recognised the new state on October 2, 1949, and Zhou Enlai became premier, a post he held until 1976 ([EBSCO Research Starters](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/politics-and-government/mao-zedong-proclaims-communist-peoples-republic-china)).

The first two decades were violent and disruptive. The Great Leap Forward, launched in 1958, produced an economic depression lasting through 1961, and severe food shortages in 1959–1961 caused widespread famine and millions of deaths ([EBSCO Research Starters](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/politics-and-government/mao-zedong-proclaims-communist-peoples-republic-china)). China fought a border war with India in 1962, detonated its first nuclear bomb in 1964, and began the Cultural Revolution in 1966, when the Red Guard youth militia formed and schools and colleges closed ([EBSCO Research Starters](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/politics-and-government/mao-zedong-proclaims-communist-peoples-republic-china)).

## The world's second-largest economy — and a shrinking population

China's economy reached $19,626 billion in 2025, up 586 percent since 2000, with GDP per capita of $13,968 and $29,352 per capita at purchasing-power parity ([Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/EN/Themes/Countries-Regions/International-Statistics/Country-Profiles/china.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=18)). The stat that anchors the trade story is the scale of the surplus: China exported $3,577 billion of goods in 2024 against $2,585 billion of imports, a surplus of $991 billion, with the [United States](/places/united-states/) taking 17.2 percent of its exports and electrical goods and appliances making up 25.9 percent of what it sells abroad ([Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/EN/Themes/Countries-Regions/International-Statistics/Country-Profiles/china.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=18)). The implication is a manufacturing economy whose largest single customer is also its main geopolitical rival — a tension that shapes its trade policy.

The population story runs in the opposite direction from the economy. Britannica's 2026 estimate is 1,407,698,000, and according to United Nations estimates India surpassed China in population in 2023, ending China's long run as the world's most populous country ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)). Life expectancy at birth in 2024 was 81.0 years for women and 75.2 years for men, and the labour force numbered 773,261,000 people in 2025, with unemployment at 4.6 percent — but 15.8 percent among 15- to 24-year-olds ([Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/EN/Themes/Countries-Regions/International-Statistics/Country-Profiles/china.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=18)). Services now employ 46.2 percent of workers and produce 56.1 percent of gross value added, against 22.2 percent of workers in agriculture ([Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/EN/Themes/Countries-Regions/International-Statistics/Country-Profiles/china.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=18)).

China is also a research heavyweight: it spent 2.6 percent of GDP on research and development in 2022, counted 1,849 researchers per million inhabitants, and filed 1,079 resident patent applications per million people in 2023 ([Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/EN/Themes/Countries-Regions/International-Statistics/Country-Profiles/china.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=18)). That research base shows up in the streets of its cities. [Baidu](/organizations/baidu/), the Beijing-based internet and AI company, runs [Apollo Go](/organizations/apollo-go/), a robotaxi service operating in 28 cities that had provided more than 23 million rides as of June 2026 ([TechInAsia](https://www.techinasia.com/news/baidu-unveils-ai-world-model-train-test-robotaxis)). In November 2026 Baidu unveiled Apollo World, a world model that simulates complete ride-hailing trips through virtual cities to train and validate its robotaxis ([TechInAsia](https://www.techinasia.com/news/baidu-unveils-ai-world-model-train-test-robotaxis)).

China's human development index stood at 0.797 in 2023, ranking 78th of 193 countries, and its carbon dioxide emissions were 9.1 tonnes per capita in 2024, with renewable energy at 15.2 percent of total final energy consumption in 2021 ([Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/EN/Themes/Countries-Regions/International-Statistics/Country-Profiles/china.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=18)). It is the world's largest producer of rice and among the principal sources of wheat, corn, tobacco, soybeans, peanuts, and cotton ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/China)).
