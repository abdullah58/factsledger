## Overview

Neverland Ranch is a 2,700-acre private estate in Los Olivos, in the Santa Ynez Valley of Santa Barbara County, near Santa Barbara, California — the property Michael Jackson bought in 1988 for $19.5 million and turned into a private amusement park and zoo, and which police searched twice during child-molestation investigations ([Associated Press](https://www.wvlt.tv/2020/12/25/michael-jacksons-neverland-ranch-sold-to-billionaire/?outputType=amp); [Reuters](https://www.telegraphindia.com/amp/world/michael-jacksons-famed-neverland-ranch-finally-sold/cid/1801670)). After Jackson's 2009 death the property was renamed Sycamore Valley Ranch, and in December 2020 it sold to billionaire Ronald Burkle for $22 million — after an asking price of $100 million five years earlier ([Reuters](https://www.telegraphindia.com/amp/world/michael-jacksons-famed-neverland-ranch-finally-sold/cid/1801670)). In 2026 the ranch entered film history in an unusual way: the original opening of the biopic *Michael* showed 1993 investigators arriving at Neverland Ranch to search for evidence — a sequence that was scrapped before release ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/entertainment/hollywood/michael-jackson-biopic-reshoots-15-million-usd-remove-accusations-eyes-55-million-usd-opening-weekend-10626224/)).

The estate's story is inseparable from its owner's: a fantasy retreat that became a crime scene, then a distressed asset, then a billionaire's land bank.

## A private amusement park in the Santa Ynez Valley

Jackson bought the Los Olivos property — about 120 miles north of Los Angeles — in 1988 for $19.5 million, and renamed it Neverland after the Peter Pan tale of the boy who refused to grow up ([Reuters](https://www.telegraphindia.com/amp/world/michael-jacksons-famed-neverland-ranch-finally-sold/cid/1801670)). What he built was closer to a private theme park than a home: a zoo, a railway with a "Disney-style" train station, theme-park rides, a 50-seat movie theater, a dance studio, a fire house and barn, alongside the 12,500-square-foot main residence and a 3,700-square-foot pool house ([Reuters](https://www.telegraphindia.com/amp/world/michael-jacksons-famed-neverland-ranch-finally-sold/cid/1801670); [Associated Press](https://www.wvlt.tv/2020/12/25/michael-jacksons-neverland-ranch-sold-to-billionaire/?outputType=amp)). It became his favorite retreat, the place where he entertained children — and where one of them would later bring the molestation charges that defined the property's second life ([Reuters](https://www.telegraphindia.com/amp/world/michael-jacksons-famed-neverland-ranch-finally-sold/cid/1801670)).

## Two searches, a settlement, and an acquittal

In August 1993, following allegations that Jackson had molested 13-year-old Jordan Chandler, police searched two of the singer's homes — including, as a contemporaneous BBC report put it, his "Neverland ranch in Santa Barbara" ([BBC News](https://macaudailytimes.com.mo/1993-michael-jackson-accused-of-child-abuse-2.html)). Jackson was never criminally charged in that case; a civil case was settled out of court in early 1994 for a reported $20 million, and Evan Chandler's later $60 million claim against the singer was thrown out in 1999 ([BBC News](https://macaudailytimes.com.mo/1993-michael-jackson-accused-of-child-abuse-2.html); [Associated Press](https://news.pollstar.com/2003/11/19/trouble-in-neverland/)).

The property was searched again a decade later. In November 2003, as many as 70 law-enforcement officials served a search warrant at the ranch and hunted for evidence through the night — a day before an arrest warrant was issued for Jackson in a new child-molestation case, with the Santa Barbara County Sheriff's Department leading the investigation ([Associated Press](https://news.pollstar.com/2003/11/19/trouble-in-neverland/)). The trial that followed lasted four months and ended in June 2005 with Jackson cleared of all charges; he vowed never to return to Neverland ([BBC News](https://macaudailytimes.com.mo/1993-michael-jackson-accused-of-child-abuse-2.html); [Reuters](https://www.telegraphindia.com/amp/world/michael-jacksons-famed-neverland-ranch-finally-sold/cid/1801670)). This profile describes only the reported procedural facts and takes no position on the truth of the allegations, which are the subject of conflicting public accounts.

## From a $100 million ask to a $22 million sale

Financial distress ended Jackson's ownership in stages. In 2008, after financial troubles, he handed over the title to Colony Capital LLC, the real-estate investment firm of Tom Barrack, which held a loan on the property ([Reuters](https://www.telegraphindia.com/amp/world/michael-jacksons-famed-neverland-ranch-finally-sold/cid/1801670)). After his death in 2009 the estate was renamed Sycamore Valley Ranch, and it sat on the market for years: the asking price was $100 million in 2015/2016, cut to $67 million in 2017, and the property was eventually taken off the market unsold ([Reuters](https://www.telegraphindia.com/amp/world/michael-jacksons-famed-neverland-ranch-finally-sold/cid/1801670); [Associated Press](https://www.wvlt.tv/2020/12/25/michael-jacksons-neverland-ranch-sold-to-billionaire/?outputType=amp)). In December 2020, billionaire Ronald Burkle — a former family friend of Jackson's and co-founder of the Yucaipa Companies — bought it for $22 million, his spokesman describing the purchase as a land-banking opportunity after Burkle spotted the estate from the air while scouting neighboring Zaca Lake for a Soho House club ([Associated Press](https://www.wvlt.tv/2020/12/25/michael-jacksons-neverland-ranch-sold-to-billionaire/?outputType=amp)).

The price history is the profile's central statistic: AP valued the ranch at $12.3 million in 2003; it asked $100 million in 2015/2016 and sold for $22 million in 2020 ([Associated Press](https://news.pollstar.com/2003/11/19/trouble-in-neverland/); [Associated Press](https://www.wvlt.tv/2020/12/25/michael-jacksons-neverland-ranch-sold-to-billionaire/?outputType=amp)). The implication is stark — Neverland's fame carried no market premium; stigma and carrying costs erased the celebrity markup that the $100 million ask assumed.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where is Neverland Ranch located?**
In Los Olivos, in the Santa Ynez Valley of Santa Barbara County, California — about 120 miles north of Los Angeles and near the city of Santa Barbara. It is now officially known as Sycamore Valley Ranch.

**Who owns Neverland Ranch now?**
Billionaire Ronald Burkle, who bought the 2,700-acre estate in December 2020 for $22 million. His spokesman described the purchase as a land-banking opportunity; Burkle is the co-founder of Yucaipa Companies and the controlling shareholder of Soho House.

**How much did Michael Jackson pay for Neverland Ranch?**
$19.5 million in 1988. He owned it until his death in 2009, though he handed the title to Colony Capital LLC in 2008 after financial troubles.

**Why is it called Neverland?**
Jackson named it after Neverland, the fantasy island in the Peter Pan tale of the boy who refused to grow up — a name that matched the private amusement park, zoo and railway he built there.

**What happened in the 2026 film Michael?**
The biopic's original opening showed 1993 investigators arriving at Neverland Ranch to search for evidence, but the entire third act — built around the 1993 allegations — was scrapped after lawyers found the Chandler settlement barred depicting Jordan Chandler. The released film ends in Jackson's 1988 Bad era with no depiction of the allegations.
