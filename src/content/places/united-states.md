## Overview

The United States of America is a federal republic of 50 states in North America, with a population of about 340 million and a 2024 GDP of $28.8 trillion — the world's largest economy. It is the country where the record-breaking [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) biopic [Michael](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/) (2026) was made and where it earned $372.3 million of its $1.0287 billion worldwide total ([World Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/?locations=US-CN-EU-ZJ); [ET Now](https://www.etnownews.com/entertainment/michael-jackson-biopic-michael-crosses-1-billion-the-business-behind-the-biggest-biopic-ever-article-156244963)).

Britannica describes the United States as "a federal republic of 50 states" and the world's fourth-largest country by area, with a national capital — Washington — coextensive with the federal [District of Columbia](/places/district-of-columbia/), created in 1790 ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/United-States)). The country is the first nation to be founded, in Britannica's account, "on the premise that sovereignty rests with its citizens and not with the government." It is an immigrant nation: Britannica notes that the country's diversity "to a great degree has come from an immense and sustained global immigration," and it has no official language or religion. This profile documents the country as this site's record-breaking film's production home and home market — then as the country-reference it is.

## The $372-million home market: the United States behind the Michael record

No standard country profile connects the United States to the largest biopic gross in cinema history — this one does. *Michael*, the Antoine Fuqua-directed biopic of Michael Jackson starring his nephew Jaafar Jackson, was made in America: filming was planned for Santa Barbara, California, delayed by the 2023 SAG-AFTRA strikes, then ran from January 22 to May 31, 2024 ([Collider](https://collider.com/michael-jackson-biopic-release-date-cast-filming/)). Before filming, the California Film Commission awarded the production $21.1 million in state tax credits on $120 million of qualified in-state spending — among the program's largest allocations ever ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/entertainment/hollywood/hollywood-michael-jackson-biopic-gets-21-million-in-california-tax-credits-1.1678191033746)).

The domestic audience did its share: Lionsgate's July 2026 announcement of $1.001 billion — the first biopic ever to clear a billion and the studio's biggest title — put $371.8 million from North America ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/michael-jackson-movie-becomes-the-first-biopic-to-cross-1-billion-at-the-global-box-office/)). The finished run stood at $1,028,706,645: $372.3 million domestic, $656.4 million international. Roughly 64% was earned outside the United States, which ET Now reads as evidence that Jackson's global appeal — not just American fandom — drove the record ([ET Now](https://www.etnownews.com/entertainment/michael-jackson-biopic-michael-crosses-1-billion-the-business-behind-the-biggest-biopic-ever-article-156244963)).

## A federal republic: governance and territory

The United States is a federal republic of 50 states plus the federal District of Columbia. The 48 conterminous states occupy the middle latitudes of North America, bounded by Canada to the north, Mexico and the Gulf of Mexico to the south, and the Atlantic and Pacific oceans; Alaska sits at the continent's northwestern extreme and Hawaii in the mid-Pacific Ocean — together the fourth-largest national territory on Earth at 9,866,632 square kilometres ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/United-States)). Federal legislative power sits with a bicameral Congress: a 100-seat Senate and a 435-seat House of Representatives, also per Britannica.

The constitutional frame is unusually durable. The Declaration of Independence from the British Empire dates to 1776; the U.S. Constitution was written in 1787, ratified in 1788, and in operation since 1789 — which congress.gov calls "the world's longest surviving written charter of government" — and the first ten amendments, the Bill of Rights, were ratified in 1791 ([congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/founding-documents)). No official language or religion is established ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/United-States)).

## The world's largest economy

The World Bank puts U.S. GDP at $28.8 trillion for 2024 against $18.7 trillion for China, the nearest peer — making the American economy about 54% larger than the next-largest in the world. The implication is direct: the same country that supplied more than a third of *Michael*'s $1 billion record gross is, by itself, a larger economy than any other nation ([World Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/?locations=US-CN-EU-ZJ); [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/michael-jackson-movie-becomes-the-first-biopic-to-cross-1-billion-at-the-global-box-office/)).

Britannica describes the country as the "world's greatest economic power" measured in GDP terms, attributing the position to rich natural resources and enormous agricultural output — but "more to the country's highly developed industry" — and notes the economy is more diversified than any other on Earth, sustaining "one of the world's highest standards of living" for most of its people ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/United-States)). GNI per capita stood at $84,534 in 2024 ([World Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/?locations=US-CN-EU-ZJ)). By population the United States is the world's third-largest country, after India and China ([World Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/?locations=US-CN-EU-ZJ)).

## Frequently asked questions

**How many states are in the United States?**
Fifty. The 48 conterminous states sit between Canada and Mexico, with Alaska in the northwest of the continent and Hawaii in the mid-Pacific. The capital, Washington, belongs to no state — it is coextensive with the federal District of Columbia.

**Why is the United States called "America"?**
"America" is a byname — Britannica lists the official name as the United States of America, abbreviated U.S. or U.S.A., with "America" as the common short form. The site uses "United States" throughout because the terms are also used in a broader continental sense.

**Does the United States have an official language?**
No. Britannica's country profile lists the official language as "none": English dominates everyday use and government business, but no language is established by law at the federal level.

**What share of Michael's record gross came from the United States?**
At the $1.001 billion milestone, $371.8 million came from North America; the finished run stood at $1,028,706,645 with $372.3 million domestic and $656.4 million international — about 36% of the total from the U.S. market.
