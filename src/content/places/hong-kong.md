## Overview

Hong Kong is a Special Administrative Region of [China](/places/china/) on the country's south coast, east of the Pearl River estuary, with a provisional population of 7,510,800 at the end of 2025 ([Census & Statistics Department](https://www.news.gov.hk/eng/2026/02/20260212/20260212_163847_941.html)). It consists of Hong Kong Island, the southern Kowloon Peninsula, and the New Territories, together with 230 offshore islands, in a total land area of 1,115 square kilometres — a territory that is 100 percent urban and one of the most densely populated places on Earth, at 6,815 people per square kilometre ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Hong-Kong)).

Hong Kong was ceded and leased to Great Britain in stages — Hong Kong Island in 1842, Kowloon in 1860, the New Territories on a 99-year lease from 1898 — and returned to China on July 1, 1997, under the Sino-British Joint Declaration of December 19, 1984 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Hong-Kong)). Under "one country, two systems" it keeps a high degree of autonomy, including its own capitalist economy and legal system, for 50 years, while China handles defence and foreign affairs ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Hong-Kong)). It is led by a Chief Executive, John Lee, with a Legislative Council as its single legislative house ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Hong-Kong)).

## A harbour that became a financial centre

Hong Kong developed on the basis of its excellent natural harbour — its Chinese name means "fragrant harbour" — and the China trade, and it has since become one of the world's major trade and financial centres ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Hong-Kong)). Since 1969 it has been a major financial hub in the Asia-Pacific, built on free-port status and laissez-faire policies; it has no central bank, with those functions delegated to the Hong Kong Monetary Authority and selected commercial banks, and it operates without exchange controls ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Hong-Kong)). Its foreign-exchange and stock markets are among the most active in the region ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Hong-Kong)).

The population is barely growing: 31,100 births against 50,000 deaths in 2025 produced a natural decrease of 18,900, and the overall 0.1 percent increase came from talent attraction and labour-importation measures bringing in residents from elsewhere ([Census & Statistics Department](https://www.news.gov.hk/eng/2026/02/20260212/20260212_163847_941.html)). Of the 7,510,800 people, 7,232,900 were usual residents and 266,000 were mobile residents ([Census & Statistics Department](https://www.news.gov.hk/eng/2026/02/20260212/20260212_163847_941.html)). Life expectancy is among the world's highest, at 83.3 years for men and 88.7 for women in 2025 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Hong-Kong)). Chinese and English are the official languages; Cantonese is the most commonly spoken, with Mandarin use increasing as the territory reintegrates with China ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Hong-Kong)).

## Baidu's second home market

The fact most Hong Kong profiles miss is how central the territory is to [Baidu](/organizations/baidu/). On March 23, 2021, Baidu made its secondary listing on the main board of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong under stock code 9888.HK, issuing 95,000,000 shares at HK$252 per share and raising a net amount of about HK$23,682 million ([King & Wood Mallesons](https://www.kingandwood.com/cn/en/about-us/media-center/kwm-advises-baidu-on-its-hk-secondary-listing.html)). The proceeds were earmarked for continuous investment in technology, commercialising AI-centred innovations, and growing the Baidu Mobile Ecosystem ([King & Wood Mallesons](https://www.kingandwood.com/cn/en/about-us/media-center/kwm-advises-baidu-on-its-hk-secondary-listing.html)). The listing made Hong Kong Baidu's second home market alongside its Nasdaq listing — and the exchange where its shares trade in the same time zone as its Beijing headquarters.

Hong Kong is also where [Apollo Go](/organizations/apollo-go/) is proving itself in right-hand-drive traffic. In August 2026, the MTR Corporation began road-testing four sixth-generation Baidu Apollo robotaxis in West Kowloon for a shuttle between the West Kowloon high-speed rail hub and the Airport Express station at Kowloon, with the service set to debut in the second quarter of 2027 ([China Daily Asia](https://www.chinadailyasia.com/hk/article/638222)). Apollo Go is the project's contractor, and the first day of on-road testing ran safely and stably on the city's busy roads ([China Daily Asia](https://www.chinadailyasia.com/hk/article/638222)). The opening phase carries no passengers, with a safety operator on board, and MTR will review the results before moving toward passenger tests, subject to Transport Department approval ([China Daily Asia](https://www.chinadailyasia.com/hk/article/638222)). On launch, a dedicated counter at Hong Kong West Kowloon Station will offer in-town check-in and baggage drop-off, sparing travellers the walk through the Elements shopping mall between the two stations ([China Daily Asia](https://www.chinadailyasia.com/hk/article/638222)).

It does not claim a 2025 GDP total, because no fully-read tier1 source gave one; it uses 2024 GNI instead. It does not claim the Apollo Go shuttle is a commercial service — it is a trial with no passengers in its opening phase, and the Q2 2027 launch depends on Transport Department approval. It does not take a position on the state of Hong Kong's autonomy beyond Britannica's description of "one country, two systems" and the 2020 security law. And it does not claim Hong Kong Island is Hong Kong: the island is about 81 square kilometres of a 1,115-square-kilometre territory.
