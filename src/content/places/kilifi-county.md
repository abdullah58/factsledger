## Overview

Kilifi County is one of Kenya's 47 counties (county code 003), a coastal county on the Indian Ocean formed in 2010 through the merger of Kilifi District and Malindi District ([Council of Governors, Kenya](https://maarifa.cog.go.ke/node/803)). Its capital is Kilifi town and its largest town is Malindi; it covers 12,245.9 square kilometres and had a population of 1,453,787 ([Council of Governors, Kenya](https://maarifa.cog.go.ke/node/803)). The county sits north and northeast of Mombasa, and it is the home of two UNESCO-recognised sites — Gedi and the Mijikenda Kaya forests — and some of Africa's earliest marine protected areas ([Council of Governors, Kenya](https://maarifa.cog.go.ke/node/803); [The Eastleigh Voice](https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/coast/62466/kilifis-gedi-ruins-listed-as-unesco-world-heritage-site); [Kenya Wildlife Service](https://kws.go.ke/park/watamu-marine-national-park-reserve/)).

## Gedi: a World Heritage site in the county

The story no top search result tells about Kilifi County is its 2024 elevation in the heritage world. On 31 July 2024, at the 46th session of the World Heritage Committee in New Delhi, the Historic Town and Archaeological Site of Gedi — in Watamu, Kilifi County — was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List ([UNESCO World Heritage Centre](https://whc.unesco.org/en/news/2708/)). The listing came after years of nomination work supported by UNESCO's technical assistance on the continent.

Gedi is the second site in Kilifi County to receive the recognition, after the sacred Mijikenda Kaya forests, and the eighth World Heritage site in Kenya ([The Eastleigh Voice](https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/coast/62466/kilifis-gedi-ruins-listed-as-unesco-world-heritage-site)). The recognition followed deliberations by 195 countries and more than 2,500 delegates, with Kenya's delegation led by Professor Peter Ngure, the country's Permanent Delegate to UNESCO and Ambassador to India ([The Eastleigh Voice](https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/coast/62466/kilifis-gedi-ruins-listed-as-unesco-world-heritage-site)). Kilifi's governor, Gideon Mung'aro, celebrated the announcement publicly, crediting the National Museums of Kenya and the county's Department of Gender, Culture, and Social Services for their roles in securing it ([The Eastleigh Voice](https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/coast/62466/kilifis-gedi-ruins-listed-as-unesco-world-heritage-site)).

The Council of Governors describes the ruins as mosques and tombs dating from the 11th to the 17th century, making Gedi one of the county's signature historical sites ([Council of Governors, Kenya](https://maarifa.cog.go.ke/node/803)). June Chepkemei, CEO of the Kenya Tourism Board, said the designation would enhance Kenya's appeal as a tourist destination and help preserve its diverse heritage ([The Eastleigh Voice](https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/coast/62466/kilifis-gedi-ruins-listed-as-unesco-world-heritage-site)).

## Coast, reefs and one of Africa's first marine reserves

Kilifi's coastline runs north and northeast of Mombasa, with tourist beach areas at Kikambala, Watamu, Malindi and Kilifi ([Council of Governors, Kenya](https://maarifa.cog.go.ke/node/803)). But the coast's deeper distinction is conservation, not just tourism. The Watamu Marine National Park and Reserve was gazetted on 26 March 1968 through Legal Notices No. 98 and 99, established to conserve representative coral reef and mangrove ecosystems, promote scientific research and support sustainable use of marine resources ([Kenya Wildlife Service](https://kws.go.ke/park/watamu-marine-national-park-reserve/)). The park itself covers approximately 10 square kilometres and is a no-take zone; the surrounding reserve, including Mida Creek, allows controlled and sustainable resource use ([Kenya Wildlife Service](https://kws.go.ke/park/watamu-marine-national-park-reserve/)).

The broader Watamu Marine Protected Area also takes in part of the Malindi Marine National Reserve, and all three components were gazetted simultaneously in 1968, making Watamu one of Africa's earliest formally protected marine ecosystems ([Kenya Wildlife Service](https://kws.go.ke/park/watamu-marine-national-park-reserve/)). Then, in 1979, Watamu and Malindi became the first marine protected areas in Africa — and the third anywhere in the world — to be designated UNESCO Man and the Biosphere reserves, recognised as a global model for balancing biodiversity conservation, sustainable development and scientific research ([Kenya Wildlife Service](https://kws.go.ke/park/watamu-marine-national-park-reserve/)).

## Governance

Kilifi County came into being with Kenya's devolved government system in 2010 ([Council of Governors, Kenya](https://maarifa.cog.go.ke/node/803)). Its second governor, Gideon Mung'aro, was sworn in during the last week of August 2022, succeeding Amason Kingi, who had just finished his second and final term ([K24 Digital](https://k24.digital/uncategorized/mungaro-sacks-all-chief-officers/amp/)).

Early in his administration, Mung'aro laid out a programme centred on reviving tourism: building a sports complex, sponsoring a bill to create a Kilifi Tourism Board with its own marketing strategy rather than relying on the national Kenya Tourism Board, and promoting the beaches, marine national parks, sports and conference tourism ([Kenya News Agency](https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/kilifi-govt-to-promote-sports-tourism-mungaro/)). He pointed to two infrastructure projects — the expansion of the Malindi International Airport and the upgrading of the Mombasa-Malindi road to a dual carriageway — as levers for tourism growth, and he drew on his own past, having been Malindi's mayor between 2000 and 2002, when he said the town was once declared the cleanest in East and Central Africa ([Kenya News Agency](https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/kilifi-govt-to-promote-sports-tourism-mungaro/)).

## 2025 New York Times travel list

In 2025, Kilifi, Kenya was named on the [New York Times](/organizations/new-york-times/)' annual "52 Places to Go" travel list. Independent accounts of the list differ on its exact numbered position: Time Out reports it as the 31st entry, while The Travel Sisters report it as 32nd ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/news/these-are-the-52-places-to-go-in-2025-according-to-the-new-york-times-010725); [The Travel Sisters](https://thetravelsisters.com/new-york-times-52-places-to-go-in-2025/)).
