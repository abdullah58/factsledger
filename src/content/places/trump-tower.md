## Overview

Trump Tower was built in a hurry, and the hurry is the story. [Donald Trump](https://factsledger.com/people/donald-trump/) needed to finish construction by the end of 1983 to qualify for a major city tax break — so the team built the 58-story tower in reinforced concrete instead of steel, letting the schedule drive the engineering ([Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/trump-towerdesigned-by-der-scutt/)). The rush left marks beyond the structure. The handsome Art Deco Bonwit Teller department store that stood on the site was demolished to make way for the tower, and two of its bas-relief sculptures — which Trump had promised to donate to the Metropolitan Museum of Art — were smashed to pieces on site instead, deemed too expensive to remove ([BDC Magazine](https://bdcmagazine.com/2016/11/how-trumps-towers-explain-his-politics/)). What rose in their place at 721–725 Fifth Avenue was a 664-foot exercise in branding as architecture: a jagged, 28-sided shaft of bronze-tinted glass whose sawtooth shape was engineered, quite deliberately, to maximize corner apartments and their Fifth Avenue views ([Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/trump-towerdesigned-by-der-scutt/)).

## The building

Der Scutt, lead architect at Swanke Hayden Connell, designed the tower to express its developer's ambition — Trump and Scutt had begun the project in the late 1970s, before Trump owned the property, held air rights from neighboring Tiffany's, or had financing in place ([Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/trump-towerdesigned-by-der-scutt/)). Since its construction it has functioned as [the Trump Organization](/organizations/trump-organization/)'s corporate headquarters as well as Trump's own residence ([Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/trump-towerdesigned-by-der-scutt/)). The base holds a five-story indoor arcade with an 80-foot waterfall, marble walls, and stores; above it sit offices, then 39 levels of residences, with Trump's triplex penthouse crowning the top ([Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/trump-towerdesigned-by-der-scutt/)). The brass "TRUMP TOWER" sign over the door was set in Stymie Extra Bold — Scutt wanted 17-inch letters, and Trump insisted on 34 inches so they would be visible to people flying into New York ([Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/trump-towerdesigned-by-der-scutt/)). The building has 58 actual stories, though its own marketing has long claimed 68 by skipping floor numbers ([BDC Magazine](https://bdcmagazine.com/2016/11/how-trumps-towers-explain-his-politics/)). The branding outgrew the building: the oversized logo, repeated constantly throughout the tower, became the template for subsequent Trump properties, publications, merchandise, and even board games ([Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/trump-towerdesigned-by-der-scutt/)).

## The stage

Few buildings have served as a political stage the way this one has. On June 16, 2015, Donald Trump gave his presidential announcement speech in the tower's atrium, descending a gold escalator before a crowd of supporters ([NPR](https://www.wcmu.org/politics/2015-06-16/donald-trump-jumps-into-gop-presidential-race)). The tower then served as his campaign headquarters, and after the election the surrounding blocks of Midtown were turned into a semi-permanent security barricade ([Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/trump-towerdesigned-by-der-scutt/)). A year after the escalator ride, the tower hosted the June 2016 meeting in which Donald Trump Jr. — expecting, he was told, damaging information about [Hillary Clinton](https://factsledger.com/people/hillary-clinton/) as part of a Russian government effort to aid his father's campaign — sat down with Jared Kushner, then-campaign chairman Paul Manafort, Russian lawyer Natalia Veselnitskaya, and lobbyist Rinat Akhmetshin ([NPR](https://www.kedm.org/politics-government/2017-07-14/russian-lobbyist-also-attended-meeting-with-donald-trump-jr?_amp=true)).

The tower's other career is television. It has been the filming location of NBC's *The Apprentice* since 2004, its boardroom and the catchphrase "You're fired!" becoming a national fixture ([Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/trump-towerdesigned-by-der-scutt/)). Politics never fully moved out, either: reporting has noted a MAGA-aligned political action committee paying $37,500 a month for office space in the building, plus $3,000 for a nonoperational lobby kiosk ([Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/trump-towerdesigned-by-der-scutt/)). And in its most recent act, the building houses the offices of Dominari Securities, the Trump-family-linked brokerage — Eric Trump holds a significant stake and both Trump brothers sit on its advisory board, according to trade-press reporting ([Coinpedia](https://www.tradingview.com/news/coinpedia:049d6ad37094b:0-trump-family-linked-brokerage-dominari-securities-faces-us-lawmakers-probe-over-chinese-stocks/)). Dominari's subsidiaries, in turn, run the [American Ventures](https://factsledger.com/organizations/american-ventures/) investment vehicles, per Financial Times reporting ([Financial Times via reprint](https://wemaple.canadiana.news/2026/05/01/trump-sons-crypto-linked-bets-run-into-mining-security-and-financing-conflict-overseas-ft/)).
