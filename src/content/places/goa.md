**Goa** is a state on India's western Konkan coast, capital Panaji, which became India's 25th state on 30 May 1987 after more than 450 years of Portuguese rule ([TFIpost](https://tfipost.com/2022/05/goa-statehood-day-the-journey-of-goa-from-a-colony-to-an-indian-state/)). Its 40-member Legislative Assembly governs the state ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/goa/bjp-goa-pramod-sawant-cm-assembly-polls-7813265/lite/)), and since 2004 Goa has been the permanent home of the [International Film Festival of India](/events/international-film-festival-of-india/) (IFFI), organized jointly by the National Film Development Corporation, the [Ministry of Information and Broadcasting](/organizations/ministry-of-information-and-broadcasting/), and the Entertainment Society of Goa ([Press Information Bureau](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190314&reg=3&lang=2)).

Goa combines India's highest per-capita income with one of its densest tourist economies: ₹3,57,611 per-capita NSDP in FY 2023-24 ([Times Now](https://www.timesnownews.com/business-economy/economy/the-richest-and-the-poorest-states-of-india-revealed-by-nso-see-where-your-state-ranks-article-152324901)) against 104.09 lakh tourist arrivals in 2024 — roughly the highest visitor-to-resident ratio of any Indian state ([The Navhind Times](https://navhindtimes.in/goanews/21-growth-rate-promises-positive-trend-of-tourism-economic-survey/)).

## From Portuguese colony to India's 25th state

The Portuguese arrived in Goa in the early 16th century and refused to leave after Indian independence in 1947; the Indian Army's Operation Vijay ended more than 450 years of colonial rule on 19 December 1961, and Goa was organized as a union territory with Daman and Diu ([TFIpost](https://tfipost.com/2022/05/goa-statehood-day-the-journey-of-goa-from-a-colony-to-an-indian-state/)). On 16 January 1967, Goans voted in a referendum on merging with Maharashtra or remaining a union territory and chose to stay separate ([TFIpost](https://tfipost.com/2022/05/goa-statehood-day-the-journey-of-goa-from-a-colony-to-an-indian-state/)). Full statehood came on 30 May 1987 under the Goa, Daman and Diu Reorganisation Act, which fixed that date as the appointed day and inserted Goa as entry 25 in the Constitution's First Schedule — India's 25th state — with Daman and Diu remaining a union territory ([CourtKutchehry](https://www.courtkutchehry.com/bare-acts/goa-daman-and-dil-reorganisation4ct-1987/)). Panaji became the capital and Konkani the official language ([TFIpost](https://tfipost.com/2022/05/goa-statehood-day-the-journey-of-goa-from-a-colony-to-an-indian-state/)). The state is governed by a unicameral Legislative Assembly of 40 seats ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/goa/bjp-goa-pramod-sawant-cm-assembly-polls-7813265/lite/)) and falls under the common High Court of Bombay ([CourtKutchehry](https://www.courtkutchehry.com/bare-acts/goa-daman-and-dil-reorganisation4ct-1987/)).

## The permanent home of the International Film Festival of India

Since 2004, Goa has been the permanent home of the International Film Festival of India, jointly organized by the National Film Development Corporation, the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, and the Entertainment Society of Goa ([Press Information Bureau](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190314&reg=3&lang=2)). Founded in 1952, IFFI is South Asia's only FIAPF-accredited competitive film festival ([Press Information Bureau](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190314&reg=3&lang=2)). Its 56th edition ran from 20 to 28 November 2025 with 240+ films from 81 countries, drawing a record 2,314 submissions from 127 countries ([Press Information Bureau](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190314&reg=3&lang=2)). That scale is the payoff of the 2004 decision: two decades of a fixed Goa address have converted a domestic film festival into a global submission funnel, which in turn gives the festival — and its host state — a standing platform for government-backed cultural launches.

IFFI Goa has become exactly that kind of launch platform. The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting chose the festival to launch Season 2 of the animated series [Krish, Trish and Baltiboy](/creative-works/krish-trish-and-baltiboy/) — subtitled "Bharat Hain Hum," a co-production dramatizing lesser-known freedom fighters — with actor Nagarjuna at the launch; the season released in 12 Indian and 7 international languages and on [Prasar Bharati](/organizations/prasar-bharati/)'s Waves OTT platform ([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/entertainment/second-season-of-animated-series-krish-trish-and-baltiboy-bharat-hain-hum-launched-at-iffi/)).

## Economy and tourism in numbers

Goa recorded India's highest per-capita income: ₹3,57,611 per-capita NSDP in FY 2023-24 at constant prices, per National Statistical Office data placed before the Lok Sabha by Minister of State for Finance Pankaj Chaudhary — ahead of Sikkim (₹2,92,339) and Delhi (₹2,71,490) ([Times Now](https://www.timesnownews.com/business-economy/economy/the-richest-and-the-poorest-states-of-india-revealed-by-nso-see-where-your-state-ranks-article-152324901)). The state Economic Survey 2025-26 estimated 2024-25 GSDP at ₹1,12,525.47 crore, up 15.01% over the previous year, with per-capita income at current prices estimated at ₹7,09,045 — growth led by manufacturing, power and aviation, with Manohar International Airport's operations lifting transport's share of GSDP ([The Navhind Times](https://navhindtimes.in/goanews/manufacturing-power-aviation-propel-goas-gsdp-to-15-growth/)). Tourism is the public face of that economy: Goa received 104.09 lakh visitors in 2024 — 99.41 lakh domestic and about 4.68 lakh foreign — a 21% rise over 86.28 lakh in 2023 ([The Navhind Times](https://navhindtimes.in/goanews/21-growth-rate-promises-positive-trend-of-tourism-economic-survey/); [The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/as-goa-sees-an-uptick-in-tourists-govt-has-some-reasons-why-9999320/lite/)). Registered hotels nearly tripled from roughly 3,000 in 2022 to about 9,000 in 2024, and the Survey describes Goa as the first Indian state to adopt a regenerative-tourism model ([The Navhind Times](https://navhindtimes.in/goanews/21-growth-rate-promises-positive-trend-of-tourism-economic-survey/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Goa become a state of India?**
On 30 May 1987, under the Goa, Daman and Diu Reorganisation Act, which fixed that date as the appointed day and inserted Goa as entry 25 in the Constitution's First Schedule — making it India's 25th state, with Daman and Diu remaining a union territory. Goa Statehood Day is observed every 30 May.

**Why is the International Film Festival of India held in Goa?**
Goa was declared IFFI's permanent venue in 2004. The festival is jointly organized there by the National Film Development Corporation, the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, and the Entertainment Society of Goa. The 56th edition in November 2025 screened 240+ films from 81 countries.

**What is Goa's per-capita income?**
Goa has India's highest: ₹3,57,611 per-capita NSDP in FY 2023-24 at constant prices, per National Statistical Office data tabled in the Lok Sabha by Minister of State for Finance Pankaj Chaudhary — ahead of Sikkim and Delhi. The state Economic Survey estimated per-capita income at current prices at ₹7,09,045 in 2024-25.

**What is the connection between Goa and "Krish, Trish and Baltiboy"?**
The Ministry of Information and Broadcasting launched Season 2 of the animated series — subtitled "Bharat Hain Hum" — at IFFI Goa, with actor Nagarjuna at the launch. The season dramatizes lesser-known freedom fighters and released in 12 Indian and 7 international languages, including on Prasar Bharati's Waves platform.
