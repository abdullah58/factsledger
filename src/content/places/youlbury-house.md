## Overview

Youlbury House is a Grade II listed modernist house on Boars Hill, three miles southwest of Oxford, built in
1969–1971 on the site of a much larger Victorian mansion that had belonged to the archaeologist Sir Arthur
Evans, excavator of Knossos ([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2016/may/15/classic-1970s-house-in-oxfordshire)).
It is one of only three houses ever designed by the landscape architect Hal Moggridge, and its interior was
photographed for the RIBA's architectural collection in 1972
([RIBA pix](https://www.ribapix.com/youlbury-house-boars-hill-oxford_riba43155)).

## Arthur Evans's Youlbury

Evans began building his mansion in 1893, the year his wife Margaret died, on Boar's Hill south of Oxford,
against his father's advice — he named it Youlbury after the locality
([The New York Review of Books](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/2001/11/01/the-labyrinth-of-arthur-evans/)).
Richard Jenkyns, writing in the Review, describes "an enormous rambling mansion" where a dozen servants attended
to Evans's comforts: there were marble and columns in the hall, a mosaic of the Minotaur, and a marble Roman
bath with steps leading down into it
([The New York Review of Books](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/2001/11/01/the-labyrinth-of-arthur-evans/)).
The same account gives the house as twenty-two bedrooms and five bathrooms — against the Guardian's description
of a 26-bedroom house, a discrepancy this profile flags rather than resolves
([The New York Review of Books](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/2001/11/01/the-labyrinth-of-arthur-evans/)).
Evans's Christmas parties at Youlbury were famous, and he was known to enjoy the company of the young
([The New York Review of Books](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/2001/11/01/the-labyrinth-of-arthur-evans/)).

After Evans's death in 1941 the mansion fell into disrepair and was eventually demolished
([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2016/may/15/classic-1970s-house-in-oxfordshire)),
but the lake Evans made in the garden survives, set in woods thick with rhododendrons
([The New York Review of Books](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/2001/11/01/the-labyrinth-of-arthur-evans/)).

## A rare Moggridge house

William Goodhart — a human rights lawyer and Liberal Democrat politician later made a life peer as Baron
Goodhart — and his wife Celia acquired the site in 1966
([Country Life](https://www.countrylife.co.uk/property/a-very-private-property-79719)). Their new house was
designed by Hal Moggridge, Celia's brother-in-law. The RIBA's photo archive records the design under the
practice name Colvin & Moggridge — the firm Moggridge co-founded with the landscape architect Brenda Colvin —
and notes that it was built for William and Celia Goodhart and listed Grade II in 2009
([RIBA pix](https://www.ribapix.com/youlbury-house-boars-hill-oxford_riba43155)). It remains one of only three
residential buildings Moggridge — later known chiefly as a landscape architect — ever designed
([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2016/may/15/classic-1970s-house-in-oxfordshire)).

Moggridge has said he drew on Frank Lloyd Wright and Alvar Aalto, and that the house was originally designed
with a fully glazed front before concerns about overexposure led to windows "punched into the wooden walls"
instead ([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2016/may/15/classic-1970s-house-in-oxfordshire)).
Country Life adds that the structural engineering was by Ove Arup and Partners, and describes the design as
recalling Wright's Fallingwater, with the building stepping down the hillside from a single storey at one end to
three storeys at the other above a lake
([Country Life](https://www.countrylife.co.uk/property/a-very-private-property-79719)). WowHaus's account of
the Savills listing fills in the interior: the entrance leads into a hallway that "diagonally transects" the
length of the building, with the kitchen and dining room at the northern end and a dual-aspect master bedroom at
the southern end; at the centre, a curving cement staircase connects the lower-level playroom to the drawing
room above, whose double-height ceiling is lined in Parana pine
([WowHaus](https://www.wowhaus.co.uk/2015/10/23/on-the-market-1970s-hal-moggridge-designed-youlbury-house-in-oxford-oxfordshire/)).

The house received Grade II listed status in July 2009, which Country Life notes is unusual for a building of
its era ([Country Life](https://www.countrylife.co.uk/property/a-very-private-property-79719);
[Historic England](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1393396)).

## The Goodharts and the sale

The Goodharts' daughter, Laura Watts, told the Guardian the house felt "a bit like a treehouse," recalling deer
and frogs in the surrounding woodland and games played in the built-in storage cupboards
([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2016/may/15/classic-1970s-house-in-oxfordshire)). The
same article describes the house as a hub for political and academic gatherings, given William Goodhart's career,
while noting the family "usually avoided talking politics" with a Conservative-MP relative who lived nearby
([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2016/may/15/classic-1970s-house-in-oxfordshire)).

By 2015, the estate — by then comprising the main house on 9.6 acres plus separate cottages and land totalling
around 27 acres — was put on the market through Savills, split into several lots with a combined guide price of
about £4.5 million ([Country Life](https://www.countrylife.co.uk/property/a-very-private-property-79719)).
WowHaus reported the main house alone at a guide price of £1,950,000, and described the separate lots: an
agent's cottage with walled gardens that had planning permission for a six-bedroom replacement property, plus a
gardener's cottage and a bailiff's cottage
([WowHaus](https://www.wowhaus.co.uk/2015/10/23/on-the-market-1970s-hal-moggridge-designed-youlbury-house-in-oxford-oxfordshire/)).
William Goodhart died on 10 January 2017, as announced to the House of Lords
([Hansard](https://hansard.parliament.uk/lords/2017-01-12/debates/A5BE4D5E-411D-4F74-AC48-327F9DFEFA5D/DeathOfAFormerMemberLordGoodhart)).

## A different Youlbury

The Youlbury Scout activity centre — a facility set in 42 acres of woodland at Boars Hill, Oxford
([UK School Trips](https://www.ukschooltrips.co.uk/scout-adventures-youlbury-activity-centre-oxfordshire/)) —
is a separate entity near the house, not part of it. This profile covers only the house.
