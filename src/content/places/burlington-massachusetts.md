## Overview

Burlington is a town of 26,377 people in Middlesex County, Massachusetts, that stayed small and slow-growing until Route 128 was built along its southern border in the 1950s turned it into one of the Boston area's main technology hubs. Settled in 1641 and incorporated in 1799, it is governed by a 126-member Representative Town Meeting and has hosted the headquarters of [Nuance Communications](/organizations/nuance-communications/), [Cerence](/organizations/cerence/), Keurig Dr Pepper and Avid Technology.

The town's population was 26,377 at the 2020 census, up from 24,498 in 2010, with an estimated 26,778 residents as of July 1, 2025 ([U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/burlingtontownmiddlesexcountymassachusetts/PST045224)). Burlington is a town, not a city: it has never adopted a city form of government, and its legislative body is a Representative Town Meeting of 126 elected members ([Town of Burlington](https://www.burlington.org/1396/Town-Meeting-Member-Information)). The Commonwealth of Massachusetts records Burlington as settled in 1641 and incorporated in 1799 ([Commonwealth of Massachusetts (Mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/cities-and-towns-of-the-commonwealth-of-massachusetts-at-250)).

## From slow-growing settlement to Route 128 boom town

Burlington's early history is one of patience. A meetinghouse was raised in 1732 as a second parish of Woburn, and the town was incorporated separately in 1799 ([Society of Architectural Historians (SAH Archipedia)](https://sah-archipedia.org/essays/MA-01-0009-0002)). Growth remained slow until after World War II, and the town was bypassed by the Boston–Merrimac railroad, which routed its expansion elsewhere ([Society of Architectural Historians (SAH Archipedia)](https://sah-archipedia.org/essays/MA-01-0009-0002)).

The automobile changed everything. Construction of Route 128 in the 1950s along the town's southern border "ignited a development wildfire," in the words of the Society of Architectural Historians ([Society of Architectural Historians (SAH Archipedia)](https://sah-archipedia.org/essays/MA-01-0009-0002)). Between 1955 and 1965 Burlington grew faster than any other town in Massachusetts, tripling its population between 1955 and 1960 by adding over 1,520 new residents a year ([Society of Architectural Historians (SAH Archipedia)](https://sah-archipedia.org/essays/MA-01-0009-0002)). The Burlington Mall, a massive regional shopping and office complex along the 128 corridor, opened in 1968 ([Society of Architectural Historians (SAH Archipedia)](https://sah-archipedia.org/essays/MA-01-0009-0002)). The mall later became a film location: the Kevin James movie *Paul Blart: Mall Cop* was filmed in the Burlington Mall ([Commonwealth of Massachusetts (Mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/cities-and-towns-of-the-commonwealth-of-massachusetts-at-250)).

## A headquarters cluster on the 128 corridor

Burlington's post-war growth was driven by technology and consumer-goods companies that put their headquarters on the Route 128 corridor. Nuance Communications listed its principal executive offices at 1 Wayside Road, Burlington, in a 2019 SEC filing ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002517/000095014219000145/0000950142-19-000145.txt)). Cerence, the automotive-AI company spun out of Nuance in 2019, is headquartered in Burlington; its October 2024 announcement appointing [Brian Krzanich](/people/brian-krzanich/) as chief executive described the company as "headquartered in Burlington, Massachusetts" ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1768267/000119312524233891/d869370dex991.htm)).

The cluster extends beyond speech and automotive software. Keurig Dr Pepper's principal executive offices are at 53 South Avenue, Burlington, and the company describes itself as having "two corporate headquarters, located in Burlington, Massachusetts and Frisco, Texas" ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1418135/000141813521000005/kdp-20201231.htm)). Avid Technology, the media-software company, was headquartered at 75 Network Drive, Burlington, according to its 2017 annual report ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896841/000089684118000036/avid-12312017x10k.htm)). The commercial weight of the town is visible in the census data: Burlington recorded $1,830,648,000 in retail sales in 2022, or $70,288 per capita — far above the level a town of 26,000 would generate from its own residents alone ([U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/burlingtontownmiddlesexcountymassachusetts/PST045224)).

## Government and demographics

Burlington is governed by a Representative Town Meeting of 126 members, 18 from each of seven precincts, elected for staggered three-year terms, with a quorum of 64 ([Town of Burlington](https://www.burlington.org/1396/Town-Meeting-Member-Information)). The form was established by Chapter 686 of the Acts of 1970, signed on August 14, 1970, and approved by Burlington voters on March 6, 1971, by 4,307 votes to 1,557; the first members were elected on March 4, 1972 ([Town of Burlington](https://www.burlington.org/1396/Town-Meeting-Member-Information)). The Town Meeting holds three regularly scheduled meetings each year: on the second Monday of May, when the annual budget is considered, and on the fourth Monday of September and January ([Town of Burlington](https://www.burlington.org/1396/Town-Meeting-Member-Information)). Executive authority rests with the Select Board, which prepares and issues the Town Meeting warrant ([Town of Burlington](https://www.burlington.org/1396/Town-Meeting-Member-Information)).

The town's population is affluent and highly educated by Massachusetts standards. Median household income in 2020–2024 was $146,436, per capita income was $69,012, and 5.9% of residents lived in poverty ([U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/burlingtontownmiddlesexcountymassachusetts/PST045224)). Some 58.4% of residents aged 25 and older hold a bachelor's degree or higher, and 95.9% hold a high school diploma or higher ([U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/burlingtontownmiddlesexcountymassachusetts/PST045224)). The median value of an owner-occupied home was $740,600, median gross rent was $2,718, and 17.9% of residents were foreign-born ([U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/burlingtontownmiddlesexcountymassachusetts/PST045224)).

## Notable people

Two of Burlington's best-known names come from opposite ends of public life. Roderick MacKinnon was born in Burlington on February 19, 1956, and won the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 2003 for structural and mechanistic studies of ion channels ([NobelPrize.org](http://nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2003/MacKinnon/facts/)). Comedian and actress Amy Poehler, born in Newton, grew up in Burlington; she was a cast member of *Saturday Night Live* from 2001 to 2008 and starred in the sitcom *Parks and Recreation* from 2009 to 2015 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://mendel-preview.dev-ext.britannica.com/biography/Amy-Poehler/Introduction)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Burlington, Massachusetts a city or a town?**
Burlington is a town, not a city. It is governed by a Representative Town Meeting of 126 elected members, with executive authority held by the Select Board ([Town of Burlington](https://www.burlington.org/1396/Town-Meeting-Member-Information)).

**How many people live in Burlington, Massachusetts?**
The 2020 census recorded 26,377 residents, up from 24,498 in 2010, with an estimated 26,778 as of July 1, 2025 ([U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/burlingtontownmiddlesexcountymassachusetts/PST045224)).

**Why is Burlington known for technology?**
Route 128 was built along the town's southern border in the 1950s, and the highway corridor drew technology companies to Burlington, including Nuance Communications and Cerence ([Society of Architectural Historians (SAH Archipedia)](https://sah-archipedia.org/essays/MA-01-0009-0002)).

**Was Paul Blart: Mall Cop filmed in Burlington?**
Yes. The Kevin James movie *Paul Blart: Mall Cop* was filmed in the Burlington Mall, according to the Commonwealth of Massachusetts ([Commonwealth of Massachusetts (Mass.gov)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/cities-and-towns-of-the-commonwealth-of-massachusetts-at-250)).

**What companies are headquartered in Burlington, Massachusetts?**
Nuance Communications listed its principal executive offices at 1 Wayside Road, Cerence is headquartered in the town, Keurig Dr Pepper lists 53 South Avenue as a corporate headquarters, and Avid Technology was headquartered at 75 Network Drive — all verified from SEC filings ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1002517/000095014219000145/0000950142-19-000145.txt); [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1768267/000119312524233891/d869370dex991.htm); [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1418135/000141813521000005/kdp-20201231.htm); [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896841/000089684118000036/avid-12312017x10k.htm)).

## What this profile does not claim

- This profile does not assert that Burlington was settled in 1640: the Commonwealth of Massachusetts and the Massachusetts Secretary of State record 1641, and 1641 is used here.
- It does not assert that the town's name comes from Bridlington, Yorkshire; that derivation is believed but has never been confirmed.
- It does not give a list of Burlington's bordering towns, schools or conservation areas, because those details were found only in secondary sources that were not read in full for this profile.
- It does not list Progress Software among Burlington's headquarters, because the filing placing it at 15 Wayside Road was not read in full.
- It does not claim that Nuance Communications is still headquartered at 1 Wayside Road today: the filing cited is from 2019, before Microsoft acquired the company in 2022.
- Burlington is a town in [Massachusetts](/places/massachusetts/) in the [United States](/places/united-states/); this profile is about that town and not about any other place named Burlington.
