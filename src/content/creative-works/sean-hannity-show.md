## Overview

*The Sean Hannity Show* is the rare national institution whose birth date doubles as a historical marker. The conservative talk-radio program entered national syndication in September 2001 — its syndicator, Premiere Networks, frames the launch itself as "a storied syndication launch the day before the 9/11 attacks," which dates it to September 10, 2001 ([Premiere Networks](https://www.premierenetworks.com/press/sean-hannity-show-marks-20th-year-syndication); [Inside Radio](https://www.insideradio.com/free/sean-hannity-marks-25-years-in-national-syndication/article_8e3e94f8-c8b0-4576-ac0f-e2ef3630ce10.html)). A quarter century later, the show remains the format's anchor: in September 2026 Premiere announced the program's 25th year in syndication on more than 700 stations reaching 99% of the United States, with Premiere calling [Sean Hannity](https://factsledger.com/people/sean-hannity/) the most-listened-to talk radio host in the country and the show's podcast feed past 250 million downloads ([Inside Radio](https://www.insideradio.com/free/sean-hannity-marks-25-years-in-national-syndication/article_8e3e94f8-c8b0-4576-ac0f-e2ef3630ce10.html)). The program airs live weekdays from 3 to 6 p.m. ET on iHeartRadio and Hannity.com ([Everand](https://www.everand.com/podcast/448196262/Just-The-Tweets)).

## From college radio to national syndication

Hannity's radio career began inauspiciously. His first talk-radio experience came in 1989 as a host on KCSB-FM, the volunteer college station of UC Santa Barbara; he then placed advertisements in radio publications billing himself "the most talked about college radio host in America," which won him a job at WVNN in Athens, Alabama, before he succeeded Neal Boortz at WGST in Atlanta in 1992 ([The Sean Hannity Show (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20120105234529/http://www.hannity.com/article/about-sean-hannity/14852)). His break came in September 1996, when Fox News founder Roger Ailes hired him to co-host *Hannity & Colmes*, and the radio show's New York flagship was WABC (AM) by 2012 ([The Sean Hannity Show (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20120105234529/http://www.hannity.com/article/about-sean-hannity/14852)).

The station history took a dramatic turn in January 2014, when Hannity shifted the program from WABC to WOR in New York, with Michael Savage taking over his old slots ([The American Spectator](https://spectator.org/hannity-radio-surges/)). The early Nielsen returns vindicated the move: comparing January 2013 with January 2014, the WOR audience in Hannity's time slot was up 22% in the coveted 25–54 demographic while Savage's inherited WABC audience was down 34.9%, with a similar pattern in Dallas, where Savage fell 40% and Hannity quadrupled his new station's ratings ([The American Spectator](https://spectator.org/hannity-radio-surges/)).

## The audience, the awards, and the microphone

The show's business case has always been its audience, and the numbers in its own anniversary materials are the most defensible: at the 20th anniversary in September 2021, Premiere put the program on more than 650 stations reaching "tens of millions" of listeners, with a +19% audience gain in adults 25–54 (Nielsen Audio, FA'20) and a #1 ranking among news/talk stations in New York, Philadelphia, Miami, Tampa, Raleigh, Norfolk, Jacksonville, West Palm Beach, Houston, San Antonio, Phoenix, San Diego, Las Vegas, Atlanta, and other markets ([Premiere Networks](https://www.premierenetworks.com/press/sean-hannity-show-marks-20th-year-syndication)). Trade recognition followed the scale: the TALKERS industry ranking placed Hannity No. 1 on its Heavy Hundred list of the most important radio talk hosts in 2021 ([Premiere Networks](https://www.premierenetworks.com/press/sean-hannity-show-marks-20th-year-syndication)), and Premiere says he has held that No. 1 spot for five consecutive years through 2026 ([Inside Radio](https://www.insideradio.com/free/sean-hannity-marks-25-years-in-national-syndication/article_8e3e94f8-c8b0-4576-ac0f-e2ef3630ce10.html)). The trophy case includes two National Association of Broadcasters Marconi Awards for Nationally Syndicated Personality of the Year, three consecutive Radio & Records National Talk Show Host of the Year wins, and a 2017 induction into the Radio Hall of Fame ([Premiere Networks](https://www.premierenetworks.com/press/sean-hannity-show-marks-20th-year-syndication); [Inside Radio](https://www.insideradio.com/free/sean-hannity-marks-25-years-in-national-syndication/article_8e3e94f8-c8b0-4576-ac0f-e2ef3630ce10.html)).

The program's reach shows up in the episodes that became news events. On October 10, 2019, the show broadcast live from the Minnesota Trump rally, with Fox News contributor [Lawrence Jones](/people/lawrence-jones/) and author [Trisha Hope](https://factsledger.com/people/trisha-hope/) as guests — and Hope used the airtime to announce a fundraising tie-in with [Bikers for Trump](https://factsledger.com/organizations/bikers-for-trump/): $5.00 of each sale of her book *Just the Tweets* would go to the group when buyers used the coupon code "bikers" ([Everand](https://www.everand.com/podcast/448196262/Just-The-Tweets)). A talk show that launched the day before 9/11 had become, two decades on, a rally floor with its own merchandise economy.

## What this profile does not claim

It does not assert a single current weekly-listener figure — estimates in older coverage range from roughly 11 to 16 million depending on the era and the source, and this profile instead uses Premiere Networks' own 2021 and 2026 framings with attribution. It does not give the years of the two Marconi Awards (commonly cited as 2003 and 2007), because those years were not verified to a source read in full. It does not repeat reported contract figures, including a widely cited 2008 five-year, $100 million deal, for the same reason. The early career history comes from the host's own archived site — usable for uncontested dates and stations, but first-party. And the "most-listened-to talk radio host" claim is Premiere's, reported by the trade press — not an independent audit.
