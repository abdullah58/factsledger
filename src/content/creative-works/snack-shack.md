## Overview

**Snack Shack** is Adam Rehmeier's 2024 coming-of-age comedy about AJ (Conor
Sherry) and Moose (Gabriel LaBelle), inseparable best friends who run the
rundown snack shack at their local Nebraska City pool in the summer of 1991 —
until summer visitor Brooke (Mika Abdalla), a cool lifeguard, puts their plans
and their friendship at risk ([Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/snack_shack)).
Semi-autobiographical and shot in Rehmeier's hometown, it opened in limited
U.S. theaters on 15 March 2024 through Republic Pictures, grossed $437,100
domestically, and earned warm reviews including a *New York Times* Critic's
Pick ([Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/snack_shack);
[MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)).

## A love letter built from twenty years of note cards

The script was assembled from note cards Rehmeier kept over 20 to 30 years —
individual sense memories (greyhound racing, jumping bikes into the pool,
selling hot dogs) stitched together with an amalgamation of the people he knew
([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)). The
autobiography is concrete, not decorative: like AJ, Rehmeier and his best
friend gambled at the greyhound track and ran a real home-brew beer operation
they intended to sell — "it was very drinkable," he says
([ComingSoon](http://www.comingsoon.net/movies/features/1591851-snack-shack-interview-director-adam-rehmeier-on-growing-up-nebraska-city)).
Location was non-negotiable. "It's a love letter to my community that I grew
up in the most loving way possible," he told ComingSoon — and he convinced the
producers to shoot in Nebraska City even though "there's not a tax incentive
in Nebraska," the reason productions usually decamp to Ohio or Georgia
([ComingSoon](http://www.comingsoon.net/movies/features/1591851-snack-shack-interview-director-adam-rehmeier-on-growing-up-nebraska-city)).
The young cast got two weeks in town before shooting: bikes, a real snack
shack at the pool, and the run of Nebraska City, so they could "absorb the
community in which I was raised" ([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)).

## Republic Pictures and the March 2024 release

*Snack Shack* arrived through Republic Pictures — Paramount's revived
acquisitions label — in a limited U.S. theatrical release on 15 March 2024,
with streaming following on 2 April ([Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/snack_shack)).
The choice of 1991 was deliberate: a "post-Gulf War, pre-*Nevermind*" window
when the nineties "hadn't been decided yet," Rehmeier told MovieWeb — late-80s
clothes, Yo! MTV Raps and Headbangers Ball on the stereo, everything in
Nebraska running a year behind the coasts except Nirvana
([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)). The
stat, source and implication: $437,100 in domestic box office on a limited
release (Rotten Tomatoes) — a modest theatrical footprint implying, as
Rehmeier himself argued in the same interview cycle, that mid-size original
comedies now survive theatrically on goodwill and word of mouth rather than
scale ([Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/snack_shack);
[MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)). The film
runs 1 hour 52 minutes and is rated R for pervasive language, alcohol and drug
use, smoking and some sexual material ([Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/snack_shack)).

## Reception: a Critic's Pick and warm reviews

Rotten Tomatoes records 82% positive reviews from 34 critics, with a consensus
calling it "deftly directed," "witty and charming enough to largely transcend
its lack of originality" ([Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/snack_shack)).
The *New York Times*' Calum Marsh made it a Critic's Pick, writing that "with
its rambling momentum and quick-witted, almost musical dialogue, it feels less
like *Superbad* than a Robert Altman movie, sort of like a pint-size
*California Split*" — as quoted on Rotten Tomatoes
([Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/snack_shack)). *Variety*'s
J. Kim Murphy found it "settling into a generic but warm glow," and the A.V.
Club noted that "as in *Dinner in America*, Rehmeier gets terrific
performances from his cast"
([Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/snack_shack)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is *Snack Shack* based on a true story?**
It is semi-autobiographical. Rehmeier built the script from 20–30 years of note cards of his Nebraska City childhood: he and his best friend really did gamble at the greyhound track and run a home-brew beer operation they meant to sell. AJ mirrors his own upbringing; Moose is inspired by his childhood best friend.

**Where was *Snack Shack* filmed?**
In Nebraska City, Nebraska — Rehmeier's hometown and the film's setting. He insisted on it as a "love letter" to his community even though Nebraska offers no film tax incentive, and gave the young cast two weeks in town before shooting so they could absorb it.

**Who released *Snack Shack*?**
Republic Pictures, Paramount's revived acquisitions label, gave it a limited U.S. theatrical release on 15 March 2024; it moved to streaming on 2 April 2024.

**How was *Snack Shack* received?**
Warmly but modestly: 82% of 34 critics' reviews on Rotten Tomatoes are positive, and the *New York Times* made it a Critic's Pick. Commercially it was small — $437,100 domestic on a limited release — consistent with an original R-rated indie comedy in 2024.
