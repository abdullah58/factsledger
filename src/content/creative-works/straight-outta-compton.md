## Overview

**Straight Outta Compton** is a 2015 American biographical drama about the rise and fall of the hip-hop group N.W.A, directed by F. Gary Gray, produced by N.W.A members Ice Cube and Dr. Dre, and released by Universal Pictures on August 14, 2015 ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/straight-outta-compton-reaches-200-million-worldwide/)). It opened to $60.2 million — the biggest opening weekend ever for a musical biopic at the time — and grossed $201.6 million worldwide on a $28 million budget ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/straight-outta-compton-reaches-200-million-worldwide/); [Boxoffice Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.com/this-weekend-in-box-office-history-straight-outta-compton-40-year-old-virgin-turns-15-more-apatow-crew-hits-district-9-80s-throwbacks-crazy-rich-asians-tropic-thunder-more/)).

This profile covers the film — not N.W.A's 1988 debut album of the same name, released August 9, 1988 ([BET](https://www.bet.com/article/ejgynj/n-w-a-finally-becomes-rock-and-roll-hall-of-fame-inductees)). The film's documented real-world story is what happened after the credits: a former manager's $110 million defamation suit that ended in full dismissal, a lone Oscar nomination that became a flashpoint in the #OscarsSoWhite debate, an N.W.A Rock and Roll Hall of Fame induction the film helped tip over the line — and an opening-weekend record that stood for eleven years until the 2026 [*Michael*](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/) biopic broke it ([SPIN](https://www.spinmagazine.com/2018/12/jerry-heller-straight-outta-compton-lawsuit-dismissed/); [ET Online](https://www.etonline.com/news/181142_straight_outta_compton_will_be_represented_by_white_nominees_at_2016_oscars_academy_responds?amp); [BET](https://www.bet.com/article/ejgynj/n-w-a-finally-becomes-rock-and-roll-hall-of-fame-inductees); [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/michael-makes-box-office-world-record-jaafar-jackson-starrer-becomes-first-ever-biopic-to-cross-1-billion-milestone/articleshow/132363062.cms?from=mdr)).

## The record opening weekend

Released by Universal Pictures on August 14, 2015, *Straight Outta Compton* bowed to $60.2 million in its opening weekend — the second-biggest debut of the year by an original film, trailing only Pixar's *Inside Out* at $90.4 million ([Boxoffice Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.com/this-weekend-in-box-office-history-straight-outta-compton-40-year-old-virgin-turns-15-more-apatow-crew-hits-district-9-80s-throwbacks-crazy-rich-asians-tropic-thunder-more/)). TheWrap reported it as the No. 1 R-rated August opening ever, and the film held the top spot at the North American box office for three consecutive weeks ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/straight-outta-compton-reaches-200-million-worldwide/)). Against a $28 million production budget, it finished with $161.2 million domestically and $201.6 million worldwide ([Boxoffice Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.com/this-weekend-in-box-office-history-straight-outta-compton-40-year-old-virgin-turns-15-more-apatow-crew-hits-district-9-80s-throwbacks-crazy-rich-asians-tropic-thunder-more/)). The opening set the genre's benchmark: the Economic Times, reporting via Variety on the 2026 *Michael* film's debut, noted that *Michael*'s $97 million domestic and $217 million worldwide opening surpassed "the previous opening weekend record for a musical biopic held by *Straight Outta Compton* (2015)" — meaning Gray's film held the genre's opening record for roughly eleven years ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/michael-makes-box-office-world-record-jaafar-jackson-starrer-becomes-first-ever-biopic-to-cross-1-billion-milestone/articleshow/132363062.cms?from=mdr)).

## The manager who sued — and lost

Former N.W.A manager Jerry Heller, portrayed in the film by Paul Giamatti, sued former clients Ice Cube and Dr. Dre for defamation in 2015, claiming his portrayal was unfair and that he had not given permission for the use of his likeness; he also sued NBCUniversal and Eazy-E's estate, seeking at least $110 million in damages ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/straight-outta-compton-reaches-200-million-worldwide/)). The case did not go his way: U.S. District Judge Michael Fitzgerald tossed most of the suit in 2016 for insufficient evidence, and after Heller died at age 75 that year — his estate continued the action — Fitzgerald closed the case in full in December 2018 ([SPIN](https://www.spinmagazine.com/2018/12/jerry-heller-straight-outta-compton-lawsuit-dismissed/), via The Hollywood Reporter). The judge's final words on the matter: "The action is, at last, DISMISSED." Heller's attorney Mickey Shapiro had claimed publicly that the stress of the film's portrayal contributed to Heller's fatal heart attack — a claim SPIN attributes to Shapiro via TMZ, and which remains unproven ([SPIN](https://www.spinmagazine.com/2018/12/jerry-heller-straight-outta-compton-lawsuit-dismissed/)).

## One Oscar nomination, one controversy, one induction

The film's awards story is a study in near-misses. Writers Andrea Berloff and Jonathan Herman were nominated for an Oscar in the Best Original Screenplay category ([Boxoffice Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.com/this-weekend-in-box-office-history-straight-outta-compton-40-year-old-virgin-turns-15-more-apatow-crew-hits-district-9-80s-throwbacks-crazy-rich-asians-tropic-thunder-more/)) — the film's only Academy Award nomination, shared with story writers S. Leigh Savidge and Alan Wenkus ([ET Online](https://www.etonline.com/news/181142_straight_outta_compton_will_be_represented_by_white_nominees_at_2016_oscars_academy_responds?amp)). The nomination landed amid the #OscarsSoWhite controversy: the four nominated writers were white, while producer Ice Cube and the film's Black cast were left off the ceremony guest list, prompting the Academy to issue a statement that it "invites the nominees only" ([ET Online](https://www.etonline.com/news/181142_straight_outta_compton_will_be_represented_by_white_nominees_at_2016_oscars_academy_responds?amp)). N.W.A itself was announced for induction into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame's class of 2016 — on its fourth appearance on the ballot — with BET noting the induction "may've been prompted by the overwhelming success the group saw after being portrayed in the biopic" ([BET](https://www.bet.com/article/ejgynj/n-w-a-finally-becomes-rock-and-roll-hall-of-fame-inductees)). The ceremony took place April 8, 2016, at Brooklyn's Barclays Center and aired on HBO ([BET](https://www.bet.com/article/ejgynj/n-w-a-finally-becomes-rock-and-roll-hall-of-fame-inductees); [EURweb](https://eurweb.com/n-w-a-straight-into-the-rock-and-roll-hall-of-fame/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is *Straight Outta Compton* based on a true story?**
Yes. It dramatizes the rise and fall of the [Los Angeles](/places/los-angeles/) hip-hop group N.W.A, and was produced by two of the group's members, Ice Cube and Dr. Dre. Its title comes from the group's 1988 debut album of the same name.

**How much money did *Straight Outta Compton* make?**
It opened to $60.2 million domestically, finished with $161.2 million in the US and Canada, and $201.6 million worldwide — off a $28 million production budget, per Boxoffice Pro.

**What happened to Jerry Heller's lawsuit over the film?**
Heller sued for at least $110 million in 2015 over his portrayal by Paul Giamatti. A judge tossed most of the suit in 2016, Heller died at 75 with his estate continuing the case, and the suit was dismissed in full in December 2018.

**Was *Straight Outta Compton* nominated for an Oscar?**
Once: Best Original Screenplay, for Andrea Berloff, Jonathan Herman, S. Leigh Savidge and Alan Wenkus. The all-white nominee list became a flashpoint in the 2016 #OscarsSoWhite debate.

**How long did its opening-weekend record stand?**
About eleven years. Its $60.2 million was the biggest opening ever for a musical biopic until the 2026 *Michael* film opened to $97 million domestically, per Variety via the Economic Times.
