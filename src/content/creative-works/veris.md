## Overview

Veris is a distributed-ledger network for post-trade reconciliation and lifecycle management of equity swaps, built by the New York firm [Axoni](/organizations/axoni/) and launched in February 2020. ([Finadium](https://finadium.com/axoni-and-15-large-firms-launch-distributed-ledger-for-equity-swaps-processing/)) It lets counterparties share and compare data on swap deals, positions, trades and related cash flows, streamlining reconciliation and preventing cash-flow breaks. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf)) Its users grew to include [BlackRock](/organizations/blackrock/), [Goldman Sachs](/organizations/goldman-sachs/), [Citi](/organizations/citigroup/), HSBC and UBS, and in October 2024 the [London Stock Exchange Group](/organizations/london-stock-exchange-group/) completed its acquisition of the platform. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf))

Veris matters because it is one of the few enterprise blockchain networks that reached production with the world's largest financial institutions as live users — and then changed hands to a stock-exchange group, a rare exit for the technology. The market it serves is large: Bank for International Settlements data put the global equity-linked forwards and swaps market at about $3.6 trillion. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2021/09/08/blackrock-to-use-axoni-blockchain-for-equity-swaps)) Yet Veris is deliberately narrow in scope: it does not execute, settle or clear transactions, and all equity swaps are executed and settled outside the platform. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf))

## What Veris does

Veris attacks the reconciliation problem at its root: in equity swaps, each counterparty traditionally keeps its own books, so the same trade exists as two records that drift apart over amendments, cash flows and corporate actions. Veris gives both sides one shared, synchronized record instead. The platform ingests post-execution swap data via an API or FIX engine, pairs each party's version of a trade, compares the paired records field by field, and marks differences outside agreed thresholds as exceptions for the parties to fix upstream. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf)) It also calculates positions independently, updates them for corporate actions such as stock splits, supports amendments and cancellations, and lets parties affirm a counterparty's values for a paired record. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf)) The design rests on the ISDA Common Domain Model, an open-source standard for representing derivative trades, so firms describe the same events the same way. ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/blackrock-goes-live-on-axoni-blockchain-network-for-equity-swaps/)) Veris also works single-sided: one party can manage trades without its counterparty being onboard, which lowered the barrier to joining. ([Axoni](https://axoni.com/press/hsbc-and-ubs-go-live-on-axoni-equity-swaps-network/))

## From pilots to a production network

Veris was the product of years of bank pilots before it went live. In 2016 Axoni ran an 85-test-case credit-default-swap pilot and a 133-test-case equity-swaps pilot with groups including JPMorgan, Credit Suisse, Barclays, Citi, Markit, [Thomson Reuters](/organizations/thomson-reuters/) and Capco, both reporting 100% success. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2021/09/08/blackrock-to-use-axoni-blockchain-for-equity-swaps)) The February 2020 launch, built with a 15-firm working group on Axoni's AxCore software, processed its first live trade data between Citi and Goldman Sachs. ([Finadium](https://finadium.com/axoni-and-15-large-firms-launch-distributed-ledger-for-equity-swaps-processing/)) The network's breakthrough came in September 2021, when BlackRock began using Veris and integrated Axoni's software with its [Aladdin](/creative-works/aladdin/) operating system, with BlackRock's Mark Cox saying Veris would "help us build scalability while mitigating risks in the investment life cycle beginning with equity swaps." ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/blackrock-goes-live-on-axoni-blockchain-network-for-equity-swaps/)) HSBC and UBS went live in June 2022, after cash-flow matching had been added the previous year to solve one of the swap lifecycle's most persistent pain points. ([Axoni](https://axoni.com/press/hsbc-and-ubs-go-live-on-axoni-equity-swaps-network/))

## The LSEG acquisition and the exemption question

In September 2024 Axoni announced the sale of its Veris post-trade business and related intellectual property to the London Stock Exchange Group, with the deal expected to complete in the fourth quarter; LSEG completed the acquisition in October 2024. ([FStech](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Lseg_acquires_post_trade_processing_specialist_veris.php); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf)) After a transition period LSEG owns and operates the network, while Veris customers keep access to HYDRA, Axoni's data-replication platform. ([FStech](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Lseg_acquires_post_trade_processing_specialist_veris.php)) The sale also surfaced a regulatory loose end: Veris had operated under a temporary SEC exemption for post-trade services in security-based swaps, and in August 2024 Axoni filed for a permanent exemption from clearing-agency registration. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf)) On August 26, 2026 the SEC published notice of LSEG's application and invited public comments — a filing notice, not a grant, and the application remains pending. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf)) LSEG's filing also sketches Veris's next steps: data normalization, cross-referencing identifiers such as RIC, SEDOL, CUSIP and ISIN, and phased cash-flow matching during 2026. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf))

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Veris?
Veris is a distributed-ledger platform for post-trade reconciliation and lifecycle management of equity swaps. It lets counterparties share and compare data on deals, positions, trades and cash flows so both sides stay synchronized through the life of a swap. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf))

### Who owns Veris now?
The London Stock Exchange Group. Axoni announced the sale of its Veris post-trade business to LSEG in September 2024, and LSEG completed the acquisition in October 2024, after which it owns and operates the network. Veris customers kept access to HYDRA, Axoni's data-replication platform, after the sale. ([FStech](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Lseg_acquires_post_trade_processing_specialist_veris.php))

### Does Veris settle or clear equity swaps?
No. Veris does not execute, settle or clear transactions; all equity security-based swap transactions are executed and settled outside the platform, and counterparties transmit their post-execution data to Veris for matching and reconciliation. Its job begins after execution: pairing records, reconciling data and flagging exceptions. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf))

### How is Veris regulated in the United States?
Veris operated under a temporary SEC exemption for post-trade services in security-based swaps. Axoni filed for a permanent exemption from clearing-agency registration in August 2024, and the SEC published notice of LSEG's application in August 2026 to solicit comments; the exemption has been applied for, not granted. ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf))

### What problem does Veris solve?
It attacks reconciliation breaks: when each counterparty keeps its own books, the same swap exists as two records that drift apart through amendments and cash flows, forcing costly manual reconciliation. Veris gives both sides one synchronized record and flags differences as exceptions. ([Finadium](https://finadium.com/axoni-and-15-large-firms-launch-distributed-ledger-for-equity-swaps-processing/))
