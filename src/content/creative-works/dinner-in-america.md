## Overview

**Dinner in America** is Adam Rehmeier's 2020 punk-rock romantic comedy starring
Kyle Gallner as Simon, an on-the-lam punk rocker, and Emily Skeggs as Patty, a
socially awkward young woman obsessed with his band
([Sundance Institute](https://www.sundance.org/blogs/2020-sundance-film-festival-118-feature-films-announced-3/);
[RogerEbert.com](https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/sundance-2020-wander-darkly-dinner-in-america)).
It world-premiered in the U.S. Dramatic Competition at Sundance 2020 and won
the audience-voted Grand Prix at the 11th Odesa International Film Festival
that October ([Cineuropa](https://cineuropa.org/newsdetail/393331/)).
Produced by Ben Stiller among others, its U.S. release waited until 2022,
after which it grew into a cult item crowned by a John Waters top-ten berth
([RogerEbert.com](https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/dinner-in-america-movie-review-2022);
[The Film Experience](http://thefilmexperience.net/blog/2022/12/2/john-waters-kicks-off-an-already-swinging-top-ten-season.html)).

## A script fourteen years in the making

The film's gestation is unusually well documented because Rehmeier narrated it
himself. Simon came to him "all at once" in 2006, walking in the snow — the
rhythm of his boots conjuring "a predatory shark-like creature moving through
space-time" — and the first ten minutes were sketched at his parents' house
that holiday ([PopMatters](https://www.popmatters.com/adam-rehmeier-interview-dinner-america)).
In 2009, blocked on another project, he sketched Patty and her conservatively
religious Midwestern family, writing the opening taco-salad dinner scene in one
sitting. Four years later he fused the two: the moment he combined "a day in
the life of Simon and a day in the life of Patty, it suddenly became
interesting" ([PopMatters](https://www.popmatters.com/adam-rehmeier-interview-dinner-america)).
He then wrote and refined the script for months, found producers — including
Ben Stiller and Ross Putman — and waited six more years for the premiere. The
design, he says, was subconscious: "a punk song — abrasive on the outside but
it has a deeper message," built from 29 years of home recording and a Midwest
upbringing ([PopMatters](https://www.popmatters.com/adam-rehmeier-interview-dinner-america)).

## The festival run: Sundance competition, Odesa Grand Prix

Sundance's own announcement placed the film in the U.S. Dramatic Competition —
the category whose winner takes the Grand Jury Prize — as a world premiere,
crediting Rehmeier as director and screenwriter and listing Ben Stiller,
Nicholas Weinstock, David Hunter, Ross Putman, John Covert and Sam Slater as
producers ([Sundance Institute](https://www.sundance.org/blogs/2020-sundance-film-festival-118-feature-films-announced-3/)).
RogerEbert.com's festival dispatch called it a punk *Heathers* with a
*Napoleon Dynamite* sense of humor, noting that its abrasiveness "starts to
dissipate" as Simon and Patty become a team — "almost becomes sweet"
([RogerEbert.com](https://www.rogerebert.com/festivals/sundance-2020-wander-darkly-dinner-in-america)).
Eight months later, at the hybrid 11th Odesa International Film Festival (25
September–3 October 2020), the film won the Grand Prix of the International
Competition, decided by audience vote; Rehmeier said he would "immediately run
to share the news with the filming crew" ([Cineuropa](https://cineuropa.org/newsdetail/393331/)).
One disambiguation matters: Odesa's jury gave its separate Best Film award to
Valentyn Vasyanovych's *Atlantis* — the Grand Prix was the audience's prize,
not the jury's ([Cineuropa](https://cineuropa.org/newsdetail/393331/)).

## The long road to release

The film's American commercial life was delayed well past its festival window:
RogerEbert.com's 2022 review confirms it was "now playing in select theaters
and available on digital platforms and on demand on June 7th" — a full two
years after Sundance ([RogerEbert.com](https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/dinner-in-america-movie-review-2022)).
The slow burn paid off in cult credibility: that December, John Waters put the
film on his year-end top-ten list, published for Film Forum via *Artforum*
([The Film Experience](http://thefilmexperience.net/blog/2022/12/2/john-waters-kicks-off-an-already-swinging-top-ten-season.html)).
The stat, source and implication: a January 2020 premiere to a 2022 U.S.
release — a two-plus-year gap (Sundance Institute; RogerEbert.com) — implying
a reputation built through word of mouth, not a launch campaign.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is *Dinner in America* based on a true story?**
No. It is an original script Rehmeier developed between 2006 and 2019: the Simon character arrived in 2006, Patty's family was sketched in 2009, and the two were fused in 2013. Rehmeier says he drew on his Midwest upbringing and decades of home music recording, and calls the film a punk song — abrasive outside, sweet inside.

**What did *Dinner in America* win at the Odesa festival?**
The Grand Prix of the International Competition at the 11th Odesa International Film Festival in 2020 — a prize decided by audience vote. Note the distinction: the jury's separate Best Film award went to Valentyn Vasyanovych's *Atlantis*.

**When was *Dinner in America* released in the United States?**
In 2022, two years after its Sundance premiere. RogerEbert.com's review from that year confirms a select-theaters run with digital and on-demand availability from 7 June 2022. The exact distributor was not documented in this profile's sources.

**Why is *Dinner in America* called a cult film?**
Its reputation grew slowly after the delayed release: critics converted from irritation to affection, and in December 2022 John Waters named it to his annual year-end top-ten list (published for Film Forum via *Artforum*). Claims of a 2024 TikTok-driven resurgence could not be verified to this profile's sourcing standard.
