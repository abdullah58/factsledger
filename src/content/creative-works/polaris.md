## Overview

Polaris is [Behavox](/organizations/behavox/)'s trade-surveillance platform, introduced in 2025 to detect market abuse across 10 major asset classes on a single AI-native platform, using agentic AI to pull related chats, emails, voice recordings and archive records into one governed case ([Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/behavox-raises-175-million-hps-investment-partners-part-blackrock-accelerate)). It works standalone or alongside Quantum, Behavox's communications-surveillance product, so firms can unify trade and communications surveillance in one workflow ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260225067628/en/Behavox-Grows-Global-Customer-Base-by-86-in-2025-Introduces-Polaris-for-Trade-Surveillance)). But its public record is a launch story with an unfinished ending: as of 20 October 2026, independent analyst GAII Intelligence could find no general-availability announcement and described Polaris as still in preview ([GAII Intelligence](https://www.gaiiinstitute.com/research-updates/behavox-initiation-of-coverage/)).

Behavox Ltd. was incorporated in [London](/places/london/) in 2014 by founder and CEO [Erkin Adylov](/people/erkin-adylov/), formerly an equities researcher at [Goldman Sachs](/organizations/goldman-sachs/) and a portfolio manager at Man Group ([GAII Intelligence](https://www.gaiiinstitute.com/research-updates/behavox-initiation-of-coverage/)). Polaris sits inside the Behavox Unified Controls Platform with Quantum (communications surveillance), Intelligent Archive (regulatory data retention) and Pathfinder (policy management) ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512050554/en/Behavox-Extends-Polaris-Trade-Surveillance-to-Prediction-Markets-Expanding-Risk-Coverage-to-10-Asset-Classes)). Its arrival marked Behavox's move beyond communications surveillance into trade surveillance — a move funded first by debt and an acquisition, then by a $175 million investment from [HPS Investment Partners](/organizations/hps-investment-partners/), part of [BlackRock](/organizations/blackrock/) ([Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/behavox-raises-175-million-hps-investment-partners-part-blackrock-accelerate)). It is not Polaris Inc., the vehicle maker, nor the North Star, nor any other vendor's software of the same name.

That wording cannot be reconciled with the February and May announcements unless it is read as a present-tense description written in June 2026 and backdated to the launch; the discrepancy is flagged in the open items.

## What Polaris does: agentic AI and the single case

Trade surveillance is the monitoring of orders, trades and market data for manipulation patterns such as spoofing, wash trading and insider trading — and its hard problem is context. A single trade alert, on its own, rarely tells the whole story. Polaris's answer, as Behavox describes it, is agentic AI inside governed investigation workflows: the system automatically pulls related chats, emails, voice recordings and archived records into a single case, giving investigators "complete contextual visibility and defensible evidence lineage" ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260225067628/en/Behavox-Grows-Global-Customer-Base-by-86-in-2025-Introduces-Polaris-for-Trade-Surveillance)). The stated aim is to improve investigative reproducibility and defensibility under regulatory scrutiny while reducing manual investigation time ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260225067628/en/Behavox-Grows-Global-Customer-Base-by-86-in-2025-Introduces-Polaris-for-Trade-Surveillance)).

Founder and CEO Erkin Adylov explained the design in a March 2026 interview with FinTech Global: with Polaris, agentic AI workflows pull in contextual data automatically — related chats and emails, voice, control-room communications, news and corporate events — and the AI reviews that bundle and helps decide whether something looks like a genuine issue or an obvious false positive ([FinTech Global](https://fintech.global/2026/03/20/how-purpose-built-ai-is-transforming-compliance-from-detective-controls-into-preventive-controls/)). Very often, he said, closing an alert is as simple as matching a trade to a wall-crossing record or a news timestamp, and AI can do that work at scale — even closing obviously benign alerts on its own, so humans focus on quality assurance and judgement ([FinTech Global](https://fintech.global/2026/03/20/how-purpose-built-ai-is-transforming-compliance-from-detective-controls-into-preventive-controls/)).

The second job Adylov claimed for AI is speed of deployment. Getting a trade-surveillance system live can take months or even years, largely because of field mapping — aligning each firm's data model to the vendor's schema. With Polaris, he said, AI helps infer and map different data types and sources from the client environment into the schema needed to generate alerts, with humans reviewing and confirming ([FinTech Global](https://fintech.global/2026/03/20/how-purpose-built-ai-is-transforming-compliance-from-detective-controls-into-preventive-controls/)). All of this is the vendor's account of its own product; no independent test of Polaris's detection accuracy, false-positive rate or deployment speed was found.

The platform is built on the same AI-native foundation as the rest of Behavox's platform, with direct lineage from geography to regulator to asset class to risk taxonomy, configurable scenarios, and integrated tooling for evidence gathering and regulatory filing ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512050554/en/Behavox-Extends-Polaris-Trade-Surveillance-to-Prediction-Markets-Expanding-Risk-Coverage-to-10-Asset-Classes)). Independent trade press A-Team Insight, writing in 2025 before the launch, described Polaris as a "pioneering AI-driven cross-asset, cross-market trade-surveillance platform" positioned alongside Quantum and [Mosaic Smart Data](/organizations/mosaic-smart-data/), and reported that its teaser page invited firms to "register your interest", signalling a general release in 2026 ([A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/meeting-the-72-hour-trade-reconstruction-rule-10-holistic-surveillance-leaders-for-2025/)). The same account expected Polaris to deliver a single timeline stitching pre-trade conversations to child orders and executions across venues — the holistic correlation regulators demand when they require a trade to be reconstructed, in the United States within 72 hours under the CFTC's Dodd-Frank swap-dealer rules ([A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/meeting-the-72-hour-trade-reconstruction-rule-10-holistic-surveillance-leaders-for-2025/)).

Behavox positions Polaris explicitly against the incumbents: it "is designed to replace the legacy, point-solution surveillance stacks sold by vendors such as NASDAQ SMARTS and NICE Actimize", which require firms to stitch together coverage across separate engines per asset class ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512050554/en/Behavox-Extends-Polaris-Trade-Surveillance-to-Prediction-Markets-Expanding-Risk-Coverage-to-10-Asset-Classes)). The February 2026 launch release added TradingHub to that list of legacy providers ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260225067628/en/Behavox-Grows-Global-Customer-Base-by-86-in-2025-Introduces-Polaris-for-Trade-Surveillance)). Independent analyst GAII frames the competition the same way, but with a condition: Behavox competes with Nasdaq SMARTS, NICE Actimize, Eventus and TradingHub in trade surveillance "once Polaris ships" ([GAII Intelligence](https://www.gaiiinstitute.com/research-updates/behavox-initiation-of-coverage/)).

## Prediction markets: the tenth asset class and the regulatory trigger

Polaris's most documented product change came on 12 May 2026, when Behavox announced prediction markets as a new asset class, bringing out-of-the-box risk coverage to 10 classes ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512050554/en/Behavox-Extends-Polaris-Trade-Surveillance-to-Prediction-Markets-Expanding-Risk-Coverage-to-10-Asset-Classes)). Coverage was available in beta to existing Polaris customers immediately, with general availability scheduled for summer 2026 ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512050554/en/Behavox-Extends-Polaris-Trade-Surveillance-to-Prediction-Markets-Expanding-Risk-Coverage-to-10-Asset-Classes)). FinanceFeeds, reporting independently the same week, confirmed the tenth-class framing and the beta-then-summer timetable ([FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/behavox-adds-prediction-markets-to-polaris-surveillance-platform/)).

The timing was driven by regulators, not by product fashion. In March 2026 the CFTC's Division of Market Oversight issued Staff Advisory Letter 26-08, signalling a more structured approach toward event-based derivatives markets, and in April 2026 the agency brought its first-ever insider-trading enforcement action concerning event contracts ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512050554/en/Behavox-Extends-Polaris-Trade-Surveillance-to-Prediction-Markets-Expanding-Risk-Coverage-to-10-Asset-Classes)). Broker-dealers trading event contracts also face supervisory obligations under FINRA Rule 3110, broadening surveillance demand beyond CFTC-regulated venues ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512050554/en/Behavox-Extends-Polaris-Trade-Surveillance-to-Prediction-Markets-Expanding-Risk-Coverage-to-10-Asset-Classes)). Adylov called prediction markets "the next frontier for trade surveillance", saying customers want controls in place before regulatory and reputational risks increase further ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512050554/en/Behavox-Extends-Polaris-Trade-Surveillance-to-Prediction-Markets-Expanding-Risk-Coverage-to-10-Asset-Classes)).

FinanceFeeds' analysis explains why event contracts are a genuinely different surveillance problem: in equities, surveillance focuses on trading ahead of earnings, mergers or analyst reports, but in prediction markets sensitive information may involve government decisions, health data, geopolitical developments, legal rulings, elections, sports outcomes or economic releases — a wider universe of information-sensitive trading ([FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/behavox-adds-prediction-markets-to-polaris-surveillance-platform/)). Chief Revenue Officer Nabeel Ebrahim said customer demand for prediction-markets surveillance had "accelerated quickly over the past quarter, driven by the growth of the asset class itself, the new CFTC regulatory framework, and recent enforcement actions targeting the misuse of non-public information in event contracts" ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512050554/en/Behavox-Extends-Polaris-Trade-Surveillance-to-Prediction-Markets-Expanding-Risk-Coverage-to-10-Asset-Classes)).

## Funding, demand and a launch timetable that slipped

Polaris was not built in isolation from Behavox's balance sheet. In autumn 2024 the company secured a $70 million venture-debt facility from [Hercules Capital](/organizations/hercules-capital/) to support strategic expansion, including the acquisition of Mosaic Smart Data and a strategic investment in [b-next](/organizations/b-next/); together, those transactions accelerated Behavox's move into trade surveillance by adding fixed-income, currencies and commodities analytics expertise ([Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/behavox-raises-175-million-hps-investment-partners-part-blackrock-accelerate)).

In June 2026, HPS Investment Partners, part of BlackRock, made a $175 million preferred-equity investment in Behavox — its first equity financing since [SoftBank](/organizations/softbank-group/)'s $100 million round in 2020 — positioned to accelerate global growth and expand the Unified Controls Platform, with continued investment in Polaris among the stated uses ([Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/behavox-raises-175-million-hps-investment-partners-part-blackrock-accelerate)). As part of the transaction Behavox fully repaid and retired the $70 million Hercules Capital debt facility ([Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/behavox-raises-175-million-hps-investment-partners-part-blackrock-accelerate)). Financial IT reported, on the company's account, that Polaris pipeline growth of more than 80% since the beginning of the year "has demonstrated strong market demand for one governed workflow that unifies communications and trade surveillance" ([Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/behavox-raises-175-million-hps-investment-partners-part-blackrock-accelerate)).

Against that demand story, the delivery timetable is the profile's central unresolved fact. The February 2026 release said Polaris was "currently in preview with select customers, with General Availability scheduled for Q2 of this year" ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260225067628/en/Behavox-Grows-Global-Customer-Base-by-86-in-2025-Introduces-Polaris-for-Trade-Surveillance)). The May 2026 release put general availability for prediction-markets coverage in summer 2026 ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512050554/en/Behavox-Extends-Polaris-Trade-Surveillance-to-Prediction-Markets-Expanding-Risk-Coverage-to-10-Asset-Classes)). But GAII Intelligence, in its independent initiation of coverage dated 20 October 2026, reported: "Polaris was targeted for late 2025, then the second quarter and then summer 2026; no general-availability announcement was located" — and listed "Polaris general-availability date and current clients" among its open questions to the company ([GAII Intelligence](https://www.gaiiinstitute.com/research-updates/behavox-initiation-of-coverage/)). GAII scored Behavox 6.83 out of 10 overall ("Established", in the Emerging quadrant), with its strongest dimension Data & Content at 7.58 and its weakest, Commercial Value, at 5.00, and advised prospective buyers to "confirm Polaris timing before replacing trade surveillance" ([GAII Intelligence](https://www.gaiiinstitute.com/research-updates/behavox-initiation-of-coverage/)). GAII also found no public API (with MCP and REST interfaces described as "coming soon") and no published order- or execution-management connectors, and noted that recall and false-positive reductions claimed for Behavox's products are vendor-run figures ([GAII Intelligence](https://www.gaiiinstitute.com/research-updates/behavox-initiation-of-coverage/)).

That caution is the independent counterweight to the launch narrative. Adylov told FinTech Global that Behavox had invested over $200 million in research and development and had AI in production for three years across more than 100 institutions, including a central bank and a regulator as customers ([FinTech Global](https://fintech.global/2026/03/20/how-purpose-built-ai-is-transforming-compliance-from-detective-controls-into-preventive-controls/)). Those are company claims about the wider platform, made in an interview, not an independent audit of Polaris — and GAII's review, which credits Behavox with tier-one references and audited FY2024 revenue of $35.6 million, still could not verify a generally available trade-surveillance product eight months after its launch was announced ([GAII Intelligence](https://www.gaiiinstitute.com/research-updates/behavox-initiation-of-coverage/)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not claim Polaris is generally available: the February 2026 release promised general availability in Q2 2026 and the May 2026 release promised it for summer 2026, but GAII Intelligence found no general-availability announcement as of 20 October 2026 and described the product as still in preview. It does not settle whether Polaris covered 10 asset classes at its 2025 introduction or only from May 2026, because Financial IT and Behavox's own dated announcements disagree on that point. It does not repeat performance claims — four to five times more true positives, alert-volume reductions, higher capture of real misconduct, or pipeline growth above 80% — as verified facts, because all of them are company claims with no independent evaluation located. It does not name any Polaris customer, price, or integration, because none was found in the sources read. It does not assert Behavox's profitability date, because Financial IT's "profitable since 2023" (company statement) conflicts with GAII's note that profitability statements conflict with the audited FY2023 loss. And it does not present the February and May 2026 BusinessWire releases as independent reporting: they are the company's own announcements, used here only for uncontested product facts and clearly attributed claims, with the independent check supplied by FinanceFeeds, FinTech Global, A-Team Insight and GAII Intelligence.
