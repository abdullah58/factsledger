## Overview

Aladdin — short for Asset, Liability, Debt and Derivative Investment Network — is [BlackRock](/organizations/blackrock/)'s investment and risk-management technology platform, the system the world's largest asset manager uses to run its own portfolios and licenses to other institutions. ([BlackRock](https://ir.blackrock.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2019/BlackRock-to-Acquire-eFront----Industry-Leading-Alternatives-Investment-Software-Provider/default.aspx)) Launched in 1988 on a single Sun Microsystems workstation, it grew into the industry's dominant portfolio operating system: by 2020 it was used to manage some $21.6 trillion in assets. ([Data Center Dynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/blackrock-brings-aladdin-investment-platform-to-aws/))

What makes Aladdin unusual is that its vendor is also its largest user. BlackRock built the platform to manage its own business first, then sold the same capabilities to clients — a "provider/user" dynamic BlackRock says imposes more discipline than a typical third-party vendor faces. ([BlackRock](https://ir.blackrock.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2019/BlackRock-to-Acquire-eFront----Industry-Leading-Alternatives-Investment-Software-Provider/default.aspx)) Competitors such as Charles River, SimCorp and MSCI have spent a decade trying to match its breadth, and the platform's scale — more than 210 institutional clients, including direct competitors such as Vanguard — has made it both the industry standard and a subject of regulatory attention. ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsxpilyzo8y3xdlej7r4/portfolio/can-anyone-bury-blackrock))

## From one workstation to the industry's operating system

Aladdin's origin story is a crisis story. In 1994 General Electric hired BlackRock to liquidate the mortgage portfolio of Kidder Peabody, its scandal-hit securities subsidiary, and the risk analytics BlackRock had built for itself proved to be the product. ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsxpilyzo8y3xdlej7r4/portfolio/can-anyone-bury-blackrock)) "That really planted the seed for Aladdin," chief operating officer Rob Goldstein later recalled. ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsxpilyzo8y3xdlej7r4/portfolio/can-anyone-bury-blackrock)) The first external implementation was finished in 2000, creating the BlackRock Solutions business, and by the end of that year assets under risk management on the platform exceeded $1.3 trillion. ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsxpilyzo8y3xdlej7r4/portfolio/can-anyone-bury-blackrock)) Early adoption came from pension funds: in 2006 the California State Teachers' Retirement System chose Aladdin for its then-$32 billion bond portfolio over Wilshire Analytics and Charles River Development. ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsxpilyzo8y3xdlej7r4/portfolio/can-anyone-bury-blackrock)) The 2008 financial crisis then made risk management the industry's obsession, and the Federal Reserve Bank of New York picked BlackRock to manage a $30 billion portfolio of Bear Stearns assets. ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsxpilyzo8y3xdlej7r4/portfolio/can-anyone-bury-blackrock))

## What Aladdin does and who uses it

Aladdin combines risk analytics, exposure and performance analysis with portfolio management, trading, compliance and operational tools on a single platform. ([BlackRock](https://ir.blackrock.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2019/BlackRock-to-Acquire-eFront----Industry-Leading-Alternatives-Investment-Software-Provider/default.aspx)) Institutional Investor reported the platform supporting more than $18 trillion across 210 institutional clients, including asset owners such as CalSTRS and even direct competitors such as Vanguard. ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsxpilyzo8y3xdlej7r4/portfolio/can-anyone-bury-blackrock)) BlackRock says Aladdin serves about 20 percent of the top 200 asset managers globally, 23 percent of the top 100 US pension funds, and 17 percent of the world's 250 largest insurance companies. ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsxpilyzo8y3xdlej7r4/portfolio/can-anyone-bury-blackrock)) The platform has also moved downmarket into wealth management: UBS Global Wealth Management was the first financial advisory firm to adopt it, and Morgan Stanley Wealth Management built a custom platform on Aladdin's risk analytics engine. ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsxpilyzo8y3xdlej7r4/portfolio/can-anyone-bury-blackrock)) Rivals have responded by consolidating — State Street bought Charles River Development to build a front-to-back alternative, while SimCorp's Dimension pitches itself as the other integrated option. ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsxpilyzo8y3xdlej7r4/portfolio/can-anyone-bury-blackrock))

## The whole-portfolio build-out: eFront, Preqin, climate and AI

Since 2019 BlackRock has rebuilt Aladdin from a public-markets platform into a "whole portfolio" system. That year it agreed to acquire eFront, the Paris-based alternatives software provider, for $1.3 billion in cash, adding private equity, real estate and infrastructure capabilities used by more than 700 clients in 48 countries. ([BlackRock](https://ir.blackrock.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2019/BlackRock-to-Acquire-eFront----Industry-Leading-Alternatives-Investment-Software-Provider/default.aspx)) In December 2020 it launched Aladdin Climate, the first software to measure both physical and transition climate risk at the security level, drawing on more than 1,200 ESG indicators from Sustainalytics and Refinitiv. ([Markets Media](https://www.marketsmedia.com/blackrock-launches-aladdin-climate/)) A generative-AI copilot for Aladdin and eFront followed in 2023, letting clients query the platform in natural language. ([FStech](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Blackrock_Rolling_Out_GenAI_Tools_To_Staff_And_Clients_In_January.php)) In March 2025 BlackRock completed the £2.55 billion acquisition of private-markets data provider Preqin, with Preqin benchmarks made available inside Aladdin. ([SEC](https://d18rn0p25nwr6d.cloudfront.net/CIK-0002012383/83cd6991-592a-4c1e-b9d3-ed47fe8e30de.pdf)) In December 2025 it partnered with Amazon Web Services to offer Aladdin on AWS infrastructure, complementing its Microsoft Azure deployment, with Amazon Treasury among the first adopters. ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/blackrock-and-aws-partner-to-bring-aladdin-onto-its-cloud/))

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Aladdin stand for?
Aladdin stands for Asset, Liability, Debt and Derivative Investment Network. It is BlackRock's investment and risk-management technology platform, combining portfolio management, trading, compliance and operational tools on one system. BlackRock built it for its own portfolios first and now licenses the same system to other institutions. ([BlackRock](https://ir.blackrock.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2019/BlackRock-to-Acquire-eFront----Industry-Leading-Alternatives-Investment-Software-Provider/default.aspx))

### How much money runs through Aladdin?
As of 2020, Aladdin was used to manage some $21.6 trillion in assets. BlackRock has since stopped reporting a current total, so no newer figure is stated here; an earlier Institutional Investor report put the platform at more than $18 trillion across 210 institutional clients. ([Data Center Dynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/blackrock-brings-aladdin-investment-platform-to-aws/))

### Who uses Aladdin besides BlackRock?
More than 200 institutions, including pension funds such as CalSTRS, insurers, and even rival asset managers such as Vanguard. BlackRock says it serves about a fifth of the world's top 200 asset managers. Franklin Templeton selected Aladdin in 2024 to unify its technology across public-market asset classes. ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsxpilyzo8y3xdlej7r4/portfolio/can-anyone-bury-blackrock))

### Is Aladdin the same as BlackRock the asset manager?
No. Aladdin is BlackRock's technology platform, run by its BlackRock Solutions division under Global Head Sudhir Nair. BlackRock is also Aladdin's largest user, running its own portfolios on the same system it licenses to clients. The asset-management business and the technology platform are separate lines under one company. ([SEC](https://d18rn0p25nwr6d.cloudfront.net/CIK-0002012383/83cd6991-592a-4c1e-b9d3-ed47fe8e30de.pdf))

### What is Aladdin Climate?
Aladdin Climate, launched in December 2020, measures both the physical risk of climate change and the transition risk to a low-carbon economy at the security level, with climate-adjusted valuations and risk metrics. It draws on more than 1,200 ESG indicators from data providers Sustainalytics and Refinitiv. ([Markets Media](https://www.marketsmedia.com/blackrock-launches-aladdin-climate/))
