**Oppenheimer** is Christopher Nolan's 2023 biographical drama about J. Robert
Oppenheimer, the American theoretical physicist who led the Los Alamos Laboratory
during World War II's Manhattan Project ([CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/entertainment/10-rare-facts-about-christopher-nolan-oppenheimer-that-you-did-not-know-17161221.htm)).
Starring Cillian Murphy as Oppenheimer, with Robert Downey Jr., Emily Blunt, Matt
Damon and Florence Pugh, it is adapted from the Pulitzer Prize-winning biography
*American Prometheus* ([The Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/magazines/panache/oppenheimer-hits-the-theatres-5-interesting-facts-about-christopher-nolans-explosive-epic/amp_articleshow/102002996.cms);
[Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/13787/american-prometheus-by-kai-bird-and-martin-sherwin/9780375412028)).
Made for $100 million, it grossed $975.8 million worldwide — the highest-grossing
biopic ever from September 2023 until *Michael* passed it in June 2026
([SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2204416/christopher-nolan-oppenheimer-biggest-biopic-box-office-michael/)).

For clarity: this profile is about the **2023 film** — not J. Robert Oppenheimer
the physicist. It opened theatrically on 21 July 2023 through Universal, on the
same day Warner Bros. released *Barbie*, and the shared date ignited
"Barbenheimer" ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/christopher-nolan-warner-bros-universal-pictures-studio-odyssey-oppenheimer-126073000661_1.html);
[SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2204416/christopher-nolan-oppenheimer-biggest-biopic-box-office-michael/)).
It was Nolan's first film away from Warner Bros. since *Memento* (2000), after
the studio's 2020 day-and-date HBO Max plan ended their association ([CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/entertainment/10-rare-facts-about-christopher-nolan-oppenheimer-that-you-did-not-know-17161221.htm);
[Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/christopher-nolan-warner-bros-universal-pictures-studio-odyssey-oppenheimer-126073000661_1.html)).
Cineuropa lists it as a USA/UK production — a partial UK co-production
([Cineuropa](https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/457038/)).

## The record run: a $100M gamble to the biopic crown

Universal's $100 million production bet — matched by a reported $100 million
marketing spend — looked like a gamble on a three-hour, R-rated, dialogue-heavy
drama released at midsummer ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/christopher-nolan-warner-bros-universal-pictures-studio-odyssey-oppenheimer-126073000661_1.html);
[Associated Press](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20230920-137698/)).
The gamble paid off: by September 2023 the film had passed $912 million to become
the highest-grossing biopic ever, overtaking *Bohemian Rhapsody* ($910.8 million),
and finished its run at $975.8 million ($330 million domestic, $645.7 million
international) ([Associated Press](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20230920-137698/);
[SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2204416/christopher-nolan-oppenheimer-biggest-biopic-box-office-michael/)).
Along the way it became the highest-grossing World War II film of all time,
passing Nolan's own *Dunkirk* ($530 million), and the then-second-highest-grossing
R-rated film behind *Joker* ($1.07 billion)
([Collider](http://collider.com/christopher-nolan-dunkirk-ww2-movie-streaming-peacock-november-2025/)).
The stat, source and implication: $975.8 million on a $100 million production
budget (Variety reporting via SlashFilm) — a near-10x return implying that a
talky, R-rated historical drama can outgross most franchises when a director's
brand and premium-format scarcity do the selling.

## How it was made: black-and-white IMAX and a no-CGI Trinity test

Nolan shot the film entirely on large-format stock — a combination of IMAX 65mm
and Panavision 65mm cameras, which he praised for their "sharpness and the clarity
and the depth of the image" ([CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/entertainment/10-rare-facts-about-christopher-nolan-oppenheimer-that-you-did-not-know-17161221.htm)).
The film's two timelines are told in different stocks: colour for Oppenheimer's
subjective experience, black-and-white for the more objective viewpoint of other
characters ([CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/entertainment/10-rare-facts-about-christopher-nolan-oppenheimer-that-you-did-not-know-17161221.htm)).
To match the colour footage's quality, Kodak engineered and Fotokem developed
the first-ever black-and-white IMAX film stock ([The Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/magazines/panache/oppenheimer-hits-the-theatres-5-interesting-facts-about-christopher-nolans-explosive-epic/amp_articleshow/102002996.cms)).
The Trinity nuclear test was recreated with practical effects and minimal computer
graphics — Nolan told *Total Film* that recreating the test without CGI was "a
huge challenge" ([CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/entertainment/10-rare-facts-about-christopher-nolan-oppenheimer-that-you-did-not-know-17161221.htm)).
The production shot in New Mexico's extraordinary weather, and the film runs three
hours, making it Nolan's longest, ahead of *Interstellar* at 2 hours 49 minutes ([CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/entertainment/10-rare-facts-about-christopher-nolan-oppenheimer-that-you-did-not-know-17161221.htm);
[The Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/magazines/panache/oppenheimer-hits-the-theatres-5-interesting-facts-about-christopher-nolans-explosive-epic/amp_articleshow/102002996.cms)).
The Universal deal guaranteed a 120-day exclusive theatrical window and roughly
20% of first-dollar gross for Nolan and producer Emma Thomas
([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/christopher-nolan-warner-bros-universal-pictures-studio-odyssey-oppenheimer-126073000661_1.html)).

## The awards sweep

The film's awards run began in January 2024 with five Golden Globes — Best
Picture–Drama, Best Director for Nolan, Best Actor–Drama for Murphy, Best
Supporting Actor for Robert Downey Jr., and Best Original Score for Ludwig
Göransson — at the 81st ceremony in January 2024 ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/feature/golden-globes-2024-oppenheimer-leads-with-five-wins-heres-the-full-list-of-winners/1914581/)).
In February it swept seven BAFTAs, including Best Film, where it was entered as a
USA/UK production ([Cineuropa](https://cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/457038/)). The
run peaked on 10 March 2024 at the Dolby Theatre, where the 96th Academy Awards
gave it seven Oscars from thirteen nominations: Best Picture, Director, Actor,
Supporting Actor, Cinematography for Hoyte van Hoytema, Editing for Jennifer Lame,
and Original Score ([Associated Press](https://www.shootonline.com/article/oppenheimer-tops-oscars-7-wins-including-best-picture-director-actor-cinematography-and/)).
It was the first Academy Award for the then-53-year-old Nolan, long regarded as
Hollywood's foremost big-screen auteur ([Associated Press](https://www.shootonline.com/article/oppenheimer-tops-oscars-7-wins-including-best-picture-director-actor-cinematography-and/)).
SlashFilm notes it was the first blockbuster to win Best Picture in 20 years — a
claim recorded here as the outlet's characterization ([SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2204416/christopher-nolan-oppenheimer-biggest-biopic-box-office-michael/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is *Oppenheimer* (2023) based on a true story?**
It is adapted from *American Prometheus: The Triumph and Tragedy of J. Robert Oppenheimer* by Kai Bird and Martin J. Sherwin, which won the 2006 Pulitzer Prize for Biography. The film dramatizes Oppenheimer's studies, his leadership of the Los Alamos Laboratory, and his 1954 security hearing — a screenplay dramatization, not a documentary record.

**Is *Oppenheimer* still the highest-grossing biopic of all time?**
Not since June 2026. Antoine Fuqua's *Michael* passed it at $977.4 million worldwide — $370.2 million domestic plus $607.2 million overseas — against Oppenheimer's $975.8 million. Oppenheimer had held the record since September 2023, when it overtook *Bohemian Rhapsody* ($910.8 million).

**Why is part of *Oppenheimer* in black and white?**
The colour/black-and-white split is a narrative device: colour shows Oppenheimer's subjective experience — the bulk of the film — while black-and-white presents the more objective viewpoint of other characters. Kodak engineered and Fotokem developed the first-ever black-and-white IMAX film stock for those scenes.

**Which countries produced *Oppenheimer*?**
It is a United States–United Kingdom co-production. European film-industry outlet Cineuropa describes it as "a partial co-production with the UK" and lists its BAFTA Best Film win as a USA/UK entry. Its producers are Emma Thomas, Charles Roven and Christopher Nolan; Universal Pictures distributed it worldwide.
