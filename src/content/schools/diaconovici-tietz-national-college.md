## Overview

Diaconovici-Tietz National College is a bilingual Romanian-German school in Reșița, in Romania's Banat
region, founded in the autumn of 1877 and locally known as "Bastilia" ("the Bastille"), for the fortress-like
stone building it has long occupied
([Caon.ro](https://caon.ro/bastilia-a-sarbatorit-140-de-ani-de-invatamant/1911244/)). It is the oldest school
in Reșița and was elevated to Colegiul Național (National College) rank in 2016
([Colegiul Național Diaconovici-Tietz](https://cndt.ro/)). In June 2026 it celebrated its 149th graduating
class, and in September 2026 it opened its 149th school year — a numbering consistent with the 1877 founding
([Conviețuiri](https://www.convietuiri.ro/stiri/comunitatea-germana/zilele-absolventului-2026-la-colegiul-national-diaconovici-tietz-din-resita-emotii-amintiri-si-noi-inceputuri-pentru-promotia-149-12904.html);
[Conviețuiri](https://www.convietuiri.ro/stiri/comunitatea-germana/149-de-ani-de-scoala-la-bastilia-din-resita-elevii-s-au-intors-in-banci-cu-emotii-si-planuri-pentru-noul-an-scolar-2-16584.html)).

Accounts of the school's original form conflict. The school's own history describes the 7 October 1877
founding as Școala Superioară Comunală (Communal Higher School), initially co-educational, serving the
children of local steelworks and mining families
([Colegiul Național Diaconovici-Tietz](https://cndt.ro/)), while independent local outlet Caon.ro instead
describes the founding as "prima Școală Civilă de Băieți" ("the first Civil School for Boys"), a boys-only
designation it says the school retained until 1919
([Caon.ro](https://caon.ro/bastilia-a-sarbatorit-140-de-ani-de-invatamant/1911244/)). It has changed names and
structure repeatedly across Austro-Hungarian, Romanian, and Communist-era administrations.

## The two namesakes

The school's name honors two figures from the Banat's Romanian and German cultural traditions. Corneliu
Diaconovici was a Banat-born Romanian publicist, journalist, jurist and encyclopedist who coordinated and
edited Enciclopedia Română — the first encyclopedia published in the Romanian language, issued in three
volumes — and who was related to the school's other namesake. Alexander Tietz was a Reșița teacher,
ethnographer and German-language writer who taught foreign languages and geography for more than four
decades and published volumes of legends, stories and ethnographic observations about life and traditions
in Banatul Montan in German and Romanian; his name is also borne by the German library in Reșița. The name
was assigned by local-council decision at the initiative of former teachers and school directors Ana Kremm
and Boris Vatzulik, intended to honor the unity of Romanian and German cultural values in Banatul Montan
([Ziarul Revolutionarul](https://www.ziarulrevolutionarul.ro/de-la-educatie-incepe-totul-interviu-cu-doamna-profesoara-sonia-maria-cwoik-director-adjunct-la-colegiul-national-diaconovici-tietz-din-resita/)).
The school took its current name in 1999 as Liceul Teoretic "Diaconovici-Tietz"
([Colegiul Național Diaconovici-Tietz](https://cndt.ro/)).

## Academics and the German-language section

The school is a DSD school — its students can sit the Deutsches Sprachdiplom international German-language
certificates — and it operates the only native-German section in Reșița. Through that role it collaborates
with schools with German sections across Romania and with the education ministry's minorities department, and
it maintains partnerships with the German Forum of Reșița, the German Consulate in Timișoara, the Austrian
Honorary Consulate in Timișoara, the ZfA (the Central Agency for German Schools Abroad), and the
Donauschwaben Museum in Ulm, alongside Erasmus+ projects
([Ziarul Revolutionarul](https://www.ziarulrevolutionarul.ro/de-la-educatie-incepe-totul-interviu-cu-doamna-profesoara-sonia-maria-cwoik-director-adjunct-la-colegiul-national-diaconovici-tietz-din-resita/)).

On examination results, the county's official school-inspectorate data put the school 8th of 27 high schools
in Caraș-Severin in the initial Bacalaureat 2026 results, with a 71.21% pass rate (68 enrolled, 66 present,
47 promoted), against a county average of 67.19%
([Caraș-Severin.ro](https://www.caras-severin.ro/2026/07/07/bac-2026-caras-severin-clasamentul-complet-al-liceelor/)).
One of its candidates recorded a 9.75 average — the fifth-highest in the county before appeals — and the
school's candidates also took 10 grades on written exams
([Din inimă pentru tine](https://dininimapentrutine.ro/cea-mai-mare-medie-inregistrata-in-judetul-caras-severin-inainte-de-contestatii-este-985-rezultatul-apartine-unui-candidat-de-la-liceul-teologic-baptist-resita/)).
Enrollment was reported at around 950 students across preschool through high school levels in 2017
([Caon.ro](https://caon.ro/bastilia-a-sarbatorit-140-de-ani-de-invatamant/1911244/)).

## A notable alumnus

The school marked 140 years of operation in October 2017; Caon.ro reported that Reșița mayor Ioan Popa
attended the celebration and that the school named mathematician Ciprian Foiaș among its notable alumni
([Caon.ro](https://caon.ro/bastilia-a-sarbatorit-140-de-ani-de-invatamant/1911244/)). Foiaș's own notability
is independently well established: the American Mathematical Society's memorial notice records that "Ciprian
Ilie Foias was born on July 20, 1933, in Resita, Romania," and that in 1995 he "was awarded the very
prestigious Norbert Wiener Prize from the American Mathematical Society and the Society for Industrial and
Applied Mathematics for his extensive research in applied mathematics"
([Notices of the American Mathematical Society](https://www.ams.org/journals/notices/202209/noti2545/noti2545.html)).
That notice confirms his birthplace but does not name a specific school; his attendance at this college in
particular is reported only by the school and by local press that appears to draw on the school's own alumni
list.

## Renovation and international exchanges

In November 2019 a financing contract was signed for the school's energy rehabilitation — a project valued at
5,661,058.78 lei in total, of which 4,705,053.96 lei was non-reimbursable funding through Regio-POR
2014-2020, with a 26-month implementation period
([Colegiul Național Diaconovici-Tietz](https://cndt.ro/2019/11/20/56-milioane-de-lei-pentru-colegiul-national-diaconovici-tietz/)).
The official EU programme record independently documents the project as a €1.14 million undertaking with a
€0.82 million EU contribution, beneficiary Primăria Municipiului Reșița, finalized status, implemented under
Priority Axis 3 for the transition to a low-carbon economy — including thermal insulation, roof
rehabilitation, modernized heating and lighting, solar and photovoltaic panels, and an integrated energy
management system ([ADR Vest](https://vest.ro/api/blog/am-investit-peste-5-milioane-de-lei-pentru-ca-unul-dintre-liceele-emblem-ale-banatului-de-munte-s-ofere-cele-mai-bune-condi-ii-de-nv-are-pentru-elevii-s-i/pdf)).

The school maintains European student-exchange partnerships. In May 2025, it hosted eight students and two
teachers from Gesamtschule Else Lasker-Schüler in Wuppertal, Germany, for a week-long Erasmus+ visit that
included classroom observation, a tour of the Radio România Reșița studios, and a joint Europe Day celebration,
coordinated by deputy director Sonia Maria Chwoika
([Radio România Reșița](https://www.radioresita.ro/actualitate/vizita-erasmus-la-colegiul-national-diaconovici-tietz-resita-parteneriat-international-cu-germania-5-9-mai-2025)).

## The school in 2026

The 2026 graduation ceremonies marked the 149th graduating class: 72 twelfth-grade students received their
diplomas, and director Leontin Prodan — attending his first graduation ceremony as director — told them
that "school never ends, because life itself is a school, and each day is a lesson." The graduates received
symbolic "bastilion" banknotes issued by the school and illustrated with the portraits of Corneliu Diaconovici
and Alexander Tietz
([Conviețuiri](https://www.convietuiri.ro/stiri/comunitatea-germana/zilele-absolventului-2026-la-colegiul-national-diaconovici-tietz-din-resita-emotii-amintiri-si-noi-inceputuri-pentru-promotia-149-12904.html)).

At the September 2026 school-year opening — the school's 149th — director Prodan reported growing enrollment
requests from Germanophone countries, especially for the German-language section, noting that the incoming
ninth-graders would be the first generation to sit the new-format Bacalaureat. The school educates students
from preparatory classes through grade twelve, plus kindergarten and affiliated primary and lower-secondary
structures. Former director Viorica Gârtoi attended as the new director of the Caraș-Severin Teachers'
Training House
([Conviețuiri](https://www.convietuiri.ro/stiri/comunitatea-germana/149-de-ani-de-scoala-la-bastilia-din-resita-elevii-s-au-intors-in-banci-cu-emotii-si-planuri-pentru-noul-an-scolar-2-16584.html)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not assert, as independently confirmed, that Ciprian Foiaș personally attended this specific
school — only that he was born in the same city and that the school and local press name him as an alumnus.
It does not settle the conflicting accounts of the school's 1877 founding form (co-educational communal
school versus boys-only civil school). It does not state an enrollment figure more recent than the roughly
950 students reported in 2017, and it does not repeat an apparent local-press claim of a 100% Bacalaureat pass
rate that could not be opened and verified during this research. It does not determine which rendering of the
namesake's first name — Cornel or Corneliu Diaconovici — is correct. It does not state the organization's
current staffing or budget beyond what the cited sources document.
