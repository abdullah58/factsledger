## Overview

The University of Texas at Dallas is a public research university in Richardson, Texas, established in 1969 as a member of the University of Texas System ([City of Richardson Economic Development](https://richardsoneconomicdevelopment.com/milestone-reached-ut-dallas-qualifies-for-states-tier-one-fund/)). Its defining story is speed: in under fifty years it went from founding to qualifying for the state's National Research University Fund, becoming the youngest institution — and only the third overall — in Texas to meet the "Tier One" benchmarks ([City of Richardson Economic Development](https://richardsoneconomicdevelopment.com/milestone-reached-ut-dallas-qualifies-for-states-tier-one-fund/)). It pairs that research ascent with an unusual campus identity: a collegiate chess powerhouse that won the spring 2026 Collegiate Chess League title ([Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/news/view/2026-ccl-spring-finals/)).

## The sprint to Tier One

Texas created the National Research University Fund through House Bill 51, signed in June 2009, to lift emerging universities toward national research prominence; UT Dallas qualified in July 2018 after hitting the Texas Higher Education Coordinating Board's benchmarks two years running — $45 million in annual restricted research expenditures, a $400 million endowment, a high-achieving freshman class, and high-quality faculty ([City of Richardson Economic Development](https://richardsoneconomicdevelopment.com/milestone-reached-ut-dallas-qualifies-for-states-tier-one-fund/)). The funding that fiscal year totaled $7.5 million, earmarked for undergraduate research, equipment, library materials, faculty salaries, and graduate stipends ([City of Richardson Economic Development](https://richardsoneconomicdevelopment.com/milestone-reached-ut-dallas-qualifies-for-states-tier-one-fund/)). The trajectory was steep: restricted research expenditures averaged $36.5 million in FY2008-09 and grew 40% to over $51 million by FY2016-17, and Carnegie classified UT Dallas as an R1 doctoral institution of highest research activity in 2016 ([City of Richardson Economic Development](https://richardsoneconomicdevelopment.com/milestone-reached-ut-dallas-qualifies-for-states-tier-one-fund/)). Governor Greg Abbott called it "a national leader in research" in the announcement; UT System regents chair Sara Martinez Tucker congratulated its "ascendancy as a research powerhouse" ([City of Richardson Economic Development](https://richardsoneconomicdevelopment.com/milestone-reached-ut-dallas-qualifies-for-states-tier-one-fund/)). At the time, the university enrolled more than 27,600 students — 18,380 undergraduates and 9,250 graduate students — with a faculty including a Nobel laureate, six members of the National Academies, and more than 575 tenured and tenure-track professors ([City of Richardson Economic Development](https://richardsoneconomicdevelopment.com/milestone-reached-ut-dallas-qualifies-for-states-tier-one-fund/)). The Nobel connection is Aziz Sancar, who earned his PhD in molecular and cell biology at UT Dallas in 1977 and won the 2015 Nobel Prize in Chemistry ([City of Richardson Economic Development](https://richardsoneconomicdevelopment.com/milestone-reached-ut-dallas-qualifies-for-states-tier-one-fund/)).

## Growth with guardrails

UT Dallas grew by getting more selective, not less. Freshman enrollment of about 3,100 had been rising 5 to 7 percent a year for a decade; a third of students ranked in the top 10 percent of their high-school classes, the middle 50 percent of SAT scores ran 1210 to 1430, and white students made up only 37 percent of undergraduates ([Inside Higher Ed](https://www.insidehighered.com/admissions/article/2018/02/12/university-texas-dallas-ends-automatic-admission-test-score)). In 2018 the university ended a decade-old policy of automatic admission for SAT scores of 1200+, after finding that a small number of high-scoring but low-diligence admits were not succeeding; non-top-10% applicants moved to holistic review of rank, curriculum rigor, activities, and essays ([Inside Higher Ed](https://www.insidehighered.com/admissions/article/2018/02/12/university-texas-dallas-ends-automatic-admission-test-score)). Executive vice president Hobson Wildenthal framed the philosophy: "We don't want to turn away qualified students. We still want to grow, but we want to grow with good students" ([Inside Higher Ed](https://www.insidehighered.com/admissions/article/2018/02/12/university-texas-dallas-ends-automatic-admission-test-score)). The long-run target was capacity for 3,600 freshmen a year ([Inside Higher Ed](https://www.insidehighered.com/admissions/article/2018/02/12/university-texas-dallas-ends-automatic-admission-test-score)).

## Chess

UT Dallas is one of the premier programs in the Collegiate Chess League, described by Chess.com as the premier online chess competition for college students ([Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/news/view/2026-ccl-spring-finals/)). After falling 8.5–7.5 to Saint Louis University in the fall 2025 final, the Comets won the spring 2026 title 9.5–6.5 over SRM Institute of Science and Technology, with every player scoring between 1.5 and 3 points across the four-game match ([Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/news/view/2026-ccl-spring-finals/)). Grandmasters Gergely Kantor and Yair Parkhov delivered key wins in round three as UTD went undefeated to take the lead, and GM Aditya Samant escaped a troubled opening to win the endgame that put the match out of reach ([Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/news/view/2026-ccl-spring-finals/)).
