## Overview

Harvard Business School (HBS) is the graduate business school of [Harvard University](/universities/harvard-university/), founded in 1908 on a 40-acre campus in Boston, [Massachusetts](/places/massachusetts/). It was the first school to require entering students to hold an undergraduate degree, and it created the Master of Business Administration (M.B.A.) — Harvard's first diploma written in English rather than Latin ([Harvard Gazette](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2011/10/leading-business-education)). In its first century it pioneered the case method of management teaching, built the first executive education programmes, and grew into a school of more than 250 faculty teaching a two-year residential MBA, doctoral programmes, and executive education ([Harvard Gazette](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2011/10/leading-business-education)). Today it counts 90,000 alumni, leads PitchBook's ranking of MBA programmes for entrepreneurs, and is the school where [Cloudflare](/organizations/cloudflare/)'s founders won the 2009 Business Plan Contest ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/annualreport/2025/year-in-review)).

## Founding and the case method

HBS was founded in 1908 as what future Harvard President A. Lawrence Lowell called a "delicate experiment" — a five-year trial approved by the Harvard Corporation ([Harvard Gazette](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2011/10/leading-business-education)). It began with 15 faculty members in space borrowed from the Graduate School of Arts and Sciences, under founding dean Edwin Francis Gay, who defined business as the activity of making "a decent profit, decently" ([Harvard Gazette](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2011/10/leading-business-education)). The school's own history calls it the world's first MBA programme ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/history?ref=michaelshoe.com)).

The school's defining contribution to management education is the case method: students are put in the position of managers facing a real decision, analyse the available information, and defend a recommendation in class discussion. HBS pioneered the method's adoption for management instruction — along with the distinctive tiered classroom built to support it — and established it as the primary method of instruction in 1924 ([Harvard Gazette](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2011/10/leading-business-education)). The school also built the field's infrastructure around it: the first executive education programme, the Advanced Management Program, was held in 1945 for 60 executives and recently demobilised veterans, and the school offered the country's first business-school course in entrepreneurship in 1947 ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/history?ref=michaelshoe.com)). Women graduates of the Harvard-Radcliffe Program in Business Administration were admitted to the second year of the MBA programme in 1959 ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/history?ref=michaelshoe.com)). Harvard Business School Online, its digital learning platform, was founded in 2013 ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/history?ref=michaelshoe.com)).

## The MBA today

HBS remains one of the most selective MBA programmes in the world. In fiscal year 2025 more than 9,400 people applied for the MBA and more than 900 for the doctoral programmes, with acceptance rates of 12 percent and 4 percent respectively ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/annualreport/2025/year-in-review)). The MBA Class of 2026 enrolled 934 students at an 85 percent yield, and nearly half of all MBA students — 802 in total — received a fellowship averaging $47,345, with full-tuition scholarships for roughly the 10 percent of students with the greatest financial need ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/annualreport/2025/year-in-review)). On 29 May 2025, 925 graduates were awarded MBA degrees, 49 of them with high distinction as Baker Scholars ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/annualreport/2025/year-in-review)).

The PitchBook ranking of top MBA programmes for entrepreneurs — based on more than 173,000 VC-backed founders — puts HBS first, with just over 1,900 alumni founding companies in the last decade ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/annualreport/2025/year-in-review)). That entrepreneurial tilt shows in the classroom too: nearly 25 percent of the MBA Class of 2025 pursued founding, joining, or acquiring a company after graduation ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/annualreport/2025/year-in-review)). The programme keeps changing with the field — it received a STEM designation beginning with the Class of 2025, and launched a required course, Data Science and AI for Leaders, that teaches data analysis without requiring students to write code ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/annualreport/2025/year-in-review)). Srikant Datar has been dean since 1 January 2021, succeeding Nitin Nohria, who stepped down in December 2020 after more than ten years ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/annualreport/2021/)).

## The Cloudflare connection

HBS's most consequential recent alumni startup began as a class project. In January 2009, second-year MBA students [Matthew Prince](/people/matthew-prince/) and [Michelle Zatlyn](/people/michelle-zatlyn/) were on a class trip to [Silicon Valley](/places/silicon-valley/) when Prince described an open-source spam-tracking project he was building with programmer [Lee Holloway](/people/lee-holloway/) — [Project Honey Pot](/organizations/project-honey-pot/) — and Zatlyn saw a bigger opportunity: not just tracking online threats but stopping them ([Poets&Quants](https://poetsandquants.com/2013/11/22/the-harvard-startup-that-supercharges-websites/?pq-category=students)). That evening they sketched the vision for a cybersecurity service, refined it as an independent study under entrepreneurship professor Tom Eisenmann, and entered the school's Business Plan Contest ([Poets&Quants](https://poetsandquants.com/2013/11/22/the-harvard-startup-that-supercharges-websites/?pq-category=students)).

They won. At the final round of the 13th annual contest in April 2009, a record 93 teams having entered, Prince and Zatlyn took top honours in the traditional track with CloudFlare, a web security venture for small websites ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/news/releases/2009bizplancontest)). The team received $25,000 in cash and $25,000 of in-kind accounting and legal services, plus the Dubilier Prize, which honours the late Martin Dubilier (MBA '52), cofounder of Clayton, Dubilier & Rice ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/news/releases/2009bizplancontest)). The contest was run by the school's Arthur Rock Center for Entrepreneurship and the Social Enterprise Initiative, and it is the capstone of the MBA entrepreneurship curriculum ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/news/releases/2009bizplancontest)). By November 2009 the founders had raised more than $2 million in Series A funding, and the school project had become a company ([Poets&Quants](https://poetsandquants.com/2013/11/22/the-harvard-startup-that-supercharges-websites/?pq-category=students)). The contest continues under its current name, the New Venture Competition — its 28th annual finale was held in March 2025, with grand prizes of $75,000 per track ([Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/about/annualreport/2025/year-in-review)). Regional alumni clubs, including the [Harvard Business School Club of New York](/organizations/harvard-business-school-club-of-new-york/), host the competition's regional rounds.

## Frequently asked questions

### When was Harvard Business School founded?

In 1908, as the Harvard Graduate School of Business Administration. It began as a five-year trial — a "delicate experiment" — with 15 faculty members, and it created the MBA degree, the first Harvard diploma written in English rather than Latin. Its founding dean was historian Edwin Francis Gay.

### What is the case method?

It is HBS's signature teaching approach: students analyse a real managerial decision from the information available at the time, then debate and defend a proposed course of action in class. HBS pioneered the method for management education and made it its primary method of instruction in 1924, building tiered classrooms to support the discussion format.

### How selective is the HBS MBA?

Very. In fiscal year 2025 more than 9,400 people applied and the acceptance rate was 12 percent. The entering Class of 2026 had 934 students, and nearly half of MBA students received fellowships averaging $47,345. About 10 percent of students with the greatest financial need receive full-tuition scholarships.

### Did Cloudflare really start at Harvard Business School?

Yes. Co-founders Matthew Prince and Michelle Zatlyn were second-year MBA students (Class of 2009) when they sketched the idea on a Silicon Valley class trip in January 2009, building on Prince's Project Honey Pot work with Lee Holloway. They won the school's Business Plan Contest that April, then raised a Series A round in November 2009.

### Who is the dean of Harvard Business School?

Srikant Datar, who became dean on 1 January 2021, succeeding Nitin Nohria after more than a decade in the role. The school's programmes include the two-year MBA, doctoral programmes, more than 175 executive education programmes, and HBS Online.
