## Overview

Southern Utah University (SUU) is a public university in Cedar City, Utah, founded in 1897 as a branch normal school — a teacher-training institution — by act of the Utah Legislature ([Utah History Encyclopedia](http://uen.org/utah_history_encyclopedia/s/SOUTHERN_UTAH_UNIVERSITY.shtml)). Its founding story is unusual in American higher education: after the state attorney general ruled that the town's borrowed Ward Hall did not satisfy the law's requirement for a state-owned building, the people of Cedar City spent the winter of 1897–98 hauling logs from Cedar Mountain to build Old Main, the campus's first building, in time to keep the school ([Utah History Encyclopedia](http://uen.org/utah_history_encyclopedia/s/SOUTHERN_UTAH_UNIVERSITY.shtml)). That building still anchors the center of campus. Today SUU is a comprehensive university in the Utah System of Higher Education with 15,444 students — an enrollment that has doubled in a decade ([Utah Business](https://www.utahbusiness.com/press-releases/2024/10/16/southern-utah-university-doubles-enrollment/)).

## From branch school to university

The institution cycled through four names before becoming a university. In 1913 it became a branch of Utah State Agricultural College at Logan and was renamed the Branch Agricultural College (BAC); in 1953 it became the College of Southern Utah; it took the Southern Utah State College name in the late 1960s/early 1970s; and it was granted university status in the early 1990s, becoming Southern Utah University ([Utah History Encyclopedia](http://uen.org/utah_history_encyclopedia/s/SOUTHERN_UTAH_UNIVERSITY.shtml)). The statutory change was made by Senate Bill 119, passed at the 1990 session of the state legislature and effective January 1, 1991, though some histories date the university era to 1992 (see the sourcing note below). In 2011 the Board of Regents approved SUU's designation as Utah's designated public liberal arts and sciences university ([Utah Legislative Fiscal Analyst](https://le.utah.gov/interim/2025/pdf/00000593.pdf)).

The "University of the Parks" identity grew naturally from geography: the campus sits within a half-day's drive of Zion National Park, Bryce Canyon, Cedar Breaks National Monument, and Brian Head ski resort, and students claim Zion and Cedar Breaks as de facto extensions of campus ([Utah History Encyclopedia](http://uen.org/utah_history_encyclopedia/s/SOUTHERN_UTAH_UNIVERSITY.shtml)). The most distinctive campus institution is the Utah Shakespeare Festival, founded in 1961 by Fred C. Adams and staged each summer and fall on SUU's campus — a six-decade tradition noted in the Congressional Record, where the festival's Engelstad Shakespeare Theater, modeled on the Globe, draws families from around the world ([Congressional Record](https://www.congress.gov/congressional-record/volume-167/issue-140/senate-section/article/S5928-1)).

## A decade of growth

SUU's defining modern trend is enrollment growth far outpacing the state system. As early as 2015, when the Utah System of Higher Education released its annual report, SUU posted a 16% enrollment increase — the largest gain of any Utah public college or university that year, a surge officials attributed partly to missionaries returning under the LDS Church's lowered missionary age ([KVNU/AP](https://kvnutalk.com/utah-public-colleges-universities-see-surge-in-enrollment/)). The growth compounded: by fall 2024 the student population reached an all-time high of 15,444, roughly double the figure of ten years earlier, with more than 3,500 fully online students driving much of the expansion ([Utah Business](https://www.utahbusiness.com/press-releases/2024/10/16/southern-utah-university-doubles-enrollment/)). Independent IPEDS data confirms the 15,444 total and shows 8,868 full-time and 6,576 part-time students ([Data USA](https://datausa.io/profile/university/southern-utah-university&)). Alongside the headcount, SUU reported a record 60.7% graduation rate and 75.5% student retention ([Utah Business](https://www.utahbusiness.com/press-releases/2024/10/16/southern-utah-university-doubles-enrollment/)), and the Legislative Fiscal Analyst counted 12,829 full-time-equivalent enrollments for 2024 ([Utah Legislative Fiscal Analyst](https://le.utah.gov/interim/2025/pdf/00000593.pdf)).

## Leadership and athletics

In July 2022, the Utah Board of Higher Education selected Mindy Benson as SUU's 17th president following an extensive national search — the first woman to hold the presidency in the institution's 125-year history ([Utah System of Higher Education](https://ushe.edu/mindy-benson-named-as-17th-president-at-southern-utah-university/)). Benson, a Cedar City native who earned both her bachelor's and master's degrees at SUU, had served as interim president since July 2021 after Scott L. Wyatt stepped down to lead the system's statewide online education program ([Utah System of Higher Education](https://ushe.edu/mindy-benson-named-as-17th-president-at-southern-utah-university/)).

The same month, SUU's 17 athletic teams — the Thunderbirds — joined the Western Athletic Conference, effective July 1, 2022, leaving the Big Sky Conference as part of a five-school WAC expansion that also reinstated FCS football in the league ([SwimSwam](https://swimswam.com/western-athletic-conference-announces-five-school-expansion/)). SUU is regionally accredited by the Northwest Commission on Colleges and Universities, with additional specialized accreditations in nursing, art and design, dance, music, and teacher education ([College Factual](https://www.collegefactual.com/colleges/southern-utah-university/academic-life/accreditation/)).

## What this profile does not claim

- The precise years of the Southern Utah State College rename differ between sources (1969 in the state board history, 1971 in the Utah History Encyclopedia), and the university-status year is given as both 1991 and 1992 — the profile states the statutory effective date and flags both discrepancies rather than choosing silently.
- No endowment figure is stated; the $45.9 million figure circulating in tertiary references was not independently verified.
- Enrollment figures are third-week census counts reported through the Utah System of Higher Education, not audited IPEDS year-end totals; the 2024 IPEDS-based figures are stated separately via Data USA.
- No rankings, program-level enrollment breakdowns, or tuition figures are stated; none was independently verified in this pass.
