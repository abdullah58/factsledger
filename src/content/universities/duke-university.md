## Overview

Duke University is a private research university in Durham, North Carolina, whose history runs from a one-room schoolhouse to a tobacco-fortune endowment ([Columbia Encyclopedia](https://www.FactMonster.com/encyclopedia/social-science/education/college-us/duke-university)). It opened in 1838 as Brown's Schoolhouse in Randolph County, was chartered as Union Institute in 1841, reorganized as Normal College, and became Trinity College (Methodist) in 1859 before moving to Durham in 1892 ([Columbia Encyclopedia](https://www.FactMonster.com/encyclopedia/social-science/education/college-us/duke-university)). In 1924, tobacco and electric-power industrialist James B. Duke signed the Indenture of Trust creating The Duke Endowment, and the institution was renamed Duke University in his family's honor ([Columbia Encyclopedia](https://www.FactMonster.com/encyclopedia/social-science/education/college-us/duke-university)).

## The campus a Black architect designed but couldn't attend

Duke's most architecturally distinguished feature — the neo-Gothic West Campus crowned by Duke Chapel — was designed by Julian Abele, a Black architect who, by race, could not have been admitted to study at the university he was building ([Manning Architects](https://manning.xyz/news/honoring-black-architects-who-paved-way-julian-abele)). As chief designer for Horace Trumbauer's firm from 1924 until his death in 1950, Abele designed more than thirty buildings on Duke's campus; the school was not desegregated until 1962 ([Manning Architects](https://manning.xyz/news/honoring-black-architects-who-paved-way-julian-abele)). Abele was the first African American to earn an architectural degree from the University of Pennsylvania and also served as chief designer for Harvard's Widener Library ([Manning Architects](https://manning.xyz/news/honoring-black-architects-who-paved-way-julian-abele)). Duke later renamed its central quadrangle for him — a posthumous recognition of a contribution that went largely uncredited in his lifetime ([Manning Architects](https://manning.xyz/news/honoring-black-architects-who-paved-way-julian-abele)).

## The $100 million centennial gift

To mark the 2024 centennial of both the university and the independent Duke Endowment, the Endowment awarded Duke $100 million in November 2023 — the largest single award in the university's history ([Inside Higher Ed](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/quick-takes/2023/11/29/duke-receives-100m-gift-financial-aid-work-based-learning)). The gift funds full-tuition grants for undergraduates from North and South Carolina with family incomes below $150,000, financial support for graduate and professional students who earned degrees at historically Black colleges and universities and other minority-serving institutions, doctoral and professional fellowships, and a fund for Duke's law clinics ([Inside Higher Ed](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/quick-takes/2023/11/29/duke-receives-100m-gift-financial-aid-work-based-learning)). The award arrived as Duke expanded financial-aid plans in the wake of the U.S. Supreme Court's ruling restricting race-conscious admissions ([Inside Higher Ed](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/quick-takes/2023/11/29/duke-receives-100m-gift-financial-aid-work-based-learning)).
