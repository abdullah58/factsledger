## Overview

McNeese State University is a public university in Lake Charles, Louisiana, established by the Louisiana Legislature in 1939 to expand access to public higher education in Southwest Louisiana ([University of Louisiana System](https://ul-system.files.svdcdn.com/production/resources/board-members/Presidential-Searches/2026-msu-leadership-packet.pdf?dm=1774556076)). Renamed early in its history for John McNeese, a pioneering educator and the first superintendent of schools in Imperial Calcasieu Parish, it is one of nine member institutions governed by the University of Louisiana System Board of Supervisors ([University of Louisiana System](https://ul-system.files.svdcdn.com/production/resources/board-members/Presidential-Searches/2026-msu-leadership-packet.pdf?dm=1774556076)). In fall 2025 the university enrolled 6,556 students — 92% undergraduates — with an FY26 operating budget of $78.4 million and an endowment exceeding $91.4 million ([University of Louisiana System](https://ul-system.files.svdcdn.com/production/resources/board-members/Presidential-Searches/2026-msu-leadership-packet.pdf?dm=1774556076)).

## Two hurricanes and a rebuild

McNeese's modern history is inseparable from the storms of 2020. Hurricane Laura — a Category 4 storm and the tenth-strongest hurricane to strike the mainland United States — roared ashore in Lake Charles on August 27, 2020; six weeks later, Hurricane Delta dumped torrential rain into the still-open wounds ([Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/college/2021/02/09/mcneese-state-football-fcs-hurricane-laura)). The two-year-old, $44 million health and human performance complex took the worst of it: Laura ripped a hole in the building, and Delta then dumped 16 inches of rain into the basketball arena, rendering it useless for the season ([Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/college/2021/02/09/mcneese-state-football-fcs-hurricane-laura)). The stadium's mangled light poles had to be removed, the press box was rendered non-operational, and the goalposts bent by Laura's 130-plus mph winds were replaced ([Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/college/2021/02/09/mcneese-state-football-fcs-hurricane-laura)).

The financial toll ran deeper than the physical one. Total damage estimates reached as high as $200 million; by early 2021 the school had spent $100 million, drawn from state funds, insurance payments, and FEMA, much of it fronted by McNeese before reimbursement arrived ([Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/college/2021/02/09/mcneese-state-football-fcs-hurricane-laura)). Enrollment fell about 15%, costing roughly $3 million, and the president at the time warned that "in the future, the budget is going to be a big issue," noting that after Hurricane Rita in 2005 it had taken five years to return to growth ([Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/college/2021/02/09/mcneese-state-football-fcs-hurricane-laura)). Five months after Laura, recovery was visibly underway — 13 of 16 sports were hosting games at their own facilities, roughly half of dorms were operational, and football, baseball, and softball had new turf fields — even as the football team, down 21 players from transfers, played its season in spring 2021 after COVID-19 wiped out the fall ([Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/college/2021/02/09/mcneese-state-football-fcs-hurricane-laura)). The rebuilt stadium later gained a new name, Navarre Stadium, under a $5 million, 10-year naming rights agreement announced in 2025 — the largest naming rights gift in both McNeese Athletics and Southland Conference history — with former head coach Matt Viator returning to lead the Cowboys ([Crescent City Sports](https://crescentcitysports.com/mcneese-moves-forward-with-hurricanes-of-the-past-in-mind/)).

## A national role in LNG safety

In May 2025, U.S. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy and Senator John Kennedy announced that McNeese State University had been selected as the site of the PHMSA National Center of Excellence for Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) Safety ([U.S. Department of Transportation](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/transportation-secretary-duffy-and-sen-kennedy-announce-new-lng-project-america)). The center, mandated by the PIPES Act of 2020, is intended to concentrate federal expertise on LNG operations, serve as a repository of best practices, and coordinate work among the Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, the U.S. Coast Guard, the Department of Energy, and the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission ([U.S. Department of Transportation](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/transportation-secretary-duffy-and-sen-kennedy-announce-new-lng-project-america)). McNeese was chosen in part because it is the first undergraduate institution in the United States to offer a certificate program in LNG Business and already operates its own LNG Center of Excellence ([U.S. Department of Transportation](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/transportation-secretary-duffy-and-sen-kennedy-announce-new-lng-project-america)). The university's own center — a planned 24,000-square-foot complex — drew a $1 million pledge from Tellurian for construction and student scholarships for the LNG Business Certificate ([1012 Industry Report](https://www.1012industryreport.com/philanthropy/tellurian-pledges-1-million-for-new-lng-center-for-excellence-scholarships/)).

## Leadership turnover and athletics

Presidential leadership at McNeese has been unusually unsettled in the mid-2020s. Wade Rousse became president in July 2024 and led the university during the LNG center designation and an enrollment rebound — fall 2025 enrollment grew 5% to 6,556, with the largest first-time freshman class in many years ([U.S. Department of Transportation](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/transportation-secretary-duffy-and-sen-kennedy-announce-new-lng-project-america)) ([University of Louisiana System](https://ul-system.files.svdcdn.com/production/resources/board-members/Presidential-Searches/2026-msu-leadership-packet.pdf?dm=1774556076)). But in November 2025 Rousse stepped down to become president of the LSU System ([Louisiana First News](https://www.louisianafirstnews.com/news/local-news/baton-rouge/lsu-leadership-contracts-approval/)). The UL System Board named senior vice president Kedrick Nicholas — a former McNeese football player with nearly two decades of campus leadership, including dean of students — interim president effective November 17, 2025 ([KHLA Lake Charles](https://929thelake.com/kedrick-nicholas-interim-president-mcneese/)). Nicholas was still interim when the board interviewed finalists at a special meeting on May 19, 2026, with the search seeking a permanent president ([UL System Board](https://ul-system.files.svdcdn.com/production/Board-Meetings/May-19-2026-Special-Meeting-Minutes.pdf?dm=1782995148)).

Athletically, the Cowboys and Cowgirls are the longest-running members of the Southland Conference, entering their 54th season in 2025–26 ([University of Louisiana System](https://ul-system.files.svdcdn.com/production/resources/board-members/Presidential-Searches/2026-msu-leadership-packet.pdf?dm=1774556076)). The department's recent high-water marks include men's basketball winning back-to-back Southland regular-season and tournament titles in 2024 and 2025 — with the 2025 run producing the program's first NCAA Tournament win, a first-round upset of Clemson — and softball capturing four straight conference regular-season championships ([University of Louisiana System](https://ul-system.files.svdcdn.com/production/resources/board-members/Presidential-Searches/2026-msu-leadership-packet.pdf?dm=1774556076)). The football program holds a league-record 14 championships and 16 FCS playoff appearances, with two FCS national championship game appearances (1997, 2002) ([University of Louisiana System](https://ul-system.files.svdcdn.com/production/resources/board-members/Presidential-Searches/2026-msu-leadership-packet.pdf?dm=1774556076)).
