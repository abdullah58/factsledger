## Overview

Cégep du Vieux Montréal (CVM) is a French-language public CEGEP in downtown [Montreal](/places/montreal/), Quebec, [Canada](/places/canada/), founded in 1968 from the merger of several arts, technical and nursing schools. It enrols more than 6,000 regular day students across 52 pre-university and technical programmes, and is best known for its arts identity: it houses Montreal's only Cartooning DEC and the Institut des métiers d'art, whose eight craft specialties range from ceramics to guitar lutherie ([Cégep du Vieux Montréal](https://www.cvm.qc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Bilan-environnemnetal-2022_CVM.pdf); [Animation Career Review](https://www.animationcareerreview.com/articles/top-10-professional-animation-training-programs-canada-2026-school-rankings)).

The college sits at 255 rue Ontario Est in the Quartier Latin, and its pool offers free open swim times to the public ([Ville de Montréal](https://montreal.ca/en/places/pool-cegep-du-vieux-montreal)). It is one of Quebec's 48 public CEGEPs and a member of [Colleges and Institutes Canada](/organizations/colleges-and-institutes-canada/) ([Gouvernement du Québec](https://www.quebec.ca/en/education/cegeps-private-colleges/studying/overview); [Colleges and Institutes Canada](https://www.collegesinstitutes.ca/colleges-and-institutes-in-your-community/our-members/?_protocols=sdg-accord)). Unlike most Quebec colleges, its public profile is built less on scale than on a distinctive mix: fine crafts, animation, digital creation and a long-standing mandate to support students with disabilities across western Quebec.

## A 1968 merger in the Quartier Latin

CVM was created in 1968 from the merger of Collège Mont-Saint-Louis, the Institut de technologie de Montréal, the Institut des arts appliqués, the École des beaux-arts de Montréal, Collège Sainte-Marie and several downtown hospital nursing schools ([Cégep du Vieux Montréal](https://www.cvm.qc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Bilan-environnemnetal-2022_CVM.pdf)). That inheritance still shapes what it teaches: technical training in nursing, physical techniques and applied arts, and pre-university programmes in natural sciences, human sciences, arts and letters all descend directly from the predecessor schools ([Cégep du Vieux Montréal](https://www.cvm.qc.ca/projet-educatif-initial/)). Since 1976 its teaching has been regrouped at a single main campus in the heart of the city, among university, technological, artistic and commercial institutions ([Cégep du Vieux Montréal](https://www.cvm.qc.ca/projet-educatif-initial/)). The college describes its mission in humanist terms — forming competent, autonomous, cultured people engaged in their community — and its educational project gives the arts a dominant place, from visual arts, dance-ballet, photography and graphic design to architecture technology, industrial design and the Institut des métiers d'art ([Cégep du Vieux Montréal](https://www.cvm.qc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Bilan-environnemnetal-2022_CVM.pdf); [Cégep du Vieux Montréal](https://www.cvm.qc.ca/projet-educatif-initial/)).

## Programmes: pre-university, technical and craft

Quebec's CEGEP system sits between secondary school and university: pre-university DEC programmes take two years of full-time study, technical DEC programmes take three years and include at least one workplace practicum, and short attestations of college studies prepare students for immediate employment ([Gouvernement du Québec](https://www.quebec.ca/en/education/cegeps-private-colleges/studying/overview)). At CVM about two-thirds of students are in the technical sector and one-third in pre-university programmes, across 52 programmes, options and profiles, and more than 6,000 students enrol in regular daytime study ([Cégep du Vieux Montréal](https://www.cvm.qc.ca/projet-educatif-initial/); [Cégep du Vieux Montréal](https://www.cvm.qc.ca/wp-content/uploads/Bilan-environnemnetal-2022_CVM.pdf)) — a technical-heavy mix, which means most of its students are training directly for a trade or profession rather than for university transfer.

| Pathway | Length | Leads to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-university DEC | 2 years full-time | University |
| Technical DEC | 3 years full-time | Job market (or university) |
| Attestation of college studies (AEC) | Short-term | Immediate employment |

The craft and screen-arts programmes are the college's calling card. The Institut des métiers d'art teaches eight specialties — ceramics, textile construction, textile printing, artisanal cabinetmaking, jewellery, guitar lutherie, leatherwork and glass ([Canadian Crafts Federation](https://canadiancraftsfederation.ca/resources/education/)). In animation, CVM ranked seventh in Canada in Animation Career Review's 2026 ranking: it is a Toon Boom Center of Excellence and Houdini Certified School, houses Montreal's only Cartooning DEC, and runs DEC programmes in 3D Production and Image Synthesis and 3D Animation, with graduates hired by Toon Boom Animation, Gameloft, Framestore, Cinesite, Mikros Animation and Rodeo FX ([Animation Career Review](https://www.animationcareerreview.com/articles/top-10-professional-animation-training-programs-canada-2026-school-rankings)).

## Access, community and a 2026 split

Since 1982 the college has held a suprarégional mandate to support every CEGEP and subsidised private college in western Quebec in offering adapted services to students with disabilities, through its Collégial Centre for Integration Support, the CCSI de l'ouest, which serves dozens of college and university establishments ([Cégep du Vieux Montréal](https://www.cvm.qc.ca/projet-educatif-initial/)). Its digital-creation arm, Campus ADN, run jointly with Cégep de Matane, has trained students in video games, digital creation and interactive arts for more than 20 years and moved to Îlot Balmoral in the Quartier des spectacles from September 2026 ([Cégep du Vieux Montréal](https://www.cvm.qc.ca/minisite/formation-continue/nouvelles-fc/article/24149/)). In November 2026 the college separated from its fundraising foundation after a dispute over the foundation's purpose: *La Presse* reported the cégep had sent no representative to the foundation's board since 2024, founder Michel Gagné said the foundation would drop the cégep's name but keep supporting its students, and Directrice générale Mylène Boisclair said the college was considering options such as an internal endowment fund ([Academica Group](https://academica.ca/top-ten/cegep-du-vieux-montreal-separates-from-cegep-foundation/)).

## FAQ

### What is Cégep du Vieux Montréal?

It is a French-language public CEGEP in downtown Montreal, founded in 1968 from the merger of several arts, technical and nursing schools. It enrols more than 6,000 regular day students across 52 pre-university and technical programmes, and is known for its arts identity, including the Institut des métiers d'art and Montreal's only Cartooning DEC.

### What is a CEGEP, and how long do its programmes take?

A CEGEP is a Quebec public college between secondary school and university. Pre-university programmes leading to a Diploma of College Studies take two years of full-time study, technical programmes take three years and include a workplace practicum, and short attestations prepare students for immediate employment.

### What does the Institut des métiers d'art teach?

At its Montreal campus it teaches eight fine-craft specialties: ceramics, textile construction, textile printing, artisanal cabinetmaking, jewellery, guitar lutherie, leatherwork and glass. The programmes lead to a Diploma of College Studies in fine crafts and are listed nationally by the Canadian Crafts Federation.

### What makes the college's animation programmes distinctive?

CVM ranked seventh in Canada in Animation Career Review's 2026 ranking. It is a Toon Boom Center of Excellence and Houdini Certified School, houses Montreal's only Cartooning DEC, and runs DEC programmes in 3D Production and Image Synthesis and 3D Animation, with graduates hired by studios including Gameloft and Framestore.

### Why did the college separate from its foundation in 2026?

The college and its fundraising foundation officially separated at the beginning of November 2026 after a conflict over the foundation's purpose. The cégep had sent no representative to the foundation's board since 2024; the foundation will drop the cégep's name but continue supporting its students.
