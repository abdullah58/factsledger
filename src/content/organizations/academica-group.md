## Overview

Academica Group is a Canadian market research and consulting firm that works exclusively with colleges, universities and polytechnics, publishing the Academica Top Ten daily news digest and running the University/College Applicant Study (UCAS™), one of the largest surveys of postsecondary applicants anywhere. Based in London, [Ontario](/places/ontario/), [Canada](/places/canada/), it provides market research, brand consulting and strategic marketing planning to higher-education institutions ([Canadian Journal of Communication](https://cjc.utpjournals.press/doi/pdf/10.22230/cjc.2013v38n4a2652); [The League for Innovation in the Community College](https://www.league.org/node/18122)).

Its research feeds both client work and public sector analysis: in 2023 more than 78,000 applicants shared their motivations through UCAS, and by 2025 the firm said it connected regularly with more than 30,000 members of the postsecondary sector ([Academica Group](https://forum.academica.ca/academicaarticles); [Academica Group](https://academica.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/SRP-Final-Report-Mar-2025.pdf)). One basic fact is unsettled: sources give three different founding dates — 1995, 2003 and 2005 — and this profile reports all three rather than choosing one.

## Three dates for one founding

London's economic development corporation traces the firm to Acumen Research Group, founded in 1995, which pioneered applying consumer research to higher-education policy, branding, alumni relations and recruitment ([London Economic Development Corporation](https://www.ledc.com/business-directory/largest-employers?page=12)). Co-founder Ken Steele's biography gives a different start: he conceived and co-founded Academica Group in 2003 and created the Top Ten, leaving in 2012 for his speaking practice Eduvation ([The League for Innovation in the Community College](https://www.league.org/node/18122)). A peer-reviewed 2013 study gives a third date: Academica Group "was formed in London, Ontario" in 2005, its principals having worked in education marketing for the previous decade ([Canadian Journal of Communication](https://cjc.utpjournals.press/doi/pdf/10.22230/cjc.2013v38n4a2652)). The three accounts could form a sequence — predecessor, co-founding, formation — but no source states it, so the founding year is left open. The sources agree on the niche: with Toronto's Edge Interactive, Academica was one of two Canadian firms specializing in education marketing, and it claimed a twelve-year track record as North America's leader in education-sector quantitative consumer research ([Canadian Journal of Communication](https://cjc.utpjournals.press/doi/pdf/10.22230/cjc.2013v38n4a2652); [London Economic Development Corporation](https://www.ledc.com/business-directory/largest-employers?page=12)).

## The research engine: UCAS and StudentVu

The firm's best-known product is the University/College Applicant Study, which had surveyed applicants for more than nineteen years by 2016 on how they choose an institution, tracking demographics, forty-eight decision factors, thirty information sources and funding worries ([Academica Group](https://cdn.agilitycms.com/colleges-ontario/documents-library/document-files/2016%20-%20UCAS.pdf)). In the 2016 Ontario-college round, 65,804 applicants were invited through the Ontario College Application Service; 14,999 completed the survey, a 23% response rate accurate to ±0.7 points ([Academica Group](https://cdn.agilitycms.com/colleges-ontario/documents-library/document-files/2016%20-%20UCAS.pdf)). The data serves the wider sector: the Ontario Council on Articulation and Transfer used 2025 UCAS data for a student-mobility report, finding mobile applicants older, often employed full-time, and focused on career outcomes and living close to home ([Academica Group](https://academica.ca/top-ten/oncat-report-explores-differences-between-mobile-non-mobile-students/)). Alongside UCAS, the firm runs the StudentVu panel of more than 10,000 current students, who weigh in on housing, wellness, artificial intelligence and student life ([Academica Group](https://forum.academica.ca/academicaarticles)).

## The Top Ten and the firm's place in the sector

The Academica Top Ten is a daily digest of top news and opinion affecting postsecondary education in Canada, published as a free service, its story selection informed by the firm's research and consulting work and the hundreds of stories its team reviews daily ([Academica Group](https://mailchi.mp/academica/qdn8bjj2by?e=ac4ffb8485)). A biweekly Indigenous Top Ten and the Academica Careers job board extend the platform ([Academica Group](https://mailchi.mp/academica/qdn8bjj2by?e=ac4ffb8485)). In 2020 readership reached more than 28,000 higher-education professionals a day, and the firm's 2025 sector report says it connects with more than 30,000 sector members — two different measures of one reach ([eSchool News](https://www.eschoolnews.com/newsline/2020/08/19/how-our-changing-world-is-shifting-the-way-gen-z-is-influenced-by-post-secondary-marketing/); [Academica Group](https://academica.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/SRP-Final-Report-Mar-2025.pdf)). That reach turns into research: for its 2024 sector study the firm surveyed 699 decision-makers and found financial pressure the top issue, named by 85.6%, ahead of international-student enrolment at 81.0% ([Academica Group](https://academica.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/SRP-Final-Report-Mar-2025.pdf)) — and because institutions as well as applicants answer Academica's surveys, its findings circulate in boardrooms as well as newsrooms. Its topical research draws on [Colleges and Institutes Canada](/organizations/colleges-and-institutes-canada/), Polytechnics Canada, Universities Canada, Times Higher Education and University Affairs ([Academica Group](https://academica.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/SRP-Final-Report-Mar-2025.pdf)).

## FAQ

### What is Academica Group?

Academica Group is a Canadian market research and consulting firm based in London, Ontario, that works exclusively with colleges, universities and polytechnics. It runs large applicant and student surveys, advises institutions on brand and recruitment strategy, and publishes the Academica Top Ten, a daily digest of Canadian higher-education news.

### When was Academica Group founded?

Sources disagree, so no single year is asserted: London's economic development corporation dates the firm to Acumen Research Group in 1995, co-founder Ken Steele's biography says he co-founded Academica in 2003, and a peer-reviewed study says it was formed in London, Ontario in 2005. The likeliest reading is predecessor, then co-founding, then formation — but no source states that sequence.

### What is the UCAS study?

The University/College Applicant Study (UCAS™) is Academica's annual survey of people applying to Canadian postsecondary institutions, running for more than nineteen years by 2016. It tracks who applicants are, what drives their choices — program reputation mattered most to 34% in 2016 — and how they research institutions. More than 78,000 applicants took part in 2023.

### What is the Academica Top Ten?

The Academica Top Ten is a free daily digest of the news and opinion affecting postsecondary education in Canada, edited by Academica's team. Story selection draws on the firm's research and consulting work and the hundreds of stories reviewed daily. More than 28,000 higher-education professionals received it daily in 2020, and a biweekly Indigenous Top Ten covers Indigenous postsecondary news.
