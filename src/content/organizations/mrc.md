## Overview

MRC is an American independent film and television studio founded by [Modi Wiczyk](/people/modi-wiczyk/) and [Asif Satchu](/people/asif-satchu/), best known for producing *House of Cards* and *Ozark* — and, for four years, for owning the music and trade publications *Billboard* and *[The Hollywood Reporter](/organizations/the-hollywood-reporter/)* ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/guggenheim-capital-invests-200-million-house-cards-producer-media-rights-capital/); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220805005474/en/Eldridge-and-MRC-Announce-Separation-of-Media-Assets)). Between 2018 and 2022 it sat at the centre of two corporate experiments, Valence Media and the PMRC joint venture with [Penske Media Corporation](/organizations/penske-media-corporation/), before a 2022 split returned it to being a standalone studio ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/313954/thr-billboard-owner-consolidates-properties-i.html); [Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2020/09/23/pmrc-formed-to-operate-billboard-variety-hollywood-reporter-as-penske-mrc-joint-venture/)).

This profile covers that company — originally Media Rights Capital, renamed Valence Media in 2018 and back to MRC in 2020 — not the Medical Research Council or any other organisation sharing the initials ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/guggenheim-capital-invests-200-million-house-cards-producer-media-rights-capital/); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/variety-parent-penske-media-to-take-over-hollywood-reporter-billboard-in-joint-venture-with-mrc/)). It is based in the [United States](/places/united-states/), with a large office in Beverly Hills, California ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/todd-boehly-mrc-breakup-hfpa-golden-globes/)). One caution up front: sources disagree on the founding year. A contemporary 2014 trade report says Wiczyk and Satchu launched the company in 2006; reference entries now say 2004 ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/guggenheim-capital-invests-200-million-house-cards-producer-media-rights-capital/)). This profile follows the 2006 report and flags the 2004 variant rather than picking one silently.

## From Media Rights Capital to Valence Media

Wiczyk and Satchu launched MRC in 2006 backed by Goldman Sachs, AT&T, the advertising group WPP and private-equity firm ABRY Partners, building it as a co-financier and producer that kept the licences to its own films and shows ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/guggenheim-capital-invests-200-million-house-cards-producer-media-rights-capital/)). It launched a television division in 2008 and made shows for the CW, ABC and HBO, while its film work included Seth MacFarlane's *Ted* and Neill Blomkamp's *Elysium* ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/guggenheim-capital-invests-200-million-house-cards-producer-media-rights-capital/)). The model worked: in May 2014 a Guggenheim Partners-led investor group put $240 million into the company at a valuation of more than $1 billion, with Wiczyk and Satchu retaining control ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/guggenheim-capital-invests-200-million-house-cards-producer-media-rights-capital/)). The studio's defining title came earlier, in television: David Fincher began developing an American *House of Cards* in 2011, Netflix outbid HBO and AMC for it, and the first season arrived all at once in February 2013 — the first major show to run exclusively on a streaming service, earning nine Emmy nominations, a first for a streaming-only programme ([HISTORY](https://www.History.com/this-day-in-history/february-1/house-of-cards-netflix-first-original-series-starts-streaming)).

## The Valence years: Billboard, data and PMRC

On 1 February 2018 Eldridge Industries folded its media assets into MRC to form Valence Media, a new company led by Satchu and Wiczyk as co-chief executives with Todd Boehly as chairman ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/313954/thr-billboard-owner-consolidates-properties-i.html)). The combination joined the studio with the Billboard-Hollywood Reporter Media Group, Dick Clark Productions — producer of the [Billboard Music Awards](/events/billboard-music-awards/) and the annual Golden Globe Awards telecast — and a minority stake in the film distributor A24 ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/313954/thr-billboard-owner-consolidates-properties-i.html); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/356161/penske-media-mrc-to-jointly-own-magazines-includ.html)). Valence kept buying: in December 2019 it acquired Nielsen Music's insights and product suite, creating the MRC Data division and reuniting Billboard's charts with their data after a decade apart ([Music Ally](https://musically.com/2019/12/19/billboard-parent-company-buys-nielsen-music-as-battle-of-charts-heats-up/)). Then, in 2020, Valence rebranded as MRC, and on 23 September Penske Media Corporation and MRC announced PMRC, a joint venture operating *Billboard*, *The Hollywood Reporter* and *Vibe* alongside [Variety](/organizations/variety/), *[Rolling Stone](/organizations/rolling-stone/)* and *[Music Business Worldwide](/organizations/music-business-worldwide/)*, with Penske leading day-to-day operations and MRC developing film, television and live projects from its stories and intellectual property ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/variety-parent-penske-media-to-take-over-hollywood-reporter-billboard-in-joint-venture-with-mrc/); [Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2020/09/23/pmrc-formed-to-operate-billboard-variety-hollywood-reporter-as-penske-mrc-joint-venture/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/356161/penske-media-mrc-to-jointly-own-magazines-includ.html)).

## The 2022 split — and what MRC is now

The roll-up unravelled in four years. On 5 August 2022 Eldridge and MRC announced they had separated their combined media assets: MRC kept film, television and non-fiction plus stakes in Civic Center Media and T-Street, while Eldridge kept Dick Clark Productions, the PMRC interest, the Luminate data business and stakes in A24, Fulwell 73 and Sugar 23, remaining a significant minority shareholder in MRC ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220805005474/en/Eldridge-and-MRC-Announce-Separation-of-Media-Assets); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/mrc-eldridge-todd-boehly-pmrc-reorg/)). Valence was formed with an enterprise value approaching $3 billion in 2018, yet by September 2022 analysts consulted by TheWrap valued MRC below $500 million — a collapse that explains why the roll-up was unwound rather than sold at the multibillion-dollar exit its founders had planned ([WilmerHale](https://www.wilmerhale.com/api/sitecore/module/pdfgeneration?itemurl=%2fen%2finsights%2fnews%2f2018-02-08-wilmerhale-advises-on-the-formation-of-valence-media-a-new-diversified-media-company&itemid=%7bc09ff46c-546b-4a30-a6a0-849d10ac3024%7d); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/todd-boehly-mrc-breakup-hfpa-golden-globes/)). TheWrap put the Golden Globes at the centre of the break: NBC's cancellation of the 2022 telecast cost Dick Clark Productions $30 million, and Boehly — the Hollywood Foreign Press Association's interim chief executive since October 2021 — bought the association outright in July 2022 ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/todd-boehly-mrc-breakup-hfpa-golden-globes/)).

## What MRC actually makes

Strip away the corporate deals and MRC is a hit-making studio. Its television slate has run from *House of Cards* and *Ozark* to *The Great*, *The Terminal List* and *Poker Face*, and its films from *Ted* and *Baby Driver* to *Knives Out* and *Saltburn*, with its films grossing more than $6 billion at the worldwide box office ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220805005474/en/Eldridge-and-MRC-Announce-Separation-of-Media-Assets)). As of the August 2022 separation its productions had been nominated for 111 Emmy Awards, 29 Golden Globes, 12 Academy Awards and two Grammys ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220805005474/en/Eldridge-and-MRC-Announce-Separation-of-Media-Assets)). The chart connection runs the other way too: in March 2017 [Nicki Minaj](/people/nicki-minaj/) passed [Aretha Franklin](/people/aretha-franklin/) for the most Hot 100 entries by a woman, 76 to 73, with two of the three songs that carried her past the record featuring [Lil Wayne](/people/lil-wayne/) — a record on the Billboard chart, the publication that would join MRC in the Valence merger a year later ([Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2017/3/queen-of-rap-nicki-minaj-slays-a-new-record-title-466245)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What does MRC stand for?** MRC originally stood for Media Rights Capital, the name Wiczyk and Satchu gave the studio they launched in 2006 ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/guggenheim-capital-invests-200-million-house-cards-producer-media-rights-capital/)). The company later traded simply as MRC, and the initials should not be confused with the Medical Research Council or the Media Research Center, two unrelated organisations.

**Does MRC still own Billboard?** No. MRC operated Billboard from the 2018 Valence merger until the August 2022 separation, latterly through the PMRC joint venture with Penske Media Corporation ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/313954/thr-billboard-owner-consolidates-properties-i.html); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220805005474/en/Eldridge-and-MRC-Announce-Separation-of-Media-Assets)). In the split, Billboard stayed with Eldridge Industries and the PMRC venture, while MRC returned to film, television and non-fiction production ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220805005474/en/Eldridge-and-MRC-Announce-Separation-of-Media-Assets)).

**What was PMRC?** PMRC was the joint venture announced on 23 September 2020 by Penske Media Corporation and MRC ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2020/09/23/pmrc-formed-to-operate-billboard-variety-hollywood-reporter-as-penske-mrc-joint-venture/)). It operated *Billboard*, *The Hollywood Reporter* and *Vibe* alongside *Variety*, *Rolling Stone* and *Music Business Worldwide*, with Penske leading day-to-day operations ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/variety-parent-penske-media-to-take-over-hollywood-reporter-billboard-in-joint-venture-with-mrc/)). A second venture had MRC developing film, TV and live projects from the brands' stories ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/356161/penske-media-mrc-to-jointly-own-magazines-includ.html)).

**What is MRC best known for producing?** Its signature titles are the Netflix dramas *House of Cards* and *Ozark* and the films *Ted*, *Knives Out* and *Baby Driver* ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220805005474/en/Eldridge-and-MRC-Announce-Separation-of-Media-Assets)). As of August 2022 its productions had been nominated for 111 Emmy Awards, 29 Golden Globes, 12 Academy Awards and two Grammys, and its films had grossed more than $6 billion worldwide ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220805005474/en/Eldridge-and-MRC-Announce-Separation-of-Media-Assets)).

**Why did MRC and Eldridge split up?** The roll-up's economics broke down: NBC's cancellation of the 2022 Golden Globes telecast cost Dick Clark Productions $30 million, streaming deals paid producers a capped cost-plus margin of about 20%, and analysts valued MRC below $500 million against its $1 billion 2014 valuation ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/todd-boehly-mrc-breakup-hfpa-golden-globes/)). The two sides agreed to separate on 5 August 2022 ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220805005474/en/Eldridge-and-MRC-Announce-Separation-of-Media-Assets)).
