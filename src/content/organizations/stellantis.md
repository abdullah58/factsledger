## Overview

Stellantis N.V. is a multinational automotive manufacturing corporation formed on January 16, 2021, when French automaker Groupe PSA merged with Italian-American Fiat Chrysler Automobiles (FCA) in a 50-50 all-stock deal ([Stellantis](https://www.media.stellantis.com/em-en/fca-archive/press/the-merger-of-fca-and-groupe-psa-has-been-completed-1610796950); [Sullivan & Cromwell](https://www.sullcrom.com/About/News-and-Events/Highlights/2021/January/SC-Advises-Fiat-Chrysler-Automobiles-in-60-Billion-Merger-with-Peugeot)). Headquartered near Amsterdam in the Netherlands, it is the world's fourth-largest automaker by vehicles produced annually, selling cars under 14 brands — including Jeep, Ram, Peugeot, Citroën, Fiat, Opel, Alfa Romeo and Maserati — with about 250,000 employees and a presence in more than 130 countries ([Carfax](https://www.carfax.com/buying/what-is-stellantis)). In 2025 the group went through a leadership change and a painful strategic reset: founding chief executive Carlos Tavares resigned in December 2024, Antonio Filosa took over in June 2025, and the company reported a €22.3 billion net loss for 2025 after €25.4 billion of charges tied to rethinking its electric-vehicle push ([Reuters](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2025:newsml_L5N3S006I:0-jeep-maker-stellantis-names-insider-to-lead-revival/); [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1605484/000160548426000019/stellantisnvfy2025pressrel.htm)).

## How the merger created Stellantis

The deal was announced on October 31, 2019, when FCA and PSA said they intended to merge on a 50-50 basis, and shareholders approved it on January 4, 2021, with more than 99% of the votes cast in favour ([Stellantis](https://www.media.stellantis.com/em-en/fca-archive/press/the-merger-of-fca-and-groupe-psa-has-been-completed-1610796950)). The merger became effective on January 16, 2021, and the combined company's shares began trading on Euronext [Paris](/places/paris/) and [Milan](/places/milan/) on January 18 and on the [New York Stock Exchange](/organizations/new-york-stock-exchange/) on January 19, 2021, under the ticker STLA ([Sullivan & Cromwell](https://www.sullcrom.com/About/News-and-Events/Highlights/2021/January/SC-Advises-Fiat-Chrysler-Automobiles-in-60-Billion-Merger-with-Peugeot)). The name comes from the Latin verb "stello", meaning "to brighten with stars", chosen to signify two successful carmakers aligning for a new era of mobility ([Carfax](https://www.carfax.com/buying/what-is-stellantis)).

The logic was scale. Both parents faced a fundamental industry transition toward connected, autonomous, shared and electric vehicles, and consolidation was meant to fund that investment, cut redundancies and improve profitability ([Carfax](https://www.carfax.com/buying/what-is-stellantis)). Then-CEO Carlos Tavares said the merger would add about $30 billion in shareholder value and save about $6.1 billion, with 80% of the savings realised before 2025 ([Carfax](https://www.carfax.com/buying/what-is-stellantis)). The largest shareholders are the Peugeot family of France and the Agnelli family of Italy — the Agnellis through their holding company Exor — with Bpifrance, Vanguard and Amundi among the other investors ([Carfax](https://www.carfax.com/buying/what-is-stellantis); [Reuters](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2025:newsml_L5N3S006I:0-jeep-maker-stellantis-names-insider-to-lead-revival/)).

## Fourteen brands, from city cars to luxury

Stellantis sells vehicles under 14 car brands inherited from its two parents: nine from FCA and five from PSA, plus mobility, parts and commercial-vehicle arms such as Free2move, Leasys, Mopar and Fiat Professional ([Carfax](https://www.carfax.com/buying/what-is-stellantis)). The portfolio spans mass-market European cars (Peugeot, Citroën, Opel, Fiat), American trucks and SUVs (Jeep, Ram, Dodge, Chrysler), and premium and luxury marques (Alfa Romeo, Maserati, DS Automobiles, Lancia) ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1605484/000160548426000019/stellantisnvfy2025pressrel.htm)). A partnership with Chinese automaker Leapmotor, launched in 2024, added a low-cost electric-vehicle channel ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1605484/000160548425000010/stellantisnvfy2024pressrel.htm)).

The company's Dare Forward 2030 plan, unveiled on March 1, 2022, set an ambitious electrification course: more than 75 battery-electric models by 2030, 100% of passenger-car sales in Europe and 50% in the United States to be battery-electric, 5 million annual BEV sales, carbon net zero by 2038, and €300 billion of net revenues by 2030 ([CBT News](https://www.cbtnews.com/stellantis-presents-dare-forward-2030-a-decade-of-evolution/)). In early 2025 the company also partnered with Mistral AI to explore an advanced in-car assistant, and unveiled STLA AutoDrive 1.0, its first in-house automated driving system with Hands-Free and Eyes-Off (SAE Level 3) functionality ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1605484/000160548425000010/stellantisnvfy2024pressrel.htm)). In August 2026, [Cerence](/organizations/cerence/) signed a multi-brand award for its xUI in-car AI platform with Stellantis, according to Cerence's own quarterly announcement ([StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/CRNC/cerence-ai-reports-third-quarter-results-revenue-up-12-yo-y-1cj6zaaey1e3.html)).

## The 2024 slump, the 2025 reset

Stellantis's first years were profitable — 2023 net profit was €18.6 billion — but 2024 exposed the cost of the transition. Full-year 2024 net revenues fell 17% to €156.9 billion and net profit fell 70% to €5.5 billion, with consolidated shipments down 12% to 5.415 million vehicles as the company cut bloated US dealer inventories and changed over to next-generation products ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1605484/000160548425000010/stellantisnvfy2024pressrel.htm)). Tavares resigned in December 2024 after overseeing the fall in profits and sales, and chairman John Elkann steered the company while the board searched for a successor ([Reuters](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2025:newsml_L5N3S006I:0-jeep-maker-stellantis-names-insider-to-lead-revival/)).

On May 28, 2025, the board unanimously named Antonio Filosa — the North American chief and former Jeep chief, who had joined Fiat as a trainee in 1999 — as chief executive, effective June 23, 2025 ([Reuters](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2025:newsml_L5N3S006I:0-jeep-maker-stellantis-names-insider-to-lead-revival/)). Filosa's first full year brought a decisive reset: on February 6, 2026, Stellantis announced a major business reset, and full-year 2025 results showed net revenues of €153.5 billion, down 2%, but a net loss of €22.3 billion driven by €25.4 billion of charges, mostly from resetting the product plan and EV supply chain, revising warranty provisions and workforce reductions in Europe ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1605484/000160548426000019/stellantisnvfy2025pressrel.htm)). The board suspended the 2026 dividend and authorised up to €5 billion of hybrid bonds to preserve the balance sheet ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1605484/000160548426000019/stellantisnvfy2025pressrel.htm)).

Filosa attributed the loss to "over-estimating the pace of the energy transition" and said the reset would put customer choice — electric, hybrid and internal-combustion — back at the heart of the company's plans ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1605484/000160548426000019/stellantisnvfy2025pressrel.htm)). There were early signs of stabilisation: second-half 2025 net revenues rose 10% year over year and consolidated shipments rose 11%, with North America up 39% as inventory dynamics normalised ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1605484/000160548426000019/stellantisnvfy2025pressrel.htm)). New models anchoring the recovery include the Jeep Cherokee and Dodge Charger SIXPACK in North America, the Ram 1500 HEMI V8, the Ram Dakota pickup in South America, and the Citroën C5 Aircross BEV, Jeep Compass BEV and Fiat 500 Hybrid in Europe ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1605484/000160548426000019/stellantisnvfy2025pressrel.htm)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What brands does Stellantis own?** Stellantis owns 14 car brands: Abarth, Alfa Romeo, Chrysler, Citroën, Dodge, DS Automobiles, Fiat, Jeep, Lancia, Maserati, Opel, Peugeot, Ram and Vauxhall, plus mobility and parts brands including Free2move, Leasys and Mopar ([Carfax](https://www.carfax.com/buying/what-is-stellantis)).

**Where is Stellantis headquartered?** Its global headquarters is near Amsterdam in the Netherlands, with operational headquarters in North America, Italy and France and a commercial presence in more than 130 countries ([Carfax](https://www.carfax.com/buying/what-is-stellantis)).

**Who is the CEO of Stellantis?** Antonio Filosa has been chief executive since June 23, 2025, succeeding founding CEO Carlos Tavares, who resigned in December 2024; chairman John Elkann led the company in the interim ([Reuters](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2025:newsml_L5N3S006I:0-jeep-maker-stellantis-names-insider-to-lead-revival/)).

**Is Stellantis profitable?** Not in 2025: it reported a net loss of €22.3 billion on net revenues of €153.5 billion, after €25.4 billion of charges from a strategic reset, though second-half revenues rose 10% year over year ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1605484/000160548426000019/stellantisnvfy2025pressrel.htm)).

**What does the name Stellantis mean?** It comes from the Latin verb "stello", meaning "to brighten with stars", and was chosen to signify two successful carmakers aligning for the next era of mobility ([Carfax](https://www.carfax.com/buying/what-is-stellantis)).
