## Overview

In May 2023 CNN aired a live town hall with Donald Trump in which the former president repeated false claims about the 2020 election, said he would pardon supporters convicted over the January 6, 2021 attack on the U.S. Capitol, and called moderator Kaitlan Collins a "nasty person" while a pro-Trump audience cheered ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2023/6/7/cnns-embattled-ceo-chris-licht-is-out)). Days later The Atlantic published a lengthy, critical profile of CNN chief Chris Licht headlined "Inside the Tremendous Meltdown at CNN" — described as embarrassing and likely the piece that sealed his fate — and Warner Bros. Discovery fired him on the network's morning editorial call, effective immediately ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2023/6/7/cnns-embattled-ceo-chris-licht-is-out)).

That four-week stretch tells you more about CNN's modern life than the origin story most profiles repeat. CNN is the American cable news network Ted Turner co-founded with Reese Schonfeld in Atlanta on June 1, 1980 — the first U.S. television channel to run news around the clock ([Gray News](https://gray-whns-prod.gtv-cdn.com/2026/05/06/cnn-founder-ted-turner-dies-87-2/); [NPR](https://www.nprillinois.org/2026-05-06/a-trailblazer-a-rabble-rouser-a-do-gooder-cnn-founder-ted-turner-dies-at-87)). It made its name with live coverage of the 1986 Challenger disaster and, five years later, with live reports from Baghdad during the Gulf War that turned it into the default channel for breaking news ([NPR](https://www.nprillinois.org/2026-05-06/a-trailblazer-a-rabble-rouser-a-do-gooder-cnn-founder-ted-turner-dies-at-87)).

As of September 30, 2026, CNN belongs to Warner Bros. Discovery — it still sat in the company's brand portfolio in its second-quarter 2026 results ([Warner Bros. Discovery](https://www.wbd.com/news/warner-bros-discovery-reports-second-quarter-2026-results)). Paramount Skydance's roughly $110 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery was reported in September 2026 as on track to close "in the coming weeks," but it had not closed, so CNN's owner on that date was still Warner Bros. Discovery ([Reason](https://reason.com/2026/09/22/paramount-bends-the-knee-to-rob-bonta); [New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/23/media/cnn-ceo-mark-thompson-wants-to-stay-at-network-post-paramount-merger-and-be-useful-to-new-bosses/)). The network is run by chair and CEO [Mark Thompson](/people/mark-thompson/), the former BBC director-general, who took over in October 2023 ([Associated Press](https://www.newslooks.com/cnn-names-mark-thompson-as-its-new-chair-and-ceo/)).

CNN's ratings in 2026 tell a two-part story: the audience is growing again, but the gap with the leader is still wide. In August 2026 it averaged 624,000 prime-time viewers — up 45.1% from a year earlier — while [Fox News](/organizations/fox-news/) led the category by a distance ([Barrett Media](https://barrettmedia.com/2026/09/02/cnn-sees-45-increase-as-fox-news-leads-august-cable-news-ratings/)).

## The 24-hour gamble that worked

In 1980 the idea sounded absurd: a television channel that did nothing but news, all day and night. Ted Turner launched it anyway on June 1, 1980 — co-founding CNN with Reese Schonfeld — from a converted country club in Atlanta, creating America's first continuous all-news television station ([Gray News](https://gray-whns-prod.gtv-cdn.com/2026/05/06/cnn-founder-ted-turner-dies-87-2/); [NPR](https://www.nprillinois.org/2026-05-06/a-trailblazer-a-rabble-rouser-a-do-gooder-cnn-founder-ted-turner-dies-at-87)).

Two moments made the gamble pay off. When the Challenger shuttle broke apart live on television in 1986, CNN's continuous coverage gave it national prominence for the first time ([NPR](https://www.nprillinois.org/2026-05-06/a-trailblazer-a-rabble-rouser-a-do-gooder-cnn-founder-ted-turner-dies-at-87)). Five years later, its reporters stayed on the air from Baghdad as the Gulf War began, and CNN became the channel the whole world turned to when something big happened ([NPR](https://www.nprillinois.org/2026-05-06/a-trailblazer-a-rabble-rouser-a-do-gooder-cnn-founder-ted-turner-dies-at-87)). Turner himself died on May 6, 2026, at 87 ([Gray News](https://gray-whns-prod.gtv-cdn.com/2026/05/06/cnn-founder-ted-turner-dies-87-2/)).

## Passed from owner to owner

CNN has changed corporate hands three times since Turner sold it. In 1996 Turner sold Turner Broadcasting — CNN included — to Time Warner for about $7.34 billion ([NPR](https://www.nprillinois.org/2026-05-06/a-trailblazer-a-rabble-rouser-a-do-gooder-cnn-founder-ted-turner-dies-at-87)). In June 2018 AT&T completed its acquisition of Time Warner, a deal worth more than $80 billion, and renamed the company WarnerMedia ([Indian Retailer](https://www.indianretailer.com/brandlicense/archives/news/att-completes-time-warner-acquisition-adds-warner-bros-cnn-hbo-its-portfolio)). Then in April 2022 WarnerMedia combined with Discovery to form Warner Bros. Discovery, CNN's current owner ([Associated Press](https://tvnewscheck.com/digital/article/cnn-shutting-down-a-month-after-launch/)).

A fourth change may be coming. In September 2026 Paramount Skydance settled antitrust suits with 12 state attorneys general — California plus 11 other states — over its roughly $110 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery, and the takeover was reported on track to close "in the coming weeks" ([Reason](https://reason.com/2026/09/22/paramount-bends-the-knee-to-rob-bonta); [New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/23/media/cnn-ceo-mark-thompson-wants-to-stay-at-network-post-paramount-merger-and-be-useful-to-new-bosses/)). Paramount reportedly expects at least $6 billion in cost savings from the merger, which implies several thousand jobs going away ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/23/media/cnn-ceo-mark-thompson-wants-to-stay-at-network-post-paramount-merger-and-be-useful-to-new-bosses/)). None of it had closed as of September 30, 2026.

## Three chief executives — and the 2023 meltdown

CNN's leadership churned hard in 2022 and 2023. Jeff Zucker was pushed out in February 2022 over an undisclosed relationship with a fellow executive ([Associated Press](https://www.newslooks.com/cnn-names-mark-thompson-as-its-new-chair-and-ceo/)). His replacement, Chris Licht, arrived with a mandate to pull the network toward the political center and win back Republican viewers — and lasted about 13 months before being fired in June 2023 ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2023/6/7/cnns-embattled-ceo-chris-licht-is-out)).

Two episodes ended him. On May 10, 2023, the network broadcast a live town hall with Donald Trump, moderated by Kaitlan Collins. Trump repeated false claims about the 2020 election, said he would pardon many supporters convicted over the January 6, 2021 Capitol attack, and called Collins a "nasty person" as a pro-Trump audience cheered ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2023/6/7/cnns-embattled-ceo-chris-licht-is-out)). The broadcast drew wide criticism, including from CNN's own journalists. The network's morning-show revamp also imploded around the same time, with presenter Don Lemon fired ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2023/6/7/cnns-embattled-ceo-chris-licht-is-out)).

The final blow was editorial, not ratings. The Atlantic published a lengthy, critical profile of Licht headlined "Inside the Tremendous Meltdown at CNN" — described as embarrassing and likely the piece that sealed his fate ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2023/6/7/cnns-embattled-ceo-chris-licht-is-out)). Two days earlier Licht had promised on the morning editorial call that he would fight to regain the trust of CNN's employees; on that same call, Warner Bros. Discovery chief David Zaslav fired him, effective immediately, and installed a four-person interim team — Amy Entelis, Virginia Moseley, Eric Sherling and David Leavy ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2023/6/7/cnns-embattled-ceo-chris-licht-is-out)). That May, CNN's prime-time audience had been less than half of rival MSNBC's, with Fox News still leading the category ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2023/6/7/cnns-embattled-ceo-chris-licht-is-out)).

The board then reached outside the American cable-news world. On August 30, 2023, CNN appointed Mark Thompson as chair and CEO — the man who ran the BBC as director-general from 2004 to 2012 and then the New York Times as president and CEO — and he started on October 9, 2023 ([Associated Press](https://www.newslooks.com/cnn-names-mark-thompson-as-its-new-chair-and-ceo/)). Thompson was still in the job as of September 23, 2026, when he told staff at a quarterly town hall that he wanted to stay on and be "useful to new bosses" after the Paramount takeover ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/23/media/cnn-ceo-mark-thompson-wants-to-stay-at-network-post-paramount-merger-and-be-useful-to-new-bosses/)).

## The CNN+ flameout

One of the shortest-lived experiments in streaming history happened at CNN in the spring of 2022. CNN+ launched on March 29, 2022, backed by about $100 million in development spending and roughly 500 assigned employees — and shut down at the end of April, less than a month later ([Associated Press](https://tvnewscheck.com/digital/article/cnn-shutting-down-a-month-after-launch/)).

Part of the problem was structural: contracts with cable and satellite companies meant CNN could not put its live TV network on the streaming service, so subscribers were paying for a product without the thing CNN is known for ([Associated Press](https://tvnewscheck.com/digital/article/cnn-shutting-down-a-month-after-launch/)). The shutdown landed right as WarnerMedia was combining with Discovery, and the new leadership pulled the plug rather than keep funding it. CNN's news programming has since been folded into the Max streaming service instead ([Associated Press](https://www.newslooks.com/cnn-names-mark-thompson-as-its-new-chair-and-ceo/)).

## Where CNN stands in 2026

The ratings are moving in the right direction, just from a low base. August 2026 brought 624,000 prime-time viewers (up 45.1% year over year) and 437,000 total-day viewers (up 31.6%) ([Barrett Media](https://barrettmedia.com/2026/09/02/cnn-sees-45-increase-as-fox-news-leads-august-cable-news-ratings/)). Its top program that month was NewsNight with Abby Phillip, averaging 725,000 viewers — 24th overall among cable programs, with Fox News sweeping the top six slots ([Barrett Media](https://barrettmedia.com/2026/09/02/cnn-sees-45-increase-as-fox-news-leads-august-cable-news-ratings/)). In the week of September 7, 2026, Nielsen's big-data-plus-panel figures had CNN at 490,000 prime-time viewers and 77,000 adults 25–54 — fifth among all cable networks in prime time, fourth in total day, with Fox News leading every category and MS NOW (the former MSNBC) second ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/tvnewser/week-of-sept-7-2026-cable-news-ratings/)).

The business underneath has shrunk since the Trump-era ratings peak: CNN earned $892 million in profit in 2022, down from $1.08 billion in 2020, according to S&P Global Market Intelligence figures cited by the AP ([Associated Press](https://www.newslooks.com/cnn-names-mark-thompson-as-its-new-chair-and-ceo/)). And September 2026 brought a fight with the White House: CNN joined MS NOW and Politico in suing the Trump administration after press credentials were revoked and reporters were banned from White House grounds ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/23/media/cnn-ceo-mark-thompson-wants-to-stay-at-network-post-paramount-merger-and-be-useful-to-new-bosses/)).
