## Overview

Georgian is a [Toronto](/places/toronto/) growth-equity firm that invests in high-growth B2B software companies and helps them deploy artificial intelligence — not just with capital, but with an in-house AI Lab of engineers who write code inside its portfolio companies ([Georgian](https://georgian.io/)). Founded in 2008 by three software entrepreneurs, it manages $5.8 billion as of December 31, 2025, has made 87 investments with 51 companies currently in its portfolio, and is investing from its sixth flagship Growth Fund ([Georgian](https://georgian.io/for-investors)). Its best-known early bets were [Shopify](/organizations/shopify/) and [ClickUp](/organizations/clickup/), and its current focus is the AI stack: applied and agentic AI, conversational AI, trust and cybersecurity, and — since 2026 — physical AI and defence technology.

The firm traded for most of its life as Georgian Partners. It now brands itself simply as Georgian — its website is georgian.io and its recent releases all use the short name — but the legal and advisory entity behind the funds is still Georgian Partners Growth LP, as the source line on its own March 2026 announcement shows ([CNW](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/georgian-secures-100m-strategic-investment-from-navigator-global-839866105.html)). The press has been slow to follow: The Globe and Mail still called it "Georgian Partners" in 2026 coverage of its Dominion Dynamics investment ([Stockwatch](https://www.stockwatch.com/News/Item/Z-C!RY-3837250/C/RY)). This profile uses the current brand, Georgian, and notes the former name where the older sources use it.

## Founding and the applied-analytics thesis

Georgian was founded in 2008 by three software entrepreneurs — Justin LaFayette, Simon Chong, and John Berton — with two stated goals: to empower founders with the kind of support they wished they had during their own journeys, and to harness technology to help portfolio companies innovate ([Georgian](https://georgian.io/for-investors); [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkcxuk8k1g)). All three still sit at the top of the firm: SEC filings for its fifth fund name Berton, Chong and LaFayette as directors of the general partner, and the three founders were the sellers in the 2026 Navigator Global transaction ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1788761/000178898220000001/xslFormDX01/primary_doc.xml); [CNW](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/georgian-secures-100m-strategic-investment-from-navigator-global-839866105.html)). The firm is based in Toronto, at 2 St Clair Ave West, and invests primarily in the [United States](/places/united-states/), [Canada](/places/canada/), and EMEA ([Georgian](https://georgian.io/); [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkcxuk8k1g)).

The investment idea has stayed remarkably stable while its vocabulary changed. In 2014, lead investor Steve Leightell described Georgian as a "thesis-driven" growth-equity firm backing expansion-stage software companies exploiting "Applied Analytics" — the convergence of cloud-based business solutions, data, and analytic tools that turn data into insight ([FinSMEs](https://www.finsmes.com/2014/05/georgian-partners-interview-with-steve-leightell.html)). The firm looked for companies already generating about $500,000 or more in monthly recurring revenue, and its pitch to founders was entrepreneur-led help with analytics across business development, marketing, SaaS metrics, technology architecture, strategy, and sales, plus alliances, partnerships, and M&A ([FinSMEs](https://www.finsmes.com/2014/05/georgian-partners-interview-with-steve-leightell.html)). A decade later the thesis areas are Applied AI (including generative and agentic AI, plus traditional machine learning such as anomaly detection), Conversational AI (including voice AI), and Trust, which the firm treats as a strategic differentiator for software companies ([CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkcxuk8k1g)). Its own map of the AI stack runs from Applied Analytics through Applied AI, Security, Conversational AI, Gen AI and Agentic AI to Physical AI and Quantum Computing ([Georgian](https://georgian.io/for-investors)).

## The funds

Georgian runs two fund families: flagship Growth Funds that make new investments, and Alignment Funds — large special-purpose vehicles that double down on existing portfolio companies. The sequence that can be verified from sources read in full is:

| Fund | Size | What is verified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Growth Fund V | $850 million | SEC Form D/A: offering fully sold, first sale September 27, 2019, 120 investors |
| Alignment Fund I | $1.02 billion | Closed late March 2021; invested in Top Hat, Tealium, WorkFusion, and IEX Group |
| Growth Fund VI | Not verified | The firm's sixth flagship Growth Fund; deploying since at least 2025 |
| Alignment Fund II | Not verified | Deploying alongside Growth Fund VI as of February 2025 |

Growth Fund V is documented precisely because it was filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission: Georgian Partners Growth Fund V, LP and its international parallel fund offered $850 million of pooled fund interests and sold all $850 million, with 120 investors, in an offering whose first sale was September 27, 2019 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1788761/000178898220000001/xslFormDX01/primary_doc.xml)). Alignment Fund I, by contrast, is known through press reporting: BetaKit and The Globe and Mail reported it closed in late March 2021 at $1.02 billion, raised to invest in four existing portfolio companies — Toronto edtech company Top Hat, U.S. customer-data company Tealium, New York's WorkFusion, and stock exchange IEX Group — with $130 million of it committed to Top Hat in February 2021 ([Sophic Capital](https://sophiccapital.com/april-18-2021canadian-capital-markets-excited-for-a-couple-ipos/)). Georgian was a longtime Top Hat investor, having co-led its $55 million Series D in February 2020 with Inovia Capital ([Sophic Capital](https://sophiccapital.com/april-18-2021canadian-capital-markets-excited-for-a-couple-ipos/)). By February 2025 the firm was deploying Growth Fund VI and Alignment Fund II, with five active Growth funds behind it ([CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkcxuk8k1g)). The sizes of Growth Funds I–IV and of Fund VI appear only in directories and were not verified from a fully-read source, so this profile does not state them.

## The AI Lab: capital plus code

What separates Georgian from a conventional growth-equity firm is its AI Lab — an in-house team of 20-plus machine-learning professionals, full-stack engineers, data scientists, and researchers who work directly with portfolio companies' R&D and product teams ([CNW](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/georgian-secures-100m-strategic-investment-from-navigator-global-839866105.html); [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkcxuk8k1g)). The Lab conducts technical diligence before investment, then embeds inside portfolio engineering teams after it — running secondments, hackathons, and advisory engagements — and shares what it learns through open-source toolkits and a "Transferred Learnings" community of more than 1,000 AI scientists and engineers ([CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkcxuk8k1g)). The firm says the Lab is its technical edge across the whole investment lifecycle, "from sourcing and underwriting to scaling AI-native and business software platforms" ([CNW](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/georgian-secures-100m-strategic-investment-from-navigator-global-839866105.html)).

The model is deliberately concentrated to make that hands-on work possible: about six new investments a year, initial cheques targeting $25 million-plus with follow-on potential, typically leading Series B, C, or D rounds with $20–75 million cheques into companies doing $6–50 million in annual recurring revenue ([Georgian](https://georgian.io/for-investors); [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkcxuk8k1g)). The firm engages CEOs and technical founders three to six months before an investment, scoping projects and solving technical problems ahead of a term sheet — a courtship it credits with winning priority access to deals ([Georgian](https://georgian.io/for-investors)). Its customer-success and community teams then support portfolio companies on go-to-market, partnerships, M&A, and exit planning ([CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkcxuk8k1g)).

The clearest public example is ClickUp. Georgian participated in the productivity company's $35 million Series A in June 2020, and ClickUp's own announcement singled the firm out: "Many firms claimed to be avid ClickUp users, but no one stacked up to Georgian," praising its machine-learning reputation and in-house engineering team ([ClickUp](https://clickup.com/blog/clickup-raises-35m-series-a/)). Georgian then led ClickUp's $100 million Series B that December, which valued the company at $1 billion ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/12/15/clickup-hits-1-billion-valuation-in-100m-series-b-raise/)). When ClickUp raised its $400 million Series C at a $4 billion valuation in October 2021, TechCrunch named Georgian among its earlier backers ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2224795)). On Georgian's own site, ClickUp founder-CEO [Zeb Evans](/people/zeb-evans/) says the AI Lab "did more than just advise; they embedded an engineer with technical skills we lacked internally. Georgian's support accelerated our launch timing for ClickUp Brain" ([Georgian](https://georgian.io/)).

## The portfolio

Georgian's portfolio spine runs from its early Canadian bets to a cybersecurity cluster and, most recently, defence technology. The names below are all verified in sources read in full:

| Company | Sector | What is verified about Georgian's involvement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shopify | E-commerce software | In the portfolio by 2014; cited as an "analytically optimized company" using data-driven insights |
| Top Hat | Education technology | Longtime holding; $55M Series D co-led in 2020; $130M from Alignment Fund I in 2021 |
| ClickUp | Workplace productivity | Series A participant (2020); earlier backer at the $400M Series C (2021); AI Lab embedded an engineer for ClickUp Brain |
| Tealium | Customer data | Target of Alignment Fund I (2021) |
| WorkFusion | Automation / AI | Target of Alignment Fund I (2021) |
| IEX Group | Stock exchange | Target of Alignment Fund I (2021) |
| Armis, Cyera, Island | Cybersecurity | Lead investor Steve Leightell's Israeli portfolio; among 8 Israeli-based companies backed |
| Siemplify, NoName, RiskIQ | Cybersecurity | Named as successful Israeli investments and exits |
| Dominion Dynamics | Defence technology | Led its $139M Series A (2026), the largest Series A by a Canadian defence startup |

The early portfolio shows the thesis working before it had an AI name. In 2014 the firm's holdings already included Shopify, Top Hat, Razorsight, Kony Solutions, and DataCandy, and Leightell pointed to Shopify as a company using data-driven insights to win in retail platforms, and to Top Hat as one helping professors understand students to drive better outcomes ([FinSMEs](https://www.finsmes.com/2014/05/georgian-partners-interview-with-steve-leightell.html)). Cybersecurity became a second spine: the firm has backed 41st Parameter (exited), Armis, Cyera, DefenseStorm, Devo, eSentire, Flashpoint, Island, NoName (exited), RiskIQ (exited), Siemplify (exited), and Trusona, and runs an in-house Cybersecurity Centre of Excellence ([CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkcxuk8k1g)). Eight of its companies are based in Israel, and Leightell — with Georgian since inception — led five of those investments, including Siemplify, NoName, Armis, Island, and Cyera ([CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkcxuk8k1g)).

The newest direction is physical. In 2026, Dominion Dynamics — a Canadian defence-technology startup founded in June 2025 — raised $139 million in Series A funding led by Georgian, bringing its total raised to $169 million; its CEO called it the largest Series A early-stage venture financing ever by a Canadian defence startup ([Stockwatch](https://www.stockwatch.com/News/Item/Z-C!RY-3837250/C/RY)).

## The Navigator stake

On March 30, 2026, Georgian announced a $100 million strategic investment from Navigator Global Investments (ASX: NGI), an Australian-listed alternative asset manager ([CNW](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/georgian-secures-100m-strategic-investment-from-navigator-global-839866105.html)). Navigator acquired a 4.5% passive, indirect equity stake plus a preferred economic interest in the founders' holding vehicles — $5 million at closing on March 28, 2026, and $95 million deferred over three years — with no proceeds distributed to founders as secondary liquidity and the vast majority earmarked for future funds ([CNW](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/georgian-secures-100m-strategic-investment-from-navigator-global-839866105.html)). Georgian retains full operational and investment independence with no change to strategy, and Goldman Sachs advised the firm on the deal ([CNW](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/georgian-secures-100m-strategic-investment-from-navigator-global-839866105.html)). Navigator's chief investment officer said the in-house AI Lab was central to the decision: "We believe Georgian is building something genuinely different. … The in-house AI Lab fits squarely with our strategy" ([CNW](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/georgian-secures-100m-strategic-investment-from-navigator-global-839866105.html)). Co-founder John Berton framed the AI wave as "a generational opportunity for investment" that the partnership would help Georgian capitalise on alongside its limited partners ([CNW](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/georgian-secures-100m-strategic-investment-from-navigator-global-839866105.html)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Georgian?** It is a Toronto growth-equity firm founded in 2008 that invests in high-growth B2B software companies, distinguished by an in-house AI Lab of 20-plus engineers and scientists who write code with portfolio companies ([Georgian](https://georgian.io/); [CNW](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/georgian-secures-100m-strategic-investment-from-navigator-global-839866105.html)).

**Is Georgian the same as Georgian Partners?** Yes — Georgian Partners was the firm's former brand name; it now trades simply as Georgian, while the legal/adviser entity remains Georgian Partners Growth LP ([CNW](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/georgian-secures-100m-strategic-investment-from-navigator-global-839866105.html)).

**How big is Georgian?** It manages $5.8 billion as of December 31, 2025, has made 87 investments, holds 51 companies in its current portfolio, and counts 34 full or partial exits ([Georgian](https://georgian.io/)). A March 2026 release gave a slightly different figure, $5.9 billion — see the open items.

**What does Georgian invest in?** High-growth B2B software across the AI stack — applied and agentic AI, conversational AI, trust and cybersecurity, developer tools — typically leading Series B, C, or D rounds with $20–75 million cheques, about six new investments a year ([CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rkcxuk8k1g); [Georgian](https://georgian.io/for-investors)).

**What are Georgian's best-known investments?** Shopify and Top Hat from its early portfolio, ClickUp (Series A 2020, earlier backer at the $400 million Series C in 2021), a cybersecurity cluster including Armis, Cyera, and Island, and Dominion Dynamics' $139 million Series A in 2026 ([FinSMEs](https://www.finsmes.com/2014/05/georgian-partners-interview-with-steve-leightell.html); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2224795); [Stockwatch](https://www.stockwatch.com/News/Item/Z-C!RY-3837250/C/RY)).
