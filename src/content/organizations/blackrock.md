## Overview

BlackRock, Inc. is the world's largest asset manager, a [New York City](/places/new-york-city/)-based firm founded in 1988 that managed $14.04 trillion for clients at the end of 2025 ([Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/news/marketwatch/20260115222/blackrock-assets-surge-to-14-trillion-record-as-it-looks-to-private-markets)). It runs the iShares exchange-traded fund franchise, the world's largest, and the [Aladdin](/creative-works/aladdin/) investment technology platform, and it acts as a fiduciary — the money it manages belongs to its clients, not to itself ([BlackRock](https://careers.blackrock.com/who-we-are)). Half of the money it manages is for retirement ([BlackRock](https://careers.blackrock.com/who-we-are)). Its clients range from large institutions such as charities, family offices and pension plans to millions of individual savers ([BlackRock](https://careers.blackrock.com/who-we-are)).

BlackRock's scale is the story: $14.04 trillion at the end of 2025, up from $11.55 trillion a year earlier, after a record $698 billion of net inflows in the year, including $342 billion in the fourth quarter alone ([BlackRock](https://files.quartr.com/reports/f2919-2026-01-15-11-13-37.pdf?ref=U3RvY2tBbmFseXNpcw%3D%3D)). Full-year revenue reached $24.2 billion, up 19% ([Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/news/marketwatch/20260115222/blackrock-assets-surge-to-14-trillion-record-as-it-looks-to-private-markets)). That scale makes BlackRock's flows a market event in themselves: when the world's largest manager gathers nearly $700 billion in a single year, the direction of its clients' money moves prices across equities, bonds and private markets alike.

## Built on risk: the first two decades

BlackRock was founded in 1988 by eight colleagues "with a single goal — to provide a more thoughtful, analytical approach to investing that could deliver better returns for investors" ([BlackRock](https://careers.blackrock.com/who-we-are)). The firm began by building software that could analyse a portfolio and assess thousands of risk factors — a system called Aladdin ([BlackRock](https://careers.blackrock.com/who-we-are)). In 1999 BlackRock began offering Aladdin to outside clients, and the following year it founded BlackRock Solutions, with Aladdin as its basis, marking the start of its business as a technology provider ([Vault](https://legacy.vault.com/company-profiles/investment-management/blackrock)). Growth then came by acquisition: BlackRock bought Merrill Lynch Investment Managers for $9.6 billion in 2006 ([Financial News](https://charlesskorina.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Inside-BlackRocks-Once-in-a-Lifetime-Deal-with-BGI.pdf)).

The transformational deal was Barclays Global Investors. Announced in June 2009, BlackRock agreed to buy the entire BGI business for $13.5 billion, a figure that rose to $15.2 billion by the time the deal completed that December ([Financial News](https://charlesskorina.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Inside-BlackRocks-Once-in-a-Lifetime-Deal-with-BGI.pdf)). Barclays had earlier accepted a $4.4 billion offer from buyout firm CVC for the iShares ETF division alone, and CVC walked away with a $175 million break fee ([Financial News](https://charlesskorina.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Inside-BlackRocks-Once-in-a-Lifetime-Deal-with-BGI.pdf)). The purchase made BlackRock the world's largest fund manager overnight, with combined assets above $2.7 trillion at announcement ([Financial News](https://charlesskorina.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Inside-BlackRocks-Once-in-a-Lifetime-Deal-with-BGI.pdf)).

## iShares and Aladdin: the two engines

The BGI deal gave BlackRock two engines that still drive it. The first is iShares: the ETF franchise held about $300 billion in assets when the deal completed and had swelled to almost $2 trillion within a decade ([Financial News](https://charlesskorina.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Inside-BlackRocks-Once-in-a-Lifetime-Deal-with-BGI.pdf)). By the end of 2025, BlackRock's ETF assets stood at $5.47 trillion after a record $527 billion of ETF net inflows in the year ([BlackRock](https://files.quartr.com/reports/f2919-2026-01-15-11-13-37.pdf?ref=U3RvY2tBbmFseXNpcw%3D%3D)). The second engine is Aladdin, the platform first built to track BlackRock's own financial risk and now sold to wealth managers and other clients; BlackRock earned more than $1 billion in technology services revenue from it in 2020 ([Bloomberg](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/crypto/blackrock-joins-blockchain-platform-axoni-for-equity-swap-trades)).

Aladdin's reach extends well beyond BlackRock itself: by 2019 it was used by almost 90 asset managers worldwide ([Financial News](https://charlesskorina.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Inside-BlackRocks-Once-in-a-Lifetime-Deal-with-BGI.pdf)). That reach explains BlackRock's 2021 move onto [Axoni](/organizations/axoni/)'s Veris network for equity swaps: the firm integrated Axoni's software with its Aladdin operating system to match and reconcile post-trade data on stock swaps, joining [Goldman Sachs](/organizations/goldman-sachs/) and [Citigroup](/organizations/citigroup/) on the network ([Bloomberg](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/crypto/blackrock-joins-blockchain-platform-axoni-for-equity-swap-trades)). The market was about $3.6 trillion in equity-linked forwards and swaps, according to Bank for International Settlements data ([Bloomberg](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/crypto/blackrock-joins-blockchain-platform-axoni-for-equity-swap-trades)).

## The private-markets turn

BlackRock's latest transformation is into private markets. The firm has acquired infrastructure manager Global Infrastructure Partners, private-credit manager HPS Investment Partners and alternatives-data provider Preqin, and chairman and chief executive Laurence Fink says 2026 "will be our first full year as a unified platform with GIP, HPS and Preqin" ([BlackRock](https://files.quartr.com/reports/f2919-2026-01-15-11-13-37.pdf?ref=U3RvY2tBbmFseXNpcw%3D%3D); [Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/news/marketwatch/20260115222/blackrock-assets-surge-to-14-trillion-record-as-it-looks-to-private-markets)). The goal is $400 billion in private-markets fundraising by 2030 ([BlackRock](https://files.quartr.com/reports/f2919-2026-01-15-11-13-37.pdf?ref=U3RvY2tBbmFseXNpcw%3D%3D)). Fink says BlackRock enters 2026 "with accelerating momentum across our entire platform", and the pipeline spans public and private mandates, technology and data ([Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/news/marketwatch/20260115222/blackrock-assets-surge-to-14-trillion-record-as-it-looks-to-private-markets)).

The 2025 results show why the firm can fund that push: revenue of $24.2 billion, up 19%, and record inflows across nearly every product, with digital-asset products reaching $78 billion under management ([BlackRock](https://files.quartr.com/reports/f2919-2026-01-15-11-13-37.pdf?ref=U3RvY2tBbmFseXNpcw%3D%3D)). BlackRock also returned $5 billion to shareholders in 2025 and raised its quarterly dividend 10% to $5.73 per share ([BlackRock](https://files.quartr.com/reports/f2919-2026-01-15-11-13-37.pdf?ref=U3RvY2tBbmFseXNpcw%3D%3D)). The firm that began as an eight-person risk shop in 1988 now manages more money than any other — and is using that scale to buy its way into the parts of finance its index funds never reached.

## Frequently asked questions

**How big is BlackRock?**
BlackRock managed $14.04 trillion at 31 December 2025, up from $11.55 trillion a year earlier, after record net inflows of $698 billion in 2025 — the largest asset manager in the world by a wide margin. Its ETF arm alone held $5.47 trillion ([BlackRock](https://files.quartr.com/reports/f2919-2026-01-15-11-13-37.pdf?ref=U3RvY2tBbmFseXNpcw%3D%3D)).

**What is Aladdin?**
Aladdin is BlackRock's investment technology platform, first built to track its own financial risk and analyse portfolios across thousands of risk factors; it has been sold to outside clients since 1999 and was used by almost 90 asset managers worldwide by 2019 ([BlackRock](https://careers.blackrock.com/who-we-are); [Financial News](https://charlesskorina.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Inside-BlackRocks-Once-in-a-Lifetime-Deal-with-BGI.pdf)).

**What is iShares?**
iShares is BlackRock's exchange-traded fund business, acquired with Barclays Global Investors in 2009; it held about $300 billion then and $5.47 trillion at the end of 2025, after a record $527 billion of inflows in the year ([Financial News](https://charlesskorina.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Inside-BlackRocks-Once-in-a-Lifetime-Deal-with-BGI.pdf); [BlackRock](https://files.quartr.com/reports/f2919-2026-01-15-11-13-37.pdf?ref=U3RvY2tBbmFseXNpcw%3D%3D)).

**Who founded BlackRock?**
BlackRock was founded in 1988 in New York by eight colleagues; its chairman and chief executive is Laurence D. Fink ([BlackRock](https://careers.blackrock.com/who-we-are); [Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/news/marketwatch/20260115222/blackrock-assets-surge-to-14-trillion-record-as-it-looks-to-private-markets)). This profile does not name all eight founders, as no fully-read tier1 or tier2 source names them all.
