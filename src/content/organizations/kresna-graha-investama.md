## Overview

Kresna Graha Investama is the Indonesian investment group that lived two lives: first as a traditional brokerage that reinvented itself into a Forbes-blessed digital-investment story, then as the holding company at the centre of one of Indonesia's largest insurance-default scandals. Founded in Jakarta in 1999 by Michael Steven and listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange under the ticker KREN, it spent two decades as an investment bank offering investment management, securities brokerage and underwriting ([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/special/digital-fortune-the-journey-of-kresna-graha-investama/54927/1); [IDNFinancials](https://www.idnfinancials.com/kren/pt-quantum-clovera-investama-tbk)). In 2015 Steven launched Kresna Creativentures, a tech incubator and investment division that became the group's growth driver — and by 2018, with revenue up 2.5 times to Rp7.2 trillion ($508 million), the company had catapulted onto *Forbes Asia*'s Best Under A Billion list ([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/special/digital-fortune-the-journey-of-kresna-graha-investama/54927/1)). Within five years, the story had inverted: its affiliated insurer Kresna Life lost its licence over a Rp6.4 trillion default, Steven was named a money-laundering suspect, and in June 2023 — one day before OJK revoked Kresna Life's licence — shareholders voted to bury the Kresna name and rename the company PT Quantum Clovera Investama Tbk ([lap.or.id](https://lap.or.id/pdf-post/buron-kresna-grup-michael-steven-masuk-daftar-merah-interpol); [OJK](https://ojk.go.id/id/berita-dan-kegiatan/siaran-pers/Documents/Pages/Pencabutan-Izin-Usaha-Kresna-Life/SP%20CIU%20Kresna%20Life%20Tepat.pdf)).

## The digital-fortune years

For most of its history, Kresna was an unremarkable financial-services firm. The 2015 reinvention changed that: Kresna Creativentures invested in Indonesian tech startups, and when the startups were ready to go public, Kresna used its core financial-services strength to manage their IPOs ([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/special/digital-fortune-the-journey-of-kresna-graha-investama/54927/1)). In 2018, Creativentures accounted for 88% of the group's Rp7.2 trillion in revenue, and net profit rose 1.5 times to Rp729 billion; the company targeted Rp8 trillion in 2019, with 92% expected from the tech business ([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/special/digital-fortune-the-journey-of-kresna-graha-investama/54927/1)). Its showcase wins stacked up fast: kiosk operator M Cash Integrasi, backed by Creativentures in April 2017, went public in an IPO oversubscribed ten times that raised Rp300 billion, with Kresna retaining a 13% stake worth about $28 million; NFC, an M Cash subsidiary, raised another Rp300 billion at its July 2018 listing; and the June 17, 2019 listing of Bali Bintang Sejahtera — the company behind Bali United, billed as Southeast Asia's first football-club IPO — raised almost Rp600 billion against a Rp350 billion target ([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/special/digital-fortune-the-journey-of-kresna-graha-investama/54927/1)). The group also made two investments (2017 and 2018) in Singapore fintech MatchMove Pay, alongside NTT Docomo Ventures ([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/special/digital-fortune-the-journey-of-kresna-graha-investama/54927/1)). Kresna's shares rose fivefold in four years to Rp555, giving it a $715 million market capitalisation at the time of the Forbes feature ([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/special/digital-fortune-the-journey-of-kresna-graha-investama/54927/1)).

## The Kresna Life collapse

The unraveling centred on the affiliated insurer, PT Asuransi Jiwa Kresna (Kresna Life). After policyholders reported unpaid claims, OJK froze the insurer's business in August 2020 over Rp6.4 trillion in obligations it could not meet, then — after ten rejected financial-rehabilitation plans — revoked its licence outright on 23 June 2023 ([OJK](https://ojk.go.id/id/berita-dan-kegiatan/siaran-pers/Documents/Pages/Pencabutan-Izin-Usaha-Kresna-Life/SP%20CIU%20Kresna%20Life%20Tepat.pdf); [lap.or.id](https://lap.or.id/pdf-post/buron-kresna-grup-michael-steven-masuk-daftar-merah-interpol)). OJK's stated grounds were damning for a group holding company: examinations found Kresna Life's insurance funds concentrated in shares affiliated with the Kresna group, liabilities recorded below their true value, and a solvency (risk-based capital) ratio below the regulatory floor ([OJK](https://ojk.go.id/id/berita-dan-kegiatan/siaran-pers/Documents/Pages/Pencabutan-Izin-Usaha-Kresna-Life/SP%20CIU%20Kresna%20Life%20Tepat.pdf)). The controlling shareholder never injected fresh capital — OJK noted that payments presented as shareholder responsibility came from Kresna Life's existing assets — and a proposed scheme to convert policyholder claims into subordinated loans collapsed when most policyholders refused ([OJK](https://ojk.go.id/id/berita-dan-kegiatan/siaran-pers/Documents/Pages/Pencabutan-Izin-Usaha-Kresna-Life/SP%20CIU%20Kresna%20Life%20Tepat.pdf)). OJK simultaneously issued a written order to certain parties to compensate Kresna Life for losses caused by their actions ([OJK](https://ojk.go.id/id/berita-dan-kegiatan/siaran-pers/Documents/Pages/Pencabutan-Izin-Usaha-Kresna-Life/SP%20CIU%20Kresna%20Life%20Tepat.pdf)). The day before the revocation, on 22 June 2023, shareholders of the listed parent voted to rename it PT Quantum Clovera Investama Tbk, effective 25 July 2023 ([lap.or.id](https://lap.or.id/pdf-post/buron-kresna-grup-michael-steven-masuk-daftar-merah-interpol); [Simply Wall St](https://simplywall.st/stock/idx/kren)).

## The founder's legal arc

Michael Steven — the founder who had reinvented the company — became the target of the criminal case. In 2023 the Indonesian National Police's Directorate of Special Economic Crimes named "Michael Steve," identified as the owner of Kresna Group, a suspect in a money-laundering investigation ([Indonesian National Police](https://inp.polri.go.id/artikel/kresna-group-owner-named-suspect-in-money-laundering-case)). The charges spanned the Capital Market Law (Article 103 in conjunction with Article 30 of Law 8/1995), embezzlement and fraud articles of the Criminal Code (372, 378), and the Anti-Money-Laundering Law (Articles 3–5 of Law 8/2020), carrying a maximum 20 years' imprisonment and a Rp10 billion fine ([Indonesian National Police](https://inp.polri.go.id/artikel/kresna-group-owner-named-suspect-in-money-laundering-case)). Police alleged Steven and three co-suspects issued investment products through PT PUP and PT MSL using PT KS securities without investment-management licences, misappropriating customer funds without consent — with nine investors suffering Rp337.4 billion in losses ([Indonesian National Police](https://inp.polri.go.id/artikel/kresna-group-owner-named-suspect-in-money-laundering-case)). Steven fled abroad; Indonesia's NCB Interpol placed him on a red notice effective 19 September 2025 ([lap.or.id](https://lap.or.id/pdf-post/buron-kresna-grup-michael-steven-masuk-daftar-merah-interpol)). On 12 March 2026 Moroccan police arrested him at Indonesia's request, and after Morocco granted extradition, he arrived back in Indonesia on 21 June 2026 to face proceedings for alleged capital-market crimes, fraud, embezzlement and money laundering involving Kresna Life, with alleged investor losses of about Rp337.4 billion ([jawawa.id](https://jawawa.id/newsitem/interpol-fugitive-and-kresna-life-boss-michael-steven-arrested-in-morocco-1782104030)).
