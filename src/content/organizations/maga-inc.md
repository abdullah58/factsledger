**MAGA Inc.** — legally **Make America Great Again Inc.** — is the super PAC that has served as [Donald Trump](https://factsledger.com/people/donald-trump/)'s best-funded outside vehicle since its launch in September 2022. After spending **$377 million** in the 2024 cycle, it entered the 2026 midterms with a stockpile above **$400 million**, making it one of the most consequential forces in Republican politics. 【sources: Trump's super PAC MAGA Inc. says it has more than $400 million stockpile】 Its full-circle personnel story is Karoline Leavitt's: the PAC's former spokeswoman left the White House press secretary's job in August 2026 and came back. 【sources: Former White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt shopping potentially explosive book: sources】

## Launch: September 2022

Trump's leading political advisers launched MAGA Inc. in **September 2022** to spend heavily in the final weeks before the midterm elections backing Trump-endorsed candidates. The committee's FEC paperwork was filed on **September 23, 2022**, and Trump spokesman **Taylor Budowich** — a veteran of the 2020 campaign — was named executive director. 【sources: Trump advisers launch new super PAC MAGA Inc. to spend millions in midterms backing endorsed candidates】

The launch reflected a frustration: Trump's existing vehicle, the [Save America](/organizations/save-america/) leadership PAC, was sitting on nearly $100 million but faced far stricter fundraising and spending limits, and Trump had transferred little of it to fellow Republicans. As a super PAC, MAGA Inc. could raise unlimited sums and spend them freely — but, by law, could not coordinate directly with candidates or parties. 【sources: Trump advisers launch new super PAC MAGA Inc. to spend millions in midterms backing endorsed candidates】

The early team read like a Trump campaign in exile: Republican strategist **Chris LaCivita** as chief strategist, longtime Trump pollster **Tony Fabrizio** running polling, communications aides **Steven Cheung** and **Alex Pfeiffer**, and **Karoline Leavitt** — fresh off a 2022 House run in New Hampshire — as spokeswoman. 【sources: Trump advisers launch new super PAC MAGA Inc. to spend millions in midterms backing endorsed candidates; Karoline Leavitt Shares Her Unexpected New Project After White House Exit】

## 2023: the DeSantis war

With Trump facing a primary challenge from Florida governor Ron DeSantis, MAGA Inc. became the attack arm of the Trump operation. In **March 2023**, it sent a **15-page letter** to the chairman of the Florida Commission on Ethics accusing DeSantis of running a "shadow" presidential campaign — citing his book tour through early primary states, his state PAC's fundraising, and potential changes to Florida's resign-to-run law. 【sources: Trump super PAC MAGA Inc. alleges DeSantis committed ethics violations】 DeSantis's office dismissed it as a "frivolous and politically motivated" attack, and the ethics commission said it had not received the correspondence. 【sources: Trump super PAC MAGA Inc. alleges DeSantis committed ethics violations】

On the airwaves, the group's ads hammered DeSantis for decade-old congressional votes touching Social Security and Medicare — the opening barrage of a spending spree that defined the 2024 Republican primary. 【sources: Trump, DeSantis allies drive a 2024 election spending spree】

## 2024: Mellon's millions and a $377 million blitz

The 2024 general election turned MAGA Inc. into a billionaire-funded juggernaut. **Timothy Mellon**, the reclusive banking heir, wrote a **$50 million** check to the PAC the day after Trump's conviction on 34 felonies in New York, then gave **another $50 million in July 2024** — over 90 percent of everything the group raised that month. 【sources: Billionaire Timothy Mellon has poured $165 million into 2024 elections】 His total support for MAGA Inc. reached **$125 million** for the cycle, and his $165 million-plus in overall 2024 giving made him the top individual donor to outside spending groups in the country. 【sources: Billionaire Timothy Mellon has poured $165 million into 2024 elections】

All told, the group **spent $377 million** during the 2024 election campaign. 【sources: Trump's super PAC MAGA Inc. says it has more than $400 million stockpile】

## 2026: a $400 million war chest

Rather than winding down after victory, MAGA Inc. kept raising. In **June 2026**, [Reuters](https://factsledger.com/organizations/reuters/) reported a **$356 million war chest**, with $1.7 million already spent on special elections and LaCivita serving as a senior adviser. 【sources: Trump's political operation reports $356 million war chest ahead of 2026 midterms】 A month later, Reuters reported the stockpile had grown past **$400 million** by the end of June 2026 — with June donations including **$1 million from Jared Isaacman** and **$10 million from the Winklevoss brothers**. 【sources: Trump's super PAC MAGA Inc. says it has more than $400 million stockpile】

The money is already moving: the Center for American Progress Action Fund reported in September 2026 that MAGA Inc. had set up funded spinoff super PACs — including No Going Back PAC and the Safety and Affordability PAC — with **$160 million in ad reservations** tracked by AdImpact and nearly **$80 million spent** as of September 20, 2026. 【sources: How President Trump and His Administration Are Benefiting MAGA Inc. Super PAC Donors】

## The Leavitt full circle

The most human thread in the MAGA Inc. story belongs to **Karoline Leavitt**. She served as the group's national press secretary before becoming a leading voice of Trump's 2024 campaign, then the **youngest person ever to serve as White House press secretary** when Trump returned to office in January 2025. 【sources: Karoline Leavitt Shares Her Unexpected New Project After White House Exit】 She stepped down at the **end of August 2026** to spend more time with her family — and confirmed she would **return to MAGA Inc.**, a move Fox News confirmed, positioning one of Trump's most recognizable communicators at the center of the midterm fight. 【sources: Former White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt shopping potentially explosive book: sources; Karoline Leavitt Shares Her Unexpected New Project After White House Exit】

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not state a total 2024 fundraising figure — only the $377 million in spending verified by Reuters. It does not repeat allegations about coordination between the PAC and the Trump campaign or White House, which are claims by outside watchdog groups, not established facts. Donor details beyond Mellon, Isaacman, and the Winklevoss brothers are not claimed, and the 2026 spending figures attributed to the Center for American Progress Action Fund are that organization's analysis, presented as such.
