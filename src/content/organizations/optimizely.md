## Overview

Optimizely is a digital experience platform company that has had two lives: it was founded in 2010 by Dan Siroker and Pete Koomen, two former Google product managers, as a self-serve A/B testing startup incubated at Y Combinator, and it became the name of a much larger company in January 2021, when Swedish-rooted content management vendor Episerver — itself acquired by Insight Partners in 2018 — rebranded the combined business under the Optimizely name after buying the original Optimizely ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2014/05/05/optimizely-raises-a-lot-of-money/), [Technology Record](https://www.technologyrecord.com/article/episerver-rebrands-as-optimizely-and-focuses-on-digital-experience)). It is privately held, with Insight Partners as its owner via the 2018 Episerver buyout ([KMWorld](https://www.kmworld.com/Articles/News/News/Insight-Venture-Partners-Acquires-Episerver-127308.aspx)).

The origin story explains why the acquirer took the target's name. Siroker had been director of analytics for Barack Obama's 2008 presidential campaign, where testing which fundraising buttons raised the most money became a defining tactic; the insight was that commercially available testing tools required engineers, so Siroker and Koomen built a platform any organization could use with no statistics degree ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2014/05/05/optimizely-raises-a-lot-of-money/)). By May 2014, when Andreessen Horowitz led a $57 million Series B, the company reported more than 7,000 customers, half a million experiments created, and seven billion web experiences optimized ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2014/05/05/optimizely-raises-a-lot-of-money/)).

## The acquisition that renamed the acquirer

In September 2020 Episerver announced it had agreed to acquire Optimizely for an undisclosed sum in a cash-and-stock deal, with Insight Partners acting as strategic advisor and sponsor and Goldman Sachs advising Optimizely ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/09/03/episerver-acquires-optimizely/)). At the time, Optimizely had raised more than $200 million from investors including Goldman Sachs, Index Ventures, Andreessen Horowitz and GV, served more than 1,000 customers including Gap, StubHub, IBM and The Wall Street Journal, had laid off 15% of its staff earlier that year citing COVID-19, and was led by CEO Jay Larson, who had replaced Siroker in 2017 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/09/03/episerver-acquires-optimizely/)). The acquisition was expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2020 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/09/03/episerver-acquires-optimizely/)).

Then came the unusual move. In January 2021 Episerver announced it was rebranding the combined company as Optimizely, launching an Optimization-as-a-Service offering alongside a new release of its content management software ([Technology Record](https://www.technologyrecord.com/article/episerver-rebrands-as-optimizely-and-focuses-on-digital-experience)). CEO Alex Atzberger — who had joined Episerver in December 2019 after leading customer experience at SAP — later explained the logic: he had long questioned whether the Episerver brand resonated in the U.S. market, and a "digital gravity study" measuring brand awareness relative to search results showed the Optimizely name was far better known among digital leaders and CMOs, with even content-management searches surfacing Optimizely ([TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/searchcustomerexperience/news/252522587/Optimizely-CEO-talks-roadmap-plans-A-B-testing-and-DXP?ref=dxpreport.com)). As Atzberger put it, the acquired company's origin story — a Silicon Valley startup born from the Obama campaign's fundraising tests — gave it a brand cachet that a European CMS vendor could not manufacture ([TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/searchcustomerexperience/news/252522587/Optimizely-CEO-talks-roadmap-plans-A-B-testing-and-DXP?ref=dxpreport.com)).

## Private equity ownership and the build-up after the merger

The company Optimizely is today sits inside a private-equity ownership arc. Insight Partners agreed to acquire Episerver in September 2018 in a transaction valuing it at $1.16 billion — confirmed by deal advisor Houlihan Lokey, which announced the deal on September 6, 2018 ([KMWorld](https://www.kmworld.com/Articles/News/News/Insight-Venture-Partners-Acquires-Episerver-127308.aspx), [Houlihan Lokey](https://hl.com/about-us/transactions/houlihan-lokey-serves-as-special-advisor-on-episerver-sale/)). Episerver's own roots run back to Stockholm in 1994 as a content management software company ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/09/03/episerver-acquires-optimizely/)). Insight sponsored the Optimizely acquisition two years later and remains the owner of the combined company; no independent reporting of a subsequent ownership change was found ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/09/03/episerver-acquires-optimizely/)).

Post-rebrand, Atzberger kept acquiring. In March 2021 Optimizely acquired Zaius, a customer data platform, for an undisclosed sum — the deal was announced March 22 and confirmed by Zaius's legal counsel WilmerHale ([TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/enterprise-software/news/252498293/Optimizely-shores-up-DX-platform-with-Zaius-CDP-acquisition), [WilmerHale](https://www.wilmerhale.com/insights/news/20210329-wilmerhale-represents-zaius-in-acquisition-by-optimizely)). Zaius brought more than 50 prebuilt connectors to e-commerce platforms and data clouds and 125 marketing orchestration "recipes"; Optimizely had sought a CDP for over a year after concluding it needed to ingest customer data from outside sources, and buying was faster than building ([TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/enterprise-software/news/252498293/Optimizely-shores-up-DX-platform-with-Zaius-CDP-acquisition)). The acquisition also became the basis for the Optimizely Data Platform ([TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/enterprise-software/news/252498293/Optimizely-shores-up-DX-platform-with-Zaius-CDP-acquisition)). Atzberger told TechTarget in 2022 he had done five acquisitions since becoming CEO — a count that includes idio, Insite, Optimizely, Zaius and the marketing work-management vendor Welcome, acquired in early 2022 ([TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/searchcustomerexperience/news/252522587/Optimizely-CEO-talks-roadmap-plans-A-B-testing-and-DXP?ref=dxpreport.com)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not claim a single founding year for the original Optimizely: TechCrunch reported 2010 in 2014 and 2009 in 2020, and the discrepancy is flagged, not resolved. It does not state a single figure for Insight Partners' 2018 Episerver buyout — the reported number is $1.16 billion per KMWorld, Houlihan Lokey and Insight's own announcement, versus $1.1 billion in TechCrunch's 2020 reporting. The price Episerver paid for Optimizely was never disclosed and is not stated. As a private company, Optimizely does not publish revenue or customer figures; the profile states only dated customer counts from reporting at the time. The current ownership claim rests on the absence of any reported later sale — private-company ownership can change without public disclosure.
