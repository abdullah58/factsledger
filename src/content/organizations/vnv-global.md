## Overview

VNV Global AB (publ) is a Stockholm-listed investment company that invests permanent capital in non-listed, fast-growing companies with network effects — businesses whose value grows as more people use them ([VNV Global](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/dad4f7b02c471b24990494d2b43e5d28-2026-07-14-13-48-57.pdf?ref=dGlwcmFua3M=)). Its shares trade on Nasdaq Stockholm's Mid Cap segment under the ticker VNV, and its chief executive is Per Brilioth ([VNV Global](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/dad4f7b02c471b24990494d2b43e5d28-2026-07-14-13-48-57.pdf?ref=dGlwcmFua3M=)). The company's best-known holdings are BlaBlaCar, the French carpooling marketplace, and Voi, the European e-scooter operator, and its best-known exit is Avito, the Russian classifieds platform it held for twelve years at a reported 37% internal rate of return ([VNV Global](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/dad4f7b02c471b24990494d2b43e5d28-2026-07-14-13-48-57.pdf?ref=dGlwcmFua3M=)).

The firm's identity is inseparable from its name changes. It began as Vostok New Ventures Ltd., incorporated in Bermuda on April 5, 2007, in a lineage the company's own investor deck traces back through Vostok Nafta to 1996 ([VNV Global](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/dad4f7b02c471b24990494d2b43e5d28-2026-07-14-13-48-57.pdf?ref=dGlwcmFua3M=)). In May 2020 it renamed itself VNV Global Ltd. and then redomesticated from Bermuda to Sweden, becoming VNV Global AB (publ) ([MFN](https://mfn.se/a/vnv-global/vostok-new-ventures-ltd-becomes-vnv-global-ltd.iframe)). The rename was more than cosmetic: it marked the firm's shift from its Russian-investing origins toward a portfolio of European network-effect businesses, and it is the reason the company's older filings appear under a name that no longer exists.

## A permanent-capital network-effects investor

VNV's strategy, as stated in its own investor update, is to invest in non-listed, fast-growing companies with network effects, using permanent capital ([VNV Global](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/dad4f7b02c471b24990494d2b43e5d28-2026-07-14-13-48-57.pdf?ref=dGlwcmFua3M=)). Permanent capital matters here: unlike a venture fund with a ten-year life, VNV has no forced exit date, so it can hold a company for twelve years — as it did with Avito — and can keep funding winners through cycles. The network-effects focus is the other half of the thesis: a carpooling marketplace, a scooter network, a classifieds site and a housing marketplace all get more valuable as more users join, which is what makes them defensible once they reach scale.

The portfolio at the second quarter of 2026 shows the thesis in practice: mobility accounted for 51% of portfolio value, marketplaces for 27% and digital health for 11%, and 71% of portfolio value sat in companies that were adjusted-EBITDA positive ([VNV Global](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/dad4f7b02c471b24990494d2b43e5d28-2026-07-14-13-48-57.pdf?ref=dGlwcmFua3M=)). The concentration is deliberate — two holdings, BlaBlaCar and Voi, together made up nearly half the portfolio — and it is also the firm's main risk, as the 2026 revaluations would show. Other holdings include HousingAnywhere (first invested 2018), Numan, Flo/Palta, Hungry Panda, Bokadirekt and Breadfast ([VNV Global](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/dad4f7b02c471b24990494d2b43e5d28-2026-07-14-13-48-57.pdf?ref=dGlwcmFua3M=)).

The rename and redomestication of 2020 deserves a precise account, because the sequence is easy to get wrong. The annual general meeting resolved the name change from Vostok New Ventures Ltd. to VNV Global Ltd. on May 12, 2020, and the change was registered on May 27, 2020 ([MFN](https://mfn.se/a/vnv-global/vostok-new-ventures-ltd-becomes-vnv-global-ltd.iframe)). The Swedish depository receipts and senior secured bonds 2019/2022 continued trading on Nasdaq Stockholm under the new name, the ISIN codes were unchanged, the short name VNV SDB was unchanged, and the website became vnv.global ([MFN](https://mfn.se/a/vnv-global/vostok-new-ventures-ltd-becomes-vnv-global-ltd.iframe)). A special meeting the same day approved the redomestication to Sweden by way of a Bermuda scheme of arrangement, with the Swedish depository receipts cancelled and exchanged for shares in the new Swedish parent, VNV Global AB (publ). The 2020 rights issue that followed was significantly oversubscribed and raised about SEK 872 million ([VNV Global](http://www.vostoknewventures.com/netcat_files/12/13/vnv_3q20_eng.pdf)).

## Avito: the defining exit

Avito is the investment that made VNV's reputation, and the firm still presents it as the template for its strategy. VNV held Avito for twelve years and reports a 37% internal rate of return and $579 million of exit proceeds ([VNV Global](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/dad4f7b02c471b24990494d2b43e5d28-2026-07-14-13-48-57.pdf?ref=dGlwcmFua3M=)). Those are the company's own figures, from its own investor deck, and no independent tier1 or tier2 confirmation of VNV's own Avito proceeds was found — a caveat worth keeping, because the Avito story has a second, separate exit that is easily confused with it. Prosus/Naspers sold its own Avito Group stake for RUB 151 billion in October 2022; that was a different shareholder's exit, not VNV's, and the two should not be conflated.

What Avito demonstrates is the permanent-capital model working as intended: a classifieds marketplace with strong network effects, held for over a decade while it compounded, and exited at scale. The firm's later holdings were chosen on the same pattern — marketplaces and mobility networks where a leading position, once won, is hard to dislodge. The question the rest of the portfolio has to answer is whether BlaBlaCar and Voi can repeat Avito's arc, or whether they will remain good businesses whose valuations keep moving with peer multiples.

## BlaBlaCar and Voi: the two pillars

BlaBlaCar is VNV's oldest current holding and its largest by portfolio weight. VNV first invested in the French carpooling marketplace in 2015, and by the second quarter of 2026 it had invested $204 million in total, carried the stake at a net asset value of $121 million, owned 13.5% of the company, and counted BlaBlaCar as 25% of its portfolio ([VNV Global](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/dad4f7b02c471b24990494d2b43e5d28-2026-07-14-13-48-57.pdf?ref=dGlwcmFua3M=)). The company's scale is substantial: 40 million yearly unique active users, 150 million passengers in 2025, €2 billion of gross merchandise value, headquarters in France and operations in more than 20 countries ([VNV Global](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/dad4f7b02c471b24990494d2b43e5d28-2026-07-14-13-48-57.pdf?ref=dGlwcmFua3M=)).

Voi is the second pillar and the more operationally intense of the two. VNV first invested in 2018, and by the second quarter of 2026 it had invested $106 million, carried the stake at $106 million, owned 20.8% and counted Voi as 21.9% of its portfolio ([VNV Global](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/dad4f7b02c471b24990494d2b43e5d28-2026-07-14-13-48-57.pdf?ref=dGlwcmFua3M=)). The company has passed 400 million cumulative rides across more than 130 cities in 13 countries, and its last-twelve-months revenue to the first quarter of 2026 was €188.3 million, up 36%, with adjusted EBITDA of €29.7 million at a 15.8% margin ([VNV Global](https://files.quartr.com/conference-calls/dad4f7b02c471b24990494d2b43e5d28-2026-07-14-13-48-57.pdf?ref=dGlwcmFua3M=)). Voi's business is won city by city, through tenders and licences. In September 2026 it secured a €150 million revolving credit facility and said it would redeem its outstanding bonds ([VNV Global](https://mfn.se/a/vnv-global/vnv-global-ab-publ-financial-report-for-the-third-quarter-and-the-first-nine-months-2026)).

## The Gett exit, twice

Gett is the cautionary tale in VNV's recent history, and the record needs care because the exit happened twice. In May 2024 VNV announced it had agreed to fully exit its investment in Gett, the Israeli ride-hailing company, to Pango Pay & Go Ltd. for a total net consideration of $83 million. That deal did not close: in May 2025 VNV said the previously announced transaction would not proceed to closing, and it resumed discussions with the other Gett shareholders. Those discussions produced a new stock purchase agreement, signed on August 15, 2025, with an investor consortium primarily composed of Israel-based investors, for a total net consideration of approximately $89.1 million ([VNV Global](https://storage.mfn.se/17165a00-c61c-44f7-b466-83013791c6af/vnv-global-exits-gett-for-usd-89-1-million.pdf)). Under the amended terms VNV would receive about $86.7 million at closing, which occurred early the following week, with the remaining $2.5 million held in escrow and released in full after the second anniversary of closing ([VNV Global](https://storage.mfn.se/17165a00-c61c-44f7-b466-83013791c6af/vnv-global-exits-gett-for-usd-89-1-million.pdf)). The $83 million figure from the first announcement is therefore not the exit value, and any source citing it as the completed deal is out of date.

The Gett exit also freed capital for the firm's next phase: with the increased liquidity VNV said it would consider repurchasing or redeeming its outstanding bonds 2024/2027 and increasing its investment activity, including initiating buybacks of its own shares ([VNV Global](https://storage.mfn.se/17165a00-c61c-44f7-b466-83013791c6af/vnv-global-exits-gett-for-usd-89-1-million.pdf)).

## Net asset value: 2020 peak to 2026 trough

VNV's net asset value tells the story of the last six years in one line. At September 30, 2020, shortly after the rename and redomestication, NAV was $902.67 million, or $9.49 per share, up from $776.98 million ($9.94 per share) at the end of 2019 ([VNV Global](http://www.vostoknewventures.com/netcat_files/12/13/vnv_3q20_eng.pdf)). At that date the portfolio was dominated by Babylon (28.9% of NAV), BlaBlaCar (19.1%), Gett (9.2%) and Voi (7.8%), and [Booksy](/organizations/booksy/) — then a small classifieds holding — was valued at $14.528 million, 1.5% of the portfolio, with VNV owning 11.8% of the company ([VNV Global](http://www.vostoknewventures.com/netcat_files/12/13/vnv_3q20_eng.pdf)).

By September 30, 2026 the picture had changed sharply. NAV was $439.08 million, or $3.43 per share, down 19% in US dollars over the nine-month period, and the net result for the period was a loss of $106.67 million, primarily as a result of negative revaluations of BlaBlaCar and Voi ([VNV Global](https://mfn.se/a/vnv-global/vnv-global-ab-publ-financial-report-for-the-third-quarter-and-the-first-nine-months-2026)). The decline was driven by changes in peer multiples and foreign-exchange movements rather than by the operating performance of the underlying companies — Voi's revenue grew 36% in the twelve months to the first quarter of 2026 — but for a listed investment company, the NAV is the scoreboard, and the 2026 scoreboard is down by more than half from 2020. The firm responded with capital management: it repurchased 593,048 of its own shares during the period, and in September 2026 the board authorised further buybacks of up to 10% of the shares ([VNV Global](https://mfn.se/a/vnv-global/vnv-global-ab-publ-financial-report-for-the-third-quarter-and-the-first-nine-months-2026)).

VNV's connection to this site's loop is Booksy. It participated in Booksy's $70 million Series C in January 2021, led by [Cat Rock Capital](/organizations/cat-rock-capital/), alongside [OpenOcean](/organizations/openocean/), [Piton Capital](/organizations/piton-capital/), [Sprints Capital](/organizations/sprints-capital/), [Enern](/organizations/enern/) and others; the round took Booksy's total raised to $119 million ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/01/27/booksy-raises-70m-war-chest-to-acquire-salon-appointment-apps-expand-internationally/)). Booksy had already been in VNV's portfolio since before the Series C — the Q3 2020 report lists it at a $14.5 million fair value — so the Series C was a follow-on investment in an existing holding, not a new position ([VNV Global](http://www.vostoknewventures.com/netcat_files/12/13/vnv_3q20_eng.pdf)).
