## Overview

Quantile is a London-based portfolio optimisation firm that makes the derivatives market smaller — not by trading less, but by cancelling out the trades that no longer need to exist ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/london-stock-exchange-to-bolster-post-trade-solutions-with-quantile-acquisition/)). Founded in 2015 by chief executive Andrew Williams, it runs multilateral optimisation services that compress interest-rate portfolios and rebalance counterparty risk, reducing the notional, margin and capital banks must hold against over-the-counter derivatives ([FF News](https://ffnews.com/news/lseg-to-acquire-quantile-group-limited)).

In December 2021 the [London Stock Exchange Group](/organizations/london-stock-exchange-group/) agreed to buy it for up to £274 million, and after an eight-month competition investigation the deal was cleared and Quantile became an LSEG Business inside the Post Trade division ([FF News](https://ffnews.com/news/lseg-to-acquire-quantile-group-limited); [The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-wins-cma-clearance-for-quantile-acquisition/)).

Quantile's clients include all of the G15 top-tier global banks alongside regional banks, buy-side firms and other large institutional participants, served from offices in London, New York, Amsterdam and Sydney ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/london-stock-exchange-to-bolster-post-trade-solutions-with-quantile-acquisition/); [London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/quantile/2023/quantiles-counterparty-risk-optimisation-service-for-fx-now-supports-swapagent-fx-forwards)). It is a plumbing company for the financial system — invisible to the public, but handling the risk arithmetic behind trillions of dollars of derivatives.

## What optimisation actually does

Optimisation sounds abstract; its two halves are concrete. Interest-rate compression takes a web of offsetting swaps between banks and replaces them with fewer trades carrying the same net risk, cutting gross notional — the headline size of the market — without changing anyone's economic position ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/quantile/2023/quantiles-counterparty-risk-optimisation-service-for-fx-now-supports-swapagent-fx-forwards)). Counterparty risk optimisation does the same for margin: it analyses the risk of transactions between participants and rebalances portfolios with new market-risk-neutral hedges that reduce risk and release capital ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/quantile/2023/quantiles-counterparty-risk-optimisation-service-for-fx-now-supports-swapagent-fx-forwards)). Quantile pioneered the multilateral version of this for foreign-exchange initial margin in 2017, and has since extended it to cleared and uncleared initial margin and to risk-based capital under the SA-CCR and IMM frameworks ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/quantile/2023/quantiles-counterparty-risk-optimisation-service-for-fx-now-supports-swapagent-fx-forwards)). Since launching its first services in 2017 it has eliminated trillions of dollars of gross notional through compression and billions of dollars of initial margin through risk optimisation.

| Service | What it reduces | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interest-rate compression | Gross notional | Replaces offsetting swaps with fewer trades at the same net risk |
| Counterparty risk optimisation (FX, rates) | Initial margin and capital | Rebalances portfolios with market-risk-neutral hedges |
| Counterparty risk optimisation (cleared/uncleared) | Margin under SA-CCR and IMM | Multilateral runs across participants |
| SwapAgent FX Forwards support | Risk and capital usage by ~30–60% | Moves risk to SwapAgent's standardised infrastructure |

## The £274 million deal and the eight-month investigation

LSEG announced the deal on 6 December 2021, offering up to £274 million for a six-year-old firm with 80-plus employees, and promising Quantile would stay a standalone entity reporting to Daniel Maguire, LSEG's Group Head of Post Trade ([FF News](https://ffnews.com/news/lseg-to-acquire-quantile-group-limited)). The price reflected a strategic fit: Quantile's compression complemented LSEG's LCH clearing house, which already cleared much of the market Quantile optimised ([FF News](https://ffnews.com/news/lseg-to-acquire-quantile-group-limited)). That same fit alarmed the UK Competition and Markets Authority, which opened an investigation in March 2022 and referred the deal for a Phase 2 review in May: compression providers depend on clearing houses like LCH to build their proposals, and the CMA feared LCH could refuse third-party providers or favour rivals on worse terms ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-wins-cma-clearance-for-quantile-acquisition/)). In October 2022 the CMA cleared the deal unconditionally, finding LSEG would lack the incentive to disadvantage rivals because customers could stop such efforts ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-wins-cma-clearance-for-quantile-acquisition/)). Both parties said they expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2022.

## Life inside LSEG

Inside LSEG, Quantile kept its name and its standalone status, and its work deepened rather than narrowed. In 2023 it enhanced its foreign-exchange counterparty risk service to support SwapAgent FX Forwards — an industry first — executing the market's first such forward between two participants including Nomura, and cutting risk and capital usage by roughly 30–60% by moving risk onto SwapAgent's standardised infrastructure ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/quantile/2023/quantiles-counterparty-risk-optimisation-service-for-fx-now-supports-swapagent-fx-forwards)). The stat that captures its scale: since 2017 Quantile has eliminated trillions of dollars of gross notional and billions of dollars of initial margin ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/quantile/2023/quantiles-counterparty-risk-optimisation-service-for-fx-now-supports-swapagent-fx-forwards)) — which implies that post-trade optimisation, once a niche service, is now load-bearing infrastructure for the derivatives market. The regulatory picture caught up in June 2026, when the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission gave Quantile Technologies, Capitolis Partners and TriOptima no-action relief from registering as swap execution facilities for portfolio rebalancing and basis-risk mitigation, noting they were registered as introducing brokers ([Finadium](https://finadium.com/cftc-takes-no-action-on-capitolis-quantile-and-osttra-for-sef-registration/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Quantile?**
Quantile is a London-based provider of portfolio, margin and capital optimisation and compression services for the global financial services market, reducing counterparty risk, notional and capital requirements for firms trading OTC derivatives ([FF News](https://ffnews.com/news/lseg-to-acquire-quantile-group-limited)). It is an LSEG Business within the Post Trade division.

**Who founded Quantile?**
Andrew Williams, the firm's CEO and co-founder, founded Quantile in 2015 and still led it at the time of the LSEG acquisition ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/london-stock-exchange-to-bolster-post-trade-solutions-with-quantile-acquisition/)). Williams remained Quantile's chief executive inside LSEG's Post Trade division, as LSEG's 2023 product release confirms ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/quantile/2023/quantiles-counterparty-risk-optimisation-service-for-fx-now-supports-swapagent-fx-forwards)). Stephen O'Connor was chairman and a minority shareholder, not the founder, per fully-read sources.

**Why did the CMA investigate the LSEG deal?**
Because multilateral compression providers depend on clearing houses like LCH — majority-owned by LSEG — to build compression proposals, the CMA feared LCH could shut out Quantile's rivals or treat them worse ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-wins-cma-clearance-for-quantile-acquisition/)). After an eight-month inquiry it cleared the deal unconditionally in October 2022.

**How much did LSEG pay for Quantile?**
LSEG agreed to pay a maximum aggregate consideration of up to £274 million, subject to customary adjustments, funded from existing cash and credit facilities ([FF News](https://ffnews.com/news/lseg-to-acquire-quantile-group-limited)). Evercore acted as sole financial adviser and sponsor to LSEG on the transaction, and Reuters later rendered the price as up to $362 million in dollars.

**What happened to Quantile after the acquisition?**
It remained a standalone entity within LSEG's Post Trade division, expanded its services — including the 2023 SwapAgent FX Forwards enhancement — and in June 2026 received CFTC no-action relief alongside Capitolis and TriOptima for its post-trade risk reduction services ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/quantile/2023/quantiles-counterparty-risk-optimisation-service-for-fx-now-supports-swapagent-fx-forwards); [Finadium](https://finadium.com/cftc-takes-no-action-on-capitolis-quantile-and-osttra-for-sef-registration/)).
