## Overview

Bisnow is a commercial real estate news and events company founded in 2005 by Mark Bisnow and his son Elliott, and it has now been sold twice: to the private equity firm Wicks Group for about $50 million in 2016, according to Fortune, and to the German media group Axel Springer in March 2026 ([Capitol Communicator](https://capitolcommunicator.com/well-this-isnt-boring/); [citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/813253/axel-springer-to-acquire-commercial-real-estate-publisher-bisnow/)). It publishes newsletters and a website and runs live events for the commercial real estate industry, with more than 400 events a year in 47 local markets across the [United States](/places/united-states/), Canada, the UK, Ireland and the Netherlands, reaching more than 1.7 million subscribers ([Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/axel-springer-acquires-b2b-media-and-events-company-bisnow-and-establishes-brew-media-group,260472); [BrightTower](https://www.thebesttimes.com/financial/brighttower-advises-b2b-media-and-events-company-bisnow-in-sale-to-axel-springer/article_d9a1e631-edc2-569e-b997-1f4134edc71b.html)).

This profile covers that company — the CRE media business, not the Bisnow family name generally and not Summit Series, Elliott Bisnow's separate events venture. It is based in New York, New York, having launched in Washington DC ([Capitol Communicator](https://capitolcommunicator.com/well-this-isnt-boring/)). Its slogan, "(Almost) Never Boring", is also its editorial programme: a self-aware, slightly irreverent tone that trade media rarely attempted when Bisnow started ([Media Mergers](https://www.mediamergers.co.uk/bisnow-acquired-by-private-equity-firm-the-wicks-group/)). Axel Springer, its current owner, also owns Politico, Business Insider, BILD and WELT, and is building a US business-to-business portfolio around Bisnow and Morning Brew ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/813253/axel-springer-to-acquire-commercial-real-estate-publisher-bisnow/); [Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/axel-springer-acquires-b2b-media-and-events-company-bisnow-and-establishes-brew-media-group,260472)).

## A newsletter that became a CRE institution

Bisnow began in Washington DC as a digital newsletter and grew by covering commercial real estate with personality, in short emailed editions for a niche business audience, then turning the readership into a live-events business ([Capitol Communicator](https://capitolcommunicator.com/well-this-isnt-boring/); [Media Mergers](https://www.mediamergers.co.uk/bisnow-acquired-by-private-equity-firm-the-wicks-group/)). The model was sponsorships and tickets rather than subscriptions: the company made most of its revenue that way across the 300 events it organized annually, 25 of them in Washington DC alone ([Capitol Communicator](https://capitolcommunicator.com/well-this-isnt-boring/)). The citation-magnet figure is the growth rate: in 2013 Bisnow had $13.8 million in sales with a three-year growth rate of 258%, according to Crain's New York — the kind of number that explains why a private equity firm came calling three years later ([Capitol Communicator](https://capitolcommunicator.com/well-this-isnt-boring/)). By 2016 it operated in 27 cities in the US and Canada with 75 employees, drew over 400,000 unique web visitors a month and 60,000 attendees to its events each year; Mark Bisnow had stepped back as chief executive in 2013 and Will Friend had taken over ([Capitol Communicator](https://capitolcommunicator.com/well-this-isnt-boring/); [Media Mergers](https://www.mediamergers.co.uk/bisnow-acquired-by-private-equity-firm-the-wicks-group/)).

## Two sales: Wicks in 2016, Axel Springer in 2026

In May 2016 the Wicks Group, a New York-based private equity firm, acquired Bisnow for about $50 million, according to Fortune, with the parties themselves not disclosing terms ([Capitol Communicator](https://capitolcommunicator.com/well-this-isnt-boring/); [Media Mergers](https://www.mediamergers.co.uk/bisnow-acquired-by-private-equity-firm-the-wicks-group/)). Elliott Bisnow left the company as part of the deal, and Wicks planned to expand it to new international locations — a plan that played out over the next decade as Bisnow moved into the UK, Ireland and the Netherlands ([Capitol Communicator](https://capitolcommunicator.com/well-this-isnt-boring/)). The second sale came ten years later: on 2 March 2026 Axel Springer closed its acquisition of Bisnow from Wicks, again on undisclosed terms, and placed it with Morning Brew Inc. in a newly formed Brew Media Group led by Robert Dippell, who remains chief executive of Morning Brew ([Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/axel-springer-acquires-b2b-media-and-events-company-bisnow-and-establishes-brew-media-group,260472); [citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/813253/axel-springer-to-acquire-commercial-real-estate-publisher-bisnow/)). Dippell knew the asset: he had previously served as Bisnow's chief revenue officer and executive adviser ([Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/axel-springer-acquires-b2b-media-and-events-company-bisnow-and-establishes-brew-media-group,260472)). Bisnow and Morning Brew operate as separate, independently run companies inside the group, and Bisnow's chief executive Gregg Mayer stayed in place ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/813253/axel-springer-to-acquire-commercial-real-estate-publisher-bisnow/)).

## Bisnow and Bilt Rewards

Bisnow's connection to this site's Day-36 loop is its own reporting: on 26 October 2022 it reported that [Bilt Rewards](/organizations/bilt/) had raised $150 million at a $1.5 billion valuation in a round led by Left Lane Capital, with backers including Wells Fargo, Greystar, Invitation Homes, Camber Creek, Fifth Wall, Smash Capital, Prosus and Kairos ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/technology/rent-loyalty-program-bilt-rewards-valuation-skyrockets-to-15b-116053)). The report captured Bilt at its unicorn moment — more than 500,000 active members, $3 billion in annualized rent payments, and Bilt Mastercard customers spending at an annualized rate of about $1.6 billion — and noted its landlord partners AvalonBay Communities, Blackstone, Related Companies and Equity Residential ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/technology/rent-loyalty-program-bilt-rewards-valuation-skyrockets-to-15b-116053)). It also warned about the wider market: proptech fundraising had dropped from the prior year's record highs, and venture investors told Bisnow that more startups were being forced to swallow lower-than-expected valuations — one called it "the hardest fundraising environment I've ever witnessed" ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/technology/rent-loyalty-program-bilt-rewards-valuation-skyrockets-to-15b-116053)). That is Bisnow doing what it has always done — covering the money moving through commercial real estate, in this case the loyalty programme built on top of it.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Bisnow?** Bisnow is a commercial real estate media and events company founded in 2005 by Mark Bisnow and his son Elliott ([Capitol Communicator](https://capitolcommunicator.com/well-this-isnt-boring/)). It publishes newsletters and a website and hosts more than 400 live events a year in 47 local markets across the US, Canada, the UK, Ireland and the Netherlands, reaching more than 1.7 million subscribers ([Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/axel-springer-acquires-b2b-media-and-events-company-bisnow-and-establishes-brew-media-group,260472)).

**Who owns Bisnow now?** Axel Springer, the German media group behind BILD, Business Insider, Politico and WELT, which acquired Bisnow from the Wicks Group in a transaction that closed on 2 March 2026 ([Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/axel-springer-acquires-b2b-media-and-events-company-bisnow-and-establishes-brew-media-group,260472)). Bisnow sits in the newly formed Brew Media Group alongside Morning Brew Inc., led by Robert Dippell, with Bisnow chief executive Gregg Mayer remaining in place ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/813253/axel-springer-to-acquire-commercial-real-estate-publisher-bisnow/)).

**How much did Bisnow sell for?** The 2016 sale to the Wicks Group was reported by Fortune at about $50 million, though the parties did not disclose terms, and Fortune's figure has never been confirmed by the parties themselves ([Capitol Communicator](https://capitolcommunicator.com/well-this-isnt-boring/); [Media Mergers](https://www.mediamergers.co.uk/bisnow-acquired-by-private-equity-firm-the-wicks-group/)). The price of the 2026 sale to Axel Springer was not disclosed ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/813253/axel-springer-to-acquire-commercial-real-estate-publisher-bisnow/)).

**How does Bisnow make money?** Mostly from sponsorships and tickets to its live events, rather than subscriptions: in 2016 it made most of its revenue that way across the 300 events it organized annually ([Capitol Communicator](https://capitolcommunicator.com/well-this-isnt-boring/)). By 2026 the events business had grown to more than 400 live events a year, alongside newsletters, branded content and recruiting solutions ([Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/axel-springer-acquires-b2b-media-and-events-company-bisnow-and-establishes-brew-media-group,260472)).

**What did Bisnow report about Bilt Rewards?** On 26 October 2022 Bisnow reported Bilt Rewards' $150 million round at a $1.5 billion valuation, led by Left Lane Capital with Prosus and Kairos among the backers ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/technology/rent-loyalty-program-bilt-rewards-valuation-skyrockets-to-15b-116053)). It put Bilt's scale at more than 500,000 active members and $3 billion in annualized rent payments, and named AvalonBay, Blackstone, Related and Equity Residential as landlord partners ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/technology/rent-loyalty-program-bilt-rewards-valuation-skyrockets-to-15b-116053)).
