## Overview

Turkish Airlines is Turkey's flag carrier and the airline that flies to more countries than any other carrier in the world ([Türkiye Today](https://www.turkiyetoday.com/lifestyle/turkish-airlines-named-europes-best-airline-for-11th-time-3228475)). Founded on May 20, 1933, as the State Airlines Administration — a department of the Defense Ministry with five aircraft and 28 seats ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/business/2019/05/21/turkish-airlines-celebrates-its-86th-year-in-the-sky-with-modern-fleet-of-336-aircraft)) — it has grown into a global giant anchored at Istanbul Airport, named Europe's best airline eleven times ([Türkiye Today](https://www.turkiyetoday.com/lifestyle/turkish-airlines-named-europes-best-airline-for-11th-time-3228475)). What distinguishes it from its rivals is its role as an instrument of Turkish statecraft: it sponsored Turkey's Oscar campaign for a war film, moved its entire operation to a new airport in 45 hours, and places aircraft orders large enough to arrive with presidential diplomacy ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633); [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/business/2019/04/06/turkish-airlines-says-hello-to-new-home-in-istanbul-airport); [eturbonews](https://eturbonews.com/turkish-airlines-boeing-737-max-order/)).

## The airline as an instrument of statecraft

In 2017, Turkish Airlines main-sponsored the Oscar PR campaign for *Ayla: The Daughter of War*, Turkey's foreign-language Oscar submission, with the Culture and Tourism Ministry as the campaign's main supporter ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633)). The film, directed by Can Ülkay and starring İsmail Hacıoğlu, Çetin Tekindor, [Kim Seol](/people/kim-seol/), and [Lee Kyung-jin](/people/lee-kyung-jin/), tells the true story of Lieutenant Süleyman Dilbirliği and the Korean War orphan he named Ayla — its tour timed to the 60th anniversary of Turkish soldiers landing in Busan ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633)). The same logic drove the April 2019 "Great Move": a 45-hour relocation from Atatürk Airport to Istanbul Airport — 1,800 personnel, equipment covering 33 football pitches, 400,000 kilometers of trucking — hailed by İlker Aycı as the greatest logistical operation in history ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/business/2019/04/06/turkish-airlines-says-hello-to-new-home-in-istanbul-airport)). With 49.12% of its shares held by the Turkey Sovereign Wealth Fund ([anews.com.tr](https://www.anews.com.tr/turkey/2019/05/20/turkeys-flag-carrier-marks-its-86th-anniversary)), the carrier is half state-owned — which is why Oscar bids and airport moves carry national-policy significance.

## Playing Airbus against Boeing

In December 2023, Turkish Airlines ordered 230 Airbus aircraft with purchase rights for 125 more — 150 A321 NEOs and 60 widebody A350-900s, at a catalogue price above $40 billion, as part of its 2023–2033 strategic plan ([The Japan Times](https://www.thejapantimes.jp/Boulevard/243208-turkish-airlines-makes-huge-airbus-order-in-bid-for-air-supremacy.html)). Airbus counted 220 of those as new, and the count was never publicly reconciled ([The Japan Times](https://www.thejapantimes.jp/Boulevard/243208-turkish-airlines-makes-huge-airbus-order-in-bid-for-air-supremacy.html)). In September 2025 the carrier announced up to 75 Boeing 787s and up to 150 Boeing 737 MAXs; the MAX portion was finalized on September 23, 2026 — 100 firm 737-8s, 50 options, substitution rights for the 737-10 — its largest Boeing order, signed with President Erdoğan present ([eturbonews](https://eturbonews.com/turkish-airlines-boeing-737-max-order/)). The motive is commercial: with a 2033 target of more than 800 aircraft, depending on one manufacturer concentrates supply risk, and keeping both competing improves its leverage on price, delivery slots, and financing ([eturbonews](https://eturbonews.com/turkish-airlines-boeing-737-max-order/)). The MAX signing proceeded without an engine-maintenance agreement for the CFM International LEAP-1B engines — serious enough that the airline considered walking away ([eturbonews](https://eturbonews.com/turkish-airlines-boeing-737-max-order/)).

## From five aircraft to a global network

Turkish Airlines began as a Defense Ministry department in 1933 and moved under the Transport Ministry in 1938 as the State Airlines General Directorate ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/business/2019/05/21/turkish-airlines-celebrates-its-86th-year-in-the-sky-with-modern-fleet-of-336-aircraft)). Its first international flight ran from Ankara to Athens via Istanbul on February 12, 1947, aboard a Douglas DC-3 ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/business/2019/05/21/turkish-airlines-celebrates-its-86th-year-in-the-sky-with-modern-fleet-of-336-aircraft)). Nicosia, Beirut, and Cairo followed in 1951, and in 1956 the government reorganized the carrier as Turkish Airlines Inc., capitalized at TL 60 million ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/business/2019/05/21/turkish-airlines-celebrates-its-86th-year-in-the-sky-with-modern-fleet-of-336-aircraft)). By its 50th anniversary in 1983 it operated 30 aircraft and carried 2.5 million passengers ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/business/2019/05/21/turkish-airlines-celebrates-its-86th-year-in-the-sky-with-modern-fleet-of-336-aircraft)). In 2019 it flew 75.2 million passengers on a 336-aircraft fleet with 82% seat occupancy, generating TL 62.9 billion in revenue and TL 4 billion in profit across 309 destinations in 124 countries, with 15 subsidiaries including Turkish Technic, Turkish Cargo, and SunExpress ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/business/2019/05/21/turkish-airlines-celebrates-its-86th-year-in-the-sky-with-modern-fleet-of-336-aircraft); [anews.com.tr](https://www.anews.com.tr/turkey/2019/05/20/turkeys-flag-carrier-marks-its-86th-anniversary)). The September 2026 Skytrax honors — Europe's best airline for the 11th time, eight awards, fifth in the world — mark the current summit ([Türkiye Today](https://www.turkiyetoday.com/lifestyle/turkish-airlines-named-europes-best-airline-for-11th-time-3228475)).

## FAQ

### Why does Turkish Airlines fly to more countries than any other airline?

Its geography does the heavy lifting: Istanbul sits within narrowbody range of Europe, the Middle East, Central Asia, North and East Africa, and western Asia, making short- and medium-haul feeding routes profitable ([eturbonews](https://eturbonews.com/turkish-airlines-boeing-737-max-order/)). The airline claims the "most countries in the world" title, and Guinness World Records recognizes a network spanning six continents ([The Japan Times](https://www.thejapantimes.jp/Boulevard/243208-turkish-airlines-makes-huge-airbus-order-in-bid-for-air-supremacy.html); [Türkiye Today](https://www.turkiyetoday.com/lifestyle/turkish-airlines-named-europes-best-airline-for-11th-time-3228475)).

### Who owns Turkish Airlines?

The Turkey Sovereign Wealth Fund holds 49.12% of the shares, and the rest are publicly traded on Borsa Istanbul ([anews.com.tr](https://www.anews.com.tr/turkey/2019/05/20/turkeys-flag-carrier-marks-its-86th-anniversary)). This near-half state stake is why the airline's biggest business decisions — airport moves, mega-orders, Oscar sponsorships — routinely carry national-policy significance ([eturbonews](https://eturbonews.com/turkish-airlines-boeing-737-max-order/)).

### Why did Turkish Airlines order from both Airbus and Boeing?

It is a deliberate dual-supplier strategy. With a 2033 target of more than 800 aircraft, depending on one manufacturer concentrates risk in backlogs, engine supply, certification slips, and production interruptions ([eturbonews](https://eturbonews.com/turkish-airlines-boeing-737-max-order/)). Keeping Airbus and Boeing competing gives the airline leverage on aircraft prices, financing, spare parts, training, and delivery slots ([eturbonews](https://eturbonews.com/turkish-airlines-boeing-737-max-order/)).

### Was the Boeing order part of a political deal?

The 2025 Boeing announcement followed a Washington meeting between Turkey's and the United States' presidents, and President Erdoğan attended the 2026 signing ([eturbonews](https://eturbonews.com/turkish-airlines-boeing-737-max-order/)). But there is no publicly available evidence of an aircraft-for-concessions bargain — commercial aviation and defense procurement moved through the bilateral relationship simultaneously, which is not the same as a deal ([eturbonews](https://eturbonews.com/turkish-airlines-boeing-737-max-order/)).

### What is the Turkish DO&CO partnership?

Turkish DO&CO is Turkish Airlines' catering partner, and its food program is a competitive asset: at the 2026 Skytrax awards the airline won World's Best Business Class Onboard Catering for the tenth time, with Skytrax's CEO singling out the Turkish DO&CO partnership for setting industry standards ([Türkiye Today](https://www.turkiyetoday.com/lifestyle/turkish-airlines-named-europes-best-airline-for-11th-time-3228475)).
