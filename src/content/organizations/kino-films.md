**Kino Films** (株式会社キノフィルムズ, Kino Films Co., Ltd.) is a Tokyo-based Japanese film production and distribution company owned by the [Kinoshita Group](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/51f8ded0e900b5cf9a5ea9528af9595575bdc4d3) and led by Naoya Kinoshita, the group's president and CEO ([Nikkan Sports](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/51f8ded0e900b5cf9a5ea9528af9595575bdc4d3)). It is not the similarly named American distributor Kino Lorber. In 2025 it distributed *Conclave* in Japan to a gross past ¥1 billion, and in 2026 it handled the Japanese release of the [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) biopic [*Michael*](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/) — a ¥8.66 billion Japanese run, one of 2026's biggest foreign-film hits in the country ([Mainichi Shimbun](https://mainichi.jp/articles/20261005/spp/sp0/006/242000c); [Mid-day](https://www.mid-day.com/amp/entertainment/hollywood-news/article/michael-jackson-biopic-delayed-to-now-release-in-april-2026-instead-of-october-2025-23586360)).

## A Kinoshita Group film company

Kino Films operates as the film arm of the Kinoshita Group under Naoya Kinoshita, who Nikkan Sports describes as a lifelong film obsessive — president and group CEO of Kinoshita Group at 60, leading Kino Films' distribution of foreign titles and production of Japanese ones ([Nikkan Sports](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/51f8ded0e900b5cf9a5ea9528af9595575bdc4d3)). The business spans planning and production of video content, theatrical distribution, and film investment. By 2025, the company had self-produced, invested in, or distributed roughly 520 Japanese and foreign films released in Japan — a scale that makes it one of the country's most prolific independent film outfits, even though it operates outside the Toei–Shochiku major-studio system ([Nikkan Sports](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/51f8ded0e900b5cf9a5ea9528af9595575bdc4d3)). Kinoshita's investment appetite is long-standing: the group had participated in every one of actress Sayuri Yoshinaga's starring films since Toei's 2008 *The Tale of Yamataikoku*, before Kino Films itself took on producing and distributing her films ([Nikkan Sports](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/51f8ded0e900b5cf9a5ea9528af9595575bdc4d3)).

## Foreign-film distribution: Conclave and Michael

Kino Films' breakout as a foreign-film distributor came with *Conclave*, Edward Berger's US–UK co-production about a papal election. Released in Japan on March 20, 2025, it won the foreign-film prize at the 38th Nikkan Sports Film Awards after an extraordinary coincidence: Pope Francis died on April 21, 2025, a real conclave began on May 7, and the first American-born pope, Leo XIV, was elected on May 8 — a run of real-world events that, in Kinoshita's own words, created a once-in-a-generation tailwind, carrying the film's Japanese gross past ¥1 billion ([Nikkan Sports](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/51f8ded0e900b5cf9a5ea9528af9595575bdc4d3)). The company's largest release followed: in March 2024 it struck a deal with Lionsgate and GK Films to distribute the Michael Jackson biopic *Michael* in Japan, announced by Lionsgate international president Helen Lee-Kim and Kinoshita ([MJVibe](https://www.mjvibe.com/kino-films-to-distribute-michael-jackson-biopic-in-japan/)). The split gave Lionsgate the US, Universal the rest of the world, and Kino Films Japan ([Mid-day](https://www.mid-day.com/amp/entertainment/hollywood-news/article/michael-jackson-biopic-delayed-to-now-release-in-april-2026-instead-of-october-2025-23586360)). The film opened in Japan on June 12, 2026 and had contributed $24.4 million to the international total by early July ([ComingSoon](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2158649-michael-jackson-biopic-box-office-1-billion)); by October its Japanese gross reached ¥8.66 billion ([Mainichi Shimbun](https://mainichi.jp/articles/20261005/spp/sp0/006/242000c)).

## Domestic production, investment, and the move into the US

Alongside foreign distribution, Kino Films produces and invests in Japanese films. Its most symbolic production credit is Sayuri Yoshinaga's 124th film, *てっぺんの向こうにあなたがいる* (released October 31, 2025), on which Kinoshita served as executive producer — the first Yoshinaga-starring film produced and distributed outside the major studios, a milestone Nikkan Sports frames as proof of the company's ambitions in domestic production ([Nikkan Sports](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/51f8ded0e900b5cf9a5ea9528af9595575bdc4d3)). Kinoshita has also joined the production committee of Yoji Yamada's *TOKYO Taxi*, and Nikkan Sports describes the group's film investment as among the country's leading ([Nikkan Sports](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/51f8ded0e900b5cf9a5ea9528af9595575bdc4d3)). In 2024 the company established a [Los Angeles](/places/los-angeles/) office, operating from 2025 — investing in two Hollywood films and buying several screenplays, with eventual self-production in the US stated as the long-term goal ([Nikkan Sports](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/51f8ded0e900b5cf9a5ea9528af9595575bdc4d3)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Kino Films?**
Kino Films (株式会社キノフィルムズ) is a Tokyo-based Japanese film production and distribution company owned by the Kinoshita Group and led by Naoya Kinoshita, the group's president and CEO. Its business covers planning and producing video content, theatrical distribution, and film investment, across both Japanese and foreign titles.

**Is Kino Films the same as Kino Lorber?**
No. Kino Films Co., Ltd. is a Japanese company owned by the Kinoshita Group, led by Naoya Kinoshita. Kino Lorber is a separately owned American distributor. The two share only a similar name.

**What is Kino Films' biggest release?**
The Japanese release of the 2026 Michael Jackson biopic *Michael*, which it distributed under a deal with Lionsgate and GK Films. The film opened in Japan on June 12, 2026 and grossed ¥8.66 billion there, making it one of 2026's biggest foreign-film hits in Japan — ahead of *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* (¥5.81B) and *The Devil Wears Prada 2* (¥5.47B) in Mainichi Shimbun's October 2026 tally.

**Who owns Kino Films?**
The Kinoshita Group. Naoya Kinoshita, the group's president and CEO, leads Kino Films; he is also credited as executive producer on the company's domestic productions.

**Does Kino Films operate outside Japan?**
Yes. In 2024 it established an office in Los Angeles, operating from 2025. It has invested in two Hollywood films and bought several screenplays, with the stated long-term goal of producing its own films in the United States.
