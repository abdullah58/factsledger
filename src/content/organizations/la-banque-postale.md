## Overview

La Banque Postale is the postal bank of [France](/places/france/), created on 1 January 2006 as a subsidiary of [La Poste](/organizations/la-poste/) and wholly owned by it ([CNP Assurances](https://www.cnp.fr/en/the-cnp-assurances-group/who-we-are/the-governance/shareholder)). It sells banking and insurance through the post-office network and online, and since 2022 it has owned [CNP Assurances](/organizations/cnp-assurances/) outright, after a takeover bid at €21.90 a share that delisted the insurer ([Eurex](https://www.eurex.com/ex-en/rules-regs/corporate-actions/corporate-action-information/CNP-Assurances-S.A.-Takeover-Updated-06-June-2022--2827330)).

In 2024 it reported net banking income of €7,553 million and attributable net profit of €1,186 million, on total assets of €741 billion ([La Banque Postale](https://www.labanquepostale.fr/content/dam/lbp/documents/communiques-de-presse/2025/PR-Results-FY24.pdf)).

## A bank built on the post-office network

Postal banking in France predates the bank itself by more than a century. Post offices were authorised to offer savings products in 1875, the Caisse Nationale d'Epargne was created in 1881, letting savers use any post office as a savings bank, and postal current accounts arrived under the law of 7 January 1918 ([FundingUniverse](https://www.fundinguniverse.com/company-histories/la-poste-history/)). In 1988 La Poste and the [Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations](/organizations/caisse-des-depots-et-consignations/) set up Sogéposte as their financial subsidiary; in 1997 the government forced La Poste to detach its financial services from mail ([FundingUniverse](https://www.fundinguniverse.com/company-histories/la-poste-history/)). The banking activities then became a distinct subsidiary, La Banque Postale, on 1 January 2006, to comply with the law ([FundingUniverse](https://www.fundinguniverse.com/company-histories/la-poste-history/)). Its model is distinctive: it reaches customers through the post-office network rather than branches of its own, and carries the public-service mission of banking accessibility entrusted to La Poste by the law of 2 July 1990 ([La Poste Groupe](https://lapostegroupe.net.extra.laposte.fr/en/our-public-service-missions)). Moody's rates the bank A2 and ranks it the sixth largest bank in France by total assets ([Moody's](https://www.caissedesdepots.fr/sites/cdc.fr/files/2025-12/Moody's%20Credit_Opinion-Caisse-Des-Depots-et-30%20October%202025.pdf)).

## The CNP Assurances takeover

On 30 August 2018 the economy minister announced a public financial pole, and on 4 March 2020 La Banque Postale became CNP Assurances' majority shareholder with 62.1%, after the competition authority cleared it unconditionally in December 2019 ([La Banque Postale](https://www.labanquepostale.com/content/dam/lbp/documents/communiques-de-presse/en/2020/CP-creation-large-public-financial-pole-2020.pdf); [Autorité de la concurrence](https://www.autoritedelaconcurrence.fr/en/print/pdf/node/6782)). On 28 October 2021 the bank announced it would buy [Groupe BPCE](/organizations/groupe-bpce/)'s 16.1% stake at €21.90 per share, taking it to 78.9%, and offer minorities the same price, aiming to squeeze out minorities and delist ([CNP Assurances](https://www.cnp.fr/en/the-cnp-assurances-group/newsroom/press-releases/2021/la-banque-postale-and-groupe-bpce-intend-to-streamline-their-shareholding-relationships-and-strengthen-their-industrial-partnerships)). The €21.90 price was a 36.2% premium and valued CNP at ~€15 billion, and the shares jumped 35% on the announcement ([Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2021/10/28/639703.htm)). The offer ran from 2 to 31 May 2022, and on 3 June 2022 the bank announced all conditions met, holding 97.79% of the capital and 98.56% of the voting rights, and requested the mandatory squeeze-out ([Eurex](https://www.eurex.com/ex-en/rules-regs/corporate-actions/corporate-action-information/CNP-Assurances-S.A.-Takeover-Updated-06-June-2022--2827330)). The squeeze-out was confirmed 7 June, the buyback was triggered on 20 June 2022 at the €20.90 ex-dividend price, and CNP was delisted ([CNP Assurances](https://www.cnp.fr/en/the-cnp-assurances-group/who-we-are/the-governance/shareholder)).

## A bancassurer with a public-service mandate

Buying CNP turned the postal bank into a bancassurer. In 2022 the group reported attributable net profit of €1,060 million, launched its corporate and investment bank on 1 January, and built wealth management around Louvre Banque Privée, with €73 billion under management at year-end ([La Poste Groupe](https://www.lapostegroupe.com/en/news/la-banque-postale-2022-annual-results)). By 2024 the model was producing growth: net profit rose 19.1% to €1,186 million on net banking income of €7,553 million, with the cost-income ratio improving to 64.6% and a CET1 ratio of 17.8% ([La Banque Postale](https://www.labanquepostale.fr/content/dam/lbp/documents/communiques-de-presse/2025/PR-Results-FY24.pdf)). Its corporate and local development loan originations rose 25.4% in 2024 to €20.3 billion ([La Banque Postale](https://www.labanquepostale.fr/content/dam/lbp/documents/communiques-de-presse/2025/PR-Results-FY24.pdf)) — growth in its oldest franchise, since it has been the leading lender to local authorities and public hospitals since 2015. The bank is a mission-led company: about 32% of its new loans in 2024 were sustainable financing, it holds a CDP "A" rating for climate action, and La Banque Postale is CNP's leading commercial partner, contributing 31% of the insurer's 2024 revenue ([La Banque Postale](https://www.labanquepostale.fr/content/dam/lbp/documents/communiques-de-presse/2025/PR-Results-FY24.pdf)).

## FAQ

### What is La Banque Postale?

It is France's postal bank, created on 1 January 2006 as a wholly owned subsidiary of La Poste. It provides retail banking, insurance through CNP Assurances, asset and wealth management, and lending to businesses and local authorities, mainly through the post-office network and online.

### Who owns La Banque Postale?

La Poste owns 100% of it. La Poste itself is 66% owned by the Caisse des Dépôts et Consignations and 34% by the French State, so La Banque Postale sits inside France's public financial group at one remove from the state.

### How did La Banque Postale acquire CNP Assurances?

It became majority shareholder with 62.1% in March 2020, then announced in October 2021 it would buy Groupe BPCE's 16.1% stake at €21.90 a share and offer the same price to minorities. The offer ran in May 2022, and after a squeeze-out the insurer was delisted on 20 June 2022.

### Is La Banque Postale a big bank?

Yes, by French standards. Moody's ranks it the sixth largest bank in France by total assets, with €741 billion of total assets at the end of 2024. It reported attributable net profit of €1,186 million in 2024 and a CET1 ratio of 17.8%.
