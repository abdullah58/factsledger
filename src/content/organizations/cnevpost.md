## Overview

CnEVPost is an English-language news website covering China's electric vehicle industry, founded in 2020 by Phate Zhang, a former China Daily and [Reuters](/organizations/reuters/) journalist. Based in [China](/places/china/), it reports on EV makers, monthly deliveries, battery supply chains, and policy, and its sales data is regularly cited by international EV press such as InsideEVs ([Telum Media](https://www.telummedia.com/public/media-industry-news/telum-talks-to-phate-zhang-founder-cntechpost-cnevpost/q0lemj43vk); [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/759580/gm-overtakes-tesla-china-nev-sales-april/)).

The site exists because of a gap Zhang saw from inside China: developments in the US tech industry were reported in Chinese media almost in real time, while much of what was interesting about Chinese tech companies was not searchable in English-language coverage at all ([Telum Media](https://www.telummedia.com/public/media-industry-news/telum-talks-to-phate-zhang-founder-cntechpost-cnevpost/q0lemj43vk)). CnEVPost is the EV half of a two-site project — its sister site CnTechPost covers Chinese technology more broadly — and its reporting leans heavily on data: monthly sales and registration figures, weekly insurance registrations, model specifications, and raw-material prices ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/industry/page/155/); [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2023/05/08/introducing-cnevdata/)).

## A one-journalist answer to a coverage gap

Zhang had been a journalist since 2009, starting at China Daily, then working at Reuters and spending six years at the financial media outlet Wallstreetcn, where he covered macroeconomics, financial markets, and company news ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/author/phate-zhang/)). In 2019 he bought a cloud-server package for CNY 79 (about $12) a year and built a Chinese-language blog about consumer electronics — phones, network-attached storage — but after two months it had gained little readership, and he concluded the tips it offered were already available through search engines ([Telum Media](https://www.telummedia.com/public/media-industry-news/telum-talks-to-phate-zhang-founder-cntechpost-cnevpost/q0lemj43vk)).

That failure produced the idea that became the business. Being in China, Zhang could see Chinese tech developments first-hand and report them in English, so he launched CnTechPost in late 2019 with the mission of telling English readers about Chinese tech companies and industry dynamics, and discontinued the Chinese blog ([Telum Media](https://www.telummedia.com/public/media-industry-news/telum-talks-to-phate-zhang-founder-cntechpost-cnevpost/q0lemj43vk)). About a year later, EV coverage on CnTechPost had grown so fast that Zhang worried it would distract from the site's tech focus and turn CnTechPost into, in effect, CnEVPost — so he created CnEVPost as a dedicated channel for Chinese EV and related technology developments ([Telum Media](https://www.telummedia.com/public/media-industry-news/telum-talks-to-phate-zhang-founder-cntechpost-cnevpost/q0lemj43vk)).

The two sites are separate websites but, in Zhang's words, "two different channels under one project": tech stories go to CnTechPost and EV stories to CnEVPost ([Telum Media](https://www.telummedia.com/public/media-industry-news/telum-talks-to-phate-zhang-founder-cntechpost-cnevpost/q0lemj43vk)). By the end of 2020 CnTechPost was generating some Google AdSense revenue — not a large amount, but enough to show potential — and Zhang resigned from his employer, leaving completely at the beginning of April 2021 to work on the sites full-time; that year he hired two part-time workers to cover more of the field ([Telum Media](https://www.telummedia.com/public/media-industry-news/telum-talks-to-phate-zhang-founder-cntechpost-cnevpost/q0lemj43vk)).

## What it covers and how it reports

CnEVPost describes its beat as China's electric vehicle industry, the world's largest market for new energy vehicles (NEVs), covering industry-wide developments from monthly sales and registration data released by the China Passenger Car Association (CPCA) and the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers (CAAM) to the subsidies and regulations shaping the market ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/industry/page/155/)). Its coverage includes NEV penetration trends, price wars and market competition, charging infrastructure, China's EV exports and overseas expansion, tariffs and trade policy, supply-chain news, and auto shows ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/industry/page/155/)).

The reporting method is deliberately small-scale and source-first. Zhang monitors dozens of websites and a large number of Weibo and Twitter accounts every day for leads, confirms each lead, and then writes his own story from the source of that lead — a company announcement, Weibo content, or video ([Telum Media](https://www.telummedia.com/public/media-industry-news/telum-talks-to-phate-zhang-founder-cntechpost-cnevpost/q0lemj43vk)). He writes in the simplest possible expressions, partly because English is not his native language and partly to be understandable to everyone, and avoids expressing emotion in stories in favour of a neutral tone ([Telum Media](https://www.telummedia.com/public/media-industry-news/telum-talks-to-phate-zhang-founder-cntechpost-cnevpost/q0lemj43vk)). That neutrality is a stated principle, not an accident: Zhang told Telum Media that after ten years in the industry it was clear neutral reporting "does not spread nearly as widely as reporting with emotion", but that he believed it was "the basis for long-term growth as a media outlet" and would stick to it rather than exaggerate ([Telum Media](https://www.telummedia.com/public/media-industry-news/telum-talks-to-phate-zhang-founder-cntechpost-cnevpost/q0lemj43vk)).

The capital-markets background shows in what the site chooses to track. Its author bio says Zhang's reporting history "shapes CnEVPost's focus: the business and investment side of China's EV industry — earnings, deliveries, analyst research, and policy shifts that move stocks" ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/author/phate-zhang/)). Early evidence of that focus is the site's October 8, 2020 report that Chinese EV maker [Nio](/organizations/nio/) had selected [Cerence](/organizations/cerence/) (Nasdaq: CRNC) for a collaboration on voice technologies in its vehicles — a company-level, investor-relevant story published in the site's first months ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2020/10/08/nio-cerence-collaboration-on-voice-tech/)).

## CnEVData and the Bloomberg Terminal feed

Because so much of its coverage is data-based, CnEVPost accumulated a large internal database over its first years — and on May 8, 2023 it began sharing it as CnEVData, a separate membership-based website ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2023/05/08/introducing-cnevdata/)). CnEVData publishes raw data in table form on the delivery volumes of the automakers CnEVPost covers, plus sales data for makers it does not cover with articles, such as SAIC and Seres Group; it also shares the weekly insurance-registration data the team obtains, key specifications of new EV models in a unified format, and EV supply-chain data including daily lithium carbonate quotes ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2023/05/08/introducing-cnevdata/)). The membership system is Patreon-based, and only patrons can view the details of the data ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2023/05/08/introducing-cnevdata/)).

The data work also reached professional terminals. In October 2022, CarNewsChina announced it had teamed up with Phate Zhang of CnEVPost and begun implementing an API feed for the [Bloomberg](/organizations/bloomberg/) Terminal, alongside a new product for retail investors, consultants, and journalists called China-Crunch, offering Chinese EV sales data, event calendars, an EV specification database, and news before it appears on the free website ([CarNewsChina](https://carnewschina.com/2022/10/13/china-crunch-launched-do-you-want-our-news-even-faster-and-more/)). The partnership put CnEVPost's reporting pipeline next to a data feed aimed at the same professional audience its coverage already served.

## Why other outlets cite it

CnEVPost's clearest mark of standing is that international EV press treats it as a source rather than a competitor. In February 2023, InsideEVs reported — citing a CnEVPost article via Electrek — that CATL was responsible for 37% of all EV battery sales across the globe in 2022, and that the battery maker would offer strategic customers including Huawei, Li Auto, Nio, and Zeekr a price deal tied to lithium carbonate at RMB 200,000 per ton ([InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/653620/tesla-not-benefit-catl-strategic-partner-cost-cuts/)). Two years later, in May 2025, InsideEVs again leaned on CnEVPost, citing CPCA data via the site to report that Tesla had dropped from third to eighth in China's April EV market share — 3.2%, down from 4.6% a year earlier — while BYD led with 29.7%, followed by the [Geely](/organizations/geely/) Group at 13.1% ([InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/759580/gm-overtakes-tesla-china-nev-sales-april/)). The implication is direct: a site founded by one journalist in 2020 had, within a few years, become a reference point other newsrooms check when they need Chinese EV numbers in English.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who founded CnEVPost?**
Phate Zhang founded CnEVPost in 2020 and is its Editor-in-Chief. He had been a journalist since 2009, working at China Daily and Reuters before six years at the financial outlet Wallstreetcn ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/author/phate-zhang/); [Telum Media](https://www.telummedia.com/public/media-industry-news/telum-talks-to-phate-zhang-founder-cntechpost-cnevpost/q0lemj43vk)).

**What does CnEVPost cover?**
China's electric vehicle industry: automaker deliveries, monthly CPCA and CAAM sales data, weekly insurance registrations, new-model launches and specifications, earnings, charging infrastructure, exports, tariffs, and supply-chain news ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/industry/page/155/)).

**What is CnEVData?**
A separate membership-based website launched by CnEVPost on May 8, 2023, sharing the raw data behind its reporting — delivery volumes, weekly insurance registrations, model specifications, and lithium carbonate quotes — with access via Patreon membership ([CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2023/05/08/introducing-cnevdata/)).

**What is CnTechPost?**
The sister site to CnEVPost, launched by Zhang in late 2019 to cover Chinese technology companies in English. The two sites are separate websites but operate as two channels under one project: tech stories go to CnTechPost, EV stories to CnEVPost ([Telum Media](https://www.telummedia.com/public/media-industry-news/telum-talks-to-phate-zhang-founder-cntechpost-cnevpost/q0lemj43vk)).

**Is CnEVPost's reporting used by other news outlets?**
Yes. InsideEVs has cited CnEVPost's reporting and data — on CATL's 37% share of global EV battery sales in 2022, and on CPCA figures showing Tesla's fall to eighth in China's April 2025 EV market share — and CarNewsChina partnered with its founder on a Bloomberg Terminal data feed ([InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/653620/tesla-not-benefit-catl-strategic-partner-cost-cuts/); [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/759580/gm-overtakes-tesla-china-nev-sales-april/); [CarNewsChina](https://carnewschina.com/2022/10/13/china-crunch-launched-do-you-want-our-news-even-faster-and-more/)).
