## Overview

The U.S. Department of the Treasury is the federal government's finance department, created by Congress in 1789 and first led by Alexander Hamilton ([U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/about/history/history-overview/history-of-the-treasury)). It collects federal taxes through the [Internal Revenue Service](/organizations/internal-revenue-service/), manages the national debt, produces the nation's currency, and enforces economic sanctions through the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) ([U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/about/history/history-overview/history-of-the-treasury); [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/about-irs/the-agency-its-mission-and-statutory-authority)). Since January 2025 it has been led by Secretary [Scott Bessent](/people/scott-bessent/), the 79th Treasury secretary, confirmed by the Senate 68-29 ([Reuters](https://hot96.com/2025/01/27/scott-bessent-wins-senate-confirmation-as-us-treasury-secretary/)). In recent years the department has made crypto-related sanctions a central weapon against illicit finance, designating the mixer [Tornado Cash](/organizations/tornado-cash/) in 2022 and the ruble-backed [A7A5](/organizations/a7a5/) token network in 2025 ([U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/press-releases-united-states-department-treasury-6111/us-treasury-sanctions-notorious-virtual-currency-mixer-tornado-cash-619309/fulltext); [U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/treaspr/2025/2025-08-14_sb0225.pdf?utm_source=direct_download)).

## A two-century-old fiscal department

Congress created the Department of the Treasury on September 2, 1789, as a permanent institution for managing government finances, with a Secretary as its head alongside a Comptroller, Auditor, Treasurer, Register, and an Assistant to the Secretary ([U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/about/history/history-overview/history-of-the-treasury)). Alexander Hamilton, appointed by George Washington, took the oath as the first Secretary on September 11, 1789, and served until 1795 ([U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/about/history/history-overview/history-of-the-treasury)). His first official act was a report to Congress insisting on federal assumption and dollar-for-dollar repayment of the $75 million war debt, plus revenue from customs duties and the Bank of the United States as the government's fiscal agent ([U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/about/history/history-overview/history-of-the-treasury)). During the Civil War, the loss of Southern customs revenues led to the creation of the Bureau of Internal Revenue and paper-currency printing ([U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/about/history/history-overview/history-of-the-treasury)). Many functions of the federal government were first placed under Treasury — the Postal Service (until 1829), the Coast Guard (until 1967), and the Bureau of Narcotics, whose functions moved to the Justice Department as today's DEA — before other departments took them over ([U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/about/history/history-overview/history-of-the-treasury)). Treasury's role at the 1944 Bretton Woods Conference established the International Monetary Fund and the World Bank, chaired by Secretary Henry Morgenthau Jr., the 52nd Secretary (1934–1945) ([U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/about/history/history-overview/history-of-the-treasury)).

## The sanctions machine: OFAC and financial-intelligence enforcement

The department's economic-sanctions power runs through the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC), under the Under Secretary of the Treasury for Terrorism and Financial Intelligence ([U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/treaspr/2025/2025-08-14_sb0225.pdf?utm_source=direct_download); [U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/press-releases-united-states-department-treasury-6111/us-treasury-sanctions-notorious-virtual-currency-mixer-tornado-cash-619309/fulltext)). Adding a person or entity to OFAC's Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list blocks their U.S.-jurisdiction property and bars U.S. persons from transacting with them; the stated goal is "a positive change in behavior," not punishment ([U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/treaspr/2025/2025-08-14_sb0225.pdf?utm_source=direct_download)).

Its two landmark crypto sanctions actions show how the tool works in practice:

| | Tornado Cash (August 8, 2022) | Garantex / Grinex / A7A5 (August 14, 2025) |
|---|---|---|
| **Target** | Virtual currency mixer Tornado Cash, created 2019 | Crypto exchange Garantex (redesignated), successor exchange Grinex, A7 network, Old Vector |
| **Legal authority** | E.O. 13694 (malicious cyber-enabled activities); followed the May 6, 2022 Blender.io designation | E.O. 13694 as amended (cyber authorities); Garantex originally designated April 5, 2022 under E.O. 14024 |
| **Scale alleged** | Laundered more than $7 billion since 2019, including $455 million by the DPRK's Lazarus Group, $96M+ from the Harmony Bridge heist, $7.8M from the Nomad heist | Garantex processed over $100 million linked to illicit activities since 2019; Grinex moved billions after taking over Garantex's customers |
| **Crypto hook** | The mixer itself was the laundering tool | The ruble-backed A7A5 token, issued by Kyrgyzstani firm Old Vector, became blocked property |
| **Treasury message** | Under Secretary Brian E. Nelson: mixers should "in general be considered as high-risk" and Treasury would "aggressively pursue" them | Under Secretary John K. Hurley: Treasury supports the digital asset industry's integrity and "will not tolerate abuse of this industry to support cybercrime and sanctions evasion" |

## The multi-tool playbook: how a crypto-enforcement action actually works

Beyond designations, Treasury's crypto enforcement is a whole-of-government operation. The August 2025 action illustrates the playbook: when Garantex was hit, its customers and funds migrated to successor exchange Grinex, built by Garantex officers — so OFAC designated the successor too, along with the executives and partner companies behind the workaround ([U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/treaspr/2025/2025-08-14_sb0225.pdf?utm_source=direct_download)). The action also froze the rails themselves: the A7A5 token, created for Russian customers of A7 LLC — which Treasury says runs sanctions-evasion settlement platforms and is owned by sanctioned Moldovan businessman Ilan Mironovich Shor and sanctioned Russian bank Promsvyazbank (PSB) — was declared blocked property ([U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/treaspr/2025/2025-08-14_sb0225.pdf?utm_source=direct_download)). The playbook extends past Treasury's walls: the action followed a March 2025 Secret Service operation with German and Finnish law enforcement that seized Garantex's domain and froze over $26 million in crypto, Justice Department indictments of Garantex executives, and State Department reward offers of up to $5 million ([U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/treaspr/2025/2025-08-14_sb0225.pdf?utm_source=direct_download)). Treasury also layers fines: in 2020, FinCEN assessed a $60 million civil money penalty against a mixer operator for Bank Secrecy Act violations ([U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/press-releases-united-states-department-treasury-6111/us-treasury-sanctions-notorious-virtual-currency-mixer-tornado-cash-619309/fulltext)).

## Recent leadership and scrutiny

The Senate confirmed Scott Bessent as the 79th Treasury secretary on January 27, 2025, by a 68-29 vote with 16 Democrats supporting the nomination ([Reuters](https://hot96.com/2025/01/27/scott-bessent-wins-senate-confirmation-as-us-treasury-secretary/)). [Reuters](/organizations/reuters/) described his remit: sway over the nation's tax collections and the $28 trillion Treasury debt market, with vast influence over fiscal policy, financial regulations, international sanctions, and overseas investment ([Reuters](https://hot96.com/2025/01/27/scott-bessent-wins-senate-confirmation-as-us-treasury-secretary/)). As fifth in line to the presidency, he is also the highest-ranking openly gay federal official in history ([Reuters](https://hot96.com/2025/01/27/scott-bessent-wins-senate-confirmation-as-us-treasury-secretary/)).

In November 2025, Senators [Elizabeth Warren](/people/elizabeth-warren/) and [Jack Reed](/people/jack-reed/) sent a letter to Secretary Bessent and Attorney General Pamela Bondi calling for information on any efforts to investigate [World Liberty Financial](/organizations/world-liberty-financial/), a Trump-backed crypto venture, over reports it sold governance tokens to buyers linked to North Korea, Russia, and other illicit actors ([Senate Banking Committee](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/as-congress-considers-potential-market-structure-legislation-warren-and-reed-press-doj-and-treasury-on-national-security-risks-posed-by-trump-affiliated-crypto-ventures-sale-of-tokens-to-illicit-actors)). Citing [Accountable.US](/organizations/accountable-us/)'s September 2025 report, they argued the sales raised "serious questions about WLF's due diligence policies or procedures, including whether its tokens or other products enable sanctions evasion, money laundering, and terrorist financing," and requested answers by December 1, 2025 ([Senate Banking Committee](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/as-congress-considers-potential-market-structure-legislation-warren-and-reed-press-doj-and-treasury-on-national-security-risks-posed-by-trump-affiliated-crypto-ventures-sale-of-tokens-to-illicit-actors)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What does the U.S. Department of the Treasury do?** Treasury is the federal government's finance department, created by Congress in 1789 ([U.S. Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/about/history/history-overview/history-of-the-treasury)). It collects federal taxes through the IRS, manages the national debt, and enforces economic sanctions through OFAC ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/about-irs/the-agency-its-mission-and-statutory-authority); [Reuters](https://hot96.com/2025/01/27/scott-bessent-wins-senate-confirmation-as-us-treasury-secretary/)).

**Who is the current Treasury Secretary?** Scott Bessent, confirmed by the Senate 68-29 on January 27, 2025, as the 79th Secretary of the Treasury ([Reuters](https://hot96.com/2025/01/27/scott-bessent-wins-senate-confirmation-as-us-treasury-secretary/)). He is the highest-ranking openly gay federal official in history and fifth in line to the presidency ([Reuters](https://hot96.com/2025/01/27/scott-bessent-wins-senate-confirmation-as-us-treasury-secretary/)).

**Is the IRS part of the Treasury Department?** Yes. The IRS describes itself as "a bureau of the Department of the Treasury," organized to carry out the Secretary's responsibilities under section 7801 of the Internal Revenue Code, with a Commissioner appointed under section 7803 ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/about-irs/the-agency-its-mission-and-statutory-authority)).

**Why did Treasury sanction Tornado Cash?** On August 8, 2022, OFAC sanctioned the mixer under E.O. 13694 after finding it had laundered more than $7 billion since 2019, including $455 million by North Korea's Lazarus Group ([U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/press-releases-united-states-department-treasury-6111/us-treasury-sanctions-notorious-virtual-currency-mixer-tornado-cash-619309/fulltext)). Under Secretary Brian E. Nelson said such mixers should "in general be considered as high-risk" by the industry ([U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/press-releases-united-states-department-treasury-6111/us-treasury-sanctions-notorious-virtual-currency-mixer-tornado-cash-619309/fulltext)).

**What is the A7A5 token that Treasury targeted in 2025?** A7A5 is a ruble-backed digital asset issued by Kyrgyzstani firm Old Vector for Russian customers of A7 LLC, which Treasury says runs sanctions-evasion settlement platforms and is owned by sanctioned businessman Ilan Mironovich Shor and bank Promsvyazbank ([U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/treaspr/2025/2025-08-14_sb0225.pdf?utm_source=direct_download)). On August 14, 2025, OFAC designated Old Vector and declared A7A5 blocked property ([U.S. Treasury via FRASER](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/treaspr/2025/2025-08-14_sb0225.pdf?utm_source=direct_download)).
