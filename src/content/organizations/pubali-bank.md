## Overview

Pubali Bank PLC — one of Bangladesh's largest private commercial lenders ([TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/pubali-bank-emerges-potential-buyer-standard-chartered-retail-business-1556296)) — employs Everest mountaineers. In May 2026 the bank sponsored the Everest expedition of [Nurunnahar Nimni](/people/nurunnahar-nimni/), a Principal Officer in its own General Banking Division; on 27 May she became the third Bangladeshi woman to summit Everest, the first in 14 years ([Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/lifestyle/n3rzdszry9); [Bangladesh Pratidin](https://en.bd-pratidin.com/national/2026/05/27/63432)). It is a detail no bank profile leads with, but it captures something real about Pubali: an old institution whose roots go back to 1959, and one that its chief executive markets on people — the meritorious young workforce he boasts about, and the climber on the payroll ([Financial Express](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/special-issues/banks-of-excellence/pubali-banks-enviable-feat-in-a-dismal-banking-scenario-1789413263)).

## Three lives of one bank

The bank began in 1959 as Eastern Mercantile Bank Limited in East Pakistan, set up by Bengali entrepreneurs under the Bank Companies Act 1913 to give Bengali businesses credit they could not get elsewhere ([Financial Express](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/special-issues/banks-of-excellence/pubali-banks-enviable-feat-in-a-dismal-banking-scenario-1789413263)). After Bangladesh's independence it was nationalised in 1972 and renamed Pubali Bank; in 1983 it was denationalised and returned to the private sector as Pubali Bank Limited ([Financial Express](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/special-issues/banks-of-excellence/pubali-banks-enviable-feat-in-a-dismal-banking-scenario-1789413263)).

## From a 54% bad-loan book to the industry's best

The privatised bank inherited a wreckage: by 1984 classified loans had reached 54% and Pubali was tagged a "problem bank" ([Financial Express](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/special-issues/banks-of-excellence/pubali-banks-enviable-feat-in-a-dismal-banking-scenario-1789413263)). The new board's answer was a long-term revival plan built on governance "at every stage of the banking service" ([Financial Express](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/special-issues/banks-of-excellence/pubali-banks-enviable-feat-in-a-dismal-banking-scenario-1789413263)). It worked slowly — through crisis years in which staff went without increments in a poor working environment — until the bank shed the problem-bank tag in 2005 and Bangladesh Bank withdrew its administrator two years later, in 2007 ([Financial Express](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/special-issues/banks-of-excellence/pubali-banks-enviable-feat-in-a-dismal-banking-scenario-1789413263)). Managing Director and CEO Mohammad Ali calls the outcome "42 years of consistent strategy" ([Financial Express](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/special-issues/banks-of-excellence/pubali-banks-enviable-feat-in-a-dismal-banking-scenario-1789413263)).

The governance doctrine survived the revival: zero tolerance of fraud, top professionals in management, and an unusually deep pre-approval vetting of borrowers that weighs even the client's personal and social stability — "if a borrower's personal life is not stable, there is a risk that the loan could become non-performing," Ali said, adding that clients heavily involved in politics or leading "a care-free life" are generally discouraged from borrowing ([Financial Express](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/special-issues/banks-of-excellence/pubali-banks-enviable-feat-in-a-dismal-banking-scenario-1789413263)). The bank says 80% of its workforce is under 40 and more than half earned first-class results in all major exams of their academic careers ([Financial Express](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/special-issues/banks-of-excellence/pubali-banks-enviable-feat-in-a-dismal-banking-scenario-1789413263)).

## Scale, record 2025 results and the digital push

By the end of 2025 the bank operated 517 branches, 274 sub-branches, 22 Islamic Banking Windows and 901 ATM/CRM booths across Bangladesh ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/organisation-news/news/strong-asset-quality-and-secure-portfolio-pubali-bank-maintains-npl-220-4202366)). Its 2025 results were records: total assets up 20.25% to Tk118,013 crore, deposits of Tk89,519 crore (up more than 20%), loans and advances of Tk71,140 crore (up 13%), consolidated operating profit of Tk2,907 crore and net profit of Tk1,079 crore ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/organisation-news/news/strong-asset-quality-and-secure-portfolio-pubali-bank-maintains-npl-220-4202366)). Earnings per share stood at Tk8.30, return on equity at 15.51% and return on assets at 1.00%, with a capital-to-risk-weighted-assets ratio of 15.34%, comfortably above the regulatory requirement ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/organisation-news/news/strong-asset-quality-and-secure-portfolio-pubali-bank-maintains-npl-220-4202366)).

Asset quality is the bank's signature statistic: non-performing loans stood at 2.20% at end-2025 against an industry average of around 30.60%, which the bank attributes to prudent lending, data-driven monitoring and recovery ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/organisation-news/news/strong-asset-quality-and-secure-portfolio-pubali-bank-maintains-npl-220-4202366)). In trade finance it handled Tk38,664 crore of exports and Tk47,314 crore of imports in 2025, with inward remittances of Tk11,362 crore ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/organisation-news/news/strong-asset-quality-and-secure-portfolio-pubali-bank-maintains-npl-220-4202366)).

On digital, the PI platform reached 528,276 active users and 18.6 million transactions in 2025 — up 78% and 115% year-on-year — supported by 25,525 merchant POS terminals and more than 145,000 Bangla QR codes deployed nationwide ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/organisation-news/news/strong-asset-quality-and-secure-portfolio-pubali-bank-maintains-npl-220-4202366)). Nearly 30% of the loan portfolio qualified as sustainable finance; the bank put solar power in 411 branches and 453 ATM/CRM booths, financed 37 LEED-certified factories, and adopted a Net-Zero 2050 decarbonisation policy ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/organisation-news/news/strong-asset-quality-and-secure-portfolio-pubali-bank-maintains-npl-220-4202366)). Its Strategic Plan 2026-2028 includes a year-long Cashless Banking Campaign in 2026 ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/organisation-news/news/strong-asset-quality-and-secure-portfolio-pubali-bank-maintains-npl-220-4202366)).

The market has noticed: at the June 2026 annual general meeting, Ali said the share price had risen from Tk16-20 in 2022 to more than Tk37, and paid-up capital had grown about 56% — from nearly Tk1,000 crore to Tk1,560 crore — through successive stock dividends, more than tripling a long-term investor's market value ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/organisation-news/news/strong-asset-quality-and-secure-portfolio-pubali-bank-maintains-npl-220-4202366)).

## 2026: bidding for StanChart's retail book

In 2026 Pubali emerged as a potential buyer of Standard Chartered Bangladesh's retail banking business, alongside BRAC Bank and City Bank, as the British lender weighed exiting retail to focus on corporate and institutional banking; no deal had been finalised ([TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/pubali-bank-emerges-potential-buyer-standard-chartered-retail-business-1556296)). StanChart's retail and CMSME loan portfolio stood at about Tk8,479 crore at end-2025 with retail deposits of about Tk8,000 crore; Pubali's own retail loan book is around Tk8,000 crore — over 10% of its total loans, spanning personal, home, car and nano loans — and Ali has named retail banking a key future growth area ([TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/pubali-bank-emerges-potential-buyer-standard-chartered-retail-business-1556296)). The bank recorded about Tk685 crore in consolidated profit in the first half of 2026 and opened its 1,000th ATM/CRM booth during the year ([TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/pubali-bank-emerges-potential-buyer-standard-chartered-retail-business-1556296)).

Branch and financial figures are end-2025 and first-half 2026 as reported; the bank's own website returned an HTTP 403 to the fetch client, so no first-party figures are used. Nationalisation is dated 1972 per the bank's managing director in the Financial Express interview; some tertiary sources say 1971.
