## Overview

**Dread Central** is an American horror-news website founded in 2006, covering horror film, television, and culture through news, interviews, and reviews ([Variety](https://www.imdb.com/news/ni65647378/); [Be Afraid Media](https://www.einpresswire.com/article_pdf/880926852/be-afraid-media-launches-with-acquisition-of-horror-authority-dread-central)). It won the Rondo Hatton Classic Horror Award for Best Website four years running (2009–2012) ([Classic Horror Film Board](http://rondoaward.com/rondo/RONDOIXRESULTS.html)), spent 2017–2026 inside Epic Pictures Group — which built the DREAD label, DreadTV, and DreadXP around it ([Epic Pictures](https://dev.epic-pictures.com/modals/bio-modal.php?id=11)) — and returned to independent ownership in January 2026 under Brad Miska's Be Afraid Media ([Variety](https://www.imdb.com/news/ni65647378/)).

That independence matters: for two decades the site has been the outlet horror filmmakers hand exclusives to — from the 2011 PollyGrind Festival winners to the US-distribution deal for the era's most controversial horror film, [The Bunny Game](/creative-works/the-bunny-game/) ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/28065/exclusive-second-annual-pollygrind-film-festival-announces-winners/); [Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/31660/a-home-found-for-the-bunny-game/)).

## Two decades, two owners, one horror brand

Dread Central was founded in 2006 by co-founders Steve Barton and Jon Condit ([Notisia 365 / THR](https://www.notisia365.com/horror-shake-up-website-dread-central-gets-a-new-owner-in-bloody-disgusting-co-founder-brad-miska-exclusive/); [Be Afraid Media](https://www.einpresswire.com/article_pdf/880926852/be-afraid-media-launches-with-acquisition-of-horror-authority-dread-central)), and it earned its authority early: the Classic Horror Film Board's fan-voted Rondo awards named it Best Website in 2009, 2010, 2011, and 2012 — four straight wins, with the 2010 citation noting it had "repeated as Best Website" ([Classic Horror Film Board](http://rondoaward.com/rondo/RONDOIXRESULTS.html); [Classic Horror Film Board](http://rondoaward.com/rondo/RondoXwinners.html); [Classic Horror Film Board](http://rondoaward.com/rondo/rondos.html)). The electorate kept growing — over 2,900 ballots in 2011, a record 3,100-plus in 2012 — which implies the site's standing rested on a broad, engaged horror-fan base rather than trade-jury favor ([Classic Horror Film Board](http://rondoaward.com/rondo/RondoXwinners.html)). In 2017, after a financially precarious stretch, Epic Pictures Group acquired the site and built a horror empire on the brand: the DREAD distribution label, the DreadTV channel, and the DreadXP gaming site ([Epic Pictures](https://dev.epic-pictures.com/modals/bio-modal.php?id=11); [Notisia 365 / THR](https://www.notisia365.com/horror-shake-up-website-dread-central-gets-a-new-owner-in-bloody-disgusting-co-founder-brad-miska-exclusive/)).

## The outlet filmmakers hand exclusives to

That authority is what made the January 2026 sale notable: Be Afraid Media — led by Brad Miska, the Bloody Disgusting co-founder who had sold that rival site in 2021, alongside former Bloody Disgusting co-owners Heather and Peter Luttrell — bought Dread Central from Epic Pictures, taking the DreadTV channel with it and signing a multi-year streaming distribution agreement with Epic, whose CEO Patrick Ewald joined Be Afraid's board; Dread Releasing and DreadXP stayed with Epic ([Variety](https://www.imdb.com/news/ni65647378/); [Be Afraid Media](https://www.einpresswire.com/article_pdf/880926852/be-afraid-media-launches-with-acquisition-of-horror-authority-dread-central)). The pattern of trust predates the deal: in October 2011, PollyGrind founder Chad Clinton Freeman gave Dread Central the festival's winners exclusively — with *The Bunny Game* taking the top jury prize — and the site later reported Autonomy Pictures' SXSW 2012 acquisition of the film's US rights ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/28065/exclusive-second-annual-pollygrind-film-festival-announces-winners/); [Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/31660/a-home-found-for-the-bunny-game/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who owns Dread Central now?**
Since January 6, 2026, Dread Central has been owned by Be Afraid Media, an independent horror-media company led by Bloody Disgusting co-founder Brad Miska with Heather and Peter Luttrell, the digital-media executives and IndieClick founders who were former Bloody Disgusting co-owners. Epic Pictures CEO Patrick Ewald — the seller — joined Be Afraid's board as part of the transaction.

**What awards has Dread Central won?**
It won the Rondo Hatton Classic Horror Award for Best Website four years in a row, for 2009 through 2012, in fan votes run by the Classic Horror Film Board that drew up to 3,400-plus ballots. The award is named for Rondo Hatton, a 1940s B-movie villain, and recognizes horror research, creativity, and preservation.

**What is the DREAD label's relationship to Dread Central?**
Under Epic Pictures' ownership (2017–2026), the company launched the DREAD horror distribution label, the DreadTV streaming channel, and the DreadXP gaming site around the Dread Central brand. In the 2026 sale, the Dread Central site and DreadTV went to Be Afraid Media; Dread Releasing and DreadXP remained Epic Pictures businesses.

**How is Dread Central connected to The Bunny Game?**
Twice over: in October 2011, PollyGrind founder Chad Clinton Freeman gave the site the festival's winners exclusively, with The Bunny Game taking the top jury prize; and the site reported Autonomy Pictures' acquisition of the film's US rights at SXSW 2012. Both were first-party items on the site's own archive.
