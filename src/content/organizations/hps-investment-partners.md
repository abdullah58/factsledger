## Overview

HPS Investment Partners is a global credit-focused investment firm founded in 2007 inside [J.P. Morgan](/organizations/jpmorgan-chase/) Asset Management, bought out by its own principals in 2016, and acquired by [BlackRock](/organizations/blackrock/) for about $12 billion in July 2025 ([PitchBook](http://pitchbook.com/news/articles/blackrock-hps-purchase-record-year-gp-consolidation?utm_medium=newsletter&utm_source=daily_pitch&sourcetype=newsletter); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov:443/Archives/edgar/data/2012383/000119312525153573/d78785dex991.htm)). Headquartered in [New York](/places/new-york-city/), it manages about $186 billion as of March 2026 across direct lending, junior capital, liquid credit and asset-based finance, and its founders now lead BlackRock's Private Financing Solutions unit ([HPS Investment Partners](https://mycelium.global/emissions/hps-investment-partners/filing/2025.pdf); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov:443/Archives/edgar/data/2012383/000119312525153573/d78785dex991.htm)).

It began life as Highbridge Principal Strategies, the credit and private-equity platform of Highbridge Capital Management, a hedge fund house under the J.P. Morgan Asset Management umbrella ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bszq4ilqe2jaq0otdvy8/portfolio/highbridge-expands-credit-business)). Highbridge itself had been founded by childhood friends Glenn Dubin and Henry Swieca, and J.P. Morgan completed its purchase of the firm in 2009 ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bszq4ilqe2jaq0otdvy8/portfolio/highbridge-expands-credit-business)). HPS was created in 2007 after Highbridge brought in Scott Kapnick as chief executive; Kapnick had previously been a managing director at [Goldman Sachs](/organizations/goldman-sachs/), where he had been co-head of global banking ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bszq4ilqe2jaq0otdvy8/portfolio/highbridge-expands-credit-business); [IPE](https://www.ipe.com/news/blackrock-nears-12bn-acquisition-of-private-credit-behemoth-hps/10126645.article)). PitchBook records that Kapnick founded HPS together with Scot French and Michael Patterson ([PitchBook](http://pitchbook.com/news/articles/blackrock-hps-purchase-record-year-gp-consolidation?utm_medium=newsletter&utm_source=daily_pitch&sourcetype=newsletter)).

The growth since then tracks the rise of private credit itself: HPS passed $100 billion of assets under management in 2023 and reached about $186 billion by March 2026 ([HPS Investment Partners](https://www.hpspartners.com/our-story/history); [HPS Investment Partners](https://mycelium.global/emissions/hps-investment-partners/filing/2025.pdf)).

## Three owners in eighteen years

HPS has changed ownership twice, and each change marks a phase of the private credit market: it started as a unit of a bank-owned hedge fund, became independent, and then became part of the world's largest asset manager ([PitchBook](http://pitchbook.com/news/articles/blackrock-hps-purchase-record-year-gp-consolidation?utm_medium=newsletter&utm_source=daily_pitch&sourcetype=newsletter)).

The first phase was building the platform inside J.P. Morgan. HPS launched a liquid alternative credit strategy in 2007, added its junior private credit strategy, Strategic Investment Partners, and opened a [London](/places/london/) office in 2008, then introduced Specialty Direct Lending in 2010 and Core Senior Lending in 2013 ([HPS Investment Partners](https://www.hpspartners.com/our-story/history)). In 2015 it added asset-based lending and a [Sydney](/places/sydney/)-based team to cover Asia-Pacific ([HPS Investment Partners](https://www.hpspartners.com/our-story/history)). In 2017 it invested about $850 million of a $1.4 billion refinancing — one of the earliest private credit deals with a tranche above $1 billion ([HPS Investment Partners](https://www.hpspartners.com/our-story/history)).

The second phase began in 2016, when the firm's principals bought HPS from J.P. Morgan Asset Management, creating one of the largest independent, employee-owned firms specialising in non-investment-grade alternative credit ([HPS Investment Partners](https://www.hpspartners.com/our-story/history)). Independence brought outside minority capital: Dyal Capital Partners took a passive minority stake in 2018, the same year investment professionals from Tålamod Asset Management joined to build distressed and special-situations capability ([HPS Investment Partners](https://www.hpspartners.com/our-story/history)). Guardian Life Insurance acquired a strategic minority stake in 2022 and expanded the partnership in 2024, placing about $30 billion of assets under HPS management ([HPS Investment Partners](https://www.hpspartners.com/our-story/history)). The firm also moved toward individual investors, launching HLEND, its first non-traded business development company, in 2022 ([HPS Investment Partners](https://www.hpspartners.com/our-story/history)).

The third phase was the BlackRock deal. BlackRock announced the definitive agreement on 3 December 2024: about $12 billion, paid 100% in BlackRock equity through 12.1 million SubCo Units exchangeable one-for-one into BlackRock common stock, with 9.2 million units paid at closing, 2.9 million deferred about five years, up to 1.6 million more tied to performance milestones, and up to $675 million set aside for an employee retention pool ([BlackRock](https://ir.blackrock.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2024/BlackRock-to-Acquire-HPS-Investment-Partners-to-Deliver-Integrated-Solutions-Across-Public-and-Private-Markets/default.aspx?os=w&ref=app)). The deal closed on 1 July 2025 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov:443/Archives/edgar/data/2012383/000119312525153573/d78785dex991.htm)). BlackRock presented the combination as an integrated private credit franchise of about $220 billion in client assets, alongside its $3 trillion public fixed income business, expected to lift private-markets fee-paying assets under management by 40% — in a market BlackRock expects to more than double to $4.5 trillion by 2030 ([BlackRock](https://ir.blackrock.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2024/BlackRock-to-Acquire-HPS-Investment-Partners-to-Deliver-Integrated-Solutions-Across-Public-and-Private-Markets/default.aspx?os=w&ref=app); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov:443/Archives/edgar/data/2012383/000119312525153573/d78785dex991.htm)). It was BlackRock's third big private-markets purchase in about a year, after the $12.5 billion acquisition of Global Infrastructure Partners in October 2024 and the £2.55 billion purchase of Preqin ([IPE](https://www.ipe.com/news/blackrock-nears-12bn-acquisition-of-private-credit-behemoth-hps/10126645.article)).

Notably, the acquisition was structured for continuity rather than absorption. BlackRock and HPS formed a new Private Financing Solutions business unit led by Kapnick, French and Patterson, combining the firms' private credit, GP/LP solutions and CLO businesses; the flagship strategies kept their HPS branding, and the business is now known as "HPS, a part of BlackRock" ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov:443/Archives/edgar/data/2012383/000119312525153573/d78785dex991.htm)). Kapnick joined BlackRock's Global Executive Committee and became an observer to the BlackRock board ([BlackRock](https://ir.blackrock.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2024/BlackRock-to-Acquire-HPS-Investment-Partners-to-Deliver-Integrated-Solutions-Across-Public-and-Private-Markets/default.aspx?os=w&ref=app)). The deal was first reported by the Financial Times, and Bloomberg noted it would take BlackRock's credit business past Carlyle's in size and into competition with KKR and Apollo ([PitchBook](http://pitchbook.com/news/articles/blackrock-hps-purchase-record-year-gp-consolidation?utm_medium=newsletter&utm_source=daily_pitch&sourcetype=newsletter)).

## What HPS invests in

HPS describes itself as a credit-focused alternative investment firm that provides "creative capital solutions" across the capital structure — privately negotiated senior debt; junior capital in debt, preferred and equity formats; liquid credit including syndicated leveraged loans, collateralised loan obligations and high-yield bonds; and asset-based finance including real estate ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov:443/Archives/edgar/data/2012383/000119312525153573/d78785dex991.htm)). Its flagship funds are Strategic Investment Partners (SIP), the Specialty Loan Fund (SLF), the Core Senior Lending Fund (CSL) and the Corporate Lending Fund (HLEND) ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov:443/Archives/edgar/data/2012383/000119312525153573/d78785dex991.htm)). The scale of individual funds is considerable: Specialty Loan Fund VI raised $14.3 billion of capital commitments in June 2024 — $21.1 billion including leverage — which the data provider With Intelligence calculated as 26% of all private credit fundraising in the first half of that year ([IPE](https://www.ipe.com/news/blackrock-nears-12bn-acquisition-of-private-credit-behemoth-hps/10126645.article)).

| Strategy | AUM, 31 March 2026 | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct Lending | $95 billion | Privately negotiated senior debt to middle and upper-middle-market companies |
| Junior Capital Solutions | $34 billion | Junior capital in debt, preferred and equity formats |
| Liquid Credit | $27 billion | Syndicated leveraged loans, CLOs and high-yield bonds |
| Asset-Based Financing | $30 billion | Asset-based finance, including real estate |

The four strategy totals add to the firm's reported $186 billion, with direct lending alone accounting for just over half ([HPS Investment Partners](https://mycelium.global/emissions/hps-investment-partners/filing/2025.pdf)). The client base is institutional: insurance companies, pensions, sovereign wealth funds, wealth managers and retirement savers are the long-dated capital the firm says private credit is built to match ([BlackRock](https://ir.blackrock.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2024/BlackRock-to-Acquire-HPS-Investment-Partners-to-Deliver-Integrated-Solutions-Across-Public-and-Private-Markets/default.aspx?os=w&ref=app)). Insurance is a particular focus — HPS created a dedicated Insurance Solutions Group in 2020, and Guardian Life's expanded $30 billion mandate in 2024 shows how large a single insurer's allocation can be ([HPS Investment Partners](https://www.hpspartners.com/our-story/history)). The firm is run by seven Founding Partners — Kapnick, Patterson, French, Purnima Puri, Faith Rosenfeld, Paul Knollmeyer and Kathy Choi — with more than 890 staff including over 290 investment professionals, working from more than a dozen offices across four continents ([HPS Investment Partners](https://mycelium.global/emissions/hps-investment-partners/filing/2025.pdf); [BlackRock](https://ir.blackrock.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2024/BlackRock-to-Acquire-HPS-Investment-Partners-to-Deliver-Integrated-Solutions-Across-Public-and-Private-Markets/default.aspx?os=w&ref=app)). Beyond investing, the firm partnered with the Kapnick Foundation in 2021 to establish the HPS Center for Financial Excellence at Howard University, aimed at preparing students for finance careers ([HPS Investment Partners](https://www.hpspartners.com/our-story/history)).

## After the acquisition: a $175 million bet and a fraud shock

HPS's first year inside BlackRock produced both a headline investment and a serious test of its underwriting. In June 2026, funds and accounts managed by HPS made a $175 million preferred-equity investment in [Behavox](/organizations/behavox/), the AI compliance platform — Behavox's first equity financing since [SoftBank](/organizations/softbank-group/)'s $100 million round in 2020 ([A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/behavox-secures-175-million-investment-to-expand-unified-controls-platform/)). The capital is earmarked for global expansion, product development and selective acquisitions; HPS joined existing investors including SoftBank, [Citigroup](/organizations/citigroup/), [Index Ventures](/organizations/index-ventures/) and [Hoxton Ventures](/organizations/hoxton-ventures/) ([A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/behavox-secures-175-million-investment-to-expand-unified-controls-platform/)). As part of the transaction, Behavox fully repaid and retired its $70 million venture-debt facility with [Hercules Capital](/organizations/hercules-capital/), a facility secured in autumn 2024 that had funded the acquisition of [Mosaic Smart Data](/organizations/mosaic-smart-data/) and an investment in [b-next](/organizations/b-next/) ([A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/behavox-secures-175-million-investment-to-expand-unified-controls-platform/)). Behavox says it has been profitable since 2023 and that its customer base grew 86% over the prior year to more than 100 major financial institutions across five continents — company claims reported by A-Team Insight, not audited figures ([A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/behavox-secures-175-million-investment-to-expand-unified-controls-platform/)).

Weeks after the BlackRock acquisition closed, a very different story surfaced. HPS became aware of alleged asset-backed finance fraud tied to Carriox Capital, a borrower, and its owner Bankim Brahmbhatt ([Transacted](https://www.transacted.io/hps-investment-partners-navigates-500m-asset-backed-finance-fraud-allegations)). Lenders including BNP Paribas say Brahmbhatt owes them more than $500 million, after two facilities totalling $530 million were extended in August 2024 against customer invoices that the lenders now say largely never existed ([Transacted](https://www.transacted.io/hps-investment-partners-navigates-500m-asset-backed-finance-fraud-allegations)). A complaint filed in Delaware Chancery Court describes fake web domains, fake contracts, forged signatures and interest serviced with the proceeds of new loans ([Transacted](https://www.transacted.io/hps-investment-partners-navigates-500m-asset-backed-finance-fraud-allegations)). HPS had worked with Deloitte on random customer checks to verify the collateral and engaged the accounting firm CBIZ for annual asset checks; even so, the concerns surfaced only about a year later, when an HPS employee noticed irregularities in email addresses tied to Carriox customers, as reported by the Wall Street Journal ([Transacted](https://www.transacted.io/hps-investment-partners-navigates-500m-asset-backed-finance-fraud-allegations)). When approached, Brahmbhatt told the firm there was nothing to worry about and then stopped answering calls, and an HPS associate who visited the company's Garden City, New York offices later that month found them empty ([Transacted](https://www.transacted.io/hps-investment-partners-navigates-500m-asset-backed-finance-fraud-allegations)). Working with CBIZ and the law firm Quinn Emanuel, the lender group found purported customers often unaware of the dealings described in the invoices, and the lenders allege Brahmbhatt moved pledged collateral to offshore accounts in India and Mauritius ([Transacted](https://www.transacted.io/hps-investment-partners-navigates-500m-asset-backed-finance-fraud-allegations)).

Carriox had first borrowed from HPS in 2020, with the borrowing supposedly secured by receivables from reputable customers including T-Mobile, Telstra and Telecom Italia ([Transacted](https://www.transacted.io/hps-investment-partners-navigates-500m-asset-backed-finance-fraud-allegations)). Brahmbhatt, who had personally guaranteed the loans at the lenders' request, filed for personal bankruptcy on 12 August 2025, the same day several of his corporate entities entered Chapter 11, and he disputes the fraud allegations through an attorney ([Transacted](https://www.transacted.io/hps-investment-partners-navigates-500m-asset-backed-finance-fraud-allegations)). HPS wrote down roughly $150 million tied to the loans, held within its Asset Value strategy — a painful sum, but small against nearly $180 billion of assets under management at the time ([Transacted](https://www.transacted.io/hps-investment-partners-navigates-500m-asset-backed-finance-fraud-allegations)). The episode sits alongside similar asset-backed blow-ups at Tricolor and First Brands ([Transacted](https://www.transacted.io/hps-investment-partners-navigates-500m-asset-backed-finance-fraud-allegations)).
