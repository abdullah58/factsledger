## Overview

BET — Black Entertainment Television — is an American cable television network built for Black audiences, launched on 25 January 1980 by media entrepreneur Robert L. Johnson, and now owned by Paramount Skydance through the BET Media Group ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Black-Entertainment-Television); [BlackPast.org](https://blackpast.org/african-american-history/black-entertainment-television-bet-1980/)). It began as two hours of weekly programming and grew into the leading U.S. cable network for Black viewers, before a sale to Viacom whose price the sources still dispute ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Black-Entertainment-Television); [Black Enterprise](https://www.blackenterprise.com/bet-first-black-billionaire/amp/)). This profile is about that television network — not a betting company, and not any other business using the letters BET.

Johnson, a former cable-industry lobbyist, spotted a gap no channel served: television made for African American viewers ([BlackPast.org](https://blackpast.org/african-american-history/black-entertainment-television-bet-1980/)). He started with a $15,000 bank loan and a $500,000 investment, and by the end of the first year BET was broadcasting 24 hours a day across North America and the Caribbean ([Black Enterprise](https://www.blackenterprise.com/bet-first-black-billionaire/amp/); [BlackPast.org](https://blackpast.org/african-american-history/black-entertainment-television-bet-1980/)).

## Founding and the Black-owned years

Johnson launched BET after leaving his cable-lobbying job, convinced Black audiences were ignored by existing television ([BlackPast.org](https://blackpast.org/african-american-history/black-entertainment-television-bet-1980/)). It premiered modestly — two hours a week in select east-coast cities — but broadcast 24 hours a day by the end of 1980, across North America and the Caribbean ([BlackPast.org](https://blackpast.org/african-american-history/black-entertainment-television-bet-1980/)). It lost money at first, turned profitable by the mid-1980s, and by 1985 had diversified beyond music videos into news, comedy, talk shows and sports ([BlackPast.org](https://blackpast.org/african-american-history/black-entertainment-television-bet-1980/)).

BET News arrived in 1988, with Ed Gordon anchoring the first half-hour news programme aimed at an African American audience; in 1996 Gordon interviewed O.J. Simpson after his acquittal, and Tavis Smiley debuted BET Talk ([BlackPast.org](https://blackpast.org/african-american-history/black-entertainment-television-bet-1980/)). In 1991 BET became the first NYSE-listed company with majority ownership controlled by African Americans, then went private again in 1998 with Liberty Media ([Black Enterprise](https://www.blackenterprise.com/bet-first-black-billionaire/amp/)). Black Enterprise credits Sheila Johnson, Robert Johnson's wife at the time, as a co-founder — a detail most sources omit ([Black Enterprise](https://www.blackenterprise.com/bet-first-black-billionaire/amp/)).

## The Viacom sale and the price question

Viacom announced the purchase of BET Holdings II in 2000; the deal closed a year later ([Black Enterprise](https://www.blackenterprise.com/bet-first-black-billionaire/amp/)). The sale ended BET's status as a Black-owned business and made Johnson America's first Black billionaire, with $2.4 billion of his payout in Viacom stock under a five-year contract ([Black Enterprise](https://www.blackenterprise.com/bet-first-black-billionaire/amp/)). At the time BET reached 62.4 million households, and Viacom also gained a jazz network with about 11 million subscribers ([Black Enterprise](https://www.blackenterprise.com/bet-first-black-billionaire/amp/)). That reach is the point: BET was the only cable network targeting Black viewers at national scale, 

The price, however, is genuinely disputed. Britannica, Black Enterprise and BlackPast.org all put the deal at $3 billion, while TheWrap (2025) states Paramount "would then acquire it for $2.3 billion in 2000" ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Black-Entertainment-Television); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/david-ellison-paramount-no-bet-sale/)). Johnson's $2.4 billion stock payout suggests the lower figure may be the stock component only, but no source read here settles it. Radio One founder Cathy Hughes called the sale "a great day for Bob Johnson, but a significant loss for African American ownership" ([Black Enterprise](https://www.blackenterprise.com/bet-first-black-billionaire/amp/)).

## Programming: music, news, and the BET Awards

Music built BET: music videos were an early staple before the network broadened into politics, comedy, talk and sports ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Black-Entertainment-Television)). In 2002 a restructuring switched the focus to entertainment, mainly rap videos, eliminated the news staff, and cancelled every news programme except BET Nightly News, itself cancelled in 2005 ([BlackPast.org](https://blackpast.org/african-american-history/black-entertainment-television-bet-1980/)). Critics including George Curry, Aaron McGruder, Spike Lee and Boyce Watkins charged the network with promoting sexism, anti-intellectualism and anti-Black stereotypes, while BET's management said it was giving the market what it demanded ([BlackPast.org](https://blackpast.org/african-american-history/black-entertainment-television-bet-1980/)).

The flagship is the BET Awards, aired since 2001 for Black achievement in music, acting, sports and other fields, and its highest-rated programme ([BlackPast.org](https://blackpast.org/african-american-history/black-entertainment-television-bet-1980/)). The first ceremony took place on 19 June 2001 at Paris Las Vegas, hosted by Steve Harvey and Cedric the Entertainer, with Whitney Houston receiving the Lifetime Achievement award ([People](https://people.com/vintage-startracks-bet-awards-2001-12005753)). Destiny's Child won Best Female Group, Eve won Best Female Hip-Hop Artist, OutKast took Best Male Group and Video of the Year, and a teenage Bow Wow won the Viewers' Choice prize ([People](https://people.com/vintage-startracks-bet-awards-2001-12005753)).

## 106 & Park and the music-countdown era

If the BET Awards are the network's biggest night, 106 & Park was its daily habit. Created by Stephen G. Hill, the countdown premiered on 11 September 2000 from a studio in East Harlem, [New York City](/places/new-york-city/), at East 106th Street and Park Avenue — the address behind its name ([The Beat London](https://thebeat1036.com/106-park-to-celebrate-25-years-with-iconic-host-reunion-star-studded-performances-live-at-bet-awards-2025-10-june-at-100am-wat-on-bet-dstv-channe/)). Its first guests were Jermaine Dupri and Lil Bow Wow, and its first hosts were A.J. Calloway and Free ([The Beat London](https://thebeat1036.com/106-park-to-celebrate-25-years-with-iconic-host-reunion-star-studded-performances-live-at-bet-awards-2025-10-june-at-100am-wat-on-bet-dstv-channe/)). Later hosts included Big Tigger and Julissa Bermudez, Terrence J and Rocsi Diaz, and Bow Wow, before the finale on 19 December 2014 ended a run BET puts at 3,710 episodes ([The Beat London](https://thebeat1036.com/106-park-to-celebrate-25-years-with-iconic-host-reunion-star-studded-performances-live-at-bet-awards-2025-10-june-at-100am-wat-on-bet-dstv-channe/)).

Scott Mills called it "the heartbeat of Black youth culture", and the 25th BET Awards in June 2025 reunited its hosts in Los Angeles ([The Beat London](https://thebeat1036.com/106-park-to-celebrate-25-years-with-iconic-host-reunion-star-studded-performances-live-at-bet-awards-2025-10-june-at-100am-wat-on-bet-dstv-channe/)). Johnson stepped down as CEO in 2005, succeeded by Debra L. Lee, who led BET until 2018 ([Cornell Chronicle](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2024/02/former-ceo-bet-shares-her-struggles-successes)). By 2010 the channel was viewed in more than 90 million homes worldwide ([BlackPast.org](https://blackpast.org/african-american-history/black-entertainment-television-bet-1980/)).

## Streaming, and the sale that never happened

BET's streaming move came in 2019, when BET Networks and Tyler Perry Studios announced BET+ on 24 June, a joint venture launching that autumn with more than 1,000 hours of library content plus originals ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/convergent-tv/viacoms-bet-networks-to-introduce-svod-service-this-fall//)). BET became the home for Perry's catalogue — House of Payne, Madea films, stage plays — with Perry also producing new originals ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/convergent-tv/viacoms-bet-networks-to-introduce-svod-service-this-fall//)). The BET Media Group now spans BET, BET+, BET Gospel, BET HER, BET International, BET Jams, BET Soul, BET Studios and VH1 ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/david-ellison-paramount-no-bet-sale/)).

Then came the sale saga. In 2023 Paramount backed off talks to sell its majority stake after interest from Tyler Perry, Byron Allen and Group Black; Perry publicly called the bidding process "disrespectful" ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/david-ellison-paramount-no-bet-sale/)). Speculation revived in July 2024, when Bloomberg reported a group including BET CEO Scott Mills and Chinh Chu was in talks to acquire BET ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/david-ellison-paramount-no-bet-sale/)). It closed on 13 August 2025, days after the Skydance merger, when new Paramount CEO David Ellison said the company had no plans to shop BET to buyers ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/david-ellison-paramount-no-bet-sale/)).

## BET and the Billboard charts

BET's relationship with Billboard is that of a fellow music institution reporting on the charts that measure its own world. When Billboard and MRC launched the Billboard Global 200 and Billboard Global Excl. U.S. in September 2020 — the first authoritative worldwide song rankings, built from official-only streams and downloads across more than 200 territories — BET covered the debut on its own site ([Music Ally](https://musically.com/2020/09/15/billboard-and-mrc-launch-two-new-global-music-charts/)). Cardi B and Megan Thee Stallion's "WAP" topped the first Global 200, while Maluma's "Hawái" led the first chart excluding the [United States](/places/united-states/), and BET's report put "WAP" at 100.9 million global streams that week ([BET](https://www.bet.com/article/8suyei/cardi-b-s-wap-tops-billboard-s-new-global-200-chart); [Music Ally](https://musically.com/2020/09/15/billboard-and-mrc-launch-two-new-global-music-charts/)).

The same report showed how fast a chart-topper could move: "WAP", released on 7 August, had pulled 93 million first-week streams — the most of any song in 2020 — went gold with the RIAA within a week, and that same week reclaimed the Hot 100 from BTS's "Dynamite" ([BET](https://www.bet.com/article/8suyei/cardi-b-s-wap-tops-billboard-s-new-global-200-chart)). The connection runs both ways: BET's shows create the moments Billboard's charts measure, while BET treats those charts as the scoreboard for its culture.

## FAQ

**When was BET founded?**
BET first aired on 25 January 1980, founded by Robert L. Johnson, a former cable-industry lobbyist who saw that Black audiences had almost no television made for them. It began as two hours of weekly programming and expanded to 24 hours by the end of that first year. ([BlackPast.org](https://blackpast.org/african-american-history/black-entertainment-television-bet-1980/))

**Who owns BET now?**
BET is owned by the BET Media Group, a unit of Paramount Skydance, which was formed when Paramount Global merged with Skydance Media in 2025. Paramount's previous owner explored selling BET in 2023 and 2024, but new CEO David Ellison said in August 2025 that BET is not for sale. ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/david-ellison-paramount-no-bet-sale/))

**How much did Viacom pay for BET?**
The price is disputed: Britannica, Black Enterprise and BlackPast.org report about $3 billion for the deal announced in 2000 and closed in 2001, while TheWrap reports $2.3 billion. Johnson personally received $2.4 billion in Viacom stock, which may explain the lower figure. ([Black Enterprise](https://www.blackenterprise.com/bet-first-black-billionaire/amp/); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/david-ellison-paramount-no-bet-sale/))

**What were the BET Awards and 106 & Park?**
The BET Awards, held since 2001, honour Black achievement in music, film, sports and philanthropy; the first ceremony was on 19 June 2001 in Las Vegas. 106 & Park was BET's daily music-video countdown, running from 2000 to 2014, and was celebrated with a host reunion at the 25th BET Awards in 2025. ([People](https://people.com/vintage-startracks-bet-awards-2001-12005753); [The Beat London](https://thebeat1036.com/106-park-to-celebrate-25-years-with-iconic-host-reunion-star-studded-performances-live-at-bet-awards-2025-10-june-at-100am-wat-on-bet-dstv-channe/))

**Is BET a betting company?**
No. BET stands for Black Entertainment Television, an American cable network founded in 1980 for Black audiences, now owned by Paramount Skydance through the BET Media Group. It has no connection to gambling or sports betting, despite the shared letters. ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Black-Entertainment-Television))
