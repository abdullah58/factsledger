## Overview

The Grammy Awards are the music industry's peer-voted annual honors, presented by the [Recording Academy](https://factsledger.com/organizations/recording-academy/) to recognize achievements during a defined eligibility period ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/entertainment/music/2025/01/03/what-is-a-grammy-award/77326899007/)). The 68th edition is scheduled for February 1, 2026, at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, with 95 categories and winners announced live on CBS and Paramount+ ([Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/637101/grammy-voting-explained-how-nominees-and-winners-are-picked)). The most prestigious prizes are the "Big Four" in the general field — Album of the Year, Record of the Year, Song of the Year, and Best New Artist ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/entertainment/music/2025/01/03/what-is-a-grammy-award/77326899007/)). Search results mostly repeat the same voting explainer; the less-told story is the organization behind the trophy — its membership, its revenue, and the reforms it has been forced to make.

## The organization behind the golden gramophone

[The Recording Academy](/organizations/recording-academy/), the society behind the Grammys, describes itself as a movement powered by 30,000 active songwriters, performers, producers, engineers, and industry professionals — a figure it states on its own homepage ([The Recording Academy](https://www.recordingacademy.com)). It offers three membership types: voting membership for creative professionals currently working in the recording industry, professional membership for music businesspeople, and Grammy U for full-time students ([The Recording Academy](https://www.recordingacademy.com); [Boardroom](https://boardroom.tv/harvey-mason-jr-recording-academy-grammy-awards/)). Only voting members determine the winners each year ([Boardroom](https://boardroom.tv/harvey-mason-jr-recording-academy-grammy-awards/)). The organization has been led by President and CEO Harvey Mason Jr., a songwriter and producer with credits alongside Beyoncé, Aretha Franklin, Britney Spears, and Justin Bieber, who served 18 months as interim chief before formally accepting the role ([Boardroom](https://boardroom.tv/harvey-mason-jr-recording-academy-grammy-awards/)). Mason has been unusually candid about the show's economics: the awards were designed to generate revenue, and the CBS broadcast deal funds the Academy's year-round programming for the music community ([Boardroom](https://boardroom.tv/harvey-mason-jr-recording-academy-grammy-awards/)).

## History: 1959 and the first golden gramophones

The first Grammy Awards ceremony took place on May 4, 1959, honoring music released the previous year ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/entertainment/music/2025/01/03/what-is-a-grammy-award/77326899007/)). Just 28 categories were awarded that night, including three of today's Big Four ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/entertainment/music/2025/01/03/what-is-a-grammy-award/77326899007/)). Record of the Year and Song of the Year both went to Domenico Modugno's "Nel Blu Dipinto di Blu (Volare)" — the songwriting award shared with Franco Migliacci — while Henry Mancini's "The Music from Peter Gunn" took Album of the Year ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/entertainment/music/2025/01/03/what-is-a-grammy-award/77326899007/)). The fourth of the Big Four, Best New Artist, was first awarded at the 2nd annual ceremony, going to Bobby Darin ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/entertainment/music/2025/01/03/what-is-a-grammy-award/77326899007/)). The awards span genres from pop, jazz, electronic, rock, and R&B to reggae, alongside craft honors for producing, songwriting, album packaging, instrumental composition, and arrangement ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/entertainment/music/2025/01/03/what-is-a-grammy-award/77326899007/)).

## How the voting actually works

Artists, labels, and Academy members submit recordings, which are screened by more than 350 experts to confirm eligibility and category placement ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/entertainment/music/2025/01/03/what-is-a-grammy-award/77326899007/); [Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/637101/grammy-voting-explained-how-nominees-and-winners-are-picked)). Voting members then determine the nominees, typically in the fall, using a membership dashboard — and they can only vote for music that has been submitted and vetted ([Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/637101/grammy-voting-explained-how-nominees-and-winners-are-picked)). Members are permitted to vote in up to 10 categories across three genre fields, plus the six general-field categories — record, album, song, producer (non-classical), songwriter (non-classical), and best new artist of the year — a design meant to keep experts voting where they are peers ([Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/637101/grammy-voting-explained-how-nominees-and-winners-are-picked)). Becoming a voting member requires proof of a primary career in music, two industry recommendations, and verifiable credits, though current-year winners and nominees skip the recommendation requirement and nominees from the past five years skip the credit proof ([Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/637101/grammy-voting-explained-how-nominees-and-winners-are-picked)). Final-round ballots are tabulated by the independent accounting firm Deloitte & Touche LLP, and winners are announced live at the ceremony ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/entertainment/music/2025/01/03/what-is-a-grammy-award/77326899007/)).

## The fairness reforms

The current system is the product of a bruising reform. Until the 2021 awards, voters could roam across every genre and vote for their favorite artists in each category, and some members even traded votes strategically ([Boardroom](https://boardroom.tv/harvey-mason-jr-recording-academy-grammy-awards/)). In 2021 the Academy adopted "10-3" voting — three genre fields, ten categories within them — to force discernment and keep voters in the genres they know ([Boardroom](https://boardroom.tv/harvey-mason-jr-recording-academy-grammy-awards/)). The change followed years of snubs that poisoned the Academy's relationship with hip-hop: Jay-Z boycotted his first Grammys in solidarity with DMX's 1998 shutout, Drake used a 2019 acceptance speech to tell artists they didn't need the award, and in 2021 Lil Baby's *My Turn*, Roddy Ricch's *Please Excuse Me for Being Antisocial*, and The Weeknd's *After Hours* all received zero nominations ([Boardroom](https://boardroom.tv/harvey-mason-jr-recording-academy-grammy-awards/)). Mason's diagnosis was structural: underrepresentation in the awards reflects underrepresentation in the voting body ([Boardroom](https://boardroom.tv/harvey-mason-jr-recording-academy-grammy-awards/)). In June 2021 the Academy admitted 2,700 new members — 48% female, 32% Black, 13% Hispanic or Latino, and 4% Asian or Pacific Islander — and set a goal of adding 2,500 additional female voting members by 2025 ([Boardroom](https://boardroom.tv/harvey-mason-jr-recording-academy-grammy-awards/)).

## Best New Artist: the trickiest category

Best New Artist is the category the rules have to keep reinventing. Nominations hinge on whether "the artist had attained a breakthrough or prominence," a judgment delegated to a screening committee ([Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/637101/grammy-voting-explained-how-nominees-and-winners-are-picked)). Eligible artists must have released at least five singles or one album — but there is no longer a maximum, which is why Sabrina Carpenter, breaking out in the summer of "Espresso," was nominated for the 2025 trophy despite being on her sixth full-length release ([Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/637101/grammy-voting-explained-how-nominees-and-winners-are-picked)). For 2026 the category was expanded to include acts featured on previous Album of the Year nominees, so long as they account for less than 20% of the album's music ([Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/637101/grammy-voting-explained-how-nominees-and-winners-are-picked)). The award has launched and bruised careers in equal measure — the rapper [Nicki Minaj](/people/nicki-minaj/) was among its 2011 nominees — and the snubs it produces, like Macklemore's 2014 Best New Artist win over Kendrick Lamar, are still argued over years later ([Boardroom](https://boardroom.tv/harvey-mason-jr-recording-academy-grammy-awards/)).

## The 2026 awards

The categories keep shifting. For 2026, Best Country Album was divided into Best Contemporary Country Album and Best Traditional Country Album; the Best Recording Package and Best Boxed or Special Limited Edition Package categories were merged into a single Best Recording Package, with Best Album Cover spun out on its own ([Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/637101/grammy-voting-explained-how-nominees-and-winners-are-picked)). To be eligible for the 68th Grammys, recordings and music videos had to be released between August 31, 2024 and August 30, 2025 ([Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/637101/grammy-voting-explained-how-nominees-and-winners-are-picked)). Nominees were announced in November 2025, with final-round voting running December 12 through January 5 ahead of the February 1 ceremony ([Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/637101/grammy-voting-explained-how-nominees-and-winners-are-picked)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not claim the 30,000-member figure as a stable fact — the Academy itself reported roughly 24,000 members with about 12,000 voters in a 2022 interview, so the current figure reflects recent growth. It does not predict 2026 winners or endorse any snub narrative as fact; the snubs and boycotts are reported as the artists' and the CEO's own accounts. Category counts and eligibility windows change annually — the 95 categories and the 2026 window are specific to that cycle. The diversity figures describe the Academy's stated membership initiatives, not a judgment on whether representation is now adequate.
