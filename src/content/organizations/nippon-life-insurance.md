## Overview

Nippon Life Insurance Company — Nissay — is Japan's largest life insurer by revenue, a mutual company headquartered in Osaka ([The Royal Gazette](https://www.royalgazette.com/reinsurance/business/article/20241212/nippon-life-takes-over-bermuda-reinsurer/)). It was founded on July 4, 1889, by Shiga banker Sukesaburo Hirose, who sensed that secular financial protection would find ready buyers in Osaka's mercantile bustle; backed by the city's bankers' association, the company began operations as a limited company and became Japan's first major life insurance company ([Encyclopedia.com](http://www.encyclopedia.com/social-sciences-and-law/economics-business-and-labor/businesses-and-occupations/nippon-life)). In 1898 it paid Japan's first policyholder profit dividends, and in 1947 — under the postwar occupation — it became the first of Japan's life insurers to reorganize as a mutual company ([Encyclopedia.com](http://www.encyclopedia.com/social-sciences-and-law/economics-business-and-labor/businesses-and-occupations/nippon-life)).

## A century of firsts, then the world

Nippon Life's history is a catalogue of industry firsts: made-to-order insurance in 1959, the industry's first overseas real estate subsidiary in 1981, and that same year the first Japanese life insurer to buy US real estate — a 50% interest in a New York City office building ([Encyclopedia.com](http://www.encyclopedia.com/social-sciences-and-law/economics-business-and-labor/businesses-and-occupations/nippon-life)). The 21st century turned those firsts into a strategy. In December 2024, Nippon Life agreed to take its Bermudian affiliate Resolution Life — in which it already held 23% — private, paying $8.2 billion for the remaining shares in a deal valuing Resolution Life at $10.6 billion ([The Royal Gazette](https://www.royalgazette.com/reinsurance/business/article/20241212/nippon-life-takes-over-bermuda-reinsurer/)). Fitch placed Resolution Life's ratings on positive watch, calling the deal part of Nippon Life's "broader strategic initiative... to expand its life insurance business outside of Japan" ([The Royal Gazette](https://www.royalgazette.com/reinsurance/business/article/20241212/nippon-life-takes-over-bermuda-reinsurer/)). The transaction also folded in the remaining 20% of Australia's MLC Life, integrating it with Resolution Life Australasia ([The Royal Gazette](https://www.royalgazette.com/reinsurance/business/article/20241212/nippon-life-takes-over-bermuda-reinsurer/)).

## The mutual advantage

Nippon Life's mutual structure — no shareholders demanding buybacks or higher dividends — gives it unusual capital flexibility, and Fitch calls its capital adequacy among the strongest of Japan's major life insurers ([Insurance Asia](https://insuranceasia.com/insurance/news/nippon-life-profit-seen-supporting-capital-global-earnings-expand)). Core profit margin reached 20% in the year to March 2026, and the company has earmarked $12.69 billion for mergers and acquisitions under its FY2025–2027 plan, expecting about 20% of consolidated core profit to come from outside Japan ([Insurance Asia](https://insuranceasia.com/insurance/news/nippon-life-profit-seen-supporting-capital-global-earnings-expand)). The Blackstone partnership that manages Resolution Life's directly originated private-credit and real-estate assets continues under the new ownership ([The Royal Gazette](https://www.royalgazette.com/reinsurance/business/article/20241212/nippon-life-takes-over-bermuda-reinsurer/)).
