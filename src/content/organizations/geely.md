## Overview

Zhejiang Geely Holding Group is a privately held Chinese automotive conglomerate headquartered in Hangzhou, founded by Li Shufu in 1986 ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/); [Zhejiang Geely Holding Group](https://mb.cision.com/Main/23620/4198481/3565062.pdf)). It began making refrigerator parts, moved through mopeds and motorcycles into cars in 1997, and grew by acquisition — most famously buying Volvo Cars from [Ford](/organizations/ford/) for $1.8 billion in 2010 — into a group spanning Geely, Volvo, Zeekr, Lynk & Co, Lotus and Proton that sold 3.34 million vehicles in 2024 ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/); [Shanghai Metals Market](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103126315-geely-holding-boasts-22-yoy-leap-in-2024-full-year-automobile-sales)).

That arc — from a parts workshop to the owner of Sweden's most famous carmaker — is unusual in one respect: unlike its big state-owned Chinese rivals, Geely did it without a foreign joint-venture partner building its cars for it. Li Shufu believed China could produce its own cars without partnering with foreign manufacturers, and Geely's early history is a record of working around the state's reluctance to let a private entrepreneur build cars at all ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)).

## From refrigerator parts to cars

Li Shufu's early ventures were photography, electronic-waste recycling, refrigerator parts and magnesium-aluminium decorative panels before he spotted an opening in mopeds as affordable private transport ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)). Geely became the first company in mainland China to produce mopeds when it entered that business in 1994, and later motorcycles, before the market was spoiled by what Li described as disorderly and even unfair competition such as tax evasion ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)).

By 1997 foreign carmakers were entering [China](/places/china/), but private entrepreneurs could not get state support to build cars, and the authorities actively discouraged it, according to journalists at the time ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)). Li approached a local partner in Deyang, Sichuan, which already ran a car factory; with a 7% capital injection he obtained the necessary licence, and together with two Geely engineers he studied automotive technology and built Geely's first car by hand ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)). The turning point came in 2001, when China joined the World Trade Organization and included Geely as the first private automotive manufacturer on the list it submitted, enabling it to sell cars nationally ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)). In 2002 Geely released the Ziyoujian ("Free Cruiser") four-door sub-compact sedan, and in 2005 it became the first privately owned Chinese automaker invited to the Frankfurt Auto Show and listed on the [Hong Kong Stock Exchange](/organizations/hong-kong-stock-exchange/) the same year ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)). Vehicle sales reached 143,279 in 2005, up 46% on the previous year ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)).

## The acquisition decade: Volvo, Lotus, Proton, Daimler

Geely's international expansion began in 2006 with a partnership with Manganese Bronze, maker of London's black cabs, which allowed Geely to build the models in its Shanghai factory; in 2010 Geely rescued the company from administration for £11 million ($13.7 million) and invested more than £325 million in the brand between 2010 and 2017, opening a new factory in Coventry in 2017 ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)). The same year brought the deal that defined Geely: on 2 August 2010 it completed the acquisition of 100% of Volvo Car Corporation from Ford for $1.8 billion — a $200 million note plus $1.3 billion in cash — after more than a year of talks ([Fleet News](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/2010/8/3/zhejiang-geely-completes-acquisition-of-volvo-cars/37302/)). Volvo kept its headquarters and manufacturing presence in Sweden and Belgium, with management autonomy under the board chaired by Li Shufu ([Zhejiang Geely Holding Group](https://mb.cision.com/Main/23620/4198481/3565062.pdf)).

The buying continued. In May 2017 Geely agreed to buy a 51% stake in British sports-car maker Lotus and a 49.9% stake in Malaysian carmaker Proton from DRB-Hicom ([MotorTrend](https://www.motortrend.com/news/geely-buys-majority-stake-lotus)). In February 2018 Li Shufu personally acquired a 9.69% stake in [Daimler](/organizations/daimler/), the parent of Mercedes-Benz, for about $9 billion, becoming its single largest shareholder, ahead of Kuwait at 6.8% ([Motor Authority](https://www.motorauthority.com/news/1114332_geely-buys-9-7-percent-of-daimler-in-9b-deal)). IMD's account adds joint ventures and stakes from the same period with France's Renault, a partnership with [Baidu](/organizations/baidu/), a 2018 battery-cell joint venture with CATL and a 50:50 battery joint venture with LG Chem ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)).

## Going electric, and going global

On 18 November 2015 Geely announced Blue Geely, an initiative to move its portfolio to pure electric vehicles, hybrids and plug-in hybrids, with targets including new-energy models accounting for 90% of sales by 2020 and research into hydrogen and metal fuel cells ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)). That 90% target was an ambition rather than a result — the group's actual new-energy share in 2024 was 45% — but the direction held: Geely created its China Europe Vehicle Technology centre in Gothenburg to develop the Compact Modular Architecture, and built a digital backbone around Caocao Mobility ride-hailing and ECARX smart-mobility technology, whose GKUI smart ecosystem passed two million users by 2020 ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)). Li even looked beyond cars, buying flying-car company Terrafugia in 2017 and investing $55 million in Germany's Volocopter in 2019 ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/)).

The electric push produced a premium brand, Zeekr, whose shares began trading on the [New York Stock Exchange](/organizations/new-york-stock-exchange/) on 10 May 2024 after an upsized IPO of 21 million shares at $21, raising $441 million — the first major US market debut by a China-based company since 2021 ([Reuters](https://srnnews.com/chinas-zeekr-set-to-debut-on-nyse-after-upsized-ipo/)). The group's 2024 sales show how far the portfolio had come: 3,336,534 vehicles, up 22% on 2023, of which 1,487,954 were new-energy vehicles (up 52%) and 1,221,852 were sold overseas (up 21%) ([Shanghai Metals Market](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103126315-geely-holding-boasts-22-yoy-leap-in-2024-full-year-automobile-sales)).

## The Cerence thread: voice AI from ARK to xUI

Geely's cars have carried [Cerence](/organizations/cerence/) voice technology since before the current AI wave. On 12 March 2020, Geely's China Euro Vehicle Technology centre (CEVT) selected Cerence ARK to build AI-powered automotive assistants for Geely Auto Group, for models built on the Compact Modular Architecture jointly developed by Geely and Volvo, working with ECARX as lead supplier for the in-car interface ([Cerence Inc.](https://investors.cerence.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/326/geelys-china-euro-vehicle-technology-selects-cerence-ark-to-bring-ai-powered-auto-experience-to-geely-car-models)). CEVT, fully owned by Zhejiang Geely Holding Group and established in 2013, employed more than 2,000 people on software and hardware for the wider group ([Cerence Inc.](https://investors.cerence.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/326/geelys-china-euro-vehicle-technology-selects-cerence-ark-to-bring-ai-powered-auto-experience-to-geely-car-models)).

Six years later the relationship moved to large language models: on 5 January 2026 Geely Auto announced it will deploy Cerence xUI, a hybrid agentic AI platform, to upgrade its overseas in-car voice experience, starting with the Geely Galaxy M9 from April 2026, with features including multi-intent recognition and "Say What You See" voice control of the interface ([Cerence Inc.](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/geely-auto-selects-cerence-xui-power-its-next-gen-llm-powered-car-ai-experience-2026)). Cerence described Geely Auto as one of the first customers to deploy xUI, and said more than 525 million cars have shipped with Cerence technology ([Cerence Inc.](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/geely-auto-selects-cerence-xui-power-its-next-gen-llm-powered-car-ai-experience-2026)). The pairing matters because Geely's overseas growth — 1.22 million vehicles sold abroad in 2024 — depends on voice assistants that work in local languages and driving cultures, which is exactly what the xUI deployment is built around.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who owns Geely?** Geely is privately held by its founder, Li Shufu, who started the business in 1986 and remains its chairman ([IMD](https://www.imd.org/ibyimd/innovation/geely-the-ever-evolving-business/); [Zhejiang Geely Holding Group](https://mb.cision.com/Main/23620/4198481/3565062.pdf)). The listed carmaker, Geely Automobile Holdings, trades in Hong Kong, but the holding group itself is private.

**Does Geely own Volvo?** Yes. Geely bought 100% of Volvo Car Corporation from Ford in August 2010 for $1.8 billion, and Volvo kept its Swedish headquarters and management autonomy ([Fleet News](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/2010/8/3/zhejiang-geely-completes-acquisition-of-volvo-cars/37302/)).

**Which brands does Geely own?** The group's portfolio includes Geely, Volvo Cars, Zeekr, Lynk & Co, Lotus (51%), Proton (49.9%), Polestar, Smart (joint venture), LEVC and Farizon, among others ([Shanghai Metals Market](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103126315-geely-holding-boasts-22-yoy-leap-in-2024-full-year-automobile-sales); [MotorTrend](https://www.motortrend.com/news/geely-buys-majority-stake-lotus)).

**How many cars did Geely sell in 2024?** The group sold 3,336,534 vehicles, up 22% year on year; 45% were new-energy vehicles and 1,221,852 were sold overseas ([Shanghai Metals Market](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103126315-geely-holding-boasts-22-yoy-leap-in-2024-full-year-automobile-sales)).

**What is Geely's link to Cerence?** Geely Auto Group has used Cerence voice assistants since 2020, when CEVT selected Cerence ARK, and in January 2026 Geely Auto announced it would deploy Cerence xUI in overseas models, starting with the Galaxy M9 in April 2026 ([Cerence Inc.](https://investors.cerence.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/326/geelys-china-euro-vehicle-technology-selects-cerence-ark-to-bring-ai-powered-auto-experience-to-geely-car-models); [Cerence Inc.](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/geely-auto-selects-cerence-xui-power-its-next-gen-llm-powered-car-ai-experience-2026)).
