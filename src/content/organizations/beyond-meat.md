## Overview

Beyond Meat, Inc. — rebranded in March 2026 as Beyond, The Plant Protein Co. — is an American company that makes plant-based alternatives to beef, pork and poultry from pea protein and other plant ingredients. Founded in 2009 by Ethan Brown and headquartered in El Segundo, [California](/places/california/), it became the first pure-play maker of vegan "meat" to go public, listing on [Nasdaq](/organizations/nasdaq/) as BYND in May 2019 — then watched its revenue fall for four straight years from a 2021 peak as the plant-based boom faded ([Associated Press](https://www.inc.com/associated-press/beyond-meat-ipo-shares-more-than-double.html); [FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/04/01/beyond-meat-2025-full-year-results/)).

Beyond Meat is not the same company as Impossible Foods, its main rival, whose burgers are built on soy leghemoglobin and supplied Burger King's Impossible Whopper; the two are often confused, but they are separate companies with different technology and different backers. Beyond Meat's pitch was always the meat case, not the vegetarian aisle: its burgers were designed to sit beside animal meat in grocery stores and to win over meat eaters, not just vegetarians ([Associated Press](https://www.inc.com/associated-press/beyond-meat-ipo-shares-more-than-double.html)).

## From a university licence to the meat case

Brown, a former clean-energy executive, grew up spending weekends on a family part-owned dairy farm in Maryland, and came to question whether animals were needed to produce meat at all ([Associated Press](https://www.inc.com/associated-press/beyond-meat-ipo-shares-more-than-double.html)). He licensed technology from two University of Missouri professors, Fu-hung Hsieh and Harold Huff, who had been developing soy-based meatless protein since the 1980s, and built the company on their extrusion process for texturising plant protein ([Associated Press](https://www.inc.com/associated-press/beyond-meat-ipo-shares-more-than-double.html); [FoodNavigator-USA](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2016/06/30/Plant-based-Beyond-Burger-to-roll-out-across-Whole-Foods/?nocount)).

The first product was a chicken substitute, and by 2013 Beyond Meat was selling plant-based chicken strips nationwide at Whole Foods ([Associated Press](https://www.inc.com/associated-press/beyond-meat-ipo-shares-more-than-double.html)). The breakthrough came in May 2016 with the Beyond Burger, a pea-protein patty that "bleeds" with beet juice and was the first plant-based burger sold in the meat case of grocery stores rather than the frozen vegetarian aisle ([Associated Press](https://www.inc.com/associated-press/beyond-meat-ipo-shares-more-than-double.html); [FoodNavigator-USA](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2016/06/30/Plant-based-Beyond-Burger-to-roll-out-across-Whole-Foods/?nocount)). Trials in two Whole Foods stores — Pearl Street in Boulder, Colorado, and Pentagon City, Virginia — were so successful that the burger rolled out across Whole Foods in the autumn of 2016, and by mid-2016 the company was in more than 11,000 stores ([FoodNavigator-USA](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2016/06/30/Plant-based-Beyond-Burger-to-roll-out-across-Whole-Foods/?nocount)). Brown's stated goal was to go after the multi-billion-dollar meat market rather than settle for a slice of the meat-alternatives niche ([FoodNavigator-USA](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2016/06/30/Plant-based-Beyond-Burger-to-roll-out-across-Whole-Foods/?nocount)).

## Funding, the IPO, and the fast-food bet

[Kleiner Perkins](/organizations/kleiner-perkins/) says it was the only institutional investor in Beyond Meat's first financing round in 2011, joined the board, and helped build manufacturing facilities using the University of Missouri extrusion technology ([Kleiner Perkins](https://www.kleinerperkins.com/perspectives/beyond-meat-the-future-of-protein)). By the IPO the company had raised $122 million from backers including Obvious Ventures, Kleiner Perkins, Bill Gates and Tyson Foods ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/business/beyond-meat-prices-ipo-at-top-of-range/)). Tyson Foods, through its Tyson Ventures arm, took a 5 percent stake in October 2016 and raised it to 6.5 percent by the time Beyond Meat filed for its IPO — then sold the whole stake in April 2019, saying the time was right to exit, as it prepared its own alternative-protein products ([Just Food](https://www.just-food.com/news/tyson-exits-beyond-meat-roster-ahead-of-ipo/)).

### The CircleUp connection, stated precisely

[CircleUp](/organizations/circleup/) is credited with supplying over $1 billion in capital to more than 500 consumer companies, including Beyond Meat — a figure stated in the announcement of Brightflow AI's acquisition of CircleUp in June 2023 ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/425249/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup/?abkw=citybizsanfrancisco)). CircleUp had already singled the company out in 2018, naming it to its CircleUp25 list and identifying it as having the strongest brand score in the meat-and-seafood-alternatives category, with Tyson Foods and the Humane Society of the United States among its investors ([New Hope Network](https://www.newhope.com/industry-insights/natural-brands-fill-circleup25-s-2018-list)). What the read sources do not say is the form that capital took: CircleUp ran both a Growth Partners equity fund and a Credit Advisors loan business, and no source read for this profile specifies whether its money to Beyond Meat was equity, a loan, or another facility, or how much it was. The honest statement is that CircleUp supplied capital to Beyond Meat among hundreds of consumer brands — not that it was an equity investor.

### The IPO and the fast-food deals

Beyond Meat priced its IPO at $25 a share on May 2, 2019 — the top of a $23–$25 range raised from $19–$21 — selling 9.625 million shares for about $240.6 million at a valuation of about $1.5 billion ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/business/beyond-meat-prices-ipo-at-top-of-range/)). The shares closed their first day at $65.75, up 163 percent, for a market value of about $3.8 billion — the best first-day gain for a US listing of that size since at least 2008 ([Associated Press](https://www.inc.com/associated-press/beyond-meat-ipo-shares-more-than-double.html)). The company had never made an annual profit: 2018 revenue was $87.9 million, up 170 percent year over year, with a net loss of $29.9 million ([Associated Press](https://www.inc.com/associated-press/beyond-meat-ipo-shares-more-than-double.html)).

At the peak of the excitement, the fast-food giants came calling. On January 26, 2021, PepsiCo and Beyond Meat formed The PLANeT Partnership, a joint venture to develop, produce and market snack and beverage products made from plant-based protein, on undisclosed financial terms ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/01/26/pepsico-and-beyond-meat-launch-poorly-named-joint-venture-for-new-plant-based-food-and-drinks/)). A month later, on February 25, 2021, Beyond Meat signed a three-year global agreement making it McDonald's preferred supplier for the McPlant patty — preferred, not exclusive — and a global collaboration with Yum! Brands to co-create plant-based menu items for KFC, Pizza Hut and Taco Bell ([Canadian Grocer](https://canadiangrocer.com/beyond-meat-inks-deal-mcdonalds-yum)). The deals built on earlier limited tests with both chains ([Canadian Grocer](https://canadiangrocer.com/beyond-meat-inks-deal-mcdonalds-yum)).

The McPlant then failed in the United States. McDonald's tested it in eight restaurants from November 2021, expanded to roughly 600 stores in February 2022, and ended the test in July 2022 with no national rollout ([QSR Magazine](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/food/menu-innovations/mcdonalds-ends-mcplant-test-us/)). The early eight-store test had sold about 500 burgers a week, or roughly 70 a day, but the expanded test sold about 20 a day — and three to five a day in rural stores — with franchisee sentiment described as "underwhelming" and no evidence to support a national rollout ([QSR Magazine](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/food/menu-innovations/mcdonalds-ends-mcplant-test-us/)). McDonald's US president Joe Erlinger later said the test "was not successful in either market" ([QSR Magazine](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/food/menu-innovations/mcdonalds-ends-mcplant-test-us/)). The McPlant fared better in Europe, launching in all restaurants across the UK and Ireland after a 250-store pilot ([QSR Magazine](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/food/menu-innovations/mcdonalds-ends-mcplant-test-us/)).

## The long decline and the rebrand to Beyond

Revenue peaked in 2021 and then fell for four consecutive years, while losses ran into the hundreds of millions. In 2022 management pivoted from a growth-above-all model to one prioritising cash flow and sustainable growth, cutting headcount and shrinking its North American co-manufacturer footprint from a peak of eight to three ([Supermarket Perimeter](https://www.supermarketperimeter.com/articles/9363-beyond-meat-strategizes-to-get-back-on-track-after-2022-losses)). In February 2025 the board approved a further cut of about 44 employees — roughly 17 percent of the global non-production workforce — and suspended operational activities in China, cutting about 95 percent of the China workforce ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1655210/000165521025000020/ex991pressrelease-q4andful.htm)).

The balance sheet was shored up in stages. In May 2025 the company closed a financing facility of up to $100 million in new senior secured debt from Unprocessed Foods, LLC, an affiliate of the Ahimsa Foundation, at 12.0 percent interest payable in kind, with warrants for up to 12.5 percent of outstanding shares ([Secured Finance Network](https://www.sfnet.com/home/industry-data-publications/the-secured-lender/tsl-express-daily-articles-news/tsl-express-daily-articles-news/2025/05/09/beyond-meat-announces-$100-million-new-senior-secured-financing-from-ahimsa-foundation-affiliate)). The debt restructuring that followed produced the $548.7 million gain behind the 2025 net income figure ([FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/04/01/beyond-meat-2025-full-year-results/)). The share price tells the same story in miniature: from a peak of nearly $235 in 2019, it fell below $1 in October 2025 after a debt deal, briefly spiked to $3.62 as a meme stock, then faded to $0.76 ([Los Angeles Times](https://edition.pagesuite.com/tribune/article_popover.aspx?guid=35acf1ee-6d72-49e1-84c4-5838a2531c57)).

In March 2026 the company rebranded as Beyond, The Plant Protein Co. — simply "Beyond" on packaging — and moved into protein drinks, launching the sparkling protein drink Beyond Immerse in January 2026 and planning a protein bar for the summer ([Los Angeles Times](https://edition.pagesuite.com/tribune/article_popover.aspx?guid=35acf1ee-6d72-49e1-84c4-5838a2531c57); [FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/04/01/beyond-meat-2025-full-year-results/)). Brown said the shorter name "provides for reduced emphasis on facsimile, a now-complicated frame that overshadows the real, high-quality protein offerings we provide to consumers," and the company stressed it is not moving away from plant-based meat ([Los Angeles Times](https://edition.pagesuite.com/tribune/article_popover.aspx?guid=35acf1ee-6d72-49e1-84c4-5838a2531c57)).

## Frequently asked questions

**When was Beyond Meat founded, and by whom?**
Beyond Meat was founded in 2009 by Ethan Brown, a former clean-energy executive. He licensed meatless-protein technology from University of Missouri professors Fu-hung Hsieh and Harold Huff, who had worked on it since the 1980s ([Associated Press](https://www.inc.com/associated-press/beyond-meat-ipo-shares-more-than-double.html)).

**What is CircleUp's connection to Beyond Meat?**
CircleUp is credited with supplying over $1 billion in capital to more than 500 consumer companies, including Beyond Meat, and its Helio platform named Beyond Meat to its CircleUp25 list in 2018 ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/425249/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup/?abkw=citybizsanfrancisco); [New Hope Network](https://www.newhope.com/industry-insights/natural-brands-fill-circleup25-s-2018-list)). No read source specifies the form, size or date of that capital, so this profile does not call CircleUp an equity investor.

**Why did the McPlant fail in the United States?**
McDonald's tested the Beyond Meat patty in eight US restaurants from November 2021 and expanded to about 600 stores in February 2022, but sales fell to roughly 20 burgers a day per store — three to five in rural areas — and franchisees saw no case for a national rollout, so the test ended in July 2022 ([QSR Magazine](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/food/menu-innovations/mcdonalds-ends-mcplant-test-us/)).

**Is Beyond Meat profitable?**
Not on operations. It has never made an annual operating profit, and its 2025 net income of $219.9 million came from a $548.7 million non-cash gain on restructuring its debt, while its operating loss more than doubled to $332.7 million ([FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/04/01/beyond-meat-2025-full-year-results/)).

**What is Beyond Meat called now?**
Since March 2026 it has branded itself as Beyond, The Plant Protein Co. — "Beyond" on packaging — as it expands from meat imitations into protein drinks and bars, while still making its plant-based burgers, chicken and other meat alternatives ([Los Angeles Times](https://edition.pagesuite.com/tribune/article_popover.aspx?guid=35acf1ee-6d72-49e1-84c4-5838a2531c57)).

## What this profile does not claim

It does not settle whether Brent Taylor was a co-founder — one 2016 source says so, all others credit Ethan Brown alone.
