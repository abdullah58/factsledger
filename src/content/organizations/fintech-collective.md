## Overview

FinTech Collective is a global early-stage venture capital firm based in [New York City](/places/new-york-city/), founded in 2012 to back entrepreneurs rewiring how money moves through the world ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)). It invests across capital markets, wealth and asset management, banking, lending, payments and insurance, and since 2021 also in decentralised finance and digital assets ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)). Its portfolio spans the United States, Latin America, the UK and Europe, and Africa, including [Axoni](/organizations/axoni/), Vestwell, Flutterwave, MoneyLion, NYDIG and Simetrik ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)).

What sets the firm apart is that its leaders are operators first. Managing Partners Brooks Gibbins and Gareth Jones worked together for more than two decades as entrepreneurs in fintech, building and selling four businesses for proceeds of more than $1.5 billion before founding the firm ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)). In August 2021 the firm raised a combined $250 million — $200 million for early-stage investments and $50 million for decentralised finance — taking its total assets under management past $500 million ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)).

## Operators turned investors

Gibbins and Jones met in their mid-twenties and spent the next twenty years building, scaling and exiting fintech businesses before turning to investing — a background the firm presents as its edge over career investors ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)). Gibbins has said the firm was among the first venture firms to recognise the fintech opportunity opened up by the global financial crisis, which he called a catalyst for a generation of innovation ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)). The pitch is hands-on: the firm says it "rolls up its sleeves" to help founders with strategy and operations, not just capital ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.io/)). Its limited partners include large institutional asset managers such as the State of Wisconsin Investment Board, the Teachers' Retirement System of the State of Illinois, Greenspring Associates and StepStone Group ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)). The firm is an SEC-registered investment adviser, and it has published a weekly fintech newsletter since 2014, with over 14,000 subscribers across 395 issues by August 2021 ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)).

## The portfolio: from Axoni to Vestwell

The earliest defining bet was Axoni: in December 2016 FinTech Collective joined the blockchain firm's $18 million Series A, led by [Wells Fargo](/organizations/wells-fargo/) and ICAP's [Euclid Opportunities](/organizations/euclid-opportunities/), alongside [Goldman Sachs](/organizations/goldman-sachs/), J.P. Morgan, Thomson Reuters, [Andreessen Horowitz](/organizations/andreessen-horowitz/), [F-Prime](/organizations/f-prime-capital/) and [Digital Currency Group](/organizations/digital-currency-group/) ([Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/vc-deal-of-the-day-axioni-raises-18m-series-a-46944.html?print)). It led Bogotá-based Simetrik's $24 million Series A in 2022 and backed its $55 million Series B in February 2024, led by Growth Equity at Goldman Sachs Asset Management ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/investment-banking/colombia-s-simetrik-secures-55m-series-b-round-led-by-goldman-sachs)). Simetrik, founded in 2019, automates financial-operations work such as reconciliation, and claimed at its Series B to monitor 200 million-plus records a day in 35-plus countries ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/investment-banking/colombia-s-simetrik-secures-55m-series-b-round-led-by-goldman-sachs)).

Vestwell is the flagship savings bet: backed from seed, FinTech Collective joined its $125 million round in December 2023, led by Lightspeed Venture Partners, taking its total raised to $227.5 million ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/12/20/vestwell-raises-125m-in-preempted-round-of-funding-to-help-businesses-power-workplace-savings-programs/)). Over 1 million people across 300,000 businesses used it, powering nearly $30 billion in savings assets; Vestwell powered 80% of live US state auto-IRA programmes ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/12/20/vestwell-raises-125m-in-preempted-round-of-funding-to-help-businesses-power-workplace-savings-programs/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is FinTech Collective?**
FinTech Collective is a global early-stage venture capital firm founded in New York in 2012, investing in fintech across capital markets, wealth management, banking, lending, payments, insurance and digital assets ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)). It is led by Managing Partners Brooks Gibbins and Gareth Jones, who previously built and sold four fintech businesses ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)).

**How big is FinTech Collective?**
In August 2021 it raised a combined $250 million — $200 million for early-stage investments and $50 million for decentralised finance — taking total assets under management past $500 million ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)). At that point it had invested in 53 portfolio companies across the US, Latin America, the UK and Europe, and Africa ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)).

**What are its best-known investments?**
Its best-known investments include Axoni, Vestwell, Flutterwave, MoneyLion, NYDIG, Ocrolus, Quovo and Simetrik ([Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)). Vestwell, its savings-infrastructure bet, served over 1 million savers and nearly $30 billion in assets by late 2023, powering 80% of live US state auto-IRA programmes ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/12/20/vestwell-raises-125m-in-preempted-round-of-funding-to-help-businesses-power-workplace-savings-programs/)).
