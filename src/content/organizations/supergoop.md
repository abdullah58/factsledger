## Overview

Supergoop! is an American sunscreen and skincare brand founded by former teacher Holly Thaggard in 2005, built on the idea that sunscreen should be worn every day rather than only at the beach. Based in San Antonio, [Texas](/places/texas/)Texas, it sells SPF-first skincare through prestige retailers and its own website, and since December 2021 it has been majority-owned by [Blackstone](/organizations/blackstone/)Blackstone Growth ([Cosmetics Business](https://cosmeticsbusiness.com/blackstone-buys-controlling-stake-of-sunscreen-brand-supergoop--197794); [Blackstone](https://www.blackstone.com/news/press/supergoop-and-blackstone-growth-bxg-partner-with-new-investors-to-teach-even-more-people-about-the-importance-of-daily-spf/)).

Thaggard had no beauty-industry background: she was a former third-grade teacher who had then spent a decade performing as a professional harpist. The trigger was personal — a close friend was diagnosed with skin cancer at 29 — and it pushed her to spend 2004 studying a sunscreen market she found sleepy and seasonal, with clinical-looking products that only appeared on shelves for a few summer weeks ([Foundr](https://foundr.com/articles/building-a-business/holly-thaggard); [Cosmetics Business](https://cosmeticsbusiness.com/blackstone-buys-controlling-stake-of-sunscreen-brand-supergoop--197794)). Her answer was a brand built entirely around SPF, positioned as skincare rather than beach care, with the tagline "Every. Single. Day." ([The Zoe Report](https://www.thezoereport.com/beauty/supergoop-sunscreen-movement)). BeautyMatter described it as the first prestige beauty brand 100% dedicated to UV protection ([BeautyMatter](https://beautymatter.com/articles/supergoop-gets-minority-investment-from-encore-capital)).

This profile covers the Supergoop! sunscreen company only. It makes no health or efficacy claims about the products — only business facts about the company, its funding, its leadership and its retail expansion.

## Founding and the school-first model

Thaggard did not start with a business plan. She wrote a sun-safety curriculum and took the product into schools, teaching children, parents and teachers why daily sun protection mattered before she tried to sell them anything ([Foundr](https://foundr.com/articles/building-a-business/holly-thaggard)). Public schools were closed to her — sunscreen is an FDA-regulated product and could not be handed out on campus — so she went door to door at private schools instead, and the programme eventually reached six schools across Louisiana and Texas ([Foundr](https://foundr.com/articles/building-a-business/holly-thaggard)). The brand launched in 2007, two years after its founding, with that curriculum as its first distribution channel ([Foundr](https://foundr.com/articles/building-a-business/holly-thaggard)).

The education mission outlived the school-sales model. Supergoop!'s Ounce by Ounce programme ships complimentary product to classrooms, and [Blackstone](https://www.blackstone.com/news/press/supergoop-and-blackstone-growth-bxg-partner-with-new-investors-to-teach-even-more-people-about-the-importance-of-daily-spf/)'s May 2022 announcement said it had donated more than 4,300 SPF pumps to over 1,600 schools and youth organisations across America ([Blackstone](https://www.blackstone.com/news/press/supergoop-and-blackstone-growth-bxg-partner-with-new-investors-to-teach-even-more-people-about-the-importance-of-daily-spf/)). Thaggard also made changing the rules part of the job, lobbying on Capitol Hill for laws allowing sunscreen in schools — a campaign Foundr calls her passion project ([Foundr](https://foundr.com/articles/building-a-business/holly-thaggard)).

## From classrooms to prestige retail

School-by-school selling could not scale, so Thaggard learned retail instead. Her first stockists were children's boutiques — Giggle, Pottery Barn Kids and FAO Schwarz — where she would stand in the store on weekends and talk to shoppers about SPF ([Foundr](https://foundr.com/articles/building-a-business/holly-thaggard)). Sephora then became the brand's first prestige retail partner, a deliberate choice: Thaggard wanted sunscreen shelved beside serums and moisturisers, not with seasonal beach gear ([The Zoe Report](https://www.thezoereport.com/beauty/supergoop-sunscreen-movement)).

The Sephora negotiation shows how stubbornly she defended that positioning. When the retailer first offered a warm-weather-only placement, Thaggard turned it down — seasonalising the product would have defeated the daily-use mission — and only accepted once the brand won full-year distribution on the retailer's "skincare favorites" wall ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/melissa-angell/supergoop-holly-thaggard-amanda-baldwin.html)). The strategy worked: the brand's hero product, Unseen Sunscreen SPF 50, is a clear, jelly-like formula that doubles as a makeup primer, and it became one of the brand's most popular products ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/commerce/supergoop-partners-with-target-and-the-pga-as-it-chases-mass-appeal/?itm_source=parsely-api)). By late 2021 the range ran to nearly 50 formulas, spanning the Unseen and Glowscreen lines, the PLAY family line, and SPF makeup and moisturisers ([WebWire](https://www.webwire.com/ViewPressRel.asp?aId=283053); [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/supergoop-names-amazon-vet-melis-del-rey-ceo/806763/)). International distribution followed the same prestige route: products were available in the United Kingdom, Canada and Sephora stores across Southeast Asia from 2017, with further European expansion in 2023 and a Middle East launch due by Christmas that year ([The Zoe Report](https://www.thezoereport.com/beauty/supergoop-sunscreen-movement)).

## Funding and the Blackstone majority investment

Supergoop! grew slowly and on small money for a decade. By 2015 it had raised $6.5 million in funding, and tennis star Maria Sharapova had invested in 2014 ([The Zoe Report](https://www.thezoereport.com/beauty/supergoop-sunscreen-movement); [BeautyMatter](https://beautymatter.com/articles/supergoop-gets-minority-investment-from-encore-capital)). The first institutional milestone came in March 2018, when Encore Consumer Capital took a minority stake — terms undisclosed, with the money earmarked mainly for marketing — at a point when WWD industry sources estimated the brand could do $45–50 million in retail sales that year ([BeautyMatter](https://beautymatter.com/articles/supergoop-gets-minority-investment-from-encore-capital)).

The December 2021 deal was the turning point. Funds managed by Blackstone Growth (BXG) entered into a definitive agreement for a majority investment on December 21, 2021, with Thaggard, chief executive Amanda Baldwin and the senior management team keeping significant equity ([WebWire](https://www.webwire.com/ViewPressRel.asp?aId=283053)). Earlier investors rolled their stakes over, including the Thaggard and Emery families, Encore Consumer Capital, CULT Capital, Green Park & Golf Ventures, [CircleUp](/organizations/circleup/)CircleUp, Grace Beauty and SWAT Equity ([Cosmetics Business](https://cosmeticsbusiness.com/blackstone-buys-controlling-stake-of-sunscreen-brand-supergoop--197794)). CircleUp's presence on the cap table is confirmed by both the 2018 BeautyMatter report, which names it among the earlier backers, and the 2021 deal announcement ([BeautyMatter](https://beautymatter.com/articles/supergoop-gets-minority-investment-from-encore-capital); [Cosmetics Business](https://cosmeticsbusiness.com/blackstone-buys-controlling-stake-of-sunscreen-brand-supergoop--197794)). Supergoop! was advised by Goldman Sachs and Greenberg Traurig; Blackstone's financial adviser was Raymond James and its legal adviser Simpson Thacher & Bartlett ([WebWire](https://www.webwire.com/ViewPressRel.asp?aId=283053)).

The deal's terms were never disclosed, but its scale can be read from what followed. The Zoe Report put the company's valuation after the Blackstone deal at closer to $700 million, and Foundr reported $250 million in sales for 2022 ([The Zoe Report](https://www.thezoereport.com/beauty/supergoop-sunscreen-movement); [Foundr](https://foundr.com/articles/building-a-business/holly-thaggard)). In May 2022 the company announced a new investor group drawn from business, media and entertainment — Hugh Jackman, Jenna Lyons and Michelle Wie, plus Sara Blakely of SPANX and Whitney Wolfe Herd of Bumble from Blackstone's own portfolio, alongside Main Street Advisors, the I Promise School, G9 Ventures and SC Holdings ([Blackstone](https://www.blackstone.com/news/press/supergoop-and-blackstone-growth-bxg-partner-with-new-investors-to-teach-even-more-people-about-the-importance-of-daily-spf/)).

## Leadership changes, 2023–2025

The growth years were led by Amanda Baldwin, who joined as president in 2016 and took the company from $1 million to $40 million in annual sales before becoming chief executive in 2021, the same year Blackstone invested ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/melissa-angell/supergoop-holly-thaggard-amanda-baldwin.html)). That stability ended in 2023, when Baldwin announced she would leave to become chief executive of Olaplex. Lisa Sequino, formerly chief executive of JLo Beauty, succeeded her — but left after about a year and a half to join The Estée Lauder Companies, and Gregory Polcer took over as interim chief executive in May 2025 ([Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/supergoop-names-amazon-vet-melis-del-rey-ceo/806763/)). Thaggard herself stepped away from day-to-day governance in April 2025, two decades after founding the brand ([Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/supergoop-names-amazon-vet-melis-del-rey-ceo/806763/)).

The search ended in December 2025 with Melis del Rey, named chief executive and board director effective December 8, 2025. She was most recently general manager of [Amazon](/organizations/amazon/)Amazon U.S. Health and Beauty, spent over ten years at Amazon, and previously spent more than a decade at Procter & Gamble in marketing and sales leadership across its global beauty portfolio ([Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/supergoop-names-amazon-vet-melis-del-rey-ceo/806763/)). Polcer stayed on the board, and Supergoop!'s chairman Edgar Huber said del Rey's experience across beauty, e-commerce and omnichannel strategy suited her to lead the brand "into its next era of growth" ([Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/supergoop-names-amazon-vet-melis-del-rey-ceo/806763/)).

## The mass-market push: Target and the PGA TOUR

Under del Rey, Supergoop! is moving from prestige niche toward the mainstream. In January 2026 the brand expanded into Target, its first mass-market retailer, joining a roster that already included Amazon, Ulta Beauty, Sephora and TikTok Shop alongside direct-to-consumer ecommerce ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/commerce/supergoop-partners-with-target-and-the-pga-as-it-chases-mass-appeal/?itm_source=parsely-api)). The same push reshaped its marketing: Lauren Weinberg joined as chief marketing officer in February 2026 from Peloton, January Digital was named agency of record for media planning and buying, and the brand began sponsoring sports — a deal with Alterra Mountain Company puts samples across 13 mountain destinations ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/commerce/supergoop-partners-with-target-and-the-pga-as-it-chases-mass-appeal/?itm_source=parsely-api)).

The biggest stage for that strategy is golf. On March 4, 2026 the PGA TOUR and Supergoop! announced a multi-year partnership naming the brand Official Suncare of the PGA TOUR and PGA TOUR Champions, running through 2030, with 13-time PGA TOUR winner Justin Rose joining as brand ambassador ([PGA TOUR](https://www.pgatour.com/article/news/company/2026/03/03/supergoop-named-official-sun-care-of-pga-tour-champions-tour)). The deal includes a "Reapplication Station" sampling activation that debuted at THE PLAYERS Championship, March 10–15 at TPC Sawgrass, and product placement at select TPC Network properties and tournament fan shops ([PGA TOUR](https://www.pgatour.com/article/news/company/2026/03/03/supergoop-named-official-sun-care-of-pga-tour-champions-tour)). Del Rey framed it as the mission on "one of the world's most visible outdoor stages" — golf, she said, is defined by extended time outdoors and a focus on performance, which mirrors how the brand wants SPF to show up in everyday life ([PGA TOUR](https://www.pgatour.com/article/news/company/2026/03/03/supergoop-named-official-sun-care-of-pga-tour-champions-tour)).

The bet is that awareness, not loyalty, is the constraint: the brand's own data puts customer repurchase intent at 90%, so the 2026 campaigns are built around introducing Unseen Sunscreen to people who have never tried it, rather than selling to existing customers ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/commerce/supergoop-partners-with-target-and-the-pga-as-it-chases-mass-appeal/?itm_source=parsely-api)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who founded Supergoop!?**
Holly Thaggard, a former third-grade teacher and professional harpist, founded Supergoop! in 2005 after a close friend was diagnosed with skin cancer at 29. The brand launched in 2007, sold first through a sun-safety curriculum in private schools ([Foundr](https://foundr.com/articles/building-a-business/holly-thaggard); [Cosmetics Business](https://cosmeticsbusiness.com/blackstone-buys-controlling-stake-of-sunscreen-brand-supergoop--197794)).

**Who owns Supergoop!?**
Funds managed by Blackstone Growth have held a majority stake since December 2021. Founder Holly Thaggard, the senior management team and earlier investors including CircleUp kept equity in the business; the deal's terms were never disclosed ([WebWire](https://www.webwire.com/ViewPressRel.asp?aId=283053)).

**Who is Supergoop!'s chief executive?**
Melis del Rey, effective December 8, 2025. She previously ran Amazon U.S. Health and Beauty. She succeeded interim chief executive Gregory Polcer; before him, Lisa Sequino and Amanda Baldwin held the role ([Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/supergoop-names-amazon-vet-melis-del-rey-ceo/806763/)).

**Where is Supergoop! sold?**
Through prestige retailers Sephora and Ulta Beauty, mass retailer Target (from January 2026), Amazon and TikTok Shop, and directly to consumers online ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/commerce/supergoop-partners-with-target-and-the-pga-as-it-chases-mass-appeal/?itm_source=parsely-api)).

**What is Unseen Sunscreen?**
Unseen Sunscreen SPF 50 is Supergoop!'s hero product: a clear, jelly-like formula that doubles as a makeup primer and is one of the brand's most popular products ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/commerce/supergoop-partners-with-target-and-the-pga-as-it-chases-mass-appeal/?itm_source=parsely-api)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not claim a settled founding year: Blackstone and Cosmetics Business say 2005, while BeautyMatter says the brand was founded in 2007 and Foundr says it launched in 2007 — this profile uses 2005 as the founding year and 2007 as the launch. It does not claim the friend whose diagnosis prompted the founding died of skin cancer: that version appears only in The Zoe Report, while Blackstone and Cosmetics Business say she was diagnosed. It does not state the year Supergoop! entered Sephora, the size of Blackstone's majority stake, or the deal's terms, because none was verified from a fully read source. It does not state current annual sales: the latest verified figure is $250 million for 2022. It makes no claim about whether the products work, how effective they are, or whether they are safe — this is a business profile, not a product review. The company-stated awards (two TIME Best Invention honours, seven consecutive Allure Best of Beauty seals) are reported as the company's claims, not independently verified honours.
