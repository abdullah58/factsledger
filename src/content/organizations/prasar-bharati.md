Prasar Bharati is India's statutory public-service broadcaster, established under the Prasar Bharati (Broadcasting Corporation of India) Act, 1990, operating since 1997 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/prasoon-joshi-appointed-chairman-prasar-bharati-126050200754_1.html)). It is the parent of the country's two legacy broadcast networks — [Doordarshan](/organizations/doordarshan/) on television and Akashvani, formerly All India Radio, on radio ([State Media Monitor](https://statemediamonitor.com/2026/07/prasar-bharati/)) — and it runs the WAVES streaming platform, where the second season of [Krish, Trish and Baltiboy](/creative-works/krish-trish-and-baltiboy/) is available ([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/entertainment/second-season-of-animated-series-krish-trish-and-baltiboy-bharat-hain-hum-launched-at-iffi/)).

## A statutory corporation under law, a ministry portfolio in practice

Constituted under the 1990 Act and brought into existence on 23 November 1997, Prasar Bharati absorbed All India Radio and Doordarshan — until then departments of the [Ministry of Information and Broadcasting](/organizations/ministry-of-information-and-broadcasting/) — out of direct government control ([State Media Monitor](https://statemediamonitor.com/2026/07/prasar-bharati/)). A fifteen-member board governs it: a part-time chairman, a chief executive, finance and personnel members, six part-time members, the two directors-general, a ministry nominee and two employee representatives ([State Media Monitor](https://statemediamonitor.com/2026/07/prasar-bharati/)). Gaurav Dwivedi, a 1995-batch IAS officer, has been CEO since 22 November 2022 ([State Media Monitor](https://statemediamonitor.com/2026/07/prasar-bharati/); [Exchange4Media](https://www.exchange4media.com/media-tv-news/linear-tv-is-among-waves-otts-top-genres-amid-cable-tussle-gaurav-dwivedi-158785.html)); Prasoon Joshi was appointed chairman by the ministry in May 2026 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/prasoon-joshi-appointed-chairman-prasar-bharati-126050200754_1.html)). The link is structural: it functions "within the portfolio" of the ministry, and section 23 of the Act lets the government issue binding directions on sovereignty, security or public-order grounds ([State Media Monitor](https://statemediamonitor.com/2026/07/prasar-bharati/)); hence the monitor's "state-controlled" classification — attributed here, not this profile's verdict.

## Two networks that blanket the country

Doordarshan is the national television service — DD National, DD News, DD India, DD Sports, DD Bharati and DD Kisan among its national feeds, alongside regional and state services — while Akashvani runs roughly 470 broadcasting centres producing terrestrial programming in 23 languages and 179 dialects ([State Media Monitor](https://statemediamonitor.com/2026/07/prasar-bharati/)). In May 2023 the corporation directed that references to "All India Radio" be replaced with "Akashvani", the name used in the 1990 Act itself, and both the corporation and the ministry now use Akashvani as the primary designation ([State Media Monitor](https://statemediamonitor.com/2026/07/prasar-bharati/)). Distribution extends beyond transmitters: DD Free Dish is India's only free-to-air direct-to-home service and its largest distributed DTH platform, while the digital stable includes the NewsOnAir app, the Prasar Bharati News Service and PB-SHABD — a syndication service launched in March 2024 supplying news material to other organisations across some fifty categories in major Indian languages ([State Media Monitor](https://statemediamonitor.com/2026/07/prasar-bharati/)).

## WAVES: the streaming leg

Prasar Bharati launched WAVES, its OTT platform, at the 55th [International Film Festival of India](/events/international-film-festival-of-india/) in Goa on 20 November 2024 ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2024/Nov/20/prasar-bhartis-ott-waves-launched); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/waves-ott-crosses-1-2-crore-users-as-prasar-bharati-opens-platform-to-creators-ws-o-111199.htm)). It debuted with 65 channels (40 live) in 10 languages, folding in Doordarshan and All India Radio services plus ONDC-integrated shopping ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2024/Nov/20/prasar-bhartis-ott-waves-launched)). Dwivedi said the shift was imperative: "When the viewers are consuming content digitally today, it was imperative for us to shift to this mode" ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2024/Nov/20/prasar-bhartis-ott-waves-launched)). By late September 2026 the platform reported 1.2 crore registered users and 1.5 crore downloads, with 24,000 titles in 130-plus countries and 26-plus languages ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/waves-ott-crosses-1-2-crore-users-as-prasar-bharati-opens-platform-to-creators-ws-o-111199.htm)) — a pace implying genuine scale for the digital pivot. Disambiguation: the separate World Audio Visual and Entertainment Summit, held in Mumbai in May 2025, shares the WAVES acronym but is an industry event, not the platform ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/waves-ott-crosses-1-2-crore-users-as-prasar-bharati-opens-platform-to-creators-ws-o-111199.htm)).

## The state's loudest microphone: Mann Ki Baat

Prasar Bharati's most politically visible programme is one it merely carries: [Mann Ki Baat](/creative-works/mann-ki-baat/), the Prime Minister's monthly radio address, which CEO Dwivedi said is broadcast "on the entire All India Radio and Doordarshan network" at 11 am on the last Sunday of every month ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/media/entertainment/media/mann-ki-baat-increased-radio-listening-renewed-peoples-interest-in-medium-prasar-bharati-ceo/articleshow/99851609.cms?from=mdr)). The programme began on 3 October 2014, and Dwivedi credited it with increasing radio listening and renewing public interest in the medium ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/media/entertainment/media/mann-ki-baat-increased-radio-listening-renewed-peoples-interest-in-medium-prasar-bharati-ceo/articleshow/99851609.cms?from=mdr)). A study he cited put its cumulative reach at more than 100 crore listeners — a striking figure that rests on that cited study, flagged in openItems rather than stated as fact ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/media/entertainment/media/mann-ki-baat-increased-radio-listening-renewed-peoples-interest-in-medium-prasar-bharati-ceo/articleshow/99851609.cms?from=mdr)). All India Radio translates the programme into 22 Indian languages, 29 dialects and 11 foreign languages, and it airs from more than 500 AIR broadcast centres ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/media/entertainment/media/mann-ki-baat-increased-radio-listening-renewed-peoples-interest-in-medium-prasar-bharati-ceo/articleshow/99851609.cms?from=mdr)). That carriage scale explains why the monthly address is the corporation's highest-profile recurring programme.

## Frequently asked questions

**What exactly is Prasar Bharati?**
India's statutory public-service broadcaster, created under the Prasar Bharati Act, 1990 and operating since 1997. It owns Doordarshan (television) and Akashvani/All India Radio (radio), runs the WAVES OTT platform and DD Free Dish, and functions within the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting's portfolio.

**Is All India Radio still called that?**
The 1990 Act names the radio network Akashvani, and in May 2023 the corporation directed that "All India Radio" be replaced with "Akashvani" in official usage; both the corporation and the ministry now use Akashvani as the primary designation. The abbreviation AIR persists in older references.

**What is WAVES, and is it the same as the WAVES Summit?**
WAVES is Prasar Bharati's OTT streaming platform, launched at the 55th IFFI in Goa on 20 November 2024 — live TV and radio, on-demand video and audio, games, publications and ONDC-integrated shopping. It is not the World Audio Visual and Entertainment Summit, the media-industry event first held in Mumbai in May 2025 that shares the acronym.

**Where can I watch Krish, Trish and Baltiboy Season 2?**
Season 2, subtitled *Bharat Hain Hum*, was launched at IFFI in Goa and is available on Waves, Prasar Bharati's OTT platform, alongside its multi-platform, multilingual release in 12 Indian and 7 international languages, per The Tribune's launch report.
