## Overview

Localazy is a Czech software-localization (translation-management) company founded by developer-entrepreneur Václav
Hodek and launched publicly in March 2020 in Brno
([CzechCrunch](https://cc.cz/kazdy-ma-pravo-pouzivat-mobilni-aplikace-ve-svem-jazyce-veri-vaclav-hodek-jeho-nastroj-localazy-ziskava-desitky-milionu/);
[Lupa.cz](https://www.lupa.cz/aktuality/brnenske-localazy-ziskalo-desitky-milionu-na-automaticke-preklady-aplikaci/)).
The platform combines machine and AI translation, professional human translators, and community contributors to help
software teams ship products in many languages, and the language-industry press reported users from 113 countries in
2023
([Slator](https://slator.com/localazy-secures-1m-remove-language-barriers-1b-people-new-investment-round/)).
The company raised a seed round from Prague-based Lighthouse Ventures in 2020 and a second round in June 2023 at a
€4.5 million valuation, bringing total reported funding to nearly €1 million
([Slator](https://slator.com/localazy-secures-1m-remove-language-barriers-1b-people-new-investment-round/);
[Forbes Czech Republic](https://forbes.cz/zaujali-i-hlavenku-startupiste-od-brna-ziskali-milionovou-investici/)).

## Founder and origins

Before Localazy, Hodek ran his own software studio and then concentrated on his own mobile apps — most notably
Floating Apps, an Android multitasking app that CzechCrunch reported at 7.5 million downloads in 2020
([CzechCrunch](https://cc.cz/kazdy-ma-pravo-pouzivat-mobilni-aplikace-ve-svem-jazyce-veri-vaclav-hodek-jeho-nastroj-localazy-ziskava-desitky-milionu/)).
Selling apps worldwide, he noticed two things: users struggled with untranslated apps, and every new language
unlocked faster growth — Japan, famously hard to enter, became his second-largest market after the US. But managing
translations across 35 languages with the tools then available was painful, so he decided to build his own solution
([FoundersToday](https://www.founderstoday.news/interview-with-vaclav-hodek-ceo-localazy/)). He developed the first
version of Localazy as a side project alongside client work and went full-time once early users responded
([FoundersToday](https://www.founderstoday.news/interview-with-vaclav-hodek-ceo-localazy/)). The company was
incorporated as Localazy s.r.o. on 18 March 2020 with Hodek as managing director
([Chytrý rejstřík](https://www.chytryrejstrik.cz/ico-09050451/localazy-s-r-o)), and within its first month public the
platform secured seed investment from Lighthouse Ventures, whose managing partner Michal Zálešák called it "a bet on
a capable entrepreneur who has already achieved success previously"
([CzechCrunch](https://cc.cz/kazdy-ma-pravo-pouzivat-mobilni-aplikace-ve-svem-jazyce-veri-vaclav-hodek-jeho-nastroj-localazy-ziskava-desitky-milionu/)).

## Platform and product

Localazy is a translation-management system offering a choice of approaches: integrated AI and machine translation,
professional human translation services, an interface for a customer's own translators, or community volunteers —
with no seat limits, so a project's community can participate freely
([FoundersToday](https://www.founderstoday.news/interview-with-vaclav-hodek-ceo-localazy/)). Hodek describes the
typical turnaround for a medium-sized project as about one week, while machine-translation-first workflows can
deliver initial translations in seconds for later human review
([FoundersToday](https://www.founderstoday.news/interview-with-vaclav-hodek-ceo-localazy/)). The company says the
platform integrates with more than 50 frameworks, tools and file formats and is certified to ISO 27001 and SOC 2 —
company-supplied claims from its own release
([Newsfile](https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/newsfile-2024-8-28-localazy-strengthens-its-position-in-czech-tech-by-joining-innovation-promoting-ceftas)).
Documented integrations include a Figma plugin, a Strapi plugin, Webflow and Zendesk connectors, webhooks, a public
API and a CLI
([Localazy blog](https://localazy.com/blog/release-update-introducing-more-transparent-machine-translation-usage);
[Localazy blog](https://localazy.com/blog/bridging-the-gap-with-localazy-4-2025-in-review)). In October 2023
Localazy partnered with Poedit — the desktop translation editor created in 2001 by Czech developer Václav Slavík —
on an integration that syncs cloud-based management with offline desktop translation work
([MultiLingual](https://multilingual.com/localazy-and-poedit-join-forces-for-offline-friendly-translation-workflows/)).

## Funding and ownership

In June 2023 Forbes Czech Republic reported a second funding round of approximately €500,000, led by the Garage
Angels group — including named investors Jiří Hlavenka and Karel Obluk — alongside 12Bullets, JIC Ventures and
returning investor Lighthouse Ventures
([Forbes Czech Republic](https://forbes.cz/zaujali-i-hlavenku-startupiste-od-brna-ziskali-milionovou-investici/)).
Slator's contemporaneous industry reporting clarifies the arithmetic behind the conflicting headlines: the fresh
round was "several hundred thousands euros," the similar-sized 2020 Lighthouse round brought the cumulative total to
"nearly €1M," and the company's valuation reached €4.5M
([Slator](https://slator.com/localazy-secures-1m-remove-language-barriers-1b-people-new-investment-round/)). AIN's
headline instead announced "fresh €1 million funding" — the one outlier phrasing, documented in the open items
([AIN](https://en.ain.ua/2023/06/30/czech-localazy-secures-1m/)). Both Slator and Forbes described the company as
already profitable at the time
([Slator](https://slator.com/localazy-secures-1m-remove-language-barriers-1b-people-new-investment-round/)).
Czech business-register data records Hodek with a 56.22% majority stake, alongside Lighthouse Seed Fund (24.89%),
LWi (10%), GA Investiční LCL (4.44%), JIC Ventures (2.22%) and 12bullets (2.22%); the same data puts 2024 turnover at
CZK 10–30 million and the 2025 net result at CZK −1.78 million on CZK 8.37 million in assets — aggregator figures,
flagged as indicative rather than certified
([Chytrý rejstřík](https://www.chytryrejstrik.cz/ico-09050451/localazy-s-r-o)).

## Growth, clients and recognition

Named clients in independent press include the virtual-cycling platform ROUVY and the cloud provider Scaleway, plus
— per Slator — banks and automotive manufacturers; non-profit and social-enterprise users named include Plastic Bank,
Donar Online and CARE
([AIN](https://en.ain.ua/2023/06/30/czech-localazy-secures-1m/);
[Slator](https://slator.com/localazy-secures-1m-remove-language-barriers-1b-people-new-investment-round/)). In April
2024 Localazy expanded into the Polish market, covered by AIN and independently by Tech.eu
([AIN](https://en.ain.ua/2024/04/30/localazy-expands-into-polish-cee-markets/);
[Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2024/04/30/localazy-brings-its-localised-translation-services-to-polish-brands/)); Hodek
called Poland "the natural next step," noting that part of the team is based in Katowice
([FoundersToday](https://www.founderstoday.news/interview-with-vaclav-hodek-ceo-localazy/)). In August 2024 the
company joined the Czech Fintech Association (CEFTAS), a network of more than 40 Czech tech businesses
([Newsfile](https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/newsfile-2024-8-28-localazy-strengthens-its-position-in-czech-tech-by-joining-innovation-promoting-ceftas)).
Hodek has also described the company's non-profit work: supporting organizations at the start of the war in Ukraine,
backing environmental causes, and drawing on his relationship with the Czech deaf community — "any non-profit is
welcome to contact us," he said
([FoundersToday](https://www.founderstoday.news/interview-with-vaclav-hodek-ceo-localazy/)). In HackerNoon's
Startups of the Year 2024 program, Localazy earned an honorable mention in the AI Wrappers industry category
([HackerNoon](https://sia.hackernoon.com/startups-of-the-year-2024-winners-ai-industry)).

## 2025: AI releases and the year in review

In July 2025 Localazy replaced its 2-million-character machine-translation fair-use cap with a transparent credit
system — for example, the $39 Professional plan includes $5 in credits (12,500 words) and bulk automated
translation costs $0.0004 per word across engines
([Localazy blog](https://localazy.com/blog/release-update-introducing-more-transparent-machine-translation-usage)).
In October 2025 the company launched style guides — defining tone, formality, audience and per-language rules in one
place — alongside a new Localazy AI translation engine that automatically follows the project's glossary and style
guide; DeepL and Google Translate were also made to respect glossary terms
([Localazy blog](https://localazy.com/blog/release-update-ai-translation-that-understands-your-audience)), a launch
noted independently by the locanucu localization-industry newsletter
([locanucu](https://www.locanucu.com/2025/11/localization-news-24112025.html)). Dark mode shipped in beta in November
2025, and the company's 2025 year-in-review added rule-based Automations, Webflow and Strapi v5 integrations, a
navigation redesign, an ambassador rewards program, and four new human-translation languages — Somali, Tigrinya,
Punjabi and Bengali — alongside 18 podcast episodes, two hackathons and two new team members
([Localazy blog](https://localazy.com/blog/bridging-the-gap-with-localazy-4-2025-in-review)). For scale, the same
review reports over 200 million processed translations across 38,000 projects in 2025, with French, German and
Spanish the top target languages — company-reported figures, not independently verified
([Localazy blog](https://localazy.com/blog/bridging-the-gap-with-localazy-4-2025-in-review)).
