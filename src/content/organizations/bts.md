## Overview

BTS is a seven-member South Korean boy band — RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V and Jungkook — formed under Big Hit Entertainment and debuted in 2013 ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band); [Recording Academy](https://www.grammy.com/artists/bts/287749/)). The group became the first Korean act to top both the Billboard Hot 100 and the Billboard 200, earned five Grammy nominations, paused for mandatory military service in 2022, and returned with the album ARIRANG in March 2026 ([Recording Academy](https://www.grammy.com/artists/bts/287749/); [EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)).

The name BTS stands for Bangtan Sonyeondan, Korean for "Bulletproof Boy Scouts" ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)). The members co-write and co-produce much of their own output, and their debut single criticised South Korea's demanding education system ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)). This profile covers that boy band only: the same three letters are also used as an abbreviation for other things, including "behind the scenes" in film and television production, which are not this subject. The group's fans are collectively known as ARMY, and by 2022 BTS had become the best-selling artist in South Korean history ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)). Big Hit Entertainment later rebranded as HYBE, with Big Hit Music as the group's label ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)).

## From debut to the Billboard 200

BTS debuted on 13 June 2013, when their first live performance aired on M Countdown, performing "No More Dream" from their debut single album 2 Cool 4 Skool, which had been released the day before ([Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/p/11-iconic-bts-moments-in-honor-of-the-bands-6th-anniversary-17991873)). Their climb up the American charts was gradual: their first album to reach a Billboard chart, The Most Beautiful Moment in Life, Pt. 2, reached No. 171 on the Billboard 200 in 2015, and Wings peaked at No. 26 in 2016 ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/entertainment/the-members-of-bts-finally-acknowledge-that-theyve-made-it/11833359); [PopCrush](https://y105music.com/bts-billboard-charts-history-timeline/)). In 2017 "DNA" became the group's first Hot 100 entry, at No. 85, and rose to No. 67, then the highest position for a K-pop group, while the "MIC Drop" remix reached No. 28, the first Top 40 entry for any K-pop group ([PopCrush](https://y105music.com/bts-billboard-charts-history-timeline/)). That same year BTS became the first K-pop group to win Top Social Artist at the [Billboard Music Awards](/events/billboard-music-awards/), and in May 2018 they became the first K-pop group to perform at the ceremony ([PopCrush](https://y105music.com/bts-billboard-charts-history-timeline/)).

## The album firsts: Love Yourself: Tear

Love Yourself: Tear, released in May 2018, debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200, making BTS the first Korean act to top the chart, with 135,000 equivalent album units in its first week, of which 100,000 were traditional album sales ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/bts-conquers-no-1-spot-on-the-billboard-200/11628166)). It was also the first primarily foreign-language No. 1 album in almost 12 years, and "Fake Love" became the first Top 10 Hot 100 song by a K-pop group, at No. 10 ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/bts-conquers-no-1-spot-on-the-billboard-200/11628166); [PopCrush](https://y105music.com/bts-billboard-charts-history-timeline/)). The singles chart proved harder than the albums chart, because it needs a song to reach the general public rather than only fans buying albums ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/entertainment/the-members-of-bts-finally-acknowledge-that-theyve-made-it/11833359)).

## Dynamite, Life Goes On and Butter

"Dynamite", released on 21 August 2020, debuted at No. 1 on the Hot 100 in the [United States](/places/united-states/), making BTS the first K-pop act and the first all-South-Korean act to top the chart; the previous best for a Korean act had been Psy's "Gangnam Style" at No. 2 in 2012 ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/entertainment/the-members-of-bts-finally-acknowledge-that-theyve-made-it/11833359)). It drew 33.9 million US streams and 300,000 sales in its first week, and also topped the Global 200 for three weeks ([Reuters](https://www.tbsnews.net/glitz/bts-score-first-korean-band-billboard-chart-dynamite-126697); [Soompi](https://www.soompi.com/article/1441102wpp/bts-sets-records-as-life-goes-on-tops-billboards-global-200-charts)). "Life Goes On" then entered the Hot 100 at No. 1 as the first song sung predominantly in Korean to top the chart, and topped the Global 200 with 152.5 million streams and 84,000 downloads in a single week ([Billboard](https://musicnow.iprorecords.com/?p=2058); [Soompi](https://www.soompi.com/article/1441102wpp/bts-sets-records-as-life-goes-on-tops-billboards-global-200-charts)). "Butter" spent ten weeks at No. 1 on the Hot 100, making it only the 40th single in the chart's 63-year history to reach ten weeks — evidence that a Korean act could hold the summit of the American singles chart, not merely reach it ([Billboard](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2021/09/07/bts-butter-rebounds-for-10th-week-atop-billboard-hot-100-kanye-west-walker-hayes-hit-top-10/)).

## The military pause and the 2026 return

In June 2022 the group announced in a video a break of then-unspecified length; RM said ten years on BTS's schedule had left him unable to mature further ([NPR](https://www.mtpr.org/2022-10-17/bts-announces-upcoming-korean-military-service)). On 17 October 2022, Big Hit announced that all seven members would serve in the South Korean military and come back together "around 2025", with Jin, the oldest, enlisting first ([NPR](https://www.mtpr.org/2022-10-17/bts-announces-upcoming-korean-military-service)). South Korea requires all able-bodied men to serve at least 18 months; a 2020 revision of the Military Service Act had let members delay enlistment until 30 ([NPR](https://www.mtpr.org/2022-10-17/bts-announces-upcoming-korean-military-service); [Associated Press](https://gmg-wkmg-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/entertainment/2025/06/20/all-of-bts-has-returned-as-suga-is-discharged-from-an-alternative-form-of-military-service/)). All seven renewed their contracts in 2023 to reunite after service ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)). Discharges came in sequence: Jin in June 2024, J-Hope in October 2024, RM and V on 10 June 2025, Jimin and Jungkook on 11 June 2025, and Suga, the last, on 21 June 2025 ([Associated Press](https://gmg-wkmg-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/entertainment/2025/06/20/all-of-bts-has-returned-as-suga-is-discharged-from-an-alternative-form-of-military-service/)). Six served in the army; Suga served as a social service agent, an alternative form of service ([Associated Press](https://gmg-wkmg-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/entertainment/2025/06/20/all-of-bts-has-returned-as-suga-is-discharged-from-an-alternative-form-of-military-service/)). On 20 March 2026 BTS released ARIRANG, their first full-group album in nearly four years, followed by a world tour ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)).

## Beyond the charts: the UN, ARMY and cultural reach

BTS launched the "Love Myself" campaign with UNICEF in November 2017, backing its #ENDviolence programme for children and young people ([UNICEF](https://www.unicef.org/press-releases/we-have-learned-love-ourselves-so-now-i-urge-you-speak-yourself)). On 24 September 2018, RM addressed the 73rd session of the UN General Assembly in [New York City](/places/new-york-city/) at the launch of Generation Unlimited, closing: "we have learned to love ourselves, so now I urge you to 'speak yourself'" ([UNICEF](https://www.unicef.org/press-releases/we-have-learned-love-ourselves-so-now-i-urge-you-speak-yourself)). The group addressed the UN again in 2020 and a third time in 2021, when it promoted vaccination during COVID-19 ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)).

The recognition was not only musical. BTS became the first Korean act, and the first primarily non-English-language act, named IFPI Global Recording Artist of the Year for 2020, and earned five Grammy nominations between 2021 and 2023 without a win ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band); [Recording Academy](https://www.grammy.com/artists/bts/287749/)). Economists estimated the group added over $5 billion a year to the South Korean economy ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)). The fandom's organised power drew press attention: [Variety](/organizations/variety/) reported on how BTS and ARMY could change the music industry, and BTS collaborated with Western artists including [Nicki Minaj](/people/nicki-minaj/) ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band); [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/p/11-iconic-bts-moments-in-honor-of-the-bands-6th-anniversary-17991873)).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who are the members of BTS?

BTS has seven members: RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V and Jungkook ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)). RM is the group's leader, Jin is the oldest member and Jungkook the youngest, and the members co-write and co-produce much of their own output rather than only performing songs written for them ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)).

### When did BTS debut?

BTS debuted in June 2013 under Big Hit Entertainment, with the single album 2 Cool 4 Skool ([Recording Academy](https://www.grammy.com/artists/bts/287749/); [EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)). Their first live performance aired on M Countdown on 13 June 2013, when they performed "No More Dream", the album's lead single ([Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/p/11-iconic-bts-moments-in-honor-of-the-bands-6th-anniversary-17991873)).

### What was BTS's first Billboard Hot 100 No. 1?

"Dynamite", released on 21 August 2020, debuted at No. 1 on the Hot 100 in September 2020 ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/entertainment/the-members-of-bts-finally-acknowledge-that-theyve-made-it/11833359)). It made BTS the first K-pop act to top the chart, beating the previous Korean-act record of No. 2 set by Psy's "Gangnam Style" in 2012 ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/entertainment/the-members-of-bts-finally-acknowledge-that-theyve-made-it/11833359)).

### Why did BTS pause in 2022?

The group paused so all seven members could complete South Korea's mandatory military service of at least 18 months for able-bodied men ([NPR](https://www.mtpr.org/2022-10-17/bts-announces-upcoming-korean-military-service)). Big Hit announced the plan in October 2022, the members were discharged between June 2024 and June 2025, and the group returned with ARIRANG in March 2026 ([Associated Press](https://gmg-wkmg-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/entertainment/2025/06/20/all-of-bts-has-returned-as-suga-is-discharged-from-an-alternative-form-of-military-service/); [EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/bts-boy-band)).

### Has BTS won a Grammy?

No. BTS earned five Grammy nominations between 2021 and 2023 — for "Dynamite", "Butter", "Yet To Come", "My Universe" with Coldplay, and Album of the Year as featured artists on Coldplay's Music of the Spheres — but has not won ([Recording Academy](https://www.grammy.com/artists/bts/287749/)). They were the first K-pop act to receive Grammy nominations ([Recording Academy](https://www.grammy.com/artists/bts/287749/)).
