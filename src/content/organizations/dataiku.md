## Overview

Dataiku is the enterprise AI platform that spent a decade making data science accessible to non-specialists before the generative-AI boom made that idea fashionable. Founded in Paris in 2013 and re-centered on New York, it sells an end-to-end platform for designing, deploying and managing AI and analytics applications, pitched explicitly at "democratizing AI" — letting analysts and business users build predictive models and draw insights from unstructured data without depending on a team of data scientists ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/data-infrastructure/dataiku-raises-400m-to-democratize-ai-in-the-enterprise); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/13/ai-and-analytics-platform-dataiku-raises-200m-at-a-reduced-valuation/)). It reached a $4.6 billion valuation in 2021, took a down round to $3.7 billion in 2022 without the layoffs that hit rivals, and in 2023 pivoted into the generative era with the LLM Mesh — a governance backbone for enterprise large-language-model applications ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/13/ai-and-analytics-platform-dataiku-raises-200m-at-a-reduced-valuation/); [DBTA](https://www.dbta.com/Editorial/News-Flashes/Dataiku-Launches-LLM-Mesh-in-Collaboration-with-Snowflake-Pinecone-and-AI21-Labs-160826.aspx)).

## A Paris founding, documented in the registry

Unlike most startup origin stories, Dataiku's founding is a matter of public record. The French business registry's creation announcement of 24 February 2013 (RCS Paris 791 012 081) lists the company at 39 rue Sedaine, Paris, with a four-person founding administration: Florian Douetteau as president, Marc Batty and Thomas Cabrol as deputy chief executives, and Clément Stenac as a member of the direction committee ([Pappers/Bodacc](https://www.pappers.fr/entreprise/dataiku-791012081)). Douetteau is the co-founder and CEO who stayed with the company; Stenac became its chief technology officer ([Global Corporate Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2020/08/24/dataiku-documents-100m-series-d-round/); [DBTA](https://www.dbta.com/Editorial/News-Flashes/Dataiku-Launches-LLM-Mesh-in-Collaboration-with-Snowflake-Pinecone-and-AI21-Labs-160826.aspx)).

The transatlantic arc is also in the registry. Dataiku established its US presence in New York City in 2015; that March, the four founders left the French entity's direction committee, and in December 2016 the record installs DATAIKU INC., a Delaware company, as president of the French company — the formal re-centering on the United States ([Pappers/Bodacc](https://www.pappers.fr/entreprise/dataiku-791012081)). Batty and Cabrol later left the French entity's administration in 2020 ([Pappers/Bodacc](https://www.pappers.fr/entreprise/dataiku-791012081)). The early product philosophy was democratization by design: a visual interface over data preparation, modeling and deployment, with connectors to sources from Amazon S3 to [Snowflake](/organizations/snowflake/), so that business users could participate in work previously reserved for data scientists ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/data-infrastructure/dataiku-raises-400m-to-democratize-ai-in-the-enterprise)).

## The funding arc: unicorn, peak, down round

Dataiku's financing traces the enterprise-AI cycle. After early rounds — $28 million Series B led by Battery Ventures in 2017, $101 million Series C led by ICONIQ Capital in late 2018 — Alphabet's growth fund CapitalG bought shares from Serena Capital in a December 2019 secondary transaction at a $1.4 billion valuation, making Dataiku a unicorn ([Global Corporate Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2020/08/24/dataiku-documents-100m-series-d-round/)). A $100 million Series D led by Stripes followed in August 2020, with Tiger Global, Battery, Dawn, FirstMark, Iconiq and CapitalG participating, the valuation remaining above $1 billion ([Global Corporate Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2020/08/24/dataiku-documents-100m-series-d-round/)).

The peak came in August 2021: $400 million in Series E led by Tiger Global at a $4.6 billion valuation, with [Google](/organizations/google/)'s CapitalG, Snowflake Ventures, Insight Partners, Iconiq Growth, Battery Ventures and FirstMark Capital participating, and enterprise clients including Unilever, Westpac, OVH, NXP, Merck and Ubisoft ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/data-infrastructure/dataiku-raises-400m-to-democratize-ai-in-the-enterprise)). Then the macro turn: in December 2022 Dataiku raised $200 million in Series F led by Wellington Management at a $3.7 billion valuation, down from $4.6 billion — an SEC filing showed it could close up to $275 million, bringing total raised to roughly $600 million ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/13/ai-and-analytics-platform-dataiku-raises-200m-at-a-reduced-valuation/)). Notably, Dataiku avoided the major layoffs that hit competitors such as DataRobot, revealed $150 million in ARR for the first time, and hired CFO Adam Towns — a hire that fueled IPO speculation, though the Series F suggested a listing was "a ways off" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/13/ai-and-analytics-platform-dataiku-raises-200m-at-a-reduced-valuation/)).

## The LLM Mesh pivot

Dataiku's second act began at its Everyday AI Conference in New York in September 2023, when it unveiled the LLM Mesh: a "common backbone" sitting between large-language-model providers and end-user applications, offering universal AI service routing, secure access and auditing, private-data screening and response moderation, and performance and cost tracking — designed to let enterprises deploy generative AI with governance rather than ad hoc experiments ([DBTA](https://www.dbta.com/Editorial/News-Flashes/Dataiku-Launches-LLM-Mesh-in-Collaboration-with-Snowflake-Pinecone-and-AI21-Labs-160826.aspx)). Snowflake, Pinecone and AI21 Labs joined as launch partners, contributing containerized data and compute, vector databases and LLM builders; features entered public and private preview that October ([DBTA](https://www.dbta.com/Editorial/News-Flashes/Dataiku-Launches-LLM-Mesh-in-Collaboration-with-Snowflake-Pinecone-and-AI21-Labs-160826.aspx)). CTO and co-founder Clément Stenac framed it as bridging "the promise and reality of using Generative AI in the enterprise" ([DBTA](https://www.dbta.com/Editorial/News-Flashes/Dataiku-Launches-LLM-Mesh-in-Collaboration-with-Snowflake-Pinecone-and-AI21-Labs-160826.aspx)). By the end of 2022 the customer base stood at over 500 companies, including more than 150 of the world's largest enterprises, using the platform for predictive maintenance, supply-chain optimization, quality control and marketing optimization, per co-founder and CEO Douetteau ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/13/ai-and-analytics-platform-dataiku-raises-200m-at-a-reduced-valuation/)).
