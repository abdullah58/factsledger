## Overview

Roblox is an online platform where users play and build millions of user-created games, called experiences, and spend its virtual currency, Robux. It was founded in 2004 by David Baszucki and Erik Cassel, launched publicly in 2006, and is run by Roblox Corporation, a San Mateo company listed on the [New York Stock Exchange](/organizations/new-york-stock-exchange/) since 2021 ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/public/roblox-shares-surge-on-first-day-of-trading-as-another-gaming-company-joins-the-public-markets/)). The platform is unusual in that its players are also its developers: the games are built by users in Roblox Studio, and the company supplies the tools, the hosting, and the economy rather than the content itself ([Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/roblox/when-was-roblox-made-complete-history/)).

Scale is the story of the last few years. In the third quarter of 2025 Roblox averaged 151.5 million daily active users, up 70% year over year, with 39.6 billion hours engaged, revenue of $1.36 billion, and bookings of $1.92 billion — alongside a net loss of $257 million for the quarter ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2025/robloxs-150-million-daily-users-still-havent-translated-into-profitability/)). Roblox is headquartered in San Mateo, California, in the [United States](/places/united-states/), and is one of [Brex](/organizations/brex/)'s larger customers, named alongside [DoorDash](/organizations/doordash/), Flexport, Compass and Shein ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/12/fintech-brex-abandons-co-ceo-model-talks-ipo-cash-burn-and-plans-for-a-secondary-sale/)).

## From DynaBlocks to the New York Stock Exchange

Baszucki and Cassel began building the platform in 2004, trying the names GoBlocks and DynaBlocks before settling on Roblox, and launched it publicly on September 1, 2006 ([Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/roblox/when-was-roblox-made-complete-history/)). Growth was slow for years: the platform reached iOS in 2012 and Android in 2014, and only then did a generation of young players, streamers, and creators turn it into a mass market ([Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/roblox/when-was-roblox-made-complete-history/)). By the time of its stock-market filing, a quarter of its users were under 9 and another 29% were aged 9 to 12, with 31.1 million daily active users ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/public/roblox-shares-surge-on-first-day-of-trading-as-another-gaming-company-joins-the-public-markets/)).

Money followed the audience. Roblox raised a $150 million Series G led by [Andreessen Horowitz](/organizations/andreessen-horowitz/) in February 2020, backed by investors including Greylock, Index Ventures, and [Tiger Global](/organizations/tiger-global/), with [First Round Capital](/organizations/first-round-capital/) among its largest stockholders ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/public/roblox-shares-surge-on-first-day-of-trading-as-another-gaming-company-joins-the-public-markets/)). It then went public on March 10, 2021, not with a traditional IPO but a direct listing on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker RBLX: the exchange set a $45 reference price, shares opened at $64.50, and closed the first day at $69.50 ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/public/roblox-shares-surge-on-first-day-of-trading-as-another-gaming-company-joins-the-public-markets/)).

## The Robux economy: two creator economies

Roblox's business is a marketplace for other people's creativity, and the money runs through Robux. Creators generally keep 70% of what they sell in Robux inside their own experience, and 30% of what they sell in the Marketplace, and can turn earned Robux into real money through the Developer Exchange, or DevEx ([Syntaqx](https://www.syntaqx.com/books/gaming/creator-economies)). The exchange rate is set by Roblox, not a market: since September 5, 2025 it has paid $0.0038 per Earned Robux, with a minimum cash-out of 30,000 Earned Robux, worth $114 ([Syntaqx](https://www.syntaqx.com/books/gaming/creator-economies)). Developer exchange fees were over 30% of bookings in the third quarter of 2025, up from the mid-20s a year earlier ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2025/robloxs-150-million-daily-users-still-havent-translated-into-profitability/)).

The headline total and the typical creator's reality are two different economies, and that gap is the most useful number Roblox publishes. At its September 2026 developer conference the company said creators had earned about $1.7 billion through DevEx in the past twelve months, and more than $5 billion since 2013, as reported by PocketGamer.biz — but the median creator in the programme received only about $1,500 in the twelve months to June 30, 2026, across more than 42,000 participants, as reported by Dexerto ([Xarmo](https://xarmo.com/news/games/roblox-s-two-creator-economies-the-billion-dollar-payout-and-the-median-cheque/)). The top 1,000 creators averaged $1.4 million each over the same period, and the top ten $65.7 million each, on hits such as Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot ([Xarmo](https://xarmo.com/news/games/roblox-s-two-creator-economies-the-billion-dollar-payout-and-the-median-cheque/)). The platform's growth in 2025 was driven by exactly that kind of viral hit, and its fastest-growing audience was users aged 13 and older — an audience aging up rather than down ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2025/robloxs-150-million-daily-users-still-havent-translated-into-profitability/)).

## Beyond games — and the safety reckoning

Roblox has spent years pushing past games into concerts, brands, and advertising. The early proof was Lil Nas X in November 2020: four ten-minute sets inside Roblox drew 33 million views and debuted his single "Holiday", more than the 27 million-plus who watched Travis Scott's concert in Fortnite that April ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/lil-nas-xs-concert-inside-roblox-was-a-huge-success/1100-6484509/)). Since then the company has built time-based social events under its "Moments" initiative and an advertising business with Google as a partner, aiming at older teens and young adults as well as its original young audience ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2025/robloxs-150-million-daily-users-still-havent-translated-into-profitability/)).

That young audience is also the company's deepest problem. In 2023, 58% of its daily users were aged 16 or under, including 21% aged 9 or under ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/roblox-ceo-on-child-safety-concerns-if-you-re-not-comfortable-don-t-let-your-kids-be-on-roblox-)). In October 2024, Hindenburg Research accused Roblox of inflating user metrics and running deficient child-safety measures; Roblox rejected the claims, and the following month rolled out new parental controls and limits on under-13 chat ([NYU Stern Center for Business & Human Rights](https://bhr.stern.nyu.edu/quick-take/a-new-report-alleges-that-roblox-remains-an-x-rated-pedophile-hellscape-and-what-this-says-about-the-need-for-regulation/)). In March 2025, chief executive David Baszucki told the BBC that parents uneasy about safety should keep their children off the platform: "if you're not comfortable, don't let your kids be on Roblox" ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/roblox-ceo-on-child-safety-concerns-if-you-re-not-comfortable-don-t-let-your-kids-be-on-roblox-)). Then, on August 14, 2025, Louisiana attorney general Liz Murrill sued, alleging the platform had failed to implement effective safety measures and let predators "thrive, unite, hunt and victimize kids"; Roblox said the allegation that it would intentionally put users at risk was "categorically untrue", and pointed to an AI system that had produced 1,200 reports to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children in the first half of 2025 ([Associated Press](https://listenupyall.com/2025/08/15/louisiana-sues-roblox-alleging-the-popular-gaming-site-fails-to-protect-children/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**When was Roblox founded?**
Roblox was founded in 2004 by David Baszucki and Erik Cassel, who began development that year under the working names GoBlocks and DynaBlocks before settling on Roblox ([Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/roblox/when-was-roblox-made-complete-history/)). The platform itself launched publicly on September 1, 2006 ([Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/roblox/when-was-roblox-made-complete-history/)).

**How does Roblox make money?**
Players buy Robux, the platform's virtual currency, and spend it inside user-created experiences on passes, items, and products. Creators generally keep 70% of Robux sales in their own experience, Roblox keeps the rest, and creators can cash out earned Robux through the Developer Exchange at $0.0038 each ([Syntaqx](https://www.syntaqx.com/books/gaming/creator-economies)).

**How big is Roblox?**
In the third quarter of 2025 it averaged 151.5 million daily active users, up 70% year over year, with 39.6 billion hours engaged, revenue of $1.36 billion, and bookings of $1.92 billion — but still a net loss of $257 million ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2025/robloxs-150-million-daily-users-still-havent-translated-into-profitability/)).

**What do creators typically earn on Roblox?**
Very little, on average. The median creator in the Developer Exchange programme received about $1,500 in the twelve months to June 30, 2026, while the top 1,000 creators averaged $1.4 million each — so the headline billions mostly describe a small number of hits ([Xarmo](https://xarmo.com/news/games/roblox-s-two-creator-economies-the-billion-dollar-payout-and-the-median-cheque/)).

**Why is Roblox being sued over child safety?**
Louisiana's attorney general sued on August 14, 2025, alleging Roblox failed to implement effective safety measures and let predators target children. Roblox disputes the allegations and says it has rigorous safeguards; the case is a civil enforcement action, not a criminal one ([Associated Press](https://listenupyall.com/2025/08/15/louisiana-sues-roblox-alleging-the-popular-gaming-site-fails-to-protect-children/)).
