## Overview

Cardless is a [San Francisco](/places/san-francisco/) financial technology company that lets brands launch their own co-branded credit cards, handling underwriting, issuance and customer service for them ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/07/14/cardless-raises-40m/)). Founded in 2019 by Stanford engineering graduates Michael Spelfogel and Scott Kazmierowicz, it sells speed: brands can launch a card in weeks, where traditional bank issuers take months or more than a year ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201118005639/en/Cardless-Launches-Co-Branded-Credit-Card-Platform); [Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/)). Its clients include [Coinbase](/organizations/coinbase/), Qatar Airways and [Alibaba](/organizations/alibaba/), and it services the new Bilt Card 2.0 ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/)).

Cards are issued by First Electronic Bank of Salt Lake City, with Cardless handling underwriting and compliance ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/)).

## Why brands were locked out of credit cards

Co-branded cards look simple — a brand's name, rewards for its fans — but they were a closed shop. Of roughly 200 co-branded cards in the [United States](/places/united-states/), only one came from a company less than two decades old, according to Spelfogel, and most belong to legacy businesses such as Costco and Sam's Club ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/07/14/cardless-raises-40m/)). Eleven banks operate "the vast majority" of co-branded cards in a $77 billion industry, Spelfogel says, while consumer credit cards yielded an estimated $150 billion in revenue for traditional banks in 2019 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/26/cardless-swipes-up-30m-to-build-a-new-generation-of-co-branded-credit-cards/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/07/14/cardless-raises-40m/)). Spelfogel saw the problem first-hand at Lyft, where an attempt to build a card with Synchrony "ultimately never launched" ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201118005639/en/Cardless-Launches-Co-Branded-Credit-Card-Platform)). Cardless's answer is a "collective bargaining approach": spreading risk from a single lender across a network of capital sources and bank issuers, so it can offer brands a better product at lower cost ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201118005639/en/Cardless-Launches-Co-Branded-Credit-Card-Platform)). The company says it was the first new mass-market credit card firm in more than 25 years, after Capital One — its own claim, not an independent finding ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201118005639/en/Cardless-Launches-Co-Branded-Credit-Card-Platform)).

## From sports teams to Coinbase and Bilt

Cardless started with fans: its first programs, launched in 2021, were digital cards for the Cleveland Cavaliers, Manchester United and the Miami Marlins, offering rewards such as player autographs and money-can't-buy experiences ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/07/14/cardless-raises-40m/)). It then moved upmarket: by 2024 its clients included Qatar Airways and Alibaba — the latter aiming card services at small American businesses — and it offers Visa, [Mastercard](/organizations/mastercard/) and American Express options ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/26/cardless-swipes-up-30m-to-build-a-new-generation-of-co-branded-credit-cards/)). It also launched the American Express Coinbase card ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250710461384/en/Bilt-Raises-$250-Million-at-$10.75-Billion-Valuation-to-Accelerate-Housing-and-Neighborhood-Commerce-Platform)). Its biggest win came in 2025, when it landed [Bilt Rewards](/organizations/bilt/) as a customer: Bilt Card 2.0 — three cards with no-fee, $95 and $495 annual fees — is developed with Cardless and launched in February 2026, replacing [Wells Fargo](/organizations/wells-fargo/) as Bilt's card partner ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250710461384/en/Bilt-Raises-$250-Million-at-$10.75-Billion-Valuation-to-Accelerate-Housing-and-Neighborhood-Commerce-Platform)). Bilt founder and CEO [Ankur Jain](/people/ankur-jain/) pointed to Cardless's "technological capabilities and focus on customer experience" as differentiators ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/)). Cardless's platform offers application programming interfaces and prebuilt components, letting brands put the card inside their own app and ecosystem instead of sending customers to a bank's app ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/)).

## The money: funding, revenue and the road to profit

Cardless has raised $170 million in venture funding, most recently $60 million in September 2025 led by Spark Capital, with Activant, Industry Ventures and Pear VC ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/)). It also holds a $75 million three-year warehouse credit facility from i80 Group, signed in May 2023 for customer receivables ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/paytech/san-francisco-fintech-cardless-secures-75m-credit-facility-from-i80-group)). The company was valued at just over $350 million at its 2021 Series B, with no valuations disclosed for later rounds ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/26/cardless-swipes-up-30m-to-build-a-new-generation-of-co-branded-credit-cards/)). Spelfogel said revenues grew fivefold in the twelve months to November 2024, and annualised revenue was about $15 million as of the second quarter of 2025, projected to pass $150 million by the second quarter of 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/26/cardless-swipes-up-30m-to-build-a-new-generation-of-co-branded-credit-cards/); [Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/)). Cardless says a brand can launch a card in 90 days, against up to a year with a bank; that speed won it Bilt, the highest-profile card switch in co-branding ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/)). It had about 50 employees in September 2025, aimed to roughly double headcount within a year and was targeting profitability by the end of 2026 ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Cardless?** Cardless is a San Francisco fintech that lets brands launch and run their own co-branded credit cards, handling program creation, underwriting, lending, issuance and customer service ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/07/14/cardless-raises-40m/)). Its platform offers APIs and prebuilt components so brands can run a card programme inside their own app ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/)).

**Who founded Cardless?** Michael Spelfogel and Scott Kazmierowicz, Stanford engineering graduates, founded Cardless in San Francisco in 2019 ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201118005639/en/Cardless-Launches-Co-Branded-Credit-Card-Platform)). Spelfogel, the president, previously worked at the payments company Visa and at Lyft; Kazmierowicz, the CEO, previously worked in banking at Allen & Company and Goldman Sachs ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201118005639/en/Cardless-Launches-Co-Branded-Credit-Card-Platform)).

**Which brands use Cardless?** Early clients included the Cleveland Cavaliers, Manchester United and the Miami Marlins; later ones include Qatar Airways, Alibaba and Coinbase ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/07/14/cardless-raises-40m/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/26/cardless-swipes-up-30m-to-build-a-new-generation-of-co-branded-credit-cards/)). It also services Bilt Card 2.0, the card programme developed with Bilt Rewards, which launched in February 2026 ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/)).

**How much funding has Cardless raised?** It has raised $170 million in venture funding, most recently $60 million in September 2025 led by Spark Capital ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/cardless-raises-60m-credit-card-fintech-bilt-spark-spelfogel/760741/)). It also holds a $75 million three-year warehouse credit facility from i80 Group, signed in May 2023 to fund customer receivables ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/paytech/san-francisco-fintech-cardless-secures-75m-credit-facility-from-i80-group)).
