## Overview

Smash Capital is a growth equity firm based in [New York](/places/new-york-city/) and Los Angeles that invests in later-stage technology companies across consumer internet, enterprise AI, financial services and healthcare ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). It was founded in 2021 by former Walt Disney Company executives Kevin Mayer, Eric Garland and Evan Richter, together with longtime technology investor Brad Twohig, and it backs founders building what it calls generational brands ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). Its portfolio includes ElevenLabs, TradingView, Plaid, [Bilt Rewards](/organizations/bilt/), Fever, Redo and Epic Games ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)).

This profile is about that firm — the Smash Capital of smashcap.com, based in the [United States](/places/united-states/) — and it resolves one naming trap up front: the firm's own fund vehicles use both names. The general partner of its first fund is Smash Ventures GP I LLC, and an earlier vehicle was called Smash Ventures SPV LLC, so "Smash Ventures" in fund filings refers to this same firm family, not a rival ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1896761/0001567619-22-000241.txt); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1756507/000156761918004453/0001567619-18-004453.txt)). What remains genuinely unclear is the founding date: the firm says 2021, yet the 2018 filing shows the same principals operating under the Smash Ventures name three years earlier, and this profile states both facts rather than choosing between them.

## From Smash Ventures to Smash Capital

The paper trail starts in October 2018, when Smash Ventures SPV LLC filed a Form D with the SEC for a $500 million offering out of West Hollywood, naming Richter and Garland as managing members and Smash Ventures Management Company LP as investment manager ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1756507/000156761918004453/0001567619-18-004453.txt)). The firm's own account dates its founding to 2021, the year its first flagship fund began selling: Smash Capital Fund I L.P. recorded its first sale on 22 December 2021 and, by its January 2022 filing, had sold $500 million to 154 investors, with a $10 million minimum commitment that the general partner could waive ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1896761/0001567619-22-000241.txt); [CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). The fund's general partner was Smash Ventures GP I LLC and its manager Smash Capital Advisors LP, both at the same Rangely Avenue address as the 2018 vehicle ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1896761/0001567619-22-000241.txt)). Whether 2021 was a fresh start or a rebrand is not settled by any source read here.

## The model: operators who invest in brands

Smash Capital's pitch is that its partners are operators first and investors second, and that brand and media skill is its edge over rival growth funds ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). Mayer previously led Disney's direct-to-consumer and international operations, helped launch Disney+, ESPN+ and Hulu, worked on the Pixar, Marvel, Lucasfilm, 21st Century Fox and BAMTech acquisitions, and later served as chief executive of TikTok and chief operating officer of ByteDance ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). Tom Staggs held senior Disney roles including chief operating officer, chief financial officer and chairman of Disney Parks and Resorts Worldwide, while Garland and Richter led venture and growth investments for Disney and Twohig and Paul Szurek came from Insight Partners and Lightspeed ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). The firm says the team previously invested more than $4 billion in venture capital and took part in more than $100 billion of control transactions — its own claim, not independently verified ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)).

## The AI turn: 2024–2026

Smash Capital's early flagship bets were consumer and fintech names — Epic Games, Fever, Bilt Rewards — and its first big led round in the record here was Wagestream's $175 million Series C in April 2022, a $60 million equity tranche led with BlackRock plus $115 million of debt from Silicon Valley Bank ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/); [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2022/04/13/bringing-financial-wellbeing-to-frontline-workers-streams-in-175-million-for-wagestream)). From 2024 the deal flow tilted sharply toward artificial intelligence: it joined ElevenLabs' $80 million Series B in January, led Read AI's $50 million Series B at a $450 million valuation in October, with Twohig joining that board, and led Ovoko's €20 million Series B — its first investment in Lithuania and that country's largest startup round of the year ([Wilson Sonsini](https://www.wsgr.com/en/insights/wilson-sonsini-advises-elevenlabs-on-dollar80-million-series-b-financing.html); [TechFundingNews](https://techfundingnews.com/read-ai-lands-50m-to-integrate-its-copilot-with-slack-zoom-teams-and-gmail/); [ArcticStartup](https://arcticstartup.com/ovoko-raises-e20m-series-b/?noamp=mobile)). In 2026 it led Firecrawl's $75 million Series B and co-led CodeRabbit's $143 million round at a $1.5 billion valuation, less than a year after CodeRabbit's $60 million Series B ([PR.com](https://pr.ashlandtownnews.com/article/Firecrawl-Raises-dollar75M-Series-B-Launches-Alexandria/6ab2872bac03d400027e28e1); [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/ai-code-review-platform-coderabbit-valued-15-billion-latest-funding-round-2026-08-12/)). That CodeRabbit valuation — $1.5 billion on a $143 million round co-led by Smash Capital ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/ai-code-review-platform-coderabbit-valued-15-billion-latest-funding-round-2026-08-12/)) — implies the firm's AI bets are now its largest and fastest-appreciating positions, a sharp shift from the consumer-led portfolio it started with.

In June 2026 the firm added a different kind of senior resource, naming David Urban as operating partner in a newly created role ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). Urban, a veteran of politics, law, technology and media with more than 40 years of experience, was brought in to advise portfolio companies on strategic positioning, business development and market expansion, including in defense technology, media rights and consumer technology ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). He remains a managing director at BGR Group, is of counsel at Torridon Law PLLC and a senior political contributor at CNN, and sits on boards including Virtu Financial, EOS Energy Enterprises and SAIC ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). Kevin Mayer, the firm's founding partner and executive chairman, said Urban's network, judgment and cross-sector experience would be invaluable to the team and its portfolio companies ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**When was Smash Capital founded?** The firm says 2021, when Kevin Mayer, Eric Garland, Evan Richter and Brad Twohig launched it ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). But an SEC filing shows a Smash Ventures vehicle with the same principals operating from 2018, so the 2021 date may mark a refounding or rebrand rather than a cold start ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1756507/000156761918004453/0001567619-18-004453.txt)).

**Who founded Smash Capital?** Former Disney executives Kevin Mayer, Eric Garland and Evan Richter, plus longtime technology investor Brad Twohig ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). Partners Paul Szurek, Tom Staggs and Jacqueline Murphy are also senior leaders, and David Urban joined as operating partner in June 2026 ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)).

**What does Smash Capital invest in?** Later-stage technology companies across consumer internet, enterprise AI, financial services and healthcare, with an emphasis on founders building generational brands ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). It aims to help founders scale in markets shaped by technology adoption and regulatory complexity ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)). Its named portfolio includes ElevenLabs, TradingView, Plaid, Bilt Rewards, Fever, Redo and Epic Games ([CityBiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/859151/david-urban-joins-smash-capital-as-operating-partner/)).

**Is Smash Capital the same as Smash Ventures?** In fund filings, yes — the names belong to one firm family. Smash Ventures GP I LLC is the general partner of Smash Capital Fund I, and Smash Ventures SPV LLC was an earlier vehicle of the same principals ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1896761/0001567619-22-000241.txt); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1756507/000156761918004453/0001567619-18-004453.txt)). The firm itself trades as Smash Capital.

**How big is Smash Capital's first fund?** Smash Capital Fund I reported $500 million sold to 154 investors in its January 2022 SEC filing, with the first sale on 22 December 2021 ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1896761/0001567619-22-000241.txt)). No fully-read source gives a current assets-under-management figure, so this profile states the fund size only.
