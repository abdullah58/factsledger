## Overview

CircleUp Credit Advisors was the lending arm of [San Francisco](/places/san-francisco/) fintech [CircleUp](/organizations/circleup/), issuing asset-backed working-capital loans of $25,000 to $600,000 to emerging consumer packaged goods brands. Launched in July 2017 and funded through a $20 million facility from [Community Investment Management](/organizations/community-investment-management/), it had originated more than $300 million in loans by 2019 — and the company later claimed more than $1 billion in credit funding — before its loan book passed to [Brightflow AI](/organizations/brightflow-ai/) in 2023 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/); [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/circleup-200-million-funding/)).

CircleUp Credit Advisors was not the same thing as its parent or as its sibling fund. The parent, CircleUp, began in 2012 as an equity crowdfunding marketplace founded by investors [Ryan Caldbeck](/people/ryan-caldbeck/) and [Rory Eakin](/people/rory-eakin/), and in the same year as the credit launch it also raised a $125 million equity fund, [CircleUp Growth Partners](/organizations/circleup-growth-partners/) ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-breakup-lending-fund-sold-vc-fund-in-transit-ai-play-fate-unknown/)). The credit arm's job was narrower: where the marketplace and the equity fund sold ownership stakes, the credit arm sold no equity at all — it lent against the working-capital assets a growing brand already had ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/circleup-200-million-funding/); [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2017/07/119974-circleup-rolls-new-credit-advisors-affiliate/)).

## The lending model: data-driven credit for brands banks ignored

The credit arm existed because of a gap in how consumer brands were financed. A small brand with a purchase order from a major retailer still had to pay its manufacturer before the retailer paid it, and banks were generally uninterested in lending to small businesses — a point Community Investment Management managing director Jacob Haar made when the partnership was announced ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/)). CircleUp's answer was to apply the same data it used for equity investing to lending: its proprietary Helio system analyzed a company's financial performance and its competitors' performance, along with brand strength, retail and online distribution, and the team behind it, to judge creditworthiness ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/)).

The loans were revolving lines of credit backed by working-capital assets — accounts receivable, purchase orders, and inventory — rather than by the founders' personal guarantees or by equity ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2017/07/119974-circleup-rolls-new-credit-advisors-affiliate/)). Borrowers used them to fund future purchase orders, build inventory, and bridge the gap while waiting on late payments from retailers ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/)). The target customer was a brand with roughly $2 million to $10 million in revenue and domestic distribution, ready to scale toward national distribution — big enough to have real orders to finance, too small and too early for a bank ([Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/circleup-pilots-20m-loan-program-for-cpgs/448234/)).

CircleUp argued its underwriting edge came from volume: more than 17,000 companies had already applied to its investment platform by 2017, giving Helio millions of data points to evaluate borrowers at a far lower cost than a traditional lender, with the savings passed on as lower rates ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/); [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2017/07/119974-circleup-rolls-new-credit-advisors-affiliate/)). By 2019 the company said Helio tracked more than 1.4 million companies across more than 200 sources, and by 2022 it put the figure at 2.5 million brands ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/circleup-200-million-funding/); [SalesTechStar](https://salestechstar.com/sales-marketing/circleup-launches-helio-brings-to-market-a-broader-earlier-and-smarter-dataset-to-unlock-insights-into-the-consumer-goods-ecosystem-and-keep-companies-from-flying-blind/)). The pitch to founders was straightforward: working capital without dilution. "Raising equity is very expensive," early borrower Carlos Ramirez, founder of Powerful Yogurt, told [TechCrunch](/organizations/techcrunch/) — his company had previously raised $2 million in equity through CircleUp, and borrowing let him fund working capital "which is what i wanted to do from day one."

## Scale and funding: from a $20 million pilot to a $200 million raise

The credit arm grew fast from a small pilot. It had already issued 50 loans to consumer brands before its public launch and expected to make about 200 over the following year ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/)). Community Investment Management's initial $20 million facility was designed to be recycled — as borrowers repaid, the same money funded new loans — and Haar expected it to support more than $100 million in cumulative originations, with the facility growing if the pilot performed ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/); [Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/circleup-pilots-20m-loan-program-for-cpgs/448234/)).

The September 2019 raise was the arm's high-water mark as a standalone story. CircleUp Credit Advisors announced it had raised more than $200 million to give emerging consumer brands access to non-dilutive working capital, with Michigan Retirement Systems and Pacific Life Insurance Company among the investors and a financing facility led by Community Investment Management ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/circleup-200-million-funding/)). By then it had originated more than $300 million in loans since 2017, with originations growing roughly threefold in the preceding year, and its managing director Asher Hochberg said borrowers were growing at roughly 200% a year ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/circleup-200-million-funding/)). The same announcement noted that nearly half the companies across CircleUp's equity and credit portfolio were female-founded or female-led teams ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/circleup-200-million-funding/)).

The regulatory record gives a more precise — and smaller — picture of the fund itself. CircleUp Credit Fund I, LP, a Delaware limited partnership formed in 2018 and based in San Francisco, [California](/places/california/), filed a Form D with the [US Securities and Exchange Commission](/organizations/us-securities-and-exchange-commission/) in March 2019 showing $46.85 million sold to eight investors, with its first sale on February 21, 2019; an amended filing a year later showed $57.05 million sold ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1765746/000090266419001398/0000902664-19-001398.txt); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1765746/000090266420001449/primary_doc.xml)). The fund's general partner was CircleUp Credit Fund Partners GP, LLC, its investment manager was CircleUp Credit Fund Advisors, LLC, and Hochberg was a managing director of the general partner ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1765746/000090266419001398/0000902664-19-001398.txt)). The gap between the fund's $46.85 million and the announced $200 million was never broken down in a source read for this profile, so the profile does not state how much of the $200 million was fund capital and how much was facility capital. In May 2022 the company said the credit arm had provided more than $1 billion in credit funding since its inception — a company-reported figure, not an independently audited one ([SalesTechStar](https://salestechstar.com/sales-marketing/circleup-launches-helio-brings-to-market-a-broader-earlier-and-smarter-dataset-to-unlock-insights-into-the-consumer-goods-ecosystem-and-keep-companies-from-flying-blind/)).

## Borrowers: who actually took the loans

Three borrowers illustrate what the credit arm was for, at three different stages of a brand's life. Powerful Yogurt was the proof of concept at launch: a high-protein yogurt maker that had already raised equity through CircleUp and then borrowed instead of diluting further ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/)). [Liquid I.V.](/organizations/liquid-iv/), the hydration brand that later became one of CircleUp's best-known portfolio names, used the working capital as it scaled; its founder and chief executive Brandin Cohen said taking on working capital "was a crucial next step for our business" and called CircleUp Credit Advisors "a fantastic partner in accelerating that growth" ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/circleup-200-million-funding/)). And Gravity Blanket used the credit offering while Greg Lopez was its chief financial officer; Lopez later called CircleUp "a great partner" even as he guessed the loan book had proved unprofitable for the lender ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-breakup-lending-fund-sold-vc-fund-in-transit-ai-play-fate-unknown/)).

That last observation points to the tension inside the model. Lending to not-yet-profitable brands at scale is a risk business, and the credit arm's own borrowers were, by design, companies traditional lenders would not touch.

## Community Investment Management: the funder behind the lender

Community Investment Management was more than a launch partner — it was the credit arm's funding backbone from start to finish. CIM, an investment manager focused on the economic development and social impact of backing small and medium-sized businesses, supplied the initial $20 million facility in 2017 and led the financing facility behind the 2019 raise ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/); [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/circleup-200-million-funding/)). For CIM, the partnership fit its mandate precisely: Haar said most banks were uninterested in small-business lending and many available lenders were not transparent, while CircleUp wanted to offer reasonable, responsible products to help small businesses grow ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/)).

## The 2023 breakup: from lender to Brightflow AI

The ending came in two steps in 2023. In March, CircleUp began divesting its financing vehicles, and its loan fund came under the control of Brightflow AI, a venture-backed platform offering financial tracking services for brands and lenders; Brightflow also absorbed several CircleUp team members as part of the transaction ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-breakup-lending-fund-sold-vc-fund-in-transit-ai-play-fate-unknown/)). Sources told NOSH the lending exit was driven by rising interest rates and a desire to lower the company's risk profile, and Lopez guessed that many of the brands it had lent to were not yet profitable, "creating a high-risk portfolio that in turn was not profitable for them" ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-breakup-lending-fund-sold-vc-fund-in-transit-ai-play-fate-unknown/)).

The two companies had already been working together: in March 2023 Brightflow's software and data began servicing CircleUp's loan portfolio, helping brands apply for business loans ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/acquisitions/2023/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup-to-bolster-financial-insights-tool/)). Then on June 1–2, 2023, Brightflow AI acquired CircleUp itself, combining Helio's data and machine-learning capabilities with Brightflow's financial intelligence toolkit; terms were not disclosed ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/acquisitions/2023/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup-to-bolster-financial-insights-tool/)). Brightflow had raised $19 million in September 2022 to grow its platform for small and medium-sized businesses ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/acquisitions/2023/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup-to-bolster-financial-insights-tool/)). CircleUp chairman and founder Ryan Caldbeck said the acquisition would let the combined company give customers "the data, insights, and tools they need to grow their businesses faster and more efficiently" ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/acquisitions/2023/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup-to-bolster-financial-insights-tool/)). At the same time, the Growth Partners venture fund was being sold to an unidentified multi-strategy asset manager ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-breakup-lending-fund-sold-vc-fund-in-transit-ai-play-fate-unknown/)).

## FAQ

**What was CircleUp Credit Advisors?**
It was the lending arm of San Francisco fintech CircleUp, launched in July 2017 to make working-capital loans to emerging consumer packaged goods brands, using the company's Helio data platform to underwrite borrowers banks typically ignored ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/)).

**How large were its loans?**
Loans ranged from $25,000 to $600,000, structured as revolving lines of credit backed by accounts receivable, purchase orders, and inventory, and used to fund purchase orders, build inventory, and bridge late retailer payments ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/); [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2017/07/119974-circleup-rolls-new-credit-advisors-affiliate/)).

**Who funded CircleUp Credit Advisors?**
Community Investment Management provided the initial $20 million facility in 2017 and led the financing facility behind the 2019 raise of more than $200 million, which also drew in investors including Michigan Retirement Systems and Pacific Life ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/07/26/circleup-credit-advisors/); [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/circleup-200-million-funding/)).

**What happened to it in 2023?**
In March 2023 the loan fund came under the control of Brightflow AI, which also absorbed several CircleUp team members, and in June 2023 Brightflow AI acquired CircleUp itself; sources attributed the lending exit to rising interest rates and the risk of a portfolio of not-yet-profitable borrowers ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-breakup-lending-fund-sold-vc-fund-in-transit-ai-play-fate-unknown/); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/acquisitions/2023/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup-to-bolster-financial-insights-tool/)).
