## Overview

London Stock Exchange Group plc (LSEG) is a British financial-markets infrastructure and data group headquartered in the United Kingdom. It owns the London Stock Exchange and the FTSE Russell index business, runs the LSEG Workspace data platform, holds a majority stake in the LCH clearing house, and since October 2024 has owned [Axoni](/organizations/axoni/)'s Veris post-trade network ([Reuters](https://wtbx.com/2026/10/22/lsegs-two-decades-of-major-deals-partnerships-and-strategic-pivots/); [U.S. SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf)). The group was formed in 2007, when the London Stock Exchange — with a history stretching back more than 300 years — merged with Borsa Italiana in a €1.6 billion deal ([Reuters](https://wtbx.com/2026/10/22/lsegs-two-decades-of-major-deals-partnerships-and-strategic-pivots/)). It employs over 26,000 people across 65 countries, more than half of them in Asia Pacific ([LSEG](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/2024/london-stock-exchange-group-plc-h1-2024-interim-results)).

That scale reflects a two-decade transformation: LSEG is no longer mainly a stock-exchange operator. Its 2024 income was spread across Data & Analytics (£4.01 billion), Capital Markets (£1.83 billion), Post Trade (£1.19 billion), FTSE Russell (£918 million) and Risk Intelligence (£531 million), with total income up 7.7% on the year ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-overall-revenue-up-following-strong-performance-in-capital-markets-division/)). The exchange itself remains prominent — the London Stock Exchange was Europe's number-one venue for raising equity capital in 2024, with £25 billion raised for 274 companies ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-overall-revenue-up-following-strong-performance-in-capital-markets-division/)).

## From stock exchange to data giant

LSEG's modern shape was built by acquisition. After the 2007 merger it bought a majority stake in the clearing house LCH Clearnet in 2013, then paid $2.7 billion in 2014 for the Frank Russell Company, whose Russell Indexes became half of FTSE Russell ([Reuters](https://wtbx.com/2026/10/22/lsegs-two-decades-of-major-deals-partnerships-and-strategic-pivots/)). A planned €29 billion all-share merger with Deutsche Börse, agreed in 2016, was blocked by the EU Competition Regulator in 2017 ([Reuters](https://wtbx.com/2026/10/22/lsegs-two-decades-of-major-deals-partnerships-and-strategic-pivots/)). The defining deal came in 2019, when LSEG agreed to buy Refinitiv — the financial-data business carved out of [Thomson Reuters](/organizations/thomson-reuters/) — for $27 billion ([Reuters](https://wtbx.com/2026/10/22/lsegs-two-decades-of-major-deals-partnerships-and-strategic-pivots/)).

The Refinitiv purchase completed on 29 January 2021, after the European Commission approved it only on condition that LSEG sell Borsa Italiana, including the MTS bond-trading platform, to Euronext for €4.3 billion ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-completes-27-billion-refinitiv-acquisition/)). Chief executive David Schwimmer called completion "an important milestone in LSEG's history" ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-completes-27-billion-refinitiv-acquisition/)). The deal nearly tripled the group's revenue and shifted its centre of gravity from trading volumes to recurring data subscriptions — a shift LSEG has pressed further since, including a strategic partnership with Microsoft, which bought a near-4% stake in 2022 ([Reuters](https://wtbx.com/2026/10/22/lsegs-two-decades-of-major-deals-partnerships-and-strategic-pivots/)).

## The post-trade build-out: Acadia, Quantile and Veris

While Refinitiv remade LSEG's data business, a quieter programme rebuilt its post-trade arm. In 2021 it agreed to acquire [Quantile](/organizations/quantile/) Technologies, a portfolio, margin and capital optimisation specialist, for up to $362 million, and in 2022 it bought multi-asset post-trade provider [Acadia](/organizations/acadia/) for about $700 million, having held a minority stake in the firm since 2018 ([Reuters](https://wtbx.com/2026/10/22/lsegs-two-decades-of-major-deals-partnerships-and-strategic-pivots/); [The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-acquires-axoni-post-trade-technology/)). Acadia supplies risk management, margining and collateral services for uncleared derivatives markets ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-acquires-axoni-post-trade-technology/)). The programme's next step was Axoni's Veris network, a post-trade lifecycle and reconciliation platform for equity swaps launched in 2020 ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-acquires-axoni-post-trade-technology/)).

LSEG agreed to buy Axoni's post-trade technology in September 2024, with completion expected in the fourth quarter ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-acquires-axoni-post-trade-technology/)). The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission later confirmed the purchase closed in October 2024, when LSEG amended its clearing-agency exemption application to reflect its acquisition of Veris ([U.S. SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf)). Veris lets counterparties share and compare data on equity-swap deals, positions, trades and cash flows, reducing reconciliation effort and preventing cash-flow breaks ([U.S. SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf)). The deal's financial terms were not disclosed ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-acquires-axoni-post-trade-technology/)).

## What LSEG looks like now

LSEG's five divisions each grew in 2024, but Capital Markets grew fastest at 18.2%, driven by Tradeweb's request-for-quote, portfolio trading and automated execution products, while Data & Analytics grew 2.0% to £4.01 billion and FTSE Russell rose 8.8% on subscription growth ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-overall-revenue-up-following-strong-performance-in-capital-markets-division/)). The balance matters: data and indices now sit alongside exchanges, clearing and post-trade as roughly equal pillars rather than side businesses. Chief executive David Schwimmer said in 2024 that the group had "delivered on its strategy" with top-line growth and improving profitability ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-overall-revenue-up-following-strong-performance-in-capital-markets-division/)). The deal-making has not stopped: in October 2026 LSEG agreed to buy financial-data firm Octus from Permira for about $3.54 billion, its biggest acquisition since Refinitiv ([Reuters](https://wtbx.com/2026/10/22/lsegs-two-decades-of-major-deals-partnerships-and-strategic-pivots/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What does LSEG actually own?**
LSEG owns the London Stock Exchange, the FTSE Russell index business, the LSEG Workspace data platform built from Refinitiv, a majority stake in clearing house LCH, and the Veris post-trade network acquired from Axoni in October 2024 ([Reuters](https://wtbx.com/2026/10/22/lsegs-two-decades-of-major-deals-partnerships-and-strategic-pivots/); [U.S. SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf)).

**When did LSEG buy Refinitiv?**
LSEG agreed to buy Refinitiv for $27 billion in August 2019 and completed the acquisition on 29 January 2021, after EU regulators approved it on condition that LSEG sell Borsa Italiana to Euronext for €4.3 billion. The deal nearly tripled group revenue and shifted its centre of gravity toward data subscriptions ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-completes-27-billion-refinitiv-acquisition/)).

**What is Veris and why did LSEG want it?**
Veris is a post-trade platform for equity swaps that lets counterparties share and compare deal, position, trade and cash-flow data, cutting reconciliation work and preventing cash-flow breaks ([U.S. SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/34-106200.pdf)). LSEG bought it from Axoni in October 2024, extending its post-trade business alongside Acadia and Quantile.

**Is LSEG still mainly a stock exchange?**
No. In 2024 its largest division was Data & Analytics at £4.01 billion, ahead of Capital Markets at £1.83 billion, and the London Stock Exchange itself now accounts for a minority of group income. The group describes itself as a financial-markets infrastructure and data provider ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-overall-revenue-up-following-strong-performance-in-capital-markets-division/)).
