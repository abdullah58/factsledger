## Overview

Forter is an e-commerce fraud prevention company founded in 2013 and led by co-founder and CEO Michael Reitblat ([Fintech Futures](https://prod.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/forter-raises-125m-in-series-e-funding-to-secure-1-3bn-valuation); [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/e-commerce-fraud-prevention-startup-forter-raises-300m/600822/)). Its product is an identity-intelligence network: rather than each merchant fighting fraud alone, Forter's platform pools transaction data across merchants, banks, and payment providers to make real-time trust decisions from sign-up through checkout ([Fintech Futures](https://prod.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/forter-raises-125m-in-series-e-funding-to-secure-1-3bn-valuation)).

## The six months that tripled the valuation

Forter's funding history compresses the entire 2020–2021 e-commerce boom into two rounds. In November 2020 the company raised $125 million in Series E funding at a valuation above $1.3 billion, led by Bessemer Venture Partners, Felix Capital, and Itai Tsiddon, with Sequoia Capital, NewView Capital, Scale Venture Partners, March Capital Partners, and Commerce Ventures participating ([Fintech Futures](https://prod.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/forter-raises-125m-in-series-e-funding-to-secure-1-3bn-valuation)). At the time, its network already covered more than $200 billion in annual online transactions protecting over 800 million shoppers, and Reitblat reported doubled revenue, a doubled team across eight global offices, and the company's first cash-flow-positive quarter ([Fintech Futures](https://prod.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/forter-raises-125m-in-series-e-funding-to-secure-1-3bn-valuation)).

Six months later, in May 2021, Forter raised $300 million in Series F funding at a $3 billion valuation — tripling its valuation in half a year ([Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/38121/e-commerce-fraud-prevention-firm-forter-scores-300m)). Tiger Global Management led the round, with Third Point Ventures, Adage Capital Management, and existing investors including Bessemer, Sequoia, March Capital, NewView Capital, [Salesforce](/organizations/salesforce/) Ventures, and Scale Venture Partners joining ([Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/38121/e-commerce-fraud-prevention-firm-forter-scores-300m)). By then the network processed over $250 billion in annual online commerce transactions, protecting more than a billion consumers from credit-card fraud, account takeover, and identity theft, with revenue up more than 100% in twelve months ([Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/38121/e-commerce-fraud-prevention-firm-forter-scores-300m); [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/e-commerce-fraud-prevention-startup-forter-raises-300m/600822/)).

Tiger Global partner John Curtius said the firm had spoken with dozens of customers of competing solutions and concluded Forter was "the leader in performance and scale" ([Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/38121/e-commerce-fraud-prevention-firm-forter-scores-300m)).

## The private unicorn that stayed private

Unlike many 2021-vintage unicorns, Forter never went public — and said at the time it intended to stay independent. Reitblat stated during the Series E that the company would "remain an independent company as we continue to grow our footprint across the commerce ecosystem" ([Fintech Futures](https://prod.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/forter-raises-125m-in-series-e-funding-to-secure-1-3bn-valuation)). Its customer roster spans fashion and retail giants — ASOS, Boohoo, Hugo Boss, Adobe at the Series E; Farfetch, ASOS, and Shein added by the Series F ([Fintech Futures](https://prod.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/forter-raises-125m-in-series-e-funding-to-secure-1-3bn-valuation); [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/e-commerce-fraud-prevention-startup-forter-raises-300m/600822/)).
