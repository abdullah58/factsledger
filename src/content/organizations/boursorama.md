## Overview

Boursorama is a French online financial-services company that runs boursorama.com, [France](/places/france/)'s leading stock-market information portal, and the online bank BoursoBank, called Boursorama Banque until October 2023 ([Décideurs Magazine](https://www.decideurs-magazine.com/finance/63873-les-banques-en-ligne-rebattent-les-cartes-du-modele-bancaire-traditionnel.html)). It is a wholly owned subsidiary of Société Générale, which completed its buyout in June 2015 by acquiring CaixaBank's remaining 20.5% stake for €218 million ([Retail Banker International](https://www.retailbankerinternational.com/news/societe-generale-buys-caixabanks-remaining-stake-in-boursorama-190615-4604822/)). By the end of 2025 the bank counted 8.8 million clients — a tenfold increase in a decade, from a business that began as a stock-quotes website ([BoursoBank](https://www.boursobank-group.com/fr/espace-presse/communiques-de-presse/2025-une-nouvelle-annee-record-pour-boursobank)).

## Two startups become one group

Boursorama was born from two separate French internet ventures. In 1995 Société Générale's brokerage arm created Fimatex, which let retail investors trade on the Matif, the Monep and the stock market in real time over Minitel, and in 1996 launched the Global Trading System, the first real-time information-and-trading software ([Boursorama Document de référence 2011](https://echanges.dila.gouv.fr/OPENDATA/AMF/MAN/2012/10/FCMAN124408_20121022.pdf)). Separately, Patrice Legrand and Stéphane Mathieu founded Finance Net in 1996 and launched the free financial-information site boursorama.com in 1998; by 1999 it was already France's leading online financial-information site ([Boursorama Document de référence 2011](https://echanges.dila.gouv.fr/OPENDATA/AMF/MAN/2012/10/FCMAN124408_20121022.pdf)). Fimatex listed on the Nouveau Marché in March 2000. In March 2002 the two strands combined: Fimatex and Finance Net merged, the group bought the online broker Selftrade that December, and in January 2003 the group took the name Boursorama ([Boursorama Document de référence 2011](https://echanges.dila.gouv.fr/OPENDATA/AMF/MAN/2012/10/FCMAN124408_20121022.pdf)). The banking turn followed: Boursorama Invest became Boursorama Banque in December 2005, and the group absorbed CaixaBank France in 2006 ([Boursorama Document de référence 2011](https://echanges.dila.gouv.fr/OPENDATA/AMF/MAN/2012/10/FCMAN124408_20121022.pdf)).

## The portal: France's most-read financial news site

From the start, boursorama.com's model was aggregation: it gathered economic, financial and wealth-management news from many providers for everyone from active traders to long-term investors, and by December 2011 it drew 10.6 million unique visitors a month ([Boursorama Document de référence 2011](https://echanges.dila.gouv.fr/OPENDATA/AMF/MAN/2012/10/FCMAN124408_20121022.pdf)). A year later it topped the OJD's certified ranking of French economic-and-financial news sites for a second month running, with 25 million visits and nearly 257 million page views in December 2012 and about 500,000 daily unique visitors ([Boursorama](https://www.boursorama-group.com/uploads/attachments/2013_01_10-cp-classement-ojdvdef.pdf)). The portal also carries outside publishers' content: on 2 November 2021 it published Le Revenu's analysis of the takeover offers then open on the Paris market, setting out [La Banque Postale](/organizations/la-banque-postale/)'s bid for [CNP Assurances](/organizations/cnp-assurances/) at €21.90 a share — a 36.2% premium valuing the insurer at about €15 billion — alongside offers for Prodware and S.T. Dupont ([Boursorama / Le Revenu](https://www.boursorama.com/bourse/actualites/opa-des-primes-attractives-pour-cnp-assurances-prodware-et-s-t-dupont-739164beaf965dd9e1850c864ef9dcd1)). That episode shows the portal's dual role: a market-data utility for investors and a channel through which French takeover news reaches retail investors.

## Société Générale takes full control — then BoursoBank

Société Générale was already the majority shareholder, holding 55.7% of Boursorama at the end of 2011, with La Caixa holding 20.5% ([Boursorama Document de référence 2011](https://echanges.dila.gouv.fr/OPENDATA/AMF/MAN/2012/10/FCMAN124408_20121022.pdf)). In June 2014 the bank launched a simplified takeover offer at €12 a share, delisted Boursorama from Euronext [Paris](/places/paris/), and ended the operation with 79.5% of the capital ([Retail Banker International](https://www.retailbankerinternational.com/news/societe-generale-buys-caixabanks-remaining-stake-in-boursorama-190615-4604822/)). A year later, in June 2015, it bought CaixaBank's remaining 20.5% stake for €218 million, becoming the sole shareholder with 100% of the capital and voting rights and ending a partnership that had begun in 2006 ([Retail Banker International](https://www.retailbankerinternational.com/news/societe-generale-buys-caixabanks-remaining-stake-in-boursorama-190615-4604822/)). The bank then used Boursorama as its digital growth engine: on 2 October 2023 Boursorama Banque was renamed BoursoBank, with a plan to grow from 5 million clients to 8 million by 2026 — a target reached ahead of schedule, with 8.8 million clients by the end of 2025 ([Presse-citron](https://www.presse-citron.net/pourquoi-boursorama-change-de-nom-pour-devenir-boursobank/); [Décideurs Magazine](https://www.decideurs-magazine.com/finance/63873-les-banques-en-ligne-rebattent-les-cartes-du-modele-bancaire-traditionnel.html)). The renaming left the boursorama.com portal and the Boursorama company name untouched; only the bank's consumer brand changed.

BoursoBank added 1.9 million clients in 2025 alone, reaching 8.8 million — a 22% annual rise reported by Décideurs Magazine — which means roughly one French person in eight now banks with a brand that began as a stock-quotes website ([Décideurs Magazine](https://www.decideurs-magazine.com/finance/63873-les-banques-en-ligne-rebattent-les-cartes-du-modele-bancaire-traditionnel.html); [BoursoBank](https://www.boursobank-group.com/fr/espace-presse/communiques-de-presse/2025-une-nouvelle-annee-record-pour-boursobank)).

## FAQ

### What is Boursorama?

Boursorama is a French online financial-services company with two activities: the boursorama.com portal, France's leading online stock-market information site, and the online bank BoursoBank (formerly Boursorama Banque). It is a wholly owned subsidiary of Société Générale and counted 8.8 million clients at the end of 2025.

### Is Boursorama the same as BoursoBank?

They are the same company under different brand names. On 2 October 2023 the banking arm, Boursorama Banque, was renamed BoursoBank, while the company stayed Boursorama SA and the boursorama.com information portal kept the Boursorama name. The rename changed the consumer brand, not the ownership or the offers.

### Who owns Boursorama?

Société Générale owns 100% of Boursorama. It became the sole shareholder in June 2015, when it bought CaixaBank's remaining 20.5% stake for €218 million at €12 a share, ending a partnership that had begun in 2006. Before that, Société Générale had held 55.7% since the 2011 shareholding structure.

### What did Boursorama report about the CNP Assurances takeover?

On 2 November 2021 Boursorama published Le Revenu's analysis of the open takeover offers, reporting that La Banque Postale would buy BPCE's 16.1% stake in CNP Assurances at €21.90 a share — a 36.2% premium valuing the insurer at about €15 billion — then offer minorities the same price, aiming for a squeeze-out. The offer completed on 3 June 2022.
