**Lionsgate** — formally Lionsgate Studios Corp. — is a Canadian-American film and television studio founded in 1997 by financier Frank Giustra, whose film labels operate from Santa Monica, California ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/lionsgate-partners-founder-new-tv-partnership-36200/); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/two-hollywood-studios-combine-112011500020_1.html)). It became a pure-play studio in May 2025 when it completed its separation from Starz ([Reuters](https://kwsn.com/2025/04/23/lions-gate-shareholders-approve-split-of-studio-and-starz-business-source-says/); [Lionsgate](https://files.quartr.com/reports/96d9b-2025-08-07-08-19-11.pdf?ref=U3RvY2tBbmFseXNpcw==)), and in 2026 its US release *[Michael](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/)* — the [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) biopic — crossed $1 billion worldwide to become the studio's highest-grossing film of all time ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/michael-makes-box-office-world-record-jaafar-jackson-starrer-becomes-first-ever-biopic-to-cross-1-billion-milestone/articleshow/132363062.cms?from=mdr)).

Lionsgate's business model is acquisition-led: rather than being built from a classic Hollywood lot, it grew by buying distributors and their libraries — Summit Entertainment for $412.5 million in 2012, and Entertainment One (eOne) for about $500 million from Hasbro in 2024 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/two-hollywood-studios-combine-112011500020_1.html); [Prolific North](https://www.prolificnorth.co.uk/news/lionsgate-acquires-daisybeck-studios-owner-from-hasbro/)). Its marquee franchises include *The Hunger Games*, *The Twilight Saga* (via Summit), *John Wick*, and *Saw* ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/two-hollywood-studios-combine-112011500020_1.html); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/business/lionsgate-earnings-q2-2026/)).

## From Vancouver startup to Hollywood mini-major

TheWrap's 2012 reporting on the studio's founder confirms Giustra founded Lionsgate in 1997 and "helped establish the company with a series of hits such as *American Psycho* and *Gods and Monsters*" before stepping down as chairman in 2000 ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/lionsgate-partners-founder-new-tv-partnership-36200/)). The early 2000s were defined by hostile attention as much as output: activist investor Carl Icahn accumulated more than 30% of the company and pushed for control until he liquidated his stake in 2011, after which MHR Fund Management — run by a former Icahn associate — became the largest shareholder ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/markets/hunger-games-not-lions-gates-only-blockbuster-11467699)). The January 2012 Summit deal was the turning point that followed: $412.5 million in cash and stock for the *Twilight* studio, giving Lionsgate the final installment of the vampire saga plus library rights to the first four films, which had generated $2.5 billion in worldwide ticket sales ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/two-hollywood-studios-combine-112011500020_1.html)). *The Hunger Games* arrived two months later and turned Lionsgate into a franchise studio: the 2012 original grossed $408 million domestically and its 2013 sequel, *Catching Fire*, reached $424.6 million ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/business/lionsgate-earnings-q2-2026/)). On television, the studio produced Emmy-winning series including *Mad Men* and *Nurse Jackie* ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/lionsgate-partners-founder-new-tv-partnership-36200/)).

## The Starz split and the eOne deal

In January 2024 Lionsgate completed its roughly $500 million acquisition of eOne from Hasbro — which had paid £3.3 billion for the same assets in 2019 — adding 6,500 titles including *1917*, *Atomic Blonde*, *Green Book*, *Grey's Anatomy*, *Criminal Minds* and *Designated Survivor*, plus film-development rights to Hasbro's *Monopoly* ([Prolific North](https://www.prolificnorth.co.uk/news/lionsgate-acquires-daisybeck-studios-owner-from-hasbro/)). CEO Jon Feltheimer called it a deal that "checks off all the boxes," strengthening scripted and unscripted television and expanding the studio's presence in Canada and the UK ([Prolific North](https://www.prolificnorth.co.uk/news/lionsgate-acquires-daisybeck-studios-owner-from-hasbro/)). The bigger structural change came in 2025: shareholders approved splitting the studio from Starz in April, and the separation was completed in May, leaving two separately traded companies — Lionsgate Studios Corp., holding the Motion Picture Group, the television studio and a library of more than 20,000 film and television titles, and Starz Entertainment Corp., holding the premium subscription platform ([Reuters](https://kwsn.com/2025/04/23/lions-gate-shareholders-approve-split-of-studio-and-starz-business-source-says/); [Lionsgate](https://files.quartr.com/reports/96d9b-2025-08-07-08-19-11.pdf?ref=U3RvY2tBbmFseXNpcw==)).

## The *Michael* year and the franchise slate

2026 was the year the acquisition-led strategy finally produced a billion-dollar film. *Michael* — distributed by Lionsgate in the US with Universal handling most international markets — grossed $371 million domestically and $1.001 billion worldwide, the first biopic ever to enter the billion-dollar club ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/michael-makes-box-office-world-record-jaafar-jackson-starrer-becomes-first-ever-biopic-to-cross-1-billion-milestone/articleshow/132363062.cms?from=mdr); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/business/lionsgate-earnings-q2-2026/)). The financial picture was mixed: Q1 2026 brought a $70.2 million profit on $906 million in revenue, helped by *The Housemaid*'s run to nearly $400 million worldwide — but Q2, despite 48% revenue growth to $776.6 million, still ended in a $28.8 million net loss, with net debt at $1.5 billion ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/lionsgate-q1-2026-earnings-2/); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/business/lionsgate-earnings-q2-2026/)). A *Michael* sequel is confirmed: on the August 2026 earnings call, film chair [Adam Fogelson](/people/adam-fogelson/) targeted production in late 2026 or early 2027 and release in late 2027 or the first half of 2028, reusing shot musical sequences ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/business/lionsgate-earnings-q2-2026/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who founded Lionsgate?**
Canadian financier Frank Giustra founded Lionsgate in 1997 in Vancouver. He stepped down as chairman in 2000 and later sold most of his stake, though he returned to the studio's board in 2010, per trade-press reporting ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/lionsgate-partners-founder-new-tv-partnership-36200/)).

**Is Lionsgate still part of Starz?**
No. In May 2025 Lionsgate completed the separation: Lionsgate Studios Corp. holds the Motion Picture Group, the television studio and a 20,000+ title library, while Starz Entertainment Corp. holds the premium subscription platform ([Reuters](https://kwsn.com/2025/04/23/lions-gate-shareholders-approve-split-of-studio-and-starz-business-source-says/)).

**What is Lionsgate's highest-grossing movie?**
*Michael* (2026), the Michael Jackson biopic, at $1.001 billion worldwide — Lionsgate's first billion-dollar film, ahead of *The Hunger Games: Catching Fire* ($865 million) and *Breaking Dawn – Part 2* ($850 million) ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/michael-makes-box-office-world-record-jaafar-jackson-starrer-becomes-first-ever-biopic-to-cross-1-billion-milestone/articleshow/132363062.cms?from=mdr)).

**Who runs Lionsgate?**
Jon Feltheimer is CEO and Michael Burns is vice-chairman; Adam Fogelson chairs the Motion Picture Group and leads its earnings calls with analysts ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/two-hollywood-studios-combine-112011500020_1.html); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/business/lionsgate-earnings-q2-2026/)).
