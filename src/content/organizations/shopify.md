## Overview

Shopify is a Canadian commerce-software company, based in Ottawa, that sells subscription tools letting independent merchants run their own online stores instead of selling through a marketplace ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-shopify-ipo/canadian-software-maker-shopify-valued-at-1-27-billion-at-ipo-price-idUSKBN0O52OG20150520/)). Founded in 2006 and led by founder-CEO Tobias "Tobi" Lütke, it went public in 2015 and grew into one of the largest e-commerce platforms in the world ([IT Voice](https://www.itvoice.in/shopify-announces-100-million-funding)). Its defining strategic episode, however, is not its rise but its reversal: after spending years and a major acquisition building a "port to porch" fulfillment network, Shopify sold most of its logistics business to Flexport in 2023, cut a fifth of its workforce, and returned to its software roots ([Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/shopify-sells-logistics-business-cuts-workforce-20-to-refocus-on-its-main-quest/131664/)).

## What the organization is known for

Shopify is known for democratizing e-commerce: giving small and mid-size merchants the software to design, build, and manage sales across the web, mobile apps, and brick-and-mortar stores, rather than depending on large retail platforms ([IT Voice](https://www.itvoice.in/shopify-announces-100-million-funding)). At its 2015 IPO, [Reuters](/organizations/reuters/) described it as software that "helps small and medium-sized retailers to set up online storefronts," charging monthly subscriptions of $29 to $179, with 162,261 merchants subscribed across about 150 countries ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-shopify-ipo/canadian-software-maker-shopify-valued-at-1-27-billion-at-ipo-price-idUSKBN0O52OG20150520/)). By 2023 the company said it worked with "millions of merchants" overall ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/26/shopify-expands-into-credit-as-it-pushes-further-into-the-fintech-space/)).

## Products and services

The platform's core is the subscription storefront, but Shopify has layered financial products on top, almost all of them built with payments partner Stripe. Shopify Payments lets merchants accept cards without a third-party gateway; Shopify Capital (launched 2016) advances merchants working capital; Shopify Balance is a money-management account; and Shopify Credit, launched in July 2023, is a pay-in-full business credit card powered by Stripe and issued by Celtic Bank, with eligibility based on sales performance rather than credit checks and KYC requirements ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/26/shopify-expands-into-credit-as-it-pushes-further-into-the-fintech-space/)). On the retail side, Shopify POS extends the platform into physical stores — the target of the company's 2013 funding round ([IT Voice](https://www.itvoice.in/shopify-announces-100-million-funding)). In 2023 the company also launched a modular headless-commerce offering, Commerce Components, and an AI shopping assistant in its Shop app ([Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/shopify-sells-logistics-business-cuts-workforce-20-to-refocus-on-its-main-quest/131664/)).

## Ownership, founders and leadership

Shopify is a public company, dual-listed on the New York Stock Exchange (SHOP) and the Toronto Stock Exchange (SH) since its May 2015 IPO ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-shopify-ipo/canadian-software-maker-shopify-valued-at-1-27-billion-at-ipo-price-idUSKBN0O52OG20150520/)). Tobias Lütke is identified as "Founder and CEO" in the company's December 2013 funding announcement ([IT Voice](https://www.itvoice.in/shopify-announces-100-million-funding)). At the IPO, the largest outside investors were Bessemer Venture Partners with a 30% stake and FirstMark Capital with nearly 12%; Morgan Stanley, Credit Suisse, and RBC were among the underwriters ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-shopify-ipo/canadian-software-maker-shopify-valued-at-1-27-billion-at-ipo-price-idUSKBN0O52OG20150520/)). Shopify's president is Harley Finkelstein, who fronted the 2022–2023 logistics announcements ([Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/shopify-sells-logistics-business-cuts-workforce-20-to-refocus-on-its-main-quest/131664/)).

## Funding and acquisitions

Shopify raised modest venture capital before going public: $7 million in Series A and $15 million in Series B across 2010–2011 ($22 million combined), then a $100 million Series C in December 2013 led by OMERS Ventures and Insight Venture Partners, with Bessemer, FirstMark, Georgian Partners, and Felicis participating ([IT Voice](https://www.itvoice.in/shopify-announces-100-million-funding)). The May 2015 IPO raised about $131 million at $17 per share — above the expected range — valuing the company at about $1.27 billion; shares jumped as much as 69% in the debut ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-shopify-ipo/canadian-software-maker-shopify-valued-at-1-27-billion-at-ipo-price-idUSKBN0O52OG20150520/)).

On acquisitions, the record this profile can verify is the logistics arc: Shopify acquired fulfillment provider Deliverr in May 2022, then in May 2023 agreed to sell most of its logistics business — including Deliverr — to Flexport in an all-stock deal, receiving stock representing a 13% equity interest in Flexport on top of its existing holding, with Flexport becoming Shopify's official logistics partner and the preferred provider for Shop Promise, its delivery-guarantee program ([Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/shopify-sells-logistics-business-cuts-workforce-20-to-refocus-on-its-main-quest/131664/)). Lütke framed the exit in a letter to employees as shedding a "side quest" to refocus on the company's "main quest" of making commerce simpler and more democratized ([Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/shopify-sells-logistics-business-cuts-workforce-20-to-refocus-on-its-main-quest/131664/)).

## Regulation, licenses and compliance record

The sources read for this profile contain no record of regulatory enforcement actions against Shopify. The one compliance-relevant detail verified: Shopify Credit is issued by Celtic Bank (Shopify is not the card issuer), and merchant eligibility requires KYC (know-your-customer) checks ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/26/shopify-expands-into-credit-as-it-pushes-further-into-the-fintech-space/)).

## Competitors and partners

Shopify's most durable partnership is with **Stripe**, which has powered its payments stack for roughly a decade (Shopify Payments, Balance, Credit) ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/26/shopify-expands-into-credit-as-it-pushes-further-into-the-fintech-space/)). **Flexport** — led by Dave Clark, who built [Amazon's](/organizations/amazon/) logistics and fulfillment network — is now Shopify's official logistics partner following the 2023 sale ([Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/shopify-sells-logistics-business-cuts-workforce-20-to-refocus-on-its-main-quest/131664/)). Shopify was also named Intuit's preferred POS partner as Intuit sunset its QuickBooks Desktop POS product ([Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/shopify-sells-logistics-business-cuts-workforce-20-to-refocus-on-its-main-quest/131664/)). As a competitive marker, the company's 2013 announcement cited third-party research showing Shopify stores drew more Cyber Monday traffic than Walmart, Best Buy, and Target ([IT Voice](https://www.itvoice.in/shopify-announces-100-million-funding)).
