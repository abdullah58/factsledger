## Overview

Systems Limited is Pakistan's largest listed technology company: a Lahore-based software-development and business-process-outsourcing firm, founded in 1977 as a private limited company and listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange under the ticker SYS in 2015, with clients across the US, UK, EU and the Middle East ([Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40397822)). This profile covers that Pakistani company — and not any of the unrelated firms elsewhere that share the "Systems" name. The company has been named to [Forbes](/organizations/forbes/) Asia's Best Under a Billion list six times through 2026 ([TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/systems-limited-named-among-forbes-asias-best-under-a-billion-2026-companies/)).

## Five decades of export-led growth

BR Research traces a steep growth curve: revenue rose 42% to Rs5.35 billion in 2019, 40% to Rs7.51 billion in 2020 as COVID-19 accelerated global digitalization, 58% to Rs11.9 billion in 2021, 73% to Rs20.6 billion in 2022, and 55% to Rs32 billion in 2023 — with profitability fluctuating on exchange-rate shifts and rapid headcount expansion ([Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40397822)). In 2024 revenue grew 20% to Rs38.5 billion, but gross profit slipped as rising salaries and hardware/software spending outpaced revenue and rupee appreciation squeezed local-currency earnings, pushing net profit down 29% to Rs6.1 billion; the workforce that year reached 6,701 employees ([Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40397822)). Growth reaccelerated in 2025: profit after tax for the first nine months reached Rs7.94 billion, up 46% year-on-year, on net sales of Rs57.4 billion, with gross margins recovering to 26.9% ([Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40397822)).

## The Confiz amalgamation and the AI bet

A defining strategic event was the acquisition of Confiz, structured as a 100% share swap with no cash outflow — expected to add roughly 10% to revenues and profits at higher revenue-per-employee, with about 4% dilution and low client overlap — alongside the integration of the BAT shared-services acquisition to strengthen the higher-value BPO vertical ([Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40397822)). The Confiz merger was completed after sanction by the Lahore High Court, expanding the group's talent base, intellectual property portfolio and delivery capacity; in June 2026 the board separately authorised management to explore another IT-services acquisition ([CW Pakistan](https://cwpakistan.com/systems-limited-board-authorises-exploration-of-it-services-business-acquisition/)).

Management's current growth story centers on AI-led enterprise spending, particularly in the Middle East. For the first half of 2026 the company reported net profit of Rs6.1 billion (up 17%) on revenue of Rs49.7 billion (up 35%), with the Middle East and Africa contributing 57% of revenue, North America 20% and Pakistan 13% ([Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40437897)). The company describes a structurally favourable currency position — 94% of revenue denominated in foreign currency against roughly 56% of costs in rupees — while cautioning that continued rupee appreciation could pressure its 25–26% margin target ([Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40437897)). The client base is scaling too: 196 customers generated more than $100,000 in revenue in the half, up from 181 a year earlier, and Pakistan remains the main delivery centre with 83% of headcount ([Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40437897)).

## Six-time Forbes recognition

Forbes Asia has recognized Systems Limited's consistency repeatedly. It first made the Best Under a Billion list in 2020 — the only Pakistani IT company on that year's list — with CEO Asif Peer expressing the ambition "that we would like to be not 'under a billion dollars', but a billion-dollar company out of Pakistan very soon" ([TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/systems-limited-wins-forbes-asias-best-under-a-billion-award-2020/)). The company appeared again in 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024, and in August 2026 was named to the 2026 edition for the sixth time, with Forbes citing $286 million in sales and a $759 million market value; the list covers 200 publicly traded Asia-Pacific companies selected on debt, sales and earnings-per-share growth and return on equity ([TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/systems-limited-named-among-forbes-asias-best-under-a-billion-2026-companies/); [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/three-pakistani-firms-make-it-to-forbes-list-of-asias-best-under-a-billion-2655326)).
