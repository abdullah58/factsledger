## Overview

The SIGNAL IDUNA Group is a German insurance and financial-services group created on 1 July 1999 through the merger of the Dortmund-based Signal Versicherungen and the Hamburg-based Iduna Nova Group ([Coliseum](https://www.coliseum-online.com/signal-iduna-borussia-dortmund-bonhomie-on/)). It is one of Germany's largest financial groups, operating in Germany, Poland, Hungary, Switzerland and Romania ([DoingBusiness.ro](https://doingbusiness.ro/post/signal-iduna-romania-to-announce-the-introduction-of-its-own-telemedicine-service-in-corporate-health-insurance-plans-5665)). In 2025 it wrote EUR 7.2 billion in gross premiums and held EUR 67 billion in total assets, ranking 12th in the German primary insurance market ([Fitch Ratings](https://www.sire.ch/fileadmin/web/documents/Si_Re_Press_Release_Fitch_affirms_SI_Re-s_Insurer_Financial_Strength_rating_of_A.pdf)).

What distinguishes the group structurally is ownership: it is headed by three mutual insurers — Versicherungsvereine auf Gegenseitigkeit — meaning it is ultimately owned by its policyholders rather than shareholders ([Fitch Ratings](https://www.sire.ch/fileadmin/web/documents/Si_Re_Press_Release_Fitch_affirms_SI_Re-s_Insurer_Financial_Strength_rating_of_A.pdf)). Fitch affirmed the group's core insurers at Insurer Financial Strength 'A' with a stable outlook in July 2026, citing strong capitalisation — a group Solvency II ratio of 164% at end-2025 — and a competitive position built on a large network of tied agents and deep links to Germany's crafts, commerce and public-service bodies ([Fitch Ratings](https://www.sire.ch/fileadmin/web/documents/Si_Re_Press_Release_Fitch_affirms_SI_Re-s_Insurer_Financial_Strength_rating_of_A.pdf)).

## The mutual structure behind the group

Fitch describes Signal Iduna's company profile as a "well-established, diversified business franchise" of moderate operating scale in the German market ([Fitch Ratings](https://www.sire.ch/fileadmin/web/documents/Si_Re_Press_Release_Fitch_affirms_SI_Re-s_Insurer_Financial_Strength_rating_of_A.pdf)). The three mutual head companies sit atop operating subsidiaries including the German non-life insurer SIGNAL IDUNA Allgemeine Versicherung AG and the Swiss reinsurer SIGNAL IDUNA Reinsurance Ltd., both of which Fitch views as core to the parent mutual life insurer SIGNAL IDUNA Lebensversicherung a.G. ([Fitch Ratings](https://www.sire.ch/fileadmin/web/documents/Si_Re_Press_Release_Fitch_affirms_SI_Re-s_Insurer_Financial_Strength_rating_of_A.pdf)).

The mutual form shapes the business: Fitch notes the group's competitive position benefits from its large network of tied agents and exclusive partners and from strong links to the official bodies of crafts and commerce and the public service — a legacy of predecessor companies founded by artisans and tradespeople in the early 20th century ([Fitch Ratings](https://www.sire.ch/fileadmin/web/documents/Si_Re_Press_Release_Fitch_affirms_SI_Re-s_Insurer_Financial_Strength_rating_of_A.pdf)). Profitability has been pressured — return on equity fell to 2.6% at end-2025 from 3.7% a year earlier — though non-life profitability improved in 2025 on double-digit premium increases and the cancellation of unprofitable business ([Fitch Ratings](https://www.sire.ch/fileadmin/web/documents/Si_Re_Press_Release_Fitch_affirms_SI_Re-s_Insurer_Financial_Strength_rating_of_A.pdf)).

## The stadium that made the name famous

Outside insurance circles, the group's name is best known from football. It has sponsored Borussia Dortmund since 1974, and in 2005 the club's Westfalenstadion — Germany's largest football stadium at 81,365 capacity — was renamed Signal Iduna Park ([Coliseum](https://www.coliseum-online.com/signal-iduna-borussia-dortmund-bonhomie-on/)). The partnership was extended in 2022 until 2031 in a deal reported at EUR 100 million over nine years, making it one of the longest continuous sponsorships in the Bundesliga ([Coliseum](https://www.coliseum-online.com/signal-iduna-borussia-dortmund-bonhomie-on/)). In UEFA competitions the stadium is still called BVB Stadion Dortmund, since UEFA does not permit sponsor names ([Coliseum](https://www.coliseum-online.com/signal-iduna-borussia-dortmund-bonhomie-on/)).
