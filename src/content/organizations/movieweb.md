## Overview

MovieWeb is a [United States](/places/united-states/)-based entertainment news site online since 1995, making it one of the longest-running film and TV outlets on the web ([Mergr](https://mergr.com/movieweb-overview); [Caribbean News Global](https://caribbeannewsglobal.com/acquisition-of-movieweb-com-and-tvweb-com-by-valnet-inc/)). Since 2021 it has been owned by **Valnet Inc.**, which acquired MovieWeb.com and TvWeb.com from **WatchR Media, Inc.**, joining Valnet's entertainment portfolio ([Caribbean News Global](https://caribbeannewsglobal.com/acquisition-of-movieweb-com-and-tvweb-com-by-valnet-inc/)). In 2024 the site interviewed **Adam Rehmeier** about his coming-of-age comedy *Snack Shack* ([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)).

## Founding and ownership

MovieWeb was founded in 1995 and is based in **Las Vegas, Nevada**, operating as a digital media company covering movies and television ([Mergr](https://mergr.com/movieweb-overview)). A January 2006 post on the site announced a full redesign with a new Television section, and its author's bio — truncated but explicit — identified the writer as a "Co-founder of Movieweb," confirming the site began as a founder-led independent publication ([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/welcome-to-the-new-moviewebcom/)). The site's own corporate history describes MovieWeb.com as one of the first online destinations for movie information news and media, built over two and a half decades ([Caribbean News Global](https://caribbeannewsglobal.com/acquisition-of-movieweb-com-and-tvweb-com-by-valnet-inc/)). In 2021, **Valnet Inc.** completed the acquisition of MovieWeb.com and TvWeb.com from **WatchR Media, Inc.**, whose chairman and president, **Brandon Calder**, described the sale as moving both sites into Valnet's portfolio alongside **Screen Rant**, **Collider** and **CBR** ([Caribbean News Global](https://caribbeannewsglobal.com/acquisition-of-movieweb-com-and-tvweb-com-by-valnet-inc/)). Valnet is a digital content investment company that owns and operates a diversified portfolio of publishing and studio assets ([Caribbean News Global](https://caribbeannewsglobal.com/acquisition-of-movieweb-com-and-tvweb-com-by-valnet-inc/)).

## Audience and editorial scope

At the time of the Valnet deal, the acquisition release stated that MovieWeb.com had served more than 8 million unique monthly visitors in July 2021 ([Caribbean News Global](https://caribbeannewsglobal.com/acquisition-of-movieweb-com-and-tvweb-com-by-valnet-inc/)). That figure matters because it positioned the site as a mass-market entertainment news destination rather than a niche outlet: the release described it as a "premiere online destination for movie and television show enthusiasts," providing news and information on casting and development, release dates, trailers, interviews and clips ([Caribbean News Global](https://caribbeannewsglobal.com/acquisition-of-movieweb-com-and-tvweb-com-by-valnet-inc/)). **WatchR Media** had launched TvWeb.com in 2013 as a companion TV brand, and both sites were acquired together, reflecting the company's TV-era expansion ([Caribbean News Global](https://caribbeannewsglobal.com/acquisition-of-movieweb-com-and-tvweb-com-by-valnet-inc/)). The editorial formula — daily industry news, trailers, lists and interviews — is the standard entertainment-publishing playbook, with interviews like the Rehmeier one forming part of its original reporting mix ([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)).

## The 2024 Rehmeier interview

On March 14, 2024, MovieWeb published an interview by contributing writer **Richard Fink** titled "Snack Shack Director on Real Teen Movies: 'Why Aren't We Demanding That?'" ([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)). Rehmeier discusses *Snack Shack* (2024), his autobiographical comedy about two young men who buy the snack shack at their local pool in the summer of 1991, released theatrically by **Republic Pictures** on March 15, 2024 ([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)). The interview's biographical framing establishes that Rehmeier worked as a camera operator and cinematographer before his debut, [*The Bunny Game*](/creative-works/the-bunny-game/) (2011), and that *Dinner in America* was nominated for a **Grand Jury Prize** at the **Sundance Film Festival** in 2020 and took the **Grand Prix** at the **Odessa International Film Festival**, winning 20+ directing and audience awards at festivals including **Tallinn**, **Neuchatel**, **Dublin International** and **Nightstream** ([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)). Rehmeier shot *Snack Shack* in **Nebraska City** because it draws on his own childhood, and the cast worked at an actual pool snack shack before filming ([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who owns MovieWeb?**
Valnet Inc. has owned MovieWeb.com and TvWeb.com since 2021, when it acquired them from WatchR Media, Inc. Valnet is a digital content investment company that owns and operates a diversified portfolio of publishing and studio assets, including Screen Rant, Collider and CBR ([Caribbean News Global](https://caribbeannewsglobal.com/acquisition-of-movieweb-com-and-tvweb-com-by-valnet-inc/)).

**How old is MovieWeb?**
MovieWeb has been online since 1995, per both the acquisition release and Mergr's directory profile, making it one of the longest-running entertainment publishers on the web — over three decades of continuous operation as of 2026, and three years older than Google ([Mergr](https://mergr.com/movieweb-overview)).

**What does MovieWeb cover?**
The site covers movie and TV news: casting and development, release dates, trailers, interviews and clips. It also publishes original interviews with filmmakers, such as its March 2024 conversation with director Adam Rehmeier about his coming-of-age comedy *Snack Shack* ([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)).

**What did the 2024 Rehmeier interview establish?**
The interview confirms *Snack Shack* (2024) is a Republic Pictures theatrical release set in summer 1991, shot in Rehmeier's hometown of Nebraska City. It also records his bio: camera operator/cinematographer, *The Bunny Game* (2011) debut, and *Dinner in America*'s Sundance nomination and Odessa Grand Prix ([MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/snack-shack-adam-rehmeier-intervie/)).

**Where is MovieWeb based?**
Mergr lists MovieWeb as a digital media company headquartered in Las Vegas, Nevada, in the United States, giving a P.O. box address there. Valnet, its owner since 2021, is a Montreal-based digital content company operating the site within its wider entertainment portfolio ([Mergr](https://mergr.com/movieweb-overview)).
