## Overview

TradingView is a browser-based charting platform and social network for traders and investors, founded in 2011 by Stan Bokov, Denis Globa and Constantin Ivanov. Used by more than 60 million traders and investors worldwide, it combines advanced charting, market data and a trading community — and it has had no publicly named chief executive since Oleg Mukhanov resigned in March 2025 ([IG](https://www.ig.com/en/trading-platforms/tradingview/what-is-tradingview-how-to-use-it); [FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/executives/tradingview-ceo-oleg-mukhanov-resigns/)).

The company is privately held and headquartered in [London](/places/london/), having moved its head offices there from [New York](/places/new-york-city/) in 2020 ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2020/03/156240-leading-financial-trading-technology-firm-tradingview-moves-head-offices-from-new-york-to-london/)). Its rise tracks the retail-trading boom: from 8 million monthly active users in early 2018 to more than 30 million by late 2021, when a $298 million round led by Tiger Global Management valued it at $3 billion ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2018/05/21/cryptocurrency-and-a-stock-market-boom-pushes-tradingview-to-37-million-in-new-funding/); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2021-10-14-charting-platform-tradingview-raises-298-million-at-3-billion-valuation-120628)). What most profiles of the company miss is what happened next: two chief executives in fourteen months, a contractual wall built by the maker of MetaTrader, and a quiet expansion into AI-written financial news.

## From free browser charts to a $3 billion company

TradingView began as an attempt to put professional-grade charting in a browser window, free, with no installation — an HTML5 platform that worked the same on any operating system ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2020/03/156240-leading-financial-trading-technology-firm-tradingview-moves-head-offices-from-new-york-to-london/)). The founders graduated from the Techstars Chicago accelerator batch in 2013, backed by seed investors including Irish Angels, Techstars and iTech Capital ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2018/05/21/cryptocurrency-and-a-stock-market-boom-pushes-tradingview-to-37-million-in-new-funding/)). Growth came not from advertising but from giving the product away: TradingView offered its charting software free to partner websites such as SeekingAlpha, Bitfinex and the [Nasdaq](/organizations/nasdaq/), a strategy that carried it to 8 million monthly active users by March 2018, 61% of them arriving directly ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2018/05/21/cryptocurrency-and-a-stock-market-boom-pushes-tradingview-to-37-million-in-new-funding/)).

The money followed the users. In May 2018 the company raised $37 million in a Series B round led by Insight Venture Partners, with DRW Venture Capital and Jump Capital participating ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2018/05/21/cryptocurrency-and-a-stock-market-boom-pushes-tradingview-to-37-million-in-new-funding/)). In April 2019 it made its first acquisition, buying TradeIt — formerly Trading Ticket, an API that lets users trade on the spot from any site — for a reported under $20 million ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/04/02/tradingview-acquires-tradeit-to-add-instant-trading-apis-to-its-investor-toolkit/)). TradeIt had raised about $12 million from investors including Valar Ventures and Citi Ventures, and its chief executive Nathan Richardson and co-founder Betsy Eisenberg — who had built Yahoo Finance from scratch — joined TradingView with the deal ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/04/02/tradingview-acquires-tradeit-to-add-instant-trading-apis-to-its-investor-toolkit/)). Together the two companies said they served more than 10 million monthly active users in 150 countries, covering some $70 billion in linked assets ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/04/02/tradingview-acquires-tradeit-to-add-instant-trading-apis-to-its-investor-toolkit/)).

Then came the pandemic retail-trading surge. Visitors to the platform rose 237% over 18 months, and in October 2021 TradingView raised $298 million in a round led by Tiger Global Management, valuing the company at $3 billion — after having raised just over $40 million in total before that round ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2021-10-14-charting-platform-tradingview-raises-298-million-at-3-billion-valuation-120628)). The company was profitable at the time, according to Bloomberg's reporting of the round, and was said to be eyeing global expansion ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2021-10-14-charting-platform-tradingview-raises-298-million-at-3-billion-valuation-120628)).

## What the platform actually does

TradingView is a front-end, not a broker: it supplies the charts, screeners, heatmaps, indicators and social layer, while a partner broker handles execution behind the scenes ([Finance Magnates](https://ru.tradingview.com/news/financemagnates%3A5607dabf9094b%3A0-brokers-are-leaving-metatrader-behind-when-integrating-tradingview-here-s-why/)). Its charting covers stocks, forex, indices, commodities and cryptocurrencies, with more than 100 indicators, over 50 drawing tools, real-time data, news feeds, an economic calendar and fundamental data, plus a community where traders publish ideas, chat and stream ([IG](https://www.ig.com/en/trading-platforms/tradingview/what-is-tradingview-how-to-use-it)). Users can trade directly from the chart through an integrated broker — IG, for example, lets its clients place trades through the TradingView interface ([IG](https://www.ig.com/en/trading-platforms/tradingview/what-is-tradingview-how-to-use-it)).

Two design choices explain its spread. The first is Pine Script, a proprietary JavaScript-based language that lets any user build custom indicators and analysis tools ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2018/05/21/cryptocurrency-and-a-stock-market-boom-pushes-tradingview-to-37-million-in-new-funding/)). The second is the freemium model: most users connect and exchange trading ideas for free, and the company charges when customers want more charts, data and real-time server-side alerts — three paid plans cost $15, $30 and $60 a month in 2018, and about 75% of revenue then came from individual subscriptions ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2018/05/21/cryptocurrency-and-a-stock-market-boom-pushes-tradingview-to-37-million-in-new-funding/)). The same embeddable charts power other companies' products: partners named in 2019 included Crunchbase, Investopedia, SeekingAlpha, Zacks, [Binance](/organizations/binance/), CME Group and Entrepreneur, and by December 2024 a total of 83 brokers and trading platforms had integrated TradingView ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/04/02/tradingview-acquires-tradeit-to-add-instant-trading-apis-to-its-investor-toolkit/); [Finance Magnates](https://ru.tradingview.com/news/financemagnates%3A5607dabf9094b%3A0-brokers-are-leaving-metatrader-behind-when-integrating-tradingview-here-s-why/)).

Its audience skews young: in 2019 nearly half of its users were millennials, with a further 13% from Generation Z ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/04/02/tradingview-acquires-tradeit-to-add-instant-trading-apis-to-its-investor-toolkit/)). The user base grew roughly fourfold between 2018 and 2021, and the figures reported since then scatter widely — a discrepancy the profile states openly rather than smoothing over.

## The leadership gap and the MetaTrader wall

The least-covered part of TradingView's story is its leadership. Co-founder Denis Globa ran the company from its founding until January 2024, when Oleg Mukhanov — who had joined in mid-2022 as group chief financial officer after a venture-capital career at Enso Ventures and six years in investment banking at UBS — took over as chief executive ([FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/executives/exclusive-oleg-mukhanov-named-ceo-of-tradingview-as-denis-globa-departs/)). Globa left the company but remained a co-controlling shareholder, while Stan Bokov moved from chief operating officer to chief strategy officer ([FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/executives/exclusive-oleg-mukhanov-named-ceo-of-tradingview-as-denis-globa-departs/)). Fourteen months later, in March 2025, Mukhanov resigned, saying the company had achieved its "best-ever results on record" and that he would remain a friend and advisor; the US chief and general manager, Pierce Crosby, had also recently left, and no replacement chief executive had been announced ([FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/executives/tradingview-ceo-oleg-mukhanov-resigns/); [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/executives/tradingview-tastytrade-fca-and-more-executive-moves-of-the-week/)).

TradingView also faces a structural rival it cannot buy or build around. MetaQuotes, the developer of the MetaTrader 4 and 5 platforms, restricts brokers from integrating TradingView with MT4 and MT5 on the backend, and has stopped multiple brokers from using MetaTrader with TradingView ([Finance Magnates](https://ru.tradingview.com/news/financemagnates%3A5607dabf9094b%3A0-brokers-are-leaving-metatrader-behind-when-integrating-tradingview-here-s-why/)). MetaTrader's usage terms were drafted to restrict integration with other third-party platforms — the terms do not name TradingView, but cover all non-MetaTrader environments — and one broker cited its MetaQuotes contract to Finance Magnates, saying apps that do not rely on MetaQuotes' API "cannot be connected to MetaTrader" ([Finance Magnates](https://ru.tradingview.com/news/financemagnates%3A5607dabf9094b%3A0-brokers-are-leaving-metatrader-behind-when-integrating-tradingview-here-s-why/)). Indirect integration through a third-party technology stack is technically possible, but requires MetaQuotes' approval first ([Finance Magnates](https://ru.tradingview.com/news/financemagnates%3A5607dabf9094b%3A0-brokers-are-leaving-metatrader-behind-when-integrating-tradingview-here-s-why/)). TradingView's vice president of international growth, Rauan Khassan, told Finance Magnates the company saw "no reason to implement any restrictive measure from either side" and was "open to working across asset classes, brokerages, and platforms" ([Finance Magnates](https://ru.tradingview.com/news/financemagnates%3A5607dabf9094b%3A0-brokers-are-leaving-metatrader-behind-when-integrating-tradingview-here-s-why/)). One brokerage representative put the stakes plainly: "TradingView is versatile and is the only platform that can challenge MetaTrader's dominance" ([Finance Magnates](https://ru.tradingview.com/news/financemagnates%3A5607dabf9094b%3A0-brokers-are-leaving-metatrader-behind-when-integrating-tradingview-here-s-why/)).

A third, quieter expansion is into financial news itself. TradingView News publishes AI-powered summaries of documents filed with the SEC — for example, its 26 February 2026 summary of [Asure Software](/organizations/asure-software/)'s 8-K reporting fourth-quarter and full-year 2025 results, which carries the disclaimer that the summary "may contain inaccuracies" and is "solely based on documents filed with the SEC" ([TradingView News](https://www.tradingview.com/news/tradingview:b002ea8c3bf2b:0-asure-software-reports-strong-q4-and-full-year-2025-results/)). The platform that began as a charting tool is now also a publisher of machine-written earnings coverage — a line of business none of the top-ranking TradingView profiles mention.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is TradingView?**
TradingView is a browser-based charting platform and social network for traders and investors, founded in 2011. It offers advanced charts, screeners, market data and a community for sharing trading ideas, and is used by more than 60 million traders and investors worldwide ([IG](https://www.ig.com/en/trading-platforms/tradingview/what-is-tradingview-how-to-use-it)).

**Who founded TradingView?**
Stan Bokov, Denis Globa and Constantin Ivanov founded TradingView in 2011. Globa ran the company as chief executive until January 2024, when Oleg Mukhanov succeeded him ([FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/executives/exclusive-oleg-mukhanov-named-ceo-of-tradingview-as-denis-globa-departs/); [IG](https://www.ig.com/en/trading-platforms/tradingview/what-is-tradingview-how-to-use-it)).

**Is TradingView a broker?**
No. TradingView is a front-end for analysis and charting; trades are executed by partner brokers integrated with the platform, such as IG, and users trade directly from the chart through those brokers ([IG](https://www.ig.com/en/trading-platforms/tradingview/what-is-tradingview-how-to-use-it); [Finance Magnates](https://ru.tradingview.com/news/financemagnates%3A5607dabf9094b%3A0-brokers-are-leaving-metatrader-behind-when-integrating-tradingview-here-s-why/)).

**Who is TradingView's CEO now?**
No chief executive has been publicly named since Oleg Mukhanov resigned in March 2025, about a year after succeeding co-founder Denis Globa. Mukhanov stayed on as an advisor, and the company's US chief, Pierce Crosby, had also recently departed ([FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/executives/tradingview-ceo-oleg-mukhanov-resigns/)).

**How much is TradingView worth?**
Its last publicly reported valuation was $3 billion, set by the $298 million funding round led by Tiger Global Management in October 2021. No later valuation has been publicly disclosed in the sources read for this profile ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2021-10-14-charting-platform-tradingview-raises-298-million-at-3-billion-valuation-120628)).
