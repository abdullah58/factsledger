## Overview

Ostrum Asset Management is the [Paris](/places/paris/)-based institutional asset manager that runs fixed-income, insurance-related and quantitative strategies for insurers, pension funds and large corporates across Europe. An affiliate of [Natixis](/organizations/natixis/) Investment Managers inside [Groupe BPCE](/organizations/groupe-bpce/), it manages €384 billion for large institutional clients and was ranked the ninth-largest European institutional asset manager by IPE's Top 500 survey at the end of 2024 ([Ostrum Asset Management](https://www.ostrum.com/sites/default/files/1-ostrum-mediatheque/communiques-de-presse/eng/pr-maud-pierre-minuit-appointment.pdf)). Its modern history is a sequence of rebrands, a record regulatory fine, a state-bank merger and a buyout — renamed from Natixis Asset Management in 2018, combined with [La Banque Postale](/organizations/la-banque-postale/)'s asset management arm in 2020, and wholly owned by Natixis since May 2022 ([Natixis](https://natixis.groupebpce.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/pr_namrenamedostrumam_en_7march2018.pdf); [Groupe BPCE](https://newsroom-en.groupebpce.fr/download-pdf/627e80c308de164c260fe401)).

## A rebrand with a record fine behind it

Ostrum's current name dates to 3 April 2018, when Natixis Asset Management was renamed as part of Natixis' "New Dimension" brand-alignment plan, refocusing on its core capabilities in fixed income, equity and insurance ([Natixis](https://natixis.groupebpce.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/pr_namrenamedostrumam_en_7march2018.pdf)). The name was chosen deliberately: "Ostrum" has Latin roots and means "purple coloured", tying the firm to its parent companies Natixis and Groupe BPCE, and its then-chief executive Jean Raby called it Natixis Investment Managers' largest affiliate ([Funds Europe](https://www.funds-europe.com/natixis-am-rebranded-ostrum-asset-management/feed/)). At the time it managed €324.5 billion, Matthieu Duncan took over as chief executive on the day of the rename, and its US and Asian entities followed suit by June 2018, with all funds renamed by the end of the year ([Natixis](https://natixis.groupebpce.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/pr_namrenamedostrumam_en_7march2018.pdf)). The company said its bond funds were mostly top-quartile: 90% ranked in the first or second quartile by Morningstar at the end of 2017 ([Natixis](https://natixis.groupebpce.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/pr_namrenamedostrumam_en_7march2018.pdf)).

But the rebrand followed a bruising regulatory episode: on 25 July 2017 the [AMF](/organizations/autorite-des-marches-financiers/)'s Enforcement Committee had fined Natixis Asset Management €35 million — then the largest fine the committee had ever imposed — for breaching its professional obligations in managing 133 "formula funds" between 2012 and 2015 ([Autorité des Marchés Financiers](https://www.amf-france.org/sites/institutionnel/files/pdf/56868/en/The_AMF_Enforcement_Committee_fines_Natixis_Asset_Management_EUR35_million.pdf?1789109370)). The committee found misleading prospectus information, unjustified charges of €15.6 million taken from fund assets, and management-fee caps exceeded by €3.6 million and €12.5 million; the firm denied irregularity and said it would appeal ([Funds Europe](https://funds-europe.com/natixis-am-fine-was-a-record-for-french-regulatory-enforcers/)). A second AMF decision followed in September 2019, fining Natixis Investment Managers International €2 million and Natixis Asset Management Finance €1 million over securities-financing transactions run since 2009, including €24.7 million of cash-collateral remuneration the regulator said investors had not been told about ([Autorité des Marchés Financiers](https://www.amf-france.org/sites/institutionnel/files/pdf/61028/en/The_Enforcement_Committee_of_the_Autorite_des_Marches_Financiers_fines_Natixis_Investment_Managers_International_and_Natixis_Asset_Management_Finance_for_breaching_their_professional_obligations.pdf?1780628579)).

## The 2020 combination and the 2022 buyout

The firm's shape today comes from two state-bank transactions. On 31 October 2020 Natixis and La Banque Postale completed the combination of the fixed-income and insurance-related asset management businesses of Ostrum AM and La Banque Postale Asset Management inside Ostrum AM, creating a manager of more than €430 billion under management and over €590 billion under administration, owned 55% by Natixis and 45% by La Banque Postale ([La Banque Postale](https://www.labanquepostale.com/content/dam/lbp/documents/communiques-de-presse/en/2020/PR-closing-ostrum-nov2020.pdf)). Almost 75% of those assets were insurance-related, and the combined firm ranked among the top ten European asset managers for institutional investors, serving insurers, pension funds and large corporates under liability constraints ([La Banque Postale](https://www.labanquepostale.com/content/dam/lbp/documents/communiques-de-presse/en/2020/PR-closing-ostrum-nov2020.pdf)). Its new tagline, "Enhancing your power to act", framed investment as a way for clients to meet their own commitments to European citizens' health and retirement ([La Banque Postale](https://www.labanquepostale.com/content/dam/lbp/documents/communiques-de-presse/en/2020/PR-closing-ostrum-nov2020.pdf)).

The deal was one half of a wider swap: on 28 October 2021 La Banque Postale agreed to buy Groupe BPCE's 16.1% stake in [CNP Assurances](/organizations/cnp-assurances/) at €21.90 a share — a 36.2% premium valuing the insurer at about €15 billion — while BPCE, through Natixis, agreed to buy La Banque Postale's 45% stake in Ostrum and its 40% stake in [AEW Europe](/organizations/aew-europe/) ([Allnews](https://allnews.ch/content/corporate/la-banque-postale-veut-monter-%C3%A0-100-dans-cnp-assurances); [Boursorama / Le Revenu](https://www.boursorama.com/bourse/actualites/opa-des-primes-attractives-pour-cnp-assurances-prodware-et-s-t-dupont-739164beaf965dd9e1850c864ef9dcd1)). La Banque Postale then held just over 62% of CNP, the block purchase would take it to 78.9%, and the whole operation would total more than €5.5 billion ([Allnews](https://allnews.ch/content/corporate/la-banque-postale-veut-monter-%C3%A0-100-dans-cnp-assurances)). That second leg closed on 13 May 2022: Natixis Investment Managers acquired the 45% Ostrum interest and now owns 100% of the firm, with the groups' industrial partnerships extended to the end of 2030 ([Groupe BPCE](https://newsroom-en.groupebpce.fr/download-pdf/627e80c308de164c260fe401)). At the end of 2021 Ostrum managed €442 billion, against €38 billion at AEW Europe — a scale that made the fixed-income house, not the property arm, the weightier half of the buyout ([Groupe BPCE](https://newsroom-en.groupebpce.fr/download-pdf/627e80c308de164c260fe401)).

## Scale, responsible investment and the Generali deal that wasn't

Ostrum presents itself as a responsible-investment leader for liability-driven clients, and the record partly supports the claim: it was one of the first French asset managers to sign the UN Principles for Responsible Investment, in 2008, and at the 2020 combination it pledged to apply ESG criteria to all investments from 2021 and to hold SRI accreditation on 98% of its open-ended-fund assets by 2022 ([La Banque Postale](https://www.labanquepostale.com/content/dam/lbp/documents/communiques-de-presse/en/2020/PR-closing-ostrum-nov2020.pdf)). Under chief executive Olivier Houix it appointed Maud Pierre-Minuit to head a Sustainable Transitions team in February 2026; she brought more than 20 years in asset management, including running fixed income at Groupama AM and total-return strategies at La Française AM ([Ostrum Asset Management](https://www.ostrum.com/sites/default/files/1-ostrum-mediatheque/communiques-de-presse/eng/pr-maud-pierre-minuit-appointment.pdf)). The firm reported €384 billion under management at the end of December 2025, with IPE's Top 500 survey ranking it the ninth-largest European institutional asset manager at the end of 2024 ([Ostrum Asset Management](https://www.ostrum.com/sites/default/files/1-ostrum-mediatheque/communiques-de-presse/eng/pr-maud-pierre-minuit-appointment.pdf)).

The one big strategic move that did not happen was the BPCE–Generali joint venture: a non-binding memorandum in January 2025 would have combined Generali Investments and Natixis Investment Managers — Ostrum included — into a 50-50 company with $2.2 trillion under management, ranked ninth globally and the leading asset manager in Europe, with $4.8 billion in revenues ([Citywire](https://citywire.com/pro-buyer/news/natixis-and-generali-end-merger-negotiations/a2480503)). The two groups jointly ended negotiations in December 2025, saying the conditions for a final agreement were not present ([Citywire](https://citywire.com/pro-buyer/news/natixis-and-generali-end-merger-negotiations/a2480503)). The deal had faced opposition from the Italian government and Generali's two main investors, the Del Vecchio family's Delfin holding company and construction tycoon Francesco Gaetano Caltagirone, and the partners had already agreed in September to drop severance payments if the talks failed — a collapse that left Ostrum inside Natixis, and Natixis inside Groupe BPCE, rather than inside a new European champion ([Citywire](https://citywire.com/pro-buyer/news/natixis-and-generali-end-merger-negotiations/a2480503)).

## FAQ

### What is Ostrum Asset Management?

Ostrum Asset Management is a Paris-based institutional asset manager and an affiliate of Natixis Investment Managers. It manages fixed-income, insurance-related, equity and quantitative strategies for insurers, pension funds, health insurers and large corporates, and reported €384 billion under management at the end of 2025.

### What was Ostrum called before?

It was Natixis Asset Management until 3 April 2018, when it was renamed Ostrum Asset Management as part of Natixis' "New Dimension" brand alignment. "Ostrum" is Latin for "purple coloured", chosen to tie the firm to its parents Natixis and Groupe BPCE. Its US and Asian units followed by June 2018.

### Who owns Ostrum Asset Management?

Natixis Investment Managers owns 100% of Ostrum. It bought La Banque Postale's 45% stake in May 2022, as the other side of La Banque Postale's purchase of Groupe BPCE's 16.1% stake in CNP Assurances at €21.90 a share. The purchase closed on 13 May 2022.

### Why was Ostrum fined by the AMF?

In July 2017 the AMF fined its predecessor, Natixis Asset Management, a record €35 million over "formula funds" managed between 2012 and 2015, citing misleading prospectus information and €15.6 million of unjustified charges. The firm denied irregularity and said it would appeal.

### What happened to the Generali joint venture?

BPCE and Generali signed a non-binding memorandum in January 2025 to combine their asset management operations, including Ostrum, into a 50-50 venture with $2.2 trillion under management. They jointly ended negotiations in December 2025, saying the conditions for a final agreement were not present.
