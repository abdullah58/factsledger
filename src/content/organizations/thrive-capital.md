## Overview

Thrive Capital is the New York venture capital firm Joshua Kushner founded in 2009, built on an unfashionable doctrine: make very few bets, make them very large, and stay close enough to help build the company rather than merely fund it. From a $5 million first fund in 2010 it grew to a $5 billion ninth fund in 2024, powered by early, concentrated positions in [Instagram](/organizations/instagram/), [Stripe](/organizations/stripe/) and [OpenAI](/organizations/openai/) ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/thrive-capital-5-billion-vc-fund)). Its most recent defining position is [Anysphere](/organizations/anysphere/), whose $900 million Series C Thrive led in 2025 before backing its $2.3 billion Series D ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/05/cursors-anysphere-nabs-9-9b-valuation-soars-past-500m-arr/); [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-defense-tech-anysphere/)).

## Built to build, not just to bet

Thrive's founding pitch was, by the standards of 2010 venture capital, deliberately unconventional: a single vehicle agnostic to stage, sector and geography, which would not only invest in companies but build them, and which would act as a service provider and embedded operational team for founders ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/)). Kushner seeded the firm with backing from General Catalyst's Joel Cutler, who gave $1 million to start it, and won the support of Princeton University's endowment chief Andy Golden, whose $10 million seed and the $30 million that followed formed Thrive's first $40 million institutional fund ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/)). The build side was not rhetoric: Kushner started Oscar Health at the same time as Thrive, at age 24, after spending a weekend reading the 906-page Affordable Care Act with his co-founder, and Oscar went on to IPO in 2021 and reach 1.8 million members by 2025 ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/)). The same instinct shows in Thrive's portfolio work: when GitHub hit a management crisis shortly after Thrive invested in 2015, the firm dispatched partner Nabil Mallick to serve effectively as GitHub's interim CFO for nine months, finalising its budget and analytics ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/)). Decisions are attributed to the team rather than to individuals, which is why, Colossus notes, no Thrive investor appears on Forbes' Midas List of individual investors ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/)).

## Concentration in practice: from Instagram to OpenAI

The doctrine produced a run of precisely timed, oversized bets. Thrive put $12 million into Instagram's growth round just 72 hours before Facebook acquired the company for $1 billion — double the $500 million valuation of Thrive's round — winning the allocation because founder Kevin Systrom regarded Kushner as harder-working and more independent-minded than larger rival firms ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/)). A $6 million cheque into Spotify's 2012 growth round closed a gap its chief executive Daniel Ek needed exactly filled ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/)). The GitHub position began at 7% of Thrive's $400 million fund and grew by a further $120 million across two tranches bought at a 25% discount ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/)). By 2023 the eighth fund, at $3.3 billion, carried a $2 billion investment in Stripe at a $50 billion valuation ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/)). The pattern reached its extreme with OpenAI: in spring 2022 Thrive submitted a $130 million term sheet at a $29 billion valuation that was the only term sheet OpenAI received, had committed around $700 million by November 2023, and then led the $6.6 billion round of October 2024 at a $157 billion post-money valuation, investing around $1.3 billion with an exclusive option to add up to $1 billion more at the same valuation through 2025 ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/02/openai-raises-6-6b-and-is-now-valued-at-157b/)). The stat that captures the concentration best is the fund ladder itself: $5 million in 2010, $400 million by the mid-2010s, $3.3 billion in 2023, and over $5 billion for Fund IX in August 2024 — split $1 billion for early-stage and $4 billion for growth bets ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/thrive-capital-5-billion-vc-fund)).

## Cursor and the AI-era portfolio

Thrive's Anysphere position extends the same playbook into AI-native software. Thrive led Anysphere's $900 million Series C in June 2025 at a $9.9 billion valuation, with DST Global among the participants, and then backed the $2.3 billion Series D in November 2025 at a $29.3 billion post-money valuation ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/05/cursors-anysphere-nabs-9-9b-valuation-soars-past-500m-arr/); [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-defense-tech-anysphere/)). The through-line from Instagram to Cursor is the firm's original claim: back a small number of companies, back them heavily, and stay inside the building. Colossus reports the firm's portfolio by 2024 also included Twitch, Slack, Robinhood, Hims, Skims, Anduril, Wiz, A24 and Databricks, with Thrive partners on the boards of many ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/)). Whether concentration on this scale survives a downturn is the open question the profile's sources do not answer — they document the hits, not the misses, and describe the portfolio as carrying "a dizzying ratio of misses to hits" without enumerating the misses ([Colossus](https://colossus.com/article/joshua-kushner-thrive-new-world/)).
