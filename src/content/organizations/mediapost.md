## Overview

MediaPost is an American trade publisher covering advertising, marketing and media, founded in 1996 by Kenneth Fadner, a co-founder of Adweek. It grew from a single weekly email newsletter into a publisher of the magazines OMMA and MEDIA, more than 50 email newsletters, and a programme of industry awards and conferences serving media planners and buyers ([MediaPost](http://s3.amazonaws.com/media.mediapost.com/uploads/2016_MediaPost_MediaKit_F6kTNoF.pdf); [Interactive Advertising Bureau](https://www.iab.com/news/interactive-advertising-bureau-iab-mediapost-announce-inaugural-online-media-marketing-advertising-awards-ommas/)).

The company is based in [New York City](/places/new-york-city/) in the [United States](/places/united-states/), and its publications are written for the people who plan and buy advertising rather than for consumers ([OMMA Magazine](http://media.mediapost.com.s3.amazonaws.com/pdf/magazines/OMMA_WINTER2012.pdf)). It is not the French direct-mail company Mediapost, founded in 1985 and headquartered in Lyon, France ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/57823-93)). MediaPost's business rests on three legs — publishing, awards and events — and it also reports on the ownership of the media it covers, including the 2020 deal that brought Billboard under joint ownership ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/356161/penske-media-mrc-to-jointly-own-magazines-includ.html)).

Its newsroom was led by editor-in-chief Joe Mandese, with Barbara Lippert and Bob Garfield as editors at large and specialist editors covering programmatic, mobile, agency, social media, publishing, email, search, television and brand marketing — a bench the company presented as the largest full-time journalism staff of any advertising trade publisher ([MediaPost](http://s3.amazonaws.com/media.mediapost.com/uploads/2016_MediaPost_MediaKit_F6kTNoF.pdf)).

## Founding and the Adweek connection

MediaPost's founder came to it with a trade-publishing pedigree. Kenneth Fadner had co-founded Adweek in 1978 with Jack Thomas and Penn Tudor, merging three regional advertising publications into a single national title ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/brand-marketing/adweek-co-founder-jack-thomas-dies-87-165840/)). Thomas and Fadner had met at New York magazine in the 1970s and left together after Rupert Murdoch bought it in 1976; the trio then built A/S/M Communications, publisher of Adweek, Brandweek and Mediaweek ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/brand-marketing/adweek-co-founder-jack-thomas-dies-87-165840/)). Thomas stayed with Adweek long after his partners left, keeping a corner office into the 1990s and retiring in 2000 ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/brand-marketing/adweek-co-founder-jack-thomas-dies-87-165840/)). Fadner launched MediaPost in 1996, and Adweek later described him as "now the publisher and principal force behind Mediapost" ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/brand-marketing/adweek-co-founder-jack-thomas-dies-87-165840/)). The new venture began as a single weekly email newsletter and moved fast: by May 2000 MediaPost.com was selling online media buying and publishing two email newsletters, and it was about to launch four new daily newsletters and a monthly print magazine — an expansion conceived in mid-April, staffed on 1 May and sold out of advertising by 26 May ([MarketingSherpa](https://www.marketingsherpa.com/article/former-adweek-publisher-speeds-to); [MediaPost](http://s3.amazonaws.com/media.mediapost.com/uploads/2016_MediaPost_MediaKit_F6kTNoF.pdf)).

## Publications, newsletters and audience

By 2004 MediaPost's catalogue already included MEDIA magazine, three editions of MediaDailyNews, The Online Spin, Just an Online Minute, The Research Brief, The Email Insider and Real Media Riffs, alongside its conferences ([Interactive Advertising Bureau](https://www.iab.com/news/interactive-advertising-bureau-iab-mediapost-announce-inaugural-online-media-marketing-advertising-awards-ommas/)). By 2016 the company said it produced more than 50 email newsletters, ran four award programmes and staged more than 30 major industry events, with subscribers exceeding 200,000 across more than 6,000 agencies, 86% of them active in planning or buying advertising ([MediaPost](http://s3.amazonaws.com/media.mediapost.com/uploads/2016_MediaPost_MediaKit_F6kTNoF.pdf)). Its audience included the final agency and brand-side decision-maker for 87% of all advertising dollars spent in 2013, and its site drew 643,015 monthly unique visitors in March 2016 ([MediaPost](http://s3.amazonaws.com/media.mediapost.com/uploads/2016_MediaPost_MediaKit_F6kTNoF.pdf)). In a fourth-quarter 2014 Advertiser Perceptions survey of 2,000 interactive agency executives and advertisers, 60% named MediaPost the publication providing the best coverage of the media, marketing and advertising landscape — ahead of Adweek, Mashable, eMarketer and Digiday — a result that put it ahead of every rival trade title in the survey ([MediaPost](http://s3.amazonaws.com/media.mediapost.com/uploads/2016_MediaPost_MediaKit_F6kTNoF.pdf)).

## Awards, events and Billboard reporting

MediaPost's awards began with a partnership: in August 2004 it joined the Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB) to launch the OMMAs, the first awards devoted to interactive advertising, with a gala on 21 September 2004 during Advertising Week in New York ([Interactive Advertising Bureau](https://www.iab.com/news/interactive-advertising-bureau-iab-mediapost-announce-inaugural-online-media-marketing-advertising-awards-ommas/)). The judging panel was fifteen senior marketing executives — mostly client-side marketers from companies including Visa USA, Reebok, Revlon, Masterfoods, Johnson & Johnson and IBM — and the entries included work for Lexus, Sun Microsystems, Adidas and Best Buy ([Interactive Advertising Bureau](https://www.iab.com/news/iab-mediapost-announce-judging-panel-online-media-marketing-advertising-awards-ommas)). OMMA magazine's own Agency of the Year awards followed from 2005, with Digitas taking gold in 2011 — as it had in 2008 — and AKQA silver ([OMMA Magazine](http://media.mediapost.com.s3.amazonaws.com/pdf/magazines/OMMA_WINTER2012.pdf)). MediaPost also reports on the ownership of the media it covers: in September 2020 it reported that Penske Media and MRC would form PMRC, a joint venture bringing PMC's [Variety](/organizations/variety/), Rolling Stone and Music Business Worldwide together with MRC's The Hollywood Reporter, Billboard and Vibe, with no leadership changes and terms undisclosed ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/356161/penske-media-mrc-to-jointly-own-magazines-includ.html)). MRC owns Dick Clark Productions, which produces the [Billboard Music Awards](/events/billboard-music-awards/) ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/356161/penske-media-mrc-to-jointly-own-magazines-includ.html)).

## FAQ

**What is MediaPost?**

MediaPost is an American trade publisher covering advertising, marketing and media, based in New York City. It publishes the magazines OMMA and MEDIA, more than 50 email newsletters, and runs industry awards and events for media planners and buyers, with subscribers exceeding 200,000 across more than 6,000 agencies ([MediaPost](http://s3.amazonaws.com/media.mediapost.com/uploads/2016_MediaPost_MediaKit_F6kTNoF.pdf)).

**Who founded MediaPost?**

Kenneth Fadner founded MediaPost in 1996, launching it as a single weekly email newsletter. Fadner had co-founded Adweek in 1978 with Jack Thomas and Penn Tudor by merging three regional advertising publications, and Adweek later called him the publisher and principal force behind MediaPost. Thomas, his Adweek co-founder, died in 2015 at the age of 87 ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/brand-marketing/adweek-co-founder-jack-thomas-dies-87-165840/)).

**What are the OMMA Awards?**

The OMMAs were launched in 2004 by MediaPost and the Interactive Advertising Bureau as the first awards devoted to interactive advertising, judged by senior client-side marketers. OMMA magazine's own Agency of the Year awards followed from 2005, honouring agencies such as Digitas, which won gold in 2011 ([Interactive Advertising Bureau](https://www.iab.com/news/interactive-advertising-bureau-iab-mediapost-announce-inaugural-online-media-marketing-advertising-awards-ommas/); [OMMA Magazine](http://media.mediapost.com.s3.amazonaws.com/pdf/magazines/OMMA_WINTER2012.pdf)).

**Is MediaPost the same company as Mediapost?**

No. The French company spelled "Mediapost" is a direct-mail advertising services provider founded in 1985 and headquartered in Lyon, France, with about 150 employees. The American trade publisher MediaPost was founded in 1996 in New York and covers advertising, marketing and media ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/57823-93)).

**What did MediaPost report about Billboard?**

In September 2020 MediaPost reported that Penske Media and MRC would form PMRC, a joint venture bringing PMC's Variety, Rolling Stone and Music Business Worldwide together with MRC's The Hollywood Reporter, Billboard and Vibe, with no leadership changes and financial terms undisclosed. MRC owns Dick Clark Productions, which produces the Billboard Music Awards ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/356161/penske-media-mrc-to-jointly-own-magazines-includ.html)).
