## Overview

Halo Top Creamery is an American light ice cream brand founded in [Los Angeles](/places/los-angeles/), [California](/places/california/), that sells low-calorie, high-protein pints with the calorie count on the tub. Created by former lawyer Justin Woolverton as a kitchen experiment in 2011, it became the best-selling pint in the [United States](/places/united-states/) in 2017 after a viral magazine stunt, was sold to Wells Enterprises in September 2019 for an undisclosed sum, and passed to the Ferrero Group in January 2023 ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html); [DairyReporter](https://WWW.DAIRYREPORTER.COM/Article/2019/09/11/Halo-Top-acquired-by-Blue-Bunny-producer/); [PRNewswire](https://stockstreetnews.com/financial/ferrero-group-reports-consolidated-financial-statements-for-the-2022-2023-financial-year/)).

This profile covers Halo Top Creamery, the ice cream brand — not any other business using the "Halo" name. In its first year the product was sold as Eden Creamery, and the legal entity behind the 2019 sale was Eden Creamery, LLC ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html); [DairyReporter](https://WWW.DAIRYREPORTER.COM/Article/2019/09/11/Halo-Top-acquired-by-Blue-Bunny-producer/)). The pitch was simple: a pint marketed at 240 to 360 calories with at least 20 grams of protein, against roughly 1,000 calories for a basic pint from a major brand, at a premium $6.99 a pint ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html)). The [Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d) called it a poster child for the challenger-brand wave — an upstart that broke into a category long dominated by Unilever and Nestlé.

## From a kitchen experiment to Eden Creamery

Woolverton was a corporate lawyer at Latham & Watkins in Los Angeles in 2011, bored with his job and managing his blood sugar with a low-sugar diet, when he started tinkering in his kitchen — first freezing Greek yogurt with fruit and stevia, then buying a $20 ice cream maker and spending about a year perfecting a recipe sweetened with stevia and erythritol ([Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d); [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html)). The first store to stock the result, in 2012, was the Canyon Country Store in Los Angeles ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html)). Woolverton met Doug Bouton, another former lawyer, in an amateur basketball league; Bouton joined in 2013 as president and chief operating officer, and the pair funded the business from family and friends, student loans and credit-card debt ([MarketingWeek](https://www.marketingweek.com/halo-top-ice-cream/); [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/magazine/201802/burt-helm/halo-top-healthy-ice-cream.html)).

The early years were close to fatal. In 2014 and 2015 the brand was discontinued in a bunch of stores, and the founders begged retailers for one more year ([MarketingWeek](https://www.marketingweek.com/halo-top-ice-cream/)). *Inc.* reported that two years before the 2017 breakout, the founders were broke and hundreds of thousands of dollars in debt, having made "every possible mistake" — a first formula so thick it jammed factory machinery, and a brand name already trademarked by a litigious competitor ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/magazine/201802/burt-helm/halo-top-healthy-ice-cream.html)).

After a year selling as Eden Creamery, the company renamed itself Halo Top in 2013, preemptively, to avoid a lawsuit from another Eden — keeping the angelic theme and turning the lid into a golden halo ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html)). The rename also marked a positioning shift: rather than a frozen Greek yogurt, which Woolverton feared the yogurt giants would crush, the product became an all-natural, low-calorie, low-sugar ice cream — a category that did not yet exist ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html)). The [Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d) reports the founders raised $1.5 million from friends and family, angel investors and crowdfunding and retained majority control ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/magazine/201802/burt-helm/halo-top-healthy-ice-cream.html)).

## The viral breakout and the 2017 peak

Halo Top's growth did not really tick up until January 2016, when *GQ* contributor Shane Snow ate nothing but Halo Top for 10 days and published his account ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html)). The article went viral, and Halo Top averaged 77 percent growth in each of the three months that followed; sales rose about 2,500 percent in 2016 over 2015 ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html)). Woolverton credits the stunt with catapulting the brand from steady to explosive growth, while stressing he does not recommend anyone copy the diet ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html)). A mid-2015 packaging redesign had set the stage: the calorie count moved to the center of the tub in large figures, a design rivals would soon imitate ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/ryan-caldbeck/how-halo-top-became-a-consumer-sensation.html); [Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d)).

The numbers that followed were extraordinary for a company that had nearly died two years earlier. Revenue grew from $230,000 in 2013 to $49 million in 2016 and more than $100 million in 2017, earning Halo Top the No. 5 spot on the 2017 Inc. 5000 list, with 20,944 percent three-year growth ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/magazine/201802/burt-helm/halo-top-healthy-ice-cream.html); [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html)). By 2017 the brand was in more than 19,000 US stores and had sold 50 million pints that year, making it the best-selling pint in the country, ahead of Häagen-Dazs and Ben & Jerry's ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html)). IRI's 2017 New Product Pacesetters report ranked it the No. 1 food and beverage launch of the year, with $324.2 million in year-one multi-outlet dollar sales ([Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/healthy-and-indulgent-products-dominate-new-launches-in-2017-iri-finds/521585/)). *Time* named Halo Top one of its 25 Best Inventions of 2017 — the only food product on the list ([Dairy Foods](https://www.dairyfoods.com/articles/92577-halo-top-ice-cream-makes-time-magazines-list-of-the-25-best-inventions-of-2017)).

## Funding: the CircleUp distinction

Halo Top's funding story is widely misreported, and the correction is the most useful thing this profile adds. The brand raised hardly any equity: according to CircleUp co-founder [Ryan Caldbeck](/people/ryan-caldbeck/), writing in *Inc.*, the only equity it raised was on CircleUp in 2013 and 2015, in two modest rounds ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/ryan-caldbeck/how-halo-top-became-a-consumer-sensation.html)). PitchBook records those rounds as a $445,000 seed on September 26, 2013 and a $395,000 seed on March 27, 2015, and lists CircleUp, Fort Ventures and One Degree Ventures among the former investors ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/110813-23)). Caldbeck's account should be read with its authorship in mind — he ran the platform the rounds were raised on — but the dates and amounts are corroborated by PitchBook.

What Halo Top was not is a CircleUp Growth Partners portfolio company. CircleUp Growth Partners was a $125 million venture fund that [CircleUp](/organizations/circleup/) launched on October 31, 2017 — two years after Halo Top's last CircleUp round — to invest directly in early-stage consumer brands using its Helio machine-learning platform ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/10/31/circleup-announced-125-million-venture-fund/)). Its first two investments were HUM Nutrition and 4505 Chicharrones, and its limited partners included [Temasek](/organizations/temasek/) ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/10/31/circleup-announced-125-million-venture-fund/)). Halo Top's connection to CircleUp was to the older marketplace, where brands raised money from individual investors — not to the fund. PitchBook lists one wrinkle: a Series A dated January 8, 2016 with no amount shown, and $5.84 million raised in total, against $840,000 after the two seed rounds ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/110813-23)). That conflicts with Caldbeck's account, and the profile flags the contradiction rather than resolving it.

The near-miss sale is also part of the story. MarketingWeek reported in September 2018 that Unilever had considered buying the company for $2 billion in January of that year ([MarketingWeek](https://www.marketingweek.com/halo-top-ice-cream/)). PitchBook separately lists a merger/acquisition deal dated August 7, 2017 as "Cancelled", without naming a counterparty ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/110813-23)). Whether the two were the same talks is unverified.

## International expansion and the copycat squeeze

Success attracted imitators fast. Unilever's chief financial officer admitted the giant "had missed the trend" for healthier ice cream and said Halo Top was taking share "very, very quickly" — 1.5 share points, by one account ([Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d); [MarketingWeek](https://www.marketingweek.com/halo-top-ice-cream/)). Unilever launched Breyers Delights in mid-2017, mimicking Halo Top by putting the calorie count in large figures on the tub, and Nestlé followed ([Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d)). The squeeze showed in the numbers: Halo Top became North America's sixth-biggest ice cream brand with 3.7 percent market share in 2018, but US retail sales fell 6.4 percent to $329 million, even as units sold rose 4 percent to 77.5 million ([Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d)).

The response was to go abroad before the multinationals could use their scale to follow — a harder job than expected, since US-made ice cream had to be shipped frozen ([Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d)). The breakthrough came from a stranger: Matt Fulbrook, a former Ben & Jerry's sales director in the UK, emailed Bouton in the summer of 2017, and within weeks his company Brand of Brothers had a deal to expand Halo Top in Europe for a commission on sales ([Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d)). Tesco signed up first, and by the fourth quarter of 2017 eight more UK customers had agreed to stock it ([Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d)).

Halo Top launched in the [United Kingdom](/places/united-kingdom/) in January 2018 and, according to IRI figures, had sold nearly 3.3 million pints there by September 8, 2018, stocked in the big four supermarkets plus Ocado ([MarketingWeek](https://www.marketingweek.com/halo-top-ice-cream/)). UK sales were £13.1 million in the 52 weeks to September 2018 by Nielsen's measure, and £20.2 million in its first year by IRi's ([The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/news/halo-top-sold-to-us-ice-cream-giant-wells-enterprises/597436.article); [Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d)). A contract manufacturer was found in Germany, the brand also started selling in the Netherlands and Germany, and Bouton told the FT it was in 12 countries ([Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d)).


## The Wells sale and the Ferrero era

On September 9, 2019, Wells Enterprises — the Le Mars, [Iowa](/places/iowa/), maker of Blue Bunny, founded in 1913 — signed a purchase agreement with Eden Creamery, LLC to acquire the Halo Top brand, with the deal expected to close that month ([DairyReporter](https://WWW.DAIRYREPORTER.COM/Article/2019/09/11/Halo-Top-acquired-by-Blue-Bunny-producer/)). Financial terms were not disclosed, and the deal expanded Wells' portfolio to five dessert brands ([DairyReporter](https://WWW.DAIRYREPORTER.COM/Article/2019/09/11/Halo-Top-acquired-by-Blue-Bunny-producer/)). Wells chief executive Mike Wells said Halo Top had "met a consumer need that no one else in the category was serving" ([DairyReporter](https://WWW.DAIRYREPORTER.COM/Article/2019/09/11/Halo-Top-acquired-by-Blue-Bunny-producer/)). The sale split the brand geographically: Wells also agreed to license Halo Top outside the United States and Canada to a new company Bouton planned to operate, with Bouton telling *The Grocer* he was excited to keep developing the brand internationally, "especially in the UK" ([The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/news/halo-top-sold-to-us-ice-cream-giant-wells-enterprises/597436.article); [DairyReporter](https://WWW.DAIRYREPORTER.COM/Article/2019/09/11/Halo-Top-acquired-by-Blue-Bunny-producer/)).

Under Wells, the brand's most visible change was a recipe overhaul. In April 2022 Halo Top reformulated its dairy light ice cream with ultrafiltered skim milk for a creamier texture, debuting the change with a new Chocolate Cake Batter flavor carrying a "Now Creamier" label at $4.99 ([Dairy Foods](https://www.dairyfoods.com/articles/95587-halo-top-debuts-new-recipe-for-dairy-ice-cream?v=preview)).

The ownership chain moved once more at the end of January 2023, when the Ferrero Group completed its acquisition of Wells Enterprises — its operations, four US manufacturing facilities and brand portfolio including Blue Bunny, Blue Ribbon Classics, Bomb Pop and Halo Top — with Wells remaining a stand-alone business ([PRNewswire](https://stockstreetnews.com/financial/ferrero-group-reports-consolidated-financial-statements-for-the-2022-2023-financial-year/)). Ferrero had announced the agreement on December 7, 2022, as part of its push into North American ice cream ([PRNewswire](https://stockstreetnews.com/financial/ferrero-group-reports-consolidated-financial-statements-for-the-2022-2023-financial-year/)). Halo Top thus went, in four years, from a founder-owned challenger to a brand inside one of the world's largest sweet-packaged-food companies.

**Who founded Halo Top?** Justin Woolverton, a former corporate lawyer in Los Angeles, after kitchen experiments in 2011; Doug Bouton joined as president and COO in 2013 ([Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d); [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/kaitlyn-wang/2017-inc5000-halo-top-creamery.html)).

**How much was Halo Top sold for in 2019?** The price was never disclosed. Wells announced the acquisition on September 9, 2019 with terms undisclosed, and no source read for this profile states a figure ([DairyReporter](https://WWW.DAIRYREPORTER.COM/Article/2019/09/11/Halo-Top-acquired-by-Blue-Bunny-producer/); [The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/news/halo-top-sold-to-us-ice-cream-giant-wells-enterprises/597436.article)).

**Was Halo Top a CircleUp Growth Partners investment?** No. Halo Top raised two small seed rounds on the CircleUp marketplace in 2013 and 2015; the $125 million Growth Partners fund launched in October 2017, after those rounds, and its first investments were HUM Nutrition and 4505 Chicharrones ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/ryan-caldbeck/how-halo-top-became-a-consumer-sensation.html); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/10/31/circleup-announced-125-million-venture-fund/)).

**Who owns Halo Top now?** Wells Enterprises of Le Mars, Iowa, which acquired the brand in September 2019; Wells itself was acquired by the Ferrero Group, completing at the end of January 2023 ([DairyReporter](https://WWW.DAIRYREPORTER.COM/Article/2019/09/11/Halo-Top-acquired-by-Blue-Bunny-producer/); [PRNewswire](https://stockstreetnews.com/financial/ferrero-group-reports-consolidated-financial-statements-for-the-2022-2023-financial-year/)).

**Why did Halo Top's sales fall after 2017?** US retail sales fell 6.4 percent in 2018 to $329 million as Unilever and Nestlé launched copycat healthier ice creams ([Financial Times](https://www-ft-com.ezp-prod1.hul.harvard.edu/content/b5ffbe6e-1360-11e9-a168-d45595ad076d)).
