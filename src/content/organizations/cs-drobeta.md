## Overview

CS Drobeta-Turnu Severin is a football club based in Drobeta-Turnu Severin, Mehedinți County, Romania, founded in 2025 by the city's municipality and the county council as a publicly funded club ([ProSport](https://liga2.prosport.ro/liga-4/cs-drobeta-este-noua-campioana-judeteana-de-la-mehedinti-19389737), [Sport.ro](https://www.sport.ro/fotbal-intern/sunt-doua-echipe-cu-acelasi-nume-chiar-si-la-drobeta-turnu-severin-una-dintre-ele-este-antrenata-de-florin-soava.html)). What the standard accounts stop short of telling is what happened after the fairy-tale debut: the club not only won promotion in June 2026 but immediately appointed Flavius Stoican — a manager who had just finished a Liga I spell with Farul Constanța — as head coach in mid-July 2026 ([Transfermarkt](https://www.transfermarkt.co.in/flavius-stoican/profil/trainer/22654)). The gamble opened well: Drobeta started its first Liga III season at the top of Group 6 ([Sportradar](https://sportradar.com/?q=4200675429247460090)).

## A debut season built for immediate promotion

The project was conceived from the start as a fast track back to the national leagues: the municipal club named former Romanian international [Florin Șoavă](/people/florin-soava/), a Mehedinți native, as its first head coach and Marian Balaci as president, with promotion to Liga III stated as the explicit objective ([Sport.ro](https://www.sport.ro/fotbal-intern/sunt-doua-echipe-cu-acelasi-nume-chiar-si-la-drobeta-turnu-severin-una-dintre-ele-este-antrenata-de-florin-soava.html), [ProSport](https://liga2.prosport.ro/liga-4/cs-drobeta-este-noua-campioana-judeteana-de-la-mehedinti-19389737)). The debut campaign delivered exactly that. Reporting at the start of June 2026 had the club at 24 wins and 1 draw with a goal difference of 168-15, already the Mehedinți county (Liga IV) champion ([ProSport](https://liga2.prosport.ro/liga-4/cs-drobeta-este-noua-campioana-judeteana-de-la-mehedinti-19389737)). The county association's own official portal confirms both halves of the double in its published communications: CS Drobeta Turnu Severin as Liga 4 Mehedinți champion for 2025-2026, and as winner of the county stage of the Cupa României in the same edition ([AJF Mehedinți](https://frf-ajf.ro/mehedinti)). The cup run included a 7-0 final win over the local rival CS Drobeta 2024 ([ProSport](https://liga2.prosport.ro/liga-4/nou-infiintata-cs-drobeta-a-izbutit-eventul-la-mehedinti-19390724)).

## Promotion and the Stoican era

County champions qualify exclusively for the promotion playoff, decided by two-legged knockout ties drawn across seven geographic regions, with the 2026 legs fixed for 20 June (18:00) and 27 June (17:30); Mehedinți was placed in Region 4 West alongside Arad, Caraș-Severin, Hunedoara, Timiș and Gorj ([FRF](https://www.frf-ajf.ro/upload/sm/files/Regulament organizare baraj liga 3 pentru 2026 2027.pdf)). CS Drobeta won its tie in late June 2026 and was promoted to Liga III for the 2026-27 season — a fact confirmed by the club's presence in that season's Group 6, where it sat top of the table after five matches with 4 wins, 1 draw and 13 points ([Sportradar](https://sportradar.com/?q=4200675429247460090)).

With promotion sealed, the club changed head coach. Transfermarkt lists Flavius Stoican as CS Drobeta manager from 16 July 2026, succeeding debut-season coach Florin Șoavă ([Transfermarkt](https://www.transfermarkt.co.in/flavius-stoican/profil/trainer/22654), [Sport.ro](https://www.sport.ro/fotbal-intern/sunt-doua-echipe-cu-acelasi-nume-chiar-si-la-drobeta-turnu-severin-una-dintre-ele-este-antrenata-de-florin-soava.html)). The appointment was an ambitious one: Stoican holds a UEFA Pro licence and had finished a Liga I spell with FCV Farul on 2 June 2026, so he took over a newly promoted third-tier club just six weeks after leaving the top flight ([Transfermarkt](https://www.transfermarkt.co.in/flavius-stoican/profil/trainer/22654)).

## Two clubs, one city

The municipal project shares its city — and a near-identical name — with CS Drobeta 2024, a separate, privately run club that also competes in Mehedinți's county football ([Sport.ro](https://www.sport.ro/fotbal-intern/sunt-doua-echipe-cu-acelasi-nume-chiar-si-la-drobeta-turnu-severin-una-dintre-ele-este-antrenata-de-florin-soava.html)). Independent reporting distinguishes the two by their funding and ownership structures rather than their badges ([Sport.ro](https://www.sport.ro/fotbal-intern/sunt-doua-echipe-cu-acelasi-nume-chiar-si-la-drobeta-turnu-severin-una-dintre-ele-este-antrenata-de-florin-soava.html)). Official county records list this club's foundation year as 2025, which is why this profile treats it as a new entity rather than a continuation of the city's earlier clubs, including the FC Drobeta-Turnu Severin of 1958-2011 and the CS Turnu Severin of 2007-2013 ([ProSport](https://liga2.prosport.ro/liga-4/cs-drobeta-este-noua-campioana-judeteana-de-la-mehedinti-19389737)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile deliberately limits its claims to the club's 2025 founding, its 2025-2026 debut season, and its 2026 promotion. It does not assert any continuity between this club and Drobeta-Turnu Severin's earlier, separately constituted football clubs, and it does not independently confirm the club's ownership structure, budget, or current playing squad. The exact leg scores of the June 2026 promotion playoff are not stated here: search-indexed sources agree on a 3-2 aggregate win over the Timiș County champions, but no such report was read in full for this pass, and the profile claims only what is independently established — that the playoff was won and the club entered Liga III ([FRF](https://www.frf-ajf.ro/upload/sm/files/Regulament organizare baraj liga 3 pentru 2026 2027.pdf), [Sportradar](https://sportradar.com/?q=4200675429247460090)). The season record figures of 24 wins, 1 draw and a 168-15 goal difference reflect reporting dated 1 June 2026, before the final round of fixtures; final-table figures for the completed season were not re-verified from a full AJF standings page. The club's 2026-27 Cupa României regional-stage results were likewise not independently verified and are not reported here.
