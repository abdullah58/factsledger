## Overview

TrendForce is a Taipei-based technology market-intelligence firm that tracks the semiconductor, display, LED, green-energy and AI-infrastructure supply chains and sells the resulting forecasts to businesses and investors ([Digit.in](https://www.digit.in/news/mobile-phones/huawei-to-overtake-apple-and-become-second-largest-smartphone-maker-in-2019-trendforce-45839.html)). Founded in 2000 as DRAMeXchange, a price-tracking service for the DRAM memory market, it grew by acquisition into five research divisions and is now one of the most frequently cited sources of chip-market data in the global tech press ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200120005101/en/TrendForce-Strengthens-the-Knowledge-Economy-Through-Technological-Innovation-as-It-Celebrates-20-Years-of-Continued-Excellence)). Its current footprint spans Taipei, Shenzhen, Silicon Valley, New York and Tokyo, and its research now covers the AI-server build-out — including the nine largest cloud providers, among them [Baidu](/organizations/baidu/) ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/about)).

## From DRAM prices to a five-division research house

TrendForce began in 2000 when founder and CEO Kevin Lin launched DRAMeXchange, a business-to-business service tracking DRAM prices and supply ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200120005101/en/TrendForce-Strengthens-the-Knowledge-Economy-Through-Technological-Innovation-as-It-Celebrates-20-Years-of-Continued-Excellence)). The firm expanded division by division: WitsView for display research in 2004, LEDinside for LEDs in 2007, and EnergyTrend for green energy in 2010, while extending its presence into [China](/places/china/) with service points in Shenzhen and Beijing from 2004 ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/about)). In 2006 it acquired the LCD research institute WitsView outright to deepen its display coverage, and in January 2015 it became the majority shareholder of Topology Research Institute — founded in 1996 — taking over its operating and management rights, with Lin becoming Topology's chairman ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200120005101/en/TrendForce-Strengthens-the-Knowledge-Economy-Through-Technological-Innovation-as-It-Celebrates-20-Years-of-Continued-Excellence)). The acquisition made TrendForce Taiwan's largest private technology research organization at the time.

The firm's model is supply-chain fieldwork converted into priced forecasts. Its analysts survey component suppliers and device makers, then publish contract-price, shipment and capacity projections that traders, manufacturers and journalists treat as market signals. By its 20th anniversary in 2020 it reported a team of more than 200 experts based in Greater China and a subscriber base of 500,000 across the technology and financial-services sectors ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200120005101/en/TrendForce-Strengthens-the-Knowledge-Economy-Through-Technological-Innovation-as-It-Celebrates-20-Years-of-Continued-Excellence)). In 2017 it added an AI arm, Prophet, an AI-solutions company built on its twenty years of industrial data to serve enterprise digital transformation ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200120005101/en/TrendForce-Strengthens-the-Knowledge-Economy-Through-Technological-Innovation-as-It-Celebrates-20-Years-of-Continued-Excellence)).

## The forecaster the tech press quotes

TrendForce's public visibility comes less from its own brand than from how often its numbers are quoted. In January 2019 it forecast global smartphone production of 1.41 billion units for the year, down 3.3 percent, and predicted Huawei would overtake Apple as the world's second-largest smartphone maker while Samsung stayed first — a call the trade press carried verbatim ([Digit.in](https://www.digit.in/news/mobile-phones/huawei-to-overtake-apple-and-become-second-largest-smartphone-maker-in-2019-trendforce-45839.html)). That pattern — a specific number, a ranking, and an implication — is the firm's signature: a stat, its source, and what it means for the market.

The AI-infrastructure boom made its forecasts more prominent still. In August 2026 TrendForce revised its 2026 AI-server shipment growth forecast upward from 28 percent to nearly 31 percent year on year, and estimated that the combined 2026 capital expenditure of the world's nine largest cloud providers — [Google](/organizations/google/), [Amazon](/organizations/amazon/), [Meta](/organizations/meta/), [Microsoft](/organizations/microsoft/), Oracle, ByteDance, [Tencent](/organizations/tencent/), Alibaba and Baidu — would exceed US$886.7 billion, up about 90 percent year on year, reaching about US$1.3 trillion in 2027 ([Evertiq](https://Evertiq.com/news/2026-08-05-ai-server-shipments-forecast-raised-to-31-yoy-in-2026)). In October 2026 it projected server CPU shipments would grow 31 percent in 2026 and more than 40 percent in 2027, as AI inference workloads push the CPU-to-GPU ratio from about 1:8 toward 1:2 or even 1:1, with Arm-based processors taking about 30 percent of the server CPU market in 2027 ([EE Times Asia](https://www.eetasia.com/trendforce-forecasts-server-cpu-shipments-to-rise-more-than-40-in-2027/)).

## The Baidu connection

TrendForce's reporting also documents the capital-market side of China's AI chip industry. In May 2026 it reported, citing EE Times China, that Baidu's chip unit [Kunlunxin](/organizations/kunlunxin/) had launched its STAR Market listing guidance process on 7 May 2026, advancing a dual listing in Shanghai and [Hong Kong](/places/hong-kong/), and noted, citing Jiemian, that Baidu holds a 57.67 percent stake in Kunlunxin ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/05/08/news-baidu-chip-unit-kunlunxin-reportedly-launches-star-market-ipo-process-hk-listing-valuation-seen-near-hk100b/)). The report placed Kunlunxin in a wider wave of Chinese AI-chip listings, with MetaX and Moore Threads listed on the STAR Market in December 2025 and Biren and Iluvatar CoreX listed in Hong Kong ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/05/08/news-baidu-chip-unit-kunlunxin-reportedly-launches-star-market-ipo-process-hk-listing-valuation-seen-near-hk100b/)).
