## Overview

DataDome is the cybersecurity company built by two founders who had already sold a company that watched the web — and then decided to defend it. Founded in 2015 by Benjamin Fabre and Fabien Grenier, the New York-headquartered firm sells an AI-driven platform that detects and blocks malicious bots and online fraud in real time across websites, mobile apps and APIs ([SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/anti-bot-software-firm-datadome-banks-42m-financing/); [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/datadome-raises-35m-to-defend-ecommerce-from-bot-attacks)). It has raised about $81 million in venture funding and grown from point bot-defense into a broader cyberfraud platform — adding an invisible CAPTCHA alternative, account-takeover protection and ad-fraud defense ([SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/anti-bot-software-firm-datadome-banks-42m-financing/); [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2024/03/20/datadome-account-protect/)).

## The founders who switched sides

DataDome was the third company Fabre and Grenier founded together. Their previous venture, TrendyBuzz, was a social-media-monitoring company — software that watched and analyzed web activity at scale — which Linkfluence acquired in 2014 ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/datadome-raises-35m-to-defend-ecommerce-from-bot-attacks)). The founders soon realized that hackers were using similar techniques to build bad bots and attack digital commerce businesses, and that easily deployable solutions to combat those attacks barely existed ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/datadome-raises-35m-to-defend-ecommerce-from-bot-attacks)). DataDome was their answer: a SaaS platform whose bot-detection engine compares every request to a website against an in-memory pattern database, using stacked supervised, unsupervised and semi-supervised models to decide in under two milliseconds whether access should be granted ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/datadome-raises-35m-to-defend-ecommerce-from-bot-attacks)). By 2021 the engine was processing more than a trillion pieces of data a day from 25 points of presence; by 2023 the company said it analyzed 3 trillion data points daily and protected more than 300 enterprises, including Reddit, Patreon and AngelList ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/datadome-raises-35m-to-defend-ecommerce-from-bot-attacks); [Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/fraud-prevention-ai-startup-datadome-bags-42m-to-stop-bot-driven-cyberattacks/)).

The timing was pandemic-driven. With e-commerce surging, 71% of companies reported an increase in successful bot attacks, e-commerce seeing the largest increase of any segment — and DataDome's own research found a 47% increase in bot fraud attacks over six months ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/datadome-raises-35m-to-defend-ecommerce-from-bot-attacks)). The $35 million Series B led by Elephant in May 2021 (with ISAI participating) brought total funding to nearly $40 million; CEO Grenier reported $15 million in ARR and expected it to double within twelve months, with customers including Axel Springer, AngelList Talent, Australia Post and Foot Locker ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/datadome-raises-35m-to-defend-ecommerce-from-bot-attacks)).

## From bot defense to fraud platform

The $42 million Series C in March 2023, led by InfraVia Growth with Elephant and ISAI participating, funded what the company called its mission "to rid the web of bot-driven cyberattacks and fraud" — and a deliberate product expansion beyond core bot protection ([SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/anti-bot-software-firm-datadome-banks-42m-financing/); [Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/fraud-prevention-ai-startup-datadome-bags-42m-to-stop-bot-driven-cyberattacks/)). InfraVia partner Guillaume Santamaria framed the thesis as "bot management as a foundation for fighting online fraud," endorsing the team's vision of expanding from bots into fraud more broadly ([Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/fraud-prevention-ai-startup-datadome-bags-42m-to-stop-bot-driven-cyberattacks/)). DataDome said it had stopped over 250 billion online fraud attempts in real time in 2022 ([Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/fraud-prevention-ai-startup-datadome-bags-42m-to-stop-bot-driven-cyberattacks/)).

The expansion came in three products. In December 2023 DataDome announced Device Check, described as an "invisible challenge" — an alternative to CAPTCHAs that validates device-specific signals with proofs of work behind the scenes, without any visible interaction; an early-access luxury retailer saw detection accuracy improve and the false-positive rate fall to 0.001%, with fewer than 1 in 10,000 human requests challenged ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2023/12/12/next-generation-bot-defense-datadomes-device-check-offers-advanced-protection/)). Co-founder and CEO Fabre argued that "CAPTCHAs should never be the first line of defense against bots" ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2023/12/12/next-generation-bot-defense-datadomes-device-check-offers-advanced-protection/)). In March 2024 came Account Protect, targeting account takeovers and fake account creation at login and registration endpoints: analyzing a reported 5 trillion data points per day, it interrogates every request in under 10 milliseconds and can trigger password resets and multi-factor authentication automatically — one luxury brand discovered 75% of its 100,000–300,000 daily sign-ups were fraudulent, with a false-positive rate of 0.00091% ([Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2024/03/20/datadome-account-protect/)). Alongside it launched Ad Protect against ad fraud from inception, plus an expanded bug-bounty program ([Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2024/03/20/datadome-account-protect/)). By then the company counted 180 people from more than 30 nationalities across offices in North America, Europe and Asia ([Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/fraud-prevention-ai-startup-datadome-bags-42m-to-stop-bot-driven-cyberattacks/)).
