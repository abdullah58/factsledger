## Overview

Descript is a San Francisco-based audio and video editing platform founded in 2017 by Andrew Mason, the former CEO of Groupon ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/15/ai-powered-media-editing-app-descript-lands-fresh-cash-from-openai/)). Its defining idea: edit media by editing text. The platform generates an instant transcript of audio or video, and cutting a sentence from the transcript removes it from the recording — "as easy as editing a doc or slides," as Mason put it ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/15/ai-powered-media-editing-app-descript-lands-fresh-cash-from-openai/)).

## Born from a failed walking-tour startup

Descript was not planned — it was discovered. In 2013 Mason was famously fired as Groupon's CEO; his second act, Detour, made podcast-like audio walking tours and required producing hours of spoken-word content ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/40506494/groupons-andrew-mason-is-back-with-a-word-processor-for-audio)). Editing those tours was miserable: "trying to edit waveforms… is tedious and requires you to disengage your editorial brain and engage your engineering brain," Mason told [Fast Company](/organizations/fast-company/) ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/40506494/groupons-andrew-mason-is-back-with-a-word-processor-for-audio)). Detour built its own editing tooling to survive — and then Mason spun that technology out into a new company with $5 million in seed funding from Andreessen Horowitz ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/40506494/groupons-andrew-mason-is-back-with-a-word-processor-for-audio)). Descript launched as a Mac app and web service that transcribed audio into written text and let users rearrange or delete the audio through a word-processor-like interface ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/40506494/groupons-andrew-mason-is-back-with-a-word-processor-for-audio)).

## The OpenAI bet

In November 2022 Descript raised $50 million in a Series C led by the OpenAI Startup Fund — only the fund's second investment, after Mem — with Andreessen Horowitz, Redpoint Ventures, Spark Capital and Daniel Gross participating ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/15/ai-powered-media-editing-app-descript-lands-fresh-cash-from-openai/)). The round brought total funding to $100 million; The Information reported a valuation around $550 million, more than double the $260 million figure from January 2021, though Mason declined to confirm it ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/15/ai-powered-media-editing-app-descript-lands-fresh-cash-from-openai/)). Alongside the raise, Descript unveiled its Storyboard redesign — background removal, multitrack screen recording, stock media — and its Overdub voice-cloning feature, powered by the 2019 acquisition of the Canadian AI company Lyrebird ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/15/ai-powered-media-editing-app-descript-lands-fresh-cash-from-openai/)). By 2021 its customers already included NPR, VICE, The Washington Post and The New York Times ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/15/ai-powered-media-editing-app-descript-lands-fresh-cash-from-openai/)).
