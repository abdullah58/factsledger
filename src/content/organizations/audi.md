## Overview

Audi AG is a German luxury automaker headquartered in Ingolstadt, Bavaria, and a subsidiary of the Volkswagen Group since 1965. Founded in Zwickau in 1909 by engineer August Horch — "Audi" is Latin for "horch", German for "listen" — it is best known for quattro permanent all-wheel drive, its four-ring emblem, and the slogan "Vorsprung durch Technik" ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/company-history-17227)). In 2024 the Audi Group earned €64.5 billion in revenue and delivered 1,671,218 Audi-brand vehicles, including 164,480 fully electric cars, while the wider Brand Group Progressive — Audi, Bentley, Lamborghini and Ducati — delivered 1,692,548 vehicles ([Volkswagen Group](https://www.volkswagen-group.com/en/articles/fiscal-year-2024-audi-pushes-ahead-with-transformation-and-model-initiative-19084)).

## Four rings: from Horch to Auto Union

Audi's story starts twice. August Horch founded A. Horch & Cie. in Cologne on 14 November 1899, moved the business to Zwickau, and left it after a dispute — then founded a second car company in 1909, taking the Latin word for his own name because he was barred from using "Horch" again ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/company-history-17227)). The first Audi-branded vehicle was delivered in May 1910, and Audi won the Austrian Alpine Rally three years running, 1912–1914 ([Audi MediaCenter](https://uploads.audi-mediacenter.com/system/production/uploaded_files/24123/file/8f33886885fa55d408bf7577841fbfce0680828f/AUDI_AG_Historie_Kurzversion_EN.pdf?1691658833&)). In 1928 the company was bought by Jörgen Skafte Rasmussen, owner of DKW, and on 29 June 1932 Audi, DKW, Horch and the car division of Wanderer merged to form Auto Union AG, Germany's second-largest motor vehicle manufacturer at the time ([Audi MediaCenter](https://uploads.audi-mediacenter.com/system/production/uploaded_files/24123/file/8f33886885fa55d408bf7577841fbfce0680828f/AUDI_AG_Historie_Kurzversion_EN.pdf?1691658833&)).

The four interlocking rings are that merger drawn as a logo: one ring for each founding company, each assigned its own market segment — DKW small cars and motorcycles, Wanderer midsize cars, Audi upper-midsize cars, and Horch luxury cars ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/company-history-17227)). The Second World War ended Auto Union's first incarnation: Audi's own history records about 17,300 forced labourers and 3,700 prisoners from Flossenbürg satellite camps working for the company, which was expropriated after the war and deleted from the Chemnitz commercial register in 1948 ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/company-history-17227)). Managers who fled west re-established Auto Union GmbH in Ingolstadt on 3 September 1949, and the company passed through two owners — [Daimler](/organizations/daimler/)-Benz, which took 88 per cent of the shares in April 1958, and Volkswagenwerk AG, which took over in 1965 and revived the Audi name on its first post-war four-stroke car ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/company-history-17227)). The 1969 merger with NSU created AUDI NSU AUTO UNION AG, renamed AUDI AG in 1985, with headquarters moved from Neckarsulm to Ingolstadt ([Audi MediaCenter](https://uploads.audi-mediacenter.com/system/production/uploaded_files/24123/file/8f33886885fa55d408bf7577841fbfce0680828f/AUDI_AG_Historie_Kurzversion_EN.pdf?1691658833&)).

## quattro and the climb to premium

Audi's defining technology was born in the snow. During winter tests in Sweden in 1977, engineers noticed that a Volkswagen Iltis military off-roader easily outperformed their front-wheel-drive prototypes, and Audi spent years building a lighter, road-car all-wheel-drive system around a secondary shaft sending half the engine's power to each axle through a manually-locking centre differential ([Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/news/audi-quattro-40-years-anniversary-history)). The Audi quattro, a 200-horsepower five-cylinder turbo coupé, was unveiled at the Geneva Motor Show in March 1980, and its permanent all-wheel drive carried Audi to World Rally Championship manufacturers' titles in 1982 and 1984 and drivers' titles in 1983 (Hannu Mikkola) and 1984 (Stig Blomqvist) ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/company-history-17227)). Michèle Mouton's Sanremo win made her the first woman to win a World Championship rally event, and Audi collected 23 WRC wins through 1985 ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/company-history-17227); [Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/news/audi-quattro-40-years-anniversary-history)).

Rivals copied the formula: [BMW](/organizations/bmw/) offered all-wheel drive on the 325i in 1985 and Mercedes-Benz introduced 4Matic in 1987 ([Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/news/audi-quattro-40-years-anniversary-history)). Audi kept the technology at the centre of its identity — the 1986 ski-jump commercial, in which rally driver Harald Demuth drove a 100 CS quattro up an 80%-gradient jump in Finland, made quattro famous beyond motorsport, and Audi had built nearly 11 million all-wheel-drive cars by 2020 ([Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/news/audi-quattro-40-years-anniversary-history)). The system survived electrification: the 2018 e-tron was Audi's first fully electric car, delivering quattro through one motor per axle instead of clutches and differentials ([Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/news/audi-quattro-40-years-anniversary-history)). On track, Audi won the 24 Hours of Le Mans 13 times between 2000 and 2014, including the first diesel-powered winner and later a hybrid ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/company-history-17227)).

## Audi today: the Brand Group Progressive

Audi no longer reports as a single car brand. Its financials cover the Brand Group Progressive — Audi, Bentley, Lamborghini and Ducati — and the 2024 figures show a group under pressure: revenue of €64.5 billion, down 7.6 per cent, operating profit of €3.9 billion at a 6.0 per cent margin, and net cash flow of €3.1 billion ([Volkswagen Group](https://www.volkswagen-group.com/en/articles/fiscal-year-2024-audi-pushes-ahead-with-transformation-and-model-initiative-19084)). Audi-brand deliveries fell 11.8 per cent to 1,671,218, including 164,480 fully electric cars, while operating profit absorbed restructuring expenses from ending production at the Brussels site ([Volkswagen Group](https://www.volkswagen-group.com/en/articles/fiscal-year-2024-audi-pushes-ahead-with-transformation-and-model-initiative-19084)). The group employed 88,563 people at the end of 2024, and its largest markets were Europe (670,859 deliveries), China including Hong Kong (653,016) and the USA (202,969) ([Audi AG](https://emea-dam.audi.com/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:e82f145e-7f3e-4e8b-b9bf-482b11f03d9b/original/as/audi-quarterly-update-q4-2024.pdf)).

Gernot Döllner has chaired the Board of Management since 1 September 2023 and was confirmed for a further five years in autumn 2025; he also chairs Audi Motorsport AG, which Audi took over for its planned Formula 1 entry in 2026 ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/biographies/gernot-doellner-chairman-of-the-board-of-management-15562)).

## The voice in the car: Cerence and ChatGPT

Audi's longest-running technology partnership is the one passengers talk to. From July 2024, owners of around two million Audi models built since 2021 with the third-generation MIB 3 infotainment system can use ChatGPT-enhanced voice control, delivered through Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service ([Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news-releases/audi-enhances-voice-control-in-current-and-future-models-with-chatgpt/)). Newer models such as the Q6 e-tron, built on the E3 1.2 electronics architecture, receive ChatGPT through [Cerence](/organizations/cerence/) Chat Pro, an extension of the Audi assistant that drivers wake with "Hey Audi" ([Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news-releases/audi-enhances-voice-control-in-current-and-future-models-with-chatgpt/)). The assistant answers vehicle functions, navigation and weather itself and only forwards general-knowledge questions to ChatGPT; Audi says all questions and answers are deleted after processing and ChatGPT never has access to vehicle data ([Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news-releases/audi-enhances-voice-control-in-current-and-future-models-with-chatgpt/)). The point of the system, in Audi's framing, is safety as much as convenience: drivers ask in natural language instead of taking their eyes off the road ([Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news-releases/audi-enhances-voice-control-in-current-and-future-models-with-chatgpt/)).

## Dieselgate: the conviction at the top

Audi's modern history includes the industry's largest scandal. Former chief executive Rupert Stadler pleaded guilty in a Munich court on 16 May 2023 to charges tied to the diesel emissions scandal, becoming the highest-ranking executive convicted over cars that cheated emissions tests with illegal software ([Associated Press](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/16920021/ex-audi-chief-pleads-guilty-in-automakers-diesel-emissions-scandal)). He admitted failing to keep rigged cars off the market after the scandal became public, and his plea deal brought probation and a €1.1 million fine; prosecutors had charged him with fraud and false certification for letting cars with rigged software be sold after September 2015, when the US Environmental Protection Agency issued a Clean Air Act notice of violation ([Associated Press](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/16920021/ex-audi-chief-pleads-guilty-in-automakers-diesel-emissions-scandal)). The software switched emission controls on during laboratory tests and off on the road, and the scandal cost Volkswagen more than $30 billion in fines and settlements — while pushing the whole industry away from diesel and accelerating the move into electric vehicles ([Associated Press](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/16920021/ex-audi-chief-pleads-guilty-in-automakers-diesel-emissions-scandal)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What does the name Audi mean?**
Audi is the Latin translation of the German word "horch", meaning "listen" — the surname of founder August Horch, who could not use his own name for his second company after leaving his first ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/company-history-17227)).

**Who owns Audi?**
Audi is part of the Volkswagen Group, which took over Auto Union in 1965 and revived the Audi brand on its first post-war four-stroke car; Gernot Döllner has chaired Audi's Board of Management since 1 September 2023 ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/company-history-17227)).

**What do Audi's four rings stand for?**
One ring for each of the four companies that merged into Auto Union AG on 29 June 1932: Audi, DKW, Horch and Wanderer, each originally assigned its own market segment ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/company-history-17227)).

**Does Audi use Cerence technology in its cars?**
Yes. Audi's voice assistant uses Cerence technology, and newer models receive ChatGPT through Cerence Chat Pro, an extension of the Audi assistant that handles general-knowledge questions the car cannot answer itself ([Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news-releases/audi-enhances-voice-control-in-current-and-future-models-with-chatgpt/)).

**Is Audi going into Formula 1?**
Audi took over Audi Motorsport AG for a planned Formula 1 entry in 2026, with chief executive Gernot Döllner chairing the motorsport company ([Audi AG](https://www.audi.com/en/biographies/gernot-doellner-chairman-of-the-board-of-management-15562)).
