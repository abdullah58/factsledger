## Overview

Anysphere, Inc. is the company behind Cursor, the AI code editor that became one of the defining products of the "vibe coding" era — where developers describe what they want in natural language and AI handles much of the coding. Founded in 2022 by four MIT graduates — Michael Truell, Sualeh Asif, Aman Sanger and Arvid Lunnemark — it rose from a startup to a $29.3 billion valuation in three years, then became the target of the largest startup acquisition on record: SpaceX's $60 billion all-stock purchase in 2026 ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/new-updates/who-is-aman-sanger-how-the-25-year-old-indian-origin-entrepreneur-became-one-of-the-youngest-billionaires-after-elon-musks-spacex-acquired-his-ai-startup-cursor-for-60-billion/articleshow/133704665.cms)).

## The identity: Anysphere, Inc. ("Cursor")

In SpaceX's regulatory filings, the company is named exactly once: "Anysphere, Inc. ('Cursor')" — the parent company behind the Cursor product ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026043411/spaceexplorationtechnologi.htm)). The distinction matters because most public discussion uses "Cursor" for both the company and the product. The founders' early work explored different AI applications, including computer-aided design, before they focused on the core problem — programmers constantly switching between their editor and separate AI chatbots — and brought AI directly into the coding environment ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/new-updates/who-is-aman-sanger-how-the-25-year-old-indian-origin-entrepreneur-became-one-of-the-youngest-billionaires-after-elon-musks-spacex-acquired-his-ai-startup-cursor-for-60-billion/articleshow/133704665.cms)).

## The $29.3 billion Series D

In November 2025, Anysphere closed a $2.3 billion Series D — one of the largest venture rounds of the year — setting a $29.3 billion post-money valuation, more than triple its June valuation just six months earlier. The backer list reads like a map of the AI power structure: Accel, Thrive Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, DST Global and Coatue alongside strategic investors Nvidia and Google ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-defense-tech-anysphere/)). The company was reportedly used by engineering teams across 64% of the Fortune 500 ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/new-updates/who-is-aman-sanger-how-the-25-year-old-indian-origin-entrepreneur-became-one-of-the-youngest-billionaires-after-elon-musks-spacex-acquired-his-ai-startup-cursor-for-60-billion/articleshow/133704665.cms)).

## The SpaceX acquisition — verified from the filing

In April 2026, SpaceX and Anysphere announced a collaboration that included an option: SpaceX could acquire the company for $60 billion or pay $10 billion for the partnership alone ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/new-updates/who-is-aman-sanger-how-the-25-year-old-indian-origin-entrepreneur-became-one-of-the-youngest-billionaires-after-elon-musks-spacex-acquired-his-ai-startup-cursor-for-60-billion/articleshow/133704665.cms)). On June 16, 2026, SpaceX signed the Agreement and Plan of Merger: subsidiary X67 Inc. would merge into Anysphere, with Anysphere surviving as a wholly owned SpaceX subsidiary. Every Anysphere share converts into the right to receive SpaceX Class A common stock (ticker SPCX on Nasdaq), priced at the seven-day volume-weighted average before closing, based on an implied equity value of $60.0 billion ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026043411/spaceexplorationtechnologi.htm)). SpaceX expected the deal to close in Q3 2026, subject to regulatory approvals.

Per Forbes reporting cited by the Economic Times, the four founders each held roughly 4.5% of Anysphere — stakes worth about $2.7 billion apiece at the $60 billion valuation ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/new-updates/who-is-aman-sanger-how-the-25-year-old-indian-origin-entrepreneur-became-one-of-the-youngest-billionaires-after-elon-musks-spacex-acquired-his-ai-startup-cursor-for-60-billion/articleshow/133704665.cms)).
