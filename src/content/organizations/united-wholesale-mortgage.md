## Overview

United Wholesale Mortgage (UWM) is the largest mortgage lender in the [United States](/places/united-states/), and it reached the top without ever lending directly to a homebuyer: it underwrites loans only through independent mortgage brokers, from its Pontiac, Michigan headquarters, as the operating company of the New York Stock Exchange-listed UWM Holdings Corporation (NYSE: UWMC) ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221104005189/en/UWM-Surpasses-Rocket-Mortgage-as-the-1-Overall-Mortgage-Lender)). The company began in 1986, when lawyer Jeff Ishbia founded the 12-person Shore Mortgage, and his son Mat Ishbia joined in 2003 as the 12th team member, became president in 2009 and chief executive in 2013 ([Detroit Jewish News](https://www.thejewishnews.com/news/business/mat-ishbia-s-hot-hand-from-a-motivated-basketball-competitor-to-a-mortgage-industry-giant/article_93cd3eca-1ef6-5c46-a96b-c6ecebb02796.html); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221104005189/en/UWM-Surpasses-Rocket-Mortgage-as-the-1-Overall-Mortgage-Lender)). Under him UWM became the country's biggest wholesale lender in 2015 and its biggest lender overall in 2022, originating $163.4 billion in loans in 2025 ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221104005189/en/UWM-Surpasses-Rocket-Mortgage-as-the-1-Overall-Mortgage-Lender); [The Mortgage Note](https://www.themortgagenote.org/uwm-announces-2025-results-ishbia-says-they-will-continue-to-dominate/)). This profile is about the Pontiac wholesale lender — not Rocket Mortgage, its Detroit retail rival, and not a retail bank: UWM has no branches and no direct-to-consumer lending at all ([Detroit Jewish News](https://www.thejewishnews.com/news/business/mat-ishbia-s-hot-hand-from-a-motivated-basketball-competitor-to-a-mortgage-industry-giant/article_93cd3eca-1ef6-5c46-a96b-c6ecebb02796.html)).

The wholesale model is the whole business, so the distinction matters. In the retail channel a borrower contacts a lender directly and deals with its own loan officer; in the wholesale channel the borrower hires a broker who is supposed to shop several lenders for the best deal, and the broker — not the borrower — deals with the lender ([U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan](https://business.cch.com/ald/EscuevUnitedWholesaleMortgageLLC1032025.pdf)). UWM's pitch is that the broker works for the borrower; the lawsuits described below argue that UWM's own contracts and incentives turned many of those brokers into something closer to a single lender's sales force.

| | Wholesale channel | Retail channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who the borrower deals with | An independent mortgage broker | The lender's own loan officer |
| Who shops for the loan | The broker, across multiple lenders | The lender, offering only its own products |
| Who the intermediary serves | The borrower, in principle | The lender |
| UWM's place in it | Underwrites and funds loans brokers bring; never meets the borrower | UWM does not operate in retail |

## From Shore Mortgage to the largest wholesale lender

Jeff Ishbia, a lawyer, started Shore Mortgage in 1986 as a 12-person company, and his son Mat joined in 2003 after Michigan State, starting as an account executive ([Detroit Jewish News](https://www.thejewishnews.com/news/business/mat-ishbia-s-hot-hand-from-a-motivated-basketball-competitor-to-a-mortgage-industry-giant/article_93cd3eca-1ef6-5c46-a96b-c6ecebb02796.html); [National Mortgage Professional](https://nationalmortgageprofessional.com/news/24621/legends-lending-united-wholesale-mortgage)). Mat Ishbia became president in 2009, when UWM ranked 75th among wholesale lenders, and was appointed president and chief executive in 2013 ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221104005189/en/UWM-Surpasses-Rocket-Mortgage-as-the-1-Overall-Mortgage-Lender); [UWM](https://res.cloudinary.com/uwm/image/upload/v1764016982/UWM.com/Documents/Misc/UWMHistoryInfographic-v3.pdf)). He built the company on two bets: never offering subprime loans, which left it strong as a Federal Housing Administration specialist, and moving all account executives in-house, so brokers dealt with trained inside staff rather than travelling salespeople ([HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/34621-interview-mat-ishbia-ceo-of-united-shore-financial-services/)). As the biggest banks left the wholesale market, UWM filled the gap, became the #1 wholesale lender in 2015, dropped retail lending in 2014 to focus exclusively on wholesale, and moved its headquarters to Pontiac in 2018 ([National Mortgage Professional](https://nationalmortgageprofessional.com/news/24621/legends-lending-united-wholesale-mortgage); [UWM](https://res.cloudinary.com/uwm/image/upload/v1764016982/UWM.com/Documents/Misc/UWMHistoryInfographic-v3.pdf); [Detroit Jewish News](https://www.thejewishnews.com/news/business/mat-ishbia-s-hot-hand-from-a-motivated-basketball-competitor-to-a-mortgage-industry-giant/article_93cd3eca-1ef6-5c46-a96b-c6ecebb02796.html)). Ishbia named [Wells Fargo](/organizations/wells-fargo/) among the giants he was competing against with far less money — and said he would outwork them instead ([Detroit Jewish News](https://www.thejewishnews.com/news/business/mat-ishbia-s-hot-hand-from-a-motivated-basketball-competitor-to-a-mortgage-industry-giant/article_93cd3eca-1ef6-5c46-a96b-c6ecebb02796.html)).

## The SPAC listing, the race past Rocket, and the Suns

UWM went public on 22 January 2021 by merging with Gores Holdings IV, a special-purpose acquisition company headed by Alec Gores, in a deal valuing the lender at $16.1 billion — the largest SPAC transaction in history at the time — with the Ishbia family keeping about 94% of the merged company ([HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/uwms-ipo-is-now-scheduled-for-january/); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221104005189/en/UWM-Surpasses-Rocket-Mortgage-as-the-1-Overall-Mortgage-Lender)). In June 2021 Ishbia set the goal of becoming the #1 overall mortgage lender by 2024; in the third quarter of 2022 UWM passed Rocket Mortgage to take the top spot, the first wholesale-only lender ever to do so ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221104005189/en/UWM-Surpasses-Rocket-Mortgage-as-the-1-Overall-Mortgage-Lender)). UWM originated $139.4 billion in loans in 2024 and earned $329.4 million, turning around a $69.8 million loss in 2023 — a scale built entirely through brokers, which is why the company's broker relationships, not branches, decide its market share ([HousingWire](https://preprod.housingwire.com/articles/uwm-q4-2024-earnings-mat-ishbia/)).

### Results, partnerships, and the Suns

In 2025 it originated $163.4 billion, struck a partnership with [Bilt](/organizations/bilt/) backed by at least $100 million of UWM funding, and pursued the acquisition of Two Harbors Investment Corp. to expand its servicing business, while Ishbia welcomed a new [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau](/organizations/consumer-financial-protection-bureau/) director as a "massive win" for the industry ([The Mortgage Note](https://www.themortgagenote.org/uwm-announces-2025-results-ishbia-says-they-will-continue-to-dominate/); [HousingWire](https://preprod.housingwire.com/articles/uwm-q4-2024-earnings-mat-ishbia/)). The listing also made Ishbia a billionaire, and in February 2023 the NBA approved his $4 billion purchase of a majority stake in the Phoenix Suns and the Phoenix Mercury, by a 29–0 vote with the Cleveland Cavaliers abstaining ([Associated Press](https://www.thescore.com/nba/news/2567764)). At $4 billion it was the highest sale ever of an NBA franchise, topping Joe Tsai's $2.35 billion purchase of the Nets in 2019 ([Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/nba/2022/12/20/mat-ishbia-billionaire-mortgage-lender-finalizing-suns-purchase-owner-robert-sarver-report)). Hunterbrook reported the purchase was helped by $4.4 billion in loans from J.P. Morgan collateralised by Ishbia's UWMC shares ([Hunterbrook Media](https://hntrbrk.com/investigations/dear-reader)).

## The broker-loyalty model on trial

In March 2021 UWM told its brokers to choose: keep sending loans to UWM, or keep working with Rocket Mortgage and Fairway Independent Mortgage — not both — under an "All-In" policy that applied even where a competitor offered a cheaper loan, with liquidated damages for breaches ([U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan](https://business.cch.com/ald/EscuevUnitedWholesaleMortgageLLC1032025.pdf)). The policy worked commercially: brokers submitted 17,000 more applications in April 2021 than in February, only 600 of 12,000 brokers refused to sign, and by 2023, of 30,229 loan officers who sent UWM at least one loan, 12,936 directed more than 75% of their loans to it ([U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan](https://business.cch.com/ald/EscuevUnitedWholesaleMortgageLLC1032025.pdf)). Some brokers called the ultimatum a deal breaker that removed their ability to choose the best options for clients, and one called UWM the "anti-broker model" ([U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan](https://business.cch.com/ald/EscuevUnitedWholesaleMortgageLLC1032025.pdf)).

### Hunterbrook, the courts, and Ohio

On 2 April 2024 Hunterbrook Media published an investigation arguing that UWM became the biggest U.S. lender "based on a lie" about broker independence, estimating $39 billion in mortgages came from brokers who sent UWM virtually all their business, with borrowers paying hundreds of millions to billions more in closing costs — while disclosing that its affiliate was short UWMC and long Rocket ([Hunterbrook Media](https://hntrbrk.com/investigations/dear-reader)). A borrower class action filed that day alleged racketeering and consumer-protection violations, but on 30 September 2025 Judge Brandy R. McMillion dismissed the racketeering claims with prejudice, holding the brokers, not UWM, proximately caused the borrowers' injuries, leaving only a narrow RESPA claim for two plaintiffs and a Florida claim for three ([U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan](https://business.cch.com/ald/EscuevUnitedWholesaleMortgageLLC1032025.pdf)). Ohio's attorney general sued in April 2025, alleging UWM issued nearly $605 million in Ohio mortgages via brokers who referred 99% of their business to it; UWM moved to dismiss, calling it a hedge fund's scheme, not an Ohio investigation ([The Mortgage Note](https://www.themortgagenote.org/state-of-ohios-deceptive-business-practices-lawsuit-against-uwm-continues/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is United Wholesale Mortgage?** UWM is a wholesale mortgage lender based in Pontiac, Michigan, that underwrites home loans only through independent mortgage brokers — it has no branches and takes no applications directly from homebuyers ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221104005189/en/UWM-Surpasses-Rocket-Mortgage-as-the-1-Overall-Mortgage-Lender)). Its parent, UWM Holdings Corporation, trades on the New York Stock Exchange as UWMC ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250226975571/en/UWM-Holdings-Corporation-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-Full-Year-2024-Results/)).

**Who runs UWM, and who owns it?** Mat Ishbia is chairman, president and chief executive, having joined in 2003 as the 12th team member and become president in 2009 ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221104005189/en/UWM-Surpasses-Rocket-Mortgage-as-the-1-Overall-Mortgage-Lender)). His father Jeff Ishbia founded the business in 1986 as Shore Mortgage ([Detroit Jewish News](https://www.thejewishnews.com/news/business/mat-ishbia-s-hot-hand-from-a-motivated-basketball-competitor-to-a-mortgage-industry-giant/article_93cd3eca-1ef6-5c46-a96b-c6ecebb02796.html)). After the 2021 listing, the Ishbia family retained about 94% of the merged company ([HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/uwms-ipo-is-now-scheduled-for-january/)).

**Is UWM really the largest mortgage lender in America?** Yes, by origination volume: it passed Rocket Mortgage in the third quarter of 2022 and has been the largest wholesale lender since 2015 ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20221104005189/en/UWM-Surpasses-Rocket-Mortgage-as-the-1-Overall-Mortgage-Lender)). It originated $139.4 billion in 2024 and $163.4 billion in 2025, and HousingWire called it "the largest originator in America" ([HousingWire](https://preprod.housingwire.com/articles/uwm-q4-2024-earnings-mat-ishbia/); [The Mortgage Note](https://www.themortgagenote.org/uwm-announces-2025-results-ishbia-says-they-will-continue-to-dominate/)).

**What happened to the lawsuits saying brokers were steered to UWM?** The April 2024 borrower class action largely failed: in September 2025 a judge dismissed the racketeering claims, finding the brokers, not UWM, caused the injuries, leaving only a narrow RESPA claim and a Florida claim ([U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan](https://business.cch.com/ald/EscuevUnitedWholesaleMortgageLLC1032025.pdf)). Ohio's separate April 2025 suit was unresolved in late 2025 ([The Mortgage Note](https://www.themortgagenote.org/state-of-ohios-deceptive-business-practices-lawsuit-against-uwm-continues/)).

**Has UWM faced criticism of its workplace culture?** Yes.
