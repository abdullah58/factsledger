## Overview

Monday.com Ltd. (styled lowercase "monday.com") is a Tel Aviv work-management software company that sells a cloud platform — a "Work OS" — on which teams build the tools to run their work, from project tracking to sales pipelines and IT service desks ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article.aspx?did=1001373994)). Founded in 2012 as dapulse by Roy Mann and Eran Zinman, it listed on [Nasdaq](/organizations/nasdaq/) in June 2021 at a $6.8 billion valuation, passed $1 billion in annual recurring revenue in August 2024, and reported $1,232.0 million of revenue for fiscal 2025, up 27% year over year ([Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3909641,00.html); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1845338/000117891324002760/exhibit_99-1.htm); [monday.com Investor Relations](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx)).

The company is the direct competitor named in the [ClickUp](/organizations/clickup/) profile's own competitive context: another all-in-one productivity platform that grew up in the same 2021 funding vintage and is now pushing upmarket with its own AI features ([UC Today](https://uctoday.com/monday-com-q4-and-fy2025-earnings-revenue-tops-1-2-billion-as-ai-products-take-centre-stage)). What sets monday.com apart in its own telling is who its customers are: by 2018 roughly 70% of them were non-technology businesses — architecture firms, restaurants, manufacturers — and its founders argued the real competition was not other software but Excel files and whiteboards ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2018/07/11/monday-com-raises-50m-at-a-500m-valuation-led-by-stripes-for-its-workplace-collaboration-tools/)).

## From a Wix tool to dapulse

Monday.com did not start in a garage but inside another Israeli software company. The software began around 2010 as an internal development tool at Wix.com, the DIY website builder based in [Israel](/places/israel/), and was spun off as a separate startup a couple of years later, raising $1.5 million in seed funding ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/monday.com-ipo%3A-what-you-need-to-know-2021-06-08)). Wix was the product's first client and, in co-founder Roy Mann's account, the impetus for starting the company — some of the DIY-design ethos of building tools for non-technical users carried over into the product ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2018/07/11/monday-com-raises-50m-at-a-500m-valuation-led-by-stripes-for-its-workplace-collaboration-tools/)).

The company was founded in 2012 as dapulse, and the product launched commercially in 2014 ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article.aspx?did=1001373994); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/dapulse-becomes-mondaycom-to-reflect-their-vision-to-solve-work-challenges-for-all-teams-300554473.html)). One wrinkle in the record: TechCrunch's 2019 funding coverage says the company "first started life as Dapulse in 2014" — that is the launch year, not the founding year, and this profile uses 2012 as the founding date, with 2014 as the commercial launch.

On November 13, 2017, dapulse announced it was renaming itself monday.com, effective immediately, with the new name rolling out across the platform through the rest of the year ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/dapulse-becomes-mondaycom-to-reflect-their-vision-to-solve-work-challenges-for-all-teams-300554473.html)). Mann's explanation was that he had "never agreed with the term 'Project Management'" — "it isn't really projects that we're managing, it's people" — and the new name was meant to capture a broader mission of improving how people work together ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/dapulse-becomes-mondaycom-to-reflect-their-vision-to-solve-work-challenges-for-all-teams-300554473.html)). At the rebrand the company had over 18,000 paying teams in more than 140 countries and had raised $34.1 million ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/dapulse-becomes-mondaycom-to-reflect-their-vision-to-solve-work-challenges-for-all-teams-300554473.html)).

## The funding ladder and the IPO

The rebrand preceded a steep funding climb. In July 2018 monday.com raised a $50 million Series C led by Stripes Group, with Insight Venture Partners and Entrée Capital participating and LeumiTech extending a credit line, at what sources close to the company put at a ~$500 million pre-money valuation — bringing total funding to $84.1 million ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2018/07/11/monday-com-raises-50m-at-a-500m-valuation-led-by-stripes-for-its-workplace-collaboration-tools/)). A year later, in July 2019, came a $150 million Series D led by Sapphire Ventures, with Hamilton Lane, HarbourVest Partners, ION Crossover Partners and Vintage Investment Partners, at a $1.9 billion valuation — nearly four times the prior round — bringing total funding to $234.1 million ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/07/30/monday-com-raises-150m-more-now-at-1-9b-valuation-for-workplace-collaboration-tools/)). Customers had more than doubled in that year, from 35,000 to 80,000 organizations, spanning some 200 industry verticals, with clients including Carlsberg, Discovery Channel, Philips, Hulu and WeWork ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/07/30/monday-com-raises-150m-more-now-at-1-9b-valuation-for-workplace-collaboration-tools/)). Mann told TechCrunch an IPO was under consideration and that the Series D could be the last round before a listing ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/07/30/monday-com-raises-150m-more-now-at-1-9b-valuation-for-workplace-collaboration-tools/)).

The IPO itself, on June 10, 2021, priced at $155 per share — above the $125–140 range — with 3.7 million new shares raising $574 million, plus a further $57.4 million if underwriters exercised their option, at a $6.8 billion valuation ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article.aspx?did=1001373994)). [Goldman Sachs](/organizations/goldman-sachs/) and [JPMorgan Chase](/organizations/jpmorgan-chase/) led the underwriting, and [Salesforce](/organizations/salesforce/) Ventures and Zoom Video Communications each committed to buy $75 million of shares in a concurrent private placement ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article.aspx?did=1001373994); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3909641,00.html)). The shares rose 15% to $178 on the first day of trading, and Calcalist called it the most lucrative IPO ever by an Israeli company — the previous biggest, Mobileye in 2014, had gone out at $5.3 billion ([Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3909641,00.html)).

The listing also made the ownership public. The largest shareholder was Insight Partners at 42.7%, followed by Sonnipe, part of Entrée Capital and the company's first major investor, at 12.4%; Mann held 15.3% and Zinman 5.6%, while Wix founder and CEO Avishai Abrahami owned 4% ([Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3909641,00.html)). The valuation path behind it was steep: $1.9 billion in the 2019 round, $2.7 billion in a 2020 secondary sale of existing shareholders' stock, then $6.8 billion at the IPO ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article.aspx?did=1001373994)). Going into the listing, 2020 revenue had grown to $161 million from $78.1 million in 2019, against a net loss that widened from $91.6 million to $152 million; first-quarter 2021 revenue was $59 million, up 84.7% year over year ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article.aspx?did=1001373994)).

## From one product to a platform

Monday.com's pitch has always been that it is not a project-management tool but a "work operating system" — a low-code/no-code platform of modular building blocks on which organizations of any size can create the tools and processes they need to manage every aspect of their work ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article.aspx?did=1001373994); [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/monday.com-ipo%3A-what-you-need-to-know-2021-06-08)). By August 2024 that framing had become a multi-product suite: monday work management, monday CRM, monday dev, and monday service (then in beta), each tailored to a different use case on the same underlying platform ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1845338/000117891324002760/exhibit_99-1.htm)).

On August 26, 2024 the company announced it had passed $1 billion in annual recurring revenue — a decade after launching the Work OS and eight years after reaching $1 million in ARR ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1845338/000117891324002760/exhibit_99-1.htm)). At that point it reported over 225,000 customers across more than 200 industries and 200 countries and territories, with offices in Tel Aviv, New York, Denver, Chicago, London, Warsaw, Sydney, Melbourne, São Paulo and Tokyo ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1845338/000117891324002760/exhibit_99-1.htm)).

The founders were candid about where monday.com sits in a crowded field. In 2019 they named [Airtable](/organizations/airtable/) and [Notion](/organizations/notion/) as the closer rivals, plus project-management tools such as [Jira](/creative-works/jira/), Wrike and [Asana](/organizations/asana/) — while pointing out that all of them could also be integrated into monday.com ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/07/30/monday-com-raises-150m-more-now-at-1-9b-valuation-for-workplace-collaboration-tools/)). By 2026 the competitive frame had shifted upmarket: UC Today described monday.com competing with Atlassian's Jira and Confluence in technical workflows, Asana in visual work management, Smartsheet in project-heavy industries, and privately held ClickUp and Notion pushing upmarket with their own all-in-one positioning and AI features ([UC Today](https://uctoday.com/monday-com-q4-and-fy2025-earnings-revenue-tops-1-2-billion-as-ai-products-take-centre-stage)).

## Fiscal 2025, the AI pivot, and the guidance reset

Fiscal 2025 was monday.com's strongest year on paper and its most sobering in the market. Revenue reached $1,232.0 million, up 27% from $972.0 million in fiscal 2024, with fourth-quarter revenue of $333.9 million, up 25% ([monday.com Investor Relations](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx)). The GAAP operating loss narrowed to $1.7 million from $21.0 million, non-GAAP operating income was $175.3 million at a 14% margin, and adjusted free cash flow was $322.7 million ([monday.com Investor Relations](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx); [UC Today](https://uctoday.com/monday-com-q4-and-fy2025-earnings-revenue-tops-1-2-billion-as-ai-products-take-centre-stage)).

The figures rest on the [company's results release](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx), corroborated by [UC Today](https://uctoday.com/monday-com-q4-and-fy2025-earnings-revenue-tops-1-2-billion-as-ai-products-take-centre-stage) for revenue, margin, free cash flow and the enterprise cohorts.

The growth mix is the story: the company is moving upmarket. Paid customers with more than 10 users numbered 63,914, up 8%, but the larger cohorts grew far faster — customers above $50,000 in ARR rose 34% to 4,281 and now represent 41% of ARR, and those above $100,000 rose 45% to 1,756 ([monday.com Investor Relations](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx)). Net dollar retention was 110% overall but 116% for the larger cohorts, and total remaining performance obligations rose 37% to $839 million ([monday.com Investor Relations](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx)).

The other half of the year was AI. The company now offers one unified AI platform with four capabilities — monday sidekick, monday vibe, monday agents and monday workflows — and co-CEO Eran Zinman framed the shift as moving "from helping customers manage work to actually doing the work for them" ([UC Today](https://uctoday.com/monday-com-q4-and-fy2025-earnings-revenue-tops-1-2-billion-as-ai-products-take-centre-stage)). Monday vibe, which lets customers build applications on top of their monday data, passed $1 million in ARR just 2.5 months after pricing launched in mid-October 2025, the fastest any monday.com product has reached that mark; Sidekick has processed over half a million user messages and the company's AI blocks have powered more than 77 million actions ([monday.com Investor Relations](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx); [UC Today](https://uctoday.com/monday-com-q4-and-fy2025-earnings-revenue-tops-1-2-billion-as-ai-products-take-centre-stage)).

The market, however, looked past the year and at the outlook. Fiscal 2026 revenue was guided at $1,452–1,462 million, implying 18–19% growth, below the roughly 21% Wall Street expected — and the shares fell about 15% in pre-market trading on February 9, 2026 ([UC Today](https://uctoday.com/monday-com-q4-and-fy2025-earnings-revenue-tops-1-2-billion-as-ai-products-take-centre-stage); [monday.com Investor Relations](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx)). The company also pulled its previously shared 2027 targets, citing uncertainty in the AI landscape and persistent weakness in its self-serve channels, where customer-acquisition costs have risen and returns have fallen below historical levels ([UC Today](https://uctoday.com/monday-com-q4-and-fy2025-earnings-revenue-tops-1-2-billion-as-ai-products-take-centre-stage)). Monday.com built much of its early growth on bottom-up adoption by small teams; that channel is now underperforming, and the company says it is shifting investment toward higher-return enterprise opportunities ([UC Today](https://uctoday.com/monday-com-q4-and-fy2025-earnings-revenue-tops-1-2-billion-as-ai-products-take-centre-stage)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is monday.com?** It is a cloud-based work-management platform — a "Work OS" — from Tel Aviv that lets teams build custom boards, workflows and apps to manage projects, sales, software development and service requests on one platform ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article.aspx?did=1001373994); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1845338/000117891324002760/exhibit_99-1.htm)).

**Who founded monday.com?** Roy Mann and Eran Zinman, who serve as co-CEOs. They founded the company in 2012 as dapulse, after building the software as an internal tool at Wix.com, and renamed it monday.com in November 2017 ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article.aspx?did=1001373994); [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/monday.com-ipo%3A-what-you-need-to-know-2021-06-08)).

**When did monday.com go public?** On June 10, 2021, on Nasdaq under the ticker MNDY, priced at $155 per share, raising $574 million at a $6.8 billion valuation; the shares rose 15% to $178 on the first day ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article.aspx?did=1001373994); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3909641,00.html)).

**How big is monday.com now?** Fiscal 2025 revenue was $1,232.0 million, up 27%, and the company passed $1 billion in annual recurring revenue in August 2024; it reported over 225,000 customers at that time, with larger enterprise accounts now driving the growth mix ([monday.com Investor Relations](https://ir.monday.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2026/monday-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1845338/000117891324002760/exhibit_99-1.htm)).

**What are monday.com's main competitors?** Asana, ClickUp, Notion, Airtable and Atlassian's Jira, in a crowded work-management market where the comparison is increasingly about AI execution and enterprise governance rather than features alone ([UC Today](https://uctoday.com/monday-com-q4-and-fy2025-earnings-revenue-tops-1-2-billion-as-ai-products-take-centre-stage); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/07/30/monday-com-raises-150m-more-now-at-1-9b-valuation-for-workplace-collaboration-tools/)).
