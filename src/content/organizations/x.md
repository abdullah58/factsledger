## Overview

X is the social media platform Elon Musk bought as Twitter for $44 billion in 2022, rebranded with a black-and-white X logo in 2023, and folded — via his xAI — into SpaceX's $1.25 trillion merger in 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/27/elon-musk-officially-owns-twitter-here-are-the-immediate-changes-to-expect/); [TechJournal](https://techjournal.org/spacex-xai-merger)). For this wiki, though, its defining story is different: X was the platform that gave [Tucker Carlson](/people/tucker-carlson/) his post-cable career. Weeks after his abrupt April 2023 split from [Fox News](/organizations/fox-news/), Carlson launched *Tucker on X* — a long-form video show native to the platform — and in doing so proved Musk's X could be a broadcast network for exiled television talent, not just a feed of short posts ([Reuters](https://mix929.com/tucker-carlson-calls-first-show-on-twitter-tucker-on-twitter/)).

## The show that made X a network

On June 6, 2023, Carlson released the first episode of *Tucker on Twitter* — the show's original name — as a roughly ten-minute video posted directly to the platform ([Reuters](https://mix929.com/tucker-carlson-calls-first-show-on-twitter-tucker-on-twitter/)). It opened with an unsubstantiated claim that Ukraine had destroyed a dam in Russian-controlled territory, then turned into a manifesto about his new medium: "You are being lied to. The people in charge systematically attempt to lie to you about what is happening in Ukraine," he said, adding that he had "no gatekeepers in his new program" and comparing the platform to "short-wave radio under the blankets" ([Reuters](https://mix929.com/tucker-carlson-calls-first-show-on-twitter-tucker-on-twitter/)). Musk — who has called Carlson's show a step toward becoming a platform for "citizen journalism" — welcomed the launch, saying it would be great to have shows from all parts of the political spectrum on the platform ([Reuters](https://mix929.com/tucker-carlson-calls-first-show-on-twitter-tucker-on-twitter/)).

Carlson had parted with Fox News in late April 2023, days after the network paid $787.5 million to settle Dominion Voting Systems' defamation lawsuit ([Reuters](https://mix929.com/tucker-carlson-calls-first-show-on-twitter-tucker-on-twitter/)). The show stayed on X under the name *Tucker on X* after the rebrand, and its biggest moments — including his February 2024 interview with Vladimir Putin, covered in detail in that profile — cemented X as the distribution channel for one of the most-watched figures in American conservative media.

## The Musk era: takeover, rebrand, exodus

The platform Musk inherited was worth less than he paid for it. He closed the $44 billion deal on October 27, 2022, after months of litigation over his attempt to back out, and his first act was to fire CEO Parag Agrawal, CFO Ned Segal, chief legal officer Vijaya Gadde, and general counsel Sean Edgett — posting "the bird is freed" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/27/elon-musk-officially-owns-twitter-here-are-the-immediate-changes-to-expect/)). The road there had run from a 9.2% stake taken in April 2022, through a bid, a walkaway attempt, and a court battle in Delaware's Chancery Court that ended only when Musk agreed to close at the original price ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/27/elon-musk-officially-owns-twitter-here-are-the-immediate-changes-to-expect/)). TechCrunch reported at the time that Musk planned deep workforce cuts, a rollback of content moderation, and a possible reintroduction of long-banned accounts ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/27/elon-musk-officially-owns-twitter-here-are-the-immediate-changes-to-expect/)).

Then came the rebrand. On July 24, 2023, Musk unveiled a minimalist Art Deco "X" to replace Twitter's famous blue bird, posting "And soon we shall bid adieu to the twitter brand and, gradually, all the birds" — and telling questioners that tweets would henceforth be called "Xs" ([AP](https://www.shootonline.com/shoot_newsbriefs/elon-musk-rebrands-twitter-x-logo-replace-blue-bird/)). The rebrand was the visible form of a stated strategy: building an "everything app" on the model of China's WeChat, combining video chats, messaging, streaming, and payments — a vision [Elon Musk](/people/elon-musk/) had nursed since founding his 1999 startup X.com, which later became PayPal ([AP](https://www.shootonline.com/shoot_newsbriefs/elon-musk-rebrands-twitter-x-logo-replace-blue-bird/)). Experts quoted by the AP predicted the new name would only confuse an audience already souring on the platform — and the timing was awkward, with Meta's Threads app launching as a direct rival for Twitter users ([AP](https://www.shootonline.com/shoot_newsbriefs/elon-musk-rebrands-twitter-x-logo-replace-blue-bird/)).

The business side nearly collapsed. After Musk bought the company he gutted the workforce, advertisers fled, and revenue declined sharply; Linda Yaccarino, the NBCUniversal executive Musk named CEO in May 2023 to repair advertiser relationships, presided over the turnaround effort — payments via X Money, Community Notes, new creator programming — until she stepped down in July 2025 after two years ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/x-ceo-linda-yaccarino-steps-down)). Brands began returning, in Reuters' account, as Musk's influence in the Trump administration grew ([Reuters](https://srnnews.com/musks-xai-buys-social-media-platform-x-in-33-billion-deal/)).

## From social network to AI infrastructure

In March 2025, Musk's artificial-intelligence company xAI acquired X for $33 billion plus $12 billion in assumed debt — an all-stock deal that valued xAI itself at $80 billion ([Reuters](https://srnnews.com/musks-xai-buys-social-media-platform-x-in-33-billion-deal/)). "xAI and X's futures are intertwined," Musk posted. The strategic logic was blunt: xAI needed training data for its Grok chatbot, and X's firehose of real-time, user-generated content was the asset — Musk had already been training xAI on X posts ([Reuters](https://srnnews.com/musks-xai-buys-social-media-platform-x-in-33-billion-deal/)). DA Davidson analyst Gil Luria noted the $33 billion valuation was roughly $1 billion higher than what Musk paid to take the company private — a surprising markup for what he called "a company that isn't doing so well" ([Reuters](https://srnnews.com/musks-xai-buys-social-media-platform-x-in-33-billion-deal/)). Saudi Arabia's Kingdom Holding had become the second-largest investor in the combined entity ([Reuters](https://srnnews.com/musks-xai-buys-social-media-platform-x-in-33-billion-deal/)).

The platform's corporate journey did not end there. On February 2, 2026, SpaceX acquired xAI — including X and Grok — in an all-stock merger valuing the combined company at $1.25 trillion ($1 trillion for SpaceX, $250 billion for xAI), which CNBC called the largest private merger ever ([TechJournal](https://techjournal.org/spacex-xai-merger)). The rationale, per reporting: xAI was burning roughly $1 billion a month in its race with OpenAI and Anthropic, and SpaceX's balance sheet could fund it — while Musk pushed his long-standing vision of space-based data centers ([TechJournal](https://techjournal.org/spacex-xai-merger)). Tesla, which held a roughly $2 billion xAI stake, was left out of the deal ([TechJournal](https://techjournal.org/spacex-xai-merger)). A social network bought for $44 billion in 2022 had, in under four years, become one component of a trillion-dollar space-and-AI conglomerate.
