**Graphiti Multimedia** — styled "Graphiti Studios" in recent government and industry reporting — is a [Mumbai](/places/mumbai/)-based Indian animation studio launched in 1995, whose operating leaders are co-founders [Munjal Shroff](/people/munjal-shroff/) and [Tilak Raj Shetty](/people/tilak-raj-shetty/) and which was chaired by Ram Mohan, the "father of Indian animation" ([Animation Magazine](https://www.animationmagazine.net/2019/10/ram-mohan-father-of-indian-animation-dies-at-88/)). It is best known as the studio behind the folk-tales franchise [Krish, Trish and Baltiboy](/creative-works/krish-trish-and-baltiboy/), including the 52-episode government co-production *Bharat Hain Hum* ([Hum Hindustani (PIB republication)](https://humhindustani.news/?p=5750); [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/business/in-other-news/iict-partners-with-graphiti-studios-to-provide-students-with-live-industry-projects-professional-mentoring-and-production-led-training-1977384)).

Industry press describes it as one of India's most established animation and immersive-media studios, with more than three decades of work spanning animation, television, advertising, films, immersive technologies and intellectual-property development — its original IPs include the Netflix Original *Deepa & Anoop*, *YOM* and *J Bole Toh Jadoo* ([Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/business/in-other-news/iict-partners-with-graphiti-studios-to-provide-students-with-live-industry-projects-professional-mentoring-and-production-led-training-1977384)).

## From advertising house to original-IP studio

BusinessToday's 2010 profile names Tilak Shetty co-founder and CEO and Munjal Shroff chief operating officer, noting both began their careers under Ram Mohan, "the guru of animation in India" ([BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/magazine/special/story/animation-farm-15796-2010-10-13)). The profile frames 2002 as "the beginning of Graphiti Multimedia" — an original-IP business, "till then known for its advertising films" — when Shetty and Shroff chose to create intellectual property rather than take outsourced work ([BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/magazine/special/story/animation-farm-15796-2010-10-13)). Early commissions proved the pivot: Star TV ordered an animated character for a game show, and after the alien Jadoo became a hit in Rakesh Roshan's *Koi Mil Gaya*, Graphiti produced a television series around the character with Roshan's permission ([BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/magazine/special/story/animation-farm-15796-2010-10-13)). In 2005 the studio landed its first foreign co-production, partnering with Fido Dido creator Joanna Ferrone on *The World of OO*, a toddler-learning concept; Ferrone said Graphiti delivered world-class animation while informing the creative process dynamically ([BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/magazine/special/story/animation-farm-15796-2010-10-13)). By 2010 the magazine reported five owned IPs alongside ongoing advertising work and an animation school ([BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/magazine/special/story/animation-farm-15796-2010-10-13)).

## Krish, Trish and Baltiboy, and a government co-production

Graphiti's flagship is Krish, Trish and Baltiboy, the folk-tales anthology whose second film was nominated in the children's animation category at the [21st Cairo International Film Festival for Children](/events/cairo-international-film-festival-for-children/) in March 2012 — AnimationXpress calls the producer "Mumbai based Graphiti Multimedia," quotes Shroff and Shetty as the studio's directors, and notes three more KTB features then in pre-production built on Chitrakathi, Gond, Phad and Kalighat art forms, reportedly never before adapted to animation ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/event/online-events/awards/krish-trish-and-balti-boy-2-nominated-at-21st-cairo-international-film-festival-for-children/)). In 2023 the franchise became a state project: *Bharat Hain Hum*, 52 eleven-minute episodes (26 per season), co-produced with the [Central Bureau of Communication](/organizations/central-bureau-of-communication/) of the [Ministry of Information and Broadcasting](/organizations/ministry-of-information-and-broadcasting/) and created by Shroff and Shetty ([Hum Hindustani (PIB republication)](https://humhindustani.news/?p=5750); [Cinema Express](https://www.cinemaexpress.com/hindi/news/2023/Oct/11/ib-minister-anurag-thakur-launches-trailer-of-animated-show-bharat-hain-hum-48527.html)). [Anurag Thakur](/people/anurag-thakur/) launched the trailer in October 2023; the series streamed on [Doordarshan](/organizations/doordarshan/), [Netflix](/organizations/netflix/) and [Amazon](/organizations/amazon/) Prime Video in 12 Indian and 7 international languages, launched at the [International Film Festival of India](/events/international-film-festival-of-india/) in Goa with Nagarjuna, and was praised by Prime Minister [Narendra Modi](/people/narendra-modi/) in his December 2024 Mann Ki Baat address ([Hum Hindustani (PIB republication)](https://humhindustani.news/?p=5750); [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/entertainment/second-season-of-animated-series-krish-trish-and-baltiboy-bharat-hain-hum-launched-at-iffi/); [Prime Minister's Office](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/pms-address-in-the-117th-episode-of-mann-ki-baat/)).

## Ram Mohan's chairmanship, the school, and recent work

The studio's most storied chairman was Ram Mohan — known as the father of Indian animation, winner of two National Film Awards for animation and the 2014 Padma Shri — who served as Graphiti Multimedia's chairman and chief creative officer until his death in October 2019 at 88 ([Animation Magazine](https://www.animationmagazine.net/2019/10/ram-mohan-father-of-indian-animation-dies-at-88/)). He had also established the Graphiti School of Animation in 2006 ([Animation Magazine](https://www.animationmagazine.net/2019/10/ram-mohan-father-of-indian-animation-dies-at-88/)). Recent reporting lists the studio's original IPs as the Netflix Original *Deepa & Anoop*, *YOM* and *J Bole Toh Jadoo* alongside *Bharat Hain Hum*, calling Graphiti "one of India's most established animation and immersive-media studios" ([Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/business/in-other-news/iict-partners-with-graphiti-studios-to-provide-students-with-live-industry-projects-professional-mentoring-and-production-led-training-1977384)). In August 2026 the studio signed a memorandum of understanding with the Indian Institute of Creative Technologies (IICT), the AVGC-XR sector's National Centre of Excellence, giving students live industry projects, masterclasses, internships and placement exposure — the studio's model now extending from making content to training the next generation ([Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/business/in-other-news/iict-partners-with-graphiti-studios-to-provide-students-with-live-industry-projects-professional-mentoring-and-production-led-training-1977384)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who founded Graphiti Multimedia?**
The press names Munjal Shroff and Tilak Raj Shetty as the operating co-founders — Shetty as co-founder and CEO, Shroff as COO in 2010 reporting. Both trained under Ram Mohan, who served as chairman and chief creative officer. Whether Ram Mohan also held a founder's role was not verified in a full-read source and is not asserted.

**Is it Graphiti Multimedia or Graphiti Studios?**
Both names describe the same Mumbai studio and leadership. Earlier and trade press use "Graphiti Multimedia"; 2023-onwards government and industry reporting (including the Deccan Chronicle) uses "Graphiti Studios." No source in this pass confirmed whether this is a formal rebrand or a trade-style name.

**What is the studio's most famous work?**
The folk-tales franchise Krish, Trish and Baltiboy, whose 52-episode *Bharat Hain Hum* was co-produced with the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting's Central Bureau of Communication, streamed on Doordarshan, Netflix and Amazon Prime Video, and was praised by the Prime Minister in Mann Ki Baat. The Netflix Original *Deepa & Anoop* is its other marquee credit.

**What did Ram Mohan have to do with Graphiti?**
Ram Mohan — the "father of Indian animation," a two-time National Film Award winner and 2014 Padma Shri recipient — was chairman and chief creative officer of Graphiti Multimedia, a company launched in 1995, and established the Graphiti School of Animation in 2006. He died in October 2019 at 88.
