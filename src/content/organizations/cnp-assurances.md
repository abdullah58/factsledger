## Overview

CNP Assurances is a French personal insurer whose corporate identity is inseparable from the French state. It was born in 1959 as the Caisse Nationale de Prévoyance, the merger of two life-and-accident funds of the Caisse des Dépôts, and it remained a creature of the public sector — first as an arm of the Caisse des Dépôts, then as a state-owned company with public-establishment status, then as a listed joint-stock company still held inside the public Caisse des Dépôts group ([Assemblée Nationale](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/q15/15-199QE.htm)). Its listed life ended in 2022, when La Banque Postale — the banking arm of La Poste — completed a simplified takeover bid at €21.90 a share and moved to squeeze out the minorities and delist it from Euronext Paris, the final act of the "grand pôle financier public" announced by the Economy Minister in 2018 ([Allnews](https://allnews.ch/content/corporate/la-banque-postale-veut-monter-%C3%A0-100-dans-cnp-assurances); [Eurex](https://www.eurex.com/ex-en/rules-regs/corporate-actions/corporate-action-information/CNP-Assurances-S.A.-Takeover-Updated-06-June-2022--2827330)).

## A public-sector insurer by design

CNP's lineage is fully documented in the French parliament's own records. The Caisse Nationale de Prévoyance was created in 1959 from the fusion of the "caisse nationale d'assurance sur la vie" and the "caisse nationale en cas d'accident," and it was made a public industrial-and-commercial establishment (EPIC) by the decree of 12 October 1987 ([Assemblée Nationale](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/q15/15-199QE.htm)). Law 92-665 of 16 July 1992 converted it into a société anonyme — CNP Assurances — while keeping it a subsidiary of the Caisse des Dépôts group, which the law treats as "a public group serving the general interest" ([Assemblée Nationale](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/q15/15-199QE.htm)). That public-sector DNA mattered decades later: the terms of the 2022 takeover were driven not by a private buyer but by the state's decision to fold the insurer into a public bancassurance group.

## The 2021–2022 takeover: a state-engineered bid

In August 2018, Economy and Finance Minister Bruno Le Maire presented the project of creating a "grand pôle financier public" — a large public financial group — by combining La Poste, La Banque Postale, and CNP Assurances, alongside a parallel La Poste–Caisse des Dépôts rapprochement ([Allnews](https://allnews.ch/content/corporate/la-banque-postale-veut-monter-%C3%A0-100-dans-cnp-assurances)).

The decisive move came on 28 October 2021. La Banque Postale — which then held "a little over 62%" of CNP's capital — signed a memorandum with the mutualist banking group BPCE to buy its entire 16.1% stake in the insurer at €21.90 per share, and announced it would then file a simplified tender offer for the minority shareholders at the same price, explicitly "en visant le retrait obligatoire de la cote" — targeting a mandatory squeeze-out and delisting from the Paris bourse ([Allnews](https://allnews.ch/content/corporate/la-banque-postale-veut-monter-%C3%A0-100-dans-cnp-assurances)). The operation would total more than €5.5 billion, and CNP's share price jumped 35.18% to €21.73 on the announcement ([Allnews](https://allnews.ch/content/corporate/la-banque-postale-veut-monter-%C3%A0-100-dans-cnp-assurances)).

The French financial press reported the economics: the block purchase would take La Banque Postale to 78.9% of the capital; the €21.90 offer price (cum dividend) represented a 36.2% premium over the €16.075 pre-announcement price and valued CNP at about €15 billion; and the offer was expected to open in the first quarter of 2022, subject to regulatory approvals ([Boursorama / Le Revenu](https://www.boursorama.com/bourse/actualites/opa-des-primes-attractives-pour-cnp-assurances-prodware-et-s-t-dupont-739164beaf965dd9e1850c864ef9dcd1)).

The deal was also a swap of partnerships: BPCE, through its subsidiary Natixis, agreed to buy La Banque Postale's stakes in the asset managers Ostrum (45%) and AEW Europe (40%) to own 100% of them, while the two groups extended and prolonged their industrial partnerships ([Allnews](https://allnews.ch/content/corporate/la-banque-postale-veut-monter-%C3%A0-100-dans-cnp-assurances)).

On 3 June 2022, La Banque Postale announced that all conditions of the offer were fulfilled: it held 97.79% of the capital and 98.56% of the voting rights of CNP Assurances, and moved to request the AMF's mandatory squeeze-out procedure ([Eurex](https://www.eurex.com/ex-en/rules-regs/corporate-actions/corporate-action-information/CNP-Assurances-S.A.-Takeover-Updated-06-June-2022--2827330)). Positions on the derivatives market were settled at the theoretical fair value based on the €20.90 ex-dividend tender price ([Eurex](https://www.eurex.com/ex-en/rules-regs/corporate-actions/corporate-action-information/CNP-Assurances-S.A.-Takeover-Updated-06-June-2022--2827330)).
