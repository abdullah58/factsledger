## Overview

Allen & Company is a privately held boutique investment bank based in New York, founded in 1922 by Charles Robert Allen Jr. and run by the Allen family for three generations ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)). It advises on technology, media and entertainment deals, invests alongside its clients, and hosts the invitation-only Sun Valley Conference in Idaho each July ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/trends-secretive-new-york-bank-allen-co-gets-into-silicon-valley-media-tech); [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/07/14/tim-cook-and-eddy-cue-attend-sun-valley-conference/)). Its president is Herbert A. Allen III, who took control in 2002 ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/trends-secretive-new-york-bank-allen-co-gets-into-silicon-valley-media-tech); [Coca-Cola](https://investors.coca-colacompany.com/filings-reports/all-sec-filings/content/0001193125-25-274197/coke-ex99_4.htm)).

The firm is small by Wall Street standards and deliberately obscure: it has no public website, issues almost no press releases, and declined to speak to reporters who profiled it ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires); [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/trends-secretive-new-york-bank-allen-co-gets-into-silicon-valley-media-tech)). That obscurity is the point. Its business rests on close, personal, long-term relationships with media and technology chiefs, and on a conference that turns those relationships into mandates ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/trends-secretive-new-york-bank-allen-co-gets-into-silicon-valley-media-tech); [NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)). This profile is about the New York bank, not Allen & Company of Florida, a separate Lakeland firm founded in 1932.

## A family firm since 1922

Charles Robert Allen Jr. founded the firm in 1922 and was soon joined by his brothers, Herbert A. Allen Sr. and Harold Allen, building a boutique that specialised in real estate and in media and entertainment ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)). Herbert Allen Jr. expanded the client list, and control passed to his son, Herbert Allen III, in 2002 ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/trends-secretive-new-york-bank-allen-co-gets-into-silicon-valley-media-tech)). As part of that transition, the firm created Allen & Co. LLC to handle all of its operations, starting with $40 million in capital and with employees holding shares, while the original corporation continued as a family investment vehicle worth around $1 billion as of 2004 ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/trends-secretive-new-york-bank-allen-co-gets-into-silicon-valley-media-tech)). A November 2025 Coca-Cola filing still lists Herbert A. Allen III as president of Allen & Company LLC, at 711 Fifth Avenue in New York ([Coca-Cola](https://investors.coca-colacompany.com/filings-reports/all-sec-filings/content/0001193125-25-274197/coke-ex99_4.htm)).

## Columbia Pictures and the 1995 triple

The firm's first great principal investment was Columbia Pictures: it took a controlling interest in the studio in 1973 and sold it to Coca-Cola in 1982 at a significant profit, after which Herbert Allen Jr. held a place on Coca-Cola's board ([SportsBusinessJournal](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Journal/Issues/1999/03/01/No-Topic-Name/Billionaire-And-Agent-Mull-Deal)). The episode taught the firm to own as well as advise, a habit that still shapes it. In 1995 it advised on three huge deals in a single year: Disney's $18.8 billion purchase of Capital Cities/ABC, Westinghouse's $5.4 billion purchase of CBS, and Seagram's $5.7 billion purchase of 80 percent of MCA from Matsushita ([SportsBusinessJournal](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Journal/Issues/1999/03/01/No-Topic-Name/Billionaire-And-Agent-Mull-Deal)). Partner Nancy Peretsman, who joined in 1995 after working at Salomon Brothers, added her own list, including Comcast's $60 billion acquisition of MediaOne and the sale of BET to Viacom for nearly $2 billion ([TVWeek](https://www.tvweek.com/in-depth/2002/05/art-of-the-media-deal/)).

## The Sun Valley conference

Herbert Allen Jr. founded the Sun Valley Conference in 1983, and it has been held each July in Sun Valley, Idaho, ever since, at the Sun Valley Lodge resort ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/07/14/tim-cook-and-eddy-cue-attend-sun-valley-conference/)). It is private and invitation-only, a week of hiking, golf, tennis and rafting in the Sawtooth Mountains, with panels, talks by political leaders, cocktail parties and lavish dinners, all paid for by Allen & Company ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)). The 2020 gathering was suspended during the pandemic and returned in 2021 ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)). The 2025 edition ran for four days in July, with artificial intelligence the dominant topic and attendees including Tim Cook of [Apple](/organizations/apple/), Satya Nadella of [Microsoft](/organizations/microsoft/), Sam Altman of [OpenAI](/organizations/openai/) and Bob Iger of Disney, alongside Sundar Pichai of Google, Mark Zuckerberg of Meta, Andy Jassy of Amazon and David Zaslav of Warner Bros. Discovery ([Fast Company](https://fastcompany.co.za/tech/2025-07-16-sun-valley-2025-the-biggest-news-and-deals-you-need-to-know/); [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/07/14/tim-cook-and-eddy-cue-attend-sun-valley-conference/)).

## Notable deals

The firm's record runs from Hollywood to Silicon Valley, and the table below collects the deals verified from sources read in full for this profile. Its early media work set the pattern: advise the buyer or seller, stay close to the principals, and let the relationship produce the next mandate. The Google and Facebook underwritings carried that pattern into technology, and the WhatsApp and Time Warner mandates showed it could still win a seat on the largest transactions of their years ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)). The [Blend](/organizations/blend/) IPO, by contrast, put the firm in a lead book-running role alongside much larger banks ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/corporate/blend-labs-lands-on-nyse-in-360m-ipo/)).

Facebook's acquisition of WhatsApp, announced on 19 February 2014, was for approximately $16 billion — $4 billion in cash and about $12 billion in shares — plus $3 billion in restricted stock for WhatsApp's founders and employees, taking the total to $19 billion ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000132680114000010/exhibit991_pressrelease219.htm)). In the AT&T–Time Warner deal, announced on 22 October 2016 at $107.50 per share, Allen & Co. LLC acted as a financial adviser to Time Warner alongside Citigroup and Morgan Stanley ([Reuters](https://investadvocateng.com/2016/10/23/att-buy-time-warner-85-billion-create-telecom-media-giant/); [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1105705/000095015716002366/ex99-1.htm)). The equity value was $85.4 billion and the total value including debt was $108.7 billion ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1105705/000095015716002366/ex99-1.htm)).

## How the firm works

Allen & Company does not pitch like other banks: it does no research and only brokers deals with principals, relying on relationships built over decades ([TVWeek](https://www.tvweek.com/in-depth/2002/05/art-of-the-media-deal/)). Herbert Allen Jr. described its investing as "circles around circles of investing" — when an attractive opportunity surfaces, the firm invests, its people invest, and the Allens personally invest ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/trends-secretive-new-york-bank-allen-co-gets-into-silicon-valley-media-tech)). That model explains its hiring: its deal-makers have included former CIA director George Tenet, former senator Bill Bradley and former representative Will Hurd, people chosen at the prime of their careers with relationships already built ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/trends-secretive-new-york-bank-allen-co-gets-into-silicon-valley-media-tech); [NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)). NPR's account of the economics is blunt: bankers sit and wait, building a relationship "with the hopes of doing a deal every four or five years", and "it culminates in a large fee when the time is right" ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Allen & Company?** Allen & Company is a privately held boutique investment bank based in New York, founded in 1922 and run by the Allen family for three generations ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)). It specialises in technology, media and entertainment advisory and investing, and hosts the Sun Valley Conference ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/trends-secretive-new-york-bank-allen-co-gets-into-silicon-valley-media-tech)).

**What is the Sun Valley Conference?** It is Allen & Company's private, invitation-only annual gathering in Sun Valley, Idaho, held each July since 1983 at the Sun Valley Lodge ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/07/14/tim-cook-and-eddy-cue-attend-sun-valley-conference/)). Executives, founders, investors and political figures meet informally over hiking, golf and rafting, and the event is widely regarded as a venue where major deals are first discussed ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/07/14/tim-cook-and-eddy-cue-attend-sun-valley-conference/)).

**What deals is Allen & Company known for?** It advised Facebook on the WhatsApp acquisition, Time Warner on the AT&T deal, and Disney on the Capital Cities/ABC purchase, and was an underwriter on the Google IPO ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000132680114000010/exhibit991_pressrelease219.htm); [Reuters](https://investadvocateng.com/2016/10/23/att-buy-time-warner-85-billion-create-telecom-media-giant/); [NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)). In 2021 it was a lead book-running manager on Blend's $360 million IPO alongside [Goldman Sachs](/organizations/goldman-sachs/) and [Wells Fargo](/organizations/wells-fargo/) Securities ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/corporate/blend-labs-lands-on-nyse-in-360m-ipo/)).

**Why is Allen & Company so secretive?** The firm has no public website and rarely makes public statements, a stance confirmed by both VentureBeat and NPR, which it declined to speak to ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/trends-secretive-new-york-bank-allen-co-gets-into-silicon-valley-media-tech); [NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)). Its model depends on discretion: clients come for relationships that are not leaked before a deal closes, and the firm waits years between mandates ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not state the firm's revenue, assets under management, headcount or current deal volume, because none was verified from a fully-read source — the employee figures in older sources are dated and are not presented as current ([SportsBusinessJournal](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Journal/Issues/1999/03/01/No-Topic-Name/Billionaire-And-Agent-Mull-Deal)). It does not claim that any particular deal was caused by the Sun Valley Conference: the Disney–ABC, YouTube, Washington Post and Yahoo deals are traced to Sun Valley discussions by the sources cited, and the Bezos purchase is described by NPR only as "reportedly decided" there ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/07/14/tim-cook-and-eddy-cue-attend-sun-valley-conference/); [NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1012587989/moguls-deals-and-patagonia-vests-a-look-inside-summer-camp-for-billionaires)). It does not state the deals attributed to the firm only in reference works, such as the Twitter IPO or the Activision–Vivendi merger, which were not verified here.
