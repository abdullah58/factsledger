## Overview

**Jinga Films** is a [United Kingdom](/places/united-kingdom/)-based genre film sales agent and distributor founded by Welsh filmmaker Julian Richards, best known as the world sales agent behind two of the most controversial horror releases of the 2010s — *A Serbian Film* and [The Bunny Game](/creative-works/the-bunny-game/) ([Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/news/jinga-scores-sales-on-two-horrors-at-pre-cannes-screenings-exclusive/5161314.article); [Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/controversial-horror-the-bunny-game-gets-blu-ray-release-from-jinga-films/)). In 2020 it launched Danse Macabre, a horror distribution arm co-owned by Richards and Rosana Coutinho, which reissued *The Bunny Game* on US Blu-ray and DVD in summer 2025 ([Love Horror](https://lovehorror.co.uk/horror-news/69034/jinga-films-announce-launch-of-horror-distribution-arm-danse-macabre/); [Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/controversial-horror-the-bunny-game-gets-blu-ray-release-from-jinga-films/)). This profile is about the film company only — not the unrelated Chinese budget-smartphone brand sold in Russia under the same name ([TAdviser](https://tadviser.com/index.php/Company:Jinga)).

## From filmmaker to sales agent

Richards came to sales from directing: after putting his own money into *The Last Horror Movie* and needing to get it back, he moved into film sales as "the most immediate and accessible way" to learn the business side — and stayed ([The People's Movies](https://thepeoplesmovies.com/arrow-video-frightfest-halloween-interview-julian-richards-reborn/)). His directing CV runs through *Darklands* — which he describes as arguably the first Welsh horror movie — the BAFTA Cymru award-winning *Summer Scars*, *Shiver* (2012), *Daddy's Girl* and *Reborn*, and the sales operation grew in parallel: from handling three films a year to ten, with the titles getting stronger ([The People's Movies](https://thepeoplesmovies.com/arrow-video-frightfest-halloween-interview-julian-richards-reborn/)). The scale-up is measurable — a more-than-threefold increase in annual throughput — and Richards dates Jinga's arrival precisely: "by the time we released A SERBIAN FILM in 2012, we had established ourselves as one of the key genre companies in the World" ([The People's Movies](https://thepeoplesmovies.com/arrow-video-frightfest-halloween-interview-julian-richards-reborn/)). The company's reputation titles, per Richards, span *The Last Horror Movie*, *Rabies*, *A Serbian Film*, *The House at the End of Time*, *The Canal*, *Our Evil* and *Still/Born* ([Love Horror](https://lovehorror.co.uk/horror-news/69034/jinga-films-announce-launch-of-horror-distribution-arm-danse-macabre/)).

## The controversy portfolio: A Serbian Film and The Bunny Game

Jinga's two defining sales mandates took opposite paths through the censors. As Flickering Myth notes, Jinga was "the global sales agent behind *A Serbian Film*," which "managed to get past UK censors with cuts," while *The Bunny Game* "ran afoul of the British Board of Film Classification and was refused a certificate, effectively banning the film in the UK to this day" ([Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/controversial-horror-the-bunny-game-gets-blu-ray-release-from-jinga-films/)). Jinga sold *The Bunny Game* territory by territory: UK rights went to Trinity Films for its Trinity X label in June 2011, followed by Germany (Illusion Unlimited) and Scandinavia (Njuta), with a North American deal expected by the end of the American Film Market ([Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/distribution/trinity-picks-up-horror-the-bunny-game-for-uk/5029271.article); [Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/jinga-closes-more-deals-for-the-bunny-game-bbfc-refuses-to-classify-film/5034286.article)). That North American deal became the March 2012 US acquisition by Autonomy Pictures — which Richards himself negotiated on the filmmakers' behalf — completing a sales chain in which one film passed the UK censors only after cuts and the other never passed at all ([Bloody Disgusting](http://bloody-disgusting.com/news/28671/autonomy-pictures-comes-out-playing-the-bunny-game/)).

## Danse Macabre and the 2025 Bunny Game reissue

In 2020 Jinga launched Danse Macabre as a dedicated horror distribution arm for the English-speaking territories — the UK, Ireland, US, Canada, Australia and New Zealand — co-owned by Richards and Rosana Coutinho ([Love Horror](https://lovehorror.co.uk/horror-news/69034/jinga-films-announce-launch-of-horror-distribution-arm-danse-macabre/)). The label was designed to do double duty: distribute horror directly while acting as a horror sales sub-label inside Jinga, as the parent company expanded into wider genres including arthouse ([Love Horror](https://lovehorror.co.uk/horror-news/69034/jinga-films-announce-launch-of-horror-distribution-arm-danse-macabre/)). Richards positioned it explicitly against a predecessor — "Taking our inspiration from former UK label Tartan Films, we will offer genre fans a selection of exotic titles that might never find distribution in the mainstream market" — and the 2020 slate (*Antrum*, *The Ringmaster*, *Gags the Clown*, *Purgatory Road*, *All the Creatures Were Stirring*) mixed theatrical and DVD/digital releases ([Love Horror](https://lovehorror.co.uk/horror-news/69034/jinga-films-announce-launch-of-horror-distribution-arm-danse-macabre/)). The label's arc came full circle in summer 2025, when Danse Macabre reissued *The Bunny Game* on US Blu-ray and DVD with a "Behind The Scenes – Caretaking The Monster" featurette plus trailer and teaser — fourteen years after Jinga first sold the film, and the same title returning through the company's own label ([Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/controversial-horror-the-bunny-game-gets-blu-ray-release-from-jinga-films/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who founded Jinga Films?**
Welsh filmmaker Julian Richards — director of *Darklands*, *The Last Horror Movie*, *Summer Scars*, *Shiver*, *Daddy's Girl* and *Reborn*. He moved into film sales after financing *The Last Horror Movie* himself and wanting to ensure he recouped the investment, then built Jinga from a three-films-a-year operation into one handling ten a year.

**What films is Jinga Films known for?**
It was the world sales agent for *A Serbian Film* and *The Bunny Game*. Its wider reputation titles, per Richards, include *The Last Horror Movie*, *Rabies*, *The House at the End of Time*, *The Canal*, *Our Evil* and *Still/Born*.

**What is Danse Macabre?**
Jinga's horror distribution arm, launched in 2020 and co-owned by Julian Richards and Rosana Coutinho. It distributes horror films across the UK, Ireland, US, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, and doubles as a horror sales sub-label while Jinga itself broadens into arthouse and other genres.

**Is Jinga Films connected to Jinga phones?**
No. "Jinga" is also a Chinese budget-smartphone brand sold in Russia — an entirely separate company with no connection to the London-based film sales agent profiled here.

**Did Jinga reissue The Bunny Game in 2025?**
Yes. In summer 2025 its Danse Macabre label released *The Bunny Game* on Blu-ray and DVD in the US, with a "Behind The Scenes – Caretaking The Monster" featurette, the trailer and a teaser as extras.
