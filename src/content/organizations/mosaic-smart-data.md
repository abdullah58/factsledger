## Overview

Mosaic Smart Data is a data-analytics firm founded in 2014 by former Deutsche Bank managing director Matthew Hodgson. Its MSX platform gives banks real-time analytics across fixed income, currencies and commodities (FICC) trading, and [JPMorgan Chase](/organizations/jpmorgan-chase/) was both its client and an investor before [Behavox](/organizations/behavox/) acquired the company in December 2024 ([ScaleWise](https://www.scalewise.com/case-study/mosaic-smart-data-case-study/); [The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/jp-morgan-invests-fixed-income-analytics-startup/); [A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/behavox-acquires-mosaic-smart-data-to-strengthen-trade-surveillance-offering/)).

Mosaic says it was founded to address a combination of problems facing sell-side banks: rapidly declining FICC revenue and the migration to, and fragmentation of, electronic trade flows across a multitude of venues ([Mosaic Smart Data](https://mosaicsmartdata.com/fstech-shortlists-mosaic-as-fintech-startup-of-the-year/)). Its answer was the MSX platform, which aggregates, normalises and analyses transaction data using AI-driven models to extract actionable insights ([A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/behavox-acquires-mosaic-smart-data-to-strengthen-trade-surveillance-offering/)). The software supports liquidity discovery and decision-making in FICC markets and is deployed across five continents by leading global capital-markets institutions, including JPMorgan and ING ([A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/behavox-acquires-mosaic-smart-data-to-strengthen-trade-surveillance-offering/); [The Full FX](https://thefullfx.com/behavox-acquires-mosaic-smart-data/)).

The firm's commercial model was subscription software: the UK government, which named Mosaic a winner in the Financial Services and FinTech category of its 2024 Made in the UK, Sold to the World Awards, describes a primarily SaaS subscription licence model with a cloud-hosted, fully managed solution, customers on every continent, and over 70 percent of paying customers international, generating approximately 70 percent of total revenue ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-government-honours-exceptional-exporters-with-prestigious-made-in-the-uk-sold-to-the-world-awards)). That export weight — roughly 70 percent of revenue from outside the UK — is an independent measure of how far a small analytics vendor had travelled before its acquisition. The award citation is from the Department for Business and Trade, announced 23 April 2024 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-government-honours-exceptional-exporters-with-prestigious-made-in-the-uk-sold-to-the-world-awards)).

## Founding and the JPMorgan relationship

Hodgson came to Mosaic from the trading floors it would serve: he was a former managing director at Deutsche Bank and Citigroup before founding the company in 2014 ([ScaleWise](https://www.scalewise.com/case-study/mosaic-smart-data-case-study/); [Mosaic Smart Data](https://mosaicsmartdata.com/fstech-shortlists-mosaic-as-fintech-startup-of-the-year/)). Within two years the firm had public recognition, a shortlisting as a finalist in the Fintech Startup of the Year category at the 2016 FStech Awards ([Mosaic Smart Data](https://mosaicsmartdata.com/fstech-shortlists-mosaic-as-fintech-startup-of-the-year/)). The MSX platform was built around three functions — aggregation, analytics and prediction — with the stated aim of boosting sell-side productivity and improving return on investment, and it standardised and aggregated multiple data sets to strengthen audit trails and reporting so banks could meet mounting regulatory requirements ([Mosaic Smart Data](https://mosaicsmartdata.com/fstech-shortlists-mosaic-as-fintech-startup-of-the-year/)).

The relationship that defined Mosaic's first decade was with JPMorgan. In October 2017 the bank signed a multi-year deal to implement Mosaic's technology across its fixed-income and rates sales and trading business, and Mosaic became the first firm to graduate from JPMorgan's In-Residence FinTech programme, which supports technology startups commercialising their products ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/jp-morgan-invests-fixed-income-analytics-startup/)). In March 2018 JPMorgan went further, taking a minority stake and adding Mosaic to its strategic investments portfolio, while already a client with the platform in production in its rates business ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/jp-morgan-invests-fixed-income-analytics-startup/)). Hodgson called the investment "testament that our technology has been built specifically for the challenges of capital markets" ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/jp-morgan-invests-fixed-income-analytics-startup/)).

JPMorgan's interest was commercial, not merely financial. Warren Rabin, then co-head of global macro sales and marketing at the bank, said tools that make sense of vast data sets in real time were "going to become a differentiating factor for banks as they look to add value in their client discussions" ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/commerzbank-leads-9-million-investment-ficc-analytics-firm/)). Mosaic's pitch was that banks could use prediction analysis and AI on vast volumes of data within sales, trading, management and compliance teams, so traders could better visualise and anticipate market and client activity ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/jp-morgan-invests-fixed-income-analytics-startup/)).

## The MSX platform and the $9 million round

MSX works by pulling together transaction data from across an institution, plus external sources, normalising it and applying AI models to extract actionable insights ([The Full FX](https://thefullfx.com/behavox-acquires-mosaic-smart-data/)). Its users span banks, buy-side firms, custodians, exchanges and regulators, and the platform is designed for sales, trading, management and compliance teams to use prediction analysis and machine-learning technology on large volumes of data ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/commerzbank-leads-9-million-investment-ficc-analytics-firm/)). In Mosaic's own description, the platform analyses core metrics to monitor client-trading behaviour, helps institutions target profitable clients, identify market-share gaps in real time, and configure automated alerts so sales and trading teams can interact with their trade flow as it happens ([Mosaic Smart Data](https://mosaicsmartdata.com/fstech-shortlists-mosaic-as-fintech-startup-of-the-year/)).

In April 2019 Mosaic closed a $9 million investment round led by CommerzVentures, the venture-capital fund of Commerzbank, and co-led alongside Octopus Ventures, with JPMorgan participating and increasing its initial stake ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/commerzbank-leads-9-million-investment-ficc-analytics-firm/)). The round was raised to support rapid product development and global expansion, and the trade press framed it against a market in which annual spending on data analytics and market data had grown to $30.5 billion a year ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/commerzbank-leads-9-million-investment-ficc-analytics-firm/)). That $30.5 billion figure is the context that explains why a bank-owned stake and a venture round followed each other so closely: Mosaic was selling into one of the largest technology budgets in finance, not a niche experiment, and its backers were betting that analytics would become a prerequisite for performance in the markets ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/commerzbank-leads-9-million-investment-ficc-analytics-firm/)). Heiko Schwender, a principal at CommerzVentures, said Mosaic had "built a platform which sets the gold standard for capital markets analytics" ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/commerzbank-leads-9-million-investment-ficc-analytics-firm/)).

## Recognition and acquisition by Behavox

The April 2024 export award was Mosaic's most authoritative independent recognition. The Department for Business and Trade named it one of ten small and medium-sized winners of the 2024 Made in the UK, Sold to the World Awards, which celebrate outstanding small businesses achieving exporting success ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-government-honours-exceptional-exporters-with-prestigious-made-in-the-uk-sold-to-the-world-awards)). The award citation records that Mosaic's product uses artificial intelligence and machine learning to help customers better understand and service their own customers, and that exports had been central to its growth, with customers on every continent and over 70 percent of paying customers international ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-government-honours-exceptional-exporters-with-prestigious-made-in-the-uk-sold-to-the-world-awards)).

Eight months later the company was sold. Behavox, the AI insights company, completed its acquisition of Mosaic Smart Data in December 2024 and announced the deal in February 2025; terms were not disclosed ([A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/behavox-acquires-mosaic-smart-data-to-strengthen-trade-surveillance-offering/); [The Full FX](https://thefullfx.com/behavox-acquires-mosaic-smart-data/)). Behavox said it would use Mosaic's expertise and technology to enhance its Trade Surveillance offering, then in customer testing and planned for release in the second half of 2025, and to integrate its own commercial insights — Indications of Interest and Requests for Quotes — into Mosaic Smart Data across both text and voice ([A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/behavox-acquires-mosaic-smart-data-to-strengthen-trade-surveillance-offering/)). [Erkin Adylov](/people/erkin-adylov/), Behavox's CEO, said the combination would let the firms integrate those insights into Mosaic's analytics, while Hodgson said Mosaic would be "the first company to offer FICC desks a comprehensive view of market activity, integrating both transaction and communication data" ([The Full FX](https://thefullfx.com/behavox-acquires-mosaic-smart-data/)).

Inside Behavox, Mosaic Smart Data became the front-office analytics element of an integrated product ecosystem alongside Quantum, its electronic communication surveillance platform, Intelligent Archive, its regulatory archiving solution, and Pathfinder, its AI-powered knowledge and policy management system ([A-Team Insight](https://a-teaminsight.com/blog/behavox-acquires-mosaic-smart-data-to-strengthen-trade-surveillance-offering/)). The deal was funded with debt: Behavox secured a $70 million venture-debt facility from [Hercules Capital](/organizations/hercules-capital/) in autumn 2024 to support strategic expansion including the Mosaic acquisition, and the company says it has been profitable since 2023 ([Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/behavox-raises-175-million-hps-investment-partners-part-blackrock-accelerate)). In 2025 Behavox introduced Polaris, its trade-surveillance platform supporting market-abuse detection across all 10 major asset classes on a single AI-native platform — the product the Mosaic acquisition was bought to accelerate ([Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/behavox-raises-175-million-hps-investment-partners-part-blackrock-accelerate)).
