## Overview

Bilt Rewards is a New York-based fintech whose pitch is simple: turn rent — the largest monthly expense for most households, and historically a rewards dead zone — into loyalty points ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/technology/rent-loyalty-program-bilt-rewards-valuation-skyrockets-to-15b-116053)). Founded by Ankur Jain and launched in 2021, it operates a loyalty program and co-brand credit card that let renters earn points on rent payments with no transaction fees, later expanding into mortgage payments, condo HOA fees and student housing ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/bilt-valued-around-108-billion-latest-funding-round-2025-07-10/); [Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/technology/rent-loyalty-program-bilt-rewards-valuation-skyrockets-to-15b-116053)). In July 2025 it raised $250 million at a $10.75 billion valuation in a round co-led by General Catalyst and GID, with United Wholesale Mortgage participating ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/bilt-valued-around-108-billion-latest-funding-round-2025-07-10/)).

The business model runs on payment plumbing: Bilt earns roughly 2.2% in interchange fees on card transactions plus referral fees from property-management partners, with no monthly fee and no reliance on interest income ([ainvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/bilt-10-75-billion-bet-rent-payments-broke-worst-time-2606/)). That makes transaction volume — and the reliability of the rails moving the money — the entire business.

## The valuation climb

Bilt's private valuation has compounded at unusual speed. The 2022 round led by Left Lane Capital put $150 million in at a $1.5 billion valuation, with Wells Fargo, Greystar, Invitation Homes, Camber Creek, Fifth Wall, Smash Capital, Prosus and Kairos participating; at the time the startup had more than 500,000 active members and processed $3 billion in annualized rent payments ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/technology/rent-loyalty-program-bilt-rewards-valuation-skyrockets-to-15b-116053)). Landlord partners including AvalonBay Communities, Blackstone, Related Cos. and Equity Residential funneled tenants onto the platform, and on-time rent payments were reported to Experian, TransUnion and Equifax so renters could build credit histories ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/technology/rent-loyalty-program-bilt-rewards-valuation-skyrockets-to-15b-116053)).

The January 2024 round led by General Catalyst doubled the valuation to $3.1 billion, and the July 2025 round more than tripled it again to $10.75 billion ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/bilt-valued-around-108-billion-latest-funding-round-2025-07-10/)). The company said the 2025 funding would accelerate expansion into condos, homeowners associations, student housing and mortgages, and projected crossing $1 billion in revenue by the first quarter of 2026 ([Stocktwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/bilt-rockets-to-10-8-b-valuation-after-wells-fargo-split-what-s-next-for-the-rent-rewards-unicorn/ch8cPpiR5md)).

## The Wells Fargo breakup and Bilt 2.0

The original Bilt Mastercard was issued by Wells Fargo — and the partnership bled money. The Wall Street Journal reported that Wells Fargo planned to terminate the co-brand agreement after it became a money-losing venture, with losses at one point running to about $10 million a month; some inside the bank had also raised money-laundering concerns, later addressed ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/bilt-valued-around-108-billion-latest-funding-round-2025-07-10/); [Stocktwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/bilt-rockets-to-10-8-b-valuation-after-wells-fargo-split-what-s-next-for-the-rent-rewards-unicorn/ch8cPpiR5md)). The agreement had originally been set to run through 2029.

Bilt's answer was "Bilt 2.0": a new card lineup on the Cardless platform — a no-fee card plus premium tiers at $95 and $495 annual fees — planned for February 2026, with existing cardholders to be moved off Wells Fargo ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/bilt-valued-around-108-billion-latest-funding-round-2025-07-10/); [Stocktwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/bilt-rockets-to-10-8-b-valuation-after-wells-fargo-split-what-s-next-for-the-rent-rewards-unicorn/ch8cPpiR5md)). The migration proved rocky: a 2026 analysis reported customers facing missed rent and mortgage payments, landlord late fees and damaged credit scores during the switch, followed by a consumer-advocacy letter to the CFPB and an inquiry from Senator Elizabeth Warren demanding data on how many customers were affected ([ainvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/bilt-10-75-billion-bet-rent-payments-broke-worst-time-2606/)). Those accounts come from a single analysis piece and have not been independently corroborated.
