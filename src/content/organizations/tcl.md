## Overview

TCL Technology Group Corp. is a Chinese electronics company headquartered in Huizhou, Guangdong province, that began in 1981 as a maker of audio cassette tapes and is now best known for the display panels inside televisions — including panels sold to Samsung — and for the TCL-branded TVs that rank among the world's top two by shipments ([EDN](https://www.edn.com/branded-in-china/); [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/2290817/samsung-tv-display-made-by-tcl/?zsource=yahoo)). The company most people call "TCL" is actually two companies sharing a brand: the Shenzhen-listed TCL Technology, which makes semiconductor displays and solar materials, and the separately held consumer business, TCL Electronics, which sells the TVs, phones and appliances ([Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2020-01-14/chinese-electronics-giant-tcl-to-rename-itself-tcl-tech-focus-on-display-panels-101503704.html); [TCL Electronics Holdings Limited (HKEX filing)](https://cdn.financialreports.eu/financialreports/media/filings/50591/2025/RNS/50591_rns_2025-04-28_059ca1ff-e177-4cc7-9f00-86446ac36e1a.pdf)). In 2025 the group became a respondent in a US patent case brought by [Cerence](/organizations/cerence/), which alleges that voice technology in TCL smart TVs infringes its patents ([U.S. International Trade Commission](https://www.usitc.gov/sites/default/files/secretary/fed_reg_notices/337/337_1461_notice09172026sgl.pdf)).

## From cassette tapes to televisions

TCL was founded in 1981 in Huizhou, in China's southern Guangdong province, as TTK, a manufacturer of audio cassette tapes ([EDN](https://www.edn.com/branded-in-china/)). Like many Chinese companies of its era it was heavily backed and still partly owned by the government ([EDN](https://www.edn.com/branded-in-china/)). After Japanese cassette maker TDK sued over intellectual property, the company rebranded from TTK to TCL — short for Telephone Communication Limited — in the mid-1980s, and shifted its focus to telephones and other electronics ([SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/1788802/tcl-tvs-who-owns-where-made/)). TCL entered the television market in 1991 with its first TV sets, and by 2003 it had revenue of $3.4 billion and profit of $68.8 million ([SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/1788802/tcl-tvs-who-owns-where-made/); [EDN](https://www.edn.com/branded-in-china/)).

Global expansion came through other companies' brands. In 2003, France's Thomson sought a partner for its struggling TV operations, and the two agreed to merge their TV efforts into TCL-Thomson Electronics (TTE), with TCL holding 67% and Thomson 33%; the venture began operations in July 2004 with more than 29,000 employees and factories producing about 18.5 million TVs a year, then the largest TV output in the world ([EDN](https://www.edn.com/branded-in-china/)). The following month TCL formed a second joint venture, with France's Alcatel, to develop and sell mobile handsets, TCL holding 55% ([EDN](https://www.edn.com/branded-in-china/)). TCL entered the North American TV market in 2013, and in 2019 it launched the world's first Mini LED TV ([SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/1788802/tcl-tvs-who-owns-where-made/); [AAP / PRNewswire](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20240227ae46587/)).

## The 2019 pivot: from TVs to the panels inside them

In December 2018 TCL announced a restructuring that split the company in two: it would sell its stakes in nine mostly consumer-facing subsidiaries to TCL Industries Holdings, set up that September, and focus the listed company on semiconductors and displays ([TechNode](http://technode.com/2018/12/11/tcl-pivot-to-panel/)). The company said it would concentrate on research and development of next-generation display technology and materials, and warned that group operating income would shrink to RMB 50.1 billion from RMB 111.7 billion in 2017 once the consumer businesses were gone ([TechNode](http://technode.com/2018/12/11/tcl-pivot-to-panel/)). In April 2019 it finished spinning off most of its consumer product businesses, and in January 2020 it announced plans to rename itself TCL Technology Group Corp. to match the new focus on semiconductor display panels and materials ([Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2020-01-14/chinese-electronics-giant-tcl-to-rename-itself-tcl-tech-focus-on-display-panels-101503704.html)). The rename took effect in 2020 ([SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/1788802/tcl-tvs-who-owns-where-made/)).

The pivot was built on a panel business TCL had been growing since 2009. Its display arm, TCL CSOT (China Star Optoelectronics Technology), began mass production at an 8.5-generation LCD line in Shenzhen in October 2011 — a joint venture among TCL, the Shenzhen municipal government and Samsung ([TCL Multimedia / CSOT](https://doc.irasia.com/listco/hk/tclelectronics/press/p121013.pdf)). In August 2020, CSOT agreed to buy a 60% stake in Samsung's LCD panel factory in Suzhou, while Samsung Display paid $739 million to become CSOT's second-largest shareholder; in June 2022 Samsung Display handed its LCD patent portfolio — about 2,000 patents — to CSOT ([SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/2290817/samsung-tv-display-made-by-tcl/?zsource=yahoo)). The result is an unusual rivalry: TCL sells TVs that compete with Samsung's, while CSOT supplies roughly one in five of the LCD panels in Samsung TVs — an estimated 7.3 million panels in 2026, according to Omdia ([SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/2290817/samsung-tv-display-made-by-tcl/?zsource=yahoo)).

## The TV business today

The consumer arm's scale is what most people see. In 2024, TCL Electronics shipped 29 million TCL TVs, up 14.8% year over year to a record high, taking a 13.9% global market share and ranking among the top two TV brands in the world, according to Omdia data cited in its annual report ([TCL Electronics Holdings Limited (HKEX filing)](https://cdn.financialreports.eu/financialreports/media/filings/50591/2025/RNS/50591_rns_2025-04-28_059ca1ff-e177-4cc7-9f00-86446ac36e1a.pdf)). Revenue reached HK$99.32 billion, up 25.7%, and profit after tax rose 123.6% to HK$1.85 billion ([TCL Electronics Holdings Limited (HKEX filing)](https://cdn.financialreports.eu/financialreports/media/filings/50591/2025/RNS/50591_rns_2025-04-28_059ca1ff-e177-4cc7-9f00-86446ac36e1a.pdf)). Mini LED TV shipments grew 194.5% to 1.7 million sets, and the company's innovative business — including smart glasses and other new products — grew 44.9% to HK$27.01 billion ([TCL Electronics Holdings Limited (HKEX filing)](https://cdn.financialreports.eu/financialreports/media/filings/50591/2025/RNS/50591_rns_2025-04-28_059ca1ff-e177-4cc7-9f00-86446ac36e1a.pdf)). TCL operates in more than 160 markets, with TV assembly in Shenzhen, Mexico, Vietnam, India, Poland, Brazil, Australia and Pakistan ([AAP / PRNewswire](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20240227ae46587/); [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/1788802/tcl-tvs-who-owns-where-made/)).

## The Cerence patent case

In August 2025, Cerence — the automotive voice-AI company — filed complaints against Sony and TCL, alleging that voice-interaction features in certain smart TVs use its patented technology without authorisation, and seeking a limited exclusion order from the US International Trade Commission that would block imports, plus monetary damages in the US District Court for the Eastern District of Texas ([Gizmochina](https://www.gizmochina.com/2025/08/05/cerence-sues-sony-and-tcl-over-voice-tech-in-smart-tvs/)). Court documents named TCL models including the TCL50 QM5K and TCL55 F35 as examples of the allegedly infringing products ([Gizmochina](https://www.gizmochina.com/2025/08/05/cerence-sues-sony-and-tcl-over-voice-tech-in-smart-tvs/)). The ITC instituted Investigation No. 337-TA-1461 on September 30, 2025, naming TCL Technology Group Corporation, TCL Industries Holdings, TCL Electronics Holdings and ten other TCL entities as respondents, over four US patents (7,840,579; 7,894,598; 8,819,810; 9,171,541) ([U.S. International Trade Commission](https://www.usitc.gov/sites/default/files/secretary/fed_reg_notices/337/337_1461_notice09172026sgl.pdf)). By September 2026 the Commission had terminated the investigation as to many of the asserted claims, after Cerence withdrew some claims in August 2026; Amazon had intervened in December 2025 and the Commission's investigative staff withdrew from participation in February 2026 ([U.S. International Trade Commission](https://www.usitc.gov/sites/default/files/secretary/fed_reg_notices/337/337_1461_notice09172026sgl.pdf)). No final determination of infringement had been made as of September 17, 2026, and neither Sony nor TCL had issued a public statement on the allegations at the time of the filing ([Gizmochina](https://www.gizmochina.com/2025/08/05/cerence-sues-sony-and-tcl-over-voice-tech-in-smart-tvs/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What does TCL stand for?** The letters originally stood for Telephone Communication Limited; the company now also markets the name as "The Creative Life" ([SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/1788802/tcl-tvs-who-owns-where-made/)).

**Is TCL a Chinese company?** Yes. TCL Technology Group Corp. is headquartered in Huizhou, Guangdong province, China, and is partly state-owned; its consumer arm, TCL Electronics, is listed in [Hong Kong](/places/hong-kong/) ([EDN](https://www.edn.com/branded-in-china/); [Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2020-01-14/chinese-electronics-giant-tcl-to-rename-itself-tcl-tech-focus-on-display-panels-101503704.html)).

**Who makes TCL TVs?** TCL Electronics, the consumer arm of the wider TCL group, designs and sells them; it shipped a record 29 million TVs in 2024, making TCL one of the world's top two TV brands ([TCL Electronics Holdings Limited (HKEX filing)](https://cdn.financialreports.eu/financialreports/media/filings/50591/2025/RNS/50591_rns_2025-04-28_059ca1ff-e177-4cc7-9f00-86446ac36e1a.pdf)).

**Does TCL make panels for Samsung TVs?** Yes, through its display arm CSOT, which supplies about one in five LCD panels in Samsung TVs — an estimated 7.3 million panels in 2026 — after buying into Samsung's former Suzhou LCD factory in 2020 ([SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/2290817/samsung-tv-display-made-by-tcl/?zsource=yahoo)).

**What is the Cerence lawsuit against TCL about?** Cerence alleges that voice features in certain TCL (and Sony) smart TVs infringe its patents, and asked the US ITC to block imports of the TVs; the ITC case, opened in September 2025, had narrowed by September 2026 with no final infringement finding ([U.S. International Trade Commission](https://www.usitc.gov/sites/default/files/secretary/fed_reg_notices/337/337_1461_notice09172026sgl.pdf)).
