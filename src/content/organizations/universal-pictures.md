**Universal Pictures** is the American film production and distribution company that serves as the flagship studio of the Universal Filmed Entertainment Group — the film-studio arm of NBCUniversal, which is itself the media arm of Comcast ([TheWrap](http://thewrap.com/universal-box-office-hot-streak-explained/); [Reuters](https://duke.fm/2023/07/06/comcast-president-cavanagh-shakes-up-nbcuniversal-leadership-memo/)). This profile covers the studio — the company that makes and releases films like *Oppenheimer*, *Jurassic World*, and the *Despicable Me* franchise — not NBCUniversal (the broader conglomerate behind NBC, Peacock, and Bravo) and not Comcast (the parent corporation). Founded in 1912 by Carl Laemmle through a merger of independent producers, it opened its 230-acre studio in California in 1915 ([Britannica](http://www.britannica.com/biography/Carl-Laemmle)). In 2026 it handled the international distribution of *[Michael](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/)*, the [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) biopic, in a year its worldwide grosses cleared $5 billion ([The Music Universe](https://themusicuniverse.com/michael-jackson-biopic-crosses-1-billion-at-global-box-office/); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/universal-chief-reacts-minions-monsters-box-office-drop/)).

The distinction matters because the name "Universal" is used loosely. Reuters documented the chain in 2023 when Comcast president Mike Cavanagh — who runs NBCUniversal — named film chief Donna Langley chairman of the NBCUniversal Studio Group and chief content officer ([Reuters](https://duke.fm/2023/07/06/comcast-president-cavanagh-shakes-up-nbcuniversal-leadership-memo/)). TheWrap calls Comcast "Universal's parent company," while the studio's own labels and partnerships — Illumination, DreamWorks Animation, Focus Features, and first-look deals with Blumhouse, Monkeypaw, and 87North — belong to the studio, not the conglomerate ([TheWrap](http://thewrap.com/universal-box-office-hot-streak-explained/)).

## A 1912 studio inside a 21st-century conglomerate

Carl Laemmle, a German-born immigrant who opened a Chicago nickelodeon in 1906, founded the Independent Motion Picture Co. in 1909 and, in 1912, "merged with smaller companies to form Universal Pictures," opening its 230-acre California studio in 1915 ([Britannica](http://www.britannica.com/biography/Carl-Laemmle)). Laemmle — considered the father of the Hollywood star system, having built early careers around names like Mary Pickford, with future moguls Irving Thalberg and Harry Cohn among his employees — was forced by financial setbacks to sell the company in 1935 ([Britannica](http://www.britannica.com/biography/Carl-Laemmle)). Through the MCA era and beyond, the studio survived where its Golden Age contemporaries were absorbed, arriving in the 2020s as one of the few traditional majors still releasing an industry-leading volume of films — 55 titles a year worldwide at the time of TheWrap's 2024 reporting ([TheWrap](http://thewrap.com/universal-box-office-hot-streak-explained/)).

## The Langley partnership model

Universal's modern run is built on partnerships rather than acquisitions. The roots trace to 2007, when former 20th Century Fox Animation president Chris Meledandri founded Illumination Entertainment and signed a distribution deal with Universal that began with 2010's smash *Despicable Me*; a year later, *Fast Five* introduced Dwayne Johnson to the *Fast & Furious* franchise and turned it into the studio's top moneymaker of the 2010s ([TheWrap](http://thewrap.com/universal-box-office-hot-streak-explained/)). Donna Langley — a 23-year studio veteran promoted to chairman in 2013 — extended the model: a 2014 first-look distribution deal with Jason Blum's Blumhouse, the 2016 acquisition of DreamWorks Animation, an exclusive 2019 deal with Jordan Peele's Monkeypaw after *Get Out*, and a 2019 first-look with David Leitch and Kelly McCormick's 87North ([TheWrap](http://thewrap.com/universal-box-office-hot-streak-explained/)). The 2021 signing of Christopher Nolan and *Oppenheimer* was the capstone: $972 million worldwide, the studio's 10th Best Picture Oscar win, and its first film to take seven Oscars since *Schindler's List* in 1993 ([TheWrap](http://thewrap.com/universal-box-office-hot-streak-explained/)). The result, per TheWrap: four years of record global growth, two straight years leading the domestic box office in market share ($3.36 billion combined), and two of the most profitable years in the studio's history ([TheWrap](http://thewrap.com/universal-box-office-hot-streak-explained/)). The core team held — global distribution chairman Peter Levinsohn, marketing chief Michael Moses, Universal Pictures president Peter Cramer — even as NBCUniversal's own CEO departed ([TheWrap](http://thewrap.com/universal-box-office-hot-streak-explained/)).

## A landmark 2026, and *Michael*'s international engine

2026 became one of the biggest years in Universal's history: worldwide grosses cleared $5 billion — only the third time ever, after 2015 and 2017 — with three of the year's five $1 billion films distributed by Universal: Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey*, *The Super Mario Galaxy Movie* (Illumination and Nintendo), and *Michael* ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/universal-chief-reacts-minions-monsters-box-office-drop/); [Innovation Village](https://innovation-village.com/universal-becomes-first-studio-to-cross-5-billion-at-2026-global-box-office/)). Over 60% of that theatrical revenue came from outside the United States — and *Michael* is the clearest case study ([Innovation Village](https://innovation-village.com/universal-becomes-first-studio-to-cross-5-billion-at-2026-global-box-office/)). Universal Pictures International — led by president Veronika Kwan Vandenberg — distributed *Michael* in most international markets outside the US and Japan, setting biopic records in 31 territories and musical-biopic records in 55 ([The Music Universe](https://themusicuniverse.com/michael-jackson-biopic-crosses-1-billion-at-global-box-office/); [ET Now](https://www.etnownews.com/entertainment/michael-jackson-biopic-michael-crosses-1-billion-the-business-behind-the-biggest-biopic-ever-article-156244963)). "It has been a distinct privilege to bring this groundbreaking film to international audiences," Vandenberg said, adding that decades after his stardom, Michael Jackson "remains a global superstar like no other" ([The Music Universe](https://themusicuniverse.com/michael-jackson-biopic-crosses-1-billion-at-global-box-office/)). The geography tells the story: about $656 million of the film's $1.028 billion total came from international markets — roughly 64% ([ET Now](https://www.etnownews.com/entertainment/michael-jackson-biopic-michael-crosses-1-billion-the-business-behind-the-biggest-biopic-ever-article-156244963)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between Universal Pictures and NBCUniversal?**
Universal Pictures is the film production and distribution company — the studio that makes and releases movies. NBCUniversal is the broader media conglomerate that owns it, and Comcast is NBCUniversal's parent. This profile covers the studio, not the conglomerate ([Reuters](https://duke.fm/2023/07/06/comcast-president-cavanagh-shakes-up-nbcuniversal-leadership-memo/); [TheWrap](http://thewrap.com/universal-box-office-hot-streak-explained/)).

**Who owns Universal Pictures?**
It sits inside NBCUniversal's film arm, the Universal Filmed Entertainment Group, within NBCUniversal — which is run as part of Comcast ([TheWrap](http://thewrap.com/universal-box-office-hot-streak-explained/)).

**Who founded Universal Pictures?**
Carl Laemmle, a German-born immigrant who opened a Chicago nickelodeon in 1906; in 1912 he merged his company with smaller producers to form Universal Pictures and opened its 230-acre California studio in 1915 ([Britannica](http://www.britannica.com/biography/Carl-Laemmle)).

**Who runs Universal Pictures?**
Donna Langley — chairman of the Universal Filmed Entertainment Group since 2013, elevated in July 2023 to chairman of the NBCUniversal Studio Group and chief content officer ([TheWrap](http://thewrap.com/universal-box-office-hot-streak-explained/); [Reuters](https://duke.fm/2023/07/06/comcast-president-cavanagh-shakes-up-nbcuniversal-leadership-memo/)).
