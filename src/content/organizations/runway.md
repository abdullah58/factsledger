## Overview

Runway AI, Inc. — known as Runway or RunwayML — is a New York-based company building generative AI models for video, images, and multimedia content ([Bloomberg](https://www.advisorperspectives.com/articles/2023/06/29/ai-video-startup-runaway-google-nvidia)). It was founded in 2018 by Cristóbal Valenzuela, Alejandro Matamala and Anastasis Germanidis, who met while at art school at NYU and shared a curiosity about AI's creative potential; they began building AI tools for moviemakers, cinematographers and photographers ([TechCrunch](http://techcrunch.com/2023/06/29/runway-a-startup-building-generative-ai-for-content-creators-raises-141m/?ref=promptengineering.org)). Runway helped create the original text-to-image model Stable Diffusion and went on to release the Gen-1 and Gen-2 video generation models ([Bloomberg](https://www.advisorperspectives.com/articles/2023/06/29/ai-video-startup-runaway-google-nvidia)). This profile is about Runway AI, Inc. — not the similarly named venture-debt lender Runway Growth Finance.

## The $1.5 billion breakout

In June 2023, Runway raised a $141 million extension to its Series C from Google, Nvidia and [Salesforce](/organizations/salesforce/) (via Salesforce Ventures), valuing the company at $1.5 billion — triple its December 2022 valuation — and bringing total funding to about $237 million ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/ai-company-runway-valued-15-bln-latest-funding-source-2023-06-29/)). Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang said generative AI was "breathing new life into stories and ideas that were not imaginable" ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/ai-company-runway-valued-15-bln-latest-funding-source-2023-06-29/)). A year later, Runway debuted Gen-3 Alpha, which generates 10-second videos from text prompts with improved motion depiction and frame consistency — and, notably, a provenance system based on the C2PA standard that embeds metadata marking outputs as AI-generated ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2024/06/17/runway-debuts-new-gen-3-alpha-model-generating-videos/)).

## From creative tool to world-model company — and investor

By 2026 Runway had outgrown the "AI video tool" framing. In February it raised $315 million at a $5.3 billion valuation, and by September its annual recurring revenue had doubled since April to $200 million ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/runway-ai-hits-200-million-revenue-and-expands-robotics-capabilities/)). The Paris-based Kinetix research team — specialists in 3D human motion and physically grounded video generation — joined Runway to push its world models into robotics: "The next step is teaching them how to take actions in the world," co-founder and co-CEO Anastasis Germanidis said ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/runway-ai-hits-200-million-revenue-and-expands-robotics-capabilities/)).

Then Runway started investing like the giants it once courted. In March 2026 it launched a $10 million venture fund for pre-seed and seed AI, media, and world-simulation startups — writing checks up to $500,000 — alongside a Builders program offering startups API credits and access to its real-time video-agent API ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/31/exclusive-runway-launches-10m-fund-builders-program-to-support-early-stage-ai-startups/)). Having raised close to $860 million itself, Runway seeded the fund with its existing investors and partners; co-founder Alejandro Matamala-Ortiz framed it as ecosystem-building around "video intelligence" the 150-person company couldn't pursue alone ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/31/exclusive-runway-launches-10m-fund-builders-program-to-support-early-stage-ai-startups/)).
