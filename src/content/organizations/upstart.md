## Overview

Upstart Holdings is a U.S. financial-technology company founded in 2012 by Dave Girouard — who previously worked at Google — along with Paul Gu and Anna Counselman ([Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/markets/usptart-holdings-ceo-interview-rise-of-the-ai-lender/)). Rather than lending directly, the company operates an AI-driven lending platform that works through bank partners, and more than 90% of its loans are fully automated, with no human involvement in the approval decision ([Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/markets/usptart-holdings-ceo-interview-rise-of-the-ai-lender/)).

## The AI underwriting model

Upstart's business model is built around partnerships with banks: partner banks pay Upstart referral fees, and the company's AI assesses a borrower's creditworthiness using factors that go beyond the traditional FICO score ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/upstart-jumps-in-trading-debut-for-fintech-company)). The automation is the point — over 90% of loans on the platform are decided entirely by the model, a level of hands-off underwriting that made the company both a fintech darling and a target for regulatory scrutiny ([Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/markets/usptart-holdings-ceo-interview-rise-of-the-ai-lender/)).

## Boom, bust, and the regulator

Upstart went public in December 2020, pricing its shares at $20 and closing up about 47% on its first day of trading ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/upstart-jumps-in-trading-debut-for-fintech-company)). What followed was one of the more dramatic boom-and-bust arcs of the pandemic-era fintech wave: the stock rose roughly 1,200% from its IPO price before falling roughly 90% ([Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/markets/usptart-holdings-ceo-interview-rise-of-the-ai-lender/)).

Running in parallel was an unusual regulatory relationship. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) had granted Upstart a special no-action/model-approval agreement covering its AI lending model — effectively a regulator's blessing of an algorithmic underwriting approach. That agreement was terminated in 2022 at Upstart's own request ([Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/markets/usptart-holdings-ceo-interview-rise-of-the-ai-lender/)). The company employs around 1,200 people ([Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/markets/usptart-holdings-ceo-interview-rise-of-the-ai-lender/)).
