## Overview

Fannie Mae — the Federal National Mortgage Association — is a [United States](/places/united-states/) government-sponsored enterprise that buys mortgages from lenders, guarantees them, and sells them as mortgage-backed securities, keeping money flowing to homebuyers. Chartered in 1938, it has been a publicly traded, shareholder-owned company since 1968, but has operated under the conservatorship of the Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA) since September 2008. Its guaranty book of business stood at $4.1 trillion in 2026, and it earned $4.2 billion in the third quarter of that year ([Fannie Mae](https://last10k.com/sec-filings/fnma/0000310522-26-000089.htm); [Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/)).

It does not lend directly to homebuyers: it buys mortgages that meet its standards, guarantees timely payment to investors, pools them into mortgage-backed securities (MBS), and either keeps or sells the MBS ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)). In 2024 the company provided $381 billion of liquidity and helped about 1.4 million households buy, refinance, or rent a home, and in the first nine months of 2026 it provided $344 billion ([Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/media/54816/display); [Fannie Mae](https://last10k.com/sec-filings/fnma/0000310522-26-000089.htm)).

## From New Deal agency to shareholder-owned company

Fannie Mae was created by Congress in 1938 through amendments to the National Housing Act, as part of Franklin D. Roosevelt's New Deal, to expand the secondary mortgage market during the Great Depression ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)). In 1954 Congress re-chartered Fannie Mae as a mixed government and private-sector entity ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)). In 1968 Congress split the firm in two: the secondary-market arm kept the Fannie Mae name as a privately owned company, while the government functions became the Government National Mortgage Association, or Ginnie Mae ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)). In 1970 Congress authorized Fannie Mae to buy conventional mortgages and created the Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation, Freddie Mac, to widen the secondary market further ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)).

## How Fannie Mae works: the secondary mortgage market

Fannie Mae runs two businesses, single-family and multifamily. The single-family book covers homes of four or fewer units; in 2024 it stood at $3.6 trillion, with a weighted-average credit score at origination of 753, a mark-to-market loan-to-value ratio of 50% and a serious delinquency rate of 0.56% ([Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/media/54816/display)). The multifamily book, covering buildings of five or more units, was $500 billion at the end of 2024 and grew to $542.5 billion by March 2026 ([Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/media/54816/display); [Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/media/document/pdf/q12026release.pdf)). Revenue comes mainly from guaranty fees charged on the loans it backs, on net revenues of $29.1 billion in 2024 ([Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/media/54816/display); [Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/media/document/pdf/q12026release.pdf)). In 2024 it acquired about 778,000 single-family purchase loans plus 204,000 refinances, and financed about 420,000 multifamily rental units ([Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/media/54816/display)). Together with Freddie Mac, it guaranteed $6.6 trillion in agency mortgage-backed securities at December 2024, about half of all outstanding US mortgage debt ([Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/)). It also pays fees to the [U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development](/organizations/us-department-of-housing-and-urban-development/): in 2024 it recognized $3.8 billion in expenses paid to Treasury, HUD and the FHFA combined ([Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/media/54816/display)).

## Conservatorship: the 2008 rescue and the net-worth sweep

When the housing bubble burst, Fannie Mae lost $58.7 billion in 2008 alone, and Freddie Mac lost $50.1 billion ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)). In September 2008 the FHFA placed both enterprises into conservatorship ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)). The [U.S. Department of the Treasury](/organizations/us-department-of-the-treasury/) agreed Senior Preferred Stock Purchase Agreements, initially committing up to $100 billion per enterprise and later doubling that to $200 billion, receiving $1 billion in senior preferred stock from each plus warrants for 79.9% of its common stock ([Federal Housing Finance Agency](https://www.fhfa.gov/media/32951)). Treasury provided $116.1 billion in support to Fannie Mae and $71.3 billion to Freddie Mac, and neither enterprise has needed support since the second quarter of 2012 ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)). In August 2012 a Third Amendment replaced Treasury's fixed 10% dividend with a variable payment equal to each enterprise's entire quarterly profit — the "net-worth sweep" ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)). Shareholders sued, and in Collins v. Yellen (June 2021) the Supreme Court held the FHFA director's for-cause removal protection unconstitutional but declined to unwind the sweep; a fourth amendment in January 2021 had already ended the variable dividend ([Supreme Court](https://www.cit.uscourts.gov/sites/cit/files/Excerpts%20from%20Collins%20v.%20Yellen.pdf)). Since 2019 the enterprises have been allowed to retain profits and rebuild capital ([Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/)).

## Rent history in Desktop Underwriter — and the Blend connection

Desktop Underwriter (DU), Fannie Mae's automated underwriting system, decides whether a loan is eligible for sale to the company. From 18 September 2021, DU began identifying recurring rent payments in an applicant's bank-statement data, with the borrower's permission ([Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/newsroom/fannie-mae-news/fannie-mae-introduces-new-underwriting-innovation-help-more-renters-become-homeowners?eType=EmailBlastContent&eId=d811c41b-7043-4632-a108-0993f3ac4ea9)). The change is positive-only: consistent rent payments can improve eligibility, but missed or inconsistent payments found in bank data do not count against the applicant ([Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/newsroom/fannie-mae-news/fannie-mae-introduces-new-underwriting-innovation-help-more-renters-become-homeowners?eType=EmailBlastContent&eId=d811c41b-7043-4632-a108-0993f3ac4ea9)). It matters because fewer than 5% of renters have their rent reported on their credit bureau report, and about 20% of the US population has little established credit history — a group in which Black and Hispanic consumers are disproportionately represented ([Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/newsroom/fannie-mae-news/fannie-mae-introduces-new-underwriting-innovation-help-more-renters-become-homeowners?eType=EmailBlastContent&eId=d811c41b-7043-4632-a108-0993f3ac4ea9)). In a Fannie Mae sample, 17% of applicants who had not owned a home in three years and received an unfavourable DU recommendation could have received an Approve/Eligible one if rent history had been considered ([Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/newsroom/fannie-mae-news/fannie-mae-introduces-new-underwriting-innovation-help-more-renters-become-homeowners?eType=EmailBlastContent&eId=d811c41b-7043-4632-a108-0993f3ac4ea9)). That is where [Blend](/organizations/blend/) comes in: Blend Mortgage Suite users can use the DU rent feature with Blend, with Blend-powered data fed directly to DU and 12 months of asset data retrieved to support the review ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2022/01/185708-digital-lending-software-firm-blend-is-helping-to-increase-homeownership-opportunities-by-leveraging-data-and-automation/)). The integration automates the transfer of data from the application portal to the underwriter's desk ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2022/01/185708-digital-lending-software-firm-blend-is-helping-to-increase-homeownership-opportunities-by-leveraging-data-and-automation/)).

## Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac: same rescue, different companies

The two enterprises are often named as a pair, but they are distinct companies with separate charters, books and capital requirements. Fannie Mae was chartered in 1938; Freddie Mac was created in 1970 to compete with it ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)). Both have been in FHFA conservatorship since September 2008 ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)).

| | Fannie Mae | Freddie Mac |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chartered | 1938 | 1970 |
| Treasury support drawn | $116.1 billion | $71.3 billion |
| Capital requirement (2025) | $187 billion | $141 billion |

The figures are from the Congressional Research Service and the Real Estate Roundtable ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html); [Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/)). Fannie Mae is the larger of the two on every measure in the table ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html); [Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/)).

## The long road out: capital, leadership and the exit debate

Conservatorship was not meant to be indefinite, yet more than eighteen years later it continues ([Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/)). The arithmetic explains why: Treasury's liquidation preference on its senior preferred shares stood at $400 billion in the first quarter of 2026, against combined net worth of $179 billion, and Fannie Mae alone faces a $187 billion capital requirement against its $119.6 billion of net worth ([Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/); [Fannie Mae](https://last10k.com/sec-filings/fnma/0000310522-26-000089.htm)). Retiring the government's stake would require a refinancing of massive scale, a taxpayer gift of tens of billions of dollars, or both ([Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/)). Administration officials, FHFA Director Bill Pulte and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent have expressed a desire to end conservatorship, but Bessent's guardrail is the effect on mortgage rates, and a rapid exit is considered unlikely ([Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/)). Without a paid-for explicit government guarantee on their mortgage-backed securities, critics warn rates could rise by 0.2 to 0.8 percentage points ([Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/)). Leadership turned over too: chief executive Priscilla Almodovar stepped down on 22 October 2025, chief operating officer Peter Akwaboah became acting CEO, and John Roscoe and Brandon Hamara became co-presidents ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/capital-markets/fannie-mae-ceo-steps-down-as-white-house-weighs-ipo-131496)). Almodovar had previously worked at [JPMorgan Chase](/organizations/jpmorgan-chase/) and Enterprise Community Partners, and Akwaboah was still acting chief executive when third-quarter 2026 results were reported ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/capital-markets/fannie-mae-ceo-steps-down-as-white-house-weighs-ipo-131496); [Fannie Mae](https://last10k.com/sec-filings/fnma/0000310522-26-000089.htm)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Fannie Mae?** Fannie Mae is a US government-sponsored enterprise that buys mortgages from lenders, guarantees them, and sells them as mortgage-backed securities ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)). Chartered in 1938, it is a publicly traded, shareholder-owned company under FHFA conservatorship since September 2008 ([Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/)).

**Is Fannie Mae part of the government?** No — it is a federally chartered, shareholder-owned corporation, not a government agency, but the FHFA has controlled it as conservator since September 2008 ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)). Treasury holds senior preferred stock and warrants for 79.9% of its common stock ([Federal Housing Finance Agency](https://www.fhfa.gov/media/32951)).

**How is Fannie Mae different from Freddie Mac?** Fannie Mae was chartered in 1938; Freddie Mac was created in 1970 to compete with it ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html)). Fannie Mae drew $116.1 billion in Treasury support against Freddie Mac's $71.3 billion, and its 2025 capital requirement is $187 billion against Freddie Mac's $141 billion ([Congressional Research Service](https://www.EveryCrsReport.com/files/20130813_R42760_167d2d4f0974ca91aba45fd1b0f5cf7418dbc3fd.html); [Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/)).

**Can rent payments help you get a mortgage?** Yes — since September 2021, Fannie Mae's Desktop Underwriter can identify recurring rent payments in bank-statement data, with the borrower's permission, and count consistent payments toward eligibility ([Fannie Mae](https://www.fanniemae.com/newsroom/fannie-mae-news/fannie-mae-introduces-new-underwriting-innovation-help-more-renters-become-homeowners?eType=EmailBlastContent&eId=d811c41b-7043-4632-a108-0993f3ac4ea9)). It is positive-only, and Blend's software can feed the data directly to DU ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2022/01/185708-digital-lending-software-firm-blend-is-helping-to-increase-homeownership-opportunities-by-leveraging-data-and-automation/)).

**Will Fannie Mae leave conservatorship?** There is no approved plan or timetable. Officials want to end it, but the $400 billion Treasury liquidation preference and the gap between Fannie Mae's $119.6 billion net worth and its $187 billion capital requirement make a rapid exit unlikely ([Real Estate Roundtable](https://www.rer.org/resource/fannie-mae-freddie-mac-and-the-future-of-housing-finance-in-the-u-s-fact-sheet-2/); [Fannie Mae](https://last10k.com/sec-filings/fnma/0000310522-26-000089.htm)).
