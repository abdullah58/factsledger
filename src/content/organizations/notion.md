## Overview

Notion is a [San Francisco](/places/san-francisco/), [California](/places/california/) productivity-software company whose all-in-one workspace — notes, documents, wikis, databases and project tracking in a single customisable tool — passed 100 million users in August 2024 ([Notion](https://www.notion.com/blog/100-million-of-you)). Founded in 2013 by Ivan Zhao and Simon Last, it nearly died in 2015, was rebuilt from scratch in Kyoto, Japan, and reached a $10 billion valuation in 2021 and an $11 billion mark in a 2026 employee tender ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)).

The product's pitch is consolidation through flexibility. Teams build a central catalogue of documents and other materials in Notion, organised through a sidebar that works like an index, and mix document formats freely — a strategy document, a to-do checklist, an engineering team's feature-request board — so one cloud tool does work that normally takes several ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2021/10/08/productivity-startup-notion-valued-10b-latest-275m-funding-round/)). That flexibility is also the product's famous difficulty: the customisation options for something as simple as a to-do list can overwhelm new users, which is why "how I use Notion" tutorial videos became a genre of their own ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)). Notion is a direct rival of [ClickUp](/organizations/clickup/) and of [Asana](/organizations/asana/) and [Monday.com](/organizations/monday-com/) in the crowded productivity-software market, and its stated ambition goes further — an "everything app for the office" that might one day challenge Microsoft and [Google](/organizations/google/), which together control 99% of the $52 billion (2022 sales) productivity-suite market, according to [Gartner](/organizations/gartner/) ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)).

## The near-death rebuild

Zhao started Notion on a deliberately abstract idea: that you should be able to do as much with a word processor as you can with a blank piece of paper. He had studied cognitive science at the University of British Columbia — immigrating to Canada with his mother from China when he was 17 — and spent a year learning Silicon Valley at e-textbook startup Inkling before pitching Notion, before he even had a prototype ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)). The pitch worked on a few: a $2 million seed round in 2013, with [First Round Capital](/organizations/first-round-capital/) co-founder Josh Kopelman writing the biggest check after a pitch that wandered into the history of paper, plus angel money from Facebook product chief Mike Vernal, Akshay Kothari ($75,000 from selling his first startup to LinkedIn), and a first $50,000 check from Inkling co-founder Matt MacInnis ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)).

Two years later the company was nearly finished. People did not understand the software editor Zhao had built, he had not found a compelling way to explain it, and the money was almost gone. In the summer of 2015, Zhao and co-founder Simon Last laid off all their employees, sublet the San Francisco office and moved to Kyoto, Japan, to cut costs; a $150,000 emergency loan from Zhao's mother bought them time to reboot ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)). Zhao's own account says they rebuilt Notion four times in those "lost years" — in one version they had the right product but the wrong tech stack, so he and Last went to Japan to rewrite it, which is why their main git repository is still called "notion-next" ([Notion](https://www.notion.com/blog/100-million-of-you)).

The reboot worked. Notion 1.0 launched on Product Hunt in August 2016 and was the site's most popular product of the day, then the week, then the month; within weeks the company, free for individuals and charging power users upwards of $8 a month, was turning a profit ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)). Real traction came with Notion 2.0 in 2018, which added databases and spreadsheets — Zhao himself dates the turnaround to that launch — and the company passed its first million users in 2019 ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1); [Notion](https://www.notion.com/blog/100-million-of-you)). Growth came by word of mouth, with 80% of users outside the [United States](/places/united-states/): students used it for to-do lists and class notes, entrepreneurs replaced the traditional pitch deck with it, and teams at DoorDash, Nike and McKinsey adopted it ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)). Kothari joined as chief operating officer in 2018 — he had run LinkedIn's 1,000-person India operation — spent his first six months answering support tickets, and was later given the co-founder title for building the sales, recruiting and finance teams ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)).

## Funding: from a $2 million seed to a $10 billion valuation — and $11 billion

Notion's funding history is unusual for a company of its size: very little of it, raised very late, on the founders' terms. Zhao was sceptical of raising money and for years turned investors away; it took the uncertainty of the pandemic to convince him to raise rainy-day funds, and even then he called the shots — no board seats, low dilution ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)). [Index Ventures](/organizations/index-ventures/) led a $50 million investment at a $2 billion valuation, announced April 1, 2020 — Forbes reports the deal was agreed in February 2020, within 36 hours of Zhao starting fundraising — bringing total funding to $67 million after a prior $10 million raise at an $800 million valuation ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/04/01/notion-hits-2-billion-valuation-in-new-raise/); [Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)). The raise came as startups braced for disruption from the [COVID-19 pandemic](/events/covid-19-pandemic/), and Notion said the money would last "at least" 10 years; its users had nearly quadrupled from one million ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/04/01/notion-hits-2-billion-valuation-in-new-raise/)).

The October 2021 round is the centrepiece. Notion's user base had grown 500% since the prior round and its enterprise edition was growing 350% year over year; the round took total funding past $300 million, and the platform was used by thousands of organisations including IBM and Samsung ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2021/10/08/productivity-startup-notion-valued-10b-latest-275m-funding-round/)). Zhao offered Sequoia first dibs on $100 million on a Friday morning, if the firm could decide that day; Sequoia agreed within 30 minutes of reviewing the financials, though the $10 billion valuation was "very painful", partner Pat Grady admitted ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)). Between the two big rounds, TikTok virality had nearly broken the company: in January 2021 "how I use Notion" videos overwhelmed the servers, forcing Zhao to pause all product development for six months, when the app had 20 million users ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)).

What the money did not buy was control. None of the venture capitalists who put some $330 million into Notion held a board seat; Zhao added his first outside board member, a financial auditor, only in 2022. Forbes estimated Zhao still owned at least 30% of the company, worth $1.5 billion on a $5 billion secondary-market valuation — a secondary estimate, not a priced round, and notably below the $10 billion the company had raised at ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)). By April 2024 the company had 650 employees across offices in the US, Ireland, India, South Korea and Japan; Zhao estimated half of Y Combinator startups run on Notion, and Forbes estimated 2023 revenue at about $250 million, with the company still profitable ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)).

No new primary funding has followed since 2021. Instead, in January 2026, new investor GIC joined returning investors Sequoia Capital and Index Ventures in buying shares directly from current and former employees — a tender of around $270 million at an $11 billion valuation, with the one-year vesting cliff on options waived so almost every employee could take part ([Notion](https://www.notion.com/blog/gic-sequoia-index-purchase-notion-shares)). The money went to employees, not to the company — and the tender's price, about 10% above the 2021 mark after four years, says as much about Notion's discipline as about its growth.

## From notes app to AI workspace

Notion's second act is AI, and it started earlier than the hype suggests. During an October 2022 company offsite in Cancún, Simon Last received an email from friends at OpenAI offering early access to GPT-4; he and Zhao spent days holed up in their hotel room building a bare-bones AI writing assistant, so Notion was in the market before ChatGPT launched the next month ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)). The assistant entered private alpha in November 2022, drawing nearly two million waitlist signups, and launched to everyone on February 22, 2023 — 20 free AI responses per member, then $10 per member per month ($8 with annual billing), addable to any plan ([Notion](https://www.notion.com/blog/notion-ai-is-here-for-everyone)). In November 2023 came a Q&A bot that surfaces anything stored inside a workspace — ask it for the takeaways from last week's meetings and it pulls them from the documents it judges most important — built mostly on GPT-4, Anthropic's Claude and a few open-source models ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1); [Reworked](https://www.reworked.co/collaboration-productivity/notion-wants-users-not-cios-to-adopt-its-productivity-platform/)).

The product also grew sideways. Notion bought calendar app Cron and work-tracking platform Flowdash in 2022, and in January 2024 relaunched Cron as Notion Calendar, its first standalone app ([Reworked](https://www.reworked.co/collaboration-productivity/notion-wants-users-not-cios-to-adopt-its-productivity-platform/); [Cron](https://www.cron.com/blog/2024-01-17-cron-is-now-notion-calendar)). Forbes reported a February 2024 acquisition that might yield an email client, and a Notion 3.0 release planned for later that year ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)). The bet paid off in revenue terms: Notion told CNBC in September 2025 that annualised revenue had passed $500 million — up from Forbes's ~$250 million estimate for 2023 — as it previewed custom AI agents that work in the background, and co-founder Akshay Kothari said enterprise demand for the AI tools was outrunning supply, naming clients including Kaiser Permanente, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Nvidia and Volvo Cars ([Digital Market Reports](https://digitalmarketreports.com/news/48985/notions-annual-revenue-exceeds-500-million-with-ai-powered-growth/)). The share of customers paying for AI add-ons had risen from 10–20% in 2024 to over 50% by September 2025, and by the end of 2025 more than half of Notion's ARR came from AI-enabled customers, a share that had more than doubled in a year ([Digital Market Reports](https://digitalmarketreports.com/news/48985/notions-annual-revenue-exceeds-500-million-with-ai-powered-growth/); [Notion](https://www.notion.com/blog/gic-sequoia-index-purchase-notion-shares)).

The growth model behind all of this is deliberately bottom-up. Notion did not advertise until August 2022, relying on early adopters to "start a movement": individual users — students, freelancers, design-minded founders — brought it into their workplaces, where it spread team by team rather than through CIO-led sales ([Reworked](https://www.reworked.co/collaboration-productivity/notion-wants-users-not-cios-to-adopt-its-productivity-platform/)). That is also why the numbers skew global: 80% of users, 70% of the businesses using it, and 80% of its business sit outside the United States ([Reworked](https://www.reworked.co/collaboration-productivity/notion-wants-users-not-cios-to-adopt-its-productivity-platform/)). The question now is whether the same grassroots motion works at enterprise scale — Zhao is building a sales team to target larger clients, where usage is often still limited to individual teams, and the competition is the Microsoft–Google duopoly plus smaller rivals like Coda, whose CEO says both companies really compete against "the old set of tools" ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)). Microsoft's Loop collaboration app arrived weeks after Notion's October 2021 round, and Microsoft and Google now push Copilot and Gemini into the same workplaces ([Digital Market Reports](https://digitalmarketreports.com/news/48985/notions-annual-revenue-exceeds-500-million-with-ai-powered-growth/)). One telling data point: at Ramp, nine out of ten of the company's 1,200 employees now use Notion's AI features every month ([Digital Market Reports](https://digitalmarketreports.com/news/48985/notions-annual-revenue-exceeds-500-million-with-ai-powered-growth/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who founded Notion?** Ivan Zhao and Simon Last founded Notion in San Francisco in 2013. Akshay Kothari joined as chief operating officer in 2018 and was later given the co-founder title; some sources list a fuller founding roster including Chris Prucha, Jessica Lam and Toby Schachman ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)).

**How much is Notion worth?** Its last priced round valued it at $10 billion in October 2021 ($275 million led by Sequoia Capital and Coatue). A January 2026 employee tender, in which GIC, Sequoia and Index Ventures bought about $270 million of shares from staff, valued it at $11 billion ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2021/10/08/productivity-startup-notion-valued-10b-latest-275m-funding-round/); [Notion](https://www.notion.com/blog/gic-sequoia-index-purchase-notion-shares)).

**How many people use Notion?** It passed 100 million users in August 2024, according to the company — up from 1 million in 2019/2020 and 20 million in January 2021. The counts are company-reported registered users, not audited figures or paying seats ([Notion](https://www.notion.com/blog/100-million-of-you)).

**What is Notion AI?** An AI layer inside the workspace: a writing assistant launched in February 2023 after a November 2022 alpha, a Q&A bot that searches everything stored in Notion (November 2023), and custom AI agents previewed in September 2025. By end of 2025 over half of Notion's ARR came from AI-enabled customers ([Notion](https://www.notion.com/blog/notion-ai-is-here-for-everyone); [Notion](https://www.notion.com/blog/gic-sequoia-index-purchase-notion-shares)).

**Is Notion a public company?** No. It is private, has raised about $330 million in total, and has not filed to go public. Forbes estimated 2023 revenue at about $250 million and described the company as still profitable; no audited financials exist ([Forbes](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/billionaires/notion-the-ai-app-vying-to-break-microsoft-and-googles-workplace-dominance/92931/1)).
