## Overview

Equity Residential was a [Chicago](/places/chicago/)-based apartment real estate investment trust, founded by Sam Zell and listed on the New York Stock Exchange in August 1993, that grew into one of the largest apartment owners in the [United States](/places/united-states/) before merging with [AvalonBay Communities](/organizations/avalonbay-communities/) in August 2026 and becoming Vivmark Residential ([Nareit](https://www.reit.com/news/articles/sam-zell-architect-of-modern-reit-era-and-former-nareit-chair-dies-at-81); [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://investors.avalonbay.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001104659-26-097833/tm2623381d1_8k.htm)). Under Zell it grew into a $31 billion apartment owner, developer and operator and a member of the S&P 500 ([Nareit](https://www.reit.com/news/articles/sam-zell-architect-of-modern-reit-era-and-former-nareit-chair-dies-at-81)). At the end of 2025, its last full year as a standalone company, it owned 312 rental properties with 85,190 apartment units and reported rental income of $3.09 billion ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260205461247/en/Equity-Residential-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-2025-Results)). Same-store revenues rose 2.6% and net operating income 2.2% in 2025, with physical occupancy of 96.4% and the highest full-year resident retention in the company's history ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260205461247/en/Equity-Residential-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-2025-Results)). In that year it was a net seller, disposing of 11 properties for about $1.1 billion while buying nine for about $637 million, and it returned about $1.38 billion to shareholders through dividends and buybacks ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260205461247/en/Equity-Residential-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-2025-Results)). Its residents were not only young urban renters: the median resident was 34 years old, and a fifth of its units were occupied by residents aged 50 and older ([Nareit](https://www.reit.com/news/reit-magazine/july-august-2017/one-one-equity-residential-ceo-david-neithercut)). Zell died in May 2023, and the company he built survived him by barely three years as an independent name.

## From a student business to a listed REIT

Zell founded Equity Group Investments in 1964 with partner Robert Lurie, after the two had run an apartment management business while attending the University of Michigan, and that business became the predecessor of Equity Residential ([Nareit](https://www.reit.com/news/articles/sam-zell-architect-of-modern-reit-era-and-former-nareit-chair-dies-at-81)). Zell took the company public in August 1993, and it grew fast: within eight years of its initial public offering it became the first apartment REIT to be listed in the S&P 500 ([Nareit](https://www.reit.com/news/reit-magazine/july-august-2017/one-one-equity-residential-ceo-david-neithercut)). David Neithercut, who had joined Zell's Equity Group Investments in 1990 and became Equity Residential's chief financial officer after the listing, was named president and chief executive in 2006 ([Nareit](https://www.reit.com/news/reit-magazine/july-august-2017/one-one-equity-residential-ceo-david-neithercut)). The company was repeatedly recognised by Fortune magazine as one of the world's most admired companies in real estate ([Nareit](https://www.reit.com/news/reit-magazine/july-august-2017/one-one-equity-residential-ceo-david-neithercut)). Early growth was fed by deals as well as development: Starwood Capital Group had sold EQR a portfolio of apartments acquired after the early-1990s savings and loan crisis for a stake of more than 20% of the company ([GlobeSt](https://www.globest.com/amp/2015/10/26/starwood-strikes-5-4b-deal-with-equity-residential/)).

## Repositioning: Archstone, the coastal pivot and the Starwood sale

Neithercut presided over Equity Residential's most significant acquisition, the $9 billion purchase of 60% of the Archstone apartment portfolio from [Lehman Brothers](/organizations/lehman-brothers/) in 2013, won after a multi-year pursuit and the receipt of a $150 million break-up fee ([Nareit](https://www.reit.com/news/reit-magazine/july-august-2017/one-one-equity-residential-ceo-david-neithercut)). The deal accelerated a deliberate transformation: a portfolio that had once spanned 50 markets and consisted largely of suburban garden apartments was refocused on high-density, coastal urban and walkable close-in suburban markets ([Nareit](https://www.reit.com/news/reit-magazine/july-august-2017/one-one-equity-residential-ceo-david-neithercut)). In 2016 the company disposed of close to $7 billion of properties to exit markets it did not see as long-term holdings, leaving the portfolio concentrated in coastal gateway markets ([Nareit](https://www.reit.com/news/reit-magazine/july-august-2017/one-one-equity-residential-ceo-david-neithercut)). The pivot's set piece came in October 2015, when Starwood Capital agreed to buy 23,262 units in 72 communities for $5.365 billion, and Zell said the company was shifting its focus from suburban markets into downtown urban centres ([GlobeSt](https://www.globest.com/amp/2015/10/26/starwood-strikes-5-4b-deal-with-equity-residential/)).

## The end of independence: merger into Vivmark — and the RealPage settlement

On 17 August 2026, Equity Residential completed its merger of equals with AvalonBay Communities, changed its name to Vivmark Residential and began trading as VMRK the next day, with each AvalonBay share converted into 2.793 Vivmark shares ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://investors.avalonbay.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001104659-26-097833/tm2623381d1_8k.htm)). The combined company is dual-headquartered in Chicago and Arlington, Virginia, has more than 185,000 apartment homes, and expects $135 million of annual synergies by March 2028 ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261104847582/en/Vivmark-Residential-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-and-Provides-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2026-Guidance)). At 31 December 2025 Equity Residential owned 312 properties with 85,190 apartment units and reported 2025 rental income of $3.09 billion, a scale that made it an equal partner, not a takeover target ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260205461247/en/Equity-Residential-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-2025-Results)). The merger's final months also brought a legal bill: in May 2026 Equity Residential agreed to pay $56 million to settle claims it conspired with rivals to inflate rents using RealPage's revenue-management software, denying any wrongdoing, in a $218 million second settlement wave that included a $50 million [Greystar](/organizations/greystar/) payment ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/equity-residential-other-apartment-owners-pay-218-million-settle-antitrust-2026-05-15/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Equity Residential still a separate company?** No. On 17 August 2026 it completed a merger of equals with AvalonBay Communities, changed its name to Vivmark Residential, and its shares began trading on the New York Stock Exchange as VMRK the next day ([U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://investors.avalonbay.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001104659-26-097833/tm2623381d1_8k.htm)). The legal and capital structure of the combined company continues that of Equity Residential.

**Who founded Equity Residential?** Sam Zell, who founded Equity Group Investments in 1964 with Robert Lurie after the two ran an apartment management business at the University of Michigan, and took Equity Residential public in August 1993 ([Nareit](https://www.reit.com/news/articles/sam-zell-architect-of-modern-reit-era-and-former-nareit-chair-dies-at-81)). Zell died on 18 May 2023, aged 81, and David Neithercut succeeded him as chairman.

**How big was Equity Residential before the merger?** At 31 December 2025 it owned 312 rental properties with 85,190 apartment units, concentrated in coastal markets such as Southern California, San Francisco, Washington, D.C. and New York, and reported 2025 rental income of $3.09 billion ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260205461247/en/Equity-Residential-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-2025-Results)). Its average monthly rent across the portfolio was $3,092 ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260205461247/en/Equity-Residential-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-2025-Results)). The combined Vivmark has more than 185,000 apartment homes.

**What was the RealPage settlement?** In May 2026 Equity Residential agreed to pay $56 million to settle allegations that it and other large landlords conspired to inflate rents using RealPage's revenue-management software, while denying any wrongdoing ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/equity-residential-other-apartment-owners-pay-218-million-settle-antitrust-2026-05-15/)). It was part of a $218 million second wave of settlements in litigation filed in 2023.
