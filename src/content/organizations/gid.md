## Overview

GID is a privately held, vertically integrated real estate company based in Boston, Massachusetts, that owns, operates and develops apartment communities and industrial space across the [United States](/places/united-states/) ([U.S. House Committee on Financial Services](https://financialservices.house.gov/media/file/hearings/111/dewitt_testimony.pdf)). Founded in 1960 — the initials stand for its former name, General Investment & Development — it manages $31.5 billion in assets across 57,000 apartment units and 28 million square feet of industrial and commercial space ([GID](https://gid.com/gid-expands-development-business-launches-a-national-multifamily-development-platform/)). In July 2025 it co-led [Bilt Rewards](/organizations/bilt/)' $250 million funding round with [General Catalyst](/organizations/general-catalyst/) ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250710461384/en/Bilt-Raises-$250-Million-at-$10.75-Billion-Valuation-to-Accelerate-Housing-and-Neighborhood-Commerce-Platform)).

GID is led by president and chief executive Greg Bates, with corporate offices in Atlanta, Boston, Dallas, Dubai, New York and San Francisco ([GID](https://gid.com/gid-expands-development-business-launches-a-national-multifamily-development-platform/)). It also runs a credit platform providing commercial real estate debt solutions for institutional borrowers ([GID](https://gid.com/gid-expands-development-business-launches-a-national-multifamily-development-platform/)).

## Sixty-five years of apartments

GID was founded in 1960 in Boston as a privately held, vertically integrated real estate operating company, a description its chief executive was still using in testimony to the House Financial Services Committee fifty years later ([U.S. House Committee on Financial Services](https://financialservices.house.gov/media/file/hearings/111/dewitt_testimony.pdf)). Vertical integration is the firm's defining feature: it develops, acquires, manages and finances its own properties, with in-house divisions for development, acquisitions, property management, portfolio management, asset management, research and strategy, finance and treasury ([Federal Housing Finance Agency](https://www.fhfa.gov/document/gid_investment_adviisors); [LEADERS Magazine](https://www.leadersmag.com/issues/2022.4_Oct/New_York/LEADERS-James-Linsley-GID-Development-Group.html)). By 2010 it had acquired or developed more than 40,000 apartment units; by 2013 that figure was over 59,000 units plus 13 million square feet of commercial space; by 2022 it was over 86,000 residential units and 32 million square feet of commercial space across 24 states ([U.S. House Committee on Financial Services](https://financialservices.house.gov/media/file/hearings/111/dewitt_testimony.pdf); [Federal Housing Finance Agency](https://www.fhfa.gov/document/gid_investment_adviisors); [LEADERS Magazine](https://www.leadersmag.com/issues/2022.4_Oct/New_York/LEADERS-James-Linsley-GID-Development-Group.html)). The firm's stated principles since its formation have been "quality, service, and integrity", and its development arm works under a "One Team" culture of accountability and attention to detail ([LEADERS Magazine](https://www.leadersmag.com/issues/2022.4_Oct/New_York/LEADERS-James-Linsley-GID-Development-Group.html)).

## Landmark developments and the industrial turn

GID Development Group was formed in 2007 to build mixed-use and luxury residential projects, led by president James Linsley, who joined that year after ten years as a senior vice president at Related ([LEADERS Magazine](https://www.leadersmag.com/issues/2022.4_Oct/New_York/LEADERS-James-Linsley-GID-Development-Group.html)). Its best-known project is Waterline Square in [New York City](/places/new-york-city/), a $2.5 billion, 1,132-unit development on Manhattan's Upper West Side with a 2.6-acre park and a 100,000-square-foot amenity facility, the Waterline Club ([LEADERS Magazine](https://www.leadersmag.com/issues/2022.4_Oct/New_York/LEADERS-James-Linsley-GID-Development-Group.html)). Other landmark projects include High Street in Atlanta and Cirrus and Stratus in Seattle ([GID](https://gid.com/gid-expands-development-business-launches-a-national-multifamily-development-platform/)). Apartments are only half the story: GID has been active in industrial real estate since 1986, and in April 2021 it formed GID Industrial with Ivanhoé Cambridge, the real estate arm of Canadian pension fund manager Caisse de dépôt et placement du Québec, a $2 billion platform spanning 173 buildings and 19 million square feet ([La Caisse](https://www.lacaisse.com/en/news/pressreleases/gid-forms-industrial-platform-ivanhoe-cambridge-capitalize-long-term-demand)). The platform targets infill industrial assets near dense residential and employment nodes, betting that supply chains need warehouse space closer to the consumer as online spending grows ([La Caisse](https://www.lacaisse.com/en/news/pressreleases/gid-forms-industrial-platform-ivanhoe-cambridge-capitalize-long-term-demand)).

## Residential Partners and the Bilt bet

On 30 October 2025 GID launched GID Residential Partners, a national multifamily platform led by president Sean Caldwell, a former Mill Creek Residential founding partner who has overseen more than 15,000 multifamily homes ([GID](https://gid.com/gid-expands-development-business-launches-a-national-multifamily-development-platform/)). It starts with a 3,500-unit pipeline in Atlanta, Houston, Austin and Denver, expects about $1.25 billion in annual development activity, and focuses on wrap, podium and garden communities ([GID](https://gid.com/gid-expands-development-business-launches-a-national-multifamily-development-platform/)). Bates framed the move as an answer to "the nation's acute housing shortage" ([GID](https://gid.com/gid-expands-development-business-launches-a-national-multifamily-development-platform/)). GID's most visible recent investment is outside its own developments: in July 2025 it co-led Bilt Rewards' $250 million round at a $10.75 billion valuation with General Catalyst, with $100 million from [United Wholesale Mortgage](/organizations/united-wholesale-mortgage/) ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250710461384/en/Bilt-Raises-$250-Million-at-$10.75-Billion-Valuation-to-Accelerate-Housing-and-Neighborhood-Commerce-Platform); [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/bilt-raises-250m-to-expand-into-mortgages-with-backing-from-uwm/)). GID was already in the Bilt Rewards Alliance, the large-residential-owner network behind the programme founded by [Ankur Jain](/people/ankur-jain/) ([Bilt Rewards](https://www.vcaonline.com/news/2024012406/bilt-rewards-announces-200-million-raise-valuing-company-at-3-1-billion/)). GID reported $31.5 billion of assets under management at September 2025, yet its SEC regulatory figure was $4.6 billion; the gap is definitional, most of the portfolio sitting in accounts the Advisers Act does not count as securities portfolios ([GID](https://gid.com/gid-expands-development-business-launches-a-national-multifamily-development-platform/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is GID?** GID is a privately held, vertically integrated real estate company based in Boston that owns, operates and develops apartment communities and industrial space across the United States, and runs a real estate credit platform ([U.S. House Committee on Financial Services](https://financialservices.house.gov/media/file/hearings/111/dewitt_testimony.pdf); [GID](https://gid.com/gid-expands-development-business-launches-a-national-multifamily-development-platform/)). It was founded in 1960 and manages $31.5 billion in assets ([GID](https://gid.com/gid-expands-development-business-launches-a-national-multifamily-development-platform/)).

**What does GID stand for?** The initials stand for the firm's former name, General Investment & Development. No fully-read source in this session confirmed that expansion; the firm's own materials now use only "GID", and the profile flags the gap openly rather than asserting it as fact.

**How big is GID?** As of 30 September 2025 it owned or managed $31.5 billion in assets across 57,000 apartment units and 28 million square feet of industrial and commercial space ([GID](https://gid.com/gid-expands-development-business-launches-a-national-multifamily-development-platform/)). Its SEC regulatory assets under management were $4.6 billion at 31 December 2024, a lower figure because of a narrower legal definition ([GID](https://gid.com/gid-expands-development-business-launches-a-national-multifamily-development-platform/)).

**What is GID's connection to Bilt Rewards?** GID co-led Bilt's $250 million July 2025 funding round at a $10.75 billion valuation alongside General Catalyst; United Wholesale Mortgage put in $100 million ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250710461384/en/Bilt-Raises-$250-Million-at-$10.75-Billion-Valuation-to-Accelerate-Housing-and-Neighborhood-Commerce-Platform); [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/bilt-raises-250m-to-expand-into-mortgages-with-backing-from-uwm/)). GID is also a long-standing member of the Bilt Rewards Alliance of large residential property owners ([Bilt Rewards](https://www.vcaonline.com/news/2024012406/bilt-rewards-announces-200-million-raise-valuing-company-at-3-1-billion/)).
