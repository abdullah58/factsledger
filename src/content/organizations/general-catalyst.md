## Overview

General Catalyst is a global investment and transformation company headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, in the [United States](/places/united-states/), led by chief executive and managing director Hemant Taneja and chairman and managing director Ken Chenault ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/general-catalyst-raises-new-capital/); [Bilt Rewards](https://www.vcaonline.com/news/2024012406/bilt-rewards-announces-200-million-raise-valuing-company-at-3-1-billion/)). Founded in 1999, it began as a venture capital firm backing companies such as [Stripe](/organizations/stripe/) and Airbnb, and has since expanded into company creation, healthcare ownership and large-scale buyouts ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/general-catalysts-8-billion-vc-mega-funds); [General Catalyst](https://www.generalcatalyst.com/stories/fundxii)). In October 2024 it raised about $8 billion in new capital, its largest fundraise ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/general-catalyst-raises-new-capital/)).

The Fund XII capital breaks down as follows:

| Strategy | Amount | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core VC funds | ~$4.5 billion | Seed and growth equity (Ignition, Endurance, Health Assurance) |
| Creation strategy | ~$1.5 billion | Hatching new companies |
| Separately managed accounts | ~$2 billion | Selected limited partners |

The split was announced by Taneja in October 2024, with $6 billion of the total sitting in Fund XII itself ([General Catalyst](https://www.generalcatalyst.com/stories/fundxii); [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/general-catalyst-raises-new-capital/)). The firm stayed busy while much of venture capital slowed: it made 99 funding deals in 2023 and 102 in 2024 up to October, including leading a $130 million Series C for AtoB and participating in a $150 million Series C for the AI coding assistant Codeium ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/general-catalyst-raises-new-capital/)). Its Bilt connection runs deep: it led [Bilt Rewards](/organizations/bilt/)' $200 million January 2024 round at a $3.1 billion valuation, with Chenault becoming Bilt's chairman, and co-led Bilt's $250 million July 2025 round at $10.75 billion with [GID](/organizations/gid/) ([Finovate](https://finovate.com/bilt-rewards-lands-150-million-for-resident-loyalty-program/); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250710461384/en/Bilt-Raises-$250-Million-at-$10.75-Billion-Valuation-to-Accelerate-Housing-and-Neighborhood-Commerce-Platform)).

## From venture capital to transformation company

General Catalyst was founded in 1999 in Cambridge by Joel Cutler and David Fialkow, according to a University of Michigan investment memo — most reference works give 2000, a discrepancy this profile flags rather than resolves ([University of Michigan](https://regents.umich.edu/files/meetings/02-18/2018-02-IX-5.pdf)). Its early bets included Stripe, Airbnb, Instacart and Mistral AI, and it describes itself as an early investor in the French open-source AI company Mistral ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/general-catalyst-raises-new-capital/); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/general-catalysts-8-billion-vc-mega-funds)). In 2024 it combined with La Famiglia in Europe and Venture Highway in India to broaden its seed-stage reach, and says it has hatched 45 companies and counting, including Kayak, Demandware, Livongo and Commure ([General Catalyst](https://www.generalcatalyst.com/stories/fundxii)). Beyond investing, it invented a Customer Value Strategy that funds startups' sales and marketing with non-dilutive capital, and set up the GC Institute to connect startups with governments on public policy ([General Catalyst](https://www.generalcatalyst.com/stories/fundxii)). Taneja's framing is explicit: the firm aims to "transcend the traditional definition of venture capital" ([General Catalyst](https://www.generalcatalyst.com/stories/fundxii)).

## Buying a hospital: the Summa Health experiment

Healthcare is the clearest expression of that ambition. General Catalyst's Health Assurance Transformation Company, or HATCo, signed a letter of intent in January 2024 to acquire Summa Health, the nonprofit Akron, Ohio health system, and convert it to for-profit ([MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/general-catalyst-launches-percepta-ai-modernization-across-enterprises)). The two sides signed a $485 million definitive agreement in November 2024, and Ohio Attorney General Dave Yost gave conditional approval in June 2025, with ten requirements including $30 million in cash and equity for a new community foundation, a three-year restriction on selling equity, and ten years of oversight ([Becker's Hospital Review](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/hospital-transactions-and-valuation/general-catalyst-completes-485m-deal-for-summa-health/)). The deal closed on 1 October 2025, the purchase amount helping Summa pay off $850 million in debt and the proceeds establishing the Trailhead Community Health Foundation of Greater Akron ([Becker's Hospital Review](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/hospital-transactions-and-valuation/general-catalyst-completes-485m-deal-for-summa-health/)). Summa runs more than 30 locations around Akron and Canton, including hospitals, a multispecialty group and the SummaCare health plan ([MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/general-catalyst-launches-percepta-ai-modernization-across-enterprises)). General Catalyst launched Percepta, a fully owned company that puts researchers, engineers and product managers inside enterprises to run AI transformation, with AWS and Anthropic ([MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/general-catalyst-launches-percepta-ai-modernization-across-enterprises)).

## The Janus Henderson take-private

In December 2025 General Catalyst agreed with Trian Fund Management to take asset manager Janus Henderson private in an all-cash deal worth about $7.4 billion ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/trian-general-catalyst-buy-janus-henderson-74-billion-2025-12-22/)). Shareholders were to receive $49.00 per share in cash, an 18% premium to the 24 October 2025 closing price ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/trian-general-catalyst-buy-janus-henderson-74-billion-2025-12-22/)). Trian, Janus Henderson's largest shareholder at 20.6%, had held board seats since 2022; the Qatar Investment Authority and Hong Kong's Sun Hung Kai & Co. were co-investors ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/trian-general-catalyst-buy-janus-henderson-74-billion-2025-12-22/)). Janus Henderson had about $484 billion in assets under management at 30 September 2025, more than 2,000 employees and offices in 25 cities, with chief executive Ali Dibadj to continue leading it ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/trian-general-catalyst-buy-janus-henderson-74-billion-2025-12-22/)). At $7.4 billion, the deal is nearly as large as General Catalyst's $8 billion Fund XII — a measure of how far the firm has moved from venture cheques to buying whole companies ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/trian-general-catalyst-buy-janus-henderson-74-billion-2025-12-22/); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/general-catalysts-8-billion-vc-mega-funds)). In March 2026 Janus Henderson's board rejected a rival $8.6 billion offer from Victory Capital, calling the Trian-General Catalyst agreement the only actionable proposal, on track to close in mid-2026 ([Chief Investment Officer](https://www.ai-cio.com/news/janus-henderson-rejects-victory-capital-acquisition-proposal/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is General Catalyst?** General Catalyst is a global investment and transformation company headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, led by CEO Hemant Taneja and chairman Ken Chenault ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/general-catalyst-raises-new-capital/)). It invests from seed to growth stages and also creates companies, transforms healthcare systems and buys established businesses ([General Catalyst](https://www.generalcatalyst.com/stories/fundxii)).

**How big is General Catalyst's Fund XII?** About $8 billion in new capital was announced in October 2024, including around $4.5 billion for core venture funds, $1.5 billion for the Creation strategy and $2 billion in separately managed accounts ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/general-catalyst-raises-new-capital/)). PitchBook called it the largest haul by a venture capital firm in over two years ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/general-catalysts-8-billion-vc-mega-funds)).

**Why did General Catalyst buy a hospital?** Its HATCo subsidiary bought Akron-based Summa Health for $485 million to use it as a proving ground for the technology its portfolio companies sell, including AI-powered healthcare tools ([MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/general-catalyst-launches-percepta-ai-modernization-across-enterprises)). Taneja called the deal a "generational investment in community health infrastructure" ([Becker's Hospital Review](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/hospital-transactions-and-valuation/general-catalyst-completes-485m-deal-for-summa-health/)).

**What is the Janus Henderson deal?** In December 2025 Trian Fund Management and General Catalyst agreed to acquire asset manager Janus Henderson for about $7.4 billion in cash, at $49 per share ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/trian-general-catalyst-buy-janus-henderson-74-billion-2025-12-22/)). The deal was expected to close in mid-2026, with Janus Henderson going private under CEO Ali Dibadj ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/trian-general-catalyst-buy-janus-henderson-74-billion-2025-12-22/)).
