## Overview

Umbrex is a global community of independent management consultants, launched in 2015 by former McKinsey consultant Will Bachman with Margarita Soto ([Amanda Setili, Fearless Growth](https://setilipodcast.transistor.fm/episodes/a-conversation-with-will-bachman-co-founder-of-umbrex/transcript)). Its members are mostly alumni of McKinsey, Bain, and BCG — over 90% by Umbrex's own account — and the network matches them with client projects while charging members nothing ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/); [Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/join-community/)). Today it counts 8,500+ independent consultants and says it has staffed more than 1,000 projects ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/join-community/)).

The idea came from Bachman's own frustration as a new independent. After five years at McKinsey he left in 2008 to start his own practice, and found he knew how to run consulting projects but not how to run a consulting practice — health insurance, proposals, contracts, pricing ([IdeaMensch](http://ideamensch.com/will-bachman/)). Reading Seth Godin's *Tribes*, he began connecting with other McKinsey alumni running their own firms, and that informal circle grew into Umbrex ([IdeaMensch](http://ideamensch.com/will-bachman/)). The name comes from "umbrella of excellence" ([IdeaMensch](http://ideamensch.com/will-bachman/)). Its stated mission is to create opportunities for top-tier independent management consultants to build relationships, share lessons learned, and collaborate ([IdeaMensch](http://ideamensch.com/will-bachman/)).

Bachman himself is a former nuclear-trained submarine officer who served in the U.S. Navy from 1992 to 2000, then spent five years at McKinsey as an Engagement Manager before going independent ([IdeaMensch](http://ideamensch.com/will-bachman/); [Amanda Setili, Fearless Growth](https://setilipodcast.transistor.fm/episodes/a-conversation-with-will-bachman-co-founder-of-umbrex/transcript)). He is a graduate of Harvard College and Columbia Business School and lives in [New York City](/places/new-york-city/) ([IdeaMensch](http://ideamensch.com/will-bachman/)). Umbrex describes itself as the first global community connecting top-tier independent management consultants with one another ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/)).

## A community, not a firm — and how it makes money

Umbrex's pitch to clients is top-tier talent without a big firm's overhead: consultants who have done the specific work before and can start quickly, available part-time or full-time, junior or senior, remote or on site ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/)). Its members span the full seniority range, from former business analysts a couple of years out of the firm to former partners and senior partners ([Deb Zahn, Craft of Consulting](https://www.craftofconsulting.com/podcast-transcript/episode-77)). The work is organised into 27 service lines grouped into six areas — Strategy, Operations, Organization, Marketing, Sales, and Pro Bono — and 32 industry practices covering every major sector, from Aerospace & Defense to Wine, Beer & Spirits ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/)).

The business model is the part the homepage never explains. Membership is free: Umbrex charges no membership or application fees, membership is highly selective and requires an application, and decisions typically come within 48 hours ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/join-community/)). Revenue comes from the client side: when Umbrex places a consultant, it invoices the consultant through Umbrex and adds a modest margin ([Deb Zahn, Craft of Consulting](https://www.craftofconsulting.com/podcast-transcript/episode-77)). Bachman is candid that the model free-rides, in his words, on McKinsey, Bain, and BCG, which recruit and train consultants straight out of college and business school; Umbrex's members arrive already knowing the craft ([Deb Zahn, Craft of Consulting](https://www.craftofconsulting.com/podcast-transcript/episode-77)). Members are not employees — they remain independent, and Umbrex shares some of its margin with members who refer client work ([Deb Zahn, Craft of Consulting](https://www.craftofconsulting.com/podcast-transcript/episode-77)). Around 2020, Bachman said revenues were in the 8-figures ([IdeaMensch](http://ideamensch.com/will-bachman/)).

## Growth, events, and a second community

Umbrex's growth is best read as a timeline rather than a single number. Around 2020 it was a community of 650 top-tier independent management consultants across 30 countries ([IdeaMensch](http://ideamensch.com/will-bachman/)). By August 2021 Bachman had interviewed 200 people on his podcast and passed 400 episodes ([Amanda Setili, Fearless Growth](https://setilipodcast.transistor.fm/episodes/a-conversation-with-will-bachman-co-founder-of-umbrex/transcript)). Today the join page reports 8,500+ members, 1,000+ projects staffed, and 20+ project opportunities shared with members each week ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/join-community/)).

Community, rather than placement alone, is the product. Umbrex runs 50+ professional development events a year and says it has organised over 250 events in total ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/)). Before COVID it ran about 40 in-person events a year, including two-day professional development events in North America and Europe, alongside a members-only online forum ([Deb Zahn, Craft of Consulting](https://www.craftofconsulting.com/podcast-transcript/episode-77)). Recent mixers have been held in Washington DC, Toronto, Tokyo, Singapore, Los Angeles, London, Denver, Dallas, and [Austin](/places/austin-texas/), and members have attended live Umbrex events in New York City ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/join-community/); [Amanda Setili, Fearless Growth](https://setilipodcast.transistor.fm/episodes/a-conversation-with-will-bachman-co-founder-of-umbrex/transcript)). Members also train each other, volunteering sessions on their own areas of expertise ([Deb Zahn, Craft of Consulting](https://www.craftofconsulting.com/podcast-transcript/episode-77)).

Umbrex is not the only community Bachman runs. Veritux is a second, broader community for alumni of other top firms and former executives who have become independent consultants; its name comes from the Latin for "true guide", and it offers similar services — virtual events, resources, connectivity, a forum, and a weekly email ([Deb Zahn, Craft of Consulting](https://www.craftofconsulting.com/podcast-transcript/episode-77)). Applicants who are a good fit are invited to join either Umbrex, primarily for former McKinsey, Bain, and BCG consultants, or Veritux ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/join-community/)).

Umbrex also produces thought leadership of its own. Bachman hosts *Unleashed — How to Thrive as an Independent Professional*, a podcast on building an independent practice, which had over 230 episodes around 2020 and over 400 by August 2021 ([IdeaMensch](http://ideamensch.com/will-bachman/); [Amanda Setili, Fearless Growth](https://setilipodcast.transistor.fm/episodes/a-conversation-with-will-bachman-co-founder-of-umbrex/transcript)). It publishes a video guide to setting up a consulting practice that more than 1,000 consultants have used, and Bachman has been an invited speaker on independent consulting at Harvard, Wharton, Columbia, and Kellogg ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/)). Umbrex says Business Insider recognised Bachman as a "Power Player in Consulting" ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/)). Less visibly, Umbrex publishes structured company strategy profiles — for example, its profile of [Asure Software](/organizations/asure-software/) analyses the payroll-centred SMB HCM company founded in 1985 and renamed from Forgent Networks in 2009, competing with [ADP](/organizations/adp/), [Paychex](/organizations/paychex/), [Paycom](/organizations/paycom/), and Paylocity ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/resources/company-profiles/asure-software/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Umbrex?**
Umbrex is a global community of independent management consultants, launched in 2015 by former McKinsey consultant Will Bachman with Margarita Soto. Over 90% of its members are alumni of McKinsey, Bain, or BCG, and it matches them with client projects ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/); [Amanda Setili, Fearless Growth](https://setilipodcast.transistor.fm/episodes/a-conversation-with-will-bachman-co-founder-of-umbrex/transcript)).

**How does Umbrex make money if membership is free?**
It charges members nothing — no membership or application fees — and earns revenue on the client side: when it places a consultant, Umbrex invoices the consultant through Umbrex and adds a modest margin, sharing some of that margin with members who refer work ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/join-community/); [Deb Zahn, Craft of Consulting](https://www.craftofconsulting.com/podcast-transcript/episode-77)).

**What is Veritux?**
Veritux is Umbrex's second, broader community, for alumni of firms other than McKinsey, Bain, and BCG and for former executives who became independent consultants. Its name comes from the Latin for "true guide", and it offers similar events, resources, and forums ([Deb Zahn, Craft of Consulting](https://www.craftofconsulting.com/podcast-transcript/episode-77)).

**Who can join Umbrex?**
Membership is highly selective and requires an application, with a decision typically within 48 hours. It is primarily for former McKinsey, Bain, and BCG consultants, from former business analysts to former senior partners; other applicants may be invited to Veritux instead ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/join-community/); [Deb Zahn, Craft of Consulting](https://www.craftofconsulting.com/podcast-transcript/episode-77)).

**What is the Unleashed podcast?**
*Unleashed — How to Thrive as an Independent Professional* is Umbrex's podcast, hosted by Will Bachman, about building a successful independent consulting practice. It had over 230 episodes around 2020 and passed 400 episodes by August 2021 ([IdeaMensch](http://ideamensch.com/will-bachman/); [Amanda Setili, Fearless Growth](https://setilipodcast.transistor.fm/episodes/a-conversation-with-will-bachman-co-founder-of-umbrex/transcript)).
