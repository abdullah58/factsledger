## Overview

The Royal Bank of Canada (RBC) is [Canada](/places/canada/)'s largest bank by market capitalisation — a multinational financial services company founded in Halifax in 1864 as the Merchants Bank of Halifax, with its corporate headquarters in [Toronto](/places/toronto/) and its head office in [Montreal](/places/montreal/). In fiscal 2025 it earned C$20.4 billion on revenue of C$66.6 billion, serving more than 19 million clients in Canada, the United States and 27 other countries ([Royal Bank of Canada](https://www.rbc.com/investor-relations/_assets-custom/pdf/ar_2025_e.pdf); [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3304065/canadas-big-five-banks-all-just-hit-new-52-week-highs-up-27-ytd-this-stock-leads-the-pack)).

This profile is about the Canadian bank trading as RBC, listed in Toronto and New York under the ticker RY — not any other institution that happens to carry "Royal Bank" in its name. Its scale is the story: RBC calls itself Canada's biggest bank and one of the largest in the world by market capitalisation, and its own report puts it 11th globally and 6th in North America on that measure at the end of fiscal 2025 ([Royal Bank of Canada](https://www.rbc.com/investor-relations/_assets-custom/pdf/ar_2025_e.pdf)). The bank's reach extends beyond banking into Canadian college life, too: RBC was among the Corporate Alliance partners of [Colleges and Institutes Canada](/organizations/colleges-and-institutes-canada/) from 2001, alongside [TD Bank](/organizations/toronto-dominion-bank/), [Cisco](/organizations/cisco/) and Dell.

## From a Halifax waterfront bank to a national bank

RBC began as the Merchants' Bank, founded in 1864 on the Halifax waterfront to provide credit to tradesmen importing and exporting through the port; its eight founding merchants included Thomas Kinnear, Edward Kenny and William Cunard ([RBC Royal Bank](https://discover.rbcroyalbank.com/celebrating-150-years-a-look-back-at-where-rbc-came-from/)). It gained a federal charter in 1869, shortly after Confederation, and began crossing borders early — Hamilton, Bermuda in 1882, then Havana and [New York City](/places/new-york-city/) in 1899 ([RBC Royal Bank](https://discover.rbcroyalbank.com/celebrating-150-years-a-look-back-at-where-rbc-came-from/)). In 1901 executives favouring westward expansion won the boardroom debate and the bank took the name Royal Bank of Canada, with branches already operating coast to coast, including in British Columbia for the Klondike gold rush ([RBC Royal Bank](https://discover.rbcroyalbank.com/celebrating-150-years-a-look-back-at-where-rbc-came-from/)).

The centre of gravity moved with the country: headquarters shifted from Halifax to Montreal in 1907, and by 1925 amalgamations with institutions including Union Bank of Canada and Northern Crown had given the bank 18.7 per cent of Canadian bank assets and branches in 801 Canadian communities ([RBC Royal Bank](https://discover.rbcroyalbank.com/celebrating-150-years-a-look-back-at-where-rbc-came-from/)). Place Ville-Marie in Montreal became its headquarters in 1962, and Royal Bank Plaza in Toronto opened in 1976, taking over some head-office functions as Toronto grew as a financial centre ([RBC Royal Bank](https://discover.rbcroyalbank.com/celebrating-150-years-a-look-back-at-where-rbc-came-from/)). Deregulation in 1986 let the bank buy Dominion Securities in 1987, adding a brokerage arm, while the later sale of its American regional retail operations to PNC Financial Services Group for $3.45 billion in 2012 refocused the U.S. business on wealth and capital markets ([RBC Royal Bank](https://discover.rbcroyalbank.com/celebrating-150-years-a-look-back-at-where-rbc-came-from/)).

## Scale and segments in fiscal 2025

RBC's fiscal year ends on 31 October, and 2025 was a record year on its own numbers: total revenue of C$66,605 million, up 16.1 per cent on 2024, and net income of C$20,369 million, up 25.4 per cent, with diluted earnings per share of C$14.07 and a return on common equity of 16.3 per cent ([Royal Bank of Canada](https://www.rbc.com/investor-relations/_assets-custom/pdf/ar_2025_e.pdf)). Total assets reached C$2,325,006 million, assets under management C$1,573,800 million, and the bank employed 96,628 full-time-equivalent staff across 1,263 branches ([Royal Bank of Canada](https://www.rbc.com/investor-relations/_assets-custom/pdf/ar_2025_e.pdf)). Its market capitalisation on the Toronto Stock Exchange was C$287,681 million at 31 October 2025, up 20.8 per cent in a year — and an independent check later put it at $297.46 billion, ahead of Toronto-Dominion Bank at $206.76 billion, making RBC Canada's largest bank and its largest public company ([Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3304065/canadas-big-five-banks-all-just-hit-new-52-week-highs-up-27-ytd-this-stock-leads-the-pack)).

The business is spread across five segments, and no single one dominates. Wealth Management brought in the most revenue in 2025 at C$22.4 billion, ahead of Personal Banking at C$19.9 billion, but Personal Banking earned the most profit at C$7,105 million ([Royal Bank of Canada](https://www.rbc.com/investor-relations/_assets-custom/pdf/ar_2025_e.pdf)). Capital Markets earned C$5,393 million, Wealth Management C$4,289 million, Commercial Banking C$3,020 million and Insurance C$828 million ([Royal Bank of Canada](https://www.rbc.com/investor-relations/_assets-custom/pdf/ar_2025_e.pdf)). In Canada, Personal Banking claims the number-one market share in all key retail products, supported by 1,159 branches and 3,869 ATMs ([Royal Bank of Canada](https://www.rbc.com/investor-relations/_assets-custom/pdf/ar_2025_e.pdf)). Geographically, 63 per cent of fiscal-2025 revenue came from Canada, 26 per cent from the United States and 11 per cent from the rest of the world ([Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3304065/canadas-big-five-banks-all-just-hit-new-52-week-highs-up-27-ytd-this-stock-leads-the-pack)).

RBC earned C$20,369 million in fiscal 2025, up 25.4 per cent on 2024 ([Royal Bank of Canada](https://www.rbc.com/investor-relations/_assets-custom/pdf/ar_2025_e.pdf)) — and the growth was broad rather than concentrated in one division, with Personal Banking, Wealth Management and Capital Markets each earning over C$4 billion, which is why the bank describes itself as an all-weather, diversified bank rather than a purely domestic retail lender.

## The HSBC Canada acquisition

The bank's biggest recent move was the acquisition of HSBC Bank Canada. On 21 December 2023, Chrystia Freeland, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Finance, approved the sale under the Bank Act, subject to strict conditions imposed on RBC ([Department of Finance Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2023/12/government-of-canada-approves-sale-of-hsbc-bank-canada-to-the-royal-bank-of-canada-with-conditions.html)). The conditions protected HSBC's Canadian workforce, required a new Global Banking Hub in Vancouver supporting more than 1,000 jobs and about 440 net-new jobs in British Columbia, added 100 jobs at the Winnipeg client operations centre, kept banking services at a minimum of 33 HSBC branches for four years, waived certain fees for HSBC clients for 18 months, and committed RBC to C$7 billion in financing for affordable-housing construction across Canada, supporting an estimated 25,000 new homes ([Department of Finance Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2023/12/government-of-canada-approves-sale-of-hsbc-bank-canada-to-the-royal-bank-of-canada-with-conditions.html)). The Competition Bureau had reported the deal would not substantially lessen competition, and the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions had no objections and recommended approval ([Department of Finance Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2023/12/government-of-canada-approves-sale-of-hsbc-bank-canada-to-the-royal-bank-of-canada-with-conditions.html)).

RBC completed the acquisition on 28 March 2024, welcoming 4,500 employees and 780,000 clients from [HSBC](/organizations/hsbc/) Canada, and the former HSBC branches opened as RBC locations on 1 April 2024 ([Royal Bank of Canada](https://stockhouse.com/news/press-releases/2024/03/28/rbc-completes-acquisition-of-hsbc-bank-canada)). The deal followed an earlier U.S. expansion under David McKay, appointed President and CEO in 2014: the roughly $5 billion purchase of [Los Angeles](/places/los-angeles/)-based City National Bank in 2015, with its 3,800 employees, expanding RBC's U.S. private and commercial banking and wealth management business ([RBC Royal Bank](https://discover.rbcroyalbank.com/celebrating-150-years-a-look-back-at-where-rbc-came-from/)). Together the two deals show the pattern of RBC's modern growth — buying scale in its second home market, the United States, and consolidating at home in Canada, where its main rivals are Toronto-Dominion Bank, [CIBC](/organizations/cibc/), Bank of Montreal and Bank of Nova Scotia. RBC's own report notes it entered fiscal 2026 as the 6th largest bank in North America by market capitalisation ([Royal Bank of Canada](https://www.rbc.com/investor-relations/_assets-custom/pdf/ar_2025_e.pdf)).

## FAQ

### What is the Royal Bank of Canada?

The Royal Bank of Canada (RBC) is Canada's largest bank by market capitalisation, a multinational financial services company founded in Halifax in 1864 as the Merchants Bank of Halifax. It is listed in Toronto and New York under the ticker RY, with corporate headquarters in Toronto and its head office in Montreal.

### How big is RBC?

In fiscal 2025 RBC reported total revenue of C$66,605 million, net income of C$20,369 million, total assets of C$2,325,006 million and assets under management of C$1,573,800 million. It served more than 19 million clients, employed 96,628 full-time-equivalent staff and returned C$11.3 billion to shareholders.

### Who is RBC's chief executive?

David McKay is RBC's President and CEO, appointed in 2014. He led the bank through the City National Bank purchase in 2015 and the HSBC Bank Canada acquisition completed in 2024, and in fiscal 2025 RBC earned C$20,369 million with a return on common equity of 16.3 per cent under his leadership.

### What did RBC acquire from HSBC?

RBC completed its acquisition of HSBC Bank Canada on 28 March 2024, welcoming 4,500 employees and 780,000 clients. The federal approval carried conditions, including a Vancouver Global Banking Hub, at least 33 branches kept open for four years, and C$7 billion in affordable-housing financing.

### What are RBC's main business segments?

Its five segments are Personal Banking, Commercial Banking, Wealth Management, Insurance and Capital Markets. In fiscal 2025 Wealth Management led on revenue (C$22.4 billion) while Personal Banking led on net income (C$7,105 million); Capital Markets earned C$5,393 million and Commercial Banking C$3,020 million.
