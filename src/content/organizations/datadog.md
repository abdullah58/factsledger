## Overview

Datadog is a New York-based cloud monitoring and observability company founded in 2010 by Olivier Pomel and Alexis Lê-Quôc, two engineers who had worked together at Wireless Generation, an education-technology firm that built data systems for K-12 schools and was later acquired by News Corp ([BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/technology/olivier-pomel-french-open-source-engineer-devops-pioneer-and-co-founder-and-ceo-of-datadog)). The founding insight came directly from that experience: development teams and operations teams worked from incompatible data about the health of their systems, and Datadog's mission was to build a unified monitoring and analytics platform that eliminated those silos ([BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/technology/olivier-pomel-french-open-source-engineer-devops-pioneer-and-co-founder-and-ceo-of-datadog)). Pomel, who has served as chief executive since the founding, is a French engineer with a master's degree in computer science from École Centrale Paris; before Datadog he worked at IBM Research and held software engineering roles at several internet startups, then served as Wireless Generation's vice president of technology, growing its engineering team to roughly 100 engineers ([BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/technology/olivier-pomel-french-open-source-engineer-devops-pioneer-and-co-founder-and-ceo-of-datadog)). The same profile describes him as one of the original authors of the open-source VLC media player — an open-source root that predates his enterprise-software career ([BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/technology/olivier-pomel-french-open-source-engineer-devops-pioneer-and-co-founder-and-ceo-of-datadog)).

## What the organization is known for

Datadog is best known for its cloud-native observability platform, which combines metrics, traces, and logs to provide a unified view of infrastructure and application performance ([ZDNet](https://www.zdnet.com/article/datadog-to-raise-nearly-650-million-in-ipo/)). By the time of its 2019 IPO filing it served more than 8,000 customers and estimated its market opportunity at $35 billion ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/datadog-files-to-go-public-with-quick-revenue-growth-slim-losses-and-history-of-profitability/)). It stood out in the 2019 IPO class for pairing fast growth with comparatively slim losses: revenue grew from $100.8 million in 2017 to $198.1 million in 2018, reaching $153.3 million in the first half of 2019 against a $13.7 million operating loss ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/datadog-files-to-go-public-with-quick-revenue-growth-slim-losses-and-history-of-profitability/)). Its dollar-based net retention was 146% in the first half of 2019, meaning existing customers substantially increased their spending year over year ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/datadog-files-to-go-public-with-quick-revenue-growth-slim-losses-and-history-of-profitability/)). The September 2019 market debut was strong: shares priced at $27 opened at $40.35, about 49% higher, valuing the company at roughly $7.8 billion ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/datadog-debuts-above-ipo-target-range-15096457)). Before the listing, Cisco had approached Datadog with a takeover offer that Bloomberg reported would have significantly exceeded the post-IPO valuation; Datadog reportedly rebuffed it ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/datadog-debuts-above-ipo-target-range-15096457)).

## Products and services

Datadog sells a software-as-a-service platform for monitoring infrastructure, applications, and logs, with integrations for [Amazon Web Services](/organizations/amazon-web-services/), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud ([ZDNet](https://www.zdnet.com/article/datadog-to-raise-nearly-650-million-in-ipo/)). From its origins in infrastructure monitoring, the company expanded under Pomel into application performance monitoring, log management, security, user-experience monitoring, and database monitoring ([BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/technology/olivier-pomel-french-open-source-engineer-devops-pioneer-and-co-founder-and-ceo-of-datadog)). Its February 2021 acquisitions added an observability data pipeline — Timber's Vector, which collects, enriches, and routes logs and other telemetry — and application-security technology in Sqreen's runtime application self-protection ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2021/02/11/datadog-acquires-timber-sqreen-reports-lower-expected-guidance/)). The platform is designed to sit at the center of a customer's monitoring setup: as early as 2016, TechCrunch noted it could pull data from point tools such as New Relic, AppDynamics, and Splunk into a single dashboard for IT staff ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1260755)). Named partners have included AWS, Slack, MongoDB, and Fastly ([ZDNet](https://www.zdnet.com/article/datadog-to-raise-nearly-650-million-in-ipo/)).

## Ownership, founders and leadership

Datadog was founded by Olivier Pomel and Alexis Lê-Quôc, who together held more than 22% of the company at the IPO — Pomel, the chief executive, just over 14% and Lê-Quôc over 8% ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/datadog-files-to-go-public-with-quick-revenue-growth-slim-losses-and-history-of-profitability/)). The largest outside shareholders ahead of the listing were Index Ventures with just over 20%, OpenView Venture Partners with 16%, and ICONIQ Capital with 11.3% ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/datadog-files-to-go-public-with-quick-revenue-growth-slim-losses-and-history-of-profitability/)). Pomel has remained chief executive from the 2010 founding through the IPO and beyond ([BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/technology/olivier-pomel-french-open-source-engineer-devops-pioneer-and-co-founder-and-ceo-of-datadog)). In 2016 he and Lê-Quôc were named Ernst & Young Entrepreneurs of the Year for the New York region ([BBN Times](https://www.bbntimes.com/technology/olivier-pomel-french-open-source-engineer-devops-pioneer-and-co-founder-and-ceo-of-datadog)).

## Funding and acquisitions

Datadog raised $147.9 million in venture funding before going public, including a $94.5 million Series D in January 2016 led by ICONIQ Capital, with participation from Index Ventures, OpenView Ventures, Amplify Partners, and Contour Ventures ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/datadog-files-to-go-public-with-quick-revenue-growth-slim-losses-and-history-of-profitability/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1260755)). The September 2019 IPO raised roughly $648 million ([ZDNet](https://www.zdnet.com/article/datadog-to-raise-nearly-650-million-in-ipo/)). In February 2021 the company acquired Timber Technologies, maker of the Vector observability data pipeline, and Sqreen, an application-security platform, both for undisclosed sums ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2021/02/11/datadog-acquires-timber-sqreen-reports-lower-expected-guidance/)). Timber, founded in 2016, had raised $5.8 million in venture funding; Sqreen, founded in 2015, had raised $18 million, including a $14 million round in April 2019 from investors including Greylock Partners and Y Combinator ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2021/02/11/datadog-acquires-timber-sqreen-reports-lower-expected-guidance/)).

## Regulation, licenses and compliance record

No regulatory enforcement actions against Datadog surfaced in the sources read for this profile. The company's public compliance record consists principally of two self-disclosed security incidents. In July 2016, Datadog detected unauthorized activity on several production infrastructure servers, including a database storing user credentials, and forced a password reset on all user and administrator accounts; its chief security officer said passwords were protected with bcrypt hashing and unique salts, and that the company's monitoring agents were unaffected ([Threatpost](https://threatpost.com/datadog-forces-password-reset-following-breach/119179/)). In January 2023, following the breach at CI/CD provider CircleCI, Datadog said one of its RPM package-signing keys and its passphrase had potentially been exposed; it rotated the key and shipped newly signed packages out of caution, stating it had no indication the key was actually leaked or misused and that its official package repositories were not compromised ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/datadog-rotates-rpm-signing-key-exposed-in-circleci-hack/)).

## Competitors and partners

In 2016 Pomel named legacy vendors IBM, HP, BMC, and CA as competitors, while saying the strongest competition came from open-source solutions and companies building their own tooling; he positioned Datadog as broader than point tools such as New Relic, AppDynamics, and Splunk ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1260755)). The company's platform integrates with Amazon Web Services, [Microsoft](/organizations/microsoft/) Azure, and [Google](/organizations/google/) Cloud, and its named partners have included AWS, Slack, MongoDB, and Fastly ([ZDNet](https://www.zdnet.com/article/datadog-to-raise-nearly-650-million-in-ipo/)).
