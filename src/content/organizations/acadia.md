## Overview

Acadia is a derivatives risk and margin firm headquartered in Norwell, Massachusetts, that runs one of the financial system's least visible but most systemically important networks: a platform moving over $1 trillion of collateral between derivatives counterparties every day ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/2022/lseg-to-acquire-acadia)). Founded as AcadiaSoft — its co-founder Craig Welch formed the company in 2003, according to a 2025 Massachusetts court decision — it automated the margin call and grew into the industry's shared utility for uncleared derivatives ([JD Supra](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/acadiasoft)).

In April 2023 the [London Stock Exchange Group](/organizations/london-stock-exchange-group/) completed its takeover of the firm, ending a relationship that began with a minority stake in 2018 ([Asset Servicing Times](https://www.assetservicingtimes.com/assetservicesnews/clearingandsettlementarticle.php?article_id=14526&navigationaction=clearingandsettlementnews&newssection=Clearing%20and%20Settlement)).

Acadia's importance is out of proportion to its fame. Its products span every over-the-counter derivative asset class and connect more than 2,000 market participants, from the largest banks to buy-side firms ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/2022/lseg-to-acquire-acadia)). This profile is not about the cloud-computing joint venture of the same name launched by Cisco, EMC, VMware and Intel in 2009 — it is about the derivatives firm, formerly AcadiaSoft, now an LSEG Post Trade business ([eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/blogs/storage-station/emc-cisco-intel-startup-acadia-picks-former-hp-ceo-as-ceo/)).

## From margin calls to a market utility

AcadiaSoft began with the most boring problem in finance: the margin call. Its flagship product, MarginSphere, was a central messaging service that let derivatives counterparties exchange margin calls, substitutions and interest statements electronically instead of by email and phone ([Global Custodian](https://www.globalcustodian.com/acadiasoft-appoints-chris-walsh-as-coo/)). By 2012 the banks that helped develop it — Deutsche Bank, Goldman Sachs, HSBC and J.P. Morgan — were joined on the platform by Bank of America Merrill Lynch, Barclays, Credit Suisse and Morgan Stanley ([Global Custodian](https://www.globalcustodian.com/acadiasoft-appoints-chris-walsh-as-coo/)). The firm was built as an industry collaborative backed by 16 major market participants and infrastructures, and its Hub standardised margin calculation, reconciliation, communication, disputes and collateral transfer, while AcadiaPlus later covered the whole risk-mitigation lifecycle from exposure calculation to settlement ([Traders Magazine](https://assets-global.website-files.com/6086ce0b372be99b99ffd266/60c0e068140c2663b501f75c_AcadiaSoft_MarketsMedia1.8.19.pdf)). By the 2021 rebrand to Acadia, more than 1,600 firms used the platform ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210621005385/en/AcadiaSoft-Is-Now-Acadia)).

## The regulation that built the business

AcadiaSoft was created as a direct response to the financial crisis and the regulations that followed it, above all the Uncleared Margin Rules, which began phasing in from September 2016 ([Traders Magazine](https://assets-global.website-files.com/6086ce0b372be99b99ffd266/60c0e068140c2663b501f75c_AcadiaSoft_MarketsMedia1.8.19.pdf)). The rules forced firms trading derivatives outside clearing houses to post and collect margin on a standardised timetable — and suddenly every bank and hedge fund needed exactly what AcadiaSoft sold. By January 2020, chief executive Chris Walsh said 99% of firms then in scope for the rules were engaged with AcadiaSoft, with about 300 more firms due in 2020 and 800 in 2021 ([Traders Magazine](https://assets-global.website-files.com/6086ce0b372be99b99ffd266/60c0e068140c2663b501f75c_AcadiaSoft_MarketsMedia1.8.19.pdf)). The same logic drove the 2021 acquisition of Dublin-based Quaternion, which added risk analytics to a firm that had started in pure collateral messaging ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210621005385/en/AcadiaSoft-Is-Now-Acadia)). Regulation made Acadia's plumbing mandatory; the firm spent a decade making itself the standard pipe.

## The LSEG arc

LSEG's courtship of Acadia took five years. It bought a circa 16% minority stake in 2018, alongside a collaboration between its LCH SwapAgent service and AcadiaSoft to automate the non-cleared margin process ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/2018/lseg-acquires-minority-stake-acadiasoft)). On 19 December 2022 LSEG agreed to buy the rest; the announcement disclosed no price and the deal needed regulatory approval, with Acadia to sit in LSEG's Post Trade division under Daniel Maguire ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/2022/lseg-to-acquire-acadia)). The takeover closed in April 2023, and that July Acadia launched Settlement Manager, a centralised settlement tool built on the LSEG SWIFT gateway ([Asset Servicing Times](https://www.assetservicingtimes.com/assetservicesnews/clearingandsettlementarticle.php?article_id=14526&navigationaction=clearingandsettlementnews&newssection=Clearing%20and%20Settlement)). The stat that frames the deal: Acadia's network moves over $1 trillion in collateral daily across more than 2,000 participants ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/2022/lseg-to-acquire-acadia)) — and Reuters later reported the price at about $700 million, implying LSEG paid for a utility, not a startup.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Acadia?**
Acadia is a derivatives risk and margin firm headquartered in Norwell, Massachusetts, providing automated uncleared-margin processing and integrated risk services across all OTC derivative asset classes, connecting over 2,000 market participants ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/2022/lseg-to-acquire-acadia)). It was formerly called AcadiaSoft.

**Who founded Acadia?**
Craig Welch formed AcadiaSoft in 2003, according to a 2025 Massachusetts Appeals Court decision, and Dan Moyse was a co-founder; Welch sold his stock back to the company for $4.7 million in 2015 ([JD Supra](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/acadiasoft); [Global Custodian](https://www.globalcustodian.com/acadiasoft-appoints-chris-walsh-as-coo/)). Chris Walsh joined as COO in 2012 and later became CEO.

**When was Acadia founded?**
Sources disagree: a court decision says Welch formed AcadiaSoft in 2003, directories give 2005, and LSEG's acquisition announcement says Acadia was established in 2009. This profile follows the court record (2003) and flags the disagreement openly rather than silently picking one date.

**How much did LSEG pay for Acadia?**
LSEG's December 2022 announcement did not disclose the terms, but Reuters reported in October 2026 that LSEG acquired Acadia for about $700 million ([London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/2022/lseg-to-acquire-acadia); [Reuters](https://yall1067.com/2026/10/22/lsegs-two-decades-of-major-deals-partnerships-and-strategic-pivots/)). The $700 million figure is attributed to Reuters, not confirmed by LSEG.

**Is Acadia still independent?**
No. LSEG completed its takeover in April 2023, and Acadia now sits in LSEG's Post Trade division, with its CEO reporting to LSEG's Group Head of Post Trade ([Asset Servicing Times](https://www.assetservicingtimes.com/assetservicesnews/clearingandsettlementarticle.php?article_id=14526&navigationaction=clearingandsettlementnews&newssection=Clearing%20and%20Settlement); [London Stock Exchange Group](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/2022/lseg-to-acquire-acadia)). Its Settlement Manager product was built on the LSEG SWIFT gateway after the takeover closed.
