## Overview

Business Wire is a press-release distribution newswire — a distributor of company-issued news, not a journalism outlet — founded in 1961 by Lorry I. Lokey and owned since 2006 by Berkshire Hathaway as a wholly owned subsidiary ([Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/warren-buffetts-berkshire-hathaway-to-buy-business-wire,113379)). It electronically disseminates full-text corporate news releases and other content to the media, the Internet, online services, databases, and the investment community; at the time of the Berkshire acquisition it was moving about 1,000 releases a day across 150 countries in 45 languages ([Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/warren-buffetts-berkshire-hathaway-to-buy-business-wire,113379)). For this site's sourcing purposes, a Business Wire release is first-party content from the issuing company — useful for what a company said, never as independent verification.

## From a journalist's startup to a global newswire

Lorry I. Lokey — Stanford class of 1949, onetime editor of the *Stanford Daily*, a veteran journalist and public-relations executive — launched Business Wire in 1961 and ran it for 45 years ([STANFORD](https://stanfordmag.org/contents/a-philanthropist-at-heart); [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/international-news/internationalwarren-buffett's-berkshire-hathaway-buys-out-business-wire-20089.html)). By 2006 it had grown into a multi-channel delivery network reaching news agencies, financial-information providers, and web news services across North America, Europe, Asia, Latin America, the Middle East, and Africa, with 24 U.S. offices plus Paris, Frankfurt, London, Brussels, Tokyo, and Sydney, and had posted double-digit growth in 2005 ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/international-news/internationalwarren-buffett's-berkshire-hathaway-buys-out-business-wire-20089.html); [Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/warren-buffetts-berkshire-hathaway-to-buy-business-wire,113379)). Lokey, who lived modestly and gave away more than $800 million — over 90 percent of his wealth — died on October 1, 2022, in Atherton, California, at 95 ([STANFORD](https://stanfordmag.org/contents/a-philanthropist-at-heart)).

## The 2006 Berkshire Hathaway acquisition

On January 17, 2006, Berkshire Hathaway announced an agreement to buy Business Wire; terms were not disclosed ([Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/warren-buffetts-berkshire-hathaway-to-buy-business-wire,113379)). The company would operate as a wholly owned subsidiary, its management team remaining in place and day-to-day operations unaffected — the classic Berkshire pattern ([Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/warren-buffetts-berkshire-hathaway-to-buy-business-wire,113379)). Warren Buffett's quoted rationale: "In making this acquisition of Business Wire, we have followed our blueprint of buying profitable companies that are industry leaders, yet have significant growth potential" ([Editor & Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/warren-buffetts-berkshire-hathaway-to-buy-business-wire,113379)). The acquisition completed in early 2006; CEO Cathy Baron Tamraz said the response from clients, partners, and employees had been "overwhelmingly positive" ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/international-news/internationalwarren-buffett's-berkshire-hathaway-buys-out-business-wire-20089.html)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What does Business Wire do?**
It distributes full-text corporate press releases and regulatory filings to the media, online services, databases, and the investment community. It does not produce journalism — its releases are written by the issuing companies.

**Who owns Business Wire?**
Berkshire Hathaway, which acquired it in 2006 as a wholly owned subsidiary. The financial terms were never disclosed.

**Who founded Business Wire?**
Lorry I. Lokey, a journalist and PR executive (Stanford '49), who launched it in 1961, sold it to Berkshire Hathaway in 2006, and died in 2022 at 95.

**Is a Business Wire press release an independent source?**
No. For FactsLedger's sourcing rules, a release distributed via Business Wire is first-party content from the company that issued it — citable for what that company said, never as independent verification of the claim.
