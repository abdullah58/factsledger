## Overview

Camber Creek is a venture capital firm based in the Washington, D.C. area that invests only in technology for the real estate industry — so-called proptech — and it was among the first firms anywhere to specialise in the sector. Founded in 2011 by Casey Berman, it grew from $6 million raised from friends and family to more than $500 million in assets under management by 2022, and to nearly $1 billion by 2026 ([Washington Business Journal](https://greatink.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Washington-Biz-Journal-This-local-proptech-investor-just-raised-325M.-Heres-where-its-looking-to-spend.-03.09.22.pdf); [Real Estate Weekly](https://rew-online.com/vc-firm-camber-creek-closes-325m-fund-iv-for-investment-in-real-estate-technology/); [My Startup World](https://mystartupworld.com/dubai-future-district-fund-commits-to-camber-creek/)).

This profile covers the venture firm, not any place or business sharing the Camber Creek name. It is based in the [United States](/places/united-states/), with its D.C.-area office in Rockville, Maryland and further offices in New York and San Francisco ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201027005322/en/); [Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/startups/camber-creek-closes-30-million-fund-to-invest-in-more-real-estate-startups/)). Its model is hands-on and concentrated: small portfolios of 10 to 15 companies per core fund, tested with real customers before any money is committed, and backed by a network of more than 300 real estate groups that double as both investors and customers ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2022/04/camber-creek-casey-berman-proptech-investment/); [Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2023/02/camber-creek-100-million-proptech-fund/)).

## The funds: from $6 million to nearly $1 billion

Camber Creek's fundraising tells the story of proptech itself. Berman launched the firm in 2011 with $6 million from friends and family, at a time when less than $100 million a year flowed into the whole proptech sector ([Washington Business Journal](https://greatink.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Washington-Biz-Journal-This-local-proptech-investor-just-raised-325M.-Heres-where-its-looking-to-spend.-03.09.22.pdf); [Global Property Inc](https://globalpropertyinc.com/2021/05/26/proptech-hits-puberty-insights-from-camber-creeks-casey-berman/)). A $30 million second fund followed in June 2018, by which point the firm had reached $100 million in assets under management ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/startups/camber-creek-closes-30-million-fund-to-invest-in-more-real-estate-startups/); [Washington Business Journal](https://greatink.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Washington-Biz-Journal-This-local-proptech-investor-just-raised-325M.-Heres-where-its-looking-to-spend.-03.09.22.pdf)). Fund III closed oversubscribed at $155 million in October 2020, Fund IV doubled that to $325 million in February 2022, and an Opportunity Fund of more than $100 million followed in 2023 for follow-on bets on existing winners ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201027005322/en/); [Real Estate Weekly](https://rew-online.com/vc-firm-camber-creek-closes-325m-fund-iv-for-investment-in-real-estate-technology/); [Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2023/02/camber-creek-100-million-proptech-fund/)).

## The Beta Lab: diligence by trial, not by pitch

Camber Creek's distinguishing method is what it calls the Beta Lab: during due diligence it puts a startup's product in front of its own limited partners — real estate owners and operators — and watches how real customers use it before deciding whether to invest ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2022/04/camber-creek-casey-berman-proptech-investment/)). The aim is to get a product into the hands of multiple customers and collect feedback within seven to 14 days, with no fee or warrant attached to the trial ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2022/04/camber-creek-casey-berman-proptech-investment/)). The firm tests a company against its three closest competitors before choosing one, and it may start working with a company at pre-seed or seed stage long before it invests ([Global Property Inc](https://globalpropertyinc.com/2021/05/26/proptech-hits-puberty-insights-from-camber-creeks-casey-berman/); [Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2022/04/camber-creek-casey-berman-proptech-investment/)).

That network is the firm's real asset. Its limited partners include public pensions, foundations, endowments, brokerage houses, lending groups, family offices and high-net-worth real estate groups, and the LPs collectively own more than one million apartment units ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2022/04/camber-creek-casey-berman-proptech-investment/)). The partner team brings more than 60 years of real estate industry experience and more than 30 years of venture investing, and in 2019 the firm added Mitchell Schear — formerly president of Vornado/Charles E. Smith from 2003 to 2017 — as executive partner ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201027005322/en/); [Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/washington-dc/technology/former-vornado-exec-mitchell-schear-joins-proptech-venture-capital-firm-100051)). Berman's stated philosophy is "provide value first" — help the startup and the investor before asking for anything in return ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2022/04/camber-creek-casey-berman-proptech-investment/)).

## Portfolio: Bilt Rewards and the exits

Camber Creek's best-known bet is [Bilt Rewards](/organizations/bilt/), the renter loyalty programme founded by [Ankur Jain](/people/ankur-jain/) that launched in June 2021 out of the [Kairos](/organizations/kairos/) startup studio ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/24/bilt-rewards-brings-in-200m-led-by-general-catalyst-doubles-valuation-to-3-1b/)). Camber Creek joined Bilt's $150 million growth round in October 2022, led by [Left Lane Capital](/organizations/left-lane-capital/) at a $1.5 billion valuation alongside [Fifth Wall](/organizations/fifth-wall/), [Greystar](/organizations/greystar/), [Invitation Homes](/organizations/invitation-homes/), [Smash Capital](/organizations/smash-capital/), [Wells Fargo](/organizations/wells-fargo/), [Prosus](/organizations/prosus/) and Kairos ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/real-estate-property-tech/unicorn-startup-bilt-rewards-homeownership/)). It participated again in January 2024, when [General Catalyst](/organizations/general-catalyst/) led a $200 million round that doubled Bilt's valuation to $3.1 billion ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/24/bilt-rewards-brings-in-200m-led-by-general-catalyst-doubles-valuation-to-3-1b/)). Bilt's co-branded [Mastercard](/organizations/mastercard/), issued by Wells Fargo, lets renters earn points on rent with no transaction fees ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/24/bilt-rewards-brings-in-200m-led-by-general-catalyst-doubles-valuation-to-3-1b/)).

The wider portfolio spans the real estate lifecycle: VTS, Notarize, Flex, Measurabl, Latch, Curbio, FlyHomes, HappyCo, Building Engines, Fundrise, CompStak, WhyHotel and Bowery Valuation among them ([Real Estate Weekly](https://rew-online.com/vc-firm-camber-creek-closes-325m-fund-iv-for-investment-in-real-estate-technology/); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201027005322/en/)). Two exits anchor the track record: TaskEasy was sold to WorkWave and Building Engines to JLL ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2023/02/camber-creek-100-million-proptech-fund/)). The firm was also an early investor in VTS, which reached a $1 billion valuation, and it backed Latch, whose building operating system Berman called "Proptech 2.0" before Latch went public through a SPAC with Tishman Speyer ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/washington-dc/technology/former-vornado-exec-mitchell-schear-joins-proptech-venture-capital-firm-100051); [Global Property Inc](https://globalpropertyinc.com/2021/05/26/proptech-hits-puberty-insights-from-camber-creeks-casey-berman/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Camber Creek?** Camber Creek is a venture capital firm founded in 2011 by Casey Berman that invests exclusively in technology companies transforming the real estate industry ([Real Estate Weekly](https://rew-online.com/vc-firm-camber-creek-closes-325m-fund-iv-for-investment-in-real-estate-technology/)). It describes itself as the original VC firm focused on real estate technology and is based in the Washington, D.C. area, with offices in New York and San Francisco ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201027005322/en/)).

**How big is Camber Creek?** Its fourth core fund closed at $325 million in February 2022, when assets under management passed $500 million ([Real Estate Weekly](https://rew-online.com/vc-firm-camber-creek-closes-325m-fund-iv-for-investment-in-real-estate-technology/)). A May 2026 announcement put assets at nearly $1 billion with more than 47 portfolio companies, though that figure comes from a single lower-tier source and is attributed rather than settled ([My Startup World](https://mystartupworld.com/dubai-future-district-fund-commits-to-camber-creek/)).

**What is the Beta Lab?** The Beta Lab is Camber Creek's diligence method: before investing, it introduces a startup's product to its limited partners — real estate owners and operators — and gathers customer feedback within seven to 14 days ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2022/04/camber-creek-casey-berman-proptech-investment/)). The trial carries no fee or warrant, and the firm tests a company against its three closest competitors before choosing one ([Global Property Inc](https://globalpropertyinc.com/2021/05/26/proptech-hits-puberty-insights-from-camber-creeks-casey-berman/)).

**What companies has Camber Creek backed?** Its best-known investment is Bilt Rewards, which it backed in the $150 million 2022 round and the $200 million 2024 round ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/real-estate-property-tech/unicorn-startup-bilt-rewards-homeownership/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/24/bilt-rewards-brings-in-200m-led-by-general-catalyst-doubles-valuation-to-3-1b/)). Others include VTS, Notarize, Flex, Measurabl, Latch and Curbio, with exits including TaskEasy to WorkWave and Building Engines to JLL ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2023/02/camber-creek-100-million-proptech-fund/)).

**When was Camber Creek founded?** 2011, according to the firm's own releases and most trade reporting ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20201027005322/en/); [Real Estate Weekly](https://rew-online.com/vc-firm-camber-creek-closes-325m-fund-iv-for-investment-in-real-estate-technology/)). Some secondary profiles give 2009 instead; no fully-read source in this session established the 2009 date, so this profile follows 2011 and flags the variant openly.
