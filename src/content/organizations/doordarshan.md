**Doordarshan** is India's public-service television broadcaster: an autonomous broadcaster founded by the Government of India on 15 September 1959 and, since 1997, one of the two divisions of [Prasar Bharati](/organizations/prasar-bharati/) ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf); [The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2023/Sep/16/64-years-of-doordarshans-unforgettable-tales-iconic-works-2615359.html)). Its network spans 35 satellite channels produced from 66 studio centres, and DD Free Dish — India's only free-to-air DTH service — reaches about 45 million households ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf)). It is the television arm that carries government programming from ["Mann Ki Baat"](/creative-works/mann-ki-baat/) to the animated ["Krish, Trish and Baltiboy"](/creative-works/krish-trish-and-baltiboy/) series ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/why-air-doordarshans-revenue-from-pm-modis-mann-ki-baat-fell-by-90-in-the-last-3-yrs/699680/); [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/animation-characters-krish-trish-and-batliboy-to-recount-tales-of-unsung-heroes-of-indias-freedom-struggle/articleshow/104405243.cms)).

The Ministry of Information & Broadcasting describes Doordarshan as "the face of India's metamorphosis from a modest experiment in public service telecasting" to a global digital-communications player ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf)). Its 67th foundation day fell on 15 September 2026 ([Akashvani News](https://newsonair.gov.in/doordarshan-marks-67-years-of-broadcasting-reaching-nearly-100-of-indias-population/)).

## From an experimental telecast to a national network

Doordarshan began on 15 September 1959, when President Rajendra Prasad inaugurated its first Delhi broadcast — an experiment built on closed-circuit TV equipment a leading European company had gifted after an international exposition ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf); [The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2023/Sep/16/64-years-of-doordarshans-unforgettable-tales-iconic-works-2615359.html)). Regular service started in 1965 around the national capital, reached Mumbai and Amritsar by 1972 and seven more cities by 1975 — all as the television wing of All India Radio ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf)). On 1 April 1976 television was split from radio and Doordarshan became a department of the [Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](/organizations/ministry-of-information-and-broadcasting/), gaining its National Institute of Design signature image and Pandit Ravi Shankar's signature tune ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2023/Sep/16/64-years-of-doordarshans-unforgettable-tales-iconic-works-2615359.html)). India's first colour telecast came on 15 August 1982 with the national programme ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf)); institutional autonomy followed on 23 September 1997, when Prasar Bharati was established as an autonomous corporation under the 1990 Act ([Akashvani News](https://newsonair.gov.in/doordarshan-marks-67-years-of-broadcasting-reaching-nearly-100-of-indias-population/)).

## Structure and scale

The ministry describes Doordarshan as one of India's largest broadcasting organisations by studio and transmitter infrastructure ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf)). Its September 2023 figures: 35 satellite channels, 66 studio centres, a DD DTH earth station at Todapur in Delhi, 55 transmitters of varying power — the 35 channels produced by 66 Doordarshan Kendras ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf); [The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2023/Sep/16/64-years-of-doordarshans-unforgettable-tales-iconic-works-2615359.html)). DD India reaches 190+ countries, and Prasar Bharati holds 41 programme-exchange MoUs with foreign broadcasters ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf)). DD Free Dish, the country's only free-to-air DTH service, hosts 167 TV channels — 91 Doordarshan channels (51 co-branded educational) plus 76 private ones ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf)). Prasar Bharati's own news service credits the network with 24+ languages and dialects and near-100% population reach via satellite and DTH ([Akashvani News](https://newsonair.gov.in/doordarshan-marks-67-years-of-broadcasting-reaching-nearly-100-of-indias-population/)).

## Channel portfolio

DD National is the flagship channel of the Doordarshan network ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf)). Alongside it, DD News, DD Bharati, DD India and DD Kisan were named — with Doordarshan National — as the live television carriers of the Prime Minister's "Mann Ki Baat" broadcast ([IndianTelevision.com](https://old.indiantelevision.com/regulators/ib-ministry/no-policy-announcements-its-simply-mann-ki-baat-161206)). DD Sports has been upgraded to DD Sports HD, while DD India carries the network's international presence — including on the global OTT platform Yupp TV across the US, UK, Europe, the Middle East, Singapore, Australia and New Zealand ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf)). The network also runs regional channels from its 66 Kendras; DD Arunprabha was created for Arunachal Pradesh and launched by the Prime Minister on 9 February 2019 ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf)).

## Government programming on Doordarshan: two batch-level cases

What the history-focused coverage rarely mentions is Doordarshan's role as the television arm for flagship government programming — the angle this batch documents. "Krish, Trish and Baltiboy: Bharat Hain Hum" premiered its first season simultaneously on Doordarshan, Netflix and Prime Video on 15 October 2023, telecast across DD in 12 Indian and 7 international languages ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/animation-characters-krish-trish-and-batliboy-to-recount-tales-of-unsung-heroes-of-indias-freedom-struggle/articleshow/104405243.cms)). Its second season followed the same simultaneous-carriage model from 1 December 2024 — streaming on Doordarshan, Netflix, Prime Video and Prasar Bharati's own Waves OTT platform, with an All India Radio radio series airing at 10:30 am on Sundays, fifteen minutes before the animation aired on Doordarshan at 11 am ([Afaqs](https://www.Afaqs.com/news/ott-streaming/animated-series-on-freedom-fighters-to-stream-on-doordarshan-prime-video-netflix-and-waves-7599512)). The Prime Minister's monthly "Mann Ki Baat" address is likewise carried across the Doordarshan network: a July 2021 report put the figure at 34 Doordarshan channels plus 91 private satellite channels, generating Rs 30.8 crore in advertising revenue since 2014 ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/why-air-doordarshans-revenue-from-pm-modis-mann-ki-baat-fell-by-90-in-the-last-3-yrs/699680/)) — consistent with the "34 DD channels" figure documented in this batch's "Mann Ki Baat" profile.

DD Free Dish's scale is the citation-magnet stat: about 45 million households watch through it (FICCI E&Y 2023, via the ministry's 2023 backgrounder) — which implies the free-to-air public network's household footprint rivals any commercial DTH platform, even as private channels dominate the paid-television market ([Press Information Bureau / Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/sep/doc2023915251801.pdf)).

## Frequently asked questions

**When was Doordarshan founded?**
Doordarshan was founded on 15 September 1959, when then-President Rajendra Prasad inaugurated India's first television broadcast in Delhi as an experimental service. It became a regular daily service in 1965 while still part of All India Radio, and was separated into its own department in 1976.

**Who runs Doordarshan today?**
Doordarshan is one of the two divisions of Prasar Bharati, India's autonomous public broadcasting corporation, established on 23 September 1997 under the Prasar Bharati Act, 1990. It functions as an autonomous public-service broadcaster under the Government of India.

**Is Doordarshan free to watch?**
Yes, via DD Free Dish — India's only free-to-air DTH service, which requires only a one-time investment of about Rs 1,500–2,000 for a set-top box and dish antenna, with no monthly subscription. It hosts 167 TV channels and reaches about 45 million households, per the FICCI E&Y 2023 report.

**Does Doordarshan still matter in the streaming era?**
It operates 35 satellite channels from 66 studio centres, offers video-on-demand, a NewsOnAIR mobile app and YouTube channels, and runs DD India in 190+ countries alongside Prasar Bharati's Waves OTT platform. It also remains the television arm for flagship government programming such as "Mann Ki Baat" and "Krish, Trish and Baltiboy."
