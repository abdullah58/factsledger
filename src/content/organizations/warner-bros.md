## Overview

Warner Bros. spent its first century becoming a Hollywood institution — founded by four brothers in 1923, a pioneer of synchronized sound, home to the *Harry Potter* franchise, HBO and DC Studios. [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece) It spent its 103rd year as the most contested prize in media history. In October 2026, a California federal judge approved the settlement that finally cleared Paramount Skydance's $110 billion takeover of [Warner Bros. Discovery](/organizations/warner-bros-discovery/) — ending a bidding war that saw [Netflix](/organizations/netflix) agree to a $72 billion deal, lose it to a hostile Paramount bid, and walk away. [AFP](https://iol.co.za/entertainment/2026-10-01-paramount-cleared-to-take-over-warner-bros-discovery/) [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece)

## A century of mergers, ending in the biggest one

The studio's merger history reads like a syllabus of American media consolidation. The Warner brothers' 1923 founding led to the sound era, the 1990 Time Warner combination, and in 2022 the $43 billion merger of WarnerMedia and Discovery that created Warner Bros. Discovery. [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece) The endgame began on June 9, 2025, when WBD announced it would split into two companies — streaming and studios on one side, cable on the other — effectively putting the crown jewels on the auction block. [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece)

## The bidding war, week by week

The auction was a three-month siege. On November 18, 2025, WBD's board told Paramount Skydance it would need to sweeten its bid to $30 a share — valuing the company at $74.34 billion — and three days later WBD received preliminary bids from Paramount, Comcast and Netflix, asking all to improve their offers. [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece) A second round followed on December 1, including a mostly-cash offer from Netflix, before Paramount accused WBD of running an unfair sale process that favored Netflix, in a letter reported by CNBC on December 4. [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece)

On December 5, 2025, Netflix agreed to buy WBD's film and TV studios and streaming division for $72 billion — $27.75 a share, $82.7 billion in enterprise value. [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece) Four days later, Paramount Skydance — the company run by David Ellison — went hostile with a $108.4 billion all-cash bid at $30 a share. [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece) WBD's board rejected the hostile bid on December 17, saying it lacked adequate financing assurances; Paramount responded on December 23 by amending the offer to include a $40.4 billion personal guarantee from Larry Ellison. [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece)

The fight rolled into 2026: WBD rejected the amended bid on January 7; Paramount sued on January 12 to force disclosure of the Netflix deal's details and announced plans to nominate its own directors; Netflix amended its bid to an all-cash offer on January 20 and secured unanimous WBD board approval without raising the $82.7 billion price; Paramount extended its hostile tender offer to February 20. [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece) U.S. senators grilled Netflix co-CEO Ted Sarandos at a February 3 hearing over the deal's competitive effects, and Trump — after months of commentary — said on February 5 that he would stay out of the bidding war. [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece) Netflix declined to raise its offer on February 26, 2026, and the next day WBD agreed to the Paramount Skydance takeover at an $81 billion equity value. [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece) The Justice Department's antitrust division cleared the deal on June 12, 2026 — and the Trump administration approved it the same month without demanding business changes, per the AFP wire. [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/variety/paramount-skydance-and-warner-bros-discovery-merger-a-timeline-of-the-110-billion-deal/article71494273.ece) [AFP](https://iol.co.za/entertainment/2026-10-01-paramount-cleared-to-take-over-warner-bros-discovery/)

## The settlement that closed the deal

Twelve states, led by California, had sued to block the merger, arguing it would create a media behemoth capable of driving up film and television prices. [AFP](https://iol.co.za/entertainment/2026-10-01-paramount-cleared-to-take-over-warner-bros-discovery/) On September 21, 2026, Paramount settled with them, and on October 1 U.S. District Judge Araceli Martinez-Olguin signed the consent decree — a five-year package of commitments enforceable by the court. [AFP](https://iol.co.za/entertainment/2026-10-01-paramount-cleared-to-take-over-warner-bros-discovery/) The commitments include 30 theatrical releases a year for the first two years and 32 a year for the three after that, plus a board designed to protect CNN's editorial independence. [AFP](https://iol.co.za/entertainment/2026-10-01-paramount-cleared-to-take-over-warner-bros-discovery/)

The financing reveals how the deal got done: about $24 billion in equity from the sovereign wealth funds of Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Abu Dhabi, with Oracle co-founder Larry Ellison providing major funding and a personal guarantee. [AFP](https://iol.co.za/entertainment/2026-10-01-paramount-cleared-to-take-over-warner-bros-discovery/) Leadership was settled the same week: Mattel CEO Ynon Kreiz — who led the toymaker's *Barbie* film-era expansion — was named co-CEO of the combined company, alongside David Ellison. [AFP](https://iol.co.za/entertainment/2026-10-01-paramount-cleared-to-take-over-warner-bros-discovery/) Paramount had won the February 2026 bidding war against Netflix for a stable of assets spanning Warner Bros. Pictures, CNN and HBO Max. [AFP](https://iol.co.za/entertainment/2026-10-01-paramount-cleared-to-take-over-warner-bros-discovery/)
