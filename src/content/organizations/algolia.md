## Overview

Algolia is a search-as-a-service company founded in Paris in 2012 by Nicolas Dessaigne and Julien Lemoine, now based in [San Francisco](/places/san-francisco/), that sells a hosted search API so other companies never have to build search themselves ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2014/01/21/algolia-provides-spotlight-for-the-web-with-its-turbocharged-realtime-search-api/)). It joined [Y Combinator](/organizations/y-combinator/)'s Winter 2014 cohort, raised $315 million by its July 2021 Series D at a $2.25 billion valuation, and rebuilt its product around AI hybrid search after acquiring Search.io in 2022 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/07/28/search-api-startup-algolia-raises-150-million-at-2-25-billion-valuation/); [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/algolia-improves-site-search-functionality-with-search-io-acquisition)). Its customers have included Slack, Stripe, Twitch, Medium and Zendesk, and by 2025–2026 the company said it served more than 18,000 businesses and powered 1.75 trillion searches a year ([Silicon UK](https://www.silicon.co.uk/press-release/algolia-named-a-leader-in-2025-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-search-and-product-discovery)).

## An offline mobile tool that customers pulled into the cloud

Algolia did not start as a cloud service. Dessaigne and Lemoine first built an offline search engine that could be embedded in mobile apps and run locally on the device, but it found no clear market, and customers kept asking for the queries to run on Algolia's servers instead — so the founders pivoted to a hosted search API ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2014/01/21/algolia-provides-spotlight-for-the-web-with-its-turbocharged-realtime-search-api/); [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2020/04/we-will-begin-to-see-a-true-ai-search-evolution-interview-with-algolias-ceo-nicolas/)). The product they landed on was a developer-friendly hosted engine for database objects — the small chunks of text inside an e-commerce store, a movie database or a CRM, rather than whole web pages — with results updating on every keystroke, which TechCrunch compared to Apple's Spotlight on the web ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2014/01/21/algolia-provides-spotlight-for-the-web-with-its-turbocharged-realtime-search-api/)). Early customers included TVShow Time and Socialcam, and by January 2014 the company had raised $1.6 million from Index Ventures, Alven Capital and Point Nine Capital, was a member of the Paris accelerator TheFamily, and had just been accepted into Y Combinator's latest batch ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2014/01/21/algolia-provides-spotlight-for-the-web-with-its-turbocharged-realtime-search-api/)).

The pivot's logic stayed with the company: sell search as an API that developers can wire in with a few lines of code, instead of asking each customer to build and run its own engine. By April 2020 Algolia said it was on a run rate to power over one trillion searches across more than 8,600 customers, including Stripe, Slack, Discovery, Medium, LVMH, Decathlon, Zendesk and Birchbox, with six offices across North America, Europe and Japan and 350 employees ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2020/04/we-will-begin-to-see-a-true-ai-search-evolution-interview-with-algolias-ceo-nicolas/)). Dessaigne described the ambition as building a "borderless" company from the start, neither French nor American, aimed at developers — a market that is the same worldwide ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2020/04/we-will-begin-to-see-a-true-ai-search-evolution-interview-with-algolias-ceo-nicolas/)).

## The funding ladder to unicorn status

[Accel](/organizations/accel/) led all three of Algolia's first institutional rounds — an $18.3 million Series A in 2015, a $53 million Series B in June 2017, and a $110 million Series C in October 2019, when Salesforce Ventures joined as a new investor ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/06/08/algolia-raises-53-million-for-its-search-engine-api/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/10/15/algolia-finds-110m-from-accel-and-salesforce-for-its-search-as-a-service-used-by-slack-twitch-and-8k-others/)). The customer count tracked the money: 3,000 customers and 25 billion searches a month at the Series B, more than 8,000 at the Series C, and over 10,000 by the Series D ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/06/08/algolia-raises-53-million-for-its-search-engine-api/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/07/28/search-api-startup-algolia-raises-150-million-at-2-25-billion-valuation/)).

Around the same time the company crossed $100 million in revenue: CEO Bernadette Nixon told Inc. in March 2021 that Algolia had surpassed $100 million in revenue, with its fiscal year ending January 2021 delivering its best two quarters, and that annual recurring revenue was over $100 million ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/peter-cohan/algolias-ceo-has-a-vision-for-google-challenger.html)). One lesser-known marker of how embedded Algolia was in the startup ecosystem: when [Brex](/organizations/brex/) ran its private launch, it accumulated about 1,000 customers including Flexport, Algolia and [Affirm](/organizations/affirm/) before opening publicly in June 2018 ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/brex-launches-credit-card-for-startups-raises-57-million-from-y-combinator-paypal-founders-others)).

## From keyword search to AI retrieval

For its first decade Algolia's edge was speed on keyword matching; the AI turn came by acquisition. In September 2022 it bought Search.io, whose Neuralsearch engine used a hashing technique to make vector search cheap enough to run at scale without specialised hardware, and set out to combine keyword and vector search in a single API ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/algolia-improves-site-search-functionality-with-search-io-acquisition)). The result, NeuralSearch, launched in May 2023: it reads a query's meaning as well as its exact words, and the company said early users saw about 70% fewer searches returning zero results and conversion uplifts of up to 20% ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/new-breed-search-with-ai-assist-combines-vector-database-keywords)). Frasers Group, an early user, reported a roughly 65% drop in zero-result searches and up to a 17% conversion uplift across two of its brands, while Everlane reported 9% increases in both click-through and conversion rates ([BusinessWire](https://www.finanznachrichten.de/nachrichten-2023-05/58970705-algolia-launches-ai-powered-algolia-neuralsearch-the-world-s-fastest-hyper-scalable-and-cost-effective-vector-and-keyword-search-api-004.htm/)).

Analyst recognition followed the rebuild: Gartner named Algolia a Leader in its inaugural 2024 Magic Quadrant for Search and Product Discovery, positioning it furthest in Completeness of Vision among 18 vendors, and named it a Leader again in 2025, when the company was pitching its search index as the retrieval layer for AI agents ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240517530189/en/Algolia-Named-a-Leader-in-2024-Gartner-Magic-Quadrant-for-Search-and-Product-Discovery); [Silicon UK](https://www.silicon.co.uk/press-release/algolia-named-a-leader-in-2025-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-search-and-product-discovery)). Leadership changed with the strategy: Dessaigne had handed the CEO role to Bernadette Nixon in May 2020, saying someone with stronger go-to-market experience was better placed to realise the company's potential ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/05/19/algolia-gets-a-new-ceo-as-founder-steps-down/)). On April 2, 2026, Stephen Lynch — previously General Manager and EVP of Enterprise at AlphaSense — was appointed CEO, succeeding Nixon, who had stepped down at the end of December and remains on the board ([Algolia](https://www.algolia.com/about/news/algolia-appoints-stephen-lynch-as-chief-executive-officer)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What does Algolia do?**
Algolia sells search as a service: a hosted API that lets a website or app add fast, typo-tolerant search over its own content — products, articles, records — with results updating on every keystroke, instead of the customer building and running its own search engine ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2014/01/21/algolia-provides-spotlight-for-the-web-with-its-turbocharged-realtime-search-api/)).

**Who founded Algolia?**
Nicolas Dessaigne and Julien Lemoine founded it in Paris in 2012, after working together on search technology; Dessaigne was CEO until May 2020, when Bernadette Nixon took over, and Stephen Lynch succeeded her in April 2026 ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2020/04/we-will-begin-to-see-a-true-ai-search-evolution-interview-with-algolias-ceo-nicolas/); [Algolia](https://www.algolia.com/about/news/algolia-appoints-stephen-lynch-as-chief-executive-officer)).

**How much has Algolia raised, and what is it worth?**
It raised $315 million by its July 2021 Series D — a $150 million round led by Lone Pine Capital at a $2.25 billion post-money valuation, more than four times the Series C valuation ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/07/28/search-api-startup-algolia-raises-150-million-at-2-25-billion-valuation/)).

**What is Algolia NeuralSearch?**
Launched in May 2023, it combines vector-based semantic search (from the acquired Search.io) with Algolia's keyword engine in a single API, so natural-language queries return relevant results even without exact keyword matches; early users reported sharply fewer zero-result searches ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/new-breed-search-with-ai-assist-combines-vector-database-keywords)).

**Is Algolia a public company?**
No. It is privately held, venture-funded, and has not filed for an IPO. Its last disclosed valuation was $2.25 billion at the July 2021 Series D, and its revenue figures come from CEO statements rather than public filings ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/07/28/search-api-startup-algolia-raises-150-million-at-2-25-billion-valuation/)).

## What this profile does not claim

- This profile does not assert a market ranking for Algolia. Inc.'s 2021 column calls it the number-two search engine, but that is the columnist's framing of company-supplied volume figures, not an independently verified market share.
- It does not state a single current customer or search count. The figures moved every year — 8,600 customers in 2020, 10,000 in 2021, 17,000 in 2023, 18,000-plus in 2025–2026 — so each is dated in the text.
- Revenue above $100 million is stated as CEO Bernadette Nixon's statement to Inc. in 2021, not as an audited figure; Algolia is private and does not publish financials.
- It does not state a $2.3 billion valuation or $334.2 million in total funding; those aggregator figures conflict with the contemporaneous $2.25 billion and $315 million, and are flagged in the open items above.
- It does not state Julien Lemoine's later role: his reported move to an adviser role in December 2022 was not verified from a tier1 or tier2 source read in full.
- Algolia is not a consumer search engine. It powers the search box inside other companies' websites and apps; it does not crawl the web or offer a public search destination like Google.
