## Overview

**Bloody Flicks** is a UK horror blog at bloody-flicks.co.uk, run by **Paul Downey** — whom the **Grimmfest** festival site identifies as the person who "runs the excellent horror site Bloody Flicks" ([Grimmfest](https://www.grimmfest.com/paul-downey-talks-to-us-about-his-book-the-shark-is-roaring-the-story-of-jaws-the-revenge-and-you-can-win-a-signed-copy/)). The site publishes horror news, reviews and reader competitions, champions independent horror and cult cinema, and stages its own annual event, the **Bloody Flicks Awards**, at the Boiler Works in Stoke-on-Trent ([Horror News Network](https://horrornews.net/172049/bloody-flicks-awards-returns-for-2023/)). In July 2025 it covered the North American collector's-edition Blu-ray/DVD release of [*The Bunny Game*](/creative-works/the-bunny-game/) ([Bloody Flicks](http://bloody-flicks.co.uk/2025/07/09/previously-banned-the-bunny-game-released-in-north-america/)).

## Paul Downey, the operator behind the site

Bloody Flicks is a one-operator outlet in the classic genre-blog mould, and its operator has a public footprint of his own. Grimmfest's interview with **Paul Downey** — about his book *The Shark Is Roaring: The Story of Jaws The Revenge*, following his earlier *It's Me, Billy: Black Christmas Revisited* (co-written with **Dave Hastings**) — opens by noting that "Paul runs the excellent horror site Bloody Flicks," independently confirming who is behind it ([Grimmfest](https://www.grimmfest.com/paul-downey-talks-to-us-about-his-book-the-shark-is-roaring-the-story-of-jaws-the-revenge-and-you-can-win-a-signed-copy/)). The interview also establishes Downey's standing in the UK genre scene: a film-history obsessive whose *Jaws: The Revenge* book features the first interview with star **Lorraine Gary** in over 30 years, and who was about to judge "my short film festival The Bloody Flicks Awards" in Stoke-on-Trent ([Grimmfest](https://www.grimmfest.com/paul-downey-talks-to-us-about-his-book-the-shark-is-roaring-the-story-of-jaws-the-revenge-and-you-can-win-a-signed-copy/)). The site's commercial footprint is real too: in September 2026 it teamed up with **Picturehouse Cinemas** on a competition giving readers the chance to win merchandise for the queer horror *Leviticus*, released in [UK](/places/united-kingdom/) cinemas on 2 September 2026 ([Bloody Flicks](https://bloody-flicks.co.uk/2026/09/01/competition-win-leviticus-merch-ahead-of-uk-release/)).

## The Bloody Flicks Awards

The site's most distinctive asset is its own film festival. The **Bloody Flicks Awards** is an annual event that ran physically in 2020, went virtual in 2021, and returned to a physical edition screening short films and one feature on 25 February 2023 at the Boiler Works in Stoke-on-Trent, according to **Horror News Network** ([Horror News Network](https://horrornews.net/172049/bloody-flicks-awards-returns-for-2023/)). The 2023 edition offered five audience-voted categories — Best Film, Best Foreign Film, Best Director, Best Actor/Actress and Best Music — with winners announced on the Bloody Flicks website and social channels, and entries taken through **FilmFreeway** ([Horror News Network](https://horrornews.net/172049/bloody-flicks-awards-returns-for-2023/)). The audience vote is the point: five categories decided by attendees rather than a jury marks this as a grassroots fan event, not an industry showcase — which implies its value to the site is community-building rather than prestige. Festival Director Paul Downey describes the site as "a continued champion of the independent horror and cult cinema scene" ([Horror News Network](https://horrornews.net/172049/bloody-flicks-awards-returns-for-2023/)).

## Covering The Bunny Game's North American release

On 9 July 2025 Bloody Flicks published "Previously banned The Bunny Game released in North America," reporting that **Danse Macabre** was releasing a collector's edition of **Adam Rehmeier's** *The Bunny Game* on Blu-ray and DVD in summer 2025 ([Bloody Flicks](http://bloody-flicks.co.uk/2025/07/09/previously-banned-the-bunny-game-released-in-north-america/)). The article frames the film as the "provocative and unflinching debut feature" that launched Rehmeier's directing career — noting his later *Dinner in America* and *Snack Shack*, and his then in-post-production *Carolina Caroline* and *Elegy for an American Dream* — and as still banned in the UK ([Bloody Flicks](http://bloody-flicks.co.uk/2025/07/09/previously-banned-the-bunny-game-released-in-north-america/)). Its distinctive contribution is the **Julian Richards** angle: the article reports that Richards (*The Last Horror Movie*) discovered the film at the Downtown Heavy Metal Horror Film Festival in Los Angeles and acquired it for **Jinga Films**, the world sales agent that represented *A Serbian Film*, with Jinga's Danse Macabre label handling the release and its special features (behind-the-scenes featurette, teaser, trailer) ([Bloody Flicks](http://bloody-flicks.co.uk/2025/07/09/previously-banned-the-bunny-game-released-in-north-america/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who runs Bloody Flicks?**
Paul Downey. The Grimmfest festival site identifies him as the person who "runs the excellent horror site Bloody Flicks." Beyond the blog, Downey is a film author (*It's Me, Billy: Black Christmas Revisited*, co-written with Dave Hastings; *The Shark Is Roaring: The Story of Jaws The Revenge*) and Festival Director of the Bloody Flicks Awards.

**What is the Bloody Flicks Awards?**
An annual film festival run by the site. It was a physical event in 2020, went virtual in 2021, and returned physically on 25 February 2023 at the Boiler Works in Stoke-on-Trent, screening short films and one feature. The 2023 categories — Best Film, Best Foreign Film, Best Director, Best Actor/Actress, Best Music — were voted on by the audience.

**What does Bloody Flicks publish?**
Horror news, reviews and reader competitions from bloody-flicks.co.uk. Its stated editorial focus is independent horror and cult cinema. It has run reader competitions with Picturehouse Cinemas, including a 2026 giveaway of merchandise for the queer horror *Leviticus* ahead of its UK cinema release.

**How did Bloody Flicks cover The Bunny Game's 2025 release?**
On 9 July 2025 it published "Previously banned The Bunny Game released in North America," covering Danse Macabre's collector's-edition Blu-ray/DVD release. The article noted the film's ongoing UK ban, Rehmeier's subsequent career, and Julian Richards' role in discovering the film and acquiring it for Jinga Films.

**Who is Paul Downey beyond the blog?**
A UK genre author and festival organiser. His books include *It's Me, Billy: Black Christmas Revisited* (with Dave Hastings) and *The Shark Is Roaring: The Story of Jaws The Revenge*, the latter featuring the first interview with *Jaws: The Revenge* star Lorraine Gary in over 30 years, per his Grimmfest interview.
