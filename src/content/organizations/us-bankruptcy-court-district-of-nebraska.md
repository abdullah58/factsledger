# United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Nebraska

**The United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Nebraska is the
federal bankruptcy court for the state of Nebraska, with courthouses in
Lincoln and Omaha.** Nebraska is a single district in the U.S. district
court system, and the bankruptcy court is a unit of the U.S. District
Court for the District of Nebraska. Appeals route through the Bankruptcy
Appellate Panel for the Eighth Circuit.

## Structure and jurisdiction

Congress authorized one bankruptcy judgeship for the District of
Nebraska on July 10, 1984 (98 Stat. 336) and expanded it to two on
October 27, 1986 (100 Stat. 3088), per the Federal Judicial Center. The
Omaha courthouse is the Roman L. Hruska United States Courthouse at 111
South 18th Plaza, Suite 1175, Omaha, NE 68102; the court also sits in
Lincoln (court homepage).

## Judges and notable cases

The current roster, per the Nebraska Legislative Bluebook: Chief
Bankruptcy Judge Thomas L. Saladino (Omaha) and Bankruptcy Judge Brian S.
Kruse (Lincoln).

Two cases on this court's docket connect to this research batch:

- **In re [Acceptance Insurance](/organizations/acceptance-insurance/) Companies Inc. (2005):** on January 7,
  2005, the parent filed a voluntary Chapter 11 petition (Case No.
  05-80059) in this court, while affiliates Acceptance Insurance
  Services, Inc. and American Agrisurance, Inc. filed Chapter 7 petitions
  (Case Nos. 05-80056 and 05-80058) the same day.
- **In re Hansen-Mueller Co. (2025–26):** a grain-elevator Chapter 11
  (BK25-81226-TLS) before Judge Saladino, with opinions on producer
  reclamation claims, § 557 producer claims, and secured-lender priority
  over surety claims on grain proceeds — typical of an
  agriculture-state docket.

## FAQ

**Is this the same as the federal district court in Nebraska?**
No. The bankruptcy court is a unit of the U.S. District Court for the
District of Nebraska (the Article III trial court); appeals from it go to
the Eighth Circuit's Bankruptcy Appellate Panel.

**Who are the current judges?**
Chief Judge Thomas L. Saladino (Omaha) and Judge Brian S. Kruse
(Lincoln), per the Nebraska Legislative Bluebook.

**Why two courthouses?**
The court's official homepage states its courthouses are located in
Lincoln and Omaha.
