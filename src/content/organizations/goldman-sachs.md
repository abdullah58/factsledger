## Overview

Goldman Sachs is an American multinational investment bank founded in [New York City](/places/new-york-city/) in 1869, headquartered at 200 West Street in Manhattan, and led since 2018 by chairman and chief executive David M. Solomon ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/goldman-sachs-company-history-timeline-facts)). In 2024 it reported net revenues of $53.51 billion and net earnings of $14.28 billion, supervised $3.1 trillion in client assets, and employed 46,500 people across offices in more than 40 countries ([Goldman Sachs](http://www.GoldmanSachs.com/pressroom/press-releases/2025/2025-01-15-q4-results); [Goldman Sachs](http://www.gs.com/investor-relations/financials/current/annual-reports/2024-annual-report/multimedia/2024-annual-report.pdf)).

The firm began when German immigrant Marcus Goldman opened a one-room basement office on Pine Street in Lower Manhattan, buying local merchants' promissory notes and reselling them to commercial banks — work that laid the groundwork for the modern commercial paper market ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Goldman-Sachs-Group-Inc)). His son-in-law Samuel Sachs joined in 1882, and his son Henry Goldman joined in 1885, when the firm took the name Goldman, Sachs & Co. ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Goldman-Sachs-Group-Inc)). It joined the New York Stock Exchange as a member in 1896 and led its first initial public offering — Sears, Roebuck and Co. — in 1906 ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/goldman-sachs-company-history-timeline-facts)). From that base it became one of the world's most influential investment banks, advising on mergers, underwriting securities, trading markets, and managing money for corporations, governments, and wealthy individuals worldwide ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Goldman-Sachs-Group-Inc)). In 2025, Forbes ranked it twentieth on its annual Global 2000 list of the world's largest publicly traded companies ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/goldman-sachs)).

## From commercial paper to a public bank

Goldman Sachs grew by adding one business at a time to its advisory core. It began investment banking in the early 1900s, added equity sales and trading in 1945, built its securities underwriting business in the 1950s, formed one of the first mergers-and-acquisitions units in the 1960s, added real estate services in 1969 and fixed income in 1972, and moved into commodities by acquiring J. Aron & Company in 1981 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Goldman-Sachs-Group-Inc)). Sidney Weinberg — who started as a janitor's assistant at $3 a week and became senior partner in 1930 — led the firm into its modern era, including as lead adviser on the Ford IPO in 1956 ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/goldman-sachs-company-history-timeline-facts)). The road was not smooth: the Goldman Sachs Trading Corp., a closed-end fund launched in 1928, collapsed in the 1929 crash amid accusations of share-price manipulation and insider trading ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/goldman-sachs-company-history-timeline-facts)).

The partnership structure lasted more than a century before the partners voted in 1998 to go public. On May 4, 1999, Goldman Sachs ended 130 years as a private partnership, selling 69 million shares at $53 and raising $3.657 billion in what was then the second-largest [U.S.](/places/united-states/) IPO ever; the shares closed the first day at $70.375 ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/goldman-sachs-company-history-timeline-facts)). In the 2008 crisis it converted to a bank holding company on September 21, took a $5 billion Berkshire Hathaway preferred investment two days later, and received a $10 billion Treasury TARP injection on October 27 ([Goldman Sachs](https://www.GoldmanSachs.com/our-firm/history/moments/2008-buffett-investment)).

## Two franchises: banking, markets, and managed money

Goldman Sachs now reports two main franchises plus a smaller platform unit. Global Banking & Markets covers investment banking — mergers-and-acquisitions advice and financing through stock and bond issues — alongside fixed income, currencies, and commodities (FICC) and equities trading ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Goldman-Sachs-Group-Inc)). It produced $34.94 billion of net revenues in 2024, and the firm has ranked first in advisory net revenues for 22 consecutive years ([Goldman Sachs](http://www.gs.com/investor-relations/financials/current/annual-reports/2024-annual-report/multimedia/2024-annual-report.pdf)). Its financing businesses are growing fast: FICC and equities financing revenues hit a record $9.1 billion in 2024, up at a 15% compound annual rate since 2019 ([Goldman Sachs](http://www.gs.com/investor-relations/financials/current/annual-reports/2024-annual-report/multimedia/2024-annual-report.pdf)). Asset & Wealth Management runs public and alternative investments and private wealth services; its assets under supervision reached a record $3.1 trillion in 2024, with about $1.6 trillion in wealth-management client assets, and alternatives fundraising topped $70 billion in the year ([Goldman Sachs](http://www.gs.com/investor-relations/financials/current/annual-reports/2024-annual-report/multimedia/2024-annual-report.pdf)).

Goldman supervised $3.1 trillion in client assets at the end of 2024 ([Goldman Sachs](http://www.gs.com/investor-relations/financials/current/annual-reports/2024-annual-report/multimedia/2024-annual-report.pdf)) — a scale that makes its fee-based asset business, not just deal fees, a durable earnings engine, which is why management highlights 28 straight quarters of long-term fee-based inflows. The third unit, Platform Solutions, houses transaction banking and card partnerships such as the Apple Card, and it posted an $859 million net loss in 2024 ([Reuters](https://srnnews.com/goldman-sachs-profit-surges-as-investment-banking-trading-fuel-bumper-quarter/)). The firm employed 46,500 people at the end of 2024, half of them in the Americas, 30% in Asia, and 20% in Europe, the Middle East, and Africa ([Goldman Sachs](http://www.gs.com/investor-relations/financials/current/annual-reports/2024-annual-report/multimedia/2024-annual-report.pdf)).

## The consumer detour — and the Axoni connection

In 2016 Goldman made two bets on new territory. It launched Marcus, an online consumer bank offering fixed-rate, no-fee personal loans, on October 13, 2016, and it joined the $18 million Series A of [Axoni](/organizations/axoni/), announced December 22, 2016, a New York blockchain firm building distributed-ledger infrastructure for capital markets, alongside [Wells Fargo](/organizations/wells-fargo/), [J.P. Morgan](/organizations/jpmorgan-chase/), Thomson Reuters, [Andreessen Horowitz](/organizations/andreessen-horowitz/), and others ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Goldman-Sachs-Group-Inc); [Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/vc-deal-of-the-day-axioni-raises-18m-series-a-46944.html?print)). The consumer bet soured: Goldman bought home-improvement lender GreenSky — a technology company connecting consumers with lenders for home-improvement loans — in 2021 in an all-stock deal valued at about $2.24 billion, sold substantially all of the Marcus loan portfolio in 2023, and completed the GreenSky sale in 2024, while its Apple Card partnership — contracted to 2030 — may end early, according to CEO David Solomon ([Goldman Sachs](http://www.gs.com/investor-relations/financials/current/annual-reports/2024-annual-report/multimedia/2024-annual-report.pdf); [Reuters](https://srnnews.com/goldman-sachs-profit-surges-as-investment-banking-trading-fuel-bumper-quarter/)).

The Axoni bet, by contrast, moved from experiment to production. In November 2017 Goldman joined JPMorgan, BNP Paribas, [Credit Suisse](/organizations/credit-suisse/), and [Citi](/organizations/citigroup/) in a second-phase Axoni pilot that processed equity swaps end-to-end on Axoni's AxCore ledger in a test environment, with no real trades or money changing hands ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2017/11/20/jpmorgan-goldman-sachs-trial-dlt-for-equity-swaps)). In September 2021 [BlackRock](/organizations/blackrock/) joined Goldman Sachs and Citigroup on Axoni's live Veris network, integrating Axoni's software with its Aladdin system to match and reconcile post-trade data on stock swaps in a market worth about $3.6 trillion ([Bloomberg](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/crypto/blackrock-joins-blockchain-platform-axoni-for-equity-swap-trades)). Goldman was thus both an early backer and a production user of the network.

## Settlements and scrutiny

Goldman's record includes some of the largest financial penalties of the modern era. In 2010 it paid $550 million to settle SEC charges that it misled investors selling subprime-mortgage debt securities while betting against them ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Goldman-Sachs-Group-Inc)). A decade later, in October 2020, it admitted conspiring to violate U.S. anti-bribery law over 1Malaysia Development Berhad (1MDB): its bankers helped the Malaysian fund raise about $6.5 billion in three bond deals, earning roughly $606 million in fees, while more than $1.6 billion in bribes was paid to officials in Malaysia and Abu Dhabi and over $2.7 billion was diverted from the offerings ([U.S. Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/usao-edny/pr/goldman-sachs-resolves-foreign-bribery-case-and-agrees-pay-over-29-billion)). The bank admitted its control staff had seen significant red flags — including the involvement of financier Jho Low — and ignored them or addressed them only nominally so the deals could proceed ([U.S. Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/usao-edny/pr/goldman-sachs-resolves-foreign-bribery-case-and-agrees-pay-over-29-billion)).

The resolution cost more than $2.9 billion, with the parent entering a deferred prosecution agreement and its Malaysian subsidiary pleading guilty — then the largest U.S. foreign-bribery penalty ever ([U.S. Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/usao-edny/pr/goldman-sachs-resolves-foreign-bribery-case-and-agrees-pay-over-29-billion)). Later settlements were smaller but steady: $215 million in 2023 to settle claims that nearly 3,000 female associates and vice presidents had been underpaid since 2002, and $89 million in 2024, ordered by the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau with Apple, for mishandling the Apple Card ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Goldman-Sachs-Group-Inc)). Former Goldman people also keep surfacing in government — Treasury secretaries Hank Paulson, Robert Rubin, and Steve Mnuchin among them — feeding the firm's "Government Sachs" nickname ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/goldman-sachs-company-history-timeline-facts)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What does Goldman Sachs do?**
Goldman Sachs is an investment bank and financial services firm with two main franchises: Global Banking & Markets, which advises on mergers and acquisitions, underwrites securities, and trades fixed income, currencies, commodities, and equities; and Asset & Wealth Management, which manages investments and private wealth ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Goldman-Sachs-Group-Inc)).

**How big is Goldman Sachs?**
In 2024 it reported net revenues of $53.51 billion and net earnings of $14.28 billion, supervised a record $3.1 trillion in client assets, and employed 46,500 people in offices across more than 40 countries; its shares rose 48.4% in 2024, the biggest gain among the six largest U.S. lenders ([Goldman Sachs](http://www.GoldmanSachs.com/pressroom/press-releases/2025/2025-01-15-q4-results); [Goldman Sachs](http://www.gs.com/investor-relations/financials/current/annual-reports/2024-annual-report/multimedia/2024-annual-report.pdf); [Reuters](https://srnnews.com/goldman-sachs-profit-surges-as-investment-banking-trading-fuel-bumper-quarter/)).

**Is Goldman Sachs a bank?**
Yes, since September 21, 2008, when it converted from an investment bank to a bank holding company during the financial crisis, making it eligible for Federal Reserve emergency programs and the Treasury's TARP, from which it took $10 billion ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Goldman-Sachs-Group-Inc); [Goldman Sachs](https://www.GoldmanSachs.com/our-firm/history/moments/2008-buffett-investment)).

**What is Goldman Sachs' connection to Axoni?**
Goldman was an early investor, participating in Axoni's $18 million Series A in December 2016, then a pilot participant in Axoni's 2017 equity-swaps trials, and finally a production user of Axoni's live Veris network for equity-swap reconciliation alongside BlackRock and Citigroup ([Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/vc-deal-of-the-day-axioni-raises-18m-series-a-46944.html?print); [Bloomberg](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/crypto/blackrock-joins-blockchain-platform-axoni-for-equity-swap-trades)).

**Why did Goldman Sachs leave consumer banking?**
Its Marcus consumer push, launched in 2016 and expanded with the 2021 GreenSky acquisition, lost billions; Goldman sold substantially all of the Marcus loan portfolio in 2023, completed the GreenSky sale in 2024, and its Platform Solutions unit — which houses the remaining consumer operations — posted an $859 million net loss in 2024 ([Reuters](https://srnnews.com/goldman-sachs-profit-surges-as-investment-banking-trading-fuel-bumper-quarter/)).
