## Overview

Sheer Logistics is a St. Louis-area 4PL and managed-transportation company founded in 2009 as
SheerTrans Solutions by Joe Egertson. Its distinguishing trait is not brokerage but a
technology-driven, fee-based model: instead of taking brokerage margins on each load, the company
earns fees for network analysis, procurement operations, business intelligence, freight auditing,
and dock scheduling, delivered through its proprietary SheerExchange integration platform
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/freighttech-titans-invest-in-sheer-logistics-modern-approach-to-managed-transportation)).
The angle no competing profile leads with is the company's second act as an emissions-data
vendor: in 2023 it introduced customizable Scope 3 carbon-dioxide dashboards for shippers,
powered by the same SheerExchange backbone, and the technology went on to win recognition from
Supply & Demand Chain Executive and an honorable mention in [Gartner](/organizations/gartner/)'s inaugural 2025 Magic
Quadrant for Fourth-Party Logistics
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/sheer-logistics-emissions-dashboards-aims-to-advance-sustainable-supply-chains);
[Global Trade Magazine](https://www.globaltrademag.com/sheer-logistics-unveils-advanced-solutions-for-enhanced-supply-chain-efficiency/);
[GlobeNewswire](https://rss.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/12/04/3200271/0/en/Sheer-Logistics-Recognized-with-an-Honorable-Mention-in-the-Inaugural-2025-Gartner-Magic-Quadrant-for-Fourth-Party-Logistics-4PL.html)).

## Managed transportation, not brokerage

Egertson founded the company in 2009 on the belief that there was an opening for a
technology-driven, mode-agnostic and carrier-agnostic managed transportation service distinct
from ordinary brokerage
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/freighttech-titans-invest-in-sheer-logistics-modern-approach-to-managed-transportation)).
The company began as a pure-play technology provider and only moved into providing logistics
services at the request of a few clients; Egertson told [FreightWaves](/organizations/freightwaves/) that "the need for
another broker in the sea of brokers was low when we opened our doors in 2009"
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/freighttech-titans-invest-in-sheer-logistics-modern-approach-to-managed-transportation)).
Over its first 13 years the company built Sheer Exchange, a proprietary middleware
infrastructure that acts as a single integration hub, capturing large amounts of shipment data
for client decision-making; its technology advantage lets it onboard new clients in an average
of about eight weeks, versus 60 to 90 days or more at many outmoded managed-transportation
providers
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/freighttech-titans-invest-in-sheer-logistics-modern-approach-to-managed-transportation)).
The company is based in Chesterfield, Missouri, and targets mid-market firms it describes as
traditionally underserved by the industry's largest 4PL providers
([Global Trade Magazine](https://www.globaltrademag.com/sheer-logistics-unveils-advanced-solutions-for-enhanced-supply-chain-efficiency/)).
In 2017 it joined the Blockchain in Transport Alliance, an industry consortium founded that
August to develop standards for blockchain use in transportation and logistics; Rob Cook, then
vice president of technology, cited potential applications including global product visibility,
customs support, payment reconciliation, and the carrier-shipper bid process
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/2017-11-30-sheer-logistics-joins-blockchain-in-transport-alliance)).

## Investment and acquisition growth

On 7 October 2022, Woodlawn Partners led a debt and equity co-investment in the company
alongside Monroe Capital LLC, on undisclosed terms
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/freighttech-titans-invest-in-sheer-logistics-modern-approach-to-managed-transportation)).
Industry veterans Eddie Leshin and Brian Winshall — who had been part of the American
Backhaulers team and later worked at C.H. Robinson, which acquired American Backhaulers in
1999 — joined as operating partners with Woodlawn and took board member roles, with Winshall
also becoming chief revenue officer; Egertson retained significant ownership and continued in
his executive role
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/freighttech-titans-invest-in-sheer-logistics-modern-approach-to-managed-transportation)).
In January 2024 the company brought in Joel Gard as CEO to spearhead its digital
transformation, focused on enhancing its transportation management system and SheerExchange
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/sheer-logistics-acquires-cargobarn-to-expand-brokerage-specialties)).
That April, Sheer Logistics acquired the freight brokerage CargoBarn to diversify the types of
freight in its portfolio and better handle future cyclical market shifts; terms were not
disclosed
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/sheer-logistics-acquires-cargobarn-to-expand-brokerage-specialties)).
The deal added offices in Fresno, California; Atlanta; Dallas; and Jacksonville, Florida, and
roughly doubled the company's size while strengthening its truckload brokerage capabilities
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/sheer-logistics-acquires-cargobarn-to-expand-brokerage-specialties);
[Global Trade Magazine](https://www.globaltrademag.com/sheer-logistics-unveils-advanced-solutions-for-enhanced-supply-chain-efficiency/)).
Gard told FreightWaves this was not a "one-and-done" deal and that further M&A activity was
"certainly not outside the realm of outcomes"
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/sheer-logistics-acquires-cargobarn-to-expand-brokerage-specialties)).
By 2024 the company had offices across six U.S. states and in Mexico
([Global Trade Magazine](https://www.globaltrademag.com/sheer-logistics-unveils-advanced-solutions-for-enhanced-supply-chain-efficiency/)).

## Emissions tracking and industry recognition

The company's most distinctive recent offering is its Scope 3 CO2 emissions dashboard line.
Announced in August 2023, the customizable dashboards let clients track and measure their
Scope 3 carbon-dioxide emissions in real time across all modes of transportation in their
supply chains
([DC Velocity](https://www.dcvelocity.com/articles/58191-sheer-logistics-introduces-co2-emissions-dashboards-to-help-shippers-achieve-their-esg-goals)).
SheerExchange acts as a "universal translator" connecting ERPs, fuel programs, rating engines,
and real-time transportation visibility platforms; the aggregated data is normalized,
warehoused, and measured against the framework developed by the Global Logistics Emissions
Council (GLEC), a harmonized and globally recognized standard
([DC Velocity](https://www.dcvelocity.com/articles/58191-sheer-logistics-introduces-co2-emissions-dashboards-to-help-shippers-achieve-their-esg-goals)).
CTO Rob Cook told FreightWaves the dashboards aggregate data from truckload, less-than-truckload,
flatbed, rail, ocean, air, and small parcel, applying globally recognized emission standards to
calculate CO2 accurately, so shippers can focus decarbonization efforts where they have the
greatest impact
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/sheer-logistics-emissions-dashboards-aims-to-advance-sustainable-supply-chains)).
The company showed off the dashboards at FreightWaves' Net-Zero Carbon Summit in September
2023
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/sheer-logistics-emissions-dashboards-aims-to-advance-sustainable-supply-chains)).
The emission-reporting technology earned the company recognition from Supply & Demand Chain
Executive magazine, and at FreightWeekSTL 2024's Innovation Day in St. Louis, Cook positioned
the SKU-level cost and emissions visibility as central to the company's mission
([Global Trade Magazine](https://www.globaltrademag.com/sheer-logistics-unveils-advanced-solutions-for-enhanced-supply-chain-efficiency/)).
In December 2025 the company announced it had received an honorable mention in the inaugural
Gartner Magic Quadrant for Fourth-Party Logistics, after being named a representative vendor in
the 2024 Gartner Market Guide for 4PL; it describes itself as a "native 4PL" serving
midmarket shippers in food and beverage, manufacturing, retail and consumer products, and
chemicals
([GlobeNewswire](https://rss.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/12/04/3200271/0/en/Sheer-Logistics-Recognized-with-an-Honorable-Mention-in-the-Inaugural-2025-Gartner-Magic-Quadrant-for-Fourth-Party-Logistics-4PL.html)).
