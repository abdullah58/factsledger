## Overview

Nyca Partners is a New York-based fintech venture capital firm founded in 2014 by Hans Morris, a former president of Visa Inc. and longtime Citigroup banker ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/jeremy-quittner/fintech-fund-nyca-partners-latest-investment-payrange.html)). The firm invests in early-stage companies where technology creates an edge in financial services, pairing Wall Street expertise with Silicon Valley connections through a network of senior industry advisors ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/jeremy-quittner/fintech-fund-nyca-partners-latest-investment-payrange.html)).

It is best known as the co-leader, with [Goldman Sachs](/organizations/goldman-sachs/), of blockchain firm [Axoni](/organizations/axoni/)'s $32 million Series B in 2018, and it is currently investing from its fifth fund with over 90 active investments ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.frb.io/articles/axoni-closes-series-b-led-by-goldman-sachs); [Nyca Partners](https://www.nyca.com/team)).

Morris founded the firm because the venture capital cultures of Wall Street and Silicon Valley "don't get each other" ([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2bsvbp0aiucnvhb9skmps/video-the-fintech-finance-35/hans-morris-and-nyca-partners-bridge-the-coasts)). The firm is headquartered in [New York City](/places/new-york-city/) with a San Francisco office, and by December 2025 it reported over $870 million in assets under management and investments in more than 100 portfolio companies ([Nyca Partners](https://www.nyca.com/team); [Venture Capital Access Online](https://www.vcaonline.com/news/2025120402/imtc-closes-12-million-series-a-led-by-nyca-partners-with-strategic-investment-from-lord-abbett/)).

## The bridge between Wall Street and Silicon Valley

Morris spent 27 years in financial services before founding Nyca: at Citigroup from 1980 to 2007 in roles including chief financial officer and head of finance, technology and operations for the institutional businesses, then as president of Visa from 2007 to 2010, then as a managing director at General Atlantic from 2010 to 2014 ([Nyca Partners](https://www.nyca.com/team)). He built Nyca around people with the same dual background — early partners included Ravi Mohan, a Citigroup and J.P. Morgan veteran, and investment partners such as Max Levchin, co-founder of PayPal, and Osama Bedier, former head of Google Payments ([Nyca Partners](https://www.nyca.com/team); [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/jeremy-quittner/fintech-fund-nyca-partners-latest-investment-payrange.html)). The firm's structure is its Limited Partner Advisor model: more than 90 professionals from finance, technology and academia who invest in the funds and advise portfolio companies ([Venture Capital Access Online](https://www.vcaonline.com/news/2025120402/imtc-closes-12-million-series-a-led-by-nyca-partners-with-strategic-investment-from-lord-abbett/)). Morris's pitch was blunt — "none of the venture capitalists are experts in the financial system" — and Nyca's answer was to sell that expertise alongside the cheque ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/jeremy-quittner/fintech-fund-nyca-partners-latest-investment-payrange.html)).

## The fund sequence

Nyca's fundraising tells the story of fintech's growth in miniature. The first fund closed at $30 million in July 2014, when the firm aimed to invest about $25 million a year ([Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/nyca-partners-launches-125m-fintech-fund-47107.html); [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/jeremy-quittner/fintech-fund-nyca-partners-latest-investment-payrange.html)). The second fund closed in January 2017 with more than $125 million, after the team had backed more than 30 companies across merchant payment solutions, alternative credit, digital advice and financial infrastructure, including Axioma, EmBroker, Ladder, LeapYear and Personetics ([Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/nyca-partners-launches-125m-fintech-fund-47107.html)). Fund III closed at $210 million in 2019, drawing 21 institutional investors and a bench of 55 Limited Partner Advisors ([e27](https://e27.co/kasikornbank-owned-beacon-vc-invests-in-global-fintech-investors-nyca-partners-20190925/)). The sizes of Funds IV and V were not disclosed in any fully-read source, but the firm says it is currently investing from its fifth fund ([Nyca Partners](https://www.nyca.com/team)).

## The Axoni thread

Nyca's most consequential bet was Axoni, the enterprise blockchain firm. In August 2018 Nyca co-led Axoni's $32 million Series B with Goldman Sachs, bringing Axoni's total funding to $55 million to finish moving the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation's $11 trillion Trade Information Warehouse onto a blockchain ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.frb.io/articles/axoni-closes-series-b-led-by-goldman-sachs)). The round's investors included [Andreessen Horowitz](/organizations/andreessen-horowitz/), Citi, [Coatue](/organizations/coatue/), Digital Currency Group, [Franklin Templeton](/organizations/franklin-templeton/), J.P. Morgan, NEX Group, Wells Fargo and [Y Combinator](/organizations/y-combinator/) — and Morris said he backed Schvey for his ability to get large organisations to agree, and for his low burn rate ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.frb.io/articles/axoni-closes-series-b-led-by-goldman-sachs)). In December 2025 Nyca led a $12 million Series A for IMTC, a fixed-income technology firm first backed in 2022, with Lord Abbett as a strategic investor ([Venture Capital Access Online](https://www.vcaonline.com/news/2025120402/imtc-closes-12-million-series-a-led-by-nyca-partners-with-strategic-investment-from-lord-abbett/)). The stat that captures the arc: Fund III's $210 million was seven times the $30 million first fund ([e27](https://e27.co/kasikornbank-owned-beacon-vc-invests-in-global-fintech-investors-nyca-partners-20190925/); [Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/nyca-partners-launches-125m-fintech-fund-47107.html)) — growth that implies Nyca's advisory model, not just its capital, is what kept institutional investors returning.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Nyca Partners?**
Nyca Partners is a New York-based venture capital firm founded in 2014 that invests in early-stage fintech companies, from seed to growth, across payments, lending, digital banking, capital markets and financial infrastructure ([Venture Capital Access Online](https://www.vcaonline.com/news/2025120402/imtc-closes-12-million-series-a-led-by-nyca-partners-with-strategic-investment-from-lord-abbett/)). It pairs capital with advice from senior financial-services professionals.

**Who founded Nyca Partners?**
Hans Morris, a former president of Visa Inc. (2007–2010) and longtime Citigroup banker, founded Nyca in 2014 after four years as a managing director at General Atlantic ([Nyca Partners](https://www.nyca.com/team)). He remains managing partner and chaired the board of LendingClub from 2016 to 2026.

**What does Nyca invest in?**
Early-stage fintech where technology creates a competitive advantage in financial services, across four verticals: payments, credit and insurance risk, wealth and digital insurance, and bank modernization and fintech infrastructure ([Nyca Partners](https://www.nyca.com/)). Early bets included LendingClub, Affirm and PayRange, and recent investments include Asseta, Astra and Blaise ([Inc.](https://www.inc.com/jeremy-quittner/fintech-fund-nyca-partners-latest-investment-payrange.html); [Nyca Partners](https://www.nyca.com/)).

**Did Nyca invest in Axoni?**
Yes. Nyca co-led Axoni's $32 million Series B with Goldman Sachs in August 2018, bringing Axoni's total funding to $55 million to complete work on AxCore and the DTCC Trade Information Warehouse project ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.frb.io/articles/axoni-closes-series-b-led-by-goldman-sachs)). The round also included Andreessen Horowitz, Citi, Coatue, Digital Currency Group, Franklin Templeton, J.P. Morgan, NEX Group, Wells Fargo and Y Combinator.

**How big is Nyca Partners?**
The firm reported over $870 million in assets under management and investments in more than 100 portfolio companies in December 2025, and says it has over 90 active investments and is investing from its fifth fund ([Venture Capital Access Online](https://www.vcaonline.com/news/2025120402/imtc-closes-12-million-series-a-led-by-nyca-partners-with-strategic-investment-from-lord-abbett/); [Nyca Partners](https://www.nyca.com/team)).
