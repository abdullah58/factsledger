## Overview

The most interesting thing about Truth Social in 2026 is that it's barely a social network story anymore — it's a balance-sheet story. Donald Trump's platform, launched on iOS in April 2022 and the web that May, is the flagship of Trump Media & Technology Group, a Nasdaq-listed company (ticker DJT) that in one year and a half grew its financial assets from $274 million at its March 2024 public debut to $3.1 billion by September 30, 2025 ([ADVFN](https://www.advfn.com/stock-market/NASDAQ/DJT/stock-news/97184423/trump-media-reports-third-quarter-2025-results)). The engine wasn't advertising — ads are the "substantial majority" of operating revenue, but revenue was $1.7 million in the second quarter of 2026 against an adjusted loss of $238.1 million ([Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/articles/trump-media-earnings-djt-stock-price-e92ce5fc); [Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/how-truth-social-makes-money-8635271)). The engine was Bitcoin.

## The crypto pivot

TMTG accumulated about $2 billion worth of Bitcoin in July 2025 and held 9,477 coins with a fair value of $557.1 million at the end of June 2026 ([Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/articles/trump-media-earnings-djt-stock-price-e92ce5fc)). When Bitcoin slumped 46% over twelve months, "the vast bulk" of the company's $238.1 million Q2 2026 adjusted loss came from unrealized losses on digital assets ([Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/articles/trump-media-earnings-djt-stock-price-e92ce5fc)). The company monetizes the strategy actively too: in Q3 2025 it generated $15.3 million of realized income from premiums on bitcoin-related options, plus $13.4 million in interest income from other holdings ([ADVFN](https://www.advfn.com/stock-market/NASDAQ/DJT/stock-news/97184423/trump-media-reports-third-quarter-2025-results)).

Alongside the treasury sits an expanding ecosystem. A partnership with Crypto.com brought the CRO token onto the platform — Trump Media bought about 684.4 million CRO for $50 million in cash plus $47 million in stock, took a minority interest in a CRO treasury company, and made CRO the rewards currency for Truth Social and Truth+ users ([ADVFN](https://www.advfn.com/stock-market/NASDAQ/DJT/stock-news/97184423/trump-media-reports-third-quarter-2025-results)). The platform itself added AI search powered by Perplexity, paid Truth+ Patriot Package subscriptions (edit posts, schedule posts, access drafts across devices), and Truth Predict, billed as the first prediction-markets integration on a social platform ([ADVFN](https://www.advfn.com/stock-market/NASDAQ/DJT/stock-news/97184423/trump-media-reports-third-quarter-2025-results)). The most on-brand product is the Truth API: traders pay for instant access to Trump's posts, with more than 10 customers signed up ([Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/articles/trump-media-earnings-djt-stock-price-e92ce5fc)).

## The platform as presidential instrument

Truth Social remains Trump's own megaphone: he has posted as @realDonaldTrump since his January 20, 2025 inauguration ([Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/how-truth-social-makes-money-8635271)). On September 18, 2026, that megaphone became the instrument of a press ban — Trump posted that CNN, MS NOW, and [Politico](https://factsledger.com/organizations/politico/) were banned from the White House "effective immediately" for alleged "FAKE NEWS," warning other outlets could follow ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/18/trump-bans-news-outlets-cnn-ms-now-and-politico-from-white-house)). Days later, U.S. District Judge [Timothy Kelly](https://factsledger.com/people/timothy-kelly/) — a Trump first-term appointee — ordered access restored, finding the ban likely violated due-process and First Amendment rights ([Livemint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-news/us-judge-orders-trump-admin-to-restore-white-house-access-for-cnn-ms-now-politico-11790226583998.html)).
