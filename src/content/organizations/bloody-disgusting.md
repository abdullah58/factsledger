## Overview

**Bloody Disgusting** is the horror-media brand founded in 2001 by Brad Miska ("Mr. Disgusting") and Tom Owen, billing itself "Your Number 1 News Source for All Things Horror" ([Cinedigm](https://markets.financialcontent.com/ibtimes/article/accesswire-2021-9-21-cinedigm-slashes-its-way-to-the-forefront-of-horror-streaming-with-latest-acquisition-bloody-disgusting-the-leading-source-of-all-things-horror); [Beet.TV](https://www.beet.tv/2008/10/bloody-disgusti-1.html)). It grew from a Hollywood news site into a producer (the *V/H/S* franchise), distributor, and podcast network before Cinedigm's September 2021 buyout ([Cinedigm](https://markets.financialcontent.com/ibtimes/article/accesswire-2021-9-21-cinedigm-slashes-its-way-to-the-forefront-of-horror-streaming-with-latest-acquisition-bloody-disgusting-the-leading-source-of-all-things-horror)); since 2025 it has been Cineverse's horror division, run by John Squires and Meagan Navarro ([Cineverse](https://ptechhub.online/cineverse-promotes-john-squires-and-megan-vavarro-to-co-managing-directors-of-leading-horror-brand-bloody-disgusting-302445091-html/)).

## From bedroom blog to horror conglomerate

Bloody Disgusting's arc tracks the commercialization of online fandom. Launched in 2001 by Miska and Owen, it had by 2008 become a viable vertical publisher — 250,000 monthly uniques per Compete.com, major studios advertising, OVGuide ranking it among the top Halloween video destinations ([Beet.TV](https://www.beet.tv/2008/10/bloody-disgusti-1.html)). In 2011 it crossed into the business itself: The Collective, AMC Theatres, and the site announced a distribution partnership putting indie horror into 32 AMC markets monthly — starting with *Cold Fish*, *Rammbock*, and *YellowBrickRoad* — with unrated DVDs via Vivendi Universal, while the site's production arm created the *V/H/S* found-footage franchise ([GeekTyrant](https://geektyrant.com/news/2011/3/10/amc-theaters-teams-up-with-bloody-disgusting-to-find-and-dis.html); [Cinedigm](https://markets.financialcontent.com/ibtimes/article/accesswire-2021-9-21-cinedigm-slashes-its-way-to-the-forefront-of-horror-streaming-with-latest-acquisition-bloody-disgusting-the-leading-source-of-all-things-horror)). Cinedigm's September 2021 buyout reframed the site as an audience engine: the release claimed the Bloody Disgusting media brand reached more than 30 million unique horror fans a year across its website, podcast network, app, and social footprint — which implies the acquisition was priced on streaming-era reach, not pageviews ([Cinedigm](https://markets.financialcontent.com/ibtimes/article/accesswire-2021-9-21-cinedigm-slashes-its-way-to-the-forefront-of-horror-streaming-with-latest-acquisition-bloody-disgusting-the-leading-source-of-all-things-horror)).

## The 360-degree horror brand, and the Bunny Game connection

Today the brand spans the namesake website, Bloody FM (the chart-topping horror arm of the Cineverse podcast network), Bloody Press publishing, merchandise, and SCREAMBOX — the streaming service the BD team was put in charge of after the Cinedigm deal, and the home of the theatrically unrated *Terrifier 2* and *Terrifier 3* ([Cineverse](https://ptechhub.online/cineverse-promotes-john-squires-and-megan-vavarro-to-co-managing-directors-of-leading-horror-brand-bloody-disgusting-302445091-html/)). Editorially, the site still does what built it: it published director Adam Rehmeier's on-record responses to the BBFC's refusal to classify [The Bunny Game](/creative-works/the-bunny-game/) — including his line that the film was "way less disturbing than *Pretty Woman*" — and covered Autonomy Pictures' pickup of the film as its first release ([Bloody Disgusting](http://bloody-disgusting.com/news/28671/autonomy-pictures-comes-out-playing-the-bunny-game/)). The co-founder split in 2025 — Miska out, Tom Owen continuing as Cineverse VP of Network Strategies with Squires and Navarro running the brand — closed the founder era without closing the site ([Cineverse](https://ptechhub.online/cineverse-promotes-john-squires-and-megan-vavarro-to-co-managing-directors-of-leading-horror-brand-bloody-disgusting-302445091-html/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who founded Bloody Disgusting?**
Brad Miska — who wrote as "Mr. Disgusting" — and Tom Owen founded the site in 2001. Both were quoted as co-founders in Cinedigm's 2021 acquisition announcement. Miska left the company in 2025; Owen continues as Cineverse's vice president of network strategies.

**Who owns Bloody Disgusting now?**
Cinedigm — now renamed Cineverse — acquired Bloody Disgusting in September 2021. It operates as Cineverse's horror division, spanning the website, the Bloody FM podcast network, Bloody Press publishing, merchandise, and the SCREAMBOX streaming service. Since 2025 the brand has been run by co-managing directors John Squires and Meagan Navarro.

**What was Bloody Disgusting Selects?**
A 2011 distribution partnership among The Collective, AMC Theatres, and BloodyDisgusting.com to acquire and market indie horror and thriller titles from festivals, screened monthly in 32 AMC markets and released on unrated DVD. Its first slate was Cold Fish, Rammbock, and YellowBrickRoad — and the same year's production push created the V/H/S anthology franchise.

**What did Bloody Disgusting publish about The Bunny Game?**
The site published director Adam Rehmeier's direct, on-record responses to the BBFC's refusal to classify the film — including his argument that it was "way less disturbing than Pretty Woman" and "a modern cautionary tale grounded in reality" — and covered Autonomy Pictures' acquisition of the film as the new distributor's first release.
