## Overview

Stocktwits is the social network for investors and traders that invented the cashtag — the dollar sign before a ticker symbol, as in $AAPL — and turned it into the organising principle of a financial community ([AlleyWatch](https://www.alleywatch.com/2021/12/stocktwits-community-investors-trading-stock-crypto-conversations-tickers-platform-social-investment-rishi-khanna/)). Founded in 2008 and launched that October by Howard Lindzon and Soren Macbeth as an application on Twitter, it grew from a filter for stock chatter into one of the largest financial social networks, reporting more than 6 million registered users and over 5 million monthly users by early 2022 ([Bloomberg Businessweek](https://www.brookstoneventurecapital.com/blog/2009/02/11/stocktwits-may-change-how-you-trade/); [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2022/02/03/social-media-site-stocktwits-taps-ftx-to-launch-crypto-trading-service)).

This profile covers that company — the [New York City](/places/new-york-city/)-based platform, historically focused on [United States](/places/united-states/) equities, not the cashtag convention generally and not Twitter, the network it began on. Its users range from retail investors to finance professionals, and its 2009 pitch — "a Bloomberg for the average guy" — still describes the product ([Bloomberg Businessweek](https://www.brookstoneventurecapital.com/blog/2009/02/11/stocktwits-may-change-how-you-trade/); [Green, Koenraadt, Markov & Pinto](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=255836)). Its story has three acts: a clever invention in 2008, a pandemic-era boom that made it a mainstream retail-trading hub, and a 2024 experiment with livestreamed earnings calls that academics now use to study echo chambers ([AlleyWatch](https://www.alleywatch.com/2021/12/stocktwits-community-investors-trading-stock-crypto-conversations-tickers-platform-social-investment-rishi-khanna/); [Green, Koenraadt, Markov & Pinto](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=255836)).

## The cashtag: an invention from Twitter's margins

The idea was small and exact. People were already talking about stocks on Twitter, so Macbeth came up with a way to tag stock-related messages and aggregate them — a filter to separate the chatter and zero in on finance through a platform that was global, lightweight, stable and in real time ([Bloomberg Businessweek](https://www.brookstoneventurecapital.com/blog/2009/02/11/stocktwits-may-change-how-you-trade/)). Lindzon, who had founded WallStrip, a finance video blog bought by CBS, supplied the instinct: the site needed no registration or email verification, unlike rivals Covestor and Stockpickr — just a Twitter username and password dropped you into a virtual trading floor ([Bloomberg Businessweek](https://www.brookstoneventurecapital.com/blog/2009/02/11/stocktwits-may-change-how-you-trade/)). By February 2009 it had 10,000-plus subscribers averaging 15 minutes on the site and contributing 3,000 unique tweets a day, and Lindzon called it "a Bloomberg for the average guy" ([Bloomberg Businessweek](https://www.brookstoneventurecapital.com/blog/2009/02/11/stocktwits-may-change-how-you-trade/)). The design forced accountability: a trader's reputation was always on the line, since users created a trading record scrutinized daily ([Bloomberg Businessweek](https://www.brookstoneventurecapital.com/blog/2009/02/11/stocktwits-may-change-how-you-trade/)). The cashtag did the work: it turned a ticker into a room, and the room into a market of opinions.

## The pandemic boom, the Alameda round and FTX

Stocktwits was built for a moment that arrived twelve years late. When lockdowns pushed millions into day trading, the number of shares traded by the customers of large brokers rose from 700 million a day before the pandemic to 2.9 billion a day, and the GameStop and AMC episodes made retail blocs a force professional managers had to respect ([AlleyWatch](https://www.alleywatch.com/2021/12/stocktwits-community-investors-trading-stock-crypto-conversations-tickers-platform-social-investment-rishi-khanna/)). Stocktwits surged with it: by late 2021 it had over 5 million active monthly users for a freemium product with paid tiers offering proprietary research and subscriber communities, on a business model primarily ad-based with subscription revenue on top ([AlleyWatch](https://www.alleywatch.com/2021/12/stocktwits-community-investors-trading-stock-crypto-conversations-tickers-platform-social-investment-rishi-khanna/)). The funding figure: in December 2021 it raised $30 million in a Series B led by Alameda Research Ventures, the venture arm of FTX chief executive Sam Bankman-Fried, joined by Times Bridge, bringing total funding to $43.2 million ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2022/02/03/social-media-site-stocktwits-taps-ftx-to-launch-crypto-trading-service); [AlleyWatch](https://www.alleywatch.com/2021/12/stocktwits-community-investors-trading-stock-crypto-conversations-tickers-platform-social-investment-rishi-khanna/)). In February 2022 it tapped FTX US to launch crypto trading, with the exchange providing the infrastructure at the same fees as its own platform ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2022/02/03/social-media-site-stocktwits-taps-ftx-to-launch-crypto-trading-service)).

## Earnings calls and the echo-chamber experiment

Stocktwits' most interesting recent chapter is not a funding round but a research result. In July 2024 it introduced livestreamed earnings conference calls inside the platform, each with a dedicated chat for real-time reaction ([Green, Koenraadt, Markov & Pinto](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=255836)). Academics from Emory University, the London School of Economics and the University of Texas at Dallas used the launch as a natural experiment, with large effects: streamed calls were associated with 73% higher firm pageviews and 66% more messages about the company than non-streamed calls, and selective following fell by about 61% ([Green, Koenraadt, Markov & Pinto](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=255836)). That cuts against the baseline problem documented in earlier research: self-described bulls are five times more likely to follow other bulls than bears, so feeds turn homogeneous and beliefs polarise ([Green, Koenraadt, Markov & Pinto](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=255836)). The paper's mechanism is simple: embedding the primary disclosure inside the social feed, with a chat making counter-attitudinal comments visible, lowers the friction of reaching the source — and the sentiment-tagging system, where users mark posts bullish or bearish, makes the disagreement measurable ([Green, Koenraadt, Markov & Pinto](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=255836)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Stocktwits?** Stocktwits is a social network for investors and traders that organizes conversations around stock tickers across stocks, crypto and other emerging investments, founded in 2008 and based in New York ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2022/02/03/social-media-site-stocktwits-taps-ftx-to-launch-crypto-trading-service)). It invented the cashtag — a dollar sign before a ticker, as in $AAPL — and describes itself as the first financial platform with social in its DNA ([AlleyWatch](https://www.alleywatch.com/2021/12/stocktwits-community-investors-trading-stock-crypto-conversations-tickers-platform-social-investment-rishi-khanna/)).

**Who founded Stocktwits?** Howard Lindzon and Soren Macbeth, who launched it in October 2008 as an application on Twitter that tagged stock-related messages ([Bloomberg Businessweek](https://www.brookstoneventurecapital.com/blog/2009/02/11/stocktwits-may-change-how-you-trade/)). Lindzon had previously founded WallStrip, a finance video blog bought by CBS, and Macbeth devised the tagging system that became the cashtag ([Bloomberg Businessweek](https://www.brookstoneventurecapital.com/blog/2009/02/11/stocktwits-may-change-how-you-trade/)).

**How many users does Stocktwits have?** Reporting in late 2021 and early 2022 put it at more than 6 million registered users and over 5 million monthly users ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2022/02/03/social-media-site-stocktwits-taps-ftx-to-launch-crypto-trading-service)). A December 2025 academic paper reports 3.5 million users; the figures use different dates and counting bases, so this profile reports both rather than merging them ([Green, Koenraadt, Markov & Pinto](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=255836)).

**What was the Alameda Research investment?** In December 2021 Stocktwits raised $30 million in a Series B led by Alameda Research Ventures, the venture arm of FTX chief executive Sam Bankman-Fried, joined by Times Bridge, bringing total funding to $43.2 million ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2022/02/03/social-media-site-stocktwits-taps-ftx-to-launch-crypto-trading-service); [AlleyWatch](https://www.alleywatch.com/2021/12/stocktwits-community-investors-trading-stock-crypto-conversations-tickers-platform-social-investment-rishi-khanna/)). Two months later it launched crypto trading on FTX US infrastructure ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2022/02/03/social-media-site-stocktwits-taps-ftx-to-launch-crypto-trading-service)).

**What did the earnings-call study find?** That livestreaming earnings calls inside Stocktwits weakened echo chambers: streamed calls drew 73% more pageviews and 66% more messages, and bulls' tendency to follow only other bulls fell about 61% ([Green, Koenraadt, Markov & Pinto](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=255836)). Bulls exposed to streamed calls also posted fewer net-bullish messages over the following 30 days, consistent with gradual belief revision ([Green, Koenraadt, Markov & Pinto](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=255836)).
