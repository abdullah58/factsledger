## Overview

Levitate is a Raleigh, North Carolina-based marketing platform and customer-relationship solution provider that sells an AI-powered "keep-in-touch" toolkit to relationship-based small businesses ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/levitate-raises-6m-for-its-keep-in-touch-email-marketing-solution-aimed-at-smaller-businesses/)). Founded in late 2017 by serial entrepreneur Jesse Lipson — who had previously built and sold the file-sharing startup ShareFile — the company had raised $71 million in total capital by March 2026 and reported more than 8,000 business customers ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/levitate-16-million-raised-for-ai-powered-relationship-marketing-platform/)). This profile covers only the Raleigh marketing-software company: the name is also used by unrelated ventures, including the Danish startup Levitate Technology, which makes running blades for amputees ([Disability Insider](https://disabilityinsider.com/2022/08/17/technology/danish-start-up-launches-levitate-gravel-sole-for-running-prosthetics/)).

## A ShareFile founder's second act

Lipson's path to Levitate ran through one of the Research Triangle's notable exits. He founded the cloud storage and file-sharing startup ShareFile and sold it to Citrix for a reported $93 million in 2011, then stayed on at Citrix, most recently as corporate vice president and general manager of the Citrix Cloud Services business unit ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/levitate-raises-6m-for-its-keep-in-touch-email-marketing-solution-aimed-at-smaller-businesses/)). In 2017 he left the corporate world to build Levitate, which by mid-2020 had scaled to more than 1,000 businesses paying on a subscription basis and a team of 80 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/levitate-raises-6m-for-its-keep-in-touch-email-marketing-solution-aimed-at-smaller-businesses/)).

The founding insight came from watching relationship-based businesses — in financial services, real estate, insurance, and legal markets — struggle to stay genuinely in touch with clients once deals closed ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/levitate-raises-6m-for-its-keep-in-touch-email-marketing-solution-aimed-at-smaller-businesses/)). Rather than positioning the product as another CRM, Lipson framed it as a new category he called "keep-in-touch marketing," explicitly contrasted with mass-blast email marketing: "We're not really a CRM," he told TechCrunch, describing email marketing as impersonal blasts "to users who can't reply," versus curating "your list of people who you truly know" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/levitate-raises-6m-for-its-keep-in-touch-email-marketing-solution-aimed-at-smaller-businesses/)).

## How the platform works

The software operates as an email add-on integrating with major providers like Microsoft and Google, as well as a standalone iOS and Android app ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/levitate-raises-6m-for-its-keep-in-touch-email-marketing-solution-aimed-at-smaller-businesses/)). Business customers can import contacts from Excel or CSV files and from CRM and agency-management systems including Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics, AMS 360, and others, with API integrations offered in industries like insurance and financial advisory ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/levitate-raises-6m-for-its-keep-in-touch-email-marketing-solution-aimed-at-smaller-businesses/)). Imported contacts are enhanced with publicly available information such as photos, social media links, company details, and bios ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/levitate-raises-6m-for-its-keep-in-touch-email-marketing-solution-aimed-at-smaller-businesses/)).

The toolset centers on staying in touch rather than broadcasting: rules about who to contact and how, reminders to maintain relationships, customizable email templates, and relationship analytics ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/levitate-raises-6m-for-its-keep-in-touch-email-marketing-solution-aimed-at-smaller-businesses/)). AI features suggest who to contact and when, automate workflows such as new-customer welcome kits, and clean up contact data — for example, deciding whether a name field contains a real first name or a company name so outreach does not sound impersonal ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/levitate-raises-6m-for-its-keep-in-touch-email-marketing-solution-aimed-at-smaller-businesses/)). Early pricing was $150 per month for five users, with a free single-user personal plan and a $199-per-month tier adding live onboarding, training, and a personal marketing coach — the human-coaching layer that, combined with the software, defined the offering ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/levitate-raises-6m-for-its-keep-in-touch-email-marketing-solution-aimed-at-smaller-businesses/), [citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/18521/levitate-raises-8m-in-additional-series-b-funding/)).

## Funding and growth

The capital history shows steady, compounding rounds. In July 2020 Levitate raised an additional $6 million, bringing total funding to $12 million from investors including Tippet Venture Partners, Bull City Venture Partners, and Veeva Systems co-founder and CEO Peter Gassner ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/levitate-raises-6m-for-its-keep-in-touch-email-marketing-solution-aimed-at-smaller-businesses/)). In February 2021 it added $8 million in Series B funding — backed by existing investors Tippet, Gassner, and Bull City, with Lipson participating personally — taking total fundraising to $20 million, with the company reporting 2,000 business customers and revenue that had quadrupled in 2020 ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/18521/levitate-raises-8m-in-additional-series-b-funding/)).

In April 2023 the company closed $14 million in growth capital led by Bull City Venture Partners, with Tippet Partners, Protagonist, Duke Capital Partners, The Tweener Fund, Lipson himself, and more than 60 individual entrepreneurs — including former Citrix and DigitalOcean CEO Mark Templeton, Bronto Software co-founders Joe Colopy and Chaz Felix, and iContact founder Aaron Houghton — reporting $40 million raised since inception and more than 4,000 small-business customers ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/409313/levitate-raises-14m/?abkw=citybizcharlotte)). That round also launched the company's AI Assistant, which helps customers write, reword, and edit emails and social posts and translates content into ten languages ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/409313/levitate-raises-14m/?abkw=citybizcharlotte)).

The August 2024 Series D raised $15 million led by Harbert Growth Partners with Northwestern Mutual Future Ventures and Bull City Venture Partners, bringing total capital to $55 million ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/levitate-relationship-based-management-company-raises-15-million-series-d/)). The company reported 6,000-plus customers and the general availability of online-presence tools — website design, Google Review management, and blog content creation — powered by the AI Assistant ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/levitate-relationship-based-management-company-raises-15-million-series-d/)). Northwestern Mutual's Craig Schedler framed the appeal in financial-services terms: the platform could expand advisors' reach while deepening client relationships ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/levitate-relationship-based-management-company-raises-15-million-series-d/)). In March 2026 Levitate raised another $16 million led by Harbert Growth Partners with Bull City Venture Partners and Northwestern Mutual Future Ventures participating, taking total capital raised to $71 million ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/levitate-16-million-raised-for-ai-powered-relationship-marketing-platform/)). The company said the funding would accelerate an AI innovation roadmap and expand a "Service-as-Software" model — marketing automation combined with hands-on strategic support — while growing customer-success and sales teams across the United States and Canada ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/levitate-16-million-raised-for-ai-powered-relationship-marketing-platform/)). Customer count stood at more than 8,000 businesses ([Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/levitate-16-million-raised-for-ai-powered-relationship-marketing-platform/)).
