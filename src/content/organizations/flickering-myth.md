## Overview

**Flickering Myth** is a UK film and pop-culture publication founded in 2009 by **Gary Collinson**, covering film, television and genre culture with news, reviews, interviews, features and opinion pieces ([Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/about-flickering-myth/)). In 2025 it covered Jinga Films' US Blu-ray and DVD reissue of [*The Bunny Game*](/creative-works/the-bunny-game/) through Jinga's Danse Macabre label ([Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/controversial-horror-the-bunny-game-gets-blu-ray-release-from-jinga-films/)). Beyond publishing, the brand has moved into actual film production via **Flickering Myth Films**, whose first feature, *The Baby in the Basket*, was acquired by **Shudder** for the US ([Malevolent Dark](https://malevolentdark.com/baby-in-the-basket-joins-the-july-lineup-at-shudder/)).

## Founding, scope and masthead

Gary Collinson founded Flickering Myth in 2009; the site describes itself as "an independent film and pop culture media brand combining editorial coverage with real-world filmmaking and industry experience" ([Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/about-flickering-myth/)). Its editorial mix spans news, reviews, interviews, features and opinion across film, television and genre culture, and it calls itself "one of the UK's longest-running film and entertainment websites," claiming more than 150 million visitors since inception — both first-party claims, stated here with that attribution ([Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/about-flickering-myth/)). Independent confirmation of the site's identity comes from a 2012 **WhatCulture** review of Collinson's book *Holy Franchise, Batman! Bringing the Caped Crusader to the Screen* (published by **Robert Hale**), which notes his craft was honed "through his own film website Flickering Myth" ([WhatCulture](https://whatculture.com/offbeat/holy-franchise-batman-a-bat-fans-delight)). Collinson remains Editor-in-Chief, with producing credits on *The Baby in the Basket*, *Death Among the Pines* and *Robert Returns*, and a background that includes a decade teaching film and media ([Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/about-flickering-myth/)).

## From reviews to filmmaking

The masthead gives the site's criticism its aggregation weight: Chief Film Critic **Robert Kojder** is Rotten Tomatoes-approved (**Chicago Film Critics Association**, **Critics Choice Association**, **Online Film Critics Society**); **EJ Moreno** (joined 2018, runs the FMTV YouTube channel, 27,000+ subscribers) is also RT-approved; **Amie Cranswick** has been Executive Editor for over a decade; Senior Staff Writer **Tom Jolliffe** doubles as a producer ([Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/about-flickering-myth/)). RT-approved status is what puts an outlet's reviews into the Tomatometer pool. Flickering Myth Films has taken the brand behind the camera: its first completed feature, the gothic horror *The Baby in the Basket* — directed by **Nathan Shepka** from a Jolliffe script, starring **Amber Doig-Thorne**, **Maryam d'Abo**, **Paul Barber** and **Annabelle Lanyon** — premiered in 2025, sold to more than 15 international territories, and joined Shudder's [US](/places/united-states/) lineup on 20 July 2026 ([Malevolent Dark](https://malevolentdark.com/baby-in-the-basket-joins-the-july-lineup-at-shudder/)). Follow-ups: *Death Among the Pines* (**Tubi** US) and the *Robert the Doll* reboot *Robert Returns* (**4Digital Media**, early 2027) ([Malevolent Dark](https://malevolentdark.com/baby-in-the-basket-joins-the-july-lineup-at-shudder/)). The 15-territory sale is the number that counts: real distribution beyond the editorial audience.

## Covering The Bunny Game's US reissue

In 2025 Flickering Myth reported Jinga Films' US home-video release of *The Bunny Game* with the news item "Controversial horror The Bunny Game gets Blu-ray release from Jinga Films": a new Blu-ray and DVD edition of **Adam Rehmeier's** 2011 exploitation horror, co-written by and starring **Rodleen Getsic**, issued in the United States through Jinga's **Danse Macabre** label, with bonus materials including the "Caretaking the Monster" behind-the-scenes featurette, the trailer and the teaser ([Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/controversial-horror-the-bunny-game-gets-blu-ray-release-from-jinga-films/)). The article notes Jinga's history as the global sales agent behind *A Serbian Film* and that *The Bunny Game* was refused a certificate by the **BBFC**, "effectively banning the film in the [UK](/places/united-kingdom/) to this day" ([Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/controversial-horror-the-bunny-game-gets-blu-ray-release-from-jinga-films/)). It was not the site's first encounter with the film: the 2021 "Extreme Cinema" feature by **Liam Underwood** argued against the BBFC's ban while panning the film itself as a boring exercise in "torture porn," unfavourably compared with **Martyrs** ([Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/extreme-cinema-the-bunny-game/)). The two pieces together show the outlet's genre-press posture — distribution-milestone news plus long-form critical features with an explicit anti-censorship line.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who founded Flickering Myth and when?**
Film writer and producer Gary Collinson founded Flickering Myth in 2009 as an online film and entertainment publication. He remains Editor-in-Chief, has over 20 years of industry experience including a decade teaching film and media, and authored *Holy Franchise, Batman! Bringing the Caped Crusader to the Screen* (Robert Hale), per the site's about page.

**What does Flickering Myth cover?**
The site covers film, television and genre culture, publishing news, reviews, interviews, features and opinion pieces. It describes itself as one of the UK's longest-running film and entertainment websites and claims a global audience of more than 150 million visitors since 2009 — both the site's own figures.

**What is Flickering Myth Films?**
It is the brand's film-production arm, developing commercially oriented features for international audiences. Its first completed feature, the gothic horror *The Baby in the Basket* (2025), sold to 15-plus territories and joined Shudder's US lineup in July 2026. Follow-ups are *Death Among the Pines* and *Robert Returns*.

**How did Flickering Myth cover The Bunny Game's 2025 US reissue?**
With a 2025 news item on Jinga Films' US Blu-ray and DVD release through its Danse Macabre label — noting Jinga's *A Serbian Film* history, the BBFC's ongoing UK ban, and the disc's bonus materials. The site had already covered the film in a 2021 "Extreme Cinema" feature opposing the BBFC ban while criticising the movie.

**Who are the key people at Flickering Myth?**
Founder and Editor-in-Chief Gary Collinson; Executive Editor Amie Cranswick; Senior Staff Writer and producer Tom Jolliffe; Chief Film Critic Robert Kojder (Rotten Tomatoes-approved, Chicago Film Critics Association, Critics Choice, Online Film Critics Society); and FMTV executive producer EJ Moreno — across editorial and production.
