## Overview

This profile covers **Even Financial** — the New York embedded-finance marketplace founded in 2014 by Phillip (Phil) Rosen — and not other businesses that use the name "Even." Even built a search, comparison and recommendation engine, delivered through an embeddable API, that matched consumers with personalized financial products from banks, insurers and fintech companies ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/embedded-finance/moneylion-acquires-embedded-finance-marketplace-even-financial-for-440m)). It operated as a business-to-business marketplace: consumer apps and websites embedded Even's technology to offer their own users financial products, while financial institutions used it to reach new customers ([FinTech Magazine](https://fintechmagazine.com/venture-capital/fintech-profile-even-financial-ultimate-api)). In February 2022 [MoneyLion](/organizations/moneylion/) acquired it for up to $440 million, and in March 2023 the business was rebranded as "Engine by MoneyLion" ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2023/03/204045-moneylion-enhances-its-embedded-finance-tech-with-rebranding-of-even-financial/)).

## The marketplace model

Even's proposition had two sides. Channel partners — publishers and consumer apps — could monetize their user bases with turnkey access to a financial marketplace covering loans, credit cards, savings and more; financial-institution partners gained what Even described as an organic network for reaching new customers, with the API said to optimize monetization, lower acquisition costs and reduce delinquencies ([FinTech Magazine](https://fintechmagazine.com/venture-capital/fintech-profile-even-financial-ultimate-api)). The platform's components included a real-time API for personalized offers, multi-product search widgets, customizable decisioning models, a recommendation engine, real-time reporting and third-party data augmentation ([FinTech Magazine](https://fintechmagazine.com/venture-capital/fintech-profile-even-financial-ultimate-api)).

By the time of its acquisition, Even's embedded-finance marketplace spanned more than 400 financial institutions and 500 channel partners across loans, credit cards, mortgages, savings and insurance products ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/embedded-finance/moneylion-acquires-embedded-finance-marketplace-even-financial-for-440m)). FinTech Magazine reported the platform had enabled more than $400 billion in consumer applications for financial services, generated over 60 million consumer profiles, and routed more than a million applications a month ([FinTech Magazine](https://fintechmagazine.com/venture-capital/fintech-profile-even-financial-ultimate-api)). Its investor roster read like a who's who of financial incumbents and fintech VCs: Goldman Sachs, American Express Ventures, [SoFi](/organizations/sofi/), F-Prime Capital, MassMutual Ventures, LendingClub, Canaan Partners and GreatPoint Ventures ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/embedded-finance/moneylion-acquires-embedded-finance-marketplace-even-financial-for-440m)), with a $24 million strategic round co-led by Citi Ventures and MassMutual Ventures in 2019 ([FinTech Magazine](https://fintechmagazine.com/venture-capital/fintech-profile-even-financial-ultimate-api)).

## Acquisition and rebrand

MoneyLion acquired Even Financial for up to $440 million, the deal
closing in February 2022: $360 million upfront — $15 million in cash and
$345 million in MoneyLion shares — with up to $80 million more available
subject to revenue targets ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/embedded-finance/moneylion-acquires-embedded-finance-marketplace-even-financial-for-440m); [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2023/03/204045-moneylion-enhances-its-embedded-finance-tech-with-rebranding-of-even-financial/)). Even continued as an independent subsidiary under its existing management, with founder Phillip Rosen staying on as CEO and joining MoneyLion's executive committee ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/embedded-finance/moneylion-acquires-embedded-finance-marketplace-even-financial-for-440m)).

In March 2023 MoneyLion rebranded the business as "Engine by MoneyLion," describing it as its enterprise embedded-finance platform for financial-product search, APIs and marketplace software ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2023/03/204045-moneylion-enhances-its-embedded-finance-tech-with-rebranding-of-even-financial/)). By then the network had grown to over 1,000 partners and the company said it had facilitated more than $7 billion in financial products since inception; Rosen was promoted to MoneyLion's chief technology officer while also leading the Engine web-services business ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2023/03/204045-moneylion-enhances-its-embedded-finance-tech-with-rebranding-of-even-financial/)). The Even brand thus survived not as a company but as the engine inside MoneyLion's B2B offering.

The rebrand was presented as more than a name change. MoneyLion launched a new brand identity and moved the platform to the engine.tech domain, describing the technology as delivered through a SaaS platform giving partners transparency and controls, customizable integrations, AI-backed data and insights, and reporting ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2023/03/204045-moneylion-enhances-its-embedded-finance-tech-with-rebranding-of-even-financial/)). All existing Even products and services continued without interruption under the new brand ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2023/03/204045-moneylion-enhances-its-embedded-finance-tech-with-rebranding-of-even-financial/)). MoneyLion co-founder and CEO Dee Choubey said the combination was "delivering on our vision to make financial services more accessible to everyone, powering a remarkably vast set of products and services for our partners through a simple yet robust integration" ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2023/03/204045-moneylion-enhances-its-embedded-finance-tech-with-rebranding-of-even-financial/)). For MoneyLion, the logic was distribution: combining its consumer products with Even's recommendation engine and partner network widened the range of products available within its own platform ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/embedded-finance/moneylion-acquires-embedded-finance-marketplace-even-financial-for-440m)).
