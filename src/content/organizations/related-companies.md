## Overview

The Related Companies is a privately owned American real estate developer founded in [New York City](/places/new-york-city/) in 1972 by Stephen Ross, best known for building the $25 billion Hudson Yards neighborhood on Manhattan's West Side and for owning the fitness brands Equinox and SoulCycle. It began in affordable housing and remains one of the largest affordable-housing owners in the [United States](/places/united-states/), with more than 65,800 affordable residences ([Related Companies](https://www.related.com/affordable-housing); [Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/south-florida/commercial-real-estate/stephen-ross-steps-down-at-related-cos-launches-florida-focused-firm-125052)).

Headquartered in New York City, Related describes itself as the most prominent privately owned real estate firm in the United States, with a portfolio of more than $70 billion in assets owned or under development and about 4,000 professionals ([Related Companies](https://www.related.com/press-releases/2026-01-05/related-companies-and-oxford-properties-group-complete-full-245-billion)). Ross stepped down as chairman in July 2024 after 52 years, remaining non-executive chairman and the firm's largest shareholder, while chief executive Jeff Blau, president Bruce Beal Jr. and chief operating officer Kenneth Wong run the company ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/south-florida/commercial-real-estate/stephen-ross-steps-down-at-related-cos-launches-florida-focused-firm-125052)). This profile covers that New York firm — not the separate Miami-based Related Group, run by Jorge Pérez, with which it ended a partnership in late 2022 ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/south-florida/commercial-real-estate/stephen-ross-steps-down-at-related-cos-launches-florida-focused-firm-125052)).

## From affordable housing to Time Warner Center

Ross, a former tax attorney, started the company as Related Housing Companies with a $10,000 loan from his mother, investing in government-supported affordable housing; by the end of the 1970s it had built 5,000 affordable units ([CityRealty](https://www.cityrealty.com/nyc/market-insight/features/people-shaping-ny/developer-spotlight-the-related-companies/4582)). As Ross later put it, "I knew I didn't want to stay in affordable housing forever," and the firm diversified into offices, market-rate housing and retail in several Manhattan neighborhoods ([CityRealty](https://www.cityrealty.com/nyc/market-insight/features/people-shaping-ny/developer-spotlight-the-related-companies/4582)). Its first big break came in 1980, winning the city's bid to develop River Walk just north of Stuyvesant Town, a win that put Related on the front page of The New York Times ([CityRealty](https://www.cityrealty.com/nyc/market-insight/features/people-shaping-ny/developer-spotlight-the-related-companies/4582)). The decisive luxury project was the Time Warner Center: Ross secured Time Warner as anchor tenant before building the $1.7 billion office, retail and residential complex at Columbus Circle, which opened in 2004 ([CityRealty](https://www.cityrealty.com/nyc/market-insight/features/people-shaping-ny/developer-spotlight-the-related-companies/4582); [6sqft](https://www.6sqft.com/?p=157692)). Within four years, nearby retail rents were up 400 percent, according to Forbes ([CityRealty](https://www.cityrealty.com/nyc/market-insight/features/people-shaping-ny/developer-spotlight-the-related-companies/4582)).

## Hudson Yards: the $25 billion neighborhood

Related lost the original bid for the Metropolitan Transportation Authority railyards to Tishman Speyer, but re-bid and entered lease negotiations with the MTA in 2008 after Tishman Speyer backed out two months later ([CityRealty](https://www.cityrealty.com/nyc/market-insight/features/people-shaping-ny/developer-spotlight-the-related-companies/4582)). With the market crashing and original partner Goldman Sachs out, Ross negotiated a creative MTA lease and brought in Canadian pension fund Oxford Properties as replacement partner; groundbreaking followed by 2012 ([CityRealty](https://www.cityrealty.com/nyc/market-insight/features/people-shaping-ny/developer-spotlight-the-related-companies/4582)). The 28-acre neighborhood, built on a platform over an active rail yard, opened its first phase to the public in March 2019: five acres of public square and gardens, the Vessel staircase sculpture, and a million-square-foot retail center ([World Property Journal](https://www.worldpropertyjournal.com/real-estate-news/united-states/new-york-city-real-estate-news/hudson-yards-grand-opening-in-new-york-city-march-2019-stephen-ross-the-related-companies-hudson-yards-news-new-york-real-estate-news-11323.php)). The $25 billion project was billed as the largest private development in American history, and it was projected to contribute nearly $19 billion a year to the city's economy once fully built ([World Property Journal](https://www.worldpropertyjournal.com/real-estate-news/united-states/new-york-city-real-estate-news/hudson-yards-grand-opening-in-new-york-city-march-2019-stephen-ross-the-related-companies-hudson-yards-news-new-york-real-estate-news-11323.php)).

## Beyond buildings: Equinox, SoulCycle and the lifestyle arm

In December 2005 Related agreed to buy Equinox Holdings for $505 million, with the deal closing in 2006; chief executive Harvey Spevak stayed on ([American Spa](https://www.americanspa.com/news/equinox-to-be-sold); [Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2025/11/related-and-oxford-sell-hudson-yards-equinox-hotel-for-541m/)). Equinox kept expanding under Related, launching the budget chain Blink Fitness and acquiring the indoor-cycling studio SoulCycle in 2011 ([Club Solutions Magazine](https://clubsolutionsmagazine.com/2014/07/equinox-acquiring-sports-clubla-reebok-sports-clubny/)). The two businesses fused at Hudson Yards, where the first Equinox Hotel opened in August 2019 with 212 rooms ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2025/11/related-and-oxford-sell-hudson-yards-equinox-hotel-for-541m/)). In November 2025 Related and Oxford sold the hotel and the lower 38 floors of that tower to Tokyo-based Mori Trust for $541 million — a sale of the property, not of the Equinox brand ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2025/11/related-and-oxford-sell-hudson-yards-equinox-hotel-for-541m/)). The lifestyle arm also brought Related its sharpest backlash: in August 2019, news that Ross would host a Trump re-election fundraiser, with tickets up to $250,000, set off cancellations and a #BoycottEquinox campaign, and both brands publicly distanced themselves, calling Ross a passive investor ([Athletic Business](https://www.athleticbusiness.com/operations/programming/article/15157771/political-fundraiser-finds-fitness-brands-in-tough-spot)).

## Succession and the company today

In July 2024, Ross, 84, stepped down as chairman and spun off Related's Southeast division into Related Ross, a separate West Palm Beach-focused company, already the largest downtown commercial owner there ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/south-florida/commercial-real-estate/stephen-ross-steps-down-at-related-cos-launches-florida-focused-firm-125052)). Blau, Beal and Wong kept the New York company, focused on luxury residential in New York, Jersey City, Chicago, Boston, Austin, San Francisco, Los Angeles and London, plus Boston life sciences and US data centers ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/south-florida/commercial-real-estate/stephen-ross-steps-down-at-related-cos-launches-florida-focused-firm-125052)). In January 2026 Related and Oxford closed a $2.45 billion capitalization of 70 Hudson Yards, including a $1.6 billion construction loan arranged by [Wells Fargo](/organizations/wells-fargo/), Bank of America and Standard Chartered, for a 72-story tower that will house Deloitte's new US headquarters ([Related Companies](https://www.related.com/press-releases/2026-01-05/related-companies-and-oxford-properties-group-complete-full-245-billion)). And in November 2026 [Bilt](/organizations/bilt/) named Related among the real-estate partners for its Neighborhood OS membership platform, which will let residential communities offer rewards at nearby businesses from 2027 — turning Related's apartments into a distribution channel for a network reaching one in four US apartment buildings ([View From The Wing](https://viewfromthewing.com/new-bilt-deals-with-hyatt-marriott-and-sports-team-reveal-bigger-bet-on-rewards-across-your-whole-neighborhood/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who founded The Related Companies?** Stephen Ross founded it in 1972 as Related Housing Companies, with a $10,000 loan from his mother and a business plan focused on government-supported affordable housing ([CityRealty](https://www.cityrealty.com/nyc/market-insight/features/people-shaping-ny/developer-spotlight-the-related-companies/4582)). By the end of the 1970s the firm had built 5,000 affordable housing units before diversifying into luxury, office and retail development ([CityRealty](https://www.cityrealty.com/nyc/market-insight/features/people-shaping-ny/developer-spotlight-the-related-companies/4582)).

**What is Hudson Yards and how much did it cost?** Hudson Yards is a 28-acre mixed-use neighborhood built by Related and Oxford Properties on a platform over Manhattan's West Side railyards, with offices, residences, shops, parks and a public school ([CityRealty](https://www.cityrealty.com/nyc/market-insight/features/people-shaping-ny/developer-spotlight-the-related-companies/4582)). Its first phase opened in March 2019 at a reported total cost of $25 billion, making it the largest private development in US history ([World Property Journal](https://www.worldpropertyjournal.com/real-estate-news/united-states/new-york-city-real-estate-news/hudson-yards-grand-opening-in-new-york-city-march-2019-stephen-ross-the-related-companies-hudson-yards-news-new-york-real-estate-news-11323.php)).

**Does Related own Equinox and SoulCycle?** Yes. Related agreed to buy Equinox Holdings for $505 million in December 2005, closing in early 2006, and Equinox acquired SoulCycle in 2011 ([American Spa](https://www.americanspa.com/news/equinox-to-be-sold); [Club Solutions Magazine](https://clubsolutionsmagazine.com/2014/07/equinox-acquiring-sports-clubla-reebok-sports-clubny/)). The November 2025 sale of the Equinox Hotel property at 35 Hudson Yards to Mori Trust for $541 million covered the building, not the fitness brands ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2025/11/related-and-oxford-sell-hudson-yards-equinox-hotel-for-541m/)).

**Who runs Related Companies now?** Jeff Blau, chief executive since 2012, leads the company with president Bruce Beal Jr. and chief operating officer Kenneth Wong ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/south-florida/commercial-real-estate/stephen-ross-steps-down-at-related-cos-launches-florida-focused-firm-125052)). Founder Stephen Ross stepped down as chairman in July 2024 but remains non-executive chairman and the firm's single largest shareholder ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/south-florida/commercial-real-estate/stephen-ross-steps-down-at-related-cos-launches-florida-focused-firm-125052)).

**Is Related Companies the same as Related Group?** No. Related Group is a separate Miami-based developer run by Jorge Pérez; the two firms worked together in South Florida until an amicable breakup of the partnership announced in late 2022 ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/south-florida/commercial-real-estate/stephen-ross-steps-down-at-related-cos-launches-florida-focused-firm-125052)). Ross's new Florida venture, Related Ross, is a spin-off of Related Companies' Southeast division, not part of Related Group ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/south-florida/commercial-real-estate/stephen-ross-steps-down-at-related-cos-launches-florida-focused-firm-125052)).
