## Overview

Victory Park Capital is a global private credit manager based in Chicago, Illinois, in the [United States](/places/united-states/), founded in 2007 by Richard Levy and Brendan Carroll, specializing in asset-backed lending — the business of financing loans that banks will not touch ([Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-announces-acquisition-of-global-private-credit-manager-victory-park-capital/)). Since 2010 it has lent against consumer and small-business finance, financial and hard assets, and real estate credit, and by mid-2024 it had invested approximately $10.3 billion across more than 220 investments with about $6.0 billion in assets under management ([Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-announces-acquisition-of-global-private-credit-manager-victory-park-capital/)). In October 2024 Janus Henderson Group bought a majority stake in the firm, completing the acquisition on 2 October ([Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-completes-victory-park-capital-acquisition/)). In the FactsLedger graph, Victory Park is the money behind [Avant](/organizations/avant/)'s rise: it supplied the online lender's first credit facility in 2013 and grew that commitment to $500 million within two years ([Private Equity Wire](https://www.privateequitywire.co.uk/vpc-and-kkr-lead-inaugural-usd175m-asset-backed-securitisation-avant-consumer-loan/)).

The firm's founding thesis was disarmingly simple. "When we set up, the thesis was pretty simple; to provide private debt to basically anything that a bank wouldn't," co-founder Brendan Carroll said in 2018 ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/alternative-credit/2018/subprime-lending-power-player-victory-park-eyes-overseas-expansion/)). That thesis was a bet on a structural gap: after the 2008 financial crisis, mainstream banks retreated from riskier borrowers to repair their balance sheets, and a generation of online lenders stepped into the space they vacated — needing, in turn, someone to fund their loan books.

## The lender banks would not be

Victory Park Capital was founded in 2007 by Richard Levy, its founder, chief executive and chief investment officer, and Brendan Carroll, co-founder and senior partner ([Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-announces-acquisition-of-global-private-credit-manager-victory-park-capital/)). It is a privately held, SEC-registered alternative investment firm headquartered in Chicago, with offices in New York, Los Angeles and London, and a team of about 60 people ([Pacific Current Group](https://paccurrent.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/PAC_Strategic_Investment_Victory_Park_Capital_LLC.pdf); [Janus Henderson](https://s203.q4cdn.com/353037891/files/doc_events/2024/08/JHG-VPC-Presentation-vf.pdf)).

The firm invests primarily in senior-secured, floating-rate, asset-backed loans secured by diversified balance-sheet assets, and its capabilities span small-business and consumer finance, financial and hard assets, real estate credit and legal finance, plus custom investment management for insurance companies and structured financing through its affiliate platform, Triumph Capital Markets ([Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-announces-acquisition-of-global-private-credit-manager-victory-park-capital/)). Its clients are overwhelmingly institutional — insurance companies account for 29% of the capital it has raised, alongside pensions and superannuation funds, sovereign wealth funds, endowments and foundations, and high-net-worth and family offices ([Janus Henderson](https://s203.q4cdn.com/353037891/files/doc_events/2024/08/JHG-VPC-Presentation-vf.pdf)).

Its structural advantage is the shape of its money. Like a private equity firm, Victory Park raises capital from investors that is locked up for five to ten years, which lets it build long-term relationships with emerging platforms and extend credit lines that grow as the businesses grow, rather than making one-off loans ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/alternative-credit/2018/subprime-lending-power-player-victory-park-eyes-overseas-expansion/)). An Oppenheimer analyst put the logic bluntly to the Financial Times, as quoted by PYMNTS: "The future is asset managers that manage long-term, locked-up capital, especially for the riskier assets that carry real credit and liquidity risk" ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/alternative-credit/2018/subprime-lending-power-player-victory-park-eyes-overseas-expansion/)).

The scale of that model grew steadily. Total investments were reported at around $6.5 billion across more than 90 deals in January 2018; by July 2024 the firm had invested approximately $10.3 billion across 223 transactions, with assets under management of about $6.0 billion ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/alternative-credit/2018/subprime-lending-power-player-victory-park-eyes-overseas-expansion/); [Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-announces-acquisition-of-global-private-credit-manager-victory-park-capital/)).

## The fintech financier

Victory Park's name is most closely associated with the first wave of American online lending. Its consumer-lending partners included Avant, Elevate and LendUp — digital lenders serving near-prime, subprime and thin-file borrowers — and it backed small-business credit platforms including Kabbage, [OnDeck Capital](/organizations/ondeck-capital/) and Square ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/alternative-credit/2018/subprime-lending-power-player-victory-park-eyes-overseas-expansion/)). Pacific Current Group described it in 2018 as "one of the largest and most active financial technology and non-bank financing investors globally," spanning direct lending, small-business investment and distressed opportunities ([Pacific Current Group](https://paccurrent.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/PAC_Strategic_Investment_Victory_Park_Capital_LLC.pdf)).

The firm then took the model global. By 2018 it had extended its portfolio to Australia's zipMoney, Germany's Kreditech and the UK's Oakam, and it launched a fund with the International Finance Corporation — a member of the World Bank Group — focused on fintech and non-bank financing companies in emerging markets ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/alternative-credit/2018/subprime-lending-power-player-victory-park-eyes-overseas-expansion/); [Pacific Current Group](https://paccurrent.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/PAC_Strategic_Investment_Victory_Park_Capital_LLC.pdf)). Carroll's reasoning was demographic: "I see a worldwide opportunity," he said, "noting a steady rise in 'underbanked' populations around the world" ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/alternative-credit/2018/subprime-lending-power-player-victory-park-eyes-overseas-expansion/)).

## The Avant arc: from $25 million to $2.2 billion

No relationship illustrates Victory Park's model better than Avant. In April 2013, AvantCredit — then a Chicago startup offering personal loans of $1,000 to $10,000 — completed a $34 million round: a $9 million Series A led by [August Capital](/organizations/august-capital/), plus a $25 million credit facility from Victory Park Capital ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/online-lender-avantcredit-secures-34m-in-funding)). Carroll's rationale was explicit: "The team at AvantCredit has developed a robust suite of risk management processes that evaluate consumer credit and make highly competitive loans to underserved borrowers. As traditional banks remain hesitant to lend across consumer segments, we will see the growth of new and innovative credit solutions like AvantCredit" ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/online-lender-avantcredit-secures-34m-in-funding)).

Four months later, in August 2013, AvantCredit raised a $20 million Series B co-led by August Capital and Victory Park Capital, to expand beyond the 16 U.S. states where it operated ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2013/08/14/avantcredit-raises-20m-to-grow-its-machine-powered-online-lending-platform/)). The facility then grew with the company: in July 2014 AvantCredit raised a $75 million Series C led by Tiger Global Management alongside a $200 million credit facility led by Victory Park, taking total equity raised and debt committed past $300 million ([Private Equity International](https://www.privateequityinternational.com/avantcredit-racks-up-275-mln-from-tiger-global-and-vpc/)). Since the Series B, AvantCredit's quarterly loan volume had risen more than 500% and its headcount from 70 to over 300 ([Private Equity International](https://www.privateequityinternational.com/avantcredit-racks-up-275-mln-from-tiger-global-and-vpc/)).

By November 2015 the relationship had become structural. Victory Park's initial credit facility to Avant "now totals USD500 million," and the firm had a long-term agreement to buy newly originated Avant loans, totalling over $2.2 billion in originations since January 2013 ([Private Equity Wire](https://www.privateequitywire.co.uk/vpc-and-kkr-lead-inaugural-usd175m-asset-backed-securitisation-avant-consumer-loan/)). That month, Victory Park and KKR led a $175 million asset-backed securitization of unsecured consumer loans originated through Avant — three tranches of unrated notes backed by an initial pool of about $195 million in loans, with Avant remaining the servicer — marking Victory Park's first issuance into the asset-backed securitization market ([Private Equity Wire](https://www.privateequitywire.co.uk/vpc-and-kkr-lead-inaugural-usd175m-asset-backed-securitisation-avant-consumer-loan/)). KKR separately provided $100 million for Avant's Institutional Marketplace, which lets institutional investors buy Avant-originated loans ([Private Equity Wire](https://www.privateequitywire.co.uk/vpc-and-kkr-lead-inaugural-usd175m-asset-backed-securitisation-avant-consumer-loan/)).

## Ownership: from boutique to Janus Henderson affiliate

Victory Park's ownership history tracks its growth. In July 2018, Pacific Current Group, an Australian-listed multi-boutique asset manager, bought a 24.9% stake for A$94.6 million (US$70 million), when the firm's funds under management were about US$3.2 billion ([Pacific Current Group](https://paccurrent.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/PAC_Strategic_Investment_Victory_Park_Capital_LLC.pdf)). Pacific Current's chief executive joined Victory Park's board in an advisory capacity, while senior management kept full control of operations and strategy ([Pacific Current Group](https://paccurrent.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/PAC_Strategic_Investment_Victory_Park_Capital_LLC.pdf)).

Six years later, the firm was absorbed into a much larger manager. On 12 August 2024 Janus Henderson Group — a global active asset manager with about $361 billion under management — announced a definitive agreement to acquire a majority stake in Victory Park Capital Advisors, paying a mix of cash and Janus Henderson shares ([Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-announces-acquisition-of-global-private-credit-manager-victory-park-capital/)). The terms, set out in Janus Henderson's investor presentation, were a 55% ownership interest at closing, with a defined path to 100% over time; existing owners would retain 45%, there would be no changes to senior management or day-to-day operations, and Victory Park would keep its brand and operate as a standalone affiliate ([Janus Henderson](https://s203.q4cdn.com/353037891/files/doc_events/2024/08/JHG-VPC-Presentation-vf.pdf)). The acquisition completed on 2 October 2024 ([Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-completes-victory-park-capital-acquisition/)). Janus Henderson's chief executive, Ali Dibadj, framed the deal as an expansion into asset-backed private credit, complementing the firm's $36.3 billion securitized credit franchise ([Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-announces-acquisition-of-global-private-credit-manager-victory-park-capital/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Victory Park Capital?**
Victory Park Capital is a Chicago-based global private credit manager founded in 2007 by Richard Levy and Brendan Carroll, specializing in asset-backed lending — financing consumer and small-business loans, financial and hard assets, and real estate credit ([Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-announces-acquisition-of-global-private-credit-manager-victory-park-capital/)). It had about $6.0 billion in assets under management as of June 2024 ([Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-announces-acquisition-of-global-private-credit-manager-victory-park-capital/)).

**Who owns Victory Park Capital?**
Janus Henderson Group owns a majority stake — 55% acquired at closing on 2 October 2024, with a defined path to 100% ownership over time ([Janus Henderson](https://s203.q4cdn.com/353037891/files/doc_events/2024/08/JHG-VPC-Presentation-vf.pdf); [Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-completes-victory-park-capital-acquisition/)). The firm keeps its brand and operates as a standalone affiliate, led by founders Richard Levy and Brendan Carroll.

**How is Victory Park Capital connected to Avant?**
Victory Park was AvantCredit's earliest and largest institutional funder: a $25 million credit facility in April 2013, co-lead of the $20 million Series B that August, a $200 million facility in 2014, and a $500 million total commitment by 2015, with over $2.2 billion in Avant loans purchased under a long-term agreement ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/business/online-lender-avantcredit-secures-34m-in-funding); [Private Equity Wire](https://www.privateequitywire.co.uk/vpc-and-kkr-lead-inaugural-usd175m-asset-backed-securitisation-avant-consumer-loan/)). It also co-led Avant's first asset-backed securitization, $175 million with KKR in November 2015.

**What does "asset-backed lending" mean here?**
It means lending secured by pools of assets — in Victory Park's case, mainly senior-secured, floating-rate loans backed by diversified loan books, receivables and other balance-sheet assets, rather than by a single company's credit alone ([Janus Henderson](https://s203.q4cdn.com/353037891/files/doc_events/2024/08/JHG-VPC-Presentation-vf.pdf)). The firm's thesis was to fund the lending that post-2008 banks had abandoned ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/alternative-credit/2018/subprime-lending-power-player-victory-park-eyes-overseas-expansion/)).

**How big is Victory Park Capital?**
About $6.0 billion in assets under management as of June 2024, with approximately $10.3 billion invested across more than 220 investments since its 2007 founding, and a team of about 60 people ([Janus Henderson](https://www.janushenderson.com/corporate/press-releases/janus-henderson-announces-acquisition-of-global-private-credit-manager-victory-park-capital/)).
