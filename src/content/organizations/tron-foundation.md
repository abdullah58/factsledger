## Overview

In March 2026 the [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](/organizations/us-securities-and-exchange-commission/) walked away from its fraud case against the TRON Foundation — the nonprofit that built one of the world's largest blockchain networks — in a move tangled up with presidential politics, crypto wealth and allegations of pay-to-play ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/sec-settles-case-against-investor-in-trump-linked-crypto-projects-amid-pay-to-play-allegations-2000730615)). The case's collapse marked the end of a three-year legal siege and the final chapter of founder [Justin Sun](/people/justin-sun/)'s turbulent relationship with the network he created.

TRON is a layer-1 smart-contract blockchain founded in September 2017 by Sun, a Chinese-born crypto entrepreneur, and developed under the Tron Foundation, a Singapore non-profit ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/sponsored/2026-09-23-tron-surpasses-30t-in-total-transaction-volume-as-it-secures-its-place-as-leading-chain-for-stablecoins-416228); [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2020/01/27/former-employees-sue-justin-sun-and-tron-foundation-alleging-workplace-hostilities)). Its defining feature is speed and cost: transactions settle fast and for fractions of a cent, which made it the preferred chain for moving dollar-pegged stablecoins. By September 2026, TRON DAO claimed the network had processed more than $30 trillion in total transaction volume and carried more than $94 billion in circulating USDT — the largest USDT supply on any blockchain ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/sponsored/2026-09-23-tron-surpasses-30t-in-total-transaction-volume-as-it-secures-its-place-as-leading-chain-for-stablecoins-416228)).

## The stablecoin network

TRON's MainNet launched in May 2018, moving the project from Ethereum-based tokens to its own chain ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/sponsored/2026-09-23-tron-surpasses-30t-in-total-transaction-volume-as-it-secures-its-place-as-leading-chain-for-stablecoins-416228)). The network was designed as a layer-1 smart-contract platform with a delegated proof-of-stake model, but its commercial identity quickly became stablecoin transfers: by mid-2026 TRON was processing 12.7 million transactions a day and claimed $4.2 trillion in USDT transfer volume year to date, with Token Terminal data cited by the network showing $6 trillion in USDT volume over the trailing twelve months and a $25 billion daily average ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/10/3325494/0/en/USDT-on-TRON-Exceeds-90-Billion-as-TRON-Leads-USDT-Transfer-Volume-with-4-2-Trillion-YTD.html); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/sponsored/2026-09-23-tron-surpasses-30t-in-total-transaction-volume-as-it-secures-its-place-as-leading-chain-for-stablecoins-416228)). Stablecoin research cited by TRON DAO credited the network with the highest active wallet count of any chain. CoinDesk Research figures carried in the same announcement put TRON's share of crypto card spending at 33% in the first quarter of 2026 rising to 34% in the second — the highest share of any chain tracked ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/sponsored/2026-09-23-tron-surpasses-30t-in-total-transaction-volume-as-it-secures-its-place-as-leading-chain-for-stablecoins-416228)).

2026 was also the year TRON entered traditional finance's plumbing. September saw the launch of the Canary Staked TRX ETF under the ticker TRXS, spot and futures trading of TRX on the U.S.-regulated Bitnomial exchange, native TRX staking and custody at Anchorage, and the Hamilton Lane SCOPE Fund becoming the first [Securitize](/organizations/securitize/)-issued asset to launch on the network; TRON also ranked among the top five assets in the S&P Pantera Digital Asset Index ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/sponsored/2026-09-23-tron-surpasses-30t-in-total-transaction-volume-as-it-secures-its-place-as-leading-chain-for-stablecoins-416228)).

## From foundation to DAO

Sun's departure was the network's defining governance moment. In an open letter published in December 2021, he announced he was stepping down as CEO, leaving TRON to its token holders, and taking up a post as [Grenada](/places/grenada/)'s ambassador to the [World Trade Organization](/organizations/world-trade-organization/) ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2021/12/17/justin-sun-is-retiring-from-tron-but-not-crypto)). The plan called for the TRON Foundation itself to dissolve by 2022 — in fact it had already dissolved itself on July 25, 2021 ([CryptoNews](https://cryptonews.net/news/altcoins/2903181/)) — with three super nodes linked to Sun retired from the network and governance passing to 27 super representatives elected by the community — a conversion into a fully decentralized autonomous organization. Sun framed the move as taking inspiration from El Salvador's embrace of bitcoin and said he wanted to focus on legitimizing blockchain at the nation-state level.

The foundation's earlier years had been noisier. A 2020 CoinDesk report on a $15 million workplace lawsuit filed in California Superior Court by two former employees described the Tron Foundation as staffed in Beijing and San Francisco and accused Sun and his engineering chief of harassment and retaliatory firings tied to objections over how BitTorrent software was being rushed to release; the foundation contested the charges' motivations, jurisdiction and factual substance ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2020/01/27/former-employees-sue-justin-sun-and-tron-foundation-alleging-workplace-hostilities)).

## The SEC case — filed, paused, dismissed

In 2023 the SEC sued Sun, the Tron Foundation and the [BitTorrent](/organizations/bittorrent/) Foundation, alleging unregistered crypto-asset distributions, more than 600,000 wash trades in TRX that generated $31 million in proceeds, and concealed payments to celebrities including Lindsay Lohan and Jake Paul to promote the tokens ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/sec-settles-case-against-investor-in-trump-linked-crypto-projects-amid-pay-to-play-allegations-2000730615)). The case sat largely frozen after Donald Trump's return to the presidency; in February 2025 the SEC paused it "citing public interest" ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/wlfs-zach-witkoff-usd1-selected-official-stablecoin-mgx-investment-binance-2025-05-01/)).

On March 5, 2026, the SEC moved to dismiss all claims against Sun and both foundations, while Rainberry — the corporate successor of BitTorrent Inc. — agreed to pay a $10 million civil penalty without admitting or denying the allegations, with court approval still pending at the time of reporting ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/sec-settles-case-against-investor-in-trump-linked-crypto-projects-amid-pay-to-play-allegations-2000730615)). The timing drew fierce criticism: Senator Elizabeth Warren said Sun had "poured $90 million into Trump's crypto ventures" and called him Trump's "lap dog," House Financial Services Democrats raised pay-to-play allegations, and former SEC policy official Amanda Fischer argued the agency was abandoning a case in which "TRON was a securities offering" that had generated enormous investor losses ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/sec-settles-case-against-investor-in-trump-linked-crypto-projects-amid-pay-to-play-allegations-2000730615)).

## The Trump orbit

By 2025 TRON was embedded in the Trump family's crypto empire. In May 2025, at the TOKEN2049 conference in Dubai, [World Liberty Financial](/organizations/world-liberty-financial/) co-founder [Zach Witkoff](/people/zach-witkoff/) announced that the firm's USD1 stablecoin — launched that March — was being integrated into Tron, on a panel moderated by Sun with Eric Trump also on stage ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/wlfs-zach-witkoff-usd1-selected-official-stablecoin-mgx-investment-binance-2025-05-01/)). The announcement tied USD1 to the $2 billion investment by Abu Dhabi's MGX in [Binance](/organizations/binance/). Sun was by then WLF's biggest known investor and an adviser, with more than $75 million invested, and Reuters noted that Trump had handed management of his assets to his children — making the Tron–WLF integration a transaction between the Sun-built network and the Trump family's business operation.

## Fighting crypto crime

Less noticed than the legal battles is TRON's role in policing its own network. The T3 Financial Crime Unit — a joint operation of TRON, Tether and blockchain-intelligence firm TRM Labs — had frozen more than $450 million in criminal assets across five continents by July 2026, a figure TRON DAO highlighted as the stablecoin-heavy chain tried to position itself as a compliant venue for large flows ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/10/3325494/0/en/USDT-on-TRON-Exceeds-90-Billion-as-TRON-Leads-USDT-Transfer-Volume-with-4-2-Trillion-YTD.html)).
