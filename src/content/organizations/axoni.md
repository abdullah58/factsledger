## Overview

Axoni — legally Schvey, Inc., doing business as Axoni — is a New York-based capital-markets technology firm that builds distributed-ledger infrastructure for financial institutions ([Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/vc-deal-of-the-day-axioni-raises-18m-series-a-46944.html?print)). Founded in 2013 by a team of distributed-ledger entrepreneurs and cryptography specialists and led by CEO Greg Schvey, it developed blockchain software for post-trade data management across credit-default swaps, equity swaps and foreign-exchange derivatives in partnership with more than a dozen global banks ([Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/vc-deal-of-the-day-axioni-raises-18m-series-a-46944.html?print)). Its flagship product, the Veris network, became one of the few production-grade institutional blockchain networks in finance — and in 2024 the business was acquired by the London Stock Exchange Group ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-acquires-axoni-post-trade-technology/)).

## From pilot to production: the equity-swaps network

Axoni's defining project was the application of distributed ledgers to one of finance's most operationally painful problems: reconciling equity swaps. In October 2016 the company organized a working group of Citi, Credit Suisse and J.P. Morgan to test blockchain-based post-trade processing of equity swaps ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/dlt-provider-secures-major-bank-funding/)), followed by a months-long pilot with the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board, Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, Citi and Credit Suisse of a peer-to-peer blockchain infrastructure using smart contracts ([Investment Executive](https://www.investmentexecutive.com/news/industry/working-group-tests-blockchain-system-for-equity-swaps/)). The goal, as Citi's head of prime finance put it, was fewer valuation disputes, less reconciliation and real-time data access ([Investment Executive](https://www.investmentexecutive.com/news/industry/working-group-tests-blockchain-system-for-equity-swaps/)).

In 2020 Axoni turned the pilot into a live network: Veris, a distributed-ledger platform for post-trade lifecycle management and reconciliation of equity swaps ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-acquires-axoni-post-trade-technology/)). Veris lets counterparties share and compare data on swap deals, positions, trades and related cash flows, streamlining reconciliation and preventing cash-flow breaks ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-acquires-axoni-post-trade-technology/)). Its breakthrough moment came in September 2021, when BlackRock — the world's largest money manager — joined Veris alongside Goldman Sachs and Citigroup, integrating Axoni's software with its Aladdin operating system to match and reconcile post-trade data on stock swaps ([Bloomberg](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/crypto/blackrock-joins-blockchain-platform-axoni-for-equity-swap-trades)). The market it served was substantial: Bank for International Settlements data put the global equity-linked forwards and swaps market at about $3.6 trillion ([Bloomberg](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/crypto/blackrock-joins-blockchain-platform-axoni-for-equity-swap-trades)).

## A bank-backed cap table

Unusually for a startup, Axoni's investors were largely its prospective customers. The December 2016 Series A raised $18 million led by Wells Fargo and ICAP's fintech investment arm Euclid Opportunities, with Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, Thomson Reuters, Andreessen Horowitz, FinTech Collective, F-Prime and Digital Currency Group participating, taking total funding above $20 million ([Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/vc-deal-of-the-day-axioni-raises-18m-series-a-46944.html?print)). In November 2018 HSBC joined the Series B, bringing that round to $36 million and total funding to $59 million, with the money earmarked for AxCore — Axoni's data-synchronization platform — and AxLang, an Ethereum-compatible smart-contract language designed for formal verification ([Finadium](https://finadium.com/hsbc-invests-in-enterprise-blockchain-fintech-axoni/)). By then Axoni's investor roster spanned the industry's biggest names — Goldman Sachs, Nyca Partners, Andreessen Horowitz, Citi, Coatue Management, Digital Currency Group, F-Prime Capital, Franklin Templeton Investments, J.P. Morgan, NEX Group, Wells Fargo and Y Combinator — effectively turning its customer base into its cap table ([Finadium](https://finadium.com/hsbc-invests-in-enterprise-blockchain-fintech-axoni/)). HSBC's global-banking COO said at the time that distributed-ledger technology would "clearly be important in modernizing the shared infrastructure of capital markets" and that Axoni had "demonstrated that they are the leader in this space" ([Finadium](https://finadium.com/hsbc-invests-in-enterprise-blockchain-fintech-axoni/)).

## The LSEG acquisition

In September 2024 the London Stock Exchange Group moved to acquire Axoni's post-trade technology, including the Veris network, with the transaction expected to complete in the fourth quarter; after a transition period LSEG would own and operate Veris ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-acquires-axoni-post-trade-technology/)). The deal followed LSEG's 2022 acquisition of Acadia and its Quantile approval as part of a deliberate build-out of multi-asset post-trade capabilities ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-acquires-axoni-post-trade-technology/)). Axoni's CEO Greg Schvey framed it as a step toward modernizing over-the-counter derivatives post-trade infrastructure ([The TRADE](http://www.thetradenews.com/lseg-acquires-axoni-post-trade-technology/)). The outcome is a rare one in enterprise blockchain: a bank-built ledger network now operated by a stock-exchange group.
