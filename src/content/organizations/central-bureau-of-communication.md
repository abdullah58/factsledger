**The Central Bureau of Communication (CBC)** is the outreach wing of India's [Ministry of Information & Broadcasting](/organizations/ministry-of-information-and-broadcasting/): the ministry's nodal body for taking government policies and programmes to the public through advertising, field publicity, exhibitions and folk-media performances ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/governance/govt-media-advisory-body-gets-new-name-central-bureau-of-communication-aim-is-uniformity/1008992/); [MIB induction material](http://mib.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-06/induction-material.pdf)). It was created on 8 December 2017 by merging three older media units — the Directorate of Advertising and Visual Publicity (DAVP), the Directorate of Field Publicity (DFP) and the Song & Drama Division — into a single body first called the Bureau of Outreach and Communication, then renamed the Central Bureau of Communication in June 2022 ([ANI via LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/agency-news/india-news-government-notifies-organisational-set-up-of-central-bureau-of-communication-3864874.html/amp)).

The bureau's distinctive feature is its physical reach: 23 regional offices and 148 field offices, built for face-to-face communication in rural and semi-urban India rather than media buying alone ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/governance/govt-media-advisory-body-gets-new-name-central-bureau-of-communication-aim-is-uniformity/1008992/)). In 2023 it stepped outside that role, co-producing the animated series ["Krish, Trish and Baltiboy: Bharat Hain Hum"](/creative-works/krish-trish-and-baltiboy/) with Graphiti Studios — a move the I&B Secretary framed as the bureau's first attempt at earning revenue rather than merely spending it ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/ib-minister-launches-trailer-of-animated-series-on-indian-freedom-struggle/article67408846.ece)).

## Three media units, one bureau

The merger the bureau rests on happened on 8 December 2017, when the I&B Ministry folded the DAVP, the Directorate of Field Publicity and the Song & Drama Division into one integrated body, the Bureau of Outreach and Communication ([ANI via LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/agency-news/india-news-government-notifies-organisational-set-up-of-central-bureau-of-communication-3864874.html/amp)). In June 2022 a gazette notification — signed by joint secretary C. Senthil Rajan, per ThePrint — gave that body a single, uniform name at every level: "Central Bureau of Communication" at headquarters, zonal, regional and field offices alike ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/governance/govt-media-advisory-body-gets-new-name-central-bureau-of-communication-aim-is-uniformity/1008992/); [ANI via LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/agency-news/india-news-government-notifies-organisational-set-up-of-central-bureau-of-communication-3864874.html/amp)). The stated reason was "maintaining uniformity and streamlining" the vocabulary across central and regional divisions, and ministry sources stressed the structure itself was unchanged ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/governance/govt-media-advisory-body-gets-new-name-central-bureau-of-communication-aim-is-uniformity/1008992/)). Speaking to The Daily Jagran, the bureau's Principal Director-General Satyendra Prakash tied the move to the government's "Minimum Government, Maximum Governance" doctrine, and described how the bureau still reaches places with no direct media dialogue through folk songs, street plays, public meetings and exhibitions ([The Daily Jagran](https://www.thedailyjagran.com/india/davp-renamed-to-bureau-of-outreach-and-communication-know-reason-behind-govts-decision-10044804)).

## What the bureau does: 360-degree communication

The ministry describes the CBC's job as providing "360 degrees communication solutions" to government bodies, and acting as the government's adviser on media strategy ([MIB](http://www.mib.gov.in/index.php/en/ministry/organizations/central-bureau-communication)). Its mandate: branding the government as the "prime facilitator of people's empowerment" via print, audio-visual, exhibitions, outdoor and new media ([MIB](http://www.mib.gov.in/index.php/en/ministry/organizations/central-bureau-communication)). The physical stat behind it — 23 regional offices and 148 field offices, independently confirmed by ThePrint — is why the bureau's work is weighted toward field publicity rather than metropolitan media buying ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/governance/govt-media-advisory-body-gets-new-name-central-bureau-of-communication-aim-is-uniformity/1008992/)). Three divisions do the work: the Advertising & Visual Communication Division (the old DAVP) as the nodal arm for scheme publicity; a Folk Communication Division using drama, dance-drama, puppetry, ballets, operas and folk recitals for interpersonal outreach; and a Field Communication Division for direct rural outreach, including local-language Special Outreach Programmes with state governments and local bodies ([MIB induction material](http://mib.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-06/induction-material.pdf)). Alongside the [Press Information Bureau](/organizations/press-information-bureau/) — the ministry's nodal agency for the print and electronic press — the CBC implements the national "Development Communication & Information Dissemination" scheme ([MIB induction material](http://mib.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-06/induction-material.pdf)).

## An expenditure body turns producer: the Bharat Hain Hum co-production

On 11 October 2023, Union Minister [Anurag Thakur](/people/anurag-thakur/) launched the trailer of *KTB–Bharat Hain Hum*, produced by the Central Bureau of Communication and Graphiti Studios: 52 episodes of 11 minutes each, dramatizing the 1500s–1947 freedom struggle, created by [Munjal Shroff](/people/munjal-shroff/) and Tilakraj Shetty ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/ib-minister-launches-trailer-of-animated-series-on-indian-freedom-struggle/article67408846.ece)). Doordarshan, Netflix and Amazon Prime telecast it simultaneously in 12 Indian languages, with dubs in French, Spanish, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and Korean; the Minister announced it would be shown to all parliamentarians, with a stated focus on women and tribal freedom fighters ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/ib-minister-launches-trailer-of-animated-series-on-indian-freedom-struggle/article67408846.ece)). What makes it institutionally notable is I&B Secretary Apurva Chandra's remark at the launch: it was the first time the CBC, "an expenditure body," was seeking to earn revenue ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/ib-minister-launches-trailer-of-animated-series-on-indian-freedom-struggle/article67408846.ece)). Season 2 launched at the [International Film Festival of India](/events/international-film-festival-of-india/) in [Goa](/places/goa/) on 23 November 2024, with actor Nagarjuna at the event; it streams on Prasar Bharati's Waves platform and spotlights fighters including [Wazir Ram Singh Pathania](/people/wazir-ram-singh-pathania/) of Himachal Pradesh and Birsa Munda of Jharkhand ([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/entertainment/second-season-of-animated-series-krish-trish-and-baltiboy-bharat-hain-hum-launched-at-iffi/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**When was the Central Bureau of Communication formed?**
It was set up on 8 December 2017, when the I&B Ministry merged the Directorate of Advertising and Visual Publicity, the Directorate of Field Publicity and the Song & Drama Division into one body — first named the Bureau of Outreach and Communication, then renamed the Central Bureau of Communication by a June 2022 gazette notification.

**Why was it renamed in 2022?**
The ministry said the rename was to bring uniformity of nomenclature across headquarters, zonal, regional and field offices, which had previously carried different names. Ministry sources stressed the organizational structure itself did not change — only the naming, signed off in a notification by joint secretary C. Senthil Rajan.

**How is the CBC different from the Press Information Bureau?**
Both are I&B Ministry media units with split jobs: the PIB is the nodal agency for the print and electronic press — the interface between government and media — while the CBC handles outreach: advertising, field publicity, exhibitions and folk communication. With the New Media Wing, they implement the national Development Communication & Information Dissemination scheme.

**What was the bureau's role in "Krish, Trish and Baltiboy: Bharat Hain Hum"?**
The CBC co-produced the animated series with Graphiti Studios: 52 episodes of 11 minutes dramatizing lesser-known freedom fighters from the 1500s to 1947, telecast simultaneously on Doordarshan, Netflix and Amazon Prime Video. I&B Secretary Apurva Chandra called it the bureau's first revenue-seeking production — a departure for what had been purely an expenditure body.
