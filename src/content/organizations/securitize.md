## Overview

Securitize is a tokenization platform that puts traditional securities on blockchain rails — and in July 2026 it became a publicly traded company itself, listing on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker SECZ after a SPAC merger with Cantor Equity Partners II that raised more than $400 million ([CoinDesk](https://Www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/06/securitize-eyes-acquisitions-with-usd400-million-war-chest-after-going-public-ceo-says)). Founded in 2017 and led by co-founder and CEO Carlos Domingo, the company provides issuance, transfer-agency and fund-administration services for tokenized securities to clients including BlackRock, Apollo, KKR, Hamilton Lane and VanEck ([CoinDesk](https://Www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/06/securitize-eyes-acquisitions-with-usd400-million-war-chest-after-going-public-ceo-says)).

Its defining relationship is with BlackRock: Securitize is the SEC-registered transfer agent, tokenization platform and placement agent for the BlackRock USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund (BUIDL) — BlackRock's first tokenized fund on a public blockchain, launched in March 2024 and the world's largest tokenized Treasury fund at roughly $2.5 billion ([Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328344/20260930/securitize-integrates-adi-chain-tokenized-blackrock-funds-now-have-uae-sovereign-rails.htm)). BlackRock also led a $47 million strategic investment in Securitize alongside BUIDL's launch, making the asset manager both its largest institutional client and a significant shareholder ([Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328344/20260930/securitize-integrates-adi-chain-tokenized-blackrock-funds-now-have-uae-sovereign-rails.htm)).

## The licensing moat

Securitize's pitch to institutions is regulatory rather than technological: it operates as an SEC-registered broker-dealer and transfer agent and runs an alternative trading system, while its European unit works under the EU DLT Pilot Regime — a combination its backers present as uniquely complete for regulated digital-securities infrastructure across both jurisdictions ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/private-companies/securitize-to-list-on-nyse-after-completing-cantor-spac-merger)). Its tokenized fund shares embed compliance in the token itself, using the ERC-3643 standard so that transfers automatically verify KYC and accreditation before settling ([Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328344/20260930/securitize-integrates-adi-chain-tokenized-blackrock-funds-now-have-uae-sovereign-rails.htm)).

At its NYSE debut the company tokenized its own shares on Solana and Avalanche — described as the largest tokenized stock globally at launch — and by July 2026 it had issued roughly $4.4 billion in tokenized assets, the most of any issuer tracked by RWA.xyz ([CoinDesk](https://Www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/06/securitize-eyes-acquisitions-with-usd400-million-war-chest-after-going-public-ceo-says); [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328344/20260930/securitize-integrates-adi-chain-tokenized-blackrock-funds-now-have-uae-sovereign-rails.htm)). CEO Domingo told CoinDesk the $400 million war chest would fund acquisitions of complementary businesses — "not rivals," which he said would add nothing technologically — toward a one-stop shop for institutional tokenization ([CoinDesk](https://Www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/06/securitize-eyes-acquisitions-with-usd400-million-war-chest-after-going-public-ceo-says)).

## The UAE push

In September 2026 Securitize signed a memorandum of understanding with Dubai's Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority and integrated ADI Chain — a blockchain built by a subsidiary of Abu Dhabi conglomerate International Holding Company and licensed under the Abu Dhabi Global Market — as the sixth network in its multichain ecosystem ([Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328344/20260930/securitize-integrates-adi-chain-tokenized-blackrock-funds-now-have-uae-sovereign-rails.htm)). The move creates a technical pathway for tokenized products from clients like BlackRock, KKR, Apollo and Hamilton Lane to be issued and settled on Gulf infrastructure under a dual US–UAE regulatory framework — though no product had actually deployed on ADI Chain at announcement ([Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328344/20260930/securitize-integrates-adi-chain-tokenized-blackrock-funds-now-have-uae-sovereign-rails.htm)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not claim Securitize is the only licensed tokenization venue — "first" and "unique" characterizations come from industry press, not from SEC records checked here. Asset figures are mid-2026 values and move with markets. The UAE integrations are pathways and MOUs, not launched products. The profile does not assess whether tokenized equities will achieve the multi-trillion-dollar scale projected by Domingo, Citi or BCG.
