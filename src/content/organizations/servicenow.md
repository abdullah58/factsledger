## Overview

ServiceNow is an American enterprise software company whose defining episode
came before most people had heard of it: in late 2011, VMware offered about
$2.5 billion for the company, founder Fred Luddy and much of the board wanted
to sell, and Sequoia Capital's Doug Leone blocked the deal — offering to buy
out any seller at the VMware price, an offer nobody took up
([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/amp/article/cross-border/servicenow-techs-ultimate-second-act/50581/1)).
Six months later the company went public, and the rejected takeover became the
founding myth of its independence. Today it sells the Now Platform, a cloud
system for automating IT and business workflows, under chief executive Bill
McDermott, the former SAP chief who took over in January 2020
([TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/enterprise-software/news/252472769/SAPs-McDermott-joins-ServiceNow-to-push-enterprise-growth)).

## What the organization is known for

ServiceNow began in IT service management — software that runs corporate IT
help desks — and spent the 2010s expanding that workflow engine into human
resources, customer service, security operations and other departments
([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/amp/article/cross-border/servicenow-techs-ultimate-second-act/50581/1)).
By 2023 it described itself as a broad platform for digital transformation
achieved by digitizing and further automating workflows, reporting full-year
2022 revenue of $7.2 billion, up 23 percent on 2021
([Computer Weekly](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366542113/ServiceNows-Bill-McDermott-a-work-in-progress)).
Its commercial signature, reported in 2018, was a 98 percent customer renewal
rate across more than 4,000 customers, including 850 of the Global 2000
([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/amp/article/cross-border/servicenow-techs-ultimate-second-act/50581/1)).

## Products and services

The core product is the Now Platform, which aims to help enterprises integrate
legacy systems and refine enterprise workflows and processes
([ZDNet](https://www.zdnet.com/article/servicenow-acquires-artificial-intelligence-startup-element-ai/)).
The original pitch was simplicity for the average office worker: a single
collection centre for requests, data points and checklists that algorithms
could then analyze to predict needs and flag concerns
([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/amp/article/cross-border/servicenow-techs-ultimate-second-act/50581/1)).
Under McDermott the company layered generative AI onto the platform, most
visibly with Now Assist, announced at the Knowledge 23 customer event in May
2023 as a conversational system for employee self-service
([Computer Weekly](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366542113/ServiceNows-Bill-McDermott-a-work-in-progress)).
ServiceNow says it runs its SaaS platform on [Microsoft](/organizations/microsoft/)'s
Azure cloud in addition to its own infrastructure
([ZDNet](https://www.zdnet.com/article/servicenow-acquires-artificial-intelligence-startup-element-ai/)).

## Ownership, founders and leadership

Fred Luddy founded the company in 2003 as Glidesoft, working from his San
Diego home at the age of 49, after losing a roughly $35 million stake in the
2002 accounting-fraud collapse of Peregrine Systems, where he had been chief
technology officer; Peregrine's top executives went to prison, while Luddy was
never charged with wrongdoing
([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/amp/article/cross-border/servicenow-techs-ultimate-second-act/50581/1)).
Luddy stepped aside from the CEO role in 2011 to become chief product
officer, making way for Frank Slootman, the former Data Domain chief hired
with Sequoia's backing to build an enterprise sales machine
([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/amp/article/cross-border/servicenow-techs-ultimate-second-act/50581/1)).
Slootman was succeeded by former eBay CEO John Donahoe in 2017, and Donahoe by
Bill McDermott in January 2020 after Donahoe left to run Nike
([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/amp/article/cross-border/servicenow-techs-ultimate-second-act/50581/1);
[TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/enterprise-software/news/252472769/SAPs-McDermott-joins-ServiceNow-to-push-enterprise-growth)).
McDermott had spent 17 years at SAP, becoming CEO of SAP America in 2002,
co-CEO in 2010 and sole CEO in 2014 — the first non-German to hold the post
([TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/enterprise-software/news/252472769/SAPs-McDermott-joins-ServiceNow-to-push-enterprise-growth)).

## Funding and acquisitions

The company raised a $2.5 million Series A led by JMI Equity in July 2005,
followed by two further rounds totaling $11 million, and took $41.4 million
in a Sequoia-led Series D in late 2009, by which point revenue was doubling
annually and the business was cash-flow positive
([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/amp/article/cross-border/servicenow-techs-ultimate-second-act/50581/1)).
It went public on June 29, 2012, pricing at $18 — above the expected $15–17
range — and opening at $23.16, up 29 percent; the sale of 11.65 million shares
raised $209.7 million, of which founder Luddy sold 2.65 million shares, with
Morgan Stanley leading the underwriting
([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-servicenow-ipo-idUSBRE85S0W620120629/)).
In 2020, under McDermott, ServiceNow made four AI acquisitions — Loom Systems,
Passage AI, Sweagle and Montreal-based Element AI, co-founded by
machine-learning pioneer Yoshua Bengio, who agreed to stay on as a technical
advisor; financial terms were not disclosed
([ZDNet](https://www.zdnet.com/article/servicenow-acquires-artificial-intelligence-startup-element-ai/)).

## Regulation, licenses and compliance record

None of the five fully-read sources for this profile documents a regulatory
enforcement action, license proceeding or compliance certification involving
ServiceNow. McDermott has publicly framed the platform's enterprise pitch
around data governance, telling Computer Weekly that customer data stays the
customer's — "we don't even have the right to look at it" — with compliance
and security guardrails built in
([Computer Weekly](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366542113/ServiceNows-Bill-McDermott-a-work-in-progress)).
A reported 2014 patent-infringement settlement and a 2013 federal security
authorization appear only in tertiary leads and are not asserted here (see
open items).

## Competitors and partners

Forbes India names [Salesforce](/organizations/salesforce/), Microsoft, IBM and
Workday as the competitive set ServiceNow was pivoting against as it moved
beyond IT departments
([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/amp/article/cross-border/servicenow-techs-ultimate-second-act/50581/1)).
On partnerships, ServiceNow runs its SaaS platform on Microsoft's Azure in
addition to its own cloud infrastructure
([ZDNet](https://www.zdnet.com/article/servicenow-acquires-artificial-intelligence-startup-element-ai/)).
McDermott's own framing is complementarity rather than replacement: the core
transactional systems stay in place while ServiceNow provides the action layer
above them
([Computer Weekly](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366542113/ServiceNows-Bill-McDermott-a-work-in-progress)).
