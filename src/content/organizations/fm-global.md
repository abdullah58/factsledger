## Overview

FM — known for most of its modern history as FM Global — is a mutual commercial property insurer headquartered in Johnston, Rhode Island, specializing in loss prevention for large corporations ([Insurance News](https://www.insurancenews.com.au/daily/fm-global-aims-to-strengthen-position-through-rebrand), [Providence Business News](https://pbn.com/insurer-fm-signs-on-as-principal-partner-of-team-in-new-golf-league/)). It traces its roots to 1835, when Rhode Island textile mill owner Zachariah Allen persuaded fellow manufacturers to create the Manufacturers Mutual Fire Insurance Company, insuring factories that took steps to minimize fire risk ([Insurance News](https://www.insurancenews.com.au/daily/fm-global-aims-to-strengthen-position-through-rebrand)). The company's legal name in the Rhode Island registry remains Factory Mutual Insurance Co.; "FM" is its communicative brand ([Providence Business News](https://pbn.com/insurer-fm-signs-on-as-principal-partner-of-team-in-new-golf-league/)).

## The 2024 rebrand and the prevention doctrine

In July 2024, FM Global rebranded as simply FM, uniting the mutual insurance company, the middle-market insurer renamed FM Affiliated, and its cargo and boiler-and-machinery reinsurance businesses under one brand ([Insurance News](https://www.insurancenews.com.au/daily/fm-global-aims-to-strengthen-position-through-rebrand)). Chairman and CEO Malcolm Roberts framed it around the company's founding doctrine: "the majority of property loss is preventable" ([Insurance News](https://www.insurancenews.com.au/daily/fm-global-aims-to-strengthen-position-through-rebrand)). The rebrand also launched a renewables unit — FM's first new market product in 25 years — aimed at bespoke risk transfer for solar and other clean-energy assets, including research into hail-resilient solar panels ([Insurance News](https://www.insurancenews.com.au/daily/fm-global-aims-to-strengthen-position-through-rebrand)). Under the mutual model, eligible policyholders have received more than $5 billion in credits since 2001, on roughly $10 billion in global annual premium ([Insurance News](https://www.insurancenews.com.au/daily/fm-global-aims-to-strengthen-position-through-rebrand)).

## Wildfire intelligence and an unusual sponsorship play

FM's prevention doctrine drove its August 2026 acquisition of FortressFire, a firm combining machine learning with physics-based modeling to quantify wildfire ignition risk down to individual structures — prescribing mitigation, not just mapping hazard zones ([Reinsurance News](https://www.reinsurancene.ws/fm-acquires-wildfire-intelligence-provider-fortressfire/)). FortressFire continues as an independent, wholly owned division serving the broader market ([Reinsurance News](https://www.reinsurancene.ws/fm-acquires-wildfire-intelligence-provider-fortressfire/)). And in a break from the industry's low profile, FM bought into sports: the FM Championship became the first women's professional golf event in New England in 20 years, and FM signed as principal partner of Boston Common Golf — Rory McIlroy's team — in the tech-forward TGL golf league ([Providence Business News](https://pbn.com/insurer-fm-signs-on-as-principal-partner-of-team-in-new-golf-league/)).
