## Overview

On September 26, 2026, a Hollywood sitcom producer stood on a charity-gala stage in Los Angeles and begged the Democratic National Committee to save itself from itself. Accepting the Founders Angel Award at [Project Angel Food](/organizations/project-angel-food/)'s Angel Awards, [Chuck Lorre](/people/chuck-lorre) — the 73-year-old creator of *Two and a Half Men* and *The Big Bang Theory* — told the room: "I'd like to beg the [Democratic National Committee] to run from the suicidal insanity of the far-left woke culture in the coming election" ([Reality Tea](https://www.realitytea.com/2026/09/29/chuck-lorre-calls-out-donald-trump-los-angeles-speech/)). He urged the party to "seek the common sense middle ground that emanates from the hard-working, blue-collar heart and soul of America" ([Hollywood in Toto](https://www.hollywoodintoto.com/chuck-lorre-woke-1-aoc/)). No public reply from the committee was found in the sources read for this profile — but the plea landed in the middle of an argument the committee is already having with itself about exactly that question.

The Democratic National Committee is the principal governing organization of the Democratic Party of the United States ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Democratic_National_Committee)). It oversees the writing and promotion of the party platform every four years and provides national leadership on campaign strategy, fundraising, political activity and election tactics ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Democratic_National_Committee)). Founded in 1848, it is the oldest national party committee in American politics, headquartered in Washington, D.C. ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Democratic_National_Committee)). Its main counterpart is the Republican National Committee, the campaign arm of the rival [Republican Party](/organizations/republican-party) ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Democratic_National_Committee)). The committee is made up of the chairs and vice chairs of each state Democratic Party plus more than 200 elected members from all 50 states, the District of Columbia and the territories ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Democratic_National_Committee)).

## The speech that named the committee

Lorre's plea was the part of his speech that most coverage buried. The headlines went to his attack on President Trump — he called the president a "bloated orange piece of s–t" and closed with a tweaked Serenity Prayer asking God for "the serenity to no longer give a s–t about all the stupid people in this country," labeling Trump a "Russian asset" ([Parade](https://parade.com/news/chuck-lorre-blasts-trump-in-brutal-acceptance-speech)). He called Melania Trump "cold-hearted" and contrasted her unfavorably with Michelle Obama, and he mocked Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. by saying working-class Americans "will likely die of measles because Bobby Kennedy told them not to get vaccinated" before doing an impression of Kennedy's voice ([Reality Tea](https://www.realitytea.com/2026/09/29/chuck-lorre-calls-out-donald-trump-los-angeles-speech/)).

But between the jokes about the White House, Lorre turned his fire on his own side's left flank. The venue made the moment stranger: the fundraiser was held at the newly named [Chuck Lorre Family Foundation](/organizations/chuck-lorre-family-foundation) Campus in Los Angeles, and it netted over $1 million for Project Angel Food — announced by the charity's CEO, Richard Ayoub ([Memesita](https://www.memesita.com/chuck-lorre-targets-donald-trump-and-rfk-jr-at-fundraiser/)). Lorre's philanthropy had paid for that campus and its kitchen, valued at $55.5 million, and helped Project Angel Food triple its annual meal production from 1.5 million to more than 4.5 million meals ([Memesita](https://www.memesita.com/chuck-lorre-targets-donald-trump-and-rfk-jr-at-fundraiser/)). So the man publicly begging the DNC to run toward the center was doing it from a stage he had effectively built himself, at a charity gala his own foundation's campus made possible.

## The Martin era: $216 million and a fired consultant class

The committee Lorre was addressing has been run since February 1, 2025, by Ken Martin, the former Minnesota Democratic-Farmer-Labor Party chair. Martin won the DNC chairmanship at the party's winter meeting with 246.5 of 428 votes cast, defeating Wisconsin party chair Ben Wikler (134.5) and former Maryland governor Martin O'Malley (44) from a field of eight qualified candidates ([NPR](https://www.kacu.org/2025-02-01/the-democratic-party-votes-for-new-leadership-choosing-minnesotas-ken-martin-as-chair)). In his acceptance speech he promised the party would not be divided under him: "We have one team, one team, the Democratic Party. We have one fight, one fight. The fight's not in here" ([NPR](https://www.kacu.org/2025-02-01/the-democratic-party-votes-for-new-leadership-choosing-minnesotas-ken-martin-as-chair)). He is the first white man to chair the committee since now-Senator Tim Kaine; the chair had been held by women and/or people of color since 2011 ([NPR](https://www.kacu.org/2025-02-01/the-democratic-party-votes-for-new-leadership-choosing-minnesotas-ken-martin-as-chair)).

Nineteen months into the job, Martin made his case in unusually blunt terms. In a September 17, 2026 interview with MeidasTouch's Scott MacFarlane, he said the DNC had raised a record $216 million — compared with $116 million raised by then-chair Tom Perez at the same point ahead of the 2018 midterms — and claimed, "We've raised more money than any other chair without the White House in the history of the Democratic Party" ([MeidasTouch](https://meidasnews.com/news/dnc-chair-reveals-his-216-million-midterm-bet)). The money, he said, was being spent on "a different theory of the case": challenging the conventional wisdom of Washington consultants, investing more heavily in state parties, year-round organizing and infrastructure that reaches voters earlier ([MeidasTouch](https://meidasnews.com/news/dnc-chair-reveals-his-216-million-midterm-bet)). He revealed he had fired the consultants tied to the old approach — "I fired all those consultants. We're doing it a different way" — and admitted the gamble could go either way: "This is going to be a bet that either fails miserably or succeeds amazingly" ([MeidasTouch](https://meidasnews.com/news/dnc-chair-reveals-his-216-million-midterm-bet)).

## The center-vs-left fight, in the committee's own words

Lorre's plea did not come out of nowhere. Days before the gala, Martin himself was publicly policing the boundary between the party and its far-left fringe. Asked how the party was handling divisions as socialist candidates gained prominence, Martin called far-left streamer Hasan Piker's past remarks "absolutely disgusting and deplorable" while insisting, "he does not represent the Democratic Party and he certainly doesn't represent the viewpoints of almost every one of our candidates and our elected officials" ([Fox News Digital](https://foxnews.com/politics/top-dem-operative-draws-line-deplorable-hasan-piker-touting-partys-big-tent)). Piker had been scrutinized for comments including the claim that the U.S. "deserved 9/11" ([Fox News Digital](https://foxnews.com/politics/top-dem-operative-draws-line-deplorable-hasan-piker-touting-partys-big-tent)). Martin framed the party as "a big tent" — "no one should confuse unity with unanimity in the Democratic Party, right? … We have lots of different viewpoints and ideas" — while drawing a hard line at rhetoric blaming Jewish Americans for threats and violence, which he had previously called "dangerous, wrong, and antisemitic" ([Fox News Digital](https://foxnews.com/politics/top-dem-operative-draws-line-deplorable-hasan-piker-touting-partys-big-tent)).

The pressure behind that line-drawing is visible in the party's own ranks. The [Democratic Socialists of America](/organizations/democratic-socialists-of-america) counts New York City's mayor among its members, and Martin's "big tent" defense came in response to questions about navigating divisions as socialist candidates — including Michigan's Democratic Senate nominee Abdul El-Sayed, who campaigned with Piker during the primary — gained prominence ([Fox News Digital](https://foxnews.com/politics/top-dem-operative-draws-line-deplorable-hasan-piker-touting-partys-big-tent)). El-Sayed has since tried to distance himself from the streamer as Republicans turned the association into a midterm attack line ([Fox News Digital](https://foxnews.com/politics/top-dem-operative-draws-line-deplorable-hasan-piker-touting-partys-big-tent)).

## The 2026 midterms: counterprogramming without a convention

Notably, the DNC chose not to stage its own showpiece for the 2026 midterms. It "opted not to hold its own national convention, an event typically held only during presidential election cycles," leaving the stage to the GOP's unusual two-day midterm convention in Dallas ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/11/republican-midterm-convention-wraps-up-in-dallas-texas-key-takeaways)). Instead, the committee went on offense from the sidelines: in early September, House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries and the DNC announced counterprogramming plans, sending four House members — Oversight ranking member Robert Garcia and Reps. Jason Crow, Madeleine Dean and Sydney Kamlager-Dove — to Texas to "engage with everyday Americans who are drowning in the Republican economy" ([Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/campaigns/congressional/4714539/democrats-counterprogramming-rnc-convention-dallas/)). The DNC itself held a press conference on the convention's first day featuring Illinois Governor JB Pritzker and Texas Democratic Party chairman Kendall Scudder, with the message that "Trump and Republicans are the reason why families are unable to pay their bills" ([Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/campaigns/congressional/4714539/democrats-counterprogramming-rnc-convention-dallas/)).

Martin, for his part, was projecting total confidence. With 47 days to the election, he told [Fox News](/organizations/fox-news/) Digital: "Mark my words right here… The Democratic Party is going to take control of the House back and we're going to control the Senate back" ([Fox News Digital](https://foxnews.com/politics/top-dem-operative-draws-line-deplorable-hasan-piker-touting-partys-big-tent)). Whether the fired consultants' replacements deliver — and whether the "common sense middle ground" Lorre begged for is where the party ends up — is the bet Martin himself says could "fail miserably or succeed amazingly" ([MeidasTouch](https://meidasnews.com/news/dnc-chair-reveals-his-216-million-midterm-bet)).
