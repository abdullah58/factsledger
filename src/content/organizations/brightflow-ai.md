## Overview

Brightflow AI is an American fintech company that gives small businesses — mainly e-commerce brands — real-time visibility into their cash flow, with forecasting tools and access to growth capital. Founded in 2019 by Robbie Bhathal and Jeffrey Priebe, it raised a $15 million Series A in September 2022, and in June 2023 it acquired the older fintech [CircleUp](/organizations/circleup/) — not the other way round — taking over its Helio brand-data engine in a deal in which no cash changed hands ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/21/brightflow-small-business-capital/); [NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-sells-vc-funds-final-home-still-unknown/)).

Brightflow AI is not the same company as Brightflow SAS, a Paris-based medical-device firm developing a heart pump for end-stage heart failure, which raised a €16.5 million Series A in September 2024 ([Cardiovascular News](https://cardiovascularnews.com/brightflow-sas-raises-e16-5m-in-series-a-financing-round/)). The shared name has caused the two companies' funding news to be confused in database listings, so the distinction matters: one sells cash-flow software to small businesses, the other builds cardiac devices.

The company's pitch starts from a stark statistic: cash-flow problems account for 82% of small-business failures, according to U.S. Bank data cited by [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/21/brightflow-small-business-capital/). Brightflow's answer is to pull a business's scattered financial data into one dashboard and forecast what happens next, so owners are not "blindsided by their finances" and can make adjustments in real time ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/21/brightflow-small-business-capital/)).

## The product: cash-flow intelligence for e-commerce brands

Brightflow AI describes itself as a financial intelligence platform that "demystifies finance for the everyday owner," with an initial focus on e-commerce ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brightflow-ai-raises-15-million-series-a-100-million-in-debt-financing-to-bridge-financial-gap-for-small-business-owners-301629271.html)). Its cash-flow calculator pulls financial data from platforms including Shopify, Amazon, QuickBooks, NetSuite, Xero, Plaid, Facebook and Google Ads, then gives a real-time view of cash flow ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/21/brightflow-small-business-capital/)). On top of that sit demand forecasts, profitability tracking, return-on-ad-spend (ROAS) and customer-lifetime-value (LTV) data, plus a business credit score that evaluates financial health and creditworthiness ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brightflow-ai-raises-15-million-series-a-100-million-in-debt-financing-to-bridge-financial-gap-for-small-business-owners-301629271.html)).

The point of differentiation, as founder and CEO Robbie Bhathal put it to TechCrunch, is automation: most rivals help companies sell more online, while Brightflow focuses on "the automation of financial services and disseminating that information in a way that a business owner can understand" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/21/brightflow-small-business-capital/)). The platform also runs a capital-lending program, offering growth capital during periods of volatility or high interest rates for traditional credit ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/21/brightflow-small-business-capital/)). It sells on a freemium model, with a free limited tier scaling up to $5,000 a month for enterprise-level packages ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-sells-vc-funds-final-home-still-unknown/)).

Bhathal's own background shaped the product. Before starting Brightflow in 2019, he co-founded Suiteness, a hotel-booking site, and said the experience of growing that company — and the difficulty of managing accounting, finance and forward planning while growing fast — is what led him to start Brightflow with Jeffrey Priebe ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/21/brightflow-small-business-capital/)). "I don't come from too much money so to access capital, you're trying to find the best places," he told TechCrunch, framing the company as a bid to democratize finance for owners who lack his own hard-won connections ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/21/brightflow-small-business-capital/)).

## Funding: a $15 million Series A and a $100 million debt facility

Brightflow AI's funding history, as announced in September 2022, runs through three tranches: a previously unannounced $4.2 million seed round led by Bonfire Ventures, a $15 million Series A led by Haymaker Ventures, and $100 million in debt financing from i80 Group — over $19 million in equity raised in total ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brightflow-ai-raises-15-million-series-a-100-million-in-debt-financing-to-bridge-financial-gap-for-small-business-owners-301629271.html); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/21/brightflow-small-business-capital/)). The company said it would use the money to enhance the platform, recruit talent and provide growth opportunities to small and medium-sized businesses more rapidly ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brightflow-ai-raises-15-million-series-a-100-million-in-debt-financing-to-bridge-financial-gap-for-small-business-owners-301629271.html)).

## The CircleUp arc: partnership, breakup, acquisition

Brightflow AI's defining move came in 2023, when it absorbed a much older and much better-funded fintech in three steps. CircleUp was founded in 2012 as an equity-crowdfunding marketplace for consumer brands, pivoted in 2017 to an investment-and-financing model with a $125 million [CircleUp Growth Partners](/organizations/circleup-growth-partners/) fund, and had raised over $250 million over its lifetime ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-breakup-lending-fund-sold-vc-fund-in-transit-ai-play-fate-unknown/); [NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-sells-vc-funds-final-home-still-unknown/)). By early 2023 it was unwinding itself, and Brightflow — then a four-year-old startup with just over $19 million in equity funding — ended up holding most of the pieces.

The first step was a partnership. On March 7, 2023 the two companies announced that CircleUp would transition the servicing of its loan portfolio to Brightflow AI, so brands could monitor their credit profile and apply for new business loans using Brightflow's software and data ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brightflow-ai-and-circleup-announce-strategic-partnership-301764197.html)). In the prior year Brightflow had unlocked more than $100 million in growth capital for its customers, and CircleUp's then-CEO Danny Mitchell said Brightflow's monitoring tools would give brands "the best long-term financing options" ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brightflow-ai-and-circleup-announce-strategic-partnership-301764197.html)).

The second step was the breakup. In March 2023 CircleUp began divesting its financing vehicles: the [Credit Advisors](/organizations/circleup-credit-advisors/) loan fund passed to Brightflow AI, which also absorbed several CircleUp team members, while the Growth Partners venture fund was put up for sale to an unidentified multi-strategy asset manager ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-breakup-lending-fund-sold-vc-fund-in-transit-ai-play-fate-unknown/)). Sources told NOSH the lending exit was driven by rising interest rates and a wish to lower the company's risk profile; one former borrower guessed the consumer-goods loan book had been unprofitable because many borrowers were not yet profitable ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-breakup-lending-fund-sold-vc-fund-in-transit-ai-play-fate-unknown/)). At that point, what would become of Helio — CircleUp's AI-driven brand-data platform — was still unclear ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-breakup-lending-fund-sold-vc-fund-in-transit-ai-play-fate-unknown/)).

The third step answered that question. On June 1, 2023 Brightflow AI acquired CircleUp itself, including Helio ([IBS Intelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup/)). The terms were not disclosed, but Bhathal told NOSH that no cash was exchanged: CircleUp's investors joined Brightflow AI's own cap table instead ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-sells-vc-funds-final-home-still-unknown/)). Several CircleUp engineers joined the combined company, which then employed 36 people ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-sells-vc-funds-final-home-still-unknown/)).

## Why Helio mattered

Helio was the prize. CircleUp's machine-learning platform evaluated consumer companies by tracking over 1.6 million brands and 16 million attributes, and Brightflow said acquiring it would deepen its data, machine-learning and analytics capabilities and strengthen the cash-flow forecasts, cash-management tools and underwriting analyses at the core of its platform ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-sells-vc-funds-final-home-still-unknown/); [IBS Intelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup/)). "By acquiring CircleUp's Helio platform, we're able to deepen our data, machine learning, and analytics capabilities," Bhathal said in the announcement, "and provide our customers with the most comprehensive solution available" ([IBS Intelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup/)).

The plan was to fold Helio into Brightflow's forecasting capabilities, giving brands a fuller picture of their financial health — including insights into untapped market whitespace and trends — rather than leaving Helio to stand alone as a product for large companies and investors ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-sells-vc-funds-final-home-still-unknown/)). That mattered because Helio had struggled as a standalone business: some brands had told NOSH it was hard to gain sustained value from it given its price point and limited dataset, and Bhathal attributed its troubles to being "too ahead of its time" and to the "growth at all costs" model falling out of fashion ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-sells-vc-funds-final-home-still-unknown/)). His argument was that inside a larger software offering aimed at emerging brands — who value ease of use and speed of integration — the same data would finally pay ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-sells-vc-funds-final-home-still-unknown/)).

The deal also gave Brightflow a much larger customer story to tell. At the time of the acquisition, CircleUp was credited with supplying over $1 billion in capital to more than 500 consumer companies, including Beyond Meat, Supergoop!, Nom Nom Now, Liquid I.V., SmartyPants, Function of Beauty, Kitu Life, Halo Top and Nutpods, backed by investors including Union Square Ventures, GV, Canaan Partners, QED Advisors, TPG and Temasek ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/425249/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup/?abkw=citybizsanfrancisco)). CircleUp chairman and founder Ryan Caldbeck called the deal "a new standard for financial intelligence for consumer companies," and founder Rory Eakin said it was "a positive step forward for CircleUp and the industry" ([IBS Intelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup/)). Bhathal — himself a former CircleUp customer — said the combined company would hit profitability that quarter, a forecast no later source read for this profile confirmed ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-sells-vc-funds-final-home-still-unknown/)).

## FAQ

**What does Brightflow AI do?**
Brightflow AI is a financial intelligence platform for small businesses, focused on e-commerce brands. It consolidates data from sales, accounting and advertising platforms to give real-time cash-flow visibility, forecasts, a business credit score and access to growth capital ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/21/brightflow-small-business-capital/); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brightflow-ai-raises-15-million-series-a-100-million-in-debt-financing-to-bridge-financial-gap-for-small-business-owners-301629271.html)).

**Who founded Brightflow AI?**
Robbie Bhathal (CEO) and Jeffrey Priebe founded the company in 2019. Bhathal had previously co-founded the hotel-booking site Suiteness ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/21/brightflow-small-business-capital/)).

**Did CircleUp acquire Brightflow AI?**
No — the reverse happened. Brightflow AI acquired CircleUp on June 1, 2023, including its Helio insights engine. No cash was exchanged; CircleUp's investors joined Brightflow AI's cap table ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2023/circleup-sells-vc-funds-final-home-still-unknown/); [IBS Intelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/brightflow-ai-acquires-circleup/)).

**How much funding did Brightflow AI raise?**
Over $19 million in equity by September 2022 — a $4.2 million seed round led by Bonfire Ventures and a $15 million Series A led by Haymaker Ventures — plus a $100 million debt facility from i80 Group ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brightflow-ai-raises-15-million-series-a-100-million-in-debt-financing-to-bridge-financial-gap-for-small-business-owners-301629271.html)).

**Is Brightflow AI the same as Brightflow SAS?**
No. Brightflow SAS is an unrelated Paris-based medical-device company developing a heart pump; it raised a €16.5 million Series A in September 2024 ([Cardiovascular News](https://cardiovascularnews.com/brightflow-sas-raises-e16-5m-in-series-a-financing-round/)).
