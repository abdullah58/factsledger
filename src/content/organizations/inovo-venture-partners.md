## Overview

Inovo Venture Partners is a Warsaw-based early-stage venture capital firm investing in Polish and Central and Eastern European technology startups, at late seed and Series A, after traction. Its partners are Tomasz Swieboda, Michał Rokosz and Maciej Małysz ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/)). It closed its third fund at €105 million in June 2023, and its portfolio of 30 companies includes six valued above €100 million: [Booksy](/organizations/booksy/), Infermedica, Packhelp, Preply, Spacelift and Tidio ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/)). Inovo was Booksy's earliest institutional backer, participating in the $800,000 round in 2015 alongside [Müller Medien](/organizations/muller-medien/) and [Piton Capital](/organizations/piton-capital/) ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2021/01/29/booksy-slots-in-70m/)).

## Three funds, one thesis

Inovo was launched in 2011, and its fund history reads as a ladder of increasing institutional backing ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/)). Fund I, launched around 2011, was in exit phase by 2023 with a 1.2x DPI and a 3.2x return on a 15-company portfolio, with Booksy and Restaumatic among the unrealised top performers ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/)). Fund II was announced in April 2020 at €40 million, backed by the European Investment Fund and others, and closed at €54 million in April 2021 ([Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/polish-vc-firm-inovo-venture-partners-bags-e40m-to-back-disruptive-tech-startups-in-central-and-eastern-europe/), [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/)). Fund III closed at €105 million in June 2023, above its €100 million target ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/)).

| Fund | Vintage | Size | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fund I | ~2011 | Not stated | 15 companies; 3.2x return; 1.2x DPI by 2023 |
| Fund II | 2020–2021 | €54m final close | EIF-backed; announced at €40m in April 2020 |
| Fund III | 2023 | €105m final close | Target €100m; IFC, EIF and PFR Ventures as LPs |

Fund III's limited partners mark the firm's institutional arrival: the IFC (World Bank Group) committed €15 million — its first venture capital investment in Central and Eastern Europe — alongside the European Investment Fund and PFR Ventures, and more than 65% of the private capital came from recurring investors, including family offices and Polish tech entrepreneurs such as Rafał Brzoska of InPost, Marcin Żukowski, co-founder of [Snowflake](/organizations/snowflake/), and the Aternus and Wałach brothers ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/)). The fund plans 20–30 companies, with tickets of €0.5 million to €4 million and follow-ons up to €10 million, 60% of capital to Polish startups and 40% to the rest of Central and Eastern Europe ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/)).

## What it backs

Inovo is a generalist with deep pockets in a few areas: healthcare (Infermedica, Jutro Medical), developer tools (Spacelift, Archbee), AI and machine learning (Zowie, AI Clearing), and SaaS-enabled marketplaces and B2B software ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/)). Its Fund I names included Booksy, Brand24 (which IPO'd), Restaumatic, ECC Games (which IPO'd) and Sotrender, and its Fund II names included Spacelift, Infermedica, Tidio, Packhelp, Preply, Eyerim, Allset, SunRoof and Archbee ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/), [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/polish-vc-firm-inovo-venture-partners-bags-e40m-to-back-disruptive-tech-startups-in-central-and-eastern-europe/)).

The firm was named the best venture capital investor in Poland by entrepreneurs in 2018, in the Golden Book of Venture Capital in Poland, and "House of the Year – Venture Capital" by PSIK ([Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/polish-vc-firm-inovo-venture-partners-bags-e40m-to-back-disruptive-tech-startups-in-central-and-eastern-europe/)). Six of its 30 portfolio companies were valued above €100 million by 2023: Booksy, Infermedica, Packhelp, Preply, Spacelift and Tidio ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/)). The Fund II portfolio shows the range: Spacelift, Infermedica, Tidio, AI Clearing, Zowie, Jutro Medical, Intiaro, Packhelp, Preply, Eyerim, Allset, SunRoof and Archbee ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/), [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/polish-vc-firm-inovo-venture-partners-bags-e40m-to-back-disruptive-tech-startups-in-central-and-eastern-europe/)).

## The Booksy connection

Inovo's Booksy link is the earliest in the company's funding history. It participated in the $800,000 round in 2015 alongside Müller Medien and Piton Capital, and Booksy went on to become one of Fund I's top performers ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2021/01/29/booksy-slots-in-70m/), [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/)). Booksy founder [Stefan Batory](/people/stefan-batory/) has been quoted as saying that if he started a new startup he would ask Inovo to be his first venture investor again — a remark this profile does not quote verbatim, because the article carrying it was not read in full, but the substance (Inovo as Booksy's earliest institutional backer) is confirmed by the funding record and by Inovo's own fund reporting ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/06/warsaw-based-inovo-vc-closes-its-third-fund-at-e105-million-to-back-early-stage-founders-from-cee/)).
