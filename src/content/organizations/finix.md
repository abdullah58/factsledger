## Overview

Finix is the rare startup whose defining moment was an investor walking away. The San Francisco payments company was co-founded by Richie Serna and Sean Donovan and launched its payments processing solution in 2015, built by a team drawn from PayPal, First Data and Worldpay ([FStech](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Finix_Raises_17m_For_Payments_Solution.php)). In 2020, Sequoia Capital led Finix's $35 million Series B — then walked away weeks later after concluding Finix competed too directly with [Stripe](/organizations/stripe/), one of Sequoia's biggest private holdings. Finix kept the money but lost Sequoia as a backer ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/24/finix-raises-75-million-to-take-on-stripe-as-a-payment-processor/?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAI3k4FyoGk6rFes-qctJ0WO0CCrOtNXyIsJJggTDJJs4UopQDonHuCQ662Bxjbe7XjS3TohSirhRsKqNIBZCzfdbbNSENIqjAgXiSCZfMlKFzfXvCLzYTVeVozQ33M2NLNXOE5lCO_h3wQxT8WHh_NBerLj-iquEF4xdfZl1c5Gw)). Four years later, Finix had become exactly what Sequoia feared: a full-stack payment processor competing with Stripe head-on, backed by $208 million in total funding after a $75 million Series C in October 2024 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/24/finix-raises-75-million-to-take-on-stripe-as-a-payment-processor/?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAI3k4FyoGk6rFes-qctJ0WO0CCrOtNXyIsJJggTDJJs4UopQDonHuCQ662Bxjbe7XjS3TohSirhRsKqNIBZCzfdbbNSENIqjAgXiSCZfMlKFzfXvCLzYTVeVozQ33M2NLNXOE5lCO_h3wQxT8WHh_NBerLj-iquEF4xdfZl1c5Gw)).

## From infrastructure toolkit to processor

Finix did not start out trying to be Stripe. Its original pitch, as Serna told TechCrunch, was "payments infrastructure" — an API-driven stack that let software platforms, marketplaces and enterprises build their own payment facilitators in as little as two months, at a fraction of the $3–5 million it typically cost to build in-house ([FStech](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Finix_Raises_17m_For_Payments_Solution.php); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/24/finix-raises-75-million-to-take-on-stripe-as-a-payment-processor/?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAI3k4FyoGk6rFes-qctJ0WO0CCrOtNXyIsJJggTDJJs4UopQDonHuCQ662Bxjbe7XjS3TohSirhRsKqNIBZCzfdbbNSENIqjAgXiSCZfMlKFzfXvCLzYTVeVozQ33M2NLNXOE5lCO_h3wQxT8WHh_NBerLj-iquEF4xdfZl1c5Gw)). Early funding followed that story: a 2019 Series A of $17.5 million led by Bain Capital Ventures with participation from [Visa](/organizations/visa/), Insight Venture Partners and Aspect Ventures took total capital past $20 million ([FStech](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Finix_Raises_17m_For_Payments_Solution.php)), and a $30 million investment in 2022 brought the total to $133 million as Finix — by then a registered payment facilitator — doubled its annual payments volume from 2020 to 2021 and served more than 12,000 small businesses, schools and places of worship a month ([Finovate](https://finovate.com/paytech-finix-secures-30-million-investment/)). At that point Finix was already, by its own Nilson-list comparison, a top-50 US merchant acquirer by total processing volume and merchant count ([Finovate](https://finovate.com/paytech-finix-secures-30-million-investment/)).

The decisive shift came in 2023, when Finix became a full-stack acquirer/processor — connecting directly to the card networks instead of riding on a third-party processor, as Stripe does ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/24/finix-raises-75-million-to-take-on-stripe-as-a-payment-processor/?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAI3k4FyoGk6rFes-qctJ0WO0CCrOtNXyIsJJggTDJJs4UopQDonHuCQ662Bxjbe7XjS3TohSirhRsKqNIBZCzfdbbNSENIqjAgXiSCZfMlKFzfXvCLzYTVeVozQ33M2NLNXOE5lCO_h3wQxT8WHh_NBerLj-iquEF4xdfZl1c5Gw); [Finovate](https://finovate.com/finix-brings-in-75-million/)). Serna called becoming a processor "hugely transformational" for the business and the main driver of the October 2024 $75 million raise, led by Acrew Capital and co-led by Leap Global and Lightspeed Venture Partners, with Citi Ventures and Tribeca Venture Partners joining existing investors ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/24/finix-raises-75-million-to-take-on-stripe-as-a-payment-processor/?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAI3k4FyoGk6rFes-qctJ0WO0CCrOtNXyIsJJggTDJJs4UopQDonHuCQ662Bxjbe7XjS3TohSirhRsKqNIBZCzfdbbNSENIqjAgXiSCZfMlKFzfXvCLzYTVeVozQ33M2NLNXOE5lCO_h3wQxT8WHh_NBerLj-iquEF4xdfZl1c5Gw)).

## The anti-Stripe pitch

Finix's competitive argument is economic transparency aimed at a specific audience. Where Stripe takes a flat 2.9% plus 30 cents per transaction, Finix uses a cost-plus model that breaks down every charge and shows its markup — a pitch aimed at the roughly 22 million US businesses that lack developers to build payments themselves, and at larger platforms that want pricing visibility ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/24/finix-raises-75-million-to-take-on-stripe-as-a-payment-processor/?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAI3k4FyoGk6rFes-qctJ0WO0CCrOtNXyIsJJggTDJJs4UopQDonHuCQ662Bxjbe7XjS3TohSirhRsKqNIBZCzfdbbNSENIqjAgXiSCZfMlKFzfXvCLzYTVeVozQ33M2NLNXOE5lCO_h3wQxT8WHh_NBerLj-iquEF4xdfZl1c5Gw)). Serna rejects the idea that Stripe owns the market: "Stripe only actually owns 6% of the US market, and less than 2% worldwide," he told TechCrunch, adding that about 91% of payments still run through systems built in the 1980s and 1990s — and naming [Adyen](/organizations/adyen/) alongside Stripe and Finix as companies with room to grow ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/24/finix-raises-75-million-to-take-on-stripe-as-a-payment-processor/?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAI3k4FyoGk6rFes-qctJ0WO0CCrOtNXyIsJJggTDJJs4UopQDonHuCQ662Bxjbe7XjS3TohSirhRsKqNIBZCzfdbbNSENIqjAgXiSCZfMlKFzfXvCLzYTVeVozQ33M2NLNXOE5lCO_h3wQxT8WHh_NBerLj-iquEF4xdfZl1c5Gw)).

Scale-wise, Finix remains far smaller than its rival: it supported more than 12,000 merchants in 2022 and, after a record deal year in 2024, Serna estimated it could have more than 24,000 — "still a long way off" from Stripe's millions of businesses, as TechCrunch put it ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/24/finix-raises-75-million-to-take-on-stripe-as-a-payment-processor/?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAI3k4FyoGk6rFes-qctJ0WO0CCrOtNXyIsJJggTDJJs4UopQDonHuCQ662Bxjbe7XjS3TohSirhRsKqNIBZCzfdbbNSENIqjAgXiSCZfMlKFzfXvCLzYTVeVozQ33M2NLNXOE5lCO_h3wQxT8WHh_NBerLj-iquEF4xdfZl1c5Gw)). But the 2024 numbers showed momentum: revenue quadrupled in a year, the company processed 432 million transactions daily across the US and Canada, and its suite — real-time payouts, no-code/low-code and API tooling, card-present and card-not-present omnichannel support, and cross-border capabilities — is now positioned as a complete processor stack ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/24/finix-raises-75-million-to-take-on-stripe-as-a-payment-processor/?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAI3k4FyoGk6rFes-qctJ0WO0CCrOtNXyIsJJggTDJJs4UopQDonHuCQ662Bxjbe7XjS3TohSirhRsKqNIBZCzfdbbNSENIqjAgXiSCZfMlKFzfXvCLzYTVeVozQ33M2NLNXOE5lCO_h3wQxT8WHh_NBerLj-iquEF4xdfZl1c5Gw); [Finovate](https://finovate.com/finix-brings-in-75-million/)).
