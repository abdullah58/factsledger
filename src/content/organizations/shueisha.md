## Overview

Shueisha Inc. is a Japanese publishing company headquartered in Chiyoda, [Tokyo](/places/tokyo/), and the largest manga publisher in the world, with about 30% of the Japanese manga market and ¥195 billion in annual sales in the 2021–22 fiscal year ([Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/85643-largest-publisher-of-manga)). It is the home of *Weekly Shōnen Jump* — the world's best-selling manga magazine — and of *One Piece*, the best-selling manga series by a single author in history ([Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2023/10/one-piece-the-record-of-the-mega-popular-manga-series-explained-760171)). Shueisha was also the lead voice of the four publishers that sued [Cloudflare](/organizations/cloudflare/) over manga piracy: it issued the joint victory statement when the [Tokyo District Court](/organizations/tokyo-district-court/) ordered Cloudflare to pay it ¥126,500,000 on 19 November 2025 ([Tokyo District Court](https://www.courts.go.jp/ip/eng/assets/ip/eng/chizai_en/chizai_en-pdf-3813.pdf)).

Shueisha was founded in 1925 as the entertainment-related publishing division of [Shogakukan](/organizations/shogakukan/), and split off to become an independent company on 8 August 1926; it was incorporated as Shueisha Inc. in 1949 ([Shueisha Inc.](https://www.shueisha.co.jp/en/)). (Publishers Weekly instead gives 1926 as the year it was founded as the division — a discrepancy flagged in the open items below.) The two companies remain close: both belong to the Hitotsubashi Group, are headquartered next to each other in Tokyo, and jointly own Viz Media, the major US manga publisher ([Publishers Weekly](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/publisher-news/article/71335-global-publishing-leaders-2016-shueisha-publishing-co-ltd.html)).

## Weekly Shōnen Jump and the scale of the catalogue

*Weekly Shōnen Jump* was first published in 1968 and is the engine of Shueisha's business ([Shueisha Inc.](https://www.shueisha.co.jp/en/)). Guinness World Records calls it the best-selling manga magazine ever, with roughly 7.5 billion copies sold since it launched in 1968 ([Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2023/10/one-piece-the-record-of-the-mega-popular-manga-series-explained-760171)). Its serialisations include *Dragon Ball*, which had sold over 150 million copies overall by 2016, and *One Piece*, over 140 million by the same date, with Shueisha titles licensed in more than 35 countries ([Publishers Weekly](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/publisher-news/article/71335-global-publishing-leaders-2016-shueisha-publishing-co-ltd.html)). *One Piece* alone has since passed 500 million copies printed worldwide: Eiichiro Oda's series, serialised in *Weekly Shōnen Jump* since December 1997, holds the record for the most copies published for the same comic-book series by a single author, first verified in 2015 and confirmed again in 2022, with 516.6 million copies printed between December 1997 and September 2022 ([Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2023/10/one-piece-the-record-of-the-mega-popular-manga-series-explained-760171)).

That scale is also what made piracy an existential problem for Shueisha — and what pushed it toward legal digital distribution. *Shōnen Jump+*, its digital manga platform, launched in September 2014, and its global version, MANGA Plus by SHUEISHA, launched worldwide in January 2019, publishing translated chapters simultaneously with [Japan](/places/japan/) ([Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2022-06-29/jump-aims-to-release-all-new-manga-series-simultaneously-in-english-after-2023/.187212)). MANGA Plus was Shueisha's first direct service to readers outside Japan, [China](/places/china/) and South Korea, delivering new chapters free of charge at the same time as their Japanese release ([Google Arts & Culture](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/sQXhtJeecZ7q4A)). From 2023, every new series on *Shōnen Jump+* has been released simultaneously in English on MANGA Plus ([Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2022-06-29/jump-aims-to-release-all-new-manga-series-simultaneously-in-english-after-2023/.187212)).

## The Cloudflare manga-piracy lawsuit

Shueisha sued Cloudflare, Inc. — headquartered in [San Francisco](/places/san-francisco/), [California](/places/california/) — together with [Kodansha](/organizations/kodansha/), Shogakukan and [Kadokawa](/organizations/kadokawa/) on 1 February 2022 in the Tokyo District Court ([Shogakukan](https://www.shogakukan.co.jp/storage/files/article/2021/20220201e.pdf)). The suit concerned two piracy sites that posted more than 4,000 manga works without permission and at their peak attracted over 300 million monthly visits combined, according to a survey by Authorized Books of Japan ([Shueisha](https://www.shueisha.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/notice20251119eng.pdf)). Shueisha's print right for the one work of its own at issue was established on 15 October 2015, its infringement notice under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act reached Cloudflare on 7 December 2020, and the damage period ran from 8 January 2021 to 28 February 2022 ([Tokyo District Court](https://www.courts.go.jp/ip/eng/assets/ip/eng/chizai_en/chizai_en-pdf-3813.pdf)).

The money figures are often confused, so they are worth stating exactly. At filing, the four publishers put total damages for the four litigated works at about ¥5.6 billion and sought about ¥115 million per company as part of that sum — about ¥460 million in total ([Shogakukan](https://www.shogakukan.co.jp/storage/files/article/2021/20220201e.pdf); [Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2025-11-19/shueisha-kodansha-shogakukan-kadokawa-win-copyright-suit-against-cloudflare/.231199)). By judgment, each publisher's claim had become ¥126.5 million. On 19 November 2025 the Tokyo District Court recognised total damages of about ¥3.6 billion (about US$24 million) for the four works, but because the publishers had claimed only a portion of what they suffered, it ordered payment of about ¥500 million in total: ¥126,500,000 to Shueisha, the same to Kodansha and Shogakukan, and ¥121,400,928 to Kadokawa, plus interest at 3% a year from 21 October 2022 ([Tokyo District Court](https://www.courts.go.jp/ip/eng/assets/ip/eng/chizai_en/chizai_en-pdf-3813.pdf); [Shueisha](https://www.shueisha.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/notice20251119eng.pdf)).

The court's reasoning mattered as much as the money. It dismissed the publishers' primary claim that Cloudflare was itself the transmitter of the pirated manga, but held that Cloudflare's failure to take timely and appropriate action after receiving infringement notices, and its negligent continuation of pirated-content distribution, constituted aiding and abetting copyright infringement — giving weight to the fact that Cloudflare had enabled a massive piracy site to operate "under circumstances where strong anonymity was secured" without identity-verification procedures ([Shueisha](https://www.shueisha.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/notice20251119eng.pdf)). A legal analysis published in April 2026 states that Cloudflare has appealed, with hearings at the Intellectual Property High Court awaited ([Monolith Law Office](https://monolith.law/en/it/cloudflare-piracy-case-liability)). Shueisha said the publishers would continue to protect the rights of works, creators and related parties and to push for the proper use of CDN services ([Shueisha](https://www.shueisha.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/notice20251119eng.pdf)).

## FAQ

**What is Shueisha?**
Shueisha Inc. is a Japanese publishing company headquartered in Chiyoda, Tokyo, founded in 1925 as the entertainment division of Shogakukan. It is the world's largest manga publisher, with about 30% of the Japanese manga market, and publishes *Weekly Shōnen Jump*.

**What is Weekly Shōnen Jump?**
*Weekly Shōnen Jump* is Shueisha's weekly manga magazine, first published in 1968. It is the best-selling manga magazine ever, with about 7.5 billion copies sold since 1968, and the home of *One Piece*, *Dragon Ball*, *Naruto* and *Demon Slayer*.

**How big is One Piece?**
*One Piece* by Eiichiro Oda has passed 500 million copies printed worldwide, with 516.6 million copies printed between December 1997 and September 2022. It holds the Guinness World Record for the most copies published for the same comic-book series by a single author.

**What is MANGA Plus?**
MANGA Plus by SHUEISHA is Shueisha's global digital manga platform, launched in January 2019. It publishes translated chapters of *Weekly Shōnen Jump* and *Shōnen Jump+* series simultaneously with Japan, free of charge, everywhere except Japan, China and South Korea.

**How much did Shueisha win from Cloudflare?**
The Tokyo District Court ordered Cloudflare to pay Shueisha ¥126,500,000 plus 3% annual interest from 21 October 2022, in a judgment dated 19 November 2025. About ¥500 million was ordered to the four publishers in total.
