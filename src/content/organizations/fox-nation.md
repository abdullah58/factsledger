## Overview

Fox Nation is Fox News Media's subscription streaming service — but it was never conceived as just another content app. From its 2018 debut it was pitched as a membership for Fox News "superfans," complete with a Founders Program that sold challenge coins, engraved rocks glasses and even a $1,200 tactical watch to its earliest subscribers ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/fox-nation-launch-date-fox-news/)). Its defining institution is not a show but a live event: the annual Patriot Awards, which honor military veterans, first responders and everyday heroes — and which have twice made national news by naming a Trump the Patriot of the Year ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/tvnewser/donald-trump-fox-nation-patriot-awards-pete-hegseth/); [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/melania-trump-awarded-patriot-of-the-year-what-is-it-and-why-did-us-first-lady-win-101762504123625.html)). Beneath that fan-club machinery sits a conventional streamer: more than 10,000 hours of original series, documentaries and specials, a $8.99 monthly price, and — since February 2026 — distribution as a Prime Video subscription ([Fox News](https://foxnews.com/media/fox-nation-takes-streaming-giants-prime-video-launch-featuring-exclusive-content-february-2026)).

## A launch built for superfans

Fox News set the debut for Tuesday, November 27, 2018, pricing the service at $5.99 a month or $64.99 a year — deliberately positioned, in the words of senior vice president of development and production John Finley, to "complement and enhance our powerhouse FOX News programming for the loyal superfans who want to remain connected to our signature platforms" ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/fox-nation-launch-date-fox-news/)). A month before launch, Fox opened the "FOX Nation Founders Program," a pre-sale built around four limited-edition packages: $60 bought a one-year subscription plus a Founder Challenge Coin; $130 added a second year and a Founder hat; $280 stretched to three years with engraved Founder rocks glasses and a Founder medal in a deluxe gift box; and the $1,200 top tier added a customized Founder Tactical Watch ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/fox-nation-launch-date-fox-news/)). Anyone who signed up on Veterans Day triggered a $5 donation to Folds of Honor, which funds educational scholarships for the children and spouses of fallen and disabled service members ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/fox-nation-launch-date-fox-news/)).

The launch was staged like a fan convention. Fox aired "FOX Nation First Look," an hour-long special in which Jesse Watters, Tomi Lahren, Pete Hegseth, Britt McHenry and Tyrus previewed their shows before an audience billed as "FNC super fans" in San Diego ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/fox-nation-launch-date-fox-news/)). That framing never left: Fox still describes the service as "a members-only destination for its most passionate and loyal super fans" ([Fox News](https://foxnews.com/media/fox-nation-takes-streaming-giants-prime-video-launch-featuring-exclusive-content-february-2026)).

## What the service became

Eight years on, the library has grown past 10,000 hours of curated original series, documentaries and specials spanning faith-based programming, history, true crime, live sports and lifestyle series ([Fox News](https://foxnews.com/media/fox-nation-takes-streaming-giants-prime-video-launch-featuring-exclusive-content-february-2026)). Its most-watched program is an unlikely prestige play: "Martin Scorsese Presents: The Saints," hosted by the filmmaker, whose second season included an episode directed by his daughter Francesca Scorsese ([Fox News](https://foxnews.com/media/fox-nation-takes-streaming-giants-prime-video-launch-featuring-exclusive-content-february-2026)). The broader slate mixes Hollywood names — Kevin Costner, Kim Kardashian, Rob Lowe, 50 Cent, Matthew McConaughey, Dan Aykroyd, Kelsey Grammer, Dennis Quaid — with Fox News star vehicles from Sean Hannity, Greg Gutfeld, Bret Baier, Brian Kilmeade and Ainsley Earhardt, plus exclusive new seasons of "COPS" and next-day access to Fox News Channel's primetime lineup ([Fox News](https://foxnews.com/media/fox-nation-takes-streaming-giants-prime-video-launch-featuring-exclusive-content-february-2026)). Sports arrived via Real American Freestyle, the startup freestyle-wrestling league whose events stream live only on Fox Nation ([Fox News](https://foxnews.com/media/fox-nation-takes-streaming-giants-prime-video-launch-featuring-exclusive-content-february-2026)).

The price crept up with the library: from $5.99 a month at launch to $8.99 a month (or $71.88 a year) by 2026 — a 50% increase ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/fox-nation-launch-date-fox-news/); [Fox News](https://foxnews.com/media/fox-nation-takes-streaming-giants-prime-video-launch-featuring-exclusive-content-february-2026)). Distribution widened in February 2026, when the full Fox Nation slate became available as a Prime Video subscription, with an option to bundle it with the Fox One streaming service for $24.99 a month — pitched at "cord-cutters" wanting Fox's news, sports and entertainment in a single app ([Fox News](https://foxnews.com/media/fox-nation-takes-streaming-giants-prime-video-launch-featuring-exclusive-content-february-2026)). The service is also carried across mobile and connected-TV apps and through pay-TV and virtual providers including DIRECTV, YouTube TV, Comcast Xfinity, The Roku Channel, DISH and SLING ([Business Wire](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/sean-hannity-host-fox-nations-signature-patriot-awards-ceremony-december-5-2024-11-19)). Morning Consult once named it one of the ten fastest-growing brands of 2023 among Gen Z adults, placing it in the top 15 overall — a data point Fox itself highlights ([Fox News](https://foxnews.com/media/fox-nation-takes-streaming-giants-prime-video-launch-featuring-exclusive-content-february-2026)).

## The Patriot Awards

The Patriot Awards, introduced in 2019, are Fox Nation's signature live property: an annual ceremony honoring "America's finest patriots," from military veterans and first responders to ordinary citizens ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/melania-trump-awarded-patriot-of-the-year-what-is-it-and-why-did-us-first-lady-win-101762504123625.html)). The sixth edition, held December 5, 2024, at the Tilles Center for Performing Arts in Brookville, New York, and emceed by Sean Hannity, was streamed live on Fox Nation with an encore on Fox News Channel — and it put the awards on the national news map ([Business Wire](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/sean-hannity-host-fox-nations-signature-patriot-awards-ceremony-december-5-2024-11-19)). President-elect Donald Trump walked onstage to "USA!" chants to receive the inaugural Patriot of the Year award from Hannity, calling the host "a friend of the country" and telling the crowd: "You have incredible people at Fox. You have incredible people at every level of Fox" — before joking that there were a couple of hosts "I don't like" ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/tvnewser/donald-trump-fox-nation-patriot-awards-pete-hegseth/)). Fellow honorees that night included the late NYPD officer Jonathan Diller, Indiana teenager Cameron Blasek, actor and televangelist Kirk Cameron, and retired General Dick Cody ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/tvnewser/donald-trump-fox-nation-patriot-awards-pete-hegseth/)).

The seventh annual ceremony, on November 6, 2025, again at the Tilles Center with Hannity hosting, kept the formula: everyday heroes onstage, Fox stars presenting, and Tunnel to Towers as a partner ([Tunnel to Towers Foundation](https://t2t.org/tunnel-to-towers-foundation-sponsors-seventh-annual-fox-nation-patriot-awards/)). The foundation presented its T2T Stephen Siller Courage Award to fallen FDNY firefighter John "JR" Gaudet, who died in the line of duty on February 12, 2025, at age 40; his widow Felly accepted on his behalf ([Tunnel to Towers Foundation](https://t2t.org/tunnel-to-towers-foundation-sponsors-seventh-annual-fox-nation-patriot-awards/)). Other categories included the Young Patriot, Salute to Service, Honor, Faith and Heroism awards ([Tunnel to Towers Foundation](https://t2t.org/tunnel-to-towers-foundation-sponsors-seventh-annual-fox-nation-patriot-awards/)). The night's headline, though, was Melania Trump receiving the Patriot of the Year award — one year after her husband. She thanked "American patriots" in the audience and at home, urging them to celebrate those who "dare to think differently": "Trust me, I know a little bit about challenging convention" ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/melania-trump-awarded-patriot-of-the-year-what-is-it-and-why-did-us-first-lady-win-101762504123625.html)). Fox never gave an official reason for the choice; reporting linked it to her children's advocacy, her role in reuniting Ukrainian children with their families, and her co-signing of the Take It Down Act in May 2025 ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/melania-trump-awarded-patriot-of-the-year-what-is-it-and-why-did-us-first-lady-win-101762504123625.html)).
