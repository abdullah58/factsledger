## Overview

SAIC Motor Corporation Limited is a Chinese state-owned automaker headquartered in Anting, Shanghai, controlled by the Shanghai municipal government, and for more than twenty consecutive years the largest automotive group in [China](/places/china/) by annual vehicle sales ([CompaniesHistory](http://www.companieshistory.com/saic-motor/); [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/gm-renews-china-joint-venture-with-saic-20-years-after-restructuring-2026-08-05/)). It was built not by selling its own cars but by hosting the world's carmakers: its joint ventures with Volkswagen, dating to 1984, and [General Motors](/organizations/general-motors/), dating to 1997, dominated the Chinese passenger-car market for decades — and when Chinese buyers turned to domestic electric cars, that same model became its biggest problem, with sales falling from a 7.05 million peak in 2018 to 4.01 million in 2024 before a rebound in 2025 ([Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2024-08-17/weekly-preview-joint-ventures-decline-while-domestic-brands-fail-to-rise-how-will-saic-win-the-battle-for-survival-102227355.html); [SAIC Motor](https://www.saicmotor.com/english/investor_relations/sales_volume/index_3.shtml)).

## The joint-venture machine

SAIC traces its institutional origin to 1955, when the Shanghai Internal Combustion Engine Spare Parts Manufacture Corporation was established in Shanghai, and for three decades it remained a small local manufacturer inside China's centrally planned system ([CompaniesHistory](http://www.companieshistory.com/saic-motor/)). Its rise began with foreign partnerships: in October 1984 SAIC Volkswagen was founded as Volkswagen Group's first joint venture in China ([Volkswagen Group China](https://volkswagengroupchina.com.cn/en/partner)), and the locally built Santana became the defining car of the Chinese market in the late 1980s and 1990s ([CompaniesHistory](http://www.companieshistory.com/saic-motor/)). General Motors followed, winning its partnership with SAIC in 1997 and growing to become one of the country's top-selling carmakers ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/gm-renews-china-joint-venture-with-saic-20-years-after-restructuring-2026-08-05/)).

SAIC also bought its way into brands of its own. After MG Rover collapsed in 2005, Nanjing Automobile paid about £53 million (around $105 million) for the MG brand; in December 2007 SAIC purchased Nanjing Auto, gaining control of MG and the Longbridge plant in England ([MotorTrend](https://www.motortrend.com/features/chinese-auto-giant-saic-buys-nanjing-snares-mg-assets-1446)). SAIC had already taken Rover expertise and technology during MG Rover's dissolution, but because [Ford](/organizations/ford/) owned the Rover name it sold those cars in China as Roewe ([MotorTrend](https://www.motortrend.com/features/chinese-auto-giant-saic-buys-nanjing-snares-mg-assets-1446)). An earlier overseas bet went wrong: in 2004 SAIC took a 48.9% stake in South Korea's SsangYong Motor for $500 million, and SsangYong filed for receivership in 2009 ([CompaniesHistory](http://www.companieshistory.com/saic-motor/)). By 2011 the company had completed its full listing on the Shanghai Stock Exchange under ticker 600104 and formally adopted the name SAIC Motor Corporation Limited ([CompaniesHistory](http://www.companieshistory.com/saic-motor/)).

## The fall: 2018–2024

At its peak SAIC sold 7.052 million vehicles in 2018, when the Volkswagen and GM joint ventures accounted for 86.6% of its sales ([Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2024-08-17/weekly-preview-joint-ventures-decline-while-domestic-brands-fail-to-rise-how-will-saic-win-the-battle-for-survival-102227355.html)). Then Chinese buyers shifted sharply to domestically developed electric and plug-in hybrid cars from [BYD](/organizations/byd/), Huawei-backed brands and newer startups, and both joint ventures lost volume and margin for years ([CompaniesHistory](http://www.companieshistory.com/saic-motor/)). By 2023 sales had fallen to 5.021 million, the joint ventures' share of sales had dropped to 72.1%, and in July 2024 SAIC sold 251,000 vehicles — 91,000 fewer than BYD that month ([Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2024-08-17/weekly-preview-joint-ventures-decline-while-domestic-brands-fail-to-rise-how-will-saic-win-the-battle-for-survival-102227355.html)).

The 2024 numbers show the depth of the fall. Wholesale sales were 4,013,023 vehicles, down 20.07% on 2023, and SAIC forecast net profit attributable to shareholders of just ¥1.5–1.9 billion — down 87–90% — with an underlying loss of ¥4.1–6 billion once non-recurring items were stripped out ([SAIC Motor](https://www.saicmotor.com/english/investor_relations/sales_volume/index_3.shtml); [Shanghai Metals Market](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103178751/SAIC-Motor-forecasts-two-digit-YoY-drop-in-2024-net-profit-attributable-to-shareholders/)). The collapse was concentrated in the GM venture: SAIC-GM sold 435,007 vehicles in 2024, down 56.54%, against 1,148,091 for SAIC-Volkswagen and 1,340,066 for SAIC-GM-Wuling ([SAIC Motor](https://www.saicmotor.com/english/investor_relations/sales_volume/index_3.shtml)). One bright spot held: the group's own brands — IM Motors, MG, Maxus, Wuling and Baojun — wholesaled 2.408 million vehicles, up 5%, and new-energy vehicles reached 1.234 million, up 9.9% ([Shanghai Metals Market](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103178751/SAIC-Motor-forecasts-two-digit-YoY-drop-in-2024-net-profit-attributable-to-shareholders/)).

## Restructuring, renewal and the 2025 rebound

GM's response to the decline was expensive: it recorded two non-cash charges totalling more than $5 billion on its China joint venture in 2024 and began a restructuring amid steep market-share losses ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/gm-renews-china-joint-venture-with-saic-20-years-after-restructuring-2026-08-05/)). In August 2026 GM renewed its joint-venture agreement with SAIC for 20 years after a restructuring that included plant closures and the elimination of some models; the 50-50 venture will focus on Cadillac and Buick in China, discontinue Chevrolet sales there, use China as an export hub, and launch at least 30 electric or hybrid vehicles by 2030 ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/gm-renews-china-joint-venture-with-saic-20-years-after-restructuring-2026-08-05/)). SAIC-GM has delivered more than 20 million vehicles over nearly three decades, and GM sold 1.9 million vehicles in China in 2025, down 51% from 2016 ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/gm-renews-china-joint-venture-with-saic-20-years-after-restructuring-2026-08-05/)).

SAIC's own response was a management reset — chairman Chen Hong retired and was replaced by former president Wang Xiaoqiu, with Jia Jianxu becoming president — and a push into its own electric brands and new technology partnerships, including a May 2024 deal with [Audi](/organizations/audi/) to co-develop high-end smart electric vehicles ([Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2024-08-17/weekly-preview-joint-ventures-decline-while-domestic-brands-fail-to-rise-how-will-saic-win-the-battle-for-survival-102227355.html); [Shanghai Metals Market](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103178751/SAIC-Motor-forecasts-two-digit-YoY-drop-in-2024-net-profit-attributable-to-shareholders/)). The 2025 figures suggest the reset is working at volume level at least: total sales rose 12.32% to 4,507,518 vehicles, with new-energy vehicles up 33.12% to 1,642,785, SAIC-GM recovering 22.99% to 535,000 and SAIC-GM-Wuling up 20.52% to 1,615,066 ([SAIC Motor](https://www.saicmotor.com/english/investor_relations/sales_volume/index_2.shtml)). The cheap Wuling Hongguang MINI EV, which became China's best-selling EV in 2021 at about ¥28,000 ($4,300), showed early on that SAIC's own brands could win on price even while the joint ventures struggled ([CompaniesHistory](http://www.companieshistory.com/saic-motor/)).

## The Cerence thread: robotaxis and the Buick Century

SAIC's link to [Cerence](/organizations/cerence/) runs through its most futuristic businesses rather than its volume brands. On 17 May 2022 SAIC Mobility Robotaxi — part of SAIC Motor — selected Cerence to provide mobility-assistant technology for its L4 self-driving robotaxi platform, described as the first of its kind in China ([Cerence Inc.](https://investors.cerence.com/_assets/_73b068af6d08cc8db83ee69cf5364b2a/cerence/news/2022-05-17_SAIC_Mobility_Robotaxi_Selects_Cerence_to_Power_200.pdf)). The robotaxi service had launched in Shanghai in December 2021, pooling SAIC Motor Passenger Vehicle, SAIC Motor AI Lab, autonomous-driving company Momenta and SAIC's ride-hailing platform, and its in-car assistant is summoned with "Xiao Ke" ([Cerence Inc.](https://investors.cerence.com/_assets/_73b068af6d08cc8db83ee69cf5364b2a/cerence/news/2022-05-17_SAIC_Mobility_Robotaxi_Selects_Cerence_to_Power_200.pdf)). A year later, on 27 February 2023, Cerence partnered with Yanfeng to bring its In-Car Communication system — an AI intercom that lets front and rear passengers talk without shouting — to SAIC-GM's Buick Century, making SAIC-GM one of the first automakers to use the system in China ([Cerence Inc.](https://investors.cerence.com/_assets/_64418cb86bd93820a0fc3d86daf9e797/cerence/news/2023-02-27_Cerence_Partners_with_Yanfeng_to_Simplify_In_Car_170.pdf)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is SAIC Motor state-owned?** Yes. It is ultimately controlled by the Shanghai municipal government through the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission, with the parent group holding a controlling stake of roughly 62–65% ([CompaniesHistory](http://www.companieshistory.com/saic-motor/); [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/gm-renews-china-joint-venture-with-saic-20-years-after-restructuring-2026-08-05/)).

**What brands does SAIC own?** Its own brands include MG, Roewe, Maxus, IM Motors and Feifan, alongside the joint-venture brands Volkswagen, Škoda, Audi, Buick, Chevrolet, Cadillac, Wuling and Baojun ([Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2024-08-17/weekly-preview-joint-ventures-decline-while-domestic-brands-fail-to-rise-how-will-saic-win-the-battle-for-survival-102227355.html); [SAIC Motor](https://www.saicmotor.com/english/investor_relations/sales_volume/index_3.shtml)).

**How did SAIC get the MG brand?** Nanjing Automobile bought MG after MG Rover collapsed in 2005, and SAIC bought Nanjing Auto in December 2007, gaining MG and the Longbridge plant in England ([MotorTrend](https://www.motortrend.com/features/chinese-auto-giant-saic-buys-nanjing-snares-mg-assets-1446)).

**Why did SAIC's sales fall after 2018?** Its Volkswagen and GM joint ventures, built for an era when foreign brands dominated China, lost ground as buyers shifted to domestic electric and plug-in hybrid cars; sales fell from 7.05 million in 2018 to 4.01 million in 2024 before rebounding to 4.51 million in 2025 ([Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2024-08-17/weekly-preview-joint-ventures-decline-while-domestic-brands-fail-to-rise-how-will-saic-win-the-battle-for-survival-102227355.html); [SAIC Motor](https://www.saicmotor.com/english/investor_relations/sales_volume/index_2.shtml)).

**What is SAIC's link to Cerence?** SAIC Mobility Robotaxi selected Cerence for its L4 robotaxi in 2022, and Cerence voice technology went into SAIC-GM's Buick Century in 2023 ([Cerence Inc.](https://investors.cerence.com/_assets/_73b068af6d08cc8db83ee69cf5364b2a/cerence/news/2022-05-17_SAIC_Mobility_Robotaxi_Selects_Cerence_to_Power_200.pdf); [Cerence Inc.](https://investors.cerence.com/_assets/_64418cb86bd93820a0fc3d86daf9e797/cerence/news/2023-02-27_Cerence_Partners_with_Yanfeng_to_Simplify_In_Car_170.pdf)).

## What this profile does not claim

- It does not assert a precise founding narrative beyond the 1955 institutional origin, which rests on a tier3 company history; SAIC has no individual founder, having been created as a state enterprise.
- It does not give a year for the extension of the SAIC-Volkswagen joint venture to 2040, because sources disagree on it.
- It does not state SAIC's final 2024 net profit (¥1.666 billion in some reporting) because that figure was not read in full; it uses the January 2025 forecast instead.
- It does not claim the 2025 sales rebound restored profitability; the profile reports volumes only, and SAIC's 2024 profit collapse is stated separately.
- SAIC Motor, the Shanghai automaker, is not the US defence contractor SAIC (Science Applications International Corporation); the two are unrelated companies.
