## Overview

Müller Medien is a family-owned German media group based in Nuremberg, founded in 1950 as a telephone-directory publisher and still owned entirely by the Oschmann family. Its core business remains directories — the "Das Örtliche", "Gelbe Seiten" and "Sellwerk.de" brands — but it also holds stakes in about 60 regional radio stations, concentrated in Bavaria, and invests in digital start-ups ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/companies/company/muller-medien-115743/)). It is the reason a German directory publisher sits in the [Booksy](/organizations/booksy/) entity graph: Müller Medien backed Booksy's $800,000 funding round in 2015, and Booksy is still described in the venture press as "the Müller Medien-backed beauty services marketplace" ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2021/01/29/booksy-slots-in-70m/)). This is the Nuremberg media group, not the Müller drugstore and retail chain based in Ulm.

## Three generations of a family media group

Hans Müller founded the company in 1950 as Telefonbuch Verlag Hans Müller, a telephone-directory publisher ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/companies/company/muller-medien-115743/)). From 1962 his grandson Gunther Oschmann led the company and expanded it, particularly into regional radio and specialised media ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/companies/company/muller-medien-115743/)). Since 2003 Michael and Constanze Oschmann, the third generation, have managed the company, and the shares remain entirely in the family: Gunther holds 1%, Constanze 49.5% and Michael 49.5% ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/companies/company/muller-medien-115743/)).

The group's divisions span directories, broadcasting, book publishing and new media, with operations in Europe, the United States and Asia ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/companies/company/muller-medien-115743/)). In broadcasting it holds stakes in about 60 regional radio stations, concentrated in Bavaria — including Antenne Bayern, Radio Charivari, Neue Welle Franken, Radio FFH and Studio Gong — plus regional television in Bayern TV and Ingolstadt TV ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/individual-owners/owner/the-oschmann-family-113799/)). The Oschmann family's own page counts more than 60 regional television and radio operators in Germany and Austria, alongside newspaper and book publishers ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/individual-owners/owner/the-oschmann-family-113799/)).

The group briefly owned a newspaper. In 2010 it acquired the Nürnberger AZ, then Germany's longest-running tabloid, and discontinued its print operations in 2012 ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/companies/company/muller-medien-115743/)).

## The digital turn and the Booksy investment

Since 2003 the third generation has directed investment through subsidiaries primarily toward digital start-ups in online portals and ad-targeting, with notable ventures including gutefrage.net, mittelstand-markthalle.de and SELLWERK ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/companies/company/muller-medien-115743/)). Michael and Constanze Oschmann operate as investors and start-up founders with limited public visibility ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/individual-owners/owner/the-oschmann-family-113799/)).

The best-documented of those investments is Booksy. Müller Medien participated in an $800,000 round for Booksy in 2015 alongside [Piton Capital](/organizations/piton-capital/) and [Inovo Venture Partners](/organizations/inovo-venture-partners/), before Booksy closed a $4.2 million Series A in 2017 led by [OpenOcean](/organizations/openocean/), a $13.2 million Series B in 2018 led by Piton, a $28.5 million round in September 2019, and a $70 million Series C in 2021 ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2021/01/29/booksy-slots-in-70m/)). The 2015 cheque was small, but it is the link that ties a 1950s directory publisher to a Polish beauty-booking startup — and the reason Global Venturing still introduces Booksy as "the Müller Medien-backed" marketplace six years later ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2021/01/29/booksy-slots-in-70m/)).

## The CSU connection

The profile would be incomplete without the political record, which is the part of the Oschmann story that English-language coverage otherwise omits. Gunther Oschmann, born in Nuremberg in 1940, built the group with stakes in more than 60 regional television and radio operators in Germany and Austria plus newspaper and book publishers, and he is close to the CSU party and active in Bavarian local politics ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/individual-owners/owner/the-oschmann-family-113799/)). The Media Ownership Monitor records that he intervened in editorial coverage at his regional stations and at the Nürnberger AZ to favour positive reporting of CSU members, and that a 2011 Nürnberger AZ report on the rap career of the son of then-Bavarian Interior Minister Joachim Herrmann was never published, citing "the good relations between the Oschmann and Herrmann families" ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/individual-owners/owner/the-oschmann-family-113799/)). He was also implicated in a private-loan scandal at the Bavarian Institute for New Media (BLM) — a €30,000 loan to the BLM chairman for private house construction — with no consequences, and in 2024 he received the BLM's lifetime achievement award ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/individual-owners/owner/the-oschmann-family-113799/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Müller Medien?**
A family-owned German media group founded in Nuremberg in 1950 as a telephone-directory publisher, now spanning directories, regional radio and television, book publishing and digital investments ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/companies/company/muller-medien-115743/)).

**Is Müller Medien the same as the Müller drugstore chain?**
No. The drugstore and retail chain (Müller Ltd. & Co. KG, based in Ulm) is a different company. This profile is the Nuremberg media group owned by the Oschmann family.

**Who owns Müller Medien?**
The Oschmann family: Gunther Oschmann holds 1%, and his children Constanze and Michael hold 49.5% each ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/companies/company/muller-medien-115743/)).

**What is its link to Booksy?**
Müller Medien participated in Booksy's $800,000 funding round in 2015, alongside Piton Capital and Inovo Venture Partners ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2021/01/29/booksy-slots-in-70m/)).

**What is the CSU connection?**
Gunther Oschmann is close to the CSU party and active in Bavarian local politics, and the Media Ownership Monitor records editorial interventions favouring positive CSU coverage at his stations and newspaper, including the suppression of a 2011 report ([Media Ownership Monitor Germany](https://germany.mom-gmr.org/en/owners/individual-owners/owner/the-oschmann-family-113799/)).
