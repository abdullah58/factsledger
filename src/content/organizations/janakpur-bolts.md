## Overview

Janakpur Bolts is a Twenty20 cricket franchise based in Janakpur, Nepal, competing in the [Nepal Premier League](/events/nepal-premier-league/) (NPL). The team won the league's inaugural championship in December 2024, then finished last in the 2025 season with a single win, and in 2026 expanded beyond Nepal by acquiring a second franchise, JB Bruges, in Belgium's EU T20 League ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/sports/2026/03/05/janakpur-bolts-acquire-franchise-in-belgium-s-eu-t20-league)). The ownership picture shifted across that arc: the franchise was introduced in 2024 under Dream Sports ownership with Amit Shrestha named as team owner ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/cricket/2024/10/02/can-planning-to-develop-npl-into-a-global-premier-event)), while the March 2026 announcement of the Belgium acquisition named Utsav Sigdel as team owner — a transition the reporting does not explain ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/sports/2026/03/05/janakpur-bolts-acquire-franchise-in-belgium-s-eu-t20-league)).

## Founding and the 2024 franchise auction

The Cricket Association of Nepal (CAN) sold the eight NPL franchises through a bidding process for a combined Rs168 million in September 2024 ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/cricket/2024/10/02/can-planning-to-develop-npl-into-a-global-premier-event)); an international account later put the combined figure at under Rs169 million (about $1.23 million), with the champions' prize money at around $81,000 ([Rediff](https://www.rediff.com/cricket/report/nepal-cricket-scales-new-heights-with-t20-league/20250529.htm)).

At the October 2024 owner-reveal ceremony, Janakpur Bolts was presented with ownership listed as Dream Sports, team owner Amit Shrestha, and general manager Niraj Khatiwada. Khatiwada said the franchise would "continue the legacy of Lumbini All Stars" from the earlier Nepal T20 League while taking "Nepali cricket to a new height through collaboration with CAN" ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/cricket/2024/10/02/can-planning-to-develop-npl-into-a-global-premier-event)). Wicketkeeper-batter Aasif Sheikh was named the franchise's marquee player ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/cricket/2024/10/02/can-planning-to-develop-npl-into-a-global-premier-event)), and former Sri Lanka international Pubudu Dassanayake took charge as head coach — the coach who would deliver the title ([Online Khabar](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/meet-the-head-coaches-npl.html)).

The inaugural NPL ran from November 30 to December 21, 2024, with Star Sports Network as broadcast partner and Siddhartha Bank as title sponsor in what was described as the largest sponsorship agreement in Nepali sports history ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/cricket/2024/11/29/npl-poised-to-herald-a-new-era-in-nepali-cricket)). CAN positioned the league as a clean break from its earlier Nepal T20 League venture of the 2022/23 season, which had collapsed under spot-fixing allegations, a Central Investigation Bureau investigation, and arrests ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/cricket/2024/11/29/npl-poised-to-herald-a-new-era-in-nepali-cricket)).

## Inaugural NPL title

On December 21, 2024, Janakpur Bolts won the first Nepal Premier League title, defeating Sudurpaschim Royals by five wickets in the final at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur. The Royals batted first and scored 184/9 — Saif Zaib hit 69 off 43 balls and Binod Bhandari 41 off 31 — while Kishore Mahato and Mohammad Mohsin took three wickets each for the Bolts. In reply, Lahiru Milantha's 87 off 49 balls and captain Aasif Sheikh's 33 off 19 led the chase, which the Bolts completed in 19.2 overs with Harsh Thaker unbeaten on 30 ([LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/socially/sports/cricket/janakpur-bolts-win-nepal-premier-league-2024-beat-sudurpaschim-royals-by-five-wickets-to-clinch-inaugural-title-6508302.html)).

## Failed title defense in 2025

For the second NPL season, Janakpur did not continue with Dassanayake — he moved to coach Biratnagar Kings while also coaching the United States national team — and appointed former West Indies captain Shivnarine Chanderpaul as head coach ([Online Khabar](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/meet-the-head-coaches-npl.html)). Chanderpaul, an ICC Hall of Famer who represented the West Indies in 164 Tests and 268 ODIs, landed in Kathmandu on November 10, 2025 to take charge; at his press conference he said he had watched Nepal's series win over the West Indies that September, calling it a sign of "much promise in cricket" for the country ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/cricket/2025/11/11/shivnarine-chanderpaul-arrives-to-take-charge-of-janakpur-bolts)). The defense failed anyway: Janakpur finished bottom of the table in the second season, managing just one win ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/sports/2026/03/05/janakpur-bolts-acquire-franchise-in-belgium-s-eu-t20-league)).

## Expansion to Belgium and the season-3 rebuild

On March 5, 2026, Utsav Sigdel — identified in the report as team owner — announced that Janakpur Bolts had acquired JB Bruges, a franchise in Belgium's upcoming EU T20 League, with an estimated investment of around USD 1 million for the first season. Sigdel said the franchise was secured to prioritize Nepali cricketers and create international exposure for them, and that Chanderpaul would serve as head coach of the Belgian side ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/sports/2026/03/05/janakpur-bolts-acquire-franchise-in-belgium-s-eu-t20-league)). The EU T20 League, organized by Destino Legends Sports with the Belgian Cricket Federation, named England's World Cup-winning captain Eoin Morgan as brand ambassador and required each 16-player squad to include four Belgian players, with at least one in every playing XI ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/sports/2026/03/05/janakpur-bolts-acquire-franchise-in-belgium-s-eu-t20-league)).

Back home, the Bolts began rebuilding for the NPL's third season. The franchise released marquee player Aasif Sheikh — the national-team opener was placed in Category A for the auction — and entered the auction with the largest purse of the eight teams at Rs 6.62 million; CAN set aside an October–November 2026 window for the third season in its domestic calendar ([HimalPress](https://en.himalpress.com/over-150-players-up-for-grabs-in-npl-auction/)).
