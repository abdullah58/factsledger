## Overview

Left Lane Capital is a global venture capital firm that backs high-growth internet and consumer technology companies, headquartered in Brooklyn, [New York City](/places/new-york-city/), with an office in [London](/places/london/) ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/)). It was founded in 2019 by Harley Miller, who spent nine years investing in internet companies at Insight Partners before striking out with three former colleagues ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)). Its first fund raised $630 million; its second, closed in April 2022, raised $1.4 billion ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/)).

The firm's funds so far:

| Fund | Size | Closed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fund I | $630 million | 2020 (less than two years before Fund II) |
| Fund II (Left Lane Capital Partners II LP) | $1.4 billion | 26 April 2022 |

Fund II exceeded its $1 billion target, and the two funds together took total committed capital above $2 billion within two years of the firm's founding ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)). Left Lane invests at key growth inflection points, typically leading deals at the Series A to C stage, with check sizes generally from $5 million to $75 million ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/)). By April 2022 it had made 36 investments spanning a dozen countries, across fintech, edtech, small-business technology, software, food tech, e-commerce, health and wellness, gaming and entertainment ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/)). Its best-known bets include [Bilt Rewards](/organizations/bilt/), the lottery app Jackpocket and the online tutoring marketplace GoStudent ([Bilt Rewards](https://www.vcaonline.com/news/2024012406/bilt-rewards-announces-200-million-raise-valuing-company-at-3-1-billion/); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)).

## Four Insight Partners investors strike out on their own

Miller joined Insight Partners in 2010 straight after a bachelor's degree at the University of Pennsylvania's Wharton School, and spent his career there investing in European consumer-facing companies including Delivery Hero, HelloFresh, Trivago and N26 ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)). The founders of those companies became Left Lane's limited partners and advisers, and Insight Partners itself became a Left Lane LP — Miller said Insight formally blessed the group's departure, with track-record attribution and economic support, something he believed had never happened before for a departing Insight team ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)). Raising the first fund was still a grind: Miller said the team held about 2,000 pitch meetings over the course of a year to get Fund I to its hard cap ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)). The firm is led by four managing partners — Miller as chief executive, alongside Dan Ahrens, Jason Fiedler and Vinny Pujji — all in their early thirties when Fund II closed ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)). "I'm a career VC. It's the only profession I've ever known," Miller told PitchBook ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)).

## The consumer-tech thesis

Left Lane's bet is that internet-enabled consumer businesses with recurring models were left behind as generalist investors chased enterprise software. "Amidst a world of generalists mimicking enterprise software, SaaS investors, we saw that whitespace," Miller told TechCrunch in 2022 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/)). The firm leans on 60 to 70 high-profile chief executives and C-suite operators of internet and consumer technology companies as limited partners and advisers, arguing that this network compensates for a team with little operating experience of its own ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/)). Its style is hands-on: Miller describes the firm as having "a high regard for the human condition — not being a flyover board member", and says it takes board seats and does its own diligence rather than outsourcing it ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/)). Its 25 investment professionals source deals across North America, Europe and Israel, including companies that are not actively fundraising ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)).

## Portfolio: Bilt, Jackpocket and the first exits

Left Lane's signature exit is Jackpocket. The firm led Jackpocket's $120 million Series D in November 2021, a round also joined by Kevin Hart, Manny Machado, Whitney Cummings and Mark Cuban ([DraftKings Network](https://dknetwork.draftkings.com/2021/11/09/announcing-our-120m-series-d/)). In February 2024 DraftKings agreed to acquire Jackpocket, the first licensed third-party lottery app in the [United States](/places/united-states/), for about $750 million in cash and stock ([Sullivan & Cromwell](https://www.sullcrom.com/About/News-and-Events/Highlights/2024/February/S-C-Advises-DraftKings-in-Agreement-to-Acquire-Jackpocket)). In housing, Left Lane already backs Bilt Rewards, founded by [Ankur Jain](/people/ankur-jain/) and named alongside [Camber Creek](/organizations/camber-creek/) and [Prosus](/organizations/prosus/) in Bilt's January 2024 $200 million round led by [General Catalyst](/organizations/general-catalyst/) at $3.1 billion ([Bilt Rewards](https://www.vcaonline.com/news/2024012406/bilt-rewards-announces-200-million-raise-valuing-company-at-3-1-billion/)). It also led iTrustCapital's $125 million Series A in January 2022 at over $1.3 billion, and counts GoStudent and M1 Finance among its fast-growing investments ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)). Left Lane held about 2,000 pitch meetings in a year to close its $630 million first fund; that persistence shows in its portfolio — it led Jackpocket's $120 million Series D in 2021, and DraftKings agreed to buy the company for about $750 million in 2024 ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners); [Sullivan & Cromwell](https://www.sullcrom.com/About/News-and-Events/Highlights/2024/February/S-C-Advises-DraftKings-in-Agreement-to-Acquire-Jackpocket)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who founded Left Lane Capital?** Harley Miller founded Left Lane Capital in 2019 after nine years at Insight Partners, where he invested in Delivery Hero, HelloFresh, Trivago and N26 ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)). He leads the firm as CEO and managing partner alongside Dan Ahrens, Jason Fiedler and Vinny Pujji ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/)).

**What does Left Lane Capital invest in?** High-growth internet and consumer technology companies, typically at the Series A to C stage, with check sizes generally from $5 million to $75 million ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/)). Sectors include fintech, edtech, food tech, e-commerce, health and wellness, gaming, entertainment and software ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/)).

**How much has Left Lane Capital raised?** Its first fund raised $630 million and its second fund, closed in April 2022, raised $1.4 billion, taking total committed capital above $2 billion within two years ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/26/left-lane-second-fund/)). Miller said the team held about 2,000 pitch meetings in a year to close the first fund ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)).

**What are Left Lane Capital's best-known investments?** Bilt Rewards, where it is an existing investor; Jackpocket, whose $120 million Series D it led before DraftKings agreed to buy the company for about $750 million; GoStudent and M1 Finance; and iTrustCapital, whose $125 million Series A it led ([Bilt Rewards](https://www.vcaonline.com/news/2024012406/bilt-rewards-announces-200-million-raise-valuing-company-at-3-1-billion/); [DraftKings Network](https://dknetwork.draftkings.com/2021/11/09/announcing-our-120m-series-d/); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/left-lane-capital-fund-vc-fundraising-insight-partners)).
