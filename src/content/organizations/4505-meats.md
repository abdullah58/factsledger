## Overview

4505 Meats is a San Francisco-based meat snack company best known for its premium chicharrones — fried pork rinds made from humanely raised, antibiotic-free pork. Founded in 2009 by chef and butcher Ryan Farr, it grew from a home-kitchen side project sold at farmers markets into a national brand carried in more than 10,000 stores, raised two rounds of venture funding, and was acquired by salty-snack holding company Benestar Brands in February 2022 ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)).

4505 Meats is not the same business as 4505 Burgers & BBQ, the San Francisco restaurant Farr opened in 2014 — the restaurant is a separate venture under the same founder and brand family, while this profile covers the snack company. The pitch was to rebuild pork rinds as a premium, better-for-you product with short ingredient lists and flavours developed by a chef ([BakeryAndSnacks](https://WWW.BAKERYANDSNACKS.COM/Article/2019/04/30/Premium-chicharrones-brand-secures-Series-B-funding-to-support-innovation-and-current-retailers/)), a positioning that put 4505 at the centre of the late-2010s meat-snack boom as keto and paleo diets turned high-protein, zero-carb snacks into a growth category ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2019/still-appetite-for-meat-snacks-as-4505-raises-8-5m-round/)).

## From a home kitchen to a snack brand

Ryan Farr, a Kansas City native, trained at the Institute of Culinary Education in New York and worked in fine-dining restaurants in Napa Valley and [San Francisco](/places/san-francisco/), [California](/places/california/), before founding 4505 Meats in 2009 as an artisan whole-animal meat company ([Live Naturally Magazine](https://livenaturallymagazine.com/lifestyle/education/the-butcher-life-meet-chef-ryan-farr-of-4505-meats/); [ckbk](https://app.ckbk.com/authors/ryan-farr)). The chicharrones began as an answer to waste: as a chef and butcher, Farr was bothered by how much pork skin he had to throw away, so he developed a method of frying it and started selling the results to neighbours, local bars and markets in the Mission District while working for a non-profit and expecting his first child ([Live Naturally Magazine](https://livenaturallymagazine.com/lifestyle/education/the-butcher-life-meet-chef-ryan-farr-of-4505-meats/)). The snacks were fried in the skin's own rendered lard — skin, Farr argued, is "almost always considered a waste product in the food industry," but rendered down and fried in its own fat it becomes a sustainable product ([Live Naturally Magazine](https://livenaturallymagazine.com/lifestyle/education/the-butcher-life-meet-chef-ryan-farr-of-4505-meats/)).

What started as a side hustle became a business in stages. Farr sold at farmers markets — the company became a staple at the Ferry Plaza Farmer's Market — then rented a commercial kitchen, and in 2012 opened the 4505 Meats Butcher Shop in the Mission neighborhood. ([ckbk](https://app.ckbk.com/authors/ryan-farr); [Live Naturally Magazine](https://livenaturallymagazine.com/lifestyle/education/the-butcher-life-meet-chef-ryan-farr-of-4505-meats/)). In 2014 he opened 4505 Burgers & BBQ, the company's first restaurant, and went on to author two books on whole-animal butchery ([BakeryAndSnacks](https://WWW.BAKERYANDSNACKS.COM/Article/2019/04/30/Premium-chicharrones-brand-secures-Series-B-funding-to-support-innovation-and-current-retailers/)). The butcher shop closed in 2015, and Farr's focus shifted to the restaurant and the chicharrones, which by then had national distribution ([ckbk](https://app.ckbk.com/authors/ryan-farr)).

The snack brand took off in 2017, Farr said, thanks in part to strong support from Whole Foods and to a honed recipe and brand message that set it apart from bodega-style pork rinds ([BakeryAndSnacks](https://WWW.BAKERYANDSNACKS.COM/Article/2019/04/30/Premium-chicharrones-brand-secures-Series-B-funding-to-support-innovation-and-current-retailers/)). "Chicharrones had a bad rap and folks think it's not healthy or it doesn't taste good, but ours actually does," he told BakeryAndSnacks. The timing helped: meat snack sales had risen 7% in 2017 to a category of nearly $3 billion a year, according to Nielsen, and the pork rind category was seeing double-digit year-over-year growth ([BakeryAndSnacks](https://WWW.BAKERYANDSNACKS.COM/Article/2019/04/30/Premium-chicharrones-brand-secures-Series-B-funding-to-support-innovation-and-current-retailers/); [NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2019/still-appetite-for-meat-snacks-as-4505-raises-8-5m-round/)).

## The product line

The flagship product is 4505 Chicharrones, made from cuts from the pig's back and belly and marketed as a no-carb crunchy snack ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2019/still-appetite-for-meat-snacks-as-4505-raises-8-5m-round/)). The original flavour was Classic Chili & Salt, later joined by Jalapeño Cheddar and a Smokehouse BBQ based on the restaurant's rub for smoked ribs, plus En Fuego and Sea Salt ([BakeryAndSnacks](https://WWW.BAKERYANDSNACKS.COM/Article/2019/04/30/Premium-chicharrones-brand-secures-Series-B-funding-to-support-innovation-and-current-retailers/); [The National Provisioner](https://www.provisioneronline.com/articles/112608-4505-meats-and-tajin-release-chile-limon-chicharrones-pork-rinds)). A 2.5 oz bag carries 9 grams of protein, and the chicharrones are keto-friendly, sugar-free and made with humanely sourced pork produced without antibiotics or hormones ([BakeryAndSnacks](https://WWW.BAKERYANDSNACKS.COM/Article/2019/04/30/Premium-chicharrones-brand-secures-Series-B-funding-to-support-innovation-and-current-retailers/)).

The line expanded in step with the company's ambitions. Cracklins, made from pork shoulder skin for a crunchier texture, arrived in 2018, and at Expo West 2019 the brand moved into beef with Scratchins ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2019/still-appetite-for-meat-snacks-as-4505-raises-8-5m-round/)). By 2022 the range also included the Cheese-Charrones snack mix and Pork Crumbles, a bread-crumb alternative ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)). The 2021 launch of Butcher's Snacks was the first step beyond pork rinds proper ([Just Food](https://www.just-food.com/news/benestar-brands-bolsters-pork-rinds-line-up-with-4505-meats-acquisition/)). After the acquisition, the brand's best-known collaboration followed: Chile Limón Chicharrones with Tajín, launched in May 2022 for Cinco de Mayo and sold at select Costco warehouses and on Amazon for $11.99 ([The National Provisioner](https://www.provisioneronline.com/articles/112608-4505-meats-and-tajin-release-chile-limon-chicharrones-pork-rinds)).

## Funding and retail growth

4505's first institutional backer was [CircleUp](/organizations/circleup/), the San Francisco early-stage consumer investment fund. CircleUp invested in 2017 after meeting Farr at Natural Products Expo West, making 4505 one of the first two investments from its new data-driven Helio platform, which analysed billions of data points across more than 1.2 million companies ([Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/circleup-takes-data-driven-approach-to-startup-investment/509886/)). CircleUp said 4505 was one of the top-performing brands in Helio's analysis of the meat snacks category, and managing director Ben Lee said the firm's partners had immediately noticed the "superior nutrition profile" of the chicharrones, with sustainability a major reason for the investment ([Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/circleup-takes-data-driven-approach-to-startup-investment/509886/); [BakeryAndSnacks](https://WWW.BAKERYANDSNACKS.COM/Article/2019/04/30/Premium-chicharrones-brand-secures-Series-B-funding-to-support-innovation-and-current-retailers/)). The investment came from CircleUp's $125 million fund, whose backers included Temasek, the Singapore investment company ([Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/circleup-takes-data-driven-approach-to-startup-investment/509886/)).

A second round followed in April 2019, led by Encore Consumer Capital with CircleUp also taking part. The company declined to disclose the amount, but an SEC filing indicated a roughly $8.5 million round, which included a smaller convertible note from the previous year ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2019/still-appetite-for-meat-snacks-as-4505-raises-8-5m-round/)). The money was for working capital and sales and marketing. President Anish Sheth — who had joined in September 2018 from Encore portfolio brand CeCe's Veggie Co — said Encore's experience with meat brands (Tender Belly, Duke's, Aidell's) would help 4505 scale, particularly in operations and supply chain ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2019/still-appetite-for-meat-snacks-as-4505-raises-8-5m-round/)). Encore managing director Scott Sellers said the firm was attracted by the white space in the salty snack set: pork rinds were a sizeable category that had not traditionally appealed to health-conscious shoppers ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2019/still-appetite-for-meat-snacks-as-4505-raises-8-5m-round/)).

4505 entered Whole Foods Market in 2016 as part of the grocer's first crunchy meat snack set, then launched into all Whole Foods stores in 2018, launched nationally into Safeway/Albertson's, and tested in several Costco regions ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2019/still-appetite-for-meat-snacks-as-4505-raises-8-5m-round/); [NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)). By April 2019 a 2.5 oz bag sold for $3.99 at more than 1,000 US retail locations, including Safeway, Albertson's, Kroger, Sprouts and Costco ([BakeryAndSnacks](https://WWW.BAKERYANDSNACKS.COM/Article/2019/04/30/Premium-chicharrones-brand-secures-Series-B-funding-to-support-innovation-and-current-retailers/)). By February 2022 the products were sold in over 10,000 stores, including Costco, Whole Foods, Sprouts, Safeway-Albertsons, Kroger, Publix and Walmart ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)).

## New leadership and the Benestar acquisition

In 2021 the company changed leadership. President Anish Sheth left, and Farr stepped down as chief executive to become chairman of the board and "Chef in Chief," continuing to develop barbecue-inspired foods while focusing on his restaurants and his third book ([MEAT+POULTRY](https://www.meatpoultry.com/articles/25598-entrepreneur-duo-to-lead-4505-meats)). He was succeeded by Erik Havlick, co-founder of Duke's Freshly Crafted Smoked Meats and BIGS Sunflower Seeds — both acquired by Conagra in 2017; Greg O'Neal, also formerly of Duke's and BIGS, became chief marketing officer ([MEAT+POULTRY](https://www.meatpoultry.com/articles/25598-entrepreneur-duo-to-lead-4505-meats); [NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)). "After twelve years of running a successful snack business, I feel it's time to pass the torch," Farr said at the time ([MEAT+POULTRY](https://www.meatpoultry.com/articles/25598-entrepreneur-duo-to-lead-4505-meats)).

The new team was brought in for channel strategy, brand development and innovation — and within months the company had a buyer. On February 1, 2022, Benestar Brands, a salty-snack holding company created and backed by Dallas private equity firm Highlander Partners, acquired 4505 Meats; terms were not disclosed ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)). Havlick stayed on as chief executive, telling NOSH the company had not been actively seeking a buyer when he joined the previous September, and that the board had brought him and O'Neal in for their expertise — ultimately concluding Benestar would speed up the brand's goals ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)). Benestar had been formed in 2019 when Highlander bought the pork rind business Evans Food Group, which was already 4505's co-packer, with five facilities across the US and Mexico; its other brands included Pretzilla, Mac's, Cazo de Oro, Turkey Creek Snacks, PÖRQ and Chicas Chips ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks); [Just Food](https://www.just-food.com/news/benestar-brands-bolsters-pork-rinds-line-up-with-4505-meats-acquisition/)).

Benestar gained a premium brand and natural-channel expertise; 4505 gained supply-chain efficiencies and shared delivery with Benestar's other snack brands ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)). Despite the pandemic, 4505 had grown 35% in year-over-year dollar sales in 2021, according to O'Neal, and held the fourth-highest dollar sales in the total pork rind category — the number-one premium brand in the category, with higher sales than General Mills' Epic Provisions ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)). Under Benestar, the stated direction was to move beyond pork rinds into a wider salty-snack portfolio, including non-meat innovation ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)). Benestar's chief marketing officer later described 4505 and PÖRQ as the group's premium pork rind brands and said the company focuses on sustaining macro-trends such as low-carb rather than chasing fads ([Baking Business](https://www.bakingbusiness.com/articles/58835-benestar-brands-differentiates-through-quality)).

## FAQ

**Who founded 4505 Meats?** Chef and butcher Ryan Farr founded 4505 Meats in San Francisco in 2009, frying chicharrones in his home kitchen and selling them at farmers markets and local bars before moving to a commercial kitchen ([Live Naturally Magazine](https://livenaturallymagazine.com/lifestyle/education/the-butcher-life-meet-chef-ryan-farr-of-4505-meats/); [NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)).

**What does 4505 Meats sell?** Its flagship is 4505 Chicharrones, fried pork rinds from humanely raised, antibiotic-free pork; the range later added Cracklins, Scratchins beef rinds, Cheese-Charrones, Pork Crumbles, Butcher's Snacks and a Tajín Chile Limón flavour (2022) ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)).

**Who invested in 4505 Meats?** [CircleUp Growth Partners](/organizations/circleup-growth-partners/) invested in 2017, one of the first two investments from its data-driven Helio platform. Encore Consumer Capital led a roughly $8.5 million round in 2019, according to an SEC filing, with CircleUp also participating ([Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/circleup-takes-data-driven-approach-to-startup-investment/509886/); [NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2019/still-appetite-for-meat-snacks-as-4505-raises-8-5m-round/)).

**Who owns 4505 Meats now?** Benestar Brands, a salty-snack holding company backed by Highlander Partners, acquired 4505 Meats on February 1, 2022, for undisclosed terms; Farr stayed on as chairman and Erik Havlick remained chief executive ([NOSH](https://www.nosh.com/news/2022/4505-sells-to-benestar-brands-ceo-says-company-will-move-beyond-meat-snacks)).

**Is 4505 Meats the same as 4505 Burgers & BBQ?** No — 4505 Burgers & BBQ is the San Francisco restaurant Farr opened in 2014. 4505 Meats is the snack company, founded in 2009; the two are related ventures under the same founder, not the same business ([BakeryAndSnacks](https://WWW.BAKERYANDSNACKS.COM/Article/2019/04/30/Premium-chicharrones-brand-secures-Series-B-funding-to-support-innovation-and-current-retailers/)).
