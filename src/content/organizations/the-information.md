## Overview

The Information is a privately owned, digital-first publication covering technology and business. It was founded by journalist Jessica Lessin in late 2013 after she left The Wall Street Journal to build a subscription website for technology-focused executives ([The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/about); [POLITICO](http://www.politico.com/media/story/2016/10/the-morning-media-profile-jessica-lessins-rise-from-reporter-to-entrepreneur-004826)). The publication's official About page identifies Lessin as Founder & CEO and says the newsroom focuses on deeply reported technology stories that readers will not find elsewhere ([The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/about)).

## A subscription publication for technology professionals

The Knight Foundation's interview with Lessin describes The Information as a digital-first technology-industry site whose core readers are business professionals and leaders in technology and finance. Lessin presents subscription as the business model for differentiated reporting and says the publication is designed around an engaged audience rather than the pursuit of the lowest-common-denominator click ([Knight Foundation](https://knightfoundation.org/features/je-append-jessica-lessin-qa/)).

## Launch and historical business record

POLITICO reported that Lessin left The Wall Street Journal in June 2013, that The Information launched three years later in December 2013, and that by October 2016 it had many thousands of subscribers, a $399 annual price point, 19 full-time employees across San Francisco, New York and Hong Kong, and positive cash flow ([POLITICO](http://www.politico.com/media/story/2016/10/the-morning-media-profile-jessica-lessins-rise-from-reporter-to-entrepreneur-004826)). Those numbers are historical and are not treated as current metrics here.
