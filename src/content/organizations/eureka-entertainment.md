## Overview

**Eureka Entertainment** is a British home-video distributor best known for the *Masters of Cinema* series, the curated Blu-ray and DVD line it launched in 2004 to present classic and world cinema in restored editions with commissioned essays and booklets ([BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/SCARLET-STREET-to-Arrive-on-4K-UHDBlu-ray-via-Masters-of-Cinema-20260902)). It became nationally newsworthy in 2011 as the UK distributor of Tom Six's *[The Human Centipede 2 (Full Sequence)](/creative-works/the-human-centipede-2/)*, fighting the BBFC's June refusal of a certificate and securing an 18 certificate that October after 32 cuts totalling 2 minutes 37 seconds ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/film/bbfc-reverse-decision-to-ban-the-human-centipede-i-880747); [Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/distribution/human-centipede-ii-gets-18-certificate-in-uk-after-cuts/5033015.article)). The company describes itself as the UK's leading independent distributor of classic silent and early films ([BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/SCARLET-STREET-to-Arrive-on-4K-UHDBlu-ray-via-Masters-of-Cinema-20260902)).

## The Masters of Cinema label

Eureka's catalogue spans world cinema, action, horror, sci-fi, indie dramas, Hollywood classics, comedy, thriller and television, but its reputation rests on the Masters of Cinema Series — launched in 2004 as a specially curated collection presenting films in their original aspect ratios from recent restorations or the most pristine available elements, supplemented by commentaries, documentaries and booklets of new writing ([BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/SCARLET-STREET-to-Arrive-on-4K-UHDBlu-ray-via-Masters-of-Cinema-20260902)). Titles showcased in the series include *The General* (Keaton), *Metropolis* (Lang), *The Old Dark House* (Whale), *Paths of Glory* (Kubrick), *Touch of Evil* (Welles), *Kes* (Loach), *A Touch of Zen* (Hu) and *Shoah* (Lanzmann) ([BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/SCARLET-STREET-to-Arrive-on-4K-UHDBlu-ray-via-Masters-of-Cinema-20260902)). In 2011 Eureka struck a partnership with Universal Pictures to release select catalogue titles through the series for Universal's 2012 centenary — beginning with *Island of Lost Souls*, *Touch of Evil* and *Silent Running* ([Screen Anarchy](https://screenanarchy.com/2011/07/eureka-entertainment-announces-blu-raydvd-slate-for-fall-2011.html)). A second label, Eureka! Classics, followed in 2014 for cult titles such as *Blacula*, *Cujo*, *Police Story* and *Fright Night* ([BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/SCARLET-STREET-to-Arrive-on-4K-UHDBlu-ray-via-Masters-of-Cinema-20260902)).

## The Human Centipede 2 campaign

In June 2011 the BBFC refused a certificate for *The Human Centipede 2 (Full Sequence)* for DVD release, effectively banning it from UK distribution. Bounty Films and its UK distribution partner Eureka responded with a joint statement: disappointed but respectful of the board's authority, they strongly disagreed — and took the unusual step of publicly criticising the BBFC's press release for an "unprecedented level of detail" about the film's scenes, arguing it was inconsistent with previous decisions and could prejudice their forthcoming appeal to the Video Appeals Committee ([Eat My Brains](https://eatmybrains.com/shownews.php?id=1168)). Bounty's Tony Romeo noted the film had passed uncut in Australia, making the BBFC's decision surprising to the company ([The Raygun](http://ymlp.com/zk1ftm)). After what sales director Ian Sadler called "nearly four months of detailed discussion and debate", Eureka proposed cuts, the BBFC demanded more extensive ones, and the film passed with an 18 certificate after 32 cuts totalling 2 minutes 37 seconds ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/film/bbfc-reverse-decision-to-ban-the-human-centipede-i-880747); [Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/distribution/human-centipede-ii-gets-18-certificate-in-uk-after-cuts/5033015.article)). Unlike [The Bunny Game](/creative-works/the-bunny-game/) — which the BBFC refused outright in October 2011 — the Centipede sequel became a negotiated compromise: Sadler said the storyline was uncompromised and the horror sustained ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/film/bbfc-reverse-decision-to-ban-the-human-centipede-i-880747)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Eureka Entertainment?**
A British home-video distributor and label, best known for the Masters of Cinema Series of curated Blu-ray/DVD editions of classic and world cinema, launched in 2004. The company describes itself as the UK's leading independent distributor of classic silent and early films, with a catalogue spanning world cinema, horror, sci-fi, comedy, thrillers and television titles.

**What is the Masters of Cinema series?**
Eureka's flagship curated line, established in 2004, presenting films in their original aspect ratios from recent restorations or the finest available elements, with extras and booklets of commissioned essays. Titles include *Metropolis*, *Touch of Evil*, *Kes* and *Shoah*; a sister label, Eureka! Classics, covers cult titles such as *Cujo* and *Fright Night*.

**What happened between Eureka and the BBFC over The Human Centipede 2?**
The BBFC refused the film a certificate in June 2011. Eureka and Bounty Films announced an appeal, publicly criticising the BBFC's unusually detailed press release. After nearly four months of negotiation, the film was passed with an 18 certificate in October 2011 following 32 cuts totalling 2 minutes 37 seconds.

**Who is Ian Sadler?**
Ian Sadler was Eureka Entertainment's sales director during the 2011 campaign and the company's public voice in the BBFC negotiations. His statement — pleased at the agreement after nearly four months of debate, disappointed at the cuts but confident the storyline and horror survived — was carried by NME, Screen Daily and other outlets.

**Is Eureka Entertainment still releasing films?**
Yes. As of September 2026, the Masters of Cinema series remains active: BroadwayWorld reported a limited-edition 4K UHD/Blu-ray release of Fritz Lang's *Scarlet Street* scheduled for 23 November 2026.
