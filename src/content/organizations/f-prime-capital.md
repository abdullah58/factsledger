## Overview

F-Prime Capital is a global venture capital firm investing in healthcare and technology, led by President and Managing Partner Stephen Knight, M.D. ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)). It grew out of Fidelity Investments — its technology arm was formerly known as Devonshire Investors and its healthcare arm as Fidelity Biosciences — and it invests alongside a sister fund, Eight Roads ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2018/03/07/sp-calculates-550m-kensho-acquisition/); [NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)).

With more than $4.5 billion under management and a portfolio of over 300 companies, it is one of the larger venture firms built from a financial-services parent ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)).

The firm's distinction is that it does not only back companies — it starts them. It has created or co-created more than 30 companies, including Beam Therapeutics, Denali Therapeutics and Innovent, and helped build others including Benchling, Blueprint Medicines, Devoted Health, Flywire, Iora Health and Toast ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)). Its team mixes investors with engineers, doctors and scientists, reflecting the two halves of the portfolio ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)).

## From Fidelity's venture arm to an independent firm

F-Prime's roots are in one of America's largest asset managers: Fidelity Investments built a venture group that backed entrepreneurs in technology and life sciences for decades before the F-Prime name was adopted ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2018/03/07/sp-calculates-550m-kensho-acquisition/)). The technology side invested as Devonshire Investors — the name under which it joined Kensho's seed round in 2014 — while the healthcare side invested as Fidelity Biosciences ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2018/03/07/sp-calculates-550m-kensho-acquisition/)). The F-Prime name eventually unified the two arms, though the sources read here do not date the change. Today the firm describes itself as an independent venture capital group, and its sister fund Eight Roads carries the same model internationally, with teams in London, Shanghai, Beijing, Hong Kong, Tokyo and Mumbai ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)). F-Prime itself has offices in Cambridge, Massachusetts, San Francisco and London — three of the world's leading healthcare and technology hubs ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)).

## Healthcare and technology, in two halves

The two halves of the portfolio have different logics. In healthcare, F-Prime focuses on therapeutics, medtech and health IT and services, investing in a stage-agnostic fashion; its company-creation record sits mostly here, with Beam Therapeutics, Denali Therapeutics and Innovent among the companies it created or co-created ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)). In technology it focuses on enterprise software, fintech and frontier tech, describing itself as an early-stage investor by choice but "innovation agnostic", backing exceptional teams at any stage ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)). The firm says its funds have grown larger and more international over its history, while its small local teams are meant to preserve its entrepreneurial roots ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)). With more than $4.5 billion under management and over 300 companies, the scale implies a portfolio built as much by company formation as by picking winners — over 30 companies created in-house is an unusually high share for a venture firm ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)).

## Fintech bets: Axoni, Kensho and Quovo

F-Prime's fintech footprint runs through three deals that show its range. In December 2016 it joined [Axoni](/organizations/axoni/)'s $18 million Series A, led by [Wells Fargo](/organizations/wells-fargo/) and ICAP's [Euclid Opportunities](/organizations/euclid-opportunities/), alongside [Goldman Sachs](/organizations/goldman-sachs/), J.P. Morgan, Thomson Reuters, [Andreessen Horowitz](/organizations/andreessen-horowitz/), [FinTech Collective](/organizations/fintech-collective/) and [Digital Currency Group](/organizations/digital-currency-group/) ([Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/vc-deal-of-the-day-axioni-raises-18m-series-a-46944.html?print)). Two years earlier it had backed Kensho Technologies, the Harvard and MIT spinout building AI for finance, in its $10 million seed round alongside GV, New Enterprise Associates, Accel and General Catalyst Partners; S&P Global agreed to acquire Kensho for approximately $550 million in March 2018, then the largest acquisition of an AI company to date ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2018/03/07/sp-calculates-550m-kensho-acquisition/)). And in account aggregation it backed Quovo, which raised $10 million from F-Prime and Napier Park Global Capital; Quovo was later acquired by Plaid ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.io/articles/quovo-raises-10m-from-f-prime-napier-park); [Business Wire](https://itbusinessnet.com/2021/08/fintech-collective-raises-250-million-launches-dedicated-digital-assets-strategy/?noamp=available)). The pattern is consistent: early cheques into infrastructure that larger financial institutions eventually buy.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is F-Prime Capital?**
F-Prime Capital is a global venture capital firm investing in healthcare and technology, led by President and Managing Partner Stephen Knight, M.D. ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)). It grew from Fidelity Investments and invests alongside its sister fund Eight Roads, with offices in Cambridge, San Francisco and London ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)).

**How is F-Prime connected to Fidelity?**
F-Prime grew from Fidelity Investments: its technology arm was formerly Devonshire Investors, a Fidelity subsidiary, and its healthcare arm was formerly Fidelity Biosciences ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/blog/2018/03/07/sp-calculates-550m-kensho-acquisition/)). FinTech Collective's site likewise describes it as the venture capital firm owned by FMR, the parent of Fidelity Investments ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.io/articles/quovo-raises-10m-from-f-prime-napier-park)).

**What does F-Prime invest in?**
In healthcare it focuses on therapeutics, medtech and health IT and services; in technology it focuses on enterprise software, fintech and frontier tech ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)). It has created or co-created over 30 companies, including Beam Therapeutics, Denali Therapeutics and Innovent, and helped build Toast, Flywire, Benchling and Devoted Health ([NVCA](https://nvca.org/member-spotlight-f-prime-capital/)).

**What was F-Prime's role in Axoni's Series A?**
F-Prime Capital Partners participated in Axoni's $18 million Series A in December 2016, led by Wells Fargo and ICAP's Euclid Opportunities, alongside Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, Thomson Reuters, Andreessen Horowitz, FinTech Collective and Digital Currency Group ([Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/vc-deal-of-the-day-axioni-raises-18m-series-a-46944.html?print)). The round took Axoni's total funding above $20 million ([Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/vc-deal-of-the-day-axioni-raises-18m-series-a-46944.html?print)).
