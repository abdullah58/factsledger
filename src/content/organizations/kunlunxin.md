## Overview

Kunlunxin (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd. is the AI-chip subsidiary of [Baidu](/organizations/baidu/), spun off as an independent company in 2021 after a decade as an internal Baidu project ([The Bamboo Works](https://thebambooworks.com/a-decade-in-the-making-kunlunxin-chips-could-bring-excitement-back-to-baidu/)). It designs the Kunlun series of AI accelerators for data-centre servers, and by 2024 it ranked second in [China](/places/china/) in data-centre AI accelerator card sales, with nearly 70,000 units shipped — a distant second to [Nvidia](/organizations/nvidia/), which shipped over 1.9 million ([The Bamboo Works](https://thebambooworks.com/a-decade-in-the-making-kunlunxin-chips-could-bring-excitement-back-to-baidu/)). In January 2026 Baidu announced that Kunlunxin had confidentially filed for a main-board listing on the [Hong Kong Stock Exchange](/organizations/hong-kong-stock-exchange/) as part of a proposed spin-off, and in May 2026 the company began the listing-guidance process for Shanghai's STAR Market, advancing a dual listing ([Verdict](https://www.verdict.co.uk/newsletters/baidu-kunlunxin-files-hongkong-ipo/); [TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/05/08/news-baidu-chip-unit-kunlunxin-reportedly-launches-star-market-ipo-process-hk-listing-valuation-seen-near-hk100b/)). Whether that listing happens, and when, is unresolved: in November 2026 Bloomberg reported it may slip to 2027, a report Baidu denied ([FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/baidu-kunlunxin-ipo-investors-customers-chips/)).

## From Baidu lab to independent chipmaker

Kunlunxin began as Baidu's in-house intelligent chip and architecture department, developing its own AI chips as early as 2011 to serve Baidu's demand for computing capacity across its online services ([The Bamboo Works](https://thebambooworks.com/a-decade-in-the-making-kunlunxin-chips-could-bring-excitement-back-to-baidu/)). Baidu first unveiled the unit publicly in 2018 with the Kunlun 1 cloud AI chip, and its first chips were mass-produced in early 2020 ([KrASIA](https://amp.kr-asia.com/baidus-ai-chip-unit-kunlun-becomes-independent-company)). In 2021 Baidu spun the business off as a standalone company led by its chief chip architect, Ouyang Jian, as CEO; a March 2021 financing round led by CITIC Private Equity Funds Management, IDG Capital and Legend Capital valued the business at RMB 13 billion (about $2 billion) ([KrASIA](https://amp.kr-asia.com/baidus-ai-chip-unit-kunlun-becomes-independent-company)).

The product line advanced quickly after the spin-off. A second-generation chip rolled out in the second half of 2021, and the company later released the K100 accelerator card and the RH800 server, before debuting a third-generation P800 chip in 2025 ([KrASIA](https://amp.kr-asia.com/baidus-ai-chip-unit-kunlun-becomes-independent-company); [The Bamboo Works](https://thebambooworks.com/a-decade-in-the-making-kunlunxin-chips-could-bring-excitement-back-to-baidu/)). Its published roadmap includes the M100 chip, optimised for large-scale inference and slated for launch in 2026, and the M300, targeting ultra-large multimodal model training and inference, expected in 2027, alongside Tianchi 256 and Tianchi 512 supernodes planned for 2026 ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/05/08/news-baidu-chip-unit-kunlunxin-reportedly-launches-star-market-ipo-process-hk-listing-valuation-seen-near-hk100b/)).

Commercially, Kunlunxin has moved from captive supplier towards merchant chipmaker. Its revenue was about RMB 2 billion in 2024 with a net loss of about RMB 200 million, and it was expected to rise above RMB 3.5 billion in 2025 and break even, with external customers accounting for over half of 2025 revenue ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/05/08/news-baidu-chip-unit-kunlunxin-reportedly-launches-star-market-ipo-process-hk-listing-valuation-seen-near-hk100b/)). A RMB 1 billion order from China Mobile in August 2025 was an early sign of that outside demand ([The Bamboo Works](https://thebambooworks.com/a-decade-in-the-making-kunlunxin-chips-could-bring-excitement-back-to-baidu/)). A July 2025 fundraising round valued the company at about RMB 21 billion, roughly $3 billion ([Verdict](https://www.verdict.co.uk/newsletters/baidu-kunlunxin-files-hongkong-ipo/)). Baidu remains the controlling shareholder, holding a 57.67% stake as reported in May 2026 ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/05/08/news-baidu-chip-unit-kunlunxin-reportedly-launches-star-market-ipo-process-hk-listing-valuation-seen-near-hk100b/)).

## The dual-listing push — and its uncertain timing

Baidu's January 2026 announcement framed the [Hong Kong](/places/hong-kong/) listing as a way for Kunlunxin to "independently showcase" its business and valuation, attract investors specialising in AI semiconductors, and "enhance its market profile, broaden financing channels, and better align management accountability with performance" — part of a broader effort "to unlock the value of Baidu's AI-powered businesses" ([Verdict](https://www.verdict.co.uk/newsletters/baidu-kunlunxin-files-hongkong-ipo/)). The filing was confidential, so no prospectus was published, and Baidu stressed the plan was subject to Hong Kong Stock Exchange approval, a filing with the China Securities Regulatory Commission, and final decisions by both companies — cautioning that "there is no assurance that the Proposed Spin-off will take place or when it may take place" ([Verdict](https://www.verdict.co.uk/newsletters/baidu-kunlunxin-files-hongkong-ipo/)).

The process advanced in spring 2026: Kunlunxin launched its STAR Market listing guidance process on 7 May 2026, marking the rollout of a dual listing in China's STAR Market and Hong Kong ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/05/08/news-baidu-chip-unit-kunlunxin-reportedly-launches-star-market-ipo-process-hk-listing-valuation-seen-near-hk100b/)). Then, on 3 November 2026, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter, that the Hong Kong IPO may be deferred to 2027 because Kunlunxin was still waiting for approval from Chinese securities regulators, having originally been expected to get clearance in summer 2026 ([FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/baidu-kunlunxin-ipo-investors-customers-chips/)). A Baidu spokesman told Bloomberg the company denied the market rumours, said it cannot disclose material non-public information while Kunlunxin is in a confidential filing process, and said Baidu will make an announcement in due course ([FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/baidu-kunlunxin-ipo-investors-customers-chips/)). As of this profile's publication, no HKEX filing or Baidu announcement has settled the timing — the listing is pending, not completed.
