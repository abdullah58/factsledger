## Overview

OnDeck Capital was a New York-based online lender founded in 2006 to serve small businesses typically underserved by traditional banks, using proprietary methods it said identified creditworthiness better and faster than banks' historical approaches ([Finovate](https://finovate.com/ondeck-goes-public-raises-200-million-in-initial-public-offering/)). In December 2014 it went public on the NYSE under the symbol ONDK, raising $200 million at a $1.3 billion valuation after originating more than $1.7 billion in loans since its 2007 start ([Finovate](https://finovate.com/ondeck-goes-public-raises-200-million-in-initial-public-offering/)).

In October 2020, Enova International completed its acquisition of OnDeck for $1.89 per share — about $122 million — and ONDK ceased trading on the NYSE ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2020/10/167907-enova-announces-completion-of-ondeck-acquisition/)).

## A $1.3 billion IPO, a $122 million exit

The numbers tell the story in two snapshots six years apart. At the December 2014 IPO, priced at $20 and opening at $26.50, OnDeck was worth $1.3 billion — the second online lender to go public in a week after LendingClub, with CEO Noah Breslow calling it "a signature moment for financial technology" on CNBC ([Finovate](https://finovate.com/ondeck-goes-public-raises-200-million-in-initial-public-offering/)). By July 2020, with the pandemic crushing small-business borrowers, Enova agreed to buy the company for $1.38 per share including $0.12 in cash — roughly $90 million, a decline of more than 90% from the IPO valuation ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2020/10/167907-enova-announces-completion-of-ondeck-acquisition/)).

The consideration was mostly paper: OnDeck shareholders received 0.092 of an Enova share plus $0.12 in cash for each OnDeck share held, which is why the headline price rose to $1.89 per share (~$122 million) by the October 13 close as Enova's stock moved ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2020/10/167907-enova-announces-completion-of-ondeck-acquisition/)). Enova — a lender to non-prime consumers and small businesses with $20 billion-plus in historical originations — said the deal would take small-business lending from 15% to about 60% of its lending operations, with $50 million in annual cost synergies and $15 million in revenue synergies phased in by end of 2022 ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2020/10/167907-enova-announces-completion-of-ondeck-acquisition/)). Breslow stayed on as an Enova employee with a $1.16 million restricted-stock award vesting over twelve months ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2020/10/167907-enova-announces-completion-of-ondeck-acquisition/)).
