## Overview

Revolution LLC is a Washington, D.C.-based investment firm founded in 2005 by AOL co-founder Steve Case as a holding company to invest in companies and entrepreneurs "attacking" traditional industries, with an approach of putting larger amounts of money into fewer companies and helping them grow ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/revolution-ventures-closes-200m-fund-to-support-the-rise-of-the-rest)). The firm describes itself as "a D.C.-based firm investing in entrepreneurs building transformative companies and destinations beyond Silicon Valley," organized as a family of funds ([Revolution](https://www.revolution.com/press-release/revolution-heads-to-florida-and-puerto-rico-for-8th-rise-of-the-rest-bus-tour)).

The firm's philosophy is built around Case's idea of "the rise of the rest" — finding entrepreneurs in "off-the-beaten path places," on the premise that it is easier to start a company with fewer people and less capital than before and that founders can choose where they want to live and work ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/revolution-ventures-closes-200m-fund-to-support-the-rise-of-the-rest)).

## The Vance years

In 2017, [J.D. Vance](/people/jd-vance/) joined Revolution LLC in Washington as a partner focusing on startups, after serving as a principal at the Thiel-backed Mithril Capital Management ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/why-jd-vance-as-donald-trump-s-vp-pick-has-silicon-valley-celebrating-124071600077_1.html)). His tenure became central to one of the firm's signature initiatives: in December 2018, Case and Vance launched the Rise of the Rest Seed Fund, a $150 million fund backed by Ray Dalio, Meg Whitman, Jeff Bezos, Eric Schmidt, Sarah Blakely and other entrepreneurs, executives and investors ([Revolution](https://www.revolution.com/press-release/revolution-heads-to-florida-and-puerto-rico-for-8th-rise-of-the-rest-bus-tour)).

Vance served as Managing Partner of the Seed Fund, which invests catalytic capital in early-stage, high-growth companies across the country alongside regional investors and co-investors ([Revolution](https://www.revolution.com/press-release/revolution-heads-to-florida-and-puerto-rico-for-8th-rise-of-the-rest-bus-tour)). In early 2019 he was the firm's public face on the 8th Rise of the Rest bus tour — a five-day trip across Orlando, Tampa Bay, Miami, the Space Coast and Puerto Rico, the first Rise of the Rest trip to spend most of its time in a single state and the first to visit a U.S. territory ([Revolution](https://www.revolution.com/press-release/revolution-heads-to-florida-and-puerto-rico-for-8th-rise-of-the-rest-bus-tour)).

That tour combined ecosystem visits, fireside discussions and daily startup pitch competitions, with $100,000 to be invested from the Seed Fund at each stop ([Revolution](https://www.revolution.com/press-release/revolution-heads-to-florida-and-puerto-rico-for-8th-rise-of-the-rest-bus-tour)). Later in 2019, Vance left Revolution and returned to Ohio to form Narya Capital ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/why-jd-vance-as-donald-trump-s-vp-pick-has-silicon-valley-celebrating-124071600077_1.html)).

## The fund family

Revolution operates through a family of funds, partnering with founders "at every stage of the startup lifecycle" ([Revolution](https://www.revolution.com/press-release/revolution-heads-to-florida-and-puerto-rico-for-8th-rise-of-the-rest-bus-tour)). Revolution Ventures raised a $200 million fund — described at the time as the largest first-time venture fund outside of Silicon Valley since the 2008 financial crisis — formed by Case with Tige Savage and David Golden, and expected to make around 20 investments over three to four years ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/revolution-ventures-closes-200m-fund-to-support-the-rise-of-the-rest)). Four investments Revolution LLC had already made were moved into the Ventures portfolio: BenchPrep, Booker Software, Homesnap and RunKeeper ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/revolution-ventures-closes-200m-fund-to-support-the-rise-of-the-rest)).

Revolution Growth, founded by Case in 2011 with former AOL executives Ted Leonsis and Donn Davis, is a $450 million fund whose strategy is to concentrate $25 million to $50 million in 10 to 12 companies to help them achieve mainstream adoption or leadership in their market ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/revolution-ventures-closes-200m-fund-to-support-the-rise-of-the-rest)). Its portfolio has included FedBid, Living Social, Zipcar, Optoro and HelloWallet ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/revolution-ventures-closes-200m-fund-to-support-the-rise-of-the-rest)). The firm is based in Washington, D.C., with an office in San Francisco ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/revolution-ventures-closes-200m-fund-to-support-the-rise-of-the-rest)).
