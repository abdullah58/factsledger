The least-told part of Turning Point USA's origin story is the man who is not the famous one. When Charlie Kirk was 18 and captivating a youth audience at a Catholic college in Illinois, businessman William Montgomery told the teenager to hold off on college and spend his time pulling young people into the conservative movement instead ([Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/charlie-kirks-turning-point-changed-conservative-poltics/86084049007/)). Montgomery handled the paperwork while Kirk became the face, and the two started Turning Point USA in 2012 to build a counterweight to liberal campus groups — with the cheeky original slogan "Big Government Sucks" ([Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/charlie-kirks-turning-point-changed-conservative-poltics/86084049007/)).

## From a slogan to a network

The Arizona Republic describes the founding idea as a nonprofit aimed at introducing college students to conservative ideas ([Arizona Republic](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2026/09/09/turning-point-still-growing-post-charlie-kirk/91615587007/)). Over 14 years it grew into a network of conservative groups and political action committees with footholds in churches, high schools, and colleges across the country ([Arizona Republic](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2026/09/09/turning-point-still-growing-post-charlie-kirk/91615587007/)).

The money followed the growth. Tax records show the organization's coffers rising from $4.3 million in fiscal 2017 to $55.2 million in fiscal 2021 ([Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/charlie-kirks-turning-point-changed-conservative-poltics/86084049007/)). In 2018, former Maricopa County GOP head Tyler Bowyer joined as an executive, and by 2019 he had convinced Kirk to move headquarters from the Chicago area to Phoenix — a better political fit, in his view — taking space in a business park in southeast Phoenix ([Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/charlie-kirks-turning-point-changed-conservative-poltics/86084049007/)).

The organization built parallel arms as it matured. Turning Point Action, its political wing, made its first campaign contributions in 2020 — $1 million, all of it advocating for Donald Trump over Joe Biden ([Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/charlie-kirks-turning-point-changed-conservative-poltics/86084049007/)). In 2022, Kirk started Turning Point Faith, holding monthly meetings at the Dream City Church complex in north Phoenix — a reversal for a man who had said as recently as 2018 that politics and religion should not mix ([Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/charlie-kirks-turning-point-changed-conservative-poltics/86084049007/)). The Faith program spent $10.6 million in fiscal 2024 training nearly 10,000 pastors on "eradicating wokeism from the church," and the network now counts more than 12,000 churches and pastors ([Arizona Republic](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2026/09/09/turning-point-still-growing-post-charlie-kirk/91615587007/)).

By 2024, Turning Point had prime placement at the Republican National Convention in Milwaukee, with Kirk taking a five-minute speaking slot on the economy and telling the crowd that "the American dream has become a luxury item for the wealthy" ([Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/charlie-kirks-turning-point-changed-conservative-poltics/86084049007/)).

## The killing and the succession

On September 10, 2025, Kirk was shot and killed at a Turning Point USA event at Utah Valley University ([Arizona Republic](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2026/09/09/turning-point-still-growing-post-charlie-kirk/91615587007/)). His widow, [Erika Kirk](/people/erika-kirk/), took over the organization as CEO ([People](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229)).

The succession has not been quiet. In April, the president of the University of Georgia chapter resigned, claiming the organization had strayed from Charlie's vision under Erika's leadership ([People](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229)). Kirk's mother, Kathryn, defended her daughter-in-law in a September 2026 Vanity Fair interview, saying Charlie would have wanted Erika in the role and that the family was "100 percent behind her" ([People](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229)). A month after the killing, President Trump honored Kirk with a posthumous Presidential Medal of Freedom at the White House on October 14 ([People](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229)).

## The post-Kirk surge — and the questions

By the organization's own accounting, a year after Kirk's death it had more than a million students involved in its programs ([Arizona Republic](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2026/09/09/turning-point-still-growing-post-charlie-kirk/91615587007/)). The numbers the group cites are striking: more than 1,200 employees across its organizations; thousands of new chapters added in the 2025–26 school year; 1,500 college chapters (a 64 percent increase); 3,500 Club America high school chapters — nearly triple the pre-death count; and seven states (Texas, Oklahoma, Tennessee, Florida, Arkansas, Indiana, and Montana) officially partnering to establish chapters in schools ([Arizona Republic](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2026/09/09/turning-point-still-growing-post-charlie-kirk/91615587007/)). More than a million donors gave in the past year, over 95 percent of them under $1,000 ([Arizona Republic](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2026/09/09/turning-point-still-growing-post-charlie-kirk/91615587007/)).

The same reporting documents the turbulence: a staff purge at the start of the year, and a Republican Party fractured without Kirk as its MAGA standard-bearer ([Arizona Republic](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2026/09/09/turning-point-still-growing-post-charlie-kirk/91615587007/)). "Turning Point is, in the fall of 2026, not the same organization it was in August 2025," one Arizona political consultant told the paper, pointing to infighting with other Arizona Republicans ahead of the July 21 primary ([Arizona Republic](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2026/09/09/turning-point-still-growing-post-charlie-kirk/91615587007/)). Attendance at the Chapter Leadership Summit doubled compared with 2024, and two-thirds of the nearly 8,000 summit attendees joined their chapters only after the assassination ([Arizona Republic](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2026/09/09/turning-point-still-growing-post-charlie-kirk/91615587007/)). The Faith wing also announced in May that it would partner with the Arizona Department of Education's School Chaplains Program ([Arizona Republic](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2026/09/09/turning-point-still-growing-post-charlie-kirk/91615587007/)).

## AmericaFest 2025

The first AmericaFest after Kirk's death took place December 18–21, 2025, at the Phoenix Convention Center — a four-day gathering billed around faith, freedom, and American values, with leadership development sessions, activism training, and more than 100 partner organizations ([Turning Point USA](https://tpusa.com/live/turning-point-usas-2025-americafest-kicks-off-next-month-in-phoenix/)). The announced speakers included Erika Kirk, Tucker Carlson, Steve Bannon, [Donald Trump Jr.](/people/donald-trump-jr/), Jesse Watters, Greg Gutfeld, Megyn Kelly, Ben Shapiro, Glenn Beck, Matt Walsh, Russell Brand, Dr. Ben Carson, and Allie Beth Stuckey ([Turning Point USA](https://tpusa.com/live/turning-point-usas-2025-americafest-kicks-off-next-month-in-phoenix/)).

An eyewitness account of the convention put attendance at 31,000, with [JD Vance](/people/jd-vance/) closing the event, a call from President Trump, and an estimated 30 percent of attendees being students or young Americans ([The Post & Email](https://www.thepostemail.com/2025/12/23/it-is-time-to-rebuild-an-american-america/)). The same account reports a surprise guest: musician Nicki Minaj, appearing "going full MAGA" in a conversation with Erika Kirk — a detail that has been reported by attendee accounts rather than mainstream coverage ([The Post & Email](https://www.thepostemail.com/2025/12/23/it-is-time-to-rebuild-an-american-america/)).
