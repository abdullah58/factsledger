## Overview

8VC is an American venture capital firm based in Austin, Texas, founded in 2015 by [Palantir](/organizations/palantir/) co-founder Joe Lonsdale. It invests in defense, life sciences, healthcare, logistics and enterprise software, and differs from most venture firms by co-founding companies itself through its Build program, including Saronic and Epirus ([8VC](http://www.8vc.com/build); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/joe-lonsdales-8vc-seeking-1b-for-new-fund/)).

Since its founding it has invested in more than 600 companies, among them Asana, Flexport, Hims & Hers and [Elon Musk](/people/elon-musk/)'s The Boring Company, and it was an early backer of Anduril and of [Blend](/organizations/blend/), the mortgage-software company that went public in 2021 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/joe-lonsdales-8vc-seeking-1b-for-new-fund/); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/trump-ally-joe-lonsdale-targets-1b-for-his-new-8vc-fund)). The firm is led by Lonsdale as managing partner, employs more than 70 people including 10 investment partners, and raised $998 million for its sixth fund in 2025 before raising $1.5 billion for a seventh in July 2026 ([Forbes](https://alphaleaders.co.uk/u-s-defense-tech-investor-8vc-has-links-to-sanctioned-russian-oligarchs/amp/); [8VC](https://8vc.com/resources/announcing-8vc-fund-vi); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/from-8vc-to-benchmark-heres-whats-hiding-in-october-sec-filings/)).

## From [Formation 8](/organizations/formation-8/) to 8 Partners

8VC was born from a split. Lonsdale had co-founded Formation 8 in 2011 with Brian Koo and Jim Kim, a firm whose first fund totalled $448 million and whose second closed at $500 million, and which backed Oculus before [Facebook](/organizations/facebook/) bought it for $2 billion and RelateIQ before [Salesforce](/organizations/salesforce/) bought it for $390 million ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2015/11/04/de-formation-8/)). In November 2015 the partners wound Formation 8 down, announcing it would not raise a third fund because they had different investment styles, and Lonsdale began raising $400 million for a new firm initially called 8 Partners ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2015/11/04/de-formation-8/)). The new firm kept the investing team and the early-stage focus while dropping the Asia-bridging strategy that had divided Formation 8's founders ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2015/11/04/de-formation-8/)). Lonsdale brought an operator's background: he had co-founded Palantir, worked as an executive at [Peter Thiel](/people/peter-thiel/)'s hedge fund Clarium Capital after interning at PayPal for two summers, and co-founded the wealth-management software company Addepar ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/joe-lonsdales-8vc-seeking-1b-for-new-fund/)).

## The Build program: a venture firm that co-founds companies

What sets 8VC apart is Build, its program for founding companies rather than only funding them. Through investing, the firm spots gaps it calls whitespaces, then acts on them from internal theses or through entrepreneurs-in-residence; Lonsdale is personally involved with each Build company ([8VC](http://www.8vc.com/build)). The model targets messy, legacy technical systems in industries paralysed by their own scale — government, healthcare and logistics ([8VC](http://www.8vc.com/build)). The pattern shows in three companies:

| Build company | What it does | Verified result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saronic | Autonomous surface vessels for the US Navy | First product in under 12 months; Navy exercise Integrated Battle Problem 24.1 |
| Epirus | High-power microwave counter-drone systems | $66 million US Army contract in early 2023; $200 million Series C at a $1.35 billion valuation |
| Resilience | Domestic biomanufacturing capacity | Raised $800 million; seven GMP facilities; $410 million DoD financing |

Saronic was built with Dino Mavrookas, a former Navy SEAL with eight combat tours, as a defense entrepreneur-in-residence ([8VC](http://www.8vc.com/build)). Epirus is led by Andy Lowery, a former chief engineer for electronic warfare systems at Raytheon, and its co-founder Grant Verstandig had sold Rally Health for $7 billion to [UnitedHealth Group](/organizations/unitedhealth-group/) ([8VC](http://www.8vc.com/build)). Resilience was founded with Bob Nelsen of ARCH Venture Partners during the COVID-19 pandemic, and its customers include AstraZeneca, [Mayo Clinic](/organizations/mayo-clinic/), [Harvard University](/universities/harvard-university/), Moderna and Takeda ([8VC](http://www.8vc.com/build)).

## Defense bets: Anduril, Saronic and the frontier

Defense is where 8VC has drawn the most attention, and Lonsdale has tied the push to China: he has said China is "building really advanced things" and that the realisation "became a very scary realization to us about 10 years ago so we went hard into defense" ([Forbes](https://alphaleaders.co.uk/u-s-defense-tech-investor-8vc-has-links-to-sanctioned-russian-oligarchs/amp/)). The firm was an early investor in Anduril, and has since broadened the bet into autonomous systems ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/trump-ally-joe-lonsdale-targets-1b-for-his-new-8vc-fund)). It was a seed investor in Saronic in 2022, the maker of robotic boat drones, later reported to be in talks to raise $500 million at a $3 billion valuation ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/joe-lonsdales-8vc-seeking-1b-for-new-fund/)). In January 2025 Overland AI raised a $32 million Series A, and Lonsdale told Axios it could become the "next great defense prime" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/joe-lonsdales-8vc-seeking-1b-for-new-fund/)). The firm has also led rounds for Starpath, and was reported to be in talks to lead a $150–200 million round for Epirus ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/trump-ally-joe-lonsdale-targets-1b-for-his-new-8vc-fund)). Alongside [Andreessen Horowitz](/organizations/andreessen-horowitz/) and [General Catalyst](/organizations/general-catalyst/), 8VC has emerged as a prominent defense-tech backer ([Forbes](https://alphaleaders.co.uk/u-s-defense-tech-investor-8vc-has-links-to-sanctioned-russian-oligarchs/amp/)).

## Fintech and the Blend thread

8VC's fintech record runs through Blend. Blend's early funding came from [Greylock](/organizations/greylock/), [Emergence Capital](/organizations/emergence-capital/), 8VC, [Lightspeed](/organizations/lightspeed-venture-partners/), Nyca Partners, [Founders Fund](/organizations/founders-fund/), Formation 8, Andreessen Horowitz and Peter Thiel; 8VC backed its $130 million Series E in June 2019 and its $75 million Series F in August 2020 at a $1.7 billion valuation ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/corporate/blend-labs-looks-to-join-public-markets/)). Blend filed for an IPO in June 2021, after a $300 million Series G in January 2021 had valued it at $3.3 billion, and listed on the [New York Stock Exchange](/organizations/new-york-stock-exchange/) that July, raising $360 million at a $4 billion valuation ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/corporate/blend-labs-looks-to-join-public-markets/); [Finovate](https://finovate.com/blend-raises-360-million-in-ipo-achieves-4-billion-valuation/)). By then Blend had more than 290 lender partners covering 30% of all US mortgage volume, and had facilitated more than $1 trillion in loans in 2020 ([Finovate](https://finovate.com/blend-raises-360-million-in-ipo-achieves-4-billion-valuation/)). The firm's other big exit came in February 2024, when Cox Enterprises bought OpenGov, whose board Lonsdale sat on, for $1.8 billion, one of the largest venture-backed software exits in recent memory, the Wall Street Journal said ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2024/02/27/cox-enterprises-buys-gov-tech-leader-opengov-1-8b/)).

## The fund ladder

8VC's funds have grown steadily, and the firm talks about committed capital rather than assets under management. Its fifth fund closed at $880 million in February 2023, and its sixth closed at $998 million in new limited-partner capital in 2025 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/joe-lonsdales-8vc-seeking-1b-for-new-fund/); [8VC](https://8vc.com/resources/announcing-8vc-fund-vi)). Announcing Fund VI, the firm framed its mission in frontier terms — life sciences, healthcare, defense, logistics, enterprise and education — and said it would keep co-founding companies through Build ([8VC](https://8vc.com/resources/announcing-8vc-fund-vi)). In July 2026 it raised $1.5 billion for Fund VII, and October 2026 SEC filings showed it seeking further vehicles, including a $400 million fund called 8VC DSS III and two smaller ones, while its AI Fund XI had already raised $13.5 million ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/from-8vc-to-benchmark-heres-whats-hiding-in-october-sec-filings/)).

Health tech and life sciences account for more than a quarter of the firm's deals, including the publicly traded biotechs Senti Bio and Vir, so the fund growth has ridden on more than the defense story alone ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/joe-lonsdales-8vc-seeking-1b-for-new-fund/)).

## Austin, politics and public profile

In 2020 Lonsdale moved 8VC from Silicon Valley to Austin, Texas, saying the city was more tolerant of "ideological diversity", a move that made the firm one of the symbols of the pandemic-era exodus of tech firms from California ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/joe-lonsdales-8vc-seeking-1b-for-new-fund/)). The relocation matched the firm's thesis: Austin is where Saronic builds its vessels in 100,000 square feet of factory space, and the firm's Fund VI announcement reads as a statement of Texan frontier identity, quoting the nineteenth-century rancher Charles Goodnight on the instinct for direction ([8VC](http://www.8vc.com/build); [8VC](https://8vc.com/resources/announcing-8vc-fund-vi)). Lonsdale's public profile extends well beyond investing. He co-founded the conservative think tank Cicero Institute in 2016, backed Ron DeSantis's short-lived 2023 presidential campaign before switching to the Trump team, and helped establish America PAC, the conservative funding group led by Elon Musk ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/trump-ally-joe-lonsdale-targets-1b-for-his-new-8vc-fund)). He was tapped to help advise on Musk's government panel DOGE, tasked by President [Donald Trump](/people/donald-trump/) with finding ways to cut federal spending and curb regulation ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/trump-ally-joe-lonsdale-targets-1b-for-his-new-8vc-fund)).

## Scrutiny: the Aven and Moshkovich hires

In 2024 Forbes reported that two children of sanctioned Russian oligarchs worked at 8VC. The firm hired Denis Aven in 2022, son of Petr Aven, sanctioned by the EU, the UK and later the US Treasury ([Forbes](https://alphaleaders.co.uk/u-s-defense-tech-investor-8vc-has-links-to-sanctioned-russian-oligarchs/amp/)). Jack Moshkovich joined in 2018 as an analyst, the son of Vadim Moshkovich, founder of Rusagro, sanctioned by the EU, UK, Switzerland, Canada and Australia after Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine, but not by the US ([Forbes](https://alphaleaders.co.uk/u-s-defense-tech-investor-8vc-has-links-to-sanctioned-russian-oligarchs/amp/)). Forbes found no indication that either father has any financial relationship with 8VC, and a former State Department sanctions coordinator said hiring the son of a sanctioned person is not illegal — but "you better have done due diligence" ([Forbes](https://alphaleaders.co.uk/u-s-defense-tech-investor-8vc-has-links-to-sanctioned-russian-oligarchs/amp/)). Lonsdale responded on X that 8VC has "a strict compliance and legal framework" and that "we haven't taken any money from their fathers or their fathers' friends, and don't need it" ([Forbes](https://alphaleaders.co.uk/u-s-defense-tech-investor-8vc-has-links-to-sanctioned-russian-oligarchs/amp/)). Moshkovich, a [Stanford](/universities/stanford-university/) graduate, works as a principal and has not touched defense deals; Aven, a Yale and Harvard graduate, was launching an undisclosed venture as an entrepreneur-in-residence ([Forbes](https://alphaleaders.co.uk/u-s-defense-tech-investor-8vc-has-links-to-sanctioned-russian-oligarchs/amp/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is 8VC?** 8VC is an American venture capital firm based in Austin, Texas, founded in 2015 by Palantir co-founder Joe Lonsdale, who runs it as managing partner ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/joe-lonsdales-8vc-seeking-1b-for-new-fund/)). It invests in defense, life sciences, healthcare, logistics and enterprise software, and co-founds companies through its Build program ([8VC](http://www.8vc.com/build)).

**How is 8VC different from other venture capital firms?** Most venture firms only fund companies others have started, while 8VC also co-founds them through its Build program, working from internal theses or with entrepreneurs-in-residence ([8VC](http://www.8vc.com/build)). Saronic, Epirus and Resilience were all built this way, with Lonsdale personally involved in each ([8VC](http://www.8vc.com/build)).

**How big are 8VC's funds?** Fund V closed at $880 million in February 2023, Fund VI closed at $998 million in 2025, and Fund VII raised $1.5 billion in July 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/joe-lonsdales-8vc-seeking-1b-for-new-fund/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/from-8vc-to-benchmark-heres-whats-hiding-in-october-sec-filings/)). The firm says it measures committed capital rather than assets under management ([8VC](https://8vc.com/resources/announcing-8vc-fund-vi)).

**What was 8VC's role in Blend's IPO?** 8VC supplied some of Blend's early funding and backed its $130 million Series E and $75 million Series F rounds before Blend listed on the New York Stock Exchange in July 2021, raising $360 million at a $4 billion valuation ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/corporate/blend-labs-looks-to-join-public-markets/); [Finovate](https://finovate.com/blend-raises-360-million-in-ipo-achieves-4-billion-valuation/)).
