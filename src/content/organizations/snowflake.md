## Overview

Snowflake is a US cloud data platform company founded in 2012 in San Mateo, California, by three database architects: Benoît Dageville and Thierry Cruanes, formerly data architects at Oracle, and Marcin Żukowski, a co-founder of the database company Vectorwise whose doctoral research on vectorization underpins Snowflake's core technology ([Network World](https://networkworld.com/article/931670/data-warehouse-as-a-service-startup-snowflake-comes-out-of-stealth.html)). Their mission was to bring the database — a market dominated for decades by Oracle — to the cloud, which TechCrunch described as "a pretty radical notion in 2012" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/06/25/snowflake-co-founder-and-president-of-product-benoit-dageville-is-coming-to-tc-sessions-enterprise/)). The company spent two years in stealth, emerging in October 2014 with a $26 million Series B and an "elastic data warehouse" built on Amazon EC2 ([Network World](https://networkworld.com/article/931670/data-warehouse-as-a-service-startup-snowflake-comes-out-of-stealth.html)). In September 2020 it went public on the New York Stock Exchange in the largest US IPO of the year, raising $3.36 billion at $120 a share, with shares indicated to open at more than double that price, for a valuation above $60 billion that Reuters called the most valuable software IPO of all time ([Reuters](https://africannewsagency.com/update-1-snowflake-shares-set-to-open-at-more-than-double-their-ipo-price-38741ab0-4883-59b4-89c3-78df266301c6/)). The company was formerly known as Snowflake Computing; it is unrelated to the "snowflake schema" term used in data modeling. What distinguishes Snowflake's corporate history is its three-era chief-executive relay: a Microsoft platform veteran, a serial IPO specialist, and finally the founder of a startup Snowflake itself had acquired ([TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/data-technologies/news/252462656/Snowflake-CEO-Bob-Muglia-replaced-by-former-ServiceNow-CEO); [CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/business/companies/snowflake-new-ceo-sridhar-ramaswamy-frank-slootman-retires-to-remain-board-chairman-19163061.htm)).

## What the organization is known for

Snowflake is known for its "Data Cloud" — a platform for data warehousing, data sharing, and analytics that runs on all three major public clouds ([InfoWorld](https://www.infoworld.com/article/2338540/snowflake-acquires-neeva-to-add-generative-ai-based-search-to-data-cloud.html); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/06/25/snowflake-co-founder-and-president-of-product-benoit-dageville-is-coming-to-tc-sessions-enterprise/)). Its architecture separates compute from storage so the two scale independently — at the 2014 launch, Network World illustrated the point with the company's own analogy: traditional warehouses forced customers buying more computing power to also buy storage they did not need, "like" being upsold from a car rental to a tractor-trailer just to get a bigger engine ([Network World](https://networkworld.com/article/931670/data-warehouse-as-a-service-startup-snowflake-comes-out-of-stealth.html)). The company popularized per-second pricing and secure data sharing between organizations on its platform ([Snowflake Inc.](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/snowflake-appoints-frank-slootman-as-chairman-and-ceo-896340384.html)), and data exchange between Snowflake users became a key architectural feature driving network effects ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1943901)). It both partners and competes with the cloud giants — [Amazon Web Services](/organizations/amazon-web-services/), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud — running on their infrastructure while competing with offerings like Amazon Redshift; one analyst told TechTarget that Snowflake "got to elasticity in the data warehouse first, before [Amazon] Redshift," beating Amazon at its own game ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2020/cloud-data-platform-giant-snowflake-raises-479m-12-4b-valuation-partners-salesforce/); [TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/data-technologies/news/252462656/Snowflake-CEO-Bob-Muglia-replaced-by-former-ServiceNow-CEO)).

## Products and services

The original product was the elastic data warehouse, a fully managed service in which customers never deal directly with the underlying cloud provider ([Network World](https://networkworld.com/article/931670/data-warehouse-as-a-service-startup-snowflake-comes-out-of-stealth.html)). The platform expanded across clouds — starting on AWS and later adding Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/06/25/snowflake-co-founder-and-president-of-product-benoit-dageville-is-coming-to-tc-sessions-enterprise/)) — and into data sharing and applications. Snowflake's $800 million acquisition of Streamlit in March 2022 added an open-source framework for building data apps, filling what co-founder Benoît Dageville called the platform's missing native data-visualization piece ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2279117)). The May 2023 acquisition of Neeva brought generative-AI search to the Data Cloud ([InfoWorld](https://www.infoworld.com/article/2338540/snowflake-acquires-neeva-to-add-generative-ai-based-search-to-data-cloud.html)), and under chief executive Sridhar Ramaswamy the company launched Snowflake Cortex, a managed service for deploying AI on its platform ([CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/business/companies/snowflake-new-ceo-sridhar-ramaswamy-frank-slootman-retires-to-remain-board-chairman-19163061.htm)).

## Ownership, founders and leadership

Snowflake was founded by Dageville, Cruanes, and Żukowski; Dageville later served as president of product ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/06/25/snowflake-co-founder-and-president-of-product-benoit-dageville-is-coming-to-tc-sessions-enterprise/)). Bob Muglia, a 23-year Microsoft veteran whose last role there was president of the company's $16 billion server-and-tools business, became chief executive in mid-2014 as Snowflake prepared to exit stealth, and led it for five years until his abrupt departure in May 2019 ([Network World](https://networkworld.com/article/931670/data-warehouse-as-a-service-startup-snowflake-comes-out-of-stealth.html); [TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/data-technologies/news/252462656/Snowflake-CEO-Bob-Muglia-replaced-by-former-ServiceNow-CEO)). He was replaced as chairman and chief executive by Frank Slootman, who had run ServiceNow as chairman and CEO from 2011 to 2017 — taking it from under $100 million in revenue through an IPO to $1.4 billion — and had previously taken Data Domain through an IPO to a $2.4 billion sale to EMC ([Snowflake Inc.](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/snowflake-appoints-frank-slootman-as-chairman-and-ceo-896340384.html)). Snowflake director Mike Speiser said at the time that "the best time to make a change is when things are going well" ([Snowflake Inc.](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/snowflake-appoints-frank-slootman-as-chairman-and-ceo-896340384.html)). Slootman led the company through its IPO and retired as chief executive in February 2024, remaining chairman; his successor was Sridhar Ramaswamy, who had co-founded the AI search startup Neeva in 2019, joined Snowflake when it acquired Neeva in May 2023, and served as senior vice president of AI before his appointment ([CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/business/companies/snowflake-new-ceo-sridhar-ramaswamy-frank-slootman-retires-to-remain-board-chairman-19163061.htm)). Before Neeva, Ramaswamy spent 15 years at Google, where he helped grow the advertising business from $1.5 billion to more than $100 billion ([CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/business/companies/snowflake-new-ceo-sridhar-ramaswamy-frank-slootman-retires-to-remain-board-chairman-19163061.htm)).

## Funding and acquisitions

Snowflake raised more than $1.4 billion in venture funding before going public ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1943901); [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2020/cloud-data-platform-giant-snowflake-raises-479m-12-4b-valuation-partners-salesforce/)). Early rounds were modest — a $26 million Series B in 2014 ([Network World](https://networkworld.com/article/931670/data-warehouse-as-a-service-startup-snowflake-comes-out-of-stealth.html)) — before accelerating: $450 million in October 2018 at a $3.9 billion valuation, then a $479 million Series G in February 2020 at $12.4 billion, led by Dragoneer Investment Group with Salesforce Ventures participating for the first time alongside a new Salesforce partnership ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2020/cloud-data-platform-giant-snowflake-raises-479m-12-4b-valuation-partners-salesforce/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1943901)). Investors across its history included Sutter Hill Ventures, Redpoint Ventures, Altimeter Capital, ICONIQ Capital, Sequoia Capital, and Madrona Venture Group ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/06/25/snowflake-co-founder-and-president-of-product-benoit-dageville-is-coming-to-tc-sessions-enterprise/)). The September 2020 IPO sold 28 million shares at $120 — above the marketed $100–$110 range — raising $3.36 billion, with Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, J.P. Morgan, Allen & Co, and Citigroup as lead underwriters; Berkshire Hathaway and Salesforce Ventures backed the offering ([Reuters](https://africannewsagency.com/update-1-snowflake-shares-set-to-open-at-more-than-double-their-ipo-price-38741ab0-4883-59b4-89c3-78df266301c6/)). On acquisitions, the largest was Streamlit at $800 million in March 2022 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2279117)); Neeva followed in May 2023 for an undisclosed sum ([InfoWorld](https://www.infoworld.com/article/2338540/snowflake-acquires-neeva-to-add-generative-ai-based-search-to-data-cloud.html)), part of a string that also included LeapYear, Myst AI, Applica, Pragmatists, Polidea, and CryptoNumerics ([InfoWorld](https://www.infoworld.com/article/2338540/snowflake-acquires-neeva-to-add-generative-ai-based-search-to-data-cloud.html)).

## Competitors and partners

Snowflake set out to displace Oracle's decades-long dominance of the database market by moving it to the cloud ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/06/25/snowflake-co-founder-and-president-of-product-benoit-dageville-is-coming-to-tc-sessions-enterprise/)). It partners with and competes against Amazon Web Services, [Microsoft](/organizations/microsoft/) Azure, and [Google](/organizations/google/) Cloud ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2020/cloud-data-platform-giant-snowflake-raises-479m-12-4b-valuation-partners-salesforce/)). Salesforce became both an investor (through Salesforce Ventures) and a strategic partner in 2020, with the relationship aimed at making Salesforce data seamlessly accessible inside Snowflake ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1943901)).
