## Overview

JPMorgan Chase & Co. is an American multinational bank formed through the December 2000 merger of J.P. Morgan & Co. and The Chase Manhattan Corporation, headquartered in [New York City](/places/new-york-city/) ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/JPMorgan-Chase-and-Co)). It is the largest bank in the [United States](/places/united-states/) by total assets, with about $3.9 trillion in assets, operations in 100-plus countries and roughly 300,000 employees ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)). Its oldest roots reach back to the Bank of the Manhattan Company, founded in 1799.

The bank's history has a recurring pattern: in a crisis it grows. J.P. Morgan personally steadied the American financial system in 1893 and 1907; the modern bank absorbed Bear Stearns and Washington Mutual in 2008 and First Republic in 2023. Forbes ranked it first on its 2024 Global 2000 list of the world's largest public companies ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)). It is also a quiet blockchain pioneer: J.P. Morgan joined [Axoni](/organizations/axoni/)'s 2016 Series A and its equity-swaps pilot, and in 2019 launched JPM Coin, the first cryptocurrency from a US bank ([Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/vc-deal-of-the-day-axioni-raises-18m-series-a-46944.html?print); [EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)).

## Two centuries of mergers

JPMorgan Chase traces its oldest roots to the Bank of the Manhattan Company, founded in 1799 in New York City by Aaron Burr, which later financed the construction of the Erie Canal ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)). The Morgan branch began with Drexel, Morgan & Co., founded in 1871 by J. Pierpont Morgan and Anthony Drexel, which financed railroads and industrial consolidations including the formation of U.S. Steel, the world's first billion-dollar corporation ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/JPMorgan-Chase-and-Co)). Chase National Bank, founded in 1877 by John Thompson and named for Abraham Lincoln's Treasury secretary Salmon P. Chase, was the largest bank in the world by 1930, and merged with the Bank of the Manhattan Company in 1955 to form Chase Manhattan Bank ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)). Chemical Bank merged with Chase Manhattan in 1996, and the combined bank merged with J.P. Morgan & Co. in 2000 to form JPMorgan Chase ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)). Bank One merged in 2004, bringing Jamie Dimon aboard as president and chief operating officer; he became chief executive at the beginning of 2006 ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)).

## The house that steadied America — and its own crises

Long before it was one company, the Morgan half was America's unofficial central bank. After the Panic of 1893, J.P. Morgan organized a syndicate to bail out the U.S. Treasury with $62 million in gold, and in the Panic of 1907 he chaired a committee deciding which banks to rescue — leadership that helped lay the groundwork for the Federal Reserve in 1913 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/JPMorgan-Chase-and-Co)). Under the 1933 Glass-Steagall Act, J.P. Morgan & Co. kept commercial banking and spun off investment banking into Morgan Stanley, co-founded by Morgan's grandson Henry Morgan ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/JPMorgan-Chase-and-Co)). The modern bank has had scandals of its own. In May 2012 the "London Whale", trader Bruno Iksil, ran up credit-derivatives losses over $6 billion; the bank paid $920 million in fines ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)). In late 2013 it paid a $13 billion civil settlement to the Justice Department over mortgage lending, and in early 2014 $1.7 billion to settle felony charges over the $65 billion Bernie Madoff Ponzi scheme ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)).

## The crisis acquirer: 2008, 2023 and the blockchain thread

In 2008 JPMorgan Chase turned crisis into scale, acquiring Bear Stearns in March at a bargain price with about $30 billion of Federal Reserve financing, then buying Washington Mutual in September after regulators seized it — the largest US bank failure — for $1.9 billion ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)). The government invested $25 billion in the bank under the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act late that year ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/JPMorgan-Chase-and-Co)). The pattern repeated in May 2023, when it took over First Republic Bank after its seizure, receiving all its deposits and most of its assets ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)). The bank was also an early blockchain adopter: J.P. Morgan participated in Axoni's $18 million Series A in December 2016 and in its October 2016 equity-swaps pilot with [Citi](/organizations/citigroup/) and [Credit Suisse](/organizations/credit-suisse/), launched JPM Coin in 2019, and rebranded it Kinexys in 2024 ([Financial Adviser Magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/vc-deal-of-the-day-axioni-raises-18m-series-a-46944.html?print); [The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/dlt-provider-secures-major-bank-funding/); [EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)). Forbes ranked JPMorgan first on its 2024 Global 2000 list — a ranking built in part on absorbing failing rivals in 2008 and 2023 ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)).

## Frequently asked questions

**When was JPMorgan Chase formed?**
JPMorgan Chase & Co. was formed in December 2000, when J.P. Morgan & Co. merged with the Chase Manhattan Corporation ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/JPMorgan-Chase-and-Co)). Its oldest predecessor, the Bank of the Manhattan Company, dates to 1799 in New York City.

**Who is Jamie Dimon?**
Jamie Dimon is JPMorgan Chase's chairman and chief executive officer. He joined the bank through the 2004 Bank One merger, where he had been CEO, and became JPMorgan Chase's CEO at the beginning of 2006 ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)). He was praised for steering the bank through the 2008 financial crisis.

**What is the difference between J.P. Morgan and Chase?**
J.P. Morgan is the bank's brand for investment banking, commercial banking and asset management, descended from J.P. Morgan & Co., while Chase is the consumer and community banking brand, descended from Chase National Bank ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/JPMorgan-Chase-and-Co)). Both sit inside JPMorgan Chase & Co.

**What did JPMorgan Chase buy during the 2008 financial crisis?**
It acquired Bear Stearns in March 2008 and bought Washington Mutual in September 2008 after regulators seized it — the largest bank failure in US history — paying $1.9 billion ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/jpmorgan-chase)). It also received a $25 billion government investment that year.
