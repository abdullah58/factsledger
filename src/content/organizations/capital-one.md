## Overview

Capital One Financial Corporation is an American bank holding company headquartered in McLean, Virginia, and the largest credit card issuer in the [United States](/places/united-states/) by loan volume since its May 2025 acquisition of [Discover Financial](/organizations/discover-financial/) ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/credit-cards/2025/report-capital-one-set-to-expand-banking-and-card-businesses-after-discover-acquisition/)). It began in 1988 as a data-driven credit card division inside Richmond-based Signet Bank, built by Richard Fairbank and Nigel Morris on the idea that lending was an information business, and it became a standalone public company in 1994–95. Three decades of acquisitions — banks, an online bank, a card portfolio, a payment network and, in 2026, the fintech [Brex](/organizations/brex/) — turned a monoline card issuer into a diversified bank with $493.6 billion in assets at March 31, 2025 ([Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/about/newsroom/capital-one-completes-acquisition-of-discover/)).

## The information-based strategy: Signet to IPO

Fairbank and Morris were management consultants who believed credit cards were not banking but information: collect data on millions of people, and lending decisions follow ([jrank](https://companies.jrank.org/pages/783/Capital-One-Financial-Corporation.html)). They pitched the idea to more than 20 national retail banks before Signet Bank of Richmond signed on in 1988 and let them rebuild its credit card division from scratch ([jrank](https://companies.jrank.org/pages/783/Capital-One-Financial-Corporation.html)). The venture nearly died in the late-1980s real estate slump; a 1991 balance-transfer offer, luring cardholders to move existing debt at a lower introductory rate, saved it ([jrank](https://companies.jrank.org/pages/783/Capital-One-Financial-Corporation.html)).

Signet spun the division off as OakStone Financial in July 1994, took it public at $16 a share late that year, and completed the separation in February 1995 under the name Capital One Financial Corporation, then one of the top ten US card issuers with more than five million card customers ([jrank](https://companies.jrank.org/pages/783/Capital-One-Financial-Corporation.html)). The company kept the data-first method as its identity: a federal thrift charter in 1996 let it hold deposits, it expanded into the United Kingdom and Canada the same year, entered auto finance with Summit Acceptance in 1998, and bought PeopleFirst Finance, then the largest online direct auto lender, in October 2001 ([jrank](https://companies.jrank.org/pages/783/Capital-One-Financial-Corporation.html)). The "What's in your wallet?" campaign, launched in 2000, lifted brand awareness from 61% in June 1999 to 92% by December 2001 ([jrank](https://companies.jrank.org/pages/783/Capital-One-Financial-Corporation.html)).

## Buying a bank, then a network

Capital One spent the 2000s buying its way out of being a monoline card issuer, acquiring Hibernia National Bank in 2005, North Fork Bancorporation in 2006 and Chevy Chase Bank in 2009 ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/markets/fed-greenlights-capital-oneing-deal-11418716)). The defining deal of that era came in June 2011, when it agreed to buy ING Direct USA for $9 billion — $6.2 billion in cash plus 55.9 million Capital One shares — a purchase the Federal Reserve approved in February 2012 only after seven months and three public hearings over "too big to fail" concerns, creating the ninth-largest US bank holding company with roughly $298 billion in combined assets ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/markets/fed-greenlights-capital-oneing-deal-11418716)). Capital One also bought HSBC's US credit card portfolio for $2.6 billion in the same period ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/markets/fed-greenlights-capital-oneing-deal-11418716)).

## Breach, Discover, Brex

The growth story has a sharp interruption. In 2019 an attacker obtained the personal information of more than 100 million people from Capital One's cloud systems ([Associated Press](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2022/06/21/311136.htm?print)). Paige Thompson, a 36-year-old former Amazon software engineer who used the handle "erratic", was convicted in June 2022 of wire fraud, unauthorized access to a protected computer and damaging a protected computer, and acquitted of access device fraud and aggravated identity theft ([Associated Press](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2022/06/21/311136.htm?print)). The breach cost the company an $80 million Treasury fine and a tentative $190 million settlement with affected customers ([Associated Press](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2022/06/21/311136.htm?print)).

The two deals that define Capital One now both closed after the breach. On February 19, 2024 it announced it would acquire Discover Financial for $35.3 billion in stock; regulators approved the deal on April 18, 2025 and it closed on May 18, 2025, bringing the Discover, PULSE and Diners Club networks under Capital One and making it the largest US card issuer by loan volume ([Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/about/newsroom/capital-one-completes-acquisition-of-discover/); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/credit-cards/2025/report-capital-one-set-to-expand-banking-and-card-businesses-after-discover-acquisition/)). The announcement said the combination would create a global payments platform with 70 million merchant acceptance points in more than 200 countries and territories ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/credit-cards/2025/report-capital-one-set-to-expand-banking-and-card-businesses-after-discover-acquisition/)). Then, on January 22, 2026, the Wall Street Journal reported Capital One would acquire Brex for $5.15 billion in cash and stock — less than half of Brex's $12.3 billion 2022 valuation ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/22/capital-one-acquires-brex-for-steep-discount-to-its-peak-valuation-but-early-believers-are-laughing-all-the-way-to-the-bank/)). The deal closed in April 2026 at about $2.56 billion in cash plus about 10.7 million Capital One shares, with $950 million in transaction costs over three years and nearly 80% of the price allocated to goodwill ([Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/2897536/capital-one-completes-brex-buyout-boosts-digital-payments-strategy)). Brex's reported client roster included TikTok, Robinhood and [Intel](/organizations/intel/), and its founders — Brazilian entrepreneurs who dropped out of [Stanford](/universities/stanford-university/) — had built it with backing from [Y Combinator](/organizations/y-combinator/) and individual investors including [Peter Thiel](/people/peter-thiel/) ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/22/capital-one-acquires-brex-for-steep-discount-to-its-peak-valuation-but-early-believers-are-laughing-all-the-way-to-the-bank/)). The arc that began with two consultants selling banks on statistics now ends, for the moment, with a bank that owns the rails its cards run on — and a rival in [Ramp](/organizations/ramp/), Brex's chief competitor, whose valuation had reached $32 billion by November 2025 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/22/capital-one-acquires-brex-for-steep-discount-to-its-peak-valuation-but-early-believers-are-laughing-all-the-way-to-the-bank/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who founded Capital One?**
Richard Fairbank and Nigel Morris, two management consultants who built a data-driven credit card division inside Signet Bank in Richmond, Virginia, in 1988, after pitching their approach to more than 20 banks ([jrank](https://companies.jrank.org/pages/783/Capital-One-Financial-Corporation.html)).

**When did Capital One become independent?**
Signet announced the spin-off in July 1994, the division went public at $16 a share late that year, and the separation was completed in February 1995 under the name Capital One Financial Corporation ([jrank](https://companies.jrank.org/pages/783/Capital-One-Financial-Corporation.html)).

**How big is Capital One?**
At March 31, 2025 it had $493.6 billion in total assets and $367.5 billion in deposits, and it trades on the NYSE as COF in the S&P 100 ([Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/about/newsroom/capital-one-completes-acquisition-of-discover/)).

**What did Capital One pay for Discover?**
$35.3 billion in an all-stock deal announced February 19, 2024 and closed May 18, 2025, after Federal Reserve and OCC approval on April 18, 2025 ([Virginia Business](https://virginiabusiness.com/capital-one-discover-merger-2025/)).

**What happened in the 2019 Capital One breach?**
An attacker obtained personal information of more than 100 million people; former Amazon engineer Paige Thompson was convicted in June 2022, and the company paid an $80 million fine and a tentative $190 million customer settlement ([Associated Press](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2022/06/21/311136.htm?print)).
