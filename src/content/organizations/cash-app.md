## Overview

Cash App is [Block](/organizations/block/)'s mobile money app: a digital wallet that began in October 2013 as Square Cash, a no-fee way to send money by email, and grew into a 59-million-monthly-active platform spanning a debit card, bitcoin and stock investing, and buy now, pay later — a service Block now uses to bank its users and connect them to the merchants on its Square network ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/building-blocks-square-base/828712/)). At launch, sending money meant putting the recipient in the "To" field, cash@square.com in "CC" and the amount in the subject line, using a linked debit card — no app or account needed on the receiving end ([AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/13/10/16/squares-free-square-cash-service-allows-users-to-send-money-via-email)). Basic transfers were capped at $250 a week, rising to $2,500 with identity verification, and the service was available only in the United States ([AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/13/10/16/squares-free-square-cash-service-allows-users-to-send-money-via-email)).

## From email payments to a money app

The app added one financial product at a time. The Cash Card, a [Visa](https://factsledger.com/organizations/visa/)-branded debit card linked to Cash App balances, launched in June 2017 and reached 3.5 million active users by August 2019, when it and the fee-bearing instant-deposit feature were Cash App's two biggest revenue contributors ([Digital Transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/?p=29115)). Bitcoin buying and selling expanded to all users on January 31, 2018, after a trial, with purchases capped at $10,000 a week and priced at a mid-market rate plus a spread ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2018/01/31/square-cash-expands-bitcoin-buying-and-selling-to-all-users/102)). Stock trading followed on October 24, 2019, with fractional shares available from as little as $1 ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2019-10-24-cash-app-adds-stock-trading-44543)). Block, formerly Square, had launched the service to compete with mobile payment apps like Venmo and [PayPal](https://factsledger.com/organizations/paypal/) ([Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2024/07/18/cash-app-to-exit-uk/?ref=thisweekinfintech.com)).

## The banking pivot: depth over headcount

Cash App's recent story is less about adding users than about deepening how the users it has use money. Block reported 58 million monthly actives in September 2025, but the metric it chose to highlight was new: 8.3 million "primary banking actives" — accounts receiving wage inflows or spending at least $500 a month across Cash App — up 18% year over year ([Block, Inc.](https://s29.q4cdn.com/628966176/files/doc_financials/2025/q3/Block_3Q25_Shareholder-Letter.pdf)). In the same quarter, Block reported Cash App Borrow originations up 134% year over year and post-purchase buy now, pay later originations crossing $3 billion on an annualised basis ([Block, Inc.](https://s29.q4cdn.com/628966176/files/doc_financials/2025/q3/Block_3Q25_Shareholder-Letter.pdf)). That credit turn is the context for [Afterpay](/organizations/afterpay/): Block expanded Afterpay on the Cash App Card to eligible cardholders generally in June 2026, after a 2025 pilot in 20 states, letting users convert everyday purchases — groceries, gas, utilities — into instalments ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/block-expands-afterpay-on-cash-app-card/821881/)). The loans are offered through First Electronic Bank on the Visa network, underwritten on customers' cash-flow data rather than traditional credit files, and aimed at hourly and gig workers ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/block-expands-afterpay-on-cash-app-card/821881/)). In Block's own example, a $240 purchase carries a flat 7.5% finance fee and a fixed 65.15% annual percentage rate, repaid over six weeks ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/block-expands-afterpay-on-cash-app-card/821881/)). Separately, Afterpay Pre-Purchase — picking instalments instead of a debit charge at checkout — reached general availability in 2026, while Cash App commerce enablement volume grew 17% and consumer lending originations grew 59% ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/filings/2026/08/25/the-afterpay-co-founder-on-block-s-board-moved-18-000-shares-under-a-plan-set-in-march-here-s-what-to-know/)).

## Neighborhoods, retreat, and scrutiny

In October 2025 Block debuted Neighborhoods, a local network linking its 59 million Cash App monthly users with about 4.5 million Square merchants ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/building-blocks-square-base/828712/)). Customers order through Cash App, merchants pay a 1% processing fee — half Square's usual rate — and users earn up to 10% of an order total, capped at $10, in Local Cash rewards that Block funds for 12 months ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/building-blocks-square-base/828712/)). By August 2026 Block had added 30,000 more restaurant merchants to the network, and said Neighborhoods users accounted for about 10% of a seller's gross payment volume after roughly nine months on average ([Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/building-blocks-square-base/828712/)). The same US-first focus ended Cash App's only other market: Cash App UK closed on September 15, 2024, with Block saying it was prioritising the United States and deprioritising global expansion ([Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2024/07/18/cash-app-to-exit-uk/?ref=thisweekinfintech.com)).

Growth has also drawn scrutiny. In March 2023 short seller Hindenburg Research alleged Block had overstated Cash App user numbers for years, citing former employees who estimated 40% to 75% of accounts they reviewed were fake, involved in fraud, or duplicates; Block responded that 44 million of its more than 51 million December 2022 monthly actives were identity-verified, and Hindenburg countered that this implied 16% to 31% inflation ([Reuters](https://ktwb.com/2023/03/31/hindenburg-says-block-response-confirms-it-inflated-cash-app-user-counts/)). Then in January 2025, state regulators imposed an $80 million multistate settlement on Block for Bank Secrecy Act and anti-money-laundering violations, requiring an independent consultant's review of its compliance programme within nine months and correction of deficiencies within a year; Block cooperated with the states ([Nebraska Department of Banking and Finance](https://northplattepost.com/posts/cbef5d6d-154a-4ccd-972d-d8a350c27443)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not claim Cash App's monthly actives are unique people: Block's own definition counts accounts, which may share identifiers or belong to a single customer holding several accounts. It does not resolve the 2023 Hindenburg dispute — the allegation and Block's response are both reported, and no source read adjudicated between them. It does not state a primary-banking figure newer than September 2025, because none was verified in a source read in full. It does not present the Afterpay finance example as universal terms: eligibility is not guaranteed, loans are state-dependent, and the $240 illustration is Block's own example, not a rate card. Finally, it does not assert a headquarters city for Cash App itself, which different sources place differently for its parent, and it does not adjudicate why Cash App UK closed beyond Block's stated US-focus rationale.
