## Overview

Asure Software, Inc. (Nasdaq: ASUR) is an Austin, Texas-based provider of cloud human capital management software — payroll processing, payroll tax compliance, time and attendance, and HR services — aimed at small and mid-sized U.S. employers ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/resources/company-profiles/asure-software/)). For full-year 2025 it reported revenue of $140.5 million, up 17%, with adjusted EBITDA of $32 million ([TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/tradingview:b002ea8c3bf2b:0-asure-software-reports-strong-q4-and-full-year-2025-results/); [YCharts](https://ycharts.com/companies/ASUR/revenues_annual)). What makes Asure unusual is its past life: founded in 1985 as Forgent Networks, it spent the mid-2000s as one of the tech industry's most notorious patent enforcers, extracting more than $110 million in JPEG royalties before reinventing itself as a payroll company ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/resources/company-profiles/asure-software/); [BetaNews](http://betanews.com/article/forgent-settles-jpeg-patent-litigation/)).

## The patent-troll years

Forgent's patent campaign began with a 1997 acquisition: the dormant Compression Labs, which had filed U.S. Patent 4,698,672 — covering compression techniques at the heart of the JPEG image format — in October 1986 ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2004/04/23/forgent_jpeg_suit/)). In June 2002, Forgent announced it held the "sole and exclusive right" to license the patent's claims, and persuaded Sony to pay $15 million for a license — news that alarmed the industry so deeply that the ISO threatened to withdraw its standards blessing from JPEG, which it can only endorse royalty-free ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2004/04/23/forgent_jpeg_suit/)).

Undeterred, Forgent claimed some $90 million in royalties and, in April 2004, sued 30 companies that had rejected its terms — a defendant list spanning Japanese electronics giants (JVC, Panasonic, Ricoh), camera makers (Fuji, Agfa, Kodak), PC companies (IBM, Dell, Apple, HP, Toshiba, Gateway) and software firms including Adobe and Macromedia ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2004/04/23/forgent_jpeg_suit/)). The campaign's end came through the Public Patent Foundation, which persuaded the U.S. Patent Office to reject the patent on prior-art grounds; Forgent then settled its remaining cases for a reported $8 million — far short of the $1 billion it had once hoped for ([BetaNews](http://betanews.com/article/forgent-settles-jpeg-patent-litigation/)). Over the patent's life, Forgent said, it had "collected in excess of $110M" from more than 60 companies ([BetaNews](http://betanews.com/article/forgent-settles-jpeg-patent-litigation/)).

## Reinvention as a payroll company

Forgent Networks renamed itself Asure Software and reshaped around payroll, tax, time-and-attendance and HR software and services, built out through acquisitions ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/resources/company-profiles/asure-software/)). The strategy, as Umbrex's profile describes it: anchor the customer relationship on payroll and tax processing — high-frequency, high-consequence workflows with real switching costs — then expand into adjacent modules, competing against giants like ADP and Paychex on SMB relevance and payroll-tax expertise rather than product breadth ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/resources/company-profiles/asure-software/)).

The numbers show a small-cap SaaS business still scaling toward consistent profitability. Q4 2025 brought $39.3 million in revenue (up 28%) and net income of $0.8 million — against a $3.2 million loss a year earlier — while the full year showed $140.5 million in revenue (up 17%) but a $13.1 million net loss, with adjusted EBITDA of $32 million, up from $22.5 million in 2024 ([TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/tradingview:b002ea8c3bf2b:0-asure-software-reports-strong-q4-and-full-year-2025-results/)). Management guided Q1 2026 revenue of $41–43 million and full-year 2026 revenue of $159–162 million at 23–25% adjusted EBITDA margins ([TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/tradingview:b002ea8c3bf2b:0-asure-software-reports-strong-q4-and-full-year-2025-results/)).
