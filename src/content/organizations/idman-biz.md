## Overview

Idman.biz — branded İdman və Biz, or "Sports and Us" — is a Baku-based sports news portal launched on 26 September 2023 as a project of Trend News Agency ([AzerNews](https://www.azernews.az/sports/215315.html)). It publishes news, results, and analysis across all sports in three languages: Azerbaijani, Russian, and English ([AzerNews](https://www.azernews.az/sports/215315.html); [TurkicWorld](https://turkic.world/en/articles/other/149293)). Its stated aim is to provide high-quality, up-to-date coverage of sporting events in Azerbaijan and around the world, combining fast news with analytical material that links local and global perspectives ([Idman.biz](https://www.idman.biz/en/about)).

The portal was introduced at an official presentation ceremony in Baku attended by government, Olympic, media-regulator, and Trend officials ([TurkicWorld](https://turkic.world/en/articles/other/149293)). Since then it has published continuously, including federation-sourced reporting such as its October 2026 account of Azerbaijan's freestyle wrestling team's final training camp before the World Championships ([Idman.biz](https://www.idman.biz/en/news/wrestling/160974)). It is best understood as Trend's dedicated sports vertical: a single-sport-subject outlet inside a larger news agency's portfolio, rather than a standalone company.

## Launch and ownership

The Idman.biz portal was presented in Baku on 26 September 2023, at a ceremony attended by Youth and Sports Minister Farid Gayibov, National Olympic Committee vice-president Chingiz Huseynzade and secretary-general Azer Aliyev, Media Development Agency executive director Ahmed Ismayilov, and Trend News Agency director-general Ilgar Huseynov ([TurkicWorld](https://turkic.world/en/articles/other/149293); [AzerNews](https://www.azernews.az/sports/215315.html)). [AzerNews](/organizations/azernews/) covered the presentation, reporting the ceremony and the officials who attended ([AzerNews](https://www.azernews.az/sports/215315.html)). Trend deputy director Sahil Kerimli presented the project, saying the agency runs both international and local projects and that Idman.biz was one of them ([TurkicWorld](https://turkic.world/en/articles/other/149293)). Project manager Anar Imanov briefed guests on the website after a promotional video was shown ([TurkicWorld](https://turkic.world/en/articles/other/149293)). Every source read in full describes Idman.biz as a Trend News Agency project, and this profile follows that description: it does not present the portal as an independent company, and it does not claim any editorial separation from Trend, which no source establishes.

## Languages and coverage

Idman.biz functions in Azerbaijani, Russian, and English, with news broadcast simultaneously in all three languages ([AzerNews](https://www.azernews.az/sports/215315.html)). Its scope is all sports: the launch promised football, basketball, tennis, Formula 1, and more, with news, results, and analysis ([TurkicWorld](https://turkic.world/en/articles/other/149293)). Its own About page adds that experts and journalists produce in-depth analytical material on events, trends, and developments across disciplines, aiming for a holistic view that connects local and global perspectives ([Idman.biz](https://www.idman.biz/en/about)). Readers can also follow it on Facebook, Instagram, Telegram, and other social networks ([TurkicWorld](https://turkic.world/en/articles/other/149293)).

A typical example is its October 2026 report that Azerbaijan's senior freestyle wrestling team had begun a two-week final camp at the Guba Olympic Sports Complex ahead of the World Championships in Astana ([Idman.biz](https://www.idman.biz/en/news/wrestling/160974)). The report, citing the Azerbaijan Wrestling Federation, named head coach Khetag Gazyumov and coach Arif Abdullayev, listed the full squad across ten weight classes, and noted that twelve under-20 wrestlers had joined the camp for sparring ([Idman.biz](https://www.idman.biz/en/news/wrestling/160974)). That federation-sourced style is characteristic of its domestic coverage.

## Reception and role

At the launch, Youth and Sports Minister Farid Gayibov said sport is a state policy of Azerbaijan and thanked Trend for its focus on sports, adding that English-language broadcasting of Azerbaijani sport news would spread more information about Azerbaijani sport around the world ([TurkicWorld](https://turkic.world/en/articles/other/149293)). National Olympic Committee vice-president Chingiz Huseynzade called the trilingual portal an innovation in Azerbaijan's sports sphere and said he was confident it would take a worthy place in the Azerbaijani media space, with ample room for Olympic and non-Olympic sports ([AzerNews](https://www.azernews.az/sports/215315.html)). He also urged the creative team to prepare articles about athletes' life paths and determination, not only their victories ([AzerNews](https://www.azernews.az/sports/215315.html)). Two-time world wrestling champion Rafig Huseynov praised Trend as an accurate and unbiased media resource and said more projects like Idman.biz would mean accurate coverage of athletes' success ([TurkicWorld](https://turkic.world/en/articles/other/149293)). These are launch-event statements, and this profile presents them as such rather than as independent assessments of the portal's quality.
