## Overview

The Toronto-Dominion Bank — TD Bank Group, or simply TD — is a Canadian multinational bank headquartered in [Toronto](/places/toronto/), [Canada](/places/canada/), created on February 1, 1955, by the amalgamation of the Bank of Toronto (chartered 1855) and the Dominion Bank (chartered 1869). It is the sixth-largest bank in North America by assets, with $2.06 trillion in assets and over 27.9 million customers, and its shares trade as "TD" on the Toronto Stock Exchange and the [New York Stock Exchange](/organizations/new-york-stock-exchange/) ([TD Bank Group](https://stories.td.com/ca/en/news/2025-01-17-td-bank-group-accelerates-ceo-transition-3b-announces-board-an); [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/947263/000119312519307329/R9.htm)).

This profile is not about a single bank but about a group with two faces: a Canadian parent chartered under Canada's *Bank Act*, and a large U.S. retail arm, TD Bank, N.A., that trades as "America's Most Convenient Bank." The distinction matters because the criminal guilty plea that defines TD's recent history was entered by the U.S. subsidiaries — TD Bank, N.A. and its parent TD Bank US Holding Company — not by the Canadian parent as a legal entity ([U.S. Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/td-bank-pleads-guilty-bank-secrecy-act-and-money-laundering-conspiracy-violations-18b)). The bank's four businesses are Canadian Personal and Commercial Banking, U.S. Retail — including TD Bank and an investment in The Charles Schwab Corporation — Wealth Management and Insurance, and Wholesale Banking, including TD Securities and TD Cowen ([TD Bank Group](https://stories.td.com/ca/en/news/2025-01-17-td-bank-group-accelerates-ceo-transition-3b-announces-board-an)).

## From grain millers to a 1955 merger

TD traces its founding to 1855, when the Bank of Toronto was incorporated to serve grain millers and merchants; it opened its first branch on Church Street, Toronto, in 1856 and its first outside Ontario, in [Montreal](/places/montreal/), in 1860. The Dominion Bank, chartered in 1869, opened its first branch on King Street in Toronto in 1871, then expanded to London, England, in 1911 and New York City in 1919 ([TD Bank Group](https://www.td.com/ca/en/about-td/corporate-profile/tds-history)). By 1954 both banks were established national institutions needing greater scale, and they merged: the amalgamation was approved by Canada's Minister of Finance and took effect on February 1, 1955, creating a bank with 449 branches, about 4,700 employees, and offices in London and New York ([TD Bank Group](https://www.td.com/ca/en/about-td/corporate-profile/tds-history); [U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/947263/000119312519307329/R9.htm)). TD kept growing through technology and acquisition, buying its first computer in 1962 and introducing the Green Machine ATM in 1976, setting up Toronto Dominion Securities in 1987 and buying Waterhouse Investor Services in 1996, the year it launched WebBroker, Canada's first internet brokerage ([TD Bank Group](https://www.td.com/ca/en/about-td/corporate-profile/tds-history)).

## Building a North American bank

The modern TD was assembled by acquisition. In 2000 TD joined forces with Canada Trust, reshaping its Canadian retail business as TD Canada Trust, and in 2005 it bought 51% of Banknorth, entering U.S. retail banking. In 2008 it acquired Commerce Bancorp and integrated it with TD Banknorth to form TD Bank, "America's Most Convenient Bank," and in 2011 it added Chrysler Financial and MBNA Canada's credit card business ([TD Bank Group](https://www.td.com/ca/en/about-td/corporate-profile/tds-history)). The U.S. expansion continued with the 2022 agreement to buy First Horizon Corporation of Memphis, a regional bank with $80.7 billion in assets — but on May 4, 2023, the two banks mutually terminated the deal after TD said it had no timetable for regulatory approvals, for reasons unrelated to First Horizon, paying $200 million plus a $25 million fee reimbursement ([TD Bank Group](http://stories.td.com/ca/en/news/2023-05-04-td-bank-and-first-horizon-mutually-agree-to-terminate-merger)). The failed deal was an early public sign of the regulatory trouble that would surface the following year, leaving TD's U.S. growth to depend on the franchise it already owned.

## The U.S. AML crisis

On October 10, 2024, TD Bank, N.A. — the 10th-largest bank in the United States — and TD Bank US Holding Company pleaded guilty to Bank Secrecy Act and money-laundering conspiracy charges, agreeing to pay over $1.8 billion in criminal penalties as part of a coordinated resolution with the Federal Reserve Board, the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency and the [U.S. Department of the Treasury](/organizations/us-department-of-the-treasury/)'s Financial Crimes Enforcement Network ([U.S. Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/td-bank-pleads-guilty-bank-secrecy-act-and-money-laundering-conspiracy-violations-18b)). Attorney General Merrick B. Garland said TD became the largest bank in U.S. history to plead guilty to Bank Secrecy Act program failures and the first U.S. bank ever to plead guilty to conspiracy to commit money laundering. The failures were systemic: from 2014 to 2023 TD's U.S. anti-money-laundering program had pervasive deficiencies while senior executives enforced an internal "flat cost paradigm" that held the compliance budget flat even as profits and risk grew; new products such as Zelle launched without monitoring coverage, and from 2018 to 2024 the bank left 92% of transaction volume unmonitored ([U.S. Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/td-bank-pleads-guilty-bank-secrecy-act-and-money-laundering-conspiracy-violations-18b)).

## Resolution, asset cap and a new chief executive

TD's U.S. banking subsidiaries left 92% of transaction volume — about $18.3 trillion — unmonitored between January 2018 and April 2024 ([U.S. Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/td-bank-pleads-guilty-bank-secrecy-act-and-money-laundering-conspiracy-violations-18b)), and that failure produced a roughly $3.09 billion resolution and an asset cap that still constrains the bank's U.S. growth. The cap freezes the two U.S. banking subsidiaries at US$434 billion in total assets and subjects new U.S. products, services, markets and stores to stricter approval, while TD Securities and the Canadian and global businesses are exempt ([TD Bank Group](https://www.td.com/content/dam/tdcom/canada/about-td/pdf/investor/kiwi-oct-10-cnw-en.pdf)). TD says it has overhauled its U.S. anti-money-laundering leadership, adding 40 new leaders and over 700 specialists, and board chair Alan MacGibbon said the board had appointed new leaders across the U.S. operations and prioritised the investment needed to fix the program ([TD Bank Group](https://www.td.com/content/dam/tdcom/canada/about-td/pdf/investor/kiwi-oct-10-cnw-en.pdf)).

The fallout reached the top: then-CEO Bharat Masrani said the failures "took place on my watch as CEO," and Raymond Chun, previously chief operating officer, became Group President and CEO on February 1, 2025, months ahead of schedule. Masrani's 2024 pay was cut 89%, to $1,500,000, and 41 executives had variable pay cut by $30 million in total ([TD Bank Group](https://www.td.com/content/dam/tdcom/canada/about-td/pdf/investor/kiwi-oct-10-cnw-en.pdf); [TD Bank Group](https://stories.td.com/ca/en/news/2025-01-17-td-bank-group-accelerates-ceo-transition-3b-announces-board-an)).

## FAQ

### What is the Toronto-Dominion Bank?

The Toronto-Dominion Bank is a Canadian multinational bank headquartered in Toronto, formed on February 1, 1955, from the Bank of Toronto and the Dominion Bank. It operates as TD Bank Group, is the sixth-largest bank in North America by assets at $2.06 trillion, serves over 27.9 million customers, and lists on the Toronto and New York Stock Exchanges.

### Is TD Bank the same as Toronto-Dominion Bank?

TD Bank is the U.S. retail arm of the Toronto-Dominion Bank group, built from Banknorth and Commerce Bancorp and run through TD Bank, N.A. The legal parent is The Toronto-Dominion Bank, a Canadian bank chartered under the Bank Act; the two are often called "TD," but the 2024 guilty plea was entered by the U.S. subsidiaries, not the Canadian parent.

### Why did TD Bank plead guilty in 2024?

Its U.S. anti-money-laundering program had pervasive, decade-long deficiencies: it left 92% of transaction volume unmonitored from 2018 to 2024 while executives held the compliance budget flat. Three laundering networks moved over $670 million through the bank, and TD Bank, N.A. pleaded guilty to Bank Secrecy Act and money-laundering conspiracy charges.

### What is the asset cap on TD?

U.S. regulators capped the total assets of TD's two U.S. banking subsidiaries — TD Bank, NA and TD Bank USA, NA — at US$434 billion, their level at September 30, 2024. The cap does not apply to TD Securities or to TD's Canadian or other global businesses, but new U.S. products, markets and stores face stricter approval.

### Who runs TD Bank Group now?

Raymond Chun has been Group President and CEO since February 1, 2025, succeeding Bharat Masrani, who retired after the AML settlement. Masrani's 2024 compensation was cut 89% over the failures, and 41 executives had their variable pay reduced by $30 million in total.
