## Overview

Mambu is a SaaS banking platform — headquartered across Amsterdam and London after a 2011 Berlin founding — that sells APIs for lending, deposit, and other banking products, plus analytics and tooling for building financial services ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/09/mambu-nabs-266m-at-a-5-5b-valuation-to-double-down-on-embedded-financial-service-and-banking-apis/); [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2021/01/07/berlin-based-mambu-raises-134-million-saas-banking-platform-now-valued-2-09-billion/)). In December 2021 it raised €235 million (about $266 million) in a Series E led by EQT Growth at a €4.9 billion post-money valuation — roughly $5.5 billion — described as the largest financing round to date for a banking-software platform ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/09/mambu-nabs-266m-at-a-5-5b-valuation-to-double-down-on-embedded-financial-service-and-banking-apis/)). Co-founder and then-CEO Eugene Danilkis stated the company's ambition plainly: "Our vision is to create in financial services and banking what [Salesforce](/organizations/salesforce/) did in CRM" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/09/mambu-nabs-266m-at-a-5-5b-valuation-to-double-down-on-embedded-financial-service-and-banking-apis/)).

## The funding sprint

Mambu's valuation nearly tripled in under a year. The January 2021 round — $135 million led by TCV with Tiger Global, Arena Holdings, Bessemer Venture Partners, Runa Capital, and Acton Capital participating — valued it at $2.09 billion on approximately 100% year-on-year growth ([Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2021/01/07/berlin-based-mambu-raises-134-million-saas-banking-platform-now-valued-2-09-billion/)). By December, the EQT Growth-led Series E more than doubled that again to €4.9 billion; EQT had been following the company since before the previous round, and advisory-team partner Carolina Brochado joined Mambu's board ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/09/mambu-nabs-266m-at-a-5-5b-valuation-to-double-down-on-embedded-financial-service-and-banking-apis/)). At the time Mambu had more than 200 customers reaching 53 million end users across 65 countries — from neobanks like N26 to incumbents like Raiffeisen Bank, BancoEstado, and ABN Amro — and handled about 130 million API calls a day ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/09/mambu-nabs-266m-at-a-5-5b-valuation-to-double-down-on-embedded-financial-service-and-banking-apis/)). Later reporting put the footprint at 260+ customers, 114 million end users, and 200 million API calls per day ([Finovate](https://finovate.com/mambu-launches-composable-banking-extends-partnership-with-krom-bank/)).

## "SaaS banking platform," not embedded finance

Danilkis bristled at the industry's favorite label. "Embedded finance" — the trend of non-banks integrating financial tools via APIs — is the frame most coverage uses for Mambu, but the company's preferred term is "SaaS banking platform," which Danilkis said speaks to the more fundamental role Mambu plays in helping customers build tailored financial services for themselves ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/09/mambu-nabs-266m-at-a-5-5b-valuation-to-double-down-on-embedded-financial-service-and-banking-apis/)). The distinction matters commercially: beyond banks and fintechs, Mambu courts telcos and e-commerce companies that want to offer financial products without building them from scratch — a wider customer aperture than core banking alone ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/09/mambu-nabs-266m-at-a-5-5b-valuation-to-double-down-on-embedded-financial-service-and-banking-apis/)). Longer term, Danilkis pointed to more complex commercial products and better integration middleware, with insurance — property insurance via existing mortgage APIs, for example — "definitely on its radar" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/09/mambu-nabs-266m-at-a-5-5b-valuation-to-double-down-on-embedded-financial-service-and-banking-apis/)). Notably, Salesforce is a partner rather than a rival: Mambu's platform is built to "play nice" with customers' existing IT stacks, integrating with Salesforce, AWS, Google, Marqeta, ComplyAdvantage, and Deloitte ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/09/mambu-nabs-266m-at-a-5-5b-valuation-to-double-down-on-embedded-financial-service-and-banking-apis/)).

## Composable banking in practice

The composable model — modular, interchangeable components instead of monolithic cores — is Mambu's current product story. Its North American credit-union push offers migration paths (speedboat deployment, dual core, staged migration) for institutions stuck on legacy systems ([Finovate](https://finovate.com/mambu-launches-composable-banking-extends-partnership-with-krom-bank/)). The proof point Mambu leans on is Krom Bank: the Indonesian digital bank selected Mambu as its core banking partner in spring 2022, launched its app on Mambu's cloud-native core in 2024, and extended the partnership five years, targeting profitability and more than 20 million accounts by 2030 ([Finovate](https://finovate.com/mambu-launches-composable-banking-extends-partnership-with-krom-bank/)). Fernando Zandona is CEO ([Finovate](https://finovate.com/mambu-launches-composable-banking-extends-partnership-with-krom-bank/)).
