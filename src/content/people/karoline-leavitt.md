## Overview

Karoline Leavitt is the youngest person ever to serve as [White House](https://factsledger.com/organizations/the-white-house) press secretary — appointed at 27 in November 2024 and in the job from Donald Trump's second inauguration until the end of August 2026 ([Associated Press](https://theanswerorlando.com/regional/trump-names-karoline-leavitt-as-youngest-ever-white-house-press-secretary/4c550e04334e87d2075dc6bbe2e244bd)). She is also the first White House press secretary in history to give birth while holding the role, and that milestone — a daughter born in May 2026 — is what ended her tenure: within weeks she concluded she could not be the mother of two young children and the press secretary at once ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/08/28/white-house-press-secretary-mothers-trump-karoline-leavitt/91354289007/)). Barely a month out of the briefing room she is back in Trump's orbit — rejoining the pro-Trump super PAC [MAGA Inc.](/organizations/maga-inc/), speaking at the GOP's midterm convention, and on September 29, 2026, hosting the unveiling of America.gov, the administration's new AI-powered federal services portal ([Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/09/28/hollywood/karoline-leavitt-shopping-book-about-time-in-white-house/); [SoapCentral](https://www.soapcentral.com/entertainment/fantastic-event-karoline-leavitt-reacts-trump-administration-announces-america-gov-unveiling-featuring-elon-musk-jensen-huang)).

## From New Hampshire to the West Wing

Leavitt was born on August 24, 1997, in New Hampshire and studied communications and political science at [Saint Anselm College](/schools/saint-anselm-college/), a Catholic institution in her home state ([Associated Press](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/karoline-leavitt-makes-podium-debut-all-about-the-youngest-white-house-press-secretary/articleshow/117653946.cms)). While in college she interned at Fox News during the 2016 election and in Trump's first-term [White House press office](/organizations/white-house-press-office/) — experiences she later said gave her a "first glimpse into the world of the press" ([Associated Press](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/karoline-leavitt-makes-podium-debut-all-about-the-youngest-white-house-press-secretary/articleshow/117653946.cms)).

After graduating in 2019 she joined the first Trump administration as a presidential writer and then an assistant press secretary, a role in which her own campaign biography says she helped prepare Press Secretary Kayleigh McEnany for high-pressure briefings ([Associated Press](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/karoline-leavitt-makes-podium-debut-all-about-the-youngest-white-house-press-secretary/articleshow/117653946.cms)). After Trump's 2020 loss she became communications director for New York Representative Elise Stefanik ([NPR](https://www.wuot.org/2022-09-14/25-year-old-former-trump-aide-wins-gop-primary-in-new-hampshire)).

In 2021 she launched an audacious bid of her own: a run for Congress in New Hampshire's 1st District. On September 13, 2022, the 25-year-old won the Republican primary — the first Republican member of Generation Z to win a House primary — defeating former Trump State Department official [Matt Mowers](/people/matt-mowers/), even though House GOP leaders Kevin McCarthy and Steve Scalise had backed Mowers and Trump stayed out of the race; Stefanik backed her, her former spokeswoman ([NPR](https://www.wuot.org/2022-09-14/25-year-old-former-trump-aide-wins-gop-primary-in-new-hampshire)). She campaigned as a pro-Trump conservative who publicly asserted that Trump had won the 2020 election, then lost the November general election to Democratic incumbent [Chris Pappas](/people/chris-pappas/) ([NPR](https://www.wuot.org/2022-09-14/25-year-old-former-trump-aide-wins-gop-primary-in-new-hampshire); [Associated Press](https://theanswerorlando.com/regional/trump-names-karoline-leavitt-as-youngest-ever-white-house-press-secretary/4c550e04334e87d2075dc6bbe2e244bd)). After the loss she worked as a spokesperson for MAGA Inc., the pro-Trump super PAC, before joining Trump's 2024 campaign ([Associated Press](https://theanswerorlando.com/regional/trump-names-karoline-leavitt-as-youngest-ever-white-house-press-secretary/4c550e04334e87d2075dc6bbe2e244bd)).

Leavitt joined Trump's third presidential bid in January 2024 as national press secretary, traveling with him to rallies, events, and his Manhattan court appearances ([Associated Press](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/karoline-leavitt-makes-podium-debut-all-about-the-youngest-white-house-press-secretary/articleshow/117653946.cms)). She had been hired shortly after discovering she was pregnant; she gave birth to her first child in July 2024, stepped away briefly, and returned to work immediately — four days later, Trump was shot at a campaign rally in Butler, Pennsylvania ([Associated Press](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/karoline-leavitt-makes-podium-debut-all-about-the-youngest-white-house-press-secretary/articleshow/117653946.cms); [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/08/28/white-house-press-secretary-mothers-trump-karoline-leavitt/91354289007/)).

## The youngest press secretary

On November 15, 2024, President-elect Trump named the 27-year-old as his White House press secretary — the youngest in history, breaking the record held by [Ron Ziegler](/people/ron-ziegler/), who was 29 when he took the job under Richard Nixon in 1969 ([Associated Press](https://theanswerorlando.com/regional/trump-names-karoline-leavitt-as-youngest-ever-white-house-press-secretary/4c550e04334e87d2075dc6bbe2e244bd)). "Karoline is smart, tough, and has proven to be a highly effective communicator," Trump said in his announcement statement; Leavitt replied on X: "Thank you, President Trump, for believing in me. I am humbled and honored. Let's MAGA." ([Associated Press](https://theanswerorlando.com/regional/trump-names-karoline-leavitt-as-youngest-ever-white-house-press-secretary/4c550e04334e87d2075dc6bbe2e244bd))

She succeeded Karine Jean-Pierre on January 20, 2025, and made her briefing-room debut on January 28, during Trump's second week back in office ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/who-is-karoline-leavitt-donald-trump-press-secretary-set-to-step-down-from-white-house/article71340216.ece); [Associated Press](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/karoline-leavitt-makes-podium-debut-all-about-the-youngest-white-house-press-secretary/articleshow/117653946.cms)). Her tenure became known for combative exchanges with reporters and a staunch public defense of the president, alongside frequent appearances on Fox News and other conservative outlets to promote the administration's agenda ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/who-is-karoline-leavitt-donald-trump-press-secretary-set-to-step-down-from-white-house/article71340216.ece)). Trump himself became one of her most visible defenders: after her exit he told reporters she had made the job "very glamorous," claiming he had "never had so many job applications in my life" ([The National News Desk](https://wutv29.com/news/nation-world/vice-president-jd-vance-returns-to-white-house-briefing-room-as-press-secretary-vacancy-continues-karoline-leavitt-departure-succesor-job-applications-president-donald-trump-spokesperson)).

## Motherhood in the job, and the August 2026 exit

On May 1, 2026, Leavitt became the first White House press secretary in history to give birth while holding the role ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/08/28/white-house-press-secretary-mothers-trump-karoline-leavitt/91354289007/)). She was back on Air Force One traveling with Trump by late June — about seven weeks after giving birth — and held her first press briefing after the baby on July 16 ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/08/28/white-house-press-secretary-mothers-trump-karoline-leavitt/91354289007/)). During her May maternity leave the White House held fewer briefings and appointed no interim spokesperson; instead, senior officials including Vice President JD Vance — who took the podium twice — Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent covered media duties ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/who-is-karoline-leavitt-donald-trump-press-secretary-set-to-step-down-from-white-house/article71340216.ece); [The National News Desk](https://wutv29.com/news/nation-world/vice-president-jd-vance-returns-to-white-house-briefing-room-as-press-secretary-vacancy-continues-karoline-leavitt-departure-succesor-job-applications-president-donald-trump-spokesperson)).

It was a short return. On August 12, 2026, Trump announced on social media that Leavitt would leave at the end of the month to spend more time with her family, and that she would "now be one of my top outside advisors" and "an influential voice within the Republican Party" ahead of the midterm elections ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/who-is-karoline-leavitt-donald-trump-press-secretary-set-to-step-down-from-white-house/article71340216.ece)). In her own statement she wrote: "since returning to the White House after the birth of my daughter, I have felt in my heart that I cannot be the best mom my two young children deserve while devoting the constant time, energy and attention required of the White House Press Secretary" ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/who-is-karoline-leavitt-donald-trump-press-secretary-set-to-step-down-from-white-house/article71340216.ece)). She completed her final day on August 27 ([Dagens.com](https://www.dagens.com/news/just-weeks-after-leaving-karoline-leavitt-plans-on-releasing-book-about-trump-and-the-white-house)) — telling [NewsNation](/organizations/newsnation/) that day: "Every day as a working mother is a challenge... you have to say no to a lot of opportunities because you want to be there for bedtime with your kids every night" ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/08/28/white-house-press-secretary-mothers-trump-karoline-leavitt/91354289007/)). Her advice to whoever takes the job was characteristically Trump-centric: "the most important thing is understanding that President Donald Trump is 'his own best spokesperson'" — "he is the true communications director and press secretary here at the White House" ([The National News Desk](https://wutv29.com/news/nation-world/vice-president-jd-vance-returns-to-white-house-briefing-room-as-press-secretary-vacancy-continues-karoline-leavitt-departure-succesor-job-applications-president-donald-trump-spokesperson)). No successor had been named as of early September ([Dagens.com](https://www.dagens.com/news/just-weeks-after-leaving-karoline-leavitt-plans-on-releasing-book-about-trump-and-the-white-house)).

## September 2026: the afterlife of the podium

Leavitt confirmed she was returning to her previous job at MAGA Inc. ([Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/09/28/hollywood/karoline-leavitt-shopping-book-about-time-in-white-house/)), and she has stayed conspicuously visible. On September 10 she took the stage at the Republican midterm convention in Dallas telling the crowd: "It feels good to be back behind a podium... It feels much better speaking to a stadium full of patriots than a briefing room full of journalists," and urging voters to back Republicans up and down the ballot ([SheFinds](https://www.shefinds.com/collections/karoline-leavitt-pantsuit-gop-convention/)).

A week later she sat down with Sean Hannity's podcast, where she claimed reporters had privately apologized to her for asking "unfair" questions — "I’m sorry I had to ask that today, you know, my bosses really wanted me to ask that" — and recounted a private lunch with British Prime Minister Keir Starmer, at which Trump told him his immigration policies were "so stupid" and that he should "close your borders" ([Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/09/28/hollywood/karoline-leavitt-shopping-book-about-time-in-white-house/); [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/world/your-policies-are-so-stupid-trump-told-starmer-to-close-your-borders-during-a-private-lunch-says-leavitt-article-14039359.html/amp?utm_campaign=fullarticle&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=inshorts)). The disclosure drew fire from Newsmax host [Greg Kelly](/people/greg-kelly/), who posted on September 27 that Leavitt was "gossiping about private meetings the President had with world leaders" and called it a "breach of trust" ([Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/09/28/hollywood/karoline-leavitt-shopping-book-about-time-in-white-house/)).
