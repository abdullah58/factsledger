**Munjal Shroff** is the co-founder, director and chief operating officer of Graphiti Multimedia, the Mumbai animation studio behind the folk-art film series [Krish, Trish and Baltiboy](/creative-works/krish-trish-and-baltiboy/), which he co-created and co-directed with [Tilak Raj Shetty](/people/tilak-raj-shetty/) ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/event/online-events/awards/krish-trish-and-balti-boy-2-nominated-at-21st-cairo-international-film-festival-for-children/)). A computer engineer who moved into animation and now approaches 30 years in the industry, he also created Netflix's *Deepa & Anoop* — which won four Kidscreen Awards in 2023 — and in 2025 became chairman of FICCI's AVGC-XR Forum ([AnimationXpress](https://animationxpress.com/latest-news/munjal-shroff-appointed-chairman-of-ficcis-avgc-xr-forum/)).

His official IFFI biography credits his work across TV commercials, short films, animated features, interactive apps and audiovisual user experiences ([IFFI](https://www.iffigoa.org/web-series-jury/2025)).

## From advertising films to original IP

Shroff is a computer engineer with a specialisation in image processing ([AnimationXpress](https://animationxpress.com/latest-news/munjal-shroff-appointed-chairman-of-ficcis-avgc-xr-forum/)). Business Today reported that he and Tilak Shetty had both started out under Ram Mohan, the pioneer of Indian animation, and that — after Graphiti's 1997 advertising-industry start and its 2001 move into long-format work — the pair decided in 2002 to build original intellectual property while India's industry was still mostly an outsourcing hub ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/magazine/special/story/animation-farm-15796-2010-10-13); [AnimationXpress](https://animationxpress.com/animation/graphiti-working-business-model-around-ip/)).

Television gave the first validation: Graphiti's CG character "Genie" for Star Plus's *Khulja Sim Sim* grew from a 30-second bit to three minutes per episode on fan mail; a *Jadoo* TV series (with Rakesh Roshan's permission) followed, and in 2005 Shroff met 7UP-icon creator Joanna Ferrone at a trade show and won the studio's first foreign co-production, *The World of OO* ([AnimationXpress](https://animationxpress.com/animation/graphiti-working-business-model-around-ip/); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/magazine/special/story/animation-farm-15796-2010-10-13)).

He made the IP case in public early: at FICCI Frames 2006 he said global-market content needed "country neutral" characters; in 2009 he told a Frames panel that India lacked a working producer–broadcaster–creator ecosystem, and that studios had to learn "how to sell it and build a business around it" ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/advertising-news/developing-intellectual-property-in-indian-animation-20390.html); [Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/news/business/we-have-just-started-consuming-animation-munjal-shroff/)).

## Krish, Trish and Baltiboy: folk art as a mission

Shroff and Shetty's signature creation is KTB (KTB), the animated film series that tells Indian folk tales through regional folk-art styles. In 2012 the second film was nominated in children's animation at the 21st [Cairo International Film Festival for Children](/events/cairo-international-film-festival-for-children/), held 23–30 March ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/event/online-events/awards/krish-trish-and-balti-boy-2-nominated-at-21st-cairo-international-film-festival-for-children/)). Shroff said KTB was an attempt to "resurrect and nurture" dying folk art forms, arguing children had grown alienated from India's visual culture; he framed it as an experiment against the "Walmartisation" of kids' content, citing its Cartoon Network success as proof ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/event/online-events/awards/krish-trish-and-balti-boy-2-nominated-at-21st-cairo-international-film-festival-for-children/)). Shetty said the pair hoped to win, but were most excited to see foreign children respond to Indian stories ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/event/online-events/awards/krish-trish-and-balti-boy-2-nominated-at-21st-cairo-international-film-festival-for-children/)). The KTB characters later carried the studio's largest production, *Bharat Hain Hum* ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/latest-news/bharat-hain-hum-s2-launched-at-iffi-goa/)).

## Bharat Hain Hum: a government co-production at national scale

On 11 October 2023, Union Minister Anurag Thakur launched the trailer of *KTB – Bharat Hain Hum*, co-produced by Graphiti Studios and the [Central Bureau of Communication](/organizations/central-bureau-of-communication/) of the [Ministry of Information and Broadcasting](/organizations/ministry-of-information-and-broadcasting/). PTI credited the series as "created by Munjal Shroff and Tilak Shetty"; Season 1 — 26 episodes of 11 minutes dramatising the freedom struggle — premiered on 15 October 2023 simultaneously on [Doordarshan](/organizations/doordarshan/), [Netflix](/organizations/netflix/) and [Amazon](/organizations/amazon/) Prime Video in 12 Indian and 7 international languages ([Daily Excelsior](https://www.dailyexcelsior.com/news/archive/anurag-launches-trailer-of-animated-show-bharat-hain-hum)).

Shroff told Pickle Media the production built 700+ characters and 3,000+ backgrounds, and the streamers launched it in 150+ countries, where it ranked in Netflix's top 10 in its first week ([Pickle Media](https://pickle.co.in/mipcom-2024/time-is-ripe-for-india-munjal-shroff-on-co-productions-and-globalising-indian-ips/)). AnimationXpress reported the makers employed nearly 1,000 professionals across India — implying a government-backed Indian animated IP can be produced at a genuinely national industrial scale ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/latest-news/bharat-hain-hum-s2-launched-at-iffi-goa/)). Season 2 launched at the [International Film Festival of India](/events/international-film-festival-of-india/) in Goa in November 2024 with [Nagarjuna Akkineni](/people/nagarjuna-akkineni/), adding an All India Radio radio series and a Spotify podcast in 180 countries ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/latest-news/bharat-hain-hum-s2-launched-at-iffi-goa/); [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/entertainment/second-season-of-animated-series-krish-trish-and-baltiboy-bharat-hain-hum-launched-at-iffi/)); the next month Prime Minister Narendra Modi named the show in his [Mann Ki Baat](/creative-works/mann-ki-baat/) address ([IFFI](https://www.iffigoa.org/web-series-jury/2025)).

## Deepa & Anoop and the global studio

Shroff's other international credit is *Deepa & Anoop*, Mattel Television's first animated series, created by Shroff with writer Lisa Goldman and producer Heather Kenyon for Netflix ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/latest-news/here-are-the-winners-of-the-14th-annual-kidscreen-awards/)). At the 14th Annual Kidscreen Awards in 2023 it won four trophies — Best New Series and Best Inclusivity in preschool programming, plus Best Music and Best Voice Talent — and Shroff announced the wins from Miami, thanking the Mattel, Netflix and Graphiti teams ([AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/latest-news/here-are-the-winners-of-the-14th-annual-kidscreen-awards/)). Its second season was nominated for Best Show at Kidscreen 2024 ([Pickle Media](https://pickle.co.in/mipcom-2024/time-is-ripe-for-india-munjal-shroff-on-co-productions-and-globalising-indian-ips/)).

The wins cap a two-decade run of international partnerships — Cartoon Network, Disney, Nickelodeon, Discovery, SABC, Mattel and Netflix — and Shroff has argued that treating Indian studios as mere service partners wastes those relationships' potential ([Pickle Media](https://pickle.co.in/mipcom-2024/time-is-ripe-for-india-munjal-shroff-on-co-productions-and-globalising-indian-ips/)). Other IPs built under his leadership include *Yom*, the preschool toy concept *Toyzania* (Emmy-winning writer Jeffrey Scott as executive producer), and *Action Hero B.C.*, drawn from the *Katha Sarit Sagar* ([AnimationXpress](https://animationxpress.com/animation/graphiti-working-business-model-around-ip/); [AnimationXpress](https://animationxpress.com/latest-news/munjal-shroff-appointed-chairman-of-ficcis-avgc-xr-forum/)).

## Industry leadership: FICCI and the AVGC agenda

In 2025 Shroff was appointed chairman of FICCI's AVGC-XR Forum — the federation's animation, VFX, gaming, comics and extended-reality body — succeeding Ashish Kulkarni, after an earlier co-chair stint ([AnimationXpress](https://animationxpress.com/latest-news/munjal-shroff-appointed-chairman-of-ficcis-avgc-xr-forum/)). His official IFFI biography says he helped craft the National AVGC-XR policy and set up the Indian Institute of Creative Technologies, and serves on the Media & Entertainment Skills Council's governing council ([IFFI](https://www.iffigoa.org/web-series-jury/2025)).

At MIPCOM 2024 he argued the moment for Indian IP had arrived: India had a co-production fund covering up to 30% of a show's budget, and Indian studios had a proven record delivering animation for "some of the best shows that we see around the world" ([Pickle Media](https://pickle.co.in/mipcom-2024/time-is-ripe-for-india-munjal-shroff-on-co-productions-and-globalising-indian-ips/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is Munjal Shroff?**
He is the co-founder, director and chief operating officer of Graphiti Multimedia, a Mumbai animation studio. He co-created and co-directed *Krish, Trish and Baltiboy* with Tilak Raj Shetty, created Netflix's *Deepa & Anoop*, and in 2025 became chairman of FICCI's AVGC-XR Forum. His industry career spans nearly 30 years.

**What is Munjal Shroff best known for?**
The folk-art film series *Krish, Trish and Baltiboy* — including its government co-produced spin-off *Bharat Hain Hum* on India's freedom fighters — and the Netflix original *Deepa & Anoop*, which won four Kidscreen Awards in 2023: Best New Series, Best Inclusivity, Best Music and Best Voice Talent.

**What did Munjal Shroff study?**
He is a computer engineer with a specialisation in image processing, according to AnimationXpress's industry profile of him. That engineering background shaped his producer's perspective on filmmaking, including directing and producing TV commercials, short films and animated features.

**What is Shroff's role at FICCI?**
He was appointed chairman of FICCI's AVGC-XR Forum (animation, VFX, gaming, comics and extended reality) for 2025, succeeding Ashish Kulkarni, after serving as co-chair. His official IFFI biography credits him with helping craft the National AVGC-XR policy and setting up the Indian Institute of Creative Technologies.

**How did Shroff get into animation?**
He trained as a computer engineer and began his animation career under Ram Mohan, the pioneer of Indian animation, alongside Tilak Shetty. The two built Graphiti from advertising films into an original-IP studio, starting with TV work like the *Khulja Sim Sim* "Genie" character before creating their own franchises.
