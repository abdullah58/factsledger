## Overview

Megan Thee Stallion, born Megan Jovon Ruth Pete on 15 February 1995, is an American rapper from Houston, Texas, who rose from viral freestyle videos to three Billboard Hot 100 No. 1 singles and three Grammy Awards. Her hits "Savage", remixed with Beyoncé, and "WAP", with [Cardi B](/people/cardi-b/), topped the chart in 2020, and her solo single "Hiss" did the same in 2024, while her independent label Hot Girl Productions made her one of the most prominent artist-owners in hip-hop. She was born in San Antonio, Texas, in the [United States](/places/united-states/), and raised in Houston, where her mother, rapper Holly Thomas, performed as Holly-Wood and brought her young daughter to recording sessions ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)).

All three of her Hot 100 No. 1 singles arrived within four years, and two of them — "WAP" and "Hiss" — also topped Billboard's global chart, giving her a Billboard relevance that runs from the first week of the Global 200 to the independent era of "Hiss" ([Music Ally](https://musically.com/2020/09/15/billboard-and-mrc-launch-two-new-global-music-charts/); [Billboard](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2024/02/05/megan-thee-stallions-hiss-slithers-onto-billboard-global-200-at-no-1/)). The stage name belongs to Pete alone, taken from men calling her a "stallion" for her height and build from about the age of fifteen ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)).

## Early life and breakthrough

Pete began rapping as a teenager, but her mother convinced her to wait until she was at least twenty-one before pursuing music as a career; she enrolled at Prairie View A&M University after graduating from high school in 2013 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)). While a student there she posted Instagram freestyle videos, including cipher battles, and the clips drew wide attention ([GRAMMY.com](https://www.grammy.com/news/megan-thee-stallion-2021-grammys/)). Her first single, "Like a Stallion", appeared on SoundCloud in 2016, followed by the EP *Make It Hot* (2017) ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)). She later transferred to Texas Southern University. In 2018 she became the first female artist to sign with 1501 Certified Entertainment, a Houston independent label, then signed with 300 Entertainment in [New York City](/places/new-york-city/) ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)). *Tina Snow* followed, and its single "Big Ole Freak" (2019) was her breakout hit and first Hot 100 entry, peaking at No. 65 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)). Her mother, her manager, died of brain cancer in 2019; her grandmother died the same year ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion); [GRAMMY.com](https://www.grammy.com/news/megan-thee-stallion-2021-grammys/)).

## Chart records and Billboard relevance

*Fever* (2019) and the single "Hot Girl Summer", featuring [Nicki Minaj](/people/nicki-minaj/) and Ty Dolla $ign, peaked at No. 11 on the Hot 100, and she trademarked its title in 2022 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)). Her 2020 EP *Suga* produced the Beyoncé remix of "Savage", which topped the Hot 100 and won two 2021 Grammys ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion); [GRAMMY.com](https://www.grammy.com/news/megan-thee-stallion-2021-grammys/)). "WAP" with Cardi B also reached No. 1 on the Hot 100 and topped the first Global 200 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion); [Music Ally](https://musically.com/2020/09/15/billboard-and-mrc-launch-two-new-global-music-charts/)). *Good News*, her debut album, reached No. 2 on the Billboard album chart, and she won Top Rap Female Artist at the [Billboard Music Awards](/events/billboard-music-awards/) in 2021 and 2022 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion); [Essence](https://www.essence.com/awards-events/megan-thee-stallion-bbma-2022/)). "Hiss" (January 2024) debuted at No. 1 on the Hot 100, her third No. 1 and first solo, and opened at No. 1 on the Global 200 on 39.6 million streams and 99,000 sold in week one, making her the first female rapper in a lead role to debut atop that chart ([Billboard](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2024/02/05/megan-thee-stallions-hiss-slithers-onto-billboard-global-200-at-no-1/)).

## Independence: Hot Girl Productions and the Warner deal

The independence arc began in conflict. In 2020 she entered a series of legal battles with 1501 Certified Entertainment, alleging the label refused to renegotiate or end a contract she viewed as unfair while blocking her releases ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)). She won two lawsuits, in 2020 and 2021, allowing her to release *Suga*; a judge granted her permission to put it out, originally planned for her late mother's birthday ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion); [GRAMMY.com](https://www.grammy.com/news/megan-thee-stallion-2021-grammys/)). After three years of litigation the sides settled, and she founded her own label, Hot Girl Productions ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion); [Warner Music Group](https://www.wmg.com/news/megan-thee-stallion-warner-music-group-announce-innovative-agreement-to-enable-the-houston-native-to-maintain-independence-while-working-with-major-music-companys-global-resources)). Her first solo releases through it were "Cobra" (November 2023) and "Hiss" (January 2024) ([Warner Music Group](https://www.wmg.com/news/megan-thee-stallion-warner-music-group-announce-innovative-agreement-to-enable-the-houston-native-to-maintain-independence-while-working-with-major-music-companys-global-resources)). On 2 February 2024 she signed with Warner Music Group, keeping full ownership of her masters and publishing and full creative control, with Warner providing distribution and marketing ([Warner Music Group](https://www.wmg.com/news/megan-thee-stallion-warner-music-group-announce-innovative-agreement-to-enable-the-houston-native-to-maintain-independence-while-working-with-major-music-companys-global-resources)). Ownership stayed with the artist, and her 2019 Roc Nation management deal remained in place ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)).

## The shooting, the trial and public advocacy

In July 2020, Megan Thee Stallion was shot in the feet by fellow rapper Tory Lanez in the Hollywood Hills; she testified he fired at her feet as she walked away; surgery removed bullet fragments ([Associated Press](https://www-jamaicaobserver-com.nproxy.org/2022/12/23/tory-lanez-convicted-in-megan-thee-stallions-shooting/); [Associated Press](https://www.tmj4.com/tory-lanez-gets-10-years-in-prison-for-shooting-megan-thee-stallion)). She answered in a *New York Times* guest essay: "Black women are so unprotected… this is my real life" ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)). On 23 December 2022 a Los Angeles jury found Lanez guilty of three felonies — assault with a semiautomatic firearm, having a loaded, unregistered firearm in a vehicle, and discharging a firearm with gross negligence ([Associated Press](https://www-jamaicaobserver-com.nproxy.org/2022/12/23/tory-lanez-convicted-in-megan-thee-stallions-shooting/)). The defence blamed her friend Kelsey Harris, who denied it and named Lanez as the gunman ([Associated Press](https://www-jamaicaobserver-com.nproxy.org/2022/12/23/tory-lanez-convicted-in-megan-thee-stallions-shooting/)). On 8 August 2023 Judge David Herriford sentenced Lanez to 10 years in prison; his lawyers planned to appeal ([Associated Press](https://www.tmj4.com/tory-lanez-gets-10-years-in-prison-for-shooting-megan-thee-stallion)). She told the court: "Since I was viciously shot by the defendant, I have not experienced a single day of peace" ([Associated Press](https://www.tmj4.com/tory-lanez-gets-10-years-in-prison-for-shooting-megan-thee-stallion)).

## Philanthropy, education and later work

She kept studying at Texas Southern and received her health administration degree in 2021 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)). In 2022 she launched the Pete and Thomas Foundation, named for her late parents, supporting women, children, seniors and underserved communities in education, housing and health and wellness ([GlobalGrind](https://globalgrind.com/playlist/meg-fhu-campaign/); [Broadway.com](https://www.broadway.com/buzz/stars/megan-thee-stallion/profile/)). In September 2023 she teamed with Frito-Lay's Flamin' Hot on Flamin' Hot University, funding a $150,000 Texas Southern scholarship, plus $100,000 to her foundation, and launched "Bad Bitches Have Bad Days Too", a site linking users to free therapy ([GlobalGrind](https://globalgrind.com/playlist/meg-fhu-campaign/); [Entertainment Tonight](https://www.etonline.com/megan-thee-stallion-becomes-the-first-black-woman-to-cover-forbes-30-under-30-195145)). Beyond music she played a fictionalised version of herself in Marvel's *She-Hulk: Attorney at Law* and appeared in the 2024 documentary *Megan Thee Stallion: In Her Words* ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)). In 2024 she launched the 31-city Hot Girl Summer Tour, her first headlining tour, with GloRilla ([Live Nation](https://www.313presents.com/news/detail/megan-thee-stallion-announces-long-awaited-hot-girl-summer-global-tour-at-little-caesars-arena-saturday-may-18)). In November 2022 she became the first Black woman to cover *Forbes'* 30 Under 30; *Forbes* estimated her 2022 earnings at $13 million ([Entertainment Tonight](https://www.etonline.com/megan-thee-stallion-becomes-the-first-black-woman-to-cover-forbes-30-under-30-195145)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is Megan Thee Stallion?**
Megan Thee Stallion, born Megan Jovon Ruth Pete on 15 February 1995, is an American rapper from Houston, Texas, known for "Savage", "WAP" and "Hiss". She has won three Grammy Awards, scored three Billboard Hot 100 No. 1 singles, and releases music through her own label, Hot Girl Productions ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion)).

**How many No. 1 singles does Megan Thee Stallion have?**
She has three Billboard Hot 100 No. 1 singles: the "Savage" remix with Beyoncé and "WAP" with Cardi B, both in 2020, and "Hiss" in 2024, her first solo No. 1. "WAP" also topped the first-ever Billboard Global 200, and "Hiss" debuted at No. 1 on that chart ([Music Ally](https://musically.com/2020/09/15/billboard-and-mrc-launch-two-new-global-music-charts/); [Billboard](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2024/02/05/megan-thee-stallions-hiss-slithers-onto-billboard-global-200-at-no-1/)).

**What Grammys has Megan Thee Stallion won?**
She won three Grammys at the 2021 ceremony: Best New Artist, and Best Rap Song and Best Rap Performance for the "Savage" remix with Beyoncé. She had four nominations that year, including Record of the Year for the same remix ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Megan-Thee-Stallion); [GRAMMY.com](https://www.grammy.com/news/megan-thee-stallion-2021-grammys/)).

**What happened in the Tory Lanez trial?**
In December 2022 a Los Angeles jury found rapper Tory Lanez guilty of three felonies for shooting Megan Thee Stallion in the feet in July 2020. In August 2023 Judge David Herriford sentenced him to 10 years in prison, after denying a defence motion for a new trial ([Associated Press](https://www-jamaicaobserver-com.nproxy.org/2022/12/23/tory-lanez-convicted-in-megan-thee-stallions-shooting/); [Associated Press](https://www.tmj4.com/tory-lanez-gets-10-years-in-prison-for-shooting-megan-thee-stallion)).

**Does Megan Thee Stallion own her own label?**
