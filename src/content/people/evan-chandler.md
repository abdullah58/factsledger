**Evan Chandler was a Beverly Hills dentist and aspiring Hollywood screenwriter who, as the father of 13-year-old Jordan Chandler, made the 1993 accusation of sexual abuse against Michael Jackson — the accusation that triggered a police investigation, a civil lawsuit and a 1994 financial settlement, and whose reported terms later forced the 2026 biopic *Michael* to scrap its entire third act and reshoot it** ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/entertainment/hollywood/michael-jackson-biopic-reshoots-15-million-usd-remove-accusations-eyes-55-million-usd-opening-weekend-10626224/); [Fox News](https://foxnews.com/entertainment/michael-jackson-biopic-ends-controversial-chapter-happened-next)). **Before the case made his name, Chandler had co-written with screenwriter J. David Shapiro the screenplay that became Mel Brooks's 1993 comedy *Robin Hood: Men in Tights* — an idea his 11-year-old son had jokingly suggested after watching Kevin Costner's *Robin Hood: Prince of Thieves*** ([Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/entertainment/movies/11-dashing-facts-about-robin-hood-men-tights)). **He died in November 2009 in Jersey City, New Jersey, at 65; police ruled the death a suicide** ([ANI via Filmibeat](https://www.filmibeat.com/music/international/2009/mjs-victim-father-suicide-181109.html)).

Chandler's accusation marked what Fox News called a turning point in Jackson's life and public image: the case became one of the most widely reported celebrity scandals of the 1990s ([Fox News](https://foxnews.com/entertainment/michael-jackson-biopic-ends-controversial-chapter-happened-next)). Reporting describes the sequence this way: Chandler alleged that Jackson had sexually abused his son; an initial investigation found no evidence against Jackson; the family reached a financial settlement in early 1994; and Jackson's legal team consistently maintained the settlement was not an admission of guilt ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/entertainment/hollywood/michael-jackson-biopic-reshoots-15-million-usd-remove-accusations-eyes-55-million-usd-opening-weekend-10626224/)). Jackson was never charged in the case ([Macau Daily Times](https://macaudailytimes.com.mo/1993-michael-jackson-accused-of-child-abuse-3.html)).

## The dentist who talked his way into a Mel Brooks movie

Before 1993 made his name synonymous with the Jackson case, Chandler lived a different Hollywood story. When 11-year-old Jordi Chandler saw Kevin Costner's *Robin Hood: Prince of Thieves* (1991), he told his father the film was so bad it deserved to be parodied — and his father was unusually well placed to act on the joke ([Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/entertainment/movies/11-dashing-facts-about-robin-hood-men-tights)). Evan Chandler was a Beverly Hills dentist, and one of his patients was Hollywood screenwriter J. David Shapiro. During an appointment, Chandler pitched the idea of a *Robin Hood* spoof; Shapiro loved the concept, and together they put together a screenplay that was later sold to — and heavily revised by — Mel Brooks ([Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/entertainment/movies/11-dashing-facts-about-robin-hood-men-tights)). That script became *Robin Hood: Men in Tights* (1993), Brooks's Sherwood Forest spoof starring Cary Elwes — the film that introduced a young Dave Chappelle to mainstream audiences ([Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/entertainment/movies/11-dashing-facts-about-robin-hood-men-tights)). Within the same calendar year, the father-son joke that launched a studio comedy was eclipsed by the accusation that defined Chandler's public identity.

## The 1993 accusation and the settlement

In August 1993, Los Angeles police began investigating child-abuse allegations against Jackson: two of his homes were searched, and detectives interviewed the boy and other youngsters who visited his Neverland Ranch in Santa Barbara — including 12-year-old *Home Alone* star Macaulay Culkin ([Macau Daily Times](https://macaudailytimes.com.mo/1993-michael-jackson-accused-of-child-abuse-3.html)). Jackson's lawyer Harold Weitzman said he was confident the investigation would show no wrongdoing, while Jackson's security adviser Anthony Pellicano called the allegation part of a $20 million extortion plot ([Macau Daily Times](https://macaudailytimes.com.mo/1993-michael-jackson-accused-of-child-abuse-3.html)). Reporting identifies the accuser as the father of 13-year-old Jordie Chandler; Jackson denied the accusations and was never charged ([Macau Daily Times](https://macaudailytimes.com.mo/1993-michael-jackson-accused-of-child-abuse-3.html); [The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/entertainment/hollywood/michael-jackson-biopic-reshoots-15-million-usd-remove-accusations-eyes-55-million-usd-opening-weekend-10626224/)). The civil case was settled out of court in early 1994 — and the fully-read outlets that name a figure disagree on it:

## Later lawsuits, death, and the settlement's long shadow

Chandler went back to court once more. In 1996 he brought a claim for around $60 million, alleging Jackson had breached the agreement never to discuss the case; in 1999 a court ruled in Jackson's favour and threw the lawsuit out ([Macau Daily Times](https://macaudailytimes.com.mo/1993-michael-jackson-accused-of-child-abuse-3.html)). Jackson himself was tried on separate child-molestation charges in 2005 and acquitted on all 14 counts ([Fox News](https://foxnews.com/entertainment/michael-jackson-biopic-ends-controversial-chapter-happened-next)).

In November 2009 — five months after Jackson's death — Chandler was found dead in bed by a concierge in his luxury waterfront apartment in Jersey City, New Jersey, the gun still in his hand ([ANI via Filmibeat](https://www.filmibeat.com/music/international/2009/mjs-victim-father-suicide-181109.html)). He was 65: a single gunshot wound to the head, no note, and officers found medication consistent with a serious medical condition; police ruled the death a suicide ([ANI via Filmibeat](https://www.filmibeat.com/music/international/2009/mjs-victim-father-suicide-181109.html)). Sixteen years later, the settlement he was party to as Jordan's father resurfaced as the legal hinge of one of Hollywood's costliest course corrections, when estate attorneys discovered the reported depiction-bar clause only after *Michael*'s third act had been shot ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/entertainment/hollywood/michael-jackson-biopic-reshoots-15-million-usd-remove-accusations-eyes-55-million-usd-opening-weekend-10626224/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Was Evan Chandler really a screenwriter?**
He was an aspiring one with a real credit. Chandler, a Beverly Hills dentist, pitched a *Robin Hood* spoof to his patient, screenwriter J. David Shapiro, during a dental appointment; the two wrote a screenplay together that was sold to and heavily revised by Mel Brooks, becoming the 1993 comedy *Robin Hood: Men in Tights* (Mental Floss).

**What did Evan Chandler accuse Michael Jackson of?**
Reporting states that in 1993 Chandler alleged Jackson had sexually abused his then-13-year-old son Jordan. An initial investigation found no evidence against Jackson, the family reached a financial settlement in early 1994, and Jackson's legal team maintained it was not an admission of guilt (The Indian Express).

**Was Michael Jackson ever charged in the 1993 case?**
No. The BBC's contemporaneous report notes Jackson denied the accusations — made by the father of 13-year-old Jordie Chandler — and was never charged; the civil case was settled out of court (Macau Daily Times, reprinting the BBC).

**How did Evan Chandler die?**
In November 2009 he was found dead in his Jersey City, New Jersey, apartment at 65 — a single gunshot wound to the head, the gun still in his hand, no note. Officers found medication consistent with a serious medical condition, and police ruled the death a suicide (ANI via Filmibeat).

**What happened to Chandler's 1996 lawsuit against Jackson?**
He brought a claim for around $60 million alleging Jackson had breached the agreement never to discuss the case; in 1999 a court ruled in Jackson's favour and threw the lawsuit out (Macau Daily Times, reprinting the BBC).
