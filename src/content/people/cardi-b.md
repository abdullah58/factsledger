## Overview

Cardi B — born Belcalis Marlenis Almánzar on 11 October 1992 in the Bronx, [New York City](/places/new-york-city/) — is an American rapper who went from viral social-media posts and VH1 reality television to becoming the female rapper with the most Billboard Hot 100 No. 1 singles, five in total ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). Her debut album *Invasion of Privacy* (2018) won Best Rap Album at the 2019 Grammys, making her the first solo female rapper to win that award, and her second album, *Am I the Drama?* (2025), debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 seven years later ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B); [People](https://people.com/cardi-b-says-fan-complaints-make-her-want-to-take-the-summer-off-12058330)).

Her stage name comes from Bacardi, the rum brand: her younger sister Hennessy was named after a cognac, and Almánzar was called Bacardi to match before shortening it to Cardi B ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). She is the daughter of a Trinidadian mother and a Dominican father, attended the Renaissance High School for Musical Theater and Technology in the Bronx, and worked at a deli and then as a stripper from age 19, a job she took to earn money quickly and leave an abusive relationship ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). That unfiltered, Bronx-rooted persona — first built on Instagram and Vine clips — became the engine of her music career rather than something she left behind ([Grammy.com](https://iq.grammy.com/news/cardi-b-invasion-of-privacy-album-anniversary-2019-grammys-win-bodak-yellow-i-like-it-impact-legacy)).

## From social media to Atlantic Records

Cardi B's rise ran through reality television and self-released mixtapes rather than a traditional label development deal. She joined the cast of VH1's *Love & Hip Hop: New York* in 2015, stayed for two seasons through early 2017, and left to concentrate on music, while her candid social-media posts about her life kept going viral ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). Her musical debut came in 2015 on Shaggy's "Boom Boom" with Popcaan, followed by her first mixtape, *Gangsta Bitch Music Vol. 1*, in 2016 and *Vol. 2* in 2017, which featured Offset of Migos on "Lick" ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). Atlantic Records signed her just one month after *Vol. 2*, a speed that reflected how much demand the free mixtapes had already built ([Grammy.com](https://iq.grammy.com/news/cardi-b-invasion-of-privacy-album-anniversary-2019-grammys-win-bodak-yellow-i-like-it-impact-legacy)).

Her major-label debut single, "Bodak Yellow", arrived in June 2017 and reached No. 1 on the Hot 100 and the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop charts, with The New York Times calling it "the rap anthem of the summer" ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). It made her the second female rapper ever to top the Hot 100 with a solo song, after [Lauryn Hill](/people/lauryn-hill/) in 1998, and the first artist of Dominican descent to earn a Hot 100 No. 1 since the chart began in 1958 ([Grammy.com](https://iq.grammy.com/news/cardi-b-invasion-of-privacy-album-anniversary-2019-grammys-win-bodak-yellow-i-like-it-impact-legacy)). Collaborations with G-Eazy on "No Limit" and Migos on "MotorSport" with [Nicki Minaj](/people/nicki-minaj/) followed, and she became the first woman to place five singles in the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop top 10 at the same time ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)).

## Chart records: five Hot 100 No. 1s

*Invasion of Privacy*, released on 6 April 2018, turned the breakout into a durable chart run. Its 13 tracks included "I Like It" with Bad Bunny and J Balvin, which reached No. 1 on the Hot 100 and made Cardi B the first female rapper with two chart-toppers ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). The album earned five Grammy nominations in 2019, including Record of the Year for "I Like It", and won Best Rap Album — the first win by a solo female rapper in the category ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). By March 2022 it had become the first album by a female artist to have every track certified Platinum or higher by the [RIAA](/organizations/riaa/) ([Grammy.com](https://iq.grammy.com/news/cardi-b-invasion-of-privacy-album-anniversary-2019-grammys-win-bodak-yellow-i-like-it-impact-legacy)). She also made her feature-film debut in *Hustlers* in 2019, playing a stripper alongside Jennifer Lopez and Constance Wu ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)).

The "WAP" numbers carry the clearest measure of her commercial peak: released on 7 August 2020, the song drew 93 million [US](/places/united-states/) streams in its first week, the most for any track ever in that span, passing Ariana Grande's 85.3 million for "7 Rings" ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/cardi-b-megan-thee-stallion-wap-record-first-week-streams-billboard-2730965)). The implication is direct — a female rap collaboration set the all-genre streaming record, not just a genre one. A month later "WAP" also topped the first Billboard Global 200, the new chart ranking songs across 200-plus territories, while Maluma's "Hawái" led the companion Global Excl. U.S. chart ([Music Ally](https://musically.com/2020/09/15/billboard-and-mrc-launch-two-new-global-music-charts/)). "Up" then reached No. 1 on the 27 March 2021 chart, extending her record for the most Hot 100 No. 1s among female rappers to five ([Billboard](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2021/04/30/cardi-b-flexes-her-5-billboard-hot-100-no-1s-in-dj-khaled-collab-big-paper/)).

Her awards run matched the chart run. At the 2019 [Billboard Music Awards](/events/billboard-music-awards/) she won six awards — Top Rap Female Artist, Top Hot 100 Song, Top Selling Song, Top Radio Song, Top Collaboration and Top Rap Song — with "Girls Like You" taking the four song categories and "I Like It" taking Top Rap Song ([Billboard Music Awards](http://www.billboardmusicawards.com/2019/05/winners-announced-for-the-2019-bbmas/)). Atlantic Records puts her career tally at over 100 million RIAA-certified units with three diamond certifications, eight Billboard Music Awards in total, six Guinness World Records, and Billboard's 2020 Woman of the Year honour ([Atlantic Records](https://press.atlanticrecords.com/sites/g/files/g2000014001/files/2025-10/Cardi%20B%20-%20Am%20I%20The%20Drama%20Release%20-%209.19.25%20%28internal%29.docx.pdf)).

## The seven-year gap and Am I the Drama?

After *Invasion of Privacy*, Cardi B released singles and collaborations for years without a second album, and the wait itself became part of the story. She released "WAP" in 2020 and "Up" in 2021, both Hot 100 No. 1s, while her personal life stayed in public view: she filed for divorce from Offset in 2024 while pregnant with their third child, Blossom, who was born later that year ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). In 2020 she was sued by a security guard over a 2018 incident, and days before her second album's release a jury found her not liable ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). She also announced she was expecting a baby with her boyfriend, football player Stefon Diggs of the New England Patriots ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)).

*Am I the Drama?* finally arrived on 19 September 2025, seven years after her debut, as a 23-track album featuring Janet Jackson, Lizzo, Selena Gomez and Megan Thee Stallion, among others ([People](https://people.com/cardi-b-says-fan-complaints-make-her-want-to-take-the-summer-off-12058330)). It debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with 200,000 equivalent album units in its first week, making her the only female rapper whose first two albums both debuted atop the chart ([Billboard](https://rapindustry.com/cardi-b-scores-second-no-1-on-billboard-200-with-am-i-the-drama/)). The first week broke down as 110,000 streaming-equivalent units — 145.72 million on-demand streams — plus 88,000 album sales and 2,000 track-equivalent units, the biggest week of 2025 for an R&B/hip-hop album by a woman ([Billboard](https://rapindustry.com/cardi-b-scores-second-no-1-on-billboard-200-with-am-i-the-drama/)). She then took the album on the road for her first full headline arena tour, the Little Miss Drama Tour, a 35-date North American run that kicked off on 11 February 2026 in Palm Desert, California, and wrapped on 18 April in Atlanta ([People](https://people.com/cardi-b-says-fan-complaints-make-her-want-to-take-the-summer-off-12058330)). On 31 July 2026 she released the solo single "AH HA", which gave her a 30th career top 10 on Billboard's Hot Rap Songs chart, extending a run of at least one top 10 on that chart in each of the last 10 years ([People](https://people.com/cardi-b-says-fan-complaints-make-her-want-to-take-the-summer-off-12058330)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is Cardi B?**
Cardi B is the stage name of Belcalis Marlenis Almánzar, an American rapper born on 11 October 1992 in the Bronx, New York City ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). She rose from viral social-media posts and VH1's *Love & Hip Hop: New York* to become the female rapper with the most Hot 100 No. 1 singles, five in total ([Billboard](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2021/04/30/cardi-b-flexes-her-5-billboard-hot-100-no-1s-in-dj-khaled-collab-big-paper/)).

**What was Cardi B's breakout song?**
Her breakout single was "Bodak Yellow", released in June 2017 after she signed with Atlantic Records ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). It reached No. 1 on the Hot 100, making her the second female rapper ever to top the chart with a solo song, after Lauryn Hill in 1998 ([Grammy.com](https://iq.grammy.com/news/cardi-b-invasion-of-privacy-album-anniversary-2019-grammys-win-bodak-yellow-i-like-it-impact-legacy)).

**How many Hot 100 No. 1s does Cardi B have?**
She has five: "Bodak Yellow" (2017), "I Like It" (2018), Maroon 5's "Girls Like You" (2018), "WAP" with Megan Thee Stallion (2020) and "Up" (2021) ([Billboard](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2021/04/30/cardi-b-flexes-her-5-billboard-hot-100-no-1s-in-dj-khaled-collab-big-paper/)). That is the most Hot 100 No. 1s of any female rapper, and "Up" made her the first female rapper with two solo No. 1s.

**Did Cardi B win a Grammy?**
Yes. Her debut album *Invasion of Privacy* won Best Rap Album at the 2019 Grammys, making her the first solo female rapper to win the award ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Cardi-B)). The album had five nominations that year, including Record of the Year for "I Like It" and Album of the Year.

**When did Cardi B release her second album?**
*Am I the Drama?* was released on 19 September 2025, seven years after her debut ([People](https://people.com/cardi-b-says-fan-complaints-make-her-want-to-take-the-summer-off-12058330)). It debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with 200,000 equivalent album units, and she supported it with her first headline arena tour in 2026 ([Billboard](https://rapindustry.com/cardi-b-scores-second-no-1-on-billboard-200-with-am-i-the-drama/)).
