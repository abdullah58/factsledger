Guy Verhofstadt is a Belgian liberal politician who served as prime minister of Belgium from 1999 to 2008 — the first liberal to hold that office since 1938 — and later became one of the most prominent liberal voices in the European Parliament. [FactMonster](https://www.factmonster.com/encyclopedia/history/modern-europe/benelux-bios/verhofstadt-guy-maurice-marielouise) He is best known internationally for his two European-level roles: the Parliament's Brexit negotiator and, from 2009 to 2019, leader of its liberal group. [FactMonster](https://www.factmonster.com/encyclopedia/history/modern-europe/benelux-bios/verhofstadt-guy-maurice-marielouise)

## From Belgian politics to the premiership

Verhofstadt was born in 1953 and trained as a lawyer before entering politics. [FactMonster](https://www.factmonster.com/encyclopedia/history/modern-europe/benelux-bios/verhofstadt-guy-maurice-marielouise) He was first elected to the Belgian parliament in 1978, became head of the youth wing of the Flemish liberal Party for Freedom and Progress (PVV) in 1979, and served as the party's president in three separate stints: 1982–85, 1989–95, and 1997–99. [FactMonster](https://www.factmonster.com/encyclopedia/history/modern-europe/benelux-bios/verhofstadt-guy-maurice-marielouise) From 1985 to 1992 he was deputy prime minister and budget minister under Prime Minister Wilfried Martens. [FactMonster](https://www.factmonster.com/encyclopedia/history/modern-europe/benelux-bios/verhofstadt-guy-maurice-marielouise)

He became prime minister in 1999, breaking a run of non-liberal premierships stretching back to 1938, and stayed in the office until 2008, including a period heading an interim government in 2007–08. [FactMonster](https://www.factmonster.com/encyclopedia/history/modern-europe/benelux-bios/verhofstadt-guy-maurice-marielouise) After leaving national office he moved to Brussels, becoming a member of the European Parliament in 2009 and leader of the Alliance of Liberals and Democrats for Europe (ALDE) group, a post he held until 2019. [FactMonster](https://www.factmonster.com/encyclopedia/history/modern-europe/benelux-bios/verhofstadt-guy-maurice-marielouise) In 2017 he published *Europe's Last Chance*, a book on the future of the European Union. [FactMonster](https://www.factmonster.com/encyclopedia/history/modern-europe/benelux-bios/verhofstadt-guy-maurice-marielouise)

## On Russia's entry blacklist

In May 2015 Russia handed a list of 89 European politicians barred from entering the country to several EU embassies, in response to EU sanctions over the annexation of Crimea and the conflict in eastern Ukraine. [Tengrinews](https://en.tengrinews.kz/politics_sub/russia-issues-89-name-eu-blacklist-bars-politicians-260559/) Verhofstadt, then head of the Liberal group in the European Parliament and a former Belgian prime minister, was on it. [Tengrinews](https://en.tengrinews.kz/politics_sub/russia-issues-89-name-eu-blacklist-bars-politicians-260559/)

What set his listing apart was the trigger his own office gave. His spokesman, Jeroen Reijnen, told AFP that the ban followed Verhofstadt's call for an independent international probe into the murder of Russian opposition leader Boris Nemtsov — a personal confrontation with Moscow over a killing, not a generic sanctions tit-for-tat. [Tengrinews](https://en.tengrinews.kz/politics_sub/russia-issues-89-name-eu-blacklist-bars-politicians-260559/) The Dutch government rejected the move outright, and Russia's list was described by Dutch Prime Minister Mark Rutte as not based on international law, not transparent, and not challengeable in court. [Tengrinews](https://en.tengrinews.kz/politics_sub/russia-issues-89-name-eu-blacklist-bars-politicians-260559/)

## The European Parliament's Brexit man

On 8 September 2016, the European Parliament's Conference of Presidents appointed Verhofstadt as its point man for the Brexit negotiations, making him the parliamentary counterpart to the European Commission's negotiator Michel Barnier. [EU Reporter](https://www.eureporter.co/frontpage/2016/09/08/parliament-appoints-guyverhofstadt-as-representative-on-brexit-matters/) His brief was to keep the conference of presidents and group leaders informed and to help prepare the Parliament's negotiating position, working with the chair of the Constitutional Affairs Committee once Article 50 was triggered. [EU Reporter](https://www.eureporter.co/frontpage/2016/09/08/parliament-appoints-guyverhofstadt-as-representative-on-brexit-matters/)

He also chaired the Brexit Steering Group, the body that shaped the Parliament's stance on the withdrawal agreement. [EU Reporter](https://www.eureporter.co/frontpage/2018/10/17/brexit-steering-group-insists-on-need-for-irelandnorthern-ireland-backstop/) In October 2018, for example, the group — listed as chaired by "Guy Verhofstadt, Brexit co-ordinator and Chair" — insisted that any agreement include a workable, legally binding backstop for the Ireland–Northern Ireland border and warned that without one the Parliament could not give its consent to the withdrawal deal. [EU Reporter](https://www.eureporter.co/frontpage/2018/10/17/brexit-steering-group-insists-on-need-for-irelandnorthern-ireland-backstop/)

He arrived at the Brexit role with negotiating experience: earlier in 2016 he had led the Parliament's negotiations on the inter-institutional agreement on Better Law Making, adopted by the Parliament on 9 March 2016. [EU Reporter](https://www.eureporter.co/frontpage/2016/09/08/parliament-appoints-guyverhofstadt-as-representative-on-brexit-matters/)

## The 2024 call to sanction Tucker Carlson

In February 2024, after [Tucker Carlson](/people/tucker-carlson) interviewed Russian president Vladimir Putin, Verhofstadt posted on social media that the European Union "should explore a travel ban" for Carlson if he "enables disinformation" for Putin. [Jordan Times](http://jordantimes.com/news/world/%E2%80%98no-discussions%E2%80%99-eu-sanctions-against-tucker-carlson) The idea went nowhere. EU spokesman Peter Stano said there had been "no discussions in the relevant EU bodies" on sanctioning Carlson, and noted that sanctions require unanimity among all 27 member states — a bar no member state had moved to meet. [Jordan Times](http://jordantimes.com/news/world/%E2%80%98no-discussions%E2%80%99-eu-sanctions-against-tucker-carlson) The episode nevertheless drew attention beyond Brussels: Elon Musk amplified the speculation to his more than 171 million followers, which drew the formal denial. [Jordan Times](http://jordantimes.com/news/world/%E2%80%98no-discussions%E2%80%99-eu-sanctions-against-tucker-carlson)

## President of the European Movement International

After his long European Parliament career, Verhofstadt was elected president of the European Movement International, the long-standing pro-European network that predates the European Union itself. [European Movement International](http://europeanmovement.eu/publication-articles/guy-verhofstadt-mep-is-the-new-president-of-the-european-movement-international/) Its members elected a new board alongside him for a three-year mandate, bringing together representatives of political parties, social partners, the environmental movement, youth organizations, and civil society groups from across Europe. [European Movement International](http://europeanmovement.eu/publication-articles/guy-verhofstadt-mep-is-the-new-president-of-the-european-movement-international/) The organization, founded 75 years ago, counts more than 70 member organizations across 30 countries. [European Movement International](http://europeanmovement.eu/publication-articles/guy-verhofstadt-mep-is-the-new-president-of-the-european-movement-international/)
