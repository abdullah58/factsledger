## Overview

Alexis Ohanian is a tech founder and venture capitalist best known as the co-founder of [Reddit](/organizations/reddit/) and the founder of the venture firm [Seven Seven Six](/organizations/seven-seven-six/). He co-founded Reddit out of college with Y Combinator funding in 2005, sold it to Condé Nast in 2006, returned as executive chairman in 2014, and resigned from its board in June 2020 — then built a second investing career that led [Alt](/organizations/alt/)'s Series A and moved into women's sports ownership ([UTA Speakers](https://www.utaspeakers.com/speaker/alexis-ohanian); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/06/10/reddit-names-ycs-michael-seibel-to-board-following-co-founder-alexis-ohanians-exit/)).

Ohanian was born in Brooklyn, New York, and grew up in the suburbs of Baltimore before earning a B.A. in History and a B.S. in Commerce from the University of Virginia ([UTA Speakers](https://www.utaspeakers.com/speaker/alexis-ohanian)). He wrote the national bestselling book *Without Their Permission* and has campaigned for the open internet, STEM education and paid family leave, including a *New York Times* op-ed arguing that paternity leave mattered after the birth of his child ([UTA Speakers](https://www.utaspeakers.com/speaker/alexis-ohanian)).

## Reddit: founding, sale, return, exit

Out of college, Ohanian co-founded Reddit, which was funded by Y Combinator in 2005 and sold to Condé Nast in 2006 ([UTA Speakers](https://www.utaspeakers.com/speaker/alexis-ohanian)). He returned as executive chairman in 2014 to help lead the turnaround of the then-independent company, and stepped away from Reddit's day-to-day involvement in 2018 ([UTA Speakers](https://www.utaspeakers.com/speaker/alexis-ohanian); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/06/26/alexis-ohanian-reddit-co-founder-is-leaving-initialized-capital/)). The arc closed publicly on June 5, 2020, when Ohanian announced he was stepping down from Reddit's board and asked that his seat go to a Black board member, as communities across the United States marched for social justice after the killing of George Floyd by Minneapolis police ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/06/10/reddit-names-ycs-michael-seibel-to-board-following-co-founder-alexis-ohanians-exit/)). Reddit honoured the request five days later by appointing Y Combinator CEO Michael Seibel, who had become Y Combinator's first Black partner in 2014 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/06/10/reddit-names-ycs-michael-seibel-to-board-following-co-founder-alexis-ohanians-exit/)).

## From Initialized Capital to Seven Seven Six

Ohanian's investing career began alongside Reddit's second act. He co-founded Initialized Capital with Garry Tan in 2011, launching with a $7 million vehicle that grew into early-stage bets on Flexport, Instacart, Cruise, [Coinbase](/organizations/coinbase/) and Codecademy ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/06/26/alexis-ohanian-reddit-co-founder-is-leaving-initialized-capital/)). The firm closed a $225 million fourth fund in 2018 and by June 2020 had $500 million in assets under management across more than 200 portfolio companies ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/06/26/alexis-ohanian-reddit-co-founder-is-leaving-initialized-capital/)). That month Ohanian left Initialized to work on "a new project that will support a generation of founders in tech and beyond," remaining involved in its existing vehicles because of legal ties; the firm confirmed his departure did not trigger its key-man clause ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/06/26/alexis-ohanian-reddit-co-founder-is-leaving-initialized-capital/)).

The new project was Seven Seven Six, named for 776 BC, the year of the first Olympics, on the theory that the cook who won its Stadion race was likely a good athlete rather than necessarily the best — a founding story about overlooked talent ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/06/10/seven-seven-six-the-new-venture-firm-from-alexis-ohanian-closes-150-million-fund-1/)). The firm closed its $150 million first fund in June 2021 with Katelin Holloway and Lissie Garvin, both former Initialized and Reddit colleagues, in founding positions ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/06/10/seven-seven-six-the-new-venture-firm-from-alexis-ohanian-closes-150-million-fund-1/)). Equity and inclusion were built into its structure: half of its limited partners were women and 15% were Black or indigenous people, and a 2% "Growth and Caregiving Commitment" set aside extra capital to support founders' personal needs, from childcare to travel ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/06/10/seven-seven-six-the-new-venture-firm-from-alexis-ohanian-closes-150-million-fund-1/)). Alt was among the firm's first 17 investments, and Seven Seven Six led Alt's Series A as part of the $31 million Alt raised across its seed and Series A rounds ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/06/10/seven-seven-six-the-new-venture-firm-from-alexis-ohanian-closes-150-million-fund-1/); [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/startups/alt-raises-31m-to-buy-and-sell-sports-cards/)). Ohanian said he had been energized by the alternative-asset market for some time and was drawn to Alt's ability to make buying and trading alternative assets more structured, efficient and transparent ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/startups/alt-raises-31m-to-buy-and-sell-sports-cards/)).

## Women's sports and philanthropy

Beyond technology, Ohanian became one of the leading investors in women's sports: he is the founding control owner of Angel City Football Club and Los Angeles Golf Club, the founder of the women's track competition ATHLOS, and an investor in Chelsea FC Women ([SXSW London](https://sxswlondon.com/speakers/alexis-ohanian-bbde99d4)). In 2022 he launched the 776 Foundation to support marginalized individuals and announced a $20 million commitment to climate action through its Fellowship Program, which offers young builders a $100,000 grant and network support ([UTA Speakers](https://www.utaspeakers.com/speaker/alexis-ohanian); [SXSW London](https://sxswlondon.com/speakers/alexis-ohanian-bbde99d4)).
