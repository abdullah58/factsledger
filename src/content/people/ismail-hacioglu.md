## Overview

İsmail Hacıoğlu is a Turkish actor whose career turned on physically embodying two of Turkey's cultural icons: the young Süleyman Dilbirliği in [Ayla: The Daughter of War](https://factsledger.com/creative-works/ayla-the-daughter-of-war/) (2017), Turkey's Oscar submission that year, and Cem Karaca in *Cem Karaca'nın Gözyaşları* (2024) — a role for which he sang every song himself and underwent a 3D-scanned prosthetic transformation. Born November 30, 1985, in [Istanbul](/places/istanbul/), he won his first major festival award as a teenager ([Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/ismail_hacioglu)).

## The two lives he embodied

In *Ayla*, directed by [Can Ülkay](/people/can-ulkay/), Hacıoğlu played the young Süleyman Dilbirliği opposite Çetin Tekindor as the elderly one — Turkey's nominee for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film, launched September 8, 2017 with a campaign for the 60th anniversary of Turkish troops landing in Busan ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633)).

In 2024 came the harder embodiment: Cem Karaca in *Cem Karaca'nın Gözyaşları*, directed by Yüksel Aksu. Hacıoğlu sang every song himself and was 3D-scanned for prosthetics instead of silicone molding — described as a Turkish-cinema first. He prepared by meeting Karaca's band, Moğollar ([CafeSiyaset](https://www.cafesiyaset.com/amp/haber/ismail-hacioglu-nasil-cem-karaca-oldu-h43971.html); [Teammy](http://teammy.com/2023/07/02/41899/)).

Released January 26, 2024, the film drew almost 220,000 viewers in three weeks — then a court removed it from theaters after Karaca's widow deposited 3.5 million lira in her copyright suit ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/anatolian-rock-legends-film-removed-from-theaters-190768)). That cut the film's run short just as a biopic's word-of-mouth window typically opens; it later premiered internationally at the Boston Turkish Film Festival in November 2024 ([Boston Turkish Film Festival](https://BOSTONTURKISHFESTIVAL.ORG/competition/2024/films/tears-of-cem-karaca.html)).

## A career built before the biopics

Hacıoğlu was born November 30, 1985, in Istanbul, the son of Mehmet Hacıoğlu, a former Fenerbahçe defender nicknamed "Bulgar Mehmet" — though he himself supports Galatasaray ([Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/celebrity/ismail_hacioglu); [tv100](https://www.tv100.com/ismail-hacioglunun-babasi-bakin-kim-cikti-meger-fenerbahcenin-yildiz-ismiymis-galeri-872649)). He began acting in fifth-grade school plays, trained at the Müjdat Gezen Art Center, entered Mimar Sinan University's State Conservatory theatre department, and left after a year ([kimkimdir.net.tr](http://www.kimkimdir.net.tr/kisiler/ismail-hacioglu)).

His breakthrough came early: Osman in *Karşılaşma* (2003) won him the Promising Young Actor award at the 40th Antalya Golden Orange Film Festival, and *Çakal* (2010) brought Best Actor at the 16th Sadri Alışık Awards ([kimkimdir.net.tr](http://www.kimkimdir.net.tr/kisiler/ismail-hacioglu)). Television made him a household name — *Koçum Benim*, *Bir İstanbul Masalı*, *Osmanlı Tokadı*, *Mehmetçik Kut'ül Amare* — before he co-led Fox TV's *Mahkum* (2021–22) as Barış and Savaş Yesari with Onur Tuna and Seray Kaya ([kimkimdir.net.tr](http://www.kimkimdir.net.tr/kisiler/ismail-hacioglu); [Teammy](http://teammy.com/2024/09/04/48861/)). He also won the 2018 Culture and Art Award and the 2021 Golden Palm for Best Movie Actor ([TV Guide](https://www.tvguide.com/celebrities/ismail-hacioglu/bio/3060225749/)).

## Detention, release, and a new season

On February 17, 2026, Hacıoğlu was detained in an operation run by the Istanbul Chief Public Prosecutor's Office targeting celebrities: arrest warrants named 25 suspects on drug-use, drug-facilitation, and prostitution-related allegations, and 19 people were detained, including singers Murat Dalkılıç and Kaan Tangöze, former boxer Adem Kılıçcı, and influencer Kemal Doğulu ([Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/unlulere-yeni-uyusturucu-operasyonu-43110664)). Police reported seizing 33 grams of marijuana at Hacıoğlu's residence, part of 52.46 grams recovered from 17 suspects in total ([Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/unlulere-yeni-uyusturucu-operasyonu-43110664)). Two days later, on February 19, Hacıoğlu was released by the prosecution after giving statements and providing blood and hair samples, alongside Dalkılıç, Tangöze, and Doğulu ([Posta](https://www.posta.com.tr/magazin/unlulere-uyusturucu-operasyonu-murat-dalkilic-kaan-tangoze-ve-ismail-hacioglu-serbest-birakildi-2966592)). By September 2026 he was back at work: Turkish entertainment press reported him joining the new season of the series *Yeraltı*, alongside Şükran Ovalı, Azra Aksu, and Ekrem Tamer, in a cast led by Deniz Can Aktaş, Devrim Özkan, and Uraz Kaygılaroğlu ([tv100](https://www.tv100.com/ismail-hacioglunun-babasi-bakin-kim-cikti-meger-fenerbahcenin-yildiz-ismiymis-galeri-872649)).

## FAQ

**Who did İsmail Hacıoğlu play in *Ayla: The Daughter of War*?**
He played the young Süleyman Dilbirliği, the Korean War sergeant at the center of the film, with veteran actor Çetin Tekindor playing the elderly Dilbirliği. Directed by Can Ülkay, the film was Turkey's submission for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633)).

**Did İsmail Hacıoğlu really sing in the Cem Karaca film?**
Yes. In *Cem Karaca'nın Gözyaşları* (2024) he performed all of Karaca's songs himself, according to multiple Turkish outlets. He was also 3D face-scanned for his prosthetics — reportedly a Turkish-cinema first. The lead role had first been offered to Haluk Levent and Erdal Beşikçioğlu before going to him ([diziler.com](https://www.diziler.com/haber/cem-karacanin-gozyaslarinin-fragmani-yayinlandi-tum-sarkilari-ismail-hacioglu-seslendirdi-34067); [CafeSiyaset](https://www.cafesiyaset.com/amp/haber/ismail-hacioglu-nasil-cem-karaca-oldu-h43971.html); [Teammy](http://teammy.com/2023/07/02/41899/)).

**Why was *Cem Karaca'nın Gözyaşları* removed from theaters?**
Karaca's widow İlkim Karaca sued, arguing permission had not been obtained for the songs used in the film. After she deposited 3.5 million lira in collateral, the court ordered screenings stopped. The film had drawn almost 220,000 viewers in three weeks; the producers said Karaca's son Emrah held three-fourths of the song rights ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/anatolian-rock-legends-film-removed-from-theaters-190768)).

**Is İsmail Hacıoğlu related to football?**
His father is Mehmet Hacıoğlu, a former Fenerbahçe defender nicknamed "Bulgar Mehmet." The actor himself, however, is a well-known Galatasaray supporter — the cross-Istanbul rivalry inside one family is a recurring subject of Turkish entertainment coverage, most recently in reports on his joining the series *Yeraltı* ([tv100](https://www.tv100.com/ismail-hacioglunun-babasi-bakin-kim-cikti-meger-fenerbahcenin-yildiz-ismiymis-galeri-872649)).

**What are his most recent projects?**
In 2024 he starred in the Cem Karaca biopic and was nominated for Best Actor at the Sadri Alışık Cinema Awards ([TV Guide](https://www.tvguide.com/celebrities/ismail-hacioglu/bio/3060225749/)). After his February 2026 detention and release, he joined the new season of the series *Yeraltı* in September 2026 ([tv100](https://www.tv100.com/ismail-hacioglunun-babasi-bakin-kim-cikti-meger-fenerbahcenin-yildiz-ismiymis-galeri-872649)).
