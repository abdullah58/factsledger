## Overview

Vibhor Kapoor is the operator who became chief executive of NextRoll on February 1, 2026, after four years in senior leadership at the company, succeeding [Roli Saxena](/people/roli-saxena/) as she moved to executive chair and chief strategy officer ([Ad Age via AdRoll](https://www.adroll.com/about/news/people-on-the-move-vibhor-kapoor)). He arrived at the top through the classic enterprise-software route: a decade at Microsoft, including early work on the team that built the Azure business, senior marketing roles at Box and [Adobe](/organizations/adobe/), and then the chief business officer role at NextRoll, where he oversaw marketing and product management for both [AdRoll](/organizations/adroll/) and [RollWorks](/organizations/rollworks/) ([WBResearch](https://digitaltravelconnectus.wbresearch.com/speakers/vibhor-kapoor)). Ad Age's report on his promotion stressed the continuity: he had played a central role in unifying NextRoll's advertising and account-based marketing offerings under the AdRoll brand, evolving the platform into a full-funnel, privacy-forward advertising solution ([Ad Age via AdRoll](https://www.adroll.com/about/news/people-on-the-move-vibhor-kapoor)).

## Microsoft, Box, and Adobe

Kapoor's career, as his conference biography presents it, spans almost three decades in software-as-a-service and cloud platforms ([WBResearch](https://digitaltravelconnectus.wbresearch.com/speakers/vibhor-kapoor)). He spent a decade at Microsoft in leadership roles across marketing and product management, and was an early member of the team that built Microsoft's Azure business — the cloud platform that became one of the company's defining products ([WBResearch](https://digitaltravelconnectus.wbresearch.com/speakers/vibhor-kapoor)). Before joining Adobe, he was a vice president of marketing at Box, leading product marketing, evangelism, and demand generation for its application programming interface platform ([WBResearch](https://digitaltravelconnectus.wbresearch.com/speakers/vibhor-kapoor)). Most recently before NextRoll, he was a senior director in Adobe's Digital Media Business, leading marketing and go-to-market efforts to incubate new categories in experience design and developer platforms ([WBResearch](https://digitaltravelconnectus.wbresearch.com/speakers/vibhor-kapoor)). Earlier still, he spent several years at General Electric and Hewlett-Packard in product management, sales, and commercial operations ([WBResearch](https://digitaltravelconnectus.wbresearch.com/speakers/vibhor-kapoor)). He holds a Master of Business Administration from the Kellogg School of Management at Northwestern University and a Bachelor of Technology from the Indian Institute of Technology in Varanasi, India ([WBResearch](https://digitaltravelconnectus.wbresearch.com/speakers/vibhor-kapoor)).

That sequence — cloud infrastructure, developer platforms, then marketing technology — maps neatly onto NextRoll's own evolution from a retargeting tool to a connected advertising platform. Kapoor's NextRoll remit as chief business officer covered marketing and product management for both of its platforms: AdRoll, the marketing and advertising platform for business-to-consumer businesses, and RollWorks, its business-to-business account-based marketing platform ([WBResearch](https://digitaltravelconnectus.wbresearch.com/speakers/vibhor-kapoor)).

## The unification, then the top job

Kapoor was already the public voice of NextRoll's product story before he became CEO. At the August 27, 2025 brand unification, when RollWorks officially became AdRoll ABM, he argued that advertising had long been held back by disconnected tools and fragmented data, and that a unified brand and connected advertising ecosystem would remove that friction and give marketers a simpler, smarter way to work ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/adroll-unifies-brand-with-ai-assistant-launch-eliminating-rollworks-complexity/)). The unification he helped sell — two core offerings, multi-channel advertising and account-based marketing, under one AdRoll brand, plus a new AI Assistant — was the platform he would then inherit as CEO ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/adroll-unifies-brand-with-ai-assistant-launch-eliminating-rollworks-complexity/)).

The succession itself was framed as operational continuity. NextRoll announced in January 2026 that Kapoor, then chief business officer, would become chief executive effective February 1, 2026, after four years in senior leadership roles at the company, during which he had played the central role in bringing its advertising and account-based marketing capabilities together under the AdRoll brand ([Ad Age via AdRoll](https://www.adroll.com/about/news/people-on-the-move-vibhor-kapoor)). Saxena, who had served as CEO since 2022, moved to executive chair of the board and chief strategy officer, leaving Kapoor to run a company whose brand he had already helped reunify ([Ad Age via AdRoll](https://www.adroll.com/about/news/people-on-the-move-vibhor-kapoor)).
