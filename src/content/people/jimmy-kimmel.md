## Overview

In September 2025, ABC took *Jimmy Kimmel Live!* off the air indefinitely. By December, the same network had signed him to a one-year extension keeping him on air through mid-2027. That whiplash is the core of Kimmel's current profile: the Brooklyn-born comedian who has hosted ABC's late-night show since January 2003 — the longest-serving current late-night host in the United States — became, in the words of the debate his case ignited, a national test of free speech and political pressure on the media ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Jimmy-Kimmel); [AFP](https://nordot.app/1370904608093553273)).

The suspension came days after Kimmel's September 15, 2025 monologue about the assassination of conservative activist Charlie Kirk, in which he said the "MAGA gang" was "desperately trying to characterize this kid who murdered Charlie Kirk as anything other than one of them" ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Jimmy-Kimmel)). After FCC Chair Brendan Carr appeared to threaten the licenses of ABC affiliates broadcasting the show, Nexstar and Sinclair pulled the program from their stations and Disney suspended it nationwide ([AFP](https://nordot.app/1370904608093553273)). Following a public and Hollywood backlash, Kimmel returned September 23 after a week-long hiatus — to about 80% of ABC stations, with Sinclair and Nexstar still holding out — and delivered a monologue calling government attempts to censor him "anti-American" ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Jimmy-Kimmel); [AFP](https://nordot.app/1370904608093553273)).

## The feud goes on

The fight did not end with the extension. In March 2026, Trump called on ABC to fire Kimmel after the comedian said first lady Melania Trump had the "glow" of an expectant widow during a parody State of the Union address ([The Hill](https://www.wcia.com/news/national/abc-denies-jimmy-kimmel-live-ending-report-call-it-inaccurate/)). The conflict also escalated institutionally: the FCC launched an early review of ABC's broadcast licenses, and Disney's lawyers accused the commission in a July 2026 court filing of attempting to censor the network ([The Hill](https://www.wcia.com/news/national/abc-denies-jimmy-kimmel-live-ending-report-call-it-inaccurate/)). That legal escalation is the core of the [ABC News](https://factsledger.com/organizations/abc-news/) dispute Kimmel sits at the center of. In September 2026, when Page Six reported that the ongoing 24th season of *Jimmy Kimmel Live!* would be its last, ABC called the report "inaccurate," adding that "any reports about the future of the show at this point are purely speculative" ([The Hill](https://www.wcia.com/news/national/abc-denies-jimmy-kimmel-live-ending-report-call-it-inaccurate/)).

## The 30-year career underneath

Kimmel's career predates the feud by three decades. Raised in Las Vegas and inspired by David Letterman, he started in radio — joining KROQ's *The Kevin & Bean Show* in 1994 as a producer, then as the on-air "Jimmy the Sports Guy" ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Jimmy-Kimmel)). From 1997 to 2002 he cohosted the Comedy Central game show *Win Ben Stein's Money* with Ben Stein, winning the 1999 Daytime Emmy for outstanding game-show host; in 1999 he also launched *The Man Show* with Adam Carolla, a four-year run whose profits funded their production company Jackhole Industries and its puppet prank-call hit *Crank Yankers* (2002–07) ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Jimmy-Kimmel)). *Jimmy Kimmel Live!* debuted in 2003 in ABC's 12:05am slot, moved to an earlier slot in 2013, and built its audience on segments like "Celebrities Read Mean Tweets" and "This Week in Unnecessary Censorship" ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Jimmy-Kimmel)). Along the way he became the awards-show emcee of a generation: the Oscars in 2017, 2018, 2023, and 2024, and the Emmys in 2012, 2016, and 2020 ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Jimmy-Kimmel)).

## The Daniels chapter

Long before the White House feud, Kimmel was already tangled in Trump's orbit through [Stormy Daniels](https://factsledger.com/people/stormy-daniels/). In 2018 he invited Daniels to his show while her nondisclosure agreement about her alleged affair with Trump was still in effect — and, nodding to her fear of violating it, brought out puppets to reenact her interactions with Trump ([NPR](https://www.wkyufm.org/news/2024-03-19/threats-debt-and-trumps-advances-stormy-doc-examines-the-life-of-stormy-daniels)). He later appeared in the 2024 Peacock documentary *Stormy*, saying of Daniels: "She told the truth and she paid a price for that" ([NPR](https://www.wkyufm.org/news/2024-03-19/threats-debt-and-trumps-advances-stormy-doc-examines-the-life-of-stormy-daniels)).
