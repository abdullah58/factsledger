## Overview

The most surprising thing about Ivanka Trump in 2026 is how publicly she has re-entered the deal-making world — and how little of it shows up in the biographical summaries that still define her. In a slickly produced podcast interview published in early June 2026, she pitched her latest real-estate project herself: what she called an "unbelievable, beautiful 1,400-hectare private island in the middle of the Mediterranean," plus five miles of Albanian beachfront meant to become resorts and hotels ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/06/europe/ivanka-trump-kushner-luxury-resort-albania-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). Within days of that interview, thousands of Albanians were marching through Tirana carrying pink cardboard flamingos, chanting that their country was not for sale, over the very coastline she had been praising ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/06/europe/ivanka-trump-kushner-luxury-resort-albania-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

She is the eldest daughter of U.S. President Donald Trump and his first wife, Ivana Trump, born Ivanka Marie Trump on October 30, 1981, in Manhattan ([Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/people/ivanka-trump-680888/biography)). Before she was a political figure she was a Trump Organization executive, a fashion-brand founder, a reality-TV presence, and an author; from 2017 to 2021 she served as an unpaid senior adviser in her father's first [White House](https://factsledger.com/organizations/the-white-house) ([Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/people/ivanka-trump-680888/biography); [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-usa-trump-ivanka-idUSKBN1KE2JN/)). She announced in 2022 that she was stepping away from politics — and has since rebuilt her public life around private business instead ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/diplomacy/article/3368740/ivanka-trump-takes-front-row-seat-welcome-xi-dress-chinese-designer-guo-pei)).

## The Albania project: Sazan Island and the flamingo protests

The project has two parts. The first is Sazan Island itself — uninhabited and a former communist-era military base. The second is an undeveloped stretch of beach across the water called Pishë Poro–Narta, which sits inside a protected nature area, the Vjosa–Narta Protected Landscape, home to endangered monk seals, nesting sea turtles, and more than 200 bird species, including flamingos and pelicans ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/06/europe/ivanka-trump-kushner-luxury-resort-albania-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). Telling the podcast's host, David Senra, how she and her husband Jared Kushner first found the site, Ivanka said: "We were on a friend's boat, and we stopped for a swim, effectively, that's how we found it. We swam to the islands, we went on a hike barefoot all the way up to the top, and we were just captivated" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/06/europe/ivanka-trump-kushner-luxury-resort-albania-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

The backlash arrived almost immediately. The Europe director of the environmental charity BirdLife, Ariel Brunner, said he and other conservationists visited the site in early May 2026 and saw excavators digging up the beach and trucks laying gravel — "There was no sign whatsoever, neither by the lagoon where they were cutting the road, nor on the beach where the machinery was working... of any kind of license or permits or even just declaration of who they were" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/06/europe/ivanka-trump-kushner-luxury-resort-albania-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). Part of the anger focused on a 2024 change to Albania's nature laws that exempts "structures of excellence, 5 stars or more" and related hospitality activities from protections that would otherwise block construction — language Brunner called "one of the most brutal legislative texts I've seen in the environmental field in my career" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/06/europe/ivanka-trump-kushner-luxury-resort-albania-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). The European Commission told CNN the change clashes with EU law — a sticking point in Albania's EU accession talks — and said Albania's Environment Ministry had committed that "the construction works have been suspended" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/06/europe/ivanka-trump-kushner-luxury-resort-albania-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

On the corporate side, the venture is structured at arm's length from Kushner's private-equity firm: CNN reported that when it asked Kushner's fund about the project, it was redirected to a different company, Sazan Real Estate Development LLC, and told that investors were involved in their personal capacity ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/06/europe/ivanka-trump-kushner-luxury-resort-albania-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). That company's chairman, Asher Abehsera, said in a statement that the group was "excited about the opportunity to create a world-class destination and make one of the largest private investments in the region's history," with its focus on "responsible stewardship, environmental enhancement, job creation, and creating long-term value for local communities" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/06/europe/ivanka-trump-kushner-luxury-resort-albania-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). The story itself began years earlier: Kushner told an investment summit in 2025 that while he was sailing off Albania's coast in 2021, Prime Minister Edi Rama came onto the boat for a meeting, and the two discussed investment opportunities a year later; in 2024, Kushner posted concept art for the Albanian coast project on social media ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/06/europe/ivanka-trump-kushner-luxury-resort-albania-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

For her part, Ivanka has not played down the scale. "We'll have hotels and resorts and wellness, all of it — it's almost daunting in its size," she told Senra, adding that "community" was at the heart of the plan: "You can't just, like, impose yourself upon a country or culture — you have to understand it first to do it in a beautiful and delicate and meaningful way" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/06/europe/ivanka-trump-kushner-luxury-resort-albania-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). Whether the project proceeds at all is now in doubt: while Albania's anti-corruption prosecution office, SPAK, told CNN it had "no open investigation concerning any project associated with Mr. Jared Kushner," the European Commission has noted that inquiries "reportedly extend beyond environmental concerns" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/06/europe/ivanka-trump-kushner-luxury-resort-albania-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

## The post–White House reinvention

Ivanka left Washington in January 2021 and, in 2022, announced she was stepping away from politics and leaving the political arena — and she kept her word through her father's second-term campaign, declining any role in the new administration ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/diplomacy/article/3368740/ivanka-trump-takes-front-row-seat-welcome-xi-dress-chinese-designer-guo-pei)). Her re-entry into public life came through business, not politics. In May 2025 she appeared at the Heartland Summit in Bentonville, Arkansas — her first significant public appearance since the second Trump term began — where, in an interview with Thrive Global founder Arianna Huffington, she spoke publicly for the first time about her role as co-founder of Planet Harvest, a Chicago-based "profit-for-purpose company" she founded in 2023 ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/ivanka-trump-unveils-fresh-produce-access-initiative-in-first-public-appearance-since-trump-s-second-term-101746772744460.html)).

Planet Harvest aims to fix inefficiencies in the fresh-produce supply chain — offering what the company calls "innovative, whole harvest solutions" that change how produce is sourced and distributed, connecting surplus harvests with people who can use them ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/ivanka-trump-unveils-fresh-produce-access-initiative-in-first-public-appearance-since-trump-s-second-term-101746772744460.html)). On stage, Ivanka put it in personal terms: "Food can be used medicinally and food can be used to heal our bodies. Food can be part of the solution" ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/ivanka-trump-unveils-fresh-produce-access-initiative-in-first-public-appearance-since-trump-s-second-term-101746772744460.html)). The idea traces back to her White House years: she told Axios she developed "a real passion for supporting American farmers and getting more food into communities in need" while working on the U.S. Department of Agriculture's Farmers to Families Food Box program during the pandemic, and said she started Planet Harvest "to reimagine how American produce moves — not just through the supply chain, but across communities" ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/ivanka-trump-unveils-fresh-produce-access-initiative-in-first-public-appearance-since-trump-s-second-term-101746772744460.html)). The Heartland Summit itself was co-founded by Walmart heirs Olivia, Tom, and Steuart Walton and hosted by their Bentonville think tank, Heartland Forward ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/ivanka-trump-unveils-fresh-produce-access-initiative-in-first-public-appearance-since-trump-s-second-term-101746772744460.html)).

Then came a different kind of stage. On October 27, 2025, FIFA announced that Ivanka would join the advisory board of the FIFA Global Citizen Education Fund — a $100 million charity fund created with the anti-poverty nonprofit Global Citizen and part-funded by 2026 World Cup ticket sales — alongside Serena Williams, Shakira, The Weeknd, Hugh Jackman, and Kaká ([Associated Press](https://www.newdelhitimes.com/ivanka-trump-joins-fifa-education-charity-project-part-funded-by-world-cup-ticket-sales/)). The board, FIFA said, "will provide strategic guidance to maximize the fund's reach and impact," splitting its work between grants for grassroots education in more than 200 countries and FIFA's worldwide schools program ([Associated Press](https://www.newdelhitimes.com/ivanka-trump-joins-fifa-education-charity-project-part-funded-by-world-cup-ticket-sales/)). The fund had already raised $30 million toward its target, with $1 earmarked from each World Cup ticket sold — more than seven million spectators were expected across 104 games in 16 host cities in the United States, Canada, and Mexico from June 11 to July 19 — and grants of up to $250,000 available to organizations giving children in underserved communities access to education and sport ([Associated Press](https://www.newdelhitimes.com/ivanka-trump-joins-fifa-education-charity-project-part-funded-by-world-cup-ticket-sales/)). It was not her first encounter with the soccer body: she had appeared on stage with her son Theodore at FIFA's Club World Cup draw ceremony in Miami the previous December ([Associated Press](https://www.newdelhitimes.com/ivanka-trump-joins-fifa-education-charity-project-part-funded-by-world-cup-ticket-sales/)).

## Early life and education

As a teenager attending boarding school, Ivanka began modeling on weekends and holidays — and she was good at it fast: in May 1997 she appeared on the cover of *Seventeen*, and that same year, at 15, she co-hosted the Miss Teen USA Pageant ([Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/people/ivanka-trump-680888/biography)). She then went the academic route, graduating from the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania with a bachelor's degree in economics ([Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/people/ivanka-trump-680888/biography)). Her first job after college was not at the family company: she briefly worked for the real estate developer Forest City Ratner before joining the Trump Organization ([Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/people/ivanka-trump-680888/biography)).

## The Trump Organization and the fashion brand

At the Trump Organization she rose to executive vice president of Development & Acquisitions, charged with the domestic and global expansion of the company's real estate interests ([Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/people/ivanka-trump-680888/biography)). Soon after joining, she started building her own consumer empire: jewelry first, then shoes and apparel, launched under the Ivanka Trump name — a 2007 partnership with Dynamic Diamond Corp. expanded the fine-jewelry line ([Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/people/ivanka-trump-680888/biography)). Along the way she became a familiar television face in her own right, serving as a guest judge on *Project Runway* in 2006 and a boardroom judge on her father's *The Apprentice* ([Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/people/ivanka-trump-680888/biography)). She also published two books: *The Trump Card: Playing to Win in Work and Life* in October 2009 and *Women Who Work: Rewriting the Rules for Success* in May 2017 ([Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/people/ivanka-trump-680888/biography)). In 2010 she co-founded Girl Up with the United Nations Foundation ([Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/people/ivanka-trump-680888/biography)).

The brand did not survive her move into government. On July 24, 2018, she announced she was shutting the fashion line to focus on her role as an informal White House adviser, where she was working on advancing working women: "After 17 months in Washington, I do not know when or if I will ever return to the business, but I do know that my focus for the foreseeable future will be the work I am doing here in Washington," she said in a statement ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-usa-trump-ivanka-idUSKBN1KE2JN/)). She had already given up day-to-day management in 2017, placing the company's assets in a trust managed by family members; the closure meant licensing contracts would not be renewed, existing ones would run their course, and the 18 people working for the 11-year-old company would be laid off ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-usa-trump-ivanka-idUSKBN1KE2JN/)). The commercial headwinds were real: retailers including Nordstrom, Hudson's Bay, and Sears had dropped or sharply scaled back her products — typically blaming poor sales rather than politics — while Bloomingdale's, Dillard's, and Amazon continued to carry them, and the *Wall Street Journal* reported online sales had fallen nearly 55 percent in the year to June, citing Rakuten Intelligence data ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-usa-trump-ivanka-idUSKBN1KE2JN/)). The grassroots "Grab Your Wallet" boycott claimed the shutdown as a victory, and critics noted that much of her line was made overseas — including in China, then the target of her father's tariffs ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-usa-trump-ivanka-idUSKBN1KE2JN/)).

## The White House years, 2017–2021

On March 29, 2017, Ivanka was appointed "First Daughter and Advisor to the President," an unpaid role focused on education, job creation, workforce development, and economic opportunity for women and their families ([Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/people/ivanka-trump-680888/biography)). She served alongside her husband, Jared Kushner — whom she had married in 2009 — as one of the most prominent members of the administration's inner circle, and in August 2020 she introduced her father at the Republican National Convention ([Fandango](https://www.fandango.com/people/ivanka-trump-680888/biography)). The arrangement drew sustained ethics scrutiny: her business interests overlapped with her official role, her retained ownership of companies while serving raised conflict-of-interest questions, and the family's portfolio of real-estate and consumer-goods businesses was widely seen as clashing with Washington service ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/article/us-usa-trump-ivanka-idUSKBN1KE2JN/)).

## The China thread

One relationship has followed Ivanka through every phase of her public life: China. During her father's first term, she was an unpaid adviser who became "an important conduit for Chinese officials in Washington" — and the family dimension was literal. When Xi Jinping and his wife Peng Liyuan visited the United States in April 2017, her daughter Arabella performed the traditional Chinese ballad "Mo Li Hua" (Jasmine Flower) and recited Tang dynasty poetry in Chinese; later that year in Beijing, President Trump showed Xi private video recordings of his granddaughter addressing the Chinese leader and his wife as "Grandpa Xi" and "Grandma Peng" ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/diplomacy/article/3368740/ivanka-trump-takes-front-row-seat-welcome-xi-dress-chinese-designer-guo-pei)). Meanwhile, Ivanka secured multiple trademark approvals in China during her father's presidency in 2018 — covering standard fashion and lifestyle goods, but also services like voting machines and nursing homes — sparking widespread discussion about potential conflicts of interest ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/diplomacy/article/3368740/ivanka-trump-takes-front-row-seat-welcome-xi-dress-chinese-designer-guo-pei)).

The thread resurfaced, visibly, in September 2026. At the White House welcome ceremony for Xi Jinping and Peng Liyuan, Ivanka — now 44 and officially out of politics — had a front-row seat, wearing a robin's egg blue dress and matching jacket with silver embroidery by Beijing-based designer Guo Pei; her 15-year-old daughter Arabella, in a white belted midi dress by the London label Self-Portrait, sat beside her ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/diplomacy/article/3368740/ivanka-trump-takes-front-row-seat-welcome-xi-dress-chinese-designer-guo-pei)). They were placed alongside Vice President J.D. Vance and his wife Usha Vance — ahead of cabinet members including Secretary of State Marco Rubio, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, who were seated further back ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/diplomacy/article/3368740/ivanka-trump-takes-front-row-seat-welcome-xi-dress-chinese-designer-guo-pei)).
