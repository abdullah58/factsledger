## Overview

**Muppavarapu Venkaiah Naidu (born 1 July 1949) served as the 13th Vice President of [India](/places/india/) and ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha from 11 August 2017 to 10 August 2022. A former BJP president and Union minister under two prime ministers, he won the 2017 vice-presidential election with 516 of 760 valid votes and received the Padma Vibhushan in 2024.**

Naidu was born at Chavatapalem in Nellore district, [Andhra Pradesh](/places/andhra-pradesh/), into an agricultural family, and took a law degree from Andhra University in Visakhapatnam ([Zee News](https://zeenews.india.com/india/venkaiah-naidu-sworn-in-as-13th-vice-president-of-india-2032056.html)). He came to prominence in the Jai Andhra movement of 1972, became convenor of the Jayaprakash Narayan Chhatra Sangharsh Samiti in 1974, and protested in the streets against the Emergency ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/news-ians/venkaiah-naidu-elected-vice-president-of-india-roundup-117080501116_1.html)). He was a swayamsevak in the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) and joined the Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP) during his college days ([Zee News](https://zeenews.india.com/india/venkaiah-naidu-sworn-in-as-13th-vice-president-of-india-2032056.html)). His career then ran through the Andhra Pradesh Assembly, the Rajya Sabha, the BJP presidency, two Union Cabinets, and finally the vice-presidency — a path from pasting party posters to presiding over the Upper House ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/india/venkaiah-naidu-is-indias-13th-vice-president-elect-from-nellore-to-new-delhi-rewinding-journey-of-bjp-veteran-3898459.html?subscribeIntent=true)).

## From Nellore to the BJP presidency

Naidu was elected MLA from Udayagiri in 1978 as a Janata Party candidate, when the Congress swept the polls in Andhra Pradesh, and was re-elected on a BJP ticket when N. T. Rama Rao swept the state ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/india/venkaiah-naidu-is-indias-13th-vice-president-elect-from-nellore-to-new-delhi-rewinding-journey-of-bjp-veteran-3898459.html?subscribeIntent=true)). He entered the Rajya Sabha from Karnataka in 1998 and was re-elected from Karnataka in 2004 and 2010, before taking a fourth term from Rajasthan in 2016 ([Zee News](https://zeenews.india.com/india/venkaiah-naidu-sworn-in-as-13th-vice-president-of-india-2032056.html); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/news-ians/venkaiah-naidu-elected-vice-president-of-india-roundup-117080501116_1.html)). An ABVP activist during the Emergency, he was arrested and jailed, and he rose through the BJP as a protege of L. K. Advani before backing [Narendra Modi](/people/narendra-modi/) for the prime ministership ahead of the 2014 elections ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/india/venkaiah-naidu-is-indias-13th-vice-president-elect-from-nellore-to-new-delhi-rewinding-journey-of-bjp-veteran-3898459.html?subscribeIntent=true)). He served as BJP national president from July 2002 to October 2004 and quit after the party's defeat in the 2004 Lok Sabha elections ([NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/people/venkaiah-naidu-10-facts-about-indias-13th-vice-president-1733967)). He was known for his oratory and his talent for coining acronyms, including "MODI — Making of Developed India" for the Modi government's public outreach ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/india/venkaiah-naidu-is-indias-13th-vice-president-elect-from-nellore-to-new-delhi-rewinding-journey-of-bjp-veteran-3898459.html?subscribeIntent=true)).

## Minister in two NDA governments

Naidu held Cabinet rank under two National Democratic Alliance (NDA) prime ministers, first on the rural side and then on the urban side. As Rural Development Minister in the Atal Bihari Vajpayee government between 2000 and 2002, he was instrumental in launching the Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY), the rural roads programme ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/news-ians/venkaiah-naidu-elected-vice-president-of-india-roundup-117080501116_1.html); [NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/people/venkaiah-naidu-10-facts-about-indias-13th-vice-president-1733967)). In the Modi government he was first Parliamentary Affairs Minister, a stint in which the Real Estate Act was cleared and the Goods and Services Tax (GST) Bill was fast-forwarded, and he later held Urban Development and Housing and Information and Broadcasting ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/india/venkaiah-naidu-is-indias-13th-vice-president-elect-from-nellore-to-new-delhi-rewinding-journey-of-bjp-veteran-3898459.html?subscribeIntent=true)). As Urban Development minister he launched the Smart City Mission, the Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation (AMRUT), the Swachh Bharat Mission, and Housing for All ([NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/people/venkaiah-naidu-10-facts-about-indias-13th-vice-president-1733967)). The pairing is the spine of his executive record: rural roads under Vajpayee, urban missions under Modi.

## Vice President and Rajya Sabha Chairman

Naidu was elected Vice President on 5 August 2017, defeating the combined opposition's candidate Gopalkrishna Gandhi, grandson of Mahatma Gandhi, by 516 votes to 244 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/news-ians/venkaiah-naidu-elected-vice-president-of-india-roundup-117080501116_1.html)). Of 785 MPs in the electoral college — members of the Rajya Sabha and the [Lok Sabha](/organizations/lok-sabha/) — 771 cast votes and 11 were declared invalid ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/news-ians/venkaiah-naidu-elected-vice-president-of-india-roundup-117080501116_1.html)). That 516 of 760 valid votes, or about 67.9%, gave him a 272-vote margin — a comfortable win that reflected the NDA's parliamentary arithmetic rather than a close contest ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/news-ians/venkaiah-naidu-elected-vice-president-of-india-roundup-117080501116_1.html)). President Ram Nath Kovind administered the oath of office at Rashtrapati Bhavan on 11 August 2017, and Naidu pledged to steer the House "without fear or favour" ([Zee News](https://zeenews.india.com/india/venkaiah-naidu-sworn-in-as-13th-vice-president-of-india-2032056.html); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/news-ians/venkaiah-naidu-elected-vice-president-of-india-roundup-117080501116_1.html)). On election day he declared himself a "non-party man," saying there was no "my party or your party" for him anymore ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/india/venkaiah-naidu-is-indias-13th-vice-president-elect-from-nellore-to-new-delhi-rewinding-journey-of-bjp-veteran-3898459.html?subscribeIntent=true)).

As Chairman he presided over the [2020 Deputy Chairman election](/events/2020-deputy-chairman-rajya-sabha-election/), declaring Harivansh Narayan Singh chosen on 14 September 2020 after a motion moved by BJP president J. P. Nadda and seconded by Leader of the House Thavarchand Gehlot was carried by a voice vote, while opposition motions for Manoj Jha were not pressed ([News18](https://www.news18.com/news/india/nda-candidate-harivansh-narayan-singh-re-elected-as-rajya-sabha-deputy-chairman-2876471.html)). His chairmanship style became his public signature: famous one-liners that Modi called "wit-liners," encouragement of members to speak in their mother tongue, and the principle that disruptions beyond a point amounted to contempt of the House — "let the government propose, let Opposition oppose, let the house dispose" ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/politics/pm-hails-naidus-wit-one-liners-as-rajya-sabha-bids-farewell-to-vice-president/1073877/)). At his farewell on 8 August 2022, Modi said Rajya Sabha productivity had increased 70% during Naidu's tenure and members' attendance had risen, and praised his devotion to youth welfare programmes ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/politics/pm-hails-naidus-wit-one-liners-as-rajya-sabha-bids-farewell-to-vice-president/1073877/)). The 70% figure is Modi's tribute claim, not an audited measure, and the sources read for this profile give no baseline for it — which is why it is attributed here rather than stated as fact. Naidu completed his term on 10 August 2022 and was succeeded by Jagdeep Dhankhar ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/politics/pm-hails-naidus-wit-one-liners-as-rajya-sabha-bids-farewell-to-vice-president/1073877/); [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/president-droupadi-murmu-presents-padma-awards-in-new-delhi-on-april-22-2024/article68094604.ece/amp/)). On 22 April 2024, President Droupadi Murmu conferred the Padma Vibhushan, India's second-highest civilian award, on him for public affairs ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/president-droupadi-murmu-presents-padma-awards-in-new-delhi-on-april-22-2024/article68094604.ece/amp/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**When was M. Venkaiah Naidu Vice President of India?**
Naidu served as the 13th Vice President from 11 August 2017 to 10 August 2022, a single five-year term, after a long career in the BJP and two Union Cabinets. As Vice President he was ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha, and he was succeeded by Jagdeep Dhankhar.

**How did Naidu win the 2017 vice-presidential election?**
He defeated opposition candidate Gopalkrishna Gandhi by 516 votes to 244. Of the 785 MPs in the electoral college, 771 voted and 11 ballots were invalid, giving Naidu about 67.9% of the valid votes. The election was held on 5 August 2017, and he took the oath six days later.

**What did Naidu do as a Union minister?**
Under Vajpayee he was Rural Development Minister (2000–2002) and launched the PMGSY rural roads programme. Under Modi he handled Parliamentary Affairs, Urban Development and Housing — launching the Smart City Mission and allied urban missions — and Information and Broadcasting.

**What was Naidu known for as Rajya Sabha Chairman?**
His one-liners, his encouragement of members speaking in their mother tongue, and his insistence that disruptions beyond a point were contempt of the House. Modi's farewell tribute credited him with a 70% rise in the House's productivity. He summed up his approach as: let the government propose, let the Opposition oppose, let the House dispose.

**Did Venkaiah Naidu receive the Padma Vibhushan?**
Yes. President Droupadi Murmu conferred the Padma Vibhushan, India's second-highest civilian award, on him on 22 April 2024 for public affairs, two years after his vice-presidential term ended. The ceremony at Rashtrapati Bhavan also honoured Bindeshwar Pathak and Padma Subrahmanyam with the same award.
