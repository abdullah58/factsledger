## Overview

Drake — Aubrey Drake Graham, born 24 October 1986 in Toronto, Canada — is a Canadian rapper, singer and actor who moved from the teen drama *Degrassi: The Next Generation* to become the most-charted artist in Hot 100 history, with 402 entries, 14 No. 1 singles and 15 No. 1 albums ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake); [Billboard](https://www.kjwl.com/2026/05/28/since-2020-drake-has-had-the-most-career-hot-100-hits-since-1964-only-2-other-acts-have-held-that-record/)). This is the rapper Drake, not the 16th-century explorer Sir Francis Drake and not the singer Drake Bell: the subject here is the Toronto-born recording artist whose trademark sound blends singing with lyrical rapping ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). He has won five Grammy Awards from 56 nominations, and Billboard named him its Artist of the Decade for the 2010s ([Recording Academy](https://www.grammy.com/artists/drake/12370/); [Billboard](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2021/05/11/drake-is-billboards-artist-of-the-decade-will-receive-honor-at-2021-billboard-music-awards/)).

## From Degrassi to So Far Gone

Drake was raised by his mother in Toronto after his parents divorced when he was small, visiting his drummer father in summers ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). In 2001 he was cast as Jimmy Brooks on *Degrassi: The Next Generation*, a role he held until leaving in 2008 to focus on music ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). He had already begun rapping under his middle name, self-publishing *Room for Improvement* in 2006 and *Comeback Season* in 2007, whose "Replacement Girl", featuring Trey Songz, was highlighted on [BET](/organizations/bet/)'s *106 & Park* ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). Breakthrough came in 2009 with *So Far Gone* and its hit "Best I Ever Had", which reached No. 2 on Billboard's Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake); [AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/mn0001035294)). He then signed with [Lil Wayne](/people/lil-wayne/)'s Young Money Entertainment, which released *So Far Gone* as an EP that won a Juno Award ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). *Thank Me Later*, his first full-length studio album, arrived in 2010, debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200, and was certified platinum ([AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/mn0001035294)).

## The chart record book

No artist has occupied Billboard's charts like Drake. After first appearing on Billboard charts in 2009, he banked nine Billboard 200 No. 1s in the 2010s, from *Thank Me Later* to 2019's *Care Package*, and six Hot 100 No. 1s ([Billboard](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2021/05/11/drake-is-billboards-artist-of-the-decade-will-receive-honor-at-2021-billboard-music-awards/)). By May 2021 he already held the records for the most Hot 100 top 10s, with 45, and the most Hot 100 entries overall, with 232 — and he held the title for the most [Billboard Music Awards](/events/billboard-music-awards/), with 27 after 12 prizes in 2019, a run that won him the Artist of the Decade Award at the 2021 ceremony ([Billboard](https://radiox.cms.socastsrm.com/2021/05/11/drake-is-billboards-artist-of-the-decade-will-receive-honor-at-2021-billboard-music-awards/)). The entries record then kept growing: on the chart dated 30 May 2026 he became the first artist past 400 career Hot 100 entries, reaching 402 ([Billboard](https://www.kjwl.com/2026/05/28/since-2020-drake-has-had-the-most-career-hot-100-hits-since-1964-only-2-other-acts-have-held-that-record/)). He has held the most-entries mark since early 2020, when his 208th entry passed the Glee cast, holder since February 2011 — before that, only Elvis Presley had held it, from October 1964 ([Billboard](https://www.kjwl.com/2026/05/28/since-2020-drake-has-had-the-most-career-hot-100-hits-since-1964-only-2-other-acts-have-held-that-record/)).

## Albums and Grammys

*Take Care* (2011) won the Juno Award for rap recording of the year and the Grammy Award for Best Rap Album, Drake's first Grammy; *Nothing Was the Same* (2013) also won a Juno, and [Nicki Minaj](/people/nicki-minaj/) and Lil Wayne were notable collaborators on those albums ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). Later releases topped charts in Canada and the [United States](/places/united-states/): the 2015 mixtape *If You're Reading This It's Too Late* and the Future collaboration *What a Time to Be Alive* both debuted at No. 1, as did *Views* (2016), whose "One Dance" became Spotify's most-streamed single ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). "Hotline Bling" won two Grammys, for Best Rap Song and Best Rap Performance, though Drake insisted it is not rap at all ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). *Scorpion* (2018) went platinum instantly and broke single-day streaming records, and "God's Plan" won Best Rap Song at the 2019 Grammys ([AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/mn0001035294); [Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). He skipped Grammy consideration for *More Life* (2017), and in 2021 withdrew *Certified Lover Boy* and "Way 2 Sexy" ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)).

## OVO and the business beyond music

Away from recording, Drake co-founded the Toronto collective October's Very Own (OVO) with producer Noah "40" Shebib and Oliver El-Khatib; it became the OVO Sound label in 2012, representing acts including Majid Jordan and PartyNextDoor, and he has released all his albums on OVO Sound since *Care Package* in 2019 ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). The OVO fashion line, launched with a Canada Goose bomber jacket in 2010, opened its first shop in Toronto in 2014 before expanding to Los Angeles, [New York City](/places/new-york-city/) and London ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). He has also kept a foot in screen work since leaving *Degrassi*: he voiced a teenage mammoth in the animated film *Ice Age: Continental Drift* (2012), hosted *Saturday Night Live* in 2014 and 2016, and in 2019 became an executive producer of the HBO series *Euphoria*, starring Zendaya ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). Together, the label, fashion line and screen credits make OVO a business built on Drake's name rather than a side project ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)).

## The 2024 feud with Kendrick Lamar

In early 2024, Future and producer Metro Boomin released two collaboration albums carrying disses aimed at Drake, and Kendrick Lamar dissed him on "Like That"; Drake answered with "Push Ups", and the exchange escalated through a string of tracks ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). Lamar accused Drake in "Meet the Grahams" of hiding another child and in "Not Like Us" called him a paedophile, and Drake vehemently denied both allegations ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). Drake also used artificial intelligence to make his voice sound like Tupac Shakur's on "Taylor Made Freestyle", and took the track down after Shakur's estate threatened to sue ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). In January 2025 he sued his own label, Universal Music Group, for defamation and harassment over its promotion of "Not Like Us"; a judge later dismissed the case, saying listeners could conclude the lyrics merely express an opinion ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). This profile reports only the documented tracks, denials and lawsuit, and takes no side on the exchange ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)).

## The 2026 comeback: three albums at once

He answered doubts about his post-feud career in 2026 with three albums released simultaneously on 15 May — *Iceman*, *Habibti* and *Maid of Honour* — which claimed the top three spots on the Billboard 200, a first ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake); [XXL](https://www.xxlmag.com/drake-billboard-200-history-iceman-habibti-maid-of-honour/)). *Iceman* opened with 463,000 equivalent album units, the biggest week of 2026 for an R&B/hip-hop album, which shows that streaming volume, not just hit singles, now drives Drake's chart power ([XXL](https://www.xxlmag.com/drake-billboard-200-history-iceman-habibti-maid-of-honour/)). *Habibti* added 114,000 units at No. 2 and *Maid of Honour* 110,000 at No. 3, for 687,000 units across the three albums in a single week ([XXL](https://www.xxlmag.com/drake-billboard-200-history-iceman-habibti-maid-of-honour/)). The sweep gave Drake his 15th No. 1 album, passing Jay-Z for the most among solo men and tying Taylor Swift for the most among soloists ([XXL](https://www.xxlmag.com/drake-billboard-200-history-iceman-habibti-maid-of-honour/)). "Janice STFU", from *Iceman*, debuted at No. 1 on the Hot 100, giving Drake his 14th chart-topper and moving him past Michael Jackson for the most No. 1s by a solo male artist ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is OVO Sound?**
OVO Sound is the record label Drake co-founded in Toronto in 2012 with producer Noah "40" Shebib and Oliver El-Khatib, growing out of their October's Very Own collective ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Drake)). **What happened in Drake's 2024 feud with Kendrick Lamar?**
