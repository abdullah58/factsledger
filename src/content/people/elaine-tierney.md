## Overview

Elaine Tierney (born July 10, 1999) is an American lightweight rower whose senior international record can
be stated to the hundredth of a second: World Rowing's official athlete record lists her championship
races with exact ranks and times — second in 7:43.84 for the silver medal at Racice in 2022, third in
8:51.84 for the bronze at Belgrade in 2023, both in the lightweight women's coxless pair (LW2-) with
Solveig Imsdahl ([World Rowing](https://worldrowing.com/athlete/55455)). The 68.00 seconds separating those
two finals tells the story of two very different championship weeks. A design historian of the same name is
affiliated with the V&A Research Institute in London; this profile is about the rower, not her.

## Temple years and the senior-team breakthrough

A West Chester, Pennsylvania native who rowed out of Unionville High School, Tierney — listed at 5-9 —
spent five seasons with Temple University's varsity eight
([Temple Athletics](https://owlsports.com/news/2022/7/8/womens-rowing-elaine-tierney-earns-spot-on-us-rowing-senior-national-team.aspx)).
She was the program's Newcomer of the Year in 2018, its Women's Rowing Athlete of the Year in 2020, and a
Second Team All-American Athletic Conference selection in her final 2022 season
([Temple Athletics](https://owlsports.com/sports/womens-crew/roster/elaine-tierney/11049)). Her 2020–21
campaign included a bronze with the Varsity Eight at the Murphy Cup, and a second-place finish at the Dad
Vail Regatta that gave Temple its first Dad Vail medal in more than 20 years
([Temple Athletics](https://owlsports.com/sports/womens-crew/roster/elaine-tierney/11049)). That year she
also earned a spot on the U.S. Rowing U23 National Team and competed in the lightweight double sculls at
the 2021 World Rowing U23 Championship
([Temple Athletics](https://owlsports.com/sports/womens-crew/roster/elaine-tierney/11049)).

The breakthrough came on July 8, 2022, at the [USRowing](/organizations/usrowing/) Summer National Championships on Mercer Lake. Racing
for Vesper Boat Club, Tierney and Imsdahl won the lightweight women's pair, claiming the national
championship and a berth on the U.S. Senior National Team for that year's World Rowing Championships
([Temple Athletics](https://owlsports.com/news/2022/7/8/womens-rowing-elaine-tierney-earns-spot-on-us-rowing-senior-national-team.aspx)).
Tierney became the first Temple women's rower to earn a senior national team spot since Jana Heere did so
in 2007 in the lightweight doubles
([Temple Athletics](https://owlsports.com/news/2022/7/8/womens-rowing-elaine-tierney-earns-spot-on-us-rowing-senior-national-team.aspx)).
"It was definitely a hard fought and well worked for race," Tierney said. "We kept ourselves internal and focused
on our strokes like we do in practice. This helped us take the lead in the first 500m off the start and be in
a good position to respond well to the other crew's final sprint. I am over the moon to be able
to make my first senior team just one year out of U23s and return to Racice"
([Temple Athletics](https://owlsports.com/news/2022/7/8/womens-rowing-elaine-tierney-earns-spot-on-us-rowing-senior-national-team.aspx)).
Temple women's rowing coach Rebecca Grzybowski added: "To earn the opportunity to represent your country on
the world stage is a massive accomplishment, and the direct result of intense work, commitment, and
resilience"
([Temple Athletics](https://owlsports.com/news/2022/7/8/womens-rowing-elaine-tierney-earns-spot-on-us-rowing-senior-national-team.aspx)).

## Two world medals, told in times

At Racice, Tierney and Imsdahl placed second in their heat in 7:39.79, then finished second in the A
final in 7:43.84 — the silver medal ([World Rowing](https://worldrowing.com/athlete/55455)). USRowing's own
championship recap independently confirms the U.S. took silver in the lightweight women's pair that week,
one of three American medals alongside silver in the lightweight women's double sculls and bronze in the
women's pair
([USRowing](https://usrowing.org/news/mens-and-womens-eights-finish-fourth-at-the-2022-world-rowing-championships)).

The pair qualified for a second straight senior team on July 29, 2023, racing uncontested at the Senior
National Team Trials on Mercer Lake and finishing in 7:31.61
([Temple Athletics](https://owlsports.com/news/2023/7/28/womens-rowing-former-temple-rower-elaine-tierney-makes-2023-us-rowing-senior-national-team)).
Before heading to Serbia, Tierney and Imsdahl sat for a 30-minute interview on the Twelve Pound Podcast
about how they train and prepare for race day, published August 29, 2023, and asked listeners to support their
journey to the Worlds via a fundraising link
([Twelve Pound Podcast](https://twelve-pound-podcast.castos.com/episodes/rowed-to-glory-the-power-of-the-pair-with-solveig-imsdahl-and-elaine-tierney)).
At Belgrade, the U.S. pair ranked third in its preliminary race in 7:42.14 and third in the final in
8:51.84 — the bronze medal ([World Rowing](https://worldrowing.com/athlete/55455)).
