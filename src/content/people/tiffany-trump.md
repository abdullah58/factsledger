## Overview

Tiffany Trump is the only child of Donald Trump and his second wife, Marla Maples — and the Trump child who grew up furthest from the family orbit. Born in Florida in 1993 and raised in California by her mother, she spent her formative years across the country from the New York world of her half-siblings [Donald Trump Jr.](/people/donald-trump-jr), [Eric Trump](/people/eric-trump), and Ivanka Trump ([The Scotsman](https://www.thefreelibrary.com/Tiffany+Trump%3a+who+is+Donald+Trump%27s+daughter+and+her+fiance+Michael...-a0649138079); [Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/)). She has kept the lowest political profile of the adult Trump children, stepping into the spotlight mainly for family milestones: the 2020 Republican National Convention, her 2022 Mar-a-Lago wedding to Michael Boulos, and — in May 2025 — the birth of her son Alexander, Donald Trump's 11th grandchild ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/)).

## A California childhood, a jeweler's name

Tiffany was born in a [Palm Beach, Florida](/places/palm-beach-florida/), hospital in 1993 to Trump and Maples, who were married from 1993 to 1999 ([Palm Beach Post](https://www.palmbeachpost.com/story/news/trump/2026/05/01/tiffany-trump-baby-michael-boulos-birthday-marla-maples/89887417007/); [Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/)). She was named after the luxury jeweler Tiffany & Co. — her father had purchased the air rights above the company's New York flagship store for $5 million to build Trump Tower ([The Scotsman](https://www.thefreelibrary.com/Tiffany+Trump%3a+who+is+Donald+Trump%27s+daughter+and+her+fiance+Michael...-a0649138079)).

She is the youngest of Trump's children from his first two marriages: her three elder half-siblings, Donald Jr., Ivanka, and Eric, were born to his first wife, Ivana, to whom he was married from 1977 to 1990; her younger half-brother [Barron Trump](/people/barron-trump) is the son of his third wife, Melania, whom he married in 2005 ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/)). Raised by Maples in California, she grew up at a geographic and cultural remove from the Trump Organization world her siblings inhabited ([The Scotsman](https://www.thefreelibrary.com/Tiffany+Trump%3a+who+is+Donald+Trump%27s+daughter+and+her+fiance+Michael...-a0649138079)).

## School, a single, and a runway

Tiffany attended Viewpoint School in Calabasas, California, then the University of Pennsylvania, where she studied sociology and urban studies and graduated in May 2016 ([The Scotsman](https://www.thefreelibrary.com/Tiffany+Trump%3a+who+is+Donald+Trump%27s+daughter+and+her+fiance+Michael...-a0649138079)). Before law school she sampled the entertainment and fashion worlds: she released a pop single, "Like a Bird," in 2011; interned at *Vogue*; and modeled in a 2016 New York Fashion Week show ([The Scotsman](https://www.thefreelibrary.com/Tiffany+Trump%3a+who+is+Donald+Trump%27s+daughter+and+her+fiance+Michael...-a0649138079)). She then enrolled at the Georgetown University Law Center and graduated in 2020 ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/donald-trump-becoming-grandfather-for-11th-time-ex-us-presidents-daughter-tiffany-is-pregnant/articleshow/114139842.cms)).

## Meeting Boulos and a Rose Garden proposal

While a student at Georgetown Law, Tiffany met Michael Boulos in the summer of 2018 in Mykonos, Greece, at Lindsay Lohan's Beach Club, according to *People* reporting cited by the Akron Beacon Journal ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/)). The couple announced their engagement on January 19, 2021, at the White House — the last full day of her father's first term ([The Scotsman](https://www.thefreelibrary.com/Tiffany+Trump%3a+who+is+Donald+Trump%27s+daughter+and+her+fiance+Michael...-a0649138079)). Boulos proposed in the White House Rose Garden ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/)).

## The Mar-a-Lago wedding

Tiffany and Boulos married on November 12, 2022, at Mar-a-Lago, before more than 500 guests — just three days after Hurricane Nicole brushed past the club ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/)). For her gown she chose Lebanese designer Elie Saab, a deliberate homage to Boulos's Lebanese heritage ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/)).

## Public life at arm's length

Of the adult Trump children, Tiffany has stayed closest to the sidelines. She spoke at the 2020 Republican National Convention and appeared at the 2024 convention, and she and her husband appeared in the Manhattan courtroom during her father's hush-money trial ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/); [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/donald-trump-becoming-grandfather-for-11th-time-ex-us-presidents-daughter-tiffany-is-pregnant/articleshow/114139842.cms)). She lives in Miami ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/)). On June 14, 2025, she watched the Army's 250th-birthday parade from the presidential VIP box alongside Barron, Ivanka, Eric, and Lara Trump ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/)).

## Motherhood: Alexander Trump Boulos

In October 2024, Donald Trump announced at a Michigan campaign event that Tiffany was pregnant: "And she's going to have a baby" ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/donald-trump-becoming-grandfather-for-11th-time-ex-us-presidents-daughter-tiffany-is-pregnant/articleshow/114139842.cms)). Her son, Alexander Trump Boulos, was born at 4:44 a.m. on May 15, 2025 — the 11th grandchild of Donald Trump and the first grandchild of Marla Maples, who goes by "Gran Mar Mar" ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/); [Palm Beach Post](https://www.palmbeachpost.com/story/news/trump/2026/05/01/tiffany-trump-baby-michael-boulos-birthday-marla-maples/89887417007/)). Trump was in Abu Dhabi on his first major foreign trip of his second term when the baby arrived; the next day he said, "My daughter had a baby. I'm going back home to see that little baby" ([Palm Beach Post](https://www.palmbeachpost.com/story/news/trump/2026/05/01/tiffany-trump-baby-michael-boulos-birthday-marla-maples/89887417007/)).

Tiffany announced the birth on Instagram and shared the first portrait photos around Father's Day 2025, referring to her son online as "ATB" ([Palm Beach Post](https://www.palmbeachpost.com/story/news/trump/2026/05/01/tiffany-trump-baby-michael-boulos-birthday-marla-maples/89887417007/)). Before his arrival, Ivanka hosted a Peter Rabbit-themed baby shower for her ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/06/18/tiffany-trump-photos-boulos-marla-maples-ivanka-barron/84256522007/)). On April 6, 2026, Alexander made his White House debut alongside his parents at the annual Easter Egg Roll, hosted by first lady Melania Trump ([Palm Beach Post](https://www.palmbeachpost.com/story/news/trump/2026/05/01/tiffany-trump-baby-michael-boulos-birthday-marla-maples/89887417007/)).
