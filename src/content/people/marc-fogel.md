## Overview

The story of Marc Fogel's freedom is, on the surface, the story of one quiet plane ride. On February 11, 2025, Donald Trump's Middle East envoy [Steve Witkoff](/people/steve-witkoff/) made an unannounced stop in Moscow to pick up Fogel, the 63-year-old American history teacher who had been detained in Russia since August 2021 over 17 grams of marijuana ([Reuters](https://theprint.in/world/american-marc-fogel-greeted-by-trump-after-being-released-from-prison-by-russia/2490271/)). Witkoff flew him out on his own private plane — the first known visit of a U.S. official to Moscow since 2021 — and hours later the pair landed at Joint Base Andrews, where Trump had already left the White House for the occasion ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russia-releases-imprisoned-american-teacher-in-exchange/)).

The quietness was the point. The plane was on the ground in Moscow for only a few hours, according to Fogel's family lawyer Martin De Luca, and the return route passed through central Europe before reaching Washington ([Reuters](https://theprint.in/world/american-marc-fogel-greeted-by-trump-after-being-released-from-prison-by-russia/2490271/)). The week before, Fogel had been moved from a prison in Rybinsk, north of Moscow, to a Moscow pre-trial detention center — a transfer his Russian lawyer Dmitry Ovsyannikov described without clarity about the legal mechanism of release ([Reuters](https://theprint.in/world/american-marc-fogel-greeted-by-trump-after-being-released-from-prison-by-russia/2490271/)). National Security Adviser Mike Waltz described the event simply as an "exchange" and framed it as a step toward ending the war in [Ukraine](/places/ukraine/) ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russia-releases-imprisoned-american-teacher-in-exchange/)).

What made Fogel's case stand out from other American detainee cases was not just the outcome but the delay. For more than three years he sat in a Russian penal colony while higher-profile Americans — the WNBA star Brittney Griner, the Wall Street Journal reporter Evan Gershkovich — went home ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/who-is-marc-fogel-us-teacher-freed-russia-donald-trump-vladimir-putin-13862301.html)). His release was the payoff of a family campaign that outlasted two administrations and one major prisoner swap that left him behind.

## A Pennsylvania teacher's decade in Moscow

Before any of this, Fogel was a middle-class schoolteacher from Pennsylvania who spent nearly a decade teaching at the Anglo-American School in Moscow, primarily teaching the children of diplomats ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/who-is-marc-fogel-us-teacher-freed-russia-donald-trump-vladimir-putin-13862301.html)). Until 2020, the school's teachers had enjoyed diplomatic immunity — a protection that vanished as tensions between the United States and Russia escalated, and in 2022 Russian authorities forced the school to shut down completely ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/who-is-marc-fogel-us-teacher-freed-russia-donald-trump-vladimir-putin-13862301.html)).

That arc — from a working teacher of diplomats' children to a detainee in a remote labor camp — is what made his case resonate in Pennsylvania. Representative Chris Deluzio, part of a bipartisan group that pushed for his release, welcomed the news while noting that the details of the agreement were still unknown ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/who-is-marc-fogel-us-teacher-freed-russia-donald-trump-vladimir-putin-13862301.html)).

## The arrest that became a hostage case

Fogel was arrested in August 2021 at Sheremetyevo Airport in Moscow when authorities found the marijuana in his luggage ([Reuters](https://theprint.in/world/american-marc-fogel-greeted-by-trump-after-being-released-from-prison-by-russia/2490271/)). The drug had been prescribed in Pennsylvania, where medical marijuana is legal, for chronic back pain following spinal surgery ([Reuters](https://theprint.in/world/american-marc-fogel-greeted-by-trump-after-being-released-from-prison-by-russia/2490271/)). Russian law does not recognize medical marijuana use, and Fogel was charged with drug smuggling ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/who-is-marc-fogel-us-teacher-freed-russia-donald-trump-vladimir-putin-13862301.html)). His supporters later said he had a history of chronic pain and had planned to declare the medical marijuana at Russian customs ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/who-is-marc-fogel-us-teacher-freed-russia-donald-trump-vladimir-putin-13862301.html)).

The sentence — 14 years in a high-security penal colony — was severe even by the standards of the country that imposed it ([Reuters](https://theprint.in/world/american-marc-fogel-greeted-by-trump-after-being-released-from-prison-by-russia/2490271/)). After conviction he was transferred to a remote labor camp in northern Russia, where he endured harsh conditions and substandard medical care, and his family warned that his deteriorating health made delay dangerous ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/who-is-marc-fogel-us-teacher-freed-russia-donald-trump-vladimir-putin-13862301.html)). Former U.S. diplomat Eric Rubin, who advocated for Fogel's release, described the arrest as "essentially a hostage-taking situation" and suggested Russian authorities may have known about the medical marijuana in advance — a claim he made and this profile reports only as his statement ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/who-is-marc-fogel-us-teacher-freed-russia-donald-trump-vladimir-putin-13862301.html)). Rubin noted that Russian citizens convicted of similar offenses often receive probation ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/who-is-marc-fogel-us-teacher-freed-russia-donald-trump-vladimir-putin-13862301.html)).

## The designation fight

Fogel's family spent years arguing that his case was being neglected — and the record of what the U.S. government prioritized is the uncomfortable evidence they leaned on. Brittney Griner, detained on similar drug charges, was home within about ten months in a swap for convicted arms dealer Viktor Bout ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/who-is-marc-fogel-us-teacher-freed-russia-donald-trump-vladimir-putin-13862301.html)). In August 2024, a 24-prisoner exchange freed Evan Gershkovich and Paul Whelan — but not Fogel ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russia-releases-imprisoned-american-teacher-in-exchange/)).

The family's response was to push the U.S. government to formally classify him as "unjustly detained," a designation that succeeded in December 2024 after years of pressure ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russia-releases-imprisoned-american-teacher-in-exchange/)). The family had publicly criticized the Biden administration for not prioritizing his case earlier, and feared that his elderly mother — Malphine, who had been outspoken throughout — might never see him again ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/who-is-marc-fogel-us-teacher-freed-russia-donald-trump-vladimir-putin-13862301.html)).

## The release

The release itself was arranged with unusual discretion. The [Kremlin](/organizations/kremlin/) had "nothing to say" when asked about reports of a Gulfstream arriving in Moscow that would carry Witkoff, and said it was not planning meetings with Trump's special representative ([Reuters](https://theprint.in/world/american-marc-fogel-greeted-by-trump-after-being-released-from-prison-by-russia/2490271/)). Then Fogel was in custody of the envoy, on the plane, and gone.

The homecoming was anything but quiet. Trump greeted Fogel at the White House on a snowy evening, an American flag draped over his shoulders, and Fogel told reporters: "I feel like the luckiest man on Earth right now... I'm a middle-class school teacher who's now in a dream world" ([Reuters](https://theprint.in/world/american-marc-fogel-greeted-by-trump-after-being-released-from-prison-by-russia/2490271/)). Trump, standing beside him in the Diplomatic Reception Room, remarked: "To me he looks damned good" ([Reuters](https://theprint.in/world/american-marc-fogel-greeted-by-trump-after-being-released-from-prison-by-russia/2490271/)). Asked what had been given up in exchange, Trump said only "not much" and called the release a show of good faith from Russia ([Reuters](https://theprint.in/world/american-marc-fogel-greeted-by-trump-after-being-released-from-prison-by-russia/2490271/)).

The family's statement read: "We are beyond grateful, relieved, and overwhelmed that after more than three years of detention, our father, husband, and son, Marc Fogel, is finally coming home. This has been the darkest and most painful period of our lives, but today, we begin to heal" ([Reuters](https://theprint.in/world/american-marc-fogel-greeted-by-trump-after-being-released-from-prison-by-russia/2490271/)). Fogel's mother Malphine said the news was "just a total shock" ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/who-is-marc-fogel-us-teacher-freed-russia-donald-trump-vladimir-putin-13862301.html)). The other half of the exchange was Alexander Vinnik, a Russian national released from U.S. custody in California, who arrived at Moscow's Vnukovo Airport days later and confirmed it was a prisoner exchange ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/russian-national-freed-by-us-in-prisoner-swap-arrives-in-moscow/3481722/)). Vinnik — arrested in Greece in 2017 on U.S. crypto-fraud charges, convicted in France in 2020 on money-laundering charges, and having pleaded guilty in a U.S. court in May 2024 to conspiracy to commit money laundering — thanked Putin, Lavrov, and Trump on arrival ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/russian-national-freed-by-us-in-prisoner-swap-arrives-in-moscow/3481722/)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not give Marc Fogel's exact birth date; the sources read in full identify him only as 63 at the time of his release. It does not state the legal mechanism of his release, because his own Russian lawyer said at the time that it was unclear whether a pardon or another mechanism was used. It does not assert that Russian authorities set him up — that is former diplomat Eric Rubin's attributed suggestion, reported here only as his claim. And it does not confirm that "another person" was released the following day, as Trump suggested at the White House; that outcome was not verified in the sources read in full.
