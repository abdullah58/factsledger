## Overview

Elizabeth Warren (née Herring), born June 22, 1949, in Oklahoma City, is the Democratic [U.S. Senator](/organizations/us-senate/) from Massachusetts, a seat she has held since January 3, 2013, and the ranking member of the Senate Banking Committee ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren); [Senate Banking Committee](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/as-congress-considers-potential-market-structure-legislation-warren-and-reed-press-doj-and-treasury-on-national-security-risks-posed-by-trump-affiliated-crypto-ventures-sale-of-tokens-to-illicit-actors)). A former Harvard bankruptcy law professor, she championed the creation of the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau after the 2008 financial crisis and ran for the 2020 Democratic presidential nomination ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)).

Warren grew up in Norman, Oklahoma; after her father's heart attack the family struggled and she began waiting tables at 13 ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)). At 16 she earned a debate scholarship to George Washington University, though she graduated from the University of Houston with a B.S. in speech pathology in 1970; she then worked as a special-education teacher, earned her law degree from Rutgers University, Newark, in 1976, practiced law from her living room, and built an academic career to a tenured Harvard Law School professorship (1993–2013) as a bankruptcy expert ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)). She wrote on middle-class financial pressure, including *The Fragile Middle Class: Americans in Debt* (2000) and *The Two-Income Trap* (2003), and in 1980 married Harvard legal scholar Bruce Mann ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)).

## From TARP oversight to the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau

Warren became a national figure as chair of the Congressional Oversight Panel for the Troubled Asset Relief Program (TARP), the 2008 bank-rescue vehicle — Senate Majority Leader Harry Reid asked her to lead the panel ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)). She then championed the creation of the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau under the 2010 Dodd-Frank Act and structured and staffed the new consumer-finance watchdog ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)). President Barack Obama did not nominate her as its permanent head — reportedly fearing Republicans would block the appointment — and she emerged from the fight as a populist bellwether and liberal icon ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)).

## Senate career and the 2020 presidential campaign

In 2011 Warren began seeking the Democratic nomination for Ted Kennedy's former U.S. Senate seat, captured nearly 96 percent of the state convention vote to avoid a primary, and defeated incumbent Republican Scott Brown in November 2012 ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)). Taking office in January 2013, she became the first woman to represent Massachusetts in the U.S. Senate ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)). She was reelected in 2018 with about three-fifths of the vote over Republican Geoff Diehl, and again in 2024, when she defeated Republican challenger John Deaton ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)).

She entered the field for the 2020 Democratic presidential nomination at the end of 2018 and formally announced her candidacy on February 9, 2019, running on a progressive platform of structural economic change ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)). Her campaign's signature proposal was a 2% wealth tax on households worth more than $50 million — the "Two cents!" rally chant — and she was briefly considered a front-runner in late 2019 ([Associated Press](https://www.khsu.org/regional-interests/2020-03-05/warren-ends-2020-presidential-bid-after-a-disappointing-super-tuesday?_amp=true); [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)). After never finishing higher than third in the first four states and placing third in her home state on Super Tuesday, she withdrew from the race in March 2020 ([Associated Press](https://www.khsu.org/regional-interests/2020-03-05/warren-ends-2020-presidential-bid-after-a-disappointing-super-tuesday?_amp=true)). Joe Biden became the nominee and she was among those considered as his running mate before he selected Kamala Harris ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)).

Her Senate tenure also included a widely covered dispute over Native American heritage claims: a 2018 *Boston Globe* investigation concluded she had never used the claims to advance her career, while a DNA test she publicized suggested an ancestor 6 to 10 generations back and Cherokee Nation representatives dismissed genetic testing as irrelevant to tribal membership ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)). In February 2017 she was formally rebuked by the Senate for reading a 1986 Coretta Scott King letter opposing Jeff Sessions during debate over his attorney-general nomination, then finished reading it in a Facebook video viewed by millions ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)).

## Crypto oversight and the World Liberty Financial letter

In November 2025 Warren — as ranking member of the Senate Banking Committee — and Senator [Jack Reed](/people/jack-reed/) (D-RI), ranking member of the Senate Armed Services Committee, sent a letter dated November 17, 2025 to Attorney General [Pam Bondi](/people/pam-bondi/) and Treasury Secretary [Scott Bessent](/people/scott-bessent/) calling for information on any investigation into [World Liberty Financial](/organizations/world-liberty-financial/), a Trump-backed crypto venture, over reports it had sold governance tokens to buyers linked to North Korea, Russia, and other illicit actors ([U.S. Senate](https://banking.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/letter_to_bessent_and_bondi_on_wlfi_token_sales.pdf)). The Banking Committee publicly announced the letter on November 18, 2025 ([Senate Banking Committee](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/as-congress-considers-potential-market-structure-legislation-warren-and-reed-press-doj-and-treasury-on-national-security-risks-posed-by-trump-affiliated-crypto-ventures-sale-of-tokens-to-illicit-actors)); some secondary reporting described the letter itself as dated November 12, 2025, a date not supported by the signed document (see open items).

The letter cited the September 2025 [Accountable.US](/organizations/accountable-us/) report "American Sell-Out," which alleged that World Liberty Financial sold tokens to wallets connected to North Korea's [Lazarus Group](/organizations/lazarus-group/) hacking collective, an Iranian crypto exchange, a Russian ruble-backed sanctions-evasion tool, and the [Tornado Cash](/organizations/tornado-cash/) mixing service ([Senate Banking Committee](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/as-congress-considers-potential-market-structure-legislation-warren-and-reed-press-doj-and-treasury-on-national-security-risks-posed-by-trump-affiliated-crypto-ventures-sale-of-tokens-to-illicit-actors); [CryptoNews](https://cryptonews.com/news/watchdog-labels-trump-crypto-venture-american-sell-out-for-alleged-north-korea-iran-token-sales/)). "By selling these tokens, WLF has 'take[n] money from people with open and obvious connections to enemies of the United States' and raised national security risks by giving them 'a seat at the table' to set WLF's forward-looking governance policies," the senators wrote ([Senate Banking Committee](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/as-congress-considers-potential-market-structure-legislation-warren-and-reed-press-doj-and-treasury-on-national-security-risks-posed-by-trump-affiliated-crypto-ventures-sale-of-tokens-to-illicit-actors)). The report flagged specific transactions, including a $10,000 Inauguration Day purchase by "[Shryder](/people/shryder/).eth," a trader who had conducted 55 transactions with a Treasury-sanctioned Lazarus Group wallet, and sales to at least 62 users who had previously used Tornado Cash ([CryptoNews](https://cryptonews.com/news/watchdog-labels-trump-crypto-venture-american-sell-out-for-alleged-north-korea-iran-token-sales/)). The senators also argued the Trump family's financial ties created a conflict of interest — "every time a governance token is sold, three quarters of that money goes directly to President Trump and his family" — and asked the [Justice Department](/organizations/us-department-of-justice/) and Treasury to answer questions on national security risks and any current investigations by December 1, 2025 ([Senate Banking Committee](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/as-congress-considers-potential-market-structure-legislation-warren-and-reed-press-doj-and-treasury-on-national-security-risks-posed-by-trump-affiliated-crypto-ventures-sale-of-tokens-to-illicit-actors)). World Liberty Financial denied any wrongdoing or conflict of interest ([CryptoNews](https://cryptonews.com/news/watchdog-labels-trump-crypto-venture-american-sell-out-for-alleged-north-korea-iran-token-sales/)). These remain unproven allegations, not adjudicated findings.

## Frequently asked questions

**What did Elizabeth Warren do before entering the Senate?**
She was a tenured Harvard Law School professor from 1993 to 2013 and a bankruptcy-law expert, after earning her J.D. from Rutgers University, Newark, in 1976 ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)). After the 2008 financial crisis she chaired the Congressional Oversight Panel for the Troubled Asset Relief Program and then structured and staffed the new Consumer Financial Protection Bureau ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)).

**Why is Warren credited with creating the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau?**
She publicly proposed the agency, then helped create it under the 2010 Dodd-Frank Act and organized and staffed it as it was set up ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)). President Barack Obama did not nominate her as its permanent director, reportedly fearing Senate Republicans would block her confirmation ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Elizabeth-Warren)).

**What was her 2020 presidential campaign known for?**
A "plan for everything" platform of structural economic change, anchored by a proposed 2% wealth tax on households over $50 million that drew "Two cents!" chants at rallies ([Associated Press](https://www.khsu.org/regional-interests/2020-03-05/warren-ends-2020-presidential-bid-after-a-disappointing-super-tuesday?_amp=true)). She was briefly a front-runner in late 2019 but, after weak early-state finishes and a third-place result in Massachusetts on Super Tuesday, withdrew in March 2020 ([Associated Press](https://www.khsu.org/regional-interests/2020-03-05/warren-ends-2020-presidential-bid-after-a-disappointing-super-tuesday?_amp=true)).

**What did the November 2025 Warren–Reed letter allege about World Liberty Financial?**
Warren and Senator Jack Reed wrote that the Trump-backed venture sold governance tokens to buyers linked to North Korea's Lazarus Group, Iran, a Russian sanctions-evasion tool, and Tornado Cash — giving adversaries "a seat at the table" ([Senate Banking Committee](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/as-congress-considers-potential-market-structure-legislation-warren-and-reed-press-doj-and-treasury-on-national-security-risks-posed-by-trump-affiliated-crypto-ventures-sale-of-tokens-to-illicit-actors)). They sought a Justice Department and Treasury report on any investigation by December 1, 2025; the company denied wrongdoing ([CryptoNews](https://cryptonews.com/news/watchdog-labels-trump-crypto-venture-american-sell-out-for-alleged-north-korea-iran-token-sales/)).

**What is the Ultra-Millionaire Tax?**
A bill Warren has championed since her 2020 campaign and reintroduced in 2026: a 2% annual tax on household and trust net worth above $50 million plus a 1% surtax above $1 billion, estimated to raise $6.2 trillion over ten years, with proceeds envisioned for childcare, the Child Tax Credit, paid family leave, and other programs ([Barron's](https://www.barrons.com/articles/elizabeth-warren-proposes-tax-households-50-million-dollars-e270c959)).
