## Overview

**Chad Clinton Freeman** is the founder and director of the PollyGrind Film Festival, the Las Vegas underground genre-film event founded in 2010; he is on record calling Adam Rehmeier's *The Bunny Game* "the epitome of what PollyGrind is about" after the film swept the 2011 jury awards, including the top prize, the Biggest Baddest Mother of the PollyGrind ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/28065/exclusive-second-annual-pollygrind-film-festival-announces-winners/)). Dubbed the "new champion of unabashed B-movie sleaze" by ArrowInTheHead.com, Freeman is also an independent filmmaker and mass media specialist, and a member of the Exploitation Film Preservation Society since 1996 ([PRLog](https://www.prlog.org/11645205-pollygrind-proves-itself-as-las-vegas-premiere-underground-film-festival.html)).

## Building PollyGrind

Freeman founded PollyGrind in 2010 as an underground film festival in Las Vegas, and by its second annual edition — October 2011 at theatre7 in the Las Vegas arts district — it was screening more than 100 projects, including 30-plus feature films alongside 80-plus trailers, music videos and shorts ([PRLog](https://www.prlog.org/11645205-pollygrind-proves-itself-as-las-vegas-premiere-underground-film-festival.html)). The lineup mixed arthouse and grindhouse: the festival drew more than 20 projects from Las Vegas filmmakers and entries from India, Spain, Brazil, England, Australia, Canada, Germany, Sweden and Italy, spanning action, horror, sci-fi, experimental, dark comedy, gore, found footage and exploitation ([PRLog](https://www.prlog.org/11645205-pollygrind-proves-itself-as-las-vegas-premiere-underground-film-festival.html)). Freeman's programming instinct had commercial weight: eight of the thirteen features screened in 2010 went on to DVD distribution, and the 2011 edition dangled DVD deals from Breaking Glass Pictures, sub-distribution from Apprehensive Films and Wild Eye Releasing, and close to $10,000 in software prizes ([PRLog](https://www.prlog.org/11645205-pollygrind-proves-itself-as-las-vegas-premiere-underground-film-festival.html)) — positioning PollyGrind as, in the festival's own words, "the key festival for indie cult filmmakers to land distribution deals."

## The Bunny Game triumph

The 2011 festival belonged to Adam Rehmeier's [*The Bunny Game*](/creative-works/the-bunny-game/): it took the top jury prize, the Biggest Baddest Mother of the PollyGrind, plus best sound design, best cinematography and best editing, while Rodleen Getsic won best overall individual performance for her portrayal of Bunny ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/28065/exclusive-second-annual-pollygrind-film-festival-announces-winners/)). Freeman told Dread Central the film was "the epitome of what PollyGrind is about" — shot with a one-man crew, stylistic and beautiful to look at, "both brutal and thought provoking" ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/28065/exclusive-second-annual-pollygrind-film-festival-announces-winners/)). The relationship endured: Rehmeier returned for PollyGrind IV in 2013 with *Jonas*, one of eighteen world premieres at an edition that drew a record 777 entries and screened more than 100 films from 13 countries ([Horror Society](https://www.horrorsociety.com/2013/09/15/pollygrind-film-festival-of-las-vegas-to-world-premiere-18-films-screen-more-than-100-in-october/)). That year's program had been named one of MovieMaker Magazine's "25 Festivals Worth the Entry Fee" for 2012, and official selections had by then earned distribution deals from LionsGate, IFC Midnight, Troma, Showtime and Autonomy Pictures ([Horror Society](https://www.horrorsociety.com/2013/09/15/pollygrind-film-festival-of-las-vegas-to-world-premiere-18-films-screen-more-than-100-in-october/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is Chad Clinton Freeman?**
Chad Clinton Freeman is the founder and director of the PollyGrind Film Festival in Las Vegas, an underground genre-film event founded in 2010. Described by ArrowInTheHead.com as the "new champion of unabashed B-movie sleaze," he is also an independent filmmaker and mass media specialist and a member of the Exploitation Film Preservation Society since 1996.

**What is the PollyGrind Film Festival?**
PollyGrind is an underground film festival in Las Vegas founded by Freeman in 2010, screening independent genre cinema from arthouse to grindhouse. Its 2013 edition drew a record 777 entries and screened more than 100 films from 13 countries, and it was named one of MovieMaker Magazine's "25 Festivals Worth the Entry Fee" for 2012.

**What did Freeman say about The Bunny Game?**
After the film swept the 2011 jury awards, Freeman told Dread Central it was "the epitome of what PollyGrind is about," praising its one-man-crew craft and calling it brutal, thought provoking and unlike anything he had seen — with one of the best on-screen performances ever by an actress, Rodleen Getsic.

**Which awards did The Bunny Game win at PollyGrind?**
*The Bunny Game* won the festival's top jury prize, the Biggest Baddest Mother of the PollyGrind, at the 2011 edition, plus awards for best sound design, best cinematography and best editing; Rodleen Getsic won best overall individual performance for playing Bunny. Rehmeier's follow-up feature *Jonas* premiered at the 2013 festival.
