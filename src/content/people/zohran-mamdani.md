# Zohran Mamdani

## Overview

Zohran Kwame Mamdani is the mayor of New York City, having taken office on January 1, 2026 ([AP](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20251105-290858/)). A democratic socialist and former New York State Assembly member from Queens, he rose from near-total obscurity to the mayoralty on an affordability-focused platform — freezing rents, making city buses free, expanding childcare — after defeating former governor Andrew Cuomo twice, first in the June 2025 Democratic primary and then in the November general election ([AP](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20251105-290858/); [Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/world-news/us-news/zohran-mamdani-officially-defeats-andrew-cuomo-to-secure-new-yorknbsp-democratic-mayoral-primary/3899205/)). He is the city's first Muslim mayor, the first of South Asian heritage, and the first born in Africa, as well as its youngest mayor in more than a century ([AP](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20251105-290858/)). His direct relationship with Donald Trump has been the most watched subplot of his tenure: Trump attacked him relentlessly during the campaign as a "communist lunatic" and threatened federal funding cuts ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/world/democrat-zohran-mamdani-wins-new-york-city-mayor-s-race-article-13653812.html)), then built an uneasy working relationship with him through meetings in November 2025, February 2026 and September 2026 ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/laura-loomer-fumes-as-donald-trump-praises-zohran-mamdani-just-going-to-normalize-communism-101763764012639.html); [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/trump-praises-leftist-mamdani-after-meeting-in-new-york/article71493261.ece)).

## Early life and education

Mamdani was born on October 18, 1991, in Kampala, Uganda, to Indian parents ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/zohran-mamdani-mira-nair-son-nyc-mayor-election-13896220.html)). His mother is Mira Nair, the award-winning filmmaker behind *Monsoon Wedding*, *The Namesake* and *Mississippi Masala*; his father, Mahmood Mamdani, is an anthropology professor at Columbia University ([AP](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/international/2025/06/25/fgn98-us-ny-mamdani-profile.html)). The family lived briefly in Cape Town, South Africa, before moving to New York City when Mamdani was seven ([AP](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/international/2025/06/25/fgn98-us-ny-mamdani-profile.html)). He became a naturalized American citizen in 2018, shortly after graduating from college ([AP](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/international/2025/06/25/fgn98-us-ny-mamdani-profile.html)).

He attended the Bronx High School of Science — where, according to his legislative biography, he co-founded the school's first cricket team — and graduated from Bowdoin College in Maine in 2014 with a degree in Africana studies, co-founding the college's Students for Justice in Palestine chapter while there ([AP](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/international/2025/06/25/fgn98-us-ny-mamdani-profile.html); [Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/zohran-mamdani-mira-nair-son-nyc-mayor-election-13896220.html)). After college he worked as a foreclosure prevention counselor in Queens, helping residents avoid eviction — the job he says inspired him to run for public office ([AP](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/international/2025/06/25/fgn98-us-ny-mamdani-profile.html)). He also had a sideline in local hip-hop, rapping under the names Young Cardamom and later Mr. Cardamom, and describing himself during his first Assembly run as a "B-list rapper" ([AP](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/international/2025/06/25/fgn98-us-ny-mamdani-profile.html)). His 2019 single "Nani," a tribute to his grandmother, found a wider audience as his mayoral campaign gained momentum ([AP](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/international/2025/06/25/fgn98-us-ny-mamdani-profile.html)). In 2025 he married [Rama Duwaji](/people/rama-duwaji/), a Syrian American artist whose illustrations have appeared in *The New Yorker* and *The Washington Post*; the couple lives in the Astoria section of Queens ([AP](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/international/2025/06/25/fgn98-us-ny-mamdani-profile.html); [Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/zohran-mamdani-mira-nair-son-nyc-mayor-election-13896220.html)).

## Assembly years, 2021–2025

Mamdani joined the Democratic Socialists of America in 2017 and was elected to the New York State Assembly in 2020, representing Queens' 36th District from January 2021; he was re-elected unopposed in both 2022 and 2024 ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/zohran-mamdani-mira-nair-son-nyc-mayor-election-13896220.html)). He sponsored 20 bills during his tenure, three of which were signed into law ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/zohran-mamdani-mira-nair-son-nyc-mayor-election-13896220.html)). His most notable legislative accomplishment was pushing through a pilot program that made a handful of city buses free for a year ([AP](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/international/2025/06/25/fgn98-us-ny-mamdani-profile.html)).

His record also carried the controversies that would define his mayoral run. In 2020 he called for defunding the New York Police Department, calling it "this rogue agency" and "racist, anti-queer & a major threat to public safety"; he later apologized for those comments and said he would ask the sitting NYPD commissioner to stay on ([AP](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20251105-290858/)). He was an outspoken supporter of Palestinian causes, calling Israel's military campaign in Gaza a "genocide," saying he would honor the International Criminal Court arrest warrant for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, and refusing to condemn calls to "globalize the intifada" — positions his opponents sought to brand as antisemitic during the mayoral race ([AP](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/international/2025/06/25/fgn98-us-ny-mamdani-profile.html)).

## The 2025 upset

Mamdani officially entered the mayoral race on October 23, 2024, shortly after federal investigations were launched into the administration of Mayor [Eric Adams](/people/eric-adams/) ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/explainers/zohran-mamdani-mira-nair-son-nyc-mayor-election-13896220.html)). He was virtually unknown citywide, polling around 1 percent early in the race, while former governor Andrew Cuomo — attempting a comeback four years after resigning amid sexual-harassment allegations he continues to deny — was the presumed favorite ([AP](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20251105-290858/); [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2025-07-01/ty-article/.premium/zohran-mamdani-officially-wins-democratic-primary-for-new-york-city-mayor/00000197-c6f2-d0a5-a3ff-eeffd66f0000)). Mamdani's campaign — built on grassroots organizing, small-dollar donations and viral social-media videos — centered on affordability: freezing rents on rent-stabilized units, building affordable housing, raising the minimum wage to $30 an hour by 2030, making buses free, and raising taxes on the city's wealthiest residents ([AP](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20251105-290858/)).

On the night of June 24, 2025, Mamdani declared victory in the Democratic primary from a Queens rooftop bar after Cuomo conceded; the AP formally called the race on July 1 after the city's ranked-choice tabulation showed Mamdani beating Cuomo by 12 percentage points, with more than 545,000 votes — the highest total for a Democratic mayoral primary winner in decades ([AP](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/international/2025/06/25/fgn98-us-ny-mamdani-profile.html); [Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/world-news/us-news/zohran-mamdani-officially-defeats-andrew-cuomo-to-secure-new-yorknbsp-democratic-mayoral-primary/3899205/)). Cuomo then relaunched as an independent for the general election. On November 4, 2025, Mamdani defeated Cuomo and Republican Curtis Sliwa, with more than 2 million ballots cast — the highest turnout in a New York City mayoral race since 1969 ([AP](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20251105-290858/); [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/world/democrat-zohran-mamdani-wins-new-york-city-mayor-s-race-article-13653812.html)).

## Trump: from "communist lunatic" to an uneasy working relationship

Trump's attacks on Mamdani began during the campaign and were unsparing. He repeatedly labeled Mamdani a "communist" — a characterization news outlets noted was false, given Mamdani's self-described democratic socialism — and threatened to cut federal funds to New York if he won; on the eve of the election, Trump endorsed Cuomo ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/world/democrat-zohran-mamdani-wins-new-york-city-mayor-s-race-article-13653812.html)). In a mid-2025 Fox News interview he said, "He's not a socialist, he is a communist... I mean, he's a communist, he's gonna be mayor of New York," warned that "if he does get in... they're not getting any money," and dismissed the prospect of a Mamdani victory as "inconceivable" because he was a "pure communist" candidate ([Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/world-news/us-news/zohran-mamdani-officially-defeats-andrew-cuomo-to-secure-new-yorknbsp-democratic-mayoral-primary/3899205/)). He also falsely alleged that Mamdani — a naturalized U.S. citizen — was in the country illegally, threatened to have him deported ("I'll get him out") and to arrest him if he obstructed federal immigration enforcement, and warned that Mamdani had "better behave otherwise he is going to have some big problems" ([Livemint](https://www.livemint.com/news/donald-trump-warns-zohran-mamdani-to-behave-says-nyc-mayoral-hopeful-is-in-a-honeymoon-phase/amp-11751944877177.html)).

After the election, the tone shifted. On November 21, 2025, Trump met the mayor-elect at the White House, and the two presented a united front, each expressing a wish to work together for New York City — a rapport that visibly angered parts of the MAGA base, with activist Laura Loomer asking on X, "So we are just going to normalize communism?" ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/laura-loomer-fumes-as-donald-trump-praises-zohran-mamdani-just-going-to-normalize-communism-101763764012639.html)). The two met again in February 2026, when they discussed a proposed 12,000-home housing development that would require substantial federal funding ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/trump-praises-leftist-mamdani-after-meeting-in-new-york/article71493261.ece)).

## September 2026: praise at Gracie Mansion, friction over veterans' funding

On September 21, 2026, Trump visited Mamdani at Gracie Mansion — the mayor's official residence — for about an hour of closed-door talks, their third public meeting since the election ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/trump-praises-leftist-mamdani-after-meeting-in-new-york/article71493261.ece)). Trump, in New York ahead of the United Nations General Assembly, told Mamdani at a joint news conference: "Hopefully, you're going to be a great [mayor]. I'll be able to say you're going to be a great one, and you're off to a start" ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/trump-praises-leftist-mamdani-after-meeting-in-new-york/article71493261.ece)). The two discussed immigration enforcement — Mamdani said he expressed "very strong opinions" about the administration's culling of Temporary Protected Status for thousands of immigrants — and the 12,000-home building proposal, which would need an estimated $21 billion in federal funding ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/trump-praises-leftist-mamdani-after-meeting-in-new-york/article71493261.ece)). Mamdani said he would keep working with Trump despite "many disagreements," and pointedly welcomed all members of the press to the joint news conference — days after Trump banned CNN, MS NOW and Politico from the White House ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/trump-praises-leftist-mamdani-after-meeting-in-new-york/article71493261.ece)).

The warmth had limits. On September 25, the Mamdani administration announced it would pull New York City out of the [U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs](/organizations/us-department-of-veterans-affairs/)' Grant and Per Diem program — which helps veterans transition out of homelessness — as of October 1, alleging the Trump administration would have required the city to discriminate against transgender veterans seeking shelter to receive the funding ([The City Reporter](https://www.thecityreporter.nyc/newsletter/mamdani-admin-ditches-us-veterans-program/)). A VA spokesperson called the city's allegation "false," saying the department had evaluated the Borden Avenue Veterans Residence shelter in Long Island City and "determined that it could not justify a larger award," leaving the future funding of the shelter — which serves more than 200 veterans — uncertain ([The City Reporter](https://www.thecityreporter.nyc/newsletter/mamdani-admin-ditches-us-veterans-program/)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not assert three claims that appeared in the original research brief but could not be verified against any source read in full: (1) that Trump gave a September 22, 2026 Newsmax interview calling Mamdani "a communist" who would "destroy New York City" and "an enemy of our country" — no record of such an interview was found, and the verifiable record for those dates shows Trump praising Mamdani; (2) that VA Secretary Doug Collins sent Mamdani a September 23–24, 2026 letter accusing him of misusing the NYC Veterans Advisory Board for "woke activism" — no such letter was found; (3) that HUD Secretary Scott Turner threatened around September 23, 2026 to halt federal funding over a city "militia" — no such threat was found. It does not state Mamdani's mayoral ordinal (sources conflict between 111th and 112th), exact general-election vote percentages (not given in any fully read tier1/tier2 source), or inauguration details beyond the AP-sourced January 1 start date. The characterization of Mamdani as a "communist" is Trump's, reported as his rhetoric and flagged as false by the outlets covering it; Mamdani's own stated identity is democratic socialist.
