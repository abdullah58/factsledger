## The columnist who bet against Pete Hegseth

Paul Waldman is the political columnist and author who made one of the year's boldest cabinet predictions. In "[Pete Hegseth will be the next to fall](/people/pete-hegseth/)," written for Aaron Rupar's independent publication Public Notice, he opened with a disclaimer — "As ill-advised as it is to make predictions in politics, we'll offer this one" — and then offered it anyway: "Hegseth will be the next Trump cabinet secretary to be fired or forced to resign, and it won't be long" ([Public Notice](https://www.publicnotice.co/p/pete-hegseth-fired)).

Waldman's theory of Trump-era firing was as blunt as the prediction. "Being bad at your job won't get you the boot in Donald Trump's government; for that, you have to become enough of a public embarrassment that he decides you're reflecting poorly on him," he wrote — adding that Trump would fire Hegseth for "the pettiest of reasons," and "maybe he'll do it on a whim because he thinks it will make for good TV" ([Public Notice](https://www.publicnotice.co/p/pete-hegseth-fired)). The piece surveyed weeks of turmoil at the Pentagon: a testosterone-screening policy that was instituted and then rescinded amid widespread mockery, flag-officer purges, pervasive paranoia about leaks, and a looming shortage of critical munitions — with Trump reportedly angry at Hegseth at Camp David over depleted stockpiles ([Public Notice](https://www.publicnotice.co/p/pete-hegseth-fired)). "Hegseth is just about there," Waldman concluded ([Public Notice](https://www.publicnotice.co/p/pete-hegseth-fired)).

The piece also carried a link to paulwaldman.substack.com, confirming that Waldman publishes on Substack in addition to his other platforms ([Public Notice](https://www.publicnotice.co/p/pete-hegseth-fired)).

## An MSNBC columnist, a Public Notice regular, a Substack writer

Waldman is an American political columnist and author who writes The Cross Section on Substack ([Leigh Bureau](https://assets-global.website-files.com/64afb137c52becee3aca95ce/65fc367c3c6155f885a31a75_Waldman%20-%20BioProfile.pdf)). He is an opinion columnist for [MSNBC](/organizations/msnbc/) ([Leigh Bureau](https://assets-global.website-files.com/64afb137c52becee3aca95ce/65fc367c3c6155f885a31a75_Waldman%20-%20BioProfile.pdf)), and he cohosts the Boundary Issues podcast ([Leigh Bureau](https://assets-global.website-files.com/64afb137c52becee3aca95ce/65fc367c3c6155f885a31a75_Waldman%20-%20BioProfile.pdf)).

At Public Notice, Waldman is both a contributor and the regular co-host of the PN Pod with Aaron Rupar — "I'm thrilled to have the great Paul Waldman onboard as my regular co-host," Rupar announced when the podcast debuted ([Public Notice](https://www.publicnotice.co/p/trumps-corruption-as-2026s-overarching)). The PN Pod goes live every Friday at 2pm eastern ([Public Notice](https://www.publicnotice.co/p/trumps-corruption-as-2026s-overarching)). Public Notice itself is Rupar's independent, reader-supported publication on politics and media — "fair, not impartial," in its own words ([Public Notice](https://www.publicnotice.co/about)).

These current roles update the older biographical framing, which presents him primarily as a Washington Post blogger and American Prospect writer. He did hold those positions: the Leigh bio notes that he wrote for the American Prospect, The Week, and the Washington Post, where he wrote "The Plum Line" alongside Greg Sargent ([Leigh Bureau](https://assets-global.website-files.com/64afb137c52becee3aca95ce/65fc367c3c6155f885a31a75_Waldman%20-%20BioProfile.pdf)). His billmoyers.com author page describes him as a weekly columnist and senior writer for The American Prospect who also wrote for the Plum Line blog at the Post and The Week ([billmoyers.com](https://billmoyers.com/author/paulwaldman/)).

## The books

Waldman co-authored *White Rural Rage: The Threat to American Democracy* with Tom Schaller, and it debuted on both the New York Times and Amazon bestseller lists ([Leigh Bureau](https://assets-global.website-files.com/64afb137c52becee3aca95ce/65fc367c3c6155f885a31a75_Waldman%20-%20BioProfile.pdf)). He is also the author of four other books on politics and the media: *Being Right Is Not Enough: What Progressives Can Learn from Conservative Success*, *The Press Effect: Politicians, Journalists, and the Stories That Shape the Political World*, *Fraud: The Strategy Behind the Bush Lies and Why the Media Didn't Tell You*, and *Free Ride: John McCain and the Media* ([Leigh Bureau](https://assets-global.website-files.com/64afb137c52becee3aca95ce/65fc367c3c6155f885a31a75_Waldman%20-%20BioProfile.pdf)).
