**John McClain was a veteran music executive who served as co-executor of the [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) estate alongside [John Branca](/people/john-branca/) from 2009 until his death on May 26, 2026 ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-michael-jackson-estate-dead-age-71-11984782); [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/explained-paris-jackson-michael-jackson-estate-lawsuit-625000-legal-bonuses-john-branca-john-mcclain-court-ruling-1503593875.html)).** A Los Angeles native raised inside the city's jazz world — his father opened the famous It Club, where Miles Davis and John Coltrane played — McClain was the behind-the-scenes half of the estate's famous "John and John" partnership: while Branca took the spotlight, McClain, by the Statesman's account, stayed "virtually invisible" and did the music ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/); [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/explained-paris-jackson-michael-jackson-estate-lawsuit-625000-legal-bonuses-john-branca-john-mcclain-court-ruling-1503593875.html)). He engineered Janet Jackson's *Control*, initiated the Death Row Records deal, and helped run the estate that earned more than $3 billion after Jackson's death ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/); [PEOPLE](https://people.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-michael-jackson-estate-dead-age-71-11984782)).

## The A&R man who made *Control*

McClain's career started with instruments, not contracts: piano lessons at age 3, then guitar after falling for Jimi Hendrix, followed by work as a session guitarist for Gladys Knight, Diana Ross, Lionel Richie and Shalamar — and as musical director for R&B act the Silvers ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/); [PEOPLE](https://people.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-michael-jackson-estate-dead-age-71-11984782)). In 1984 he joined A&M Records as director of black music, and there he made the intervention that defined his A&R reputation. Janet Jackson, by the estate's own statement, had "essentially been written off by the label" — McClain tapped the young production team of Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis, and the trio's collaboration produced her first blockbuster, *Control*, then *Rhythm Nation* ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/); [PEOPLE](https://people.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-michael-jackson-estate-dead-age-71-11984782)). **The pattern repeated everywhere he went: he found undervalued talent — Janet written off, a Dr. Dre session he happened into at Interscope in 1989 that led him to initiate the Death Row Records financing-and-distribution deal — and turned it into blockbuster value** ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/)).

The rest of the executive record is a catalog of era-defining bets: at A&M he also signed Human League, Atlantic Starr and Jesse Johnson, and masterminded the Herb Alpert–Janet Jackson pairing on "Diamonds" ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/)). Lured to the newly launched Interscope by Ted Field in 1989, he pushed the label into gospel and R&B by signing Kirk Franklin and Teddy Riley, both of which produced multimillion-selling urban hits ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/)). He returned to A&M in 1997 as president of Black music — reigniting Atlantic Starr and Barry White, signing Ice Cube — and in 2001, at DreamWorks, signed Ron Isley, whose *The Isley Brothers featuring Ronald Isley aka Mr. Biggs* went straight to No. 1, followed by his pairing of Burt Bacharach with Ron Isley on the acclaimed *Here I Am* ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/)). The *Los Angeles Times* once wrote that he was "in the world's upper echelon of music industry power brokers," and Jimmy Iovine told the paper: "He is one of the great music men in this business" ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/)).

## The quiet half of the estate

Michael Jackson named McClain co-executor in his will alongside Branca — at the 2002 American Music Awards Jackson had already named him among "people that are very important in my life" ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/); [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/explained-paris-jackson-michael-jackson-estate-lawsuit-625000-legal-bonuses-john-branca-john-mcclain-court-ruling-1503593875.html)). Together with Branca, McClain oversaw the two posthumous albums *Michael* and *Xscape* (he is credited on tracks including "Love Never Felt So Good" and "Much Too Soon"), the two Cirque du Soleil productions, the four-time Tony-winning Broadway musical *MJ*, two Spike Lee documentaries, the concert film *This Is It* — and, most recently, the biopic *Michael*, produced by Graham King alongside Branca and McClain, starring Jackson's nephew [Jaafar Jackson](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/) in the title role ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/); [PEOPLE](https://people.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-michael-jackson-estate-dead-age-71-11984782); [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/us/entertainment/who-is-john-mcclain-co-executor-of-mj-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/articleshow/131342015.cms)). Where Branca "often assumed a place in the spotlight," PEOPLE notes, "McClain stayed behind the scenes" — doing the creative and commercial work of what Branca told *Hits* magazine in 2016 was really "manage that brand" ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-michael-jackson-estate-dead-age-71-11984782)). The estate's later years were not dispute-free: McClain and Branca clashed in court with [Paris Jackson](/people/paris-jackson/), who accused them of "operat[ing] in the dark" to pad their pockets — an accusation both men denied — and a judge ruled on May 13, 2026 that $625,000 in third-party law-firm bonuses had to be returned to the estate ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-michael-jackson-estate-dead-age-71-11984782)).

## Death and the estate's next chapter

McClain died on Tuesday, May 26, 2026, in Los Angeles, aged 71, of complications from a fall, according to an estate statement; PEOPLE confirmed the news through a spokesperson, noting TMZ first reported it ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/); [PEOPLE](https://people.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-michael-jackson-estate-dead-age-71-11984782)). Branca's statement called him "one of the great innovators in the world of music and music marketing": "When we were named in Michael's will, I knew that he would bring great insight into Michael's music and that his friendship and dedication to Michael would underscore all the great projects that we could bring to the world. ... It is difficult to imagine a world without him" ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/)). The Economic Times reported he had been ill for several years, though whether that contributed to his death was unclear ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/us/entertainment/who-is-john-mcclain-co-executor-of-mj-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/articleshow/131342015.cms)).

His death leaves one honest open question: no full-read source has yet confirmed a successor co-executor for the estate, and this profile does not guess at one.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who was John McClain?**
A veteran music executive and producer — the behind-the-scenes half of the Michael Jackson estate, which he co-ran with attorney John Branca from 2009 until his death on May 26, 2026. Before the estate, he was an A&R executive at A&M, Interscope and DreamWorks known for Janet Jackson's *Control*.

**How did John McClain die?**
He died on May 26, 2026, in Los Angeles, aged 71, of complications from a fall, according to an estate statement. TMZ first reported the news; PEOPLE confirmed it through a spokesperson.

**How was John McClain related to Michael Jackson?**
He was a close friend of Michael Jackson and the Jackson family — Michael named him among the most important people in his life at the 2002 American Music Awards — and, separately, his co-executor. The Statesman describes him as a "childhood friend of Jackson's."

**What did John McClain do at A&M and Interscope?**
At A&M (from 1984) he paired Janet Jackson with Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis to make *Control*, signed Human League and Atlantic Starr, and masterminded the Herb Alpert–Janet "Diamonds" duet. At Interscope (from 1989) he initiated the Death Row Records financing-and-distribution deal and signed gospel and R&B deals with Kirk Franklin and Teddy Riley.

**Who runs the Michael Jackson estate now that John McClain is dead?**
John Branca remains an executor. No full-read source has confirmed whether a successor co-executor has been appointed; the question is unresolved as of this profile's publication.
