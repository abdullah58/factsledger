## Overview

Roli Saxena is the technology executive who led NextRoll from inside its largest business: president of [AdRoll](/organizations/adroll/) for more than eighteen months, then chief executive of the whole company from November 14, 2022, and the CEO who, in August 2025, reunified the portfolio under the AdRoll brand ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/379777/roli-saxena-named-ceo-of-nextroll.html?edition=128281); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/adroll-unifies-brand-with-ai-assistant-launch-eliminating-rollworks-complexity/)). She succeeded interim CEO and chief financial officer Peter Krivkovich, who said her experience and compassion made her the obvious choice to take over, and that he felt confident stepping away knowing she would drive incredible growth, just as she had done with AdRoll ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/379777/roli-saxena-named-ceo-of-nextroll.html?edition=128281)).

## LinkedIn and Brex: the go-to-market apprenticeship

Saxena's path to the top ran through go-to-market leadership at two companies in transition. At LinkedIn she headed sales and customer success teams, in roles that ran through the company's initial public offering and its growth to more than three billion dollars in revenue ([MarTech Series](https://martechseries.com/technology/nextroll-promotes-industry-veteran-roli-saxena-to-ceo/)). She also co-founded the LinkedIn Women's Network program, which increased women's representation in senior leadership roles ([MarTech Series](https://martechseries.com/technology/nextroll-promotes-industry-veteran-roli-saxena-to-ceo/)). At Brex she served as chief customer officer, where she transitioned the company from a single product to a multi-product software-as-a-service company, accelerating its growth and transformation, with responsibility for customer revenue and for launching Brex banking products during a period of rapid growth ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/379777/roli-saxena-named-ceo-of-nextroll.html?edition=128281); [MarTech Series](https://martechseries.com/technology/nextroll-promotes-industry-veteran-roli-saxena-to-ceo/)).

That background — moving a company from one product to many, and from a single revenue motion to several — matched the stage NextRoll was in when it brought her in to run AdRoll, and MarTech Series' promotion report stressed her work growing culture and diversity efforts alongside that commercial growth ([MarTech Series](https://martechseries.com/technology/nextroll-promotes-industry-veteran-roli-saxena-to-ceo/)). She was recognized as one of the San Francisco Business Times' Most Influential Women in the Bay Area, sits on the board of Culture Amp, and is an angel investor with Operator Collective, an organization leveling the investing landscape for women and people of color ([MarTech Series](https://martechseries.com/technology/nextroll-promotes-industry-veteran-roli-saxena-to-ceo/)).

## President, then CEO, then the reunification

Saxena spent more than a year and a half as president of AdRoll, the eCommerce-focused business unit, before her promotion to group CEO ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/379777/roli-saxena-named-ceo-of-nextroll.html?edition=128281)). During that time she helped shepherd growth and traction in software and partnerships, hiring new talent and expanding opportunities with partners like Shopify ([MarTech Series](https://martechseries.com/technology/nextroll-promotes-industry-veteran-roli-saxena-to-ceo/)). As CEO she oversaw overall company performance for both of NextRoll's business units — AdRoll, the marketing and advertising platform for eCommerce brands, and [RollWorks](/organizations/rollworks/), the account-based marketing platform for business-to-business companies — and she temporarily continued to serve as president of AdRoll alongside the CEO role ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/379777/roli-saxena-named-ceo-of-nextroll.html?edition=128281); [MarTech Series](https://martechseries.com/technology/nextroll-promotes-industry-veteran-roli-saxena-to-ceo/)).

Her signature move as CEO was simplification. On August 27, 2025, as chief executive at NextRoll, she announced the brand unification that consolidated the portfolio under the AdRoll brand, with RollWorks officially becoming AdRoll ABM and a new AI Assistant launching for campaign creation and optimization ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/adroll-unifies-brand-with-ai-assistant-launch-eliminating-rollworks-complexity/)). Her stated rationale was the seismic shifts marketers faced, from the rise of AI to new realities in measurement and data privacy: by unifying the brand, the company would bring clarity to a crowded market and deliver a connected advertising platform that turned disruption into opportunity ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/adroll-unifies-brand-with-ai-assistant-launch-eliminating-rollworks-complexity/)). It was the reverse of the 2019 rename she had inherited — NextRoll folding back into AdRoll — carried out by the executive who had run AdRoll itself.
