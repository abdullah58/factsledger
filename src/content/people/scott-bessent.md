## Overview

Scott Bessent is the 79th U.S. Secretary of the Treasury, confirmed by the Senate 68–29 on January 27, 2025 and sworn in the next day — the first openly gay person to hold the office ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/scott-bessent-confirmed-as-treasury-secretary-giving-him-a-key-role-in-extending-trumps-tax-cuts/)). Before government he spent four decades in global investment management, most of it around George Soros: hired by Soros Fund Management in 1991, a leading member of the group behind the famous 1992 "Black Wednesday" bet against the British pound, and later the fund's chief investment officer before founding his own macro hedge fund, Key Square Capital Management, in 2015 ([Associated Press](https://barchart.com/story/news/29729833/trump-chooses-former-soros-money-manager-scott-bessent-to-be-treasury-secretary); [U.S. House of Representatives](http://docs.house.gov/meetings/WM/WM00/20260604/119352/HHRG-119-WM00-Bio-BessentS-20260604.pdf)).

But the résumé version of Bessent misses what the job actually became in 2026. On June 12, 2026, TechCrunch reported, citing The Wall Street Journal, that Amazon CEO Andy Jassy told Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and other government officials that Amazon researchers had used Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 to obtain information that could be used in cyberattacks. Amazon said it does not share details of such discussions, so the account remains attributed reporting rather than an independently confirmed personal warning ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/13/amazon-ceo-reportedly-raised-anthropic-model-concerns-before-government-crackdown/)). Months earlier he had been doubling as acting commissioner of the IRS, the seventh person to lead the agency in a single chaotic year ([Associated Press](https://www.srnnews.com/irs-chief-is-out-after-clashing-with-trump-administration-bessent-is-named-acting-commissioner/); [Fox Rothschild](https://www.foxrothschild.com/publications/scott-bessent-no-longer-serving-as-irs-acting-commissioner)). And in September 2026 he personally announced the extension of the U.S.–China trade truce to January 10, 2027 ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/business/us-china-trade-truce-busan-agreement-extension-january-10-14047945.html)).

## The June 2026 Fable 5 call

The strangest episode of Bessent's tenure began with a reported phone call on June 12, 2026. TechCrunch, citing The Wall Street Journal, reported that Jassy told Bessent and other government officials about Amazon researchers using Claude Fable 5 to obtain information that could be used in cyberattacks. The account was attributed to people familiar with the matter; Amazon said it did not share discussion details. TechCrunch also reported that the government subsequently imposed export controls on Fable 5 and Mythos 5 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/13/amazon-ceo-reportedly-raised-anthropic-model-concerns-before-government-crackdown/)).

The reported episode placed the former macro trader, whose career was built on currency crises, in an AI-security response story involving Jassy, Bessent and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick. The timing and details remain those of attributed media reports, not an independently confirmed account by FactsLedger ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/13/amazon-ceo-reportedly-raised-anthropic-model-concerns-before-government-crackdown/)).

## Running two agencies: acting IRS commissioner

On August 8, 2025, Bessent took on a second job. IRS Commissioner Billy Long was ousted after clashing with the Trump administration — just weeks after the Senate had confirmed Long 53–47 — and Bessent was named acting commissioner, running Treasury and the tax agency at once ([Associated Press](https://www.srnnews.com/irs-chief-is-out-after-clashing-with-trump-administration-bessent-is-named-acting-commissioner/)).

He held the acting post until March 6, 2026, when the Federal Vacancies Reform Act's 210-day limit on acting service expired ([Fox Rothschild](https://www.foxrothschild.com/publications/scott-bessent-no-longer-serving-as-irs-acting-commissioner)). The turnover was historic in its own right: Bessent was the seventh person to lead the IRS in 2025 alone, a churn that mirrored the administration's broader remaking of the federal workforce ([Fox Rothschild](https://www.foxrothschild.com/publications/scott-bessent-no-longer-serving-as-irs-acting-commissioner)).

## The China file and the September 2026 truce

Bessent's signature foreign-economic moment came on September 23, 2026. After an unscheduled meeting with Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng at World Bank headquarters in Washington, he told Fox News that the United States and China would extend their bilateral trade truce — the "Busan Agreement" — from November 10 to January 10, 2027 ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/business/us-china-trade-truce-busan-agreement-extension-january-10-14047945.html)).

The truce dated to the Trump–Xi meeting in Busan, South Korea, in late 2025, which had de-escalated a sharp confrontation: the U.S. reduced some tariffs on Chinese goods while China paused retaliatory measures, eased restrictions on critical minerals and rare earths, and committed to purchases of American agricultural products including soybeans ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/business/us-china-trade-truce-busan-agreement-extension-january-10-14047945.html)). Bessent framed the extension as a bid for something bigger — "a bigger agreement instead of just a series of smaller ones" — while publicly flagging that Chinese delivery had been imperfect: "There are some deliverables that have not been perfect on the Chinese side" ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/business/us-china-trade-truce-busan-agreement-extension-january-10-14047945.html)). He also suggested more announcements could follow, including further Chinese purchases of U.S. farm products and financial-services agreements, just ahead of Xi Jinping's planned meeting with Trump in Washington ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/business/us-china-trade-truce-busan-agreement-extension-january-10-14047945.html)).

## Bond buybacks and the 5% yield

By September 2026, Bessent was also fighting a two-front war at home: the 10-year Treasury yield had hit 5%, and critics accused him of using Treasury bond buybacks to suppress yields ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/21/scott-bessent-champions-dollar-dominance-across-global-markets-and-stablecoins)). He rejected the charge, insisting the buybacks of longer-dated bonds were meant to improve market liquidity and manage the maturity structure of a Treasury market worth more than $30 trillion — not to control rates ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/21/scott-bessent-champions-dollar-dominance-across-global-markets-and-stablecoins)).

His broader defense leaned on dollar-dominance metrics: the dollar remains on one side of 89.2% of foreign-exchange transactions, and the overwhelming majority of stablecoins are pegged to the dollar. He cited record median household income, a historically low official poverty rate, and the Atlanta Fed's 5.1% annualized estimate for third-quarter GDP growth ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/21/scott-bessent-champions-dollar-dominance-across-global-markets-and-stablecoins)). He even claimed Saudi Arabia's exit from the China-backed mBridge digital-currency platform as a win for the dollar — though the kingdom said it had merely completed a planned proof of concept in May 2025, and the platform continues elsewhere ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/21/scott-bessent-champions-dollar-dominance-across-global-markets-and-stablecoins)).

## From Soros to Key Square

Bessent's official biography describes 40 years in the global investment management business across 60 countries, and a reputation as a currency and fixed-income specialist ([U.S. House of Representatives](http://docs.house.gov/meetings/WM/WM00/20260604/119352/HHRG-119-WM00-Bio-BessentS-20260604.pdf)). Hired by Soros Fund Management in 1991, he became managing partner of its London office, serving from 1991 to 2000 ([U.S. House of Representatives](http://docs.house.gov/meetings/WM/WM00/20260604/119352/HHRG-119-WM00-Bio-BessentS-20260604.pdf)). In September 1992 he was a leading member of the Soros group behind the famous bet against the British pound on "Black Wednesday": the pound collapsed, losing 25% of its value against the dollar, in a trade that reportedly made the fund around $1 billion ([Associated Press](https://barchart.com/story/news/29729833/trump-chooses-former-soros-money-manager-scott-bessent-to-be-treasury-secretary); [Scotsman Guide](https://www.scotsmanguide.com/news/will-treasury-secretary-bessent-undermine-the-feds-powell/)).

He left Soros in 2000 to start his own hedge fund, which closed in 2005, then returned as Soros Fund Management's chief investment officer from 2011 to 2015 — a stint that included a profitable 2013 trade shorting the Japanese yen ([Scotsman Guide](https://www.scotsmanguide.com/news/will-treasury-secretary-bessent-undermine-the-feds-powell/)). In 2015 he founded Key Square Capital Management, serving as its chief executive and chief investment officer; the fund was launched with fellow Soros employee Michael Germino and bankrolled by Soros himself ([U.S. House of Representatives](http://docs.house.gov/meetings/WM/WM00/20260604/119352/HHRG-119-WM00-Bio-BessentS-20260604.pdf); [Scotsman Guide](https://www.scotsmanguide.com/news/will-treasury-secretary-bessent-undermine-the-feds-powell/)). He earned a B.A. from Yale University and later returned as an adjunct professor teaching economic history ([U.S. House of Representatives](http://docs.house.gov/meetings/WM/WM00/20260604/119352/HHRG-119-WM00-Bio-BessentS-20260604.pdf)).

The political arc is the irony his critics and supporters both note. A past supporter of Democrats who once worked for liberal megadonor George Soros, Bessent donated $1 million to Trump's 2017 inaugural committee, wrote a 2022 article praising Trump for alerting the world to expansionist China, and became a major fundraiser for the 2024 campaign ([Scotsman Guide](https://www.scotsmanguide.com/news/will-treasury-secretary-bessent-undermine-the-feds-powell/); [Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/scott-bessent-confirmed-as-treasury-secretary-giving-him-a-key-role-in-extending-trumps-tax-cuts/)). Trump had considered billionaire John Paulson and Howard Lutnick before settling on Bessent, announcing the nomination on November 22, 2024 ([Associated Press](https://barchart.com/story/news/29729833/trump-chooses-former-soros-money-manager-scott-bessent-to-be-treasury-secretary)).

## Confirmation and agenda

The Senate confirmed Bessent 68–29, with 16 Democrats voting in favor — a relatively comfortable margin that Senate Finance chair Mike Crapo called "one of the easiest votes we could ever take" ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/scott-bessent-confirmed-as-treasury-secretary-giving-him-a-key-role-in-extending-trumps-tax-cuts/)). He arrived with a defined agenda: he had warned of "economic calamity" if Congress failed to renew the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act provisions expiring December 31, 2025, and pushed for 3% annual growth, deficit reduction, and 3 million more barrels a day of domestic oil production ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/scott-bessent-confirmed-as-treasury-secretary-giving-him-a-key-role-in-extending-trumps-tax-cuts/)). On trade he called tariffs a "one [time](/organizations/time/) price adjustment" that is "not inflationary," and in a Fox News op-ed described them as "a useful tool for achieving the president's foreign policy objectives" ([Associated Press](https://barchart.com/story/news/29729833/trump-chooses-former-soros-money-manager-scott-bessent-to-be-treasury-secretary)).

His confirmation was not without friction. Democrats alleged he had avoided nearly $1 million in Medicare taxes tied to his hedge-fund limited partnership; Bessent disputed the liability, said he was in litigation over it, and committed to pay if a court rules against him ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/scott-bessent-confirmed-as-treasury-secretary-giving-him-a-key-role-in-extending-trumps-tax-cuts/)). Senator Elizabeth Warren later attacked him from the Senate floor as a billionaire who thinks "the burden should be just a little bit heavier on working people" ([Scotsman Guide](https://www.scotsmanguide.com/news/will-treasury-secretary-bessent-undermine-the-feds-powell/)). He also arrived with one discarded idea: in October 2024 he had floated naming a "shadow" Federal Reserve chair more than a year before Jerome Powell's term expired, a plan he backed away from after the election — while telling his confirmation hearing that the Fed should remain independent and that U.S. sanctions on Russian oil should be more aggressive ([Associated Press](https://barchart.com/story/news/29729833/trump-chooses-former-soros-money-manager-scott-bessent-to-be-treasury-secretary); [Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/scott-bessent-confirmed-as-treasury-secretary-giving-him-a-key-role-in-extending-trumps-tax-cuts/)).

Beyond policy, his official biography notes the philanthropy: with his family he created the McLeod Rehabilitation Center at the Shriners Children's Hospital in Greenville, South Carolina; he served as a Rockefeller University trustee, supported the Harlem Children's Zone, and is a member of the Economic Club of New York and the Council on Foreign Relations ([U.S. House of Representatives](http://docs.house.gov/meetings/WM/WM00/20260604/119352/HHRG-119-WM00-Bio-BessentS-20260604.pdf)).
