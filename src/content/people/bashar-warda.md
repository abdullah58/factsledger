## Overview

Search results about Bashar Warda converge on the same frame: archbishop since 2010, the ISIS years, the refugee relief, the university. What none of them share is the beginning of the story — that he entered St. Peter's seminary in Baghdad in 1981 at the age of twelve ([National Catholic Register](https://www.ncregister.com/news/chaldean-archbishop-true-christianity-is-a-persecuted-christianity?amp)). That boy seminarian grew into the pastor of Baghdad's largest parish and, after war scattered his flock, into the Chaldean Catholic Archbishop of Erbil — the cleric who administered relief for nearly 20,000 displaced Christian families and then tried to anchor the community's future in schools and a university ([National Catholic Register](https://www.ncregister.com/news/chaldean-archbishop-true-christianity-is-a-persecuted-christianity?amp), [Australian Catholic University](https://www.acu.edu.au/about-acu/news/2019/july/honorable-recognition-for-the-chaldean-archbishop-of-erbil)).

## Formation: a boy in the seminary

"I entered seminary in 1981 at the age of 12," Warda told the National Catholic Register. "After finishing high school, I was ordained in 1993" ([National Catholic Register](https://www.ncregister.com/news/chaldean-archbishop-true-christianity-is-a-persecuted-christianity?amp)). He joined the Redemptorists in 1995 — the order whose initials, CSsR, he carries after his name — and earned a licentiate in moral theology at the Catholic University in Louvain before returning to the Redemptorist mission in Baghdad in 1999 ([National Catholic Register](https://www.ncregister.com/news/chaldean-archbishop-true-christianity-is-a-persecuted-christianity?amp), [Australian Catholic University](https://www.acu.edu.au/about-acu/news/2019/july/honorable-recognition-for-the-chaldean-archbishop-of-erbil)).

## Baghdad's largest parish, and the violence

In 2001 he was asked to be the pastor at "Baghdad's largest parish in southern Baghdad. We had 3,000 families" ([National Catholic Register](https://www.ncregister.com/news/chaldean-archbishop-true-christianity-is-a-persecuted-christianity?amp)). The assignment coincided with the unravelling of Christian life in the capital: "By the end of 2004, the violence, bombing and the killing of Catholic priests had started in Baghdad," he recalled, and during that time three of the seminary staff were kidnapped ([National Catholic Register](https://www.ncregister.com/news/chaldean-archbishop-true-christianity-is-a-persecuted-christianity?amp)). He was asked to direct the seminary, which was moved north out of danger ([National Catholic Register](https://www.ncregister.com/news/chaldean-archbishop-true-christianity-is-a-persecuted-christianity?amp)).

## Archbishop of Erbil

"In 2010, I was enacted as archbishop of Erbil," he said. His archdiocese numbers 28,000 Chaldean Catholics, with another 2,000 Christians from other churches ([National Catholic Register](https://www.ncregister.com/news/chaldean-archbishop-true-christianity-is-a-persecuted-christianity?amp)). The [Cathedral of Saint Joseph in Ankawa](/places/cathedral-of-saint-joseph-ankawa/) is the Chaldean Catholic cathedral of his archdiocese: in August 2014 an estimated 670 families sought refuge there and in the surrounding buildings as Christians fled the Islamic State's Nineveh Plain offensive ([Abouna](https://En.abouna.org/content/6-august-2014-hundred-thousand-faces-suffering-iraqi-christians)).

During the ISIS war, Warda's responsibilities "extended far beyond normal peacetime pastoral duties": he served as the de facto chief administrator of relief services for displaced people, overseeing food, housing, education and pastoral care for nearly 20,000 threatened Christian families ([Australian Catholic University](https://www.acu.edu.au/about-acu/news/2019/july/honorable-recognition-for-the-chaldean-archbishop-of-erbil)). He became a spokesperson for the persecuted Christian and Yezidi people of northern Iraq, appearing in major news outlets around the world ([Australian Catholic University](https://www.acu.edu.au/about-acu/news/2019/july/honorable-recognition-for-the-chaldean-archbishop-of-erbil)).

## The builder: schools, a university, a hospital

Warda's answer to displacement was institution-building. In 2015 he founded the Catholic University of Erbil, deliberately open to students and scholars of all faiths ([Australian Catholic University](https://www.acu.edu.au/about-acu/news/2019/july/honorable-recognition-for-the-chaldean-archbishop-of-erbil)). The ambition predated the opening: in his Register interview he described plans for a university that could attract "as many as 5,000 Muslim students," alongside a hospital project that would create 300 jobs ([National Catholic Register](https://www.ncregister.com/news/chaldean-archbishop-true-christianity-is-a-persecuted-christianity?amp)). He went on to found several Catholic schools in Baghdad and the Mar Qardakh School in Erbil, which received its International Baccalaureate accreditation in 2015 ([Australian Catholic University](https://www.acu.edu.au/about-acu/news/2019/july/honorable-recognition-for-the-chaldean-archbishop-of-erbil)). He teaches at Babel Theological Seminary in Erbil and has been heavily involved in establishing the Maryamana Hospital there ([Australian Catholic University](https://www.acu.edu.au/about-acu/news/2019/july/honorable-recognition-for-the-chaldean-archbishop-of-erbil)).

## Recognition

In 2016 he received the Pope Francis Charity and Leadership Award from Caritas in Veritate International ([Australian Catholic University](https://www.acu.edu.au/about-acu/news/2019/july/honorable-recognition-for-the-chaldean-archbishop-of-erbil)). On 23 July 2019, Australian Catholic University conferred on him an honorary doctorate, presented at the St Mary of the Cross MacKillop Chapel in Fitzroy, in recognition of his service to the Christian community in the Middle East — especially in Catholic education and the establishment of the Catholic University in Erbil ([Australian Catholic University](https://www.acu.edu.au/about-acu/news/2019/july/honorable-recognition-for-the-chaldean-archbishop-of-erbil)). He has been a distinguished speaker at the University of Notre Dame, Georgetown University, the University of San Diego and the Franciscan University of Steubenville ([Australian Catholic University](https://www.acu.edu.au/about-acu/news/2019/july/honorable-recognition-for-the-chaldean-archbishop-of-erbil)).

His own summary of the faith he leads is the line that gave the Register interview its title: "true Christianity is a persecuted Christianity" ([National Catholic Register](https://www.ncregister.com/news/chaldean-archbishop-true-christianity-is-a-persecuted-christianity?amp)).
