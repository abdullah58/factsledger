# Frank Fertitta

Frank Joseph Fertitta III is an American billionaire businessman best known as the chairman and chief executive officer of Red Rock Resorts, the parent company of Station Casinos, the locals-focused casino empire his father founded in Las Vegas in 1976. ([Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/blog/5-richest-casino-owners-usa/)) ([Red Rock Resorts proxy statement](https://www.stocktitan.net:443/sec-filings/RRR/def-14a-red-rock-resorts-inc-definitive-proxy-statement-629a95fa8b70.html)) Along with his younger brother Lorenzo, he owned the Ultimate Fighting Championship from 2001 to 2016, buying it for $2 million and selling it for an estimated $4 billion. ([Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/blog/5-richest-casino-owners-usa/)) ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/ufc-sold-to-wme-img-led-buyers-for-4-billion-report/))

He was born in 1962; Red Rock Resorts' April 2026 proxy statement lists him at age 64. ([Red Rock Resorts proxy statement](https://www.stocktitan.net:443/sec-filings/RRR/def-14a-red-rock-resorts-inc-definitive-proxy-statement-629a95fa8b70.html))

## The $70 million anniversary gift

On September 9, 2026, Frank and Lorenzo Fertitta stood before a surprised gathering of employees at the Red Rock Casino Resort & Spa and announced one of the largest employee stock awards in Las Vegas history: more than $70 million worth of Red Rock Resorts Class A stock to nearly 10,000 team members across Station Casinos, as part of the company's 50th anniversary celebration. ([InterGame](https://www.intergameonline.com/land-based-gaming/news/station-casinos-employees-awarded-70m-in-stock))

The formula was deliberately simple and tenure-based. Team members employed as of September 9 received $1,000 in Red Rock Resorts Class A stock for every year of service. ([InterGame](https://www.intergameonline.com/land-based-gaming/news/station-casinos-employees-awarded-70m-in-stock)) Seven employees who have been with Station Casinos since the 1970s were set to receive more than $45,000 each in equity. ([InterGame](https://www.intergameonline.com/land-based-gaming/news/station-casinos-employees-awarded-70m-in-stock))

The announcement carried a deliberate echo of the company's founder. "Our father founded this company on a few simple principles: serve the local community, deliver great value and most importantly, take care of our team members," Frank said, adding that the award "reflects those values and recognises the people who have brought them to life every day for the past 50 years." ([InterGame](https://www.intergameonline.com/land-based-gaming/news/station-casinos-employees-awarded-70m-in-stock)) Lorenzo called the award "an investment in our nearly 10,000 team members, whose dedication and commitment we are deeply thankful for." ([InterGame](https://www.intergameonline.com/land-based-gaming/news/station-casinos-employees-awarded-70m-in-stock))

The stock gift was the centerpiece of a 50th anniversary program that began on July 1, 2026, at Palace Station, where the company's story started, with a fireworks and drone show and the debut of a new brand identity and the "From Vegas, For Vegas, Always Vegas" campaign. ([InterGame](https://www.intergameonline.com/land-based-gaming/news/station-casinos-employees-awarded-70m-in-stock))

## Building the Station Casinos empire

Fertitta grew up inside the business he now runs. His father, Frank Fertitta Jr., launched Station Casinos in 1976 as a single locals-oriented casino, and the company grew into one of the largest operators of neighborhood casinos in Las Vegas. ([Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/blog/5-richest-casino-owners-usa/))

Frank III joined the family company in 1985 as an officer and general manager of Palace Station. He was elected a director and appointed executive vice president and chief operating officer in 1986, became president in 1989, and took the chief executive's job in July 1992, positions he held while the company expanded through the 1990s. ([Red Rock Resorts proxy statement](https://www.stocktitan.net:443/sec-filings/RRR/def-14a-red-rock-resorts-inc-definitive-proxy-statement-629a95fa8b70.html))

After his father retired in 1993, Frank and Lorenzo led Station Casinos through its first public offering, raising $294 million and becoming principal shareholders in the process. ([Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/blog/5-richest-casino-owners-usa/)) The two brothers have run the company together ever since, with Frank as chairman and CEO and Lorenzo as vice chairman of the board of Red Rock Resorts, Inc. ([InterGame](https://www.intergameonline.com/land-based-gaming/news/station-casinos-employees-awarded-70m-in-stock)) ([Red Rock Resorts proxy statement](https://www.stocktitan.net:443/sec-filings/RRR/def-14a-red-rock-resorts-inc-definitive-proxy-statement-629a95fa8b70.html))

## The UFC trade

In 2001, the Fertitta brothers and their business partner [Dana White](/people/dana-white) bought the struggling Ultimate Fighting Championship for $2 million through their company Zuffa LLC. ([Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/blog/5-richest-casino-owners-usa/)) ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/ufc-sold-to-wme-img-led-buyers-for-4-billion-report/)) At the time, mixed martial arts was a fringe, cash-strapped sport with little mainstream acceptance.

Over the next 15 years they invested in talent, regulation, production, and marketing, turning the UFC into a global sports enterprise whose fights reached viewers in more than 156 countries. ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/ufc-sold-to-wme-img-led-buyers-for-4-billion-report/)) In July 2016, they sold the company to an investment group led by WME-IMG, backed by Silver Lake, Kohlberg Kravis Roberts, and Michael Dell's investment firm, for about $4 billion — then the most expensive transaction for a sports organization in history. ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/ufc-sold-to-wme-img-led-buyers-for-4-billion-report/)) Red Rock Resorts' proxy statement dates the brothers' exit from Zuffa to August 2016. ([Red Rock Resorts proxy statement](https://www.stocktitan.net:443/sec-filings/RRR/def-14a-red-rock-resorts-inc-definitive-proxy-statement-629a95fa8b70.html)) Lorenzo stepped down from day-to-day operations while both brothers retained passive minority stakes. ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/ufc-sold-to-wme-img-led-buyers-for-4-billion-report/))

The arc — $2 million in, roughly $4 billion out over a decade and a half — stands among the most profitable investments in sports history. ([Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/blog/5-richest-casino-owners-usa/))

## Red Rock Resorts today

Red Rock Resorts, Inc. is the publicly traded holding company for the Station Casinos properties, with its headquarters at 1505 South Pavilion Center Drive in Las Vegas. ([Red Rock Resorts proxy statement](https://www.stocktitan.net:443/sec-filings/RRR/def-14a-red-rock-resorts-inc-definitive-proxy-statement-629a95fa8b70.html)) Frank Fertitta has chaired its board since the company's formation in September 2015 and served as chief executive officer since May 2016; he had been CEO of the predecessor operating company, Station LLC, since June 2011. ([Red Rock Resorts proxy statement](https://www.stocktitan.net:443/sec-filings/RRR/def-14a-red-rock-resorts-inc-definitive-proxy-statement-629a95fa8b70.html))

Casino industry coverage estimated each brother's net worth at $2.6 billion in late 2025, built on their casino holdings and the proceeds of the UFC sale. ([Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/blog/5-richest-casino-owners-usa/)) The company's hotel-casino portfolio includes Red Rock Casino and Spa in Las Vegas and Sunset Station in Henderson, Nevada, and it also manages casinos for other owners. ([Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/blog/5-richest-casino-owners-usa/))

## Family and philanthropy

Fertitta graduated from the University of Southern California's business school in 1984. ([USC Today](https://today.usc.edu/fertittas-donate-major-gift-to-usc-marshall-for-new-undergraduate-building/)) On October 18, 2012, USC announced that he and his wife Jill had made a naming gift to the university, creating the Jill and Frank Fertitta Hall for the USC Marshall School of Business and establishing the Jill and Frank Fertitta Endowed Chair in Business. ([USC Today](https://today.usc.edu/fertittas-donate-major-gift-to-usc-marshall-for-new-undergraduate-building/))
