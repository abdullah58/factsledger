**Colman Domingo** is an American actor, playwright, and director — an Emmy winner for HBO's *Euphoria* and a two-time Academy Award Best Actor nominee for *Rustin* (2023) and *Sing Sing* (2024) — who plays Jackson family patriarch Joe Jackson in Antoine Fuqua's 2026 Michael Jackson biopic [*Michael*](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/) ([Associated Press](https://www.shootonline.com/newsbriefs/colman-domingo-will-play-joe-jackson-michael-jackson-biopic?qt-recent_and_tags_duplicate=0); [The Source](https://thesource.com/2026/02/02/michael-biopic-colman-domingo-nia-long/)).

Born 28 November 1969 in Philadelphia, Domingo studied journalism at Temple University before quitting at 21, moving to San Francisco, and spending a decade becoming a multi-hyphenate theater artist — a writer, director and producer — before Broadway came calling ([Playbill](https://playbill.com/article/playbill-vaults-today-in-theatre-history-november-28-com-109818); [PhillyVoice](https://www.phillyvoice.com/colman-domingo-joe-jackson-michael-biopic/); [NME](https://www.nme.com/features/film-interviews/colman-domingo-quit-acting-sing-sing-oscars-best-actor-3833004)). He earned a Tony nomination for *The Scottsboro Boys* in 2010, nearly quit acting in 2014 after a racially charged casting rejection, then rebuilt through AMC's *Fear the Walking Dead* (from 2015), the 2022 Emmy as Ali in *Euphoria*, and consecutive Oscar nominations — a run capped by a Time 100 honor in 2024 and co-chairing the 2025 Met Gala ([Playbill](https://playbill.com/article/colman-domingo-will-direct-and-star-in-nat-king-cole-biopic); [NME](https://www.nme.com/features/film-interviews/colman-domingo-quit-acting-sing-sing-oscars-best-actor-3833004); [Entertainment Weekly](https://ew.com/tribeca-film-festival-live-awardist-podcast-recording-guest-colman-domingo-11992584); [Entertainment Tonight](https://www.etonline.com/colman-domingo-reacts-to-lenny-kravitzs-touching-time-100-tribute-it-meant-the-world-exclusive)).

## The long road to back-to-back Oscar nominations

Domingo's awards arc is a story about 2014. That year he aced an audition for the Prohibition-era series *Boardwalk Empire*, then was told a Black maître d' of the period would be lighter-skinned — a rejection that made him contemplate quitting acting altogether ([NME](https://www.nme.com/features/film-interviews/colman-domingo-quit-acting-sing-sing-oscars-best-actor-3833004)). He gave a new management team six months; eleven years on, they are still together and he is booked solid for three years ([NME](https://www.nme.com/features/film-interviews/colman-domingo-quit-acting-sing-sing-oscars-best-actor-3833004)). The turnaround began with his 2015 casting in AMC's *Fear the Walking Dead* and the 2022 Emmy for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Drama Series as Ali, the recovering addict who sponsors Zendaya's Rue ([NME](https://www.nme.com/features/film-interviews/colman-domingo-quit-acting-sing-sing-oscars-best-actor-3833004); [Remezcla](https://remezcla.com/film/watch-colman-domingo-wins-first-emmy-euphoria/)). Then came the January 2024 Academy Award nomination for playing Bayard Rustin in Netflix's *Rustin* — and, a year later, a second consecutive Best Actor nomination for *Sing Sing* ([Associated Press](https://www.shootonline.com/newsbriefs/colman-domingo-will-play-joe-jackson-michael-jackson-biopic?qt-recent_and_tags_duplicate=0); [Entertainment Weekly](https://ew.com/tribeca-film-festival-live-awardist-podcast-recording-guest-colman-domingo-11992584)). Back-to-back nominations in that category — a run that began barely a decade after he nearly walked away from the profession ([Entertainment Weekly](https://ew.com/tribeca-film-festival-live-awardist-podcast-recording-guest-colman-domingo-11992584); [NME](https://www.nme.com/features/film-interviews/colman-domingo-quit-acting-sing-sing-oscars-best-actor-3833004)).

## The playwright and director behind the actor

What most Domingo biographies treat as a footnote is the spine of his career: he has written and directed for decades. He co-wrote the book for *Summer: The Donna Summer Musical*, which opened on Broadway in 2018, and co-produced the Pulitzer-winning *Fat Ham* — the production behind his second Tony nomination ([NME](https://www.nme.com/features/film-interviews/colman-domingo-quit-acting-sing-sing-oscars-best-actor-3833004); [Entertainment Weekly](https://ew.com/tribeca-film-festival-live-awardist-podcast-recording-guest-colman-domingo-11992584)). On *Sing Sing* he was executive producer; director Greg Kwedar gave him "the gift of bringing every part of myself as a creator": shaping direction and writing alongside his turn as John "Divine G" Whitfield, the wrongfully convicted man who finds purpose in a prison theater program ([NME](https://www.nme.com/features/film-interviews/colman-domingo-quit-acting-sing-sing-oscars-best-actor-3833004)). Next is the director's chair proper: Domingo will direct and star in a Nat King Cole musical biopic he co-wrote — his feature debut, building on the *Lights Out* stage play he made with Patricia McGregor ([Playbill](https://playbill.com/article/colman-domingo-will-direct-and-star-in-nat-king-cole-biopic)). Industry outlets report it, titled *Unforgettable*, heading to the European Film Market — though that title rests on tier3 reporting ([Cup of Soul Show](https://www.cupofsoulshow.com/entertainmentnews/colman-domingos-nat-king-cole-biopic-unforgettable-heads-to-market-with-powerhouse-team)).

## Joe Jackson in Michael

Lionsgate announced Domingo's casting as Joe Jackson in January 2024 — two days after his first Oscar nomination, the Associated Press reported — for Antoine Fuqua's then-in-production biopic from a script by John Logan ([Associated Press](https://www.shootonline.com/newsbriefs/colman-domingo-will-play-joe-jackson-michael-jackson-biopic?qt-recent_and_tags_duplicate=0); [PhillyVoice](https://www.phillyvoice.com/colman-domingo-joe-jackson-michael-biopic/)). The AP described Joe Jackson as the controversial patriarch who launched the Jackson 5 and the solo careers of Michael and Janet: "an alienating manager and abusive father" ([Associated Press](https://www.shootonline.com/newsbriefs/colman-domingo-will-play-joe-jackson-michael-jackson-biopic?qt-recent_and_tags_duplicate=0)). Domingo called it "a rich, complex and flawed character," adding that he had "a front row seat for Jaafar's incredible transformation" — Jaafar Jackson, [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/)'s real-life nephew, plays his uncle ([PhillyVoice](https://www.phillyvoice.com/colman-domingo-joe-jackson-michael-biopic/)). Preparing the role, Domingo described Joe as a cruel disciplinarian accused of abuse, yet insisted he gave "all he had" to make his children successful: "You don't do that because you don't like your children. You do it because you love them deeply" ([NME](https://www.nme.com/features/film-interviews/colman-domingo-quit-acting-sing-sing-oscars-best-actor-3833004)). The finished film, released April 24, 2026, pairs him with Nia Long as Katherine Jackson ([The Source](https://thesource.com/2026/02/02/michael-biopic-colman-domingo-nia-long/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Has Colman Domingo won an Oscar?**
No — he has been nominated twice for the Academy Award for Best Actor, for *Rustin* (January 2024) and *Sing Sing* (the following year), without winning either. His major acting win to date is the 2022 Primetime Emmy for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Drama Series for playing Ali in HBO's *Euphoria*.

**Who does Colman Domingo play in the Michael Jackson biopic?**
He plays Joe Jackson, the Jackson family patriarch — the hard-driving manager of the Jackson 5 whom the AP described as a controversial, alienating father figure. Lionsgate announced the casting in January 2024, and the film released on April 24, 2026, with Nia Long as Katherine Jackson.

**What was his role in Euphoria?**
He played Ali, a recovering drug addict who acts as a sponsor and mentor to Zendaya's Rue as she struggles with addiction. The performance won him the 2022 Emmy for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Drama Series at the Creative Arts Emmys.

**Is Colman Domingo a director as well as an actor?**
Increasingly, yes. He co-wrote the book for Broadway's *Summer: The Donna Summer Musical*, co-produced the Tony-nominated *Fat Ham*, and was an executive producer on *Sing Sing*. His feature directorial debut — a Nat King Cole musical biopic he co-wrote and will star in — is in development.
