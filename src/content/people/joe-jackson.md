## Overview

**Joseph Walter Jackson** (1928–2018) was the patriarch of the Jackson family and the manager who forged the Jackson 5 into an international pop act; he was the father of [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) and Janet Jackson, ran the family's career with a severity his children later described as abusive, and is portrayed by Colman Domingo in the 2026 biopic [*Michael*](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/) ([The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/06/27/obituaries/josephjacksondies.html); [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/film/michael-jackson-biopic-reshoots-cost-15million-removed-all-mention-of-child-abuse-allegations-3939096)).

He is not the English new-wave musician of the same name — a singer born David Ian Jackson in 1954 whose hit was "Is She Really Going Out with Him?" — a distinction this profile makes explicit below ([FactMonster](https://www.factmonster.com/people/j/joe-jackson)).

Born 26 July 1928 in Fountain Hill, Arkansas, the oldest of five children of a high-school teacher and strict father, Jackson worked as a crane operator while playing in an unsuccessful R&B band, and married Katherine Esther Scruse in 1949 ([The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/06/27/obituaries/josephjacksondies.html)). In the mid-1960s he discovered his sons playing his hidden guitar in secret, bought them instruments, and imposed a relentless rehearsal schedule of at least three hours a day ([The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/06/27/obituaries/josephjacksondies.html)). He died on 27 June 2018 at a hospice in Las Vegas at 89, of pancreatic cancer — two days after the ninth anniversary of Michael's death — with the Jackson estate's co-executors praising how he carried his family "from the steel mills of Gary, Indiana, to worldwide pop superstardom" ([The Associated Press](http://past.jamaica-gleaner.com/article/entertainment/20180627/joe-jackson-patriarch-musical-jackson-family-dies-89); [People](https://people.com/who-was-joe-jackson-michael-jackson-father-8405665)).

## The manager who built the Jackson 5

A U.S. Steel crane operator in Gary, Indiana, Jackson formed the Jackson Brothers — Jackie, Tito and Jermaine — in 1963 after catching Tito playing his hidden guitar, managing them from the start ([People](https://people.com/who-was-joe-jackson-michael-jackson-father-8405665)). Marlon and Michael joined in 1966; as the Jackson 5 they toured the Midwest opening for acts including the Temptations and Jackie Wilson ([The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/06/27/obituaries/josephjacksondies.html)).

The 1968 Motown signing delivered four Billboard No. 1 singles in little more than a year — "I Want You Back", "ABC", "The Love You Save" and "I'll Be There" ([The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/06/27/obituaries/josephjacksondies.html)). That strike rate is the core of his managerial reputation. In 1975 he moved the group to CBS at a reported ten times the royalties; only Jermaine stayed, and the band became the Jacksons ([The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/06/27/obituaries/josephjacksondies.html)).

## The domineering father, on the record

The same discipline that built the act broke the family. The children had to address him as "Joseph"; beatings were routine — "If you messed up during rehearsal, you got hit," Michael said ([The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/06/27/obituaries/josephjacksondies.html)). Jackson told Louis Theroux in 2003: "I never beat him... I whipped him with a switch and a belt" ([The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/06/27/obituaries/josephjacksondies.html)). In Martin Bashir's 2003 documentary, Michael said his father held a belt during rehearsals and threw him against the wall: "where is the love? Where is the fatherhood?" ([People](https://people.com/who-was-joe-jackson-michael-jackson-father-8405665)).

Janet Jackson told Piers Morgan in 2011 her father hit her once, when she was under eight ([People](https://people.com/who-was-joe-jackson-michael-jackson-father-8405665)). Katherine Jackson acknowledged the discipline to Oprah Winfrey in 2010; Joe defended it to Morgan in 2013 — "I'm glad I was tough because look what I came out with" ([People](https://people.com/who-was-joe-jackson-michael-jackson-father-8405665)). The marriage survived infidelity — Katherine filed for divorce twice but the couple never legally separated — and an affair with Cheryl Terrell produced an eleventh child, Joh'Vonnie ([People](https://people.com/who-was-joe-jackson-michael-jackson-father-8405665); [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/06/27/obituaries/josephjacksondies.html)).

The manager documented above is, in effect, the film's antagonist.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this Joe Jackson the singer of "Is She Really Going Out with Him?"?**
No. The singer is an English musician born David Ian Jackson on 11 August 1954 in Burton-on-Trent, England, who broke through in 1979 with the album *Look Sharp!* and that single. The Jackson family patriarch, Joseph Walter Jackson, was born 26 years earlier in Arkansas and died in 2018. They are two unrelated men who share a name.

**When and how did Joe Jackson die?**
He died on 27 June 2018 at the Nathan Adelson Hospice in Las Vegas, aged 89, of pancreatic cancer — two days after the ninth anniversary of Michael Jackson's death. The Clark County coroner confirmed the death and described it as natural.

**Why was Joe Jackson fired as his children's manager?**
Michael Jackson dismissed his father as his personal manager in 1979, according to Vanity Fair; then in 1983, at Michael's urging, the family dismissed him as the Jacksons' manager, per The New York Times. Michael later excluded his father from his 2002 will.

**Who plays Joe Jackson in the 2026 film Michael?**
Colman Domingo portrays him. After the production scrapped its third act over the settlement-clause discovery, the rebuilt film's dramatic tension centers on Michael's relationship with his domineering father.

**How many children did Joe Jackson have?**
Ten with his wife Katherine — Rebbie, Jackie, Tito, Jermaine, La Toya, Marlon, Brandon (Marlon's twin, who died shortly after birth), Michael, Randy and Janet — plus a daughter, Joh'Vonnie, with Cheryl Terrell.
