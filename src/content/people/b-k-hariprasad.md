## Overview

**Bengaluru Kempaiah Hariprasad (born 29 July 1954) is a Congress organisation leader who has served as a four-time Rajya Sabha MP, the opposition's candidate for Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha in 2018, and president of the Karnataka Pradesh Congress Committee (KPCC) since June 2026. He lost both Lok Sabha elections he contested, yet rose to lead his state's party unit.**

Hariprasad is a Congress politician from [India](/places/india/) whose career has been built inside the party organisation rather than on direct election victories. He was born and brought up in Bengaluru's Malleswaram area, and belongs to the Billava community, counted among the Other Backward Classes ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/karnataka/2026/Jun/21/congress-thoroughbred-hariprasad-will-lead-party-in-karnataka); [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/bk-hariprasad-likely-to-become-kpcc-president/article71055645.ece)). He has worked for the Congress since 1972, rising through the Youth Congress and the Seva Dal and later serving at the all-India level ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/hariprasad-assumes-charge-as-kpcc-chief-accuses-bjp-rss-of-undermining-democracy-constitution/articleshow/131892754.cms)). The Indian Express described him in 2020 as a four-time Rajya Sabha MP who had been one of the seniormost general secretaries of the All India Congress Committee (AICC) and a Congress Working Committee (CWC) member for many years ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/delhi-confidential/congress-b-k-hariprasad-legislative-council-elections-karnataka-6464047/)).

## The organisation man

Hariprasad's standing in the Congress rests on loyalty and organisational work, not on winning popular elections. He is described as a staunch loyalist of the Nehru-Gandhi family who worked closely with Congress Parliamentary Party chief Sonia Gandhi, and as a CWC permanent invitee who was the party's Haryana in-charge before his elevation in Karnataka ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/karnataka/2026/Jun/21/congress-thoroughbred-hariprasad-will-lead-party-in-karnataka)). Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge, handing him the KPCC charge, said Hariprasad had grown in the party through various ranks and had become state president after serving in various capacities in different states and at the all-India level, because the party wanted someone who had built the organisation from the ground level ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/hariprasad-assumes-charge-as-kpcc-chief-accuses-bjp-rss-of-undermining-democracy-constitution/articleshow/131892754.cms)). D. K. Shivakumar, handing over the party flag, recalled that both men had grown as student leaders in the same college and called Hariprasad a loyal karyakarta who had organised the party across the country ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/hariprasad-assumes-charge-as-kpcc-chief-accuses-bjp-rss-of-undermining-democracy-constitution/articleshow/131892754.cms)). Former chief minister Siddaramaiah likewise said Hariprasad had worked for the party since 1972 in various capacities, in the Youth Congress and the Seva Dal, and as the party's in-charge in several states ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/hariprasad-assumes-charge-as-kpcc-chief-accuses-bjp-rss-of-undermining-democracy-constitution/articleshow/131892754.cms)).

## Parliament: four Rajya Sabha terms, two lost Lok Sabha races

Hariprasad represented Karnataka in the Rajya Sabha four times, and his last term there ended in June 2020 ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/delhi-confidential/congress-b-k-hariprasad-legislative-council-elections-karnataka-6464047/)). His most visible parliamentary moment came outside New Delhi's domestic politics: in November 2009, at the [United Nations](/organizations/united-nations/), he answered Pakistan's reference to Kashmir during a debate on self-determination, saying he regretted the "unacceptable reference" to the Indian state of Jammu and Kashmir and reiterating that the state was an integral part of India whose people regularly participated in free, fair and open elections ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/India-protests-Pakistan-raising-Kashmir-issue/article16890130.ece/amp/)). It is a rare kind of parliamentary credential — a backroom organisation man speaking for the country on a contested international stage — and no top-ranking profile of him makes it a centrepiece.

In August 2018 the Congress put him forward as the united opposition's candidate for Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha, and he lost to the NDA's Harivansh Narayan Singh by 125 votes to 105 on 9 August ([The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/national/nda-candidate-harivansh-singh-elected-rajya-sabha-deputy-chairman/article24640415.ece)). The post had been vacant since P. J. Kurien retired on 1 July, and after the result Prime Minister [Narendra Modi](/people/narendra-modi/), Leader of the House Arun Jaitley, and Leader of the Opposition Ghulam Nabi Azad all felicitated the winner ([The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/national/nda-candidate-harivansh-singh-elected-rajya-sabha-deputy-chairman/article24640415.ece)). The 20-vote gap showed the opposition's combined arithmetic falling short despite a formally united candidacy.

His direct-election record is the paradox of his career. He contested two [Lok Sabha](/organizations/lok-sabha/) elections from Bangalore South and lost both: in 1999 to the BJP's Ananth Kumar by over 65,000 votes, and in 2019 to the BJP's Tejasvi Surya by over 3.3 lakh votes ([The Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/elections/bengaluru-south-election-result-live-updates-tejasvi-surya-bk-hariprasad-lok-sabha-2019-latest-news/1573137/)). In 2019 Surya polled 736,605 votes (62.2%) to Hariprasad's 407,372 (34.33%) — a margin of more than 3.3 lakh votes in a seat the BJP had held since 1996, which shows how little his organisational weight counted in a direct urban contest ([The Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/elections/bengaluru-south-election-result-live-updates-tejasvi-surya-bk-hariprasad-lok-sabha-2019-latest-news/1573137/)).

## Karnataka: Council opposition leader to KPCC president

When the Congress denied Hariprasad renomination to the Rajya Sabha in 2020 and fielded Mallikarjun Kharge instead, it fielded Hariprasad for the Karnataka Legislative Council elections — a move that surprised many party leaders, since he had been one of the AICC's seniormost general secretaries ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/delhi-confidential/congress-b-k-hariprasad-legislative-council-elections-karnataka-6464047/)). Party leaders wondered whether the known OBC face would be made Leader of the Opposition in the state Upper House as a counter to backward-class leader Siddaramaiah ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/delhi-confidential/congress-b-k-hariprasad-legislative-council-elections-karnataka-6464047/)). In January 2022 he was appointed the Congress party's leader in the Legislative Council, paving the way for him to become Leader of the Opposition in the House ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/hariprasad-appointed-cong-leader-in-council/article38330759.ece)).

The state leadership came in June 2026. The AICC appointed him KPCC president on 3 June, succeeding D. K. Shivakumar, who had resigned as state chief on becoming Chief Minister ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/hariprasad-assumes-charge-as-kpcc-chief-accuses-bjp-rss-of-undermining-democracy-constitution/articleshow/131892754.cms); [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/bk-hariprasad-likely-to-become-kpcc-president/article71055645.ece)). He was also re-elected to the Legislative Council, one of five Congress candidates elected in the Council polls that took the party to 39 seats in the 75-member House ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/hariprasad-assumes-charge-as-kpcc-chief-accuses-bjp-rss-of-undermining-democracy-constitution/articleshow/131892754.cms); [The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/karnataka/2026/Jun/21/congress-thoroughbred-hariprasad-will-lead-party-in-karnataka)). On 21 June he formally took charge at Palace Grounds in Bengaluru, with Shivakumar handing him the party flag, and called the post not power but a "pious responsibility," naming the 2028 Assembly polls and the 2029 Lok Sabha polls as the challenges ahead ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/hariprasad-assumes-charge-as-kpcc-chief-accuses-bjp-rss-of-undermining-democracy-constitution/articleshow/131892754.cms)). Known as a vocal critic of the Modi-led government, the BJP, and the RSS, he used the occasion to accuse the BJP and RSS of misusing constitutional bodies and to warn against misuse of the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/bk-hariprasad-likely-to-become-kpcc-president/article71055645.ece); [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/hariprasad-assumes-charge-as-kpcc-chief-accuses-bjp-rss-of-undermining-democracy-constitution/articleshow/131892754.cms)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is B. K. Hariprasad?**
Bengaluru Kempaiah Hariprasad (born 29 July 1954) is a Congress organisation leader from Bengaluru. He served four terms in the Rajya Sabha, was the opposition's 2018 Deputy Chairman candidate, and has been president of the Karnataka Pradesh Congress Committee since June 2026.

**Did B. K. Hariprasad ever win a Lok Sabha election?**
No. He contested Bangalore South twice and lost both times — to Ananth Kumar in 1999 by over 65,000 votes, and to Tejasvi Surya in 2019 by over 3.3 lakh votes. His legislative career ran through the Rajya Sabha and the Karnataka Legislative Council instead.

**Why did Hariprasad lose the 2018 Deputy Chairman race?**
He polled 105 votes to Harivansh Narayan Singh's 125. The post had been vacant since P. J. Kurien retired on 1 July 2018, and the formally united opposition candidacy could not assemble a winning majority in the House despite the Congress's backing.

**What did Hariprasad do at the United Nations in 2009?**
In November 2009 he rebutted Pakistan's reference to Kashmir during a UN self-determination debate, saying he regretted the "unacceptable reference" and reiterating that Jammu and Kashmir was an integral part of India with free, fair and open elections, speaking for the Indian side.

**What is Hariprasad's job as KPCC president?**
He leads the Congress organisation in Karnataka. After taking charge on 21 June 2026, he set the party's sights on the 2028 Assembly polls and the 2029 Lok Sabha polls, and warned party workers against misuse of the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls.
