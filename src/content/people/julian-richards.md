## Overview

**Julian Richards** is a Welsh horror director and the founder of **Jinga Films**, a UK-based international sales company specializing in genre films ([Filmfestivals.com](https://www.filmfestivals.com/blog/fantasporto/video_meet_julian_richards_the_man_with_18_films_at_fantasporto)). As a director he made *Darklands* (1996) — arguably the first Welsh horror film — and the found-footage hit *The Last Horror Movie* (2003); as a sales agent he turned Jinga into what he calls "one of the key genre companies in the World," handling titles such as *A Serbian Film* ([The People's Movies](https://thepeoplesmovies.com/arrow-video-frightfest-halloween-interview-julian-richards-reborn/)). He discovered Adam Rehmeier's *[The Bunny Game](/creative-works/the-bunny-game/)* at the Downtown Heavy Metal Horror Film Festival in [Los Angeles](/places/los-angeles/) and later negotiated the film's US deal with Autonomy Pictures at SXSW 2012 ([Bloody Flicks](http://bloody-flicks.co.uk/2025/07/09/previously-banned-the-bunny-game-released-in-north-america/); [Bloody Disgusting](http://bloody-disgusting.com/news/28671/autonomy-pictures-comes-out-playing-the-bunny-game/)).

This is the **director-turned-sales-agent Julian Richards of Jinga Films** — not Julian Richer, the Richer Sounds retail founder, and not any other same-name figure. The director and the sales agent are one and the same person: Richards has said he moved into sales after putting much of his own money into *The Last Horror Movie* and needing to recoup it ([The People's Movies](https://thepeoplesmovies.com/arrow-video-frightfest-halloween-interview-julian-richards-reborn/)).

## The director who needed to learn the business

Richards began directing features in Wales, receiving lottery finance from **Film Agency Wales** for *Darklands* (1996), a folk-horror debut he describes as arguably the first Welsh horror movie and which has been credited with helping kickstart a new wave of UK genre production ([The People's Movies](https://thepeoplesmovies.com/arrow-video-frightfest-halloween-interview-julian-richards-reborn/); [Filmfestivals.com](https://www.filmfestivals.com/blog/fantasporto/video_meet_julian_richards_the_man_with_18_films_at_fantasporto)). He followed it with *Silent Cry*, *The Last Horror Movie* (2003), and the **BAFTA Cymru**-winning *Summer Scars*, before relocating much of his work to the US with *Shiver* (2012), *Daddy's Girl*, and *Reborn* (2018), the last premiering at **Arrow Video FrightFest** Halloween ([The People's Movies](https://thepeoplesmovies.com/arrow-video-frightfest-halloween-interview-julian-richards-reborn/); [Filmfestivals.com](https://www.filmfestivals.com/blog/fantasporto/video_meet_julian_richards_the_man_with_18_films_at_fantasporto)). Crucially, he has said the directing and sales careers are not separate accidents: after sinking his own money into *The Last Horror Movie*, he needed a reliable income and a deeper grasp of the business, so sales became his "most immediate and accessible" route in ([The People's Movies](https://thepeoplesmovies.com/arrow-video-frightfest-halloween-interview-julian-richards-reborn/)).

## Building Jinga Films into a genre-sales force

Jinga Films is an international sales company co-founded by Richards with marketing executive **Rosana Coutinho**, built to get feature films distributed without the usual costs ([Filmfestivals.com](https://www.filmfestivals.com/blog/fantasporto/video_meet_julian_richards_the_man_with_18_films_at_fantasporto)). Richards' own account of the company's trajectory supplies the hard numbers: it grew from handling three films a year to ten films a year, and by the time Jinga released Srđan Spasojević's *A Serbian Film* in 2012, it had established itself as "one of the key genre companies in the World" ([The People's Movies](https://thepeoplesmovies.com/arrow-video-frightfest-halloween-interview-julian-richards-reborn/)). That more-than-threefold expansion in annual throughput is the measurable side of a wider reputation: the Jinga library that Richards curates includes *Big Bad Wolves*, *Rabies*, *The House at the End of the World*, *Still/Born*, *Our Evil*, and *Timer* alongside his own films ([Filmfestivals.com](https://www.filmfestivals.com/blog/fantasporto/video_meet_julian_richards_the_man_with_18_films_at_fantasporto)). The dual-career loop eventually closed: Richards credits his sales experience with creating directing opportunities — including *Daddy's Girl*, commissioned via the Indian broadcaster **Zee Studios** — that conventional agent-and-manager channels would not have opened ([The People's Movies](https://thepeoplesmovies.com/arrow-video-frightfest-halloween-interview-julian-richards-reborn/)).

## The Bunny Game: festival discovery to US deal

According to Jinga's own label announcement, Richards discovered Adam Rehmeier's *The Bunny Game* at the **Downtown Heavy Metal Horror Film Festival** in Los Angeles and acquired it for Jinga Films, the world-sales agent that had represented *A Serbian Film* ([Bloody Flicks](http://bloody-flicks.co.uk/2025/07/09/previously-banned-the-bunny-game-released-in-north-america/)). He then negotiated the film's US distribution deal on behalf of the filmmakers with the newly launched **Autonomy Pictures** — the venture of Derek Curl, David Gregory and Lewis Tice — with the agreement finalized at **SXSW** in March 2012 ([Bloody Disgusting](http://bloody-disgusting.com/news/28671/autonomy-pictures-comes-out-playing-the-bunny-game/); [ScreenAnarchy](https://screenanarchy.com/2012/06/blu-raydvd-details-for-autonomy-pictures-the-bunny-game-are-here.html)). The recognition for both halves of Richards' career came in 2020, when **Fantasporto**'s 40th edition gave him a career-tribute award with a full retrospective spanning 18 films — his own directed features alongside titles he had distributed ([Filmfestivals.com](https://www.filmfestivals.com/blog/fantasporto/video_meet_julian_richards_the_man_with_18_films_at_fantasporto)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the Jinga Films founder the same Julian Richards as the film director?**
Yes. Julian Richards — the Welsh director of *Darklands* and *The Last Horror Movie* — is also the founder of Jinga Films. He has said he moved into sales after sinking his own money into *The Last Horror Movie* and needing to recoup it while learning the business side. He is not the same person as Julian Richer, the founder of the Richer Sounds retail chain.

**How did Julian Richards become a sales agent?**
In his own telling, it was defensive: he put a lot of his own money into *The Last Horror Movie*, needed a reliable income, and wanted to understand the business side of films. Sales was the most immediate and accessible route in. He never intended to change careers, but Jinga grew fast enough that the company came to demand most of his time and energy.

**What is Jinga Films?**
Jinga Films is a UK-based international sales company specializing in genre films, co-founded by Richards and marketing executive Rosana Coutinho. Its library has included *A Serbian Film*, *Big Bad Wolves*, *Rabies*, *Still/Born*, and *The House at the End of the World*. Richards describes it as one of the key genre companies in the world, and it runs the Danse Macabre distribution label.

**What was Julian Richards' role in The Bunny Game's US release?**
According to Jinga's label announcement, Richards discovered Adam Rehmeier's *The Bunny Game* at the Downtown Heavy Metal Horror Film Festival in Los Angeles and acquired it for Jinga Films. He then negotiated the film's US rights deal with the newly launched Autonomy Pictures on behalf of the filmmakers; the deal was finalized at SXSW 2012.

**What recognition has Julian Richards received?**
At Fantasporto's 40th edition in 2020, Richards received a career-tribute award with a full retrospective covering 18 films — both titles he directed (including *Darklands* and *Reborn*) and films he had distributed through Jinga Films.
