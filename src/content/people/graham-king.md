**Graham King** is the British film producer behind [*Michael*](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/), the 2026 [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) biopic — an Academy Award winner for Best Picture as the producer of Martin Scorsese's *The Departed* (2006), the producer of the roughly $900-million-grossing *Bohemian Rhapsody* (2018), and the president and CEO of GK Films ([ScreenDaily](https://www.screendaily.com/news/british-bohemian-rhapsody-producer-graham-king-to-receive-cinemacon-award/5137832.article); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/will-hugo-kill-gk-films-33246/); [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/film/bohemian-rhapsody-producer-reportedly-secures-rights-to-a-michael-jackson-biopic-2577476)).

What distinguishes King is a narrower specialty than the credit list suggests: he is the producer music estates trust with their legends. He won the Queen camp's confidence for *Bohemian Rhapsody* in person — then the Jackson estate gave him the same green light, with access to all of Jackson's music, for *Michael* ([Boxoffice Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.com/pressure-interview-bohemian-rhapsody-producer-graham-king/); [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/film/bohemian-rhapsody-producer-reportedly-secures-rights-to-a-michael-jackson-biopic-2577476)). Along the way he collected an OBE in the 2010 New Year Honours ([The Arts Desk](https://theartsdesk.com/2010-new-years-honours-arts)).

## The producer the estates trust

King told *Boxoffice Pro* he first sat down with Queen's manager Jim Beach and surviving members Brian May and Roger Taylor in 2010 to win their trust — a surreal meeting, he said, in a London pub ([Boxoffice Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.com/pressure-interview-bohemian-rhapsody-producer-graham-king/)). The pitch was personal: King grew up in London in the 1970s and early 1980s, with Queen's rise as his teenage soundtrack ([Boxoffice Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.com/pressure-interview-bohemian-rhapsody-producer-graham-king/)). The film — his passion project and, he says, his most daunting production — won four Oscars and grossed roughly $900 million worldwide ([Boxoffice Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.com/pressure-interview-bohemian-rhapsody-producer-graham-king/); [ScreenDaily](https://www.screendaily.com/news/british-bohemian-rhapsody-producer-graham-king-to-receive-cinemacon-award/5137832.article); [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/film/bohemian-rhapsody-producer-reportedly-secures-rights-to-a-michael-jackson-biopic-2577476)).

The Jackson estate's 2019 decision follows the pattern. Deadline reported the estate gave King the green light with access to all of Jackson's music, and that GK Films retained three-time Oscar-nominated writer [John Logan](/people/john-logan/) for the script — explicitly not a sanitized rendering ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/film/bohemian-rhapsody-producer-reportedly-secures-rights-to-a-michael-jackson-biopic-2577476)). King's own framing explains the yes: "I love telling real-life stories," he said, naming *The Aviator*, *Ali* and *Argo* as proof ([Boxoffice Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.com/pressure-interview-bohemian-rhapsody-producer-graham-king/)).

## Scorsese, risk, and the Best Picture Oscar

King's defining partnership is with Martin Scorsese. *The Departed* (2006) was his third collaboration with the director, after the Oscar-nominated *Gangs of New York* and *The Aviator* — the one, as one account puts it, that finally saw Scorsese receive a much-deserved Oscar, "as well as King his own" ([ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/features/84428-exclusive-interviews-hugo-producer-graham-king-writer-john-logan)). The trade press is plainer: King produced *The Departed* with Scorsese "and won Best Picture at the Oscars" ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/will-hugo-kill-gk-films-33246/)).

*Hugo* (2011), the fourth collaboration, shows how King works. He found Brian Selznick's book, sent the manuscript to Scorsese and — because, in his words, "Marty and I had worked with John Logan on *The Aviator*, and we loved John" — recruited Logan to write it ([ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/features/84428-exclusive-interviews-hugo-producer-graham-king-writer-john-logan)). The film cost around $170 million, including $70 million of GK Films' own money — the bet behind *TheWrap*'s "fearless risk-taker" profile, and behind IM Global's Stuart Ford saying: "He's a great risk-taker… It's tough to bet against him" ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/will-hugo-kill-gk-films-33246/)).

## GK Films, the OBE, and Michael

King's operation is GK Films, where he is president and CEO; it grew out of the Initial Entertainment Group, which he founded ([ScreenDaily](https://www.screendaily.com/news/british-bohemian-rhapsody-producer-graham-king-to-receive-cinemacon-award/5137832.article)). The company has produced or executive-produced more than 45 films grossing over $3.6 billion worldwide — a slate running from *Gangs of New York* and *The Aviator* through *Rango*, *World War Z* and *Tomb Raider*, with King as executive producer on *Traffic* and *Ali* ([ScreenDaily](https://www.screendaily.com/news/british-bohemian-rhapsody-producer-graham-king-to-receive-cinemacon-award/5137832.article)). *Bohemian Rhapsody* alone was his tenth film to cross $200 million globally ([ScreenDaily](https://www.screendaily.com/news/british-bohemian-rhapsody-producer-graham-king-to-receive-cinemacon-award/5137832.article)).

The honours system noticed before Hollywood's biggest night did: King was appointed OBE in the 2010 New Year Honours — in the diplomatic and overseas list, a fine-print detail the standard bios omit ([The Arts Desk](https://theartsdesk.com/2010-new-years-honours-arts)). And the through-line held: when Lionsgate announced *Michael* in 2022, King was named as producer alongside the estate's executors [John Branca](/people/john-branca/) and John McClain, with King recalling that he first met the Jackson family in 1981 ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/michael-jackson-biopic-set-at-lionsgate/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Did Graham King win an Oscar for The Departed?**
Yes. King won the Academy Award for Best Picture as the film's producer. It was his third collaboration with Martin Scorsese, after the Oscar-nominated *Gangs of New York* and *The Aviator* — and the film that finally brought Scorsese his directing Oscar, alongside King's own.

**What is GK Films?**
GK Films is Graham King's production company, where he serves as president and CEO. It grew out of the Initial Entertainment Group, which King founded. The company has produced or executive-produced more than 45 films grossing over $3.6 billion worldwide, from *Hugo* and *The Aviator* to *Rango* and *World War Z*.

**How did Graham King get the rights to the Michael Jackson biopic?**
Deadline reported in 2019 that the Jackson estate gave King the green light with access to all of Jackson's music, and that his GK Films retained three-time Oscar-nominated writer John Logan for the script. Lionsgate announced the film in 2022 with King producing alongside estate executors John Branca and John McClain.

**Why is Graham King called a risk-taker?**
A 2011 *TheWrap* profile framed him that way around *Hugo*: the $170 million production included $70 million of GK Films' own money, and King had also launched the FilmDistrict distribution company. IM Global CEO Stuart Ford's verdict: "He's a great risk-taker… It's tough to bet against him."
