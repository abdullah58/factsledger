## Overview

Adrienne Elrod has spent nearly three decades working the rooms other strategists only get into: she held senior communications roles on all three presidential campaigns that ran against [Donald Trump](https://factsledger.com/people/donald-trump/) — Hillary Clinton's in 2016, Joe Biden's in 2020, and Biden's reelection bid and then Kamala Harris's campaign in 2024 ([Leading Authorities](https://www.leadingauthorities.com/speakers/adrienne-elrod)). Then, two months after Trump's second inauguration, she did what many career operatives do at a hinge moment: on March 17, 2025, she and fellow Democratic veteran Rufus Gifford launched the Elrod Gifford Group, a communications, fundraising and strategy firm for individual, non-profit, corporate, and political clients ([StarsAlert](https://starsalert.com/news/democratic-campaign-veterans-adrienne-elrod/1578831)). "There's something of an opportunity right now, both for the Democratic Party, but also the opposition to Trump, to reorganize and build ourselves back up again," Gifford said at the launch. "Adrienne and I want to be part of that conversation, and want to lead" ([StarsAlert](https://starsalert.com/news/democratic-campaign-veterans-adrienne-elrod/1578831)).

## The operative

Elrod's path to that launch announcement started in Siloam Springs, Arkansas. She began her political career with an internship in the Clinton White House, then worked in the White House Office of Intergovernmental Affairs and the [Department of Housing and Urban Development](https://factsledger.com/organizations/us-department-of-housing-and-urban-development/) during the Clinton administration ([Arkansas Cinema Society](https://www.arkansascinemasociety.org/otr/political-strategist-adrienne-elrod-joins-arkansas-cinema-society-board); [Milken Institute](https://milkeninstitute.org/events/global-conference-2021/speakers/adrienne-elrod)). She earned her B.A. from Texas Christian University, went home to serve as the Arkansas Democratic Party's Communications Director, and then built a Washington career the old-fashioned way — through the Capitol and the committee: regional press secretary at the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee, Deputy Chief of Staff to Congressman Ron Klein, and nearly six years as Chief of Staff to Congresswoman Loretta Sanchez ([Leading Authorities](https://www.leadingauthorities.com/speakers/adrienne-elrod); [Milken Institute](https://milkeninstitute.org/events/global-conference-2021/speakers/adrienne-elrod)).

The Clinton campaigns are the throughline. In 2008 she was a senior communications aide to Hillary Clinton's presidential bid, serving as Regional Press Secretary, Texas Communications Director during the contentious Texas primary, and Deputy Director of the Congressional Delegate Selection office ([Milken Institute](https://milkeninstitute.org/events/global-conference-2021/speakers/adrienne-elrod)). Between the two Clinton runs she was Vice President and Communications Director for American Bridge and Correct the Record, the joint communications and research operation founded to defend Clinton against what it called false and misleading attacks ([Milken Institute](https://milkeninstitute.org/events/global-conference-2021/speakers/adrienne-elrod)). In 2016 she ran the surrogate operation as Hillary for America's Director of Strategic Communications and Surrogates — deploying elected officials, civic leaders, celebrities, and other influencers across the country ([Leading Authorities](https://www.leadingauthorities.com/speakers/adrienne-elrod); [Milken Institute](https://milkeninstitute.org/events/global-conference-2021/speakers/adrienne-elrod)).

## The Biden years

In 2020 Elrod held the same portfolio for a different candidate: Director of Surrogate Strategy and Operations for Biden for President, again deploying elected leaders, celebrities, and high-level influencers across the country and multiple platforms ([Leading Authorities](https://www.leadingauthorities.com/speakers/adrienne-elrod); [Milken Institute](https://milkeninstitute.org/events/global-conference-2021/speakers/adrienne-elrod)). After the election she took on the most logistically unusual job of her career — Director of Talent and External Affairs for the Biden inauguration ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2021/02/03/peace-through-bookings-how-adrienne-elrod-may-have-helped-save-our-democracy/)).

The inaugural job was, by her account, unlike anything a political communications strategist trains for. With a five-person team she curated close to 100 artists and participants across five days of events — the swearing-in, the "Celebrating America" prime-time special, a Day of Service concert, and more ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2021/02/03/peace-through-bookings-how-adrienne-elrod-may-have-helped-save-our-democracy/)). It was her team that booked 22-year-old National Youth Poet Laureate Amanda Gorman for the swearing-in. Lady Gaga sang the anthem, Jennifer Lopez performed "This Land Is Your Land" and "America the Beautiful," and Garth Brooks — a last-minute addition announced the Monday before the ceremony, after a conversation with Dr. Biden — sang "Amazing Grace" a cappella ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2021/02/03/peace-through-bookings-how-adrienne-elrod-may-have-helped-save-our-democracy/)). Elrod, who grew up in Arkansas on country music, called his booking "a dream come true" ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2021/02/03/peace-through-bookings-how-adrienne-elrod-may-have-helped-save-our-democracy/)). The context made every booking a gamble: after the January 6 insurrection, some performers pulled out of the event, and 25,000 National Guard troops ended up guarding the ceremony. Elrod declined to name the performers who withdrew, saying she understood their reluctance ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2021/02/03/peace-through-bookings-how-adrienne-elrod-may-have-helped-save-our-democracy/)). She also said the President-elect and Vice President-elect were "very, very involved" in curating and approving the talent, alongside executive producer Stephanie Cutter and producer Ricky Kirshner ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2021/02/03/peace-through-bookings-how-adrienne-elrod-may-have-helped-save-our-democracy/)).

From the inauguration Elrod moved into the Biden administration as Director of External and Government Affairs for the CHIPS Program Office, a top aide to Commerce Secretary Gina Raimondo managing legislative affairs and communications for the effort to catalyze domestic semiconductor production ([Leading Authorities](https://www.leadingauthorities.com/speakers/adrienne-elrod)). In 2024 she joined President Biden's reelection campaign as Senior Advisor and Senior Spokesperson, and when Biden withdrew, she continued in the same position for the Harris campaign — appearing on television for the candidate as a member of the leadership team ([StarsAlert](https://starsalert.com/news/democratic-campaign-veterans-adrienne-elrod/1578831); [Leading Authorities](https://www.leadingauthorities.com/speakers/adrienne-elrod)).

## The new firm and the commentator

The Elrod Gifford Group's founding partnership is a complementary one. Gifford was finance chair of the Harris-Walz and Biden-Harris campaigns, which raised a combined $2.2 billion; he served as chief of protocol with the rank of ambassador during the Biden administration, as U.S. ambassador to Denmark in Barack Obama's second term, as national finance director of the Democratic National Committee, and as an aide on Obama's 2008 campaign ([StarsAlert](https://starsalert.com/news/democratic-campaign-veterans-adrienne-elrod/1578831)). The firm says it will work with corporate and philanthropic organizations as well as political figures and groups ([StarsAlert](https://starsalert.com/news/democratic-campaign-veterans-adrienne-elrod/1578831)). Elrod's own statement at the launch: the firm will help clients "navigate this increasingly challenging and complex world" ([StarsAlert](https://starsalert.com/news/democratic-campaign-veterans-adrienne-elrod/1578831)).

Outside the campaigns, Elrod is a regular contributor to NBC and MSNBC — earlier in her career she appeared across the dial, on CNN, MSNBC, and Fox News — and she has been described by the Arkansas Cinema Society, which appointed her to its board of directors, as a champion for women in politics ([Arkansas Cinema Society](https://www.arkansascinemasociety.org/otr/political-strategist-adrienne-elrod-joins-arkansas-cinema-society-board); [Milken Institute](https://milkeninstitute.org/events/global-conference-2021/speakers/adrienne-elrod)). Her strategic communications firm, Elrod Strategies, focused on communications and influencer engagement, is still described in her bio materials as hers ([Milken Institute](https://milkeninstitute.org/events/global-conference-2021/speakers/adrienne-elrod); [Leading Authorities](https://www.leadingauthorities.com/speakers/adrienne-elrod)).
