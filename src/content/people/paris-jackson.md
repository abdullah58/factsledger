## Overview

**Paris-Michael Katherine Jackson** (born 3 April 1998) is an American model, actress and singer — the only daughter of [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) and Debbie Rowe — who built an indie-folk music career deliberately apart from her father's sound, and who has publicly challenged the Michael Jackson estate's co-executors over the 2026 biopic [*Michael*](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/), stating she has "zero per cent involvement" in it ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/paris-jackson-challenges-fathers-biopic-and-estate-spending-as-co-executors-call-her-claims-meritless-and-accuse-her-of-media-campaign-3937068); [PAPER Magazine](https://www.papermag.com/paris-jackson-wilted?rebelltitem=7)).

She was 11 when her father died in June 2009, and her unplanned, tearful remarks at his July memorial in Los Angeles — calling him "the best father you could ever imagine" — were the public's first unfiltered glimpse of her ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/music/2026/08/13/paris-jackson-call-her-daddy-michael-funeral-speech/91290390007/)). Raised largely out of sight at Neverland Ranch, she entered public life on her own terms in 2017 — IMG Models, a *Rolling Stone* cover, an acting debut — and has since described her father as her "best friend" while insisting her art belongs to her ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/music/2026/08/13/paris-jackson-call-her-daddy-michael-funeral-speech/91290390007/); [Entertainment Tonight](https://www.etonline.com/fashion/211958_paris_jackson_signs_with_img_models_i_feel_very_lucky_and_blessed)).

## From Neverland to the memorial microphone

Paris was born during Michael Jackson's marriage to Debbie Rowe, his second wife; after the divorce Michael received full custody and raised Paris and Prince at Neverland Ranch, with half-brother Bigi joining via surrogate in 2002 ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/music/2026/08/13/paris-jackson-call-her-daddy-michael-funeral-speech/91290390007/); [The Movie Database](https://www.themoviedb.org/person/1274291-paris-jackson/translations?language=en-US)). The children were kept from public view — faces masked on outings — and after Michael's death entered their grandmother [Katherine Jackson](/people/katherine-jackson/)'s guardianship ([The Movie Database](https://www.themoviedb.org/person/1274291-paris-jackson/translations?language=en-US)).

The memorial speech defined her public image for years. She had not planned to speak — "It kind of just happened in the moment," she told the *Call Her Daddy* podcast in August 2026, calling it an "energetic initiation" into a life in front of microphones, though she has never watched the footage back ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/music/2026/08/13/paris-jackson-call-her-daddy-michael-funeral-speech/91290390007/)). She remembered Neverland's attractions as emphatically not the children's own: "That was for underprivileged children, and children that were terminally ill, that couldn't go to Disneyland" ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/music/2026/08/13/paris-jackson-call-her-daddy-michael-funeral-speech/91290390007/)).

## Model, actress, musician — her own lane

Her public career began at 18: IMG Models in March 2017, a *Rolling Stone* cover, and an acting debut on Fox's *Star* ([Entertainment Tonight](https://www.etonline.com/fashion/211958_paris_jackson_signs_with_img_models_i_feel_very_lucky_and_blessed); [The Movie Database](https://www.themoviedb.org/person/1274291-paris-jackson/translations?language=en-US)). In 2018 she formed indie-folk duo The Soundflowers with Gabriel Glenn and debuted on film in *Gringo* ([PAPER Magazine](https://www.papermag.com/paris-jackson-wilted?rebelltitem=7)).

*Wilted* is the thesis of her independence: eleven indie-folk tracks — "not an album full of R&B-tinged pop anthems" — written from heartbreak with Manchester Orchestra's Andy Hull and Robert McDowell ([PAPER Magazine](https://www.papermag.com/paris-jackson-wilted?rebelltitem=7)). "It's mainly just a story of heartbreak and love," she told *PAPER*; she describes music as therapeutic for her mental health ([PAPER Magazine](https://www.papermag.com/paris-jackson-wilted?rebelltitem=7)). Her goals are modest by dynasty standards: keep making music, acting, modeling — "I don't have a golden statue in mind" ([PAPER Magazine](https://www.papermag.com/paris-jackson-wilted?rebelltitem=7)).

## The estate fight and the biopic she disowned

After Colman Domingo said Michael's children were "very much in support" of the film, Paris refuted it: "zero per cent involvement" ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/paris-jackson-challenges-fathers-biopic-and-estate-spending-as-co-executors-call-her-claims-meritless-and-accuse-her-of-media-campaign-3937068)). She had read an early draft and given notes on what she found dishonest, then walked away when they were not addressed ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/film/michael-jackson-biopic-reshoots-cost-15million-removed-all-mention-of-child-abuse-allegations-3939096)).

The biopic is one front in a wider legal war. In 2025 she sued co-executors John Branca and John McClain, alleging mismanagement and objecting to the estate's 2021 accounting: over $10 million in executor compensation, $464 million in cash earning under 0.1%, the estate "morphed" into "a private entertainment investment fund" — the *Michael* investment a "risky investment", Branca's casting of Miles Teller as himself questioned ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/paris-jackson-challenges-fathers-biopic-and-estate-spending-as-co-executors-call-her-claims-meritless-and-accuse-her-of-media-campaign-3937068)). The $464 million is the dispute's statistical core: cash earning under a tenth of a percent is, in her filing's implication, money working for no beneficiary. The executors called her claims "meritless", "false and frivolous", a "media campaign" — countering she has "already received roughly $65 million", which her team called "outright false" ([NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/paris-jackson-challenges-fathers-biopic-and-estate-spending-as-co-executors-call-her-claims-meritless-and-accuse-her-of-media-campaign-3937068)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is Paris Jackson's mother?**
Debbie Rowe, Michael Jackson's second wife. Paris was born in 1998 during their marriage; after the divorce Michael received full custody and raised Paris and Prince at Neverland Ranch. Paris is Michael's only daughter; her younger half-brother Bigi was born via surrogate in 2002.

**What has Paris Jackson said about the Michael biopic?**
That she has "zero per cent involvement" in it — a direct refutation of Colman Domingo's claim that Michael's children supported the film. She read an early script draft, gave notes on what she found dishonest, and walked away when they were not addressed. She has called the production a "risky investment" of estate money.

**What music has Paris Jackson released?**
The Soundflowers' self-titled EP (2020) with Gabriel Glenn, then her 11-track solo debut *Wilted* on Republic Records in November 2020 — indie-folk made with Manchester Orchestra's Andy Hull and Robert McDowell, which she describes as a story of heartbreak and love.

**Is Paris Jackson suing the Michael Jackson estate?**
In 2025 she filed suit accusing co-executors John Branca and John McClain of mismanaging her inheritance, citing their compensation, uninvested cash and the *Michael* film investment. The executors call her claims "meritless" and a "media campaign"; the dispute is ongoing.
