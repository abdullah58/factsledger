## Overview

Diljit Dosanjh is a Punjabi singer and actor whose career, running since 2002, has made him the most globally visible ambassador of Punjabi-language popular culture ([Madhyamam](https://madhyamamonline.com/entertainment/diljit-dosanjh-becomes-first-punjabi-artist-to-perform-at-coachella-1150658)). On 12 September 2026 he became the first Punjabi artist to headline London's [Wembley Stadium](/places/wembley-stadium/), playing a sold-out show reported at around 90,000 people as part of his Aura World Tour — described as the biggest sold-out show of his career ([IndiaTimes](https://www.indiatimes.com/trending/whats-buzzing-on-gen-zs-feed-today-diljit-dosanjh-reaches-new-heights-at-wembley-celine-dion-gets-emotional-during-her-first-live-performance-in-six-years-more/articleshow/134233429.html); [FactFile](https://factfile.pk/2026/09/15/diljit-dosanjh-nusrat-tribute/)). His signature move on the world's biggest stages has been to stay unapologetically Punjabi — opening his historic Coachella set in 2023 with "Punjabi aa gaye, oye!" ("Punjabis have arrived!") and performing in kurta, tamba and turban on American late-night television ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/htcity/cinema/coachella-to-jimmy-fallon-everything-goat-singer-diljit-dosanjh-has-achieved-globally-101718175178485-amp.html); [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/pov/diljit-dosanjh-films-satluj-main-wapas-aunga-chamkila-punjab-militancy-partition-politics/2992270/)).

## From Punjab to the global stage

Dosanjh started his music career in 2002 and gained recognition in Punjabi music with the albums *Smile* (2005) and *Chocolate* (2008), later entering Billboard's Social 50 chart in 2020 ([Madhyamam](https://madhyamamonline.com/entertainment/diljit-dosanjh-becomes-first-punjabi-artist-to-perform-at-coachella-1150658)). In April 2023 he became the first Punjabi singer to perform at the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival in California, appearing in traditional Punjabi attire — black kurta and tamba, turban, sunglasses and gloves — and performing hits including "Patiala Peg" and "Lemonade" ([Madhyamam](https://madhyamamonline.com/entertainment/diljit-dosanjh-becomes-first-punjabi-artist-to-perform-at-coachella-1150658); [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/htcity/cinema/coachella-to-jimmy-fallon-everything-goat-singer-diljit-dosanjh-has-achieved-globally-101718175178485-amp.html)).

The 2024 Dil-Luminati tour turned him into a stadium act. A sold-out show at Dallas's American Airlines Centre (about 20,000 capacity) in May 2024 earned him the venue's traditional honours — the jerseys of the Dallas Stars and Dallas Mavericks and a signed framed poster for the arena walls ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/htcity/cinema/coachella-to-jimmy-fallon-everything-goat-singer-diljit-dosanjh-has-achieved-globally-101718175178485-amp.html)). In April 2024 he sold out Vancouver's BC Place (54,000), billed as the largest Punjabi concert ever held outside India ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/htcity/cinema/coachella-to-jimmy-fallon-everything-goat-singer-diljit-dosanjh-has-achieved-globally-101718175178485-amp.html)). Tour coverage described Dil-Luminati as the highest-grossing tour by an Indian artist ([Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/amp/news/bollywood/diljit-dosanjh-announces-ahmedabad-concert-at-narendra-modi-stadium-on-november-21-after-sold-out-wembley-show/)).

His crossover moments multiplied: in 2024 he became the first Indian artist to perform on *The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon*, dressed in kurta, tamba and pag (turban), closing with "Main hun Punjab" from *Chamkila* ([Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/amp/news/bollywood/diljit-dosanjh-announces-ahmedabad-concert-at-narendra-modi-stadium-on-november-21-after-sold-out-wembley-show/); [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/pov/diljit-dosanjh-films-satluj-main-wapas-aunga-chamkila-punjab-militancy-partition-politics/2992270/)). He returned to Fallon's show in spring 2026 to perform "Morni" with two Bhangra dancers — a performance that went viral and inspired waves of fan dance videos ([Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/amp/news/bollywood/diljit-dosanjh-announces-ahmedabad-concert-at-narendra-modi-stadium-on-november-21-after-sold-out-wembley-show/)). His international collaborations include "Hass Hass" with Sia, "Palpita" with Camilo and "Khutti" with Saweetie, and Ed Sheeran joined him to sing "Lover" at Sheeran's Mumbai concert in March 2024 ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/htcity/cinema/coachella-to-jimmy-fallon-everything-goat-singer-diljit-dosanjh-has-achieved-globally-101718175178485-amp.html)). In 2025 he made his Met Gala debut in an ensemble inspired by Maharaja Bhupinder Singh of Patiala — turban, kirpan (ceremonial sword) and a cape embroidered with a map of Punjab in Gurmukhi script — and was voted best dressed by GQ and Vogue ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/pov/diljit-dosanjh-films-satluj-main-wapas-aunga-chamkila-punjab-militancy-partition-politics/2992270/); [Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/amp/news/bollywood/diljit-dosanjh-announces-ahmedabad-concert-at-narendra-modi-stadium-on-november-21-after-sold-out-wembley-show/)). He has also received his first International Emmy Award nomination for Best Performance by an Actor ([Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/amp/news/bollywood/diljit-dosanjh-announces-ahmedabad-concert-at-narendra-modi-stadium-on-november-21-after-sold-out-wembley-show/)).

The 2026 run-up to Wembley was itself record-setting: a 15-date North American stadium and arena tour sold more than 225,000 tickets and generated over $32 million, breaking his own record for the biggest North American tour by a Punjabi artist — including two nights at Madison Square Garden, where he became the venue's first Punjabi headliner ([Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/amp/news/bollywood/diljit-dosanjh-announces-ahmedabad-concert-at-narendra-modi-stadium-on-november-21-after-sold-out-wembley-show/); [That Eric Alper](https://www.thatericalper.com/2026/09/15/diljit-dosanjh-makes-history-at-wembley-and-announces-massive-ahmedabad-stadium-show/)).

## Screen work, politics and controversy

Dosanjh's filmography is, as ThePrint puts it, "a more serious, and perhaps more responsible testament" to his Punjab-rootedness than his goofy "beeba munda" social-media persona ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/pov/diljit-dosanjh-films-satluj-main-wapas-aunga-chamkila-punjab-militancy-partition-politics/2992270/)). In *Udta Punjab* (2016) he played Sartaj, a police officer tackling the state's drug crisis ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/pov/diljit-dosanjh-films-satluj-main-wapas-aunga-chamkila-punjab-militancy-partition-politics/2992270/)). He has twice taken roles centred on the 1984 anti-Sikh violence: *Punjab 1984* (2014), later honoured with a National Award, and *Jogi* (2022) — the latter drawing controversy after he referred to the riots as a "genocide" ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/pov/diljit-dosanjh-films-satluj-main-wapas-aunga-chamkila-punjab-militancy-partition-politics/2992270/)). In Imtiaz Ali's 2024 biopic *Chamkila* he played Amar Singh Chamkila, the Dalit Sikh folk singer who became hugely popular among Punjab's working class in the late 1970s before being assassinated at 27 ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/pov/diljit-dosanjh-films-satluj-main-wapas-aunga-chamkila-punjab-militancy-partition-politics/2992270/)).

His most contested film has been *Satluj* (formerly *Punjab '95*), in which he plays human-rights activist Jaswant Singh Khalra, who investigated alleged extrajudicial killings during Punjab's counter-insurgency operations. After a four-year battle with the censor board — which demanded a title change and 127 cuts — the film was released on OTT and then withdrawn within 48 hours ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/pov/diljit-dosanjh-films-satluj-main-wapas-aunga-chamkila-punjab-militancy-partition-politics/2992270/)). Breaking his usual silence, Dosanjh wrote on Instagram: "Satluj suffered the same fate as Khalra" ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/pov/diljit-dosanjh-films-satluj-main-wapas-aunga-chamkila-punjab-militancy-partition-politics/2992270/)).

His public positions have shifted over the years. He vocally supported the farmers' protest — trolling Kangana Ranaut on social media and criticising the Modi government's handling of it — and dedicated the song *RiRi* to Rihanna after she tweeted in support of the sit-in; then, in January 2025, he met Prime Minister Modi and called it "a fantastic start to 2025," drawing fury from some farmer leaders in Punjab ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/pov/diljit-dosanjh-films-satluj-main-wapas-aunga-chamkila-punjab-militancy-partition-politics/2992270/)). He also publicly criticised "the government's incompetence and dirty politics" after fellow singer Sidhu Moosewala was shot dead in Punjab in May 2022 ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/pov/diljit-dosanjh-films-satluj-main-wapas-aunga-chamkila-punjab-militancy-partition-politics/2992270/)).

On tour, he has clashed with regulators too. Ahead of his 15 November 2024 Dil-Luminati show in Hyderabad, the Telangana government served him a notice — issued on a complaint from a Chandigarh professor citing video of his Delhi concerts — directing him not to perform songs promoting alcohol, drugs or violence, and not to bring children on stage given sound levels beyond WHO-recommended limits ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/entertainment/music/dil-luminati-india-tour-diljit-tweaks-songs-at-hyderabad-show-after-government-notice/article68878393.ece/amp/)). At the 16 November show he sang modified lyrics — "coke" for "daaru" in "Lemonade," "5 taara hotel ch" in "5 Taara" — and addressed the notice from the stage: "If an artist comes from outside the country, they can sing or do whatever they want... But when your artist is coming home to perform, you have a problem" ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/entertainment/music/dil-luminati-india-tour-diljit-tweaks-songs-at-hyderabad-show-after-government-notice/article68878393.ece/amp/)).

## The Wembley milestone

Dosanjh took over Wembley Stadium on 12 September 2026 for a sold-out London stop on his Aura World Tour, promoted by Live Nation; the concert sold out in fewer than 48 hours, making him the first Punjabi artist to headline the venue ([Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/amp/news/bollywood/diljit-dosanjh-announces-ahmedabad-concert-at-narendra-modi-stadium-on-november-21-after-sold-out-wembley-show/); [That Eric Alper](https://www.thatericalper.com/2026/09/15/diljit-dosanjh-makes-history-at-wembley-and-announces-massive-ahmedabad-stadium-show/)). He opened with his trademark "Punjabi aa gaye oye," and midway through the hit-heavy set he paused for a stripped-back tribute to Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, singing the qawwali "Sanson Ki Mala" as some 90,000 phone lights answered him — a moment several outlets singled out as the night's defining image ([FactFile](https://factfile.pk/2026/09/15/diljit-dosanjh-nusrat-tribute/)). Speaking afterwards, he said: "Nusrat Sahab was a great artist, and singing his song was not easy for anyone" ([FactFile](https://factfile.pk/2026/09/15/diljit-dosanjh-nusrat-tribute/)).

From the Wembley stage he announced the tour's next giant date: 21 November 2026 at Ahmedabad's Narendra Modi Stadium — billed as the world's largest stadium — addressing part of the crowd in Gujarati as he revealed it ([Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/amp/news/bollywood/diljit-dosanjh-announces-ahmedabad-concert-at-narendra-modi-stadium-on-november-21-after-sold-out-wembley-show/); [FactFile](https://factfile.pk/2026/09/15/diljit-dosanjh-nusrat-tribute/)). The UK leg was to close with Birmingham's Utilita Arena on 18 September and Manchester's Co-op Live on 20 September ([That Eric Alper](https://www.thatericalper.com/2026/09/15/diljit-dosanjh-makes-history-at-wembley-and-announces-massive-ahmedabad-stadium-show/)).
