**John Branca is an entertainment attorney — a partner and head of the music division at [Ziffren Brittenham LLP](https://people.com/where-is-john-branca-now-11960161) — and the co-executor of the [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) estate — alongside music executive [John McClain](/people/john-mcclain/) until McClain's death on May 26, 2026 ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/where-is-john-branca-now-11960161); [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/explained-paris-jackson-michael-jackson-estate-lawsuit-625000-legal-bonuses-john-branca-john-mcclain-court-ruling-1503593875.html); [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/)).** He first represented Jackson in 1980, brokered the landmark 1985 purchase of ATV Music — the $47.5 million deal that gave Jackson the Beatles publishing catalog — and after Jackson's death in 2009 led the estate from more than $500 million in debt to an enterprise valued at over $2 billion ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/where-is-john-branca-now-11960161); [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/explained-paris-jackson-michael-jackson-estate-lawsuit-625000-legal-bonuses-john-branca-john-mcclain-court-ruling-1503593875.html); [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-branca-artists-need-to-pay-attention-to-their-legacy-and-be-very-specific-about-it/)). In the 2026 biopic [Michael](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/) he is played by Miles Teller and is also a producer ([The Wrap](https://www.thewrap.com/miles-teller-john-branca-michael-jackson-biopic-movie/)).

## The lawyer behind Jackson's publishing empire

Branca met Jackson in 1980, shortly after *Off the Wall*, as the singer began building a professional team independent of his family — a partnership that would define both men's careers ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/where-is-john-branca-now-11960161)). The relationship ran on and off for nearly three decades: Branca told Occidental College his tenure spanned 1980 to '89 or '90, then '92 or '93 onward with gaps, ending in 2006 — before Jackson rehired him on June 17, 2009, eight days before his death ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/where-is-john-branca-now-11960161)). Between those bookends came the deals that built Jackson's publishing fortune. In 1983 Branca secured an unprecedented $1.2 million budget for the "Thriller" video by packaging and selling the *Making Michael Jackson's Thriller* documentary to MTV and Showtime; in 1985 he spearheaded the $47.5 million ATV Music acquisition — outbidding Paul McCartney himself, with Jackson telling advisers who called it too expensive, "Branca, get me that catalog"; and in 1988 he handled the approximately $17 million purchase of Neverland Ranch ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/where-is-john-branca-now-11960161)).

Branca's practice extends well beyond Jackson. Over a 47-plus-year career he has represented more than 30 Rock & Roll Hall of Fame inductees — the Rolling Stones, Aerosmith, the Doors, the Bee Gees, the Beach Boys — and describes himself as a "lawyer-manager," a lawyer who functions as part of the artist's business team ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-branca-artists-need-to-pay-attention-to-their-legacy-and-be-very-specific-about-it/)). His artist-rights record includes helping Don Henley reclaim his Eagles copyrights, securing Creedence Clearwater Revival royalties for John Fogerty, extricating Dr. Dre from onerous contracts, and winning master-ownership for the Bee Gees ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-branca-artists-need-to-pay-attention-to-their-legacy-and-be-very-specific-about-it/)). In 1995 he was instrumental in the ATV–Sony merger that created Sony/ATV, leaving Jackson with a 50% shareholding — the stake that would later finance the estate's entire recovery ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-branca-artists-need-to-pay-attention-to-their-legacy-and-be-very-specific-about-it/)).

## From $500 million in debt to a $2 billion estate

Jackson's July 7, 2002 will named Branca and John McClain as co-executors; it was filed at the Los Angeles County courthouse on July 1, 2009 and both men were confirmed by a judge on July 6, with Katherine Jackson withdrawing her initial challenge by November 2009 ([The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/explained-paris-jackson-michael-jackson-estate-lawsuit-625000-legal-bonuses-john-branca-john-mcclain-court-ruling-1503593875.html)). What they inherited was bleak: more than $500 million in debt. What they built was the most successful celebrity estate on record: by June 2024 it was valued at more than $2 billion, with total posthumous earnings reported above $3 billion ([The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/explained-paris-jackson-michael-jackson-estate-lawsuit-625000-legal-bonuses-john-branca-john-mcclain-court-ruling-1503593875.html)). The turnaround's milestones include the 2009 concert documentary *This Is It*, two posthumous studio albums, a long-term Sony recording deal, two Cirque du Soleil productions (*Michael Jackson ONE* and the *IMMORTAL* world tour), and the four-time Tony Award-winning Broadway musical *MJ* ([The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/explained-paris-jackson-michael-jackson-estate-lawsuit-625000-legal-bonuses-john-branca-john-mcclain-court-ruling-1503593875.html)).

The defining transaction came in 2016, when Sony acquired the estate's 50% of Sony/ATV for $750 million — *Forbes* reported the estate earned $825 million in that year alone ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/where-is-john-branca-now-11960161); [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-branca-artists-need-to-pay-attention-to-their-legacy-and-be-very-specific-about-it/)). **The return on Jackson's initial $47.5 million ATV investment exceeded 2,000% over 31 years, per Music Business Worldwide — and it is the single clearest measure of the Branca-era estate: a catalog purchase his client was laughed at for making became the asset that erased half a billion in debt and built a $2 billion enterprise** ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-branca-artists-need-to-pay-attention-to-their-legacy-and-be-very-specific-about-it/)). Later came the estate's 10% stake in EMI Music Publishing, bought out by Sony for $287.5 million in 2018 ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-branca-artists-need-to-pay-attention-to-their-legacy-and-be-very-specific-about-it/)). Then, in 2024, a California appeals court cleared the estate to sell half of Jackson's publishing and recorded-music catalog to Sony Music — MBW's reporting valued the rights at up to $1.5 billion, with Sony paying at least $600 million for its half — the estate's biggest catalog move since the 2016 Sony/ATV sale ([Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/john-mcclain-co-executor-of-the-michael-jackson-estate-and-veteran-music-executive-dies-at-71/)).

## The Teller portrayal and the Paris Jackson dispute

In January 2024 Miles Teller was announced to play Branca in *Michael*, the Antoine Fuqua-directed biopic — director Fuqua called him "the perfect actor to disappear into the role of John Branca" ([The Wrap](https://www.thewrap.com/miles-teller-john-branca-michael-jackson-biopic-movie/)). Branca is also a producer on the film alongside McClain ([The Wrap](https://www.thewrap.com/miles-teller-john-branca-michael-jackson-biopic-movie/)); he told TMZ of Teller's performance, "Miles is a great actor. Can't tell you how much I liked his performance" ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/where-is-john-branca-now-11960161)).

His tenure as executor has not been without dispute. In July 2025 Paris Jackson — one of the estate's three beneficiaries under the will's 40/40/20 allocation to the children, Katherine Jackson and children's charities — filed a formal objection to $625,000 in bonuses paid to three outside law firms in late 2018, calling them irregular "premium payments" ([The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/explained-paris-jackson-michael-jackson-estate-lawsuit-625000-legal-bonuses-john-branca-john-mcclain-court-ruling-1503593875.html)). The executors defended the payments as earned by "instrumental and critical" work and noted in court filings that Paris had received roughly $65 million in benefits from the estate ([The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/explained-paris-jackson-michael-jackson-estate-lawsuit-625000-legal-bonuses-john-branca-john-mcclain-court-ruling-1503593875.html)). On April 29, 2026, retired probate judge Mitchell Beckloff sided with Paris in a 23-page ruling, finding the bonus amounts appeared "arbitrary" and ordering the $625,000 returned — while crediting her as the "catalyst" behind a separate order requiring fee petitions for 2019–2024 ([The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/explained-paris-jackson-michael-jackson-estate-lawsuit-625000-legal-bonuses-john-branca-john-mcclain-court-ruling-1503593875.html)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is John Branca?**
He is an entertainment attorney — partner and head of the music division at Ziffren Brittenham LLP — who has represented more than 30 Rock & Roll Hall of Fame inductees. He is best known as co-executor of the Michael Jackson estate, a role he has held since the singer's death in 2009.

**How did John Branca become co-executor of Michael Jackson's estate?**
Michael Jackson's will, signed July 7, 2002, named Branca and music executive John McClain as executors. It was filed on July 1, 2009 and both were confirmed by a Los Angeles judge on July 6, 2009. Katherine Jackson initially contested the appointment but withdrew her objections by November 2009.

**What deals did John Branca negotiate for Michael Jackson?**
The defining ones: the $1.2 million "Thriller" video budget (1983), the $47.5 million ATV Music/Beatles-catalog purchase (1985), the $17 million Neverland Ranch acquisition (1988), and the 1995 ATV–Sony merger creating Sony/ATV — whose 2016 sale for $750 million financed the estate's recovery.

**Who plays John Branca in the Michael biopic?**
Miles Teller. His casting was announced in January 2024, with director Antoine Fuqua calling him "the perfect actor to disappear into the role." Branca also served as a producer on the film.

**What is the Paris Jackson estate dispute about?**
In July 2025 Paris Jackson objected to $625,000 in 2018 bonuses paid to three outside law firms, calling them irregular "premium payments." On April 29, 2026, retired probate judge Mitchell Beckloff sustained her objection and ordered the money returned, while acknowledging the executors' "exceptional services."
