# Sean Penn

## The war documentarian: from *Superpower* to *Black Sunflowers*

Most coverage of Sean Penn in 2026 treated him as a celebrity commenting on a war. The verified record shows something more specific: Penn is a war documentarian who was physically present in Kyiv from the first hours of the full-scale invasion and has now made *two* Ukraine documentaries — the second one aimed directly at the people running American diplomacy. ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/entertainment/english/2023/Feb/20/zelenskyy-born-for-this-moment-actorsean-penn-at-73rd-berlin-international-film-festival-2549231.html))

In February 2022, Penn and co-director Aaron Kaufman were in Kyiv filming a profile of [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](/people/volodymyr-zelenskyy/) — then still a comedic actor-turned-president of less than three years — when Russia invaded. Their first on-camera meeting with Zelenskyy happened, in the words of the report, "just hours before Russia began its invasion of Ukraine." They returned to their hotel after the interview and "the shelling started that very night." ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/entertainment/english/2023/Feb/20/zelenskyy-born-for-this-moment-actorsean-penn-at-73rd-berlin-international-film-festival-2549231.html))

"It was as if he was realizing himself, that he was born for this moment," Penn told the Associated Press at the 73rd Berlin International Film Festival in February 2023, the day after the documentary — titled *Superpower* — premiered. "It's hard to explain, but there was a resolve in reaction to something that no one has ever faced before." Penn said that when the pair first met Zelenskyy, the president wore "a proper suit and a proper office"; the next time they saw him, "he was in camos and his country was at war." ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/entertainment/english/2023/Feb/20/zelenskyy-born-for-this-moment-actorsean-penn-at-73rd-berlin-international-film-festival-2549231.html))

Penn told the AP that viewers would be most surprised by Zelenskyy's "command of the mechanisms of government": "Not only his, but all of those upon whom he is reliant, his sense of mapping the diplomatic territory. He's on fire. He has that extreme gift for politics." The film took Penn to the front lines to talk to soldiers in the trenches. ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/entertainment/english/2023/Feb/20/zelenskyy-born-for-this-moment-actorsean-penn-at-73rd-berlin-international-film-festival-2549231.html))

## Screen career

Sean Penn was born August 17, 1960, in Santa Monica, California, an American actor and director known for intense performances ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/quote-of-the-day-july-4-by-sean-penn-weve-let-the-blade-of-our-innocence-dull-over-time-and-its-only-in-innocence-that-you-find-oscar-winning-actor-of-mystic-river-and-milk-teaches-motivational-life-lessons-on-why-holding-on-to-innocence-makes-you-stronger/articleshow/132173134.cms)). He began acting with the Los Angeles Repertory Theater before his film debut in *Taps* (1981). His breakthrough came as the stoner Jeff Spicoli in *Fast Times at Ridgemont High* (1982), followed by *Bad Boys* (1983) and *The Falcon and the Snowman* (1985). ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/quote-of-the-day-july-4-by-sean-penn-weve-let-the-blade-of-our-innocence-dull-over-time-and-its-only-in-innocence-that-you-find-oscar-winning-actor-of-mystic-river-and-milk-teaches-motivational-life-lessons-on-why-holding-on-to-innocence-makes-you-stronger/articleshow/132173134.cms))

He earned Academy Award nominations for *Dead Man Walking* (1995), *Sweet and Lowdown* (1999), and *I Am Sam* (2001) before winning Best Actor for the mystery drama *Mystic River* (2003) and again for the biopic *Milk* (2008), in which he played gay rights activist Harvey Milk. ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/quote-of-the-day-july-4-by-sean-penn-weve-let-the-blade-of-our-innocence-dull-over-time-and-its-only-in-innocence-that-you-find-oscar-winning-actor-of-mystic-river-and-milk-teaches-motivational-life-lessons-on-why-holding-on-to-innocence-makes-you-stronger/articleshow/132173134.cms))

As a director, Penn made *The Indian Runner* (1991), *The Crossing Guard* (1995), *The Pledge*, *Into the Wild* (2007), and *Flag Day* (2021). ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/quote-of-the-day-july-4-by-sean-penn-weve-let-the-blade-of-our-innocence-dull-over-time-and-its-only-in-innocence-that-you-find-oscar-winning-actor-of-mystic-river-and-milk-teaches-motivational-life-lessons-on-why-holding-on-to-innocence-makes-you-stronger/articleshow/132173134.cms), [The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/entertainment/english/2023/Feb/20/zelenskyy-born-for-this-moment-actorsean-penn-at-73rd-berlin-international-film-festival-2549231.html)) Beyond film, he has a long record of humanitarian work following Hurricane Katrina and the 2010 Haiti earthquake, and he published the novel *Bob Honey Who Just Do Stuff* (2018) with a sequel in 2019. ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/quote-of-the-day-july-4-by-sean-penn-weve-let-the-blade-of-our-innocence-dull-over-time-and-its-only-in-innocence-that-you-find-oscar-winning-actor-of-mystic-river-and-milk-teaches-motivational-life-lessons-on-why-holding-on-to-innocence-makes-you-stronger/articleshow/132173134.cms))

## The Oscar loan and the Order of Merit

Penn's identification with Ukraine became theatrical in November 2022. During a visit to Kyiv — his third wartime visit — he loaned one of his Oscar statuettes to Zelenskyy, seated beside the Ukrainian leader at a ceremonial table. "It's just a symbolic, silly thing," Penn said, pressing his hand on Zelenskyy's arm. "When you win, bring it back to Malibu." ([The Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/life/entertainment/us-actor-sean-penn-visits-ukraine-president-volodymyr-zelensky-loans-him-an-oscar))

In return, Zelenskyy presented Penn with the Ukrainian Order of Merit for strengthening relations, backing Ukraine's territorial integrity, and helping to popularize the country, according to the president's office. A video showed the two men viewing a plaque bearing Penn's signature, dated February 24 — the date of the invasion — set in the paving stones of an "Alley of Courage" Zelenskyy had inaugurated in August to honor friends of Ukraine. In his nightly address, Zelenskyy said Penn had been "doing everything to help us gather international support" ever since visiting Kyiv on the day of the invasion. Russia has sanctioned Penn for criticizing its war. ([The Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/life/entertainment/us-actor-sean-penn-visits-ukraine-president-volodymyr-zelensky-loans-him-an-oscar))

The loan had a backstory. Penn had earlier threatened to smelt his Oscars in public if Zelenskyy was not included on the program for the 2022 Academy Awards telecast; the Academy instead opted for a moment of silence in support of the people of Ukraine. At the Berlinale press conference, Penn said the Oscar gift was inspired by his "continuing shame towards the leadership of the Academy" in choosing to broadcast Will Smith striking Chris Rock rather than "the greatest symbol of cinema and humanity living today." ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/entertainment/english/2023/Feb/20/zelenskyy-born-for-this-moment-actorsean-penn-at-73rd-berlin-international-film-festival-2549231.html))

## *Black Sunflowers* and the Kushner–Ivanka ask (2026)

Four years after the invasion, Penn returned with a second Ukraine documentary. *Black Sunflowers*, directed by James Marcus Haney and executive-produced by Penn, had its world premiere at the Toronto International Film Festival on September 11, 2026. The 95-minute film follows a group of young Ukrainians — ravers, street racers, artists, photographers, and frontline soldiers, some as young as 18 — navigating ordinary youth against the full-scale war. ([The New Voice of Ukraine](https://english.nv.ua/life/sean-penn-wants-jared-kushner-and-ivanka-trump-to-watch-his-new-ukraine-documentary-50641243.html))

The film captures a specific policy moment: the Trump administration's dissolution of the U.S. Agency for International Development (USAID), shown affecting LGBTQ+ service members and support programs for wounded soldiers in Ukraine. Penn called the decision "negligent homicide," while adding that he had been told not to use those terms. ([The New Voice of Ukraine](https://english.nv.ua/life/sean-penn-wants-jared-kushner-and-ivanka-trump-to-watch-his-new-ukraine-documentary-50641243.html), [ua.news](https://ua.news/en/world/shon-penn-rozkritikuvav-trampa-cherez-rishennia-shchodo-usaid))

In a joint interview with Haney for *Vanity Fair* on September 12, Penn publicly said he wanted [Jared Kushner](/people/jared-kushner/) — Trump's special representative and son-in-law — and [Ivanka Trump](/people/ivanka-trump/) to watch the film. "This film makes no effort to attack any president," Penn said. "And these kids' stories are so familiar that there's no reason not to present something that lets somebody connect and have a chance to reflect. And some will and some won't. We know what we think mostly about leadership, but I'm willing to be wrong anytime he wants." Then: "It's worth the chance." ([The New Voice of Ukraine](https://english.nv.ua/life/sean-penn-wants-jared-kushner-and-ivanka-trump-to-watch-his-new-ukraine-documentary-50641243.html))

Penn's framing of the war itself was unsparing but non-prescriptive: he said that "the Ukrainians won," but that "what military resolution will happen is not really something for anybody to comment on." Director Haney, for his part, said the war "is not going to stop until Putin stops it or is forcibly stopped," noting that the soldiers they spoke with had "no anticipation of it stopping soon" — some seeing it as a war they might be "potentially fighting their whole lives." ([The New Voice of Ukraine](https://english.nv.ua/life/sean-penn-wants-jared-kushner-and-ivanka-trump-to-watch-his-new-ukraine-documentary-50641243.html))
