Laura Loomer is a far-right activist who spent the 2020s trying to convert online provocation into elected office and, after two failed runs for Congress, into something stranger: direct personal access to Donald Trump. On November 21, 2025, Trump hosted New York City mayor-elect [Zohran Mamdani](/people/zohran-mamdani/) at the White House, and Loomer spent the night lashing out on X — "So we are just going to normalize communism?" she wrote of Mamdani, adding that "The GOP will lose the midterms" and calling it "Wild to allow a jihadist communist to stand behind the President's desk in the Oval Office" ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/laura-loomer-slams-trump-amid-rare-meet-with-nycs-mayor-elect-zohran-mamdani-101763753617854.html)). She accused Mamdani of wanting "white people to be taxed more" and wanting to "ban ICE" — yet her anger stayed aimed at the meeting, not the president who arranged it ([Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/laura-loomer-slams-trump-amid-rare-meet-with-nycs-mayor-elect-zohran-mamdani-101763753617854.html)).

The restraint is notable because of how close the two had grown. Seven months earlier, in April 2025, Loomer met Trump in the Oval Office and handed him what she described as research findings branding National Security Council staffers as disloyal; the next day, firings of council staff began ([The Independent](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/how-a-far-right-conspiracy-theorist-wielding-a-list-of-names-became-trumps-most-powerful-adviser)). The episode made her — by the Independent's account — one of the few private citizens with a pipeline straight into Trump's personnel decisions, a position she had spent nearly a decade of escalating stunts building toward.

## From campus sting to the provocation circuit

Loomer's career started with a hidden camera. As a student at Barry University, she filmed a video in which she proposed creating an ISIS-supporting club on campus; the school suspended her, and Project Veritas recruited her ([Global News](https://www.q107.com/2024/09/13/laura-loomer/)). She left Project Veritas for Rebel News in 2017 ([Global News](https://www.q107.com/2024/09/13/laura-loomer/)).

Her breakout as a provocateur came that same year, when the New York Times covered her interruption of a Central Park performance of Shakespeare's *Julius Caesar* — staged with a Trump-like Caesar — in protest of the production ([Global News](https://www.q107.com/2024/09/13/laura-loomer/)). After the November 2017 truck attack in New York City, she tweeted that she was a "proud Islamophobe" ([Global News](https://www.q107.com/2024/09/13/laura-loomer/)). She harassed the families of victims of the Pulse nightclub shooting, and her press credentials were revoked in 2018 ([Global News](https://www.q107.com/2024/09/13/laura-loomer/)). She also targeted Rep. Ilhan Omar with harassment campaigns ([Global News](https://www.q107.com/2024/09/13/laura-loomer/)).

## Banned almost everywhere

The stunts came with platform costs. Loomer was banned from Twitter, [Facebook](/organizations/facebook/), [Instagram](/organizations/instagram/), Uber, and Lyft ([Global News](https://www.q107.com/2024/09/13/laura-loomer/)). Haaretz, reporting on her 2020 campaign, pinned the May 2019 Facebook and Instagram bans to her claiming on Instagram that Omar wanted "another 9/11" and declaring that "Islam is a cancer" ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2020-11-04/ty-article/.premium/laura-loomer-florida-loses-trump-frankel/0000017f-dbe3-d3ff-a7ff-fbe323120000)). She regrouped on right-wing platforms Parler and Gab, which became the engine of a remarkably successful fundraising operation ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2020-11-04/ty-article/.premium/laura-loomer-florida-loses-trump-frankel/0000017f-dbe3-d3ff-a7ff-fbe323120000)).

## Two failed runs for Congress

In 2020, Loomer won the Republican primary for Florida's 21st Congressional District — Trump's home district, covering Palm Beach — and then lost the general election to veteran Democratic Rep. Lois Frankel, 59% to 39%, with 90% of the vote counted ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2020-11-04/ty-article/.premium/laura-loomer-florida-loses-trump-frankel/0000017f-dbe3-d3ff-a7ff-fbe323120000)). What made the race notable was the money: she outraised the incumbent, taking in more than $2 million in donations ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2020-11-04/ty-article/.premium/laura-loomer-florida-loses-trump-frankel/0000017f-dbe3-d3ff-a7ff-fbe323120000)). Trump endorsed her after the primary win, posting: "Great going Laura. You have a great chance against a Pelosi puppet!" ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2020-11-04/ty-article/.premium/laura-loomer-florida-loses-trump-frankel/0000017f-dbe3-d3ff-a7ff-fbe323120000)).

Her campaign drew establishment condemnation even as it drew Trump-world support. In September 2020, the Anti-Defamation League called on her to remove what it called a "disgusting" ad using Holocaust imagery to tie Frankel to the Black Lives Matter movement ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2020-11-04/ty-article/.premium/laura-loomer-florida-loses-trump-frankel/0000017f-dbe3-d3ff-a7ff-fbe323120000)). Her donors included conspiracy theorist Alex Jones, and she was a regular guest on his InfoWars program; she picked up endorsements from Trump-world insider Roger Stone and Rep. Matt Gaetz, with Stone and former Breitbart editor Milo Yiannopoulos speaking at her primary victory party in August ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.com/us-news/2020-11-04/ty-article/.premium/laura-loomer-florida-loses-trump-frankel/0000017f-dbe3-d3ff-a7ff-fbe323120000)).

Two years later she tried again, this time in Florida's 11th District, and lost the Republican primary to Daniel Webster ([Global News](https://www.q107.com/2024/09/13/laura-loomer/)).

## The April 2025 Oval Office meeting

What Loomer could not win at the ballot box, she gained through proximity. On April 2, 2025, she met Trump in the Oval Office and presented what the Independent described as research findings claiming National Security Council staffers were disloyal to the president ([The Independent](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/how-a-far-right-conspiracy-theorist-wielding-a-list-of-names-became-trumps-most-powerful-adviser)). The next day, removals began: Brian Walsh, Thomas Boodry, and David Feith were ousted, and 5–10 staff were removed in total ([The Independent](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/how-a-far-right-conspiracy-theorist-wielding-a-list-of-names-became-trumps-most-powerful-adviser)). The same day, the NSA and Cyber Command chief, Gen. Timothy Haugh, and his deputy, Wendy Noble, were also removed — two officials Loomer had posted online were "disloyal" ([The Independent](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/how-a-far-right-conspiracy-theorist-wielding-a-list-of-names-became-trumps-most-powerful-adviser)).

The Independent's reporting framed it as cause and effect, and Trump's own words lent weight to that framing: aboard Air Force One, he called her "a great patriot," and NPR reported that he said he sometimes acts on her recommendations ([The Independent](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/how-a-far-right-conspiracy-theorist-wielding-a-list-of-names-became-trumps-most-powerful-adviser)). The firings were followed in late May 2025 by a broader restructuring of the council under Marco Rubio, which shrank the staff from roughly 200 to under half, according to CNN ([The Independent](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/how-a-far-right-conspiracy-theorist-wielding-a-list-of-names-became-trumps-most-powerful-adviser)). Her access went beyond personnel: she was among the first people Trump called after ordering the Iran strikes, and the New York Times reported that Trump speaks with her near-monthly ([The Independent](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/how-a-far-right-conspiracy-theorist-wielding-a-list-of-names-became-trumps-most-powerful-adviser)).

## Still in the room

Her closeness to Trump has been a recurring embarrassment for Republicans who have tried to keep their distance. In September 2024 she was seen in Trump's entourage, and around the same time she posted a racist remark about Kamala Harris involving "curry" — drawing condemnation from Marjorie Taylor Greene, Lindsey Graham, Thom Tillis, and [JD Vance](/people/jd-vance/) ([Global News](https://www.q107.com/2024/09/13/laura-loomer/)). Trump, pressed on her presence, said: "She's a private citizen and longtime supporter. I don't control Laura. She says what she wants… I disagree with the statements she made" ([Global News](https://www.q107.com/2024/09/13/laura-loomer/)).

The pattern held through the November 2025 Mamdani episode: attack the people around the president, never the president himself. From a suspended college student with a hidden camera to a private citizen whose Oval Office meetings have preceded government firings, Loomer's career has been a test of how far provocation can carry someone with no office and no title — and, for nearly a decade, the answer has kept being: further than expected.
