# Migos

Migos are an Atlanta rap trio — Quavo, Offset, and Takeoff — whose 2013 breakout "Versace," the chart-topping *Culture* era, and the 2017 single "MotorSport" made them one of the defining hip-hop groups of the 2010s. ([REVOLT](https://www.revolt.tv/article/2017-02-06/24340/from-the-bando-to-the-top-of-the-billboard-migos-journey-to-a-no-1-album)) After Takeoff's killing in 2022, the surviving members drifted apart, then reunited publicly in September 2026 with plans for a tour and a final group album. ([B93](https://b93.net/ixp/812/p/quavo-offset-reunite-collab-preview/))

## Breakthrough

REVOLT first interviewed Migos in the summer of 2013 during the video shoot for "Versace," their first radio hit. Offset was absent, serving an eight-month sentence on a probation-violation charge, while Quavo and Takeoff rode the Drake-assisted breakout smash and their *Y.R.N.* mixtape through the summer. ([REVOLT](https://www.revolt.tv/article/2017-02-06/24340/from-the-bando-to-the-top-of-the-billboard-migos-journey-to-a-no-1-album))

## The mixtape years

Between "Versace" and their commercial peak, the trio released a run of mixtapes and EPs — including *Rich N—a Timeline*, *No Label 2*, and the five-track *3 Way* EP — that racked up gold and platinum certifications on tracks like "Fight Night" and turned their ad-libs and dance moves, including the dab, into pop-culture sensations. ([REVOLT](https://www.revolt.tv/article/2017-02-06/24340/from-the-bando-to-the-top-of-the-billboard-migos-journey-to-a-no-1-album)) Offset's 2014 vow to take things "full throttle" after his release from jail became, in REVOLT's telling, the defining arc of the group's rise: by 2016 they had announced their departure from 300 Entertainment, weathered label delays, and lined up *Culture* behind "Bad and Boujee." ([REVOLT](https://www.revolt.tv/article/2017-02-06/24340/from-the-bando-to-the-top-of-the-billboard-migos-journey-to-a-no-1-album))

## The *Culture* era

"Bad and Boujee," featuring Lil Uzi Vert and produced by Metro Boomin, was released on August 28, 2016, and climbed to No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 in January 2017 — the group's first chart-topping single. ([REVOLT](https://www.revolt.tv/article/2017-02-06/24340/from-the-bando-to-the-top-of-the-billboard-migos-journey-to-a-no-1-album)) On February 6, 2017, their second album *Culture* debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with 131,000 equivalent album units and 116 million U.S. streams in its first week — their first No. 1 album and the first for their label, Quality Control. ([REVOLT](https://www.revolt.tv/article/2017-02-06/24340/from-the-bando-to-the-top-of-the-billboard-migos-journey-to-a-no-1-album))

## "MotorSport"

On October 27, 2017, Migos released "MotorSport," uniting Cardi B and [Nicki Minaj](/people/nicki-minaj/) as the lead single from their third album, *Culture II*. ([REVOLT](https://www.revolt.tv/article/2017-10-27/34025/migos-unite-cardi-b-and-nicki-minaj-on-culture-ii-single-motor-sport)) Produced by Murda Beatz and Cubeatz, it was the first time all three acts had worked together; Minaj said the song had originally been just her and Quavo. ([REVOLT](https://www.revolt.tv/article/2017-10-27/34025/migos-unite-cardi-b-and-nicki-minaj-on-culture-ii-single-motor-sport))

## Takeoff's death

On November 1, 2022, Takeoff — born Kirsnick Khari Ball, 28, the group's youngest member — was shot and killed outside 810 Billiards & Bowling in Houston following a private party; police described him as an "innocent bystander." ([Associated Press](https://www.jamaicaobserver.com/2022/11/01/houston-cops-seek-shooters-in-migos-rapper-takeoffs-slaying/)) Quavo was Takeoff's uncle and Offset his cousin. ([Associated Press](https://www.jamaicaobserver.com/2022/11/01/houston-cops-seek-shooters-in-migos-rapper-takeoffs-slaying/)) On December 2, 2022, Houston police charged Patrick Clark with murder in the killing. ([Associated Press](https://www.jamaicaobserver.com/2022/12/02/man-arrested-in-fatal-shooting-of-migos-rapper-takeoff/)) Clark's trial is scheduled for November 5, 2026. ([B93](https://b93.net/ixp/812/p/quavo-offset-reunite-collab-preview/))

## Hiatus and the 2026 reunion

Quavo and Offset performed together at the 2023 BET Awards in a tribute to Takeoff — their first joint performance since his death. ([Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/2228251/offset-and-quavo-reunite-at-bet-awards-for-first-performance-since-takeoffs-death/news)) In May 2026, the Migos Instagram account was reactivated after two years dormant; its first post showed Quavo and Offset reuniting in the studio. ([B93](https://b93.net/ixp/812/p/quavo-offset-reunite-collab-preview/)) Earlier in September 2026, Quavo told V-103's DJ Greg Street: "We coming back. We're going to do it one more time… We going to tour. We going to do it right," describing a Migos tour and a group album featuring posthumous vocals from Takeoff. ([B93](https://b93.net/ixp/812/p/quavo-offset-reunite-collab-preview/))

On September 27, 2026, the pair reunited publicly at the Los Angeles release party for Quavo's album *QRÖMELIFE* (due October 2), previewing their Offset-assisted track "Away" — their first collaboration in over four years. Their last appearance on record together had been on Ye's *Donda 2* cut "We Did It" in 2022, and their last album as Migos was 2021's *Culture III*. ([B93](https://b93.net/ixp/812/p/quavo-offset-reunite-collab-preview/))
