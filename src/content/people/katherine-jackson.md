## Overview

**Katherine Esther Jackson** (born Kattie B. Screws, 4 May 1930) is the matriarch of the Jackson family — mother of ten, including [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) and Janet Jackson, widow of [Joe Jackson](/people/joe-jackson/), and the court-appointed guardian of Michael's three children after his 2009 death; she is portrayed by Nia Long in the 2026 biopic [*Michael*](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/) ([People](https://people.com/all-about-michael-jackson-mom-katherine-jackson-11959312); [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Michael-Jacksons-family)).

Born in Clayton, Barbour County, Alabama, she contracted polio as a small child and walked with a limp for the rest of her life; at four, her father changed the family surname from Screws to Scruse and renamed her Katherine Esther Scruse ([People](https://people.com/all-about-michael-jackson-mom-katherine-jackson-11959312)). The family moved to East Chicago, Indiana, where she played in her high school band, and in 1947 she met Joseph Jackson at a party; they married on 5 November 1949 and bought a small house at 2300 Jackson Street in Gary ([People](https://people.com/all-about-michael-jackson-mom-katherine-jackson-11959312); [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Michael-Jacksons-family)). A talented singer and pianist and a devout Jehovah's Witness, Britannica describes her as "a loving and supportive mother, a bedrock of strength" — the counterweight to Joe's severity ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Michael-Jacksons-family); [Men's Journal](https://www.mensjournal.com/news/katherine-jackson-michael-jackson-mom-now-is-she-still-alive-today)).

## The matriarch of the Jackson family

Katherine's musical ear shaped the dynasty before it had a name. Michael wrote in his 1988 autobiography *Moonwalk* that it was his mother who convinced Joe to let him join the brothers' group — first as a backup singer and bongo player — and Biography.com credits her as the Jackson 5's early costume designer ([People](https://people.com/all-about-michael-jackson-mom-katherine-jackson-11959312); [Men's Journal](https://www.mensjournal.com/news/katherine-jackson-michael-jackson-mom-now-is-she-still-alive-today)). Between 1950 and 1966 she gave birth to ten children — three daughters and seven sons — while Joe worked the steel mills and then managed the group full-time ([People](https://people.com/all-about-michael-jackson-mom-katherine-jackson-11959312)).

Ten children in sixteen years, and at least 27 grandchildren since ([People](https://people.com/all-about-michael-jackson-mom-katherine-jackson-11959312)) — a family scale that explains why her later guardianship fight became front-page news for weeks. In January 1990 she published her autobiography, *My Family, the Jacksons*, co-written with Richard Wiseman, followed in 2010 by the photo memoir *Never Can Say Goodbye: The Katherine Jackson Story* ([People](https://people.com/all-about-michael-jackson-mom-katherine-jackson-11959312)).

## Guardian of Michael's children

When Michael Jackson died in June 2009, his will named his mother as the legal guardian of his three children, and a court appointed her within days ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Michael-Jacksons-family); [The Associated Press](https://www.lawattstimes.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=293:temporary-guardian-sought-for-jackson-s-kids&catid=27&Itemid=117)). A settlement with Debbie Rowe, the mother of Prince and Paris, made Katherine their permanent guardian with Rowe receiving visitation rights ([People](https://people.com/all-about-michael-jackson-mom-katherine-jackson-11959312)).

The arrangement fractured in July 2012: after a week in Arizona without contacting the children, a relative reported her missing, and on 25 July a judge suspended her guardianship, naming TJ Jackson temporary guardian ([The Associated Press](https://www.lawattstimes.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=293:temporary-guardian-sought-for-jackson-s-kids&catid=27&Itemid=117)). She was found safe and reinstated as co-guardian with TJ on 9 August, the judge citing a glowing investigator's report: "I think the kids are in terrific hands" ([The Associated Press](https://www.lawattstimes.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=330:tj-jackson-emerges-as-katherine-s-co-guardian&catid=27&Itemid=117)).

She resigned as Bigi's co-guardian in 2017 when he was 15 ([People](https://people.com/all-about-michael-jackson-mom-katherine-jackson-11959312)); in 2024 she opposed the estate's ~$600 million Sony catalog sale, and after grandson Bigi asked the court not to fund her appeal, a judge let the sale proceed in August 2024 ([People](https://people.com/all-about-michael-jackson-mom-katherine-jackson-11959312)).

## Portrayed by Nia Long in Michael (2026)

In the 2026 biopic *Michael*, Katherine is played by Nia Long, opposite [Colman Domingo](/people/colman-domingo/) as Joe ([People](https://people.com/all-about-michael-jackson-mom-katherine-jackson-11959312)). Long told *People* she saw Katherine's story as "rooted in grace" and sensed "a strong and kind spirit" in the matriarch — though she had not met Katherine in person — calling the role "an opportunity of a lifetime": "I know what it is to be a mother... But I don't know what it is to raise a superstar" ([ABC Audio](https://krnb.com/nia-long-says-portraying-katherine-jackson-in-michael-was-opportunity-of-a-lifetime/)). In the film's telling, the mother-son bond is warm — coverage describes scenes of Katherine and Michael watching movies and eating ice cream together on the sofa, set against Joe's severity ([Men's Journal](https://www.mensjournal.com/news/katherine-jackson-michael-jackson-mom-now-is-she-still-alive-today)). Now 96, Katherine keeps a low public profile; she was last widely seen in a March 2026 video posted by her daughter La Toya marking her approaching 96th birthday ([Men's Journal](https://www.mensjournal.com/news/katherine-jackson-michael-jackson-mom-now-is-she-still-alive-today)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Katherine Jackson still alive?**
Yes. She was confirmed alive in May 2026 at age 96 — her daughter La Toya posted a video in March 2026 noting she would turn 96 on May 4. She keeps a low public profile and was widowed in 2018 by Joe Jackson's death.

**How many children does Katherine Jackson have?**
Ten with Joe Jackson, born between 1950 and 1966: Rebbie, Jackie, Tito, Jermaine, La Toya, Marlon, Michael, Randy and Janet — plus Marlon's twin Brandon, who died shortly after birth. She has at least 27 grandchildren.

**Why was Katherine Jackson's guardianship suspended in 2012?**
After she spent more than a week in Arizona without contacting Michael's children, a relative reported her missing and a judge temporarily suspended her guardianship on 25 July 2012, naming TJ Jackson as temporary guardian. She was found safe and reinstated as co-guardian alongside TJ on 9 August, backed by a glowing court investigator's report.

**Who plays Katherine Jackson in the 2026 film Michael?**
Nia Long. She described Katherine's story as "rooted in grace" and called the role "an opportunity of a lifetime," while noting she had not met Katherine in person before filming.

**What is Katherine Jackson's religion?**
She is a devout Jehovah's Witness, a faith she held throughout the family's rise; Britannica describes her as "a loving and supportive mother, a bedrock of strength, and a devout Jehovah's Witness."
