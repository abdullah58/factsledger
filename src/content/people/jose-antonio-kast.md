## Overview

On the morning of Wednesday 11 March 2026, hours before he was due to be sworn in as
Chile's 35th president, José Antonio Kast did something almost no English-language profile
of him mentions: he resigned from the political party he had founded himself. Kast filed
his resignation from the Republican Party online that morning — it was announced by the
electoral authority Servel — fulfilling a campaign promise he had put bluntly years
earlier: "No voy a seguir siendo militante del partido… hoy voy a representar a Chile
entero" ("I will not continue to be a party militant… today I will represent all of
Chile") ([La Tercera](https://www.latercera.com/politica/noticia/kast-cumple-compromiso-y-renuncia-al-partido-republicano-previo-a-su-asuncion/)).
The party's leader, Arturo Squella, had confirmed in February that the break had to happen
before 11 March, calling it a symbolic milestone for a president who founded his party on
10 June 2019 ([La Tercera](https://www.latercera.com/politica/noticia/kast-cumple-compromiso-y-renuncia-al-partido-republicano-previo-a-su-asuncion/)).

The resignation was the final punctuation on an unlikely political arc. Born in Santiago in
1966, Kast is the youngest of ten children of German immigrants whose family built a
successful sausage business; media investigations revealed his father had been a member of
the Nazi Party who fought in World War II — Kast has said it was a forced conscription and
that his father did not believe in Nazi ideology
([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/15/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-newly-elected-far-right-leader),
[Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/world/2026/03/12/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-new-far-right-president/212198)).
His older brother Miguel became a central bank president and government minister under
Augusto Pinochet, whose dictatorship Kast openly admires — making him, as one academic told
Al Jazeera, the first Chilean president since the dictatorship to openly support Pinochet
([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/15/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-newly-elected-far-right-leader)).
Trained as a lawyer at the city's Catholic university, Kast became a councilman for Buin
in 1996 and spent more than a decade in Congress for the centre-right Independent
Democratic Union (UDI) before breaking with it in 2016
([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/15/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-newly-elected-far-right-leader)).

## Three attempts at the presidency

Kast's first presidential run, in 2017 as an independent, won him only about eight percent
of the vote
([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/15/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-newly-elected-far-right-leader)).
By then he had founded Acción Republicana (2018) and then the harder-line Republican Party
in June 2019, appealing to voters unsettled by rising insecurity and economic stagnation
([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/15/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-newly-elected-far-right-leader),
FactsLedger). In 2021 he won the first round but lost the runoff
to Gabriel Boric with 44 percent
([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/15/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-newly-elected-far-right-leader)).
The third attempt, in December 2025, was different: Kast defeated Communist candidate
Jeannette Jara with about 58 percent of the vote, declaring in his victory speech that
"Chile needs order – order in the streets, in the state, in the priorities that have been
lost" ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/15/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-newly-elected-far-right-leader)).

## What he stands for

Kast is a 60-year-old lawyer and father of nine, married to fellow lawyer María Pia
Adriasola
([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/world/2026/03/12/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-new-far-right-president/212198)).
A staunch Catholic, he opposes abortion even in cases of rape, emergency contraception,
divorce, same-sex marriage and euthanasia — and once forbade his wife from using birth
control pills
([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/world/2026/03/12/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-new-far-right-president/212198)).
His congressional record was famously thin: his legislative achievements were limited to
laws on the construction of statues, the sale of reading glasses without a prescription and
the regulation of lotteries
([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/world/2026/03/12/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-new-far-right-president/212198)).

Security and migration are the platform's core. Kast has pointed to El Salvador's Nayib
Bukele and his "mano dura" gang crackdown as a model, arguing Chile needs "more Bukele";
he has pledged to send the military into high-crime areas, build more prisons, and
deport hundreds of thousands of undocumented migrants — mostly Venezuelans — telling them
on the eve of the vote to "pack your things and leave"
([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/15/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-newly-elected-far-right-leader),
[Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/world/2026/03/12/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-new-far-right-president/212198)).
He has proposed an ICE-inspired migration police force and border infrastructure along
Chile's northern frontier, and even threatened a state of siege in the Araucanía region
with warrantless searches, arrests and suspended civil rights to expel armed Indigenous
groups ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/15/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-newly-elected-far-right-leader)).
He claims immigration is a plot by the "radical left," and has said he favoured granting
freedom to people convicted of human-rights violations under Pinochet
([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/world/2026/03/12/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-new-far-right-president/212198),
[Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/12/15/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-newly-elected-far-right-leader)).

Those who know him describe a study in contrasts. His biographer Amanda Marton calls him
"sober, pragmatic, calm, compared to other far-right leaders"; political scientist Robert
Funk of the University of Chile says he is far more conservative and lacks charisma beside
figures like Bolsonaro, Milei or Trump — while supporters say the calm demeanour is part of
his appeal ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/world/2026/03/12/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-new-far-right-president/212198)).
Former colleagues describe an authoritarian streak — "You're with him or against him,"
recalled journalist Lily Zuniga — and during the campaign Kast appeared behind
bulletproof glass and admitted to carrying a revolver
([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/world/2026/03/12/who-is-jose-antonio-kast-chiles-new-far-right-president/212198)).

## The inauguration

At 12:28 on 11 March 2026, in the Salón de Honor of the National Congress in Valparaíso,
Kast received the presidential sash and symbols; at 12:35 he swore to "cumplir y hacer
cumplir las leyes de la República"
([La Prensa Austral](https://laprensaaustral.cl/2026/03/11/kast-asume-la-presidencia-de-chile-y-marca-el-giro-mas-fuerte-hacia-la-derecha/)).
The band was placed on him by Senate president Paulina Núñez of Renovación Nacional, while
the outgoing Gabriel Boric handed over the piocha de O'Higgins, the star-shaped jewel that
seals the Chilean presidency
([Xinhua](http://spanish.china.org.cn/txt/2026-03/12/content_118378199.htm)).
Guests included Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado, Argentine president
Javier Milei, Spain's King Felipe VI, and the presidents of Uruguay, Bolivia and Ecuador
([La Prensa Austral](https://laprensaaustral.cl/2026/03/11/kast-asume-la-presidencia-de-chile-y-marca-el-giro-mas-fuerte-hacia-la-derecha/),
[Xinhua](http://spanish.china.org.cn/txt/2026-03/12/content_118378199.htm)).

The day carried the weight of his mandate: with about 58 percent of the runoff vote he
became the most-voted president-elect in Chilean history, in the country's first election
with compulsory voting, automatic registration and fines for non-participation
([La Prensa Austral](https://laprensaaustral.cl/2026/03/11/kast-asume-la-presidencia-de-chile-y-marca-el-giro-mas-fuerte-hacia-la-derecha/),
[Xinhua](http://spanish.china.org.cn/txt/2026-03/12/content_118378199.htm)). He will be the
first president to reside in La Moneda Palace since Carlos Ibáñez del Campo in 1958, with
Pía Adriasola as First Lady
([La Prensa Austral](https://laprensaaustral.cl/2026/03/11/kast-asume-la-presidencia-de-chile-y-marca-el-giro-mas-fuerte-hacia-la-derecha/)).
The morning began with a grim omen — a police officer shot in Puerto Varas was left brain
dead — and before taking office Kast dispatched his security minister, Trinidad Steinert,
to the Los Lagos region
([La Prensa Austral](https://laprensaaustral.cl/2026/03/11/kast-asume-la-presidencia-de-chile-y-marca-el-giro-mas-fuerte-hacia-la-derecha/)).
His government promises a "gobierno de emergencia" with a first-100-days-style plan called
"Desafío 90" led by economist Bernardo Fontaine, against a 3.6-percent-of-GDP structural
fiscal deficit
([La Prensa Austral](https://laprensaaustral.cl/2026/03/11/kast-asume-la-presidencia-de-chile-y-marca-el-giro-mas-fuerte-hacia-la-derecha/)).
His cabinet included Claudio Alvarado at Interior, Jorge Quiroz at Finance and Francisco
Pérez Mackenna at Foreign Affairs — alongside two former Pinochet defence lawyers, Fernando
Rabat and Fernando Barros, as justice and defence ministers
([Xinhua](http://spanish.china.org.cn/txt/2026-03/12/content_118378199.htm)).

## The movement around him

Kast's presidency rests on a movement of young loyalists, and [Ignacio Dülger](/people/ignacio-dulger/)
is one of its clearest examples: he built his entire career inside Kast's orbit, from
youth-affairs coordinator for Acción Republicana in April 2018 to territorial chief for
Kast's 2021 presidential campaign, then into La Moneda's "Second Floor" as a presidential
adviser early in the Kast presidency, and from September 2026 as executive director of
Acción Republicana itself
(FactsLedger). The Clinic has described Dülger as one of the
militants closest to the president — a sign of how tightly Kast's governing circle is
drawn from the movement he built (FactsLedger).

or fiscal plans have been enacted into law; the presidency is described here only through
inauguration-day reporting and the movement around him. It does not claim any link between
Kast and Argentine consultant Fernando Cerimedo beyond what the Dülger profile documents:
Kast denied Cerimedo worked for him.
