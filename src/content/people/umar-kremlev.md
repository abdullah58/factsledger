## Overview

Umar Kremlev is a Russian businessman who has presided over the [International Boxing Association](/organizations/international-boxing-association/) since 2020, steering the once-insolvent Olympic boxing body through a Gazprom-funded era that ended with the International Olympic Committee cutting it loose ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)). In September 2026 he became a household name in American politics for something far from the ring: a ProPublica investigation found he had secretly paid hundreds of thousands of dollars toward the Bahamas wedding celebrations of [Donald Trump Jr.](/people/donald-trump-jr/), drawing a congressional investigation and a public defense from the president ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)).

## The man who did not exist in 2009

As ProPublica's investigation put it, "in 2009, Umar Kremlev did not yet exist" ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)). He was born Umar Lutfulloyev, with roots in the former Soviet republic of Tajikistan, and in his late twenties he carried a criminal record for extortion and battery, according to the Russian independent outlet Proekt ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)). In 2010, at age 28, he changed his name to Kremlev in Serpukhov, outside Moscow, where he had come of age boxing ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/09/21/who-is-the-putin-ally-behind-trump-jr-s-wedding-bash/91793868007/)).

What followed was a rapid climb through the institutions of Putin's Russia. Kremlev joined the Night Wolves, a government-backed biker gang dubbed "Putin's Angels" by Rolling Stone, where he rode Harleys and Ducatis and — more consequentially — grew close to Alexei Rubezhnoi, the chief of [Vladimir Putin's](/people/vladimir-putin/) presidential guard, with whom he now spars and spends holidays ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/09/21/who-is-the-putin-ally-behind-trump-jr-s-wedding-bash/91793868007/)). "He chose the right people," the Proekt journalist Mikhail Rubin told USA Today ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/09/21/who-is-the-putin-ally-behind-trump-jr-s-wedding-bash/91793868007/)). In 2017, Rubezhnoi personally intervened to install Kremlev at the head of the Russian Boxing Federation ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)).

Kremlev collected state honors along the way — the Order of Merit to the Fatherland, 2nd degree, and then the Order of Friendship, which Putin awarded him in April 2026, about a month before the Trump wedding ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/09/21/who-is-the-putin-ally-behind-trump-jr-s-wedding-bash/91793868007/)). Days before that wedding, he traveled to China as part of the delegation accompanying Putin, according to Chinese state media cited by ProPublica ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)). Ukraine has imposed personal sanctions on him over alleged ties to Putin and Russian security services; the United States has not ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)).

## The IBA years

In 2020 Kremlev became president of the International Boxing Association, a body ProPublica likened to FIFA for boxing — it had long overseen the sport in the Olympics but was beset by corruption allegations and near insolvency ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)). Kremlev brought money from Gazprom, the state-owned energy giant that operates as an arm of Putin's government, which publicly became the IBA's financial backer to the tune of tens of millions of dollars ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)). A former IBA board member told ProPublica that Kremlev had a clear political agenda: "Umar is guided by Putin. It was using the sport for soft political power. It's geopolitics. That it is boxing is just happenstance" ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)).

The arrangement collapsed in 2023, when the IOC stripped the IBA of its role organizing Olympic boxing, citing governance failures and the group's refusal to transparently explain its financing or its dependence on a single state-owned company ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)). Kremlev has raged against the Olympic committee ever since ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)). After Trump's second inauguration, in January 2025, he sent the president an open letter asking him to look into the IOC's actions ahead of the 2028 Los Angeles Games: "We look forward with great optimism to the possibility of working together to make the Olympic movement great again" ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)).

## The wedding

On May 24, 2026, Donald Trump Jr. celebrated his wedding to [Bettina Anderson](/people/bettina-anderson/) in the Bahamas — a lavish three-day party across two ultra-exclusive private islands, with helicopters and seaplanes ferrying guests, a five-tier funfetti cake airlifted from Florida, and fireworks launched from a barge over the sea ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)). About 50 guests attended, including the groom's brother-in-law [Jared Kushner](/people/jared-kushner) and brother Eric Trump — and Kremlev himself, part of a large Russian-speaking contingent whose presence puzzled other attendees ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)).

ProPublica's September 14 investigation, based on records and interviews with three people familiar with the event, found that Kremlev footed the bill for hundreds of thousands of dollars of it: he paid to rent out one of the private islands — where a reception was held and guests slept, at roughly $100,000 a night — covered the fireworks show at about $70,000, and his team helped plan the event ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)). The payments came from IB Challenger, a Dubai-based entity affiliated with the IBA that the boxing association uses for financial transactions; Kremlev's representatives said the IBA itself incurred no wedding costs but did not address the Dubai entity's payments ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)).

The couple confirmed Kremlev's role after the story broke. "Our dear friend Umar very generously hosted two incredible nights of celebrations for us AFTER our wedding," read a statement posted to Anderson's Instagram and signed "Don & Bettina." "It was an extraordinarily generous wedding gift from a friend, and something for which we were and remain incredibly grateful" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/18/politics/trump-jr-wedding-russian-billionaire?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). "Friendship doesn't require a political motive. Generosity doesn't automatically come with an agenda. And sometimes a wedding gift is simply a wedding gift," the statement continued ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/18/politics/trump-jr-wedding-russian-billionaire?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

The fallout was swift. On September 15, Rep. Robert Garcia of California, the top Democrat on the House Oversight Committee, announced an investigation into Kremlev's gift, writing that the relationship between a sitting president's son and "a member of Russian dictator Vladimir Putin's inner circle raises serious national security and public corruption concerns" ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/09/21/who-is-the-putin-ally-behind-trump-jr-s-wedding-bash/91793868007/)). On September 18, President Trump told reporters in the Oval Office that his son had paid Kremlev back: "As I understand it, he paid him back," adding that such gifts were "totally allowed" and joking that "maybe (Trump Jr.) didn't do a background check" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/18/politics/trump-jr-wedding-russian-billionaire?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). Attorney General Todd Blanche, asked about the matter, said Trump "doesn't know who this person is" and shrugged off the idea of an investigation: "Am I investigating what?" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/18/politics/trump-jr-wedding-russian-billionaire?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

How the two men met remains lightly documented. Trump Jr.'s spokesperson said Kremlev is "a personal friend" with whom he has no business relationship, and that they met through a mutual friend in the hunting world, bonding over boxing and the outdoors; Kremlev's press office said they first met "a couple of years ago" ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)). Publicly, they are known to have shared a stage in September 2025, when the IBA brought Trump Jr. to Istanbul for a panel on boxing — with Anderson sitting offstage — alongside Kremlev, Manny Pacquiao, and Muhammad Ali's daughter Rasheda ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not state the exact dollar amount Kremlev spent — every source reports it only as "hundreds of thousands of dollars." It does not treat the repayment as confirmed: President Trump said "as I understand it, he paid him back," and Trump Organization officials did not answer questions about whether the money was returned ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/18/politics/trump-jr-wedding-russian-billionaire?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). It does not resolve the dispute over his criminal record — Proekt reports convictions for extortion and battery, while his press office insists his record is "completely clean" ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)). It does not assert anything about a Senate subpoena effort, Melania Trump's office's statements, or the 2023 dispute over boxer Imane Khelif, which appeared only in sources not read in full for this profile. The word "oligarch" is the outlets' characterization, not a legal status — Kremlev does not appear on public U.S. sanction lists ([ProPublica](https://factkeepers.com/nothing-to-see-here-the-russian-oligarch-bankrolling-donald-trump-jr-s-wedding/)).
