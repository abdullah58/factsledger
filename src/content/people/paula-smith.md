# Paula Smith

## The business partner behind the career pivot

Search results treat Paula Smith as a trivia answer — "Chuck Lorre's ex-wife," padded out in content-farm biographies with unverified birth years, alma maters, and physical stats. The verifiable record is narrower but more interesting: Smith was Lorre's business partner, and Britannica places their marriage at the exact moment his career turned. "By the early 1980s Lorre had a wife, Paula Smith (married 1979, divorced 1992), and two daughters and chose to pursue television writing as a more stable career path than touring musician had been" ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Chuck-Lorre)).

Before television, Lorre — born Charles Michael Levine — had spent roughly 17 years as a guitarist and songwriter, playing weddings, bars, and cruise ships ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Chuck-Lorre)). The marriage to Smith in 1979 coincided with the end of that chapter. Multiple outlets describe her as his business partner at the time, and Nicki Swift corroborates the outline: "he first married his business partner Paula Smith in 1979 — with whom he had two children — before they divorced in 1992" ([Nicki Swift](https://www.nickiswift.com/939308/chuck-lorre-has-sad-relationship-news-yet-again/)). The marriage and the partnership ended together after 13 years.

## A deliberately thin public record

Smith has stayed out of the spotlight, and unlike Lorre — the "King of Sitcoms" behind *Two and a Half Men*, *The Big Bang Theory*, and *Young Sheldon* — she has no public career of her own on record ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Chuck-Lorre)). This profile keeps to what the citable sources support: the years of the marriage, the business partnership, and the two children. Everything else circulating about her — a 1957 birth year, a UCLA English degree, executive titles at Chuck Lorre Productions, documentary ventures — comes only from content farms that were not read or treated as sources, and is not repeated here. Her later husband [Chuck Lorre](/people/chuck-lorre/) remarried twice after their 1992 divorce ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Chuck-Lorre)).
