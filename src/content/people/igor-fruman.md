## Overview

Igor Fruman was the lesser-known half of the duo — he and Lev Parnas — who carried out Rudy Giuliani's Ukraine errands and ended up at the center of a federal campaign-finance prosecution. In September 2021 he pleaded guilty to soliciting money from a foreign national for U.S. political donations tied to a cannabis business venture; in January 2022 a judge sentenced him to a year and a day in prison. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/igor-fruman-ex-associate-giuliani-says-he-will-plead-guilty-2021-09-10/)) ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/ex-giuliani-associate-fruman-be-sentenced-campaign-finance-case-2022-01-21/)) What standard accounts usually leave out is the strange coda: his surrender was delayed a week so his children's grandparents could flee Russia's invasion of Ukraine, and he spent fewer than two months in actual Bureau of Prisons custody before moving to home incarceration. ([Inner City Press](http://www.innercitypress.com/sdnytrial18parnaskukushkinicp030222.html)) ([Inner City Press](http://www.innercitypress.com/sdnytrial32parnaskukushkinicp070622.html))

## The Giuliani connection

Fruman, a businessman, became publicly known as an associate of Giuliani's during the Trump presidency. Giuliani enlisted Fruman and Parnas to help dig up damaging material about then-presidential candidate Joe Biden and his son [Hunter Biden](/people/hunter-biden/), and the pair served as liaisons between Giuliani and Ukrainian officials and business tycoons. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/igor-fruman-ex-associate-giuliani-says-he-will-plead-guilty-2021-09-10/)) ([Associated Press](https://kvnutalk.com/giuliani-associate-gets-year-in-prison-in-foreign-donor-case/)) They also helped orchestrate the ouster of Marie Yovanovitch, the U.S. ambassador to Ukraine, who was recalled in May 2019. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/igor-fruman-ex-associate-giuliani-says-he-will-plead-guilty-2021-09-10/)) According to the indictment, Parnas sought the help of a U.S. congressman — identified as Republican Pete Sessions of Texas — to press for her removal; Trump eventually removed her and called her "bad news" in his July 25 phone call with Ukraine's president. ([Associated Press](https://www.tbsnews.net/international/politics/giuliani-associates-charged-illegally-funneling-cash-pro-trump-group)) Giuliani himself was not charged and denied wrongdoing, though he was under federal investigation over possible violations of foreign-lobbying disclosure rules, with former federal judge Barbara Jones appointed as special master to review materials seized from him. ([Associated Press](https://kvnutalk.com/giuliani-associate-gets-year-in-prison-in-foreign-donor-case/)) Giuliani told Reuters he had "no involvement" in the flow of money at the heart of the case, while acknowledging the pair's role in his Ukraine work: "Parnas and Igor helped me on certain things. They helped me with logistics. They know the Ukraine, they speak Russian. They helped me locate people in a few cases." ([Associated Press](https://www.tbsnews.net/international/politics/giuliani-associates-charged-illegally-funneling-cash-pro-trump-group))

## The charges

In October 2019, Fruman and Parnas were arrested at Dulles airport in Virginia on a Wednesday night as they prepared to board a flight on one-way tickets to Vienna — Parnas had been scheduled to sit for a deposition with House impeachment investigators the next day, Fruman the day after. ([Associated Press](https://www.tbsnews.net/international/politics/giuliani-associates-charged-illegally-funneling-cash-pro-trump-group)) Prosecutors called them a flight risk; a federal magistrate judge set $1 million bond for each man with GPS-monitored home detention. ([Associated Press](https://www.tbsnews.net/international/politics/giuliani-associates-charged-illegally-funneling-cash-pro-trump-group)) They were each charged with two counts of conspiracy, one count of false statements, and one count of falsification of business records. ([Associated Press](https://www.tbsnews.net/international/politics/giuliani-associates-charged-illegally-funneling-cash-pro-trump-group)) The indictment alleged they had donated $325,000 to the pro-Trump super PAC America First Action in May 2018, falsely reporting it as coming from a purported natural-gas company, Global Energy Producers — a shell with no real business, set up to conceal the money's true source. ([Associated Press](https://www.tbsnews.net/international/politics/giuliani-associates-charged-illegally-funneling-cash-pro-trump-group)) A parallel scheme involved raising money for a marijuana-license business: prosecutors said the pair conspired to contribute at least $1 million from an unidentified Russian businessman to candidates for federal and state offices to buy influence for the venture, funneling donations to candidates in Nevada, New York, and other states. ([Associated Press](https://www.tbsnews.net/international/politics/giuliani-associates-charged-illegally-funneling-cash-pro-trump-group)) Two other men were charged in the indictment: David Correia and Andrey Kukushkin, a Ukrainian-born businessman who had been a vice president of a Russian hedge fund before moving into California marijuana businesses. ([Associated Press](https://www.tbsnews.net/international/politics/giuliani-associates-charged-illegally-funneling-cash-pro-trump-group)) Fruman later admitted soliciting $1 million from Moscow businessman Andrey Muraviev — structured as a loan Fruman had to repay — for donations to Republicans in Nevada, Florida, and other states to support a recreational marijuana venture. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/ex-giuliani-associate-fruman-be-sentenced-campaign-finance-case-2022-01-21/)) ([Associated Press](https://kvnutalk.com/giuliani-associate-gets-year-in-prison-in-foreign-donor-case/)) ([Inner City Press](http://www.innercitypress.com/sdnytrial18parnaskukushkinicp030222.html)) Politicians who received the money, including Florida governor Ron DeSantis and Nevada's Adam Laxalt, said they had been unaware of its foreign source. ([Associated Press](https://kvnutalk.com/giuliani-associate-gets-year-in-prison-in-foreign-donor-case/)) Muraviev was not charged. ([Associated Press](https://kvnutalk.com/giuliani-associate-gets-year-in-prison-in-foreign-donor-case/))

## The guilty plea

On September 10, 2021, Fruman pleaded guilty in Manhattan federal court to a single count of soliciting money from a foreign national, in a hearing before U.S. District Judge J. Paul Oetken. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/igor-fruman-ex-associate-giuliani-says-he-will-plead-guilty-2021-09-10/)) He entered no cooperation agreement covering the separate federal probe of Giuliani's lobbying work in Ukraine. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/igor-fruman-ex-associate-giuliani-says-he-will-plead-guilty-2021-09-10/)) In his allocution he told the court, "I had little experience in the rules surrounding political donations." ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/igor-fruman-ex-associate-giuliani-says-he-will-plead-guilty-2021-09-10/)) He faced up to 46 months under the federal guidelines at sentencing, then set for January 21, 2022. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/igor-fruman-ex-associate-giuliani-says-he-will-plead-guilty-2021-09-10/))

## The sentencing

On January 21, 2022, Oetken sentenced Fruman — then 55 — to 12 months and one day in prison, plus two years of home confinement and a $10,000 fine, with four of his children present in the courtroom. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/ex-giuliani-associate-fruman-be-sentenced-campaign-finance-case-2022-01-21/)) The sentence came in well below the 37-to-46-month range prosecutors had recommended; the probation office had suggested 14 months. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/ex-giuliani-associate-fruman-be-sentenced-campaign-finance-case-2022-01-21/)) ([Inner City Press](http://www.innercitypress.com/sdnytrial18parnaskukushkinicp030222.html)) His defense lawyer, [Todd Blanche](/people/todd-blanche/), argued that eight days in jail after his arrest plus 28 months of home incarceration with GPS monitoring was already "punishment enough," and pressed for home detention instead of prison. ([Associated Press](https://kvnutalk.com/giuliani-associate-gets-year-in-prison-in-foreign-donor-case/)) ([Inner City Press](http://www.innercitypress.com/sdnytrial18parnaskukushkinicp030222.html)) Blanche also told the court that Fruman had separated from his wife in 2017 and had spent those eight days in solitary confinement. ([Inner City Press](http://www.innercitypress.com/sdnytrial18parnaskukushkinicp030222.html))

Oetken was unsparing about the offense — Fruman's conduct, he said, "undermines democracy" — but he also put the scheme in perspective: "This wasn't the Manchurian candidate. It was about a cannabis business. They were clueless." ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/ex-giuliani-associate-fruman-be-sentenced-campaign-finance-case-2022-01-21/)) ([Inner City Press](http://www.innercitypress.com/sdnytrial18parnaskukushkinicp030222.html)) He acknowledged Fruman's "significant notoriety, some of it unfair," imposed the extra day specifically so Fruman could earn 54 days of good-time credit, and recommended the Otisville facility. ([Inner City Press](http://www.innercitypress.com/sdnytrial18parnaskukushkinicp030222.html))

## After sentencing: a short stay behind bars

The aftermath did not go as the sentence suggested. On March 2, 2022 — five days before Fruman was to surrender — Blanche asked Oetken for a seven-day adjournment because "the grandparents of Mr. Fruman's three children live in Ukraine, and are currently attempting to safely leave the country because of the Russian invasion." The government did not object. ([Inner City Press](http://www.innercitypress.com/sdnytrial18parnaskukushkinicp030222.html)) A July 2022 letter from the U.S. Attorney's Office to Oetken then laid out how brief the custody was: Fruman "reported to the BOP for his 366 day sentence on March 13, 2022, he was transferred to crisis furlough on May 3, 2022 and then transferred to home incarceration beginning May 25, 2022" — under two months inside a federal facility. ([Inner City Press](http://www.innercitypress.com/sdnytrial32parnaskukushkinicp070622.html))

The brevity drew notice. At Parnas's own sentencing — Parnas had been convicted in October 2021 and received 20 months — his lawyer Joseph Bondy claimed Fruman had "gone to jail for less than three months," prompting Oetken to ask the prosecutor directly: "Why did Mr Fruman serve only three months, when I sentenced him to a year and a day?" The prosecutor said she would ask the Bureau of Prisons. ([Inner City Press](http://www.innercitypress.com/sdnytrial32parnaskukushkinicp070622.html))
