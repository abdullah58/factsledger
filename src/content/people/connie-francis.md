## Overview

Connie Francis (1937–2025) was an American pop singer who became the first woman to reach No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, with "Everybody's Somebody's Fool" in 1960, and went on to score 53 Hot 100 entries and three No. 1 singles. Born Concetta Rosa Maria Franconero in Newark, New Jersey, in the [United States](/places/united-states/), she dominated American pop from 1958 to 1964, then found a new audience in 2025 when her 1962 recording "Pretty Little Baby" went viral on TikTok weeks before her death at 87 ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1238530020/1960s-pop-star-connie-francis-has-died-the-singer-s-life-was-touched-by-tragedies); [PEOPLE](https://people.com/connie-francis-pretty-little-baby-is-tiktoks-2025-global-song-5-years-after-singer-s-death-11867240)).

Francis sold more than 40 million records before her 25th birthday and made 16 gold records, a pace that compressed her entire chart peak into roughly six years ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1238530020/1960s-pop-star-connie-francis-has-died-the-singer-s-life-was-touched-by-tragedies)). She was also a film star, a multilingual recording artist and, after a 1974 sexual assault and years of mental-health crises, a public advocate for victims of violence. She retired in 2018, and her death in July 2025 came at the height of an unlikely late-career revival ([Official Charts](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/pretty-little-baby-singer-connie-francis-dies-aged-87/); [South Florida Sun Sentinel](https://broward.us/2025/07/17/an-amazing-human-being-fort-lauderdale-icon-unlikely-tiktok-star-connie-francis-remembered/)).

## From Newark accordion prodigy to "Who's Sorry Now?"

Francis was born to an Italian-American family in Newark, and her father George Franconero, a roofing contractor who played the accordion, had her singing and playing a child-size accordion from the age of three. At nine she began appearing on television, including "Arthur Godfrey's Talent Scouts" and "The Perry Como Show", and Godfrey suggested the stage name Connie Francis ([Associated Press](https://www.air1.com/faith/news/us-and-world/50s-60s-music-star-connie-francis-has-passed-away-surprisingly-she-had-a-tiktok-resurgence-this-year-55689)). She signed with MGM Records at 17, but her first single, "Freddy", flopped, and the label decided not to renew her contract ([Associated Press](https://www.air1.com/faith/news/us-and-world/50s-60s-music-star-connie-francis-has-passed-away-surprisingly-she-had-a-tiktok-resurgence-this-year-55689); [Classic Country Music](https://classiccountrymusic.com/connie-francis-beloved-singer-behind-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-age-87/)).

She was ready to give up music and take a four-year New York University scholarship to study medicine when MGM gave her one last session, at which her father insisted she record the 1923 ballad "Who's Sorry Now?" — a song she disliked ([ExtraTV](https://extratv.com/2025/07/17/connie-francis-where-the-boys-are-singer-who-went-viral-with-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-87/)). On January 1, 1958, Dick Clark played it on "American Bandstand" and called her a surefire hit; the record reached No. 4 in the US, and MGM changed its mind ([ExtraTV](https://extratv.com/2025/07/17/connie-francis-where-the-boys-are-singer-who-went-viral-with-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-87/); [NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1238530020/1960s-pop-star-connie-francis-has-died-the-singer-s-life-was-touched-by-tragedies)). Her first modest success had actually come the year before, with the duet "The Majesty of Love" alongside Marvin Rainwater ([ExtraTV](https://extratv.com/2025/07/17/connie-francis-where-the-boys-are-singer-who-went-viral-with-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-87/)).

## Three No. 1s and the Hot 100 record

Francis scored four Top 40 singles in 1958 — "Who's Sorry Now?", "My Happiness", "Stupid Cupid" and "Fallin'" — with "Stupid Cupid", written by Neil Sedaka and Howard Greenfield, her second smash ([South Florida Sun Sentinel](https://broward.us/2025/07/17/an-amazing-human-being-fort-lauderdale-icon-unlikely-tiktok-star-connie-francis-remembered/); [ExtraTV](https://extratv.com/2025/07/17/connie-francis-where-the-boys-are-singer-who-went-viral-with-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-87/)). In 1960 "Everybody's Somebody's Fool" made her the first woman ever to top the Billboard Hot 100, and "My Heart Has a Mind of Its Own" followed to No. 1 the same year; her third No. 1, "Don't Break the Heart That Loves You", came in 1962 ([Official Charts](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/pretty-little-baby-singer-connie-francis-dies-aged-87/); [South Florida Sun Sentinel](https://broward.us/2025/07/17/an-amazing-human-being-fort-lauderdale-icon-unlikely-tiktok-star-connie-francis-remembered/)). She placed 15 songs in the Top 10 and 53 entries on the Hot 100 in total ([South Florida Sun Sentinel](https://broward.us/2025/07/17/an-amazing-human-being-fort-lauderdale-icon-unlikely-tiktok-star-connie-francis-remembered/); [Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/1930785/nicki-minaj-breaks-record-for-most-hot-100-hits-by-a-female-artist/news)).

Francis held the women's record until [Aretha Franklin](/people/aretha-franklin/) passed her on June 18, 1977, when "Break It to Me Gently" became Franklin's 54th entry; Franklin held it for nearly 40 years, until [Nicki Minaj](/people/nicki-minaj/) reached 76 entries on April 1, 2017 ([Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/1930785/nicki-minaj-breaks-record-for-most-hot-100-hits-by-a-female-artist/news)). Her last Top 40 hit was 1964's "Be Anything (but Be Mine)", and her final Hot 100 entry was "The Wedding Cake" at the end of the 1960s ([ExtraTV](https://extratv.com/2025/07/17/connie-francis-where-the-boys-are-singer-who-went-viral-with-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-87/)).

## Films, languages and international stardom

Francis was more than a singles artist: she carried four MGM teen-romance films — "Where the Boys Are" (1960), "Follow the Boys" (1963), "Looking for Love" (1964) and "When the Boys Meet the Girls" (1965) — and sang the theme of the first, which helped turn Fort Lauderdale into a spring-break destination ([ExtraTV](https://extratv.com/2025/07/17/connie-francis-where-the-boys-are-singer-who-went-viral-with-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-87/); [South Florida Sun Sentinel](https://broward.us/2025/07/17/an-amazing-human-being-fort-lauderdale-icon-unlikely-tiktok-star-connie-francis-remembered/)). The city gave her a Walk of Fame star in 2010, on the film's 50th anniversary ([South Florida Sun Sentinel](https://broward.us/2025/07/17/an-amazing-human-being-fort-lauderdale-icon-unlikely-tiktok-star-connie-francis-remembered/)). She re-recorded her hits in Italian and Spanish, among other languages, and released albums in Yiddish and Hebrew, reflecting the Jewish culture she grew up around; her 1959 album "Connie Francis Sings Italian Favorites" was her biggest album success ([Associated Press](https://www.air1.com/faith/news/us-and-world/50s-60s-music-star-connie-francis-has-passed-away-surprisingly-she-had-a-tiktok-resurgence-this-year-55689); [ExtraTV](https://extratv.com/2025/07/17/connie-francis-where-the-boys-are-singer-who-went-viral-with-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-87/)).

Her reach crossed the Atlantic as well as the language barrier: "Everybody's Somebody's Fool" peaked at No. 5 in the UK, the sixth of her 10 UK Top 10 hits, and a 1977 compilation, "The Singles Collection", topped the UK Albums Chart ([Official Charts](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/pretty-little-baby-singer-connie-francis-dies-aged-87/)). ExtraTV counts her among the biggest-selling acts of her era, behind the Beatles and Elvis Presley, and says she was on her way to selling 100 million albums ([ExtraTV](https://extratv.com/2025/07/17/connie-francis-where-the-boys-are-singer-who-went-viral-with-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-87/)). Her early romance with fellow teen idol Bobby Darin, whom she considered the love of her life, ended when her disapproving father pulled a gun on Darin at a rehearsal ([Associated Press](https://www.air1.com/faith/news/us-and-world/50s-60s-music-star-connie-francis-has-passed-away-surprisingly-she-had-a-tiktok-resurgence-this-year-55689); [South Florida Sun Sentinel](https://broward.us/2025/07/17/an-amazing-human-being-fort-lauderdale-icon-unlikely-tiktok-star-connie-francis-remembered/)).

## Tragedy, lawsuits and advocacy

Francis's career was interrupted by a run of personal disasters. In 1967 a cosmetic procedure meant to narrow her nose damaged her singing voice, leaving her with a range of about seven notes; it took three more operations before she could sing again ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1238530020/1960s-pop-star-connie-francis-has-died-the-singer-s-life-was-touched-by-tragedies)). On November 8, 1974, after performing at the Westbury Music Fair in New York, she was raped at knifepoint and beaten in her Howard Johnson motel room; the attacker was never captured ([Associated Press](https://www.air1.com/faith/news/us-and-world/50s-60s-music-star-connie-francis-has-passed-away-surprisingly-she-had-a-tiktok-resurgence-this-year-55689)). She sued the hotel chain for inadequate security; a jury awarded her $2.5 million in 1976, and the case settled out of court for $1,475,000 while an appeal was pending. It helped change hotel security standards ([Associated Press](https://www.air1.com/faith/news/us-and-world/50s-60s-music-star-connie-francis-has-passed-away-surprisingly-she-had-a-tiktok-resurgence-this-year-55689); [NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1238530020/1960s-pop-star-connie-francis-has-died-the-singer-s-life-was-touched-by-tragedies)).

Her brother George was shot to death outside his New Jersey home in 1981, and her father had her committed to a psychiatric hospital, where she was diagnosed as manic-depressive — a diagnosis she later said was wrong, blaming post-traumatic stress disorder from the 1974 attack instead ([Associated Press](https://www.air1.com/faith/news/us-and-world/50s-60s-music-star-connie-francis-has-passed-away-surprisingly-she-had-a-tiktok-resurgence-this-year-55689); [ExtraTV](https://extratv.com/2025/07/17/connie-francis-where-the-boys-are-singer-who-went-viral-with-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-87/)). She attempted suicide in 1984 and spent three days in a coma, and later said she had been committed against her will 17 times in nine years in five states ([Associated Press](https://www.air1.com/faith/news/us-and-world/50s-60s-music-star-connie-francis-has-passed-away-surprisingly-she-had-a-tiktok-resurgence-this-year-55689); [South Florida Sun Sentinel](https://broward.us/2025/07/17/an-amazing-human-being-fort-lauderdale-icon-unlikely-tiktok-star-connie-francis-remembered/)). She became an outspoken advocate for victims of violence and a national spokeswoman for Mental Health America, and her 1984 memoir "Who's Sorry Now?" became a bestseller ([South Florida Sun Sentinel](https://broward.us/2025/07/17/an-amazing-human-being-fort-lauderdale-icon-unlikely-tiktok-star-connie-francis-remembered/); [ExtraTV](https://extratv.com/2025/07/17/connie-francis-where-the-boys-are-singer-who-went-viral-with-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-87/)).

## The 2025 TikTok revival and death

Francis resumed performing in 1989 and worked steadily until her retirement in 2018, but her final chapter was the most unexpected of all ([ExtraTV](https://extratv.com/2025/07/17/connie-francis-where-the-boys-are-singer-who-went-viral-with-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-87/)). In May 2025 her 1962 recording "Pretty Little Baby" — one of 40 songs she had cut in four days in August 1961, and a track she said she had forgotten ever recording — went viral on TikTok as the soundtrack to wholesome videos about family, pets and flowers ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/connie-francis-pretty-little-baby-is-tiktoks-2025-global-song-5-years-after-singer-s-death-11867240)). She joined TikTok herself in June, telling fans she was "flabbergasted and excited", and in her final TikTok on June 26 thanked Kylie Jenner, Timothée Chalamet, Ariana Grande and Taylor Swift ([ExtraTV](https://extratv.com/2025/07/17/connie-francis-where-the-boys-are-singer-who-went-viral-with-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-87/)).

TikTok later named "Pretty Little Baby" its Top Global Song of 2025: it was used in more than 28.4 million videos in the year, generating over 68.6 billion views, with one clip of Kylie Jenner and her daughter Stormi drawing more than 132 million views, and the track earned over 130 million Spotify streams and spent weeks on the Billboard Global 200 and the UK Official Chart ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/connie-francis-pretty-little-baby-is-tiktoks-2025-global-song-5-years-after-singer-s-death-11867240)). She was too ill to capitalise on it: hospitalised on July 2 with "extreme pain" from a pelvic fracture, diagnosed with pneumonia on July 15, she died the next day, July 16, 2025, at North Broward Hospital in Pompano Beach, Florida, aged 87 ([Classic Country Music](https://classiccountrymusic.com/connie-francis-beloved-singer-behind-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-age-87/)). Ron Roberts, her friend and publicist, announced her death on Facebook; she was survived by her son Joey Garzilli, adopted during her third marriage ([Associated Press](https://www.air1.com/faith/news/us-and-world/50s-60s-music-star-connie-francis-has-passed-away-surprisingly-she-had-a-tiktok-resurgence-this-year-55689); [Official Charts](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/pretty-little-baby-singer-connie-francis-dies-aged-87/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who was Connie Francis?

Connie Francis (1937–2025) was an American pop singer, actress and author, born Concetta Rosa Maria Franconero in Newark, New Jersey. She was a top performer of the pre-Beatles era, the first woman to top the Billboard Hot 100, and the singer of "Who's Sorry Now?", "Everybody's Somebody's Fool" and "Where the Boys Are" ([NPR](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1238530020/1960s-pop-star-connie-francis-has-died-the-singer-s-life-was-touched-by-tragedies); [Associated Press](https://www.air1.com/faith/news/us-and-world/50s-60s-music-star-connie-francis-has-passed-away-surprisingly-she-had-a-tiktok-resurgence-this-year-55689)).

### What was Connie Francis's first No. 1 song?

"Everybody's Somebody's Fool" reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1960, making Francis the first woman ever to top the chart. She followed it with two more No. 1s: "My Heart Has a Mind of Its Own" later in 1960 and "Don't Break the Heart That Loves You" in 1962 ([Official Charts](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/pretty-little-baby-singer-connie-francis-dies-aged-87/); [South Florida Sun Sentinel](https://broward.us/2025/07/17/an-amazing-human-being-fort-lauderdale-icon-unlikely-tiktok-star-connie-francis-remembered/)).

### How did "Pretty Little Baby" go viral?

The 1962 recording went viral on TikTok in May 2025, 63 years after it was made, as the soundtrack to wholesome videos about family, pets and flowers. TikTok named it its Top Global Song of 2025, with 28.4 million videos and 68.6 billion views; Francis, then 87, said she had forgotten recording it and joined TikTok herself in June ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/connie-francis-pretty-little-baby-is-tiktoks-2025-global-song-5-years-after-singer-s-death-11867240)).

### How did Connie Francis die?

Francis was hospitalised on July 2, 2025, with "extreme pain" from a pelvic fracture, was diagnosed with pneumonia on July 15, and died the next day, July 16, 2025, at North Broward Hospital in Pompano Beach, Florida. She was 87. Her friend and publicist Ron Roberts announced her death on Facebook ([Classic Country Music](https://classiccountrymusic.com/connie-francis-beloved-singer-behind-pretty-little-baby-dies-at-age-87/); [Associated Press](https://www.air1.com/faith/news/us-and-world/50s-60s-music-star-connie-francis-has-passed-away-surprisingly-she-had-a-tiktok-resurgence-this-year-55689)).

### Was Connie Francis in the Broadway musical "Just in Time"?

Francis herself did not perform in it, but she is portrayed in "Just in Time", the Broadway musical about her former love Bobby Darin. Gracie Lawrence plays Francis in the show, and earned a Tony Award nomination for the role ([South Florida Sun Sentinel](https://broward.us/2025/07/17/an-amazing-human-being-fort-lauderdale-icon-unlikely-tiktok-star-connie-francis-remembered/)).
