## Overview

Sam Yam is a software engineer and entrepreneur best known as the co-founder and longtime chief technology officer of Patreon, the membership platform that lets fans pay creators directly. For more than thirteen years he ran Patreon's technology organization, having built the first version of the platform himself in 2013 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/02/12/patreon-story/)). In September 2026 he announced he was leaving Patreon to join OpenAI, where he will lead a new Creator Product division — bringing Patreon's former product head Drew Rowny and engineering head Shannon Ma with him ([GIGAZINE](http://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260924-patreon-execs-join-openai/); [Music Ally](https://musically.com/2026/09/23/ex-patreon-trio-join-openai-to-build-together-with-creators/)).

## Before Patreon: Loopt, AdWhirl and the road to 2013

Yam was Jack Conte's roommate at Stanford, where Conte studied music and Yam studied computer science ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/02/12/patreon-story/)). After graduation Yam continued toward a master's in computer science — Marc Andreessen wrote his letter of recommendation — but took a leave of absence to become one of the first engineers at the social-mapping startup Loopt ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/02/12/patreon-story/)). Loopt was acquired for $43 million in 2006, after which Yam founded AdWhirl, a service that let iPhone developers switch between ad networks dynamically; AdMob acquired it in 2009, and Google bought AdMob a few months later ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/02/12/patreon-story/)). After a short stint at Google, Yam spent three years experimenting with startup ideas, working for a time out of the Dogpatch Labs incubator — sitting, as he later recalled, across the table from Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger while they built the early, Burbn-branded version of Instagram, and doing pre-launch strategy sessions with Pinterest founder Ben Silbermann ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/02/12/patreon-story/); [Stanford eCorner](https://ecorner.stanford.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2020/01/your-plan-is-wrong-transcript.pdf)).

## Building Patreon (2013–2026)

Conte conceived Patreon in February 2013 out of his own frustration as a working musician: a $10,000 music video had earned him roughly $100 in YouTube ad revenue ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/02/12/patreon-story/)). Unable to build software himself, he pitched his old Stanford roommate. The two met at the Coffee Bar on Bryant Street in San Francisco on March 6, 2013 — the same day Yam was publicly unveiling an entirely different startup, the freelance-photographer marketplace OurSpot, with TechCrunch coverage already arranged. Conte's pitch won out: that evening Yam began coding the Patreon platform, and the pair agreed to be equal co-founders ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/02/12/patreon-story/)).

Patreon launched publicly on May 7, 2013, when Conte posted a video inviting his 100,000 YouTube subscribers to pledge support; within weeks fans were committing more than $5,000 per video, against about $100 per video in ad revenue ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/02/12/patreon-story/)). Forbes India later reported that the site went live in May 2013 with more than a hundred fans pledging upwards of $700 a month within minutes ([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/recliner/a-musicianturnedentrepreneurs-plan-to-save-creators-from-advertising/49863/1)). The founders then raised a $2.1 million seed round led by CRV and Freestyle Ventures — Reddit co-founder Alexis Ohanian joined — after initially targeting $700,000 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/02/12/patreon-story/)).

Yam was Patreon's only engineer through the summer of 2013 — writing the original PHP codebase while also handling customer service and social media — and later led its migration to Python ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/02/12/patreon-story/)). Patreon acquired the competing membership service Subbable in 2015 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/02/12/patreon-story/)). By 2018 the company had paid out more than $250 million to artists ($150 million in 2017 alone), took a 5 percent cut of pledges, and was valued at an estimated $400 million ([Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/recliner/a-musicianturnedentrepreneurs-plan-to-save-creators-from-advertising/49863/1)). By 2026 the platform reported more than 10 million monthly paying members and roughly 286,000 creators, with lifetime creator earnings above $10 billion ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/guide/patreon-users); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-brings-in-patreons-founders-to-build-a-creator-business/)).

In a January 2020 talk at Stanford, Yam described one of Patreon's formative lessons: the platform had launched around per-release payments, but about 95 percent of artists migrated to simple monthly billing because they wanted predictable income — an example, in his telling, of why "your plan is wrong" and execution has to adapt ([Stanford eCorner](https://ecorner.stanford.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2020/01/your-plan-is-wrong-transcript.pdf)).

Patreon's later years were bumpier. In July 2026 the company laid off 93 employees — 20 percent of its workforce — as part of a cost restructuring ([GIGAZINE](http://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260924-patreon-execs-join-openai/)), and its internal valuation had reportedly fallen to $1.5 billion from the $4 billion Series F valuation of 2021 ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/guide/patreon-users)).

## The OpenAI move (September 2026)

On September 22, 2026, Yam announced in a series of posts on X that he was joining OpenAI to lead Creator Product after "over 13 years since starting @Patreon," bringing "my favorite crew Drew Rowny and Shannon Ma, Patreon's (now former) Head of Product and Head of Engineering" (quoted verbatim by [GIGAZINE](http://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260924-patreon-execs-join-openai/)). "We're going to build together with Creators at OpenAI and share early access to a new set of tools that I think will be critically valuable to Creators and their communities," he wrote, adding "I believe human creativity is paramount" and pointing followers to OpenAI's DevDay on September 29 ([Music Ally](https://musically.com/2026/09/23/ex-patreon-trio-join-openai-to-build-together-with-creators/); [GIGAZINE](http://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260924-patreon-execs-join-openai/)). The hire was first reported by The Information, according to [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-brings-in-patreons-founders-to-build-a-creator-business/).

The move strips Patreon's senior product leadership in one stroke — its co-founder and CTO, its product head and its engineering head ([Music Ally](https://musically.com/2026/09/23/ex-patreon-trio-join-openai-to-build-together-with-creators/)). At OpenAI, Yam's brief is to build an ecosystem that makes products like ChatGPT "work harder for creators and their partners" ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/09/23/chatgpt-openai-hire-sam-yam-patreon-head-creator-products/)). The context, as trade press framed it: ChatGPT's share of AI prompt volume fell from 70 percent in January to 50 percent in June 2026, per Comscore ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-brings-in-patreons-founders-to-build-a-creator-business/)); OpenAI shuttered its Sora video app in April after usage fell below 500,000 ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-brings-in-patreons-founders-to-build-a-creator-business/)); and the company had begun showing ads to free-tier users the previous month ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-brings-in-patreons-founders-to-build-a-creator-business/)). OpenAI had also been courting creators through partnerships and its acquisition of the tech talk show TBPN ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/09/23/chatgpt-openai-hire-sam-yam-patreon-head-creator-products/)). What the Creator Product division will actually ship has not been announced ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-brings-in-patreons-founders-to-build-a-creator-business/)).
