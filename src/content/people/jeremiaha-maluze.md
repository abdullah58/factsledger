## Overview

Jeremiaha Maluze is the rare Bundesliga starter whose route to the first team ran
through the fifth tier — twice. Born on 11 November 2004 in Duisburg, he spent his
youth career at Rot-Weiß Oberhausen, KFC Uerdingen and MSV Duisburg before beginning
senior football in the Oberliga Niederrhein with SV Straelen and Mühlheimer FC in
2023/24, then establishing himself as a key player and regular starter for
FC Rot-Weiß Erfurt in the fourth-tier Regionalliga Nordost the following season
([Eintracht Frankfurt](https://profis.eintracht.de/news/vertragsverlaengerung-jeremiaha-maluze-178920/)).
In summer 2025 he signed a licensed-player contract with [Eintracht Frankfurt](/organizations/eintracht-frankfurt/) but was
assigned to the club's U21 squad — and there, in the fifth-tier Hessenliga, he put
up the season that changed his trajectory: 26 appearances, 3 goals and 10 assists,
nine of them in the second half of the campaign, driving the U21s' promotion to the
Regionalliga
([Eintracht Frankfurt](https://profis.eintracht.de/news/vertragsverlaengerung-jeremiaha-maluze-178920/)).

Two corrections are worth making up front, because the errors are in circulation.
First, the "3 goals and 10 assists in 26 games" line is sometimes attached to his
Erfurt season — including in the earlier version of this profile. It belongs to the
2025/26 U21 Hessenliga season; at Erfurt he was described by the club as a key player
and regular starter, with no verified goal tally attached in this profile
([Eintracht Frankfurt](https://profis.eintracht.de/news/vertragsverlaengerung-jeremiaha-maluze-178920/)).
Second, on 29 August 2026 he extended his Eintracht contract to 2031 and, within
hours, made his Bundesliga debut — from the starting lineup — in a 3-3 draw at
Union Berlin ([Ligainsider](https://www.ligainsider.de/jeremiaha-maluze_38581/sge-talent-maluze-neuer-vertrag-zum-bundesliga-debuet-417165/)).
Measured at 193 cm and listed with dual German/Nigerian nationality, the 21-year-old
had two Bundesliga appearances, two starts and one assist to his name when the season's
official data was last checked
([bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/de/spieler/jeremiaha-maluze)).

## From Duisburg youth football to the Oberliga

Maluze's youth path was unusually fragmented even before senior football. Eintracht
Frankfurt's own account names Rot-Weiß Oberhausen, KFC Uerdingen and MSV Duisburg as
the academies that trained him
([Eintracht Frankfurt](https://profis.eintracht.de/news/vertragsverlaengerung-jeremiaha-maluze-178920/)).
For MSV Duisburg's U19s in the 2022/23 A-Junioren-Bundesliga West he played 15
matches — every minute of all 15 — and scored twice
([FBref](https://fbref.com/en/players/7735395a/Jeremiaha-Maluze)).

When no professional pathway opened, he went the semi-professional route. In 2023/24
he played first for SV Straelen and then for Mühlheimer FC, both in the fifth-tier
Oberliga Niederrhein
([Eintracht Frankfurt](https://profis.eintracht.de/news/vertragsverlaengerung-jeremiaha-maluze-178920/)).
A year at FC Rot-Weiß Erfurt in the Regionalliga Nordost followed in 2024/25, where
the club says he became a "Leistungsträger und Stammkraft" — a key player and regular
starter
([Eintracht Frankfurt](https://profis.eintracht.de/news/vertragsverlaengerung-jeremiaha-maluze-178920/)).

## A season with the U21s that nobody planned as a breakthrough

Frankfurt signed Maluze in summer 2025 on a licensed-player contract but placed him
with the U21s to collect match practice
([Eintracht Frankfurt](https://profis.eintracht.de/news/vertragsverlaengerung-jeremiaha-maluze-178920/)).
That looked like a holding pattern. It turned into the most productive season of his
career: 26 Hessenliga appearances, three goals and ten assists — nine of those assists
in the Rückrunde — as the U21s won promotion to the Regionalliga
([Eintracht Frankfurt](https://profis.eintracht.de/news/vertragsverlaengerung-jeremiaha-maluze-178920/)).
Sporting director Timmo Hardung said the 21-year-old had adapted quickly to the higher
level, made a good impression with the senior team, was willing to learn and worked on
himself in a focused way — giving him, in Hardung's words, the attributes to continue
his path in professional football
([Eintracht Frankfurt](https://profis.eintracht.de/news/vertragsverlaengerung-jeremiaha-maluze-178920/)).

## Three months from the fifth tier to a Bundesliga starting XI

Maluze trained with Frankfurt's senior squad through the 2026/27 preseason and made
his first competitive appearance in the DFB-Pokal first round against SC St. Tönis,
replacing captain Robin Koch midway through the second half
([Eintracht Frankfurt](https://profis.eintracht.de/news/vertragsverlaengerung-jeremiaha-maluze-178920/)).
Frankfurt won 11-0
([Home of Sports](https://www.homeofsports.de/fussball/national/eintracht-newcomer-maluze-kickte-vor-drei-monaten-in-der-liga-94474940.html)).
Days later, on 29 August 2026, the club extended his contract to 2031 — and that same
afternoon he started the Bundesliga opener at Union Berlin, which finished 3-3
([Ligainsider](https://www.ligainsider.de/jeremiaha-maluze_38581/sge-talent-maluze-neuer-vertrag-zum-bundesliga-debuet-417165/);
[Home of Sports](https://www.homeofsports.de/fussball/national/eintracht-newcomer-maluze-kickte-vor-drei-monaten-in-der-liga-94474940.html)).
Maluze himself called it "the best time of my life" in the club's podcast
([Home of Sports](https://www.homeofsports.de/fussball/national/eintracht-newcomer-maluze-kickte-vor-drei-monaten-in-der-liga-94474940.html)).

The speed of it caught even Frankfurt's own supporters off guard. Fans who watched
the training-camp videos had assumed Maluze was only there to make up the numbers;
with the transfer window still open, everyone expected sporting CEO Markus Krösche to
sign at least one more centre-back ([SGE4EVER](https://www.sge4ever.de/chance-genutzt-sge-durchstarter-maluze-ueberzeugt/)).
Instead, Maluze won 94 percent of his duels and completed 73 percent of his passes
over 90 minutes against Union, and earned the trust immediately
([SGE4EVER](https://www.sge4ever.de/chance-genutzt-sge-durchstarter-maluze-ueberzeugt/)).

Competition arrived anyway: Lilian Brassier joined from Stade Rennes late in the
window and pushed Maluze to the bench for the 1-4 home defeat to Augsburg
([SGE4EVER](https://www.sge4ever.de/chance-genutzt-sge-durchstarter-maluze-ueberzeugt/)).
With Nnamdi Collins still injured, head coach Adi Hütter switched to a back five for
the trip to Mainz 05 and restored Maluze to the starting lineup. He responded with
another strong 90 minutes, setting up Jonathan Burkardt's 2-0 with a precise header
in a 3-1 away win
([SGE4EVER](https://www.sge4ever.de/chance-genutzt-sge-durchstarter-maluze-ueberzeugt/)).
He was measured at 33.8 km/h against Mainz — the fastest Frankfurt player recorded
that season ([SGE4EVER](https://www.sge4ever.de/chance-genutzt-sge-durchstarter-maluze-ueberzeugt/);
the league's official database lists 33.75 km/h for 2026/27, alongside 26 duels won,
17 headers won and one yellow card in two appearances
([bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/de/spieler/jeremiaha-maluze))).
Hütter, for his part, liked Maluze's front-foot defending but kept working with him
on when to stay behind his man instead
([Home of Sports](https://www.homeofsports.de/fussball/national/eintracht-newcomer-maluze-kickte-vor-drei-monaten-in-der-liga-94474940.html)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not assert any goal or assist totals for his 2024/25 season at
Rot-Weiß Erfurt — only that the club described him as a key player and regular
starter, because the competing numbers could not be verified against a fully-read
source. It does not claim he is an established Bundesliga starter: the record as
checked stands at two starts plus one cup appearance, and anything beyond that is
form in progress. It does not resolve whether his primary position is centre-back
or midfield — his club says the former, the league database says the latter. It
makes no claims about national-team involvement, about his private or family life,
or about transfer fees and agent arrangements, none of which were confirmed by
sources read in full for this rewrite.
