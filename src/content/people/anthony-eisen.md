## Overview

Anthony Eisen is the Australian fintech entrepreneur who co-founded [Afterpay](/organizations/afterpay/) with [Nick Molnar](/people/nick-molnar/) in 2014, ran it as co-CEO through its US$29 billion sale to [Block](/organizations/block/), and now sits on Block's board of directors while leading refunds startup Reshop as its chief executive ([Block, Inc. 2025 Proxy Statement](https://s29.q4cdn.com/628966176/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report-Proxy_ARS.pdf)).

The pairing was complementary: Molnar was a young online-retail entrepreneur, Eisen his neighbour and a finance veteran, and together they aimed at millennials embracing a cashless lifestyle ([AFP via TechXplore](https://techxplore.com/news/2021-08-afterpay-billion-aussie-startup.html)). Block's later account dates the founding to November 2014 ([Block, Inc. 2025 Proxy Statement](https://s29.q4cdn.com/628966176/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report-Proxy_ARS.pdf)). By the time Square moved to buy the company, Afterpay served more than 16 million consumers and nearly 100,000 merchants ([iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/square-to-buy-australias-afterpay-for-39-billion-568098)), and Block's 2025 announcement put its reach at more than 400,000 merchants and 24 million customers ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250206019469/en/Block-Names-Anthony-Eisen-Co-Founder-of-Afterpay-and-CEO-of-Reshop-to-Board-of-Directors)).

## From investment banking to Afterpay

Eisen's route into fintech ran through traditional finance. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce in Accounting and Finance from UNSW Australia and is a member of Chartered Accountants Australia ([Block, Inc. 2025 Proxy Statement](https://s29.q4cdn.com/628966176/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report-Proxy_ARS.pdf)). Before Afterpay he worked in investment banking specialising in mergers and acquisitions, then became Chief Investment Officer at Guinness Peat Group, involved in financial-services, software and technology companies in which the group was a major shareholder ([FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/executives/afterpay-names-anthony-eisen-and-nick-molnar-its-co-ceos/)).

Afterpay listed on the Australian Securities Exchange in May 2016 ([Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/refunds-platform-reshop-appoints-anthony-eisen-chief-executive-officer-and)). Eisen joined its board on 5 July 2017 and became CEO and Managing Director on 1 July 2019 ([FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/executives/afterpay-names-anthony-eisen-and-nick-molnar-its-co-ceos/)). In November 2020 the founders formalised shared leadership: Molnar became co-CEO alongside Eisen, both sharing strategy responsibility and measured on the same objectives — Molnar returning to the United States, Eisen remaining in Australia ([FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/executives/afterpay-names-anthony-eisen-and-nick-molnar-its-co-ceos/)).

The product let shoppers pay for smaller purchases in regular instalments, with no interest or joining fee and low spending limits; merchants paid transaction costs of around 4% and were not exposed to non-payment risk, while shoppers who missed payments faced late fees of up to 25% of the purchase price, capped at A$68 per order for Australian users ([AFP via TechXplore](https://techxplore.com/news/2021-08-afterpay-billion-aussie-startup.html)).

## The Block acquisition and the road to the board

Square announced an all-stock deal for Afterpay in August 2021 with an implied value of approximately US$29 billion (A$39 billion) based on Square's 30 July 2021 closing price, or 0.375 Square Class A shares per Afterpay share, a 30.6% premium; Afterpay's board unanimously recommended it ([iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/square-to-buy-australias-afterpay-for-39-billion-568098)). The deal was expected to close in the first quarter of calendar 2022 ([iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/square-to-buy-australias-afterpay-for-39-billion-568098)).

Eisen did not leave with the sale: from January 2022 he served as Block's co-lead of Afterpay until November 2024, then stayed on as a consultant aiding the transition of his duties until his election to Block's board ([Block, Inc. 2025 Proxy Statement](https://s29.q4cdn.com/628966176/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report-Proxy_ARS.pdf)). He joined the board in February 2025 ([Block, Inc. 2025 Proxy Statement](https://s29.q4cdn.com/628966176/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report-Proxy_ARS.pdf)); Block's head and chairman Jack Dorsey praised his work building a commerce ecosystem and his collaborative leadership ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250206019469/en/Block-Names-Anthony-Eisen-Co-Founder-of-Afterpay-and-CEO-of-Reshop-to-Board-of-Directors)). Block's proxy states he was selected for the board for his financial-services and fintech expertise, specifically in buy now, pay later, and as an Afterpay co-founder ([Block, Inc. 2025 Proxy Statement](https://s29.q4cdn.com/628966176/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report-Proxy_ARS.pdf)). He also serves on the board of the Technology Council of Australia ([Block, Inc. 2025 Proxy Statement](https://s29.q4cdn.com/628966176/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report-Proxy_ARS.pdf)).

His converted stake became visible in August 2026, when he sold 18,000 Block shares for about US$1.5 million under a pre-scheduled Rule 10b5-1 plan adopted in March 2026, leaving about 1.5 million shares directly held, valued at US$121.15 million — his stake is essentially the Afterpay business converted into Block stock ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/filings/2026/08/25/the-afterpay-co-founder-on-block-s-board-moved-18-000-shares-under-a-plan-set-in-march-here-s-what-to-know/)).

## Reshop: a second act on refunds

Eisen's next company attacks the other end of the purchase. He was a founding investor in Reshop and served as its chairman from inception before becoming its chief executive — a role Block's proxy dates to January 2025, announced alongside a US$17 million fundraise led by Matrix Partners, Sound Ventures, Woodson Capital and Touch Ventures ([Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/refunds-platform-reshop-appoints-anthony-eisen-chief-executive-officer-and); [Block, Inc. 2025 Proxy Statement](https://s29.q4cdn.com/628966176/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report-Proxy_ARS.pdf)). Reshop gives shoppers instant refunds when they return items to participating retailers, usable through a virtual card at stores accepting Visa debit, instead of a wait of days or weeks ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/commerce/ecommerce/2025/refunds-right-now-reshop-targets-gen-z-and-millennial-shopping-pain-points/?ref=eshopexperts.gr)).

Eisen's thesis is that returns are "the last white space of eCommerce": about 20% of purchases in America get returned, and more than US$600 billion in returns annually is "just lost in the system", benefiting neither consumer nor retailer ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/commerce/ecommerce/2025/refunds-right-now-reshop-targets-gen-z-and-millennial-shopping-pain-points/?ref=eshopexperts.gr)). The pitch targets Gen Z and millennial shoppers who pay by debit card, for whom a slow refund ties up their own money and creates purchase anxiety ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/commerce/ecommerce/2025/refunds-right-now-reshop-targets-gen-z-and-millennial-shopping-pain-points/?ref=eshopexperts.gr)). Early partner retailers included Alo Yoga, Steve Madden and Dolce Vita, and early adoption showed 1 in 2 customers making a repeat purchase, mostly within 24 hours ([Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/refunds-platform-reshop-appoints-anthony-eisen-chief-executive-officer-and)).
