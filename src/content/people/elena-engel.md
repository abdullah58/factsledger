## Overview

Elena Engel is the mother of [Dario Amodei](/people/dario-amodei/) and [Daniela Amodei](/people/daniela-amodei/), the siblings who co-founded the AI company [Anthropic](/organizations/anthropic/) — and in nearly every published account, that is all she is. She has never been the subject of a profile in her own right; each mention treats her as an adjunct to her children. The one place she appears as the central figure is a 2001 investigation by the Berkeley Daily Planet into a troubled public-works project, where she was the project manager. This profile is built on the two documented identities that actually belong to her: the Bay Area library construction project manager, and the mother of the Anthropic founders ([Berkeley Daily Planet](http://bdp.lmi.net/issue/2001-09-13/full_text); [Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Dario-Amodei); [The Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com:443/influencers-26/50jews-26/article-908079)).

A note on identity, because the name misleads: readers searching "Elena Engel" will also find a producer of the same name whose career is in film and audio production. That is a different person. None of the sources below associate this Elena Engel with entertainment work; every source identifies her as the library renovation project manager of Berkeley and San Francisco.

## The library project manager

Britannica's entry on Dario Amodei gives the plainest statement of her occupation: his mother was "a project manager for library renovation and construction projects" ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Dario-Amodei)). One profile of the Amodei siblings says she managed library construction and renovation projects across the Bay Area — that detail comes from a single source and is attributed as such ([press.farm](https://press.farm/the-amodei-siblings-how-dario-and-daniela-built-anthropics-ai-empire/)).

The documentary record of her career centers on one project: the renovation of the Berkeley Central Library. On September 13, 2001, the Berkeley Daily Planet published an investigation headlined "Central Library Renovation: Millions Over Budget, Months Behind Schedule," and the project manager named throughout is Elena Engel. The renovation was the flagship job under Measure S, the $49 million library bond Berkeley voters had approved in 1996; the Central Library portion alone was a $30 million undertaking ([Berkeley Daily Planet](http://bdp.lmi.net/issue/2001-09-13/full_text)).

By the fall of 2001 the job was in serious trouble. The Daily Planet reported the project was running millions of dollars over budget and eight to ten months behind schedule, with some 460 change orders filed by contractor Arntz Builders. Engel herself had estimated in July 2001 that the construction phase was running about $2 million over budget. The criticism was blunt: the city manager asked the library board to remove her from the project, and council members raised pointed questions about the management of the job. Engel did not keep a scheduled telephone interview with the paper, so her own account of the overruns does not appear in the piece ([Berkeley Daily Planet](http://bdp.lmi.net/issue/2001-09-13/full_text)).

That article is a snapshot, not a verdict — it captures one difficult project at one moment, and it does not record how the dispute was resolved. But it is the fullest contemporary account of her professional life that exists in the public record, and it is the reason this profile can say something concrete about her beyond her children's names.

## Family and background

Engel's husband was Riccardo Amodei, a leathersmith. He died in 2006 of hepatitis C. Their daughter Daniela is four years younger than their son Dario ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Dario-Amodei)). The Jerusalem Post describes Elena as the Jewish mother of Dario Amodei, who was born and raised in San Francisco to her and his Italian father ([The Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com:443/influencers-26/50jews-26/article-908079)).

One profile of the siblings adds family history that no other source carries, so it is presented here with that caveat: it describes Engel as a Chicago-born Jewish American whose own mother once chained herself to the Italian consulate in Chicago in the 1930s to protest Italy's invasion of Ethiopia, and says she raised Dario and Daniela in San Francisco's Mission District ([press.farm](https://press.farm/the-amodei-siblings-how-dario-and-daniela-built-anthropics-ai-empire/)).

## Mother of the Anthropic founders

Dario Amodei, born in 1983, went on to co-found and lead Anthropic; his younger sister Daniela co-founded the company with him and serves as its president ([Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/money/Dario-Amodei)). In 2026 the Jerusalem Post named Dario among its fifty influential Jews, identifying him as the son of Elena Engel ([The Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com:443/influencers-26/50jews-26/article-908079)). The children's careers are covered in their own profiles; what belongs to Elena Engel in this story is simply the documented fact of the relationship — and the wry symmetry that the siblings who now manage one of the world's most scrutinized construction projects in artificial intelligence were raised by a woman who managed construction projects herself.
