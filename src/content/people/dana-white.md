## Overview

Dana White is the president and CEO of the Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC), the Las Vegas-based promotion he built from a near-bankrupt curiosity into the world's dominant mixed martial arts organization ([SRN News](https://srnnews.com/meta-adds-three-board-members-including-ufc-boss-dana-white-key-figure-in-trumps-orbit/); [Associated Press](https://www.knkx.org/2026-06-15/ufc-boss-dana-white-says-never-again-to-another-white-house-fight-night)). He is also one of Donald Trump's longest-standing allies in American sports: a relationship that began with UFC events at Trump's Atlantic City casino in 2001 and stretched through three Republican convention speeches, a seat on Meta's board, and — in June 2026 — a UFC fight card staged on the [White House](https://factsledger.com/places/white-house) South Lawn ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/sport/white-house-to-host-ufc-next-yr-courtesy-trumps-ties-to-league-dating-back-to-taj-mahal-casino-days/2680682/); [Associated Press](https://www.knkx.org/2026-06-15/ufc-boss-dana-white-says-never-again-to-another-white-house-fight-night)).

White has described Trump as one of his very good friends, telling ESPN the president is among the ten people in his small circle, and Trump returned the compliment after the 2024 election, thanking White from the stage on election night: "You've done a hell of a job" ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/sport/white-house-to-host-ufc-next-yr-courtesy-trumps-ties-to-league-dating-back-to-taj-mahal-casino-days/2680682/); [Get More Sports](https://www.getmoresports.com/how-trump-and-dana-whites-friendship-began-at-the-taj-mahal/)).

## Early life: boxing gyms and odd jobs

White was born Dana Frederick White Jr. on July 28, 1969, in Manchester, Connecticut, and was raised by his mother and her family, shuttling between Boston, Las Vegas and Maine during his youth ([Texas News Day](https://biography.texasnewsday.com/dana-white/); [TV Guide](https://www.TVGuide.com/celebrities/dana-white/bio/3030642170/)). He attended Hermon High School in Maine and took up amateur boxing, financing himself with working-class jobs — as a bouncer and a hotel bellman — while also working as an aerobics and boxercise instructor ([Texas News Day](https://biography.texasnewsday.com/dana-white/); [TV Guide](https://www.TVGuide.com/celebrities/dana-white/bio/3030642170/)). In college he started a boxing program for inner-city youth, and in Las Vegas he took up jiu jitsu alongside Lorenzo Fertitta, the casino executive who would become his business partner ([TV Guide](https://www.TVGuide.com/celebrities/dana-white/bio/3030642170/)).

Through the Las Vegas fight scene White became the manager of two future MMA stars, [Tito Ortiz](/people/tito-ortiz/) and [Chuck Liddell](/people/chuck-liddell/) — the connection that would put him at the center of the UFC's sale ([TV Guide](https://www.TVGuide.com/celebrities/dana-white/bio/3030642170/); [Texas News Day](https://biography.texasnewsday.com/dana-white/)). When he learned that the UFC's then-owner was looking to sell, White called his childhood friend Lorenzo Fertitta; within weeks, Lorenzo and his older brother Frank bought the promotion for $2 million through their newly formed company Zuffa LLC in January 2001 and installed White as its president ([Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/sports/mma-ufc/from-sahara-ave-to-the-white-house-how-las-vegas-based-ufc-reached-global-stage-3838080/); [Get More Sports](https://www.getmoresports.com/how-trump-and-dana-whites-friendship-began-at-the-taj-mahal/)).

## Building the UFC

White's timing was improbable: the UFC was bleeding money and banned from many venues. He later told CBS's *60 Minutes* in 2025 that arenas had been reluctant to host the product, saying they "didn't believe in it" and were worried about the crowd ([Get More Sports](https://www.getmoresports.com/how-trump-and-dana-whites-friendship-began-at-the-taj-mahal/)). The turnaround came in 2005 with *The Ultimate Fighter*, a reality series in which prospects fought for a UFC contract; its success on Spike TV introduced millions of viewers to mixed martial arts and is widely credited with rescuing the company ([Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/sports/mma-ufc/from-sahara-ave-to-the-white-house-how-las-vegas-based-ufc-reached-global-stage-3838080/)).

From there the UFC became a global sports property. In 2016 the Fertitta brothers sold the company to a consortium led by [WME-IMG](/organizations/wme-img/) for more than $4 billion, with White staying on as president ([Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/sports/mma-ufc/from-sahara-ave-to-the-white-house-how-las-vegas-based-ufc-reached-global-stage-3838080/)). In 2023 the UFC and WWE merged under [TKO Group Holdings](/organizations/tko-group-holdings/), with White continuing as the UFC's top executive — titled president and CEO in company and media usage by 2025 ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/sport/white-house-to-host-ufc-next-yr-courtesy-trumps-ties-to-league-dating-back-to-taj-mahal-casino-days/2680682/); [SRN News](https://srnnews.com/meta-adds-three-board-members-including-ufc-boss-dana-white-key-figure-in-trumps-orbit/)).

The business kept evolving in the streaming era. In August 2025 TKO signed a seven-year, $7.7 billion deal with [Paramount](/organizations/paramount/) that moved 43 annual UFC events to Paramount+ and CBS starting in 2026 — an arrangement that largely ended the pay-per-view model for American fans, and one White described as approaching the distribution model he had long envisioned ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/sports/ufc/2025/08/11/dana-white-reaction-ufc-paramount-deal/85617530007/)).

## A friendship forged at the Taj Mahal

The Trump–White relationship is rooted in the UFC's desperate early days. According to a *Get More Sports* retrospective, White was already at the Trump Taj Mahal on November 17, 2000 — attending UFC 28 as the manager of fighter John Lewis, alongside Lorenzo and [Frank Fertitta](/people/frank-fertitta/) — and the group used the trip to discuss a promotion the brothers might run ([Get More Sports](https://www.getmoresports.com/how-trump-and-dana-whites-friendship-began-at-the-taj-mahal/)). Two months later, with Zuffa struggling to find venues willing to host its cards, it reached an agreement with Trump to stage events at the Taj Mahal: the first Zuffa-era card, UFC 30, on February 23, 2001 (headlined by Tito Ortiz defending his light heavyweight title), followed by UFC 31 that May ([Get More Sports](https://www.getmoresports.com/how-trump-and-dana-whites-friendship-began-at-the-taj-mahal/); [Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/sports/mma-ufc/from-sahara-ave-to-the-white-house-how-las-vegas-based-ufc-reached-global-stage-3838080/)).

Trump was, in White's telling, one of the first powerful figures to take the UFC seriously when few others would. UFC executive Craig Borsari told the *Las Vegas Review-Journal* that Trump was "very gracious" to the young company and attended its Atlantic City shows because "he loved having us there" — though White's account of Trump attending those early cards has been questioned, with ESPN reporting it could find no visual evidence and former UFC employees disputing it ([Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/sports/mma-ufc/from-sahara-ave-to-the-white-house-how-las-vegas-based-ufc-reached-global-stage-3838080/); [Get More Sports](https://www.getmoresports.com/how-trump-and-dana-whites-friendship-began-at-the-taj-mahal/)). The relationship survived even a detour: in 2008 Trump briefly partnered with Affliction, a short-lived UFC competitor, before returning to White's orbit ([Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/sports/mma-ufc/from-sahara-ave-to-the-white-house-how-las-vegas-based-ufc-reached-global-stage-3838080/)).

As Trump's political career took shape, White became a fixture of it. He spoke at the 2016 Republican National Convention, took a speaking role at the 2020 convention while praising Trump's economic record, and introduced Trump at the 2024 convention — with ESPN crediting him with introducing Trump at two conventions ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/sport/white-house-to-host-ufc-next-yr-courtesy-trumps-ties-to-league-dating-back-to-taj-mahal-casino-days/2680682/); [SRN News](https://srnnews.com/meta-adds-three-board-members-including-ufc-boss-dana-white-key-figure-in-trumps-orbit/); [Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/sports/mma-ufc/from-sahara-ave-to-the-white-house-how-las-vegas-based-ufc-reached-global-stage-3838080/)). Trump, for his part, was a frequent presence at UFC events — attending four shows while serving as president, per ESPN — and made the UFC a stop on his 2024 campaign's outreach to young male voters ([Get More Sports](https://www.getmoresports.com/how-trump-and-dana-whites-friendship-began-at-the-taj-mahal/); [SRN News](https://srnnews.com/meta-adds-three-board-members-including-ufc-boss-dana-white-key-figure-in-trumps-orbit/)). On election night 2024 White appeared on stage at Trump's victory party and spoke briefly to the crowd; days later the two attended a UFC pay-per-view at Madison Square Garden — the ninth of nine consecutive sellouts at the arena — where organizers played highlights of Trump's road back to the [White House](https://factsledger.com/organizations/the-white-house) ([SRN News](https://srnnews.com/meta-adds-three-board-members-including-ufc-boss-dana-white-key-figure-in-trumps-orbit/); [Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/sports/mma-ufc/from-sahara-ave-to-the-white-house-how-las-vegas-based-ufc-reached-global-stage-3838080/)).

The political relationship soon carried business consequences. In January 2025 Meta appointed White to its board of directors alongside auto executive John Elkann and tech investor Charlie Songhurst — a move widely read as CEO [Mark Zuckerberg](/people/mark-zuckerberg/)'s effort to repair ties with Trump, who had once been banned from [Facebook](/organizations/facebook/), following Zuckerberg's post-election dinner at Mar-a-Lago and Meta's $1 million donation to Trump's inauguration fund ([SRN News](https://srnnews.com/meta-adds-three-board-members-including-ufc-boss-dana-white-key-figure-in-trumps-orbit/)). And the friendship reached its most extraordinary milestone when Trump, at one of White's UFC shows, asked him: "Have you ever thought about doing one of these on the lawn of the White House?" — an idea Trump then announced publicly at a July 2025 rally in Iowa as part of the 250th anniversary of American independence ([Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/sports/mma-ufc/from-sahara-ave-to-the-white-house-how-las-vegas-based-ufc-reached-global-stage-3838080/); [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/sport/white-house-to-host-ufc-next-yr-courtesy-trumps-ties-to-league-dating-back-to-taj-mahal-casino-days/2680682/)).

## UFC Freedom 250: fight night on the South Lawn

On June 14, 2026 — Trump's 80th birthday — the UFC staged Freedom 250, a seven-fight card on the White House South Lawn, a live professional sporting event on the grounds that ESPN described as unprecedented ([Get More Sports](https://www.getmoresports.com/how-trump-and-dana-whites-friendship-began-at-the-taj-mahal/); [Associated Press](https://www.knkx.org/2026-06-15/ufc-boss-dana-white-says-never-again-to-another-white-house-fight-night)). Trump stayed for the entire card, congratulated new lightweight champion Justin Gaethje after his title win over Ilia Topuria, and the event drew the biggest bonuses in UFC history — $825,000 in total ([Associated Press](https://www.knkx.org/2026-06-15/ufc-boss-dana-white-says-never-again-to-another-white-house-fight-night)).

White framed the night as a celebration of the American 250th anniversary rather than a political statement, and called it a spectacular success in merchandise sales and streaming subscriptions ([Associated Press](https://www.knkx.org/2026-06-15/ufc-boss-dana-white-says-never-again-to-another-white-house-fight-night)). But he was blunt that it would not be repeated: "It was an amazing experience, this was a one-of-one," he said. "It will never happen again." The event, he said, had cost the UFC $60 million to produce ([Associated Press](https://www.knkx.org/2026-06-15/ufc-boss-dana-white-says-never-again-to-another-white-house-fight-night)). The same report noted that Conor McGregor was expected to make his long-awaited return at UFC 329 ([Associated Press](https://www.knkx.org/2026-06-15/ufc-boss-dana-white-says-never-again-to-another-white-house-fight-night)).

## Controversies and disputes

In January 2023 TMZ published video of White and his wife Anne in a physical altercation at a nightclub in Cabo San Lucas, Mexico, during their New Year's Eve vacation: the footage showed [Anne White](/people/anne-white/) slapping him first, and White slapping her back before others intervened ([ESPN](https://www.espn.ph/mma/story/_/id/35368344/ufc-dana-white-apologizes-physical-altercation-wife)). White apologized publicly, saying he was "embarrassed" and that "there's never, ever an excuse for a guy to put his hands on a woman," noting the couple had known each other since they were 12, had been married for almost 30 years, and had three children ([ESPN](https://www.espn.ph/mma/story/_/id/35368344/ufc-dana-white-apologizes-physical-altercation-wife)).

The White House card also drew legal pushback: before the event, a group sued to stop it, arguing in part that Trump had invested in the UFC's parent company — a claim White House and UFC spokespeople said had no merit ([Las Vegas Review-Journal](https://www.reviewjournal.com/sports/mma-ufc/from-sahara-ave-to-the-white-house-how-las-vegas-based-ufc-reached-global-stage-3838080/)). And as noted above, White's account of Trump's presence at the early Taj Mahal cards is disputed by ESPN's reporting ([Get More Sports](https://www.getmoresports.com/how-trump-and-dana-whites-friendship-began-at-the-taj-mahal/)).
