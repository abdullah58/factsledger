**Nia Talita Long** is an American actress whose work defined a generation of Black cinema — *Boyz n the Hood* (1991), *Friday* (1995), *Love Jones* (1997), *Soul Food* (1997) and *The Best Man* (1999) — and who plays Jackson family matriarch Katherine Jackson in Antoine Fuqua's 2026 [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) biopic [*Michael*](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/) ([Essence](https://www.essence.com/entertainment/nia-long-honored-hollywood-walk-of-fame-star/); [BET](https://www.bet.com/article/jtoad1/nia-long-tapped-to-portray-katherine-jackson-in-michael-jackson-biopic)).

Born 30 October 1970 in Brooklyn and raised mostly in South Central [Los Angeles](/places/los-angeles/) after her parents' divorce, Long is of Trinidadian extraction and found her first fame on television's *The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air* before a 1990s film run that made her one of the era's defining leading ladies ([Los Angeles Sentinel](https://lasentinel.net/quality-time-with-nia.html); [Essence](https://www.essence.com/entertainment/nia-long-honored-hollywood-walk-of-fame-star/)). On 16 September 2026 she received the 2,857th star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame — with the Hollywood Chamber of Commerce declaring it Nia Long Day — more than three decades after John Singleton's *Boyz n the Hood* introduced her to movie audiences ([Essence](https://www.essence.com/entertainment/nia-long-honored-hollywood-walk-of-fame-star/)).

## The 1990s run that made her a leading lady

Long's film debut came in 1991 with *Boyz n the Hood*, John Singleton's portrait of inner-city Los Angeles — and her television breakthrough arrived the same year as Lisa Wilkes on *The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air*, a role she held through 1995 ([LA Watts Times](https://mail.lawattstimes.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=2241:nia-expounds-on-everything-from-movies-to-motherhood&catid=27:arts-culture&Itemid=117); [Essence](https://www.essence.com/entertainment/nia-long-honored-hollywood-walk-of-fame-star/)). Then came the run: *Friday* (1995) opposite Ice Cube and Chris Tucker, the 1997 one-two of *Love Jones* — her silver-screen breakout opposite Larenz Tate, which won the Sundance Audience Award — and *Soul Food*, and finally Jordan Armstrong in *The Best Man* (1999) ([LA Watts Times](https://mail.lawattstimes.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=2241:nia-expounds-on-everything-from-movies-to-motherhood&catid=27:arts-culture&Itemid=117); [Los Angeles Sentinel](https://lasentinel.net/quality-time-with-nia.html)). In 2000, People magazine named her one of the world's 50 Most Beautiful People ([Los Angeles Sentinel](https://lasentinel.net/quality-time-with-nia.html)). Six career-defining screen roles in eight years is the kind of concentration most actors never get — and it made Long, in the Sentinel's words, an "accomplished thespian" before she turned 30 ([Los Angeles Sentinel](https://lasentinel.net/quality-time-with-nia.html)).

## Jordan Armstrong, 23 years apart

The role Long has lived with longest is Jordan Armstrong of Malcolm D. Lee's *The Best Man*. Lee describes Jordan as "very driven and unapologetic," a character he personally relates to ([Ebony](https://www.ebony.com/the-best-man-the-final-chapters-director-malcolm-d-lee-is-ready-to-close-the-book/)). She first played her in 1999, reunited with Taye Diggs, Terrence Howard, Morris Chestnut and Harold Perrineau for *The Best Man Holiday* in 2013, and closed the book with the 2022 eight-part Peacock limited series *The Best Man: The Final Chapters*, which Lee intended as the franchise's ending ([LA Watts Times](https://mail.lawattstimes.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=2241:nia-expounds-on-everything-from-movies-to-motherhood&catid=27:arts-culture&Itemid=117); [Ebony](https://www.ebony.com/the-best-man-the-final-chapters-director-malcolm-d-lee-is-ready-to-close-the-book/)). Between the films, television carried her: Officer Sasha Monroe on *Third Watch* (2003–2005), a performance that netted her NAACP Image Awards for Outstanding Actress in a Drama series, plus *House of Lies* opposite Don Cheadle and Kristen Bell, *Judging Amy*, *Boston Legal* and *Big Shots* ([LA Watts Times](https://mail.lawattstimes.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=2241:nia-expounds-on-everything-from-movies-to-motherhood&catid=27:arts-culture&Itemid=117); [Los Angeles Sentinel](https://lasentinel.net/quality-time-with-nia.html)). Few actresses have kept one character alive across three decades while building a parallel TV career — the franchise's endurance is inseparable from hers ([Ebony](https://www.ebony.com/the-best-man-the-final-chapters-director-malcolm-d-lee-is-ready-to-close-the-book/)).

## Katherine Jackson in Michael

Deadline announced Long's casting as Katherine Jackson in January 2024 — one week after Colman Domingo was cast as Joe Jackson — for Fuqua's biopic ([BET](https://www.bet.com/article/jtoad1/nia-long-tapped-to-portray-katherine-jackson-in-michael-jackson-biopic)). Fuqua praised her iconic, career-long performances and described Katherine as "the glue, the rock, and the heart of the Jackson family during its best and its most turbulent times" ([BET](https://www.bet.com/article/jtoad1/nia-long-tapped-to-portray-katherine-jackson-in-michael-jackson-biopic)). Long called Katherine "an incredible pillar of strength and grace for the entire Jackson family," selfless as a mother, who "helped build a legacy beyond measure" — and said she was honored to bring her voice to the screen ([BET](https://www.bet.com/article/jtoad1/nia-long-tapped-to-portray-katherine-jackson-in-michael-jackson-biopic)). The finished film, released April 24, 2026, pairs her with Domingo as the Jackson parents, with Jaafar Jackson — Michael's nephew — in the title role ([The Source](https://thesource.com/2026/02/02/michael-biopic-colman-domingo-nia-long/)). Essence framed the role as her "latest chapter" — and her next one is already lined up: a reunion with *Love Jones* co-star Larenz Tate for Netflix's upcoming *Don't Ever Wonder*, which Long executive produces ([Essence](https://www.essence.com/entertainment/nia-long-honored-hollywood-walk-of-fame-star/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Nia Long best known for?**
Her 1990s film run: the debut in *Boyz n the Hood* (1991), *Friday* (1995), the breakout *Love Jones* (1997) opposite Larenz Tate, *Soul Food* (1997), and Jordan Armstrong in *The Best Man* (1999) — plus Lisa Wilkes on *The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air* (1991–1995).

**Who does Nia Long play in the Michael Jackson biopic?**
She plays Katherine Jackson, the Jackson family matriarch. Deadline announced the casting in January 2024, one week after Colman Domingo was cast as Joe Jackson; Fuqua's film was released theatrically on April 24, 2026, with Long opposite Domingo as the Jackson parents.

**Did Nia Long receive a Hollywood Walk of Fame star?**
Yes — on September 16, 2026, she received the 2,857th star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in the Motion Pictures category. The Hollywood Chamber of Commerce declared it Nia Long Day; Snoop Dogg and *Michael* producer Graham King attended the ceremony.

**Is Nia Long in The Best Man: Final Chapters?**
Yes. She reprised Jordan Armstrong — the role she originated in 1999's *The Best Man* and returned to in 2013's *The Best Man Holiday* — in the eight-part Peacock limited series that premiered December 22, 2022, intended as the franchise's final chapter.
