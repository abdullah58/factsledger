## Overview

**David Gregory** is a British filmmaker and home-entertainment executive who co-founded and now leads **Severin Films**, the boutique label devoted to restoring and releasing cult, exploitation and genre cinema ([Comicon](https://comicon.com/2025/02/05/interview-severin-films-david-gregory-on-the-labels-4k-russ-meyer-releases-and-20th-anniversary-plans/)). He launched Severin in 2006 with **Carl Daft** and **John Cregan** after years producing DVD special features for **Anchor Bay** and **Blue Underground**, and as co-founder and CEO he personally signed the 2023 deal with the **Russ Meyer Trust** that brought Meyer's *Vixen* trilogy to restored 4K UHD and Blu-ray in 2025 ([Comicon](https://comicon.com/2025/02/05/interview-severin-films-david-gregory-on-the-labels-4k-russ-meyer-releases-and-20th-anniversary-plans/); [Disc Dish](http://www.discdish.com/2025/02/03/interview-david-gregory-of-severin-films/)). In 2012 he also co-founded the US horror label **Autonomy Pictures** with Derek Curl and Lewis Tice ([Bloody Disgusting](http://bloody-disgusting.com/news/28671/autonomy-pictures-comes-out-playing-the-bunny-game/)).

This is **David Gregory of Severin Films** — the co-founder/CEO and filmmaker — not any other same-name person (there are several people named David Gregory in film, television, journalism and sports). Every professional fact here comes from the on-the-record interview material and industry coverage described below.

## From DVD extras to Severin Films

Gregory entered home entertainment producing special features for fan-favorite DVD labels Anchor Bay and Blue Underground — the exact background that shaped Severin's identity, since he has said he refuses to put out subpar extras because of how he started in the business ([Comicon](https://comicon.com/2025/02/05/interview-severin-films-david-gregory-on-the-labels-4k-russ-meyer-releases-and-20th-anniversary-plans/)). He joined Carl Daft and John Cregan to launch **Severin Films** in 2006, initially concentrating on 1970s sexploitation films such as the *Black Emmanuelle* and *Felicity* franchises, before expanding into horror — especially Italian gialli — and other genres ([Comicon](https://comicon.com/2025/02/05/interview-severin-films-david-gregory-on-the-labels-4k-russ-meyer-releases-and-20th-anniversary-plans/)). Two decades on, the label is home to hundreds of genre titles, including films that had not seen the light of day since the VHS era and others never previously available in any home-video format — a scale Gregory himself did not plan for ([Comicon](https://comicon.com/2025/02/05/interview-severin-films-david-gregory-on-the-labels-4k-russ-meyer-releases-and-20th-anniversary-plans/)). The company marked its 20th anniversary in 2026 with an expanded Los Angeles summer festival and new book and release projects ([Comicon](https://comicon.com/2025/02/05/interview-severin-films-david-gregory-on-the-labels-4k-russ-meyer-releases-and-20th-anniversary-plans/)).

## The Russ Meyer restoration deal

Gregory's signature achievement at Severin is the restoration program he negotiated with the **Russ Meyer Trust**. After Meyer's 2004 death, his films languished in limited circulation without modern restoration, and Gregory — who had first researched Meyer's films for Arrow Video extras years earlier — spent 2023 making the case that Severin was the right home to the trust's sole remaining trustee, **Janice Cowart** ([Comicon](https://comicon.com/2025/02/05/interview-severin-films-david-gregory-on-the-labels-4k-russ-meyer-releases-and-20th-anniversary-plans/); [Disc Dish](http://www.discdish.com/2025/02/03/interview-david-gregory-of-severin-films/)). The deal covered five films: the *Vixen* trilogy (*Vixen!* (1968), *Supervixens* (1975), *Beneath the Valley of the Ultravixens* (1979)), officially issued on 4K Ultra HD and Blu-ray on January 28, plus *Motor Psycho* (1965) and *Up!* (1976) slated for March ([Disc Dish](http://www.discdish.com/2025/02/03/interview-david-gregory-of-severin-films/)). The stat that matters for collectors: the original negatives turned out to be in a safe place — the **Museum of Modern Art** or the Academy — and largely immaculate, so the *Vixen* trilogy restoration needed only the best possible scan and color correction, with one exception: *Ultra-Vixens* had water damage affecting frame-by-frame deterioration, costing a few hundred extra hours of work ([Comicon](https://comicon.com/2025/02/05/interview-severin-films-david-gregory-on-the-labels-4k-russ-meyer-releases-and-20th-anniversary-plans/)).

## The Autonomy Pictures detour

Outside Severin, Gregory has worked as a filmmaker — the 2012 Autonomy launch materials identify him as the filmmaker behind *Plague Town* and *The Theatre Bizarre* — and in March 2012 he co-founded **Autonomy Pictures** with Derek Curl and Lewis Tice, a US horror label whose first release was the US-rights acquisition of Adam Rehmeier's *The Bunny Game* ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/31660/a-home-found-for-the-bunny-game/); [Bloody Disgusting](http://bloody-disgusting.com/news/28671/autonomy-pictures-comes-out-playing-the-bunny-game/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is David Gregory of Severin Films?**
David Gregory is a British filmmaker and home-entertainment executive who co-founded Severin Films in 2006 with Carl Daft and John Cregan and serves as its CEO. He previously produced DVD special features for Anchor Bay and Blue Underground. He is not the same person as any of the other public figures named David Gregory in journalism, sports, or other industries.

**What is Severin Films?**
Severin Films is a US boutique home-video label founded in 2006, devoted to rescuing and releasing controversial and provocative cult, exploitation, and genre films in restored physical editions. It began with 1970s sexploitation, expanded into horror and other genres, and now holds hundreds of titles, many unavailable in any home-video format before Severin's editions.

**What did David Gregory do with Russ Meyer's films?**
In 2023 Gregory signed a deal with the Russ Meyer Trust to restore and release five Meyer films. The *Vixen* trilogy came out on 4K Ultra HD and Blu-ray on January 28, with *Motor Psycho* and *Up!* following. The restorations came from the original negatives held by the Museum of Modern Art and the Academy, with *Ultra-Vixens* requiring extensive extra work because of water damage.

**Was David Gregory involved in Autonomy Pictures?**
Yes. In March 2012 Gregory co-founded the US horror label Autonomy Pictures with Derek Curl (TLA Releasing) and Lewis Tice (Danger After Dark). The label's first acquisition was all US rights to Adam Rehmeier's *The Bunny Game*. The launch announcement also identified Gregory as the filmmaker behind *Plague Town* and *The Theatre Bizarre*.
