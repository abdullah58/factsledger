Eric Roberts (born April 18, 1956) is an American actor with two eras: 1980s
acclaim that brought three Golden Globe nominations and an Oscar nomination
for *Runaway Train*, and a second act as one of the most prolific
screen actors, with more than 700 credits
([Golden Globes](https://goldenglobes.com/person/eric-roberts/);
[ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/eric-roberts-actor-most-us-movies-shows-credits)).
Among them is a supporting role in [*Ayla: The Daughter of War*](/creative-works/ayla-the-daughter-of-war/)
(2017), Turkey's Oscar entry
([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633)).
2024 brought a memoir and a *Dancing with the Stars* run
([USA Today](https://www.ndinsider.com/story/entertainment/books/2024/09/18/eric-roberts-julia-roberts-memoir-runaway-train/75273414007/)).

## Overview

Roberts was born in Biloxi, Mississippi, and is the older brother of actress
Julia Roberts and the father of actress Emma Roberts
([Golden Globes](https://goldenglobes.com/person/eric-roberts/)). He began as a
leading man: his 1978 film debut in *King of the Gypsies* earned a Golden
Globe nomination for Best Acting Debut in a Motion Picture
([Golden Globes](https://goldenglobes.com/person/eric-roberts/)). A decade and
a half of critical heat followed — Bob Fosse's *Star 80*, Andrei
Konchalovsky's *Runaway Train* — and then a long pivot into volume: supporting
and villain roles across film, television, and independent productions that
pushed his credit count past 700
([Golden Globes](https://goldenglobes.com/person/eric-roberts/);
[ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/eric-roberts-actor-most-us-movies-shows-credits)).
The third act has been unexpectedly confessional. The memoir includes what
he calls a public apology to his sister, and television brought him to the
ballroom — two moves that put the famously prolific actor in a newly
confessional register
([USA Today](https://www.ndinsider.com/story/entertainment/books/2024/09/18/eric-roberts-julia-roberts-memoir-runaway-train/75273414007/)).

## The 1980s: an actor the critics loved

Roberts arrived as a leading man. His film debut came in *King of the Gypsies*
(1978), which earned him a Golden Globe nomination for Best Acting Debut in a
Motion Picture
([Golden Globes](https://goldenglobes.com/person/eric-roberts/)). He followed
with *Raggedy Man* (1981) opposite Sissy Spacek under Jack Fisk's direction,
and then *Star 80* (1983), written and directed by Bob Fosse and co-starring
Mariel Hemingway
([Golden Globes](https://goldenglobes.com/person/eric-roberts/)) — the
performance that brought his second Golden Globe nomination, as the villainous
Paul Snider
([ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/eric-roberts-actor-most-us-movies-shows-credits)).
He also starred opposite Mickey Rourke in *The Pope of Greenwich Village*
(1984) and opposite Jon Voight in Andrei Konchalovsky's *Runaway Train* (1985)
([Golden Globes](https://goldenglobes.com/person/eric-roberts/)), closing the
decade's first half as the intense, unpredictable face of American screen
acting. He was, by any measure, one of the decade's most-watched new actors,
and *Runaway Train* earned him both Golden Globe and Academy Award
nominations
([USA Today](https://www.ndinsider.com/story/entertainment/books/2024/09/18/eric-roberts-julia-roberts-memoir-runaway-train/75273414007/)).

## The journeyman era: volume as a working method

By the 1990s, Roberts had settled into the rhythm that would define the rest
of his career: say yes, keep working. The Golden Globes' own artist page lists
supporting turns in *The Specialist* (1994) alongside Sylvester Stallone and
Sharon Stone, *A Guide to Recognizing Your Saints* (2006), and Paul Thomas
Anderson's *Inherent Vice* (2014), plus television work on the *In Cold Blood*
miniseries and *Less Than Perfect*
([Golden Globes](https://goldenglobes.com/person/eric-roberts/)). ScreenRant's
August 2023 survey counts more than 700 acting credits, after a start as Ted
Bancroft on the daytime soap *Another World*
([ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/eric-roberts-actor-most-us-movies-shows-credits)).
His screen villains — the Master in the *Doctor Who* TV movie, Sal Maroni in
*The Dark Knight*, James Munroe in *The Expendables* — became a repertory
company of their own
([ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/eric-roberts-actor-most-us-movies-shows-credits)).
The numbers keep moving: ScreenRant reports that Brahmanandam holds the
Guinness living-actor record at 1,170 films (via The Hollywood Reporter), while
Roberts had 91 projects in production — so for Roberts, volume is a strategy,
not an accident
([ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/eric-roberts-actor-most-us-movies-shows-credits)).

## Ayla, the memoir, and the family reckoning

In 2017 Roberts took a supporting role in *Ayla: The Daughter of War*,
directed by [Can Ülkay](/people/can-ulkay/) — Turkey's submission for Best Foreign Language Film at
the 90th Academy Awards
([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633)).
The film tells the true story of Turkish sergeant Süleyman Dilbirliği and the
Korean orphan he named Ayla during the Korean War; Çetin Tekindor and İsmail
Hacıoğlu play Dilbirliği, [Kim Seol](/people/kim-seol/) and [Lee Kyung-jin](/people/lee-kyung-jin/)
play Ayla, and Roberts and [Cade Carradine](/people/cade-carradine/) are among the supporting roles
([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633)).
In September 2024 came *Runaway Train: Or, The Story of My Life So Far*,
named for his Oscar-nominated film
([USA Today](https://www.ndinsider.com/story/entertainment/books/2024/09/18/eric-roberts-julia-roberts-memoir-runaway-train/75273414007/)).
The memoir carries a "public apology" to his sister Julia Roberts, and recounts
drug abuse, a 1981 car accident and dayslong coma, and the custody battle over
his daughter Emma Roberts — which he calls ultimately right — after Julia
sided with Emma's mother, Kim Cunningham
([USA Today](https://www.ndinsider.com/story/entertainment/books/2024/09/18/eric-roberts-julia-roberts-memoir-runaway-train/75273414007/)).
That autumn he competed on season 33 of *Dancing with the Stars*
([USA Today](https://www.ndinsider.com/story/entertainment/books/2024/09/18/eric-roberts-julia-roberts-memoir-runaway-train/75273414007/)).

## FAQ

**Why is Eric Roberts considered one of Hollywood's most prolific actors?**
ScreenRant's 2023 survey credits him with more than 700 screen roles, starting
from the daytime soap *Another World* through studio films and indie projects.
As of August 2023 he had 91 more projects in production, so the total keeps
growing — the defining trait of his second act is simply never stopping
([ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/eric-roberts-actor-most-us-movies-shows-credits)).

**Was Eric Roberts nominated for an Oscar?**
Yes — once, for Best Supporting Actor in *Runaway Train* (1985), in which he
played the escapee Buck opposite Jon Voight. The nomination was accompanied by
three Golden Globe nominations: a debut nod for *King of the Gypsies* (1978),
*Star 80* (1983), and *Runaway Train* — though sources date the film's year
differently; see open items
([USA Today](https://www.ndinsider.com/story/entertainment/books/2024/09/18/eric-roberts-julia-roberts-memoir-runaway-train/75273414007/);
[Golden Globes](https://goldenglobes.com/person/eric-roberts/)).

**What does Eric Roberts do in *Ayla: The Daughter of War*?**
He holds a supporting role in the 2017 Turkish–Korean war drama directed by
Can Ülkay — Turkey's Oscar entry for Best Foreign Language Film. The film
tells the true story of Sergeant Süleyman Dilbirliği and the Korean orphan
Ayla; Roberts appears alongside Cade Carradine among the supporting cast
([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633)).

**What does his memoir reveal?**
*Runaway Train: Or, The Story of My Life So Far* (September 2024) includes a
"public apology" to his sister Julia Roberts, accounts of drug abuse, a 1981
car accident and dayslong coma, and the custody battle over his daughter Emma
Roberts — a loss he describes as ultimately the right outcome
([USA Today](https://www.ndinsider.com/story/entertainment/books/2024/09/18/eric-roberts-julia-roberts-memoir-runaway-train/75273414007/)).

**Is Eric Roberts related to Julia and Emma Roberts?**
Yes. He is Julia Roberts' older brother and Emma Roberts' father, per the
Golden Globes' own artist page. His
memoir says he expects to "remain as Julia's brother and Emma Roberts' dad
for the rest of my life" — a line framing how he sees himself after the
apology
([Golden Globes](https://goldenglobes.com/person/eric-roberts/);
[USA Today](https://www.ndinsider.com/story/entertainment/books/2024/09/18/eric-roberts-julia-roberts-memoir-runaway-train/75273414007/)).
