## Overview

James Michael "Mike" Johnson, born January 30, 1972, in Shreveport, Louisiana, is the Speaker of the [U.S. House of Representatives](https://factsledger.com/organizations/us-house-of-representatives), elected October 25, 2023, and reelected January 3, 2025 ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). A constitutional lawyer who spent 2002-2010 as an attorney for the Alliance Defense Fund (now Alliance Defending Freedom), he served in the Louisiana state House from 2015 to 2017 before winning a U.S. House seat in 2016 ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). He is a key congressional ally of Donald Trump and a central figure in the failed efforts to overturn the 2020 election — CNN describes him as a vocal Trump supporter and a key congressional figure in those efforts ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). Reuters reports he authored an unsuccessful appeal, joined by 126 House Republicans, asking the Supreme Court to overturn results in states Trump lost ([Reuters via GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/world/886347/republican-mike-johnson-elected-us-house-speaker-ending-leadership-vacuum/story/)). As speaker, he steered more than $60 billion in Ukraine aid through a fractious chamber in April 2024, survived a May 2024 ouster attempt with Democratic help, and was reelected with Trump's direct intervention in January 2025 ([Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/world/us-house-speaker-once-strongly-opposed-to-ukraine-aid-ushers-it-through); [AP](https://www.wvlt.tv/2024/05/08/rep-marjorie-taylor-greene-triggers-effort-remove-house-speaker-mike-johnson-office/); [AP](https://nation.lk/online/republican-mike-johnson-reelected-house-speaker-in-dramatic-floor-vote-290289.html)). He was scheduled to join Trump and technology executives including Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg and Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei for an AI discussion on September 29, 2026 ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/trump-host-zuckerberg-anthropics-amodei-other-ai-titans-tuesday-2026-09-29/)).

## Louisiana lawyer and rise in Congress

Johnson earned a B.S. in business administration from Louisiana State University in 1995 and a J.D. from the same university in 1998 ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). His legal career centered on constitutional and religious-freedom work: from 2002 to 2010 he was an attorney for the Alliance Defense Fund, now known as Alliance Defending Freedom, and CNN notes his history of harsh anti-gay language from his time as an attorney for that socially conservative legal group in the mid-2000s ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). Al Jazeera records that he defended Louisiana's ban on same-sex marriage in 2004 ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2023/10/26/who-is-mike-johnson-the-new-us-republican-house-speaker)). From 2010 to 2012 he was dean of a law school that never opened at Louisiana College, now Louisiana Christian University, and in the mid-2010s he founded Freedom Guard, a legal-advocacy ministry ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

He was elected to the Louisiana state House of Representatives in 2015 and served until 2017 ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2023/10/26/who-is-mike-johnson-the-new-us-republican-house-speaker); [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). On December 10, 2016, he won a runoff election to the U.S. House of Representatives with 65 percent of the vote, taking office as Louisiana's 4th District representative on January 3, 2017 ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). In Congress he rose to vice chair of the House Republican Conference from 2021 to 2023, and Al Jazeera notes he formerly chaired the conservative Republican Study Committee ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2023/10/26/who-is-mike-johnson-the-new-us-republican-house-speaker)).

A family detail shapes his public biography: when Johnson was 12, his firefighter father nearly died in a facility explosion that left him with second- and third-degree burns and permanently disabled him ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). He married Kelly Lary in 1999 and, per CNN, the couple has five children — Hannah, Abigail, Jack, Will and Michael James — having become legal guardians in 1999 ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). His religion is listed as Southern Baptist ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

## Election as speaker and the 2020-election role

Following the historic ouster of Kevin McCarthy, House Republicans tried and failed three separate times to coalesce behind a new speaker nominee before uniting around Johnson ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). He was elected speaker on October 25, 2023, by 220-209 ([Reuters via GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/world/886347/republican-mike-johnson-elected-us-house-speaker-ending-leadership-vacuum/story/)). Reuters called him the least-experienced speaker in decades at the time, and he took the gavel with a narrow majority that would require navigating bipartisan realities ([Reuters via GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/world/886347/republican-mike-johnson-elected-us-house-speaker-ending-leadership-vacuum/story/)). Al Jazeera describes him as the first Louisianan to become speaker and the first Southerner since Newt Gingrich ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2023/10/26/who-is-mike-johnson-the-new-us-republican-house-speaker)). Trump backed his speaker candidacy, per Al Jazeera ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2023/10/26/who-is-mike-johnson-the-new-us-republican-house-speaker)).

Johnson's alignment with Trump rested substantially on his 2020 role. He authored an unsuccessful appeal joined by 126 House Republicans asking the Supreme Court to overturn results in states Trump lost, per Reuters ([Reuters via GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/world/886347/republican-mike-johnson-elected-us-house-speaker-ending-leadership-vacuum/story/)). CNN identifies him as a key congressional figure in the failed efforts to overturn the 2020 election, and Al Jazeera documents his role supporting attempts to reverse the election result ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2023/10/26/who-is-mike-johnson-the-new-us-republican-house-speaker)).

## The Ukraine pivot, the ouster attempt and reelection

Johnson's most consequential legislative turn came on Ukraine. After months of blocking a vote on aid desperately needed by Ukraine's army, he softened his tone and then, in what AFP called a head-spinning shift, emerged in April 2024 as a passionate defender of a long-delayed aid package ([Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/world/us-house-speaker-once-strongly-opposed-to-ukraine-aid-ushers-it-through)). In December 2023, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky had made a last-ditch visit to Washington to plead for new aid; after a closed-door meeting with Johnson, the speaker said President Biden was asking for "billions of additional dollars with no appropriate oversight, no clear strategy to win, and none of the answers that I think the American people are owed" ([Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/world/us-house-speaker-once-strongly-opposed-to-ukraine-aid-ushers-it-through)).

On April 15, 2024, Johnson announced the House would take up separate bills to provide aid to Ukraine, Israel and Taiwan, and that he would support them ([Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/world/us-house-speaker-once-strongly-opposed-to-ukraine-aid-ushers-it-through)). He made one concession to Trump — who had demanded that Ukraine aid be at least partly in loan form — making part of the package subject to repayment, though the debt can still be forgiven and the total was almost exactly the amount Biden had requested months earlier ([Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/world/us-house-speaker-once-strongly-opposed-to-ukraine-aid-ushers-it-through)). On April 20, the chamber passed more than $60 billion in additional military and financial support for Ukraine by a strong bipartisan majority ([Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/world/us-house-speaker-once-strongly-opposed-to-ukraine-aid-ushers-it-through)). Explaining himself at an April 17 news conference, Johnson said, "To put it bluntly, I would rather send bullets to Ukraine than American boys," his voice choking with emotion as he added that his son was about to enter the U.S. Naval Academy; he called himself "a wartime speaker" and said, "We have to do the right thing — and history will judge us" ([Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/world/us-house-speaker-once-strongly-opposed-to-ukraine-aid-ushers-it-through)).

The perceived betrayal provoked an ouster attempt. On May 8, 2024, the House voted 359-43 to reject Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene's effort to remove Johnson from the speakership; only 11 Republicans voted to proceed while Democrats helped protect him ([AP](https://www.wvlt.tv/2024/05/08/rep-marjorie-taylor-greene-triggers-effort-remove-house-speaker-mike-johnson-office/)). Trump urged Republicans to table Greene's motion, having supported Johnson after an April Mar-a-Lago visit, and Johnson said backing Ukraine was important and that he wanted to be on the "right side of history" ([AP](https://www.wvlt.tv/2024/05/08/rep-marjorie-taylor-greene-triggers-effort-remove-house-speaker-mike-johnson-office/)).

On January 3, 2025, Johnson was reelected speaker on the first ballot after two holdouts switched, with Trump's public backing — Trump reportedly called dissenting lawmakers ahead of the vote ([AP](https://nation.lk/online/republican-mike-johnson-reelected-house-speaker-in-dramatic-floor-vote-290289.html)). He worked from a 219-215 Republican majority and had spent New Year's Day at Mar-a-Lago; he described himself as the "quarterback" executing plays called by Trump, the "coach" ([AP](https://nation.lk/online/republican-mike-johnson-reelected-house-speaker-in-dramatic-floor-vote-290289.html)). A year into his second term as speaker, on January 20, 2026, he became the first sitting U.S. House speaker to address the UK Parliament, during a visit marking the 250th anniversary of the Declaration of Independence ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

## The 2026 AI meeting and narrow-majority leadership

The current hook of Johnson's public profile is a planned September 29, 2026 meeting with Trump and technology executives on artificial intelligence, reported by Reuters on September 24 and September 29, 2026, with no outcome yet reported at the time of publication ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-us-house-speaker-tech-ceos-meet-ai-september-29-source-says-2026-09-24/); [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/trump-host-zuckerberg-anthropics-amodei-other-ai-titans-tuesday-2026-09-29/)). The meeting was to bring together Trump, Johnson, Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg and Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and OpenAI President Greg Brockman also expected, to discuss AI amid calls for more regulation ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/trump-host-zuckerberg-anthropics-amodei-other-ai-titans-tuesday-2026-09-29/)).
