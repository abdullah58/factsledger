# Lorenzo Fertitta

Lorenzo Fertitta was a fight regulator before he was a fight promoter, and that sequence explains almost everything about his career. In June 1997, when Mike Tyson bit off a chunk of Evander Holyfield's ear in their Las Vegas rematch, Fertitta was sitting on the Nevada State Athletic Commission. Bloomberg later reported that Fertitta "had been on the Nevada State Athletic Commission when Mike Tyson bit off a chunk of Evander Holyfield's ear in 1997." ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers))

Under oath, Fertitta filled in the timeline himself. In a deposition taken March 23, 2017, in the antitrust case against Zuffa, he testified that he joined the commission in 1996 or 1997, served about four years, and had already been off the commission for roughly six months when he and his brother bought the UFC in January 2001. He stated explicitly that he did not leave the commission in anticipation of the purchase. ([Lorenzo Fertitta deposition](https://www.scribd.com/document/389694278/Depo-of-Lorenzo-Fertitta))

He is the chairman and co-founder of Fertitta Capital, the private investment firm the brothers launched in 2017 with a $500 million seed investment, and the vice chairman of Red Rock Resorts, the publicly traded parent company of the Station Casinos empire his family built. ([Fertitta Capital](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fertitta-capital-launches-with-500-million-300448601.html)) ([Station Casinos](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/station-casinos-awards-team-members-more-than-70-million-in-red-rock-resorts-stock-as-part-of-50th-anniversary-celebration-302874527.html))

## The commissioner who bought a fight league

Fertitta's regulatory experience became the blueprint for what Zuffa did with the UFC. He believed, Bloomberg reported, that "the only way to make UFC a viable business was to establish standardized rules for the events and have it regulated as a legitimate sport." ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers)) He held a summit in New Jersey with athletic commissioners from around the country to draft new rules for mixed martial arts, and the brothers lobbied state legislatures to pass laws sanctioning MMA events. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers))

The rules they settled on — nine weight classes, most bouts limited to three five-minute rounds, standardized gloves and equipment — were the scaffolding the sport had lacked. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers)) The strategy had a personal payoff too: Marc Ratner, the Nevada commission's executive director, resigned to become Zuffa's vice president of regulatory affairs in March 2006, a hire that grew out of an association with the Fertittas dating back to the 1980s, when Lorenzo worked alongside him as a commission board member. ([Fighters Only](https://fightersonly.com/article/ext/73772/Issue+027/1))

## Raised inside the casino business

Fertitta is the younger son of Frank Fertitta Jr., who was born in Galveston, Texas, moved to Las Vegas with his wife Victoria in 1959, and worked his way up from bellman at the Tropicana through dealing, pit boss, and general manager jobs at properties including the Stardust, Sahara, and Fremont. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers))

In 1975 the elder Fertitta joined three partners to build a casino off Sahara Boulevard, in desert land west of the Strip where nobody thought a casino could work. The Casino — later renamed Palace Station — opened in 1976 with 100 slot machines, six gaming tables, and 90 employees, and was built explicitly to serve Las Vegas locals rather than tourists. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers))

Lorenzo started in the casino's accounting and food-and-beverage divisions during summers while studying business administration at the University of San Diego. After graduating, he and his brother used their own money in 1991 to expand a slot-route business — renting slot machines to pubs and smaller establishments — they had created with their brother-in-law in the mid-1980s. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers)) In May 1993, Station Casinos went public, raising $294 million in what was then the largest gaming IPO in history. Frank Jr. retired and sold his stake for $230 million. Frank III was 31 and Lorenzo was 24. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers)) Lorenzo went on to earn an MBA from NYU's Stern School of Business in 1993. ([NYU Stern](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/portal-partners/alumni/alumni-news-profiles/news/head-start-through-fertitta-veterans-program))

The expansion years were followed by a near-death experience. In 2007, the brothers took Station private in a $9 billion buyout with billionaire Tom Barrack's Colony Capital, receiving about $1 billion in the transaction and reinvesting $700 million into the private company. Then the financial crisis hit. Station couldn't make payments on the buyout debt and filed for Chapter 11 in July 2009. Frank Jr. died that year following heart surgery. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers)) The company exited bankruptcy in June 2011, with the brothers investing $238 million for a combined 58 percent stake. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers))

## The $2 million bet on the UFC

The UFC was a dying business when [Dana White](/people/dana-white) — described by Bloomberg as "a fight promoter and former high school friend of Lorenzo's" — approached the brothers about buying it. The sport had been banned from cable television because it was unregulated, head-butting was commonplace, and the only state that sanctioned the fights was New Jersey. The promotion had been founded in the early 1990s by the Gracie family of Brazilian jiu-jitsu, with its first event staged in November 1993 in Denver, Colorado. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers)) ([Lorenzo Fertitta deposition](https://www.scribd.com/document/389694278/Depo-of-Lorenzo-Fertitta))

The brothers bought the franchise in January 2001 for $2 million through their new company Zuffa LLC, which the two owned 50/50, gave White a 10 percent cut to serve as president, and invested $38 million to rehabilitate the sport. ([Lorenzo Fertitta deposition](https://www.scribd.com/document/389694278/Depo-of-Lorenzo-Fertitta)) ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers)) Lorenzo took the chairman and CEO role at Zuffa around 2007 or 2008. ([Lorenzo Fertitta deposition](https://www.scribd.com/document/389694278/Depo-of-Lorenzo-Fertitta))

To build an audience, the brothers spent millions producing a reality show, The Ultimate Fighter, and gave the footage away for free to Spike TV, the cable channel aimed at men 18 to 34. It became a hit, and by 2007 the UFC was drawing more pay-per-view buys than HBO Boxing and [WWE](/organizations/wwe/). ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers)) In 2009, Flash Entertainment, an Abu Dhabi government investment company, bought 10 percent of Zuffa in a deal valuing the company at more than $2 billion, and in July 2011 the UFC signed a seven-year television contract with Fox Media Group. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers)) By 2012 the company reported 442 fighters from 38 countries under contract, 14 pay-per-view events a year, $500 million in annual sales, and content reaching more than 1 billion homes in 148 countries and territories. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers))

On July 11, 2016, the UFC was sold for approximately $4 billion to a group led by Hollywood talent agency [WME-IMG](/organizations/wme-img/), with financial backing from Silver Lake Partners, Kohlberg Kravis Roberts, and Michael Dell's investment firm. White confirmed the sale to the Associated Press and stayed on to run the promotion; the Fertitta brothers came out of the deal with only a minority interest. ([Associated Press](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/ufc-sold-to-wme-img-for-4-billion-1.155640)) The brothers and White had purchased the promotion for $2 million fifteen years earlier, and the AP noted the UFC had been "kept afloat by the Fertittas' casino fortune while MMA gained widespread acceptance." ([Associated Press](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/ufc-sold-to-wme-img-for-4-billion-1.155640))

## Fertitta Capital and Red Rock Resorts

On May 1, 2017, [Frank Fertitta](/people/frank-fertitta) and Lorenzo formally launched Fertitta Capital, a private investment firm seeded with $500 million of their own money and focused on consumer-facing companies in technology, media, and entertainment. Former UFC chief financial officer Nakisa Bidarian — who had led the conceptualization, negotiation, and execution of the UFC sale — became CEO and co-founder, while Lorenzo took the role of chairman and co-founder. ([Fertitta Capital](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fertitta-capital-launches-with-500-million-300448601.html)) The launch release described the 2016 UFC transaction as "the largest sports-related sale in history" and noted that the brothers had also sold Fertitta Entertainment that year as part of the successful IPO of Red Rock Resorts. ([Fertitta Capital](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fertitta-capital-launches-with-500-million-300448601.html))

Lorenzo remains in the family casino business as vice chairman of Red Rock Resorts, the position confirmed in the company's September 2026 fiftieth-anniversary announcement. ([Station Casinos](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/station-casinos-awards-team-members-more-than-70-million-in-red-rock-resorts-stock-as-part-of-50th-anniversary-celebration-302874527.html)) The family's wealth is managed through Fertitta Enterprises, a single-family office employing about 60 people. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers))

## Family and philanthropy

Fertitta is married with three children; he and his brother have kept a Sunday-night dinner tradition at their mother Victoria's home. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers)) The brothers funded Fertitta Field at Bishop Gorman High School in Las Vegas. ([Bloomberg Markets](https://filsingergames.proboards.com/thread/11243/article-bloomberg-fertitta-brothers))

Their largest known charitable commitment is to education for veterans: the Fertitta Veterans Program at NYU Stern, established in 2016 with a $15 million gift from Lorenzo and Frank and followed by an additional $15 million two years later, provides financial and professional development support to incoming military-veteran full-time MBA students. ([NYU Stern](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/portal-partners/alumni/alumni-news-profiles/news/head-start-through-fertitta-veterans-program))
