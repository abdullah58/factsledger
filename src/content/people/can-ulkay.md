**Can Ülkay** — rendered "Can Ulkay" in English-language credits — is the Turkish director of [Ayla: The Daughter of War](/creative-works/ayla-the-daughter-of-war/) (2017), [Turkey](/places/turkey/)'s entry for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/amp/ayla-a-story-of-love-turkeys-oscar-entry-121072)). Before features he directed television commercials, joined Terra Film's director roster in 2019, then made *Müslüm* (2018), Netflix's *Kağıttan Hayatlar* (2021) and the 2025 hit *Uykucu* ([Marketing Türkiye](https://www.marketingturkiye.com.tr/haberler/terra-film-kadrosuna-can-ulkayi-ekledi/); [Box Office Türkiye](https://boxofficeturkiye.com/haber/box-office-turkiye-2025-te-sinemalarda-en-cok-izlenen-20-film--7447)).

Turkish industry sources spell his name with the diacritic — "Can Ülkay" — while international databases use the ASCII "Can Ulkay" ([Marketing Türkiye](https://www.marketingturkiye.com.tr/haberler/arda-turan-simit-sarayi-reklamlarinda/); [Anadolu Agency via Haberler](https://www.haberler.com/turk-filmleri-kanadali-sinemaseverlerle-bulustu-12016817-haberi/)). His commercial work included the 2013 Arda Turan campaign for Simit Sarayı and brand films for Lipton, Ülker and Türk Telekom ([Marketing Türkiye](https://www.marketingturkiye.com.tr/haberler/arda-turan-simit-sarayi-reklamlarinda/)).

## The commercial director behind the camera

Ülkay's feature career rests on a long foundation in advertising — a fact the English-language film databases skip entirely. In 2013, Turkey's advertising trade press reported that Simit Sarayı's surprise campaign starring national-team footballer Arda Turan was directed by Ülkay under the Alametifarika agency of founder Serdar Erener, with creative direction by Kenan Ünsal and Emrah Karpuzcu ([Marketing Türkiye](https://www.marketingturkiye.com.tr/haberler/arda-turan-simit-sarayi-reklamlarinda/)). The shoot paired Turan with his mother Yüksel Turan, ran with a 100-person crew at Simit Sarayı stores in Istanbul's Levent district and in Amsterdam, and the films reached audiences across TV, radio, internet, social media and outdoor advertising from 22 May 2013 ([Marketing Türkiye](https://www.marketingturkiye.com.tr/haberler/arda-turan-simit-sarayi-reklamlarinda/)). Six years later, the same trade publication reported that the director of *Ayla*, *Müslüm* and *Türk İşi Dondurma* — and of ad campaigns for TTnet, Biscolata, Tivibu, Didi, Lipton, Eti, Ülker and Türk Telekom — had joined the director roster of Terra Film, founded in 2011 ([Marketing Türkiye](https://www.marketingturkiye.com.tr/haberler/terra-film-kadrosuna-can-ulkayi-ekledi/)).

## Ayla and the Oscar campaign

*Ayla* dramatizes the real story of [Süleyman Dilbirliği](/people/suleyman-dilbirligi/), the sergeant who found an abandoned five-year-old girl during the Korean War in 1950, cared for her for a year and a half, and was reunited with her only sixty years later ([Box Office Türkiye](https://boxofficeturkiye.com/haber/merakla-beklenen-ayla-filminin-vizyon-tarihi-belli-oldu--767)). Hürriyet Daily News reported that Ülkay's film was shown to press in Istanbul on 17 October 2017 as Turkey's entry for the following year's Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film, ahead of a 27 October release ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/amp/ayla-a-story-of-love-turkeys-oscar-entry-121072)). It starred İsmail Hacıoğlu, Çetin Tekindor and Lee Kyung-jin, with a script by [Yiğit Güralp](/people/yigit-guralp/) and a score by Fahir Atakoğlu ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/amp/ayla-a-story-of-love-turkeys-oscar-entry-121072)). A month after release, Ülkay told students at Hacı Bektaş-ı Veli University that the team had believed it would contend for an Oscar from the screenplay stage, arguing an Oscar-calibre film needs an adequate budget — high by Turkish standards, low internationally ([DHA via Mynet](https://haber.mynet.com/goruntulu-haber-ayla-filminin-yonetmeni-can-ulkay-oscarlik-film-icin-yeterli-butce-de-olmali-110103389991.md)).

## Biopics, streaming, and a 2025 theatrical hit

Ülkay followed *Ayla* with *Müslüm* (2018), the biographical drama about arabesque singer Müslüm Gürses, which the trade press credits him with directing ([Marketing Türkiye](https://www.marketingturkiye.com.tr/haberler/terra-film-kadrosuna-can-ulkayi-ekledi/)). His *Türk İşi Dondurma* (*Turkish Ice-Cream*, 2019) later reached Canadian audiences at Toronto's historic Royal Cinema in the second Turkish Films Week, organized by the Yunus Emre Institute's Washington branch with the Turkish Consulate General and supported by the Culture and Tourism Ministry's Cinema Directorate ([Anadolu Agency via Haberler](https://www.haberler.com/turk-filmleri-kanadali-sinemaseverlerle-bulustu-12016817-haberi/)). In 2021 his Netflix drama *Kağıttan Hayatlar* (*Paper Lives*) — Mehmet, an ailing Istanbul waste-warehouse operator played by Çağatay Ulusoy, takes in an eight-year-old runaway — became the most-watched film on Netflix Turkey in March ([Dizi Doktoru](https://www.dizidoktoru.com/netflixte-en-cok-kagittan-hayatlar-izlendi); [Cinema Express](https://www.cinemaexpress.com/reviews/english/2021/Mar/21/kagittan-hayatlar-paper-livesmovie-review-an-unrestrained-dramathat-leaves-you-teary-eyed-23495.html)). His 2025 spy thriller *Uykucu*, starring Ulusoy with Elçin Sangu, ended the year as Turkey's fifth most-watched theatrical release ([Box Office Türkiye](https://boxofficeturkiye.com/haber/cagatay-ulusoy-ve-elcin-sangu-nun-basrollerini-paylastigi-uykucu-filminin-vizyon-tarihi-belli-oldu--7186); [Box Office Türkiye](https://boxofficeturkiye.com/haber/box-office-turkiye-2025-te-sinemalarda-en-cok-izlenen-20-film--7447)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Why do some sources spell his name "Can Ulkay"?**
Turkish-language industry sources — the advertising trade press and Anadolu Agency's cultural desk — consistently use "Can Ülkay" with the Turkish Ü, while English-language credits, databases and photo captions drop the diacritic to "Can Ulkay." Both forms refer to the same director; this profile uses the Turkish rendering.

**What is Can Ülkay best known for?**
Directing *Ayla: The Daughter of War* (2017), the Korean War drama about Sergeant Süleyman Dilbirliği that Turkey selected as its entry for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film. He later directed the Müslüm Gürses biopic *Müslüm* and Netflix's *Kağıttan Hayatlar*.

**Did he really start out in advertising?**
Yes. Before features, Ülkay directed television commercials, including the high-profile 2013 Simit Sarayı campaign starring footballer Arda Turan and campaigns for Lipton, Ülker and Türk Telekom. In 2019 he joined the director roster of the Terra Film production house, formalizing his long commercial career.

**What did Ülkay say about Ayla's Oscar chances?**
At a November 2017 university talk, he said the team had believed the film would contend for an Oscar from the moment its screenplay was being written. He argued an Oscar-calibre film needs an adequate budget — high by Turkish standards, low internationally — and that the team spent its money efficiently.

**What is his most recent film?**
The spy thriller *Uykucu* (*The Sleeper*, 2025), starring Çağatay Ulusoy and Elçin Sangu with a script by Kubilay Tat, released 29 October 2025 and produced by Poll Films. It drew 833,867 admissions and finished the year as Turkey's fifth most-watched theatrical release, a strong showing in a declining market.
