# Brett McGurk

In December 2024, Brett McGurk did something outgoing American envoys almost never do: he invited his own replacement to join him inside live hostage negotiations. McGurk, President Joe Biden's Middle East point man, brought president-elect [Donald Trump](https://factsledger.com/people/donald-trump/)'s designated envoy, Steve Witkoff, to Doha to close the [Gaza](https://factsledger.com/places/gaza-strip/) ceasefire and hostage deal his team had been chasing for more than a year ([Bangkok Post (CNN relay)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/2940975/behind-the-gaza-deal-a-us-odd-couple-and-last-minute-snags)). In the final four days of talks, McGurk and Witkoff spoke multiple times a day, and Witkoff "actually helped clinch down some of the details," National Security Council spokesman John Kirby told CNN ([Bangkok Post (CNN relay)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/2940975/behind-the-gaza-deal-a-us-odd-couple-and-last-minute-snags)). A senior Biden administration official called the pairing of two men serving bitter political rivals "historically, almost unprecedented" ([Bangkok Post (CNN relay)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/2940975/behind-the-gaza-deal-a-us-odd-couple-and-last-minute-snags)).

## The closing act

The Israel-[Hamas](/organizations/hamas/) deal came together through 18-hour days and a "remarkable" partnership between the two envoys, even as it nearly fell apart at the last minute ([Bangkok Post (CNN relay)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/2940975/behind-the-gaza-deal-a-us-odd-couple-and-last-minute-snags)). Israeli and Hamas negotiators sat on separate floors of a building in the Qatari capital, with moderators from Qatar and Egypt shuttling between them ([Bangkok Post (CNN relay)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/2940975/behind-the-gaza-deal-a-us-odd-couple-and-last-minute-snags)). The deal was made possible in part by the knowledge that a new American administration would start on January 20: "In any breakthrough diplomacy, sometimes you need a deadline," the senior Biden official said ([Bangkok Post (CNN relay)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/2940975/behind-the-gaza-deal-a-us-odd-couple-and-last-minute-snags)). Biden had introduced the outline of the plan in May, but Washington had concluded there would "never be a ceasefire" while Hamas leader [Yahya Sinwar](/people/yahya-sinwar/) was alive and Hezbollah opposed a deal; then Israel killed Hezbollah chief Hassan Nasrallah in September, Sinwar in October, and damaged Iran's air defenses, and the fall of Bashar al-Assad in December further isolated Hamas ([Bangkok Post (CNN relay)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/2940975/behind-the-gaza-deal-a-us-odd-couple-and-last-minute-snags)). Talks intensified after McGurk returned to the region on January 5 — "18 hours a day, sometimes longer" — with the hostage-and-prisoner exchange lists among the last items nailed down ([Bangkok Post (CNN relay)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/2940975/behind-the-gaza-deal-a-us-odd-couple-and-last-minute-snags)). Tidal Partners, one of McGurk's post-government employers, credits him with negotiating two Gaza ceasefires and securing the release of more than 100 hostages ([Bakersfield.com (Business Wire release)](https://bakersfield.marketminute.com/article/bizwire-2025-7-28-brett-mcgurk-joins-tidal-partners-as-senior-advisor)).

## A career redirected on 9/11

McGurk began his career as a law clerk to Chief Justice William H. Rehnquist and was at the Supreme Court on September 11, 2001 — a day that, in the words of the announcement of his later move to Tidal Partners, "changed history and led to his dedicating over two decades to nonpartisan service in Washington and overseas" ([Bakersfield.com (Business Wire release)](https://bakersfield.marketminute.com/article/bizwire-2025-7-28-brett-mcgurk-joins-tidal-partners-as-senior-advisor)). His diplomatic career ultimately spanned four presidential administrations ([Lux Capital](https://www.luxcapital.com/advisors/brett-mcgurk)).

Early on, he spent nearly a year in Iraq as a legal advisor during the transition to an interim Iraqi government and helped negotiate Iraq's interim constitution ([Bakersfield.com (Business Wire release)](https://bakersfield.marketminute.com/article/bizwire-2025-7-28-brett-mcgurk-joins-tidal-partners-as-senior-advisor)). As a senior White House official under George W. Bush, he was an early advocate for a change in Iraq war policy and helped develop "the surge" ([Lux Capital](https://www.luxcapital.com/advisors/brett-mcgurk)); as Senior Director on the National Security Council he is credited with helping develop that 2007 surge strategy, and he later negotiated the Strategic Framework Agreement that continues to guide relations between Iraq and the United States ([Bakersfield.com (Business Wire release)](https://bakersfield.marketminute.com/article/bizwire-2025-7-28-brett-mcgurk-joins-tidal-partners-as-senior-advisor)).

## The fixer across parties

Under Presidents Barack Obama and Donald Trump alike, McGurk served as Special Presidential Envoy for the Global Coalition to Defeat ISIS, building and leading a coalition of more than 80 countries alongside local forces on the ground ([Lux Capital](https://www.luxcapital.com/advisors/brett-mcgurk)). The coalition, Tidal Partners notes, remains in place to this day ([Bakersfield.com (Business Wire release)](https://bakersfield.marketminute.com/article/bizwire-2025-7-28-brett-mcgurk-joins-tidal-partners-as-senior-advisor)). In the Obama administration he also led fourteen months of secret negotiations with Iran that produced a prisoner exchange freeing four Americans, including Washington Post correspondent Jason Rezaian ([Lux Capital](https://www.luxcapital.com/advisors/brett-mcgurk)). For the hostage work he received the James Foley Freedom Award — styled the James W. Foley Hostage Freedom Award in the Tidal announcement ([Lux Capital](https://www.luxcapital.com/advisors/brett-mcgurk))([Bakersfield.com (Business Wire release)](https://bakersfield.marketminute.com/article/bizwire-2025-7-28-brett-mcgurk-joins-tidal-partners-as-senior-advisor)).

That cross-party run ended in December 2018 over a policy he could not defend. McGurk submitted his resignation to Secretary of State Mike Pompeo on Friday, December 21, effective December 31, objecting to Trump's abrupt order to withdraw the roughly 2,000 US troops from Syria ([Bangkok Post (AFP relay)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/1599134/top-us-envoy-in-fight-against-is-group-resigns-official)). Just days earlier, the Obama appointee whom Trump had kept on had told journalists that "nobody is declaring a mission accomplished" against the Islamic State; in his resignation letter he said the militants were in fact not defeated and that withdrawing prematurely could let the jihadists regain strength ([Bangkok Post (AFP relay)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/1599134/top-us-envoy-in-fight-against-is-group-resigns-official)). He had said weeks earlier: "It would be reckless if we were just to say, 'Well, the physical caliphate is defeated, so we can just leave now'" ([Bangkok Post (AFP relay)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/1599134/top-us-envoy-in-fight-against-is-group-resigns-official)). His departure, effective at year's end, capped a chaotic week that also saw Defense Secretary Jim Mattis quit over the same disagreement ([Bangkok Post (AFP relay)](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/1599134/top-us-envoy-in-fight-against-is-group-resigns-official)).

## Biden years

McGurk returned to government under Biden as Deputy Assistant to the President and White House Coordinator for the Middle East region, overseeing American engagement across the Middle East and North Africa at moments of acute crisis and opportunity ([Lux Capital](https://www.luxcapital.com/advisors/brett-mcgurk)). In that role he spearheaded US efforts to secure the release of Israeli hostages and a Gaza ceasefire, and coordinated the defense of Israel against Iranian ballistic missile attacks ([Bakersfield.com (Business Wire release)](https://bakersfield.marketminute.com/article/bizwire-2025-7-28-brett-mcgurk-joins-tidal-partners-as-senior-advisor)). His bio also credits him with championing new technology and energy corridors between India, the Gulf states, Israel, Jordan, and Europe, and with seeing firsthand how AI and computing infrastructure have become central to strategic partnerships and global influence ([Lux Capital](https://www.luxcapital.com/advisors/brett-mcgurk)).

## The post-government pivot

During his 2019–2020 period out of government, McGurk taught graduate courses on strategy and presidential decision-making at Stanford and served on the board of a Lux portfolio company, Primer, which works on AI and natural-language processing for real-time analysis in crisis and operational environments ([Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/commentary/transcript/brett-mcgurk-)). After the Biden administration ended, he moved fully into the intersection of geopolitics and technology. He is a Venture Partner at Lux Capital, where he helps founders navigate a landscape in which innovation and international cooperation are increasingly intertwined ([Lux Capital](https://www.luxcapital.com/advisors/brett-mcgurk)). In July 2025 he joined Tidal Partners as a Senior Advisor ([Bakersfield.com (Business Wire release)](https://bakersfield.marketminute.com/article/bizwire-2025-7-28-brett-mcgurk-joins-tidal-partners-as-senior-advisor)). He is also a CNN Global Affairs Analyst, a Distinguished Fellow at Harvard's Belfer Center and the Atlantic Council — where he advises the N7 Initiative — and Senior Advisor for the Middle East and International Affairs at Cisco Systems ([Bakersfield.com (Business Wire release)](https://bakersfield.marketminute.com/article/bizwire-2025-7-28-brett-mcgurk-joins-tidal-partners-as-senior-advisor)). He is writing a book about presidential decision-making and high-stakes diplomacy, to be published by Crown ([Bakersfield.com (Business Wire release)](https://bakersfield.marketminute.com/article/bizwire-2025-7-28-brett-mcgurk-joins-tidal-partners-as-senior-advisor)).
