Yiğit Güralp is the Turkish screenwriter and creative producer of [Ayla: The Daughter of War](https://factsledger.com/creative-works/ayla-the-daughter-of-war/), Turkey's 2017 Oscar entry, whose career ran from a decade at Universal Music Turkey through hit TV writing to three feature films. In December 2022, after years of public dispute with the film's producer, [Istanbul](/places/istanbul/)'s 3rd Intellectual and Industrial Rights Court ordered producer Mustafa Uslu to pay him 100,000 TL in royalties and moral damages ([BirGün](https://www.birgun.net/haber/senaristin-actigi-davada-karar-mustafa-uslu-ayla-icin-100-bin-tl-tazminat-odeyecek-416485)). He later published the essay collection *İyi Hissettiren Yazılar* (2023) ([Yeni Şafak](https://www.yenisafak.com/amphtml/hayat/hayat-aci-ve-tatlinin-karisimidir-3901787)).

## Overview

Güralp is one of the few Turkish screenwriters whose path to film ran through the music industry. Born and raised in central Bakırköy, Istanbul, he began working at 15 in retail at Galeria and later spent roughly a decade at Universal Music Turkey, working on albums by Şebnem Ferah, Athena, İzel, Emel Sayın and more than 30 other artists, as he recounted in a 2012 Akşam interview ([Akşam](https://www.aksam.com.tr/yazarlar/nilay-ornek/kenan-senaristle-aylarca-calisir-8042y/haber-197418)). His first film, *Sınav* (*Exam*, 2006), for which he wrote the story and screenplay, reached cinemas when he was 28 ([Akşam](https://www.aksam.com.tr/yazarlar/nilay-ornek/kenan-senaristle-aylarca-calisir-8042y/haber-197418)). He then wrote the most-watched first season of *Kavak Yelleri*, three years of *Doludizgin Yıllar*, episode stories for *İşler Güçler*, and a screenplay adaptation of blogger Pucca's book ([Akşam](https://www.aksam.com.tr/yazarlar/nilay-ornek/kenan-senaristle-aylarca-calisir-8042y/haber-197418)). Kenan İmirzalıoğlu's *Uzun Hikâye* (*Long Story*, 2012), adapted from Mustafa Kutlu's novel by Osman Sınav, made him a mainstream name ([Yeni Şafak](https://www.yenisafak.com/amphtml/hayat/hayat-aci-ve-tatlinin-karisimidir-3901787)); but it was *Ayla* (2017) — from the Korean War story of Sergeant [Süleyman Dilbirliği](https://factsledger.com/people/suleyman-dilbirligi/) — that defined him ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/amp/ayla-a-story-of-love-turkeys-oscar-entry-121072); [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/cinema/2017/10/28/four-new-movies-debut-this-week)).

## From record sleeves to screenwriting

Güralp's apprenticeship was a decade of albums before a single frame of film. At Universal Music Turkey he worked on more than 30 artists' albums — a crash course in marketing, media, content design and crisis management that he later said gave his film projects an unusual corporate discipline ([Akşam](https://www.aksam.com.tr/yazarlar/nilay-ornek/kenan-senaristle-aylarca-calisir-8042y/haber-197418)). The pivot came with *Sınav* in 2006, where he wrote both story and screenplay and — tellingly for a pattern that would repeat with *Ayla* — boycotted the gala in protest ([Akşam](https://www.aksam.com.tr/yazarlar/nilay-ornek/kenan-senaristle-aylarca-calisir-8042y/haber-197418)). Television then made him a name: the first and most-watched season of *Kavak Yelleri*, three years of *Doludizgin Yıllar*, and a working relationship with director Osman Sınav that produced the *Uzun Hikâye* adaptation ([Akşam](https://www.aksam.com.tr/yazarlar/nilay-ornek/kenan-senaristle-aylarca-calisir-8042y/haber-197418)). On that film, Kenan İmirzalıoğlu interrogated the script with him line by line through over a month of rehearsals — a rigor Güralp described as nearly unique in the industry ([Akşam](https://www.aksam.com.tr/yazarlar/nilay-ornek/kenan-senaristle-aylarca-calisir-8042y/haber-197418)).

## Ayla: five years of work, a blocked Instagram, and a courtroom

The Ayla project began when Güralp watched the South Korean documentary *Koreli Kızım Ayla* and spent five years developing a feature — self-funding it, gathering hundreds of minutes of wartime footage and thousands of pages of documents, plus a short film ([BirGün](https://www.birgun.net/haber/senaristin-actigi-davada-karar-mustafa-uslu-ayla-icin-100-bin-tl-tazminat-odeyecek-416485)). That short was shown to producer Mustafa Uslu without permission; Uslu joined as producer, a contract was signed, and Güralp never received his copy, per the petition ([BirGün](https://www.birgun.net/haber/senaristin-actigi-davada-karar-mustafa-uslu-ayla-icin-100-bin-tl-tazminat-odeyecek-416485)). The film opened October 27, 2017 as Turkey's Oscar entry ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/amp/ayla-a-story-of-love-turkeys-oscar-entry-121072)) and drew close to 6 million ticketed viewers ([Samsun Haber](https://www.samsunhaber.com/ayla-filminin-senaristiyle-keyifli-roportaj)) — scale that framed his later fight over credit. During the Oscar campaign the team blocked him on social media and shut him out of PR; he wrote on Instagram that "'Ayla' belongs to the audience now" ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633)). In December 2022 Istanbul's 3rd Intellectual and Industrial Rights Court ordered Uslu's company to pay 75,000 TL in royalties plus 25,000 TL in moral damages, accepting his name had been erased from posters, press and the website ([BirGün](https://www.birgun.net/haber/senaristin-actigi-davada-karar-mustafa-uslu-ayla-icin-100-bin-tl-tazminat-odeyecek-416485)).

## After Ayla: Sarıl, a book, and refusing the Ayla-guy label

Güralp has worked openly to avoid being typecast as the "'Ayla' guy." His next announced project, *Sarıl* (*The Hug*, 2018), took the true story of Kuyu — a puppy rescued after 11 days from a 65-meter well in Beykoz and adopted by HAÇİKO — and was to be his directorial debut, produced by Emre Oskay and İlkem Şahin ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/cinema/2018/01/15/scriptwriter-of-ayla-the-daughter-of-war-to-tell-story-of-dog-named-kuyu/amp); [Samsun Haber](https://www.samsunhaber.com/ayla-filminin-senaristiyle-keyifli-roportaj)). He also announced an adaptation of a famous living English novelist's book with producers from two countries, slated for a mid-2018 announcement ([Samsun Haber](https://www.samsunhaber.com/ayla-filminin-senaristiyle-keyifli-roportaj)). In January 2023 he published his first book, *İyi Hissettiren Yazılar*, with Masa Kitap — an essay collection built on encounters with Barış Manço, Zeki Müren, Tarık Akan and Sadık Şendil ([Yeni Şafak](https://www.yenisafak.com/amphtml/hayat/hayat-aci-ve-tatlinin-karisimidir-3901787)). The book's logic matches his career: if he had kept delivering the expected, he told Yeni Şafak, there would have been no *Ayla* — and *Ayla* clones would have reduced him to "the Ayla-film guy" ([Yeni Şafak](https://www.yenisafak.com/amphtml/hayat/hayat-aci-ve-tatlinin-karisimidir-3901787)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is Yiğit Güralp?**
Yiğit Güralp is a Turkish screenwriter best known as the screenwriter of *Ayla: The Daughter of War* (2017), Turkey's Oscar entry, on which he also served as creative producer. Before film he spent roughly a decade at Universal Music Turkey and wrote hit TV series including *Kavak Yelleri* and *Doludizgin Yıllar*, plus the features *Sınav* and *Uzun Hikâye*.

**What was the lawsuit between Güralp and producer Mustafa Uslu about?**
Güralp sued Uslu's Dijital Yapım Evi, alleging his name was left off *Ayla* posters, press coverage and the film's website while Uslu presented himself as the project's owner, and that he was excluded from the gala and PR. In 2022 Istanbul's 3rd Intellectual and Industrial Rights Court awarded him 75,000 TL in royalties plus 25,000 TL in moral damages ([BirGün](https://www.birgun.net/haber/senaristin-actigi-davada-karar-mustafa-uslu-ayla-icin-100-bin-tl-tazminat-odeyecek-416485)).

**What else has Yiğit Güralp written?**
Beyond *Ayla*, he wrote the story and screenplay of *Sınav* (2006), the screenplay of *Uzun Hikâye* (2012) adapted from Mustafa Kutlu's novel, the hit first season of *Kavak Yelleri*, three years of *Doludizgin Yıllar*, episode stories for *İşler Güçler*, a screenplay for blogger Pucca's book, and the essay collection *İyi Hissettiren Yazılar* (Masa Kitap, 2023).

**Why did Güralp fall out with the Ayla production team?**
During *Ayla*'s 2017 Oscar campaign, Güralp wrote on Instagram that the production team had blocked him on social media and kept him away from the entire PR process, adding that "for four years, I did not have a single, peaceful, healthy day" and that "'Ayla' belongs to the audience now" ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633)).
