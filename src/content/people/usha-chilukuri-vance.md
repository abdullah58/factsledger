## Overview

On July 19, 2026, Usha Vance gave birth to a son, Alec Neel Vance, at Walter Reed National Military Medical Center — the first child born to a sitting vice president of the United States in more than 150 years. The White House Historical Association's precedents reach back to Schuyler Colfax III, born in 1870, and John C. Calhoun's son William, born in 1829 ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/jd-and-usha-vance-welcome-a-baby-boy-the-first-child-born-to-a-sitting-vp-in-more-than-150-years/)).

Usha Vance is the second lady of the United States, the wife of Vice President [JD Vance](/people/jd-vance/) ([The White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/usha-vance/)). She is the first Asian American and the first Hindu American to hold the role ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/world/2025/04/23/jd-vance-s-india-visit-who-is-usha-vance-the-second-lady-of-the-us.html)). Before entering public life she was a trial lawyer who clerked for three federal judges, including Chief Justice John Roberts ([Associated Press](https://gmg-kprc-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/politics/2024/07/16/who-is-usha-vance-yale-law-graduate-and-wife-of-vice-presidential-nominee-jd-vance/); [The White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/usha-vance/)). As second lady she has built a public agenda around childhood literacy — an annual Summer Reading Challenge and a children's-story podcast, "Storytime with the Second Lady" ([The White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/usha-vance/)).

## From San Diego to Yale Law

Usha Bala Chilukuri Vance was raised in the suburbs of San Diego by Telugu Indian immigrant parents with roots in Andhra Pradesh; her father is a mechanical engineer who graduated from the Indian Institutes of Technology, and her mother is a molecular biologist ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/world/2025/04/23/jd-vance-s-india-visit-who-is-usha-vance-the-second-lady-of-the-us.html)). The Associated Press described her mother as a biologist and provost at the University of California at San Diego and her father as an engineer ([Associated Press](https://gmg-kprc-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/politics/2024/07/16/who-is-usha-vance-yale-law-graduate-and-wife-of-vice-presidential-nominee-jd-vance/)).

She earned a bachelor's degree in history from Yale University, a Master of Philosophy from Clare College, Cambridge, as a Gates Cambridge Scholar, and a Juris Doctor from Yale Law School, where she met her future husband ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/world/2025/04/23/jd-vance-s-india-visit-who-is-usha-vance-the-second-lady-of-the-us.html); [The White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/usha-vance/)). In JD Vance's 2016 memoir "Hillbilly Elegy," he wrote that the two got to know each other through a class assignment and that he "fell hard" for his writing partner; they graduated in 2013 and married the following year ([Associated Press](https://gmg-kprc-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/politics/2024/07/16/who-is-usha-vance-yale-law-graduate-and-wife-of-vice-presidential-nominee-jd-vance/)).

After law school she clerked for Judge Amul Thapar, then of the Eastern District of Kentucky, in 2013 ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/world/2025/04/23/jd-vance-s-india-visit-who-is-usha-vance-the-second-lady-of-the-us.html)); then spent a year clerking for Justice Brett Kavanaugh when he served as an appeals court judge in Washington, followed by a year as a law clerk to Chief Justice John Roberts ([Associated Press](https://gmg-kprc-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/politics/2024/07/16/who-is-usha-vance-yale-law-graduate-and-wife-of-vice-presidential-nominee-jd-vance/)). The White House biography lists all three clerkships ([The White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/usha-vance/)).

## Trial lawyer to the campaign trail

She became a trial lawyer at Munger, Tolles & Olson, working out of its San Francisco and Washington, D.C. offices on complex civil litigation and appeals ([Associated Press](https://gmg-kprc-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/politics/2024/07/16/who-is-usha-vance-yale-law-graduate-and-wife-of-vice-presidential-nominee-jd-vance/)). After her husband was chosen as Donald Trump's running mate, the firm announced: "Usha has informed us she has decided to leave the firm," calling her "an excellent lawyer and colleague" ([Associated Press](https://gmg-kprc-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/politics/2024/07/16/who-is-usha-vance-yale-law-graduate-and-wife-of-vice-presidential-nominee-jd-vance/)).

Outside the firm, she served on the Cincinnati Symphony Board of Directors from September 2020 to July 2023, and Ohio voter records show her as a registered Republican as of 2022 who voted in that year's Republican primary — the same election in which her husband ran in the GOP Senate primary ([Associated Press](https://gmg-kprc-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/politics/2024/07/16/who-is-usha-vance-yale-law-graduate-and-wife-of-vice-presidential-nominee-jd-vance/)).

On July 17, 2024, she introduced her husband at the Republican National Convention in Milwaukee. "He will make a great Vice President of the United States," she said, describing their different backgrounds — a white man from the Rust Belt and the daughter of Indian immigrants — his acceptance of her vegetarian diet and his cooking Indian food, and saying that their meeting and marriage was "a testament to this great country" ([Press Trust of India](https://www.telegraphindia.com/world/jd-vance-will-make-a-great-vice-president-for-united-states-says-his-indian-american-wife-usha/cid/2034509)).

## Second lady: a literacy agenda

As second lady, she has made childhood literacy her signature cause. In 2025 she launched an annual Summer Reading Challenge, an initiative to encourage young readers and foster a lifelong love of books, and she hosts "Storytime with the Second Lady," a podcast of children's stories with special guest readers, available on Spotify and YouTube ([The White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/usha-vance/)).

On April 21, 2025, she and the vice president landed in Udaipur, India, with their children — the couple's first official visit to India, described as a return to her roots, with a red-carpet entry and traditional Rajasthani rituals ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/world/2025/04/23/jd-vance-s-india-visit-who-is-usha-vance-the-second-lady-of-the-us.html)). She continues to practise Hinduism ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/world/2025/04/23/jd-vance-s-india-visit-who-is-usha-vance-the-second-lady-of-the-us.html)).

## A fourth child — and what the family said about it

In January 2026 the couple announced they were expecting a fourth child ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/jd-and-usha-vance-welcome-a-baby-boy-the-first-child-born-to-a-sitting-vp-in-more-than-150-years/)). In a June 30, 2026 interview with Moms for America, Usha said a fourth child was "really not on my bingo card at first," while JD "always really wanted to have another child" ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/07/24/usha-vance-shares-new-photos-baby-alec-neel-vance/91036359007/)).

Alec Neel Vance was born on July 19, 2026, at Walter Reed National Military Medical Center, joining siblings Ewan (9), Vivek (6), and Mirabel (4) ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/jd-and-usha-vance-welcome-a-baby-boy-the-first-child-born-to-a-sitting-vp-in-more-than-150-years/)). President Trump congratulated the family on Truth Social the next day, calling him "a perfect baby boy for the wonderful Vance family" ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/07/24/usha-vance-shares-new-photos-baby-alec-neel-vance/91036359007/)); the family released photographs as a family of six on July 24 ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/07/24/usha-vance-shares-new-photos-baby-alec-neel-vance/91036359007/)).

Asked about the decision, Usha said the killing of Charlie Kirk and Erika Kirk's public remarks about loss and family had factored into the couple's thinking, but added: "I wouldn't say that this was… the decisive factor" ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/jd-and-usha-vance-welcome-a-baby-boy-the-first-child-born-to-a-sitting-vp-in-more-than-150-years/)). JD Vance's memoir "Communion: Finding My Way Back to Faith" also links the decision to Kirk's assassination ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/07/24/usha-vance-shares-new-photos-baby-alec-neel-vance/91036359007/)). Those are the Vances' accounts, recorded as their remarks.

During the pregnancy she launched "Storytime with the Second Lady" as a video podcast ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/jd-and-usha-vance-welcome-a-baby-boy-the-first-child-born-to-a-sitting-vp-in-more-than-150-years/)). A lighter episode from the same period: after a New York Times fashion critic wrote about her maternity style, she was photographed in an $8.75 coral maternity dress from Old Navy ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/jd-and-usha-vance-welcome-a-baby-boy-the-first-child-born-to-a-sitting-vp-in-more-than-150-years/)).
