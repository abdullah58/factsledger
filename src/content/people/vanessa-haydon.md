## Overview

Vanessa Haydon — known through her marriage as Vanessa Trump — is a former model best known as the ex-wife of [Donald Trump Jr.](/people/donald-trump-jr/), with whom she was married from 2005 to 2018 and has five children. She re-entered the public eye in 2025 through her relationship with golfer Tiger Woods ([USA Today](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/nation/2025/08/06/who-is-vanessa-trump-what-to-know-about-donald-trump-jrs-ex-wife/85609170007/)). But the most consequential fact about her life is one the explainers tend to bury: a year before she filed for divorce, she became the heiress to a pasta-sauce fortune — an estimated US$62 million from her stepfather's stake in Rao's Homemade — that made her one of the financially independent figures in the Trump orbit ([SCMP](https://scmp.com/print/magazines/style/celebrity/article/3121114/vanessa-trump-who-donald-trump-jrs-low-profile-ex-wife)).

## The Rao's millions

Vanessa's stepfather was Charles Haydon, a prominent lawyer who was close to Frank Pellegrino Sr. and Ron Straci, the men behind Rao's, the celebrated East Harlem restaurant whose bottled marinara became a grocery-aisle empire. Before his death in 2005, Haydon invested $1 million in Rao's Specialty Foods and took a 30 percent stake ([The Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/eat/vanessa-trump-pasta-sauce-inheritance/050318/)). When the business was acquired for $415 million in 2017, the Haydon family's cut was enormous: Vanessa's mother bought a New York City penthouse for $6.4 million in cash, and Vanessa herself received an estimated US$62 million ([SCMP](https://scmp.com/print/magazines/style/celebrity/article/3121114/vanessa-trump-who-donald-trump-jrs-low-profile-ex-wife); [The Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/eat/vanessa-trump-pasta-sauce-inheritance/050318/)).

The money arrived with a twist. A month after she filed for divorce in March 2018, Donald Trump Jr. — a Trump Organization trustee — filed a "defendant's demand for statement of net worth" in Manhattan Supreme Court on April 27, 2018, demanding to know how much money his estranged wife had and where it came from ([The Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/eat/vanessa-trump-pasta-sauce-inheritance/050318/)). Specifics of the payout were never publicly disclosed, so no source read can give a more precise figure than the SCMP's US$62 million estimate — and this profile does not present one as definitive.

## From Pergolizzi to Haydon

She was born Vanessa Kay Pergolizzi in New York City in 1977 ([SCMP](https://scmp.com/print/magazines/style/celebrity/article/3121114/vanessa-trump-who-donald-trump-jrs-low-profile-ex-wife)). The surname the world knows her by came later: when she began modeling, she took the surname of her stepfather, Charles Haydon ([USA Today](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/nation/2025/08/06/who-is-vanessa-trump-what-to-know-about-donald-trump-jrs-ex-wife/85609170007/)).

As a model she signed with Wilhelmina Models and worked in fashion before moving into acting. In 2003 she appeared in the romantic comedy *Something's Gotta Give* alongside Jack Nicholson and Diane Keaton ([SCMP](https://scmp.com/print/magazines/style/celebrity/article/3121114/vanessa-trump-who-donald-trump-jrs-low-profile-ex-wife)). She later opened the nightclub Sessa with her sister Veronika in New York, launched the La Poshett handbag line in 2010, and appeared on *The Apprentice* in 2011 and in *Bret Michaels: Life as I Know It* in 2010 ([The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/entertainment/vanessa-trump-bio-age-net-worth-children-relationship-with-tiger-woods-1503491235.html)). None of these ventures defined her public image — her marriage did.

## Marriage to Donald Trump Jr.

Donald Trump introduced his son to Vanessa at a fashion show in 2003 ([USA Today](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/nation/2025/08/06/who-is-vanessa-trump-what-to-know-about-donald-trump-jrs-ex-wife/85609170007/)). Two years later, on November 12, 2005, they married at Mar-a-Lago in Palm Beach, Florida, before roughly 370 guests. The ceremony was officiated by Judge Maryanne Trump Barry, the groom's aunt; Ivanka Trump served as bridesmaid and Eric Trump as groomsman. The bride and groom were both 27, weeks shy of their 28th birthdays ([USA Today](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/nation/2025/08/06/who-is-vanessa-trump-what-to-know-about-donald-trump-jrs-ex-wife/85609170007/)).

The marriage produced five children: Kai, Donald Trump III, Tristan, Spencer, and Chloe ([USA Today](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/nation/2025/08/06/who-is-vanessa-trump-what-to-know-about-donald-trump-jrs-ex-wife/85609170007/)). Vanessa was largely a private figure during the marriage, though she appeared at campaign events. She did not publicly campaign for her father-in-law in 2016, 2020, or 2024, a distinction from several other Trump family members ([USA Today](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/nation/2025/08/06/who-is-vanessa-trump-what-to-know-about-donald-trump-jrs-ex-wife/85609170007/)).

## Divorce and after

In March 2018, after 12 years of marriage, Vanessa filed for divorce in what was described as an "uncontested" proceeding ([Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/vanessa-trump-files-for-divorce-from-donald-trump-jr-after-12-years-of-marriage/articleshow/63331616.cms)). The couple released a joint statement: "We have five beautiful children together and they remain our top priority. We ask for privacy during this time." Custody was settled in July 2018, and the divorce was finalized at the end of that year ([Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/vanessa-trump-files-for-divorce-from-donald-trump-jr-after-12-years-of-marriage/articleshow/63331616.cms)).

The split has been notably amicable. Vanessa lives in Jupiter, Florida, with the children, near her ex-husband ([USA Today](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/nation/2025/08/06/who-is-vanessa-trump-what-to-know-about-donald-trump-jrs-ex-wife/85609170007/)). She has appeared at major Trump family political moments since the divorce — Election Night gatherings and Inauguration Day in January 2025, where she was photographed alongside Don Jr., his former fiancée Kimberly Guilfoyle, and his current wife Bettina Anderson — as well as the 2024 Republican National Convention in Milwaukee ([USA Today](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/nation/2025/08/06/who-is-vanessa-trump-what-to-know-about-donald-trump-jrs-ex-wife/85609170007/)). The picture is of someone who left the marriage but never fully left the family orbit.

## Tiger Woods

On March 13, 2025, rumors surfaced that Vanessa was dating Tiger Woods. Ten days later, on March 23, Woods made it official with an Instagram post: "Love is in the air and life is better with you by my side! At this time we would appreciate privacy for all those close to our hearts." The post drew more than 207,000 likes within three hours. It is the only official social-media post the couple has shared together ([USA Today](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/nation/2025/08/06/who-is-vanessa-trump-what-to-know-about-donald-trump-jrs-ex-wife/85609170007/)).

The pairing made geographic sense: Woods also lives in Jupiter, Florida, and both families share golf in their DNA. Vanessa's eldest daughter, Kai, is an aspiring golfer, and Kai and Woods's son Charlie attended the same school, The Benjamin School ([USA Today](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/nation/2025/08/06/who-is-vanessa-trump-what-to-know-about-donald-trump-jrs-ex-wife/85609170007/)).
