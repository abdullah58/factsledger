Matt Mowers is what a party pipeline looks like when it works exactly as designed — and then what happens when it meets a primary electorate in a different mood. He started running a county Republican committee as a teenager, became Chris Christie's New Hampshire man, crossed to Donald Trump's campaign when Christie folded, and ended up a senior official in the State Department's global-health office. In 2020 he won a House nomination. In 2022 he had the fundraising, the endorsements, and a super PAC spending roughly $2 million on his behalf — and lost his own primary to a 25-year-old former White House aide running to his right. ([New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/mowers-loses-new-hampshire-congressional-bid/); [New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/new-jerseyan-matt-mowers-is-top-fundraiser-in-bid-for-new-hampshire-congressional-seat/))

## The Christie pipeline

Mowers is an East Brunswick, New Jersey native. His political career began unusually early: as a teenager he served as executive director of the Bergen County Republican Committee, and he worked as an aide to State Sen. Gerald Cardinale of Demarest. ([New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/new-jerseyan-matt-mowers-is-top-fundraiser-in-bid-for-new-hampshire-congressional-seat/))

He worked on Christie's 2009 and 2013 gubernatorial campaigns and in Christie's first-term administration in Trenton. Shortly after Christie's 2013 re-election victory, Mowers departed New Jersey to become executive director of the New Hampshire Republican Party — a job Christie helped line up — and laid the groundwork for Christie's 2016 presidential campaign. ([New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/mowers-loses-new-hampshire-congressional-bid/))

When Christie's presidential bid collapsed — he dropped out one day after finishing sixth in the New Hampshire primary with just 7% of the vote — Mowers returned to New Jersey and took a job at the public affairs firm Mercury. But the bridge to Trumpism was already built: he joined the Trump campaign as National Field Coordinator & Battle Ground States Director. ([New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/mowers-loses-new-hampshire-congressional-bid/); [New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/new-jerseyan-matt-mowers-is-top-fundraiser-in-bid-for-new-hampshire-congressional-seat/))

## The State Department years

Mowers served in the Trump administration first as the White House liaison to the U.S. Department of State, and then as a senior White House advisor and as chief of staff and chief policy officer in the Office of the Global AIDS Coordinator and Special Representative for Global Health Diplomacy. It is the least-discussed stretch of his résumé — most coverage introduces him only as a "former Trump State Department official" — but it was a substantive policy post, not a political appointment's parking spot. ([New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/mowers-loses-new-hampshire-congressional-bid/); [New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/new-jerseyan-matt-mowers-is-top-fundraiser-in-bid-for-new-hampshire-congressional-seat/))

## Two runs at the same seat

In 2020 Mowers moved back to New Hampshire to run for Congress in the 1st district. He easily won the GOP primary but lost to two-term Democratic Rep. Christopher Pappas by a 51-46% margin — close enough that a rematch looked natural. ([New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/mowers-loses-new-hampshire-congressional-bid/))

For 2022 he raised $840,840, including $408,065 in the fourth quarter of 2021, and held $577,426 cash-on-hand. Christie headlined a fundraiser for him in Belleville that September, and the field of challengers — [Karoline Leavitt](/people/karoline-leavitt/), a 25-year-old former Trump assistant White House press secretary; former TV newscaster Gail Huff Brown; state Rep. Tim Baxter — looked manageable. Mowers was the frontrunner. ([New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/new-jerseyan-matt-mowers-is-top-fundraiser-in-bid-for-new-hampshire-congressional-seat/))

The establishment agreed. House Minority Leader Kevin McCarthy and Louisiana Rep. Steve Scalise backed Mowers; so did former Secretary of State Mike Pompeo and former U.N. Ambassador Nikki Haley. The McCarthy-aligned Congressional Leadership Fund spent roughly $2 million to nominate him, on the view that he had the better shot against Pappas. Leavitt had her own heavyweight backing: Elise Stefanik, Jim Jordan, and Lauren Boebert. Trump himself took no side. ([NPR](https://www.wamc.org/2022-09-14/25-year-old-former-trump-aide-wins-gop-primary-in-new-hampshire); [New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/mowers-loses-new-hampshire-congressional-bid/))

## The loss

On September 13, 2022, Leavitt beat Mowers 35%-25% in a ten-candidate field, leading by 5,508 votes with 86% counted; Huff Brown took 18% for third. Leavitt ran to Mowers' right, and Mowers' Christie ties — Christie by then a prominent Trump critic within the party — earned him attacks that he wasn't conservative enough. Late polling had it close: the University of New Hampshire's Granite State Poll found Mowers narrowly leading in late August with more than a quarter of likely primary voters undecided. ([New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/mowers-loses-new-hampshire-congressional-bid/); [NPR](https://www.wamc.org/2022-09-14/25-year-old-former-trump-aide-wins-gop-primary-in-new-hampshire))

The policy gap between the two was thin — both branded themselves staunch conservatives and political outsiders trading on Trump-administration service. The real difference was on the 2020 election: Leavitt openly repeated Trump's claim that he had won; Mowers, as [NPR](/organizations/npr/) put it, "has not directly addressed it." ([NPR](https://www.wamc.org/2022-09-14/25-year-old-former-trump-aide-wins-gop-primary-in-new-hampshire))

Mowers' concession was gracious: "I got into this race to give a voice to those Granite Staters who felt betrayed by the political class in Washington and to restore American strength and leadership around the world," he said. "Unfortunately, tonight's results didn't go our way, but I will never stop fighting for those middle-class families to ensure they are not forgotten." ([New Jersey Globe](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/mowers-loses-new-hampshire-congressional-bid/))
