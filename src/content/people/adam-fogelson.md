**Adam Fogelson is the Chair of Lionsgate's Motion Picture Group and the former chairman of [Universal Pictures](/organizations/universal-pictures/), who used Lionsgate's August 2026 earnings call to lay out the studio's plan for a sequel to its billion-dollar [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) biopic [*Michael*](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/)** ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/lionsgate-adam-fogelson-motion-picture-group-chair/); [ANI](https://www.manilametro.com/news/279225675/michael-2-eyes-late-2026-or-early-2027-production-start-targets-release-by-end-of-2027-or-first-half-of-2028)). Fogelson said the studio is targeting a sequel production start in late 2026 or early 2027 for release in late 2027 or the first half of 2028 — reusing musical sequences shot for the first film that never made the final cut ([ANI](https://www.manilametro.com/news/279225675/michael-2-eyes-late-2026-or-early-2027-production-start-targets-release-by-end-of-2027-or-first-half-of-2028)).

Lionsgate is the third studio Fogelson has led, after chairmanships at Universal Pictures and the STX Motion Picture Group ([Boxoffice Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.com/joe-drake-hands-lionsgate-motion-picture-group-chair-to-adam-fogelson/)). He joined Lionsgate as vice chair of its Motion Picture Group in September 2022, reporting to Joe Drake, and was named chair in December 2023 ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/adam-fogelson-exits-stx-vice-chair-lionsgate-motion-picture-group/); [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/lionsgate-adam-fogelson-motion-picture-group-chair/)). In 2012 and again in 2013, under his leadership, Universal celebrated its highest-grossing years at the worldwide box office in the company's over 100-year history at that time — a record that is the clearest explanation for why Lionsgate handed him its film group and, with it, the *Michael* follow-up ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/adam-fogelson-exits-stx-vice-chair-lionsgate-motion-picture-group/)).

## The earnings call that sketched Michael 2

On the August 2026 earnings call, Fogelson said the studio hopes to begin production on the *Michael* sequel in late 2026 or early 2027, "with the hopes of releasing the final product at the end of next year or in the first half of 2028," per Variety's reporting of the call ([ANI](https://www.manilametro.com/news/279225675/michael-2-eyes-late-2026-or-early-2027-production-start-targets-release-by-end-of-2027-or-first-half-of-2028)). The key production detail was thrift: "We have a number of sequences, particularly some big musical sequences, that were shot previously, that are almost sure to be incorporated," he said — material cut from the first film being recycled into the follow-up ([ANI](https://www.manilametro.com/news/279225675/michael-2-eyes-late-2026-or-early-2027-production-start-targets-release-by-end-of-2027-or-first-half-of-2028)).

"We are mostly focused right now on how to make sure we can deliver, at the right price, the biggest, best sequel. That is what the audience is going to want and deserve after the experience we gave them the first time," he added, while declining to give budget guidance ([ANI](https://www.manilametro.com/news/279225675/michael-2-eyes-late-2026-or-early-2027-production-start-targets-release-by-end-of-2027-or-first-half-of-2028)). One honest qualifier: the studio had not officially greenlit the sequel as of the call — Fogelson's dates are stated targets, not a greenlight ([ANI](https://www.manilametro.com/news/279225675/michael-2-eyes-late-2026-or-early-2027-production-start-targets-release-by-end-of-2027-or-first-half-of-2028)).

## Universal, STX, Lionsgate: three chairmanships

Fogelson's career is a study in scaling film businesses. He joined Universal in 1998 as vice president of creative advertising, rose to President of Marketing and Distribution in 2007, and became chairman of Universal Pictures in 2009 — overseeing worldwide theatrical, home entertainment, partnerships, licensing, finance and communications ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/adam-fogelson-exits-stx-vice-chair-lionsgate-motion-picture-group/)). The 2012 and 2013 record years came with releases including *Ted*, *The Purge*, *Pitch Perfect* and the *Despicable Me* and *Fast & Furious* franchises — and greenlights for *Jurassic World*, *Neighbors* and *Fifty Shades of Grey* ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/adam-fogelson-exits-stx-vice-chair-lionsgate-motion-picture-group/); [Boxoffice Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.com/joe-drake-hands-lionsgate-motion-picture-group-chair-to-adam-fogelson/)). He graduated from Stanford University in 1989 with a BA in Communication and lives in Los Angeles with his wife and two daughters ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/adam-fogelson-exits-stx-vice-chair-lionsgate-motion-picture-group/)).

After departing Universal in October 2013, he chaired STX Entertainment's Motion Picture Group for eight years, overseeing *Hustlers*, the *Bad Moms* franchise, *The Upside*, *Greenland*, *The Gift*, *Molly's Game* and *The Gentlemen* ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/adam-fogelson-exits-stx-vice-chair-lionsgate-motion-picture-group/)). Lionsgate CEO Jon Feltheimer called him "one of Hollywood's most accomplished and innovative film executives" ([Boxoffice Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.com/joe-drake-hands-lionsgate-motion-picture-group-chair-to-adam-fogelson/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is Adam Fogelson?**
He is the Chair of Lionsgate's Motion Picture Group, the executive who oversees the studio's film slate. He previously chaired Universal Pictures (2009–2013) and STX Entertainment's Motion Picture Group (for eight years), making Lionsgate the third studio he has led.

**What did he say about the Michael sequel?**
On Lionsgate's August 2026 earnings call, Fogelson said the studio is targeting a sequel production start in late 2026 or early 2027 for release in late 2027 or the first half of 2028, reusing musical sequences shot for — but cut from — the first film. He declined to give budget guidance.

**Has the Michael sequel been officially greenlit?**
Not as of the August 2026 earnings call: ANI's report of Variety's coverage notes the studio had not officially greenlit the sequel at that point, even though the first film ends with "His Story Continues." Fogelson's statements are stated targets rather than a greenlight.

**What were his biggest successes at Universal?**
In 2012 and 2013, Universal had its highest-grossing years at the worldwide box office in the company's then-100-plus-year history, with hits including *Despicable Me*, *Ted*, *Pitch Perfect*, *Fast Five*, *Fast & Furious 6* and *Les Misérables* — and greenlights for *Jurassic World* and *Fifty Shades of Grey*.

**How did he come to Lionsgate?**
He joined in September 2022 as Vice Chair of the Motion Picture Group, reporting to then-chair Joe Drake and overseeing worldwide marketing and theatrical distribution. When Drake initiated a leadership transition, Fogelson was named chair in December 2023.
