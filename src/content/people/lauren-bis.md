Lauren Bis is a White House spokeswoman who arrived at the job through one of the Trump administration's most combative communications shops. By September 2026, she was the administration's on-the-record voice on two of its hardest stories: rebutting claims about Donald Trump and the Jeffrey Epstein files, and selling a nationwide push to scrub noncitizens from voter rolls ([The Independent](https://www.newswav.com/article/vance-responds-to-trump-criticism-of-israel-and-raises-concerns-about-musk-A2630061); [New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/24/us-news/white-house-pushes-purge-of-non-citizens-from-voter-rolls-ahead-of-midterms-report/)). She got there fast: in February 2026 she was running public affairs for the Department of Homeland Security, a job she had been promoted into from her deputy's post when the department's previous top spokesperson left ([NPR](https://www.knkx.org/2026-02-17/top-dhs-spokesperson-who-became-a-face-of-trump-immigration-policy-is-leaving)).

## From election-integrity communications to DHS public affairs chief

Before joining the administration, Bis was the director of communications at the Public Interest Legal Foundation (PILF), a law firm devoted to election-integrity litigation ([Public Interest Legal Foundation](https://publicinterestlegal.org/press/public-interest-legal-foundation-former-director-of-communications-promoted-to-assistant-secretary-for-public-affairs-at-u-s-department-of-homeland-security/)). PILF's announcement of her February 2026 promotion said that during her tenure the organization secured national media coverage across print, radio, and broadcast television, and that she "helped elevate complex legal and policy issues related to election integrity for a broad public audience" ([Public Interest Legal Foundation](https://publicinterestlegal.org/press/public-interest-legal-foundation-former-director-of-communications-promoted-to-assistant-secretary-for-public-affairs-at-u-s-department-of-homeland-security/)).

She entered DHS as a deputy to Tricia McLaughlin, the assistant secretary for public affairs who became the public face of the administration's mass-deportation policy ([NPR](https://www.knkx.org/2026-02-17/top-dhs-spokesperson-who-became-a-face-of-trump-immigration-policy-is-leaving)). When McLaughlin left in February 2026 — she had delayed a planned December departure to stay on after federal immigration officers shot and killed Renee Good and Alex Pretti — McLaughlin's statement named Bis as her replacement as assistant secretary for public affairs, with Katie Zacharia becoming deputy ([NPR](https://www.knkx.org/2026-02-17/top-dhs-spokesperson-who-became-a-face-of-trump-immigration-policy-is-leaving)).

## The September 2026 Epstein rebuttals

Bis's first major on-the-record moments as a White House spokeswoman came on the Epstein story. Chris Whipple's September 16, 2026 [Vanity Fair](/organizations/vanity-fair/) profile of [JD Vance](/people/jd-vance/) reported [Tucker Carlson](/people/tucker-carlson/) saying "the order to cover it up came from Trump" regarding the Epstein files ([The Independent](https://www.newswav.com/article/vance-responds-to-trump-criticism-of-israel-and-raises-concerns-about-musk-A2630061)). Bis's response, as reported by the Independent, was a full-throated rebuttal: "Just as President Trump has said, he's been totally exonerated on anything relating to Epstein. And by releasing thousands of pages of documents, cooperating with the House Oversight Committee's subpoena request, signing the Epstein Files Transparency Act, and calling for more investigations into Epstein's Democrat friends, President Trump has done more for Epstein's victims than anyone before him" ([The Independent](https://www.newswav.com/article/vance-responds-to-trump-criticism-of-israel-and-raises-concerns-about-musk-A2630061)). Notably, the White House steered reporters to Trump's April Truth Social post on the matter rather than issuing a direct denial of Carlson's account ([The Independent](https://www.newswav.com/article/vance-responds-to-trump-criticism-of-israel-and-raises-concerns-about-musk-A2630061)).

The same week, she handled a smaller but viral version of the story. A fake claim was spreading that Trump had visited Epstein's island 345 times, and her September 24, 2026 email — published in a Lead Stories fact-check — answered it: "As the President has said, he NEVER visited Epstein's Island…" ([Lead Stories](https://leadstories.com/hoax-alert/2026/09/fact-check-trump-did-not-visit-epstein-island-345-times.html)).

## The voter-integrity portfolio

Her PILF background made election messaging a natural portfolio. On September 24, 2026, the New York Post identified her as "White House spokeswoman Lauren Bis" in its report on the administration's "Unlawful Voter Initiative," which had DHS reviewing voter rolls from at least 47 states ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/24/us-news/white-house-pushes-purge-of-non-citizens-from-voter-rolls-ahead-of-midterms-report/)). Her quote set the tone: "The real story here is after decades of the media denying that non-citizens register and vote in our elections, they are finally being forced to admit after a flurry of recent arrests nationwide that it does happen," she said, adding that "Democrats must stop refusing to pass the SAVE America Act—commonsense legislation that ensures Americans, and Americans only, decide our elections" ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/24/us-news/white-house-pushes-purge-of-non-citizens-from-voter-rolls-ahead-of-midterms-report/)).

Fox News Digital carried her as the White House voice on a slate of federal election-fraud prosecutions spanning California, Kansas, Louisiana, Texas, and Massachusetts: "Despite extreme Democrats and the media claiming it never happens, the evidence of non-citizens registering and voting in our elections continues to pile up," she told the outlet. "Any non-citizens found registering and voting in our elections will be arrested and held accountable. Only Americans should be electing American leaders" ([Fox News Digital](https://foxnews.com/politics/skid-row-forgeries-illegal-votes-mayoral-candidate-election-fraud-cases-pile-up-nationwide)).
