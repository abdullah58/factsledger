## Overview

Kelly Johnson is the wife of U.S. House Speaker [Mike Johnson](/people/mike-johnson/), whom she married in 1999. Beyond that relationship, she is the lead in her own career story: a former teacher and licensed pastoral counselor who, for about a year and a half, sat opposite her husband on a weekly faith podcast — a professional life that the content-farm profiles almost never organize into a throughline, even though it is the most documented part of her biography ([Parade](https://parade.com/celebrities/speaker-mike-johnson-wife-kelly-lary); [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

Born Kelly Lary, she met Mike Johnson on May 2, 1998, at a mutual friend's wedding — Johnson later said the red dress she was wearing caught his eye — and the two married on May 1, 1999 ([Parade](https://parade.com/celebrities/speaker-mike-johnson-wife-kelly-lary)). Their marriage is a covenant marriage, a Louisiana legal status that requires premarital counseling and sharply restricts the grounds for divorce; Louisiana is one of only three states to recognize it, alongside Arkansas and Arizona ([Parade](https://parade.com/celebrities/speaker-mike-johnson-wife-kelly-lary)).

Her career began in education. She earned a degree in elementary education from Louisiana Tech University and taught at Providence Classical Academy, an evangelical elementary school, after marrying; she later became a licensed pastoral counselor ([Parade](https://parade.com/celebrities/speaker-mike-johnson-wife-kelly-lary)). She also worked with Louisiana Right to Life, the state's main anti-abortion group ([Parade](https://parade.com/celebrities/speaker-mike-johnson-wife-kelly-lary)). From March 2022 through October 2023 she co-hosted *Truth Be Told with Mike & Kelly Johnson*, a podcast built around faith and family ([Parade](https://parade.com/celebrities/speaker-mike-johnson-wife-kelly-lary); [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

The Johnsons have four children — Hannah, Abigail, Jack and Will ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). They also took custody of a young man named Michael at age 14 in 1999 under a legal guardianship arrangement; he was never formally adopted, a detail confirmed to Newsweek by the speaker's communications director ([Parade](https://parade.com/celebrities/speaker-mike-johnson-wife-kelly-lary)).
