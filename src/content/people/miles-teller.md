**Miles Teller is an American actor who plays entertainment attorney [John Branca](/people/john-branca/) in [Antoine Fuqua](/people/antoine-fuqua/)'s 2026 [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) biopic [*Michael*](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/), where Branca — Jackson's former attorney, manager, and co-executor of his estate — appears as a central real-life figure** ([JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/miles-teller-john-branca-michael-jackson/); [Cinema Online](https://Cinema.com.my/articles/news_details.aspx?search=2024.n_milestellerjoinsmjbiopic_66004)). Teller is best known for his breakout as an aspiring jazz drummer in the Oscar-winning drama *Whiplash* (2014) and for playing Lt. Bradley "Rooster" Bradshaw, Goose's son, in *Top Gun: Maverick* (2022) ([NBC Sports](https://www.nbcsports.com/american-century-championship/miles-teller); [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/etimes/trending/snl-host-miles-teller-net-worth-how-rich-is-top-gun-maverick-star/articleshow/125029085.cms)).

Born 20 February 1987 in Downingtown, Pennsylvania, Teller earned a Bachelor of Fine Arts from New York University's Tisch School of the Arts before his 2010 film debut in *Rabbit Hole* opposite Nicole Kidman, Aaron Eckhart and Dianne Wiest ([Tribute.ca](https://www.tribute.ca/people/biography/miles-teller/36098/); [NBC Sports](https://www.nbcsports.com/american-century-championship/miles-teller)). *Top Gun: Maverick* became Tom Cruise's first film to cross $1 billion worldwide and eventually earned nearly $1.5 billion globally ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/entertainment/tom-cruise-digger-worst-box-office-opening-20-years)) — a scale that helps explain why a 2025 court filing by Paris Jackson described Teller as the "sole A-list actor" in *Michael*'s cast ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/music/news/legal-dispute-surrounding-michael-jacksons-estate-from-the-legends-demise-to-paris-jacksons-legal-win/articleshow/131093940.cms)).

## Playing John Branca

Lionsgate confirmed Teller's casting as John Branca in February 2024, with producer [Graham King](/people/graham-king/) — the Oscar-winning producer of *Bohemian Rhapsody*, *The Departed* and *The Aviator* — calling him "a compelling screen presence, whom I've admired since watching him in *Whiplash*," and "the perfect actor to capture John's enigmatic persona" ([JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/miles-teller-john-branca-michael-jackson/)). Director Antoine Fuqua said Teller's "finely tuned skills as an actor are up to the challenge of playing a man who is being examined for the pivotal role he played in Michael Jackson's life" ([JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/miles-teller-john-branca-michael-jackson/)).

Branca, as the casting coverage describes him, is an entertainment lawyer and manager who helped Jackson transition from the Jackson 5 era to solo superstardom, was instrumental in the *Thriller* short film, and is co-executor of the estate — producing the biopic alongside fellow executor [John McClain](/people/john-mcclain/) ([Cinema Online](https://Cinema.com.my/articles/news_details.aspx?search=2024.n_milestellerjoinsmjbiopic_66004)). To play him, Teller met the real Branca before filming — "I've gotten to hang out with him a little bit," he told *Variety* in March 2024 — and first-look photos showed him nearly unrecognizable under a long wig and prosthetic nose ([Parade](https://parade.com/news/miles-teller-is-unrecognizable-in-new-michael-jackson-biopic)).

## From indie breakout to blockbuster redemption

Teller's career reads as a jagged arc: indie breakout, franchise stumble, cultural redemption. After the Tisch BFA and a 2009 television debut in ABC's *The Unusuals*, he played Willard Hewitt in the 2011 *Footloose* remake, then earned critical acclaim for *The Spectacular Now* (2013) and joined the three-film *Divergent* series opposite Shailene Woodley ([Tribute.ca](https://www.tribute.ca/people/biography/miles-teller/36098/); [NBC Sports](https://www.nbcsports.com/american-century-championship/miles-teller)). *Whiplash* made him the face of artistic suffering — and then *Fantastic Four* (2015), which should have been his blockbuster breakthrough, "became a career detour into mediocrity," as the *Times of India* put it ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/etimes/trending/snl-host-miles-teller-net-worth-how-rich-is-top-gun-maverick-star/articleshow/125029085.cms)).

He rebuilt in blue-collar, working-men roles: *War Dogs* (2016), *Bleed for This* (2016), firefighter Brendan McDonough in *Only the Brave* (2017), and Army sergeant Adam Schumann in *Thank You for Your Service* (2017) ([NBC Sports](https://www.nbcsports.com/american-century-championship/miles-teller); [Tribute.ca](https://www.tribute.ca/people/biography/miles-teller/36098/)). By *Top Gun: Maverick* he had, in the *Times of India*'s phrasing, "matured into the kind of actor who could match Tom Cruise's manic energy with grounded realism," giving the film its emotional gravity as the legacy-haunted Rooster ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/etimes/trending/snl-host-miles-teller-net-worth-how-rich-is-top-gun-maverick-star/articleshow/125029085.cms)). Off-screen, he has been married to Keleigh Sperry since 2019; in 2025 he hosted *Saturday Night Live* for a second time and lost his home in the Palisades wildfire ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/etimes/trending/snl-host-miles-teller-net-worth-how-rich-is-top-gun-maverick-star/articleshow/125029085.cms)).

## A casting contested in court

Not everyone welcomed the Branca casting. In a November 2025 filing, Paris Jackson — Jackson's daughter and an estate beneficiary — raised concerns over Teller playing Branca, writing: "It appears that Mr Branca used his position as an Executive Producer, a role he has never before performed in connection with any dramatic feature film, to cast the sole A-list actor in the production, Miles Teller, to play himself in the upcoming feature production Michael" ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/music/news/legal-dispute-surrounding-michael-jacksons-estate-from-the-legends-demise-to-paris-jacksons-legal-win/articleshow/131093940.cms)). The objection was part of a broader dispute: she alleged irregular payments and "skimming" by executors John Branca and John McClain, who have managed the estate since 2009 ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/music/news/legal-dispute-surrounding-michael-jacksons-estate-from-the-legends-demise-to-paris-jacksons-legal-win/articleshow/131093940.cms)).

The estate's response, as reported, characterized her claims as baseless, and in November 2025 a Los Angeles judge, Mitchell L. Beckloff, ruled against her petitions ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/music/news/legal-dispute-surrounding-michael-jacksons-estate-from-the-legends-demise-to-paris-jacksons-legal-win/articleshow/131093940.cms)). On 13 May 2026, she won an appeal on a separate matter: $625,000 in bonus payments made to law firms in the second half of 2018 were disallowed and ordered returned to the estate ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/music/news/legal-dispute-surrounding-michael-jacksons-estate-from-the-legends-demise-to-paris-jacksons-legal-win/articleshow/131093940.cms)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who does Miles Teller play in Michael?**
Teller plays John Branca, Michael Jackson's entertainment attorney and manager who became co-executor of the estate with John McClain after Jackson's 2009 death. Lionsgate announced the casting in February 2024, with producer Graham King and director Antoine Fuqua both praising Teller as the right actor to capture Branca's "enigmatic persona."

**What is Miles Teller known for?**
His breakout was the 2014 drama *Whiplash*, followed by a career dip with *Fantastic Four* (2015) and a blockbuster resurgence as Lt. Bradley "Rooster" Bradshaw — Goose's son — in *Top Gun: Maverick* (2022). His earlier work includes *Rabbit Hole*, *The Spectacular Now* and the *Divergent* trilogy.

**How did Teller prepare to play John Branca?**
He met the real Branca before filming, telling *Variety* in March 2024 that preparation means trying to "inundate yourself with as much information as you can." First-look set photos from late 2025 showed him transformed with a long wig and prosthetic nose for the role.

**Why did Paris Jackson object to his casting?**
In a November 2025 court filing she alleged Branca used his executive-producer position to cast "the sole A-list actor in the production, Miles Teller, to play himself," as part of a broader dispute over estate finances. The estate called her claims baseless; a judge ruled against her petitions that November.

**Where did Miles Teller train as an actor?**
He earned a Bachelor of Fine Arts from the Tisch School of the Arts at New York University, then debuted on television in 2009 in ABC's *The Unusuals* before his 2010 film debut in *Rabbit Hole* opposite Nicole Kidman.
