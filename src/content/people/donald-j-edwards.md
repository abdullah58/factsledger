# Donald J. Edwards

**Donald J. Edwards is a Chicago private-equity investor who served as
President and CEO of Liberté Investors Inc. from July 2002 to April
2004 — leading its $76-million-plus-stock acquisition of USAuto Holdings
before handing the CEO role to Stephen J. Harrison.** He founded the
Chicago private-equity firm Flexpoint Ford in 2004 and served on the
University of Illinois Board of Trustees from 2017 to 2025, including
six years as chairman.

## Liberté and the USAuto acquisition

Edwards ran Liberté Investors as it pivoted from a real-estate-era
legacy into insurance. On December 15, 2003, he led the company's
announcement of a definitive merger agreement to acquire USAuto Holdings
— a Tennessee non-standard auto insurer — for $76 million in cash plus
13.25 million shares. The deal's design named his own succession:
USAuto's Stephen J. Harrison would replace him as CEO while Edwards
stayed a director with a four-year advisory relationship.

He resigned as President and CEO effective on the merger's closing,
April 30, 2004. The separation agreement gave him a $1,659,000
lump-sum payout, medical and dental coverage through July 1, 2007, the
Chicago office lease (676 North Michigan Avenue, Suite 3300) assigned to
his new entity Edwards Capital, LLC, and full vesting of his 2,573,678
stock options, exercisable until July 9, 2012.

## Flexpoint Ford and the University of Illinois

Edwards founded Flexpoint Ford, a Chicago-based private-equity firm, in
2004. In 2017 Governor Bruce Rauner appointed him to the University of
Illinois Board of Trustees. An Illinois graduate himself — B.S. in
finance with Bronze Tablet honors, a four-year letter-winner on the
Illini golf team (1985–1988), and a Harvard MBA as a Baker Scholar — he
served eight years on the board, six of them as chairman, retiring in
2025.
