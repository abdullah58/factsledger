## Overview

Eron — full name Eronildo dos Santos Rocha — is a Brazilian footballer, a forward born on 16 July 1998 in Salvador, Bahia ([Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/esportes/noticia/67540-revelado-pelo-vitoria-eron-e-negociado-para-o-futebol-japones-apos-artilharia-da-serie-d)). He is best known for a remarkable 2023 season with Caxias, in which he finished as the top scorer of the Campeonato Brasileiro Série D with 14 goals — the most any player has scored in a single edition of the competition — and then moved to Japan to play for [Vegalta Sendai](/organizations/vegalta-sendai/) in the J2 League ([Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/esportes/noticia/66489-autor-do-gol-do-acesso-do-caxias-eron-se-isola-como-maior-artilheiro-de-uma-edicao-da-serie-d)). His 2024 season in Japan and his subsequent listing in Santa Cruz FC's 2026 Copa do Brasil squad are covered in detail below ([Vegalta Sendai supporters' association preview](http://www.vegalta-sa.org/tour2024/2024_No6_VVE-26-Mito.pdf); [ESPN](https://www.espn.ph/football/team/squad/_/id/4929/league/BRA.COPA_DO_BRAZIL)).

A note on the name: this profile covers only Eronildo dos Santos Rocha, the forward born in 1998 whose clubs include Caxias, Vegalta Sendai and Santa Cruz. An older, unrelated Brazilian player — Eron Santos Lourenço, born in 1992, a former Atlético Mineiro left-back — has played under the same mononym, and the two should not be confused ([Eron (footballer, born 1992)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eron_(footballer,_born_1992))).

## The 2023 breakthrough: Série D scoring record with Caxias

Eron came through the youth system of EC Vitória in his home state of Bahia ([Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/esportes/noticia/67540-revelado-pelo-vitoria-eron-e-negociado-para-o-futebol-japones-apos-artilharia-da-serie-d)). By 2023 he was playing for Caxias in the Campeonato Brasileiro Série D. During the season he scored in bursts — at one stage he struck twice in a single match to take over the competition's scoring lead while Caxias closed in on qualification ([Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/esportes/noticia/65933-eron-faz-mais-dois-gols-assume-artilharia-da-serie-d-e-caxias-encaminha-classificacao)). The defining moment came against Portuguesa-RJ, when his goal secured the club's promotion; he finished the campaign with 14 goals, the most by any player in a single Série D edition ([Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/esportes/noticia/66489-autor-do-gol-do-acesso-do-caxias-eron-se-isola-como-maior-artilheiro-de-uma-edicao-da-serie-d)).

## 2024: the season in Japan with Vegalta Sendai

In December 2023, following his scoring title, Eron transferred from Caxias to Vegalta Sendai of Japan's J2 League ([Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/esportes/noticia/67540-revelado-pelo-vitoria-eron-e-negociado-para-o-futebol-japones-apos-artilharia-da-serie-d)). A match preview published by the Vegalta Sendai supporters' association on 10 August 2024 lists him in the squad as "エロン" — No. 98, a forward, 176 cm and 76 kg, whose previous club was Caxias ([preview](http://www.vegalta-sa.org/tour2024/2024_No6_VVE-26-Mito.pdf)). The same preview placed him in the predicted starting eleven for that day's league match away to Mito HollyHock, paired with Yuta Goke in the predicted front two ([preview](http://www.vegalta-sa.org/tour2024/2024_No6_VVE-26-Mito.pdf)). Its statistics section, compiled from official J.League data, credited him with one assist at the 25-matchday mark of the season, at a point when Sendai sat sixth in the J2 table with 41 points from 25 matches ([preview](http://www.vegalta-sa.org/tour2024/2024_No6_VVE-26-Mito.pdf)).

After the season, his time in Japan came to an end: BeSoccer reported that Eron ended his Vegalta Sendai contract and became a free agent ([BeSoccer](https://www.besoccer.com/player/eronildo-a-296004)).

## 2026: Santa Cruz FC

Eron's next confirmed stop was Santa Cruz FC. ESPN lists a player named "Eronildo" — a 28-year-old Brazilian forward, 1.78 m and 73 kg — in the club's squad for the 2026 Copa do Brasil ([ESPN](https://www.espn.ph/football/team/squad/_/id/4929/league/BRA.COPA_DO_BRAZIL)). The age, nationality and position are consistent with the player covered in this profile.
