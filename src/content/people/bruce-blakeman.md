## Overview

On August 14, 2026, at the Nassau County Police Academy in Garden City, newly confirmed U.S. Attorney General [Todd Blanche](/people/todd-blanche/) — sworn in four days earlier — identified himself onstage as "attorney general of the United States" and praised "our great Nassau County Executive Bruce Blakeman." President Donald Trump then urged the crowd to elect Blakeman governor of New York while attacking Governor Kathy Hochul by name ([The Steady State](https://steadystate1.substack.com/p/the-steady-state-files-hatch-act)). Within weeks, the appearance had drawn two Hatch Act filings: a September 4 complaint to the U.S. Office of Special Counsel from The Steady State, an organization of more than 430 former national security officials, plus a related Inspector General referral; and a [Public Citizen](/organizations/public-citizen/) complaint reported by the [Associated Press](https://factsledger.com/organizations/associated-press/) later in September ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/attorney-general-blanches-appearances-at-political-events-blur-justice-department-boundaries/)). The AP described the event as "overwhelmingly a political one" ([The Steady State](https://steadystate1.substack.com/p/the-steady-state-files-hatch-act)).

That episode is the most nationally consequential public moment of Blakeman's career — which is why this profile opens with it rather than the conventional résumé. The conventional résumé runs like this: Bruce Blakeman, an attorney born in Oceanside and raised in Valley Stream, was elected the 10th Nassau County executive on November 2, 2021, becoming the county's first Jewish county executive ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive); [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-news/bruce-blakeman-s-family-and-net-worth-what-to-know-about-the-new-york-governor-candidate-11765298377185.html)). He won a second term in November 2025 and, in December 2025, announced a run for governor of New York, carrying President Trump's "100% MAGA" label into the race ([Associated Press](https://www.wamc.org/2025-12-09/long-island-republican-bruce-blakeman-is-running-for-new-york-governor-by-anthony-izaguirre)).

## The August 14 event and the ethics complaints

The event was billed as an official White House appearance on violent-crime reduction — but the guest list told a different story. Alongside Trump, Blanche and Blakeman, three sitting members of Congress and two Republican House nominees spoke ([The Steady State](https://steadystate1.substack.com/p/the-steady-state-files-hatch-act)). The Steady State's complaint asks the Office of Special Counsel to determine whether Blanche and FBI Director Kash Patel used their official authority in a way the Hatch Act forbids, and whether appropriated funds paid for the political component of the event; the group also referred the matter to the department's Inspector General and transmitted its filings to the Criminal Division's Public Integrity Section — with the pointed request that any criminal evaluation proceed under recusal procedures "because the Attorney General is himself a subject" ([The Steady State](https://steadystate1.substack.com/p/the-steady-state-files-hatch-act)).

Blanche has brushed the criticism aside. "I will always say yes to speak when the president of the United States asks me to," he told the AP, adding: "I was there purely thanking cops, law enforcement, for doing their jobs. The idea that that's turned into somehow criticism is laughable." He said he was not endorsing candidates but "talking about the great work that President Trump is doing" ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/attorney-general-blanches-appearances-at-political-events-blur-justice-department-boundaries/)). The Justice Department said Blanche's appearances at political events are made in his personal capacity, are reviewed and cleared by the department's ethics officials, and that travel expenses are reimbursed by the Attorney General in accordance with federal law ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/attorney-general-blanches-appearances-at-political-events-blur-justice-department-boundaries/)). The AP noted that attorneys general have typically stayed away from political rallies to protect the perception of impartiality in prosecutions — and that the current administration had rescinded the tighter Biden-era restrictions on such appearances ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/attorney-general-blanches-appearances-at-political-events-blur-justice-department-boundaries/)). For Blakeman, the event cemented his national role in the story: he is the candidate at the center of the most prominent Hatch Act dispute of the 2026 midterms.

## Rise to county executive

Blakeman's political career began decades before the national spotlight found him. He served as a Hempstead Town Councilman from 2015 to 2021, voting to cut property taxes in multiple years and backing budgets his office says earned the town among the highest Wall Street credit ratings available ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive)). He earlier served in the Nassau County Legislature — including as its Presiding Officer — where, by his office's account, he cut $40 million in spending from a predecessor's budget, reduced salaries of political appointees, preserved open space, and established a breast cancer resource center called Hewlett House ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive)).

In the aftermath of 9/11, New York's governor appointed Blakeman a commissioner of the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey, where his office says he worked to secure the region's airports, bridges, tunnels, ports and facilities ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive)). He was elected county executive on November 2, 2021, defeating the Democratic incumbent ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive)). Blakeman — a lifelong member of the Jewish community, affiliated with the Jewish Center of Atlantic Beach, a Modern Orthodox synagogue — became the county's first Jewish county executive ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-news/bruce-blakeman-s-family-and-net-worth-what-to-know-about-the-new-york-governor-candidate-11765298377185.html)). He lives in Atlantic Beach with his wife, Segal ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive)).

## The county executive years

Blakeman's tenure has been defined by a combative style of local governance aimed at national talking points. According to his office, he kept his promise to eliminate $150 million in tax hikes planned by the prior administration, and fiscal restraints during his term helped earn the county multiple credit-rating upgrades ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive)). On public safety, his office says he has sworn in more than 200 new police officers and launched Operation Overwatch, a roving patrol unit of police cars serving as a crime deterrent ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive)).

He has signed a string of executive orders that pushed county government into culture-war territory. One ensured that, in his words, Nassau would "never become a Sanctuary County" and not spend tax dollars on services for people who entered the country illegally ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive)). Another restored, in his framing, the right of parents to send children to school without masks — described by his office as the first such order in New York State ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive)). A third banned transgender athletes from county-owned sports facilities, which critics labeled as targeting ([Associated Press](https://www.wamc.org/2025-12-09/long-island-republican-bruce-blakeman-is-running-for-new-york-governor-by-anthony-izaguirre)). A separate local law banning the wearing of face masks in public, except for health or religious reasons, drew criticism as aimed at pro-Palestine demonstrations ([Associated Press](https://www.wamc.org/2025-12-09/long-island-republican-bruce-blakeman-is-running-for-new-york-governor-by-anthony-izaguirre)). He also created a volunteer law enforcement unit that his critics called a "militia" and directed county detectives to work alongside federal authorities in the Trump administration's immigration crackdown ([Associated Press](https://www.wamc.org/2025-12-09/long-island-republican-bruce-blakeman-is-running-for-new-york-governor-by-anthony-izaguirre)).

On antisemitism, he organized what his office describes as the region's largest rally in support of Israel — more than 6,000 people — and formed a bipartisan task force to combat antisemitism on Long Island ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive)). His office cites Regional Planning Council figures showing Long Island sending $14 billion more in taxpayer funds to Albany than it receives back — a number that doubles as his argument for a bigger share of school and road funds ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive)).

## The 2026 governor's race

Blakeman announced his run for governor on December 9, 2025, appearing on Fox News's *Fox and Friends* and calling Hochul a "failure" who needed to be replaced ([Associated Press](https://www.wamc.org/2025-12-09/long-island-republican-bruce-blakeman-is-running-for-new-york-governor-by-anthony-izaguirre)). The announcement initially set up a primary clash with fellow Trump ally Representative Elise Stefanik — though Trump himself declined to take sides at first, saying "He's great, and she's great. They're both great people" ([Associated Press](https://www.wamc.org/2025-12-09/long-island-republican-bruce-blakeman-is-running-for-new-york-governor-by-anthony-izaguirre)). By September 2026, the AP was describing Blakeman as "the Trump-endorsed Republican challenging Democratic Gov. Kathy Hochul" ([Associated Press](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/articles/associated-press-new-mexico/attorney-general-blanches-appearances-at-political-events-blur-justice-department-boundaries/)).

The Hochul campaign has tried to define him by his past defeats, issuing a statement saying he "has lost just about every race he's touched — county legislator, comptroller, Congress, even U.S. Senate," and charging that "just like Donald Trump, he takes money out of New Yorkers' pockets and squeezes working families at every turn" ([Associated Press](https://www.wamc.org/2025-12-09/long-island-republican-bruce-blakeman-is-running-for-new-york-governor-by-anthony-izaguirre)). The statement is campaign rhetoric, not a verified race-by-race record, but it points at a real pattern: bigger elective offices had eluded him before 2021, and New York has not elected a Republican governor since George Pataki, who left office about two decades ago ([Associated Press](https://www.wamc.org/2025-12-09/long-island-republican-bruce-blakeman-is-running-for-new-york-governor-by-anthony-izaguirre)).

## Family, law practice and an ex-Beatle

The most widely reported fact about Blakeman's private life is the one he finds strangest himself: his ex-wife is married to a Beatle. "It's a little surrealistic that your ex-wife is married to a Beatle. I'm not going to lie," he said on a September 2026 podcast, adding that he and Sir Paul McCartney never talk politics ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/20/us-news/bruce-blakeman-admits-its-surreal-his-ex-married-paul-mccartney/)). Blakeman was married to Nancy Shevell — a trucking executive at her family's hauling firm who previously served on the Metropolitan Transportation Authority board — for 23 years before an amicable divorce ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/20/us-news/bruce-blakeman-admits-its-surreal-his-ex-married-paul-mccartney/)). Their son is Arlen Blakeman; McCartney, he says, is a "nice guy" and a good stepfather, and spoke at Arlen's 2019 graduation ceremony from Cardozo Law School, calling him "my handsome and lovely Arlen Blakeman" ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/20/us-news/bruce-blakeman-admits-its-surreal-his-ex-married-paul-mccartney/)).

Blakeman remarried in 2015 to Segal Magori Blakeman, a Nassau County Family Court judge, calling her "a great woman" who is "smart and beautiful" ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/20/us-news/bruce-blakeman-admits-its-surreal-his-ex-married-paul-mccartney/); [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-news/bruce-blakeman-s-family-and-net-worth-what-to-know-about-the-new-york-governor-candidate-11765298377185.html)). He is stepfather to her two children, Ariel and Avi, and has three grandchildren ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/09/20/us-news/bruce-blakeman-admits-its-surreal-his-ex-married-paul-mccartney/)).

Beyond elected office, Blakeman is a working attorney — admitted to practice before the U.S. Supreme Court, the New York and New Jersey courts, the Fourth Circuit, and the federal district courts for the Southern and Eastern Districts of New York — with degrees from Arizona State University and California Western School of Law ([Nassau County](https://www.nassaucountyny.gov/481/County-Executive)). His most notable private-sector transaction was a major 2007 land deal that, according to reporting, brought him a sizable commission ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-news/bruce-blakeman-s-family-and-net-worth-what-to-know-about-the-new-york-governor-candidate-11765298377185.html)). Published estimates of his net worth — around $4 million in some outlets — have no officially confirmed figure behind them; his known county salaries were $211,821 in 2022–2023 and $227,959 in 2024 ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-news/bruce-blakeman-s-family-and-net-worth-what-to-know-about-the-new-york-governor-candidate-11765298377185.html)).
