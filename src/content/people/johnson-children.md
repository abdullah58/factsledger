[Mike Johnson](/people/mike-johnson) and his wife Kelly are the parents of four children — Hannah, Abigail, Jack and Will — and have an older son, Michael, who became part of their family in 1999, the same year the couple married. [NAB](https://www.nab.org/sites/2024slc/mikeJohnson.asp) This profile keeps deliberately to the household structure: the public record corrects the common shorthand of "five children" by distinguishing the four biological children from Michael, whom the Johnsons took in rather than adopted. [NAB](https://www.nab.org/sites/2024slc/mikeJohnson.asp) [The List](https://www.thelist.com/1435649/who-is-house-speaker-mike-johnson-son-michael/)

According to Johnson's own biographical text, hosted by the National Association of Broadcasters, he and Kelly "have four children, Hannah, Abigail, Jack and Will, and an older son Michael, who became a part of their family in 1999," and the family resides in Bossier Parish, Louisiana. [NAB](https://www.nab.org/sites/2024slc/mikeJohnson.asp) The 1999 date matches their wedding year — they married in a covenant marriage the same year they took Michael in. [The List](https://www.thelist.com/1435649/who-is-house-speaker-mike-johnson-son-michael/)

Michael's position in the family became a public question when Johnson was elected speaker in October 2023 and Michael appeared in neither the family portraits nor the official biography. Johnson's communications director, Corinne Day, told Newsweek that the Johnsons took Michael in as newlyweds when he was 14. [The List](https://www.thelist.com/1435649/who-is-house-speaker-mike-johnson-son-michael/) She explained that the Johnsons never formally adopted him — while Johnson calls Michael his son and does not correct people who describe him as "adopted." [The List](https://www.thelist.com/1435649/who-is-house-speaker-mike-johnson-son-michael/)

Day's statement also gave the reason for Michael's absence from public life: when Johnson first ran for Congress in 2016, Michael was already an adult with a family of his own, and "he asked not to be involved in their new public life." [The List](https://www.thelist.com/1435649/who-is-house-speaker-mike-johnson-son-michael/) The office said Johnson has respected that request throughout his career and maintains a close relationship with Michael. [The List](https://www.thelist.com/1435649/who-is-house-speaker-mike-johnson-son-michael/) The family's public narrative otherwise centers on their two daughters, Hannah and Abigail, and two sons, Jack and Will. [The List](https://www.thelist.com/1435649/who-is-house-speaker-mike-johnson-son-michael/)
