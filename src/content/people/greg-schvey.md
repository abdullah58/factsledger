## Overview

Greg Schvey is the co-founder and chief executive officer of [Axoni](/organizations/axoni/), the New York capital-markets technology firm that built the [Veris](/creative-works/veris/) distributed-ledger network for equity swaps — a network the London Stock Exchange Group acquired in 2024. ([FStech](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Lseg_acquires_post_trade_processing_specialist_veris.php)) With his brother Jeff Schvey, Axoni's chief technology officer, he previously co-founded TradeBlock, an institutional cryptocurrency trading-services startup, and spun Axoni off from it in 2016. ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.frb.io/articles/axoni-closes-series-b-led-by-goldman-sachs))

Schvey's public record is almost entirely a company record: there is no standalone biography of him in the major press, and every milestone in this profile is documented through Axoni's pilots, funding rounds and product launches. That is itself revealing — his reputation was built not on commentary but on persuading the world's largest banks to run production infrastructure on a startup's software. His framing of the work stayed consistent for years: blockchain, in his telling, is not about coins but about "the application of it to solve for data synchronization" between institutions that each keep their own books. ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.frb.io/articles/axoni-closes-series-b-led-by-goldman-sachs))

## From TradeBlock to Axoni

Schvey holds a bachelor's degree in finance from Cornell University and spent the first three years of his career at Citigroup as a fixed-income analyst, according to two industry profiles. ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2020/10/167937-perspective-institutional-adoption-of-stos-and-digital-assets-in-2020/)) He came to bitcoin early, around 2010, with his brother Jeff — who had been mining bitcoin — and the pair co-founded TradeBlock in 2013 to serve institutional clients in digital currencies. ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.frb.io/articles/axoni-closes-series-b-led-by-goldman-sachs)) In 2016 they spun Axoni off from TradeBlock to apply distributed-ledger technology to capital-markets infrastructure, with both brothers sitting on the boards of both companies. ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.frb.io/articles/axoni-closes-series-b-led-by-goldman-sachs)) The move put Schvey, then 31, in charge of a startup whose customers would also become its investors — Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, Citigroup, Wells Fargo, [Andreessen Horowitz](/organizations/andreessen-horowitz/) and [Y Combinator](/organizations/y-combinator/) all backed Axoni while testing its software.

## Winning the banks: pilots, funding and the DTCC project

Axoni's credibility was earned in staged pilots. In April 2016 it ran 85 test cases on post-trade lifecycle events for North American single-name credit-default swaps with Bank of America Merrill Lynch, Citi, Credit Suisse and JPMorgan, reporting a 100% success rate. ([WatersTechnology](https://www.waterstechnology.com/emerging-technologies/2454001/axoni-ceo-hails-blockchain-success-in-85-test-cases)) That October, an eight-firm group — JPMorgan, Credit Suisse, Barclays, Citi, one undisclosed bank, Markit, Thomson Reuters and Capco — completed 133 structured equity-swaps test cases, again with a 100% success rate, plus 50 tests of the underlying Axoni Core infrastructure. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2016/10/18/jp-morgan-credit-suisse-among-8-in-latest-bank-blockchain-test)) In January 2017, the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation selected Axoni's [AxCore](/creative-works/axcore/) to re-platform its $11 trillion Trade Information Warehouse, with [IBM](/organizations/ibm/) leading the initiative and R3 advising. ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.frb.io/articles/axoni-closes-series-b-led-by-goldman-sachs)) In 2018 Axoni closed a $32 million Series B led by Goldman Sachs and Nyca Partners, taking total funding to $55 million — and the Schvey brothers were named among Goldman Sachs' 100 Most Intriguing Entrepreneurs of 2018. ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.frb.io/articles/axoni-closes-series-b-led-by-goldman-sachs))

## Veris, BlackRock and the LSEG sale

Axoni's work turned into a live product in February 2020, when the Veris network processed its first live equity-swap trade data between Citi and Goldman Sachs. ([Finadium](https://finadium.com/axoni-and-15-large-firms-launch-distributed-ledger-for-equity-swaps-processing/)) The breakthrough endorsement came in September 2021, when BlackRock — the world's largest asset manager — began using Veris, integrating Axoni's software with its Aladdin operating system to match and reconcile post-trade data on stock swaps. ([The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/blackrock-goes-live-on-axoni-blockchain-network-for-equity-swaps/)) Schvey kept raising capital, closing a $20 million round led by EJF Ventures in April 2023, by which point Axoni said it had raised more than $110 million since inception. ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/axoni-announces-20-million-equity-financing-led-by-ejf-ventures-with-participation-from-laurion-capital-communitas-capital-and-existing-investors-301808202.html)) In September 2024 Axoni sold Veris and related intellectual property to the London Stock Exchange Group, with Schvey calling the deal "a step forward" in modernising over-the-counter derivatives post-trade infrastructure. ([FStech](https://www.fstech.co.uk/fst/Lseg_acquires_post_trade_processing_specialist_veris.php)) It was a rare outcome in enterprise blockchain: a founder-led startup's flagship network ending up operated by a stock-exchange group.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Greg Schvey?
Greg Schvey is the co-founder and CEO of Axoni, a New York firm that builds distributed-ledger infrastructure for capital markets. With his brother Jeff, he previously co-founded the institutional crypto firm TradeBlock and spun Axoni off from it in 2016. ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.frb.io/articles/axoni-closes-series-b-led-by-goldman-sachs))

### What did Greg Schvey do before Axoni?
He studied finance at Cornell University and worked for three years at Citigroup as a fixed-income analyst before co-founding TradeBlock in 2013. Those early-career details come from two tier3 industry profiles and were not verified against a higher-tier source. ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2020/10/167937-perspective-institutional-adoption-of-stos-and-digital-assets-in-2020/))

### What is Greg Schvey best known for?
Leading Axoni's bank pilots — 85 credit-default-swap tests and 133 equity-swaps tests in 2016, both at 100% success — and building Veris, the equity-swaps network BlackRock joined in 2021 and LSEG acquired in 2024. He also led the DTCC's $11 trillion Trade Information Warehouse project. ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2016/10/18/jp-morgan-credit-suisse-among-8-in-latest-bank-blockchain-test))

### Was Greg Schvey recognized for his entrepreneurship?
Yes. In 2018 Goldman Sachs named Greg and Jeff Schvey among its 100 Most Intriguing Entrepreneurs, honored at the Builders + Innovators Summit in Santa Barbara, California. The honor recognized the brothers as two of 100 entrepreneurs selected from multiple industries for the three-day event. ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.io/articles/goldman-sachs-honors-axoni-co-founders-for-entrepreneurship))

### Did Axoni's investors include its customers?
Unusually, yes. Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, Citigroup, Wells Fargo, NEX Group, Franklin Templeton, Andreessen Horowitz and Y Combinator invested in Axoni while also testing or using its software. Schvey himself stressed the point in 2018, calling the investors strategic partners with deep commercial engagements. ([FinTech Collective](https://fintech.frb.io/articles/axoni-closes-series-b-led-by-goldman-sachs))
