## Overview

JD Vance is the vice president of the United States, sworn in on January 20, 2025 ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). A decade before that, he was a venture capitalist and the author of *Hillbilly Elegy*, the 2016 memoir that made him the most-quoted interpreter of the white working-class voters who backed Donald Trump — and also one of Trump's fiercest critics. Vance later reversed course, apologized for his criticism, won an Ohio Senate seat in 2022, and became Trump's running mate in 2024 ([The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)). In September 2026, he was the administration's most visible spokesman for its crackdown on the H-1B skilled-worker visa program ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/world/us-news/jd-vance-supports-100000-h1b-visa-fee-trump-policy-tech-layoffs-10897482/); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/nri/visa/story/were-not-going-to-let-you-jd-vance-issues-fresh-h-1b-warning-to-us-companies-557741-2026-09-25)).

## Early life, education and the Marine Corps

Vance was born James Donald Bowman on August 2, 1984, in Middletown, Ohio, and was raised largely by his maternal grandparents while his mother struggled with addiction ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). He enlisted in the Marine Corps in 2003, served until 2007, reached the rank of corporal, and deployed in Operation Iraqi Freedom ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta); [The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)). He then earned a bachelor's degree from Ohio State University in 2009 and a law degree from Yale Law School in 2013 ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). In 2014 he married Usha Chilukuri Vance ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

## Venture capital and *Hillbilly Elegy*

After Yale, Vance worked at the law firm Sidley Austin and then moved into venture capital, with stints at Mithril Capital and Revolution before founding his own firm, [Narya Capital](/organizations/narya-capital/) ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). His national breakthrough came on June 28, 2016, with the publication of *Hillbilly Elegy*, subtitled "A Memoir of a Family and Culture in Crisis," which detailed life in Appalachian communities drifting from a Democratic Party many residents found disconnected from their struggles ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta); [The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)). The book was embraced as an explanation of Trump's appeal in middle America — where manufacturing job losses and the opioid crisis had pushed families like his into poverty, abuse and addiction — though critics said it sometimes oversimplified rural life and ignored the role of racism in modern politics ([The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)). [Ron Howard](/people/ron-howard/) turned it into a 2020 Netflix film starring Amy Adams as Vance's mother and Glenn Close as his beloved "Mamaw" ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta); [The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)).

Vance's nonprofit [Our Ohio Renewal](/organizations/our-ohio-renewal/), which he said would develop opioid-addiction treatments scalable nationally, ultimately failed at that mission and was shuttered; during the 2022 campaign, his Democratic rival Tim Ryan charged that the charity was little more than a front for Vance's political ambitions, a characterization Vance denied ([The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)).

## [U.S. Senate](/organizations/us-senate/)

Vance announced his Senate candidacy on July 1, 2021, as a newcomer to politics ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). His biggest hurdle was his own record: he had declared himself a "never-Trumper," called Trump a "total fraud," a "moral disaster" and "America's Hitler," and apologized on Fox News in July 2021 ([The Associated Press via The Japan News](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20221109-69689/); [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). Trump endorsed him anyway in a crowded Republican primary and rallied for him twice, and on November 8, 2022, the 38-year-old venture capitalist defeated 10-term Democratic Representative Tim Ryan for Ohio's open U.S. Senate seat ([The Associated Press via The Japan News](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20221109-69689/)). Vance and Trump linked Ryan to the national economic climate blamed on President Joe Biden, while Ryan failed to make stick his narrative that Vance's Ivy League education and time in the San Francisco tech industry made him out of touch with Ohio values ([The Associated Press via The Japan News](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20221109-69689/)).

As a senator, Vance showed some willingness to work across the aisle, teaming up with Ohio's senior senator, Democrat Sherrod Brown, on issues important to the state — including funding for a $20 billion Intel chip facility in central Ohio and rail-safety legislation in response to the fiery derailment in East Palestine, Ohio ([The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)).

## The 2024 running-mate pick and election

On July 15, 2024, as the [Republican National Convention](/events/republican-national-convention/) opened in Milwaukee, Trump announced in a Truth Social post that he had chosen Vance as his running mate, calling him "best suited to assume the position of Vice President of the United States" ([The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)). Hours later, Vance formally received the party's nomination and sat with Trump — who wore a large bandage on his right ear two days after an assassination attempt — in Trump's convention box ([The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)). At 39, Vance became the first millennial to join a major-party ticket ([The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)).

The pick reflected Vance's transformation from critic to one of the staunchest champions of Trump's "Make America Great Again" agenda, particularly on trade, foreign policy and immigration ([The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)). "I was certainly skeptical of Donald Trump in 2016, but President Trump was a great president and he changed my mind," Vance said in his first interview after the selection ([The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)). He had told ABC News in February 2024 that, had he been vice president on January 6, 2021, he would have told states where Trump disputed Biden wins "that we needed to have multiple slates of electors, and I think the U.S. Congress should have fought over it from there" ([The Associated Press](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/elections/2024/trump-picks-sen-jd-vance-of-ohio-a-once-fierce-critic-turned-loyal-ally-as-his-gop-running-mate/)). The ticket won the election on November 5, 2024 ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

## Vice presidency

Vance resigned his Senate seat on January 10, 2025, and was sworn in as vice president on January 20 ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). His first major foreign-policy address came at the [Munich Security Conference](/organizations/munich-security-conference/) on February 14, 2025, where he declared that Europe's greatest danger was a "threat from within," criticized migration and alleged censorship across Europe, and touched only briefly on Ukraine ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/europe/2025/02/14/munich-security-conference-jd-vance-speech/)). European leaders pushed back, and U.S. Congressman Adam Smith said Vance's remarks damaged transatlantic relations ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/europe/2025/02/14/munich-security-conference-jd-vance-speech/)).

[CNN](/organizations/cnn/)'s 2026 fast facts also record a March 2025 Signal group-chat episode involving Vance and his 2026 book *Communion* ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/jd-vance-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

## The H-1B campaign (September 2026)

On September 18, 2026, President Trump signed the executive order "Enhancing Program Integrity and Interagency Coordination in the Administration of the H-1B Nonimmigrant Visa Program," directing the Secretaries of State, Labor and Homeland Security to consider an employer's recent or planned layoffs of similarly situated U.S. workers when adjudicating H-1B labor condition applications, petitions and visas, and calling for greater interagency coordination ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/executive-order-on-h-1b-program-is-latest-sign-of-immigration-program-risks-for-employers/)). Within 30 days, the Department of Labor was directed to review data from previously filed labor condition applications to identify employers warranting further action; the order characterizes H-1B abuse as a national security threat — but it does not itself prohibit an employer from laying off U.S. workers and later sponsoring H-1B workers, instead signaling heightened scrutiny ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/executive-order-on-h-1b-program-is-latest-sign-of-immigration-program-risks-for-employers/)).

Vance became the policy's public face. On September 24, he posted on X: "We're not going to let you lay off American workers so you can replace them with cheap foreign labor," warning U.S. companies against displacing domestic workers ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/nri/visa/story/were-not-going-to-let-you-jd-vance-issues-fresh-h-1b-warning-to-us-companies-557741-2026-09-25)). Layoffs do not automatically bar sponsorship — they become a scrutiny factor — and existing H-1B wage and displacement rules already applied ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/nri/visa/story/were-not-going-to-let-you-jd-vance-issues-fresh-h-1b-warning-to-us-companies-557741-2026-09-25)).

On September 27, his vice-presidential X account posted his defense of a renewed $100,000 H-1B payment, arguing the program should enrich the economy rather than replace American workers with lower-wage foreign workers ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/world/us-news/jd-vance-supports-100000-h1b-visa-fee-trump-policy-tech-layoffs-10897482/)). In the remarks, Vance used hypothetical examples — a company laying off 5,000 workers, and a $60,000 accountant replaced by a $45,000 foreign worker — to make his case ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/world/us-news/jd-vance-supports-100000-h1b-visa-fee-trump-policy-tech-layoffs-10897482/)). The Indian Express describes the payment as applying to certain new petitions involving workers outside the United States ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/world/us-news/jd-vance-supports-100000-h1b-visa-fee-trump-policy-tech-layoffs-10897482/)).

The legal picture around the $100,000 figure is contested: Gibson Dunn notes that a September 2025 presidential proclamation imposing a $100,000 H-1B fee was struck down by a federal court in June 2026, and that the administration has since proposed, through a Department of Homeland Security rule, a $103,265 fee for new cap-subject H-1B petitions ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/executive-order-on-h-1b-program-is-latest-sign-of-immigration-program-risks-for-employers/)). The firm also flags the Labor Department's new "Project Firewall" enforcement initiative, under which the Secretary of Labor may personally direct H-1B investigations ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/executive-order-on-h-1b-program-is-latest-sign-of-immigration-program-risks-for-employers/)).

## September 29, 2026: the America.gov unveiling

Advance coverage of an all-day September 29 event at the Andrew W. Mellon Auditorium in Washington, D.C., listed Vance among the expected attendees for the administration's unveiling of America.gov, an AI tool billed as a "new front door for the federal government" and a one-stop shop for navigating federal websites ([WUTV29/TNND](https://wutv29.com/news/nation-world/trump-to-unveil-new-ai-tool-to-navigate-federal-government-websites-jd-vance-marco-rubio-mehmet-oz-joe-gebbia-elon-musk-jared-isaacson)). Trump, Secretary of State Marco Rubio, Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services Administrator Dr. Mehmet Oz and Chief Design Officer Joe Gebbia were also announced as attending, alongside tech leaders including Elon Musk, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, Blue Origin CEO [Dave Limp](/people/dave-limp/) and NASA administrator Jared Isaacson ([WUTV29/TNND](https://wutv29.com/news/nation-world/trump-to-unveil-new-ai-tool-to-navigate-federal-government-websites-jd-vance-marco-rubio-mehmet-oz-joe-gebbia-elon-musk-jared-isaacson)).
