## Overview

**Jagat Prakash Nadda** (born 2 December 1960) is an Indian politician who led the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) as its national president from January 2020 to January 2026, served as Union Health Minister from November 2014 to May 2019 and again from June 2024, and has been Leader of the House in the Rajya Sabha since June 2024 ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/jp-nadda-returns-to-modi-cabinet-after-stint-as-bjp-chief/articleshow/110852903.cms); [NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/bjps-jp-nadda-named-leader-of-house-in-rajya-sabha-5958878)). Unlike the mass leaders he served alongside, Nadda rose as an organiser: from student politics in the Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP), through the [Himachal Pradesh](/places/himachal-pradesh/) Assembly and the BJP's national secretariat, to the top of the party under Prime Minister [Narendra Modi](/people/narendra-modi/) ([India TV News](https://www.indiatvnews.com/politics/national-jp-nadda-profile-bjp-president-bihar-himachali-origin-challenges-581354)).

Nadda's career is best understood as two parallel tracks that rarely intersected in one person: a health-policy track, where he launched [India](/places/india/)'s flagship immunisation drive and seeded its largest health-insurance scheme, and an organisation track, where he ran the BJP's election machinery in state after state before taking charge of the party itself ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/naddas-1st-term-at-health-ministry-saw-launch-of-crucial-schemes-but-tough-road-lies-ahead/2148926/); [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/jp-nadda-returns-to-modi-cabinet-after-stint-as-bjp-chief/articleshow/110852903.cms)). In September 2020 he moved the nomination that returned Harivansh Narayan Singh as Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha — the parliamentary moment that ties him to this site's [2020 Deputy Chairman election](/events/2020-deputy-chairman-rajya-sabha-election/) profile ([Zee News](https://zeenews.india.com/india/harivansh-narayan-singh-re-elected-as-rajya-sabha-deputy-chairman-2309634.html)).

## From student politics to Himachal minister

Nadda was born in Bihar on 2 December 1960, into a family from Himachal Pradesh; his father was a vice-chancellor of Patna University ([India TV News](https://www.indiatvnews.com/politics/national-jp-nadda-profile-bjp-president-bihar-himachali-origin-challenges-581354)). He graduated from Patna, took a postgraduate degree in political science, and earned an LLB from Himachal Pradesh University in Shimla ([India TV News](https://www.indiatvnews.com/politics/national-jp-nadda-profile-bjp-president-bihar-himachali-origin-challenges-581354)). His politics began in 1978 as a student leader of the ABVP, the student wing of the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS), and he became president of the BJP's youth wing, the Bharatiya Janata Yuva Morcha (BJYM), in 1991 ([India TV News](https://www.indiatvnews.com/politics/national-jp-nadda-profile-bjp-president-bihar-himachali-origin-challenges-581354); [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/jp-nadda-returns-to-modi-cabinet-after-stint-as-bjp-chief/articleshow/110852903.cms)).

Electoral politics followed the same steady pattern. He won the Bilaspur Assembly seat in Himachal Pradesh in 1993, 1998, and 2007, serving as the state's Health Minister between 1998 and 2003 ([NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/bjps-jp-nadda-named-leader-of-house-in-rajya-sabha-5958878)). He lost the 2003 Assembly election, returned in 2007 as Forest Minister, and resigned from that post in 2010 after differences with Chief Minister Prem Kumar Dhumal — a rare public break in an otherwise low-friction career ([India TV News](https://www.indiatvnews.com/politics/national-jp-nadda-profile-bjp-president-bihar-himachali-origin-challenges-581354)). The same year, then BJP chief Nitin Gadkari brought him into the national organisation as general secretary, and in 2012 he was elected to the Rajya Sabha from Himachal Pradesh ([India TV News](https://www.indiatvnews.com/politics/national-jp-nadda-profile-bjp-president-bihar-himachali-origin-challenges-581354); [NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/bjps-jp-nadda-named-leader-of-house-in-rajya-sabha-5958878)). Along the way he headed the BJP's election campaigns in states from Bihar to Uttar Pradesh, Kerala, Maharashtra, and Punjab ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/jp-nadda-returns-to-modi-cabinet-after-stint-as-bjp-chief/articleshow/110852903.cms)).

## Two stints at the health ministry

Nadda entered the Union cabinet in the first expansion of the Modi government in 2014, taking the health portfolio in November 2014 after Harsh Vardhan was dropped, and serving until May 2019 ([India TV News](https://www.indiatvnews.com/politics/national-jp-nadda-profile-bjp-president-bihar-himachali-origin-challenges-581354); [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/naddas-1st-term-at-health-ministry-saw-launch-of-crucial-schemes-but-tough-road-lies-ahead/2148926/)). His first term produced a cluster of programmes that still define Indian health policy. He launched Mission Indradhanush in December 2014, a catch-up immunisation drive aimed at children who were unvaccinated or partially vaccinated against seven diseases — diphtheria, whooping cough, tetanus, polio, tuberculosis, measles, and hepatitis B ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/india-news/meet-jp-nadda-man-behind-indradhanush-back-to-steer-health-agenda-124061000968_1.html)). He oversaw the National Health Policy of 2017, which set a target of raising government health spending to 2.5% of GDP by 2025 — though a clause declaring health a right was removed during drafting ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/naddas-1st-term-at-health-ministry-saw-launch-of-crucial-schemes-but-tough-road-lies-ahead/2148926/)). He also launched the Pradhan Mantri National Dialysis Programme and the AMRIT scheme for subsidised medicines for critical illnesses, and his ministry prepared the original design of what became Ayushman Bharat ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/naddas-1st-term-at-health-ministry-saw-launch-of-crucial-schemes-but-tough-road-lies-ahead/2148926/)).

Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY) offers health cover of Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation to over 10.74 crore poor and vulnerable families ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/naddas-1st-term-at-health-ministry-saw-launch-of-crucial-schemes-but-tough-road-lies-ahead/2148926/)) — a scale that makes the scheme Nadda helped seed one of the world's largest publicly funded health insurance programmes, and the one his second stint is judged against. His first term also saw a major expansion of All India Institutes of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) across states and the upgrading of district hospitals into medical colleges ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/naddas-1st-term-at-health-ministry-saw-launch-of-crucial-schemes-but-tough-road-lies-ahead/2148926/)).

Nadda returned to the same ministry in June 2024, this time also holding Chemicals and Fertilizers, which houses the Department of Pharmaceuticals ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/india-news/meet-jp-nadda-man-behind-indradhanush-back-to-steer-health-agenda-124061000968_1.html)). The second stint's early agenda included folding HPV vaccination against cervical cancer into the universal immunisation programme, enforcing the revised Schedule M quality standards for drugmakers after substandard exports drew international criticism, and implementing the Uniform Code for Pharmaceutical Marketing Practices, 2024, aimed at curbing inducements to doctors ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/india-news/meet-jp-nadda-man-behind-indradhanush-back-to-steer-health-agenda-124061000968_1.html)). Practitioners quoted by ThePrint welcomed a "heavyweight" politician in the portfolio while warning that AB-PMJAY beneficiaries still face substantial out-of-pocket spending, especially at private hospitals ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/naddas-1st-term-at-health-ministry-saw-launch-of-crucial-schemes-but-tough-road-lies-ahead/2148926/)).

## Party president, dual roles, and succession

After the 2019 Lok Sabha polls, Nadda was made the BJP's working president, and in January 2020 he was elevated to full national president after Amit Shah moved to the Union Home Ministry ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/jp-nadda-returns-to-modi-cabinet-after-stint-as-bjp-chief/articleshow/110852903.cms)). His presidency covered a mixed electoral record: the BJP consolidated strongholds and added allies, but the 2024 Lok Sabha polls returned only 240 BJP seats — short of a majority on its own — with the BJP-led National Democratic Alliance reaching 293 ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/jp-nadda-returns-to-modi-cabinet-after-stint-as-bjp-chief/articleshow/110852903.cms)).

What makes Nadda's 2024–2026 period unusual is the accumulation of offices. In April 2024 he was elected to the Rajya Sabha from Gujarat; in June 2024 he rejoined the Union cabinet as Health Minister and Minister of Chemicals and Fertilizers; and he was then appointed Leader of the House in the Rajya Sabha, succeeding Piyush Goyal — all while remaining BJP national president ([NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/bjps-jp-nadda-named-leader-of-house-in-rajya-sabha-5958878)). NDTV reported speculation that he would step down from the party post on entering the cabinet, but he stayed on as the party's top organisational leader while its organisational elections ran their course ([NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/bjps-jp-nadda-named-leader-of-house-in-rajya-sabha-5958878)).

The succession, when it came, followed the BJP's consensus pattern. Under the party's practice, a national president is elected only after organisational elections are completed in at least half the states, and since the party's formation in 1980 no contest for the post has ever actually been held — each president has been elected unopposed ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/political-pulse/as-nitin-nabin-takes-charge-a-look-at-how-bjp-appoints-its-president-unanimous-choice-no-elections-10483441/)). In January 2026, Nitin Nabin took charge as national president, formally closing Nadda's extended tenure and restoring the party's "one person, one post" principle after Nadda's dual minister–president years ([The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/political-pulse/as-nitin-nabin-takes-charge-a-look-at-how-bjp-appoints-its-president-unanimous-choice-no-elections-10483441/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is J.P. Nadda?**
Jagat Prakash Nadda is an Indian politician who led the BJP as national president from January 2020 to January 2026 and served as Union Health Minister in 2014–2019 and again from June 2024. A former Himachal Pradesh minister and Rajya Sabha member, he is known as an organiser rather than a mass leader.

**What did J.P. Nadda do as health minister?**
In his first stint he launched Mission Indradhanush for catch-up immunisation, oversaw the National Health Policy 2017, started the national dialysis programme and the AMRIT subsidised-medicines scheme, and prepared the original design of Ayushman Bharat. His second stint from June 2024 added the Chemicals and Fertilizers portfolio.

**When was J.P. Nadda BJP president?**
He became working president in 2019 and full national president in January 2020, succeeding Amit Shah. His term was extended past its original span, and Nitin Nabin took charge as his successor in January 2026, restoring the party's one-person-one-post principle.

**Which state does J.P. Nadda represent in the Rajya Sabha?**
He was first elected to the Rajya Sabha from Himachal Pradesh in 2012. In April 2024 he was elected to the Rajya Sabha from Gujarat, and in June 2024 he became Leader of the House in the upper house following his election from Gujarat.

**What role did Nadda play in the 2020 Deputy Chairman election?**
As BJP president, Nadda moved the nomination of Harivansh Narayan Singh for re-election as Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha on 14 September 2020. The nomination was seconded by Leader of the House Thavarchand Gehlot, and Singh was re-elected by voice vote.
