## Overview

One year after the assassination of her husband, Erika Kirk sat for her first major interview as the leader of [Turning Point USA](/organizations/turning-point-usa/) — and the most revealing moment was not about the movement at all. Asked by Vanity Fair about Israeli prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu, she answered: "I don't sit around thinking about what Bibi Netanyahu is doing, I just don't. That is not where my brain is at. My brain bandwidth right now is honoring my husband, loving on my babies, and building Turning Point." ([The National News Desk](https://fox49.tv/news/nation-world/erika-kirk-says-she-doesnt-think-about-what-netanyahu-is-doing-in-vanity-fair-interview-israel-tucker-carlson-candace-owens)) The interview, published Tuesday, September 29, 2026, arrived one year after she took over the organization her husband founded in 2012, and it immediately became a flashpoint on the American right ([people.com](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229)).

Kirk, 37, inherited one of the most powerful youth organizations in conservative politics when Charlie Kirk was assassinated on September 10, 2025, while speaking at a debate event hosted by Turning Point USA at Utah Valley University ([people.com](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229)). Before that day she was known publicly as a former Miss Arizona USA with her own ministries and a podcast aimed at young Christian women; after it, she became the face of a movement in mourning — and the target of a swirl of conspiracy theories about the succession that the Vanity Fair interview finally addressed head-on.

## The family defense

The interview's most striking passage came not from Erika but from her mother-in-law. Kathryn Kirk, Charlie's mother, publicly defended her daughter-in-law's leadership: "We were 100 percent behind her. As her in-laws, we just decided that we're going to love her the best we can through this process. All the crazy conspiracy theories out there, the will, the feuds, like, all these crazy things, they are not true." ([people.com](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229)) She added that the family had "grown closer" and described herself as one of Erika's cheerleaders, saying her daughter-in-law had "grown in such a huge way" ([people.com](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229)).

The defense mattered because the conspiracy theories had traveled far. In April 2026, the president of the University of Georgia's TPUSA chapter resigned, claiming the organization was drifting from Charlie Kirk's vision ([people.com](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229)). For her part, Erika projected defiance in the interview: "I'm built different. Even though I'm a 37-year-old widow with two babies, this world will not break me. I have a backbone, a spine made of steel." ([people.com](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229)) She spoke of her husband as reunited with his creator and said she looks forward to the day they are together again: "I cannot wait to look at him and tell him, 'Look what the Lord did.'" ([people.com](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229))

## The Netanyahu question and its fallout

The Netanyahu exchange — the non-answer answer — detonated a familiar skirmish. [Tucker Carlson](/people/tucker-carlson/), the former Fox News host, had said publicly that Erika Kirk was "more critical of Israel than her late husband" and had suggested that Turning Point USA could move past Charlie Kirk ([The National News Desk](https://fox49.tv/news/nation-world/erika-kirk-says-she-doesnt-think-about-what-netanyahu-is-doing-in-vanity-fair-interview-israel-tucker-carlson-candace-owens)). Conservative commentator Candace Owens treated the Vanity Fair interview as vindication, telling her audience "I told you so" and repeating her earlier claim that Charlie Kirk's will had been updated to appoint Erika as the organization's leader ([The National News Desk](https://fox49.tv/news/nation-world/erika-kirk-says-she-doesnt-think-about-what-netanyahu-is-doing-in-vanity-fair-interview-israel-tucker-carlson-candace-owens)). The will claim remains unproven, and the Kirk family has denied the feud narratives outright.

In the background, the legal case continued: Tyler Robinson, the suspect in Charlie Kirk's killing, pleaded not guilty ([The National News Desk](https://fox49.tv/news/nation-world/erika-kirk-says-she-doesnt-think-about-what-netanyahu-is-doing-in-vanity-fair-interview-israel-tucker-carlson-candace-owens)). Charlie Kirk was posthumously awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom on October 14 (2025) ([people.com](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229)).

## AmericaFest and the Minaj moment

Three months after the assassination, Kirk closed out Turning Point USA's flagship December gathering with an unlikely co-star. On the final day of AmericaFest — Sunday, December 21, 2025 — [Nicki Minaj](/people/nicki-minaj/) made a surprise appearance, walking onstage hand-in-hand with Erika Kirk ([Access Hollywood](https://prod.accesshollywood.com/videos/nicki-minaj-calls-donald-trump-handsome-in-surprise-turning-point-usa-appearance-with-erika-kirk)).

Minaj called Donald Trump "dashing" and "handsome," took a "Newscum" swipe at California governor Gavin Newsom, and listed [JD Vance](/people/jd-vance/) as an "amazing role model" — then slipped, saying something with "assassin" in it, and covered her mouth ([Access Hollywood](https://prod.accesshollywood.com/videos/nicki-minaj-calls-donald-trump-handsome-in-surprise-turning-point-usa-appearance-with-erika-kirk)). Kirk steadied the moment onstage, telling Minaj there was "nothing new under the sun that I have not heard" ([Access Hollywood](https://prod.accesshollywood.com/videos/nicki-minaj-calls-donald-trump-handsome-in-surprise-turning-point-usa-appearance-with-erika-kirk)). The appearance cemented the strange cultural gravity Kirk was beginning to wield: a grieving widow who could draw a pop superstar onto a conservative stage.

## The life before

Erika Kirk, 36 in September 2025, grew up in metro Phoenix and was named Miss Arizona USA in 2012 ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/who-is-erika-kirk-wife-of-charlie-kirk/86101974007/)). She runs a Christian clothing line called Proclaim and operates a ministry called Biblein365, which delivers scriptures and Bible commentary daily ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/who-is-erika-kirk-wife-of-charlie-kirk/86101974007/)).

She met Charlie Kirk in 2018; they married in 2021 and had two children — a 3-year-old girl and a 1-year-old boy as of September 2025 ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/who-is-erika-kirk-wife-of-charlie-kirk/86101974007/)). The family shared much of their life on social media but kept their children's faces and names private ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/who-is-erika-kirk-wife-of-charlie-kirk/86101974007/)). Her children were later reported as born in August 2022 and May 2024 ([people.com](https://people.com/charlie-kirk-mom-defends-erika-kirk-one-year-after-she-took-over-turning-point-usa-12148229)).

Her education is unusually credentialed for a movement figure. She holds degrees from Arizona State University in political science and international relations, a master's degree in American legal studies from Liberty University, and is pursuing a doctorate in biblical studies, according to her website ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/who-is-erika-kirk-wife-of-charlie-kirk/86101974007/)). She attended Notre Dame Preparatory, a private Catholic high school in Scottsdale, graduating in 2007 and entering the school's hall of fame in 2021 ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/who-is-erika-kirk-wife-of-charlie-kirk/86101974007/)).

On her podcast, Midweek Rise Up, and as a regular presence beside her husband, she championed the same political and biblical views as Charlie Kirk — but she aimed her mentoring at young women ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/who-is-erika-kirk-wife-of-charlie-kirk/86101974007/)). She urged women to marry young and have children, even if that meant delaying career plans, and with her husband promoted traditional gender roles: men spiritually leading the family, financially providing, and physically safeguarding the wife and children ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/who-is-erika-kirk-wife-of-charlie-kirk/86101974007/)). For women and girls she called for the "revival of biblical womanhood," stressing Titus 2 — the scripture directing women to love and obey their husbands and be keepers of the home — and summoned women to enter the "spiritual war" against forces that disconnect people from God ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/who-is-erika-kirk-wife-of-charlie-kirk/86101974007/)).

"You don't need to be masculine to be strong, and you don't need to be loud to be heard," she said in June 2025 at the Young Women's Leadership Summit ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/who-is-erika-kirk-wife-of-charlie-kirk/86101974007/)). And on the priority of family over career: "You can always have a career ... you will always be able to create your own company. But children, family, marriages, that is not a renewable resource. Don't delay the eternal for the temporary." ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/arizona/2025/09/11/who-is-erika-kirk-wife-of-charlie-kirk/86101974007/))
