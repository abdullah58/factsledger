# Stephen J. Harrison

**Stephen J. Harrison is a Nashville, Tennessee insurance executive who
co-founded USAuto Insurance Company and then served as CEO of First
Acceptance Corporation from 2004 to 2011.** He became CEO and a director
when [Liberté Investors](/organizations/liberte-investors/) Inc. acquired USAuto Holdings in April 2004 and
renamed itself First Acceptance Corporation, and he was the company's
largest single shareholder at the 2008 proxy — about 14.8% of outstanding
stock.

## Career

Harrison co-founded [USAuto Insurance Company](/organizations/usauto-holdings/) in Nashville in 1995,
serving as its president until 2002 and then as chairman and CEO through
the 2004 merger. In the merger, USAuto Holdings merged with a Liberté
subsidiary and Harrison moved into the combined company's top role:
Chief Executive Officer, President, and director from April 2004 (proxy
statement, Oct. 6, 2008).

He relinquished the presidency in February 2008 while remaining CEO and
a director. In December 2011, First Acceptance bought back 7,049,515
shares from Harrison and his 2010 grantor retained annuity trust at
$1.45 per share — a buyout tied to a mutual separation and release
agreement (Exhibit 10.34).

## Name disambiguation

Racal, nor executives of the same name at Manitex or Relypsa.

## FAQ

**Was Stephen J. Harrison related to [Thomas M. Harrison](/people/thomas-m-harrison/)?**
The 2008 proxy lists both as large shareholders and executives of First
Acceptance (Stephen held 14.8%, Thomas 14.7%); it does not state a
family relationship, and none is stated here.

**How big was his stake?**
At the 2008 proxy: 7,014,999 shares owned outright plus 91,681 shares
acquirable within 60 days — roughly 14.8% of outstanding stock.
