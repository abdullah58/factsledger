## Overview

**Derek Curl** is an American genre-film producer and distribution executive who, as TLA Entertainment's acquisitions director, bought a 75% controlling interest in **TLA Releasing** and became its president and CEO, then co-founded the horror label **Autonomy Pictures** with David Gregory and Lewis Tice ([Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/tla-releasing-sells-as-parent-company-refocuses/5026847.article); [Bloody Disgusting](http://bloody-disgusting.com/news/28671/autonomy-pictures-comes-out-playing-the-bunny-game/)). As Autonomy's president, he negotiated the label's first release — all US rights to Adam Rehmeier's banned-in-Britain *The Bunny Game* — finalizing the deal at **SXSW** 2012 ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/31660/a-home-found-for-the-bunny-game/)). Curl called the film "the last word on the 'torture-porn' sub-genre," making a deliberate statement about the kind of cinema the new label would champion ([Bloody Disgusting](http://bloody-disgusting.com/news/28671/autonomy-pictures-comes-out-playing-the-bunny-game/)).

This is **Derek Curl the film producer** — president of TLA Releasing and Autonomy Pictures — not any other same-name person in an unrelated field. Every professional fact in this profile is drawn from the film-industry record described below.

## From TLA Releasing to the president's chair

Curl's producing credits before Autonomy included Ti West's *The Innkeepers* (2011) and the vampire-apocalypse film *Stake Land* (2010), both of which the Autonomy launch materials cite as his calling card ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/31660/a-home-found-for-the-bunny-game/)). His distribution career ran through Philadelphia-based **TLA Entertainment**: Screen Daily reported that TLA Entertainment's acquisitions director Derek Curl, backed by investors, purchased a 75% controlling interest in the distributor **TLA Releasing**, covering both its North American operation and its UK subsidiary, and that Curl would become president and CEO of the newly formed LLC ([Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/tla-releasing-sells-as-parent-company-refocuses/5026847.article)). The sale was part of TLA Entertainment's decision to refocus on its core business — a home-entertainment e-tailer and mail-order operation — while the distribution arm, built since 2000 around the TLA Releasing and Danger After Dark labels, moved to filmmaker control; longtime president Raymond Murray stayed on as acquisitions advisor ([Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/tla-releasing-sells-as-parent-company-refocuses/5026847.article)). Curl framed the move in industry terms: "TLA Releasing has proven, again and again, over many years, that 'independence' can translate to great success," noting that his own first distribution deal had been with TLA ([Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/tla-releasing-sells-as-parent-company-refocuses/5026847.article)).

## Launching Autonomy Pictures with The Bunny Game

In March 2012 Curl announced **Autonomy Pictures**, described in the launch materials as a filmmaker-driven company dedicated to producing and distributing "boundary-pushing, uncompromising cinema on a worldwide scale" ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/31660/a-home-found-for-the-bunny-game/)). The company was spearheaded by Curl alongside **David Gregory** (Severin Films; filmmaker of *Plague Town* and *The Theatre Bizarre*) and **Lewis Tice** (Danger After Dark), with Curl serving as president ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/31660/a-home-found-for-the-bunny-game/)). The label's launch title was a provocation by design: all US rights to Adam Rehmeier's *The Bunny Game*, starring co-writer **Rodleen Getsic**, which had recently been refused certification in the UK ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/31660/a-home-found-for-the-bunny-game/); [Bloody Disgusting](http://bloody-disgusting.com/news/28671/autonomy-pictures-comes-out-playing-the-bunny-game/)). The deal was finalized at SXSW 2012, negotiated by Curl for Autonomy Pictures and by **Julian Richards** of Jinga Films on behalf of the filmmakers, with the film set for an uncut US release in July 2012 ([Dread Central](https://www.dreadcentral.com/news/31660/a-home-found-for-the-bunny-game/); [Bloody Disgusting](http://bloody-disgusting.com/news/28671/autonomy-pictures-comes-out-playing-the-bunny-game/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is Derek Curl?**
Derek Curl is an American genre-film producer and distribution executive. As TLA Entertainment's acquisitions director, he bought a 75% controlling interest in TLA Releasing and became its president and CEO. He then co-founded and served as president of the horror label Autonomy Pictures, launching it in 2012 with the US release of Adam Rehmeier's *The Bunny Game*.

**What films did Derek Curl produce?**
The launch materials for Autonomy Pictures cite Curl's producer credits on Ti West's *The Innkeepers* (2011) and the vampire-apocalypse film *Stake Land* (2010). These credits were used to establish his genre credentials when the new label was announced.

**What is Autonomy Pictures?**
Autonomy Pictures is a US horror film label announced in March 2012 by Derek Curl with David Gregory (Severin Films) and Lewis Tice (Danger After Dark). It was described as a filmmaker-driven company for boundary-pushing, uncompromising cinema, and its first release was *The Bunny Game*, acquired for US distribution and finalized at SXSW 2012.

**Why did Derek Curl call The Bunny Game "the last word on the 'torture-porn' sub-genre"?**
The line was Curl's public framing of Autonomy's launch title. He said he had been shocked by the film's audacity and raw honesty, and that releasing it would let audiences make up their own minds. The quote positioned the new label as unafraid of films that inspire heated reaction.
