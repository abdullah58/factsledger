Çetin Tekindor is a Turkish stage, television, and film actor who first became famous as a voice: the Turkish dubbing voice of Robert Redford and of Rock Hudson's Commissioner McMillan in *McMillan & Wife* on state television TRT. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma)) He later won Best Actor awards for *Babam ve Oğlum* (2005), ([Cumhuriyet](https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/yasam/babam-ve-oglum-filmi-konusu-nedir-nerede-cekildi-oyunculari-kimler-1948952)) and played the elderly Süleyman Dilbirliği in [Ayla: The Daughter of War](https://factsledger.com/creative-works/ayla-the-daughter-of-war/), Turkey's Oscar entry, opposite [İsmail Hacıoğlu](/people/ismail-hacioglu/) as the young Dilbirliği. ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633))

His arc runs from the Ankara State Theater through a national fame built in the dubbing booth to a late-career screen renaissance.

## The voice before the face

Tekindor was born in 1945 in Sivas and began acting with minor roles at the Ankara State Theater, appearing at 18 in *Hep Vatan İçin*. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma)) After graduating from the Ankara State Conservatory in 1970, he joined the theater's production of *Murat IV*, about the Ottoman sultan. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma)) Alongside the stage he worked as a dubbing artist for Hollywood imports, and importers paid him to dub stars such as Robert Redford. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma)) He also taught stage art and diction at the Hacettepe University State Conservatory, moving to Bilkent University's Theater Department after 1998. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma)) National fame arrived in 1976, when he voiced Rock Hudson's Police Commissioner McMillan in *McMillan & Wife* on TRT — moviegoers knew his voice better than his face. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma)) That crossover was rare: after Turkish theatre and cinema split in the early 1960s, producers preferred training their own screen actors rather than hiring stage veterans. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma))

## From "Little Agha" to the mob boss

Screen fame arrived in 1983 with *Küçük Ağa*, directed by Yücel Çakmaklı from Tarık Buğra's novel about a scholar turned guerrilla fighter. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma)) The transformation was so complete that viewers called Tekindor "Little Agha" — and kept calling him that instead of his own name until the end of the 1990s. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma)) After his cinema debut *Kaçamak* (1987), his second reinvention came in 1999: a mob leader in *Yılan Hikayesi* that "changed his naive and puritan image as an actor to a multidimensional and charismatic figure," his mild voice gaining a threatening complexity. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma)) *Bir İstanbul Masalı* and *Anadolu Kaplanı* followed; his ex-wife Zerrin Tekindor — he married the stage actress in 1987, they had a son, Hira, and divorced about a decade later — called him "a funny man behind his serious posture." ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma)) He later played the kind-but-strong father of the protagonist in *Karadayı*, the Ay Yapım drama rated the number-one show in Turkey in 2014. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma); [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/events/2014/02/27/karadayi-again-tops-the-ratings))

## The patriarch, the awards, and Ayla

The signature screen role came in Çağan Irmak's *Babam ve Oğlum* (2005): Tekindor played Hüseyin Efendi, an İzmir village headman, opposite Fikret Kuşkan as his estranged, dying son, during the 12 September coup. ([Cumhuriyet](https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/yasam/babam-ve-oglum-filmi-konusu-nedir-nerede-cekildi-oyunculari-kimler-1948952)) The film became a box-office phenomenon without a single advertisement: over 3.8 million people saw it, an unbreakable record for a low-budget Turkish drama — proof a film could spread by word of mouth alone. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma)) It ranked seventh among the most-watched Turkish films in 2016, and Tekindor won Best Actor at the Sinema Yazarları Derneği and ÇASOD awards (see openItems). ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma); [Cumhuriyet](https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/yasam/babam-ve-oglum-filmi-konusu-nedir-nerede-cekildi-oyunculari-kimler-1948952)) His most internationally visible part followed: the elderly [Süleyman Dilbirliği](https://factsledger.com/people/suleyman-dilbirligi/) in Can Ülkay's [Ayla: The Daughter of War](https://factsledger.com/creative-works/ayla-the-daughter-of-war/) (2017), with İsmail Hacıoğlu as the young Dilbirliği. ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633)) The film was Turkey's entry for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film, set in the Korean War — where Turkish troops served under [U.N. command](https://factsledger.com/organizations/united-nations/) and 774 Turkish soldiers were killed. ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/amp/ayla-a-story-of-love-turkeys-oscar-entry-121072); [Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/amp/wife-of-turkish-foster-father-of-korean-war-orphan-dies-one-day-after-her-husband-123803))

## Frequently asked questions

**What role did Çetin Tekindor play in *Ayla*?**
He played the elderly Süleyman Dilbirliği — the Korean War veteran whose story the film tells — while İsmail Hacıoğlu played the young Dilbirliği. The film was directed by Can Ülkay and selected as Turkey's entry for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film. ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkeys-oscar-nominee-ayla-brings-korean-war-memories-117633))

**Which awards did he win for *Babam ve Oğlum*?**
He won Best Actor at the Sinema Yazarları Derneği (SİYAD) awards and at the ÇASOD awards for playing Hüseyin Efendi. Note the split: at the 2006 Istanbul Film Festival, Best Actor for the same film went to his co-star Fikret Kuşkan, not Tekindor. ([Cumhuriyet](https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/yasam/babam-ve-oglum-filmi-konusu-nedir-nerede-cekildi-oyunculari-kimler-1948952))

**Why is Çetin Tekindor called a voice actor?**
Before screen stardom he worked as a dubbing artist for foreign films on Turkish television — most famously as the Turkish voice of Robert Redford and of Rock Hudson's Commissioner McMillan in *McMillan & Wife* on TRT in 1976, the role that made him nationally famous. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma))

**When was Çetin Tekindor born?**
In 1945, in Sivas, Turkey. Sources disagree on the day: Daily Sabah gives July 15, 1945, while English-language databases give July 16, 1945. He graduated from the Ankara State Conservatory in 1970, and the Turkish dailies cited here consistently use the spelling Çetin Tekindor. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma))

**Did he teach acting?**
Yes. He was a lecturer in stage art and diction at the Hacettepe University State Conservatory, and after 1998 he taught the same subject at Bilkent University's Theater Department — a second career running alongside his stage and screen work. ([Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/portrait/2016/06/04/cetin-tekindor-acting-with-charisma))
