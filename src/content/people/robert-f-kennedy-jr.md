# Robert F. Kennedy Jr.

## Overview

Robert F. Kennedy Jr. is the U.S. Secretary of Health and Human Services, sworn in on February 13, 2025 after a 52–48 Senate confirmation ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/senate-to-vote-on-vaccine-skeptic-robert-f-kennedy-jr-s-bid-to-become-nations-health-secretary/)). He is also an environmental lawyer, an author, and one of the country's best-known vaccine skeptics — and in the year and a half since taking office, his skeptics have made him a target of their own. On September 26, 2026, television producer [Chuck Lorre](/people/chuck-lorre/) accepted a founders award at [Project Angel Food's](/organizations/project-angel-food/) Angel Awards 2026 in Los Angeles and turned his acceptance speech into a political roast that included a direct jab at the health secretary: "I'll just point out those hard workers will likely die of measles because Bobby Kennedy told them not to get vaccinated." Lorre then imitated Kennedy's voice, adding, "I actually practiced that" ([RealityTea](https://www.realitytea.com/2026/09/29/chuck-lorre-calls-out-donald-trump-los-angeles-speech/), per The Daily Beast's account of the speech). It was the kind of pop-culture mockery that follows a public figure whose career has always straddled celebrity and policy — and whose tenure at HHS has been dominated by his long-standing war on vaccines.

## Family and education

Kennedy was born January 17, 1954, in Washington, D.C., the third of eleven children of former U.S. Attorney General Robert F. Kennedy and Ethel Skakel Kennedy, and the nephew of President John F. Kennedy ([NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/people/robert-f-kennedy-jr-anti-vaccine-activist-who-is-now-us-health-secretary-7023554)). Both his father and his uncle were assassinated in the 1960s, a family history that has kept Kennedy in the national spotlight since childhood ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/senate-to-vote-on-vaccine-skeptic-robert-f-kennedy-jr-s-bid-to-become-nations-health-secretary/)). He studied at Harvard University, attended the London School of Economics, earned a law degree from the University of Virginia, and later completed a master's degree in environmental law at Pace University School of Law, where he co-founded the Environmental Litigation Clinic and served on the faculty for more than 30 years ([NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/people/robert-f-kennedy-jr-anti-vaccine-activist-who-is-now-us-health-secretary-7023554)).

## From heroin conviction to environmental law

Kennedy's environmental career began in court-mandated community service. After being arrested and convicted for heroin possession in 1983, he fulfilled his sentence by volunteering at the Natural Resources Defense Council, an environmental legal advocacy group — and found his calling there, going on to become a prominent environmental lawyer ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/)). He founded the Waterkeeper Alliance, a nonprofit environmental group focused on clean-water advocacy, where he worked as chairman and attorney ([NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/people/robert-f-kennedy-jr-anti-vaccine-activist-who-is-now-us-health-secretary-7023554)). His work restoring New York's Hudson River earned him the title "Hero for the Planet" from TIME Magazine and helped create more than 300 similar Waterkeeper groups worldwide; he also played a key role in the New York City watershed agreement, now viewed as a global example of sustainable development ([NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/people/robert-f-kennedy-jr-anti-vaccine-activist-who-is-now-us-health-secretary-7023554)). His legal wins include cases against Monsanto in 2018 and DuPont in 2019 for contamination — the latter inspiring the film *Dark Waters* ([NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/people/robert-f-kennedy-jr-anti-vaccine-activist-who-is-now-us-health-secretary-7023554)). He has authored several books, including the bestsellers *Crimes Against Nature* (2005) and *The Real Anthony Fauci* (2021) ([NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/people/robert-f-kennedy-jr-anti-vaccine-activist-who-is-now-us-health-secretary-7023554)).

## Children's Health Defense and vaccine activism

In 2015 Kennedy joined Children's Health Defense, then called the World Mercury Project, a nonprofit dedicated to "ending childhood health epidemics by eliminating toxic exposure" that has filed dozens of lawsuits challenging vaccines and public-health mandates ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/)). Its revenue grew from $165,937 in 2016 to $6.8 million in 2020 to almost $16 million in 2021 — coinciding with COVID — to more than $23.5 million in 2022, and Kennedy's compensation there surpassed half a million dollars in 2022 ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/)). A 2019 study found that Children's Health Defense and one other group were responsible for 54% of anti-vaccine advertisements on Facebook ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/)).

In June 2019, Kennedy traveled to Samoa on a trip paid for by Children's Health Defense, after the Samoan government suspended measles vaccinations in 2018 following two infant deaths; he wrote on his blog about officials' curiosity over the "natural experiment" created by the respite from vaccines ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/)). A subsequent measles outbreak killed 83 people, most of them children. Kennedy later said he "never told anyone not to vaccinate" ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/)). He has long promoted the disproven claim of a link between vaccines and autism — including in a 2005 MSNBC interview with Joe Scarborough and again on a 2023 episode of the Joe Rogan Experience — a theory [Al Jazeera](/organizations/al-jazeera/) notes "has been disproven by myriad scientific studies" ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/11/15/how-controversial-is-trumps-pick-of-rfk-jr-as-us-health-secretary)). During the COVID pandemic he petitioned the Food and Drug Administration to revoke the vaccine ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/)). He resigned as Children's Health Defense chairman in December 2024 ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/)).

Kennedy is known for a neurological disorder that affects his voice, which U.S. media has reported he blames on a flu vaccine he received ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/11/15/how-controversial-is-trumps-pick-of-rfk-jr-as-us-health-secretary)). His other stated positions include opposition to water fluoridation, criticism of the FDA as serving corporate interests ("If you work for the FDA and are part of this corrupt system, I have two messages for you: 1. Preserve your records, and 2. Pack your bags"), promotion of hydroxychloroquine during COVID, and a July 2023 claim that COVID-19 had been engineered to target certain ethnicities ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/11/15/how-controversial-is-trumps-pick-of-rfk-jr-as-us-health-secretary)). He denies being an "anti-vaxxer" and told NBC News he would not "take away anybody's vaccines" ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/11/15/how-controversial-is-trumps-pick-of-rfk-jr-as-us-health-secretary)).

## 2024 campaign and nomination

Kennedy initially ran for president in the 2024 election as a Democratic challenger to Joe Biden, then declared in October 2023 that he would run as an independent, before suspending his campaign on August 23, 2024 and endorsing Donald Trump ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/11/15/how-controversial-is-trumps-pick-of-rfk-jr-as-us-health-secretary)). His campaign raised $62.5 million, with outside groups raising another $40.1 million; notable backers included celebrity bodyguard Gavin de Becker ($14 million to the Team America super PAC, nearly all refunded as "bridge funding"), billionaire Timothy Mellon ($25 million to Team America), and running-mate Nicole Shanahan, who gave nearly $17 million herself ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/)).

Trump announced Kennedy as his HHS pick on November 14, 2024, writing on Truth Social: "I am thrilled to announce Robert F Kennedy Jr as the United States Secretary of Health and Human Services [HHS]" ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/11/15/how-controversial-is-trumps-pick-of-rfk-jr-as-us-health-secretary)). At an October 27 rally, Trump had said he would let Kennedy "go wild" on health, food and medicines — and Kennedy's slogan, "Make America Healthy Again" (MAHA), became the shorthand for his agenda of lowering drug prices, limiting pesticide use, reintroducing a presidential fitness test, and restricting processed foods, including from food stamps ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/11/15/how-controversial-is-trumps-pick-of-rfk-jr-as-us-health-secretary); [OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/)).

His confirmation was bitter. The Senate confirmed him 52–48 on February 13, 2025, with all Democrats opposed and Mitch McConnell — a childhood polio survivor — the only Republican voting no: "I will not condone the re-litigation of proven cures, and neither will millions of Americans who credit their survival and quality of life to scientific miracles" ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/senate-to-vote-on-vaccine-skeptic-robert-f-kennedy-jr-s-bid-to-become-nations-health-secretary/)). Hours after the vote he was sworn in that afternoon in the Oval Office by Supreme Court Justice Neil Gorsuch, joined by his wife and family ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/senate-to-vote-on-vaccine-skeptic-robert-f-kennedy-jr-s-bid-to-become-nations-health-secretary/)). Potential holdout Sen. Bill Cassidy, a physician who chairs the Senate health committee, backed him only after securing assurances Kennedy would not change existing vaccine recommendations ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/senate-to-vote-on-vaccine-skeptic-robert-f-kennedy-jr-s-bid-to-become-nations-health-secretary/)). His financial disclosure showed net worth between $8.6 million and $33.4 million, a nearly $9 million payout from his law firm, $1–5 million in Bitcoin, and $856,559 in 2024 referral fees from Wisner Baum, the firm suing Merck over the Gardasil HPV vaccine ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/)).

## HHS tenure: vaccines at the center

HHS is a roughly $2 trillion agency — about 21% of the federal budget — overseeing the FDA, NIH, CDC, Medicare and Medicaid services, and substance-abuse and mental-health programs ([OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/news/2025/01/trump-administration-profile-robert-f-kennedy-jr/)). Kennedy's tenure has been defined by vaccine policy. Since taking office he has dismissed the panel responsible for vaccine recommendations and scaled back federal guidance on COVID-19 vaccination — even as a measles outbreak spreads ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-news/robert-f-kennedy-jr-halts-500-million-in-mrna-vaccine-projects-citing-shift-toward-safer-alternatives-11754486040174.html)).

His most consequential move came in August 2026. On August 5/6, he announced that HHS would cancel 22 vaccine-development contracts totaling $500 million funded through the Biomedical Advanced Research and Development Authority (BARDA), most involving mRNA technology for COVID-19, flu, and H5N1 ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-news/robert-f-kennedy-jr-halts-500-million-in-mrna-vaccine-projects-citing-shift-toward-safer-alternatives-11754486040174.html)). In a social-media video he said: "To replace the troubled mRNA programs, we're prioritizing the development of safer, broader vaccine strategies, like whole-virus vaccines and novel platforms that don't collapse when viruses mutate" ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-news/robert-f-kennedy-jr-halts-500-million-in-mrna-vaccine-projects-citing-shift-toward-safer-alternatives-11754486040174.html)). Hours later, at a press conference in Anchorage, Alaska alongside Alaska's Republican senators, he said work was underway on a "universal vaccine" designed to mimic natural immunity and protect against multiple viruses: "It could be effective — we believe it's going to be effective — against not only coronaviruses, but also flu" ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-news/robert-f-kennedy-jr-halts-500-million-in-mrna-vaccine-projects-citing-shift-toward-safer-alternatives-11754486040174.html)).

Public-health experts reacted with alarm. Mike Osterholm of the University of Minnesota said: "I don't think I've seen a more dangerous decision in public health in my 50 years in the business," while Children's Hospital of Philadelphia's Paul Offit said of mRNA COVID vaccines: "It's certainly saved millions of lives. Walking away from it now puts us at a disadvantage" ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-news/robert-f-kennedy-jr-halts-500-million-in-mrna-vaccine-projects-citing-shift-toward-safer-alternatives-11754486040174.html)). A subsequent study from the Yale Center for Infectious Disease Modeling and Analysis found that the emerging mRNA vaccines against cancer Kennedy's cuts swept up could save 49,000 American lives annually and contribute $75 billion annually to the economy; Senator Maggie Hassan, who commissioned the analysis, called Kennedy's behavior "reckless" and has called for his resignation ([U.S. Senate](https://www.hassan.senate.gov/news/press-releases/new-study-finds-mrna-vaccine-research-cancellation-by-secretary-kennedy-could-cost-nearly-50000-lives-annually)).
