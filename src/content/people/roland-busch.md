## Overview

Roland Busch is a German physicist and business executive who has been President and CEO of [Siemens](/organizations/siemens/) since February 2021, after spending his entire 27-year career to that point inside the company — from a research project head in Erlangen to chief technology officer, deputy CEO, and finally chief executive ([Munich Re](https://munichre.com/content/dam/munichre/contentlounge/website-pieces/documents/supervisory-board/Roland-Busch_CV_EN.pdf/_jcr_content/renditions/original./Roland-Busch_CV_EN.pdf); [Manufacturing Digital](https://manufacturingdigital.com/news/siemens-ceo-roland-busch-named-top-manufacturing-leader)). As CEO he has pushed Siemens from industrial conglomerate toward industrial software: launching the Xcelerator platform, buying [Altair Engineering](/organizations/altair-engineering/) and Dotmatics, and posting record profits in fiscal 2025.

Busch was born on November 22, 1964, in Erlangen, Germany ([Munich Re](https://munichre.com/content/dam/munichre/contentlounge/website-pieces/documents/supervisory-board/Roland-Busch_CV_EN.pdf/_jcr_content/renditions/original./Roland-Busch_CV_EN.pdf)). He studied physics at Friedrich Alexander University Erlangen-Nuremberg and at the University of Grenoble in France, earning his doctorate in physics in July 1993 ([Munich Re](https://munichre.com/content/dam/munichre/contentlounge/website-pieces/documents/supervisory-board/Roland-Busch_CV_EN.pdf/_jcr_content/renditions/original./Roland-Busch_CV_EN.pdf)). That scientific training, rather than a finance or consulting background, is the through-line of his career: his first Siemens job, in 1994, was as a project head in Corporate Research and Development, and he spent his early years as a fuel-cell technology expert ([Siemens AG](https://assets.new.siemens.com/siemens/assets/api/uuid:3426920c-476e-4a2f-bcbe-9c168ec835a2/210226-CV-BuschRoland-E.pdf)).

## The long climb inside Siemens

Busch's path to the top ran through nearly every kind of Siemens business. He headed the Infotainment Solutions division of Siemens VDO from 2002, then moved to Shanghai in 2005 as President and CEO of Siemens VDO Automotive Asia Pacific ([Munich Re](https://munichre.com/content/dam/munichre/contentlounge/website-pieces/documents/supervisory-board/Roland-Busch_CV_EN.pdf/_jcr_content/renditions/original./Roland-Busch_CV_EN.pdf)). Returning to Germany, he led the Mass Transit division in 2007 and became Head of Corporate Strategies in 2008 ([Munich Re](https://munichre.com/content/dam/munichre/contentlounge/website-pieces/documents/supervisory-board/Roland-Busch_CV_EN.pdf/_jcr_content/renditions/original./Roland-Busch_CV_EN.pdf)). In 2011 he joined the Managing Board, initially as CEO of the Infrastructure & Cities sector, and went on to hold the CTO, COO and Chief Sustainability Officer roles ([Siemens AG](https://assets.new.siemens.com/siemens/assets/api/uuid:3426920c-476e-4a2f-bcbe-9c168ec835a2/210226-CV-BuschRoland-E.pdf)). From 2019 he was Deputy Chair of the Managing Board and Labor Relations Director, and by September 2020 Siemens was already describing him publicly as "Deputy CEO – and future CEO" ([Siemens AG](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-writes-new-chapter-powerful-ecosystem-instead-conglomerate)). He took over as President and CEO on February 4, 2021, succeeding Joe Kaeser ([Siemens AG](https://assets.new.siemens.com/siemens/assets/api/uuid:3426920c-476e-4a2f-bcbe-9c168ec835a2/210226-CV-BuschRoland-E.pdf); [Manufacturing Digital](https://manufacturingdigital.com/news/siemens-ceo-roland-busch-named-top-manufacturing-leader)).

Outside Siemens, Busch chairs the Asia-Pacific Committee of German Business, sits on the supervisory board of Munich Re (since April 2024), chairs the university council of his alma mater FAU Erlangen-Nuremberg, and serves on the supervisory board of Siemens Healthineers ([Siemens AG](https://assets.new.siemens.com/siemens/assets/api/uuid:3426920c-476e-4a2f-bcbe-9c168ec835a2/210226-CV-BuschRoland-E.pdf)).

## CEO: Xcelerator, acquisitions, and the software pivot

Busch's CEO agenda has a single theme: combining the real and digital worlds. Its platform expression is Siemens Xcelerator, the open digital business platform launched on June 29, 2022, for customers in industry, buildings, grids and mobility, built from a curated portfolio of IoT-enabled hardware, software and services, a partner ecosystem, and a marketplace ([Siemens AG](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/node/6496)). At the launch event Busch appeared alongside NVIDIA founder Jensen Huang, whose Omniverse platform connects to Xcelerator for industrial-metaverse tools such as digital twins of factories ([Siemens AG](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/node/6496); [Manufacturing Digital](https://manufacturingdigital.com/news/siemens-ceo-roland-busch-named-top-manufacturing-leader)).

The acquisition expression of the same strategy came in 2025. Siemens completed its purchase of Altair Engineering on March 26, 2025, at an enterprise value of about $10 billion, with Busch saying the combination would create "the world's most complete AI-powered design, engineering and simulation portfolio" and extend Siemens' leadership in industrial software through its ONE Tech Company program ([Siemens AG](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-acquires-altair-create-most-complete-ai-powered-portfolio-industrial-software)). Four months later, on July 1, 2025, Siemens completed the $5.1 billion acquisition of Dotmatics, a Boston-based life-sciences R&D software company, which Busch framed as building "a new era of innovation in Life Sciences" — an end-to-end digital thread from research through production ([Siemens AG](https://news.siemens.com/pl-pl/dotmatics-closing/)).

The financial scoreboard under Busch's tenure turned in record results: in fiscal 2025, which ended September 30, 2025, Siemens' net income climbed 16 percent to €10.4 billion — a record for the third consecutive year — on revenue of €78.9 billion, up 5 percent on a comparable basis ([Siemens AG](http://assets.new.siemens.com/siemens/assets/api/uuid:4f2a7444-211a-4f8c-b01c-16f4e4cbe3b9/251113-press-release-en.pdf)). Manufacturing Digital named him the top name on its inaugural Top 100 Manufacturing Leaders list in 2026, crediting a career that "bridged physics, engineering and digital transformation" ([Manufacturing Digital](https://manufacturingdigital.com/news/siemens-ceo-roland-busch-named-top-manufacturing-leader)). At a CES 2026 keynote he summed up the pitch: "A century ago, Siemens helped build the world in the light of electricity. Now we get to do it again in the age of intelligence" ([Manufacturing Digital](https://manufacturingdigital.com/news/siemens-ceo-roland-busch-named-top-manufacturing-leader)).
