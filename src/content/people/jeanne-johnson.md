## Overview

Jeanne Johnson is the mother of [Mike Johnson](/people/mike-johnson/), the Speaker of the [U.S. House of Representatives](/organizations/us-house-of-representatives/). CNN's Fast Facts biography of the speaker lists her as his mother and names his father as Patrick "Pat" Johnson, a firefighter and hazardous materials consultant ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)). Her son was born James Michael Johnson on January 30, 1972, in Shreveport, Louisiana ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/politics/mike-johnson-fast-facts?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta)).

She is the mother of four children — Mike, Chris, Josh, and Laura — named in the family obituary published in the Shreveport Times ([Legacy.com](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/shreveporttimes/name/james-johnson-obituary?id=11898168)). The same obituary describes her as James [Patrick Johnson](/people/patrick-johnson/)'s "former wife and the mother of his children"; at his death in December 2016, his wife was Janis Gabriel Johnson, confirming that the marriage had ended in divorce ([Legacy.com](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/shreveporttimes/name/james-johnson-obituary?id=11898168)).

## Her son's own words

The most direct public statement about Jeanne Johnson comes from her son himself. In his remarks immediately after being elected Speaker of the House in October 2023, Mike Johnson said: "I want to thank my faithful mother, Jeanne Johnson, who bore me at the age of 17" ([Rev](https://www.rev.com/transcripts/mike-johnson-delivers-remarks-after-being-elected-house-speaker-transcript)). That single sentence — her name, his gratitude, and her age when she gave birth to him — is nearly the entire public record of her in her son's own words.

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not claim a birth date, birthplace, maiden name, education, occupation, religious affiliation, or later life for Jeanne Johnson — none appears in any source read in full. A tertiary reference gives her maiden name as Messina and her birth year as 1954, but no independently readable source confirms either, so they are not repeated here. The obituary shows only that her marriage to Pat Johnson had ended before his death in December 2016; it gives no divorce date.

Dedicated pages about her on sites like MyNewsGH, ABTC and The News God are SEO content farms, and they contradict each other: one describes her as a Shreveport-raised LSU education graduate who taught for several years and then became a stay-at-home mom, another calls her an estate developer who was the first in her family to go to college, divorced Mike Johnson's father after he was injured, and remarried. None of it is verified, so none of it is stated here. No independent journalism covers her life beyond the family relationship, so this profile stays short rather than inventing detail. If independent reporting ever documents her life, it can grow; until then it will not be padded.
