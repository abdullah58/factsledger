# Seth Moulton

The fastest way to understand Seth Moulton's career is to watch how it ended — or at least its latest chapter. On October 15, 2025, the Marine veteran and congressman announced he was challenging 79-year-old Sen. Ed Markey in the Massachusetts Democratic Senate primary, telling voters he did not believe Markey "should be running for another six-year term at 80 years old" and that the Democratic Party had "clung to the status quo" ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/2025/10/15/seth-moulton-challenging-sen-ed-markey-primary/86708697007/)). Ten and a half months later, voters rejected the argument decisively: on September 1, 2026, Markey defeated Moulton roughly two-to-one, 64.9% to 35.1%, with the Associated Press calling the race at 8:31 p.m., just over half an hour after polls closed ([Election Desk](https://electiondesk.org/2026/09/03/massachusetts-primary-results-markey-moulton-minogue-2026/)). Moulton conceded to supporters gathered in Dorchester, saying he had called Markey to congratulate him and pledging support "this November to protect our democracy" ([Election Desk](https://electiondesk.org/2026/09/03/massachusetts-primary-results-markey-moulton-minogue-2026/)).

## The generational-change gamble

Moulton built the campaign around one idea: that an 80-year-old senator who had been in Congress since 1976 was not the right person for the moment. In his launch video he said, "Even more, I don't think someone who's been in Congress for half a century is the right person to meet this moment and win the future" ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/2025/10/15/seth-moulton-challenging-sen-ed-markey-primary/86708697007/)). Markey, who had served in the House from 1976 to 2013 and won the 2013 special Senate election, countered by leaning on his progressive record — including co-authoring the Green New Deal resolution — and his seniority on the Commerce, Health, and Environment and Public Works committees ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/2025/10/15/seth-moulton-challenging-sen-ed-markey-primary/86708697007/); [Election Desk](https://electiondesk.org/2026/09/03/massachusetts-primary-results-markey-moulton-minogue-2026/)). The argument was not new: in 2020 Markey had faced a similar age-and-generational-turnover challenge from then-Rep. Joe Kennedy III and won by about 10 percentage points ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/2025/10/15/seth-moulton-challenging-sen-ed-markey-primary/86708697007/)).

This time the margin was far wider. Pre-election polling had signaled the blowout — a final Emerson College survey put Markey ahead 60% to 33%, and an August UMass Amherst survey showed him leading 60% to 32% among likely Democratic primary voters with 82% of Democrats approving of his job performance — and the result tracked those numbers closely ([Election Desk](https://electiondesk.org/2026/09/03/massachusetts-primary-results-markey-moulton-minogue-2026/)). Public radio coverage the night of the vote noted that Markey, the commonwealth's 80-year-old junior senator, now had a clear path to a third six-year term in a heavily Democratic-leaning state, with 47-year-old Moulton's generational-change framing falling short just as Kennedy's had in 2020 ([WAMC](https://www.wamc.org/news/2026-09-01/us-sen-ed-markey-wins-massachusetts-democratic-primary-over-opponent-who-called-for-change)). Moulton vacated his 6th District House seat to make the run ([Election Desk](https://electiondesk.org/2026/09/03/massachusetts-primary-results-markey-moulton-minogue-2026/)).

## "It's just insane": the Witkoff comments

Before the Senate run, Moulton had become one of the most outspoken congressional critics of the Trump administration's Russia posture. In a Friday interview with former Fox News host [Tucker Carlson](/people/tucker-carlson/) in March 2025, special envoy [Steve Witkoff](/people/steve-witkoff/) praised Russian President Vladimir Putin as a "super smart guy" he takes "at his word," said he doesn't see Putin as "a bad guy," and implied Russia had legitimate claim to four Ukrainian territories — Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia, and Kherson — that it illegally seized ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/2025/03/24/seth-moulton-witkoff-trump-putin-interview/82635715007/)). Witkoff also described a meeting with Putin that "got personal," including a portrait of Trump commissioned from a "leading Russian artist" that Putin asked Witkoff to deliver to the White House, and claimed Putin had "prayed" for Trump after the assassination attempt ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/2025/03/24/seth-moulton-witkoff-trump-putin-interview/82635715007/)).

Appearing on CNN the following Monday, Moulton — a former Marine — responded bluntly: "I mean, it's just insane... it's insane that you have a U.S. negotiator taking the side of our enemy" ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/2025/03/24/seth-moulton-witkoff-trump-putin-interview/82635715007/)). "He's literally negotiating for the other side, negotiating for the aggressor, negotiating for the violator of international law," Moulton said ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/2025/03/24/seth-moulton-witkoff-trump-putin-interview/82635715007/)). The comments came two days before U.S. talks to end the war were set to restart in Saudi Arabia, and pointed to what the reporting described as an unprecedented shift in the U.S. stance toward Russia ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/2025/03/24/seth-moulton-witkoff-trump-putin-interview/82635715007/)).

## From Baghdad to the House

Moulton joined the Marines in 2001, just days after graduating from college and months before the September 11 attacks ([The Well News](https://www.thewellnews.com/2020-elections/a-decorated-veteran-seth-moultons-presidential-campaign-focuses-on-defense-and-national-security/)). As the leader of an infantry platoon, he was among the first Americans to reach Baghdad in 2003, and he served four tours in a war he did not agree with — "but he was proud to go, so no one had to go in his place" ([The Well News](https://www.thewellnews.com/2020-elections/a-decorated-veteran-seth-moultons-presidential-campaign-focuses-on-defense-and-national-security/)). He was decorated twice with the Bronze Star medal for valor and received the Navy and Marine Corps Commendation medal for valor — and chose not to publicly disclose the decorations until pressed by the Boston Globe ([The Well News](https://www.thewellnews.com/2020-elections/a-decorated-veteran-seth-moultons-presidential-campaign-focuses-on-defense-and-national-security/)).

After returning from Iraq, Moulton earned master's degrees in business and public policy in a dual program at Harvard University, then worked in the private sector in Texas on the country's first high-speed rail line ([The Well News](https://www.thewellnews.com/2020-elections/a-decorated-veteran-seth-moultons-presidential-campaign-focuses-on-defense-and-national-security/)). He ran for Congress in 2014 on a platform of new-generation leadership and won, becoming "the only Democrat to unseat an incumbent in a primary in 2014" by defeating nine-term incumbent John Tierney ([The Well News](https://www.thewellnews.com/2020-elections/a-decorated-veteran-seth-moultons-presidential-campaign-focuses-on-defense-and-national-security/)). In his early House terms he passed bipartisan bills including the Faster Care for Veterans Act and the Modernizing Government Travel Act, and was named the most effective freshman Democrat by the Center for Effective Lawmaking ([The Well News](https://www.thewellnews.com/2020-elections/a-decorated-veteran-seth-moultons-presidential-campaign-focuses-on-defense-and-national-security/)).

In 2019 he sought the 2020 Democratic presidential nomination, campaigning on defense and national security, before withdrawing ([The Well News](https://www.thewellnews.com/2020-elections/a-decorated-veteran-seth-moultons-presidential-campaign-focuses-on-defense-and-national-security/)). He lives in Salem, Massachusetts, with his wife Elizabeth ([The Well News](https://www.thewellnews.com/2020-elections/a-decorated-veteran-seth-moultons-presidential-campaign-focuses-on-defense-and-national-security/)).
