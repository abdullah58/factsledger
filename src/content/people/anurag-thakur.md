**Anurag Singh Thakur** is a five-term Bharatiya Janata Party MP from Hamirpur, Himachal Pradesh, who served as Union Minister of Information & Broadcasting and of Youth Affairs & Sports from July 2021 to June 2024 ([The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/economy/anurag-thakur-elevated-to-cabinet-minister-post/article35206937.ece/amp/); [DNA India](https://www.dnaindia.com/india/report-pm-modi-cabinet-30-portfolios-allocated-check-full-list-here-3093013)). On 11 October 2023, in that role, he launched the trailer of the animated series [Krish, Trish and Baltiboy](/creative-works/krish-trish-and-baltiboy/) — subtitled *Bharat Hain Hum* — in New Delhi ([ANI via Lokmat Times](https://www.lokmattimes.com/entertainment/union-minister-anurag-thakur-launches-enthralling-trailer-of-animated-series-ktb-bharat-hain-hum/)). Before national politics he was a cricket administrator — president of the Board of Control for Cricket in India from May 2016 until the Supreme Court removed him in January 2017 ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/from-bcci-to-union-cabinet-anurag-thakur-enters-big-league/articleshow/69585917.cms?from=mdr); [The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/sports/cricket/supreme-court-sacks-bcci-chief-secretary-anurag-thakur-says-all-the-best-4456134/)).

Born 24 October 1974 in Hamirpur district's Samirpur village, Thakur entered the Lok Sabha in a May 2008 by-election after his father, former chief minister Prem Kumar Dhumal, resigned the seat ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/from-bcci-to-union-cabinet-anurag-thakur-enters-big-league/articleshow/69585917.cms?from=mdr)). He then won it in every general election through 2024 ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/hamirpur-in-himachal-amit-shahs-remarks-boost-anurag-thakurs-campaign-stakes-high-for-sukhu/articleshow/110495369.cms); [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/shimla/himachal-pradesh-election-results-2024-live-updates-lok-sabha-general-election-poll-result-2024-winner-real-time-vote-counting-bjp-congress-kangana-ranaut-vikramaditya-singh/liveblog/110683487.cms)). He led the BJP's youth wing, became the youngest party chief whip in the Lok Sabha, chaired the Standing Committee on Information Technology, was commissioned into the Territorial Army, and received a Sansad Ratna Award ([Government of India — Bureau of Outreach and Communication](http://www.davp.nic.in/hmib_profile.html)).

## The Krish, Trish and Baltiboy trailer launch

What most separates this profile from the generic Thakur bios crowding the search results is the 11 October 2023 trailer launch. As Union Minister for Information & Broadcasting, Thakur launched *Krish, Trish and Baltiboy – Bharat Hain Hum* in New Delhi — the animated anthology about lesser-known freedom fighters produced by his ministry's [Central Bureau of Communication](/organizations/central-bureau-of-communication/) with Graphiti Studio ([ANI via Lokmat Times](https://www.lokmattimes.com/entertainment/union-minister-anurag-thakur-launches-enthralling-trailer-of-animated-series-ktb-bharat-hain-hum/)). ANI names co-creators Munjal Shroff and Tilak Shetty, notes the Season 1 format (26 eleven-minute episodes premiering 15 October on Doordarshan, Prime Video and Netflix), and records the 12 Indian and 7 international languages ([ANI via Lokmat Times](https://www.lokmattimes.com/entertainment/union-minister-anurag-thakur-launches-enthralling-trailer-of-animated-series-ktb-bharat-hain-hum/)).

At the launch Thakur named fighters such as Birsa Munda and Rani Abbakka as figures "students are unaware of", said 30 percent of the featured heroes are women, and announced the series would be screened for all Parliamentarians ([ANI via Lokmat Times](https://www.lokmattimes.com/entertainment/union-minister-anurag-thakur-launches-enthralling-trailer-of-animated-series-ktb-bharat-hain-hum/)). A [Press Information Bureau](/organizations/press-information-bureau/) release on the launch also exists — it is the release this site's Krish, Trish and Baltiboy profile cites for the trailer-launch facts; the launch itself sits inside the Azadi Ka Amrit Mahotsav campaign Thakur tied to [Narendra Modi](/people/narendra-modi/)'s vision ([ANI via Lokmat Times](https://www.lokmattimes.com/entertainment/union-minister-anurag-thakur-launches-enthralling-trailer-of-animated-series-ktb-bharat-hain-hum/)).

## Five straight wins from Hamirpur

Thakur's parliamentary record is a clean sweep: five consecutive victories from Hamirpur — a 2008 by-election plus the 2009, 2014, 2019 and 2024 general elections ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/from-bcci-to-union-cabinet-anurag-thakur-enters-big-league/articleshow/69585917.cms?from=mdr); [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/shimla/himachal-pradesh-election-results-2024-live-updates-lok-sabha-general-election-poll-result-2024-winner-real-time-vote-counting-bjp-congress-kangana-ranaut-vikramaditya-singh/liveblog/110683487.cms)). His 6,07,068 votes in 2024 against Congress rival Satpal Singh Raizada's 4,24,711 — a 1,82,357-vote margin per Election Commission figures ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/shimla/himachal-pradesh-election-results-2024-live-updates-lok-sabha-general-election-poll-result-2024-winner-real-time-vote-counting-bjp-congress-kangana-ranaut-vikramaditya-singh/liveblog/110683487.cms)) — was less than half his record 2019 margin of 3,99,572 ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/hamirpur-in-himachal-amit-shahs-remarks-boost-anurag-thakurs-campaign-stakes-high-for-sukhu/articleshow/110495369.cms)). That softening matters: it came in an election the BJP still swept across Himachal Pradesh, and The Economic Times described the Hamirpur contest as unusually competitive, with the Congress internal revolt and the Agniveer scheme's salience among the constituency's large ex-serviceman population ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/hamirpur-in-himachal-amit-shahs-remarks-boost-anurag-thakurs-campaign-stakes-high-for-sukhu/articleshow/110495369.cms)).

## The ministerial arc: from Finance to I&B, then out

Thakur joined the Union government in 2019 as Minister of State for Finance and Corporate Affairs (31 May 2019–7 July 2021, under Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman) ([Government of India — Bureau of Outreach and Communication](http://www.davp.nic.in/hmib_profile.html)). On 7 July 2021, President Ram Nath Kovind administered his Cabinet oath, handing him Information & Broadcasting and Youth Affairs & Sports — the sports charge arriving just ahead of the Tokyo Olympics ([The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/economy/anurag-thakur-elevated-to-cabinet-minister-post/article35206937.ece/amp/)).

| Period | Office |
| ------ | ------ |
| 31 May 2019 – 7 July 2021 | Minister of State, Finance & Corporate Affairs |
| 7 July 2021 – June 2024 | Cabinet Minister, Information & Broadcasting + Youth Affairs & Sports |
| June 2024 – present | Member of Parliament (Lok Sabha), Hamirpur — no ministerial portfolio |

The arc ended with the 2024 election: the 10 June 2024 allocation, directed by President Droupadi Murmu on Prime Minister Modi's advice, gave Information & Broadcasting to Ashwini Vaishnaw — and Thakur, though re-elected, was not retained in the new Council of Ministers ([PTI via The Telegraph](https://telegraphindia.com/amp/india/modi-new-cabinet-2024-shah-rajnath-sitharaman-jaishankar-shivraj-khattar-get-key-ministries/cid/2026022); [DNA India](https://www.dnaindia.com/india/report-pm-modi-cabinet-30-portfolios-allocated-check-full-list-here-3093013)). That transition is stated plainly because several tertiary biographies still describe him as the sitting Union Minister; they are stale (see openItems).

## Cricket, the courtroom, and the Territorial Army

Before the cabinet, Thakur built a parallel career in cricket administration, rising to BCCI president in May 2016 — until the Supreme Court removed him and secretary Ajay Shirke on 2 January 2017, holding Thakur had rendered himself "unfit" by obstructing its orders, and issuing a show-cause notice on perjury and contempt charges for allegedly lying under oath to block the court-appointed Lodha Committee's reforms ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/elections/lok-sabha/india/from-bcci-to-union-cabinet-anurag-thakur-enters-big-league/articleshow/69585917.cms?from=mdr); [The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/sports/cricket/supreme-court-sacks-bcci-chief-secretary-anurag-thakur-says-all-the-best-4456134/)). The coda came in early 2026: a bench of Chief Justice Surya Kant and Justice Joymalya Bagchi lifted the 2017 "cease and desist" bar, clarifying it was never meant as a permanent disqualification, with the contempt proceedings long dropped ([The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/sports/sc-lifts-ban-on-anurag-thakur-to-associate-with-bcci-1503552490.html)).

In uniform, he was commissioned as a Lieutenant in the Territorial Army's 124 Sikh Regiment in 2016 — "the first serving BJP Member of Parliament to join as a regular officer" — and promoted to Captain in 2021 ([Government of India — Bureau of Outreach and Communication](http://www.davp.nic.in/hmib_profile.html)). In Parliament he was the youngest BJP chief whip in the 16th Lok Sabha, chaired the Standing Committee on Information Technology, sat on the Public Accounts Committee, and served three consecutive terms as Bharatiya Janata Yuva Morcha national president — the only BJYM president to serve under three BJP presidents (Amit Shah, Rajnath Singh, Nitin Gadkari) ([Government of India — Bureau of Outreach and Communication](http://www.davp.nic.in/hmib_profile.html)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Was Anurag Thakur the Information & Broadcasting Minister when he launched the KTB trailer?**
Yes. He launched the *Krish, Trish and Baltiboy – Bharat Hain Hum* trailer on 11 October 2023 explicitly as Union Minister for Information & Broadcasting. He held that portfolio from 7 July 2021 until the June 2024 government transition, when Ashwini Vaishnaw took it over.

**Is Anurag Thakur still a Union Minister?**
No. He won Hamirpur for a fifth consecutive time in June 2024 but was not retained in the new Council of Ministers. He continues as a BJP Member of Parliament from Himachal Pradesh's Hamirpur constituency, which he has represented continuously since the May 2008 by-election.

**Why was he removed as BCCI president?**
The Supreme Court removed him on 2 January 2017 for obstructing the Lodha Committee's governance reforms, issuing a show-cause notice on charges of perjury and contempt. The contempt proceedings were later dropped, and in early 2026 the Court lifted the bar on his associating with the BCCI.

**What connects him to Himachal Pradesh?**
Nearly everything geographic in his career: he was born in Samirpur village of Hamirpur district, is the son of former Himachal chief minister Prem Kumar Dhumal, and has won the Hamirpur Lok Sabha seat five times in a row from 2008 to 2024.
