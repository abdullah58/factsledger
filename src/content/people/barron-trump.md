## Overview

Barron Trump is the only child of [Donald Trump](https://factsledger.com/people/donald-trump/) and Melania Trump, the youngest of Donald Trump's children, and — as of fall 2026 — a junior at New York University's Stern School of Business ([Palm Beach Post](https://www.palmbeachpost.com/story/news/2026/09/08/celebrity-kids-college-tom-brady-moynahan-kai-trump-tiger-woods-florida-barron/91652861007/); [Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/03/19/barron-trump-birthday-melania-york-jersey-how-tall/82540351007/)). He has spent most of his life off-camera: until he turned 18 he was kept largely out of the spotlight, a reflection of his mother's protective approach ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/03/19/barron-trump-birthday-melania-york-jersey-how-tall/82540351007/)). The one substantive public contribution on the record is an unusual one for a then-18-year-old — he was the behind-the-scenes "podcast adviser" who steered his father's 2024 presidential campaign onto the long-form podcasts that defined its media strategy ([Livemint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-presidential-election-2024-barron-trump-the-gen-z-adviser-behind-his-father-donald-trumps-podcast-strategy-11729876527731.html)).

## Early life and schooling

Barron Trump was born in New York on March 20, 2006 — he turned 20 on March 20, 2026 ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/03/19/barron-trump-birthday-melania-york-jersey-how-tall/82540351007/)). He attended Columbia Grammar and Preparatory School in Manhattan, then moved with his mother to the White House in the summer of 2017, at age 11, to attend St. Andrew's Episcopal School in Potomac, Maryland. After his father lost the 2020 election, the family moved to Mar-a-Lago in Palm Beach, Florida, where Barron attended Oxbridge Academy in West Palm Beach. He graduated on May 17, 2024; the only family members present were Donald Trump, Melania Trump, and Melania's father, Viktor Knavs ([Akron Beacon Journal](https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/2025/03/19/barron-trump-birthday-melania-york-jersey-how-tall/82540351007/); [Palm Beach Post](https://www.palmbeachpost.com/story/news/2026/09/08/celebrity-kids-college-tom-brady-moynahan-kai-trump-tiger-woods-florida-barron/91652861007/)).

## The 2024 campaign's podcast adviser

Though he stayed off the campaign trail, Barron stepped into an unofficial role behind the scenes: a "podcast adviser" to his father's media outreach, according to sources close to the family cited by ABC News. He played a significant part in introducing the 78-year-old candidate to the podcast medium, which the campaign shifted toward as an alternative to traditional television appearances ([Livemint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-presidential-election-2024-barron-trump-the-gen-z-adviser-behind-his-father-donald-trumps-podcast-strategy-11729876527731.html)).

Senior campaign adviser Jason Miller commended the effort, describing Barron's podcast recommendations as "ratings gold" in a Politico Playbook Deep Dive interview: "Hats off to the young man. Every single recommendation he's had has broken the internet." His first recommendation was Logan Paul's *Impaulsive*, on which Trump appeared in June 2024 — the beginning of a new media approach that later extended to the Nelk Boys, Theo Von, and *The Joe Rogan Experience* ([Livemint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-presidential-election-2024-barron-trump-the-gen-z-adviser-behind-his-father-donald-trumps-podcast-strategy-11729876527731.html)). Trump himself praised Barron's political instincts in an interview with Philadelphia's Talk Radio 1210 WPHT, calling him a "great student" with a keen understanding of current issues ([Livemint](https://www.livemint.com/news/us-presidential-election-2024-barron-trump-the-gen-z-adviser-behind-his-father-donald-trumps-podcast-strategy-11729876527731.html)).

## NYU and the 2025–26 school years

Barron began classes at NYU's Stern School of Business in September 2024, going viral when he appeared with Secret Service protection on the Greenwich Village campus ([Palm Beach Post](https://www.palmbeachpost.com/story/news/2026/09/08/celebrity-kids-college-tom-brady-moynahan-kai-trump-tiger-woods-florida-barron/91652861007/)). In a January 2026 interview, Melania Trump told Fox Business Network that her son was in his second year at Stern and that he "loves it" ([Palm Beach Post](https://www.palmbeachpost.com/story/news/2026/09/08/celebrity-kids-college-tom-brady-moynahan-kai-trump-tiger-woods-florida-barron/91652861007/)).

His whereabouts as a student have been a subject of careful reporting. According to the [Palm Beach Post](https://factsledger.com/organizations/palm-beach-post/), he did not go back to New York in fall 2025; reports placed him at a different NYU campus instead. Media outlets reported a move to NYU's Washington, D.C., academic center, but neither NYU nor the White House confirmed it. As of fall 2026 he is a junior at the Stern School of Business and projected to graduate with the class of 2028 ([Palm Beach Post](https://www.palmbeachpost.com/story/news/2026/09/08/celebrity-kids-college-tom-brady-moynahan-kai-trump-tiger-woods-florida-barron/91652861007/)).

## His mother's on-record glimpses

Melania Trump has offered rare public comments on her son's college years. After the January 2026 "loves it" remark, she appeared on Fox Business' *The Big Money Show* on September 23, 2026, and spoke about the shrinking family time of the second term: "It's less and less family time, [and there's] suffering because of it. But that's okay. We are here to work, to do a good job, and that's what matters." On Barron, she added: "Barron is already a young man, so sometimes he's maybe even happy that I just want to do my stuff. And that's okay" ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/global-trends/us-news-its-less-and-less-family-time-melania-trump-hints-at-changing-relationship-with-her-20-year-old-son-barron/articleshow/134490612.cms?from=mdr)).
