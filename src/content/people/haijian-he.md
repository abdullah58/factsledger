## Overview

Haijian He — referred to as Henry He in English-language press coverage — has been chief financial officer of [Baidu](/organizations/baidu/) since 1 July 2025 ([Baidu](https://technode.global/prnasia/baidu-announces-management-changes/); [Benzinga](https://www.webull.com.au/news-detail/15503023210947584)). He arrived from Kingsoft Cloud, where he had been CFO since January 2020, after a decade in investment banking at [Goldman Sachs](/organizations/goldman-sachs/), Bank of America Merrill Lynch and Citigroup ([Baidu](https://technode.global/prnasia/baidu-announces-management-changes/)). Within weeks of joining Baidu he also became chairman of the board of [iQIYI](/organizations/iqiyi/), its video-streaming subsidiary ([AInvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/iqiyi-announces-board-reshuffle-leadership-2508-38/)). He is the finance chief Baidu's co-founder [Robin Li](/people/robin-li/) is relying on to prove that the company's AI business can pay for itself as its legacy advertising business shrinks — and, since November 2026, a named individual defendant in a securities class action alleging Baidu overstated exactly that ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/09/3398632/3080/en/levi-korsinsky-reminds-baidu-inc-investors-of-the-pending-class-action-lawsuit-with-a-lead-plaintiff-deadline-of-november-13-2026-bidu.html)).

## Investment banking and Kingsoft Cloud

He's career before Baidu was built in two halves: deal-making, then running a listed company's finances. He worked successively at Bank of America Merrill Lynch and Citigroup Global Markets from 2010 to 2015, then joined Goldman Sachs (Asia) in September 2015 as an executive director, working first in the TMT — telecommunications, media and technology — group and then in mergers and acquisitions, until January 2020 ([Baidu](https://technode.global/prnasia/baidu-announces-management-changes/)).

In January 2020 he moved to Kingsoft Cloud Holdings as executive director and chief financial officer, a role he held for five and a half years ([Baidu](https://technode.global/prnasia/baidu-announces-management-changes/)). His education matches the banking profile: bachelor's and master's degrees in electronic engineering from Southeast University, an MBA from the University of Chicago, the Advanced Management Program at [Harvard University](/universities/harvard-university/)'s Business School, and a Chartered Financial Analyst charter ([Baidu](https://technode.global/prnasia/baidu-announces-management-changes/)). He also serves as an independent non-executive director of Sipai Health Technology and Sunwoda EVB ([Baidu](https://technode.global/prnasia/baidu-announces-management-changes/)).

## Baidu CFO: the AI payback case

Baidu announced He's appointment on 1 July 2025, effective immediately, with interim CFO Junjie He moving to senior vice president for human resources and administration ([Baidu](https://technode.global/prnasia/baidu-announces-management-changes/)). Robin Li welcomed him as bringing "a proven track record of outstanding leadership in driving business growth, enhancing operational quality, and strengthening corporate governance" ([Baidu](https://technode.global/prnasia/baidu-announces-management-changes/)). The timing was deliberate: Baidu's advertising engine was fading while its AI businesses — cloud, chips and applications — were growing, and the company needed a CFO who could fund the transition and explain it to investors.

He made that case publicly in a Bloomberg Television interview in 2026, telling the channel that Baidu's investment in AI could start generating profits and cash payback in the near term, with the profit margin on AI revenue matching that of the legacy search business ([Benzinga](https://www.webull.com.au/news-detail/15503023210947584)). AI revenue — cloud computing and applications — then generated over half of Baidu's sales, and He expected the upward trend to continue ([Benzinga](https://www.webull.com.au/news-detail/15503023210947584)). His sharpest claim was about capital discipline: investments in new GPU clusters hosting AI services "could fully pay for themselves in two to three years", a line that echoed rival Alibaba ([Benzinga](https://www.webull.com.au/news-detail/15503023210947584)). "Our cloud revenue will be at a good level of increasing — and at least faster than industry average in the next few quarters — and our capex spending will be lower than the market's expectation," he said. "So a smarter way, less spending, higher growth." ([Benzinga](https://www.webull.com.au/news-detail/15503023210947584))

The context was unforgiving. Baidu faced intense competition from bigger Chinese AI rivals including Alibaba, Tencent and ByteDance, its ERNIE models had fallen behind open-weight competitors such as Alibaba's and Moonshot's, and users were drifting to AI chatbots like ByteDance's Doubao ([Benzinga](https://www.webull.com.au/news-detail/15503023210947584)). Baidu had just reported second-quarter revenue of 31.3 billion yuan ($4.62 billion), down 4% year over year and below the $4.65 billion analyst estimate ([Benzinga](https://www.webull.com.au/news-detail/15503023210947584)). He has also spearheaded plans for an independent listing of Baidu's AI chip unit [Kunlunxin](/organizations/kunlunxin/), a $5 billion share repurchase program and the company's first-ever dividend policy, and has said he is considering capital-market options for several other Baidu assets without giving specifics ([Benzinga](https://www.webull.com.au/news-detail/15503023210947584)).

## iQIYI chairmanship and the 2026 class action

A month after joining Baidu, He added a second chairmanship. On 1 August 2025, iQIYI announced a board reshuffle in which Junjie He and Shanshan Cui resigned from the board and were replaced by Haijian He — who became board chairman and joined the compensation committee — and Zhanbing Xu ([AInvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/iqiyi-announces-board-reshuffle-leadership-2508-38/)). The move put Baidu's new CFO directly in charge of the governance of its video-streaming subsidiary.

Fifteen months later, the AI payback story He had been telling investors became the subject of litigation. A securities class action filed in the US District Court for the Southern District of New York names He and Robin Li as individual defendants, alleging materially false and misleading statements between 18 November 2025 and 17 August 2026 ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/09/3398632/3080/en/levi-korsinsky-reminds-baidu-inc-investors-of-the-pending-class-action-lawsuit-with-a-lead-plaintiff-deadline-of-november-13-2026-bidu.html)). The complaint asserts control-person claims against both men under Section 20(a) of the Securities Exchange Act, alongside Section 10(b) and Rule 10b-5 claims, on the theory that they had authority over Baidu's SEC reports, press releases and analyst presentations and access to non-public information about the pace of the legacy marketing decline relative to AI revenue ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/09/3398632/3080/en/levi-korsinsky-reminds-baidu-inc-investors-of-the-pending-class-action-lawsuit-with-a-lead-plaintiff-deadline-of-november-13-2026-bidu.html)). Baidu's American Depositary Shares fell $13.25, or 12.73%, to $90.87 on 18 August 2026, and the window to apply for lead plaintiff closed on 13 November 2026 ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/09/3398632/3080/en/levi-korsinsky-reminds-baidu-inc-investors-of-the-pending-class-action-lawsuit-with-a-lead-plaintiff-deadline-of-november-13-2026-bidu.html)). These are allegations, not findings, and this profile states them as such: whether He's payback case was a reasonable forecast or a misleading assurance is now a question for the court.
