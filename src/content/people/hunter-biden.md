## Overview

Hunter Biden is an American artist and author — and the son of former U.S. president [Joe Biden](/people/joe-biden/) — whose name spent a decade as shorthand for America's political warfare. In 2024 he was convicted of federal gun felonies in Delaware and pleaded guilty to federal tax charges in California; that December, his father pardoned him unconditionally. He re-emerged in 2026 with a strikingly blunt media tour: a sit-down with [Tucker Carlson](/people/tucker-carlson/) in which Carlson called Donald Trump "flamboyantly corrupt," talk of a possible 2028 presidential run, and — weeks later — disbarment in Connecticut. ([The Independent](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/tucker-carlson-claims-israel-was-pushing-hunter-biden-laptop-story-that-obsessed-trump-and-fox)) ([Associated Press](https://english.alarabiya.net/News/united-states/2025/12/15/hunter-biden-disbarred-in-connecticut-after-gun-and-tax-convictions))

## Early life, education, and the Navy

Biden's childhood was marked by the December 1972 car crash that killed his mother and his infant sister, weeks after his father was first elected to the U.S. Senate from Delaware; he and his older brother Beau survived with serious injuries. ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/drink-drugs-death-hunter-biden-bares-it-all-in-upcoming-memoir/articleshow/81824180.cms)) He graduated from Yale Law School in 1996 and was admitted to the Connecticut bar the following year. ([Associated Press](https://english.alarabiya.net/News/united-states/2025/12/15/hunter-biden-disbarred-in-connecticut-after-gun-and-tax-convictions)) In 2013 he joined the U.S. Navy Reserve, but was discharged in 2014 after a positive test for cocaine — at age 44, according to one account. ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/drink-drugs-death-hunter-biden-bares-it-all-in-upcoming-memoir/articleshow/81824180.cms)) ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/i-may-have-to-joe-bidens-son-hunter-hints-at-running-for-2028-us-presidential-elections/articleshow/133823287.cms))

## Addiction, the memoir, and Burisma

Biden has spoken openly about decades of alcohol and drug addiction. He described buying crack cocaine on the streets of Washington, D.C., drinking heavily in his 20s, going to rehab, and relapsing after Beau died of brain cancer in 2015 at age 46. ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/drink-drugs-death-hunter-biden-bares-it-all-in-upcoming-memoir/articleshow/81824180.cms)) He told his gun-trial jury-era press that he had been sober since 2019, and by 2021 was living as an artist in Los Angeles. ([Associated Press](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20240612-191342/)) ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/drink-drugs-death-hunter-biden-bares-it-all-in-upcoming-memoir/articleshow/81824180.cms))

In April 2021 he published *Beautiful Things*, a memoir issued by Gallery Books, an imprint of Simon & Schuster, that recounts his addiction, his grief, and the 1972 crash. ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/drink-drugs-death-hunter-biden-bares-it-all-in-upcoming-memoir/articleshow/81824180.cms)) The book also addresses his seat on the board of Burisma, the Ukrainian energy company that made him a fixture of Republican attacks during the Trump years. He insisted in its pages that he did "nothing unethical" and disputed that the board seat showed poor judgment, writing: "The episode that led to the impeachment of a president and landed me in the heart of the decade's biggest political fable is most remarkable for its epic banality." ([The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/drink-drugs-death-hunter-biden-bares-it-all-in-upcoming-memoir/articleshow/81824180.cms))

## The gun conviction

On June 11, 2024, a federal jury in Wilmington convicted Biden on all three felonies tied to his October 2018 purchase of a revolver, finding he had lied on the mandatory purchase form by claiming he was not an unlawful user of or addicted to drugs. ([Associated Press](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20240612-191342/)) He faced up to 25 years in prison. The trial aired painful family testimony — including from Hallie Biden, Beau's widow, with whom Hunter had later been in a relationship — about his crack cocaine use. His defense lawyer was Abbe Lowell, and the case was brought by Special Counsel David Weiss after a 2023 plea deal collapsed when Judge Maryellen Noreika questioned its unusual terms. ([Associated Press](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20240612-191342/)) At the [time](/organizations/time/), Joe Biden said he would accept the verdict and would not pardon his son.

## The tax guilty plea

Three months later, on September 5, 2024, Biden pleaded guilty in Los Angeles to all nine federal tax counts against him — misdemeanors and felonies — hours after jury selection was supposed to begin in a trial accusing him of failing to pay at least $1.4 million in taxes. ([Associated Press](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20240906-209563/)) He did so with no deal from prosecutors. The morning had opened with a legal twist: his lawyers offered an Alford plea, under which he would maintain his innocence while conceding the evidence, and prosecutors objected, telling the judge that "Hunter Biden is not entitled to plead guilty on special terms that apply only to him." Prosecutor Leo Wise added: "Hunter Biden is not innocent. Hunter Biden is guilty." After a break, Biden pleaded guilty outright. ([Associated Press](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20240906-209563/))

"I will not subject my family to more pain, more invasions of privacy and needless embarrassment," he said in a statement. "For all I have put them through over the years, I can spare them this, and so I have decided to plead guilty." ([Associated Press](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20240906-209563/)) The charges carried up to 17 years and $1.35 million in fines; sentencing was set for December 16 before U.S. District Judge Mark Scarsi, a Trump nominee. The indictment alleged a four-year scheme of lavish spending — on strippers, luxury hotels, and exotic cars — or, as prosecutors put it, "in short, everything but his taxes." Biden said addiction explained but did not excuse his failures, and that he had since paid every penny of back taxes with penalties. ([Associated Press](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/news-services/ap/20240906-209563/))

## The pardon

Late on the evening of Sunday, December 1, 2024, Joe Biden pardoned his son — reversing his own categorical pledge. In June he had told reporters, "I abide by the jury decision. I will do that and I will not pardon him," and as recently as November 8 his press secretary Karine Jean-Pierre had said, "Our answer stands, which is no." ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/some-democrats-are-frustrated-over-joe-biden-reversing-course-and-pardoning-his-son-hunter/)) The executive grant of clemency, recorded with the Justice Department, gave "Robert Hunter Biden" a "full and unconditional pardon" for offenses committed or taken part in from January 1, 2014 through December 1, 2024 — expressly including the two cases prosecuted by Special Counsel Weiss, docket 1:23-cr-00061-MN in Delaware and docket 2:23-CR-00599-MCS-1 in California. ([U.S. Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/d9/2024-12/biden_warrant.pdf))

In his statement, the elder Biden said "raw politics has infected this process and it led to a miscarriage of justice," arguing the charges arose only after congressional opponents "instigated them to attack me and oppose my election." He concluded: "No reasonable person who looks at the facts of Hunter's cases can reach any other conclusion than Hunter was singled out only because he is my son — and that is wrong." ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/some-democrats-are-frustrated-over-joe-biden-reversing-course-and-pardoning-his-son-hunter/)) The reversal drew fire from some Democrats — Colorado governor Jared Polis called it a "bad precedent," while Senators Michael Bennet and Gary Peters and Representative Greg Stanton criticized it in similar terms — though defenders including DNC chairman Jaime Harrison argued most people would see it as right, and Jill Biden said, "Of course I support the pardon of my son." ([Associated Press](https://srnnews.com/some-democrats-are-frustrated-over-joe-biden-reversing-course-and-pardoning-his-son-hunter/))

## Disbarment

The pardon ended the criminal cases but not the professional fallout. Biden was disbarred in Washington, D.C., in May 2026, and that September he was disbarred in Connecticut under a consent agreement in which he admitted attorney misconduct — though not criminal wrongdoing — before Judge Trial Referee Patrick L. Carroll III in Waterbury. ([Associated Press](https://english.alarabiya.net/News/united-states/2025/12/15/hunter-biden-disbarred-in-connecticut-after-gun-and-tax-convictions)) The ethics violations cited were "dishonesty, fraud, deceit or misrepresentation"; his lawyer was Ross Garber, the complainant was Paul Dorsey, and the Chief Disciplinary Counsel's Leanne Larson cited the presidential pardon itself in the proceedings. He had been admitted to the Connecticut bar in 1997, a year after graduating from Yale Law School. ([Associated Press](https://english.alarabiya.net/News/united-states/2025/12/15/hunter-biden-disbarred-in-connecticut-after-gun-and-tax-convictions))

## The 2026 media tour

Biden's loudest re-emergence came on August 10, 2026, as the guest on The Tucker Carlson Show. Carlson — who had backed Trump in 2024 — used the interview to call the president a "flamboyantly corrupt leader," part of his break with Trump over the Iran war that began in late February 2026, a war Carlson said he had tried in vain to prevent. ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/08/10/us-news/tucker-carlson-on-the-war-path-with-trump-over-iran-but-insiders-dismiss-2028-buzz-from-brain-injured-press/)) Insiders dismissed talk of a Carlson third-party 2028 run. ([New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/08/10/us-news/tucker-carlson-on-the-war-path-with-trump-over-iran-but-insiders-dismiss-2028-buzz-from-brain-injured-press/))

The interview's most startling moment concerned the laptop that had obsessed Trump-era conservative media. Carlson, who as a Fox News anchor had devoted more than two hours of airtime to the laptop story within weeks of the *New York Post*'s 2020 article (per a Media Matters estimate), now put to rest the conspiracy theory that the laptop contained abusive images of children. "Can I just say one thing though, this is what I experienced during that time – the allegation that there was abusive minors proven on the laptop – that came from the Israelis," he told Biden, adding that a man "connected to [Benjamin] Netanyahu" had given a producer on his former Fox show the misinformation directly: "They were behind that." ([The Independent](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/tucker-carlson-claims-israel-was-pushing-hunter-biden-laptop-story-that-obsessed-trump-and-fox)) The Independent said it had asked the Israeli government for comment; Carlson offered no further details about the alleged exchange. ([The Independent](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/tucker-carlson-claims-israel-was-pushing-hunter-biden-laptop-story-that-obsessed-trump-and-fox))

Biden used the appearance to deny the corruption allegations outright — "Either I'm incredibly, incredibly, incredibly good, in active addiction as a crack addict. Or it doesn't exist," he said of the "Biden crime family" narrative — while Carlson admitted he was "implicated" in helping establish Trump's credibility before the 2024 election. ([The Independent](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/tucker-carlson-claims-israel-was-pushing-hunter-biden-laptop-story-that-obsessed-trump-and-fox)) Much of the conversation was given over to common ground: Biden said he admired conservative figures who had clashed with Trump, including Representative Thomas Massie, former congresswoman Marjorie Taylor Greene, and podcaster Candace Owens — "I love Candace," he said, calling her "thoughtful" and "generous" — and the two joked about a college recommendation letter Biden had once written for Carlson's son, who was not accepted. ([The Independent](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/tucker-carlson-claims-israel-was-pushing-hunter-biden-laptop-story-that-obsessed-trump-and-fox))

Biden kept up the tour into the fall. In June 2026 he had joked about entering politics and running for president in 2028; on an upcoming episode of California governor [Gavin Newsom](/people/gavin-newsom/)'s program, "This Is Gavin Newsom," Newsom playfully introduced him as "Presidential candidate Hunter Biden." ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/i-may-have-to-joe-bidens-son-hunter-hints-at-running-for-2028-us-presidential-elections/articleshow/133823287.cms)) And in a September 2026 appearance on John Catsimatidis's "Cats Roundtable," asked about his qualifications, he said, "If I get your endorsement, I may have to [run for president]" — naming the "50 million Americans that are still suffering with their addictions" as his driving concern and offering his commitment to the Constitution and the rule of law as his qualification, while stopping short of committing: "I love Gavin Newsom, personally." ([The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/i-may-have-to-joe-bidens-son-hunter-hints-at-running-for-2028-us-presidential-elections/articleshow/133823287.cms))

It does not date his Burisma board service. It gives no calendar date for the Connecticut disbarment hearing, since the AP report itself says only "Monday." It presents Carlson's claim that Israeli figures planted false laptop allegations as Carlson's claim — uncorroborated, with no further details offered and the Israeli government asked for comment — not as established fact. It does not claim the December 2024 pardon covered anything other than federal offenses, and it does not assert that Biden will run for president in 2028; his remarks were conditional and joking in tone.
