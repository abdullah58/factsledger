**Jaafar Jackson is the nephew of [Michael Jackson](/people/michael-jackson/) — the son of Jermaine Jackson — who played his uncle in the 2026 biopic [Michael](/creative-works/michael-2026-film/) in his acting debut, after a two-year audition process and casting search ([The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/entertainment/tv-film/news/michael-jacksons-nephew-star-king-pop-biopic-3235111); [Parade](https://parade.com/movies/how-michael-jacksons-nephew-got-major-role-in-upcoming-biopic)).** Before the film he was a singer and dancer: he began performing at 12, starred in the family reality show *Living with the Jacksons* in 2015, and released his debut single "Got Me Singing" in 2019 ([Parade](https://parade.com/movies/how-michael-jacksons-nephew-got-major-role-in-upcoming-biopic)). His casting was announced in January 2023 by [Lionsgate](https://www.thedailystar.net/entertainment/tv-film/news/michael-jacksons-nephew-star-king-pop-biopic-3235111), and the film went on to become the first biopic ever to cross $1 billion at the worldwide box office ([The Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2632277/michael-jackson-biopic-michael-ends-theatrical-run-with-first-biopic-to-pass-1bn-worldwide)).

## The two-year road to the role

Jaafar did not audition in the ordinary sense — the role found him. On *The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon* in April 2026, he said he first learned a biopic was coming when he read an article online about producer Graham King acquiring the rights to Michael Jackson's story; two months later King called him for a Zoom meeting, and Jaafar "already had a feeling about what this was going to lead to" ([Parade](https://parade.com/movies/how-michael-jacksons-nephew-got-major-role-in-upcoming-biopic)). At that lunch he told King he "never wanted to be an actor" — his real ambition had been professional golf — and that he grew up watching his uncle's music videos and films ([Parade](https://parade.com/movies/how-michael-jacksons-nephew-got-major-role-in-upcoming-biopic)). Six months later King got him an acting coach and scenes to work on, and what followed was a two-year process of auditioning and turning into Michael Jackson ([Parade](https://parade.com/movies/how-michael-jacksons-nephew-got-major-role-in-upcoming-biopic)).

The announcement, carried by the Associated Press in January 2023, framed the casting as the outcome of an extensive worldwide search: King said Jaafar organically personified "the spirit and personality of Michael" and was "the only person to take on this role," while director Antoine Fuqua cited his "natural ability to emulate Michael and such a great chemistry with the camera" ([The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/entertainment/tv-film/news/michael-jacksons-nephew-star-king-pop-biopic-3235111); [PEOPLE](https://people.com/michael-jackson-biopic-everything-to-know-11941550)). The family's matriarch sealed it: Katherine Jackson said "Jaafar embodies my son," calling it "so wonderful to see him carry on the Jackson legacy of entertainers and performers" ([The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/entertainment/tv-film/news/michael-jacksons-nephew-star-king-pop-biopic-3235111)). The biopic would be an authorized portrait, scripted by John Logan and produced with the cooperation of the singer's estate ([The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/entertainment/tv-film/news/michael-jacksons-nephew-star-king-pop-biopic-3235111)).

## A singer and dancer before he was an actor

Born in July 1996 in [Los Angeles](/places/los-angeles/), Jaafar grew up inside the Jackson family machinery — the second-youngest son of Jermaine Jackson, an original member of the Jackson 5 — but his first dream was golf, not the stage ([The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/entertainment/tv-film/news/michael-jacksons-nephew-star-king-pop-biopic-3235111); [Parade](https://parade.com/movies/how-michael-jacksons-nephew-got-major-role-in-upcoming-biopic)). Music pulled him back by age 12, and his pre-biopic career was a steady accumulation of family-adjacent credits: main cast member of *Living with the Jacksons* in 2015, where he sang Sam Cooke and Marvin Gaye covers; his debut single "Got Me Singing" in 2019; appearances in *The Jacksons: Next Generation*; and the music video for Tito Jackson's 2021 single "Love One Another" ([Parade](https://parade.com/movies/how-michael-jacksons-nephew-got-major-role-in-upcoming-biopic)).

## A praised debut inside a divisive film

The reception of *Michael* split cleanly: the performance versus the film. Moneycontrol's April 22, 2026 review roundup found one clear consensus across early reactions — Jaafar Jackson was the film's "biggest strength," praised for an uncanny resemblance and stage presence, with one viewer writing he is "truly making you forget he isn't the real thing," while the film itself was called a "sanitized," overly audience-friendly biopic that avoids the complex aspects of Michael Jackson's life ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/entertainment/michael-jackson-biopic-first-reviews-out-jaafar-jackson-shines-as-king-of-pop-but-faces-flak-for-lack-of-depth-article-13896637.html)). In the trailer, audiences saw him moonwalking and recreating the "Thriller" choreography opposite Colman Domingo's Joe Jackson, Nia Long's Katherine, and Miles Teller as attorney John Branca ([PEOPLE](https://people.com/michael-jackson-biopic-everything-to-know-11941550)).

The film's final worldwide total was **$1,028,706,645** — $372,303,388 from the US and Canada plus $656,403,257 internationally, about 63.8% of the gross — making it the first biopic ever to cross $1 billion, the highest-grossing biopic on record (passing *Oppenheimer* and *Bohemian Rhapsody*), and Lionsgate's biggest release of all time, per Box Office Mojo figures reported by The Tribune ([The Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2632277/michael-jackson-biopic-michael-ends-theatrical-run-with-first-biopic-to-pass-1bn-worldwide)). That number is the career-defining frame: for an acting debut, landing at the center of one of 2026's biggest box-office stories is an outcome no casting search could have promised.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is Jaafar Jackson?**
He is the nephew of Michael Jackson and the son of Jermaine Jackson, born in Los Angeles in July 1996. A singer and dancer from age 12, he played his uncle in the 2026 biopic *Michael* — his acting debut — after a two-year casting process led by producer Graham King and director Antoine Fuqua.

**How is Jaafar Jackson related to Michael Jackson?**
He is Michael Jackson's nephew: his father is Jermaine Jackson, Michael's older brother and a member of the Jackson 5. Michael Jackson's mother, Katherine Jackson, endorsed the casting, saying "Jaafar embodies my son."

**How did Jaafar Jackson get the role in Michael?**
He read online that Graham King had acquired the rights, then got a Zoom call from King two months later. After six months of work with an acting coach, he went through a two-year audition process. King said that even after a worldwide search, Jaafar was "the only person to take on this role."

**Did Jaafar Jackson have acting experience before Michael?**
No. The biopic was his film and acting debut. His background was in music and performance: family reality television in 2015, a debut single ("Got Me Singing") in 2019, and family music projects through 2021.

**What did critics say about Jaafar Jackson's performance?**
It was the film's consensus bright spot: early reactions called it the film's "biggest strength," praising his resemblance, dance moves and stage presence, even as the film itself was criticized as sanitized. The movie went on to gross $1.029 billion worldwide — the first biopic ever past $1 billion.
