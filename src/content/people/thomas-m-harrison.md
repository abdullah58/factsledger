# Thomas M. Harrison

**Thomas M. Harrison is a Nashville insurance executive who served as
Executive Vice President and Secretary of First Acceptance Corporation
from April 2004 to December 2007, after holding the same-titled roles
(Vice President and Secretary) at [USAuto Holdings, Inc.](/organizations/usauto-holdings/) from 1995.**
At the company's 2008 proxy he disclosed about 14.7% of outstanding
stock — the second-largest disclosed stake, just behind Stephen J.
Harrison's 14.8%.

## Career

Harrison joined USAuto Holdings as Vice President and Secretary in 1995.
When Liberté Investors Inc. acquired USAuto Holdings in April 2004 —
renaming itself First Acceptance Corporation — he became Executive Vice
President and Secretary of the combined company, serving until December
2007 (DEF 14A, Oct. 6, 2008).

Title note: a December 2003 merger announcement called him USAuto's
"COO," but the 2008 proxy — the later, controlling disclosure —
consistently describes his USAuto role as Vice President and Secretary.
This profile follows the proxy and flags the discrepancy rather than
resolving it.
